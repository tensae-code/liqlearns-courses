# Describing words in a sentence — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the describing word's seat — set
by the pattern across the countries' books: home one first, then
home two, then moving between them and stacking.

## Level 1 — home one: before the noun
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: seat any
describing word directly before its noun, in pairs and inside
whole sentences, in Bangladesh's own pictured order.

## Level 2 — home two: after be
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe
subjects across be with the verb standing (never "The mango
fresh"), and land descriptions after the sense verbs (smell,
taste) the same way.

## Level 3 — moving and stacking
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: move any truth
between the two homes without breaking it, stack two describers
before a noun with their comma, and keep comparing forms parked
for their own lesson — plain words, rightly seated.
