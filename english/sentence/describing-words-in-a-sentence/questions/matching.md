# Describing words in a sentence — matching

Match each truth to its other home, and each pair to its
picture-meaning.

**Set 1 (Level 3)** — the same truth, two homes.
1. a dirty shirt — **The shirt is dirty.**
2. The cat is angry. — **an angry cat**
3. a new bike — **The bike is new.**
4. The mango is bad. — **a bad mango**
5. an old bike — **The bike is old.**

**Set 2 (Level 1)** — Bangladesh's pairs to their opposites.
1. a fresh mango — **a bad mango**
2. a clean shirt — **a dirty shirt**
3. a new bike — **an old bike**
4. a happy cat — **an angry cat**

**Set 3 (Level 2)** — which home?
1. "I ate a fresh mango." — **home one (before the noun)**
2. "The flowers smell nice." — **home two (after the linking
   verb)**
3. "Her dress is red." — **home two**
4. "He rides a red bike." — **home one**
