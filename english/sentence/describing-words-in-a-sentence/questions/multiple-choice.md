# Describing words in a sentence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which pair stands in English order?
a) a mango fresh  b) a fresh mango  c) fresh a mango
**Answer: b)** — description before the thing (Bangladesh's
eight pairs, every one).

**2. (Level 1)** "She wears ______."
a) a shirt clean  b) clean a shirt  c) a clean shirt
**Answer: c)** — the pair travels as one block inside the
sentence.

**3. (Level 2)** "The bike ______ old."
a) is  b) (nothing — word alone)  c) are for bikes
**Answer: a) is** — home two needs its verb standing; be
carries the description.

**4. (Level 2)** The sense home: "The flowers smell ______."
a) nice  b) nicely the smell  c) a nice
**Answer: a) nice** — after the sense verb the describing
word lands plain (Mongolia's own sentence).

**5. (Level 3)** Move it home: "I bought a new bike" → the
same truth from home two is —
a) "The bike is new I bought."  b) "My bike is new."
c) "A bike new is."
**Answer: b) "My bike is new."** — truth kept, home changed.

**6. (Level 3)** "a big, sweet mango" shows —
a) a mistake  b) two describers stacked before one noun,
comma between equals  c) comparing
**Answer: b)** — plain words stacked; comparing would
change their shapes (bigger, sweeter) and belongs to its
own lesson.
