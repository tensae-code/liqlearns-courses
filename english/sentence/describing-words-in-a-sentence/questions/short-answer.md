Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seat it: (angry) + (cat), as a pair.
**Answer:** "an angry cat" — word before thing, an before
the vowel sound.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Build the sentence: She / wear / a / dirty
/ shirt — no: her shirt is clean. Use (clean, shirt).
**Answer:** "She wears a clean shirt."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe from home two: the soup / hot.
**Answer:** "The soup is hot."

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sense home: the bread / smell / good.
**Answer:** "The bread smells good." (Smell is the linking
verb; good describes, plain.)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Move it: "He has an old bike" → home two.
