# Describing words in a sentence — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Seat it: (angry) + (cat), as a pair.
**Answer:** "an angry cat" — word before thing, an before
the vowel sound.

**2. (Level 1)** Build the sentence: She / wear / a / dirty
/ shirt — no: her shirt is clean. Use (clean, shirt).
**Answer:** "She wears a clean shirt."

**3. (Level 2)** Describe from home two: the soup / hot.
**Answer:** "The soup is hot."

**4. (Level 2)** Sense home: the bread / smell / good.
**Answer:** "The bread smells good." (Smell is the linking
verb; good describes, plain.)

**5. (Level 3)** Move it: "He has an old bike" → home two.
**Answer:** "His bike is old."

**6. (Level 3)** Mongolia's discovery line states this
lesson's law and border. Give the law.
**Answer:** "Adjectives describe nouns" — comparing changes
the words' shapes and is its own lesson.
