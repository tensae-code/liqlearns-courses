# Describing words in a sentence — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Home One: Before the Noun · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Home Two: After Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moving and Stacking.
Fill each blank so the description sits in its home.

**Box:** fresh · clean · is · are · smells · happy

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** I ate a ______ mango. (just picked, good)
**Answer: fresh.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** She has a ______ cat. (it smiles, it plays)
**Answer: happy.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The shirt ______ clean.
**Answer: is.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The flower ______ sweet.
**Answer: smells** — the sense verb carries the -s for its
singular who; the describer after it stands plain.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Move it: "They are ______ boys." → "The
boys are ______." — the word does not change.
**Answer: (any true describer, e.g.) happy — happy.** The
word travels; the homes change.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "The mangoes ______ fresh and sweet."
**Answer: are** — be agrees with its who, even in home two.
