# Describing words in a sentence — ordering

Stand the words in sentence order. (This type exists here
because the two homes ARE orders: word-then-thing, or
thing-be-word.)

**1. (Level 1)** mango / a / fresh
**Answer:** a fresh mango.

**2. (Level 1)** wears / she / clean / a / shirt
**Answer:** She wears a clean shirt.

**3. (Level 2)** is / the cat / happy
**Answer:** The cat is happy.

**4. (Level 2)** smell / the flowers / nice
**Answer:** The flowers smell nice.

**5. (Level 3)** sweet / a / mango / big — stack them.
**Answer:** a big, sweet mango.

**6. (Level 3)** Unseat it — move home two back to home one
as a pair: "The bike is old."
**Answer:** an old bike — be drops away; the word walks in
front of its noun.
