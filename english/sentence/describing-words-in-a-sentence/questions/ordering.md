# Describing words in a sentence — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Home One: Before the Noun · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Home Two: After Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Moving and Stacking.
Stand the words in sentence order. (This type exists here
because the two homes ARE orders: word-then-thing, or
thing-be-word.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** mango / a / fresh
**Answer:** a fresh mango.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** wears / she / clean / a / shirt
**Answer:** She wears a clean shirt.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** is / the cat / happy
**Answer:** The cat is happy.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** smell / the flowers / nice
**Answer:** The flowers smell nice.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** sweet / a / mango / big — stack them.
**Answer:** a big, sweet mango.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Unseat it — move home two back to home one
as a pair: "The bike is old."
**Answer:** an old bike — be drops away; the word walks in
front of its noun.
