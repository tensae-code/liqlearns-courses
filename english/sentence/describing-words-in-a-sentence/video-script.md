# Video script — Describing words in a sentence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A word that DESCRIBES — where does it sit?
Two homes!

Home one — BEFORE the thing:
"a **fresh** mango!" "a **happy** cat!"
The word stands in front!

Home two — AFTER "is":
"The mango **is fresh**."
"The cat **is happy**."
Same truth, other side!

Watch it travel:
"a fresh mango" → "The mango is fresh."
Back and forth — easy!

Stack them: "a **big, sweet** mango!"
Turn them: "sweet **but** small!"

Now you. Describe something. Both homes!
That is describing — in English.
