# Levels — Structures of Modification

One lesson, 3 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Heads and modifiers: words, phrases, clauses | Identify the head of a construction and its modifiers — single words, modifying phrases and modifying clauses — including adjective series, coordinated adjectives and article scope. |
| 2. Adjectives and adverbs as heads | Analyse constructions where an adjective or an adverb is itself the head — modified by qualifiers, adverbs, nouns, verbs and phrases. |
| 3. Function words and prepositions as heads | Analyse the smallest heads: a qualifier modifying a function word, and a preposition as head — and how an adverb's place before or after a prepositional phrase changes the reading. |

A learner who finishes all sub lessons owns this lesson whole: modification — how words, phrases and clauses attach to a head word and change its meaning: single-word modifiers, modifying phrases and modifying clauses, and adjectives, adverbs, function words and prepositions as heads.
