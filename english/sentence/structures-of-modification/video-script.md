# Video Script — Structures of Modification

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: the word FIRE in a frame, bare and small]

One word, standing alone: the HEAD. Watch what gathers round it!

[Picture: words SMALL, UNDER THE TREE, THAT WOULD NOT LIGHT flying in on arrows to the frame]

Words… phrases… whole clauses — all attach, all change the meaning!

[Picture: the word COLD as a head, with VERY, STONE and FREEZING attaching to it]

Even an adjective can be a head! Stone cold! Freezing cold!

[Picture: tiny word ALMOST attaching to EVERYONE; a magnifying glass over them]

Even the smallest words carry modifiers. Every attachment is a decision!
