# Lesson Plan — Structures of Modification

**Unit:** Unit 13 — Joining and Describing

**One thing:** modification — how words, phrases and clauses attach to a head word and change its meaning: single-word modifiers, modifying phrases and modifying clauses, and adjectives, adverbs, function words and prepositions as heads.

## Sub lessons

1. Heads and modifiers: words, phrases, clauses
2. Adjectives and adverbs as heads
3. Function words and prepositions as heads

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Structures of modification I: the head, single-word modifiers, adjective series vs coordinated adjectives, article scope, the adverb modifying a whole predication, modifying phrases and clauses (Lesson 4, grammar box, pp.345–347).
- Structures of modification II: adjective as head, adverb as head — their qualifiers, adverbs, nouns, verbs and phrases as modifiers (Lesson 5, grammar boxes, pp.359–360).
- Structures of modification III: function words as head, prepositions as head; the adverb before vs after a prepositional phrase (Lesson 6, after W. Nelson Francis, pp.383–384).
- Intensifying a word's meaning by adding an adjective, adverb or qualifier — modification aimed at a chosen head (Lesson 6, Task 9, p.384).
