# Hands-On — Structures of Modification

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Arrow diagrams.** Take three sentences from a story. Draw each noun phrase as the book does: head in a box, arrows in from every modifier, each labelled word / phrase / clause.
2. **Strip and rebuild.** Take a rich sentence. Strip it to its heads alone; read the skeleton. Then rebuild it modifier by modifier, saying what each addition buys.
3. **Intensify ladder.** Start from 'the night'. Each learner adds one modifier (naming its kind) until the class judges the picture complete — then the class removes the weakest modifier and says why.
