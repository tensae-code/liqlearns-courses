# Video script — Adjective order

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

One adjective is easy. A big bag.

Two? A big old bag.

Three? Now English is strict.

Words stand in a line. The line never changes.

Number first. Two.

Then quality. Bad.

Size. Small. Shape. Age. Old.

Colour. Red. Origin. American.

Material last. Plastic. Then the thing. Shoes.

Two bad small old red American plastic shoes.

Scramble the line — red old small shoes — and it sounds broken.

Every word is right. The order is wrong.

Small. Old. Brown. Bag.

Pretty. Silk. Japanese. Kimono.

Learn the line. Then stack anything.

Now you try. Pick a thing. Dress it, in order.
