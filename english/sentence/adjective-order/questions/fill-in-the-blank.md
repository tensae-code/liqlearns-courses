# Adjective order — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** Name the missing box: quantity, quality, size, ______, age, colour, nationality, material. **Answer: shape** — Bolivia's Year 6 order table, Topic 5, p.221.

**2. (Level 2)** "The big, ______, yellow blanket" (Cambodia's example — the shape word) **Answer: square** — size – shape – colour: "the big, square, yellow blanket", Cambodia Grade 10, Unit 21.

**3. (Level 2)** In "two bad small old red American plastic shoes", the word "American" fills the ______ box. **Answer: nationality** — Bolivia's worked example walks the full line to the noun.

**4. (Level 3)** "A handsome young Mongolian boy": young fills ______, Mongolian fills ______. **Answer: age / nationality (origin)** — Mongolia's labelled example — OPINION AGE NATIONALITY, p.52.
