# Adjective order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which stack stands in the book order? a) a brown old small bag  b) a small old brown bag  c) an old brown small bag **Answer: b)** — size – age – colour: Mongolia's own discovery example, English IX, Unit 4, p.52.

**2. (Level 1)** In the order table, the box that stands LAST, nearest the noun, is — a) quantity  b) colour  c) material **Answer: c) material** — material closes the line in both Bolivia's and Sudan's tables.

**3. (Level 2)** "A pretty silk Japanese kimono" runs — a) quality – material – origin  b) origin – material – quality  c) material – quality – origin **Answer: a)** — Mongolia's labelled example, p.52: QUALITY MATERIAL ORIGIN.

**4. (Level 3)** Repair: "a leather old big bag" → a) a big old leather bag  b) a leather big old bag  c) an old leather big bag **Answer: a)** — size (big) – age (old) – material (leather) – noun.
