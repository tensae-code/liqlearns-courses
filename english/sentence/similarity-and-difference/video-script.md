# Video script — Similarity and Difference

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot holds two identical cups.] "The SAME as! They look ALIKE!"

[The mascot holds two nearly identical cups.] "SIMILAR to — nearly the same!"

[The mascot holds a cup and a boot.] "DIFFERENT from! UNLIKE the cup!"

[The mascot stands between a big house and a small house.] "This one is big, WHEREAS that one is small!"
