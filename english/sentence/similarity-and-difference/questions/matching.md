## Matching

Match each form to its job. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. look like — a
2. the same as — b
3. similar to — c
4. different from — d
5. unlike — e
6. whereas — f

a) a full match, for faces and seeming
b) a full match, for things
c) a near match
d) a mismatch
e) a mismatch at the front of the sentence
f) the hinge joining two sides

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (look like — seeming), 2–b (the same as — full match), 3–c (similar to — near), 4–d (different from), 5–e (unlike + thing first), 6–f (whereas).
