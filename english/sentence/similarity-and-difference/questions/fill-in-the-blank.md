## Fill in the blank

1. "They have ___ same teacher." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: the** — have the same…

2. "The twins are ___." (matching) (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: alike** — be alike.

3. "This road is ___ to that one." (nearly) (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: similar** — similar to.

4. "Summer here is ___ from winter." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: different** — different from.

5. "___ my brother, I am quiet." (contrast at the front) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Unlike** — unlike opens the contrast.

6. "I work by day, ___ he works by night." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: whereas** — the hinge word.
