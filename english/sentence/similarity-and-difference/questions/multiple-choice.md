## Multiple choice

1. "Aly's phone is ___ yours." (a full match) (Sub lesson 1)
   a) similar to
   b) the same as
   c) different from
   **Answer: b) the same as** — a full match takes the same as.

2. "The two sisters look ___." (they match each other) (Sub lesson 1)
   a) alike
   b) whereas
   c) unlike
   **Answer: a) alike** — be alike, after the verb.

3. "Her car is ___ mine." (nearly the same, not identical) (Sub lesson 2)
   a) similar to
   b) the same as
   c) different from
   **Answer: a) similar to** — near matching takes similar to.

4. "Boutilimitt is ___ Nouakchott." (they do not match) (Sub lesson 3)
   a) similar to
   b) different from
   c) the same as
   **Answer: b) different from** — mismatching takes different from.

5. "___ Nouakchott, Boutilimitt is smaller." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Similar
   b) Unlike
   c) Whereas
   **Answer: b) Unlike** — unlike + the thing opens the contrast.

6. "He likes tea, ___ his brother prefers coffee." (Sub lesson 4)
   a) whereas
   b) similar to
   c) alike
   **Answer: a) whereas** — whereas hinges the two sides.

7. The corrected sentence is: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "My English is no like natives."
   b) "My English is not the same as a native's."
   c) "My English is alike natives."
   **Answer: b) "My English is not the same as a native's."** — the book's printed model is wrong; not the same as is the right shape.
