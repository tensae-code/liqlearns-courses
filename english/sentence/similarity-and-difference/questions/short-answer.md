## Short answer

1. Say two brothers match, with look like. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "They look like brothers."** — look like + the thing.

2. Say two bags fully match, with the same as. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "The two bags are the same." / "This bag is the same as that one."** — the same as for full matches.

3. Say her house nearly matches yours. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Her house is similar to mine."** — similar to for near matches.

4. Say your town does not match the capital, with unlike. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "Unlike the capital, my town is small."** — unlike + the thing, at the front.

5. Join with whereas: Ali is tall. Omar is short. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "Ali is tall, whereas Omar is short."** — whereas hinges the sides.
