# Video script — Present actions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two faces of NOW!

Face one — EVERY DAY:
"Mita **gets up** at six."
"He **walks** to school."
The S stands guard for he and she!

Face two — RIGHT NOW:
Look! "She **is cooking**!"
"They **are playing**!"
Be… plus ING. The verb is MOVING.

Clues! "Every day" — face one.
"Now! Look! Listen!" — face two.

But careful — some verbs say NO to ING:
know, like, see. "I **know** it." Plain face!

Now you. Say your day. Say your moment.
Two faces — both yours!
