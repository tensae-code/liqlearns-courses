# Present actions — ordering

Stand the words in sentence order. (This type exists here
because both faces are order-machines: be before -ing, the
engine before the who.)

**1. (Level 2)** is / she / cooking / rice
**Answer:** She is cooking rice.

**2. (Level 2)** playing / are / they / outside
**Answer:** They are playing outside.

**3. (Level 1)** walks / he / to school / every day
**Answer:** He walks to school every day — the -s already
sewn on walks.

**4. (Level 3)** now / you / are / reading / ?
**Answer:** Are you reading now? — question order: be walks
front.

**5. (Level 3)** does / on Fridays / cook / she / ?
**Answer:** Does she cook on Fridays? — engine front, verb
bare.

**6. (Level 3)** Unscramble the mixed one and name its
fault: Fridays / is / cooking / she / on
**Answer:** "She is cooking on Fridays" stands ordered but
wears the wrong face for its clue — Fridays wants the
every-day face: "She cooks on Fridays."
