# Present actions — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Every-Day Face · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Right-Now Face · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing the Face.
Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "He walks to school" needs the -s because
the subject is he.
**True** — he/she/it, every-day face: the -s stands guard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I am read a book now" is a right now-
sentence.
**False** — the -ing half is missing: "I am reading a book
now." Both parts must show.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The right-now face asks questions with do
and does.
**False** — it asks with the swap: be walks to the front
("Is she cooking?"). Do/does serve the every-day face.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I am knowing the way home" is good
English.
**False** — know is on Libya's refusal list; "I know the way
home," plain face, even right now.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "She cooks on Fridays" and "She is cooking
now" can both be true of the same woman.
**True** — two faces, two truths: the habit and the moment.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Simple truths like "The sun rises in the
east" wear the -ing coat.
**False** — truths wear the plain face (Ghana: simple truths
are the simple present's job).
