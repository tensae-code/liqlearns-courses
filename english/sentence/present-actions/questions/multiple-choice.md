# Present actions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Every-Day Face · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Right-Now Face · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing the Face.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "He ______ to school every day."
a) walk  b) walks  c) is walk
**Answer: b) walks** — he/she/it takes -s in the every-day
face.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A simple truth wears the plain face:
"Lemons ______ sour."
a) are being  b) is  c) are
**Answer: c) are** — Ghana's own truth; truths refuse -ing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Look! The baby ______."
a) cries  b) is crying  c) cry
**Answer: b) is crying** — Look! is a right-now clue; be +
-ing whole.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The -ing of "run" is —
a) runing  b) running  c) runeing
**Answer: b) running** — short verbs double the last letter.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ she cooking now?"
a) Does  b) Is  c) Do
**Answer: b) Is** — the right-now face asks with the swap;
be is already there (Mongolia's questions box).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is built WRONG?
a) I know the answer.  b) I am knowing the answer.  c) I am
reading the answer.
**Answer: b)** — know stands on Libya's refusal list (be,
like, know, see, understand).
