# Present actions — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Every-Day Face · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Right-Now Face · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing the Face.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the every-day sentence: Mita / get up
/ at six.
**Answer:** "Mita gets up at six" — the -s on gets, because
Mita is she.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ask it with the engine: "He walks to
school."
**Answer:** "Does he walk to school?" — walk bare after
does.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sew the -ing: make, swim, read, write.
**Answer:** making (e drops), swimming (double letter),
reading, writing (e drops).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Say it right-now: "They play football"
(happening now).
**Answer:** "They are playing football."

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which face, and why: "She is cooking rice
on Fridays."
**Answer:** mixed wrongly — Fridays is an every-day clue;
"It should be: She cooks rice on Fridays." (Or drop Fridays
for a true now.) Face follows clue.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Libya's reference gives the right-now face
three uses. Name two.
**Answer:** any two of: happening now / around now; changing
situations; planned future (Unit 1, p.10).
