# Present actions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank so the sentence wears the right face.

**1. (Level 1)** The cow ______ grass every day. (eat)
**Answer: eats** — every-day face, it takes -s.

**2. (Level 1)** We ______ up at seven. (get)
**Answer: get** — we takes no -s.

**3. (Level 2)** She ______ cooking now. (be)
**Answer: is** — be must show before -ing.

**4. (Level 2)** They are ______ football in the field.
(play)
**Answer: playing.**

**5. (Level 3)** Listen! Someone ______ at the door. (knock)
**Answer: is knocking** — Listen! calls the right-now face,
both parts.

**6. (Level 3)** I ______ this book very much. (like — think
before you sew!)
**Answer: like** — like refuses the -ing coat (Libya's list).
