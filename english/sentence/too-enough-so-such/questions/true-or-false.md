# Too, enough, so, such — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Too" means the same as "very" in "too expensive". **Answer: False** — Bolivia's rule: too = more than enough, not satisfactory — it carries a complaint very does not (Year 6, p.217).

**2. (Level 2)** "Such fast that…" is a good result sentence. **Answer: False** — fast alone is an adjective/adverb — it takes so…that; such needs a noun phrase (such a fast car that…).

**3. (Level 3)** Vietnam's Grade 12 book files so…that / such…that under adverbial clauses of result. **Answer: True** — Unit 7's Grammar box, printed p.91 — manner and result clauses, as filed.
