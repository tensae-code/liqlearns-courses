# Too, enough, so, such — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by the pusher the item actually asks about. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Too — the Door Closes, and Sub lesson 2 — Enough — the Door Opens · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — So…That — a Result After an Adjective or Adverb, and Sub lesson 4 — Such…That — a Result After a Noun Phrase · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — The Wider Intensifier Set, except items that ask about one pusher's own seat, which are marked with that pusher's sub lesson.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Too" means the same as "very" in "too expensive". **Answer: False** — Bolivia's rule: too = more than enough, not satisfactory — it carries a complaint very does not (Year 6, p.217).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** "Such fast that…" is a good result sentence. **Answer: False** — fast alone is an adjective/adverb — it takes so…that; such needs a noun phrase (such a fast car that…).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vietnam's Grade 12 book files so…that / such…that under adverbial clauses of result. **Answer: True** — Unit 7's Grammar box, printed p.91 — manner and result clauses, as filed.
