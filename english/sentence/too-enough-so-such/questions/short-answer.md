# Too, enough, so, such — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Take "the box is heavy": close its door with too, whole sentence. **Answer: (Learner's own, e.g. "The box is too heavy to lift.")** — too before the adjective, to-infinitive after it.

**2. (Level 2)** Say that a film was long, with such, and give its result. **Answer: (Learner's own, e.g. "It was such a long film that we left early.")** — such (a) + adjective + noun + that + result (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 7).

**3. (Level 3)** Why is "enough strong to lift it" wrong? Say it right in one line. **Answer: Enough stands AFTER its adjective: "strong enough to lift it."** — the one seat-swap this family never allows (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 18).
