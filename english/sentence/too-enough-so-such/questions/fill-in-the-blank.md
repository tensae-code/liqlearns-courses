# Too, enough, so, such — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by the pusher the item actually asks about. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Too — the Door Closes, and Sub lesson 2 — Enough — the Door Opens · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — So…That — a Result After an Adjective or Adverb, and Sub lesson 4 — Such…That — a Result After a Noun Phrase · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — The Wider Intensifier Set, except items that ask about one pusher's own seat, which are marked with that pusher's sub lesson.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She is too ______ to reach the shelf. (short) **Answer: short** — too + adjective + to-infinitive: too short to reach.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The water is warm ______ to swim. (enough — mind its seat) **Answer: enough** — enough stands after the adjective: warm enough to swim (Bolivia, Year 6, p.217).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** He spoke ______ quietly that nobody heard him. (so / such) **Answer: so** — an adverb alone (quietly) takes so…that (Kuwait 9B, p.62).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** It was ______ good weather that we walked all day. (so / such — no a/an) **Answer: such** — weather is uncountable, so such carries no a/an — Kuwait's countable/uncountable form rules, p.62.
