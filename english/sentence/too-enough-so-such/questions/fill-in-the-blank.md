# Too, enough, so, such — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** She is too ______ to reach the shelf. (short) **Answer: short** — too + adjective + to-infinitive: too short to reach.

**2. (Level 2)** The water is warm ______ to swim. (enough — mind its seat) **Answer: enough** — enough stands after the adjective: warm enough to swim (Bolivia, Year 6, p.217).

**3. (Level 2)** He spoke ______ quietly that nobody heard him. (so / such) **Answer: so** — an adverb alone (quietly) takes so…that (Kuwait 9B, p.62).

**4. (Level 3)** It was ______ good weather that we walked all day. (so / such — no a/an) **Answer: such** — weather is uncountable, so such carries no a/an — Kuwait's countable/uncountable form rules, p.62.
