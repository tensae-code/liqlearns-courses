# Video script — Too, enough, so, such

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

The stone is heavy.

Too heavy. I cannot lift it.

Too heavy TO LIFT. Too closes the door.

Now the man. Strong.

Strong enough. Strong enough TO LIFT it.

Enough opens the door. Enough stands after the word.

Strong enough. Old enough. Cheap enough.

Now push harder. The result comes.

She ran SO fast THAT she won.

It was SUCH a hot day THAT we stayed home.

So — with the describing word alone.

Such — with the thing itself. Such a day.

Very tired. Really hot. Absolutely perfect.

Small pushers, big work.

Now you try. Close one door. Open one. Push one to its result.
