# Lesson plan — The Future Perfect

**Course:** English (from the world's government textbooks)
**Title:** The Future Perfect
**Lesson:** The Future Perfect
**Place in the course:** Sentence course, Unit 20 (The Future in Detail) — beside future-perfect-continuous-tense and future-continuous.

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Saying that something will be finished by a point in the future: *will have* + the past participle, usually with *by* + a time.

Level: set by the material's own depth, not by a school grade — the learner's level inside the lesson is their sub lesson.

## Why this lesson exists

The sweep found the future perfect simple taught on its own in Kazakhstan's Full Blast Grade 9 (Module 4) with no lesson of its own in the course — the course carried the future perfect CONTINUOUS but not the simple future perfect the book teaches. Under the everything-goes-in rule the strand gets its own lesson.

## Sub lessons

1. Will have done — the shape
2. Using it — deadlines and things finished by a set time

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

Nothing is forced. A learner can open this lesson directly; the course shows easier lessons only as a soft hint.

## What the learner can do when they finish

Use what this lesson teaches on their own, in speech and in writing, without the lesson open in front of them.

## What you need

Nothing special — the learner's book or device and their explain companion.

## The run of the lesson

1. **See the sub lessons listed.** The learner sees both parts from the start; the second is locked.
2. **Sub lesson 1.** Learn it in lesson/lesson.md, with the media slots in hands-on.md where they fit.
3. **Prove it.** At most 5 questions drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set, mixed with earlier parts where the course has them. Fail: review the questions and answers just done, then a new set — never the same set, and nothing ever re-locks.
4. **Sub lesson 2.** Opens when sub lesson 1 is proved.
5. **Do It Yourself.** The closing block in lesson/lesson.md.

## Add-ons

- The video script lives in video-script.md (simple voice) for the parts that suit a video.
- The info button (the circled i at the top of the lesson page) carries the explanation; the lesson flow itself goes straight to the doing.

## If the learner is stuck

Send them back one step inside the same sub lesson, not to another lesson — every lesson stands alone.
