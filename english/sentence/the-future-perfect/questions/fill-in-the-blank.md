# Fill in the blank — The Future Perfect

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. By next week, I will _____ read the whole book. (have / has / had)
   **Answer: have** (Sub lesson 1)

2. By 8 o'clock, we will have _____ dinner. (eat / ate / eaten)
   **Answer: eaten** (Sub lesson 1)

3. _____ Friday, the builders will have finished the wall.
   **Answer: By** (Sub lesson 2)

4. By the time you phone, I will have _____ home. (go / went / gone)
   **Answer: gone** (Sub lesson 2)
