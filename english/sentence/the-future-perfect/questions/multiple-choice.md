# Multiple choice — The Future Perfect

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Which sentence is in the future perfect?
   A) I will finish the book.
   B) I will have finished the book by Friday.
   C) I have finished the book.
   D) I am finishing the book.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Complete the sentence: "By 2030, they ___ the new road."
   A) will have built
   B) will builds
   C) have build
   D) built yesterday
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 1)

3. What comes after "will have"?
   A) the -ing form
   B) the past participle
   C) the infinitive with to
   D) the present simple
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Which sentence uses the future perfect for a deadline?
   A) The teacher marks tests on Mondays.
   B) The teacher will have marked the tests by Monday.
   C) The teacher is marking tests now.
   D) The teacher marked the tests.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

5. "By the time we arrive, the film will have started" means:
   A) the film starts after we arrive
   B) the film starts before we arrive
   C) the film never starts
   D) we start the film
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. Which is the negative future perfect?
   A) She will not has saved enough.
   B) She won't have saved enough by June.
   C) She doesn't will have saved.
   D) She will have not save.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
