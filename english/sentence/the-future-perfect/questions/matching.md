# Matching — The Future Perfect

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the beginning to the correct ending.
   1. By June, she will have…
   2. By 2030, they will have…
   3. By eight, we will have…
   a. built the bridge.
   b. eaten dinner.
   c. passed her exam.
   **Answer: 1-c, 2-a, 3-b** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the sentence to what it talks about.
   1. The teacher will have marked the tests by Monday.
   2. By the time we arrive, the film will have started.
   3. In June we will have lived here ten years.
   a. counting forward to a point
   b. a deadline
   c. one thing finished before another
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a** (Sub lesson 2)
