# True or false — The Future Perfect

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. "Will have" is followed by the past participle.
   **Answer: True** (Sub lesson 1)

2. "By Friday, I will have finish" is correct.
   **Answer: False** — it must be "will have finished". (Sub lesson 1)

3. The future perfect is used when the finishing point matters.
   **Answer: True** (Sub lesson 2)

4. "Won't have" is the short negative of the future perfect.
   **Answer: True** (Sub lesson 2)
