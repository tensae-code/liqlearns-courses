# Short answer — The Future Perfect

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Write the future perfect of "finish" after "I will".
   **Answer:** I will have finished. (Sub lesson 1)

2. Make this a question: "You will have eaten by eight."
   **Answer:** Will you have eaten by eight? (Sub lesson 1)

3. Write one future-perfect sentence about a school project and Friday.
   **Answer:** Any sentence with will have + past participle and a by-time point, e.g. "By Friday, we will have finished our project." (Sub lesson 2)

4. Why does "By the time we arrive" NOT take "will" in its own part?
   **Answer:** The will have part carries the future; the by-the-time part stays in the present form. (Sub lesson 2)
