# Hands-on — Noun Phrases

The room and any story page are the practice material here.

## Grow the phrase

Start with one noun on the board — "house". Each learner adds one word or phrase: "a big house… a big old house… a big old house on the hill… a big old house on the hill full of children." The phrase may only grow, never restart.

## Phrase hunt

Open any story. Find the longest noun phrase on the page. Read it aloud whole, name its head noun, and count its parts.
