# Video script — Noun Phrases

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[An apple appears.] "Apple. A BIG apple. A big RED apple! The words TRAVEL with the noun — that is a noun phrase!"

[Blocks stack beside a cat picture.] "A cat — a SMALL cat — a small BLACK cat! Stack them in order!"

[A man in a blue shirt walks in.] "The man IN THE BLUE SHIRT — words AFTER the noun count too!"

[A bottle appears.] "A bottle FULL OF WATER — a whole phrase describing! Build BIG phrases!"
