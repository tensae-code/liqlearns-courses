## Matching

Match the phrase to its kind. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. a small cat — a
2. my new shoes — b
3. a big red ball — c
4. her two young brothers — d
5. a house on the hill — e
6. a bottle full of water — f

a) noun group — one describing word
b) noun group — pointing word + describing word
c) stacked group — size + colour
d) stacked group — number + describing word
e) noun phrase — words after the noun
f) noun phrase — adjective phrase inside

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (small describes cat), 2–b (my points, new describes), 3–c (big = size, red = colour), 4–d (two = number, young describes), 5–e (on the hill follows the noun), 6–f (full of water is an adjective phrase).
