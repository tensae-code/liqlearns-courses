## True or false

1. Describing words in a noun group stand after the noun: "cat small a". (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — "a small cat": describing words stand before the noun.

2. The head noun is the word everything else in the group points at. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the noun is the head of its group.

3. "A red big ball" follows the taught stacking order. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — size before colour: "a big red ball."

4. A noun phrase can have words after the noun. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — "a house on the hill."

5. "Full of water" is an adjective phrase. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — it describes like an adjective, in phrase shape.

6. An adjective phrase comes before the noun: "full of water a bottle". (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — adjective phrases sit AFTER the noun: "a bottle full of water."
