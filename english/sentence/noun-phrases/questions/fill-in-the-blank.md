## Fill in the blank

1. In "the tall tree", the head ___ is "tree". (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: noun** — describing words stand before the head noun.

2. Build the group: "a ___ cat" (use: small). (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: small** — describing word + noun.

3. Put in order: "a ___ ___ box" (wooden, small). (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: small wooden** — size before material.

4. "The story ___ a brave girl" — words after the noun. (use: about) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: about** — after-noun words belong to the phrase.

5. "A house ___ the hill" (use: on). (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: on** — the phrase points at one house: the one on the hill.

6. "Food ___ in vitamins is good for you." (use: rich) (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: rich** — "rich in vitamins" is an adjective phrase.
