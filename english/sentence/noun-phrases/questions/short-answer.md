## Short answer

1. Build a noun group for a door near you. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "The brown door."** (any describing word + noun) — the group names one door exactly.

2. Grow "a dog" with two describing words. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "A big black dog."** — stacked in order: size, colour, noun.

3. Build a noun phrase with words after the noun. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "The girl in the red dress."** — head noun + after-noun words.

4. Name the head noun in "the teacher with the loud voice". (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "Teacher."** — everything else in the phrase points at her.

5. Use an adjective phrase after a noun. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "A boy afraid of dogs."** — adjective phrase after the noun it describes.
