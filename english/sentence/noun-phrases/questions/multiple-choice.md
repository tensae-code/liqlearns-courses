## Multiple choice

1. In "a small cat", the head noun is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) small  b) cat  c) a
   **Answer: b) cat** — the noun is the head; describing words travel with it.

2. A noun group is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a verb with helpers  b) a noun with its describing words  c) a whole sentence
   **Answer: b)** — noun + describing words = one group.

3. Which is correctly stacked? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "a red big ball"  b) "a big red ball"  c) "ball big red a"
   **Answer: b) a big red ball** — size before colour before the noun.

4. "Her two young brothers" — how many describing words travel with "brothers"? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) one  b) two  c) three (two, young — and "her" points the group)
   **Answer: c)** — stacked words each add one piece of the picture.

5. In "the man in the blue shirt", the head noun is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) shirt  b) man  c) blue
   **Answer: b) man** — the after-noun words also point at the man.

6. A noun phrase can carry words… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) only before the noun  b) before AND after the noun  c) only in questions
   **Answer: b)** — "a house on the hill" carries after-noun words.

7. "Full of water" in "a bottle full of water" is… (Sub lesson 4)
   a) a verb  b) an adjective phrase describing the bottle  c) a new noun
   **Answer: b)** — adjective phrases sit after the noun they describe.
