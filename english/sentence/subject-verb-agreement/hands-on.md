# Hands-on — Subject-verb agreement

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The curtain test.** The learner writes five
   sentences with phrases between subject and verb (The
   basket of mangoes … on the mat). The companion covers
   the middle phrase with a hand; the learner reads the
   skeleton and chooses the verb, then uncovers and
   checks.
2. **One out of many.** Real objects on a table — pens,
   cups, books. The companion points: "One of the pens
   …" and the learner must finish with is / has / lies,
   never are. Then the whole set: "All of the pens are…"
3. **Weigh it as one lump.** Price cards, time cards,
   distance cards (fifteen dollars, three hours, five
   kilometres). The learner makes one true sentence per
   card with is — then one with a counted plural (three
   coins are…) and hears the difference.
4. **The police game.** The companion fires subjects —
   police, news, people, maths, furniture, one of us —
   and the learner answers with is or are in under two
   seconds each. A miss restarts the round.
