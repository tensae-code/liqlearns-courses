# Subject-verb agreement — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Subject-verb agreement — the base rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Subject-verb agreement — the tricky subjects · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 2 — Subject-verb agreement — the tricky subjects.

Fill each gap with the right verb form.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** There ______ three chairs in the room. (be, present)
**Answer: are** — after there, the verb answers to what follows — three chairs (South Africa, HL Grade 5 Workbook 1, p.28).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The police ______ looking for the bicycle. (be, present)
**Answer: are** — police is always plural — Nepal's "police were…" in the present dress.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The news ______ good today. (be, present)
**Answer: is** — news ends in -s but names one body of news; it is singular.
