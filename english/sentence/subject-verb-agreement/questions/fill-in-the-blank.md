# Subject-verb agreement — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right verb form.

**1. (Level 1)** There ______ three chairs in the room. (be, present)
**Answer: are** — after there, the verb answers to what follows — three chairs (South Africa, HL Grade 5 Workbook 1, p.28).

**2. (Level 2)** The police ______ looking for the bicycle. (be, present)
**Answer: are** — police is always plural — Nepal's "police were…" in the present dress.

**3. (Level 3)** The news ______ good today. (be, present)
**Answer: is** — news ends in -s but names one body of news; it is singular.
