# Subject-verb agreement — matching

Match each subject to the verb that agrees with it.

**1. (Level 1)** Each of the answers — is / are
**Answer: is** — each of, like one of, answers to the single one.

**2. (Level 2)** Five kilometres — is / are
**Answer: is** — a distance weighed as one lump is singular, like twenty dollars.

**3. (Level 3)** Either the pupils or the teacher — is / are
**Answer: is** — with either…or the verb answers to the nearer subject — the teacher.
