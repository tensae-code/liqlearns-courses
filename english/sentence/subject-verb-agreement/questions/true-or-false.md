# Subject-verb agreement — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In "one of the boys is ill", the verb is singular because the subject is one.
**Answer: True** — Nepal's Grade 7 concord unit files the shape itself: one of my friends is….

**2. (Level 2)** "Furniture" takes a plural verb because furniture is many things.
**Answer: False** — furniture is uncountable — one body, singular verb: The furniture was cheap.

**3. (Level 3)** In "the box of eggs are broken", the verb is right because eggs is plural.
**Answer: False** — the subject standing before the of-phrase decides: box … is (Sierra Leone, SSS2–SSS3 rule).
