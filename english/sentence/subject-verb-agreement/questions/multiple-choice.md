# Subject-verb agreement — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Subject-verb agreement — the base rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Subject-verb agreement — the tricky subjects · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Subject-verb agreement — the traps.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The dogs ___ in the yard." a) is b) are c) was (for a present picture)
**Answer: b)** — a plural subject takes a plural verb — the base rule (Sierra Leone, SSS1, Lesson 123).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "One of my friends ___ a bicycle." a) have b) has c) are having
**Answer: b)** — the subject is one, not friends — Nepal's filed example shape (Grade 7, Unit 14).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Twenty dollars ___ too much for this pen." a) are b) is c) were (counted as coins)
**Answer: b)** — a sum weighed as one price is singular — Nepal's "twenty dollars is…".

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "The basket of apples ___ heavy." a) are b) is c) were
**Answer: b)** — the subject before the of-phrase — basket — decides the verb (Sierra Leone, SSS2, Lessons 77–78).
