# Subject-verb agreement — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Say it right: "The farmer (plough / ploughs) his field every day."
**Answer: The farmer ploughs his field every day.** — a singular third-person subject takes the -s form (South Africa, HL Grade 5 Workbook 1, p.28).

**2. (Level 2)** Write one true sentence beginning "One of my…" with the right verb.
**Answer: One of my friends is … (any true ending).** — the verb must be singular — it answers to one, not to the plural noun after of.

**3. (Level 3)** Fix it and say why: "The list of names are on the door."
**Answer: The list of names is on the door.** — the subject is list; the of-phrase is a curtain, not the subject (Sierra Leone, SSS3, Lesson 105).
