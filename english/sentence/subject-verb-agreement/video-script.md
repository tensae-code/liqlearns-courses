# Video script — Subject-verb agreement

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

The verb must match the subject.
One — is. Was. Has. Plays.
Many — are. Were. Have. Play.

Easy. But subjects can hide.

"One of my friends is ill."
Many friends. But the subject is ONE.
One … is.

"Twenty dollars is a lot of money."
Twenty coins. But the price is one lump.
One lump … is.
The same for time. "Two hours is enough."
The same for distance. "Ten kilometres is far."

"The police were at the gate."
Police is a crowd word. It is always many.
Police … were. People … were.

"Information is free here."
You cannot count information.
Uncountable things are one body. Is.

"The news is good today."
News ends with S. But it is one thing.
News … is. Maths … is.

Watch the curtain.
"The box of eggs is on the table."
Box … of eggs … is.
The subject stands before OF.
Pull the curtain aside. Read the skeleton.

"There is one egg." "There are three eggs."
After THERE, look at what comes next.

Now you try.
One of my friends…
Twenty dollars…
The police…
Say the verb. Hear it match.
