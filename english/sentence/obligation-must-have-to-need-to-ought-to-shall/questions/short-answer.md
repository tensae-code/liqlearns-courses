## Short answer

1. Say an inside-rule duty with must. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I must study tonight."** — must = the rule inside.

2. Say an outside-rule duty with have to. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I have to wear the uniform."** — have to = the rule outside.

3. Tell me I don't need to come, with needn't. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "You needn't come."** — needn't + bare verb.

4. Advise a friend to apologize, with ought to. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "You ought to apologize."** — ought to = moral advice.

5. Read this rule as a formal policy: workers — follow the safety rules. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "Workers shall follow the safety rules."** — shall in written rules.
