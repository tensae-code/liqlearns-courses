## True or false

1. Must and have to differ by where the rule lives. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — must inside, have to outside.

2. "Needn't to come" is correct English. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — needn't takes the bare verb; the book's candidate form is ungrammatical.

3. Ought to is stronger than must. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — ought to is the weaker, moral word.

4. Shall is common in everyday talk. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — shall lives in formal writing and rules.

5. "Don't need to" and "needn't" say the same thing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — both name no necessity.

6. Have to changes for he/she: has to. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — he/she has to.
