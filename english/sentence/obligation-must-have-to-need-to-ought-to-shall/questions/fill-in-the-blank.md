## Fill in the blank

1. "I ___ study — I decided it myself." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: must** — the inside rule.

2. "She ___ to wear a uniform at work." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: has** — have to, third person: has to.

3. "We ___ to leave now — the last bus!" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: need** — need to — necessity.

4. "You needn't ___ early." (one word) (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: come** — needn't + bare verb — never needn't to.

5. "You ___ to say sorry." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: ought** — ought to — moral duty.

6. "Visitors ___ sign the book." (a formal notice) (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: shall** — shall in formal wording.
