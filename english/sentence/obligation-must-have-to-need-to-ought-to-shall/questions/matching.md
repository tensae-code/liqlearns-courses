## Matching

Match each duty word to where its rule lives. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. must — a
2. have to — b
3. need to — c
4. ought to — d
5. shall — e

a) necessity — it cannot be skipped
b) the formal word of written rules
c) the rule inside you
d) the moral, weaker duty
e) the rule outside you

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (must = inside), 2–e (have to = outside), 3–a (need to = necessity), 4–d (ought to = moral, weaker), 5–b (shall = formal writing).
