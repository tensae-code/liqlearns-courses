# Video script — Obligation Compared — Must, Have To, Need To, Ought To, Shall

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot points at his own chest.] "I MUST study — the rule is in ME!"

[The mascot points at a rule book.] "I HAVE TO wear it — the rule is in the BOOK!"

[The mascot holds up three fingers, then five.] "NEED TO… OUGHT TO… and in big papers — SHALL!"
