# Hands-on — Obligation Compared — Must, Have To, Need To, Ought To, Shall

## The rule book game

Cards carry duties: wear a uniform, apologize to a friend, rest when ill, follow the safety rules, be honest, arrive at eight. Each card must be spoken with its right word before it can be placed on the ladder drawn on the board — five rungs, one per word.

## Interview desk

In pairs: the employer reads duties from a card (company policies with shall, habits with must, necessities with need to); the candidate repeats each duty back in plain have to words and asks one ought to question. Then swap.
