# The passive in every tense — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Report one real work near you that is in progress, in the continuous passive. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in is/are being + participle)** — the receiver must be mid-process, doer unnamed.

**2. (Level 2)** Turn "They gave the school a new library" into the passive with the THING leading. **Answer: "A new library was given to the school."** — when the thing leads, the person/receiver returns behind to.

**3. (Level 3)** Children may stay up late in your house — say it with should be allowed to, whole. **Answer: "Children should be allowed to stay up late."** — should be allowed to rides in with Malaysia's full passive set, as filed.
