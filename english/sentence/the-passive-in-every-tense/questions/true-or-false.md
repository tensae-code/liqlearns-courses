# The passive in every tense — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** The participle changes its shape in every passive coat. **Answer: False** — the participle never changes; the be-family word in front of it carries the tense — being, been, be.

**2. (Level 2)** "He is said to be rich" and "It is said that he is rich" report the same rumour. **Answer: True** — the personal and impersonal frames are two cameras on one report (Malaysia, Form 5, p.89).

**3. (Level 3)** There is a passive form for the future continuous (will be being built) in Malaysia's Form 5 treatment. **Answer: False** — the book's Grammar Reference states plainly there is none — nor for the perfect continuous tenses — as filed.
