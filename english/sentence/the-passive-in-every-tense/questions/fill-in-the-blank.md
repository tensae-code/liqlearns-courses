# The passive in every tense — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** The road ______ (repair) all last week — the work was moving then. (past continuous passive) **Answer: was being repaired** — was being + participle: the past continuous coat (Kuwait 9B, p.38).

**2. (Level 2)** The form needs ______ (sign). (infinitive passive) **Answer: to be signed** — verbs like need take to be + participle (Malaysia, Form 5, Unit 7).

**3. (Level 2)** The hall ______ (open) next Friday. (future passive) **Answer: will be opened** — will be + participle — Yemen Book 5's future coat.

**4. (Level 3)** Maria was ______ a prize at the ceremony. (give — two-object passive, person leading) **Answer: given** — with the person in front, the thing keeps its place: Maria was given a prize.
