# The passive in every tense — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by the coat the item actually asks about. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Passive — Continuous: the Work Is Moving, and Sub lesson 2 — The Passive — Perfect: the Work Stands Finished · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Passive — Modal and Future: Rules and Tomorrow, and Sub lesson 4 — The Passive After Verbs — Being and To Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Two-Object Passives, and Sub lesson 6 — The Impersonal Passive — No Owner; each item is marked with the one coat it asks about.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The road ______ (repair) all last week — the work was moving then. (past continuous passive) **Answer: was being repaired** — was being + participle: the past continuous coat (Kuwait 9B, p.38).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The form needs ______ (sign). (infinitive passive) **Answer: to be signed** — verbs like need take to be + participle (Malaysia, Form 5, Unit 7).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The hall ______ (open) next Friday. (future passive) **Answer: will be opened** — will be + participle — Yemen Book 5's future coat.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Maria was ______ a prize at the ceremony. (give — two-object passive, person leading) **Answer: given** — with the person in front, the thing keeps its place: Maria was given a prize.
