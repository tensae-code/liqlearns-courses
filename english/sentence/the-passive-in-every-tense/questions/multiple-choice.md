# The passive in every tense — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by the coat the item actually asks about. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Passive — Continuous: the Work Is Moving, and Sub lesson 2 — The Passive — Perfect: the Work Stands Finished · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Passive — Modal and Future: Rules and Tomorrow, and Sub lesson 4 — The Passive After Verbs — Being and To Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Two-Object Passives, and Sub lesson 6 — The Impersonal Passive — No Owner; each item is marked with the one coat it asks about.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The panels ______ on the roof right now." a) are being installed  b) have being installed  c) is being install **Answer: a) are being installed** — the present continuous passive: are being + participle — the work is moving (Kuwait 9B, p.38).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "The bridge ______ last year." (finished, result stands) a) has been built  b) is being built  c) will being built **Answer: a) has been built** — the present perfect passive: has been + participle (Kuwait 9B, p.38).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Visitors ______ accompanied at all times." a) must be  b) must been  c) must being **Answer: a) must be** — after a modal, be stays plain: modal + be + participle (Kuwait, Pearls 7A).

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** "People say the tower is 500 years old." → the personal impersonal: a) The tower is said to be 500 years old.  b) It is said the tower to be.  c) The tower is saying 500 years. **Answer: a)** — subject + is said to + infinitive — Malaysia Form 5, Unit 7, Grammar 2, p.89.
