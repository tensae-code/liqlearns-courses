# Lesson plan — The passive in every tense

**Course:** English · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** The passive in every tense
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 29 — "The Passive in Every Tense" (CURRICULUM.md / CATCH-UP-PLAN.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the passive carried past its basic present/past coats: the continuous passives (is being built), the perfect passives (has been built), modal and future passives, passives with gerunds and infinitives (being done, to be done), two-object passives, and the impersonal passive (It is said that… / He is said to…) — the basic present/past passive is NOT retaught here.
**What the learner can do at the end:** build is being / was being + participle and has been / had been + participle on sight; build modal and future passives (must be done, will be finished); use being/to be passives after verbs; turn two-object actives into both their passives; and report rumours both ways — It is said that… and He is said to….

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The Passive — Continuous: the Work Is Moving** — continuous passives: is being / was being + participle — the receiver mid-process, the work still moving. (Was the first half of depth Level 1.)
2. **The Passive — Perfect: the Work Stands Finished** — perfect passives: has/have been / had been + participle — the work stands finished. (Was the second half of depth Level 1.)
3. **The Passive — Modal and Future: Rules and Tomorrow** — modal and future passives: must / can / should be + participle for rules, and will be + participle for tomorrow. (Was the first half of depth Level 2.)
4. **The Passive After Verbs — Being and To Be** — passives after verbs: being / to be / having been + participle after another verb — including should be allowed to, which rides in with this coat. (Was the second half of depth Level 2.)
5. **Two-Object Passives** — two-object passives: verbs like give, send, teach and show carry two receivers, and either can take the front seat. (Was the first half of depth Level 3.)
6. **The Impersonal Passive — No Owner** — the impersonal passive: It is said that… and He is said to… — the whisper with no owner, and the formal-writing coat. (Was the second half of depth Level 3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The passive (the basic shape — be + participle, present and past) and The present perfect. Recommended, never forced — this lesson stands alone for a learner who lands on it directly (DIRECT-ENTRY rule); the basic coats are named in one line each and not retaught.
- Speaker convention: the explanation is read in the learner's main language; the TARGET forms are never translated and are read in the English voice.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of a bridge in three frames: workers building it now, the finished bridge, and a sign saying WILL OPEN FRIDAY. What the student does: the student matches one passive coat to each frame — is being built / has been built / will be opened.**
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a locked gate with a notice: VISITORS MUST BE ACCOMPANIED. What the student does: the student reads the modal passive aloud and says who is missing from it (the doer).**
3. **Slot 3 — a picture of a crowd whispering, with a speech bubble: PEOPLE SAY HE IS RICH. What the student does: the student rebuilds the whisper both impersonal ways — It is said that… / He is said to….**

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: one rumour and one unfinished building, said first in the learner's own language.
2. **Continuous passives**: is being / was being + participle (Kuwait Pearls 9B, L6, p.38; Egypt's continuous passive box).
3. **Perfect passives**: has been / had been + participle (Kuwait 9B, p.38; Yemen Book 5).
4. **Modal and future passives**: must/can/should be + participle; will be + participle (Kuwait Pearls 7A modal passive; Yemen Book 5).
5. **Gerund and infinitive passives**: being done, to be done (Malaysia Form 5, Unit 7).
6. **Two-object passives**: both receivers take the front seat in turn (Malaysia Form 5, Unit 7 — the passive in full).
7. **Impersonal passive**: It is said that… / He is said to… (Malaysia Form 5, Unit 7, Grammar 2, p.89).
8. **The rides-in form**: should be allowed to (Malaysia's full passive set, as filed).
9. **Being/having passives**: Nepal Grade 10 Teacher's Guide, Unit 8 — teacher's-guide evidence, worded so.
10. **Do it yourself**: the three closing tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stuck on the coat: the participle never changes — find the tense in the be-family word in front of it (being = continuous, been = perfect, be after a modal or will).
- Stuck on impersonal pairs: It is said that + a whole sentence keeps the rumour at arm's length; He is said to + infinitive puts the person in front — same rumour, two cameras.
- Stuck on two-object passives: a verb like give has two receivers (the person, the thing); either can step to the front, and the other keeps its place (with to for the person when the thing leads).
- This lesson's next door: Adjective order (Unit 30) — dressing the noun the passive so often fronts.
