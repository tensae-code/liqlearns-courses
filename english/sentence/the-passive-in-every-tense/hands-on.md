# Hands-on — The passive in every tense

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The building-site report.** Find one real building, road or repair near you. Report it in three coats, live: what is being done now, what has been done, what will be done — doer never named. Your companion checks the be-word in each coat (being / been / will be).
2. **The rule board.** Write three real rules of your house, school or street as modal passives (Shoes must be left outside. Phones can be charged here.). Read them aloud as an official notice; the companion removes any doer that sneaks in.
3. **The whisper chain.** One person starts a harmless rumour about a famous person ("People say she speaks five languages"). Each next person must rebuild it in the other impersonal frame — It is said that… ↔ She is said to…. Five rumours, frames alternating strictly.
