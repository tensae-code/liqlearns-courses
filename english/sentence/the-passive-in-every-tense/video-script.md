# Video script — The passive in every tense

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

You know the passive. The bridge was built.

Today the bridge wears every coat.

Now. Moving. The bridge is being built.

Look — the workers are there. It is not finished.

Finished. The bridge has been built.

The result stands. You can cross it.

Rules. The gate must be locked. Cars can be checked.

Tomorrow. The road will be opened on Friday.

No hands in any of these sentences. Only the thing.

And the whisper shape. People say he is rich.

It is said that he is rich.

He is said to be rich.

Same whisper. Two shapes.

Now you try. One building. One rule. One whisper. Every coat.
