# The passive in every tense — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths (RANKS) inside this one thing — the passive in every coat beyond the basic present and past — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the shape first, then its job,
then its borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the moving and finished coats
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build continuous passives (is being / was being built) and perfect passives (has been / had been built) with the participle unchanged.

## Level 2 — modals, futures and verb-followers
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build modal and future passives and place being / to be passives after the verbs that take them — including should be allowed to.

## Level 3 — two receivers and no receiver
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: turn two-object actives both ways, and report with the impersonal passive in both its frames — It is said that… and He is said to….
