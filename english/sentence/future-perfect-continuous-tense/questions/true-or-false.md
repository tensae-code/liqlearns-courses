## True or false

1. The future perfect continuous says an action is finished by a future point. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it says the action is still running at that point; finished-by-then is the future perfect.

2. The tense is built will + have + been + verb-ing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. The tense can stand without any future point. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it always hangs on one: by June, this time next year.

4. "Since" measures with a length of time, like "three days". (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — since takes a starting point (since 2023); for takes the length (for three days).

5. "How long will you have been…?" is this tense's natural question. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
