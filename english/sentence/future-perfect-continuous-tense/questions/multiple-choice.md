## Multiple choice

1. Which is the future perfect continuous? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Will be learning  b) Will have learned  c) Will have been learning
   **Answer: c) Will have been learning** — will + have + been + verb-ing.

2. What does the tense always need to stand on? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A past point  b) A future point  c) A question
   **Answer: b) A future point** — by June, this time tomorrow, when you arrive.

3. "By nine I will have finished" is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Future perfect continuous  b) Future perfect — the action is done by then  c) Future continuous
   **Answer: b)** — finished by the point: future perfect, not continuous.

4. Which measure fits "…will have been living here ___ 2023"? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) For  b) Since  c) By
   **Answer: b) Since** — since takes a starting point; for takes a length.

5. The negative of "she will have been waiting" is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) She will not have been waiting  b) She not will have been waiting  c) She will have been not waiting
   **Answer: a)** — not goes right after will.
