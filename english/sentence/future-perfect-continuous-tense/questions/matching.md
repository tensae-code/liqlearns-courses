## Matching

Match the sentence to its tense. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. At nine I will be working. — b
2. By nine I will have finished. — c
3. By nine I will have been working for five hours. — a
4. By June we will have been living here since 2023. — a
5. Will you have been waiting long? — a

a) Future perfect continuous
b) Future continuous
c) Future perfect

**Answers:** 1–b (in progress then, unmeasured), 2–c (done by then),
3–a (still running then, measured), 4–a (measured with since), 5–a
(the tense's question form).
