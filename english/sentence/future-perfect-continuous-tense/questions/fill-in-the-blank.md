## Fill in the blank

1. "By June, I will have been ___ English for three years." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: learning** — the present participle closes the shape.

2. "The four blocks are: will, ___, been, verb-ing." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: have.**

3. "This time tomorrow, she will have been travelling ___ ten hours." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: for** — for + a length of time.

4. "___ you have been waiting long when I arrive?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Will** — the question turns will to the front.

5. "By December we will ___ have been studying for long — only a month." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: not.**
