# Video script — Future Perfect Continuous Tense

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A calendar page: JUNE. A runner is still running at it.]
"By JUNE… I WILL HAVE BEEN RUNNING!"

[The four blocks stack up.] "WILL. HAVE. BEEN. RUNNING."

[The runner's road is measured.] "Still going — and we say
HOW LONG. FOR three years!"

[Back to the mascot at the calendar.] "The future point, the
running action, the measure. You have the last future tense!"
