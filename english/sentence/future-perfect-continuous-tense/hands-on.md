# Hands-on — Future Perfect Continuous Tense

## Timeline bars

Draw a line to "next June". Draw an action bar that starts
before the mark and is still running at it; write its length
under the bar. Say the sentence the bar shows: "By June, I
will have been learning English for three years." Partners
draw bars for each other to read.

## Point races

A future point is called — "by December!", "this time
tomorrow!", "when school closes!" First partner to build a
true, measured sentence on the point scores.
