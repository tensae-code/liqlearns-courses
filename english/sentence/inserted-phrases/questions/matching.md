## Matching

Match the fence to its volume or job. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Commas — b
2. Dashes — a
3. Parentheses — d
4. Brackets — c
5. The lift-out test — e

a) The loud fence — the insertion steps forward for emphasis
b) The smooth, everyday fence — a pair separating phrases within a sentence
c) The clarifier — adds your own explaining words inside quoted text
d) The quiet fence — the insertion whispers as an aside
e) The check — remove the insertion and the sentence must still stand

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–a, 3–d, 4–c, 5–e.
