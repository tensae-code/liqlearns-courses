## Fill in the blank

1. A phrase dropped into the middle of a sentence is called an ______ phrase. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: inserted** (intervening).

2. An inserted phrase in mid-sentence is fenced by ______ commas. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: two.**

3. "Our dog, ______, chased the goat." Fill the gap with an inserted phrase of your own, fenced correctly. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any true extra phrase set between the two commas, e.g. "who is usually gentle" — the sentence must stand when it is lifted out.

4. For emphasis, an insertion can be enclosed by ______ instead of commas. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: dashes.**

5. Inside a quotation, a clarification you add yourself goes inside ______ brackets. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: square** ([ ]).
