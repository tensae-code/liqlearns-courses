## True or false

1. An inserted phrase can be lifted out and the sentence still stands whole. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — that is the lift-out test.

2. One comma is enough to fence a phrase inserted in the middle of a sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the fence is a pair, one on each side.

3. Commas are used to separate phrases within sentences. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

4. Parentheses make an insertion louder than dashes do. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — parentheses whisper the insertion aside; dashes thrust it forward.

5. Brackets can add a clarification inside quoted words. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — the brackets confess the added words are yours.
