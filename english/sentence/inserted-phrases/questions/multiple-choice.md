## Multiple choice

1. In "My aunt, who lives in the country, grows mangoes," what is the inserted phrase? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "My aunt"  b) "who lives in the country"  c) "grows mangoes"
   **Answer: b)** — lift it out and the sentence stands: "My aunt grows mangoes."

2. How many commas fence an inserted phrase in the middle of a sentence? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) One  b) Two — one where it steps in, one where it steps out  c) None
   **Answer: b)** — the fence is a pair.

3. Which sentence fences its insertion correctly? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "The bus, which was late arrived at noon."  b) "The bus, which was late, arrived at noon."  c) "The bus which was late, arrived at noon."
   **Answer: b)** — two commas, one on each side of the insertion.

4. Which fence makes an insertion stand out loudest? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Parentheses  b) Commas  c) Dashes
   **Answer: c) Dashes** — chosen for emphasis.

5. A writer adds a word inside a quotation to explain who "he" is. Which marks confess the added word? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Brackets [ ]  b) Commas  c) Quotation marks
   **Answer: a) Brackets** — they show the words are the writer's, not the speaker's.
