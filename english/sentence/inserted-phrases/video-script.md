# Video script — Inserted Phrases

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A sentence appears: "My brother Tom is coming home." A small
extra phrase drops into its middle: "who lives in Kingston".]
"See this extra part? It dropped INTO the sentence!"

[Two commas pop in, one on each side of the extra phrase.]
"Two commas fence it in — one in, one out!"

[The fences change: dashes, standing tall; then parentheses,
small and quiet.] "Dashes shout it. Parentheses whisper it."

[Back to the mascot.] "Extra part inside? Fence it! That is
an INSERTED PHRASE!"
