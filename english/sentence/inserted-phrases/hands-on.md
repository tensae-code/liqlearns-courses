# Hands-on — Inserted Phrases

## Lift it out

In pairs: one partner writes a sentence with extra information
dropped into its middle, unfenced. The other fences it with
commas, then lifts the fenced phrase out and reads what
remains aloud — it must stand whole. Swap after three
sentences.

## Three volumes

Take one sentence with an insertion. Write it three times:
commas, dashes, parentheses. Read the three aloud to your
partner, who must say which is smooth, which is loud and
which is quiet — and which one they would choose for a letter
to a friend, and which for a speech.
