# Stative verbs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "I ______ the answer." a) am knowing  b) know  c) knowing **Answer: b) know** — know is stative — a state, not an action — so no continuous coat (Egypt, Stative Verbs box, p.9).

**2. (Level 1)** Which verb is STATIVE? a) swim  b) believe  c) cook **Answer: b) believe** — believe sits in the thoughts/opinions group; swim and cook are actions (Egypt, p.9).

**3. (Level 2)** "I ______ lunch right now — call me later." a) am having  b) have (own)  c) am owning **Answer: a) am having** — having = eating is an ACTION, so the continuous coat opens — have can be state or action by context (Egypt, p.9).

**4. (Level 3)** "She looks ______." a) tiredly  b) tired  c) tire **Answer: b) tired** — a linking verb carries an ADJECTIVE describing the subject, never an adverb.
