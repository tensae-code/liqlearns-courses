# Stative verbs — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** We ______ (own) a small house near the sea. (present — mind the coat) **Answer: own** — owning/possessing is a state: the simple coat, no -ing.

**2. (Level 2)** He ______ (think) about the problem all morning. (action — turning it over) **Answer: is thinking / has been thinking (continuous family)** — think as an action in progress takes the continuous coat; think as an opinion does not (Egypt, p.9).

**3. (Level 2)** The soup ______ (taste) good. (bridge verb) **Answer: tastes** — taste as a linking verb joins the soup to its description: tastes + adjective.

**4. (Level 3)** It ______ (seem) late — we'd better go. **Answer: seems** — seem is stative and a bridge: simple coat, description after it.
