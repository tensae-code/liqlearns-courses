# Stative verbs — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by what the item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Stative Verbs — the Rule and Its Families · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Stative Verbs — the Honest Border: Three Movers, except bridge items, which are Sub lesson 3 — Linking Verbs — the Bridges, and the senses-and-emotions simple-forms item, which is Sub lesson 1 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We ______ (own) a small house near the sea. (present — mind the coat) **Answer: own** — owning/possessing is a state: the simple coat, no -ing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** He ______ (think) about the problem all morning. (action — turning it over) **Answer: is thinking / has been thinking (continuous family)** — think as an action in progress takes the continuous coat; think as an opinion does not (Egypt, p.9).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The soup ______ (taste) good. (bridge verb) **Answer: tastes** — taste as a linking verb joins the soup to its description: tastes + adjective.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** It ______ (seem) late — we'd better go. **Answer: seems** — seem is stative and a bridge: simple coat, description after it.
