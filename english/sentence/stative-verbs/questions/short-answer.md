# Stative verbs — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Say one true thing you own and one true thing you know, statives in their right coat. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentences in the simple coat — own / have / know)** — states stand in the simple form even when they are true right now.

**2. (Level 2)** Say "see" in both its lives: one state sentence, one action sentence. **Answer: State: "I see a bird." Action: "She is seeing the doctor at three."** — see can be state or action by context (Egypt, p.9).

**3. (Level 3)** Describe how a friend looks today and how their voice sounds — bridges only. **Answer: (Learner's own sentences: looks + adjective; sounds + adjective)** — the bridge carries an adjective describing the subject.
