# Stative verbs — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "I am wanting a new phone" is the careful, rule-following way to say it. **Answer: False** — want is on Egypt's own stative list — "I want a new phone"; statives are not used in the continuous form.

**2. (Level 2)** "I am having a car" is right, because have can take -ing. **Answer: False** — having here means OWNING — a state; the -ing opens only when have means an action (having lunch, having a shower).

**3. (Level 3)** "The soup tastes good" and "She looks tired" use taste and look as linking verbs. **Answer: True** — both join the subject to an adjective description — Vietnam's Unit 3 pairing.
