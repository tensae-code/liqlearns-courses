# Video script — Stative verbs

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some verbs run. Swim. Jump. Cook.

I am swimming. Now. Moving.

Some verbs stand still.

Know. Love. Own. Believe. Seem.

I know the answer. Not: I am knowing.

I love this song. Not: I am loving — in the careful rule.

These are stative verbs. State verbs. Still verbs.

They name a state, not an action. So no -ing coat.

Watch the border. Have can move.

I have a car. Still. Own. No -ing.

I am having lunch. Moving. Eating. -ing is fine.

And the bridge verbs. Be. Seem. Look. Feel.

She looks tired. The soup tastes good.

The bridge carries a description.

Now you try. Sort the verbs. Keep the still ones still.
