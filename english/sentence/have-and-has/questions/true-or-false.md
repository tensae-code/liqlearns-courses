## True or false

1. "She have a book" is right. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — with she, it is "She has a book."

2. "He doesn't has a bike" is right. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — after doesn't, have stays plain: "He doesn't have a bike."

3. "Do he have a dog?" is right. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — for he, the question starts with Does: "Does he have a dog?"

4. "They has two cats" is right. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — they takes have: "They have two cats."
