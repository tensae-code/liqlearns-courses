## Short answer

1. Answer in a full sentence: Do you have a bag? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: Yes, I do. I have a bag. / No, I don't. I don't have a bag.** — any true answer with the short answer and the full have sentence.

2. Say one thing your teacher has. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: (any true sentence with has)** — e.g. "My teacher has a big book."

3. Say one thing you do not have. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: (any true sentence with don't have)** — e.g. "I don't have a car."

4. Ask a question about what your partner has, using Does. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: (any Does…have question)** — e.g. "Does your mother have a phone?"
