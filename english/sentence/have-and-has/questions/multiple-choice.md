## Multiple choice

1. "I ___ a book." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) has  b) have  c) haves
   **Answer: b) have** — with I, the word is have.

2. "They ___ two dogs." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) has  b) have  c) is have
   **Answer: b) have** — they takes have, like I, you and we.

3. "She ___ a red bag." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) have  b) has  c) haves
   **Answer: b) has** — with he, she and it, have changes to has.

4. "He ___ a bike." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) has  b) have  c) do have
   **Answer: a) has** — one other person (he) takes has.

5. "He ___ a phone." — the negative: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) doesn't has  b) don't have  c) doesn't have
   **Answer: c) doesn't have** — after doesn't, the word goes back to plain have.

6. "___ she have a sister?" (Sub lesson 4)
   a) Do  b) Does  c) Is
   **Answer: b) Does** — questions about he/she/it start with Does, and have stays plain.
