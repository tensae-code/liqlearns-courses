# Video script — Have and Has (things you own)

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot holds up a book, a pen and a bag, one by one.]
"I have a book! I have a pen! I have a bag!"

[A girl appears beside him, holding a bike helmet.]
"She has a bike! He has a ball!"

[The mascot shakes his head at an empty space where a car would be.]
"I don't have a car. He doesn't have a car."

[The mascot turns to the viewer and asks.]
"Do YOU have a pen? Does she have a book? Tell me!"
