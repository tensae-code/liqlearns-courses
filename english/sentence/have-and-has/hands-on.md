# Hands-on — Have and Has (things you own)

Hands teach these words best. Do these with real things, not only
pictures.

## Show your three things

Put three things of your own on the table. Say one sentence for
each: "I have a book." "I have a pen." "I have a bag." Then point
at a partner's things and report them: "She has a book." "He has
a pen."

## The empty chair

Put an empty chair beside you — it stands for a thing nobody here
has. Take turns naming what the chair "has": "It doesn't have a
book." "It doesn't have a phone." Then ask the chair questions
and answer for it: "Do you have a bike?" — "No, I don't!"
