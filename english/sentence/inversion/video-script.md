# Video script — Inversion

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A friend puppet says: "I can swim!"]

"I can swim. And me? SO CAN I! Look — CAN steps in front of I!"

[The words CAN and I swap places with a little hop.] "That turn has a name: inversion!"

"And this one: NOT ONLY did he pass — but he came FIRST! The first part jumps to the front, and turns around. Try it!"
