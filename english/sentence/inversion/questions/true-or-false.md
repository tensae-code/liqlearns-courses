## True or false

1. In "So do I", the helper verb stands in front of the person. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. Any helper will do in a So-answer — "I came early / So do I" is fine. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the helper must match the statement: "So did I."**

3. In a Not only sentence, the but part also inverts. (Sub lesson 2) **False — only the fronted part inverts; the but part keeps its plain order.**

4. Inversion means turning the usual word order around on purpose. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
