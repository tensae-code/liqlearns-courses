## Matching

Match each statement to its agreeing So-answer. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. I like coffee.
2. He went home early.
3. We can come tomorrow.
4. She will pass.

a) So will I.
b) So can we.
c) So do I.
d) So did I.

**Answers: 1-c, 2-d, 3-b, 4-a.**
