**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Eritrea).**

This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-10 expansion read of Eritrea's English books: the read found inversion taught by name in the Grade 12 book — the "So do I" agreement turn and the "Not only … but …" fronting — with no lesson in the course for either pattern; its diff marks the topic "NEW LESSON NEEDED: inversion". The lesson teaches the two patterns the book names, and no others. The Grade 12 unit's idiomatic expressions are stocked in idioms-and-collocations. Other countries join as their books are read.
