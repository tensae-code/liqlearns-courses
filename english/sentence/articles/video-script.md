# Video script — Articles

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Small words. Big job. They point.

Here is an apple. Any apple. One apple.
A. An.
A book. A dog. A house.
An apple. An egg. An hour.
Listen to the sound. A vowel sound takes AN.

Now I pick up THE apple. This one. You see it. I see it.
THE points at the one we both know.
I bought a book yesterday. The book is on my table.
First time — a. Second time — the.

Now no word at all.
Fruit is good for you. Dogs are friendly.
The kind, in general. No a. No the. Zero.

And the map.
The river. The sea. The mountains — a range takes THE.
The Nile. The Red Sea. The Himalayas.
But a single mountain — no the. A lake — no the.
A town — no the. Most countries — no the.
Mount Kenya. Lake Victoria. Khartoum. Sudan.

Three choices.
One new thing — a, an.
The known thing — the.
The kind itself — nothing.

Now you try.
Point at things in your room.
Say a chair. Say the chair by the window.
Say: chairs are useful. Nothing before chairs.
