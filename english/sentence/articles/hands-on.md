# Hands-on — Articles

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real room, with a companion or the Explain
Companion.

1. **The market game.** Spread six things on a table.
   The buyer asks for ANY one first ("Can I have an
   apple?"), takes it, and must then talk about THE
   one in their hand ("The apple is red"). Five rounds:
   a for the choosing, the for the chosen.
2. **First and second mention.** Tell a four-sentence
   story about a new thing entering your home (a cat,
   a chair, a phone). First mention takes a/an; every
   later mention takes the. Your companion taps the
   table at every article and says which law it obeys.
3. **The general truth round.** Say five true sentences
   about kinds in general (Tea is hot. Children like
   games.) — zero article, every time. Then break one
   on purpose with the and hear why the kind refuses it.
4. **Map pointing.** Open any map — a book, a phone, a
   wall map. Point at ten names and say each with its
   right coat: the + river/sea/range; zero + peak,
   lake, town, country. Two mistakes allowed; the third
   restarts the map.
