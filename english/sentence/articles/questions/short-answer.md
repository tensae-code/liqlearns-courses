Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say one sentence that brings a cat into a story for the first time.
**Answer: A cat sat by the door. (Any first-mention sentence with a.)** — a names one new member of the kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Now say the next sentence of that story, about the same cat.
**Answer: The cat was black and hungry. (The, because the cat is now known.)** — second mention takes the.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say one true sentence about cats in general.
**Answer: Cats sleep a lot. (Zero before the plural kind.)** — kinds in general take no article.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Say two sentences: one about a river you know, one about a town you know, each with the right coat.
**Answer: The Nile is a long river. Khartoum is a big town. (River with the; town bare.)** — rivers take the, towns take zero (Armenia).
