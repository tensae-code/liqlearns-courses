# Articles — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Articles — A and An · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Articles — The, and Zero for Kinds · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Articles — the Map's Coats.

Fill each gap with a, an, the — or write ZERO for no article.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** He is ______ doctor. (one new member of a kind)
**Answer: a** — a before the consonant sound of doctor; one member of the kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ sun is very hot today.
**Answer: The** — there is only one; the one we both know by the world itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ milk is good for children. (the kind, in general)
**Answer: ZERO** — milk as a kind in general takes no article (Cambodia, G12 U9).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** We sailed on ______ Red Sea, then climbed ______ Mount Kenya.
**Answer: The / ZERO** — seas take the; a single peak stands bare (Armenia, G10 geographical names).
