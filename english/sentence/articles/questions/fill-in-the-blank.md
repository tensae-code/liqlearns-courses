# Articles — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with a, an, the — or write ZERO for no article.

**1. (Level 1)** He is ______ doctor. (one new member of a kind)
**Answer: a** — a before the consonant sound of doctor; one member of the kind.

**2. (Level 2)** ______ sun is very hot today.
**Answer: The** — there is only one; the one we both know by the world itself.

**3. (Level 2)** ______ milk is good for children. (the kind, in general)
**Answer: ZERO** — milk as a kind in general takes no article (Cambodia, G12 U9).

**4. (Level 3)** We sailed on ______ Red Sea, then climbed ______ Mount Kenya.
**Answer: The / ZERO** — seas take the; a single peak stands bare (Armenia, G10 geographical names).
