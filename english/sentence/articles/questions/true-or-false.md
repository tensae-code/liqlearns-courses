# Articles — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "An" is chosen by the first LETTER of the next word.
**Answer: False** — an is chosen by the first SOUND: an hour (silent h), a university ("yoo" sound).

**2. (Level 2)** When a kind is spoken of in general — "Fruit is good for you" — English uses no article.
**Answer: True** — the zero choice, in Cambodia's specific-vs-general Look box (G12 U9).

**3. (Level 3)** Single mountains and lakes take "the", like rivers do.
**Answer: False** — single peaks and lakes stand bare (Mount Kenya, Lake Victoria); rivers, seas and ranges take the (Armenia, G10).

**4. (Level 3)** Most country names take "the".
**Answer: False** — most countries stand bare (Sudan, Nepal, Vietnam); the map's the belongs to rivers, seas, oceans and ranges.
