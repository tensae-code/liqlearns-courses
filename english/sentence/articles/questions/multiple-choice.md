# Articles — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Articles — A and An · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Articles — The, and Zero for Kinds · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Articles — the Map's Coats.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I saw ______ elephant at the zoo."
a) a  b) an  c) the
**Answer: b) an** — elephant starts with a vowel sound, so an (judge by ear).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "She waited for ______ hour."
a) a  b) an  c) zero
**Answer: b) an** — the h in hour is silent; the sound is a vowel sound, so an.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "I bought a pen. ______ pen is blue."
a) A  b) An  c) The
**Answer: c) The** — second mention: the one we both now know.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ are friendly animals." (the kind, in general)
a) Dogs  b) The dogs  c) A dogs
**Answer: a) Dogs** — a kind spoken of in general takes zero article (Cambodia's specific-vs-general box).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ Nile flows through Egypt."
a) zero  b) A  c) The
**Answer: c) The** — rivers take the (Armenia's geographical-names rules).
