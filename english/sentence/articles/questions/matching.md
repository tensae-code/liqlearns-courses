# Articles — matching

Match each name or phrase to its coat.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ university in my town" **Answer: a** — university starts with a "yoo" consonant sound, so a, not an.

**2. (Level 2)** "______ fruit is sweet" (in general) **Answer: zero** — the kind itself takes no article (Malaysia F3 U3's four-way family).

**3. (Level 3)** "______ Himalayas" **Answer: the** — a mountain RANGE takes the; only single peaks stand bare (Armenia).
