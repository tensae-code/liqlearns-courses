# Hands-on — Future perfect

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The Friday look-back.** Write next Friday's date on paper. Under it, list three things that will have finished by that day in your real life, in this tense. On Friday, tick what truly stands finished — and rewrite what does not, with its new by-point.
2. **Correct-the-book hunt.** With your companion, hunt three participle traps aloud: go, write, take. Each person must say the pair — the wrong past-tense version crossed out in words ("not went — GONE") — and the right will have sentence.
3. **The stretch measure.** Measure one real stretch to a future point together: months of learning, days in a house, weeks at a job. Say it in the twin: "By …, we will have been … for …" Two stretches each, measures checked against a calendar.
