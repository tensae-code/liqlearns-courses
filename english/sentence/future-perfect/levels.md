# Future perfect — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths (RANKS) inside this one thing — finished by a future point — and its continuous twin — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the shape first, then its job,
then its borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the shape, corrected
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build will have + past participle for every who — and state the corrected rule against South Africa's printed one: past participle, never past tense.

## Level 2 — the by-point and the twin
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: anchor every sentence to its by-point (by, by the time, before, in …'s time) and build the continuous twin with its for-measure up to the point.

## Level 3 — the neighbours
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort live at one future point: moving then (future continuous), finished by then (this tense, simple), stretch measured to then (this tense, continuous) — no swaps.
