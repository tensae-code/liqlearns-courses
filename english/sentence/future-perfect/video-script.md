# Video script — Future perfect

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Stand on Friday. Look back.

The work is done. Already.

By Friday, I will have finished.

That is today's shape.

Will. Have. And the third shape of the verb.

Will have finished.

Will have left.

Will have built.

Careful! Not the past shape.

Will have GONE — not will have went.

One book printed that rule wrong. We say it right.

It always leans on a point.

By Friday. By the time you come. By next year.

And the long stretch?

By June, I will have been learning for two years.

Will have been. And the verb with -ing.

Moving at the point — will be working.

Finished by the point — will have finished.

Now you try.

By Sunday, what will you have done?
