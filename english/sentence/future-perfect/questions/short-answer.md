# Future perfect — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** What will you have done by Sunday? Answer whole and truly. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in will have + participle + by-point)** — the finished thing and its future point must both stand.

**2. (Level 2)** Say how long you will have been learning English by the end of this year. **Answer: (Learner's own sentence in will have been + -ing + for)** — the twin, with its measure, aimed at the year-end point.

**3. (Level 3)** Will you have finished your work by six? Give the short answer that fits your real evening. **Answer: Yes, I will. / No, I won't** — short answers ride will, the tense's first piece.
