# Future perfect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "By Friday, I ______ the report." a) will have finished  b) will have finish yesterday  c) have finish **Answer: a) will have finished** — will have + past participle, anchored to the by-point Friday.

**2. (Level 1)** The participle of "go" in this tense is — a) went  b) gone  c) going **Answer: b) gone** — will have gone — the participle, never the past tense went; that is the corrected rule.

**3. (Level 2)** "By June, she ______ English for two years." a) will have been learning  b) will have learn  c) is learn **Answer: a) will have been learning** — a stretch measured up to the by-point wears the continuous twin.

**4. (Level 3)** At six, which pair is sorted RIGHT? a) moving: will have finished; done: will be working  b) moving: will be working; done by six: will have finished  c) both are will have finished **Answer: b)** — the future continuous paints the action moving at the point; the future perfect looks at it finished by the point.
