# Future perfect — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Future Perfect Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Future Perfect By-Point and Twin · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Future Perfect Neighbours.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By the time you arrive, dinner ______ (cook). **Answer: will have been cooked** — will have + participle; the cooking stands finished by the arrival point.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** They ______ (build) the bridge by 2030. **Answer: will have built** — built is the participle of build; 2030 is the by-point.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** By next month, he ______ (work) here for five years. **Answer: will have been working** — the twin measures the stretch — for five years — up to the future point.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** It ______ (not arrive) by six, I fear. (negative) **Answer: won't have arrived** — the not folds into will: won't have + participle.
