# Conditionals — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Machine and the Always-True Rung (item 1) and Sub lesson 2 — The First Conditional — Really Possible (item 2) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Second Conditional — Imagined · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The Third Conditional — the Unchangeable Past.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the always-true law about plants
and water.
**Answer: If you don't water plants, they die.** (Any
true zero conditional earns it: present in both halves.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** It may rain tomorrow and you plan to
stay home if it does. Say the plan.
**Answer: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You have no bicycle and you dream of
riding to school. Say the dream.
**Answer: If I had a bicycle, I would ride to school.**
(The past coat marks it unreal; any true dream in the
same shape earns it.)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Give a friend advice about resting,
the "If I were you" way.
**Answer: If I were you, I would rest.** (Were serves
every who on this rung.)

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Yesterday you left late and missed the
bus. Say the regret.
**Answer: If I had left earlier, I would have caught
the bus.** (Egypt, Prep 9 T1, Tip p.31 — the rung of
regret, missed chances, and excuses.)
