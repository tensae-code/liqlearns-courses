# Conditionals — matching

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — match each sentence to its rung.
1. If you heat ice, it melts. — **zero**
2. If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home. — **first**
3. If I had wings, I would fly. — **second**
4. If I had left earlier, I would have caught the bus.
   — **third**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — match each message to the rung it
must wear.
1. a law of the kitchen — **zero**
2. a plan for a possible tomorrow — **first**
3. a wish about your life now — **second**
4. a regret about yesterday — **third**
5. advice to a friend, "if I were you" — **second**
6. a warning with unless — **first**
