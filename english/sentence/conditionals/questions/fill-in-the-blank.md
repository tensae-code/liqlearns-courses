# Conditionals — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** If you ______ (drop) a glass on stone,
it ______ (break).
**Answer: drop / breaks** — the zero rung: present,
present.

**2. (Level 1)** If she ______ (study) hard, she will
pass.
**Answer: studies** — present in the if-half; will
already stands in the result.

**3. (Level 2)** If we ______ (live) by the sea, we
would swim every day.
**Answer: lived** — the past coat for the imagined
rung.

**4. (Level 2)** Unless it ______ (rain), we will play
outside.
**Answer: rains** — unless carries the not inside
itself; the verb stays positive present.

**5. (Level 3)** If they ______ ______ (leave) at six,
they would have arrived on time.
**Answer: had left** — had + participle: the condition
built from the finished past.

**6. (Level 3)** If I had left earlier, I ______ ______
______ (catch) the bus.
**Answer: would have caught** — the third rung's
result-half, whole.
