# Conditionals — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Machine and the Always-True Rung (item 1) and Sub lesson 2 — The First Conditional — Really Possible (item 2) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 (items 3 and 5: the might dial inside the first conditional, and unless) and Sub lesson 3 — The Second Conditional — Imagined (item 4) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The Third Conditional — the Unchangeable Past.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "If you ______ ice, it melts."
a) heat  b) will heat  c) heated
**Answer: a) heat** — the zero rung: present in both
halves; will never enters the if-half.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "If it rains tomorrow, we ______ home."
a) stay  b) will stay  c) stayed
**Answer: b) will stay** — the first rung: if + present,
will in the result-half (Libya, Grade 9, p.80).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "If we hurry, we ______ catch the bus —
but it is not certain."
a) will definitely  b) might  c) would have
**Answer: b) might** — may/might mark the lower
possibility inside the first conditional (Libya, p.80).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "If I ______ wings, I would fly."
a) have  b) had  c) will have
**Answer: b) had** — the second rung wears the past coat
to mark the unreal.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "Unless you hurry, you will be late"
means —
a) If you hurry, you will be late.
b) If you don't hurry, you will be late.
c) When you hurry, you are late.
**Answer: b)** — unless = if…not (Mongolia, English IX,
Unit 7).

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** "If I ______ earlier, I would have
caught the bus."
a) left  b) had left  c) leave
**Answer: b) had left** — the third rung: if + past
perfect (had + participle), would have + participle
(Egypt, Prep 9 T1, Tip p.31).
