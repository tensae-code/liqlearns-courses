# Hands-on — Conditionals

You do it, we guide you. Real facts, real plans, real
regrets — with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The kitchen laws (rung 0).** In a kitchen or a
   garden, find four always-true ifs and say them whole:
   If you heat ice, it melts. If you don't water plants,
   they die. Your companion tries to break each one with
   a single yesterday — a law that broke once was never
   a zero.
2. **Tomorrow's plan (rung 1).** Plan one real thing for
   tomorrow that depends on the weather, money, or a
   person. Say it three ways down the dial: will
   (certain), may, might (less sure) — then swap in one
   unless warning: Unless it rains, we will go.
3. **The dream chair (rung 2).** Sit where you are and
   change one thing about your life in words — If I
   lived by the sea…, If I had a bicycle… — and build
   three results with would. Your companion asks one
   "If I were you…" advice sentence back.
4. **Regret and repair (rung 3 + rung 1).** One real
   missed chance, told whole: If I had…, I would have…
   Your companion answers with the repair for next time
   in the first conditional: If you… next time, you
   will… The past rung closes; the future rung opens.
