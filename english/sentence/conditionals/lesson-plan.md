# Lesson plan — Conditionals

**Course:** English · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Conditionals
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 18 — Deeper Sentences —
the fourth and last lesson of the unit (CURRICULUM.md).
Building it completes Unit 18.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** conditionals — the
if-machine: an if-half setting a condition and a
result-half saying what follows, with the verb coats
marking how real the condition is — zero (always true),
first (really possible), second (imagined), third (a past
that can't change).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole
Sentence title beneath it — present and past coats
(Levels 10–13), will and going to (Level 12), and this
level's perfect machinery (had + participle, would have)
from The present perfect.
**What the learner can do at the end:** build all four
rungs with exact coats; swap unless for if…not; run the
certainty dial (will / may / might / would); and pick the
rung a real message needs — a law, a plan, a dream, a
regret — without mixing coats.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The Machine and the Always-True Rung** — every
   conditional is two halves holding hands; rung 0:
   if + present, present — always true. (Was the zero
   half of depth Level 1.)
2. **The First Conditional — Really Possible** — if +
   present, will + verb; never will in the if-half; and
   unless, which is if…not. (Was the first half of depth
   Level 1; the unless swap stood in depth Level 2.)
3. **The Second Conditional — Imagined** — if + past,
   would + verb: dreams, wishes and advice, the past coat
   marking the unreal. (Was the imagined half of depth
   Level 2.)
4. **The Third Conditional — the Unchangeable Past** —
   if + had + participle, would have + participle, for
   regrets and missed chances; the books' split and the
   certainty dial (will high, may/might lower) close the
   lesson here. (Was depth Level 3; the dial stood in
   depth Level 2.)
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Going to: later and Past actions
  (will and the past coat), and The present perfect (the
  third rung is built from had + participle).
  Recommended, never forced.
- Reported speech's backshift (would as the past of
  will) is a different thing and is not taught here.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of a ladder with four rungs
   standing in four weathers: a sunny laboratory (zero),
   a cloudy street with an umbrella ready (first), a
   thought-bubble sky (second), and a faded photograph
   (third).** Written slot: "a picture of a four-rung
   ladder, each rung in a different kind of real."
   What the student does: the student points at the rung
   a sentence belongs to before building it — reality
   first, coats second.
2. **Slot 2 — a GIF (1–2 seconds) of ice melting under a
   heater, looping back to ice and melting again.**
   Written slot: "a picture of ice melting every single
   time it is heated." What the student does: the student
   says the zero sentence — If you heat ice, it melts —
   and hears that no will is allowed on the always-true
   rung.
3. **Slot 3 — a picture of a bus pulling away from a
   stop while a runner watches it go, the whole picture
   slightly faded like an old photograph.** Written
   slot: "a picture of a missed bus that can never be
   un-missed." What the student does: the student builds
   the regret — If I had left earlier, I would have
   caught it — and feels why both halves wear the
   perfect.

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first** (lesson/lesson.md): four sentences
   from the learner's own mouth — a truth, a plan, a
   dream, a regret — before any rung is named.
2. **The machine**: if-half + result-half; the halves
   can swap chairs; the coats carry the meaning.
3. **Rung 0 — zero**: if + present, present; laws,
   habits, instructions; if means whenever (Libya
   p.79; Mongolia's tables).
4. **Rung 1 — first**: if + present, will + verb; the
   certainty dial inside it — will high, may/might low
   (Libya p.80); unless = if…not (Mongolia IX U7).
5. **Rung 2 — second**: if + past, would + verb — the
   past coat as the mark of unreal; were for every who
   in advice (Libya reference U5; Mongolia X U10; Egypt
   Prep 8).
6. **Rung 3 — third**: if + had + participle, would
   have + participle; regret, missed chances, excuses
   (Egypt Prep 9 T1, Tip p.31).
7. **The books' split, shown plainly**: Libya and
   Mongolia name four rungs; Egypt's stated ladder is
   three and never names the zero.
8. **The certainty dial whole**: will / may / might /
   would, with perhaps, probably, definitely (Libya
   p.81).
9. **Do it yourself** (the three closing tasks): one
   fact four rungs, the unless swap, regret and repair.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stuck choosing the rung: ask one question — "how real
  is it?" Always true → zero. Could really happen →
  first. Only imagined → second. A past that can't
  change → third. The coats follow the answer.
- Stuck with will in the if-half: will lives in the
  result-half only; the if-half wears present (rungs 0–1),
  past (rung 2), or had + participle (rung 3).
- Stuck on the third: build it from the perfect lessons
  — had + participle is the past perfect's shape, and
  would have + participle is its twin. One piece at a
  time.
- This lesson completes Level 18, "Deeper Sentences."
  Next beyond the level: longer essays and the Novel
  title, as the reading reaches those books.
