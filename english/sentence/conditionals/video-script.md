# Video script — Conditionals

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

If you heat ice, it melts.
Every time. Always true.

If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.
It could rain. That is the plan.

If I had wings, I would fly.
A dream. Not real. I have no wings.

If I had left earlier, I would have caught the bus.
Too late now. The past cannot change.

Four sentences. One machine.
If — and what follows.

Listen to the coats.

Always true: both halves in the present.
If you heat ice, it melts.

Really possible: if — present.
Result — will.
If it rains, we will stay home.

Not sure? Say might.
If we hurry, we might catch the bus.

Imagined: if — past.
Result — would.
If I had wings, I would fly.
The past coat does not mean the past.
It means: not real.

The past that cannot change:
If — had, and the third shape.
Result — would have, and the third shape.
If I had left earlier, I would have caught the bus.

And one small word: unless.
Unless you hurry, you will be late.
Unless means: if you do NOT hurry.

Truth. Plan. Dream. Regret.
Pick how real it is.
The coats will follow.

Now you try.
One truth. One plan. One dream. One regret.
Say all four.
