# Participle adjectives — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Say how you feel in this lesson's subject, and what causes it — the pair, whole. **Answer: (Learner's own pair, e.g. "I am interested. The lesson is interesting.")** — -ed on the feeler, -ing on the cause, in either order.

**2. (Level 2)** A friend says "I am boring" after a dull film. What did they mean, and what did they say? One line each. **Answer: They MEANT: "I am bored" (the feeling is theirs). They SAID: other people find them dull.** — the swap moves the feeling to the wrong owner.

**3. (Level 3)** Build the pair from "amaze" and use both coats about one real event. **Answer: amazed / amazing — (learner's own true pair)** — Cambodia's Grade 10 unit names amazing/amazed as its model pair (Unit 11).
