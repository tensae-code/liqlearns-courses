# Participle adjectives — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "A boring teacher" means the teacher feels bored. **Answer: False** — boring is the cause coat: the teacher makes OTHERS bored; the teacher's own feeling would be bored.

**2. (Level 2)** Participle adjectives tell us when something happened. **Answer: False** — they describe, they do not tense — Mongolia lists them for describing people (English IX, Unit 1).

**3. (Level 3)** A person can be tired and boring in the same afternoon. **Answer: True** — different owners in different sentences: she feels tired (-ed); her lesson causes boredom (-ing).
