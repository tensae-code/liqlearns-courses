# Participle adjectives — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Participle Adjectives — the Pair · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Participle Adjectives — the Pair at Work · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Participle Adjectives — the Classic Swap, except the Egypt review-pair item, which is Sub lesson 2 by what it asks, and the *I am boring* analysis item in short-answer, which is Sub lesson 3 by what it asks.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "A boring teacher" means the teacher feels bored. **Answer: False** — boring is the cause coat: the teacher makes OTHERS bored; the teacher's own feeling would be bored.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Participle adjectives tell us when something happened. **Answer: False** — they describe, they do not tense — Mongolia lists them for describing people (English IX, Unit 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A person can be tired and boring in the same afternoon. **Answer: True** — different owners in different sentences: she feels tired (-ed); her lesson causes boredom (-ing).
