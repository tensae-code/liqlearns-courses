# Participle adjectives — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** The students were ______ by the long speech. (tire → feeler coat) **Answer: tired** — feelers take -ed: tired; the speech was tiring.

**2. (Level 2)** The speech was ______. (tire → cause coat) **Answer: tiring** — causes take -ing: tiring.

**3. (Level 2)** She is ______ in music. (interest) **Answer: interested** — the feeler coat + in — Cambodia's Unit 11 pair, boring/bored, amazing/amazed, runs the same way.

**4. (Level 3)** "I feel ______. This movie bores me." (Albania's own set) **Answer: bored** — the guide's three-sentence set: I feel bored / this movie bores me / this movie is boring.
