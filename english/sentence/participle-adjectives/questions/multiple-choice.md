# Participle adjectives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "The lesson was long and dull. I was ______." a) boring  b) bored  c) bore **Answer: b) bored** — the feeler wears -ed; the lesson that caused it is boring.

**2. (Level 1)** "This book is really ______. I can't put it down." a) interested  b) interesting  c) interest **Answer: b) interesting** — the cause wears -ing; you, the feeler, are interested.

**3. (Level 2)** "The ______ news surprised everyone." (before the noun) a) surprised  b) surprising  c) surprise **Answer: b) surprising** — the news causes the feeling, so -ing — in the before-noun home too.

**4. (Level 3)** "I am interesting in football" is wrong because — a) the coat and the preposition: interested in  b) football takes no adjectives  c) -ed never follows am **Answer: a)** — interested (feeler, -ed) takes in; Albania's guide vocabulary lists "interested in" as the set phrase.
