Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Participle Adjectives — the Pair · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Participle Adjectives — the Pair at Work · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Participle Adjectives — the Classic Swap, except the Egypt review-pair item, which is Sub lesson 2 by what it asks, and the *I am boring* analysis item in short-answer, which is Sub lesson 3 by what it asks.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The lesson was long and dull. I was ______." a) boring  b) bored  c) bore **Answer: b) bored** — the feeler wears -ed; the lesson that caused it is boring.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "This book is really ______. I can't put it down." a) interested  b) interesting  c) interest **Answer: b) interesting** — the cause wears -ing; you, the feeler, are interested.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The ______ news surprised everyone." (before the noun) a) surprised  b) surprising  c) surprise **Answer: b) surprising** — the news causes the feeling, so -ing — in the before-noun home too.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I am interesting in football" is wrong because — a) the coat and the preposition: interested in  b) football takes no adjectives  c) -ed never follows am **Answer: a)** — interested (feeler, -ed) takes in; Albania's guide vocabulary lists "interested in" as the set phrase.
