# Video script — Participle adjectives

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

The film is long. The film is slow.

I sleep. I feel nothing.

The movie is boring. I am bored.

Hear the two coats.

-ing: the thing that causes the feeling.

The movie is boring. The book is interesting. The lesson is tiring.

-ed: the person who feels it.

I am bored. She is interested. We are tired.

Same verb. Two coats. Two owners.

Say it wrong — "I am boring" — and you say: I make OTHER people sleep!

Who feels it? -ed.

What causes it? -ing.

Now you try. One film. Two sentences. Both coats.
