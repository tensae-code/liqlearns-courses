# The present perfect — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Present Perfect's Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Job — the Tie to Now (item 4, just happened a moment ago), except item 5, already in its seat -> Sub lesson 3 — The Travel Words and the Bright Line · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** I ______ (clean) the windows.
**Answer: have cleaned** — have with I, + the -ed
participle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** He ______ (not wash) the car.
**Answer: has not washed / hasn't washed** — Libya's box
sentence, p.32.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** She has ______ (write) three letters.
**Answer: written** — write–wrote–written; the movers
change their whole shape (Egypt's examples).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** I've ______ read a ghost story! (the
word that says it happened a moment ago, sitting between
have and the participle)
**Answer: just** — "I've just read a ghost story"
(Libya, Grade 8, p.34; its Teacher's Book states the seat).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Tariq's father has ______ seen the
Pyramids. (sooner than expected)
**Answer: already** (Libya, Grade 9, p.33).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** I haven't picked up the brochures
______.
**Answer: yet** — yet waits at the end of questions and
not-forms (Libya, Grade 9, p.33).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Have you ______ visited England? —
No, I've ______ visited England. (the experience pair)
**Answer: ever / never.**
