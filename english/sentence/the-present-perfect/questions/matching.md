# The present perfect — matching

Match each verb to its past participle, the way Libya's
Work Book tables lay the triples out.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — the -ed crowd and the movers.
1. clean — **cleaned**
2. paint — **painted**
3. go — **gone**
4. see — **seen**
5. eat — **eaten**
6. write — **written**
7. build — **built**
8. sweep — **swept**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — the triple middles. Match each verb
to its full triple (present – past – participle).
1. give — **gave – given**
2. do — **did – done**
3. forget — **forgot – forgotten**
4. drink — **drank – drunk**
5. buy — **bought – bought**
6. hear — **heard – heard**

(The triples in Set 2 are Libya's Grade 8 Work Book verb
tables, as filed; the participle — the third shape — is
the one this tense wears.)
