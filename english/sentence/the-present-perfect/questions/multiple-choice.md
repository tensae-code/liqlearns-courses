# The present perfect — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Present Perfect's Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Job — the Tie to Now · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Travel Words and the Bright Line.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "She ______ cleaned the windows."
a) have  b) has  c) is
**Answer: b) has** — has with he, she and it (Egypt's Tip).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "They ______ washed the car."
a) have  b) has  c) having
**Answer: a) have** — have with I, you, we and they.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The past participle of "go" is —
a) went  b) goed  c) gone
**Answer: c) gone** — the triple is go–went–gone; went is
the past simple's shape, not the participle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "I ______ my key. Now I can't open the door."
a) have lost  b) lose yesterday  c) am lose
**Answer: a) have lost** — a result you can see now is this
tense's home (Egypt).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "We have lived here ______ 2021."
a) for  b) since  c) ago
**Answer: b) since** — since names the starting point;
for names the length (Libya, Grade 9 reference).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is RIGHT?
a) I have cooked chicken on Thursday.
b) I have cooked chicken this week.
c) I have cook chicken this week.
**Answer: b)** — the period may be named; an exact finished
time may not (Libya, Teacher's Book).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "Have you ever been to France?" — "Yes, I
______ there last year."
a) have been  b) went  c) have gone
**Answer: b) went** — "last year" is a definite time, so
the past simple takes the seat (Libya, Work Book).
