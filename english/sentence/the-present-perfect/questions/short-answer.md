# The present perfect — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Travel Words and the Bright Line (items 1–2: the short answers are taught in the Asking close at the explanation's end) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Job — the Tie to Now · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 (item 5, the ever interview against the bright line). Items are marked by what each item actually asks.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Has Gwen cooked the chicken? (yes)
**Answer: Yes, she has.** (Libya, Grade 8, p.33 — the
short answer keeps the partner and drops the rest.)

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Have they washed the car? (no)
**Answer: No, they haven't.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your friend stands at a locked door,
holding an empty pocket. Say what happened, in the
present perfect.
**Answer: I have lost my key.** (Any true result sentence
in have/has + participle earns it.)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How long have you known your best
friend? Answer with for.
**Answer: I have known my friend for … years.** (The
learner's own true number; for + a length of time.)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask your companion about one life
experience — swimming in the sea — then ask when, and
write both sentences.
**Answer: Have you ever swum in the sea? — Yes, I have.
I swam there last summer.** (The ever question in the
perfect; the dated answer in the past simple.)
