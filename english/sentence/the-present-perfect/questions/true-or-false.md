# The present perfect — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** The present perfect is have/has + the
past participle.
**True** — the shape as Libya's Grade 8 grammar box
states it, p.32.

**2. (Level 1)** "Has" goes with I, you, we and they.
**False** — has goes with he, she and it; have goes with
I, you, we and they (Egypt's Tip, p.47).

**3. (Level 2)** "I have cooked chicken this week" is a
good present-perfect sentence.
**True** — this week is a period that reaches now; the
tense may name the period (Libya, Teacher's Book).

**4. (Level 3)** "I have cooked chicken on Thursday" is
also good.
**False** — Thursday is an exact finished time; the
present perfect does not say exactly when. Say "I cooked
chicken on Thursday." (Libya, Teacher's Book.)

**5. (Level 3)** In "Have you ever been to France? — Yes,
I went there last year," both tenses are in their right
seats.
**True** — the question asks about any time in the past;
the answer names a definite time, so the past simple
answers (Libya, Work Book, Unit 5, Lesson 3).
