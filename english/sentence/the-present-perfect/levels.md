# The present perfect — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the tense that ties the past to
now — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the shape
first (Libya and Egypt both open with the form), then the jobs,
then the travel words and the bright line against the past
simple.

## Level 1 — the shape
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build
have/has + participle for every who, statements and
not-forms, with the regulars (-ed) and the first movers
(gone, seen, eaten, written, built, swept) — and shrink
them (I've, hasn't) without a miss.

## Level 2 — the jobs
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose
the tense for its job — just happened inside the open
stretch (today, this week), a result you can see now, and
started-then-still-going with for and since — and ask with
short answers (Yes, she has. / No, they haven't.).

## Level 3 — experience and the bright line
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run an
ever interview with ever, never, already, yet and just in
their seats — and hold the bright line live: any exact
finished time (yesterday, last year, on Thursday) switches
the sentence to the past simple, every time.
