# The present perfect — sub lessons and depth

LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The

## Sub lesson 1 — The Present Perfect's Shape
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build have/has + participle for every who, statements and not-forms, with the regulars (-ed) and the first movers (gone, seen, eaten, written, built, swept) — and shrink them (I've, hasn't) without a miss.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Job — the Tie to Now
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose the tense for its job — just happened inside the open stretch (today, this week), a result you can see now, and started-then-still-going with for and since — and ask with short answers (Yes, she has. / No, they haven't.).

## Sub lesson 3 — The Travel Words and the Bright Line
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run an ever interview with ever, never, already, yet and just in their seats — and hold the bright line live: any exact finished time (yesterday, last year, on Thursday) switches the sentence to the past simple, every time.
