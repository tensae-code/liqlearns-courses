# Hands-on — The present perfect

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real room, with a companion or the Explain
Companion.

1. **The participle hunt (the books' own game).** Libya's
   Grade 8 book opens the tense with a song and asks: how
   many past participles can you find? Play it at home —
   one person sings or says done-things (I've cleaned…,
   I've washed…, I've swept…), the other writes each
   participle and marks the ones that are DIFFERENT from
   the -ed crowd (swept, tidied, gone). Score: one point
   per participle, two for a mover.
2. **The done-room walk.** Walk the home together. At each
   stop, one true present-perfect sentence about a result
   you can see — never a time. The companion's only job:
   shout "exact time!" if a finished time sneaks in, and
   the sentence must change coats to the past simple.
3. **The ever interview, live.** Five Have you ever…?
   questions to a real person (or the companion). Every
   yes earns a follow-up — When? — and the answer must
   come back in the past simple with its time. Write the
   five pairs down: perfect question, past answer.
4. **For and since with your own life.** Hold up fingers
   for the years or days: I have known my friend for six
   years. I have lived here since 2021. Three true
   sentences each way — if it stopped, it may not wear
   this tense.
