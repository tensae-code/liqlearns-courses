# Hands-on — Commas in Lists, Dates and Places

## Untangle the list

In pairs: one partner writes a sentence with a list of five things and NO commas. The other reads it aloud, then writes it again with the commas in place and "and" joining the last two. Read both versions aloud — hear how the commas slow the list into separate things.

## Place and date lines

Write three place lines (a town or city you know, then its country, with the comma between) and three full dates that matter to you (a birthday, a school day, a national day), each with its comma between the day and the year. Swap with a partner and check each other's commas.
