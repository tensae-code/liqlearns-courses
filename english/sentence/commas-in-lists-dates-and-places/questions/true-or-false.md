## True or false

1. A comma stands between the words of a list so they do not run together. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. Every word in a list, including the very last one, is followed by a comma. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the last two words are joined by "and", with no comma before the "and".

3. "Georgetown, Guyana" is written with a comma between the city and the country. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

4. A date is written with a comma between the day and the year. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — "23rd February, 1970".

5. A comma ends a sentence, the way a full stop does. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a comma is a small pause inside the sentence; the full stop ends it.
