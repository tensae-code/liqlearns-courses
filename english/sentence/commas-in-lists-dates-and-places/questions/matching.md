## Matching

Match the writing job to the comma's place in it. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. A list of fruits in a sentence — b
2. A city and its country — d
3. A day and its year — a
4. The last two words of a list — e
5. The end of a whole sentence — c

a) The comma stands between them
b) Commas stand between the words, and "and" joins the last two
c) No comma — a full stop stands there
d) The comma stands between them, city first
e) No comma — "and" joins them

**Answers:** 1–b (the series pattern), 2–d (Georgetown, Guyana), 3–a (23rd February, 1970), 4–e (oranges and pineapple), 5–c (a sentence ends with a full stop, not a comma).
