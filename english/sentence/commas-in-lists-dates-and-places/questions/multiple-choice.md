## Multiple choice

1. What does a comma do between the words of a list? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) It ends the sentence  b) It keeps each word in its own place so the words do not run together  c) It joins the sentence to the next one
   **Answer: b)** — the comma separates the items of the series.

2. Which sentence has its list commas in the right places? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "We bought mangoes, bananas, oranges and pineapple."  b) "We bought mangoes bananas, oranges, and, pineapple."  c) "We bought, mangoes bananas oranges and pineapple."
   **Answer: a)** — a comma after each list word, and "and" joining the last two.

3. In "mangoes, bananas, oranges and pineapple", how many commas stand in the list? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Four  b) Two  c) Three
   **Answer: c) Three** — one after each word except the last two, which are joined by "and".

4. Which place line is written the right way? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Georgetown Guyana"  b) "Georgetown, Guyana"  c) "Georgetown. Guyana"
   **Answer: b)** — a comma stands between the city and the country.

5. Where does the comma stand in a full date? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Between the day and the year  b) After the year  c) Before the day
   **Answer: a)** — "23rd February, 1970": day, comma, year.

6. When you read a comma aloud, your voice ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) stops for good  b) takes a small pause, then the sentence runs on  c) reads the next word twice
   **Answer: b)** — a comma is a pause, not a stop; the full stop is the stop.
