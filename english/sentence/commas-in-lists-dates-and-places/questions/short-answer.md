## Short answer

1. Name the comma's three jobs. (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer:** Between the words in a series (a list), between a city and its country, and between the day and the year in a date.

2. Fix this sentence and say what you fixed: "For lunch we had rice chicken plantain and cake." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** "For lunch we had rice, chicken, plantain and cake." — commas were missing between the words of the list.

3. Write the place line for the capital of Guyana the right way. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Georgetown, Guyana — city, comma, country.

4. How is a comma different from a full stop when you read aloud? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** At a comma your voice takes a small pause and the sentence runs on; at a full stop your voice stops and the sentence ends.
