## Fill in the blank

1. "A list of words inside one sentence is called a ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: series.**

2. "In a list, a ___ stands between the words so they do not run together." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: comma.**

3. "The last two words of a list are joined by the word ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: and.**

4. "Georgetown ___ Guyana — the missing mark between the city and the country is a comma." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: , (a comma).**

5. "Guyana became a Co-operative Republic on 23rd February ___ 1970." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: , (a comma — between the day and the year).**
