# Video script — Commas in Lists, Dates and Places

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A small hook-shaped mark appears, big.] "This is a COMMA. It is small — but it has THREE jobs."

[Four fruits pop in one by one, a comma dropping between the first three: mangoes, bananas, oranges and pineapple.] "Job one: in a LIST, the comma stands between the words. Mangoes, bananas, oranges — and pineapple. The last two join with AND!"

[The words "Georgetown, Guyana" appear, the comma glowing.] "Job two: between a CITY and its COUNTRY. Georgetown — comma — Guyana!"

[The date "23rd February, 1970" appears, the comma glowing.] "Job three: in a DATE, between the DAY and the YEAR. The twenty-third of February — comma — nineteen seventy!"

[Back to the mascot.] "Three jobs, one small mark: lists, places, dates. That is the COMMA!"
