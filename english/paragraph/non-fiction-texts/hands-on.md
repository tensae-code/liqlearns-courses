# Hands-On — Non-Fiction Texts

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, a pencil, and any newspaper, magazine or anthology you can get.

1. **Sort the page.** Take one page of a newspaper or magazine. Mark each piece: story-like (invented) or non-fiction (true)? Most newspapers are all non-fiction — find the one piece that argues an opinion.
2. **Name the kind.** From Sub lesson 2, write: speech, diary, history, disaster report. Match each to its piece in the Class VII–VIII sets.
3. **One true paragraph.** Write four sentences about something true that happened this week — who, where, what happened, what followed. That is personal non-fiction, like "The Unfortunate Break".
4. **Argue a title.** Khushwant Singh's essay is called "English Zindabad versus Angrezi Hatao". Write one sentence saying what you think the essay argues, before anyone tells you. Then check it against the lesson.
