# Short Answer — Non-Fiction Texts

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the three Class IV essays. (Sub lesson 1)
   — "Friendship"; "Some Small Mammals in Bhutan"; "Artificial Intelligence".
2. What does "Let's Talk Advertising" teach the reader to weigh? (Sub lesson 1)
   — How advertising works, why media depend on it, and the pros and cons consumers must weigh.
3. What is "The Unfortunate Break" about, and who wrote it? (Sub lesson 2)
   — A fourteen-year-old's personal narrative of Covid-19 lockdown; Jeremiah I. Reyes.
4. Which Class VIII piece is about Bhutan itself, and what is it about? (Sub lesson 2)
   — "Prayer Flags Blowing in the Wind" (Gustasp Irani) — what prayer flags are and what is really blowing in the wind.
5. What does the title "English Zindabad versus Angrezi Hatao" tell you before you read a word? (Sub lesson 3)
   — That the essay argues the case for English in India ("English Zindabad" — long live English) against the drive to remove it ("Angrezi Hatao" — remove English).
