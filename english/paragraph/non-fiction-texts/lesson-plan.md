# Lesson Plan — Non-Fiction Texts

**One thing:** the non-fiction sections of a school anthology — reading real-world prose text by text: informational essays, memoir and personal narrative, speeches and diary — and what each piece is and does.

## Sub lessons

1. Informational essays in the Classes IV–VI anthologies
2. Personal non-fiction, memoir and speech in the Classes VII–VIII anthologies
3. Non-fiction in the senior anthologies, Classes IX–XII

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Each DSE anthology carries an essay/non-fiction section between its stories and its poems (Classes IV–XII anthologies).
- The junior sets teach information prose on near topics: friendship, small mammals of Bhutan, how young animals are protected, advertising, artificial intelligence (Classes IV–VI).
- The middle sets widen to the world: a child's Earth Summit speech, Anne Frank's diary, Gandhi and the Salt March, a cyclone rescue (Classes VII–VIII).
- The senior sets are read with the books' critical-reading apparatus — the Class IX book's "Menu of Critical Reading Questions" frames the whole senior run (Classes IX–XII).
