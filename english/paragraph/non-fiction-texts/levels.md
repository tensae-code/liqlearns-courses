# Levels — Non-Fiction Texts

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Informational essays in the Classes IV–VI anthologies | Name the essay sets of the three junior anthologies and say what each piece informs about. |
| 2. Personal non-fiction, memoir and speech in the Classes VII–VIII anthologies | Name the middle sets' real-world texts — diary, speech, history, report — and their writers. |
| 3. Non-fiction in the senior anthologies, Classes IX–XII | Name the senior sets and the critical-reading frame they are studied under. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons knows the whole non-fiction spine of Bhutan's school anthologies, book by book.
