# Lesson Plan — Communicating and Researching Online

**One thing:** working through electronic media on purpose — communicating through it, gathering and documenting research on the internet and paraphrasing what you find in your own words, and creating online work (websites, presentations, reports) with the media chosen deliberately for the viewer's purpose.

## Sub lessons

1. Communicating through electronic media
2. Researching on the internet
3. Making and choosing online

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Communicating through electronic media; presenting thoughts and feelings for specified purposes and audiences; using electronic text to explore human relationships; creating audio-visual presentations (Secondary Language Arts Curriculum Guidelines, objectives 8.11–8.22, printed pp.81–83).
- Using the internet for research and documentation; gathering information via the internet; paraphrasing information taken from the internet (objectives 8.11–8.22, printed pp.81–83).
- Creating websites and internet presentations; creating electronic presentations and written reports; justifying the choice of electronic devices; selecting media forms for the viewer's purpose (objectives 8.11–8.22, printed pp.81–83).
