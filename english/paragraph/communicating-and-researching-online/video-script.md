# Video Script — Communicating and Researching Online

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a child about to send a message, two labels appearing: PURPOSE? AUDIENCE?]

Before you send — stop! What is my purpose? Who is my audience? Name them first!

[Picture: a notebook open beside a screen, sources being written down]

Research is a hunt with a notebook. Write the source down, or the fact is lost!

[Picture: a page of copied text melting and reforming in new words]

Paraphrase! Read it, close it, say it in YOUR words. Same meaning — your words!

[Picture: three doors: WEBSITE, PRESENTATION, REPORT — a viewer choosing]

Choose the form for the viewer! What does the viewer need? The viewer's purpose picks the form.
