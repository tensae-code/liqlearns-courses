# Short Answer — Communicating and Researching Online

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Rewrite this message for a teacher: "hey, the hw thing is gonna be late lol." What changed? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Register, completeness and politeness change for the audience — e.g. "Good morning. My homework will be late because…" — full sentences, respectful address, a reason given (any sound rewrite with the changes named).
2. Give the steps of honest online research. (Sub lesson 2)
   — State the question; gather from sources; document source, place and date; paraphrase in your own words; keep the meaning and name the sources.
3. Why is paraphrase the border between research and theft? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Because paraphrase takes the meaning and rebuilds it in your own words, while copying takes the writer's words themselves — the first is learning from a source, the second is taking their work.
4. A class must show parents how a science project grew. Website, presentation or report — choose and justify. (Sub lesson 3)
   — Any form with a one-line justification tied to the viewers' purpose — e.g. a presentation, because parents watch it together in sequence and each stage can be shown as it is reached.
