# Hands-On — Communicating and Researching Online

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Audience swap.** Write one message for a friend; rewrite it for a teacher and for a grandparent. Present the three versions and name what changed.
2. **Two-source hunt.** Pairs research one question in two sources, document both, and present a three-sentence answer in their own words.
3. **Paraphrase line.** Each learner takes one internet paragraph, paraphrases it, and the class checks each paraphrase against its source: meaning kept? words their own?
4. **Form court.** Three teams argue for website, presentation or report as the best form for one named job and viewer. The class judges the justifications.
