# Levels — Communicating and Researching Online

One lesson, 3 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Communicating through electronic media | Communicate through electronic media for specified purposes and audiences, create audio-visual presentations, and present thoughts and feelings through electronic text. |
| 2. Researching on the internet | Use the internet for research and documentation, gather information for a purpose, and paraphrase information taken from the internet in your own words. |
| 3. Making and choosing online | Create websites and internet presentations and electronic presentations and written reports, justify the choice of electronic devices, and select media forms for the viewer's purpose. |

A learner who finishes all 3 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: working through electronic media on purpose — communicating through it, gathering and documenting research on the internet and paraphrasing what you find in your own words, and creating online work (websites, presentations, reports) with the media chosen deliberately for the viewer's purpose.
