# Hands-On — Listening and Following Instructions

Things you do with a partner. You need nothing but a voice.

1. **Body listening.** Your partner talks for one minute about anything. Face them, keep occasional eye contact, do not interrupt. Then tell back two things they said.
2. **Two steps.** Your partner gives you one instruction of two steps ("Open the door, then close it"). Listen to the whole instruction before you move. Say the steps back in order, then do them.
3. **Three steps.** Same game, with three steps. If a step is missed, the instruction is said again once — and the listener must name which step was missed.
4. **The memory game.** Your partner reads a short list of actions as one instruction ("Touch the table, touch your head, turn around"). Hold all of them and do them in order. Build up to four and five actions.
