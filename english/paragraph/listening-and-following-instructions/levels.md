# Levels — Listening and Following Instructions

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Listening with your whole body | Listen actively when someone speaks: face the speaker, keep occasional eye contact, and stay with what is said until it ends. |
| 2. Following instructions of two steps or more | Hear an instruction of at least two steps, hold all the steps, and do them in order — in class work and in a game. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can be given a two-step instruction once, in words alone, and carry it out.
