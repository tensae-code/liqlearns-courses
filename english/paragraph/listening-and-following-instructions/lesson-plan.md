# Lesson Plan — Listening and Following Instructions

**One thing:** listening as a doing skill — first listening with your whole body (eyes on the speaker, facing them), then holding a spoken instruction of two steps or more in your head and doing the steps in order.

## Sub lessons

1. Listening with your whole body
2. Following instructions of two steps or more

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Active listening is taught as its own skill: listen when someone speaks to you, with occasional eye contact (worked example "Let's Listen to Our Friend") (NEL Guide, p.37).
- Understanding and following verbal instructions involving at least two steps is a named knowledge/skill (KSD 1.2), done listening attentively, with eye contact and facing the speaker (p.27).
- The same skill is practised inside a game: the "Memory Game" work has children listen and follow verbal instructions of at least two steps (pp.28–29).
