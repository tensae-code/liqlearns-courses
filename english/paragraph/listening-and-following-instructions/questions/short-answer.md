# Short Answer — Listening and Following Instructions

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name two things your body does in active listening. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any two of: face the speaker; keep occasional eye contact; stay with what is said until the speaker finishes (listening attentively).

2. Your friend talks to you while you look at the floor and play with a stone. What is missing? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Body listening: facing the speaker and occasional eye contact — active listening.

3. Say the instruction "Close the book and give it to me" as two numbered steps. (Sub lesson 2)
   — 1. Close the book. 2. Give it to me.

4. Why is it a mistake to start doing step one before the instruction is finished? (Sub lesson 2)
   — You miss the later steps; the rule is: hear it all first, then do it all, in order.

5. How does the Memory Game practise this lesson's skill? (Sub lesson 2)
   — The player must listen to verbal instructions of at least two steps and follow them inside the game.
