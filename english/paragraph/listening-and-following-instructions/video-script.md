# Video Script — Listening and Following Instructions

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The listener faces the speaker the whole time.

[Picture: two children talking; the listener faces the speaker, eyes on them]

When your friend speaks, listen with your whole body. Face your friend. Look at their eyes, sometimes. Listen until the end.

[Picture: a teacher speaking; a speech bubble shows two steps: "Take the book." "Put it on the table."]

Now an instruction. Two steps! First: take the book. Then: put it on the table. Listen to ALL of it first.

[Picture: the child takes the book, then puts it on the table, in order]

Hold the steps in your head. One… two… Now do them. First this. Then that. You listened — and you did it!
