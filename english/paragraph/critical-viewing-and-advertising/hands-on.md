# Hands-On — Critical Viewing and Advertising

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Five-minute autopsy.** Watch five minutes of a programme in pairs. Record purpose, creator, main idea and two visual cues; defend each from the screen.
2. **Print vs film.** Read one scene, watch it filmed. Chart the portrayal of character, plot and setting: same, cut, changed, added.
3. **Ad autopsy.** Each pair takes one advertisement through the four steps and names two techniques. The class votes on the most honest reading.
4. **Graph check.** Find two pictorial graphs in media or books. Measure the pictures against the numbers: honest growth or quiet exaggeration?
