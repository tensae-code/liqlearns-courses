# Video Script — Critical Viewing and Advertising

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a television screen opening like a machine, gears and hands visible inside]

Nothing on a screen is by accident! Someone made it — for a purpose. Ask: what is this FOR?

[Picture: a book page and a film frame side by side, the same character in both]

Same story, two forms! Compare the character. Your picture — or the actor's face? Which is stronger?

[Picture: an advertisement's bright promise, then the fine print and an empty space marked MISSING FACT]

An ad has four steps: product, promise, feeling — and the fact it does not say. Find the missing fact!

[Picture: a pictorial graph with a bottle picture twice as tall but four times as wide]

Pictures can lie with numbers! A picture twice as tall may hold four times as much. Check the graph!
