# Video script — Words you don't know

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A word you don't know? Don't STOP!

India's books say it on every page:
"**Infer their meaning from the context!**"

Step one: READ ON. Finish the sentence!
Step two: ASK the sentence.
What MUST this word mean?
Look for clues — but… because… like…
Step three: BREAK the word.
un-happy. care-ful. Parts you KNOW!
Step four: the dictionary — LAST!
Pick the meaning that fits THIS sentence.
Put it back in. Does the sentence stand?

Four steps. The text helps you!
Now you. Read past the hard word.
That is reading — in English.
