# Words you don't know — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You meet a word you don't know mid-text.
The ladder's FIRST step is —
a) open the dictionary  b) stop reading  c) read on — finish
the sentence and the paragraph
**Answer: c)** — the missing stone is not a wall.

**2. (Level 1)** "The donkey was laden with sacks, yet it
walked surely, never once stumbling." LADEN most likely
means —
a) afraid  b) loaded / carrying a load  c) singing
**Answer: b)** — the sacks and the walking carry it; yet
sets the contrast that confirms it.

**3. (Level 2)** Which clue is working in "Unlike his
brother, who was timid, Dawit was bold"?
a) a restating neighbour  b) a contrast — unlike/timid sets
bold against its opposite  c) a cause
**Answer: b).**

**4. (Level 2)** Break CARELESS: its parts vote —
a) car + less (a small car)  b) care + -less (without care)
c) it cannot be broken
**Answer: b)** — care + -less, "without care."

**5. (Level 3)** The dictionary gives a word three meanings.
You choose —
a) the first, always  b) the one that fits THIS sentence,
swap-tested in place  c) the longest
**Answer: b)** — the 'dry' law from the Dictionary lesson.

**6. (Level 3)** India's senior books print at every unit's
head: "Infer their meaning from the ______."
a) pictures  b) context  c) teacher
**Answer: b) context** — the command the whole ladder
obeys.
