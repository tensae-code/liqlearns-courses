# Words you don't know — matching

Match each item to its clue kind, step, or sense.

**Set 1 (Level 2)** — which clue carries it?
1. "He was exhausted — that is, he could not walk another
   step." — **a restating neighbour (that is…)**
2. "She sang because she was happy." (HAPPY unknown; the
   singing as evidence) — **a cause (because)**
3. "Unlike the dry season, the wet season drowned the
   fields." — **a contrast (unlike)**
4. "The laden cart groaned under its sacks." — **the words
   around (under its sacks)**

**Set 2 (Level 1–3)** — the ladder in order.
1. finish the sentence and paragraph — **step 1: read on**
2. state what the word must mean — **step 2: infer**
3. un- + happy; care + -ful — **step 3: break the word**
4. the right entry, the fitting sense — **step 4: check**
5. put the meaning back and read whole — **the swap test**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — DRY, sense by sentence (the
Dictionary lesson's word).
1. "The dry season came." — **not wet (weather)**
2. "His dry bread had no butter." — **without liquid /
   plain**
3. "Her dry answer ended the talk." — **a manner: short,
   unfriendly** (the third sense a bigger dictionary shows —
   chosen by the sentence, as always)
