# Words you don't know — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** context · infer · clue · parts · swap · log

**1. (Level 1)** Infer their meaning from the ______.
(India's command.)
**Answer: context.**

**2. (Level 1)** To work out a meaning from the words around
is to ______ it.
**Answer: infer.**

**3. (Level 2)** Every guess must point at its ______ — the
word or words that carried it.
**Answer: clue.**

**4. (Level 2)** Break long words into ______ you know and
let them vote.
**Answer: parts.**

**5. (Level 3)** After the dictionary, ______-test the
meaning: put it back in the sentence and read the sentence
whole.
**Answer: swap.**

**6. (Level 3)** The four-column record of guesses and
verdicts is the word ______.
**Answer: log.**
