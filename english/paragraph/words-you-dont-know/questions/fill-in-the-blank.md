# Words you don't know — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — Inferring from the Context (items 1–2, the infer-from-context command) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 (item 3, the clue) and Sub lesson 3 — Breaking the Word into Parts (item 4) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Checking and Swap-Testing (items 5–6).

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** context · infer · clue · parts · swap · log

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Infer their meaning from the ______.
(India's command.)
**Answer: context.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To work out a meaning from the words around
is to ______ it.
**Answer: infer.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Every guess must point at its ______ — the
word or words that carried it.
**Answer: clue.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Break long words into ______ you know and
let them vote.
**Answer: parts.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** After the dictionary, ______-test the
meaning: put it back in the sentence and read the sentence
whole.
**Answer: swap.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The four-column record of guesses and
verdicts is the word ______.
**Answer: log.**
