# Words you don't know — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
reader's ladder — reading on first, then inferring from the
clues, then the word parts, then the checked, swap-tested word
log.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 splits at the
ladder's first jump — reading on is Sub lesson 1 and inferring
is Sub lesson 2, and its pass mark splits the same way below;
old Level 2 also splits — its clue kinds are Sub lesson 2 and
its word parts are Sub lesson 3, its pass mark split the same
way; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 4.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading On
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: finish a sentence past its unknown word, every time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Inferring from the Context
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state a meaning the sentence supports — a written guess, in the margin, every time — and name the clue that carries each inference (the words around, a restating neighbour, a contrast, a cause).

## Sub lesson 3 — Breaking the Word into Parts
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: break long words into parts that vote.

## Sub lesson 4 — Checking and Swap-Testing
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run the whole ladder unhurried — guess written, dictionary checked, the sense for THIS sentence chosen, and the meaning swap-tested back into the sentence whole — and keep a word log that shows all four steps.
