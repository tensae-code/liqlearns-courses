# Video script — Library Skills

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding Books — Fiction Shelves and Dewey
- Beat 1: A camera walks a fiction shelf: surnames rolling past in alphabetical order. The presenter stops at the wanted author. Rule on screen: fiction is shelved by the author's surname.
- Beat 2: Ten numbered doors, 000 to 900. The presenter carries a book about volcanoes to the 500s door. Rule on screen: Dewey numbers put every subject in its place.

## Sub lesson 2 — Research Sources in the Library
- Beat 1: A research desk fills up: a box of old documents (archives), a film card at a viewer (microfiche), a laptop (internet), an elder mid-story (oral source). The presenter names each kind.
- Beat 2: An encyclopaedia dated 2002 next to a screen showing a 2003 news story. The presenter: a source cannot know what had not happened yet — check how new your source is.
