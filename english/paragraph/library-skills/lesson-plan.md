# Library Skills — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 15, Reading and the Paragraph

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a library shelf with book spines labelled with author surnames in alphabetical order — ALAE LUA, LAY, MISA, VA'AI — and one book pulled halfway out. *The learners read the surnames left to right and say whose book would come next after MISA.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of ten tall numbered doors in a row, marked 000, 100, 200 … 900, with a learner carrying a book about the sea towards the 500 door. *The learners say what the numbers on the doors are for.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a research desk with four things on it: an old box of documents, a small film card held up to a light, a computer screen, and an older person talking. *The learners name the four kinds of source and say one thing each is best at.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a learner standing between two shelves with an armful of books, putting two back and keeping one open. *The learners say what the learner is doing and why a researcher does not use every source found.*

## Sub lessons
1. Finding Books — Fiction Shelves and Dewey
2. Research Sources in the Library

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
