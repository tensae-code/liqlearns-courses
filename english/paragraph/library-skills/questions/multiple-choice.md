# Multiple choice — Library Skills

1. How is fiction shelved in a library? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. By the colour of the cover
   - B. By the author's surname, in alphabetical order
   - C. By the year it was printed
   - D. By the number of pages
   Answer: B

2. What does the Dewey decimal classification order? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Fiction by author
   - B. Non-fiction by subject, each subject with a number
   - C. Books by size
   - D. New books before old books
   Answer: B

3. Why can a pre-2003 encyclopaedia not tell you about SARS? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. It is written in another language
   - B. SARS was not known when it was printed — a source cannot cover what had not happened
   - C. Encyclopaedias never cover diseases
   - D. It is kept in the archives
   Answer: B

4. Which of these is an oral source? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. A microfiche card
   - B. An archive box
   - C. Interviewing someone knowledgeable
   - D. An encyclopaedia
   Answer: C
