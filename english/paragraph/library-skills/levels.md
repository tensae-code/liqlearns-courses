# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding Books — Fiction Shelves and Dewey
- Depth: how fiction is shelved, by the author's surname, and how to find a wanted author on the shelf (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 3*, printed pp.11–13); the Dewey decimal classification as an order a searcher can use, with author names as the way in (printed pp.14–15).

## Sub lesson 2 — Research Sources in the Library
- Depth: the library's other reference sources and the sources around the school (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 3*, printed pp.20–21); browsing the shelves as a research act (Samoa, *English Year 11 Book 2*, printed pp.108–109); reviewing everything gathered and choosing the best sources for the need (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 3*, printed pp.51–54); and the senior source set — archives as primary sources, microfiche, the internet's currency advantage (no pre-2003 encyclopaedia can cover SARS), and oral sources, interviewing knowledgeable people (Samoa, *English Year 12*, printed pp.61–65).
