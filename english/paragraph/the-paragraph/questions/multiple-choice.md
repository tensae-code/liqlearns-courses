# The paragraph — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A paragraph is —
a) any five sentences  b) a group of sentences all about
one idea  c) one long sentence
**Answer: b)** — belonging is the law (Ghana's test).

**2. (Level 1)** Find the stranger: "The market opens early.
Sellers sell rice and oil. Buyers come from every village.
My bicycle is red."
a) sentence 1  b) sentence 3  c) sentence 4
**Answer: c)** — the bicycle serves another idea.

**3. (Level 2)** The naming (topic) sentence of a paragraph
usually —
a) hides at the end  b) opens the paragraph and names its
idea  c) is the shortest one
**Answer: b)** — the signboard and the promise.

**4. (Level 2)** Which could open a paragraph about a farm?
a) "He went there on Tuesday."  b) "My grandfather's farm
lies beyond the river."  c) "It was very interesting."
**Answer: b)** — it names the idea whole; a) and c) lean on
things not yet given.

**5. (Level 3)** Ghana's process runs: choose topic →
______ → draft → revise → proofread.
a) publish  b) brainstorm and organise (pre-writing)
c) memorise
**Answer: b)** — B3.4.9's own order.

**6. (Level 3)** The revise question that tests a paragraph
is —
a) "Is it long enough?"  b) "Are all the sentences about
the topic?"  c) "Is my handwriting neat?"
**Answer: b)** — Ghana prints it in the revise list itself.
