# The paragraph — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Eject the stranger and name the paragraph's
idea: "We grow rice. The fields are green in June. Rice
feeds our family. The moon is beautiful tonight."
**Answer:** stranger — "The moon is beautiful tonight." The
idea: growing rice (our rice fields).

**2. (Level 2)** Write the naming sentence for a paragraph
whose serving sentences are about your grandmother's
cooking.
**Answer:** e.g. "My grandmother is the best cook in our
village." — any sentence that names the idea the rest will
serve.

**3. (Level 2)** A serving sentence or a naming one? "It has
ten classrooms."
**Answer:** serving — it works for an idea (a school)
already named.

**4. (Level 3)** Give three leading questions for "My
Village" (Bangladesh's own first topic's twin).
**Answer:** e.g. Where is my village? What stands in it?
What do people do there? — answers to these, in order, are
the paragraph.

**5. (Level 3)** Your paragraph reads as two ideas wrestling.
What does the lesson tell you to do?
**Answer:** split it where the idea changes — one paragraph
per idea — and test each half again.

**6. (Level 1)** How does a new paragraph show itself on the
page?
**Answer:** it starts on a new line (often with its first
line stepped in — the indent).
