# The paragraph — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A paragraph may hold sentences about three
ideas if they are all interesting.
**False** — one idea per paragraph; interest does not change
the belonging law.

**2. (Level 1)** A stranger sentence is a bad sentence.
**False** — it is a good sentence in the wrong drawer; it
goes to its own paragraph.

**3. (Level 2)** The topic sentence is a promise the rest of
the paragraph keeps.
**True** — the naming sentence opens; the serving sentences
deliver.

**4. (Level 3)** In Ghana's process, you correct spelling
while drafting the first version.
**False** — the draft runs "without stopping for
corrections"; proofreading (CUPS) comes at its own later
step.

**5. (Level 3)** Egypt's senior paragraphs are judged partly
by length (word counts like 150–180).
**True** — as filed; length is the exam's dress, belonging
is the paragraph's soul.

**6. (Level 2)** If a stranger is found, deleting it is the
only cure.
**False** — moving it to its own paragraph is the
lesson-standard cure; deletion is for what serves no idea
anywhere.
