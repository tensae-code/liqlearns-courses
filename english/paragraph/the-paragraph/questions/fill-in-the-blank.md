# The paragraph — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** idea · topic · stranger · serving · leading ·
revise

**1. (Level 1)** A paragraph is a group of sentences about
one ______.
**Answer: idea.**

**2. (Level 1)** A sentence that serves another idea is a
______.
**Answer: stranger.**

**3. (Level 2)** The first sentence that names the idea is
the ______ sentence.
**Answer: topic.**

**4. (Level 2)** Sentences that work for the idea are its
______ sentences.
**Answer: serving.**

**5. (Level 3)** Ghana builds paragraphs from ______
questions — the answers become the sentences.
**Answer: leading.**

**6. (Level 3)** In Ghana's process you ______ against
printed questions before you proofread.
**Answer: revise.**
