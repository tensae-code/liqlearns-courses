# Video script — The paragraph

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sentences are bricks. A PARAGRAPH is a wall —
all the bricks about ONE thing!

"My school is near the river."
"It has ten classrooms."
"The teachers are kind."

One idea: my SCHOOL.
The first sentence NAMES it — like a sign!

But look: "My cat is black."
A cat in the school wall? A STRANGER!
Out it goes — to its own wall!

Ghana's test, ask it always:
"Are ALL the sentences about the topic?"

Ask questions first. Answer them in sentences.
That is a paragraph being born!

Now you. One idea. Every sentence serving it.
That is a paragraph — in English.
