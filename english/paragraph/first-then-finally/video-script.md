# Video script — First, then, finally

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Making tea — in ORDER!

**First,** boil the water.
**Then,** put the tea in the cup.
**After that,** pour the water.
**Finally,** drink!

Hear the road? First… then… finally.
The words are SIGNPOSTS. They hold the order!

Scramble them — the tea breaks!
Boil AFTER drinking? No!
Order is meaning!

Tell your day the same way:
"First, we went… Then, we ate… Finally, we slept."

Now you. Lead me through something!
Step by step — in English.
