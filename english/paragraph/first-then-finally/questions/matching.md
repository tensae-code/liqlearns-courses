# First, then, finally — matching

Match each signpost to its seat, and each scrambled set to
its rescue.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — seats on the road.
1. First — **opens: step one**
2. Then — **the middle: the step after**
3. Next — **the middle: another step after**
4. After that — **the middle: following on**
5. Finally — **closes: the last step**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — which home?
1. "First, wash the rice. Then, boil it." — **a process
   (how-to)**
2. "First, we left home. Finally, we reached the river." —
   **a recount (a finished day)**
3. "After that, pour the water into the cup." — **a
   process**
4. "Then, the rain stopped and we went out." — **a recount**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — rescue the order (match the set to its
true first step).
1. (pour the tea / boil the water / drink) — **boil the
   water first**
2. (plant the seed / dig the hole / water it) — **dig the
   hole first**
3. (went home / woke up / ate breakfast) as a day — **woke
   up first**
