# First, then, finally — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the signpost the road needs.

**Box:** First · Then · Next · After that · Finally

**1. (Level 1)** ______, make the bed. Then, sweep the
floor.
**Answer: First.**

**2. (Level 1)** First, wash the rice. ______, boil it.
Finally, eat.
**Answer: Then** (Next also earns it).

**3. (Level 2)** First, we packed. ______, we walked to the
bus. Finally, we rode away.
**Answer: After that** (Then / Next also true).

**4. (Level 2)** ______, the story ends and everyone goes
home.
**Answer: Finally.**

**5. (Level 3)** A road that stutters "Then… then… then…"
should vary its middle with ______ and ______.
**Answer: next — after that.**

**6. (Level 3)** The signpost that may never open a road is
______.
**Answer: Finally** — it closes; it cannot begin.
