# First, then, finally — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Process Home · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Recount Home · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Judgment at the Joints.
Fill each blank with the signpost the road needs.

**Box:** First · Then · Next · After that · Finally

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______, make the bed. Then, sweep the
floor.
**Answer: First.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** First, wash the rice. ______, boil it.
Finally, eat.
**Answer: Then** (Next also earns it).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** First, we packed. ______, we walked to the
bus. Finally, we rode away.
**Answer: After that** (Then / Next also true).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ______, the story ends and everyone goes
home.
**Answer: Finally.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A road that stutters "Then… then… then…"
should vary its middle with ______ and ______.
**Answer: next — after that.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The signpost that may never open a road is
______.
**Answer: Finally** — it closes; it cannot begin.
