# First, then, finally — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Process Home · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Recount Home · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Judgment at the Joints.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The signpost that CLOSES the road is —
a) next  b) finally  c) after that
**Answer: b) finally** — the reader knows the end has come.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A how-to paragraph for planting runs:
"First, dig the hole. ______, put in the seed. Finally,
water it."
a) Finally  b) Then  c) Before everything
**Answer: b) Then** — a middle step takes a middle word.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which recount opening stands right?
a) "Then, we woke up early." b) "First, we woke up early.
Then, we ate." c) "Finally, we woke up."
**Answer: b)** — First opens a road; then carries it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt's Tip says linking words make the
story —
a) longer  b) clear and easy to follow  c) harder
**Answer: b)** — the Tip's own words.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "First, drink the tea. Then, boil the
water." The fault is —
a) the signposts are spelled wrong  b) the ORDER is untrue
in the world — the words dress a lie  c) no fault
**Answer: b)** — signposts are promises; the world's order
comes first.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Mongolia files first/then/next under the
grammar name —
a) adverbs of sequence  b) linking nouns  c) sequence verbs
**Answer: a)** — Grammar Reference, as filed.
