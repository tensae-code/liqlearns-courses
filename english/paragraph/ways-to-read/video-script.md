# Video script — Ways to read

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Reading is not one road. It is many.

First: skim. Fly over the page. Fast! What is it about? Only the big shape.

Second: scan. Hunt ONE thing. A name. A date. A number. Do not read the rest.

Then the big system. S-Q-3-R!

Survey the text. Turn the headings into Questions. Read. Recite — say it back! Review.

And K-W-L. What do I Know? What do I Want to know? What did I Learn?

Choose the road BEFORE you read. That is the whole secret.
