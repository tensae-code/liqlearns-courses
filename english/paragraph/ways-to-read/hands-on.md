# Hands-on — Ways to read

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The 3-minute skim.** Take a page you have never read. Skim it for three minutes — Ghana's timed drill — then close it and state its gist in one sentence.
2. **The scan race.** Libya's game: a partner asks for a name, an age and a country hidden in a text. Scan — do not read — and race the clock to find all three.
3. **Full SQ3R.** Run all five stops on one study text, on paper: survey notes, your questions, the reading, a spoken recite with the book closed, and a next-day review.
4. **KWL chart.** Draw K-W-L for a topic you are about to study. Fill K and W before, L after — and notice how the W column steered your reading.
