# Ways to read — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name the five stops of SQ3R, in order. **Answer: Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review** — Ghana's curriculum names the system in full (curriculum evidence)

**2. (Level 2)** In KWL, which two columns are filled BEFORE reading? **Answer: K (what I Know) and W (what I Want to know)** — the L column — Learned — is the after-reading receipt (Ghana, curriculum evidence)

**3. (Level 3)** Libya's scanning tip begins: 'look for clues in…' — finish it. **Answer: the questions** — the question tells you the target's shape — a capital for a name, a number for an age (Libya G7, p.89)
