# Ways to read — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Skimming and Scanning · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — SQ3R and KWL · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing the Reading Road.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skimming means reading every word very quickly. **Answer: False** — skimming takes only the big shape — titles, headings, first and last lines; speed-reading every word is a different, poorer act

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Scanning a text means reading it carefully from the first line. **Answer: False** — scanning is searching, not reading — the eyes jump until the target's shape appears (Libya, p.89)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** SQ3R's Survey and Question stops happen before the reading itself. **Answer: True** — the first two stops prepare the hunt; skipping them is the usual failure (Ghana, curriculum evidence)
