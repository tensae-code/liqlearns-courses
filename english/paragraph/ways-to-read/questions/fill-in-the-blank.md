# Ways to read — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** Reading fast for the gist is called ______. **Answer: skimming** — the fly-over gear: headings and edges, not every word

**2. (Level 1)** Hunting one named fact without reading the rest is called ______. **Answer: scanning** — the hunter's gear (Rwanda, P.6 U6, p.77)

**3. (Level 2)** In KWL, the last column — filled after reading — is L for ______. **Answer: Learned** — Know, Want to know, Learned (Ghana, curriculum evidence)
