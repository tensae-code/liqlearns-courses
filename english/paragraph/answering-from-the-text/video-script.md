# Video script — Answering from the text

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A question about a text?
The answer LIVES in the text!

Read the question. What does it hunt?
Scan… scan… THERE!
Your finger lands on the sentence. THAT one carries it.

Answer WHOLE. Answer in YOUR words.
The facts are the text's. The dress is yours!

From the text — or from your head?
Know your bucket! Say which one!

And if the text is silent?
Say it proudly: "The text doesn't say."
No guessing. That IS an answer!

Now you. Point first. Answer second.
That is answering — in English.
