# Answering from the text — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** carrier · evidence · whole · bucket · silent ·
point

**1. (Level 1)** The sentence in the text that carries an
answer is its ______.
**Answer: carrier.**

**2. (Level 1)** ______ first, answer second.
**Answer: point.**

**3. (Level 2)** Ghana asks personal responses to be
supported by textual ______.
**Answer: evidence.**

**4. (Level 2)** Answers ride in ______ sentences, not bare
prey.
**Answer: whole.**

**5. (Level 3)** Know your ______: from the text, or from my
head.
**Answer: bucket.**

**6. (Level 3)** When the text is ______ on a question, the
answer is "The text doesn't say."
**Answer: silent.**
