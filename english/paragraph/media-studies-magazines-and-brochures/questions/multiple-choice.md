# Multiple choice — Media Studies: Magazines and Brochures

1. A magazine made for one kind of reader, such as gardeners or surfers, is called a… (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. General-audience magazine
   - B. Target-audience magazine
   - C. Newspaper
   - D. Brochure
   Answer: B

2. In Sāmoa's Year 11 book, a magazine story is built by… (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Copying a news report
   - B. Rewriting a fable in a modern setting
   - C. Interviewing a celebrity
   - D. Listing advertisements
   Answer: B

3. Which medium does Sāmoa's Year 12 book call the most powerful? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Radio
   - B. Newspapers
   - C. Television
   - D. Magazines
   Answer: C

4. Which is an advantage of radio over television? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. It shows pictures
   - B. It can be rewound by the listener
   - C. It is cheap, runs on batteries and leaves the eyes free
   - D. It reaches fewer people
   Answer: C
