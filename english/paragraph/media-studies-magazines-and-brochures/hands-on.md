# Hands-on — Media Studies: Magazines and Brochures

- **Cover verdicts.** In pairs, take four magazine covers and sort them: general audience or target audience. Each pair must defend one verdict with cover evidence.
- **Fable transplant.** Rewrite a known fable in a modern setting as a short magazine story; swap with a partner and mark what survived and what changed.
- **Medium weigh-in.** For one real message (a storm warning), script it twice: once for television, once for radio. Note what each version gained and lost.
