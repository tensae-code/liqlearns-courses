# Video script — Media Studies: Magazines and Brochures

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Magazines and Their Stories
- Beat 1: Two covers slide in: a singer's face, huge; a harbour of fishing boats. The presenter asks the room: who is each one for? Labels appear: general audience — target audience.
- Beat 2: A tortoise and hare at a starting line melt into two office workers racing for a promotion. The presenter: the story changed its clothes; the lesson stayed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Television and Radio
- Beat 1: A television glows in a living room. The presenter: the most powerful medium — eyes and ears together, in the home.
- Beat 2: A farmer in a far field, a small radio at his ear. The presenter: cheap, battery-run, eyes free — radio reaches where television cannot.
