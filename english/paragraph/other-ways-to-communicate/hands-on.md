# Hands-on — Other ways to communicate

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Finger-spell your name.** Using India's spread shapes as your guide, finger-spell your own name, then a friend's. Slow is fine; shape is everything.
2. **Silent conversation.** Hold a two-minute conversation with a partner using no voice and no writing — hands, face, body only. Afterwards, each writes what they believe was said, and you compare.
3. **Signal weighing.** Ecuador's drill: watch any short silent video clip. List every nonverbal signal you can see — eyes, face, hands, posture — and only THEN say what the person feels. One signal alone may not convict.
4. **Gesture atlas.** Start your own gesture grid, Ecuador-style: five gestures of your community, their meanings, and any place where the same gesture means something else.
