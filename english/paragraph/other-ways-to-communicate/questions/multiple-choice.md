# Other ways to communicate — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sign Language and Braille · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reading Nonverbal Signals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gestures Across Cultures.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sign language is made of — a) signs, gestures, finger spelling and facial expressions b) pictures only c) lipreading only **Answer: a) signs, gestures, finger spelling and facial expressions** — India's Class 3 spread names its parts (U9)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Braille is — a) a sign language b) a script of raised dots read by touch c) a gesture **Answer: b) a script of raised dots read by touch** — India's Class 4 book asks for it by name: the script for children who cannot see

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ecuador's first rule for reading people is — a) trust the loudest gesture b) consider ALL the nonverbal signals together c) ignore the face **Answer: b) consider ALL the nonverbal signals together** — 'do not read too much into a single gesture' (WS1, pp.5–6)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Pointing the sole of your shoe at someone is — a) polite everywhere b) offensive in Saudi Arabia and several other cultures c) a greeting in Thailand **Answer: b) offensive in Saudi Arabia and several other cultures** — Ecuador's gesture grid's warning case (WS1, p.9)
