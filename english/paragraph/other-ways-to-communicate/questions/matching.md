# Other ways to communicate — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Namaste **Answer: palms together with a slight bow — respect (India)** — Ecuador's gesture grid (WS1, p.8)

**2. (Level 1)** finger spelling **Answer: letters shaped in the air, for names and new words** — India's Class 3 spread (U9)

**3. (Level 2)** A-okay **Answer: thumb-and-index circle — excellent or perfect (United States)** — Ecuador's grid (WS1, p.8)

**4. (Level 3)** crossed fingers **Answer: a wish for good luck — or, in some readings, a sign of lying** — Ecuador's grid gives both readings (WS1, p.9)
