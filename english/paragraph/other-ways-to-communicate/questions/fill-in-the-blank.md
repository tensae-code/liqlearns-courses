# Other ways to communicate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ spelling spells words letter by letter in the air. **Answer: Finger** — India's spread names it among sign language's parts

**2. (Level 1)** The script for readers who cannot see, read by touch, is called ______. **Answer: Braille** — asked by name in India's Class 4 book

**3. (Level 2)** Eye ______, smiling and hand gestures are Ecuador's three explained cue types. **Answer: contact** — WS1, printed p.7
