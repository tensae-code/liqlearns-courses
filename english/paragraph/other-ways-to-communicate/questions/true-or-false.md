# Other ways to communicate — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Sign Language and Braille · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reading Nonverbal Signals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gestures Across Cultures.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sign language is just informal waving, not a real language. **Answer: False** — India's spread teaches its parts — signs, finger spelling, facial expression as grammar and feeling — a full language channel

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One gesture is enough to know exactly what a person feels. **Answer: False** — Ecuador's rule: never read too much into a single gesture; weigh all the signals (WS1, p.6)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A thumbs-up means approval in the United States and Western countries, in Ecuador's grid. **Answer: True** — the grid's first entry (WS1, p.8) — and other gestures in the same grid flip at other borders
