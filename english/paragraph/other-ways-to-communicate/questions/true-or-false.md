# Other ways to communicate — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Sign language is just informal waving, not a real language. **Answer: False** — India's spread teaches its parts — signs, finger spelling, facial expression as grammar and feeling — a full language channel

**2. (Level 2)** One gesture is enough to know exactly what a person feels. **Answer: False** — Ecuador's rule: never read too much into a single gesture; weigh all the signals (WS1, p.6)

**3. (Level 3)** A thumbs-up means approval in the United States and Western countries, in Ecuador's grid. **Answer: True** — the grid's first entry (WS1, p.8) — and other gestures in the same grid flip at other borders
