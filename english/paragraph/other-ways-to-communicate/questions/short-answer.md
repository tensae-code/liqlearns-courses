# Other ways to communicate — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name three of the six signs drawn in India's Class 3 spread. **Answer: any three of: Sing, Dance, Read, Win, Sunset, Sunrise** — the spread draws and describes all six (U9)

**2. (Level 2)** What is 'total communication', in India's spread? **Answer: sign language carried together with lipreading and writing** — the channels combined, not ranked (India, U9)

**3. (Level 3)** In Thailand, the palms-together gesture of respect is called — ? **Answer: the Wai** — Ecuador's grid pairs it with India's Namaste (WS1, p.8)
