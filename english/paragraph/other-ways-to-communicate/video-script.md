# Video script — Other ways to communicate

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

You speak with more than your mouth!

Hands can talk. That is sign language. Signs. Finger spelling — letters in the air! Faces talk too.

Fingers can READ. Raised dots on a page. That is Braille — reading by touch.

Your body talks all day. Eyes. Smiles. Hands. How you stand.

But careful! Read ALL the signals. Never just one.

And gestures travel badly! A thumbs-up is good here… rude there!

Learn gestures like words. Place by place.
