# Levels — Finding Information

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** read a fact out of a table,
  figure or diagram — row meets column, bar meets scale,
  part meets label.
- **Climb:** record facts exactly, with their source, units
  and dates, and build them into a table of your own that
  another reader can trust.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** run a short
  research project — focused question first, text and
  non-text sources hunted on purpose, findings presented
  with sources named and gaps stated honestly.
