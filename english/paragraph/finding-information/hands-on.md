# Hands-On — Finding Information

Things to do away from the page.

- **Table hunt.** Find one real table this week — a price
  list, a timetable, a results sheet. Read one fact from it
  aloud, saying the row and column you used.
- **One-question project.** Ask one focused question about
  your home or street and answer it from two sources, one of
  them not a text. Record both facts with their sources.
