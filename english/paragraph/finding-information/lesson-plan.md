# Lesson Plan — Finding Information

Unit 42 — Reading Like a Detective. Planned shape: three sub
lessons — first information beyond the text (the figures,
tables and diagrams that carry facts outside the reading
passages), then recording information from sources (getting
it down truly, with its source), and last short research
projects (a focused question, sources hunted, findings
presented). The lesson is built from Ghana's NaCCA JHS
curriculum, whose writing strand carries this exact ladder:
Basic 7 — identify and record information from non-text
sources; Basic 8 — use information from non-text sources
(figures, tables, diagrams) to build and present tables;
Basic 9 — conduct short research projects based on focused
questions. It stands in this unit beside ways-to-read and
checking-facts-online: those teach how to read; this one
teaches how to gather.

## Sub lessons

1. **Information beyond the text** — figures, tables and
   diagrams carry facts; read them as carefully as words.
2. **Recording information from sources** — write the fact
   down truly, with where it came from.
3. **Short research projects** — start from a focused
   question, hunt sources, and present what you found.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
