# Fill in the Blank — Finding Information

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. Facts live in figures, tables and ____ as much as in sentences. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: diagrams**

2. The tallest bar is the biggest amount — but read its ____ first. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: scale**

3. Record the fact ____: numbers as printed, names as spelled. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: exactly**

4. Beside every recorded fact, write its ____. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: source**

5. A research project starts with a ____ question. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: focused**

6. An honest ____ beats an invented answer. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: gap**
