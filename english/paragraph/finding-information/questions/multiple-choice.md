# Multiple Choice — Finding Information

Choose the one right answer.

1. In a table, a fact sits: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) In the title only
   - B) Where its row and its column meet
   - C) Always in the first row
   - D) Under the table
   **Answer: B**

2. Before trusting a chart's bars, you must read: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Its colours
   - B) Its scale
   - C) The page number
   - D) The author's name only
   **Answer: B**

3. In the Do-it-first table, how many more children came this year than last year? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) 12
   - B) 24
   - C) 34
   - D) 246
   **Answer: C**

4. A diagram mainly shows: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) A story's ending
   - B) A thing's parts or a process's steps
   - C) The longest words
   - D) Prices
   **Answer: B**

5. A true record of a fact includes: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) The fact exactly, its source, and its units and dates
   - B) Only the fact, roughly
   - C) A rounded number and no source
   - D) Your opinion of the fact
   **Answer: A**

6. The source table says 246 children. Your notebook should say: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) About 250
   - B) Many
   - C) 246
   - D) 200–300
   **Answer: C**

7. Ghana's Basic 8 step asks learners to use gathered information to: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Hide it
   - B) Build and present tables of their own
   - C) Memorise it silently
   - D) Burn it after use
   **Answer: B**

8. A focused research question is: (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) As wide as possible
   - B) Narrow enough to answer, like "which month gets the most rain in our town?"
   - C) Any question with no sources
   - D) A question you already invented the answer to
   **Answer: B**

9. In a research project, the right order is: (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) Collect first, question later
   - B) Answer first, sources later
   - C) Question first, then sources, then present
   - D) Present first, then check
   **Answer: C**

10. What you could not find in a project should be: (Sub lesson 3)
    - A) Invented quietly
    - B) Stated plainly — an honest gap beats an invented answer
    - C) Hidden from the reader
    - D) Filled with another project's facts
    **Answer: B**
