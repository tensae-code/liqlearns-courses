# Matching — Finding Information

Match each source kind to what it best shows.

1. Match the source to its strength. (Sub lesson 1)
   - 1. A rainfall table → exact amounts month by month
   - 2. A bar chart → comparing amounts at a glance, by its scale
   - 3. A diagram of a plant → the parts and how they join
   - 4. A bus timetable → times in rows and columns
