# True or False — Finding Information

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. A sentence can give a direction ("more this year") while a table gives the exact numbers. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

2. A chart without a scale can be trusted by how its bars look. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — read the numbers and the scale, not the picture's mood.

3. Rounding a number while recording it is harmless tidying. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — tidying is how facts quietly become falsehoods.

4. Spoken sources, like two farmers' recorded memories, can serve a research project if you record who said it and when. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True**

5. It is fine to collect material first and think of a question afterwards. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — question first, always.
