# Levels — Reading Fluency

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Reading so it sounds like talking | Read aloud so it sounds like natural speech, grouping words into common phrases instead of reading word by word, accurately. |
| 2. Expression, pace and the clues on the page | Use punctuation marks and italics as visual clues, and control pacing, intonation and expression so accuracy, speed and expression grow together. |

A learner who finishes all 2 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: reading aloud so it sounds like natural speech — recognising common phrases at a glance, using the punctuation marks and italics on the page as clues, and growing in accuracy, speed and expression together.
