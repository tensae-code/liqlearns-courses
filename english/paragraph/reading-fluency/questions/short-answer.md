# Short Answer — Reading Fluency

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What is the difference between reading word by word and reading in phrases? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Word-by-word reading chops the sentence into separate words; phrase reading groups words the way speech comes, so it sounds like natural talking and the meaning flows.
2. Why must accuracy come before speed? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because a fast wrong reading is just fast and wrong — fluency is the right words, read smoothly; speed grows on top of accuracy.
3. What do a comma, a full stop and a question mark each tell the reading voice? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Comma: small pause, keep going. Full stop: land and breathe. Question mark: lift the voice.
4. Name the three things that grow together in fluency. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Accuracy, speed and expression.
