# Video Script — Reading Fluency

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a child reading word — by — word, then the same child reading smoothly in phrases]

Word by word is slow. Read in phrases — like talking! Smooth reading sounds like speech.

[Picture: a page close up: a comma, a full stop, a question mark lighting up one by one]

The marks are clues! Comma: small pause. Full stop: land and breathe. Question mark: voice goes up!

[Picture: a child reading a story to a little brother, both smiling]

Read with feeling! Happy words happy, sad words sad. That is expression.
