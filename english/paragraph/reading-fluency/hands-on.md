# Hands-On — Reading Fluency

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Phrase reading.** Take one paragraph. Draw light lines between the phrases. Read it in phrases, then rub the lines out and read it clean.
2. **Clue hunt.** Find a page with a comma, a full stop, a question mark and italics. Read it aloud, obeying every clue; a partner ticks each clue you obeyed.
3. **Three reads.** Read the same page aloud three times running. Partner scores each read: more like talking than the last?
4. **Audience reading.** Prepare one page and read it to a real audience — family or class — with pacing and expression. Ask what they pictured.
