# Levels — Reading Hypertexts and Digital Navigation

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The shape of a hypertext page | Read a hypertext page's organisers before its content: point out its global structure, say what its symbols signal, and say where its links promise to go. |
| 2. Finding your way through hypertexts | Navigate an unfamiliar hypertext on purpose — re-finding the organisers where this text puts them, following links by choice, and always knowing which place they are in. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can land on a digital text they have never seen, read how it is organised, and move through it by its structure, symbols and links instead of by guessing.
