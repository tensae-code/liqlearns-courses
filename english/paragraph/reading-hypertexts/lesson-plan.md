# Lesson Plan — Reading Hypertexts and Digital Navigation

**One thing:** how a hypertext is organised for navigation — a digital text is read by moving through its global structure, its symbols and its links, and hypertexts vary from one to another, so the reader must find the organisers of each new one.

## Sub lessons

1. The shape of a hypertext page
2. Finding your way through hypertexts

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Digital texts (hypertexts) have textual organisers that exist for navigation: they tell the reader where things are and how to move (Batxillerat programme, BOPA p.21).
- The organisers named are: the global structure of pages, their symbols, and their links (p.21).
- Hypertexts vary — the organisers change from one hypertext to another, so they must be recognised afresh each time (p.21).
