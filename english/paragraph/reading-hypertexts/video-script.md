# Video Script — Reading Hypertexts and Digital Navigation

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The hand moves across the page exactly as the words say: structure first, then symbols, then a link.

[Picture: a web page opening on a screen — the child looks, but does not click yet]

Stop. Do not click. First — look at the page! A page on the screen is not like a page in a book. You do not read down. You MOVE.

[Picture: the hand drawing a box around the whole page, then around its top and its side]

See the big shape of the page? The top, the side, the middle. This is the map of the page. It tells you: where am I?

[Picture: small signs and icons on the page lighting up one by one]

Now the little signs! This sign says "look". This sign says "listen". The signs tell you what things do.

[Picture: a word glowing in the text — a door opening in the page]

And this glowing word? It is a door! Touch it — and you go to a new place. These doors are called links.

[Picture: a new, different page — the child looks at its shape first, smiling]

A new page! But look — the signs are in new places. Every page is a little different. So we look again: the shape, the signs, the doors. Now we can go anywhere!
