# Video Script — Reading Graphs and Charts

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each bar is pointed at as it is named.

[Picture: a bar graph titled "Fruit we picked" — four bars: Mangoes 6, Bananas 4, Oranges 8, Apples 2]

This is a bar graph. The bars show numbers. A tall bar means a big number. A short bar means a small number.

[Picture: a finger running up the tallest bar, Oranges 8]

Look at the tallest bar. Oranges! Eight oranges. The tallest bar is the most.

[Picture: a finger on the shortest bar, Apples 2]

Now the shortest bar. Apples. Two apples. The shortest bar is the least.

[Picture: the same graph beside a short paragraph of text]

Sometimes a graph sits inside a text. Read the words. Then read the graph. The graph gives you facts. Now you know more than the words alone!
