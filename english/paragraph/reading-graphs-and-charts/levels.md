# Levels — Reading Graphs and Charts

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Reading a bar graph | Read the title and the numbers of a bar graph, find each bar's number, and say which bar is most and which is least. |
| 2. Interpreting information on a graph | Read a graph that sits inside a text, pull facts out of it, compare its parts, and say in their own words what the graph shows. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can open any simple graph, in a book or a newspaper, and read the facts out of it.
