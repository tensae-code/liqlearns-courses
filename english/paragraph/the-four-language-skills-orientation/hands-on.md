# Hands-on — The Four Language Skills

## Four mimes

In pairs: one mimes one of the four skills — ear cupped,
mouth open, eyes on an invisible book, hand writing. The
other names the skill, its channel and its direction:
"Reading — eyes — taking in."

## Skill spotting

Open any lesson together. Race to name every skill it
trains, with the part of the lesson that proves each one.
Two skills is a good catch; three wins the round.
