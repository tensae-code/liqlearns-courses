# Video script — The Four Language Skills

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot cups an ear.] "LISTENING — in through the ears!"

[He opens his mouth.] "SPEAKING — out through the mouth!"

[He looks at a book.] "READING — in through the eyes!"

[He writes.] "WRITING — out through the hand!"

[Back to the mascot, four squares behind him.] "Four skills.
Two in, two out. Now you hold the map of English!"
