## Short answer

1. Name the four language skills and each one's direction. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Listening (in), speaking (out), reading (in), writing (out).

2. Which pair of skills are the "taking in" pair? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Listening and reading.

3. How does reading help your speaking? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Reading grows the words and sentence shapes you have ready for speaking.

4. What is the naming habit this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** In any lesson, ask which skill you are training — and once a week, which of your four is weakest and which lessons feed it.
