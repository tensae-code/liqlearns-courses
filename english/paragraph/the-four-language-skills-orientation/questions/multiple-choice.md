## Multiple choice

1. Which are the four language skills? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Listening, speaking, reading, writing  b) Singing, dancing, drawing, counting  c) Grammar, words, sounds, stories
   **Answer: a)** — everything in English rides on these four.

2. Which two skills take language IN? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Speaking and writing  b) Listening and reading  c) Listening and speaking
   **Answer: b) Listening and reading** — ears in, eyes in.

3. Writing sends language out through… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The mouth  b) The ears  c) The hand
   **Answer: c) The hand.**

4. A lesson where a story is read aloud trains… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Reading only  b) Reading and listening  c) Writing only
   **Answer: b) Reading and listening** — the eyes read, the ears hear it read.

5. If you are strong in reading but silent in speaking, what do you need more of? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) More reading  b) The conversation lessons — speaking practice  c) Nothing
   **Answer: b)** — the map tells you which lessons feed the weakest skill.
