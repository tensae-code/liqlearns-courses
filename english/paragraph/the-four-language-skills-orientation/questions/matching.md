## Matching

Match the skill to its channel and direction. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Listening — d
2. Speaking — a
3. Reading — b
4. Writing — c

a) Mouth — sending out
b) Eyes — taking in
c) Hand — sending out
d) Ears — taking in

**Answers:** 1–d (listening: ears, in), 2–a (speaking: mouth, out),
3–b (reading: eyes, in), 4–c (writing: hand, out).
