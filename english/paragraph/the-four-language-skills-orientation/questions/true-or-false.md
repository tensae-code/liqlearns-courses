## True or false

1. There are five language skills. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — four: listening, speaking, reading, writing.

2. Speaking takes language in. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — speaking sends language out; listening and reading take it in.

3. Listening much helps you speak better. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — the skills lean on each other.

4. Grammar lessons train no skill at all. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — grammar rides on the four: a grammar lesson trains speaking and writing, and reading when it is read.

5. Naming the skill you are training helps you balance the four. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
