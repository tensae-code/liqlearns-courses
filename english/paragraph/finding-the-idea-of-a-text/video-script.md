# Video script — Finding the idea of a text

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You read the whole text. Now — what is it ABOUT?
One sentence. YOUR words!

Step one: every paragraph has an idea. Gather them!
Step two: WEIGH them.
Which idea do the others SERVE?
That heavy one — that is the MAIN idea!

Careful, traps!
Too small? "She plants tomatoes." One part only!
Too bare? "A garden." A word — no idea!
Copied? The author's words — not yours!

Say it your way, whole and short:
"Her garden does well because she works hard."

Now you. Read it. Weigh it. Say it.
That is finding the idea — in English.
