# Finding the idea of a text — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** main · supporting · own · detail · weigh · gather

**1. (Level 1)** The idea a whole text serves is its
______ idea.
**Answer: main.**

**2. (Level 1)** Sentences and paragraphs that serve the
centre are ______ details.
**Answer: supporting.**

**3. (Level 2)** First ______ each paragraph's idea; then
______ them to find the centre.
**Answer: gather — weigh.**

**4. (Level 3)** State the idea in your ______ words, never
the author's copied ones.
**Answer: own.**

**5. (Level 1)** One paragraph's business, offered as the
whole text's idea, is a ______ in idea's clothing.
**Answer: detail.**

**6. (Level 2)** Ghana's summarising strand pairs the two
kinds as "______ and supporting ideas."
**Answer: central** — the book's own word for the main idea:
the centre the weighing finds.
