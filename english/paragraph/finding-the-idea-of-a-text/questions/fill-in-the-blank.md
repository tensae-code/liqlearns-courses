# Finding the idea of a text — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Gathering the Paragraph Ideas (item 1), Sub lesson 2 — Weighing the Ideas (item 2) and Sub lesson 4 — The Traps (item 5) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 1 (item 3, gather) and Sub lesson 2 (item 6, the centre the weighing finds) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Saying the Idea in Your Own Words (item 4). Items are marked by what each item actually asks.

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** main · supporting · own · detail · weigh · gather

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The idea a whole text serves is its
______ idea.
**Answer: main.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sentences and paragraphs that serve the
centre are ______ details.
**Answer: supporting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** First ______ each paragraph's idea; then
______ them to find the centre.
**Answer: gather — weigh.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** State the idea in your ______ words, never
the author's copied ones.
**Answer: own.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** One paragraph's business, offered as the
whole text's idea, is a ______ in idea's clothing.
**Answer: detail.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghana's summarising strand pairs the two
kinds as "______ and supporting ideas."
**Answer: central** — the book's own word for the main idea:
the centre the weighing finds.
