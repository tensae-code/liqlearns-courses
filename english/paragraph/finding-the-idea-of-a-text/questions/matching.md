# Finding the idea of a text — matching

Match each item to its name or its station.

**Set 1 (Level 1–3)** — the four traps.
1. "She plants tomatoes in March." (as the whole text's
   idea) — **the detail in idea's clothing**
2. "Gardens." — **the bare topic**
3. The author's own sentence, copied word for word — **the
   copied sentence**
4. "Gardens need love, as everyone knows." (your feeling,
   not the text's) — **the outside idea**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — the three steps.
1. Collect each paragraph's naming idea — **gather**
2. Ask which idea the others serve — **weigh**
3. State the centre in one sentence of your words — **say**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — Ghana's pairs.
1. main idea — **central idea (the strand's word)**
2. the rest of the text's load — **supporting details /
   supporting ideas**
3. "Do not write the same words as the author" — **the
   summarising law**
