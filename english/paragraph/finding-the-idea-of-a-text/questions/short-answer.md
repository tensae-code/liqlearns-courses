# Finding the idea of a text — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 2)** Gather: give the three paragraph ideas of
the Auntie Rose text in a few words each.
**Answer:** ¶1 she plants a garden (March planting). ¶2 the
village trusts her seedlings. ¶3 she waters by hand when
rains fail.

**2. (Level 3)** State the main idea in your own words, one
sentence, without copying the text's.
**Answer:** e.g. "Auntie Rose's garden succeeds because she
works hard at it." — any one-sentence centre, own words,
whole text inside.

**3. (Level 1)** Which trap is this: "The text is about
gardens."
**Answer:** the bare topic — a word (with its article) but
no claim the text proves.

**4. (Level 1)** Which trap is this: "The text is about the
seedlings the village buys."
**Answer:** the detail in idea's clothing — one part
offered as the whole.

**5. (Level 3)** Why does copying the author's own best
sentence fail the lesson even when it is true?
**Answer:** copying runs on the eyes; the skill is the
weighing. Own words are the only proof the weighing
happened (Ghana's rule).

**6. (Level 2)** What do you ask of gathered paragraph ideas
to find the centre?
**Answer:** which one do the others SERVE? — the idea the
most paragraphs lean on.
