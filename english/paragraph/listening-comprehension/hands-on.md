# Hands-On — Listening Comprehension

Things to do away from the page.

- **One-read rule.** A partner reads a short paragraph aloud
  once — no repeats. You write three facts you heard. Compare
  with the paragraph; then swap.
- **Talk tracker.** During any group talk, note each speaker's
  point in one short line. At the end, say what the talk
  decided.
