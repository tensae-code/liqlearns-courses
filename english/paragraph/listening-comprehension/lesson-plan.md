# Lesson Plan — Listening Comprehension

Unit 15 — Reading Whole Texts. Planned shape: three sub
lessons — first listening on purpose (listening is work you
do, not sound you sit in), then listening to stories and
texts and answering from what you heard, and last listening
to talk — dialogues, discussions and audio-visual texts —
and saying what they were about. The lesson is built from
Ghana's NaCCA curricula, the one set read so far that names
listening comprehension as its own strand work from Basic 1
to Basic 9: active listening to information at Basic 3,
attentive listening to texts and dialogues at JHS. It stands
beside listening-to-an-interview in this unit: that lesson
teaches one kind of listening (an interview); this one
teaches listening itself.

## Sub lessons

1. **Listening on purpose** — listening is a job: settle,
   listen for the point, interact with what you hear.
2. **Listening to stories and texts** — hear a text read or
   told, then answer from what you heard, never invented.
3. **Listening to talk** — dialogues, discussions and
   audio-visual texts: follow who is speaking and what the
   talk is about, and support what you say about it.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
