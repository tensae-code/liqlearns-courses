# Levels — Listening Comprehension

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** listen on purpose to a short
  reading and answer who / when / why questions from what was
  heard.
- **Climb:** listen to stories and texts and answer detail
  questions — names, numbers, reasons — from the text as
  heard, never invented.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** follow a dialogue,
  discussion or audio-visual text — track the speakers, hold
  each one's point, and support a view of the talk by what
  was actually said.
