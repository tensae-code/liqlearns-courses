# Matching — Listening Comprehension

Match each listening job to the kind of listening it belongs to.

1. Match the job to the listening. (Sub lesson 2)
   - 1. Answering "How many hens?" after a story is read aloud → listening to a text
   - 2. Following who wants the match on Saturday in a discussion → listening to talk
   - 3. Acting out a story the teacher told → listening to a story
   - 4. Saying what a radio debate decided → listening to talk
