# True or False — Listening Comprehension

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. If your mind wanders during a listening, you should chase the lost word and ignore the next ones. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — pull your attention back to the speaker's next word.

2. You cannot act out a story you did not listen to. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. A sensible detail you invent yourself counts as an answer about a listening text. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — answers come from the text as heard.

4. The loudest voice in a discussion is always its main point. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — judge by what the talk keeps returning to.

5. Listening again, when you can, helps you catch details you missed. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**
