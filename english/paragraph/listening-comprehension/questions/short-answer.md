# Short Answer — Listening Comprehension

Answer in a few words.

1. Name three question words to ask yourself while you listen. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Who, where and why (any three question words).

2. In the Do-it-first reading, what does Ama buy first? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Tomatoes.

3. What three things do you listen for on a first hearing of a story? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Who is in it, where it happens, and what changes.

4. In the Work-it text of Sub lesson 2, what does Kofi buy on market day? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Feed (for his hens).

5. Why is talk harder to follow than a text read aloud? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** Voices change, people interrupt, and nobody reads it twice.

6. At the end of a discussion, what two things should you be able to say? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** What the talk was about, and what was decided, if anything.
