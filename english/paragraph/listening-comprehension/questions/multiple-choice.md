# Multiple Choice — Listening Comprehension

Choose the one right answer.

1. What is the difference between hearing and listening? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) There is none
   - B) Hearing is what ears do by themselves; listening is aimed work — you follow and hold what you hear
   - C) Listening is only for music
   - D) Hearing works only outdoors
   **Answer: B**

2. Before a listening starts, a good listener first: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Opens a book to a random page
   - B) Settles and knows what they are listening for
   - C) Talks to a neighbour
   - D) Closes their eyes and sleeps
   **Answer: B**

3. In the Do-it-first reading, why does Ama not buy fish? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) She has no money
   - B) The fish came late
   - C) She dislikes fish
   - D) The market is closed
   **Answer: B**

4. When you answer a question about a listening text, your answer must come from: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Your own experience
   - B) What the class next door says
   - C) What the text actually said
   - D) The longest sentence you can make
   **Answer: C**

5. A detail that sounds sensible but was never said in the text is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) A good answer
   - B) Not an answer at all
   - C) Always half marks
   - D) The teacher's favourite kind
   **Answer: B**

6. On a first listening to a story, listen for: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Every single word equally
   - B) The whole shape — who is in it, where it happens, what changes
   - C) Only the last word
   - D) The reader's shoes
   **Answer: B**

7. In a dialogue, a new voice usually means: (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) The talk is over
   - B) A new person, and often a new view
   - C) A mistake in the recording
   - D) Time to stop listening
   **Answer: B**

8. A speaker says, "I agree, but the cost…" The word "but" warns you that: (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) The talk is finished
   - B) A different view is coming
   - C) The speaker is leaving
   - D) Everyone agrees
   **Answer: B**

9. How do you judge what a discussion was mainly about? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) By the loudest voice
   - B) By the first word spoken
   - C) By what the talk keeps returning to
   - D) By the shortest speech
   **Answer: C**

10. "Support your view of what you heard" means: (Sub lesson 3)
    - A) Point to what was actually said
    - B) Shout your view louder
    - C) Repeat your view many times
    - D) Ask a friend to agree
    **Answer: A**
