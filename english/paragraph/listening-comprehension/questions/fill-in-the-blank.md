# Fill in the Blank — Listening Comprehension

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. Listening is ____ work: you settle, follow and hold what you hear. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: aimed** (also accepted: active)

2. Answer questions about a listening from what you ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: heard**

3. On a first listening, follow the ____ of the story — who is in it, where it happens, what changes. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: shape**

4. A detail the reader never said is not an ____. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: answer**

5. In talk, a new ____ usually means a new person. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: voice**

6. Hold each speaker's ____ in one short line. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: point**
