# Hands-On — Analysing Speakers and Persuasion

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Five boxes.** Listen to a two-minute speech. Fill the five boxes — tone, mood, point of view, purpose, character — with one line each.
2. **Device hunt.** From a transcript, underline generalisations, circle emotive words, and star any one-sided picture. Compare maps with a partner.
3. **Fact or leap?** A partner reads claims from a speech. For each, call out FACT (it was shown) or LEAP (an inference) — and say what the leap lands on.
4. **Source check.** Take the biggest claim in a class presentation. Find its source and judge it: who says so, and why should we trust them?
