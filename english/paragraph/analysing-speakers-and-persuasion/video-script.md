# Video Script — Analysing Speakers and Persuasion

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a detective with a magnifying glass looking at a speaker at a podium]

A speaker can be read like a clue. Tone! Mood! Point of view! Purpose! Read the speaker first.

[Picture: a speaker holding up a shiny card, the dull back of the card hidden]

Propaganda shows only the shiny half. Ask: what is on the other side of the card?

[Picture: big words ALL, EVERY, NEVER wobbling on thin legs]

"All! Every! Never!" Big claims need strong legs. No evidence? The claim falls.

[Picture: a balance scale weighing a claim against its source]

A claim is only as strong as its source. Weigh the source before you believe.
