# Media Studies: Newspapers — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 15, Reading and the Paragraph

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of three screens side by side — a newspaper page, a radio, a television — all carrying the same headline about a storm. *The learners name the three media and say what makes them 'mass' media.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of an old storyteller under a tree telling a short story with a lesson hidden inside it, listeners leaning in. *The learners say what kind of story hides a lesson inside it.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a newspaper front page laid flat with a magnifying glass over the top story. *The learners point to where the most important story sits and say how they can tell.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of two copies of the same small story, one clean and one with two words circled in red where letters are missing or a name is spelled two ways. *The learners find the two slips and say whether they change what the story means.*

## Sub lessons
1. The Mass Media
2. Reading a Newspaper

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
