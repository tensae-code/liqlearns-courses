# Multiple choice — Media Studies: Newspapers

1. Which set names the mass media as Sāmoa's book gives them? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Letters, diaries and posters
   - B. Newspapers, radio and television
   - C. Books, films and songs
   - D. Phones, computers and tablets
   Answer: B

2. What is a parable? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. A news report of a real event
   - B. A short story with a lesson hidden inside it
   - C. A joke printed in a newspaper
   - D. The biggest headline on a page
   Answer: B

3. Why does this lesson study a copied real newspaper? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. To memorise its stories
   - B. Because a real page shows how a newspaper is arranged and read
   - C. Because old newspapers are worth money
   - D. To learn its spellings by copying them
   Answer: B

4. A copied story in the book prints "struct" and "Vavafi". What are they? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Model spellings to copy
   - B. Slips in the reproduced text — to notice, read past, and never copy as spellings
   - C. Sāmoan words
   - D. Headline language
   Answer: B
