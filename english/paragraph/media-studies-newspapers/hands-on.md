# Hands-on — Media Studies: Newspapers

- **Front-page map.** Draw a quick map of one front page: boxes for each piece, numbered in reading order, with one line on what told you the order.
- **Purpose labels.** Cut or copy five pieces from one issue and label each with its job: inform, persuade, entertain, teach a lesson.
- **Slip hunt.** In pairs, find one printing slip in a real newspaper page and present it: what it says, what it should say, whether the meaning survives.
