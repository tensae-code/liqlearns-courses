# Video script — Media Studies: Newspapers

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Mass Media
- Beat 1: One storm headline arrives three ways: a newspaper page, a radio voice, a television screen. The presenter: same news, three media — all of them mass media, reaching everyone at once.
- Beat 2: A storyteller under a tree; the story ends and the listeners nod at the lesson inside it. The presenter: that hidden-lesson story is a parable — newspapers carry them too.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading a Newspaper
- Beat 1: A front page fills the screen; the camera drifts to the biggest headline, the biggest picture. The presenter: the page is arranged — arrangement is writing too.
- Beat 2: Zoom into a small story: two words glow — a word missing letters, a name spelled oddly. The presenter: real pages carry real slips; notice them, read past them, never copy them.
