# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's book only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Mass Media
- Depth: the mass media named as a set — newspapers, radio, television — media that reach everyone at once; the vocabulary a news reader meets inside the texts, including what a parable is and what a joke is doing in print (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 2*, printed pp.26–27).

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading a Newspaper
- Depth: a real copied issue of the *Samoa Observer* studied as the worked model of a newspaper (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 2*, printed p.28). The book's reproduced story carries two slips of its own — it prints "struct" and "Vavafi" — reproduced-text slips in the model, noted here so they are never mistaken for model spellings.
