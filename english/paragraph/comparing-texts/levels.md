# Levels — Comparing Texts

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Two texts on one subject | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Compare two poems on the same subject: compare their ideas and themes, compare their words, syntax and cohesion, and compare what the author is trying to do in each. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Same event, different treatment | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Write one event twice — in a sad tone and in a happy tone — then compare treatments: how the event changes when the purpose changes. |
