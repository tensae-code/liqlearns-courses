# Hands-On — Comparing Texts

1. **Side-by-side wall.** Two poems pinned side by side; the class sticks notes on each: idea, word choice, purpose.
2. **Tone swap.** Pairs write one event in opposite tones and swap papers to guess the tone from the words alone.
3. **Purpose match.** Cards with author purposes matched to short text extracts that show them.
4. **Comparison chart.** Fill a four-row chart (ideas, words, syntax/cohesion, purpose) for any two texts.
