# Lesson Plan — Comparing Texts

**Unit 42 — Reading Like a Detective.**

**One thing:** comparing two texts on the same subject — finding what each one says, how each says it, and what changes between them — with paired poems on one theme.

## Sub lessons

1. **Two texts on one subject.** *Can-do: Compare two poems on the same subject: compare their ideas and themes, compare their words, syntax and cohesion, and compare what the author is trying to do in each.*
2. **Same event, different treatment.** *Can-do: Write one event twice — in a sad tone and in a happy tone — then compare treatments: how the event changes when the purpose changes.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Comparing texts (item 419): research the characteristics of a theme, compare two poems on the same subject (ideas, themes, words, syntax, cohesion, author's purpose), then write about a chosen event in a sad tone and in a happy tone.
