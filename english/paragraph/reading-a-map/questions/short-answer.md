# Short Answer — Reading a Map

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What does a map key do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — It tells what things on the map mean.
2. Name two things the book's map key can show you on the Liberia map. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any two of: cities, rivers, mountain ranges (and the sea/coast).
3. How many cities does the book's map of Liberia show? (Sub lesson 1)
   — 16 cities.
4. Name the four compass directions. (Sub lesson 2)
   — North, South, East and West.
5. A town is at the bottom of a map, below all the other towns. In which part of the country is it? (Sub lesson 2)
   — In the south of the country.
6. You want to walk to a river that lies east of your town. Which way do you walk? (Sub lesson 2)
   — East — towards the east, where the compass points East.
