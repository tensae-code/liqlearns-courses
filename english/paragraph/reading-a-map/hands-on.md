# Hands-On — Reading a Map

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper and a pencil.

1. **Draw your place.** Draw a map of your school or your home street from above: the buildings, the road, a river or trees if they are there. Use a dot or a small square for each building.
2. **Make your key.** In a corner of your paper, draw your signs and write what each one means: "— road", "▢ house", "~ water". Your key tells what things on your map mean.
3. **Count from your map.** Count the buildings on your map. Count the roads. Say the counts as sentences: "My map shows ___ houses and ___ roads."
4. **Add the compass.** Draw a compass cross on your map: North at the top, South at the bottom, East to the right, West to the left. Now say two true sentences from your map: "The ___ is north of the ___." "The ___ is west of the ___."
