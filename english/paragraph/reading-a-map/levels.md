# Levels — Reading a Map

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a map shows, and the map key | Say what a map shows, read a map's key to learn what its signs mean, and pull facts from a map — count its cities and find its rivers and mountain ranges. |
| 2. The compass — North, South, East, West | Name the four compass directions, tell which are opposites, and use the compass on a map to find their way and say where things are. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can open any simple map, read its key first, and use it to find facts and directions.
