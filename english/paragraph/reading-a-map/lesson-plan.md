# Lesson Plan — Reading a Map

**One thing:** reading a map — first what a map shows and how its key unlocks it, then the compass that gives North, South, East and West — so the learner can pull real facts out of any map and find their way with it.

**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 15 — Reading Whole Texts (with the other reading skills)

## Sub lessons

1. What a map shows, and the map key
2. The compass — North, South, East, West

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A map shows a place: countries, cities, rivers, mountain ranges — the book's map of Liberia shows the country, its neighbours and 16 cities (Grade 1, *Where Is Liberia?*).
- Signs stand for things on a map, and the key tells what they mean: "A key tells what things on the map mean" (Grade 1, *Where Is Liberia?*, printed pp.10–16).
- Read the key first, then the map: count what the map shows (the book counts the 16 cities on its Liberia map) and find the rivers and mountain ranges through the key (Grade 1, *Where Is Liberia?*, printed pp.10–16).
- A compass gives the four directions — North, South, East and West — to find your way (Grade 1, *Where Is Liberia?*, printed pp.10–16).
