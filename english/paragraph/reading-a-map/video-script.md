# Video Script — Reading a Map

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each thing on the map is pointed at as it is named.

[Picture: a simple map of a country — dots for cities, a blue river line, triangles for mountains, the sea along one side]

This is a map. A map is a picture of a place. It shows cities. It shows rivers. It shows mountains.

[Picture: a finger on the map's key box, then matching a dot, the blue line and a triangle to the key]

What do the signs mean? Look at the key! The key tells what things on the map mean. A dot is a city. The blue line is a river. The triangles are mountains.

[Picture: a finger moving to the compass rose at the map's corner — North up, East right, South down, West left]

Now the compass. North is up. South is down. East is right. West is left. The compass helps you find your way.
