# Hands-On — Evaluating Sources

1. **Claim court.** Claims on trial: one team defends a claim's evidence, one attacks it; the class gives the verdict.
2. **Survey wall.** Pin three contradictory headlines about one food; draw lines between agrees and fights.
3. **Advert audit.** In pairs, take one advert apart: claim, who gains, evidence offered.
4. **Fact or foam?** Sort claim cards into substantiated and insubstantial piles, defending each sort aloud.
