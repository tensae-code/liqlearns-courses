# Levels — Evaluating Sources

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — When studies disagree | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Read the lesson's contradictory set: studies whose claims about a food's properties fight each other; survey what the author is saying in each and name the conflict plainly. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Judging which claims to trust | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Evaluate: is the claim substantiated — backed by evidence strong enough to hold it — or insubstantial? Weigh the methods and the proof before you believe, cite or repeat a claim. |
