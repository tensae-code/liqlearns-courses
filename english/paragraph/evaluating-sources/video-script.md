# Video Script — Evaluating Sources

Super-simple lines, one picture per line.

[Picture: two newspapers with opposite headlines about the same food]
"One says it heals. One says it harms. Who is right?"

[Picture: a balance scale: a claim on one side, evidence blocks being added to the other]
"Substantiated: the evidence is heavy enough to hold the claim."

[Picture: a big shiny claim bubble popping]
"Insubstantial: big words, no backing. Pop!"
