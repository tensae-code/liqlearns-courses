# True or false — Texting and Messaging

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Chatspeak uses numbers for sounds, like 2 for "to".
   **Answer: True** (Sub lesson 1)

2. "lol" means "lots of lessons".
   **Answer: False** — it means laughing out loud. (Sub lesson 1)

3. Chatspeak is the right way to write to a teacher.
   **Answer: False** — full words for teachers and important messages. (Sub lesson 2)

4. A good text message says who it is for, what it is about, and what happens next.
   **Answer: True** (Sub lesson 2)
