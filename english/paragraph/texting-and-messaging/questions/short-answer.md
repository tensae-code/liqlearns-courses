# Short answer — Texting and Messaging

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Read this in full words: "c u tmr, pls bring ur book".
   **Answer:** See you tomorrow, please bring your book. (Sub lesson 1)

2. What number stands for the sound "to/too" in chatspeak?
   **Answer:** 2. (Sub lesson 1)

3. Write a two-line note: you have gone to the shop and will be back at six.
   **Answer:** Any clear note with who/what/next, e.g. "Mum — gone to the shop. Back at six." (Sub lesson 2)

4. Why is one more word cheaper than a misunderstanding?
   **Answer:** Because a clear message costs almost nothing, while a misunderstood one costs time and trouble. (Sub lesson 2)
