# Matching — Texting and Messaging

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the short form to its full form.
   1. thx
   2. idk
   3. 4
   a. for
   b. thanks
   c. I don't know
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the message to the right way to write it.
   1. to your best friend
   2. to your teacher
   3. a note for your mother on the table
   a. full words, polite
   b. short forms are fine
   c. short, clear, who-what-next
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 2)
