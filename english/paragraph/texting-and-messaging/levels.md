# Levels — Texting and Messaging

How depth works in this lesson, sub lesson by sub lesson. The learner's level inside the lesson is the sub lesson they have reached and proved.

Sub lesson 1 gives the chatspeak forms and the reading skill. Sub lesson 2 gives the writing judgement — the note/message shape and when full words are owed — the writing work the source book's module teaches.

Old-layer mapping: this lesson was built new by the 2026-11-11 Kazakhstan sweep merge, so there is no old four-layer structure to map; the two sub lessons above are the depth record.
