# Video script — Reading Comics and Visual Narratives

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A cartoon strip of four panels appears behind the mascot.]

"Look — a story with no story words! Only pictures, and bubbles for the talking."

[The mascot points to the first panel, then the second.] "We read the boxes in order. One box, then the next. The bubble shows who is talking."

"Panel by panel, we can tell the whole story. Who is here? What happens? What do they say?"

"That is reading a comic."
