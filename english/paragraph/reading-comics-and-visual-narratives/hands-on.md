# Hands-on — Reading Comics and Visual Narratives

1. **Read the strip.** Take a cartoon strip and read it panel by panel out loud: for each panel say who is there, what they are doing, and what the bubble says.

2. **Tell it back.** Read a whole short comic, close it, and tell the story back in words — the people, the problem, and how it ends. Your partner checks the panels: did your telling miss anything the pictures showed?
