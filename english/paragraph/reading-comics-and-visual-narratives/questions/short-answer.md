## Short answer

1. How do you read a cartoon strip? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: panel by panel, in order — in each panel, say who is there, what they are doing, and what the speech bubble says.**

2. What did Fiji's Year 2 learners turn into a cartoon strip? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: their market role play — the dialogue they acted became the words in the speech bubbles.**

3. What story work do you do with a whole comic, like "The Adventures of Ben and Tom"? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the same as any story — name the people, watch for the problem, see how it ends — then tell the story back in words.**
