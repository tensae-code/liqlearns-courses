## True or false

1. You can read a cartoon strip's panels in any order you like. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the panels carry the story in order.**

2. A speech bubble shows the words a character says. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. A comic cannot tell a whole story, only jokes. (Sub lesson 2) **False — a comic can carry a whole narrative, with people, a problem and an ending.**

4. In a visual narrative, the pictures do part of the telling. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
