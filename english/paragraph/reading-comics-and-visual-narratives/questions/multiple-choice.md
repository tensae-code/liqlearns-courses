## Multiple choice

1. The boxes of a cartoon strip are called… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) bubbles  b) panels  c) chapters
   **Answer: b).**

2. A speech bubble's tail points to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the speaker  b) the next panel  c) the title
   **Answer: a).**

3. In Fiji's Year 2 book, the cartoon strip is made from… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a song  b) the market role play the class acted  c) a news report
   **Answer: b).**

4. A whole comic story is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) visual narrative  b) word list  c) timetable
   **Answer: a).**

5. When you finish a comic, telling the story back means naming… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) every colour in the pictures  b) the people, the problem and the ending  c) the page numbers
   **Answer: b).**
