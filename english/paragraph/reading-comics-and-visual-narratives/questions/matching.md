## Matching

Match the part of a comic to what it does. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. A panel
2. A speech bubble
3. The bubble's tail
4. The order of the panels
5. A character's face

a) shows who is speaking
b) carries the words a person says
c) one box of the story, read in its turn
d) shows the feeling
e) carries what happens first, next and last

**Answers: 1-c, 2-b, 3-a, 4-e, 5-d.**
