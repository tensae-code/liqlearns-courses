# Hands-on — Reading between the lines

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Clue hunt.** Take any short text. Underline three things it SAYS. Then write one thing it MEANS but never says, and draw an arrow from each clue that proves it.
2. **Fact–opinion sort.** Cut ten sentences from a newspaper or a book page (copy them onto cards). Sort them into FACT and OPINION piles; for every fact, say how it could be checked.
3. **Word detective.** Find one word you do not know in a real text. Cover it. Guess its meaning from the sentences before and after — Egypt's Context Clues move — then uncover and check.
4. **Weather report.** For one page, write its weather report: the writer's tone, the writer's purpose, and the mood it leaves in you — one line of the text as evidence for each.
