# Video script — Reading between the lines

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some texts hide their best part.

The words on the page are clues.

You are the detective.

One: work it out. The text does not say it. But the clues say it. That is inference.

Two: fact or opinion? A fact can be checked. An opinion is what a person thinks.

Three: a new word? Do not stop. Read the words before it. Read the words after it. Guess.

Four: how does the writer feel? That is the tone.

Why did the writer write it? That is the purpose.

How does it make YOU feel? That is the mood.

Clues first. Answer second. Always.
