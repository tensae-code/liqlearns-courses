# Reading between the lines — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Author's Purpose (Pakistan's term) **Answer: what the text is for — to inform, persuade, entertain or warn** — one of the four Literary Analysis questions, Pakistan G5, p.23

**2. (Level 1)** Author's Point of View **Answer: where the writer stands — fan, critic, insider, straight-faced reporter** — Barbados row 3.4.10 names the author's point of view (syllabus evidence)

**3. (Level 2)** Context clues **Answer: the sentences before and after an unknown word, used to guess it** — Egypt's Context Clues Tip, Prep 8 T1, U6 L3, p.92

**4. (Level 3)** Drawing conclusions (Libya's term) **Answer: working out an answer the text does not state, from where its clues must live** — Libya Grade 8, Unit 8 Lesson 4 reading tip
