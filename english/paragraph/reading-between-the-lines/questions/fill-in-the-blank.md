# Reading between the lines — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** A statement that can be checked is a ______. **Answer: fact** — the check test: counted, measured, looked up (Egypt, p.74)

**2. (Level 1)** What someone believes, which no check can settle, is an ______. **Answer: opinion** — opinions often carry flags like 'I think' and 'the best'

**3. (Level 3)** The feeling a text builds in the reader is its ______. **Answer: mood** — mood is the reader's weather; tone is the writer's
