Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, split item by item by what each item actually asks. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The bridge was built in 1962." This is — a) an opinion b) a fact c) a mood **Answer: b) a fact** — it can be checked — looked up, counted, verified (Egypt, Prep 8 T1, p.74)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "It is the finest bridge in the country." This is — a) a fact b) an opinion c) an inference **Answer: b) an opinion** — 'finest' is what someone believes; no check can settle it

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A text says: "He folded the letter twice, slowly, and put it in his pocket without a word." The best inference is — a) he is untidy b) the letter matters to him c) he cannot read **Answer: b) the letter matters to him** — the clues — twice, slowly, without a word — all point one way; the others have no clues

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** "Do not read too much into a single gesture" belongs to which detective habit? a) check every clue against the others b) guess wildly c) skip the text **Answer: a) check every clue against the others** — an inference stands on its clues together, never on one alone
