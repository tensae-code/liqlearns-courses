# Reading between the lines — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Every answer in a text is printed in so many words. **Answer: False** — Libya's tip says it plainly: you won't always find the exact information — some answers must be worked out from clues

**2. (Level 2)** An opinion becomes a fact if many people agree with it. **Answer: False** — agreement does not make it checkable; it stays a belief

**3. (Level 3)** Tone and mood are the same thing. **Answer: False** — tone is the writer's attitude; mood is the feeling built in the reader — a flat tone can still build a frightening mood
