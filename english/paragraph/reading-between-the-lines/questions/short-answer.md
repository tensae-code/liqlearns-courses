# Reading between the lines — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 2)** In one line: what is an inference? **Answer: a meaning you work out from a text's clues, which the text never states outright** — Libya's drawing-conclusions move: work out where the answer must be and read that part closely

**2. (Level 2)** You meet the unknown word 'reluctant' in: "Reluctant, she opened the exam envelope, and put it down twice before she could look." Guess its meaning. **Answer: unwilling / not wanting to do it** — the context clues — putting it down twice — carry the meaning (Egypt's Context Clues Tip, p.92)

**3. (Level 3)** A writer describes a new school with the words 'gleaming, generous, alive with morning noise'. Name the tone. **Answer: warm / admiring (positive)** — tone is the writer's weather, read from the chosen words (Pakistan G5, p.23)
