# Reading between the lines — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, split item by item by what each item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: fact/opinion and inference items -> Sub lesson 1 — Inference and the Two Sorts · guessing-words-from-context items -> Sub lesson 2 — Guessing Words from Context · tone, viewpoint, purpose and mood items -> Sub lesson 3 — The Writer's Weather.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In one line: what is an inference? **Answer: a meaning you work out from a text's clues, which the text never states outright** — Libya's drawing-conclusions move: work out where the answer must be and read that part closely

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You meet the unknown word 'reluctant' in: "Reluctant, she opened the exam envelope, and put it down twice before she could look." Guess its meaning. **Answer: unwilling / not wanting to do it** — the context clues — putting it down twice — carry the meaning (Egypt's Context Clues Tip, p.92)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A writer describes a new school with the words 'gleaming, generous, alive with morning noise'. Name the tone. **Answer: warm / admiring (positive)** — tone is the writer's weather, read from the chosen words (Pakistan G5, p.23)
