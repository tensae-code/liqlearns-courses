# Video script — Reading Visual Texts and Static Images

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A big poster appears behind the mascot: a smiling face, bright colours, one short line.]

"This poster stopped you before you read one word! Why? Look slowly."

[The mascot points at the face, then the bright colour.] "A big face. A bright colour. The maker chose them — to catch your eye."

"A picture can be a text. Read it like a paragraph: what is in it? What is it about? Who made it — and what do they want?"
