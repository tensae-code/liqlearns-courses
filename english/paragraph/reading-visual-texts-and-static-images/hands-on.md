# Hands-on — Reading Visual Texts and Static Images

1. **The slow look.** Put a poster or advertisement between you. Take turns naming everything in it — people, things, place, words — then say in one sentence what it is about.

2. **The maker's choices.** For the same image, each of you names one choice the maker made: what is biggest, what is brightest, what is left out. Say what each choice does to the reader.
