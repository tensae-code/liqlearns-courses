## True or false

1. Only written pages count as texts. (Sub lesson 1) **False — a still picture can be a text, and it can be analysed.**

2. In analysing an image, you should look slowly and name what you see. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. The words and the picture in a visual text always carry the same message. (Sub lesson 2) **False — sometimes the picture carries the real message, or steers what you think the words mean.**

4. Asking "who made this, and what do they want?" is part of reading a visual text. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
