## Multiple choice

1. A static image is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a moving video  b) a still picture used as a text  c) a page of handwriting
   **Answer: b).**

2. The first step in analysing a static image is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) naming everything you see in it  b) guessing the maker's name  c) counting its colours
   **Answer: a).**

3. The biggest, brightest thing in an image is usually there because… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) it was drawn last  b) the maker wants it to catch your eye first  c) it is always the title
   **Answer: b).**

4. A visual text leaves something outside the frame. That matters because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) every image is a choice, and what is left out is part of the maker's message  b) pictures are always unfinished  c) it does not matter
   **Answer: a).**

5. A photograph of a smiling family beside a product is meant to make the reader… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) read the small print  b) feel the product is safe and good  c) laugh at the family
   **Answer: b).**
