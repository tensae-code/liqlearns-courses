## Fill in the blank

1. A photograph, a poster and an advertisement can all be ______ images — still pictures used as texts. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: static.**

2. To analyse an image, first name everything you ______ in it. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: see.**

3. Every image is a ______: the maker picked what to show and what to leave out. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: choice.**

4. A visual text has a ______: it wants the reader to feel or do something. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: job (also right: point / purpose).**
