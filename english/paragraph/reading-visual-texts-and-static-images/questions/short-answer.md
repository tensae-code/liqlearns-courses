## Short answer

1. What are the four things you look for when you analyse a static image? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: what is in it; what is biggest, brightest or in front; what it is about; who made it and for whom.**

2. Where does Fiji's Year 11 book teach this work? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: in Unit 3, the Civics unit — analysing static images and visual texts.**

3. A poster shows only the happy buyer, never the price. What question catches that? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: "What did the maker leave out?" — the price stood outside the frame on purpose.**
