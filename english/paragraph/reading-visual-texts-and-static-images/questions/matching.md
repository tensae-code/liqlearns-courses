## Matching

Match the analysis question to what it finds. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. What is in it?
2. What is biggest and brightest?
3. What is it about?
4. Who made it, and for whom?
5. What did the maker leave out?

a) the subject, in one sentence
b) the people, things, place and words you can name
c) what stood outside the frame
d) the maker and the readers it speaks to
e) what catches the eye first

**Answers: 1-b, 2-e, 3-a, 4-d, 5-c.**
