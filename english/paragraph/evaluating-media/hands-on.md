# Hands-On — Evaluating Media for Accuracy and Effectiveness

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Thumbs up, thumbs down.** Watch a short clip as a class. Each learner writes two verdict cards — accuracy (with one claim named) and effectiveness (with one reason). Compare the spread of verdicts.
2. **Claim ledger.** For one clip, build a three-column ledger: claim / evidence shown / verdict. Fill it during a second viewing.
3. **Expected vs raised.** Before a programme, everyone writes two expected questions on the board. After viewing, the class reconciles the list together and scores the programme's effectiveness.
