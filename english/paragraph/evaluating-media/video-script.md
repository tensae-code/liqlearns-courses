# Video Script — Evaluating Media for Accuracy and Effectiveness

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a class watching a screen; a big thumbs-up and thumbs-down sign above them]

A clip can be WRONG. A clip can be WEAK. Two different questions!

[Picture: a claim card lifted out of the screen with a magnifying glass over it]

First: is it TRUE? Show me the evidence — claim by claim!

[Picture: the clip's pictures fighting its words, arrows crossing]

Second: does it WORK? Or do its pictures fight its words?

[Picture: a checklist with three questions, two ticked, one with a cross]

Ask your questions BEFORE you watch. Did it answer them? That is the test!
