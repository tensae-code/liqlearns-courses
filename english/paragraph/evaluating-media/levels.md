# Levels — Evaluating Media for Accuracy and Effectiveness

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Judging accuracy: are the claims sound? | Evaluate a viewed text's accuracy: name its claims, and judge which are supported, which are shaky, and which are false. |
| 2. Judging effectiveness: does it do its job? | Evaluate a viewed text's effectiveness for its purpose and audience, and bring your own expected questions to what a speaker actually raises. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: judging viewed material — a documentary, a clip, a broadcast — on two questions: is it accurate (are its claims sound?), and is it effective (does it do its job well?).
