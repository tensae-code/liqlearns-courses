# Lesson Plan — Evaluating Media for Accuracy and Effectiveness

**Unit:** Unit 42 — Reading Like a Detective

**One thing:** judging viewed material — a documentary, a clip, a broadcast — on two questions: is it accurate (are its claims sound?), and is it effective (does it do its job well?).

## Sub lessons

1. Judging accuracy: are the claims sound?
2. Judging effectiveness: does it do its job?

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Evaluating viewed material for accuracy and effectiveness: judging a clip's claims and how well it works (Lesson 2, Task 3 "Thumbs Up!", p.298).
- The listener's reconciliation: expected questions against the questions a speaker actually raises (Lesson 5, Task 3, p.354).
