# Levels — Parts of a Book

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The Parts of a Book | Name a book's parts — spine, cover, title, author, illustrator, publisher, title page, call number — and say what each part tells you. |
| 2. The Library and Its Words | Use the library's own words — article, bar code, loan period, magazine, thesaurus — and say what each one means. |
| 3. How a Library Is Organised | Say how a library keeps its books in order and follow its rules of use: borrow properly, mind the loan period, return the book to its place, handle books with care. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons can pick up any book, name its parts and say where each fact about it stands, and can walk into a library knowing its words, its order and its rules.
