**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Honduras).**

This lesson exists because of Honduras: its Grade 7 Telebásica book teaches the parts of a book as its own sequence ("Enjoying the reading", Block II, Secuencia 4), follows it with the library itself ("Visiting the library", Secuencia 5), and its Grade 8 book takes the library up again ("The books are my best friends", Block II, Secuencia 3). No course lesson housed that teaching: print-concepts-and-book-handling teaches a beginning reader how print runs and how to hold a book, and library-skills teaches finding books on shelves and choosing research sources — neither names the book's own parts and what each part tells you. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson. The expansion diff therefore marked it NEW LESSON NEEDED (items 37, 39 and 86), and the lesson is built new from Honduras's share, stocked from the expansion placements (honduras-placements.md).

Searchable tag: the books' own sequence names for these parts — "How to read / Enjoying the reading" (Grade 7, Block II, Secuencias 1 and 4), "Visiting the library" (Grade 7, Block II, Secuencia 5), "The books are my best friends" (Grade 8, Block II, Secuencia 3) — are kept here as tags, not as the lesson's title, which names the thing itself.
