# Hands-On — Parts of a Book

Things you do with a real book. You need one book — any book.

1. **Name the parts.** Hold the book. Point at the cover and say its name. Point at the spine. Open the book to the title page and point at the title, the author, the illustrator (if there is one) and the publisher. Say one sentence for each: "This is the spine. It joins the pages."
2. **Read the book's facts.** From the title page alone, say: the book's full title, who wrote it, who drew its pictures, and who published it. Then find the call number if the book carries one — say what it is for.
3. **The library words.** Write the five words — article, bar code, loan period, magazine, thesaurus. Beside each, write where you would meet it in a library: the bar code on the book, the loan period at the desk, the magazine on its rack.
4. **Rules of use.** Write three rules you will follow with a borrowed book: keep it clean and dry, bring it back inside the loan period, put it back in its place. Read them aloud.
