# Video Script — Parts of a Book

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each part is touched as it is named.

[Picture: a closed book standing on a table, its front cover to the camera]

This is a book. A book has parts. Every part has a name. Every part has a job.

[Picture: a finger running down the strip where the pages join]

This is the spine. The spine joins the pages. On a shelf, the spine shows the title.

[Picture: the book opened at its first inside page, with the title big at the top]

Open the book. This is the title page. Here is the title. Here is the author — the author wrote the book. Here is the illustrator — the illustrator drew the pictures. And here is the publisher — the publisher made the book.

[Picture: a library shelf with many spines, and one small numbered label]

In a library, every book has a call number. The call number says: this book lives here. It helps you find the book.

[Picture: a library desk — a book with a striped label, a rack of magazines]

A library has its own words. Bar code. Loan period — how long you can keep the book. Magazine. Thesaurus — a book full of words. Learn the words, and the library is easy.
