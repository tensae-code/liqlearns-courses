# Video script — Text Types

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Three short texts appear on cards behind the mascot, all about dalo.]

"Three texts. All about dalo! But each one is doing a different job."

[The mascot points at each card in turn.] "This one tells what happened. This one tells how to do it. This one gives facts."

"Texts come in eight kinds. A recount tells what happened. A procedure tells the steps. A report gives the facts. A narrative is a story."

"Before you read, ask: what job is this text doing? Then you know how to read it."
