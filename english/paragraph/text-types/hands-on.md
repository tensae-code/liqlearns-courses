# Hands-on — Text Types

1. **Name the job.** A partner reads out the first lines of four texts — a recipe, a story, a report about an animal, a letter of invitation. For each one, name the text type and say the job it is doing.

2. **Shape clues.** Take one text and hunt its shape clues: steps and commands, facts in the timeless present, or a story problem. Read the clues out and let your partner name the type from the clues alone.
