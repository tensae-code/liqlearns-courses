## Matching

Match the text to its text type. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. "First we lit the stove. Then we put the pot on."
2. "Wash the dalo well. Cut it into pieces."
3. "The cassava is a root crop. It grows in wet ground."
4. "Dear Ana, please come to my birthday party on Saturday."
5. "Children should wear hats at school, because the sun can burn their skin."

a) procedure  b) recount  c) transaction  d) report  e) persuasive

**Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c, 5-e.**
