## Multiple choice

1. A text that tells how to do something, step by step, is a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) report  b) procedure  c) recount
   **Answer: b).**

2. A text that gives facts about a whole class of things — what a tornado is, how chickens live — is a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) report  b) narrative  c) transaction
   **Answer: a).**

3. Which text type argues for one side, to change what the reader thinks or does? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) poetry  b) exposition and persuasive texts  c) recount
   **Answer: b).**

4. A text runs on time connectives — first, then, after that — and tells what happened in order. Its shape says it is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) recount  b) report  c) procedure
   **Answer: a).**

5. A text uses generalised nouns, technical terms and the timeless present. Its shape says it is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) narrative  b) transaction  c) report
   **Answer: c).**
