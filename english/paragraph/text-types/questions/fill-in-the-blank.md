## Fill in the blank

1. A ______ tells what happened, in the order it happened. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: recount.**

2. A letter, an invitation and a form are all ______ texts — they get something done between people. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: transaction.**

3. A story with people, a place and something going wrong is a ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: narrative.**

4. Reports speak in the timeless ______ tense, because their facts are always true. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: present.**

5. Procedures are built from ______ verbs — wash, put, stir, lift. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: action.**
