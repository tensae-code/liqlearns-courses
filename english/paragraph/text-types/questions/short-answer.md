## Short answer

1. Name the eight text types Fiji's Year 3 book teaches. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: recount, poetry, transaction, report, procedure, persuasive, narrative, exposition.**

2. What is the difference between a recount and a narrative? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a recount tells what really happened in order; a narrative is a story, built around people, a place and something going wrong.**

3. You open a text and see steps in order, with commands like "wash", "cut", "stir". What type is it, and what clue told you? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: a procedure — the ordered steps and action-verb commands are its shape.**

4. Why does knowing the text type help before you read? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the type tells you the job the text is doing, so you know what to read for — the steps, the facts, or the story.**
