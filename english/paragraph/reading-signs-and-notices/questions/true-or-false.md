## True or false

1. A sign that begins with "No" tells you a thing is allowed. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — "No" at the front means the thing is NOT allowed.

2. "No Eating" and "No Drinking" are signs you might see in a library. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. "Do Not Litter" asks you to throw your litter on the ground. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it forbids it: do not throw litter on the ground.

4. "Please Switch Off All Cell Phones" is a forbidding sign. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — Please makes it an asking sign; it asks politely.

5. The first word of a sign tells you what kind of sign it is. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — No / Do Not / Keep Off forbid; Please asks.
