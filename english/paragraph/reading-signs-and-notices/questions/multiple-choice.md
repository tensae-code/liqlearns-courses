## Multiple choice

1. A sign begins with the word "No". What is the sign doing? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Asking for something  b) Forbidding something — it is not allowed  c) Telling a story
   **Answer: b)** — No at the front means the thing is not allowed here.

2. What does "Keep Off Grass" tell you? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Walk on the grass  b) Water the grass  c) Do not walk on the grass
   **Answer: c)** — Keep Off means stay off it.

3. Which of these is a forbidding sign? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Think Green  b) Do Not Litter  c) Please Switch Off All Cell Phones
   **Answer: b)** — Do Not at the front forbids the action.

4. A sign begins with "Please". What is the sign doing? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) It asks you politely to do something  b) It forbids something  c) It names a town
   **Answer: a)** — Please is the asking word.

5. You meet a sign you have never seen. What do you read first? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The last word  b) The picture only  c) The first word — it tells you the sign's kind
   **Answer: c)** — No / Do Not forbid, Please asks; the rest names the thing.
