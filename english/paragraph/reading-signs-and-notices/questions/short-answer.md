## Short answer

1. Name the three forbidding openers of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** No, Do Not, and Keep Off.

2. What does "No Skateboards" mean, and where might the sign stand? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Skateboards are not allowed here — for example at the gate of a park or a school yard.

3. How is "Please Switch Off All Cell Phones" different from the signs of sub lesson 1? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** It asks politely instead of forbidding: Please is the asking word, and the sign requests the thing rather than banning something.

4. You see a new sign: "No Dogs". You have never seen it before. How do you read it? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** First word first: "No" forbids, so the sign says dogs are not allowed here; the rest of the sign names the thing.
