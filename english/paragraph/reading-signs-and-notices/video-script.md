# Video script — Reading Signs and Notices

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A park gate appears, with a big sign: NO PARKING.] "Look —
a SIGN! Signs talk to us. This one says: NO PARKING."

[Three signs pop up one by one: NO EATING, NO DRINKING, DO
NOT LITTER.] "NO… NO… DO NOT… These signs say: NOT ALLOWED!"

[A library door appears, with the sign PLEASE SWITCH OFF ALL
CELL PHONES.] "And this sign ASKS. PLEASE! It asks nicely."

[A green tree appears, with the sign THINK GREEN.] "THINK
GREEN! Remember the trees."

[Back to the mascot, pointing at the first word of a new
sign.] "First read the FIRST word. NO? It says no. PLEASE?
It asks. Now you can read EVERY sign!"
