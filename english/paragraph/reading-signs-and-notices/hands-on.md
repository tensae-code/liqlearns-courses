# Hands-on — Reading Signs and Notices

## Sign hunt

Walk around your school or your street with paper and a
pencil. Write down every English sign you pass. Back in
class, sort your signs into two piles: the signs that say
no (No / Do Not / Keep Off) and the signs that ask or
remind (Please, and the others). Read each sign aloud,
first word first.

## Make a sign

Make one sign for your classroom. Choose its kind first:
a "says no" sign or a "please" sign. Write it in big clear
letters, check every word's spelling, and put it where it
belongs — a sign only works where its thing happens.
