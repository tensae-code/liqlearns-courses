# Levels — Listening to an Interview

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** follow a short interview's shape
  (opener, questions, answers, closing) and pick out the
  speaker's likes, dislikes and the interviewer's compliments.
- **Climb:** listen a second time for details and answer
  questions from what was heard, never from guesses.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** take a real
  interview apart on first and second listening — who the
  guest is, what they like and dislike, what they are praised
  for — and answer from the recording alone.
