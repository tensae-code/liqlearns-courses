# Lesson Plan — Listening to an Interview

Unit 15 — Reading Whole Texts. Planned shape: four sub lessons —
first what an interview is and who does what in one, then the
shape of an interview (opener, questions, answers, closing),
then listening inside one for three things a speaker tells —
likes, dislikes and compliments — and last listening again and
answering from what you heard. The lesson is built from Mexico's
Grade 7 book, which listens to an interview with the singer
Susana Harp under the heading "Afro-México y patrimonio cultural"
(printed p. 141): identify the interviewee's likes and dislikes
and the compliments the interviewer pays her. The practice
interview in this lesson is the course's own short interview in
the same shape; the book's recording is not transcribed.

## Sub lessons

1. **What an interview is** — one person asks questions, another
   answers them; why people give interviews.
2. **Listening for the shape** — the opener, the questions, the
   answers and the closing, in order.
3. **Listening for likes, dislikes and compliments** — the three
   things a speaker tells inside an interview, and the sentences
   that carry them.
4. **Listening again and answering from what you heard** —
   answers that come from the listening, never invented.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
