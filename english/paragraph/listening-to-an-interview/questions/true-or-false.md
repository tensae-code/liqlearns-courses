# True or False — Listening to an Interview

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. In an interview, both people ask and answer questions all the time. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — one person leads with questions; the other answers.

2. An interview can be heard on the radio or watched on video. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. When you answer from a listening, you may invent details the speaker never said. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — answers come from what you heard.

4. Listening again helps you hear words you missed the first time. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True**
