# Fill in the Blank — Listening to an Interview

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. An interview opens with a ____ and the guest's name. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: welcome**

2. The interviewer asks questions and the guest ____ them. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: answers**

3. "She loves singing" tells something she ____. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: likes**

4. A compliment is a kind word that says something good about a ____. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: person**
