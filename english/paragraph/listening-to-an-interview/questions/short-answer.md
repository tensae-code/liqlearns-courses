# Short Answer — Listening to an Interview

Answer in a few words.

1. Who are the two people in an interview, and what does each one do? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The interviewer asks the questions; the guest answers them.

2. Name one thing an interview can be about. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any one of: a person's work, music, life story, knowledge or feelings.

3. Name the four parts of an interview in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The opener, the questions, the answers and the closing.

4. What does the opener tell the listeners? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Who the guest is — their name and their work.

5. In the practice interview, what does the guest say she likes most about her work? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** Singing the songs of her region.

6. What compliment does the interviewer give the guest in the practice interview? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** That her music keeps the heritage alive.

7. When did the guest in the practice interview start singing? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** As a child, with her family.
