# Multiple Choice — Listening to an Interview

Choose the one right answer.

1. What is an interview? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) A conversation where one person asks questions and another answers them
   - B) A long speech with no questions
   - C) A song sung by two people
   - D) A letter to a newspaper
   **Answer: A**

2. In an interview, who asks the questions? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) The guest
   - B) The interviewer
   - C) The listeners
   - D) Nobody — the questions are written on the wall
   **Answer: B**

3. Why do people give interviews? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) To share what they know, do and feel
   - B) To hide their work
   - C) To practise spelling
   - D) To sell questions
   **Answer: A**

4. What usually comes first in an interview? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) The goodbye
   - B) The hardest question
   - C) The opener — the welcome and the guest's name
   - D) The answers
   **Answer: C**

5. In the middle of an interview you mostly hear… (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Music
   - B) Questions and answers
   - C) Long silences
   - D) The closing
   **Answer: B**

6. What is the closing of an interview? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) The first question
   - B) The welcome
   - C) The thank-you and the goodbye at the end
   - D) The guest's name
   **Answer: C**

7. Which sentence tells a like? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) "I really enjoy singing traditional songs."
   - B) "I don't like long journeys."
   - C) "You sing beautifully."
   - D) "When did you start?"
   **Answer: A**

8. Which sentence tells a dislike? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) "I love the music of my region."
   - B) "I don't like it when people forget our traditions."
   - C) "Your work protects our heritage."
   - D) "Welcome to the programme."
   **Answer: B**

9. Which sentence is a compliment? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) "I started as a child."
   - B) "I don't enjoy cold mornings."
   - C) "Your music keeps our heritage alive."
   - D) "What do you do?"
   **Answer: C**

10. When you answer from a listening, your answer must come from… (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) What you actually heard
    - B) What you guess
    - C) What you wish the speaker had said
    - D) Another lesson
    **Answer: A**

11. If you did not catch a word, the best thing to do is… (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) Invent a word
    - B) Stop listening
    - C) Listen again for the words around it
    - D) Answer a different question
    **Answer: C**

12. A good listening answer is… (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) Long and invented
    - B) Short, and about what the speaker said
    - C) About a different speaker
    - D) Always a question
    **Answer: B**
