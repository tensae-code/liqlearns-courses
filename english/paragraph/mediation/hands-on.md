# Hands-On — Mediation: Transferring Information Between Texts and Languages

Things you do with real texts around you. You need two texts about the same thing and a pencil.

1. **Poster into a note.** Find a real poster — a school event, a match, a sale. Write a three-line note to a friend about it, leaving the day, the time and the place as gaps. Then fill the gaps from the poster only.
2. **Two texts, one truth.** Find two texts about the same event — a message and a notice, a timetable and an announcement. Where they both give a fact, check that the facts agree. Where only one gives it, that is the one you carry.
3. **Across languages.** Take a short text in English and write its facts into a note in your own language — then go back: read only your note and say the facts in English. Check against the first text: did anything change?
4. **The gap game.** Write a short paragraph about your day with four facts blanked out. A partner who knows your day must fill the gaps from what you said. Every wrong gap shows where a fact was lost on the way.
