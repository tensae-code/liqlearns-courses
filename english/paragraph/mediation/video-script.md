# Video Script — Mediation: Transferring Information Between Texts and Languages

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point at each text as it is named.

[Picture: two short texts side by side — a poster on the left, a note with empty gaps on the right]

Look. Two texts. This one tells us things. This one has empty places. We take the true things from the first text, and we put them in the empty places. This is called mediation.

[Picture: a finger underlining a date, a time and a place on the poster]

First, we find the facts. When? Where? How much? We look for names, days, times, places, numbers. We mark them.

[Picture: the same facts being written into the note's gaps, one by one]

Now we carry the facts. One gap, one fact. We look back at the first text every time. We do not guess.

[Picture: the finished note beside the poster, a finger checking fact by fact]

Last, we check. Every fact in the new text — is it the same as in the first text? Yes? Then the information travelled safely. The words can change. The facts never change.
