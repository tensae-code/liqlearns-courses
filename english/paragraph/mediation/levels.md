# Levels — Mediation: Transferring Information Between Texts and Languages

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Finding the exact information | Read one or two source texts and pick out the exact facts — names, dates, times, places, prices, what happened — telling facts from opinions. |
| 2. Carrying the facts into a new text | Fill the gaps of a new text with those facts, across languages when the new text asks for it, and check the finished text fact by fact against the sources. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can take information out of any text and land it, exact and complete, in another text — the skill exams call mediation.
