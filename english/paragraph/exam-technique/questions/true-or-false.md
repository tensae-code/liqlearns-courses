# Exam technique — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In an exam, writing everything you know about a topic always earns the most marks. **Answer: False** — marks are paid for the delivery the verb orders; the rest spends other questions' time

**2. (Level 2)** Vietnam's Exam Strategies appendix is badged from exercises through the book as 'A+ EXAM TIPS'. **Answer: True** — the badges point to pp.145–146 — the appendix is a working tool, not decoration

**3. (Level 3)** The contents page and the index do the same job. **Answer: False** — contents = the book's parts and topics; index = words and names with their pages (Rwanda, pp.76–77)
