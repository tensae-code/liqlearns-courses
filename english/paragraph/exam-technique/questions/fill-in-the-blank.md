# Exam technique — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ — the same content, much shorter, in your own words. **Answer: Summarise** — one of Rwanda's named verbs (p.87)

**2. (Level 2)** ______ — build a new piece of writing. **Answer: Compose** — Rwanda teaches its structure — introduction, main body, conclusion (pp.80–82)

**3. (Level 2)** ______ — turn a question around in words: points for, points against, weighed. **Answer: Discuss** — the discussion delivery, from Rwanda's list
