**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which verb orders you to pick one thing out from others? **Answer: identify** — Rwanda's examination-verb list (p.87)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Compare' and 'contrast': what does each add? **Answer: compare sets things side by side — same and different; contrast (differentiate) gives the differences alone** — both stand in Rwanda's list (p.87)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A question asks on which page a book treats the word 'volcano'. Contents or index — and why? **Answer: the index — it lists words and names with their pages; the contents lists the book's parts and topics** — Rwanda names both tools with the reading skills (pp.76–77)
