# Exam technique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "List three rivers" demands — a) a paragraph about rivers b) three named items, only the items c) a judgement of the rivers **Answer: b) three named items, only the items** — LIST pays for items; sentences around them buy nothing (Rwanda, p.87)

**2. (Level 1)** "State the capital" demands — a) a short, plain statement b) a discussion c) a summary of the country **Answer: a) a short, plain statement** — STATE is the shortest delivery on the list

**3. (Level 2)** "Evaluate the plan" demands — a) a copy of the plan b) a judgement with reasons c) a list of its parts **Answer: b) a judgement with reasons** — EVALUATE without reasons is an opinion, not an evaluation

**4. (Level 3)** Vietnam's appendix is organised — a) by chapter b) by question type c) by difficulty **Answer: b) by question type** — Appendix 2 is a strategy table per question type (pp.145–146)
