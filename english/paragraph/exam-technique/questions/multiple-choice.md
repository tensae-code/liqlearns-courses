# Exam technique — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, split item by item by what each item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: instruction-verb items (old Levels 1 and 2) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Instruction Verbs · strategy-by-question-type items (old Level 3, and the Exam Strategies appendix item filed under Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Strategies by Question Type · contents-and-index under-time items -> Sub lesson 3 — Finding and Finishing Under Time.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "List three rivers" demands — a) a paragraph about rivers b) three named items, only the items c) a judgement of the rivers **Answer: b) three named items, only the items** — LIST pays for items; sentences around them buy nothing (Rwanda, p.87)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "State the capital" demands — a) a short, plain statement b) a discussion c) a summary of the country **Answer: a) a short, plain statement** — STATE is the shortest delivery on the list

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "Evaluate the plan" demands — a) a copy of the plan b) a judgement with reasons c) a list of its parts **Answer: b) a judgement with reasons** — EVALUATE without reasons is an opinion, not an evaluation

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vietnam's appendix is organised — a) by chapter b) by question type c) by difficulty **Answer: b) by question type** — Appendix 2 is a strategy table per question type (pp.145–146)
