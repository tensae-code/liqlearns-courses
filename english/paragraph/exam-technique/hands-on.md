# Hands-on — Exam technique

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Verb cards.** Make eight verb cards. A partner reads a demand ('judge this plan, with reasons'); slap the right card — EVALUATE — before they finish the sentence.
2. **Same topic, three sizes.** Take one topic and answer it three ways on paper: LIST it, SUMMARISE it, EVALUATE it. Lay the three side by side and measure their shapes.
3. **Contents/index race.** Rwanda's tools against the clock: find a named topic by the contents, then a named word by the index, in a real book. Thirty seconds each. No leaf-by-leaf hunting allowed.
4. **Appendix drill.** Take one past question of each type Vietnam's table names — pronunciation, cloze, reading, transformation — and write, before answering, which strategy the appendix assigns it.
