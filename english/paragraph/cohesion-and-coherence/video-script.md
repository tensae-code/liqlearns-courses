# Video Script — Cohesion and Coherence

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: sentences as train cars, joined by bright couplings]

See the links? However… because… then… These hold the sentences together. This is COHESION!

[Picture: the same train driving in circles to nowhere]

But links are not enough! If the train goes nowhere… the SENSE is missing!

[Picture: the train now running straight to one station labelled THE POINT]

One idea. In order. Nothing wandering. This is COHERENCE — links outside, sense inside!
