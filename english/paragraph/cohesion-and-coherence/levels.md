# Levels — Cohesion and Coherence

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Cohesion: the links you can see | Name and use linking devices — the visible ties (reference, repetition, connectors) that stitch sentences together. |
| 2. Coherence: the sense that holds | Judge whether a text is coherent — whether its ideas follow in an order and logic a reader can hold — apart from its linking words. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the two ways a text holds together — cohesion, the visible links between its sentences, and coherence, the logical sense that makes it hang together as one piece of meaning.
