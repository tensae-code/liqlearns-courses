# Lesson Plan — Cohesion and Coherence

**Unit:** Unit 14 — The Paragraph

**One thing:** the two ways a text holds together — cohesion, the visible links between its sentences, and coherence, the logical sense that makes it hang together as one piece of meaning.

## Sub lessons

1. Cohesion: the links you can see
2. Coherence: the sense that holds

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Cohesion versus coherence in a text: what each means (Lesson 1, Task 9 "Quick Writes", pp.291–292).
- Linking devices differ from logical sense: the devices are surface ties; coherence is the logic of the whole (pp.291–292).
