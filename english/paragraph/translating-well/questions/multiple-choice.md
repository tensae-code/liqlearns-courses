# Translating well — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Law of Translation · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Translation Repair Shop · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Real Crossing.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bolivia's principle says word-for-word translation is — a) always best b) rarely applicable c) forbidden by law **Answer: b) rarely applicable** — it works only where both languages share the structure (p.226)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Word-for-word works when — a) the words are short b) the two languages have the same structure there c) you speak slowly **Answer: b) the two languages have the same structure there** — Bolivia's own condition for it (p.226)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An idiom in translation must be carried — a) word by word b) by its whole meaning c) not at all **Answer: b) by its whole meaning** — its meaning lives in the whole phrase; word-for-word arrives as nonsense

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The back-translation check tests — a) spelling b) whether the meaning survives the return crossing c) speed **Answer: b) whether the meaning survives the return crossing** — if a stranger returns, the crossing failed
