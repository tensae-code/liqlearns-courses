# Hands-on — Translating well

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Break it, fix it.** Translate three short sentences word-for-word, on purpose. Read the wrecks aloud. Then rebuild each one meaning-first and compare what changed — order, categories, wholes.
2. **The idiom bridge.** Take 'It's raining cats and dogs' and one idiom from your own language. Carry each across by meaning alone; then try word-for-word and exhibit the wreck beside the crossing.
3. **Bolivia's assignment.** The book's own production: find two articles — newspaper or magazine — translate them following the guidelines (one each direction), and read them in class or to your companion as finished texts.
4. **Back-translation check.** Translate a paragraph; give ONLY your translation to a partner, who translates it back. Compare the returning meaning with the original, line by line.
