# Video script — Translating well

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Translation is a bridge. What crosses? Not words. MEANING!

Word for word? Bolivia's book says: rarely! It rarely works.

Words stand in different orders. English here. Spanish there.

Words change their jobs, too. A noun here can be a verb there.

An idiom? Carry the WHOLE meaning. Never the words!

First: understand. Say the meaning in your own words.

Then: build it again, fresh, in the new language.

Read it back. Does it sound like a real text? Then it crossed.
