# Video Script — Viewing and Media Literacy

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a child looking at a busy picture, clues lighting up one by one — an arrow, a red roof, a big tree]

Look on purpose! Colours, arrows, sizes — the picture is telling you things. Read the clues!

[Picture: a simple graph with bars growing, a child pointing to the tallest]

A graph shows numbers as shapes. This bar is tallest — this number is biggest!

[Picture: a child pinning a big clear picture onto a board for a class]

When you present, make a picture that carries your point. One clear picture beats many small words.

[Picture: an advertisement with a smiling famous face, a child pointing at it and thinking]

A famous face wants your trust. Name the trick before you decide!
