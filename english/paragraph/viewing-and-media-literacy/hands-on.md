# Hands-On — Viewing and Media Literacy

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Cover and read.** Cover the words on a page and read only the picture; list three facts. Then swap: cover the picture, read the words, and compare what each side carried.
2. **Graph walk.** Find a graph or chart. Say its title, what it counts, which is biggest and which is smallest — all from the shapes.
3. **Make the visual.** Choose a short message (a rule, a warning, an invitation). Make it as one visual with almost no words; show it and ask what it says.
4. **Persuasion spotter.** In pairs, each finds one print and one screen advertisement. Name the medium, the technique and the target feeling for each.
