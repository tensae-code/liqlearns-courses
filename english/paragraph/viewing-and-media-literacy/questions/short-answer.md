# Short Answer — Viewing and Media Literacy

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A page shows a picture of a river's path beside a paragraph about rivers. What does the picture carry that the words may not? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The shape and route of the river at a glance — where it starts, bends and ends — spatial information the words describe only step by step.
2. How do you read a bar graph? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Read the title, what is being counted, the numbers along the side or bottom, then compare the bars: the taller or longer the bar, the bigger the number.
3. An advertisement shows a crying child and asks for money. Name the technique and the feeling it fishes for. (Sub lesson 2)
   — It uses a sad face/sadness to fish for sympathy, so the viewer gives money.
4. Give one difference between presenting with a printed poster and with electronic slides. (Sub lesson 2)
   — A printed poster stays fixed on the wall; electronic slides can move, sound, change and be shown in sequence (any one clear difference).
