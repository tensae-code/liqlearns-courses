# Levels — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Hearing the details and the meaning-changers | Pick out the descriptive details in something heard, and show how intonation, expression and gestures can change what the same words mean. |
| 2. Hearing tone and persuasion — and writing what you hear | Hear the tone and mood a speaker sets, name the persuasive techniques being used on you, and write dictated sentences and passages exactly as spoken. |

A learner who finishes all 2 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: listening past the words themselves — hearing the details a speaker gives, hearing how intonation, expression and gestures change what words mean, and hearing when a speaker's tone and mood are being used to persuade you — and writing down exactly what you hear (dictation).
