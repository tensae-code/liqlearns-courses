# Video Script — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a child listening, with small pictures of details appearing over the speaker — a red boat, a tall man]

Listen for the details. The small words paint the picture. Catch them!

[Picture: the same face saying "Great" flat, then bright and smiling]

Same word — different meaning! The voice changes the words. Flat: not good. Bright: wonderful!

[Picture: a speaker at a podium, warm light, an audience nodding]

A speaker sets a feeling in the room. Ask: what do you want me to feel? What do you want me to do?

[Picture: a child writing while a voice speaks slowly]

Dictation! Listen. Write exactly what you hear. Then listen again and check.
