# Hands-On — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Three voices.** Say one sentence three ways — glad, angry, surprised. Your partner guesses the feeling each time, from the voice alone.
2. **Detail hunt.** A partner describes a picture for one minute without showing it. Write down every descriptive detail you hear; then look at the picture and count what you caught.
3. **Tone detective.** Listen to an advertisement together. Each person writes the tone, the mood, and what it wants the listener to do. Compare answers.
4. **Dictation swap.** Read two sentences slowly to a partner, who writes them exactly. Swap roles, then check each other's writing word by word.
