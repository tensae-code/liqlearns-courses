# Lesson Plan — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

**One thing:** listening past the words themselves — hearing the details a speaker gives, hearing how intonation, expression and gestures change what words mean, and hearing when a speaker's tone and mood are being used to persuade you — and writing down exactly what you hear (dictation).

## Sub lessons

1. Hearing the details and the meaning-changers
2. Hearing tone and persuasion — and writing what you hear

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Listening closely for descriptive details in what is heard (Primary Language Arts Scope and Sequence, Sub-goal 1 — Listening, objective set 1.17–1.20, printed p.11).
- How intonation, expression and gestures change meaning — the same words, a different message (objective set 1.17–1.20, printed p.11).
- Persuasive techniques of tone and mood in a presentation — hearing what the speaker wants you to feel and do (objective set 1.17–1.20, printed p.11).
- Dictation: writing dictated sentences and passages exactly as spoken (objective set 1.17–1.20, printed p.11).
