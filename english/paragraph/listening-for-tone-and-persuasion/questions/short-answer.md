# Short Answer — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A classmate says "Fine." with a sigh and rolling eyes. What do you understand, and why? (Sub lesson 1)
   — That they are not fine — the intonation, expression and gesture change the meaning of the word.
2. Name two things besides the words that change what a spoken sentence means. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any two of: intonation (rise and fall of the voice), facial expression, gestures.
3. A radio advertisement sounds warm and urgent and keeps repeating "for your family". What techniques is it using? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Tone and mood as persuasive techniques — warmth (for your family) and urgency — to make the listener feel something and act.
4. How do you take a good dictation? Give the steps. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Listen once for the meaning, listen again for the exact words, write them down, then check against another hearing.
