# Multiple Choice — Listening for Tone and Persuasion

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A speaker says "Great." in a flat, low voice. What does the listener most likely understand? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Wonderful news
   b) The speaker is not pleased ✓
   c) The speaker is asking a question
   d) The speaker is shouting

2. What are the meaning-changers in listening? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The spelling of the words
   b) Intonation, expression and gestures ✓
   c) The length of the speech
   d) The number of listeners

3. A listener catches "the tall man in the red boat". What has the listener caught? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The main idea only
   b) Descriptive details ✓
   c) The tone
   d) A gesture

4. In a presentation, a speaker slows down, warms the voice and repeats one warm phrase. What is the speaker doing? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Making a mistake
   b) Using tone and mood to persuade ✓
   c) Ending the talk
   d) Reading dictation

5. What is dictation? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Speaking from memory
   b) Writing down sentences and passages exactly as spoken ✓
   c) Guessing what a speaker will say
   d) Summarising a speech in your own words

6. What is the best first question when you feel a speaker persuading you? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) How long is the talk?
   b) What does this speaker want me to feel and do? ✓
   c) Who else is listening?
   d) Can I leave?
