# Levels — Writing a Menu

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a menu holds | Say what a menu is and how it is organised, name the three meals of the day, and name dishes of two countries that stand under them. |
| 2. Building the menu | Choose a dish for a menu and write its ingredient list with the right markers — counting the countable ingredients and measuring the uncountable ones. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can read any menu, and can build a clear two-country menu of their own, dish by dish, ingredient by ingredient.
