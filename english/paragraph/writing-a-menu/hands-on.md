# Hands-On — Writing a Menu

Things you do with real food, real dishes and a pen. No special materials needed.

1. **Today's home menu.** Write the menu of your home for today: breakfast, lunch, dinner, with the real dish under each meal.
2. **Two countries.** Build a binational menu card: dishes of your country on the left, dishes of another country on the right. At least two dishes under every meal.
3. **Ingredient list.** Choose one dish you know well. Write everything that goes into it. Count the countable ingredients (three lemons); measure the uncountable ones (a spoonful of oil, two pounds of flour).
4. **Guest test.** Give your menu to a partner. They must choose a full day of eating from it — breakfast, lunch and dinner — and ask you what is in one dish. If they cannot choose, your menu is missing something: fix it.
