# Video Script — Writing a Menu

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each part of the menu appears on screen as it is said.

[Picture: a waiter handing a guest a menu card in a restaurant]

You are in a restaurant. The waiter gives you a card. This card is a menu. You choose your food from it!

[Picture: a menu with three headings — breakfast, lunch, dinner]

A menu has three parts. Breakfast — the morning meal. Lunch — the midday meal. Dinner — the evening meal.

[Picture: two columns of dish names — Haitian dishes on one side, American dishes on the other]

Under each meal: the names of dishes! Joumou… griot… buffalo wings… pecan pie… Two countries on one menu!

[Picture: a dish with its ingredients listed under it — three lemons, a spoonful of oil]

Under a dish, we write what is in it. Three lemons — we count them. A spoonful of oil — we measure it. Now anyone can cook from your menu!
