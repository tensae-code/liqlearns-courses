# Fill in the blank — Asking for Advice

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Dear Agony Aunt, I am writing to ask for _____. (advice / a story)
   **Answer: advice** (Sub lesson 1)

2. The helper must understand the _____ from your letter alone.
   **Answer: problem** (Sub lesson 1)

3. You _____ talk to your teacher about it. (should / yesterday)
   **Answer: should** (Sub lesson 2)

4. If I _____ you, I would say sorry first. (were / am tomorrow)
   **Answer: were** (Sub lesson 2)
