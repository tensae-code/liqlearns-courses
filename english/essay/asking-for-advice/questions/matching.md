# Matching — Asking for Advice

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the letter part to its job.
   1. telling the problem
   2. asking directly
   3. signing off
   a. "What should I do?"
   b. says what happened and how you feel
   c. thanks the reader and ends the letter
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the advice phrase to its strength.
   1. You should…
   2. Why don't you…?
   3. If I were you, I would…
   a. a firm suggestion
   b. a gentle suggestion
   c. advice from the other person's place
   **Answer: 1-a, 2-b, 3-c** (Sub lesson 2)
