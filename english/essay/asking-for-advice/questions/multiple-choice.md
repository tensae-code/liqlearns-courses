# Multiple choice — Asking for Advice

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. What must an advice-asking letter do FIRST after saying who you are?
   A) thank the reader
   B) tell the problem clearly
   C) give advice
   D) sign off
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Which sentence asks for advice directly?
   A) I have a problem.
   B) What should I do?
   C) Thank you for your letter.
   D) I am fourteen years old.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. An advice column is:
   A) a page that answers readers' problems
   B) a kind of exam
   C) a story book
   D) a shopping list
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Which is a kind advice sentence?
   A) Do it now, no questions.
   B) If I were you, I would talk to your friend.
   C) Your problem is stupid.
   D) Never ask me again.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

5. An advice answer should open with:
   A) orders
   B) understanding
   C) the price
   D) a joke about the problem
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. "Why don't you…?" is used to:
   A) ask about the past
   B) give advice
   C) close a letter
   D) tell a story
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
