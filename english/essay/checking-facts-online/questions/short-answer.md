## Short answer

1. Give two warning signs of fake news. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "No named source; only one place tells it."** (any two signs) — the identification work.

2. A headline screams "SHOCKING!! Nobody believes this!!" What does the style tell you? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "It is selling feelings, not facts — a warning sign."** — shouting is a sign, not proof either way; run the check.

3. Run the check: a page you never heard of says a famous singer died; no newspaper reports it. Steps and verdict? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Source unknown; other reports — none. It does not check out."** — steps 1 and 2 already fail it.

4. An old flood photo is forwarded as "the flood TODAY in our town". Which step catches it? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Step 3 — check the date."** — old material, new claim.

5. Decide and justify: you checked a claim and found three trusted reports with the same facts. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "It checks out — I may believe and share it."** — decision + the evidence that carries it.
