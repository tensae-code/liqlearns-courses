## True or false

1. If many friends forward a message, it must be true. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — forwards spread feelings; they prove nothing.

2. A story told by only one no-name page is a warning sign. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — real big events appear in many places.

3. Checking the source means asking who published the claim. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — step one of the procedure.

4. The date doesn't matter in the check. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — old stories wearing new headlines are a classic trick.

5. If you can't tell whether a claim is true, sharing it quickly is safest. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — waiting is the safe move.

6. Your name travels with what you forward. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — sharing is a decision you own.
