## Multiple choice

1. Fake news is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) news you don't like  b) a false story made to look like real news  c) old news
   **Answer: b)** — fake news is invented but dressed as real.

2. Fake news spreads fast because… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) it is always true  b) it is exciting, scary or funny — feelings travel faster than facts  c) teachers share it
   **Answer: b)** — strong feelings make people forward first.

3. A warning sign of fake news is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a named, known source  b) no source at all, and a screaming headline  c) a date on the story
   **Answer: b)** — unnamed sources and shouting headlines are signs.

4. The FIRST step of the check is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) share it quickly  b) check the source — who published it  c) believe the headline
   **Answer: b)** — source first.

5. If a big event really happened, you expect… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) only one strange page to report it  b) many trusted places to report it  c) no reports at all
   **Answer: b)** — real big events are reported widely.

6. Why check the date? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Old stories can wear new headlines  b) Dates are lucky  c) New stories are always false
   **Answer: a)** — an old photo with a new claim is a classic trick.

7. After checking, a claim does NOT check out. You should… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) share it anyway  b) not share it  c) share it only to friends
   **Answer: b)** — don't pass on what fails the check.

8. You can't tell if a claim is true. The safe move is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) share it fast  b) wait — not sharing yet is always safe  c) guess loudly
   **Answer: b)** — waiting costs nothing.
