## Fill in the blank

1. A false story made to look like real news is called ___ news. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: fake** — the kind's name.

2. Fake news spreads because ___ travel faster than facts. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: feelings** — exciting, scary or funny wins the race.

3. Step one: check the ___. Who published it? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: source** — known source or no-name page?

4. Step two: search for ___ reports. Do trusted places agree? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: other** — one report alone is weak.

5. Step three: check the ___ — an old story may wear a new headline. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: date** — when did it really happen?

6. If it doesn't check out, don't ___ it. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: share** — your name travels with what you forward.
