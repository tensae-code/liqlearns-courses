## Matching

Match the step to its question. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. Check the source — a
2. Search for other reports — b
3. Check the date — c
4. Look for evidence — d
5. It checks out — e
6. It doesn't check out — f

a) Who published it?
b) Who else tells this story?
c) Is it new, or old news in a new coat?
d) What names, places and proof are given?
e) Believe it — and you may share it
f) Don't share it

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (source = publisher), 2–b (other reports test the claim), 3–c (dates catch recycled stories), 4–d (evidence or feelings only?), 5–e (passed the check), 6–f (failed the check — stop it with you).
