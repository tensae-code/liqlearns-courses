# Hands-on — Checking Facts Online

Real claims are the practice material here — never invented easy ones only.

## The forward test

Take one message really forwarded in your family or class chat this month. Run the four steps together, in writing. End with the decision and the deciding step.

## Two headlines

Make two headlines about your school: one true, one fake, equally well written. Exchange with a partner. Each must run the check and say which is fake — and which step caught it.
