# Video script — Checking Facts Online

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A phone shows a screaming headline.] "A giant snake!! Is it TRUE? Not everything online is true — some news is FAKE!"

[A magnifying glass moves over the screen.] "CHECK it! One — who says it? Two — who ELSE says it? Three — is it new? Four — where is the proof?"

[The mascot shakes his head at the phone.] "It does not check out? Then DON'T share it. Your name travels with it!"

[The mascot smiles and nods.] "One minute of checking beats a hundred forwards. Check first!"
