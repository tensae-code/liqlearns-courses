# Hands-on — Debating

- **Order drill.** Six learners, six cards (1A, 1N, 2A, 2N, 3A, 3N). Shuffle the class, not the cards: learners stand in speaking order and each states their job in one sentence.
- **Rebuttal practice.** One learner reads a one-minute argument; a partner must first repeat the argument fairly, then answer it. Swap.
- **Quotation trail.** From a printed debate, trace one argument through the whole contest: who raised it, who answered it, who had the last word.
