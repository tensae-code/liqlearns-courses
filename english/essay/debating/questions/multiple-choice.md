# Multiple choice — Debating

1. In a debate, the team that defends the moot is called… (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. The negative
   - B. The affirmative
   - C. The judges
   - D. The audience
   Answer: B

2. Who speaks first in a debate? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. The first negative speaker
   - B. The third affirmative speaker
   - C. The first affirmative speaker
   - D. The chairperson
   Answer: C

3. What are the third speaker's two jobs? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Open the case and thank the audience
   - B. Rebut the other team's arguments and sum up their own side
   - C. Ask questions and keep time
   - D. Introduce the teams and close the hall
   Answer: B

4. Why does Sāmoa's Year 12 book follow a debate through its quotations? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. To count the words
   - B. So the reader can track who said what, for which side, answering what — and judge the debate
   - C. To copy the speeches
   - D. To check the spelling
   Answer: B
