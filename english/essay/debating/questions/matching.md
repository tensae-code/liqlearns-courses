# Matching — Debating

Match each speaker to their job.

1. First affirmative (Sub lesson 1) — opens the case for the moot
2. First negative (Sub lesson 1) — opens the case against the moot
3. Second speakers (Sub lesson 1) — build their side's case and answer the other side
4. Third speakers (Sub lesson 1) — rebut the other team and sum up their own
5. A quotation trail (Sub lesson 2) — following who said what, for which side, answering what
