# Debating — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 38, Studying Texts

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of two teams facing each other across a hall, three chairs on each side numbered 1, 2, 3, and a speaker standing at the first chair holding a card that says WE AGREE WITH THE MOTION. *The learners say which team speaks first in a debate and which chair is standing.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of the third speaker on one team with two speech bubbles: the first points back at the other team's arguments with a cross through them, the second gathers their own team's points into one bunch. *The learners say the third speaker's two jobs.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a debater mid-speech, with the audience's thought bubbles showing a tick, a question mark and a cross. *The learners say what the audience is doing while a debater speaks.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of two lines of a debate transcript on a page, with quotation marks around the spoken words and a small label saying WHO SAID IT under each. *The learners say why a reader of a debate needs to know who said each line.*

## Sub lessons
1. How a Debate Runs
2. Speaking in a Debate

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
