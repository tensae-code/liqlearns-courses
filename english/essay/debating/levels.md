# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a Debate Runs
- Depth: the speaking order and each speaker's job, taught twice at two levels — the order in the Year 9 book (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 2*, printed pp.40–42) and again in the Year 10 book (Samoa, *English Year 10 Book Two*, printed pp.39–40), where the third affirmative speaker's job is to rebut and sum up. The Year 10 book prints that instruction with an error — it tells the third affirmative speaker to "rebut all the arguments used by the affirmative team", where the negative team's arguments are meant; the error is noted in this lesson's sources and the job is taught corrected here.

## Sub lesson 2 — Speaking in a Debate
- Depth: debating language — stating and recognising opinions inside the contest (Samoa, *English Year 10 Book Two*, printed pp.39–40); a starter exercise from the Juniors debating book (Samoa, *English Year 11 Book 1*, printed pp.82–83); and understanding a whole debate by following its quotations speaker by speaker, on the moot "That sport leads to violence" (Samoa, *English Year 12*, printed pp.80–81).
