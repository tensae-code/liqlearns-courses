# Video script — Debating

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a Debate Runs
- Beat 1: Two teams, three chairs a side. Speakers rise in order — 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 — each chair lighting as its speaker stands. The presenter names each job.
- Beat 2: The third affirmative rises; two icons appear: a shield breaking the other team's arrows (rebuttal), then a knot tying the team's own threads (summing up). The presenter: rebut your opponents — then sum up your own side.

## Sub lesson 2 — Speaking in a Debate
- Beat 1: A debater states a position; across the hall, the opposing speaker writes it down word for word before answering. The presenter: rebuttal starts with hearing the argument right.
- Beat 2: A transcript on screen; quotations light up one by one, each tagged with its speaker and side. The presenter: follow the quotes, and you can judge the debate.
