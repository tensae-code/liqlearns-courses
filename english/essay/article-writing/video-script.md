# Video script — Article writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

An article is a friendly expert, in print.

First — the headline. It tells the topic. It makes you look.
Then — the hook. The first line catches you.
A question you cannot answer yet!
"Do you really have to drive?"
Or a surprise. A strange, true thing.
Now you want to read. The hook worked.

Then the body. Paragraph by paragraph.
One point in each paragraph. In a good order.
Short, clear sentences. A voice like a person, not a machine.

Last — one closing line. It lands. It stays in the mind.

A fact file is smaller. It is all facts, in boxes.
A title. Headings. Short notes. One interesting fact in each box.
"Habitat. Food. Babies. Dangers."
You do not read a fact file. You search it.

Now you try.
Choose a topic you love. A headline. A hook. Three paragraphs.
That is an article — in English.
