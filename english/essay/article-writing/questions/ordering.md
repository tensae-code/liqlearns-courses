# Article writing — Ordering

Stand the items in their true order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the fact file's work: write short notes under the headings / choose the topic and title / test with three questions / fix the headed sections. **Answer: choose the topic and title — fix the headed sections — write short notes under the headings — test with three questions** — Egypt's rules: title, sections, notes — then the search test.

**2. (Level 2)** Order an article's parts: closing line / headline / body paragraphs / hook. **Answer: headline — hook — body paragraphs — closing line** — catch, pull, inform, land.
