# Article writing — Matching

Match each item to its match. One answer per item.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: headline of an article **Answer: the topic plus a reason to look — allowed more music than a news headline** — the topic must still be findable inside it.

**2. (Level 2)** Match: ordered body **Answer: one point per paragraph, in a felt order — by sides, or by the thing's own order** — Vietnam's articles order the body by its points.

**3. (Level 3)** Match: closing line that lands **Answer: the thought the reader carries out of the page** — summary closes the information; landing closes the experience.
