# Article writing — Short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Write a rhetorical-question hook for an article about reading books. **Answer: 'When did a screen last make you cry?' (any question asked for effect, aimed at the reader, earns it).** — the hook asks what the reader cannot yet answer (Malaysia, Form 5).

**2. (Level 2)** Your article's second paragraph has two points fighting. What do you do, and what is the rule called? **Answer: Split it — one point per paragraph is the body's law; the split is found by Malaysia's paragraph-labelling surgery.** — a paragraph that cannot be labelled with one job hides two points or none.

**3. (Level 3)** A fact file on the lion: write the Habitat section's short notes (notes, not sentences). **Answer: 'Africa; grasslands; lives in a group (a pride)' — short notes under the heading earn it.** — fact files write short notes and keep the language simple (Egypt's Tip).
