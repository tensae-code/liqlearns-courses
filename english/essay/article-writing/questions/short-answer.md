# Article writing — Short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — Article writing — the hook · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Article writing — the body and closing · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Article writing — the fact file.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a rhetorical-question hook for an article about reading books. **Answer: 'When did a screen last make you cry?' (any question asked for effect, aimed at the reader, earns it).** — the hook asks what the reader cannot yet answer (Malaysia, Form 5).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Your article's second paragraph has two points fighting. What do you do, and what is the rule called? **Answer: Split it — one point per paragraph is the body's law; the split is found by Malaysia's paragraph-labelling surgery.** — a paragraph that cannot be labelled with one job hides two points or none.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A fact file on the lion: write the Habitat section's short notes (notes, not sentences). **Answer: 'Africa; grasslands; lives in a group (a pride)' — short notes under the heading earn it.** — fact files write short notes and keep the language simple (Egypt's Tip).
