# Article writing — Fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — Article writing — the hook · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — Article writing — the fact file · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Article writing — the body and closing.

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The article's first two lines must carry the ______ — the question, surprise or promise that catches a free reader. **Answer: hook** — a hook on line nine is a rescue, not an opening.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** A fact file's animal sections run: Physical Description / Habitat / Adaptations / Diet / ______ Status. **Answer: Conservation** — Egypt's model fact file's headed sections.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's composition size for the magazine article is ______ words. **Answer: 150–170** — Over to You 10B's composition measure — hook, three points, a landing; nothing else fits.
