# Article writing — True or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** An article's body should run biggest-fact-first like a news report. **Answer: False** — that is the news body's law; the article orders its POINTS in a felt order and re-earns its free reader paragraph by paragraph.

**2. (Level 2)** Three rhetorical questions in a row make a hook three times as strong. **Answer: False** — one earns its place; three become a quiz. One to open, perhaps one to turn.

**3. (Level 3)** A fact file may use full flowing sentences everywhere instead of notes. **Answer: False** — Egypt's rules say short notes — the file is searched, not read, and notes serve the searcher.
