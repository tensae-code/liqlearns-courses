# Article writing — True or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 3 — Article writing — the body and closing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Article writing — the hook · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Article writing — the fact file.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** An article's body should run biggest-fact-first like a news report. **Answer: False** — that is the news body's law; the article orders its POINTS in a felt order and re-earns its free reader paragraph by paragraph.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Three rhetorical questions in a row make a hook three times as strong. **Answer: False** — one earns its place; three become a quiz. One to open, perhaps one to turn.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A fact file may use full flowing sentences everywhere instead of notes. **Answer: False** — Egypt's rules say short notes — the file is searched, not read, and notes serve the searcher.
