Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt defines a short article as a piece that —
a) gives information or ideas about a topic in a clear and simple way  b) proves a thesis  c) answers the five W's in one sentence **Answer: a)** — the Helpful Hints definition, Preparatory 9 Term 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** A fact file is built to be —
a) read once, top to bottom  b) searched — its headed sections are its index  c) memorised **Answer: b)** — Egypt's Writing Tip: title, sections, short notes, simple language, a picture.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Malaysia's Form 5 hook tools are —
a) long words and formality  b) a direct, less formal tone and rhetorical questions  c) capital letters **Answer: b)** — getting readers' attention, Unit 4 Writing, pp.52–53.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A rhetorical question is —
a) a question the reader must answer in writing  b) a question asked for effect, not for an answer  c) a question with no question mark **Answer: b)** — 'Do you really have to drive?' hooks; it does not examine.
