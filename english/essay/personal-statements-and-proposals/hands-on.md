# Hands-on — Personal statements and proposals

Two real writing tasks, with model frames.

## Task 1 — your personal statement
You are applying to a training college to study teaching
(change the course if you like — the shape holds). Frame:
P1 — the aim, plainly: the course, the college, and the
one-line reason it is yours.
P2 — the road: study and experience that led here, as a
joined story — not a list (dates live in your CV).
P3 — the proof of character: one real episode — a class
you helped, a group you led — told in four sentences.
P4 — the forward look: what you will bring and what you
hope to become.
Check it against the CV rule: if a sentence could be a CV
line unchanged, rewrite it until it says WHY.

## Task 2 — a short formal proposal
Propose one change to your school or workplace (a library
corner, a photography club — Jordan's model's own errand,
in your setting). Sections:
**Present situation** — how things are now, in facts.
**Why change is needed** — the gap, with its cost.
**What could be done** — the plan: steps, people, cost,
time.
**Request** — the one decision you ask for.
Length: about 130–180 words (Greece's taught size).
