# Personal statements and proposals — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Jordan's statement model is written by a
student named ______. **Answer: Mariam** — the Grade 10
book's model, with its 7-point tips box beside it.

**2. (Level 1)** Egypt's CV keeps the statement in
miniature: a personal statement/______ of 2–3 sentences.
**Answer: objective** — the seed the full statement grows
from.

**3. (Level 2)** The proposal's four cards in order: NOW,
NEED, ______, ASK. **Answer: PLAN** — what could be done
is the longest card; the ask is one sentence.

**4. (Level 3)** A decider grants a plan, not a ______.
**Answer: wish** — the proposal fails when its plan card
is empty.
