# Personal statements and proposals — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What does a statement say that a CV
cannot? **Answer: the WHY — choices, reasons and
direction joining the facts** — the CV lists; the
statement joins.

**2. (Level 2)** State the proposal's shape in four
words. **Answer: situation — need — plan — ask** —
Jordan's three sections with the request made explicit.

**3. (Level 3)** Repair this statement line: "2020: I
helped in the library. 2021: I helped again."
**Answer: (any joined version, e.g.) "I helped in the
library for two years because I wanted every pupil to
find books easily"** — dates shrink; the reason grows.
