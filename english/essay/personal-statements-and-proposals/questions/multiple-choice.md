# Personal statements and proposals — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A personal statement rides with — a) a
job advert b) a university application c) a complaint
**Answer: b)** — Jordan's Grade 10 model is part of a
university application (Unit 5, pp.56–57).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Against the CV, the statement — a) lists
more dates b) joins the facts with reasons into a road
c) repeats the CV in sentences **Answer: b)** — the CV
informs; the statement joins: because, so, which is why.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jordan's proposal sections run — a) ask,
plan, need, now b) current situation, why expansion is
needed, what could be done c) hobbies, skills, references
**Answer: b)** — the headteacher's model, in its order.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Greece's formal proposal is taught at —
a) 50 words b) 130–180 words c) 500 words **Answer: b)
130–180 words** — a counted formal piece, with register
teaching (Αγγλικά 2, Unit 6).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "I am very passionate and hardworking"
fails in a statement because — a) it is too long b) it
claims what only an episode can prove c) it is informal
**Answer: b)** — praise adjectives say nothing; one true
episode says it.
