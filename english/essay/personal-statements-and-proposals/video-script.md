# Video script — Personal statements and proposals

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A CV is a list. Short lines. Facts.

A personal statement is the same life — in sentences.
Not the list. The story of the list.
Why this school? Why this work?
What did you choose? What did you learn?
Where are you going?

Write it like you speak to a kind stranger.
True. Warm. In order.

And a proposal?
A proposal says: this is now.
This is the problem.
This is my plan.
Please say yes.

Person in sentences. Plan on the table.
Now you write both.
