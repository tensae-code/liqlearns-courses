# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plan, Draft, Revise, Publish
- Depth: the process named as the thing taught — plan, draft, revise, edit, publish — with texts written in a range of types for specific purposes and audiences, and word-processor page conventions (justifying text, hyphenating at line ends) (Samoa, *English Years 1–8 Primary School Curriculum*, printed pp.30–31); the same craft at senior level in the Year 11 study of the writing process (Samoa, *English Year 11 Book 2*, printed pp.66–72).

## Sub lesson 2 — Drafting Craft — the Writer's Tools
- Depth: the tools a teacher uses to build writers — guided writing, the writer's notebook, modelling writing in front of learners, writing frames, shared writing, talking as a way to plan, and word-processing redrafting (Samoa, *English Years 1–8 Primary School Curriculum*, printed pp.86–87).
