# Hands-on — The Writing Process

- **Two-version paragraph.** Draft a paragraph fast, then revise it with a different-coloured pen. Count your changes; the count is your revision score this week.
- **Notebook habit.** Keep a writer's notebook page for a week: starts, overheard lines, questions. Use one entry as the seed of a real draft.
- **Frame to free.** Write one paragraph inside a writing frame, then write the next paragraph of the same text without the frame.
