# The Writing Process — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 16, The Essay

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of five stepping stones crossing a stream, labelled PLAN, DRAFT, REVISE, EDIT, PUBLISH, with a learner halfway across carrying a notebook. *The learners read the stones in order and say which stone a writer is on when they cross out a sentence and write a better one.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a computer page with wide margins, even edges on both sides, and one long word split with a small dash at a line end. *The learners find the split word and say what the dash is doing.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a teacher writing on a big page while learners watch and call out ideas, the teacher's pen moving as they speak. *The learners say who is doing the thinking in this kind of writing lesson, and who is holding the pen.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a small notebook open on a desk with quick sketches, half sentences and arrows, next to a neat finished page. *The learners say which page is the writer's notebook and what it is for.*

## Sub lessons
1. Plan, Draft, Revise, Publish
2. Drafting Craft — the Writer's Tools

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
