# Video script — The Writing Process

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plan, Draft, Revise, Publish
- Beat 1: Five stepping stones appear across a stream: PLAN, DRAFT, REVISE, EDIT, PUBLISH. A learner crosses, notebook in hand.
- Beat 2: At REVISE, the learner stops, crosses out a sentence in the notebook and writes a better one. The presenter: revision is where writing gets good.
- Beat 3: At PUBLISH, a clean page: straight margins both sides, one word hyphenated at a line end. The presenter names both conventions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drafting Craft — the Writer's Tools
- Beat 1: A teacher writes on a big page while learners call out ideas — shared writing. The presenter: watch the decisions, not just the words.
- Beat 2: A messy notebook page of starts and arrows flips to a neat final page. The presenter: the notebook is where writing begins, long before the draft.
