# Matching — The Writing Process

Match each stage or tool to its job.

1. Plan (Sub lesson 1) — decide the kind of text, its purpose and its audience
2. Draft (Sub lesson 1) — get the ideas down in order
3. Revise (Sub lesson 1) — move, cut and add so the text says what you mean
4. Publish (Sub lesson 1) — present the finished text cleanly
5. Writing frame (Sub lesson 2) — a skeleton of a text type for a beginner to lean on
