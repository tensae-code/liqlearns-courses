# Multiple choice — The Writing Process

1. Which list gives the stages of the writing process in order? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Publish, draft, plan, revise, edit
   - B. Plan, draft, revise, edit, publish
   - C. Draft, publish, plan, edit, revise
   - D. Edit, plan, publish, draft, revise
   Answer: B

2. What is the job of the EDIT stage? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Choosing the topic
   - B. Getting ideas down in order
   - C. Fixing spelling, punctuation and grammar
   - D. Reading the text aloud to an audience
   Answer: C

3. In shared writing, who holds the pen while the class composes? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. The fastest writer
   - B. Nobody — it is all talk
   - C. The teacher, while learners watch the decisions being made
   - D. Each learner in turn
   Answer: C

4. What is a writer's notebook for? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Only finished, published pieces
   - B. Collecting ideas, starts and fragments before they are needed
   - C. Copying model texts neatly
   - D. Recording marks
   Answer: B
