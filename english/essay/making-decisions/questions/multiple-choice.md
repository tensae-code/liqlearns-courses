# Multiple choice questions — Making Decisions

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).
The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions —
no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Multiple choice

1. **Letting the moment pass so the choice is made for you is…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A snap decision
   - b) No decision
   - c) A responsible decision
   - **Answer: b) No decision**

2. **Choosing in a flash, without weighing anything, is…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A snap decision
   - b) No decision
   - c) A responsible decision
   - **Answer: a) A snap decision**

3. **A responsible decision is one you make…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) First, before anyone else
   - b) On purpose, after stopping to think and weigh
   - c) By asking a coin
   - **Answer: b) On purpose, after stopping to think and weigh**

4. **How many steps does Bryn's responsible decision-making have?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Three
   - b) Five
   - c) Ten
   - **Answer: b) Five**

5. **The class applies Bryn's steps to…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) The teacher's decision
   - b) A story character's decision
   - c) Their own last big decision
   - **Answer: c) Their own last big decision**
