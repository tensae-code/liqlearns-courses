## True or false

1. **A snap decision is always wrong.** (Sub lesson 1) — **False** (it is made without weighing; sometimes right, sometimes wreckage)
2. **“No decision” still leads somewhere — the choice gets made by time or by others.** (Sub lesson 1) — **True**
3. **Bryn's five steps are only for decisions at school.** (Sub lesson 2) — **False** (the class applies them to real life decisions)
4. **Checking where your own decision skipped a step is part of the book's task.** (Sub lesson 2) — **True**
