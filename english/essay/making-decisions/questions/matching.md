## Matching

Match each kind of decision to how it is made. (Sub lesson 1)
1. No decision → the moment passes and the choice is made for you
2. Snap decision → chosen in a flash, without weighing
3. Responsible decision → stopped, thought, weighed, chosen on purpose

Match each part of the method to its place. (Sub lesson 2)
1. Bryn → the learner whose five steps the book teaches
2. Five steps → the path of a responsible decision
3. The last big decision → what the class applies the steps to
