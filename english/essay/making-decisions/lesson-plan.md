# Lesson plan — Making Decisions

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Making Decisions
**Lesson:** making-decisions — the three kinds of decision and Bryn's five steps for deciding responsibly, from Uruguay's Grade 12 book
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 42 — Reading the World and Thinking (International Baccalaureate)

**The one thing:** tell a snap decision from a responsible one, and walk any real decision through five responsible steps.

**This lesson's own level:** built from two sub lessons that open in order.

## Why this lesson exists
Uruguay's Grade 12 English book opens its course with decisions: a real person's chain of decisions read closely, the three kinds named, and one learner's five steps applied to the class's own lives. The course held checking facts and finding information, but no lesson on deciding itself — so this lesson is built, from Uruguay's book alone.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of decision.** Uruguay's Grade 12 book reads how a Fulbright assistant's decisions brought her to Uruguay, then sorts decisions into three kinds: making no decision at all, the snap decision, and the responsible decision.
2. **Sub lesson 2 — The five steps of a responsible decision.** One learner in the book, Bryn, lays out five steps for deciding responsibly; the class applies the steps to the last big decision they made.

Sub lesson 2 opens only when the prove-it check on sub lesson 1 is passed.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed first. Checking facts online sits nearby — facts are a decision's raw material — and can be tried first as a soft hint, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
Sort any day's choices into the three kinds, and take one real decision through all five steps without skipping.

## What you need
Nothing to bring but honesty: the learner's last big decision is the practice material.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first.** The learner tries the opening task before any teaching.
2. ADD IMAGE: a picture of the lesson's opening scene, with one line under it telling the learner what to do.
3. **Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of decision.** A real decision story is read and the three kinds are sorted.
4. **Sub lesson 2 — The five steps of a responsible decision.** Bryn's five steps are walked through on the learner's own decision.
5. ADD VIDEO: a short video of the lesson's key moment, then the learner tries it again.
