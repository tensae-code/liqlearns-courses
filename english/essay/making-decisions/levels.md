# Levels — Making Decisions

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it names three kinds of decision, then teaches a five-step method for the responsible kind. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — Three kinds of decision | The three kinds told apart by how much thought went in. |
| 2 — The five steps of a responsible decision | Bryn's five steps, applied to a real decision. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
