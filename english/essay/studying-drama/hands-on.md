# Hands-on — Studying Drama

1. **The ground.** Together, build the ground card of a set play: playwright, time, and the older story it grew from — from your book's background pages, nothing invented.

2. **Kept, changed, invented.** Take one scene. In three columns, list what the playwright kept from the source, what they changed, and what they invented outright. Read the scene again with the columns beside you.
