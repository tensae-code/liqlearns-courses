# Levels — Studying Drama

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Where a play comes from | the drama-appreciation ground Fiji's Year 13 book teaches: the playwright, his time, and the sources of his play — Shakespeare and the stories behind *Hamlet* |
| Reading a play with its background | appreciation in practice: seeing the playwright's changes against the source while you read |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
