# Video script — Studying Drama

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[An old, thick storybook appears; above it, a stage with a prince.]

"This prince — Hamlet — did not fall from the sky! A man built his play, out of older stories."

[The mascot holds up two pages side by side.] "The old story says one thing. The play says another. That change? That is the playwright's choice."

"Study the ground first: who wrote it, when — and out of what? Then read the play with your eyes open."
