## True or false

1. A play's background has nothing to do with reading the play. (Sub lesson 1) **False — appreciation reads the play with its background awake.**

2. *Hamlet* grew from older stories about a Danish prince. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. A playwright only copies the source, exactly. (Sub lesson 2) **False — the playwright keeps, changes and invents; the changes are choices.**

4. The same background-first habit serves any set play. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
