## Multiple choice

1. Drama appreciation begins with… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) memorising the play  b) the play's background — the playwright and the sources it grew from  c) acting the last scene
   **Answer: b).**

2. Fiji's Year 13 book teaches the background of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Shakespeare and the sources of *Hamlet*  b) the school choir  c) a netball final
   **Answer: a).**

3. A play's sources are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) its page numbers  b) the older stories, histories or legends the playwright rebuilt for the stage  c) its ticket prices
   **Answer: b).**

4. Knowing a source helps you see the playwright's… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) handwriting  b) choices — what they kept, changed and invented  c) house
   **Answer: b).**

5. Reading with the background awake means holding in view… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) only the scene  b) the scene, the source behind it, and the playwright's hand between them  c) only the source
   **Answer: b).**
