## Short answer

1. What ground does drama appreciation lay before a play's scenes are analysed? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: who the playwright was and when they wrote, and which older stories the play grew from — for *Hamlet*, the older stories of the Danish prince.**

2. Why learn a play's sources at all? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: because the playwright rebuilt them — knowing the source lets you see what was kept, what was changed and what was invented.**

3. What changes in your reading when the background is awake? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: a scene stops being just itself — the playwright's change from the source becomes a choice you can weigh.**
