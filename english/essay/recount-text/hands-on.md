# Hands-on — Recount Text

Real memories are the practice material here.

## Three-photo recount

Choose three photos from one day (or draw three pictures). Write the recount they tell: orientation under the first, events under the second, closing under the third.

## Shuffle and restore

Write your recount's event sentences on separate strips. Shuffle them. A partner restores the true order — using only the sequence words and the sense of time — and reads the rebuilt recount aloud.
