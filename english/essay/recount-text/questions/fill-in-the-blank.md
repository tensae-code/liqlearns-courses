## Fill in the blank

1. A ___ retells something that already happened. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: recount** — the kind's name.

2. A recount is written in the ___ tense. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: past** — happened events take past verbs.

3. The ___ tells who, where and when. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: orientation** — the recount's first part.

4. Events go in ___ order — the order they happened. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: time** — no jumping around.

5. The last part, the ___, says how it ended. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: closing** — ending + feeling.

6. "___ we went home. Then we ate. Finally we slept." — sequence words hold the middle. (use: First) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: First** — first/then/finally carry the event string.
