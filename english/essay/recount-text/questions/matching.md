## Matching

Match the sentence to its recount part. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. Last Sunday, my family went to my grandmother's village. — a
2. First, we helped her pick mangoes. — b
3. Then, we cooked lunch together. — c
4. After that, we played in the river. — d
5. Finally, we drove home as the sun went down. — e
6. I will never forget that day. — f

a) orientation
b) event 1
c) event 2
d) event 3
e) last event
f) closing

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (who/where/when opens), 2–b (First starts the events), 3–c (Then continues), 4–d (After that continues), 5–e (Finally marks the last event), 6–f (feeling closes the recount).
