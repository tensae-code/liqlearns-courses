## Multiple choice

1. A recount is a text that… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) gives instructions  b) retells past events in order  c) argues a side
   **Answer: b)** — a recount tells what happened.

2. A recount's events are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) invented for fun  b) real things that happened  c) future plans
   **Answer: b)** — personal and sports recounts both retell real events.

3. The first part of a recount is the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) closing  b) orientation  c) longest event
   **Answer: b) orientation** — who, where, when come first.

4. The middle of a recount holds… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) events in time order  b) events in any order  c) only feelings
   **Answer: a)** — time order is the recount's string.

5. The closing of a recount… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) opens a new event  b) ends with how it finished and felt  c) lists the words to learn
   **Answer: b)** — the closing lands the telling.

6. A recount runs on… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) past-tense verbs and sequence words  b) commands  c) questions only
   **Answer: a)** — past verbs carry the events; first/then/finally order them.

7. A sports recount's orientation could be… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Last Saturday, our school team played in the city stadium."  b) "First, boil the water."  c) "I think football is best."
   **Answer: a)** — who, where, when: the match set up.
