## Short answer

1. Name a true event you could recount. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "Our trip to the market last week."** (any real past event) — recounts retell real events.

2. Is a recipe a recount? Why? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "No — a recipe instructs; a recount retells what happened."** — kinds differ by their job.

3. Write an orientation sentence for a recount about a school trip. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Last Friday, our class visited the museum in the city."** — who, where, when.

4. Put in order: "Finally we went home. / First we caught the bus. / Then we saw the animals." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: First we caught the bus. Then we saw the animals. Finally we went home.** — time order restored.

5. Write a closing sentence for that recount. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "We arrived home tired but happy, and I will never forget the lions."** — ending + feeling.
