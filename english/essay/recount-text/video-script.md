# Video script — Recount Text

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[Three photos appear in a row.] "A RECOUNT tells what HAPPENED. Real events — in the order they came!"

[The first photo glows.] "First, ORIENTATION: who, where, when. Put the reader there!"

[Beads slide onto a string.] "Then the EVENTS — first, then, after that — like beads on a string. No jumping!"

[The last photo glows; the mascot yawns happily.] "Last, the CLOSING: how it ended, how you felt. Tell YOUR day!"
