# Speech writing — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "Tell them what you told them" —
**Answer: the conclusion** — Ghana's third part: the
promise kept, in fresh words.

**2. (Level 1)** "Teachers, parents, my friends — good
morning" — **Answer: the vocative address** — the
audience named before anything is asked of them.

**3. (Level 2)** A speech for or against electric cars —
**Answer: Egypt's for/against writing kind** — Secondary
11's set speech, argued from both sides.

**4. (Level 3)** Ethos — **Answer: the audience trusts
the speaker** — Greece's first appeal; who you are is
part of the argument.

**5. (Level 3)** "I have a dream" said again and again —
**Answer: repetition with powerful imagery** — Ghana
B9's device list, in the speech Greece sets beside
Mandela's.
