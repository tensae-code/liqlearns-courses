# Speech writing — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speech in Three Parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Ordered Points and the For/Against Speech · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speech Rhetoric.

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Tell what you are going to tell them.
Tell them. Tell them what you ______ them."
**Answer: told** — Ghana's rule, third part: the
conclusion returns to the promise.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The opening that names the audience —
"Teachers, parents, my friends" — is the ______
address. **Answer: vocative** — the speech's handshake.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt's writing kind is a speech ______
or against an argument. **Answer: for** — both sides
heard before the speaker's side is taken.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Saying a thing in three parallel beats —
"your country, my country, our future" — is called
______. **Answer: tripling** — Ghana B9's name; Greece's
table calls the same craft the rule of three.
