# Speech writing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speech in Three Parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Ordered Points and the For/Against Speech · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speech Rhetoric.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A speech can leave its order loose,
because listeners can re-read the hard parts.
**Answer: False** — a speech is heard once; that is why
Ghana's rule promises, delivers and repeats.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A for/against speech is stronger when
the other side is never mentioned. **Answer: False** —
the fair giving of both sides is what makes the chosen
side believed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inclusive "we" is one of Ghana B9's
speech devices. **Answer: True** — it puts the audience
inside the sentence, standing with the speaker.
