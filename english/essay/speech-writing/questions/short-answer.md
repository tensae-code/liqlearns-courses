# Speech writing — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speech in Three Parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Ordered Points and the For/Against Speech · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speech Rhetoric.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the speech's three parts with
their jobs. **Answer: introduction — tell what you are
going to tell them; body — tell them; conclusion — tell
them what you told them** — Ghana B8's printed rule.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your points are ready but unordered.
What must the plan do before drafting? **Answer: number
the points in a felt order and open each with its order
word (First / Second / Third)** — a listener hearing
once must be able to follow the road.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A speaker says: "I have taught in this
school for twenty years, and here are the examination
figures." Sort the two appeals. **Answer: ethos (the
twenty years — trust the speaker) and logos (the figures
— the true, ordered argument)** — Greece's table.
