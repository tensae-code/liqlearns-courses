# Hands-on — Speech writing

A real speaking task — write it, then SAY it aloud.

## Task 1 — the three-part speech (2 minutes)
Topic: one change you want at your school or in your town.
Frame:
- ADDRESS: "Teachers, parents, my friends — good morning."
  (Name YOUR real audience.)
- PROMISE (Part 1): "Today I will tell you three things
  about ___: first ___, second ___, third ___."
- POINTS (Part 2): one short paragraph per point. Each
  point opens with its number word — First / Second /
  Third — and holds ONE idea with one example.
- RETURN (Part 3): "So I have told you: ___, ___ and ___.
  " + ONE closing line built as a triple: "For our
  classes, for our teachers, for our future — let us
  begin today."
Rules: no sentence longer than you can say in one breath;
read the whole speech aloud twice before you call it done.

## Task 2 — the for/against speech (Egypt's kind)
Motion: "School should start later in the morning."
Write BOTH halves, fairly: three lines FOR, three lines
AGAINST — then your own side last, answering the strongest
line against you. Deliver it to a companion; they must be
able to say back which side you took and why.
