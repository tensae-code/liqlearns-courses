# Video script — Speech writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A speech is writing for the EAR.
Nobody can turn the page back. They hear it ONCE.

So a speech has three parts.
Ghana's rule says it best:
Tell them what you are going to tell them.
Tell them.
Tell them what you told them.

First — speak TO your people.
"Teachers, parents, my friends — good morning."
That is the address. Now they are listening.

Then your points. One. Two. Three.
In an order the ear can follow.

For or against? Give BOTH sides.
Fair first. Then your answer. That is strong.

And use the old tools.
Say it in THREES: "our school, our work, our future!"
Repeat the line that matters.
Say "we" — and the room stands with you.

Ethos: they trust YOU.
Logos: your facts are true.
Pathos: their hearts feel it.
Three ropes. One speech.

End with a line they carry home.
Now — stand up. Your turn.
