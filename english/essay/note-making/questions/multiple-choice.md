# Note-making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A good note is — a) a short full
sentence b) a phrase, not a sentence, with no full verbs
c) the passage copied neatly **Answer: b)** — India's
rules, stated in full in Hornbill W1.

**2. (Level 1)** India's second step is — a) numbering
b) question-and-answer the passage — What? Where? How
many? c) writing the title **Answer: b)** — the questions
split the text into its points before any are written.

**3. (Level 2)** The decimal alternative to 1 / (i) /
(a) numbering is — a) A, B, C b) 1.1, 1.1.1 c) Roman
numerals only **Answer: b)** — Hornbill names both
systems; either may carry the notes.

**4. (Level 2)** "RSVP" in a note means — a) reply very
soon, please — please reply b) rest soon, very pleased
c) return the signed paper **Answer: a)** — Uzbekistan's
standard set: please reply.

**5. (Level 3)** A sub-title is — a) the section's main
idea, at a glance b) a decoration c) the author's name
**Answer: a)** — India's W3: it also breaks the monotony
of long passages.
