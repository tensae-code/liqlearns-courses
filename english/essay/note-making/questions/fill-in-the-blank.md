# Note-making — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Step one of India's five: ______ the
load-bearing parts of the passage. **Answer: underline**
— then question the text, then write points.

**2. (Level 2)** "N.B." before a line in a note means
______ well — look at this. **Answer: note** — from
Uzbekistan's standard set.

**3. (Level 2)** Sub-sections under a main point are
numbered (i), (ii), ______ in India's first system.
**Answer: (iii)** — main sections 1, 2, 3; sub-sections
in Roman brackets; sub-sub-sections (a), (b), (c).

**4. (Level 3)** Sub-titles break the ______ of long
passages. **Answer: monotony** — India's W3 reason, with
the main-idea-at-a-glance definition.
