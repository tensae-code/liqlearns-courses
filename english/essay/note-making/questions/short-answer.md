# Note-making — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Cut to note form: "A colony of bees may
contain as many as fifty thousand bees, and it is ruled
by a single queen." **Answer: colony — up to 50,000
bees — one queen** — numbers and facts kept; verbs,
articles and joining words dropped.

**2. (Level 2)** Your notes use main points 1 and 2,
with two sub-points under 2. Write the decimal form of
the second sub-point. **Answer: 2.2** — the decimal
system: main number, dot, sub number (India, W1).

**3. (Level 3)** Divide "Bees" into three sections and
head them. **Answer: (any three main-idea headings,
e.g.) The Colony and Its Queen / The Workers' Year /
Winter in the Hive — each stating its section's main
idea at a glance, W3's test.**
