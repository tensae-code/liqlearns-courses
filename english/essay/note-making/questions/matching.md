# Note-making — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "The farmers plant rice in May because
the rains come in June" cut to notes — **Answer: "rice —
planted May — rains June"** — facts kept, scaffolding
(the, because, full verbs) dropped.

**2. (Level 2)** ASAP — **Answer: as soon as possible**
— Uzbekistan's standard abbreviations, learned as whole
signs.

**3. (Level 2)** c/o — **Answer: care of** — the note or
letter goes through another person's address.

**4. (Level 2)** THX, W8, L8R — **Answer: Kuwait's phone
text-message forms** — the note form at its fastest,
decoded and written in the Messages unit.

**5. (Level 3)** "Urban Decay" — **Answer: India's
printed example of a sub-title** — the one heading given
in W3's newspaper article; the learner heads the rest.
