# Hands-on — Note-making

A real note-making build, on a real passage.

## The passage (invented for this lesson)
"Bees live in large families called colonies. A colony
may hold fifty thousand bees, ruled by one queen. Worker
bees build the wax combs, gather nectar and pollen from
flowers, and guard the hive. In winter the colony eats
the honey it stored in summer, and the bees cluster
together to keep the queen warm. A single colony can
make thirty kilos of honey in a good year."

## Task 1 — India's five steps
1. Read and UNDERLINE the load-bearing words.
2. Question the passage: What? How many? Who does what?
   When? How much?
3. Write the points in note form (no full sentences):
   frame —
   1. Colony
      (i) large family, up to ___ bees
      (ii) one ___
   2. Workers …
   3. Winter …
4. Number and group; then the re-read test: cover the
   passage and rebuild its meaning from your notes alone.

## Task 2 — the note and the abbreviations
Cut this message to a note under 15 words, using two
standard abbreviations correctly: "Please reply as soon
as possible. Note well: the meeting is at the office of
Mr Osei, for example the small office by the gate."
Then write sub-titles for the bee passage's three parts
(Colony / Workers / Winter are the giveaway — find
sharper ones).
