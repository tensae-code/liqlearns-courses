# Video script — Note-making

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A page is long. Your memory is small.
So we make NOTES.

A note is not a small sentence.
It is a LABEL for an idea.

The birds of the forest live in the tall trees
and they eat the seeds of the fruit.
The note says: forest birds — tall trees — eat fruit seeds.
The verbs fell out. The little words fell out.
The MEANING stayed.

India's way:
Underline. Ask the page questions. What? Where? How many?
Write the points. Short. No full verbs.
Number them. Group them.
1 — 2 — 3. Or 1.1, 1.1.1.

Short forms help.
ASAP — as soon as possible!
N.B. — note well! RSVP — please reply!
e.g. — for example. c/o — care of.

And a long page? Give each part a NAME.
A sub-title. The idea, at a glance.

Last test: read your notes TOMORROW.
Still clear? Good notes.
Dark? Write them again.
