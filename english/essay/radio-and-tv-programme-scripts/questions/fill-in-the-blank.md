## Fill in the blank

1. A radio script's instruction part is called the technical script, or the production ______. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: code**

2. A very short burst of music that marks a join in a programme is a ______. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: sting**

3. The broadcasting home that sends a programme out is its ______. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: station**

4. Background sound recorded where a scene really happens is a ______ track. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: wild**

5. In a TV script, the column for everything heard is ______. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: AUDIO**

6. In a TV script, the column that says who speaks or appears is ______. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: WHO**
