## True or false

1. A radio script only needs the words to be spoken; the sounds can be decided later. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the technical script (production code) plans the sounds and their order in writing.

2. The sample radio programme script in South Sudan's Secondary 2 book is a Dinka community programme on schooling. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. Interviews and research are part of radio production. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

4. A TV script has two columns: words and pictures. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it has three: WHO, AUDIO, VIDEO.

5. In the TV script, the VIDEO column holds everything the audience hears. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — VIDEO holds everything seen; AUDIO holds everything heard.
