## Short answer

1. What is a cue in a radio programme? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: the signal or opening words and sounds that start a programme or an item.**

2. The book's sample radio programme script is a community programme in which language, about what? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: a Dinka community programme, about schooling.**

3. Name the four radio production techniques the book teaches. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: sounds, wild tracks, interviews, and research.**

4. What does research do for a programme before anyone speaks? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: it makes the programme's facts stand — the content is checked and gathered first.**

5. Name the three columns of the TV script format. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: WHO, AUDIO, VIDEO.**

6. If you read down the VIDEO column alone, what do you get? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: the whole programme playing silently — everything seen, nothing heard.**
