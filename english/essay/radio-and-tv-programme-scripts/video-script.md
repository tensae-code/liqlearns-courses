# Video script — Radio and TV Programme Scripts

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A radio glows; no picture can be seen.] "Radio! We HEAR it.
We cannot SEE it. So the script must say EVERY sound!"

[A script page fills in.] "The CUE starts us. The STING is a
short sound. The STATION is our home on the air."

[A page with three columns.] "Television: WHO speaks…
AUDIO — all we hear… VIDEO — all we see."

[The mascot holds up a finished script.] "Write it first.
Then the whole world can hear it — and see it!"
