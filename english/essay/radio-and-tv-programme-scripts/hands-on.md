# Hands-on — Radio and TV Programme Scripts

## One minute of radio

Write a one-minute radio programme for your class: an opening
cue, a short sting, one interview (two questions), and one
wild track — a real sound from around you, recorded or made
live. Read the script aloud exactly as written; if a listener
who cannot see you understands everything, the script works.

## The same minute on TV

Re-lay your radio minute as a TV script in three columns:
WHO, AUDIO, VIDEO. For every line, decide what the camera
shows while the line is heard. Notice what the radio script
had to say in words that the TV script can simply show.
