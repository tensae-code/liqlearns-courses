# Essay kinds — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** Expository essays explain; in Sierra
Leone's family they include how-to/process,
compare-contrast and cause-effect. **Answer: True** —
SSS2 Lessons 45–46 name the family outright.

**2. (Level 2)** Copying the task's sentence as your
introduction shows you understood it. **Answer: False**
— Malaysia F5: state your opinion WITHOUT copying the
task; retyping is the mark of a thought not yet had.

**3. (Level 3)** An argumentative essay is weakened by
stating the other side fairly. **Answer: False** —
Bolivia's structure explores both sides of a debate, and
the fairly-stated counter-argument, answered, is the
essay's strongest paragraph.
