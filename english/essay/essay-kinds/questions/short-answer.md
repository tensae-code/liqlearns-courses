# Essay kinds — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Sort to kinds: (a) "My town's market at
dawn" (b) "Why the wells ran dry, and what followed"
(c) "Two schools compared". **Answer: (a) descriptive
(b) cause-effect (c) compare-contrast** — name the
reader-job and the kind follows.

**2. (Level 2)** Your conclusion introduces a brand-new
argument. Repair it in one move. **Answer: cut the new
argument (or move it to the body plan); close with
summary plus a closing comment the body has earned** —
Malaysia F5's conclusion rule.

**3. (Level 3)** State the difference between analytical
and hortatory exposition in one line each.
**Answer: analytical persuades that something IS the
case (thesis – arguments – reiteration); hortatory
persuades that something SHOULD be done and closes in a
recommendation** — Indonesia, Grade 11.
