# Essay kinds — matching

Match each kind to its reader-job.

**1. (Level 1)** Descriptive — **Answer: the reader must
SEE it** — Sierra Leone's own five-paragraph lesson kind.

**2. (Level 1)** Hortatory exposition — **Answer: it
SHOULD be so, closing in a recommendation** —
Indonesia's persuasive machine, twin of the analytical.

**3. (Level 1)** Discussion text — **Answer: Issue –
Arguments for – Arguments against –
Conclusion/Recommendation** — Indonesia, Life Today,
Unit 4's printed structure.

**4. (Level 2)** Proportion — **Answer: India's check
that the parts are sized as their importance deserves**
— one of Hornbill's four: Unity, Coherence, Relevance,
Proportion.

**5. (Level 3)** Problem-and-solution in a kind list —
**Answer: one of the essay types Syria's "What is an
Essay" names** — with interpretive, analytical,
argumentative, comparative and cause-and-effect.
