# Essay kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "Discuss the advantages and
disadvantages of homework" asks for — a) an opinion
essay b) both pans of the scale loaded honestly before
any verdict c) a description **Answer: b)** — the
advantages-disadvantages kind (Malaysia, Form 4's model
kind and set topic).

**2. (Level 1)** Indonesia's analytical exposition runs
— a) Issue – For – Against – Recommendation b) Thesis –
Arguments – Reiteration c) Hook – Joke – End
**Answer: b)** — it persuades that something IS the case.

**3. (Level 2)** A strong introduction (Malaysia F5) —
a) copies the task's words exactly b) states your
opinion without copying the task c) tells a long story
**Answer: b)** — the task retyped is a thought not yet
had.

**4. (Level 2)** A good conclusion (Malaysia F5) holds —
a) a new argument b) a summary plus a closing comment
c) the introduction repeated word for word
**Answer: b)** — summary, then the earned last word.

**5. (Level 3)** The counter-argument paragraph's job is
— a) to defeat your own essay b) to state the other side
at its best, then answer it c) to fill space
**Answer: b)** — Indonesia's discussion text builds the
against-arguments into the structure itself.
