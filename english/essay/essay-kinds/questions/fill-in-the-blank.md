# Essay kinds — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Essay Kinds Map · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Essay Introductions and Conclusions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Counter-Argument.

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Analytical exposition: Thesis –
Arguments – ______. **Answer: Reiteration** — the thesis
said again at the end, Indonesia's frame.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The essay that weighs both sides before
a verdict is the ______-disadvantages essay.
**Answer: advantages** — Malaysia Form 4's modelled kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** India's four characteristics: Unity,
Coherence, ______, Proportion. **Answer: Relevance** —
everything in the essay must serve the task set.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In a discussion text, the decision
comes in the final part: Conclusion/______.
**Answer: Recommendation** — both sides first, the
verdict last (Indonesia, Grade 12).
