# Hands-on — Essay kinds

A real writing task — one topic, three kinds.

## The topic
Homework. (Every country in this lesson's sources argues
about it; now it is yours.)

## Task 1 — the kind-pick drill
Write ONLY the introduction (3–4 sentences) for each of
these three set tasks. Frame for each: hook or scene in
one sentence → the question named → your position stated
in your own words (never copied from the task):
(a) OPINION: "Homework should be abolished. Give your
    opinion."
(b) ADVANTAGES-DISADVANTAGES: "Discuss the advantages
    and disadvantages of homework."
(c) PROBLEM-SOLUTION: "Many pupils cannot finish their
    homework. Suggest solutions."
Feel how the SAME first paragraph changes its promise
with the kind.

## Task 2 — one full essay with a counter-argument
Build task (a) whole, on the five-paragraph frame you
already own. Paragraph 4 is reserved: THE OTHER SIDE,
stated fairly ("Teachers answer: homework is practice,
and practice cannot be done in class time…") — then your
answer to it ("But practice can be shorter, and chosen…").
Conclusion: summary + one closing comment. Before you
write, mark the plan with India's four checks: Unity
(one topic), Coherence (it hangs together), Relevance
(every paragraph serves the task), Proportion (the sides
sized honestly).
