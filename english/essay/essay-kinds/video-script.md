# Video script — Essay kinds

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

You know the essay's shape.
Now — the essay has KINDS. Like tools in one box.

ARGUMENTATIVE: two sides fight; you judge.
OPINION: your view, with your reasons.
PERSUASIVE: the reader must ACT. "You should…!"
DESCRIPTIVE: the reader must SEE it.
EXPOSITORY: the reader must UNDERSTAND it.
COMPARE-CONTRAST: two things, side by side.
CAUSE-EFFECT: this happened — so that happened.
PROBLEM-SOLUTION: here is the trouble; here is the fix.
ADVANTAGES-DISADVANTAGES: weigh both pans of the scale.

Indonesia gives three frames names.
Analytical: it IS so — thesis, arguments, say it again.
Hortatory: it SHOULD be so — end with: do this!
Discussion: the issue, for, against — then decide.

Every kind opens and closes the same way.
Introduction: your position, in YOUR words.
Conclusion: summarise — and one last comment.

And the strong writer plants the OTHER side…
then answers it.

Read the task. Name the kind. THEN write.
