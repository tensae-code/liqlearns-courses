# Lesson Plan — The Photo Essay

**One thing:** the photo essay — telling about your community in photographs, each with a caption that describes the scene and says why it matters, assembled in order like an essay.

## Sub lessons

1. Photographs and captions
2. Assembling the photo essay

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A photo essay is an essay whose body is photographs: the pictures carry the telling, and the words work under them (Grades 10–12 guide, Place-Conscious Learning unit, printed pp.139–140).
- The photographs are of your own community — the places and scenes you can stand in front of (printed pp.139–140).
- Every photograph carries a caption, and the caption has two jobs: describe the scene, and say its importance — why this place or moment matters (printed pp.139–140).
- The captioned photographs are assembled in an order, like an essay's paragraphs: an opening that sets the place, middle photographs that build, and a close that lands the whole message (printed pp.139–140).
