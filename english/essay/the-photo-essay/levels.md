# Levels — The Photo Essay

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Photographs and captions | Choose photographs of their own community that each add something, and write a caption for each that describes the scene and says its importance. |
| 2. Assembling the photo essay | Choose the best photographs, order them — set the place, build, land the message — and check that the captions in order carry one whole message. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can make a photo essay that shows an outsider their community and what matters in it.
