# Video Script — The Photo Essay

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each photograph is held up and pointed at as it is named.

[Picture: five photographs laid out in a row on a table]

This is a photo essay. It is an essay, but the body is photographs. The pictures tell the story.

[Picture: one photograph of a village well, with words under it]

Under every photograph, we write a caption. The caption says what the picture shows. And it says why the place matters. Two jobs!

[Picture: the five photographs being put in order, first to last]

Then we put the photographs in order. First the place. Then more and more. Last, the picture we remember. Now the whole essay speaks!
