(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A caption only names what is in the photograph. (Sub lesson 1) — False
2. The photographs of a photo essay here are photographs of your own community. (Sub lesson 1) — True
3. A caption describes the scene and says its importance. (Sub lesson 1) — True
4. The order of the photographs in a photo essay does not matter. (Sub lesson 2) — False
