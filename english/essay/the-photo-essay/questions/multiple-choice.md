# Multiple Choice — The Photo Essay

You are making a photo essay about the oldest tree in your neighbourhood.

1. A photo essay tells its story mainly with… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) long paragraphs
   b) photographs and their captions ✓
   c) drawings only
   d) songs

2. A caption has two jobs. They are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the date and the photographer's name
   b) describing the scene and saying its importance ✓
   c) a joke and a question
   d) the title and the page number

3. The photographs in this photo essay are of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) your own community ✓
   b) places you have never seen
   c) pictures copied from a magazine
   d) blank walls

4. When you assemble the essay, the photographs' order should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) be any order at all
   b) hide the strongest photograph in the middle
   c) carry the reader from part to part, like an essay ✓
   d) repeat the first photograph at the end

5. A photo essay is like a written essay because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) it has many pages
   b) its parts build one whole message ✓
   c) it must be long
   d) it has a title page

6. The last photograph of the essay should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) be the weakest one
   b) show a new place with no caption
   c) land the whole message — the one the reader remembers ✓
