# Fill in the blank — Advantages and Disadvantages

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. One _____ of living in a village is the clean air. (advantage / drawback)
   **Answer: advantage** (Sub lesson 1)

2. On the one hand, buses are cheap. On the _____ hand, they are slow.
   **Answer: other** (Sub lesson 1)

3. In a balanced piece, the _____ paragraph honestly gives the bad points.
   **Answer: bad points** (the disadvantages paragraph) (Sub lesson 2)

4. A piece that hides the bad points is an _____, not a discussion.
   **Answer: advertisement** (Sub lesson 2)
