# Multiple choice — Advantages and Disadvantages

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Which word names a BAD point?
   A) benefit
   B) advantage
   C) drawback
   D) plus
   **Answer: C** (Sub lesson 1)

2. "On the one hand … on the other hand" is used to:
   A) open a letter
   B) show the two sides of a thing
   C) end a story
   D) ask a question
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. Which is an advantage sentence?
   A) Cycling is slow in the rain.
   B) One advantage of cycling is that it is cheap.
   C) Cycling, cycling, cycling.
   D) Do you like cycling?
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

4. What comes LAST in a balanced piece?
   A) the opening
   B) the good points
   C) your weighed-up closing view
   D) the title
   **Answer: C** (Sub lesson 2)

5. A balanced piece must:
   A) hide the bad points
   B) give both sides fairly
   C) have only one paragraph
   D) use no reasons
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. Which order is right?
   A) closing view, opening, bad points, good points
   B) opening, good points, bad points, closing view
   C) good points, closing view, opening, bad points
   D) bad points only
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
