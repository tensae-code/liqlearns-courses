# Video script — Summarising

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A summary is a text, made SMALL and made YOURS.

Two moves. India names them.
One — SELECT. Keep the main points. Drop the examples.
Two — PARAPHRASE. Say it in YOUR words.
Full sentences. Joined up nicely.
NOT the writer's sentences, carried over.

How small? The rule of thumb:
about ONE THIRD of the first text.
Three hundred words? Your summary: one hundred.

Sudan gives a harder game.
Thirty words. No more.
A worked answer shows what is kept — and what is left out.

And stories? Use the five beats.
Someone — Wanted — So — But — Then.
Someone wanted a thing. So they tried.
But trouble came. Then — the end.

Too long? Climb down the ladder.
80 words. 50. 30. 20.
At every rung, the main points still stand.
The examples fall away.

Close the book. Say it YOUR way. Write THAT.
