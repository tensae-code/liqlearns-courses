# Summarising — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Selecting and Paraphrasing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Summarising to a Set Length · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Shrinking Ladder and the Story Frame.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A summary may copy the original's best
sentences if they are joined neatly. **Answer: False**
— copying is carrying, not summarising; India's second
move is paraphrase, your own words.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the shrinking ladder, a rung that
drops a main point has still succeeded if it is short
enough. **Answer: False** — every rung keeps every main
point; what falls is examples and decoration. Dropping a
point is amputation, not summary.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A story summary and a facts list of the
story are the same thing. **Answer: False** — the story
summary keeps the spine (Someone–Wanted–So–But–Then);
a facts list has no wanting and no trouble in it.
