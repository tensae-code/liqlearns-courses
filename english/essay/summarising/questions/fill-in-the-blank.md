# Summarising — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Summarising = selection + ______.
**Answer: paraphrase** — India's two moves (Hornbill W2).

**2. (Level 2)** Sudan's four rules: main idea, own
words, leave out details, about one ______ of the
passage. **Answer: third** — SPINE 5, Chapter Eight,
agreeing with India's rule of thumb.

**3. (Level 2)** South Sudan, Primary 5: "To summarise
is to ______." **Answer: tell** — tell what it is about:
the people, where, beginning, middle, end.

**4. (Level 3)** SWBST: Someone – ______ – So – But –
Then. **Answer: Wanted** — the goal that drives the
story's spine (Malaysia, Year 6).
