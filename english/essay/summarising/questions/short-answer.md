# Summarising — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** A 300-word passage, by the rule of
thumb, summarises to about how many words?
**Answer: about 100** — one-third (India; Sudan's rules
agree).

**2. (Level 2)** Summarise in one sentence, own words:
"The trader's bicycle was put together in his shop
while a crowd watched, and within five years every
teacher owned one because it was cheap and cut the walk
to school from two hours to twenty minutes."
**Answer: (any own-words version keeping both points,
e.g.) "Bicycles spread fast because they were cheap and
cut the two-hour walk to school to twenty minutes."**
— the crowd and the shop are detail; cheapness and time
are the points.

**3. (Level 3)** Run SWBST on the boy-who-cried-wolf
story in five beats. **Answer: Someone — a shepherd boy;
Wanted — amusement / attention; So — he cried "Wolf!"
when no wolf came; But — when the wolf truly came,
nobody believed him; Then — the wolf took the sheep** —
Malaysia's frame, the spine whole.
