## Fill in the blank

1. An explanation text takes the reader through a chain of ______, link by link. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: causes.**

2. Explanations speak in the timeless ______ tense, because the process always works the same way. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: present.**

3. "What Causes ______?" is Fiji's Year 6 explanation of road accidents — speed, drink-driving, carelessness, old cars. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: Accidents.**

4. When you write an explanation, lay the chain out with no step ______ and no step out of place. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: skipped (also right: missing).**
