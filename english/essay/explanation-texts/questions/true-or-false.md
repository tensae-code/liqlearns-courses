## True or false

1. An explanation text only tells what a thing is. (Sub lesson 1) **False — that is a report's job; the explanation tells how it works or why it happens.**

2. The links of an explanation can stand in any order. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the chain must follow the order of the process itself.**

3. Fiji's books teach explanation texts from Year 3 to Year 8. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. "The Importance of Trees" is one of Fiji's explanation texts. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
