## Multiple choice

1. An explanation text answers… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) who and when  b) how or why  c) how much
   **Answer: b).**

2. An explanation moves in the order of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the alphabet  b) the process itself, cause by cause  c) the author's feelings
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which of these did Fiji's Year 3 book explain? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) how cell phones work  b) how to bake bread  c) the life of a poet
   **Answer: a).**

4. In Fiji's Year 6 book, "Electric Circuits" is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a poem  b) an explanation text — how a circuit, battery, switch and filament work  c) a recount
   **Answer: b).**

5. A report and an explanation differ because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the report describes the thing; the explanation runs its machinery — how it works or why it happens  b) reports are longer  c) explanations use pictures only
   **Answer: a).**
