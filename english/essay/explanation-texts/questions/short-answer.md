## Short answer

1. What does an explanation text do? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: it explains how something works or why something happens, moving through the chain of causes in the order of the process itself.**

2. How is an explanation different from a procedure? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a procedure tells how to DO a thing, step by step; an explanation tells how a thing WORKS or why it happens — nobody performs it.**

3. Name three explanation texts from Fiji's Year 6 and Year 8 books. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any three of: "What Causes Accidents?", "The Water Cycle", "Electric Circuits" (Year 6); "Flood and Droughts", "Space Exploration" (Year 8).**
