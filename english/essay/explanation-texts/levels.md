# Levels — Explanation Texts

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| What an explanation text does | the type itself — answering how or why through a chain of causes in the timeless present — from Fiji's Years 3, 4 and 5 explanation units |
| Explanations of how the world works | the full causal-chain explanations of Fiji's Year 6 and Year 8 books (accidents, the water cycle, circuits, floods and droughts, space) and writing one of your own |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
