# Video script — Explanation Texts

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A phone rings on the far side of a drawn sea, and the sound travels across as a line of stepping stones.]

"How does your voice cross the sea? Link by link! This happens — so this happens — so this happens."

[Raindrops fall, run downhill, and join a river, one step at a time.] "An explanation text follows the chain. It answers HOW, and WHY — in order, with no link missing."

"A report tells you what a thing is. An explanation runs its machinery."
