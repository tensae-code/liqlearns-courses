# Video script — Advertisements and posters

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

An advert is a shout — a kind shout, a true shout.

Three parts. Very few words.
One — the claim. The big promise. "The phone that never sleeps."
Two — the benefits. Why is it good FOR YOU?
Strong. Fast. Cheap. Bright. Words that sell.
Three — the call. Tell them what to do!
"Buy today!" "Come and see!" "Try it now!"

Who are you talking to? Children? Mothers? Drivers?
Choose your people first. Then choose your words.
And where? On a phone? On a wall? On the radio?

A poster lives on a wall.
Big words. One picture. Read it from three steps — or it is not there.
A flyer is smaller. A headline. The good things. A phone number. Done.

An infographic shows facts with pictures.
A big number. A small chart. Very few words.

One law. Never lie.
A promise the thing cannot keep is a lie in big letters.
Sell hard. Tell the truth.

Now you try. One thing you love. Twenty words.
Claim. Benefits. Call!
That is an advert — in English.
