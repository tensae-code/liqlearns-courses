Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An advert may claim anything, because everybody knows adverts exaggerate. **Answer: False** — a claim the thing cannot keep is a lie in big letters; the review lesson's honesty law binds here hardest.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The same product may need different adverts for different target markets. **Answer: True** — Ghana's checklist puts target market inside the kind itself; method follows audience (Cambodia).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An infographic should carry its facts in full paragraphs so nothing is lost. **Answer: False** — a fact that needs a paragraph belongs in an article; the infographic carries facts by picture, number and caption.
