# Advertisements and posters — Multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana's advertisements checklist (curriculum evidence) names —
a) title, body, target market, selling points  b) plot, hero, ending  c) aim, method, statistics **Answer: a)** — the NaCCA programme's printed checklist for the kind (B7).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The call to action is —
a) the price list  b) one imperative telling the reader what to do now ('Come and try it today')  c) the writer's signature **Answer: b)** — claim, benefits, call: the advert's third working part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cambodia lists the methods of advertising as —
a) social media, posters, radio, public display  b) letters only  c) whispering **Answer: a)** — Grade 12, Unit 17 — each method with its audience.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A benefit adjective earns its place when —
a) it is long  b) a real benefit for the buyer stands under it  c) it rhymes **Answer: b)** — 'fast' sells when speed is a benefit the buyer can use; paint without a wall.
