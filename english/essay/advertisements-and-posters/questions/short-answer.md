# Advertisements and posters — Short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Advertisements and posters — the advert's anatomy · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 1 — Advertisements and posters — the advert's anatomy · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Advertisements and posters — the poster, the flyer and the infographic.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a claim for a bicycle lamp that is very bright and cheap. **Answer: 'The little lamp that lights the whole road' (any one-line true big promise earns it).** — the claim is the promise, in the title's seat — and it must be keepable.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Turn this feature into a benefit: 'The bag has six pockets.' **Answer: 'Six pockets — books, bottle, phone and lunch each in its place' (the feature said as a good FOR the buyer earns it).** — features describe the thing; benefits describe the buyer's life with it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Your poster for the school play cannot be read from three steps. Name the two repairs. **Answer: Enlarge the message (fewer, bigger words) and cut everything that is not claim, picture, benefit or call.** — one picture, few words, big letters — the wall test's discipline.
