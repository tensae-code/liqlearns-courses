# Advertisements and posters — Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** Before claim, benefits and call, the writer chooses the ______ ______ — who this advert is for. **Answer: target market** — Ghana's checklist: the same bicycle sells differently to children and to drivers.

**2. (Level 2)** Egypt's poster boxes are: plan, organize, ______, design. **Answer: language** — Secondary 10 Term 2, pp.62–63 — with a plan → draft → improve → check process.

**3. (Level 3)** A poster must survive being read from ______ steps back. **Answer: three** — posters are read by moving feet; what fails at three steps does not exist.
