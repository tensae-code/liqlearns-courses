# Video script — Presentation skills

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A presentation is a talk with a PLAN.

First — organise. What is my goal?
Say it at the start. Out loud. One sentence.

Then the steps, in order.
First. Second. Next. Finally.
Your audience walks with you.

Slides? A slide SUPPORTS you.
One picture. A few words.
YOU are the talk. Not the screen.

Syria says: open strong. Define your hard words.
Watch the clock. Finish with a bang.
And practice. Practice. Practice.
Bolivia says the same: practice is the secret.

Now — questions. Do not fear them.
Use TRACT.
T — Thank the person.
R — Repeat the question.
A — Answer it.
C — Check: "Did that answer you?"
T — Thank them again.

Five steps. No panic.
Plan it. Show it. Answer it. Done.
