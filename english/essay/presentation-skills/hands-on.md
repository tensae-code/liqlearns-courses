# Hands-on — Presentation skills

A real talk — planned, given, and questioned.

## The talk (4 minutes): "How one thing works"
Pick a process you know with your hands (making tea,
fixing a bicycle tyre, planting a seed, your morning
journey). Indonesia's Grade 8 book recycles the procedure
text as a spoken presentation — do the same.
Frame:
- SLIDE 1 (title + one picture only): the talk's name.
- OPENING (30 seconds): greet, name yourself, state the
  goal: "By the end, you will know how to ___."
- STEPS: three to five slides, one step each, one picture
  and at most five words per slide. Say the order words
  aloud: First / Second / Next / Finally.
- NOTES: one card per step, key words only — never
  sentences to read.
- ENDING: one strong last line + "Thank you. I welcome
  your questions."

## The questions (TRACT drill)
A companion asks three questions — one easy, one hard,
one you cannot answer. Run TRACT on each:
"Thank you for that question. You are asking ___
(repeat it in your own words). The answer is ___. Did
that answer your question? Thank you again."
For the unanswerable one, the honest Answer step is:
"I do not know yet — I will find out and tell you
tomorrow." Then actually find out.
