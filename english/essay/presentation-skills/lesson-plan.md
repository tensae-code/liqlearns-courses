# Lesson plan — Presentation skills

**Course:** English · **Title:** Essay · **Lesson:** Presentation skills
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 38, "Speeches and Shows".
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** planning and
giving a talk — open strongly, walk ordered points,
let slides support (never replace) you, deliver to time —
and answer questions with the TRACT pattern.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands beside Speech
writing: the speech persuades; the presentation informs
and shows, then faces questions.
**What the learner can do at the end:** plan a talk in
stages, build supporting slides and notes, keep to the
clock, and run Thank–Repeat–Answer–Check–Thank on any
question without panic.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Speech writing. Recommended, never
  forced.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of a presenter beside a slide
   with ONE picture and THREE words on it.** What the
   student does: the student says what the slide carries
   and what the speaker's mouth must still carry.
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a clock face with a talk's
   three parts marked as slices.** What the student does:
   the student points at each slice and names the stage
   that belongs there.

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: the wall-of-text slide vs the
   one-picture slide — which talk do you remember?
2. **The stages of a presentation** (Indonesia Grade 8's
   Language Focus: starting, the goal, the steps, ending).
3. **Syria's eight tips** (Workbook 12, Appendix IV).
4. **Bolivia's five steps** — organise, standard
   expressions, supporting slides, practice.
5. **Group presentation** (Cambodia G8 U25) and timing.
6. **Questions — TRACT** (Indonesia Grade 12's pupil book).
7. **Do it yourself**: the hands-on talk, questions included.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reading the slides: the slide is a picture-post; the
  talk lives in the speaker. Turn around and say it.
- Questions frighten: TRACT is a rail — hold it step by
  step; repeating the question buys thinking time lawfully.
- Next in this level: Play scripts.
