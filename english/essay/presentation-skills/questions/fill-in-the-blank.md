# Presentation skills — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** "Starting — stating the goal — showing
the steps — ______." **Answer: ending** — Indonesia's
four stages, in order.

**2. (Level 2)** Syria's tips: open strongly, and finish
with a ______. **Answer: bang** — the last line is built
to be remembered.

**3. (Level 2)** Bolivia: prepare slides that ______
your presentation. **Answer: support** — the slide is a
picture-post; the speaker is the road.

**4. (Level 3)** TRACT: Thank — Repeat — Answer — Check
— ______. **Answer: Thank (them again)** — the pattern
opens and closes with thanks.
