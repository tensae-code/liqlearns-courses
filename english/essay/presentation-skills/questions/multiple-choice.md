# Presentation skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Indonesia's stages of a presentation run
— a) ending, steps, goal, starting b) starting, stating
the goal, showing the steps, ending c) slides, notes,
questions only **Answer: b)** — the talk's skeleton, in
the Grade 8 book's order.

**2. (Level 1)** A good slide holds — a) the whole talk
in small print b) one picture and a few words, supporting
the speaker c) nothing at all **Answer: b)** — Syria's
tip: slides support; the speaker is the talk.

**3. (Level 2)** Bolivia names the secret of presenting
in English as — a) perfect slides b) practice, practice
and more practice c) memorising the script
**Answer: b)** — the compendium's own words.

**4. (Level 2)** Syria's eight tips include — a) read
your full script aloud b) finish with a bang c) avoid
defining hard words **Answer: b)** — open strongly and
finish with a bang; notes, not a script; terms defined.

**5. (Level 3)** TRACT's R step is — a) refuse the
question b) repeat the question c) run to the end
**Answer: b)** — Thank, Repeat, Answer, Check, Thank
(Indonesia, Grade 12 pupil's book).
