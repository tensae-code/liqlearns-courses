# Presentation skills — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Planning the Talk · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Giving the Talk · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Handling the Questions.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Syria's eight tips in short form.
**Answer: organise — open strongly — define terms —
finish with a bang — slides — timing — notes — practice**
— Appendix IV of the Grade 12 Workbook.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your talk runs two minutes too long in
rehearsal. Where do you cut? **Answer: the swollen
middle stage — never the ending** — the closing bang is
protected; order survives because the stages stay whole.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A question comes that you cannot
answer. Run TRACT honestly. **Answer: Thank the
questioner; repeat the question; answer truthfully —
"I do not know yet; I will find out and tell you
tomorrow"; check that this satisfies them for now;
thank them again — and keep the promise.**
