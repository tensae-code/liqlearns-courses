# Presentation skills — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** Reading your slides word for word is
the safest way to present. **Answer: False** — Syria's
tips say notes, not a script; a reader's audience stops
listening.

**2. (Level 2)** Timing is part of preparation, not
luck. **Answer: True** — Syria lists timing among the
eight tips; rehearse with a clock.

**3. (Level 3)** Repeating the question in TRACT is
wasted time. **Answer: False** — it checks you heard it,
lets the whole room hear it, and buys thinking time.
