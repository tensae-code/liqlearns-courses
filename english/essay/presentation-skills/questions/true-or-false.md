# Presentation skills — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Planning the Talk · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Giving the Talk · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Handling the Questions.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reading your slides word for word is
the safest way to present. **Answer: False** — Syria's
tips say notes, not a script; a reader's audience stops
listening.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Timing is part of preparation, not
luck. **Answer: True** — Syria lists timing among the
eight tips; rehearse with a clock.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Repeating the question in TRACT is
wasted time. **Answer: False** — it checks you heard it,
lets the whole room hear it, and buys thinking time.
