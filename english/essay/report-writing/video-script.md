# Video script — Report writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A report is not a story.
It does not say: I feel. It says: I looked. I counted. Here is what I found.

Look at the frame.
A title. Who it is to. Who it is from.
An introduction. Why this report? What did we want to know?
Findings. What we found. Only facts. Numbers. Things we can check.
Recommendations. What should we do now?
A conclusion. Short. It says the main thing again.

A survey report is a small report with a job.
First — the aim. What do we want to know?
Then — the method. Who did we ask? How many?
Then — the numbers. Most students… A few students…
Draw the numbers. A pie chart. A bar chart.
Last — the conclusion. What do the numbers tell us?

Use full words. Do not write "don't". Write "do not".
A report wears a suit. Clean. Formal. Calm.

Now you try.
Ask ten people one question.
Count the answers. Write the report.
Aim. Method. Numbers. Conclusion.
That is a report — in English.
