# Report writing — Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or part.

**1. (Level 1)** The part of a formal report that says what was found is the ______. **Answer: Findings** — findings carry facts only; advice waits for Recommendations.

**2. (Level 2)** A survey report runs: aims, ______, statistics, conclusion. **Answer: method** — Sudan's run: who was asked, how many, and how.

**3. (Level 3)** In a formal report, write "do not" — never ______. **Answer: don't** — full forms, no contractions (Egypt's formal-language rule).
