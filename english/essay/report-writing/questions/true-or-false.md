# Report writing — True or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A formal report may use contractions because they sound friendly. **Answer: False** — Egypt's rule: full forms, no contractions, polite neutral tone.

**2. (Level 2)** A two-paragraph piece can be a complete report. **Answer: True** — Kuwait's Pearls 7A reports are two paragraphs, planned topic–detail–conclusion.

**3. (Level 3)** A survey's numbers can be trusted even when the report never says who was asked. **Answer: False** — no method, no trust: aims and method stand before every statistic (Sudan).
