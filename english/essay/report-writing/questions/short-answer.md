# Report writing — Short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Report writing — the frame · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Report writing — the survey · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Report writing — the judgement.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your findings say the school tap is broken and pupils queue for water. Write the recommendation that grows out of it. **Answer: The school should repair the tap (any recommendation that follows from the finding earns it).** — recommendations grow out of findings, never out of wishes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You asked 20 pupils how they come to school: 14 walk, 4 take a bus, 2 come by car. Write the majority sentence. **Answer: Most pupils walk to school (14 of 20).** — majority/minority sentences carry the statistics in words (Sudan).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why may a report's conclusion not introduce a new problem? **Answer: Because the conclusion only restates what the findings earned; a new problem has no findings behind it.** — Malaysia's closing rule: end on the finding or the recommendation.
