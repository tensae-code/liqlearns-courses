# Report writing — Matching

Match each item to its match. One answer per item.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: Title **Answer: the report's subject in a few words** — the frame's first part (Egypt, p.57).

**2. (Level 2)** Match: Method (in a survey report) **Answer: who was asked, how many, and how** — Sudan's survey report states its method before its numbers.

**3. (Level 3)** Match: Conclusion **Answer: the main point again, briefly — nothing new** — a conclusion that births a new idea breaks the frame.
