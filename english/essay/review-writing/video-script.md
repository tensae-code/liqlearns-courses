# Video script — Review writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A review tells you: is it good? Should I go? Should I read it? Should I eat there?

A review does two jobs.
It informs. It tells you what the thing is.
And it persuades. It helps you decide.

Four parts.
One — the start. What is it? And my first feeling. Good? Bad? Mixed?
Two — the details. The story. The acting. The food. Real things I saw and heard.
Three — the good and the bad. Pros and cons. Both sides. Honest.
Four — the end. My recommendation. Go. Read it. Eat there. Or — save your money.

Be fair. A review with only good things? Nobody trusts it.
One true bad point makes the good points strong.

Strong words need reasons.
Not: "The food was bad."
Say what happened. "The soup came cold, and the bread was old."
Now I believe you.

Now you try. One film. One book. One meal.
Tell what it is. Weigh it. Recommend it.
That is a review — in English.
