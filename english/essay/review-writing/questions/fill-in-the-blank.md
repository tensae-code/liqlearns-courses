# Review writing — Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or part.

**1. (Level 1)** A piece that only informs is a summary; a piece that only persuades is an ______. **Answer: advertisement** — the review stands honest between them (Egypt's definition).

**2. (Level 2)** The part of a review that weighs the good against the bad is ______ and ______. **Answer: pros / cons** — the third of Egypt's four parts — the part an advertisement skips.

**3. (Level 3)** Yemen's commission, corrected: 'Write a short review of a book you have just read, or a film you have just ______. ' **Answer: seen** — the workbook prints 'see' for 'seen' (p.90) — taught corrected.
