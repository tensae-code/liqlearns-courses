# Review writing — True or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Review writing — the four parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Review writing — judgement with reasons · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Review writing — the trusted verdict.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A review with only good points is the most trustworthy kind. **Answer: False** — nobody trusts all praise; one real fault, fairly stated, makes the praise believable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The summary of the thing should take most of the review's space. **Answer: False** — the thing itself takes about a quarter; the weighed judgement is the rest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A short review may drop the four parts to save space. **Answer: False** — Yemen's short-review commission shrinks the parts; it does not remove them.
