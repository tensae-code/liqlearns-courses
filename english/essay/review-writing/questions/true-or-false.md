# Review writing — True or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A review with only good points is the most trustworthy kind. **Answer: False** — nobody trusts all praise; one real fault, fairly stated, makes the praise believable.

**2. (Level 2)** The summary of the thing should take most of the review's space. **Answer: False** — the thing itself takes about a quarter; the weighed judgement is the rest.

**3. (Level 3)** A short review may drop the four parts to save space. **Answer: False** — Yemen's short-review commission shrinks the parts; it does not remove them.
