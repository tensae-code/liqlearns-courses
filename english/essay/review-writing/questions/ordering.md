# Review writing — Ordering

Stand the items in their true order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order the review's parts: pros and cons / conclusion + recommendation / introduction + overall impression / specific details. **Answer: introduction + overall impression — specific details — pros and cons — conclusion + recommendation** — Egypt's four parts in their standing order.

**2.
