# Review writing — Multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Egypt's Tip Box: a review does two jobs —
a) inform and persuade  b) list and sell  c) summarise and copy **Answer: a)** — a review informs (what the thing is) and persuades (helps the reader decide).

**2. (Level 1)** The four parts, in order, are —
a) details, title, cons, song  b) introduction + overall impression, specific details, pros and cons, conclusion + recommendation  c) summary, summary, summary, stars **Answer: b)** — the Tip Box's structure, Secondary 12 Term 1, pp.93–95.

**3. (Level 2)** A judgement is 'paid for' when —
a) it uses three adjectives  b) a specific detail stands behind it as its reason  c) it is written in capitals **Answer: b)** — 'the soup came cold' pays for 'the food was poor'; the adjective alone does not.

**4. (Level 3)** Malaysia's Form 5 review rule for description is —
a) list everything  b) describe so it informs without over-listing  c) use no details **Answer: b)** — choose the details that carry the place; a list is not a picture (Unit 9, pp.118–119).
