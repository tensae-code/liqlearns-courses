# Review writing — Matching

Match each item to its match. One answer per item.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: introduction + overall impression **Answer: what the thing is, and the verdict in seed form** — part one of Egypt's four.

**2. (Level 2)** Match: specific details **Answer: the evidence — plot, acting, food, rooms — things a reader can picture and check** — part two: details carry the judgements.

**3. (Level 3)** Match: recommendation **Answer: who should go, read, watch or eat — and who should not** — part four: the conclusion spends the trust the weighing earned.
