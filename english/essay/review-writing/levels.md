Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: lay a review's parts in order — introduction + overall impression, specific details, pros and cons, conclusion + recommendation — and say each part's job.

## Sub lesson 2 — Review writing — judgement with reasons

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: order judgements so each strong word is paid for by a specific detail, across film, book and restaurant reviews.
