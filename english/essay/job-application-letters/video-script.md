# Video script — Job application letters

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

You see a job. You want the job.
You write one letter. The letter has an order.

One. I saw your advert. I want this job.
Two. This is who I am. This is my school, my training.
Three. This is why I fit. Your advert asks for this.
I have this. I did this.
Four. My CV is with this letter. Please look.
Five. I can come and meet you. Thank you.

Short. Formal. About THEIR job, not only about you.

A letter for every job is a letter for no job.
Write it for THIS job.
Now you write one.
