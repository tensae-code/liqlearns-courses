# Job application letters — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** State the five moves in order.
**Answer: the post — the person — the fit — the
enclosure — the meeting** — Cambodia's coached order,
in the lesson's words.

**2. (Level 2)** The advert asks for "careful with
records". Write the fit sentence. **Answer: (any true
matching fact, e.g.) "In my school I kept the library's
loan records for a year without an error"** — an ask
answered with a fact, not a praise word.

**3. (Level 3)** Your draft praises your singing, for a
driver's job. Repair? **Answer: cut it** — nothing is
praised that the advert never asked for; relevance is
the letter's law.
