# Job application letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The application letter's FIRST paragraph
names — a) your hobbies b) the post, the advert and its
date c) your salary hopes **Answer: b)** — the reader
must know which job, seen where, before anything else.

**2. (Level 1)** The paragraph that answers the advert's
asks one by one is — a) paragraph 1 b) paragraph 2
c) paragraph 3, the fit **Answer: c)** — the fit is the
letter's heart, after the post and the person.

**3. (Level 2)** The advert asks for Saturday work. Where
does "I am free on Saturdays" belong? a) the closing
b) the fit paragraph c) the subject line only
**Answer: b)** — every advert ask is answered in the fit,
in the advert's own order.

**4. (Level 2)** Egypt's formal-email template opens the
purpose with — a) "Listen, I need…" b) "I am writing to
[state your purpose clearly]" c) "About the job…"
**Answer: b)** — the purpose stated clearly, in a full
polite sentence.

**5. (Level 3)** A letter that could be sent for any job
fails because — a) it is too short b) it answers no
advert's asks c) it encloses a CV **Answer: b)** —
Egypt's law: personalised applications over generic ones.
