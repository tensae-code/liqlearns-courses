# Job application letters — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Job application letters — the paragraph order · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 1 — Job application letters — the paragraph order · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Job application letters — the letter judged whole.

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I am writing to ______ for the post of
Library Assistant." **Answer: apply** — the purpose verb
of the first paragraph.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I ______ my CV with this letter."
**Answer: enclose** — move four points at the CV; the CV
itself is written in its own lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The letter closes by thanking the reader
and offering to attend an ______. **Answer: interview**
— the polite push that ends the five moves.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Laos pairs the application letter with
application ______, whose boxes are filled, not
paragraphed. **Answer: forms** — the letter's paper
cousin, a different skill.
