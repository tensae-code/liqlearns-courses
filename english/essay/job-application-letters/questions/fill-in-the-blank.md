# Job application letters — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** "I am writing to ______ for the post of
Library Assistant." **Answer: apply** — the purpose verb
of the first paragraph.

**2. (Level 1)** "I ______ my CV with this letter."
**Answer: enclose** — move four points at the CV; the CV
itself is written in its own lesson.

**3. (Level 2)** The letter closes by thanking the reader
and offering to attend an ______. **Answer: interview**
— the polite push that ends the five moves.

**4. (Level 3)** Laos pairs the application letter with
application ______, whose boxes are filled, not
paragraphed. **Answer: forms** — the letter's paper
cousin, a different skill.
