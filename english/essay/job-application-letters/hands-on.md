# Hands-on — Job application letters

A real writing task, with a model frame.

## The advert (invented for this lesson)
*The Daily Post, 12 March. WANTED: Library Assistant,
City Library. Must have finished secondary school, love
books, be careful with records, and be free on Saturdays.
Write to the Head Librarian, City Library.*

## The task
Write the application letter for this job. Invent your
name, school and one true-sounding experience (helping in
a school library, keeping a shop's records — your choice).

## The model frame (shape only)
[Formal layout, as in The formal letter]
Dear Sir or Madam,
Paragraph 1 — I am writing to apply for … , advertised
in … on … .
Paragraph 2 — who you are: school finished, training,
the qualification the advert names first.
Paragraph 3 — the fit: take the advert's asks one by one
and answer each with your fact.
Paragraph 4 — I enclose my CV … I would welcome the
opportunity to attend an interview …
Yours faithfully, [full name]

## Check
- Can the reader point at the advert line each fact
  answers? Is anything praised that the advert never asked
  for? Cut it.
