# Video script — Complaint letters

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

Something went wrong. You write a letter.
Not an angry letter. A clear letter.

Say what you bought. Say when.
Say what is wrong. Facts. Only facts.
Say what you want. A new one. Your money back. A repair.

No shouting on paper. Facts are strong.

And if YOU get a complaint letter?
Three moves.
One: I am sorry.
Two: this is what happened.
Three: this is what I will do.

Complaint. Reply. Problem finished.
Now you write the pair.
