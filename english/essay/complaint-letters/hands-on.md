# Hands-on — Complaint letters

A real writing task — BOTH letters of the pair.

## The situation (invented for this lesson)
You stayed one night at the Hill View Hotel. The room was
not cleaned, the shower gave cold water only, and
breakfast — promised in the price — was charged again.

## Task 1 — the complaint
Write to the manager. Frame:
[Formal layout] Dear Sir or Madam,
P1 — I am writing to complain about my stay on [date],
room [number], booked …
P2 — the facts, in time order, one fault per sentence.
P3 — what the trouble cost you, in one sentence.
P4 — I would be grateful if you could … (the one fix:
refund of the breakfast charge, in this case.)
Yours faithfully, [name]

## Task 2 — the reply
Now be the manager. Answer the letter you just wrote:
P1 — Thank you for your letter of [date]. I am very sorry…
P2 — the honest explanation (staff illness, a new worker
untrained — Greece's model's own situation, in your words).
P3 — the action: the charge refunded, the shower repaired,
and one goodwill offer.
Yours sincerely, [manager's name — you may now sign a name,
and the pair rule follows it.]
