# Complaint letters — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Complaint letters — the complaint's moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Complaint letters — the reply's moves · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Complaint letters — tone under pressure.

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I am writing to ______ about my stay
on 4 May." **Answer: complain** — the purpose verb opens
the complaint as apply opens the application.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I would be ______ if you could refund
the breakfast charge." **Answer: grateful** — the one
fix asked in the formal request dress.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The reply's three moves in order:
apologise, ______, act. **Answer: explain** — the honest
cause stands between sorry and the fix.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A complaint sentence must pass the
proof test: could I ______ this? **Answer: prove** —
facts only; feelings are rewritten as facts or cut.
