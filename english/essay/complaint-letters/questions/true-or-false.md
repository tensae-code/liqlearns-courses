# Complaint letters — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Complaint letters — the complaint's moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Complaint letters — the reply's moves · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Complaint letters — tone under pressure.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A complaint may ask for two or three
different fixes at once. **Answer: False** — one fix,
named exactly; a list of demands reads as rage, not fact.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the reply, the explanation comes
before the apology. **Answer: False** — apologise first;
a cause offered before sorry sounds like an excuse.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The worse the trouble, the freer the
register may be. **Answer: False** — the rule runs the
other way: the worse the trouble, the steadier the dress.
