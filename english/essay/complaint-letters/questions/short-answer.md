# Complaint letters — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Complaint letters — the complaint's moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Complaint letters — the reply's moves · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Complaint letters — tone under pressure.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the complaint's four moves.
**Answer: the booking — the facts — the cost — the one
ask** — promise, trouble, damage, fix, in that order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A reply says only "We are sorry."
What two moves are missing? **Answer: the explanation
and the action** — sorry alone leaves the complaint
standing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rewrite as a fact: "The staff were
useless and lazy." **Answer: (any provable version, e.g.)
"I waited forty minutes for breakfast; no waiter came
to my table"** — a time and an event replace the insult.
