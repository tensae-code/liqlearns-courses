# Complaint letters — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Complaint letters — the complaint's moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Complaint letters — the reply's moves · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Complaint letters — tone under pressure.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The complaint's first paragraph states —
a) your feelings b) the booking: date, room, what was
promised c) the refund you demand **Answer: b)** — the
reader must stand where you stood before hearing faults.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The facts paragraph holds — a) one fault
per sentence, in time order, each provable b) every
feeling, strongly c) threats **Answer: a)** — Greece's
fault lists are worked exactly so: shower, call, tap —
named, not raged about.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The reply's FIRST move is — a) explain
b) blame the staff c) apologise, naming the trouble back
**Answer: c)** — apology first; explanation is the middle
move, action the last.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Greek manager's explanation was —
a) the guest was wrong b) staff illness and untrained
replacements c) no explanation given **Answer: b)** —
an honest cause, briefly, without blaming the writer.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence belongs in a complaint?
a) "Your hotel is a disgrace!!!" b) "The shower gave cold
water only; I reported it at 9 p.m." c) "I'll ruin you!"
**Answer: b)** — a provable fact in formal dress; the
others fail the register and the proof test.
