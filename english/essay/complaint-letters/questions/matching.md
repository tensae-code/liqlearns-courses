# Complaint letters — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Slow breakfast service — **Answer: a
fault for the facts paragraph** — Greece's model
complaint lists it among its provable troubles.

**2. (Level 1)** "I reported it at 9 p.m.; nobody came" —
**Answer: a fact with its time attached** — time order
is what makes facts checkable.

**3. (Level 2)** Staff illness — **Answer: the reply's
explanation move** — the Greek manager's honest cause.

**4. (Level 2)** Refund of the charge — **Answer: the
reply's action move (and the complaint's one ask)** —
the pair meets at the fix.

**5. (Level 3)** "can't", "gonna", "!!!" in a complaint —
**Answer: register faults to correct** — Uzbekistan's
Review exercise, in the lesson's words.
