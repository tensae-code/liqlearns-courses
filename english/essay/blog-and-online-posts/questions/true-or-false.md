# Blog and online posts — True or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A blog may wander anywhere, because its voice is friendly. **Answer: False** — the mind-map plan holds it: the voice roams inside paragraphs, never across the whole post (Malaysia).

**2. (Level 2)** A social-media post that needs a second post to make sense has still succeeded. **Answer: False** — Egypt's small room has the same laws: one point, warm, complete.

**3. (Level 3)** Disagreeing online means answering the person, firmly. **Answer: False** — answer the IDEA, with its reason; the person is not the target.
