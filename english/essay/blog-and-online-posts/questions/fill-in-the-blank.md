# Blog and online posts — Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** Egypt's voice rule for the blog: write it like telling a ______ to a friend. **Answer: story** — Secondary 11 Term 1's structure box, pp.39–40.

**2. (Level 2)** In the polite bad-news message, the four moves are: cushion, ______, kindness, door. **Answer: fact** — the fact stated plainly, once, between the cushion and the kindness.

**3. (Level 3)** A blog paragraph online usually runs ______ to four lines — short enough to breathe on a phone screen. **Answer: two** — small rooms: one idea per paragraph, white space as stairs.
