# Blog and online posts — Multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A blog post opens with —
a) a catchy title/hook, with the writer's name and date as part of the kind  b) the conclusion  c) a formal To/From line **Answer: a)** — Egypt's blog shape and its six tips; the blog is a person writing on a day.

**2. (Level 1)** Malaysia plans its blog with —
a) no plan  b) a mind-map plan — topic in the centre, one line per paragraph  c) the teacher's outline **Answer: b)** — Academy Stars Year 6: blog writing with a mind-map plan, pp.40–41.

**3. (Level 2)** Egypt's social-media post size is —
a) 90–100 words, one point, complete  b) as long as it falls out  c) exactly three words **Answer: a)** — Preparatory 8 Term 1, Unit 5: the post and its Tip.

**4. (Level 3)** A polite online comment —
a) attacks the writer when wrong  b) praises vaguely ('nice!!!')  c) is specific, answers the idea, and disagrees without wounding **Answer: c)** — Greece's comments are matched to the thing itself; specific praise is the kind with weight.
