# Blog and online posts — Short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Write a catching blog heading for a post about your class trip to a waterfall. **Answer: 'The Day the Bus Left Without Us' (any heading carrying the strangest true thing earns it).** — a heading names and catches; a label only names.

**2. (Level 2)** Rewrite this comment politely, keeping its fact: 'Your photo is terrible, learn photography.' **Answer: 'The light is dark in this one — the market photo you posted last week was much clearer' (fact kept, wound removed, specific praise or comparison added).** — answer the idea and the work, never wound the person.

**3. (Level 3)** Write the fact move of a bad-news message cancelling a Saturday class picnic. **Answer: 'The picnic this Saturday is cancelled — the ground is flooded' (the fact plain, once, no hiding).** — move two of four: cushion before it, kindness and door after it.
