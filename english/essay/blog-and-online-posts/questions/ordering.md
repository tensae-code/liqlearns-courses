# Blog and online posts — Ordering

Stand the items in their true order.

**1. (Level 1)** Order a blog post's parts: body paragraphs / catchy heading / name and date line / closing feeling or question / opening lines. **Answer: catchy heading — name and date line — opening lines — body paragraphs — closing feeling or question** — Egypt's shape: hook, person and day, then the story in short rooms.

**2. (Level 3)** Order the bad-news message: the fact / a kind true sentence (cushion) / what can still happen (door) / what you wish and are sorry for (kindness). **Answer: cushion — fact — kindness — door** — the four moves in their standing order.
