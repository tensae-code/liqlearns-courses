# Blog and online posts — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — online writing in your own voice — set by the pattern across
the countries' books: the shape first, then the working parts,
then the whole piece judged as a reader meets it. The levels
inside this lesson are RANKS: pass a rank to wear it.

## Level 1 — the blog's shape
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: lay a blog post's parts — catchy heading, dated voice, short paragraphs, conclusion — and plan one on a mind map.

## Level 2 — the small rooms
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write a social-media post complete in its small word count, and a comment that answers an idea politely.

## Level 3 — the hard messages
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give bad news politely in writing and hold the blog's voice across a whole post a stranger reads to the end.
