# Video script — Blog and online posts

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A blog is your voice, on a page, for the world.

It starts with a heading. A heading that catches the eye.
Then — who is writing? And when? A name. A date. A person, not a machine.

Short paragraphs! One, two, three lines. White space between them.
The reader is on a phone. Let the words breathe.

Write like you talk to a friend.
Tell the story. "On Tuesday, our bus left without us…"
Your feelings can come in. It is your blog. Your day. Your voice.

Before you write — plan!
A mind map. The topic in the middle. Lines out. Ideas on the lines.
Five lines — five paragraphs. The plan holds the post together.

A small post is a small room. One point only.
Say it warm. Say it whole. Stop.

And comments — be kind online.
Praise the real thing. "Your photo of the market is beautiful."
Do not like it? Answer the idea, not the person.
Bad news? Say it gently. Kind words first. The fact. Then a door: "Maybe next time."

Now you try. Plan it. Write it. Post it in your head first.
That is a blog — in English.
