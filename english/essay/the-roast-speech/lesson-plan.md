# Lesson Plan — The Roast Speech

**Unit:** Unit 38 — Speeches and Shows

**One thing:** the roast speech — a humorous, affectionate mock-insult speech honouring a guest: how to write one in five steps, and what makes it work (personal pronouns, direct quotations, polite expressions, humour and vivid description).

## Sub lessons

1. Writing a roast in five steps
2. What makes a roast effective

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The roast speech: how to write one in five steps (Lesson 3, Final Task + Task 12 "A Roast for Tarzan!", pp.330–332).
- What makes a roast effective: personal pronouns, direct quotations, polite expressions, humour and vivid description (pp.330–332).
- Polite expressions for ceremonial speaking are taught in the same lesson's preparation (pp.330–331).
