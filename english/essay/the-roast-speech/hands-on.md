# Hands-On — The Roast Speech

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Five-step plan.** In pairs, plan a roast for a fictional character: the five steps on five lines, ending with the praise line written in full.
2. **Ingredient hunt.** Read a short sample roast (teacher-provided). Label the five ingredients in the margin; find the turn to praise and mark it.
3. **Safety-rail rewrite.** Take three bare teasing lines and reframe each inside a polite expression, keeping the joke. Read both versions: what changed?
