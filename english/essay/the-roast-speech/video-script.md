# Video Script — The Roast Speech

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a smiling chief at a feast table, friends raising cups, laughing]

We tease him… because we LOVE him! This is a ROAST!

[Picture: a notebook filling with true stories and famous sayings]

First, research! True stories. His own famous words!

[Picture: a teasing arrow bouncing off a shield labelled RESPECT]

Tease the habit — never the wound. The frame stays polite!

[Picture: the room standing to applaud the chief, both hands raised]

And the last word? PRAISE. That is the law of the roast!
