# Levels — The Roast Speech

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Writing a roast in five steps | Write a roast speech by the book's five steps, keeping the target honoured and the teasing affectionate. |
| 2. What makes a roast effective | Use the book's five effectiveness ingredients: personal pronouns, direct quotations, polite expressions, humour and vivid description. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the roast speech — a humorous, affectionate mock-insult speech honouring a guest: how to write one in five steps, and what makes it work (personal pronouns, direct quotations, polite expressions, humour and vivid description).
