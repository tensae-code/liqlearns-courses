# Play scripts — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Lay out two turns of a script between
Ama and Kofi. **Answer: AMA: (line) on its own line;
KOFI: (line) on the next — name at the left, colon after
the name, a new line for each speaker** — Ghana B8.

**2. (Level 2)** Convert: "The old man entered slowly,
carrying a heavy bag, and sat down without a word."
**Answer: as a direction — [The old man enters slowly,
carrying a heavy bag. He sits down without speaking.] —
no spoken line exists in the sentence, so the body
carries it whole.**

**3. (Level 3)** Your converted script is one page
longer than the story scene. First suspicion?
**Answer: directions doing the lines' work (or lines
doing the directions') — meanings housed twice. Run the
check-off: every story sentence landed ONCE, as line,
direction or setting.**
