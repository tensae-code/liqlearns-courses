# Play scripts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In Ghana's layout, a speaker's turn
begins — a) in the middle of the last line b) with the
name at the left, a colon, and a new line for each
speaker c) with quotation marks only **Answer: b)** —
the script's standing form, brackets holding directions.

**2. (Level 1)** Stage directions are — a) spoken
loudly by the narrator b) written in brackets and never
spoken c) the play's title **Answer: b)** — they instruct
bodies, not mouths (Ghana B8; Malaysia Year 6).

**3. (Level 2)** A story says: "She slammed the door,
furious." In the script this becomes — a) one long line
about fury b) a line plus a direction: [she slams the
door] c) nothing — feelings are cut **Answer: b)** — the
narration is rehoused, not dropped.

**4. (Level 2)** Ghana's working order for dialogue
writing is — a) lines first, story later b) sketch the
scenario first, then write c) directions only
**Answer: b)** — the scene sketched before its lines.

**5. (Level 3)** India's conversion task is — a) copy
the prose into columns b) write a five-exchange script
from a prose excerpt c) memorise Chekhov
**Answer: b)** — Class 10's speaking work on "The
Proposal" unit: choosing the exchanges is the craft.
