# Play scripts — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** After the speaker's name comes a
______, then the words they say. **Answer: colon** —
names at the left, Ghana's rule.

**2. (Level 1)** Stage directions sit inside ______
brackets. **Answer: square** — [like this]: instructions
to bodies, never spoken.

**3. (Level 2)** In Malaysia's Unit 10 script, Inspector
______ solves the case of the dinosaur egg.
**Answer: Fox** — "The robbery", Academy Stars Year 6,
pp.122–123.

**4. (Level 3)** Ghana: ______ the scenario first, then
write the dialogue. **Answer: sketch** — the scene
planned before its lines, as an essay is outlined.
