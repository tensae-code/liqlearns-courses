# Video script — Play scripts

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A story tells you. A play SHOWS you.
And a script is the play on paper.

Look at the page.
The NAME stands at the left.
A colon. Then the words that person says.
New speaker? New line. Always.

MUSA: I did not take the egg.
ANA: Then who did?

But actors are bodies, not voices.
So the script whispers to the bodies too.
Words in brackets. Stage directions.
[Ana picks up the basket. She looks inside.]

In a story, the writer says: "She was angry."
In a script — no writer! So the anger moves.
Into the line. Or into a direction.
[shouting] — or a slammed door.

Ghana says: sketch the scene FIRST.
Then write the lines.

Take a story scene you love.
Names at the left. Lines one by one.
Directions in brackets.
Now your story can stand up and walk.
