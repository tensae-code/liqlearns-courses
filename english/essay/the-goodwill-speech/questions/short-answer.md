# Short Answer — The Goodwill Speech

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. How is a goodwill speech different from a persuasive speech? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A persuasive speech seeks a decision or a win; a goodwill speech seeks a relationship — nothing is attacked or demanded, and success is the warmth and trust left behind.

2. Why does goodwill 'die on a wrong name'? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Because the speech's whole claim is 'we value you'; a wrong name or ignored custom proves the opposite louder than any compliment.

3. Give the four pointers in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Know the audience; begin with thanks and a true, specific compliment; build on shared ground; end by looking forward together.

4. Why must a goodwill speech be brief? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Length and boasting spend the goodwill the speech came to build; warmth held briefly and sincerely lands, warmth stretched thin evaporates.
