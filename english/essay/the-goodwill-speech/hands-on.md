# Hands-On — The Goodwill Speech

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Occasion cards.** Draw an occasion card (visiting team, exchange school, sister town, company guests). In pairs, list the shared ground and one specific compliment a speaker could honestly give.
2. **Pointer check.** Read a sample goodwill speech (teacher-provided). Mark where each of the four pointers happens — or does not.
3. **One-minute draft and deliver.** Each learner drafts and delivers a one-minute goodwill speech for the class's chosen occasion; listeners score warmth and pointer-coverage separately.
