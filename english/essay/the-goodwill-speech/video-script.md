# Video Script — The Goodwill Speech

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a visiting student standing before a new school, smiling, both schools' flags behind]

This speech does not fight. It makes FRIENDS.

[Picture: the student thanking the hosts, pointing at their trophy shelf with real admiration]

Thank you! And mean it — name the REAL thing you admire.

[Picture: two groups discovering they love the same game, the same song]

Find what you SHARE. Say WE.

[Picture: the two groups walking out together through an open gate]

And end looking forward — together. The door stays open. Goodwill!
