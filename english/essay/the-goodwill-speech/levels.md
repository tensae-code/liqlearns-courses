# Levels — The Goodwill Speech

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a goodwill speech is | Say what a goodwill speech is and name its key points: the friendly purpose, the shared ground, the appreciation spoken aloud. |
| 2. Four pointers for writing one | Compose a goodwill speech using the book's four writing pointers. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the goodwill speech — a speech that builds friendship and good feeling between the speaker's side and the audience's — its key points, and four writing pointers for composing one.
