# Opening and closing — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Ends Judged (Set 1, diagnose) · (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Opening — Catch and Name (Set 2, catching moves) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Closing — Return and Land (Set 3, the return's parts).

Match each end to its fault or virtue.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 3)** — diagnose.
1. "This essay is about bees. Bees are useful. There are
   reasons." (whole opening) — **the form opening: names,
   never catches**
2. "BOOM! The hive exploded with ten thousand angry
   wings!" — and the paragraph never says what the essay
   is about — **the trick opening: catches, never names**
3. A closing that is the opening paragraph pasted again —
   **the echo closing**
4. "Finally, a fifth benefit of bees is…" (in the closing)
   — **the smuggler closing**
5. The body ends; there is no closing at all — **the cliff
   stop**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 1)** — catching moves.
1. "Have you ever watched a river decide who eats?" —
   **a question**
2. "The dawn found Meron already at the well, bucket in
   hand." — **a scene**
3. "One bee colony can visit fifty thousand flowers in a
   day." — **a striking fact**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 2)** — the return's parts.
1. "In conclusion…" — **the signal**
2. the points again, in new words — **the return**
3. "…and the town drinks because they do." — **the landing
   line**
