# Opening and closing — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Opening — Catch and Name · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Closing — Return and Land · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Ends Judged (the faults by name).

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** catch · name · perspective · signal · echo ·
smuggler

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana's first revise question: does the
beginning ______ the reader's attention?
**Answer: catch.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After catching, the opening must ______
the topic and main ideas.
**Answer: name.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The conclusion holds the same information
from a different ______.
**Answer: perspective.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "In conclusion" is a closing ______.
**Answer: signal.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A closing copied word for word is an
______ closing.
**Answer: echo.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A new point hiding in the last paragraph
makes it a ______ closing.
**Answer: smuggler.**
