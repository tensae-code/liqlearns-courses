# Opening and closing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The opening's FIRST job is to —
a) name every point in detail  b) catch the reader's
attention  c) conclude
**Answer: b)** — Ghana's first revise question; naming
follows inside the same paragraph.

**2. (Level 1)** Which opening move is a "catch"?
a) "This essay is about rivers."  b) "Every year, the river
takes children who never learned its language."  c)
"There are three reasons, listed below."
**Answer: b)** — a scene/fact that makes a stranger read
on; a) and c) name without catching (a useful naming,
coming after a catch).

**3. (Level 2)** Bolivia defines the conclusion as —
a) new information, strongly put  b) the same information
as the introductory paragraph, written from a different
perspective  c) the introduction copied carefully
**Answer: b)** — the table's own words.

**4. (Level 2)** "In conclusion, nobody is perfect" shows —
a) a closing signal doing its work  b) a new point  c) a
topic sentence
**Answer: a)** — Bolivia's printed example.

**5. (Level 3)** A closing repeats the opening word for word.
Its fault is —
a) the echo closing — no perspective was gained  b) none;
repetition is safety  c) the smuggler
**Answer: a).**

**6. (Level 3)** A closing announces a fourth reason, never
mentioned before. Repair:
a) keep it; endings may surprise  b) move it into the body
plan or cut it — closings spend earned weight, they do not
mint new  c) rename it "in conclusion"
**Answer: b).**
