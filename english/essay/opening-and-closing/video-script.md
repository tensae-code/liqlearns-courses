# Video script — Opening and closing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The essay has two doors. Make them GOOD!

The opening: CATCH first!
A question! A scene! A fact!
Then NAME it — the topic, the points.
Hook… then the map!

The closing: come BACK!
Same ideas — new words!
From the far side of the journey.
"In conclusion…" — and a last line that LANDS!

No copying your opening!
No NEW points hiding at the end!
Catch and name. Return and land.

Now you. Fix the two doors of your essay!
That is polish — in English.
