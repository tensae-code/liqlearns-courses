# News writing — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the news report — set by the pattern across
the countries' books: the shape first, then the working parts,
then the whole piece judged as a reader meets it. The levels
inside this lesson are RANKS: pass a rank to wear it.

## Level 1 — headline and lead
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write a headline in the headline's short present and a lead that answers who, what, where and when.

## Level 2 — tense choice
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose headline present, past simple and present perfect for news on purpose, each in its seat.

## Level 3 — the whole report
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a complete news report from notes alone — headline, lead, ordered body — and present it aloud at news speed.
