# News writing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
news report — set by the pattern across the countries' books:
headline and lead first, then the body and its tenses, then the
whole report built from notes. The sub lessons inside this
lesson are RANKS: pass a rank to wear it.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 splits at its natural
jump — ordering the body stands inside Sub lesson 2, and
building the whole report from notes is Sub lesson 3. Its pass
mark splits the same way below.

## Sub lesson 1 — News writing — headline and lead
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write a headline in the headline's short present and a lead that answers who, what, where and when.

## Sub lesson 2 — News writing — the body and tense choice
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose headline present, past simple and present perfect for news on purpose, each in its seat, and order the body's facts biggest first.

## Sub lesson 3 — News writing — the whole report
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a complete news report from notes alone — headline, lead, ordered body — and present it aloud at news speed.
