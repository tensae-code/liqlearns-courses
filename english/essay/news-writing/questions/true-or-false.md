Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A news report should run in the order things happened, like a story. **Answer: False** — news orders by importance — biggest first; time order is the story's way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A headline may drop small words like 'a' and 'the'. **Answer: True** — Cambodia's headline work: short, subject–verb–object, small words dropped.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Uzbekistan's book names and explains the present perfect as a tense inside its news unit. **Answer: False** — the filing states the forms are used inside Unit 5's news texts and gap-fills but never named or explained there — the seat is learned from the news itself.
