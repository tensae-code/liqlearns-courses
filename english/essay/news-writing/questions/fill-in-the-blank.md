# News writing — Fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sentence or two of a news report, answering who/what/where/when, is called the ______. **Answer: lead** — the lead carries the whole event before the body begins.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rewrite as a headline: 'A storm has destroyed the school roof.' → '______ destroys school roof.' **Answer: Storm** — small words dropped, verb in the short present (Cambodia, Unit 15).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's reminder box for news writing is called the five ______. **Answer: W's** — who, what, where, when, why — the lead's checklist.
