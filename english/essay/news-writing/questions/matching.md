# News writing — Matching

Match each item to its match. One answer per item.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: past simple in news **Answer: the finished event with its time ('destroyed the market last night')** — the event is closed and dated, so the past simple carries it.

**2. (Level 2)** Match: present perfect in news **Answer: news still warm ('police have warned…')** — Uzbekistan's news texts use the form for just-happened events.

**3. (Level 3)** Match: 'The mayor said…' in a report **Answer: an opinion wearing its owner — allowed, because who said it is a fact** — news reports facts; opinions enter only with their speaker named.
