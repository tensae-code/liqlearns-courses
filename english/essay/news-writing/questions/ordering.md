# News writing — Ordering

Stand the items in their true order.

**1. (Level 2)** Order a news report's parts: background details / headline / lead / most important new fact. **Answer: headline — lead — most important new fact — background details** — biggest first at every level of the piece.

**2. (Level 3)** Order the build, the books' way: design the front page / write notes of the five W's / read a model report / write the report from the notes. **Answer: read a model report — write notes of the five W's — write the report from the notes — design the front page** — Nepal Grade 10's run: model, clues, report, front page.
