# Video script — News writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

News is fast. News is short. News is true.

A big thing happened. Tell it in five words. That is the headline.
"Fire destroys market."
Short words. No small words. The verb in the present.

Now the first line. The lead.
Who? What? Where? When?
"A fire destroyed the fruit market in the town centre last night."
Four answers. One line. Now the reader knows.

Then the rest. The most important things first.
Not: first this happened, then this happened.
News is not a story. News puts the big thing at the top.

Tenses. Listen.
The event is finished? Use the past. The fire destroyed.
It just happened? Its result is here now? Use have and has.
"The President has just left." The news is still warm.

Write only facts. No "I think". No "maybe" — or say who says it.

Now you try. One real event.
A headline. A lead. Four answers in one line.
That is news — in English.
