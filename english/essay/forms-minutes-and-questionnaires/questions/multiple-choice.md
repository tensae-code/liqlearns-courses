# Forms, minutes and questionnaires — Multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The first law of form-filling is —
a) write fast, fix later  b) read the whole form before writing in any box  c) fill the hardest box first **Answer: b)** — a later box often answers an earlier one (Greece's form work).

**2. (Level 1)** Greece's registration form asks for —
a) your life story  b) name, nationality, address, passport number and dates, room, arrival, nights, departure  c) your opinions of the hotel **Answer: b)** — the form's fields as filed — facts, briefly, in their boxes.

**3. (Level 2)** Minutes record —
a) every speech made  b) who was present, what was decided, and who does what by when  c) only the chair's views **Answer: b)** — the talking is gone; the decisions are what the future needs (Ghana B9 formal writing — curriculum evidence).

**4. (Level 3)** A good questionnaire question —
a) asks two things at once for speed  b) pushes its answer  c) asks one thing, plainly, with answers that can be tallied **Answer: c)** — one thing per question; designed answer boxes (Ghana's focused-question projects — curriculum evidence).
