# Forms, minutes and questionnaires — True or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Minutes should record the discussion in full so nothing is lost. **Answer: False** — minutes record decisions and actions; a discussion that decided nothing earns one honest line.

**2. (Level 2)** Ghana's evidence for minutes in this lesson is a pupil's-book page. **Answer: False** — it is NaCCA CURRICULUM evidence — the programme names the writing and its features; worded so throughout.

**3. (Level 3)** A leading question ('Don't you agree the canteen is terrible?') makes a questionnaire stronger. **Answer: False** — a question that pushes its answer poisons the count.
