# Forms, minutes and questionnaires — Short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A form asks your date of birth and you are unsure of the year. What do you write, and why? **Answer: Leave it honestly empty (or mark it as the form directs) — never invent: a guessed date follows a person for years.** — the truth, briefly; unknown stays unknown.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Minute this decision in one line: the club talked for an hour and chose Ama to register the team by Friday. **Answer: DECIDED: the team will be registered. ACTION: Ama to register the team by Friday.** — decisions and actions only — the hour of talk earns no lines.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Repair this questionnaire question: 'Do you walk to school and enjoy it?' **Answer: Split it in two: 'How do you come to school?' (tick boxes) and, if needed, 'Do you enjoy the journey?' — one thing per question.** — a double question catches nothing countable.
