# Forms, minutes and questionnaires — Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** The paper written BEFORE a meeting, listing its numbered items, is the ______. **Answer: agenda** — the agenda plans; the minutes record.

**2. (Level 2)** An answer you do not know on a form stays ______ — never invented. **Answer: honestly empty** — a guessed fact is a lie with a signature under it.

**3. (Level 3)** In minutes, every action needs an owner and a ______ (by when). **Answer: date / deadline** — an action with no owner and no date is a wish, not a minute.
