# Forms, minutes and questionnaires — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the short factual instruments — set by the pattern across
the countries' books: the shape first, then the working parts,
then the whole piece judged as a reader meets it. The levels
inside this lesson are RANKS: pass a rank to wear it.

## Level 1 — the form
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fill a form completely and truthfully — every field in its box, nothing invented, nothing chatty.

## Level 2 — agenda and minutes
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write an agenda that plans a meeting and minutes that record its decisions and actions with owners and times.

## Level 3 — the questionnaire
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: design a questionnaire of focused, single, countable questions whose answers tally straight into a survey report.
