# Video script — Forms, minutes and questionnaires

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some writing is long. This writing is short — and it must be exact.

A form asks: who are you? Where do you live? When were you born?
Small boxes. True answers. Nothing extra.
Read the whole form first. Then write. Box by box.
Never guess. A wrong date lives for years.

A meeting has two papers.
Before — the agenda. What will we talk about? One, two, three.
After — the minutes. What did we decide? Who will do it? By when?
The minutes do not tell the talking. They tell the deciding.

A questionnaire asks many people the same questions.
Short questions. One thing in each question.
Give answers to tick. Tick — count — tally.
Good questions make numbers. Numbers make the report.

Now you try.
Fill the form. Write the agenda. Keep the minutes. Ask the questions.
Short papers. Exact papers.
That is factual writing — in English.
