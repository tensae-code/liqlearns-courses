# Video script — Film Study

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Judging a Film
- Beat 1: A cinema screen; over it, a scorecard rises with four criteria boxes. The presenter: a judgement is built, not felt.
- Beat 2: The same scene twice — a page of prose, then the film frame. A lens graphic settles over the frame. The presenter: watch through a different lens — ask what the film is doing to you.

## Sub lesson 2 — Film Language and Storyboards
- Beat 1: A camera glides on rails beside a running boy, keeping him centred. The presenter names it: a tracking shot — one uncut length of film.
- Beat 2: Six storyboard boxes draw themselves in a row; the last box matches the live scene from Beat 1. The presenter: planned on paper before it was ever filmed.
