# Hands-on — Film Study

- **Criteria first.** Before watching a set film or scene, the class agrees four judging criteria in writing. Verdicts afterwards may only use those criteria as headings.
- **Storyboard six.** In groups, storyboard six shots of a dramatic scene from a studied text; each box must say what the camera does, not only what is seen.
- **Lens questions.** While watching, each learner keeps two columns: what the film shows, and what the film is doing to me. Compare columns at the end.
