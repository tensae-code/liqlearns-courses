# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Judging a Film
- Depth: a film judged properly — the film *Maori Farmboy* watched and evaluated against criteria (Samoa, *English Year 11 Book 2*, printed pp.104–105); and media literacy at senior level: watching the film of a studied novel "through a different lens", learning visual language's power over young people, and reading a ++++ model review of the film (Samoa, *English Year 13*, printed pp.168–173).

## Sub lesson 2 — Film Language and Storyboards
- Depth: the language itself — a shot as an uncut length of film, tracking (the camera moving on tracks, a dolly or hand-held), and cutaways (Samoa, *English Year 10 Book Three*, printed pp.42–44 and *English Year 11 Book 1*, printed pp.100–101); storyboard-sequence questions, and planning a film sequence of a dramatic scene from the studied novel in groups (Samoa, *English Year 12*, printed pp.150–154).
