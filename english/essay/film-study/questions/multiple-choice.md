# Multiple choice — Film Study

1. What is a shot? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. A photograph of the actors
   - B. An uncut length of film — one continuous run of the camera
   - C. The film's poster
   - D. A line of dialogue
   Answer: B

2. What is tracking? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Following the plot
   - B. Moving the camera with the action, on tracks, a dolly or hand-held
   - C. Keeping count of the scenes
   - D. Recording the sound separately
   Answer: B

3. What does watching a film "through a different lens" mean? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Using a better camera
   - B. Asking what the film is doing to you, not only what it shows
   - C. Watching it twice
   - D. Reading the book first
   Answer: B

4. What is a storyboard? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. The film's script
   - B. A drawn plan of the sequence, shot by shot, made before filming
   - C. A review of the film
   - D. The list of actors
   Answer: B
