# Matching — Film Study

Match each term to its meaning.

1. Shot (Sub lesson 2) — one continuous, uncut run of the camera
2. Tracking (Sub lesson 2) — the camera moving with the action
3. Cutaway (Sub lesson 2) — a move away to a related image, then back
4. Storyboard (Sub lesson 2) — the sequence drawn shot by shot before filming
5. Model review (Sub lesson 1) — a review read as the standard: a judgement argued from evidence
