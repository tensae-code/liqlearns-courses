# Film Study — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 38, Studying Texts

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a judge's scorecard held up with four boxes — STORY, ACTING, PICTURES, FEELING — each with stars beside it, three filled, one empty. *The learners read the four boxes and say what a film judge is weighing.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of the same village scene shown twice: once as a page of writing, once as a glowing film frame with a camera at its edge. *The learners say two things the film version can show that the page cannot.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a camera on small rails pointing at a running boy, with an arrow showing the camera moving beside him. *The learners say what the camera is doing and what it is called.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of six small framed sketches in a row showing a boat arriving, a crowd gathering, a flag rising, each sketch numbered. *The learners say what this strip of sketches is and what it is used for before filming.*

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
