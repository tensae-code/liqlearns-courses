# Lesson Plan — Citing Sources and Bibliography

**One thing:** naming where your material comes from — citing a source inside your work, and gathering all the sources at the end in a bibliography, following the citation procedures.

## Sub lessons

1. Citing a source inside your work
2. The bibliography

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Written work that uses other texts names them: bibliographic citation is part of writing formal texts (Batxillerat programme, BOPA p.26).
- A citation inside the work points to the source of a fact, an idea or a wording at the place where it is used (p.26).
- The bibliography gathers the work's sources together at the end, so a reader can find every one of them (p.26).
- Citation follows procedures — a set way of naming sources, so every citation carries what a reader needs to find the source (p.26).
