# Video Script — Citing Sources and Bibliography

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the fact, then to its source name, exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a child writing a school project — one sentence on the page: "The Pyrenees are older than the Alps"]

Look at this sentence. Is it true? Hmm. I do not know. The writer read it in a book. But which book? It does not say!

[Picture: the same sentence, now with a small name tag beside it]

Now look! A small name stands next to the sentence. The name of the book! This is a citation. It says: "This fact comes from HERE." Now I can check it!

[Picture: the child turning to the last page of the project — a list of books]

And at the end of the work — a list! All the books, all together. This is the bibliography.

[Picture: the child's finger walking from a citation in the middle of the work to the same book in the list at the end]

See? The little name in the middle… and the same book in the list at the end. They hold hands!

Do not say "trust me." Say "check me." Name your sources!
