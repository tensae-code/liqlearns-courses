# Hands-On — Citing Sources and Bibliography

Things you do with real books and real writing. You need two books or pages you can really open, paper, and a partner.

1. **Hunt the source.** Your partner reads one fact from a book and says it aloud without naming the book. Write the fact in a sentence, then find out its source and add the citation beside the fact. Read the sentence both ways — without the citation, then with it — and say what changed.
2. **Build the end page.** Take a piece of schoolwork you have already written that used books or pages. List every source it used, named in full, on one page: that is its bibliography. Count them.
3. **Match them up.** For the same piece of work: put a mark at every place inside it where a source is used, and check that each marked source stands in the bibliography. Add any citation or entry that is missing.
4. **The follow test.** Swap work with your partner. Pick one of their citations and try to find that exact source from the bibliography alone. If you cannot, your partner fixes the naming until you can.
