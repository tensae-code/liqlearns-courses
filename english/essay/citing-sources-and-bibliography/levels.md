# Levels — Citing Sources and Bibliography

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Citing a source inside your work | Cite a source at the place where its fact, idea or words are used, naming it by the citation procedures so a reader can find it. |
| 2. The bibliography | Gather every source the work used into a bibliography at the end, each named in full, matching the citations inside the work. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons writes work a reader can check: every borrowed thing is cited where it stands, and every cited source is gathered, named in full, in the bibliography.
