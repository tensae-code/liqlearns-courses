# Job interviews — the lesson

Course: English · Title: Essay · Lesson: Job interviews
Built from: Cambodia, Grade 12, Unit 31, "At a job
interview" (printed pp.169–174) — Egypt, Secondary 10
Term 1, Unit 6, Lesson 2 (the STAR Method) — Libya,
Grade 12 (the job-interview lesson: Course Book p.97;

*Speaker note: the explanation is read in your own
language. The interview's own wording — its questions and
your answers — is the TARGET text: never translated,
always spoken and read in English.*

*If you hold a CV and a cover letter for one advert, this
is where they are cashed — but the lesson stands alone.*

## Do it first
One question — "Tell me about a time when things went
wrong at work." Answer A rambles: "Well, many times,
things go wrong, once the machine, and my uncle said…,
and generally I am good in problems." Answer B walks:
"Last March our delivery did not come (Situation). I had
to serve forty customers with half the goods (Task). I
wrote each order down, phoned the supplier twice, and
gave every customer a time to return (Action). Nobody
left angry, and we sold everything by evening (Result)."
Same worker. One answer is built. Building answers —
before the day — is this lesson.

## Then the explanation
**Preparation — the interview before the interview.**
Cambodia's Grade 12 unit "At a job interview" (Unit 31,
printed pp.169–174) opens with preparation advice — dress,
punctuality, phone, body language (as filed) — and Laos's
job unit prints a text of "Tips for interviews" beside
its full interview dialogue (Unit 10, Lesson 3,
pp.138–142), closing by ordering the job-seeking strategy
steps themselves. Armenia's Grade 11 §1.8 adds the
room's manners: interview etiquette and body language,
and useful phrases for buying thinking time — the honest
seconds a good answer needs ("That's a good question;
let me think…"). Prepare the outside (place known, clothes
ready, road known, early arrival, phone silent) so the
inside can think.

**STAR — the built answer.** Egypt's employment unit
teaches the method by name (Unit 6, Lesson 2): the STAR
Method for behavioural interview questions — the "Tell me
about a time when…" family — in four parts: **Situation**:
set the context; **Task**: explain what needed to be done;
**Action**: describe what you did; **Result**: share the
outcome. Hold its laws: ONE story per answer; the Action
is YOURS (say *I*, not *we*, for your own moves); the
Result ends the answer — with a fact or a number where
you honestly can. Cambodia's unit runs the same ground as
practice: talking about experience, the information
interviewers ask for, and answering with quality
adjectives — reliable, punctual, co-operative — each one
a claim your STAR story must cash.

**The other door — hearing the questions again.** Libya's
Grade 12 interview lesson works the interview from behind:
its Workbook (Lesson 4, "Interviews", p.121) takes a job
interview's twelve direct questions — "Where were you
working at the moment?", "Have you ever worked in the
Middle East?", "Have you got a clean driving licence?" —
and turns them into reported questions, the companion
piece to the Course Book's interview (p.97). Learn the
trick for yourself: after any interview, retell its
questions ("They asked me where…") and your next
preparation writes itself.

**The mock — practise like it counts.** Cambodia's unit
closes the chain its book built (adverts → application →
this room); Armenia sets pair interview practice with 20
sample questions. The mock is not a game bolted on: it is
the lesson's proof. Interviewer with a list, candidate
with two STAR answers ready, a score for preparation,
shape and truth — then chairs swapped, because asking the
questions teaches the answers' shape fastest.

## Do it yourself
1. Run the hands-on mock whole: prepare, build, perform,
   swap.
2. Question harvest: write the ten questions you most
   fear; build a STAR answer for the two that ask about a
   time when…; file the rest with one-line true answers.
3. After-action: retell your mock's questions as reported
   questions (Libya's way) and note the one answer you
   will rebuild.
