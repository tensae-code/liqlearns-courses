# Hands-on — Job interviews

A real performance task — a mock interview, prepared on
paper first.

## Step 1 — prepare (Cambodia's advice, as a checklist)
- The place: name it, what it does, where it is.
- Dress: clean, simple, ready the night before.
- Time: arrive ten minutes early; know the road.
- Phone: silent, in the bag, before the door.
- Papers: CV copy, certificates, this sheet.

## Step 2 — build two STAR answers (frame)
Question: "Tell me about a time when you solved a problem."
S — At [place], in [month], [the situation in one line].
T — I had to [the task — what was yours to do].
A — First I …, then I … (your actions only, in order).
R — In the end, [the result — with a number or fact].
Write a second answer for: "Tell me about a time when you
worked with other people."

## Step 3 — the mock
Companion as interviewer, with this list: Tell me about
yourself. Why this job? What experience have you? (answer
with quality adjectives — reliable, punctual, co-operative
— each backed by one fact). Then your STAR questions.
Interviewer scores: preparation / STAR shape / truth.
Swap chairs and repeat.
