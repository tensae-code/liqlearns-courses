# Job interviews — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Arrive ______ minutes early (the
hands-on rule from the books' punctuality advice): ten.
**Answer: ten** — early enough to be calm, not so early
as to burden the office.

**2. (Level 2)** STAR in order: Situation, ______,
Action, Result. **Answer: Task** — what needed to be
done stands between the scene and your moves.

**3. (Level 2)** In the Action, say ______ for your own
moves, not "we". **Answer: I** — the interviewer is
hiring a person, not a crowd.

**4. (Level 3)** Armenia's honest seconds are bought with
______-time phrases ("That's a good question; let me
think…"). **Answer: thinking** — §1.8's named craft.
