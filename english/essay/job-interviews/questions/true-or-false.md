# Job interviews — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** Preparation is luck — a good candidate
improvises everything. **Answer: False** — every book
here prepares: advice, tips, etiquette, practice.

**2. (Level 2)** In STAR, the Result may be skipped if
the Action was long. **Answer: False** — the result is
the answer's point; a story without it is unfinished.

**3. (Level 3)** In a mock interview, swapping chairs
wastes the second half. **Answer: False** — Armenia's
pair practice runs both ways: asking the questions
teaches the answers' shape fastest.
