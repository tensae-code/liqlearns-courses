# Job interviews — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Cambodia's preparation advice does NOT
include — a) dress b) punctuality c) bringing friends to
answer for you **Answer: c)** — the unit prepares the
candidate: dress, punctuality, phone, body language.

**2. (Level 1)** Your phone in the interview room is —
a) on the table b) silent, in the bag c) answered politely
**Answer: b)** — phone discipline is named preparation,
in Cambodia's advice as filed.

**3. (Level 2)** STAR's third letter, A, is — a) Answer
b) Action — what YOU did c) Apology **Answer: b) Action**
— Egypt's method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

**4. (Level 2)** A STAR answer ends with — a) the Task
repeated b) a new question c) the Result — the outcome,
with a fact or number where honest **Answer: c)** — the
result closes the story; nothing follows it.

**5. (Level 3)** Libya's Workbook turns an interview's
direct questions into — a) complaints b) reported
questions c) CV lines **Answer: b) reported questions**
— "They asked me where I was working…" (p.121).
