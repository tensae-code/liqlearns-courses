# Video script — Job interviews

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

The interview is a test you can prepare for.

Before: learn about the place.
Dress clean. Come early. Phone off. Sit straight. Smile.

They ask: Tell me about a time when…
Do not ramble. Walk four stones.

S — the Situation. Where? When?
T — the Task. What did you have to do?
A — the Action. What did YOU do?
R — the Result. What happened at the end?

Short. True. Yours.

Practise with a friend. A mock interview.
The real one will feel like the second time.
Now you prepare — and you practise.
