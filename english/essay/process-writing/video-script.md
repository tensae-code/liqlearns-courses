# Video script — Process writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some writing tells. This writing DOES.

How do you make it? How do you play it? How do you grow it?
That is a procedure.

Three parts. Always three.
First — the goal. What are we making? "How to grow bean sprouts."
Second — the things we need. The materials. The ingredients.
Third — the steps. In order. One, two, three.

The steps speak like a teacher.
Short. Strong. The verb first.
Place the beans in a jar. Soak them in water. Cover the jar. Drain the water.
No "you". Just the verb. Do this. Then this.

Words hold the steps together.
First. Then. Next. After that. Finally.
They are hinges. The steps swing on them.

Careful! Four kinds of text. Four doors.
A procedure tells you what to DO.
A description tells you what a thing IS LIKE.
A recount tells you what HAPPENED.
A report tells you what IS TRUE, for everyone.

Now you try. One real task.
Goal. Things we need. Steps.
Give it to a friend. Say nothing. Watch them do it.
If they can do it — your writing works.
That is a procedure — in English.
