Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before any step begins, a procedure lists its ______ (ingredients or materials, with amounts). **Answer: materials / ingredients** — a step that discovers a missing thing is a broken step.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Split this step: 'Wash the beans and put them in a jar and cover them.' It hides ______ actions. **Answer: three** — one step, one action: wash — put — cover.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A text that tells what a thing IS LIKE, part by part, is a ______ text. **Answer: descriptive** — one of Indonesia's four named kinds.
