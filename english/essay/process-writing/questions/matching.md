# Process writing — Matching

Match each item to its match. One answer per item.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: procedure **Answer: tells what TO DO — goal, materials, steps** — the doing kind.

**2. (Level 2)** Match: report (as a text kind) **Answer: tells what IS TRUE in general — class, then facts** — Indonesia's report genre, the report lesson's kind at text size.

**3. (Level 3)** Match: the stranger test **Answer: the page is followed by a reader who may only do what it says; every stop is a fault in the writing** — instructions that work are proved by doing, not by reading (Nepal's instruction writing).
