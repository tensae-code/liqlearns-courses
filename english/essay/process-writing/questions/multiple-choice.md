# Process writing — Multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Process writing — the shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Process writing — the hinges · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Process writing — the four doors.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A procedure text's three parts, in order, are —
a) story, song, steps  b) goal, materials, steps  c) steps, goal, ending **Answer: b)** — Indonesia's shape: the goal first, the things needed, then the steps.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A step in a procedure is written as —
a) a bare imperative ('Drain the water')  b) a long past-tense story  c) a question **Answer: a)** — the step voice: verb at the front, standing present, one action (Indonesia).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The words first, then, next, after that, finally are the steps' —
a) decorations  b) hinges — they mark the turns so the reader knows where they stand  c) materials **Answer: b)** — Malaysia's process writing carries its turns on ordering words.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 'What happened when we grew the beans' belongs behind the door named —
a) procedure  b) recount  c) report **Answer: b)** — a recount tells what HAPPENED, once, in time order (Indonesia's genres).
