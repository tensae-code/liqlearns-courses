# Process writing — Short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Write step 1 of a hand-washing procedure, as an imperative. **Answer: Wet your hands (any single bare-imperative action earns it).** — Nepal's Unit 19 writes hand-washing instructions step by step.

**2. (Level 2)** Your procedure says 'cook it a bit and add some stuff'. Name two faults. **Answer: No exact action or time ('a bit' cannot be followed) and no named material ('stuff' was never listed).** — steps must be followable by a stranger with the page alone.

**3. (Level 3)** Same subject, two doors: write the GOAL line of a procedure about a bicycle, then the opening line of a report about bicycles. **Answer: Procedure: 'How to mend a bicycle puncture' (a doing goal); Report: 'Bicycles are two-wheeled machines…' (a general-truth opening).** — the door decides the first line before any sentence is written.
