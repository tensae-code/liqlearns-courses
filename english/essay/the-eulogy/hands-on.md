# Hands-On — The Eulogy

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Shape study.** Read a short eulogy (the book's pet eulogy, or one your teacher provides). Mark the three works in the margin: R (remember), T (thank), F (farewell).
2. **One-minute delivery.** Write and deliver the opening minute of a eulogy for a historical figure you admire. A partner times the pauses and reports where they helped.
3. **Plain-words pass.** Take a grand, flowery farewell paragraph and rewrite it plain and warm, keeping every fact. Read both; say what changed.
