# Levels — The Eulogy

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a eulogy is and what it must include | Say what a eulogy is, and name what a eulogy must include: the person's life and character, shared memories, what they gave others, and the farewell. |
| 2. Delivering a eulogy | Deliver a eulogy with the word choice, stance and body language, and vocal variety the occasion asks for. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the eulogy — the speech of praise and farewell spoken for someone who has died: what it is, what it must include, and how to deliver it with word choice, stance, body language and vocal variety.
