# Video Script — The Eulogy

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a quiet hall; one speaker standing before seated listeners, a photograph of an old man smiling on a table]

Some speeches are owed to a person. This one remembers… thanks… and says goodbye.

[Picture: three small scenes — the old man teaching a child, giving bread, waving from a doorway]

A memory shows who he was. Better than big words!

[Picture: the speaker standing still, speaking slowly, listeners nodding]

Slowly. Plainly. With pauses. The voice holds the room — gently.

[Picture: the speaker's last line; the listeners bow their heads together]

Remember. Thank. Farewell. That is a eulogy.
