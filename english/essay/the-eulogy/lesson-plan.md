# Lesson Plan — The Eulogy

**Unit:** Unit 38 — Speeches and Shows

**One thing:** the eulogy — the speech of praise and farewell spoken for someone who has died: what it is, what it must include, and how to deliver it with word choice, stance, body language and vocal variety.

## Sub lessons

1. What a eulogy is and what it must include
2. Delivering a eulogy

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The eulogy: what it is and what it must include (Lesson 1, Task 11 "Speak One's Piece", pp.293–295).
- Delivering a eulogy: word choice, stance and body language, vocal variety (pp.293–295).
- The model text is a short pet eulogy — the shape at small scale (p.294).
