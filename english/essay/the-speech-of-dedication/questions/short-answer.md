# Short Answer — The Speech of Dedication

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What three works must a dedication speech do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Name the dedication (whose name/cause the place carries); honour it (why the name belongs here); hand over (declare the place open to its purpose and its people).

2. How does a dedication differ from a eulogy? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A eulogy farewells a person who has died; a dedication begins a place's life — it is grave and glad at once, an act of opening, not of parting.

3. Give the five steps in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Open with the occasion; tell the story of the name; tell the story of the place; say what the place is for; dedicate — speak the formal handing-over words.

4. Why must names be exactly right in a dedication? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because the speech's act IS a naming: the name given in the speech is the name the place will carry; an error in it is an error in the act itself.
