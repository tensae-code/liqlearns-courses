# Levels — The Speech of Dedication

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a dedication speech is | Say what a speech of dedication is: the occasion it serves, the thing dedicated, and what the speech must do — name, honour, and hand over. |
| 2. Writing one in five steps | Write a speech of dedication by the book's five-step method. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the speech of dedication — the speech that gives a building, monument or park to its cause, person or event: what it is, what it should say, and the five-step method for writing one.
