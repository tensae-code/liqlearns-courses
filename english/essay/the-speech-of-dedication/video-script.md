# Video Script — The Speech of Dedication

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a new library with a ribbon across its doors, a crowd gathered]

A new place. A name to give. Someone must say the words!

[Picture: the speaker telling of the teacher whose name the library will carry]

Tell the story of the NAME. Tell the story of the PLACE.

[Picture: builders and givers in the crowd, nodding as they are thanked]

Thank the hands that built it. Say what will happen here!

[Picture: the ribbon cut, doors opening, children streaming in]

"I dedicate this library…" — spoken once, plainly. The place BEGINS.
