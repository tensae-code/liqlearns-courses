# Hands-On — The Speech of Dedication

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Dedication walk.** Around the school: find three named places (a hall, a tree, a bench). For each, say in one line what its dedication speech must have contained.
2. **Five-step draft race.** Pairs draft a dedication for the same imaginary place (a new reading garden) in fifteen minutes, steps headed. Swap and check: is step 5 an act?
3. **Act, not summary.** Each learner speaks only a step-5 paragraph for their chosen place. The class votes: did it sound like doors opening?
