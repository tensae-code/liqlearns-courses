# Video script — CV and cover letter

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

First, the advert. A job is calling.
Then two papers answer.

Paper one: the CV. Your life on one page. Two at most.
Your name. How to reach you.
One small line: this is the worker I am.
Your schools — newest first.
Your jobs — newest first.
Your skills. Your languages.
Your interests. People who can speak for you.

Paper two: the cover letter.
Short. For THIS job only.
It says: read my CV. I fit your advert. Here is why.

Advert. CV. Letter. Interview.
That is the chain.
Now you build your two papers.
