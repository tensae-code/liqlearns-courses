Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Profile statement — **Answer: the worker
in one breath, near the CV's top** — Syria step two,
Egypt section two.

**2. (Level 1)** References — **Answer: the CV's closing
section** — names who can speak for you, or "on request".

**3. (Level 2)** Qualifications table — **Answer: India's
format for the education block** — Hornbill prints the
CV's study record as a table.

**4. (Level 2)** Armenia §1.7 — **Answer: CV techniques,
formatting rules, section order, a sample reviewed** —
the order taught, then checked against a real-shaped CV.

**5. (Level 3)** Nepal Unit 11 / Laos Unit 10 Lesson 1 —
**Answer: teach the CV from a model read whole** — a
real-shaped life first, the pupil's own after.
