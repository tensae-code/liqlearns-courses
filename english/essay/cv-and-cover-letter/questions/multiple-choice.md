# CV and cover letter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Syria's nine steps END with — a) hobbies
b) references c) personal details **Answer: b)
references** — who speaks for you closes the CV; personal
details open it.

**2. (Level 1)** The chain's true order is — a) interview
→ CV → advert → letter b) advert → CV → cover letter →
interview c) letter → advert → CV → interview
**Answer: b)** — Cambodia's arc: nothing is written
before the advert is read.

**3. (Level 2)** Education is listed — a) oldest first
b) newest first (reverse chronological) c) by subject
**Answer: b) newest first** — Egypt and India agree: the
reader reads your life backwards from now.

**4. (Level 2)** Egypt's length law for a CV is —
a) as long as your life b) 1–2 pages maximum, relevant
only c) exactly five pages **Answer: b)** — short, clear,
relevant, updated.

**5. (Level 3)** Cambodia's matching drill asks the pupil
to — a) copy a model CV word for word b) find four
missing items in a flawed CV, then write a CV to match a
letter c) memorise nine steps **Answer: b)** — the CV is
learned by repair and by matching, not by copying.
