# CV and cover letter — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** The CV's second section, in Egypt's six,
is the personal ______ — 2–3 sentences on the worker you
are. **Answer: statement/objective** — Syria calls the
same block the profile statement.

**2. (Level 2)** Experience lines are carried by action
verbs: managed, assisted, ______. **Answer: organised**
(any of Egypt's taught set — developed, analysed,
collaborated — earns it).

**3. (Level 2)** Cambodia's CV contents list ends its
personal block with contact ______. **Answer: details**
— full name, date of birth, contact details open the CV.

**4. (Level 3)** The cover letter and CV are one act in
two papers: every letter claim stands on a ______ in the
CV. **Answer: line** — the matching law, Cambodia's way.
