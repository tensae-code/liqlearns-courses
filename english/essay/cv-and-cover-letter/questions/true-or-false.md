# CV and cover letter — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** A CV and a one-page resume are the same
thing in Syria's project. **Answer: False** — Project 2
defines the CV AGAINST the resume before stepping it out.

**2. (Level 2)** Work experience may be listed in any
order the writer likes. **Answer: False** — newest
first, from the present backwards (India's format,
Egypt's rule).

**3. (Level 3)** A strong CV for one job can be sent,
unchanged, for every job. **Answer: False** — relevance
is per-advert; Cambodia writes the CV to match its letter
and its post.
