# Hands-on — CV and cover letter

A real writing task — the pair, to one advert.

## The advert (invented for this lesson)
*Needed: Shop Assistant, Bright Goods Store. Finished
secondary school; careful with money; friendly to
customers; some computer skill an advantage. Apply to
the Manager, Bright Goods Store.*

## Task 1 — your CV (frame; your facts inside)
FULL NAME
[phone] · [email] · [town]
PROFILE — one or two sentences: the worker you are.
EDUCATION — newest first: school, years, certificate.
EXPERIENCE — newest first: job or helping work, dates,
two action verbs per line (served, counted, organised…).
SKILLS — money, computer, languages, each with its level.
INTERESTS — two lines at most.
REFERENCES — two names, or "available on request".

## Task 2 — the cover letter
Use the five-move order from Job application letters,
aimed at THIS advert: post — person — fit (money care,
friendliness, computer skill, in the advert's order) —
enclosure — meeting.

## Check
- Is every fit claim in the letter found in the CV?
- Is anything in the CV there that this job never asked
  for? Move it out or shrink it.
