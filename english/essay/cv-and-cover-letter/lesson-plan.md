# Lesson plan — CV and cover letter

**Course:** English · **Title:** Essay · **Lesson:** CV and cover letter
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 35, "Writing for a Job" —
the first lesson of the level.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the CV — its
sections and their order — and the cover letter matched to
one advert, inside the whole job-seeking chain: advert →
CV → cover letter → interview.
**This lesson's own level:** it gathers The formal letter
and Job application letters into the chain Cambodia teaches
whole; the interview itself is the level's last lesson.
**What the learner can do at the end:** build a one-to-two
page CV in the books' section order and write its cover
letter to a named advert.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The formal letter; Job application
  letters. Recommended, never forced.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of a chain of four cards: ADVERT,
   CV, COVER LETTER, INTERVIEW.** What the student does:
   the student walks the chain aloud, saying what each
   card carries.
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a CV as stacked labelled
   blocks, newest study at the top of its block.**
   What the student does: the student re-stacks shuffled
   blocks into the true order.

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: a CV in the wrong order — what breaks?
2. **The nine steps** (Syria's project) and the six
   sections (Egypt) set side by side.
3. **The chain whole** (Cambodia Units 29–31's arc:
   adverts, application, interview).
4. **The cover letter matched** — Cambodia's coaching,
   India's chain.
5. **Do it yourself**: hands-on CV + cover letter pair.

## If the learner is stuck
- CV swelling past two pages: Egypt's law — short,
  relevant only; cut what this job never asked for.
- Sections shuffled: newest first inside education and
  work — the reader reads your life backwards from now.
- Next in this level: Personal statements and proposals.
