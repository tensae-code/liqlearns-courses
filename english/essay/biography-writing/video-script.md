# Video script — Biography writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A life is a long story. This lesson writes it.

Two kinds. Listen.
Biography: another person writes the life.
He was born. She worked. They built.
Autobiography: the person writes their own life.
I was born. I worked. I built.

Same facts. Different teller.

Now the road of a life. In order.
First: who is this person? Why do we care?
Then: born where? Born when? The early years.
Then: the big deeds. The work. The hard years.
Then: the late years.
Last: why this life still matters.

Dates are the road signs. Keep them.
Past tense carries it all.
He was born in 1911. He wrote. He won. He died in 2006.

Write another life first. That is biography.
Then turn it: I was born… That is autobiography.
One life. Two voices. You can write both.
