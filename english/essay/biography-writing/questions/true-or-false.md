# Biography writing — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** A biography and an autobiography differ
only in length. **Answer: False** — they differ in the
teller: another person writes the biography; the person
writes the autobiography.

**2. (Level 2)** A biography may tell the later life
before the early life if the writer prefers it.
**Answer: False** — Egypt's order is the life's order:
early life before major achievements before later life.

**3. (Level 3)** In the turn from biography to
autobiography, the facts and dates must also change.
**Answer: False** — only the person changes; South
Sudan's drill keeps the same life under a new voice.
