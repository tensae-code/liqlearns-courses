# Biography writing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Biography writing — the pair and the person · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Biography writing — life order and key dates · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 1 — Biography writing — the pair and the person.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A biography and an autobiography differ
only in length. **Answer: False** — they differ in the
teller: another person writes the biography; the person
writes the autobiography.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A biography may tell the later life
before the early life if the writer prefers it.
**Answer: False** — Egypt's order is the life's order:
early life before major achievements before later life.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the turn from biography to
autobiography, the facts and dates must also change.
**Answer: False** — only the person changes; South
Sudan's drill keeps the same life under a new voice.
