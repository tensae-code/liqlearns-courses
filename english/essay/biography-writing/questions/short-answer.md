# Biography writing — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Egypt Secondary 12's biography
questions. **Answer: who — when and where — what — why
important** — the frame the five parts answer.

**2. (Level 2)** Your biography draft has deeds but no
years anywhere. What is missing? **Answer: the key dates**
— dates and linking words carry the frame; without them
the facts are a heap.

**3. (Level 3)** South Sudan's Unit 6 sets two writings
of your own after the turn. Name them. **Answer: your own
autobiography, and a community biography** — Primary 7,
Unit 6, pp.67–82.
