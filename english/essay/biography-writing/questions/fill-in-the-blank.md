# Biography writing — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Egypt's short definition: a biography is
the story of a person's life — who they are, what they
did, and ______ they are important. **Answer: why** —
the third leg of the Biography Tip.

**2. (Level 2)** Secondary 12's five parts end with the
______ — why the life is remembered. **Answer:
conclusion** — after introduction, early life, major
achievements, later life.

**3. (Level 2)** Armenia Grade 7 sets the biography of a
famous ______ inside its Olympics unit. **Answer:
person** — English 7, Unit 5, p.119.

**4. (Level 3)** Ghana's Basic 9 curriculum has learners
analyse the ______ of events across autobiography,
biography, narrative and drama. **Answer: sequence** —
curriculum evidence only; the order is the studied thing.
