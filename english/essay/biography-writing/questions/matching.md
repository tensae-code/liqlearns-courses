# Biography writing — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Biography — **Answer: a life written by
another person** — third person, South Sudan's definition.

**2. (Level 1)** Autobiography — **Answer: a life written
by the person themselves** — first person; Malala's book
is South Sudan's worked example.

**3. (Level 2)** Key dates — **Answer: the road signs of
the life — born, schooled, the great deed, the end** —
Egypt's frame is carried by dates and linking words.

**4. (Level 2)** Ahmed Idris — **Answer: the cloze model
biography of Egypt's Preparatory 8 book** — the Nubian
soldier honoured in 2017, beside the Biography Tip.

**5. (Level 3)** Naguib Mahfouz (1911–2006) — **Answer:
Egypt Secondary 12's model biography** — Cairo Trilogy,
Nobel 1988; the structure is read off his life.
