# Biography writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** An autobiography is — a) a life written
by another person b) a life written by the person
themselves c) a life with no dates **Answer: b)** —
South Sudan Primary 7's definition, Unit 6.

**2. (Level 1)** A biography of a singer is written in —
a) the first person b) the second person c) the third
person **Answer: c)** — he/she: the teller stands beside
the life, not inside it (Egypt Secondary 12).

**3. (Level 2)** Egypt Secondary 12's order is — a) later
life, early life, introduction b) introduction, early
life, major achievements, later life, conclusion
c) conclusion first, facts after **Answer: b)** — the
life in the order it was lived.

**4. (Level 2)** The tense that carries a biography is —
a) the future b) the present continuous c) the past
**Answer: c)** — Egypt's frame: past tense, third person,
dates and linking words.

**5. (Level 3)** Rewriting a biography as an autobiography,
you change — a) the facts b) the order c) the person —
he/she becomes I **Answer: c)** — South Sudan's own drill:
same life, new teller.
