# Biography writing — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — a life told in order — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the pair and the person
first, then the life order and its dates, then the turn between
the two voices.

## Level 1 — the pair and the person
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell
biography from autobiography by who writes it, and hold the
person steady (third for another's life, first for your own).

## Level 2 — life order and key dates
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run a life
in the books' order — introduction, early life, major
achievements, later life, conclusion — with key dates
carrying it and the past tense throughout.

## Level 3 — the turn
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: rewrite a
biography as an autobiography (and back) changing only the
voice, and judge a life-piece against the kind's definition:
who, what they did, why they matter.
