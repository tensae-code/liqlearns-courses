This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — a
life told in order — cut at the lesson's own jumps: the pair
and the person first, then the life order and its dates.

## Sub lesson 1 — Biography writing — the pair and the person
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell
biography from autobiography by who writes it, and hold the
person steady (third for another's life, first for your own);
and rewrite a biography as an autobiography (and back)
changing only the voice, and judge a life-piece against the
kind's definition: who, what they did, why they matter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Biography writing — life order and key dates
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run a life
in the books' order — introduction, early life, major
achievements, later life, conclusion — with key dates
carrying it and the past tense throughout.
