# Hands-on — Biography writing

A real writing task — one life, told twice.

## Task 1 — the biography (frame; a real life inside)
Choose a person you can check facts about — a family elder,
a teacher, a known figure. Gather the key dates first.
[Name] was born in [year] in [place].
[Introduction: who they are, and why they matter in one line.]
Early life — [family, school, the young years, with dates.]
Major achievements — [the big deeds in the order they
happened, each with its date or year.]
Later life — [the last years, honours, how the life ended
or where it stands now.]
Conclusion — [why this life is remembered; the quality that
made them known.]

## Task 2 — the turn (South Sudan's own drill)
Rewrite your first two paragraphs as autobiography: the
same facts, the same order — I was born… My school was…
Change the person, nothing else.

## Check
- Can a reader walk the life from birth to the end without
  jumping backwards?
- Is every big step dated?
- Did the voice hold — one teller, start to finish?
