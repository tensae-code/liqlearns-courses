# Hands-On — Making a Short Documentary Film

Things you do with real things around you. You need a phone or tablet and a notebook.

1. **Plan on paper.** Choose a true subject you can walk to. Write the four stages: the subject; three things you must find out; the story in one sentence; a shot list of six to ten pieces, with three interview questions.
2. **Scout without filming.** Visit the place once with no camera. Watch and listen. Fix your shot list: cross out what is not there, add what you missed.
3. **Film it.** Follow the list. Two hands, steady, close enough to hear. Film each piece twice. Tick the list as you go.
4. **Show and test.** Put the pieces in order and show the film to someone who knows nothing about the subject. Ask: "What did you learn?" Write down the one thing they missed — that is the piece to film or explain better next time.
