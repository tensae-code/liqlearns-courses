# Lesson Plan — Making a Short Documentary Film

**One thing:** making a short documentary film — planning a true story in stages, filming it on a phone or tablet, and putting the pieces together so a viewer learns something real.

## Sub lessons

1. Planning a short documentary
2. Shooting and putting it together

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A documentary is a film that tells a TRUE story: real people, real places, real events — nothing invented (Grades 10–12 guide, In Search of Authentic First Peoples' Voice unit, printed pp.71–74).
- Plan before you film, in developmental stages: choose the subject, find out about it, decide what the film must show and say, and list the shots and interview questions you need (printed pp.71–74).
- Shoot on a phone or tablet: hold it steady, get close enough to see and hear, and film more pieces than you think you need (printed pp.71–74).
- Putting it together: choose the best pieces and order them so the story is clear; the editing stage is optional, and your voice or words on screen can explain what the viewer is seeing (printed pp.71–74).
