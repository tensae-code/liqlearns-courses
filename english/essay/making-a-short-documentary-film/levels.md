# Levels — Making a Short Documentary Film

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Planning a short documentary | Choose a true subject, find out about it, state the film's one-sentence story, and write a shot list with interview questions. |
| 2. Shooting and putting it together | Film steady, close pieces on a phone or tablet, choose the best ones, and order them — with voice or words where needed — so a viewer learns the story. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can plan, film and finish a short documentary about something true near them.
