# Video Script — Making a Short Documentary Film

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The phone is held up and pointed at as each step is named.

[Picture: a phone filming a baker at work in a small shop]

A documentary is a film. It tells a true story. Real people. Real places. Nothing made up.

[Picture: a paper plan with four lines: subject — find out — story — shots]

First, we plan. Choose one true thing. Find out about it. Say the story in one sentence. Write the list of shots.

[Picture: two hands holding a phone steady, filming close to the baker's hands]

Then we film. Hold the phone steady. Come close. Film more than you need.

[Picture: filmed pieces laid in a row on a table, being put in order]

Last, we put the best pieces in order. Now the viewer learns the story. You made a documentary!
