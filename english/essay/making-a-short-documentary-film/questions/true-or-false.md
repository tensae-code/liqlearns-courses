# True or False — Making a Short Documentary Film

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A documentary may invent its story and still be a documentary. (Sub lesson 1) — False
2. Planning comes before filming. (Sub lesson 1) — True
3. A shot list names the pieces you plan to film. (Sub lesson 1) — True
4. A documentary can be shot on a phone or a tablet. (Sub lesson 2) — True
5. You should film each thing only once, as fast as you can. (Sub lesson 2) — False
6. Putting a film together means choosing the best pieces and ordering them so the story is clear. (Sub lesson 2) — True
