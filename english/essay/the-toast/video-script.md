# Video Script — The Toast

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a feast table; one guest standing, glass raised, the room turned toward her]

A speech in thirty seconds! Small — and mighty. This is a TOAST.

[Picture: the guest naming the honoured elder, one bright memory flashing beside her]

Name him! One true stroke — why he deserves the glass!

[Picture: glasses rising all around the table]

A wish for him… and UP go the glasses!

[Picture: everyone drinking together, laughing]

Name. Stroke. Wish. Lift. Small speech — whole room!
