# Hands-On — The Toast

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Cut to a toast.** Pairs take one paragraph of any speech and cut it to a toast of under a minute: name, stroke, wish, lift. Deliver both; the class feels the difference.
2. **Occasion round.** Toast cards (a win, a farewell, a harvest, a newborn, Mother Earth). Each learner draws one and composes a toast on the spot in five minutes.
3. **Model study.** Read the book's kind of model toast. Mark its four beats and time its spoken length.
