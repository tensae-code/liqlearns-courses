# Levels — The Toast

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a toast is — and is not | Say what a toast is and how it differs from a full speech: length, occasion, the raised glass, the single tribute thought. |
| 2. Composing a toast for an occasion | Compose a toast for a real occasion using the book's models: name, tribute in one vivid stroke, wish, lift. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the toast — the short tribute speech spoken over a raised glass: how it differs from a full speech, and how to compose one for an occasion.
