# Hands-on — The formal letter

A real writing task, with a model frame. Write the letter;
do not copy the frame's words into your facts.

## The task
Your school library has lost the book you returned last
week and is charging you for it. Write a formal letter to
the head librarian asking for the charge to be checked and
removed. Invent the names, dates and book title.

## The model frame (shape only — your words go inside)
[Your address, top right — three short lines]
[Date]
[Reader's name or office, and address, left]
Dear Sir or Madam,   *(or: Dear Ms …, if you know the name)*
**Subject: …** *(one line naming the matter)*
Paragraph 1 — I am writing to … *(say why you write, in
one or two sentences.)*
Paragraph 2 — the facts, in time order. *(What happened,
when, what proof you have.)*
Paragraph 3 — I would be grateful if you could … *(the
one action you ask for, politely.)*
Yours faithfully,   *(or Yours sincerely, to a name)*
[Your full name]

## Check before you send it (in the lesson, not in life)
- Every part in its seat? Greeting and closing a true pair?
- No contractions, no chatty words? One point per paragraph?
