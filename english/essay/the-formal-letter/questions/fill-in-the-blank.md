Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The letter's last written line, under the
signature space, is the writer's ______.
**Answer: name** — the signature signs above the printed
full name, the anatomy's final block.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dear Mr Haddad, … Yours ______, …
**Answer: sincerely** — a greeted name pairs with the
sincerely close.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first body paragraph's one job is to
say ______ you write. **Answer: why** — purpose first,
facts after, the ask last.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Formal partner of "ask for": ______.
**Answer: request** — Uzbekistan's style pairs put the
formal word in the formal letter, everywhere.
