# The formal letter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In the formal letter's layout, what comes
immediately after the date? a) the signature b) the
receiver's address and greeting c) the subject line only
**Answer: b)** — Greece's anatomy runs date, then the
receiver's side and greeting, before the body opens.

**2. (Level 1)** The optional one-line heading that names
the letter's matter is the — a) complimentary close
b) subject line c) salutation **Answer: b) subject line**
— it sits between greeting and opening when used.

**3. (Level 2)** "Dear Sir or Madam" closes with —
a) Yours sincerely b) Yours faithfully c) Best wishes
**Answer: b) Yours faithfully** — no name was greeted, so
the faithful close pairs with it.

**4. (Level 2)** "Dear Ms Rao" closes with — a) Yours
faithfully b) Yours sincerely c) Cheers **Answer: b)
Yours sincerely** — a named greeting takes the sincerely
close; the pair must agree.

**5. (Level 3)** Which opening is formal register?
a) "Hi! I need a new certificate, quick." b) "I am
writing to request a copy of my certificate." c) "You
know my certificate? I lost it." **Answer: b)** — full
sentence, no contraction, purpose stated in one breath.
