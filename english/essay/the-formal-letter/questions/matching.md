# The formal letter — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Headed notepaper / sender's address —
**Answer: the top block of the letter** — the anatomy
begins with who sends, before who receives.

**2. (Level 1)** Complimentary close — **Answer: the
polite ending phrase above the signature** — "Yours
faithfully" and its pair live here.

**3. (Level 2)** Dear Sir or Madam — **Answer: Yours
faithfully** — the no-name pair.

**4. (Level 2)** Subject line — **Answer: names the
letter's matter in one line** — the reader knows the
business before the body begins.

**5. (Level 3)** "Tell me the price" — **Answer: "Please
inform me of the price"** — the formal partner keeps the
register whole.
