# The formal letter — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** The date stands below the receiver's
address in a formal letter. **Answer: False** — in the
books' anatomy the date sits with the sender's side,
above the receiver's address.

**2. (Level 2)** A subject line is required in every
formal letter. **Answer: False** — Greece lists it as
optional; the greeting and close are not optional.

**3. (Level 3)** Contractions like "I'm" and "can't"
belong in a formal letter. **Answer: False** — the formal
register writes full forms; contractions are the informal
dress.
