# Video script — The formal letter

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A formal letter is a letter in its best clothes.

It has a shape. Every part has its seat.

Top: your address. Then the date.
Then the address of the person who will read it.
Then: Dear Sir or Madam. Or: Dear Mr Haddad.

Then one line that says what the letter is about.
Then the letter itself. Short paragraphs. One point each.
First: why you write. Then: the facts. Last: what you ask.

Then the closing.
No name at the top? Yours faithfully.
A name at the top? Yours sincerely.
Then your name.

And the words stay formal.
Not "I want" — "I would like".
Not "tell me" — "please inform me".

Same message. Best clothes.
Now you lay one out.
