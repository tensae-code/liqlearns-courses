# Video script — Postcards and personal letters

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

You are on holiday. A friend is at home.
You want to send the day to them. Two ways.

Way one: the postcard. Small. Very small.
Front: a picture of the place.
Back: a few lines only. So choose.

Greetings from the sea!
The weather is hot. The food is delicious.
Yesterday we swam and took photos.
Wish you were here!
See you soon. Take care.

That is all. And it is enough.
Place. Weather. Food. One happy line. Your name.

Way two: the letter. The big warm room.
Dear friend. I am writing because…
Then the news — all of it, in order.
Then a kind closing. Then your name.

Small card, short love. Long letter, long love.
Both go to ONE person. Write to them like it.
