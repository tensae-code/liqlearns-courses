# Postcards and personal letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** On Egypt's model postcard, the writing
side opens with — a) the address b) a greeting c) the
stamp **Answer: b) a greeting** — then holiday news,
"Wish you were here!", closing + name (Primary 5, p.89).

**2. (Level 1)** The postcard's front carries — a) the
letter's body b) a photo of the place c) the signature
**Answer: b)** — the front says where with a picture;
the back says it in words.

**3. (Level 2)** Saudi Workbook Goal 96 asks the pupil to
— a) read Omar's postcard from Jeddah and write their own
b) memorise the Jeddah address c) write a formal report
**Answer: a)** — model first, own card after, printed p.234.

**4. (Level 2)** Malaysia Form 3 names the postcard's two
cares as — a) spelling and length of address b) linking
words and phrases, and writing the correct amount
c) rhyme and rhythm **Answer: b)** — Unit 8's writing
lesson; the correct amount IS the space limit.

**5. (Level 3)** Egypt's friendly-letter checklist runs —
a) body, greeting, signature, body again b) greeting,
introduction, body, closing, signature c) signature first
**Answer: b)** — five parts, Primary 5 Unit 1, p.22.
