# Postcards and personal letters — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Postcards and personal letters — the postcard's parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Postcards and personal letters — the space limit · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Postcards and personal letters — the personal letter.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four loads every postcard must
carry. **Answer: the place, the weather, the food, one
happening (with its feeling)** — the moves the Jeddah and
Istanbul models both run.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your card's back is full and the closing
is still unwritten. What do you cut? **Answer: a
happening** — never the place, the feeling line or the
closing; the amount must fit the card.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name Egypt's five friendly-letter parts
in order. **Answer: greeting — introduction — body —
closing — signature** — Primary 5 Unit 1, p.22.
