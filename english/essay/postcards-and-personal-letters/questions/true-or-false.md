# Postcards and personal letters — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** A postcard can carry as much writing as
a letter if the writing is small. **Answer: False** —
the space limit is the form; Malaysia teaches writing
the correct amount for the card.

**2. (Level 2)** Mongolia's Self-Check asks pupils to copy
the Istanbul card word for word. **Answer: False** — they
upgrade its adjectives, then write their OWN postcard.

**3. (Level 3)** A personal letter may open with its body,
since the reader is a friend. **Answer: False** — Egypt's
checklist holds for friends too: greeting, then the
introduction that says why you are writing.
