# Postcards and personal letters — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Postcards and personal letters — the postcard's parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Postcards and personal letters — the space limit · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Postcards and personal letters — the personal letter.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A postcard can carry as much writing as
a letter if the writing is small. **Answer: False** —
the space limit is the form; Malaysia teaches writing
the correct amount for the card.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mongolia's Self-Check asks pupils to copy
the Istanbul card word for word. **Answer: False** — they
upgrade its adjectives, then write their OWN postcard.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A personal letter may open with its body,
since the reader is a friend. **Answer: False** — Egypt's
checklist holds for friends too: greeting, then the
introduction that says why you are writing.
