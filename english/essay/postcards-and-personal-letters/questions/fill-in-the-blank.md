# Postcards and personal letters — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** Egypt's model postcard carries holiday
news in the ______ tense. **Answer: past** — what you did
yesterday, told tonight (p.89).

**2. (Level 2)** Malaysia's Writing Reference keeps a plan
and useful phrases for the postcard at printed pp.______.
**Answer: 176–181** — the reference span, postcard among
six kinds.

**3. (Level 2)** A postcard that runs past its back has
broken Malaysia's rule of writing the correct ______.
**Answer: amount** — cut happenings, keep place, weather,
food, feeling.

**4. (Level 3)** The friendly letter ends, before the
signature, with a ______ — end politely. **Answer:
closing** — part four of Egypt's checklist.
