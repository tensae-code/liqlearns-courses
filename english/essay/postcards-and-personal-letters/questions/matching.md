# Postcards and personal letters — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "Wish you were here!" — **Answer: the
postcard's feeling line, just before the closing** —
Egypt's model card, p.89.

**2. (Level 1)** Address side — **Answer: name and country
of the one person the card is for** — a postcard is
written to one named reader.

**3. (Level 2)** Istanbul card — **Answer: Mongolia VIII's
Self-Check model — read it, upgrade its adjectives, then
write your own** — printed p.90.

**4. (Level 2)** Upgrade the adjectives — **Answer:
Mongolia's second move — swap plain words for strong ones
(delicious, wonderful, fantastic) from the box** — on a
card, adjectives carry whole sentences' work.

**5. (Level 3)** Introduction (letter) — **Answer: say why
you are writing** — part two of Egypt's five-part
friendly-letter checklist.
