# Postcards and personal letters — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — writing a real day to one real
person — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
postcard's parts first, then its space limit, then the letter's
full warm shape.

## Level 1 — the postcard's parts
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the
postcard's moves in order (greeting, the place and its news,
"Wish you were here!", closing + name) and what stands on
its other side (name, country, the photo of the place).

## Level 2 — the space limit
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fit the
correct amount — place, weather, food, one happening, one
feeling — inside a card's few lines, with linking words
holding it together.

## Level 3 — the personal letter
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a
personal letter on the five-part frame (greeting, why I am
writing, body, polite closing, signature) with the warm
voice the postcard taught, at letter length.
