# Hands-on — Postcards and personal letters

A real writing task — one holiday, two sizes.

## Task 1 — the postcard (frame; your place inside)
Front: a photo or drawing of the place you are in.
Back, left side (six short lines at most):
Greetings from [place]!
[The weather in one line.]
[The food in one line.]
[Yesterday's best happening, past tense, one line.]
Wish you were here!
[Closing — See you soon! / Take care,] [your name]
Back, right side: [friend's name] / [town] / [country]

## Task 2 — the personal letter
Same holiday, letter length, to the same friend:
Dear [name],
[Introduction: why you are writing, in one or two lines.]
[Body: the holiday as news — place, days, people, food,
feelings — in two or three paragraphs.]
[Closing: end warmly and politely — a question back to
them, a wish, a promise to write again.]
[Your name]

## Check
- Cover the postcard's back: could your friend say where
  you are, how the weather and food are, and one thing you
  did? All four must survive.
- Did anything cross the card's edge? Cut happenings first,
  never the place, the feeling or the closing.
