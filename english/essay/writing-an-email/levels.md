# Levels — Writing an Email

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The address and the subject | Start an email correctly: write the recipient address at the top, and a subject line that names what the email is about. |
| 2. The body, the closing and the signature | Complete an email: write a message body that keeps to the subject, end with a closing, and sign with a signature — the five parts in their fixed order. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can build a complete email whose parts each do their own job: the address says who it is for, the subject says what it is about, the body carries the message, and the closing and signature say who sent it.
