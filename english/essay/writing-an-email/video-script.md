# Video Script — Writing an Email

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to each part of the email as its name is said.

[Picture: a computer screen with an empty email, its five parts waiting to be filled]

Look! This is an email. An email has five parts. Five! Let us build it, part by part.

[Picture: a finger pointing to the address line at the top of the email]

First, the address. Who gets this email? The address says who. It stands at the top.

[Picture: the subject line filling with words — "Football practice on Saturday"]

Next, the subject. One short line. It says: this email is about THIS. You read it before you open the email.

[Picture: the middle of the email filling with a short message]

Now the middle — the body. This is the message. The real words you want to say.

[Picture: the end of the email — a closing line, then a name]

And at the end: a closing, and your name — your signature. Now everyone knows: this email is from YOU.

Address. Subject. Body. Closing. Signature. Five parts — that is an email!
