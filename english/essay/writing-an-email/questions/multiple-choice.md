# Multiple Choice — Writing an Email

1. The very first part of an email is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The signature
   b) The recipient address ✓
   c) The closing
   d) The message body

2. The recipient address tells: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) What the email is about

3. The subject of an email is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The longest part of the email
   b) The sender's name

4. The subject is useful because it is read: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Only after the whole email

5. The message body of an email is: (Sub lesson 2)

   b) The message itself, in the middle of the email ✓
   c) The sender's name at the end
   d) The subject line written twice

6. An email ends with: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The address and the subject
   b) A second body
   c) The closing and then the signature ✓
   d) Nothing — it just stops

7. The signature in an email is: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The sender's name, signing the email off ✓
   b) The reader's address
   c) The subject written in capitals
