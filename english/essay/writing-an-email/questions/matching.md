# Matching — Writing an Email

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match each part of an email to its job.

1. Recipient address (Sub lesson 1)
2. Subject (Sub lesson 1)
3. Message body (Sub lesson 2)
4. Closing (Sub lesson 2)
5. Signature (Sub lesson 2)

   a) says what the email is about, in one short line
   b) the sender's name, signing the email off
   c) says who the email is going to
   d) the short ending line that rounds the message off
   e) the message itself

Answers: 1–c, 2–a, 3–e, 4–d, 5–b ✓
