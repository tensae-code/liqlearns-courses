# Short Answer — Writing an Email

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the five parts of an email, in their order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Recipient address, subject, message body, closing, signature.

2. What does the recipient address do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — It says who the email is going to; it stands at the very top, and the email can only reach the right person if the address is right.

3. Why is a clear subject better than "Hello"? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The subject is read before the email is opened, so it must say what the email is about; "Hello" could belong to any email and names nothing.

4. Where does the message body stand in the email, and what does it hold? (Sub lesson 2)
   — In the middle, between the subject and the closing; it holds the message itself — the thing the subject promised.

5. What question do the closing and the signature answer together? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Who sent this email — the closing rounds the message off and the signature is the sender's name signing it.
