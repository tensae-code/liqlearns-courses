# Lesson Plan — Writing an Email

**One thing:** the basic structure of an email — the five fixed parts every email has: the recipient address, the subject, the message body, the closing and the signature.

## Sub lessons

1. The address and the subject
2. The body, the closing and the signature

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The email starts with the recipient address: the person the email is going to (Segona programme, BOPA p.57).
- Then the subject: the short line that says what the email is about, read before the email is opened (p.57).
- The message body is the middle part: the message itself (p.57).
- The email ends with a closing and then the signature — the sender's name signing the message off (p.57).
- The order is fixed: address, subject, body, closing, signature (p.57).
