# The essay — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lessons 1–2 (Set 1, parts and jobs — its essay-whole item is the definition, Sub lesson 1; the part items are the structure, Sub lesson 2) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Planning the Essay (Set 2) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The Skeleton Test (Set 3, fault and repair).

Match each part to its job, and each fault to its repair.

**Set 1 (Sub lessons 1–2)** — parts and jobs (Bolivia's table).
1. Introduction — **tells what the topic and main ideas
   are**
2. Topic paragraphs — **state the points/arguments, one
   per paragraph**
3. Conclusion — **the introduction's information, from a
   different perspective**
4. The essay whole — **one topic, several main points,
   five or more paragraphs**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 3)** — Ghana's process at essay scale.
1. brainstorm — **spill every idea, unjudged**
2. mind map / piles — **group ideas into main points**
3. outline — **fix the points' order in writing**
4. drafting — **prose, from the plan**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 4)** — fault and repair.
1. a paragraph with two openers — **two points sharing a
   room: split it**
2. a point with one thin idea — **grow the pile or drop
   the point**
3. a conclusion announcing a new point — **move it to the
   body plan, or cut it**
