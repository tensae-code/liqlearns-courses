# The essay — matching

Match each part to its job, and each fault to its repair.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — parts and jobs (Bolivia's table).
1. Introduction — **tells what the topic and main ideas
   are**
2. Topic paragraphs — **state the points/arguments, one
   per paragraph**
3. Conclusion — **the introduction's information, from a
   different perspective**
4. The essay whole — **one topic, several main points,
   five or more paragraphs**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — Ghana's process at essay scale.
1. brainstorm — **spill every idea, unjudged**
2. mind map / piles — **group ideas into main points**
3. outline — **fix the points' order in writing**
4. drafting — **prose, from the plan**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — fault and repair.
1. a paragraph with two openers — **two points sharing a
   room: split it**
2. a point with one thin idea — **grow the pile or drop
   the point**
3. a conclusion announcing a new point — **move it to the
   body plan, or cut it**
