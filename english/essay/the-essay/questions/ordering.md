# The essay — ordering

Stand the items in their true order.

**1. (Level 1)** The general structure:
Topic 2 / Introduction / Conclusion / Topic 1 / Topic 3.
**Answer:** Introduction — Topic 1 — Topic 2 — Topic 3 —
Conclusion.

**2. (Level 2)** Ghana's process: drafting / outline /
brainstorm / revising / mind map.
**Answer:** brainstorm — mind map — outline — drafting —
revising (editing/proofreading after, CUPS in hand).

**3. (Level 2)** Outline these points for an essay "My
morning" in a felt order: I reach the market / I wake / I
wash and dress / I eat breakfast.
**Answer:** I wake — I wash and dress — I eat breakfast —
I reach the market (time order, felt at a glance).

**4. (Level 3)** Restore the skeleton — topic sentences in
essay order: "And that is why the river school matters." /
"The school stands by the river." / "Its teachers come by
boat." / "Its children learn the water's ways."
**Answer:** "The school stands by the river." (names the
topic) — "Its teachers come by boat." (Topic 1) — "Its
children learn the water's ways." (Topic 2) — "And that is
why the river school matters." (conclusion, returning).
