# The essay — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Essay Defined (item 1) and Sub lesson 2 — The Essay's Structure (items 2, 3 and 5) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Planning the Essay · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The Skeleton Test.

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** introduction · topic · conclusion · outline ·
several · skeleton

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An essay is about one topic with ______
main points.
**Answer: several.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ tells what the topic and main
ideas are.
**Answer: introduction.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Each ______ paragraph states one point.
**Answer: topic.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Brainstorm, then mind map, then ______ —
the plan in writing.
**Answer: outline.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ repeats the introduction's
information from a different perspective.
**Answer: conclusion.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Reading only the topic sentences is the
______ test.
**Answer: skeleton.**
