# The essay — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** introduction · topic · conclusion · outline ·
several · skeleton

**1. (Level 1)** An essay is about one topic with ______
main points.
**Answer: several.**

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ tells what the topic and main
ideas are.
**Answer: introduction.**

**3. (Level 1)** Each ______ paragraph states one point.
**Answer: topic.**

**4. (Level 2)** Brainstorm, then mind map, then ______ —
the plan in writing.
**Answer: outline.**

**5. (Level 1)** The ______ repeats the introduction's
information from a different perspective.
**Answer: conclusion.**

**6. (Level 3)** Reading only the topic sentences is the
______ test.
**Answer: skeleton.**
