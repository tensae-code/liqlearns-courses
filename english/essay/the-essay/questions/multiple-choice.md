# The essay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Bolivia's definition: an essay is a piece
of writing that usually has —
a) one paragraph about many topics  b) five or more
paragraphs, about one topic with several main points
c) exactly three paragraphs about anything
**Answer: b)** — the definition whole.

**2. (Level 1)** The introduction's job is to —
a) tell everything in detail  b) tell what the topic and
main ideas are  c) end the essay early
**Answer: b)** — it names; the body tells.

**3. (Level 1)** The conclusion holds —
a) new points never mentioned  b) the same information as
the introduction, written from a different perspective
c) the longest story
**Answer: b)** — Bolivia's table's own words.

**4. (Level 2)** Ghana's pre-writing, at essay scale, is —
a) brainstorm, mind map, outline  b) copy a model first
c) write the conclusion first, always
**Answer: a).**

**5. (Level 2)** Each topic paragraph must have —
a) five points  b) its own naming (topic) sentence and one
point  c) a picture
**Answer: b)** — Paragraph law at essay scale.

**6. (Level 3)** The skeleton test reads —
a) every word slowly  b) only the topic sentences, in
order, to hear the whole essay in miniature  c) the
conclusion only
**Answer: b)** — and a paragraph whose opener can't be
found has two points or none.
