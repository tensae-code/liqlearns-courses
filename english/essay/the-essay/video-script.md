# Video script — The essay

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An essay! Bigger than a paragraph!

Bolivia says: **five or more paragraphs,
ONE topic, SEVERAL main points.**

First — the INTRODUCTION.
It tells the topic. It names the points.
Then — TOPIC 1! Its own paragraph.
TOPIC 2! Its own paragraph.
TOPIC 3! Its own paragraph.
Last — the CONCLUSION.
The same ideas — from the other side!

Plan FIRST! Brainstorm! Piles! Outline!
Then write. The road is decided!

Read only the first sentences — the skeleton!
It tells the whole essay!

Now you. Plan it. Build it.
That is an essay — in English.
