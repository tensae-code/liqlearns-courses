# Lesson Plan — The Symposium

**Unit:** Unit 38 — Speeches and Shows

**One thing:** the symposium — a formal meeting where speakers present on a theme before an audience: its roles (resource speaker, emcee, introduction officer, moderator), its written script and slides, and the rubric it is judged by.

## Sub lessons

1. The people and parts of a symposium
2. Running one: script, slides, questions, rubric

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The symposium: running a mini-symposium with assigned roles — resource speaker, emcee, introduction officer, moderator/facilitator — a written speech/script and slides (Lesson 6, Final Task 12, pp.394–396).
- The judging rubric: organisation and time management, delivery and elocution, preparedness and fielding questions, collaboration and originality, content (pp.394–396).
