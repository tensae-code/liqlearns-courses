# Levels — The Symposium

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The people and parts of a symposium | Name the symposium's working roles — resource speaker, emcee, introduction officer, moderator/facilitator — and what each does. |
| 2. Running one: script, slides, questions, rubric | Run a mini-symposium with a written speech/script and slides, field questions, and judge it by the book's rubric: organisation and time, delivery and elocution, preparedness and fielding questions, collaboration and originality, content. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the symposium — a formal meeting where speakers present on a theme before an audience: its roles (resource speaker, emcee, introduction officer, moderator), its written script and slides, and the rubric it is judged by.
