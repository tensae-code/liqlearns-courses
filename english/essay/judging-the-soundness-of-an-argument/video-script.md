# Video Script — Judging the Soundness of an Argument

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a debater pointing at a big claim written in the air]

Believe this! — says the speaker. Wait. Let us TEST it first.

[Picture: the claim placed on a table; its evidence legs drawn under it, one leg wobbling]

Is the claim TRUE? Does the evidence HOLD it? Two legs — an argument needs both!

[Picture: a magnifying glass over a text, finding one green tick detail and one red cross detail]

In a text, hunt BOTH ways — the line that says yes, the line that says no!

[Picture: a tilted scale labelled BIAS, being straightened]

And watch the lean. A tilted text is telling on itself. Judge soundly!
