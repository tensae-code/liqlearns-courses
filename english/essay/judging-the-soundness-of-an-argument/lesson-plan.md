# Lesson Plan — Judging the Soundness of an Argument

**Unit:** Unit 42 — Reading Like a Detective

**One thing:** testing whether an argument is sound or false — taking a claim apart: what it asserts, what evidence holds it, and whether the evidence truly carries the claim.

## Sub lessons

1. Sound or false: taking a claim apart
2. Testing claims in texts: affirm, refute, bias

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Judging the soundness of an argument: testing whether a claim in a viewed debate text is sound or false (Lesson 4 objectives; Task 3, pp.334, 336).
- Identifying textual details that affirm or refute a claim, and examining bias in a text (Lesson 6 objectives, p.373).
