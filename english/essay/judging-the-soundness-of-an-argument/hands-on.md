# Hands-On — Judging the Soundness of an Argument

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Claim court.** A short debate extract (teacher-provided or recalled). Small groups prosecute one claim each through the four steps and deliver a per-claim verdict with the broken leg named.
2. **Both-ways hunt.** Each learner takes a claim about a class text and must bring two affirming details and one refuting or complicating detail. No verdict may be given with one side missing.
3. **Bias mark-up.** Give a class a one-sided paragraph. Learners highlight loaded words in one colour and missing-side gaps in another, then rewrite one sentence neutrally.
