# Levels — Judging the Soundness of an Argument

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Sound or false: taking a claim apart | Judge an argument in a debate or viewed text: isolate the claim, name its evidence, and test whether the evidence carries it. |
| 2. Testing claims in texts: affirm, refute, bias | In a written text, find the textual details that affirm or refute a claim, and examine the text for bias. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: testing whether an argument is sound or false — taking a claim apart: what it asserts, what evidence holds it, and whether the evidence truly carries the claim.
