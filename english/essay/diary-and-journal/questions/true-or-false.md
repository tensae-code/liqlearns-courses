# Diary and journal — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** A diary entry may open with its closing
remark and put the date at the end. **Answer: False** —
date first, always; the four parts stand in their order.

**2. (Level 2)** A list of the day's events with no
feelings and no closing is a complete diary entry.
**Answer: False** — both Egypt Tip Boxes require feelings
in the body and a closing thought or wish.

**3. (Level 3)** A journal must follow a rigid grammar and
argue a case like an essay. **Answer: False** — Egypt's
features box says the opposite: feelings over arguments,
less rigid grammar, flexible structure.
