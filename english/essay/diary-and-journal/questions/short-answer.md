# Diary and journal — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Diary and journal — the parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Diary and journal — the day in order · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Diary and journal — diary into journal.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Egypt Primary 4's four diary parts
in order. **Answer: date — greeting — details — closing**
— the four-paragraph frame.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your entry tells the morning twice and
never reaches the evening. What fixes it? **Answer: the
Morning / Daytime / Evening sections** — Preparatory 8's
Structure Tip; sort the day before writing it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the travel journal's three-entry
extension. **Answer: a real trip, a dreamed-of place, an
imaginary journey** — Egypt Secondary 10's mini journal,
Unit 12, Lesson 5.
