# Diary and journal — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Diary and journal — the parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Diary and journal — the day in order · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Diary and journal — diary into journal.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The very first thing on a diary page is —
a) Dear Diary b) the date c) the closing wish
**Answer: b) the date** — Egypt's Primary 4 frame opens
with the date; the diary is filed by days.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A diary entry is written in — a) the
third person, present tense b) the first person, mostly
past tense c) no person at all **Answer: b)** — I went,
I saw: the writer tells their own finished day.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt Preparatory 8's Structure Tip runs
the day in — a) alphabetical order b) Morning / Daytime /
Evening sections c) longest event first **Answer: b)** —
the day in its own order, then feelings, then the closing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nepal's Grade 8 model entry is dated —
a) Saturday, 5 March 2022 b) Monday, January 1 c) it has
no date **Answer: a)** — Neil's Dhading day trip, read
whole in Unit 4 before the pupil writes their own.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A journal entry, beyond a diary entry,
also keeps — a) only times and prices b) goals, lessons
and reflection c) other people's days **Answer: b)** —
Egypt's Secondary 10 definition: experiences, feelings,
goals, lessons.
