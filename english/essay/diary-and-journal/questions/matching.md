# Diary and journal — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Date — **Answer: the diary's first line,
the day the entry belongs to** — Egypt Primary 4's first
printed part.

**2. (Level 1)** "Dear Diary" — **Answer: the greeting —
the diary speaking to itself** — the second printed part
in both Egypt frames.

**3. (Level 2)** Feelings — **Answer: a required section,
not decoration** — Preparatory 8's Tip puts Feelings
between the day's sections and the closing.

**4. (Level 2)** Samuel Pepys, 1666 — **Answer: the famous
diary Malaysia's Year 6 book reads before teaching the
genre** — Academy Stars Unit 2, pp.22–23, direct read.

**5. (Level 3)** Travel journal — **Answer: the dated
first-person record of a journey, closed by reflection**
— Egypt Secondary 10, Unit 12, pp.91–92.
