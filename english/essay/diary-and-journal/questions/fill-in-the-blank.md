# Diary and journal — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Diary and journal — the parts · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Diary and journal — the day in order · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Diary and journal — diary into journal.

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt Primary 4's four parts in order:
date, ______, details, closing. **Answer: greeting** —
"Dear Diary" stands second.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Preparatory 8 desert diary runs to
______ words. **Answer: 90–100** — the diary kind at
exam length, with sensory detail required.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Secondary 12's Tip Box ends the body with
a closing thought or ______. **Answer: wish** — the
personal remark the entry signs off with.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The travel journal's five steps end with
______, after basics, events, feelings and sensory
details. **Answer: reflection** — what the day meant,
not only what it held.
