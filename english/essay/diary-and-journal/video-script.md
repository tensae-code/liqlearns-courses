# Video script — Diary and journal

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

Today happened. Tonight, you keep it.

Open the page. First line: the date.
Saturday, 5 March. The day and the date.
Then: Dear Diary.

Now tell the day. In order.
Morning. Daytime. Evening.
I went. I saw. I ate. I felt.
Past words. Your own voice. I — not he, not she.

Short lines are fine. Honest lines are best.
A diary is private. Nobody marks it.

End with one personal line.
A thought. A wish. A plan for tomorrow.
That closing line is the diary's signature.

A journal is the diary's big brother.
It keeps the day — and also your goals,
what you learned, what you will do better.

Date. Dear Diary. The day. Your feelings. One last thought.
Write it tonight, while the day is still warm.
