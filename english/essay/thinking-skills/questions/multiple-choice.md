## Multiple choice

1. In the Six Thinking Hats, the group wears… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) six different hats at once  b) the same hat at the same time  c) no hats
   **Answer: b).**

2. The book applies the thinking tools to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) green building  b) cooking  c) football
   **Answer: a).**

3. A double-bubble map is for… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) describing one thing  b) comparing two things  c) telling a story
   **Answer: b).**

4. A circle map… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) collects everything you know around a centre word  b) measures a circle  c) lists dangers only
   **Answer: a).**
