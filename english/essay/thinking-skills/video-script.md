# Video script — Thinking Skills

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Six hats of six colours float in a row.]

“Six hats. Six ways to think.”

[Everyone wears the same colour at once.] “One hat at a time — all minds, one direction.”

[A circle, bubbles, two circles side by side.] “And maps — to see your thinking.”
