# Letters that ask and advise — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name the four formal kinds of this
lesson. **Answer: enquiry — advice — letter to the
editor — letter to an authority** — one frame, four doors.

**2. (Level 2)** Write the editor letter's view sentence
for the school road. **Answer: (any one-sentence hedged
view, e.g.) "In my view, the road outside our school
needs a crossing before another child is hurt"** — view
in one sentence, reasons after, no more than three.

**3. (Level 3)** A pupil writes to the Minister about a
classroom quarrel with a friend. Fault? **Answer: wrong
door** — no power there matches the problem; the teacher
or the friend (advice/sympathy) is the door.
