# Letters that ask and advise — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Request (primary) — **Answer: grows into
the enquiry and the authority letter** — the child's
"please" in formal dress.

**2. (Level 1)** Letter to the editor — **Answer: asks
the public, through a newspaper** — Jordan's modelled kind.

**3. (Level 2)** Enquiry — **Answer: a numbered list of
answerable questions** — prices, dates, conditions.

**4. (Level 2)** Advice letter — **Answer: restate the
problem, counsel softly, end warmly** — hearing is half
the help.

**5. (Level 3)** Ghana's addressees (Headmaster, Director
of Education, Assembly Man/woman) — **Answer: the
authority letter's doors, in the curriculum's own list**
— curriculum evidence, worded as such.
