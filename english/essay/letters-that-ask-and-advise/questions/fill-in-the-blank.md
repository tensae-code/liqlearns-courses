# Letters that ask and advise — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** The primary kind that says "come" is the
______. **Answer: invitation** — first of Barbados's five.

**2. (Level 1)** The primary kind that shares a sorrow is
______. **Answer: sympathy** — it writes to a person,
never an office.

**3. (Level 2)** Advice wears the softened dress: "If I
______ you, I would ask the teacher first."
**Answer: were** — counsel as a shared imagining, not an
order.

**4. (Level 3)** Choose the door by asking: who can
actually ______ this? **Answer: do** — the kind follows
the power, every time.
