# Letters that ask and advise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A letter asking an office for prices and
dates is — a) an advice letter b) an enquiry c) a letter
to the editor **Answer: b) an enquiry** — its ask is a
clean list of answerable questions.

**2. (Level 1)** Which kind is NOT in Barbados's primary
five? a) praise b) sympathy c) complaint to a ministry
**Answer: c)** — the five are invitation, praise,
request, sympathy, apology; ministry letters come later.

**3. (Level 2)** A letter to an authority must carry —
a) many requests, to be safe b) the facts and ONE
grantable request c) jokes to soften the chief
**Answer: b)** — power is given a clear, small thing to do.

**4. (Level 2)** Jordan's editor-letter craft is hedging —
a) hiding your view b) the careful dress of public
opinion ("it seems to me that…") c) writing anonymously
**Answer: b)** — firm view, carefully dressed, real name
signed.

**5. (Level 3)** Your well's water is dirty. The door with
the power is — a) the editor only b) the friend whose
child drinks there c) the district health office
**Answer: c)** — the kind follows who can actually act;
the editor may follow, second.
