# Letters that ask and advise — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** Sympathy and apology are among the
primary letter kinds. **Answer: True** — Barbados's five:
invitation, praise, request, sympathy, apology.

**2. (Level 2)** An enquiry may ask anything, even what
the office cannot know. **Answer: False** — ask only
what the office can answer, one question per line.

**3. (Level 3)** A letter to the editor is signed with a
false name for safety. **Answer: False** — Jordan's door
is public: real name and town stand under the view.
