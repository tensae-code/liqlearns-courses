# Video script — Letters that ask and advise

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

One letter frame. Many jobs.

You ask an office: What is the price? When do you open?
That is an enquiry. Ask clean questions.

You ask a chief, a head teacher, a minister.
That is a letter to an authority.
Be respectful. Give the facts. Ask for ONE thing.

You write to a newspaper. Many people read it.
That is a letter to the editor.
Say your view. Say it carefully. Sign your name.

A friend has a problem. You write with care.
That is an advice letter.
Not orders. Ideas. If I were you…

And the first letters of all?
Come to my party. Thank you. Please help me.
I am sorry. I am sorry for your loss.
Invitation. Praise. Request. Sympathy. Apology.

Who can do this thing? Write to THAT door.
Now you choose a door — and write.
