# Lesson Plan — Critiquing a Speech

**Unit:** Unit 38 — Speeches and Shows

**One thing:** judging a speech that has been delivered — first with a guided checklist (content, relevance, application, timeliness, values, organisation, and delivery), then independently, on criteria of your own.

## Sub lessons

1. Critiquing with a checklist
2. Critiquing on your own criteria

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Guided critiquing of a speech delivery with a checklist: content, relevance, practical application, timeliness, values integration, organisation, and delivery — stress, enunciation, phrasing, voice command, modulation, volume, quality (Lesson 5, Final Task 16, pp.371–372).
- Independent critique of a chosen speech on one's own criteria: comparing speeches and delivery, the focal point, strengths and weaknesses, improvements (Lesson 6, Task 11, pp.389–393).
