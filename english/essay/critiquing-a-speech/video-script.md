# Video Script — Critiquing a Speech

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a judge with a long checklist, listening to a speaker]

A speech is given. Now — was it GOOD? Do not guess. Use the list!

[Picture: checklist items lighting up: CONTENT, ORDER, VALUES… then a voice-wave labelled DELIVERY]

The writing… and the voice. Both are judged!

[Picture: the checklist fading away; the judge writing their OWN three questions]

Then the scaffold comes down. YOUR criteria now — stated first, applied fairly!

[Picture: the speaker reading a short letter and nodding]

Strengths. Weaknesses. One true improvement. That is a critique!
