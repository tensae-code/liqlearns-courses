# Levels — Critiquing a Speech

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Critiquing with a checklist | Critique a delivered speech with the book's checklist: content, relevance, practical application, timeliness, values integration, organisation — and delivery (stress, enunciation, phrasing, voice command, modulation, volume, quality). |
| 2. Critiquing on your own criteria | Evaluate speeches independently: compare speeches and deliveries, assess the focal point, name strengths and weaknesses, and suggest improvements — on criteria you set and defend. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: judging a speech that has been delivered — first with a guided checklist (content, relevance, application, timeliness, values, organisation, and delivery), then independently, on criteria of your own.
