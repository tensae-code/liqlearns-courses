# Hands-On — Critiquing a Speech

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Checklist judging.** Two learners deliver short speeches (a dedication works well, as in the book). The class critiques both on the checklist, one evidence line per point.
2. **Criteria auction.** Before a speech is heard, small groups propose their own three criteria and defend them. The class adopts a set, then evaluates by it.
3. **Improvement letters.** Each judge writes the speaker a five-line letter: two strengths with evidence, one improvement that can actually be used. Speakers reply with what they will change.
