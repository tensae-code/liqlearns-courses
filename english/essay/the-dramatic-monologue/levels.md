# Levels — The Dramatic Monologue

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The one-person dramatic speech | Say what a dramatic monologue is: one speaker, in character, speaking a whole dramatic speech to a silent listener, revealing a story and a self. |
| 2. The voice that carries it: prosody | Perform with the four prosodic features — pitch, loudness, tempo, rhythm — varied deliberately to carry feeling and meaning. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the dramatic monologue — a one-person dramatic speech, performed: what it is, and the prosodic features (pitch, loudness, tempo, rhythm) that carry it to an audience.
