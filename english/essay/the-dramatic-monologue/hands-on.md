# Hands-On — The Dramatic Monologue

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Character moment.** Each learner picks a story character's dramatic moment and improvises thirty seconds of monologue. The class names what the character revealed without meaning to.
2. **Flat vs marked.** Perform a short monologue extract twice — once flat, once with a fully marked script. Listeners write what changed in their understanding.
3. **Prosody conducting.** One learner conducts (hands up = pitch up, wide = loud, fast wave = tempo up) while another speaks a neutral line. Feel how the four features move independently.
