# Video Script — The Dramatic Monologue

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a stage with ONE chair, one spotlight, one actor]

One actor. One character. One speech. A whole play in a single voice!

[Picture: the actor speaking to an empty chair, secrets spilling like light from a cracked door]

And listen — the character reveals MORE than they mean to!

[Picture: four dials labelled PITCH, LOUDNESS, TEMPO, RHYTHM turning as the actor speaks]

The voice has four tools. High, low. Loud, soft. Fast, slow. Beat, broken beat.

[Picture: the audience leaning forward, gripped, in the dark]

No scenery. Just a voice, tuned like an instrument. THAT is a dramatic monologue!
