# Hands-on — Problem-Solving Strategies and Games

1. **Five tips on a real problem.** Take one real problem from your week. Write the five tips down the side of a page and write one line against each: what the tip tells you to do about *this* problem.
2. **Play the drawing game.** Describe a picture, in words only, so a partner can draw it without seeing it. Compare the drawing with the original — every difference is a communication problem to solve.
