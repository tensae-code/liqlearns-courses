# Levels — Problem-Solving Strategies and Games

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it teaches five named strategies first, then trains them through play. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — What a problem is — and five tips | The new definition and the five tips, in order. |
| 2 — Problem-solving games | The eight games and the strategy each one trains. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
