# Multiple choice questions — Problem-Solving Strategies and Games

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).

## Multiple choice

1. **In the book's new definition, a problem is…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A punishment
   - b) A situation between where you are and where you want to be, which your actions can change
   - c) Always someone else's fault
   - **Answer: b) A situation between where you are and where you want to be, which your actions can change**

2. **What is the first of the five tips?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Ask for help wisely
   - b) Try many solutions
   - c) Ask: can you change it?
   - **Answer: c) Ask: can you change it?**

3. **“See the big picture” means…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Look at the whole situation, not only the corner that hurts
   - b) Draw a large picture
   - c) Ignore the details forever
   - **Answer: a) Look at the whole situation, not only the corner that hurts**

4. **How many problem-solving games does the book teach?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Three
   - b) Five
   - c) Eight
   - **Answer: c) Eight**

5. **In the sinking-boat game, the players must…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Rank what to save
   - b) Swim to shore
   - c) Build a boat
   - **Answer: a) Rank what to save**
