# Short answer questions — Problem-Solving Strategies and Games

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).
The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions —
no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Short answer

1. **Say the five tips in order.** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Can you change it; be calm; see the big picture; ask for help wisely; try many solutions.
2. **Name three of the book's eight games.** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** Any three of: describing a picture, guiding a blindfolded partner, circle thinking map, storytelling from a photo, sinking-boat survival ranking, Switcheroo, pyramid of importance, hot-air-balloon compass.
3. **What does the circle thinking map do?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** It grows a problem's ideas outward in a circle/map so the whole problem can be seen.
