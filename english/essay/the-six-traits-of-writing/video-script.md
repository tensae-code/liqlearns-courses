# Video Script — The Six Traits of Writing

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a piece of writing held up to six labelled windows, one at a time]

Good writing is not magic. It has SIX parts — and we can name them!

[Picture: windows 1–3 lighting up: a growing plant, a staircase, a speaking face]

Ideas that GROW. Order that BUILDS. A voice that SPEAKS.

[Picture: windows 4–6: a sharp pencil, a flowing river, a clean page]

Words that are EXACT. Sentences that FLOW. Mechanics that are CLEAN.

[Picture: a checklist with six boxes, the writer ticking them one by one]

Six traits. Read once for each. Fix trait by trait. That is how writers revise!
