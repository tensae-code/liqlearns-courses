# Hands-On — The Six Traits of Writing

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Trait scoring.** In pairs, score a provided paragraph 1–3 on all six traits, with one line of evidence each. Compare scores with another pair and defend the biggest disagreement.
2. **Read-aloud round.** Each learner reads a paragraph aloud while listeners mark only fluency: a tick where it flows, a cross where the ear trips. The writer revises only the crosses.
3. **One-trait revision.** Take your own draft and revise for exactly one trait — the weakest. Show before/after to a partner who must name the trait from the changes alone.
