# Matching — Early Writing Stages

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match each piece of a child's page to its stage.

1. A picture of a house that the child says "is my house" (Sub lesson 1)
2. Wavy lines running in rows across the page (Sub lesson 1)
3. Zigzags, loops and shapes that look like letters (Sub lesson 2)
4. The letters B K A T O in a row, one of them reversed (Sub lesson 2)
5. The letters KT written for the word *cat* (Sub lesson 3)
6. Words written the way books spell them, most sounds there (Sub lesson 4)

   a) Letter-like forms and symbols
   b) Drawing
   c) Conventional spelling
   d) Scribbling
   e) Invented spelling
   f) Random letters / letter strings

Answers: 1–b, 2–d, 3–a, 4–f, 5–e, 6–c ✓
