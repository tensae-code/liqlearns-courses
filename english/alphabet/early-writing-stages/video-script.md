# Video Script — Early Writing Stages

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each stage is shown on a child's page as it is named.

[Picture: a child's drawing of a house and a sun]

Writing starts here. A drawing! The drawing means something. The child can tell you what it says.

[Picture: wavy scribble lines running in rows across a page]

Now scribbles. Lines and rows, like writing. The child knows: this is not a picture. This is writing.

[Picture: zigzags, loops and letter-like shapes, then a row of capital letters B K A T O]

Next, shapes like letters. Lines, zigzags, loops. Then real letters, all mixed up. B, K, A, T, O. Some letters face the wrong way. That is fine. It is a stage.

[Picture: the letters KT written under a drawing of a cat]

Now the big jump! The child hears the sounds. K… T… Cat! This is invented spelling. Letters for sounds.

[Picture: the word CAT written neatly, a child sitting straight, holding the pencil in a tripod grip]

Last, real spelling. Most sounds are there. And look at the body: sitting straight, feet flat, pencil held just right. Writing grows — stage by stage.
