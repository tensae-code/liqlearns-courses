# Levels — Visual Memory

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Remembering things and patterns | Look at objects and patterns, have them covered, and name and describe them from memory. |
| 2. Remembering and describing experiences | Remember something that happened and describe it from memory — what was there, what happened, in order. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can hold what they saw in mind and say it back — the memory skill that picture reading, and then page reading, is built on.
