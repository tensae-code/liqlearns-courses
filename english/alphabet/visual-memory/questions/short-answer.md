# Short Answer — Visual Memory

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Three things are on a table: a cup, a stone, a leaf. They are covered. What do you do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Name them from memory: a cup, a stone and a leaf — then uncover and check.
2. How can you make a pattern easier to remember? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Look at it on purpose and say it quietly in your head — red, blue, red, blue — before it is covered.
3. Describe a journey you made, from memory. What two things should your description have? (Sub lesson 2)
   — The places (or things) that were there, told in order — what came first, then next.
4. After describing from memory, how do you know if you remembered well? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Look again and check: count what you missed.
