# Video Script — Visual Memory

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each thing is shown, hidden, then remembered.

[Picture: a cup, a stone and a leaf on a table]

Look! A cup. A stone. A leaf. Look well.

[Picture: a cloth covers the three things]

Now they are hidden. What was there? A cup… a stone… and a leaf!

[Picture: the cloth lifts — the three things are there]

Yes! You remembered! That is visual memory.

[Picture: a child telling a friend about a walk, places appearing above her head as she names them]

And you can remember a whole walk too — and tell it, in order.
