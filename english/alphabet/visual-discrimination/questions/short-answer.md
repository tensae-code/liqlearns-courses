# Short Answer — Visual Discrimination

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Look at two cups, one with a spot and one without. How are they the same, and how are they different? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Same: both are cups (same shape and size). Different: one has a spot, the other does not.
2. A row holds three pencils and one pen. Which is the odd one out, and why? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The pen — it is not a pencil like the others.
3. Name the three ways the books sort things by what you see. (Sub lesson 2)
   — By colour, by shape, and by size.
4. You lay stones in a line from smallest to biggest, left to right. What skill are you using? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Arranging by size in order — sorting by what you see.
