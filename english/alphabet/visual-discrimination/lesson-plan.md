# Lesson Plan — Visual Discrimination

**One thing:** the pre-reading eye skill — looking closely and telling alike from different: matching what is the same, spotting the odd one out, and sorting things by what you can see (colour, shape, size).

## Sub lessons

1. Alike and different
2. Matching and sorting by what you see

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Telling alike from different — the sample lesson plan's one skill (ECE Level 1 Term 3, topic 0.1.9).
- Odd one out; grouping objects by colour, shape and size; matching adult animals with their offspring; arranging items by size, left to right (ECE Level 1 Term 2, topic 0.1.9).
- Note: the Term 2 module also files an environmental-sounds activity inside this topic — a listening skill under a visual heading (book error E5); this lesson teaches the visual skill, and the sounds activity is recorded in the sources, not taught here.
