# Levels — Visual Discrimination

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Alike and different | Look closely at two or more things and say how they are the same and how they are different; spot the odd one out in a row. |
| 2. Matching and sorting by what you see | Match pairs that belong together, group things by colour, shape and size, and arrange things by size in order. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons has the eye skill reading starts from: seeing the small differences between things that look almost the same — the same skill that later tells b from d.
