# Video Script — Visual Discrimination

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each pair is shown as it is named.

[Picture: two cups that look the same, then one small spot appears on one cup]

Look! Two cups. Same… same… wait! This one has a spot. They are different!

[Picture: a row of four red balls and one blue ball]

Four balls. One is not like the others. Can you see it? The blue one! The odd one out.

[Picture: a hen and a chick, a cow and a calf]

Match them! The chick goes with the hen. The calf goes with the cow.

[Picture: stones laid in a line, small to big]

Now sort by size. Small… bigger… biggest! In a line, left to right.
