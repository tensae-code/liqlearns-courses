# Hands-On — Visual Discrimination

Things you do with real objects in a real classroom or home. No special materials needed.

1. **Odd one out.** Make a row of four things — three the same, one different. Your partner finds the odd one and says why. Swap.
2. **Mother and baby.** Spread out pictures or toys of adult animals and their babies, mixed up. Match each baby to its mother and say what signs you used.
3. **Three sorts.** Take one mixed handful of things. Sort it by colour. Mix it up. Sort it by shape. Mix it up. Sort it by size.
4. **The size line.** Lay five things in a line by size, smallest to biggest, left to right. Close your eyes while your partner swaps two — then open your eyes and fix the line.
