## Multiple choice

1. How many fingers are on one hand? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) four  b) five  c) six
   **Answer: b).**

2. Your left hand is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the hand on the left side of your body  b) the hand you write with, always  c) the bigger hand
   **Answer: a).**

3. When you turn around, your left hand… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) becomes your right hand  b) stays your left hand  c) changes sides
   **Answer: b).**

4. The hand you write with is called your… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) kicking hand  b) strong foot  c) writing hand
   **Answer: c).**

5. For most people, the writing hand and the kicking foot are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) on the same side  b) on different sides  c) always the left
   **Answer: a).**
