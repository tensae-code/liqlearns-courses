## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. left
2. right
3. writing hand
4. kicking foot
5. strong side

a) the foot you kick a ball with
b) the hand you hold a pencil with
c) the hand on the left side of your body
d) the hand on the right side of your body
e) the side your writing hand and kicking foot share, for most people

**Answers:** 1–c, 2–d, 3–b, 4–a, 5–e.
