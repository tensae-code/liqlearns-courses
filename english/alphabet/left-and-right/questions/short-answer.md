## Short answer

1. Show your left hand. How do you know it is the left one? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: it is the hand on the left side of my body.**

2. Which hand do you write with? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the learner's own writing hand — left or right are both right answers.**

3. Name one thing you do with your right hand and one with your left. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any two true examples from the learner's own life.**
