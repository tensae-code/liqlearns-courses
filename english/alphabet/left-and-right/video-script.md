# Video script — Left and Right

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot holds both hands up, palms out.]

"Hold up your hands, like me."

"This one is my left hand." [The left hand wiggles.]

"This one is my right hand." [The right hand wiggles.]

"Your left hand is on the left side of your body. Your right hand is on the right side."

[The mascot picks up a pencil.] "This is the hand I write with. Which hand do you write with?"

"Now you know: left — and right."
