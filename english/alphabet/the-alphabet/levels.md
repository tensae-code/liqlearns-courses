# The alphabet — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
26 letters — the whole train A to Z first, then the letters a
few at a time, grouped by sound.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2.

## Sub lesson 1 — Alphabet A to Z
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say A–Z in order,
and match every capital to its small letter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Alphabet by sounds
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given any letter,
say its sound and its book word ("T — t — tomato").
(Reading whole words by joining the sounds — sat, pat, tin, pin —
is the Blending sounds lesson, next door; it is not tested here.)
