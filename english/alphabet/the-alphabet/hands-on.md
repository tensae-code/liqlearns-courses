# The alphabet — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: your alphabet line
You need: paper, your pencil (five-step grip).
1. Write A to Z in capitals, in one line, slow.
2. Under each capital write the small letter.
3. Circle the three letters your hand found hardest. Those three are
   tomorrow's warm-up: write each one five times before anything else.

## Project 2: letter hunt
You need: anything around you with English writing on it — a box, a
book, a sign.
1. Find the letters A, B and C on real things. Say each word's first
   sound out loud.
2. Now find a word that starts with S, and one that starts with T.
3. Tell your companion the five words you found, letter first:
   "S — soap."
