# The alphabet — short answer

Short-answer questions only.

## Level 1
1. How many letters are in the English alphabet?
2. Say the alphabet from A to G, out loud. (Libya's Lesson 2 group.)
3. What word does the book give for the letter R?
4. Name the three things every letter has.

## Level 2
1. Egypt's book starts with T and I — not with A. In your own words,
   why would a book teach a few letters at a time, grouped by their
   sounds, instead of the whole alphabet at once?
2. Write the sounds-way group — the six letters that open words early.
