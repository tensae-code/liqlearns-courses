# The alphabet — fill in the blank

Fill questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. A B C D ___ F G — the missing letter is ___.
2. X Y ___ — the last letter of the alphabet is ___.
3. An ___pple starts with A. (say the whole word)
4. The word "zebra" starts with the letter ___.
5. S ___ T — the letter in the middle is between S and T: it is ___.
   (trick: there is no letter between S and T — if you wrote "none,"
   you are right. See the answer key.)

## Answers
1-E, 2-Z, 3-a (apple), 4-Z, 5-none — S and T are neighbours.
