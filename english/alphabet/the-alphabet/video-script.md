# The alphabet — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: a long train with 26 cars]

This is a train.
The train has 26 cars.
Each car is a letter.

[Picture: the first car, with a big A]

This is A.
A is the first.
A says: apple.

[Picture: the cars going past, B, C, D…]

B — ball.
C — car.
D — duck.
Every letter has a word.
Every letter has a sound.

[Picture: the last car, with a big Z]

And this is Z.
Z is the last.
Z says: zebra.

[Picture: a child pointing along the train]

A to Z.
26 letters.
Now you say it.
A… B… C…
