## Multiple choice

1. In the row 🍎 🍎 🍌 🍎, the odd one out is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) an apple  b) the banana  c) there is none
   **Answer: b).**

2. "Odd one out" means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the biggest one  b) the first one  c) the one that is not like the others
   **Answer: c).**

3. A difference can be in… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) shape, kind, colour or facing  b) price only  c) name only
   **Answer: a).**

4. To finish the skill, after spotting you must… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) close your eyes  b) say why it is different  c) count the row again
   **Answer: b).**

5. Telling b from d uses the same skill as… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) running fast  b) spotting the odd one out  c) singing
   **Answer: b).**
