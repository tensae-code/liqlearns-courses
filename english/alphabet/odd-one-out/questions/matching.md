## Matching

Match each row to its odd one out. (Sub lesson 1)

1. dog, dog, cat, dog
2. circle, circle, square, circle
3. banana, banana, banana, apple
4. car, car, car, bus
5. moon, moon, star, moon

a) the square
b) the star
c) the cat
d) the bus
e) the apple

**Answers:** 1–c, 2–a, 3–e, 4–d, 5–b.
