## Short answer

1. Look at the row: pen, pen, pencil, pen. Which is the odd one out, and why? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the pencil — the others are pens.**

2. Make your own row of four words with one odd one out. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any four words where three belong together and one does not, with the odd one named.**
