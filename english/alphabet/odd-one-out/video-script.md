# Video script — The Odd One Out

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A row appears: apple, apple, banana, apple.]

"Look at the row. Three are the same. One is different."

"Can you find it?" [A pause. The banana glows.] "The banana! The others are apples."

[Another row: circle, circle, square, circle.]

"Now this one. Which is the odd one out? … The square — the others are round."

"Your eyes are learning to catch what is different. That is how reading starts."
