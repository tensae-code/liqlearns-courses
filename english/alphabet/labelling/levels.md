# Levels — Labelling

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** draw one object and write its
  true name beside it, readable and rightly placed.
- **Climb:** label the parts of a thing as well as the whole —
  beak, wing, leg beside the hen.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** label a whole
  place or scene — objects found in the environment, peers
  and parts — as a labelled diagram that teaches on its own.
