# Short Answer — Labelling

Answer in a few words.

1. Name the three rules of a good label. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** True name, readable, rightly placed.

2. Why is a wrong label worse than no label? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** It teaches the reader a falsehood.

3. Give three labels for a classroom drawing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any three of: blackboard, desk, chair, window, door, teacher.

4. What is a labelled diagram? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** A drawing whose parts are all labelled, so the labels carry a whole lesson (like a plant with root, stem, leaf, flower).

5. What should you do when a drawing fills up with labels? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Start a second drawing.
