# True or False — Labelling

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. A label with the wrong name is worse than no label at all. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

2. A label can sit anywhere on the page, even far from its thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it must sit beside or under the thing it names.

3. You may label people, such as a drawing of a friend with their name. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

4. More labels are always better, even if nobody can read them. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — clear beats crowded.

5. Labelling is real writing, even when it is only one word. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**
