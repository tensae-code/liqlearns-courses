# Multiple Choice — Labelling

Choose the one right answer.

1. What is a label? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) A decoration on a drawing
   - B) The name of a thing, written beside its picture
   - C) A frame around a page
   - D) A colour used in drawing
   **Answer: B**

2. A good label must be: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) The thing's true name, readable, and rightly placed
   - B) As long as possible
   - C) Written in secret code
   - D) Hidden under the drawing
   **Answer: A**

3. You draw a cup. Its label should say: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) drink
   - B) hot
   - C) cup
   - D) thing
   **Answer: C**

4. If your drawing has three things, it needs: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) One label for all
   - B) No labels
   - C) Three labels, one for each thing
   - D) A label only for the biggest
   **Answer: C**

5. If you do not know a thing's name, you should: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Guess a label
   - B) Write any word
   - C) Leave the drawing blank forever
   - D) Ask — a wrong label teaches a falsehood
   **Answer: D**

6. Labelling the world around you means: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Drawing and labelling objects found in your environment
   - B) Writing on walls without permission
   - C) Labelling only one object forever
   - D) Copying someone else's drawing
   **Answer: A**

7. A plant drawing with root, stem, leaf and flower labelled is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) A mistake
   - B) A labelled diagram — labelling carrying a whole lesson
   - C) Not labelling at all
   - D) Only for teachers
   **Answer: B**

8. Ghana's Basic 1 labelling work includes drawing and labelling: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Only animals
   - B) Peers as well as objects
   - C) Only the teacher
   - D) Nothing at all
   **Answer: B**

9. A drawing with thirty squeezed labels nobody can read is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Better than ten clear labels
   - B) Worse than ten clear labels
   - C) Always correct
   - D) A labelled diagram
   **Answer: B**

10. Where should a label sit? (Sub lesson 1)
    - A) On another page
    - B) Beside or under the thing it names, close enough that nobody mistakes it
    - C) At the top of the book
    - D) Under the table
    **Answer: B**
