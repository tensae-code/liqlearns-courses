Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson. One row (Primary 1 Term 2, Lesson 75, "Handling a Book") arrives by correction: it was previously cited inside blending-sounds' block row, which teaches no book handling — sweep diff, flag F3.
