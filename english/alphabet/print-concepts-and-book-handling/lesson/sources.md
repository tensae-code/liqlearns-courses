This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep of Sierra Leone's English books. The sweep marked 14 items NEW LESSON NEEDED for this topic: before a child can learn letters, Sierra Leone's first plans teach the book itself — its parts, how to hold it, and how print runs. No course lesson housed that groundwork (holding-a-pencil in the Pencil and Lines course cites one of these plans for its book-handling part, but the print-concepts teaching itself had no home). Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the topic gets its own lesson. One row (Primary 1 Term 2, Lesson 75, "Handling a Book") arrives by correction: it was previously cited inside blending-sounds' block row, which teaches no book handling — sweep diff, flag F3.
