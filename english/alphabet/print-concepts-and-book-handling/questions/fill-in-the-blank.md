## Fill in the blank

1. "The ___ cover is the face of the book." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: front** — it carries the book's name.

2. "The last page of a book is its ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: end** — right before the back cover.

3. "Hold the book the right way ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: up** — the print stands straight.

4. "Turn ___ page at a time." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: one** — from the top corner.

5. "Lines of print run top to ___ down the page." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: bottom** — and each line runs left to right.

6. "If a word is missing, the line has a ___ in it." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: hole** — noticing the hole is part of reading.
