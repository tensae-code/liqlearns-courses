## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. Front cover — d
2. Back cover — a
3. Print — e
4. Tracking — b
5. Directionality — c

a) The book's back, behind the last page
b) Following the print with your eyes from start to end of a line
c) The way print runs: left to right, top to bottom
d) The face of the book, carrying its name
e) The letters and words on a page — the part you read

**Answers:** 1–d (the front cover carries the name), 2–a (the back cover
is the book's back), 3–e (print is the part you read), 4–b (tracking
follows the line), 5–c (directionality is the way print runs).
