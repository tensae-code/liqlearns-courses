## Short answer

1. Name the four parts of a book this lesson teaches. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The front cover, the back cover, the beginning and the end.

2. How do you know a book is the right way up? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The print stands straight — not upside down and not on its side.

3. Which way do lines run down a page? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** Top to bottom; each line itself runs left to right.

4. What do you do when a page ends? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** Go on at the next page — turn one page and keep tracking.

5. How can you tell a word is missing from a line? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** The line limps — there is a hole where the word should be, and the sense breaks.
