## True or false

1. A book's name is on its back cover. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the name is on the front cover.

2. Pictures and print are the same thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — pictures show things; print is the part you read.

3. You open a book at the back. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — you open it at the front, at its beginning.

4. Print runs left to right. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True.**

5. When a page ends, the story stops for good. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — it goes on at the next page.

6. A missing word makes a line limp — you can feel the hole. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True.**
