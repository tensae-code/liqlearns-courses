## Multiple choice

1. Which part of a book carries the book's name? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The back cover  b) The front cover  c) The last page
   **Answer: b) The front cover** — the front cover is the face of the book.

2. On a page, which part do you read? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The pictures  b) The print  c) The page number only
   **Answer: b) The print** — pictures show things; print is what you read.

3. Where do you open a book? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) At the back  b) In the middle  c) At the front
   **Answer: c) At the front** — a book opens at its beginning.

4. How do you turn pages? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Three at a time  b) One at a time  c) From the bottom corner, fast
   **Answer: b) One at a time** — from the top corner, gently.

5. Which way does a line of print run? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Right to left  b) Bottom to top  c) Left to right
   **Answer: c) Left to right** — and the lines run top to bottom down the page.

6. What is "tracking" the print? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Drawing the letters  b) Following the print with your eyes from a line's start to its end  c) Counting the pages
   **Answer: b)** — at first a finger under the line helps the eyes.
