# Video script — Print Concepts and Book Handling

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot holds a book upside down. It flips right.] "A
BOOK has parts!"

[He points at the covers.] "FRONT cover. BACK cover."

[He opens it at the front.] "It opens at the BEGINNING."

[His paw slides under a line of print.] "And the print runs
THIS way — left… to… RIGHT!"

[He tracks a whole line slowly.] "Follow the print. Page
after page. NOW you can hold a book!"
