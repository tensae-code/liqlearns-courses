# Hands-on — Print Concepts and Book Handling

All with a real book in the hands.

## Name and point

A partner names a part — "front cover", "back cover",
"beginning", "end", "print", "picture". You point at it
on your book. Ten names, no misses. Change places.

## The page-turning race (slow wins)

Close the book. On "go", open it at the beginning and turn
five pages, one at a time, without tearing or skipping. The
winner is not the fastest — it is the one whose pages are all
single and in order.

## Track and catch

One partner tracks a line with a finger while reading it
aloud. The other covers one word with a fingertip. The
reader must stop and say: "A word is missing here."
