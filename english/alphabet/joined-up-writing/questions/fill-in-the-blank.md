# Joined-up writing — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The exit flick, and Sub lesson 2 — The four basic joins (split item by item) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Steps into whole words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Trace then copy.

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cursive letter ends with a little ______ that runs into the next letter.
**Answer: flick (tail)** — the exit flick is the bridge every join is made of.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The pairs ai, ar, un are ______ joins; ou, vi, wi are ______ joins.
**Answer: diagonal; horizontal** — Barbados's four basic joins, Scope and Sequence 4.6.5.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches cursive ______ letters A–Z first, then the small letters a–p, then ______.
**Answer: capital; q–z** — Units 9, 10 and 11, printed pp.19, 20–21, 22–23.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In Kuwait's routine you ______ the grey model first, then ______ the solid model with no help.
**Answer: trace; copy** — trace the grey, copy the solid, on every one of the 40 practice pages (printed pp.11–50).
