# Joined-up writing — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — The four basic joins · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The four basic joins (its items ask the joins to letters with and without an ascender, which are taught inside the four joins) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Steps into whole words.

Match each pair or step to its name, the way the books give them.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: ai — a) horizontal join to a letter without an ascender  b) diagonal join  c) join to a letter with an ascender
**Answer: b) diagonal join** — ai, ar, un climb (Barbados, Scope and Sequence 4.6.5).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: ou — a) horizontal join  b) diagonal join  c) capital join
**Answer: a) horizontal join** — ou, vi, wi run flat along the line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: ab — a) horizontal join to a short letter  b) diagonal join  c) join to a letter with an ascender
**Answer: c) join to a letter with an ascender** — ab, ul, it climb to a tall letter such as b, l, t.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Match: ol — a) horizontal join to a letter without an ascender  b) join to a tall letter  c) diagonal join
**Answer: a) horizontal join to a letter without an ascender** — ol, wh, ot stay flat into a short letter.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Match: Bangladesh Unit 12 — a) cursive capitals  b) the days of the week in cursive  c) cursive small letters a–p
**Answer: b) the days of the week in cursive** — Unit 12, printed pp.24–25; capitals are Unit 9 and a–p is Unit 10.
