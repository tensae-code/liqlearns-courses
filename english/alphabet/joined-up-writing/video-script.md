# Video script — Joined-up writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Look at this word. C… A… T.
Three letters. Standing apart.

[Picture: the letters c, a, t apart on four lines]

Now watch the pencil.
It does not stop. It does not lift.

[Picture: the pencil writing cat in one run]

C runs into A. A runs into T.
One run. One word. CAT.

Every letter has a little tail.
The tail is the bridge.
The next letter stands on the bridge.

Some bridges go up. A to I — up!
Some bridges run flat. O to U — flat!
Some letters are tall. A to B — up to the tall one!

[Picture: four pairs — ai, ou, ab, ol]

First you trace. The grey word.
Slow. Follow the line.
Then you copy. The black word.
No grey to help you. Your hand knows now.

[Picture: a grey word, then the same word in black]

Now you try.
Write your name. One run.
Do not lift the pencil.
Joined up. That is cursive.
