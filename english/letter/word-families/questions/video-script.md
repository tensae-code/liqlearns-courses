# Word families — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: the word CAT, the "at" in bright colour]

This is cat.
Look at the end. at.
a-t. at.

[Picture: the c changing to m, then h]

Change the first letter.
m-at. MAT!
h-at. HAT!
The end stayed. The words are cousins.

[Picture: three houses on one street, labelled -at, -an, -ap]

Words live in families.
Same end, same family.
Learn the end once — every cousin opens.
Now you try it. p + an… PAN!
