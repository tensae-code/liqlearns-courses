# Word families — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. Which word belongs to the -at family?
   a) pan  b) hat  c) pet  d) dog
2. "bin, fin, win" — their family ending is:
   a) -ig  b) -ip  c) -in  d) -en
3. What do the words of one family share?
   a) The first letter  b) The ending  c) The picture  d) Nothing

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-b
