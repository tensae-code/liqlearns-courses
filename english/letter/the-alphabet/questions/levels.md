# The alphabet — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in the
type files; this file is the by-level view.

## Level 1 — the train
All Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say A–Z in order,
and match every capital to its small letter.

## Level 2 — the sounds
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given any letter,
say its sound and its book word ("T — t — tomato"); given the sounds
group s, a, t, p, i, n, read: sat, pat, tin, pin.
