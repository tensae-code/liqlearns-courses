# The alphabet — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Grouped by level. Answers at the bottom.
Words are the books' own letter words (Libya, Grade 1, Unit 1).

## Level 1
1. Which word starts with the letter B?
   a) apple  b) ball  c) car  d) duck
2. What is the first letter of the alphabet?
   a) Z  b) M  c) A  d) T
3. Which letter comes right after G?
   a) F  b) H  c) J  d) E
4. "Elephant" starts with which letter?
   a) E  b) I  c) A  d) O

## Level 2
1. Which set of letters can already make the word "sat"?
   a) q, v, z  b) s, a, t  c) x, y, j  d) o, u, w
2. Which of these words starts with the letter K?
   a) lemon  b) jam  c) kite  d) nose

## Answers
Level 1: 1-b, 2-c, 3-b, 4-a
Level 2: 1-b, 2-c
