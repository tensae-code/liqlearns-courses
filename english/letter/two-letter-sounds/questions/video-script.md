# Two-letter sounds — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: the letter c and the letter h, standing apart]

This is c. This is h.
Alone, they say their own sounds.

[Picture: c and h holding hands: ch]

Now they hold hands.
ch!
One new sound. Not c. Not h. ch.

[Picture: a spoon, a big oo over it]

And look — oo.
In spoon, oo is long.
In foot, oo is short.
One pair, two jobs.

[Picture: a queen, qu over her crown]

And q never walks alone.
q walks with u. qu!
Now you try it. s and h — what do they say together?
