# Two-letter sounds — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: pair hunt
You need: anything around you with English writing on it.
1. Find three words with a two-letter pair inside (oo, ch, sh,
   th, qu…).
2. Write each word and circle the pair.
3. Say the word to your companion, slow, and point at the pair
   when its sound comes.
