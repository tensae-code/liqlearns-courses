# Two-letter sounds — fill in the blank

Fill questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. Two letters that say one sound: in "book", the pair is ______.
2. The quiet th is in the word ______ (three's friend in the book).
3. q + ______ says the sound at the start of "queen".

## Answers
1-oo, 2-mouth, 3-u
