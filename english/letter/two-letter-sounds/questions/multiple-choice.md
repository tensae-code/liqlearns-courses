# Two-letter sounds — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. In "moon", the letters oo together say:
   a) Two different sounds  b) One sound  c) No sound  d) The letter name
2. Which pair makes the first sound of "three"?
   a) ch  b) sh  c) th  d) ng
3. In English, the letter q travels with:
   a) a  b) u  c) e  d) z

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-b
