# Before the letters — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: the five-step grip
1. Lay your pencil on the table.
2. Take it the book's way, in the five steps from the lesson.
3. Check in a mirror or with your companion: resting, not squeezed?
4. Put it down, and do it again — three times, until your hand finds
   the grip by itself.

## Project 2: hills and mountains
1. Draw one slow line of wavy hills across your page.
2. Draw one slow line of zigzag mountains under it.
3. Look at both lines: which one is smoother? Do that one again.
4. Keep the page — you will compare it with next week's page.
