# Blends — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. In "swim", you hear both s and w at the start. (True / False)
2. cl in "clock" is one sound that neither letter owns. (True / False)
3. The book prints each blend inside whole words, then uses the
   words in sentences. (True / False)

## Answers
1-True, 2-False (you hear both c and l — that is a blend), 3-True
