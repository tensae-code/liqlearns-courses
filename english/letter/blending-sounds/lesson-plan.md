# Lesson plan — Blending sounds

**Course:** English · **Title:** Letter · **Lesson:** Blending sounds
**Place in the curriculum:** Year 1, Term 1, Unit 2 "Sounds Together"
— the first lesson of the unit (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** pushing letter sounds together
until they become a word — s… a… t… → sat.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## Before this lesson
- The alphabet (the lesson before this one): the learner knows the
  first sounds group — s, a, t, p, i, n. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Take a word's sounds one by one, slowly: s… a… t…
2. Slide them together, faster, until the gaps close and the word
   stands up: sat.
3. Blend the book's first words: sit · pat · tin · pin.
4. Explain what changed between the slow way and the smooth way
   (the gaps closed).

## What you need
Nothing but a voice. Paper and a pencil for the writing step.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner says s… a… t… slowly, then again
   faster with no gaps, and hears "sat" stand up (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD AUDIO at the top of the lesson: the sounds s… a… t… said
     slowly, then sliding faster until "sat" — the student blends
     along out loud, matching the slide.
2. **The explanation** — a word is sounds holding hands; Libya's
   first group (s, a, t, p, i, n) blended into sit, pat, tin, pin;
   Egypt's letter order chosen so words open early (lesson/
   lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE under the slide: a picture of three sound bubbles —
     s, a, t — moving together into one big bubble that says SAT —
     the student points at each stage and says what they hear.
   - ADD GIF: a picture that moves — the letters s, a, t sliding
     together on a line until they touch and the word SAT lights up
     — the student says the sounds as the letters move, faster as
     they close.
3. **Say it to your companion** — blend "pat" slowly, then smooth;
   explain what changed.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in questions/video-script.md, filmed —
     sounds slid together one word at a time in the super-simple
     voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (questions/levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — questions/hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — blend sit · pat · tin · pin out loud, slow
   then smooth; write each word with its three sounds under it; mark
   the word that took the longest slide.

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- AUDIO — s… a… t… sliding into "sat" → the student blends along.
- IMAGE — a picture of three sound bubbles joining into one word
  bubble → the student points and says each stage.
- GIF — a picture that moves: the letters slide together until the
  word lights up → the student says the sounds as they close.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student blends
  each word along with it.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the
plan is the build sheet, so nothing the lesson needs gets forgotten.

## If the learner is stuck
- Says the sounds but no word stands up → slow down more; the slide
  is the skill, not the speed. Speed comes by itself.
- Guesses the word from the first sound → cover the word, blend only
  the sounds given, in order.
- This lesson's neighbours: The alphabet (before — knowing the
  letters); Two-letter sounds and Word families (next door — their
  own lessons).
