# Blending sounds — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: three letters standing apart: s … a … t]

Here are three sounds.
s… a… t…
They are apart.

[Picture: the letters sliding closer]

Now push them together.
Faster. s-a-t…

[Picture: the word SAT standing whole, a child sitting]

SAT!
The sounds became a word.
You read it.

[Picture: p, a, t sliding together]

Again. p… a… t… PAT!
Now you try it. t… i… n…
