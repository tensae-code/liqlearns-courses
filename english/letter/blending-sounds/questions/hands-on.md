# Blending sounds — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: the sliding sounds
You need: paper, your pencil.
1. Write s, a, t big, with wide spaces.
2. Say each sound as you point. Slide your finger faster and
   faster until the word stands up: sat.
3. Do the same with sit, pat, tin, pin.
4. Write each blended word whole, once, from memory.
