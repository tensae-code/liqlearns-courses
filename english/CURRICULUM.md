# English course — CURRICULUM (the path)

His order (2026-10-25): the course folders alone don't show what comes
first and what comes next — so this file is the plan a learner follows,
starting from someone who knows no English at all and ending able to
read and write real English. The lessons themselves live in their
title folders (see TITLES.md); this plan is the path through them.

**Levels, not terms (his ruling, 2026-10-26):** the plan's big parts
were first called terms, like a school year. They are LEVELS now: a
learner climbs to the next level when they are ready, not when a
calendar says so. A level is a BASE for the next level — what the
levels fix is the ORDER, what must be standing before the next thing
can stand on it — not a wall around a level's topics.

**ONE FORMER UNIT = ONE LEVEL (his ruling, 2026-10-27):** the four
broad levels first built for this course each held four units, and the
units were not the same size. He separated them: every former unit is
now its own level. **Pencil and Lines is Level 1** — his correction in
the same ruling: that is level one, not a step before Level 1 and not
Level 11. Pencil and Lines also remains its own shared course, the
recommended prerequisite before every language course. The other
sixteen levels below are the sixteen former units, in their original
order. All are already built; this ruling changes their separation
and numbering, not their lessons.

## The first page (what learners are told)

Everything in this course comes from government textbooks from around
the world — government-approved books only; that is the research this
course is built on. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a
box is from us, the people making LiqLearns — a clearer way to say a
thing, or help from other material — written to help you understand.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

The plan is built from the books actually read so far and GROWS as
each next closed country's books are read — a new book's material is
slotted into the level it belongs to, in order, and a new block the
size of a former unit becomes its own next level rather than being
stuffed into an old one. (Each book's stocking notes — which level its
basics feed — are kept with the book's own map, in EGYPT-BOOK-MAP.md
and books/<country>.md, not in this plan.) Lessons already built are
marked ✅.

## Level 18 — Deeper Sentences
The deeper sentence machinery, from Libya G8–G10's spine: the
tenses and shapes that reach further than the plain sentence.
End of level: the learner holds the past by its tie to now, can
turn a sentence around into the passive, and can hang one
sentence on another with if.
- ✅ **The present perfect** (english/sentence/the-present-perfect)
  — have/has + the past participle: just happened, result you can
  see, still going, experience — and the bright line, no exact
  time (Libya G8 CB Unit 3 pp.32–33 + Teacher's Book; Libya G9
  p.33 and Grammar Reference; Egypt Primary 6 Term 2 Unit 3 p.47 —
  2 countries).
- ✅ **The present perfect continuous**
  (english/sentence/the-present-perfect-continuous) —
  have/has + been + verb-ing: action still moving from a
  past start, for/since, the marks of a just-stopped
  action, recent habits (Libya G8 CB p.38; Libya G9 p.40;
  Libya G10 p.13 + Work Book pp.11–12; Egypt Secondary 12
  Term 1 pp.17–19 — 2 countries).
- ✅ **The passive** (english/sentence/the-passive) — be +
  the past participle, the sentence turned around: by +
  the doer kept or dropped, and be in its coats — present,
  past, future, perfect (has been done), modal (can be
  turned) (Mongolia English X pp.76–86, 131; Libya G9
  p.82 five-tense table; Egypt Prep 9 T1 Tips p.17 and
  Tip p.45 — 3 countries).
- ✅ **Conditionals** (english/sentence/conditionals) —
  the if-machine in four rungs: zero (always true), first
  (really possible, will/may/might), second (imagined),
  third (a past that can't change) (Libya G9 pp.79–81 +
  reference; Mongolia English IX/X tables and unless;
  Egypt's Prep 7→8→9 ladder, third at Prep 9 T1 Tip
  p.31 — 3 countries).

**LEVEL 18 COMPLETE 2026-10-27: 4 lessons. The English
path now stands at 51 lessons across 18 levels.**
