# English course — CURRICULUM (the year plan)

His order (2026-10-25): the course folders alone don't show what comes
first and what comes next — so this file is the plan a learner follows:
**one school year, four terms**, starting from someone who knows no
English at all and ending able to read and write real English. Inside
a term, **units**, and inside a unit, the lessons in the order they
are taken. The lessons themselves live in their title folders (see
TITLES.md); this plan is the path through them.

**How the terms work (his correction, 2026-10-25):** the term
descriptions below were his EXAMPLE, not sealed boxes. A term is a
BASE for the next term — Term 1 is the base Term 2 stands on, Term 2
is the base for Term 3, and so on up. A term is not "only" its named
topics: some lessons are huge and grow past a neat slot, and new
material slots in wherever its base is ready. What the terms fix is
the ORDER — what must be standing before the next thing can stand on
it — not a wall around each term's topics.

The plan is built from the books actually read so far (Egypt, Libya,
Nicaragua) and GROWS as each next finished country's books are read —
a new book's units are slotted into the term they belong to, in order.
Lessons already built are marked ✅; the rest are named from the book
maps already read and are built as the reading reaches them.

**Before Term 1 — Pencil and Lines** (its own course, prerequisite,
recommended never forced): holding a pencil · drawing lines ✅

## Term 1 — Letters, sounds, and first words
*The bottom of the ladder. End of term: the learner reads and writes
every letter, sounds out new words, and can say hello and their name.*

- **Unit 1 — The Alphabet**: the letters, their shapes and sounds
  (the-alphabet ✅) — Egypt KG1/KG2 teach 13 letters + 13 letters;
  Primary 1 re-teaches all 26 in sounds order.
- **Unit 2 — Sounds Together**: putting sounds together to read a word
  (blending-sounds ✅ · two-letter-sounds ✅)
- **Unit 3 — First Words**: reading whole little words (blends ✅ ·
  word-families ✅ — the -at, -an, -ig families and their kin)
- **Unit 4 — Conversation**: hello-and-your-name ✅ · how-are-you ✅ ·
  good-morning-and-good-afternoon ✅ · introducing-a-friend ✅ ·
  whats-his-and-her-name ✅ · how-old-are-you ✅

## Term 4 — Paragraphs, essays, and stories
*End of term: the learner reads whole texts, writes paragraphs and a
first essay, and retells a story — the course's first year is finished.*

- **Unit 1 — The Paragraph**: sentences about one idea, together ·
  first, then, finally
- **Unit 2 — Reading Whole Texts**: finding the idea of a text ·
  words you don't know — use the dictionary · answering from the text
- **Unit 3 — The Essay**: paragraphs about one thing, in order ·
  opening and closing
- **Unit 4 — Stories**: people, a place, things happening · telling a
  story back · the year's big reading (the graded readers bound into
  Libya's Grade 11 books mark where this road leads)
