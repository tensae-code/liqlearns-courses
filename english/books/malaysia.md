### §4 Honest negatives — what this book does NOT teach

Checked explicitly, by the Content Mapping's own grammar/sound inventory, by every Grammar in Use section, and by full-text search of the file:

- **Phonics:** NO systematic phonics. There is no letter–sound blending or decoding programme. Pronunciation work ("Say Right") is limited to discriminating and repeating vowel and diphthong contrasts (short/long vowels in Chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 12; /juː/ in Chapter 6; diphthongs in Chapters 7, 8, 9, 10; a full sound revision table in Chapter 16) and, in Chapters 13–15, word stress and intonation. Sounds are always taught through whole-word lists, never through grapheme rules.
- **Conditionals:** NONE. No if-clause grammar of any type (zero, first, second or third) is presented anywhere; the word "conditional" does not occur. (A single "If I were in your shoes, I would…" appears only as a memorised advice formula on p. 139, with no explanation or practice.)
- **Passive voice:** NOT taught. The passive is nowhere presented as a structure; the word "passive" does not occur.
- **Reported speech:** NOT taught. No direct-to-indirect speech reporting, backshift or reporting verbs are covered.
- **Present perfect:** NOT taught as a tense. Chapter 3 tabulates past participles as the third column of verb tables, but no perfect tense is ever formed or explained from them. Present-perfect forms occur only incidentally, inside Chapter 16's positive/negative transformation drills (e.g. sentences built on "has done", "have taken") and inside reading/listening texts, without any rule.
- **Present perfect continuous:** absent entirely. **Past perfect:** absent as a taught form (one incidental "had formed" occurs inside the Chapter 3 cloze narrative; it is not explained).
- **Other tense gaps:** the tense syllabus is exactly three simple tenses — simple present (Ch. 2), simple past (Ch. 3), simple future with will/going to (Ch. 14). There is no present continuous or past continuous teaching either.
- **Exam preparation:** NONE. There are no PT3- or SPM-style papers, no model examinations, no timed comprehension or essay sections, and no examination technique instruction. (The string "PT3" and "SPM" never occur.) Assessment is limited to the Let's Review pages and the four Checkpoints.
- **Answer keys:** NONE. No answers are printed for any exercise, review or checkpoint anywhere in the book.
- **Listening scripts / audio:** scripts ARE present (all 16, pp. 201–205) — but the **audio itself is not in the file**; it sits behind the printed QR codes/URLs, so the PDF alone cannot play any listening text. There is likewise no teacher's material of any kind in the file.
- **Writing ceiling:** no essay-length or continuous writing is taught; the longest taught forms are letters (formal and informal), journal/blog/e-diary entries, and single-paragraph writing. There is no summary-writing instruction.

### §4 Honest negatives — what this book does NOT teach

- **Conditionals:** Zero, first, second and third conditionals ARE taught (Unit 8, Grammar 1, p.99; Grammar Reference §§8.1–8.4), and **mixed conditionals ARE taught** (Unit 8, Grammar 2, p.101; §8.5, one pattern: past-perfect if-clause + would + infinitive main clause, i.e. present result of a hypothetical past event). **Inverted conditionals are NOT taught** — there is no *Had I known…*, *Were I…* or *Should you…* pattern anywhere in the units or the Grammar Reference. The only inversion in the book is fronting with *not only … but also* (Unit 12, Grammar 2, p.153).
- **Passive:** Fully taught — all common tenses, gerund/infinitive/modal passives, the *get*-passive (Grammar Reference only), and impersonal/personal structures (Unit 7, pp.87 and 89). The Grammar Reference (p.170) explicitly notes there is no passive form for the future continuous, present perfect continuous and past perfect continuous.
- **Reported speech:** Fully taught — statements, questions, commands and requests (Unit 10, Grammar 1, p.125) and reporting verbs with their patterns (Grammar 2, p.127).
- **Present perfect continuous:** Taught (Unit 3, Grammar 1, p.35, alongside the present perfect simple; Grammar Reference §§3.2–3.3 contrast the two).
- **Past perfect:** Both past perfect simple and past perfect continuous are taught (Unit 4, Grammar 1, p.47), and contrasted with the past simple (Grammar 2, p.49). Future perfect simple and continuous are also taught (Unit 5, Grammar 2, p.63).
- **Exam papers / marking schemes:** NONE. No SPM papers, model papers, past-paper extracts, marking schemes or examiner guidance of any kind; "SPM" never appears (see §2).
- **Answer keys:** NONE. The Reload pages, Progress Reviews and all exercises are printed without answers anywhere in the book.
- **Listening scripts:** NONE. No tapescripts or transcripts are printed for any listening or DVD task.
- **Also absent:** no contents page in this copy (lost with the front matter, §6); no dedicated pronunciation/phonetics strand (speaking work is functional — comparing, advising, clarifying); no Malaysian content — settings, people, places and currencies throughout are British/international (Bristol, Harrods, Best Choice, euros and pounds), and no Malaysian place, name or ringgit amount appears in the book; no teacher's material is bound into this student's book.
