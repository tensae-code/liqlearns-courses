## Basics found, and where they are filed
1. **Greetings are the opening basic** — Week 1 (p. 4), before any unit:
   speak *Hi / Goodbye, What's your name? / My name's*; color the two
   hands — left hand = Goodbye (red), right hand = Hello (green) — and
   trace the words Hello / Goodbye; "My name is ___" (p. 5). Unit 1 is
   "Greeting and Numbers" (p. 14). → Filed: **Greetings →
   hello-and-your-name** (3rd country; spot-checked by Muse on p. 4,
   2026-10-25 — page matches exactly).
2. **No alphabet, no letter sounds anywhere** (full-text check). Letter
   work is only "Complete with vowels" inside whole words (H_ll_, p. 14;
   Sk__rt, p. 36). → Letter title gains nothing from Nicaragua.
3. **Pre-writing = cursive tracing**, from Week 1 — every model word and
   sentence in cursive, whole sentences by Unit 6. No pencil-grip page
   (Egypt KG1 p. 1 stays the only grip source so far). → Noted for
   Pencil and Lines: a different writing model (cursive-first) exists;
   merges when its turn comes.
4. Sentence patterns arrive as tracing only ("I have a pink skirt,"
   p. 37; "I like / I don't like ice cream," Unit 8) — traced, never
   composed. → Sentence title: patterns recorded, not yet built from.
