## Grade 6 — English VI — FULLY READ + CITATION CHECK PASSED 2026-10-26

**English VI** — textbook for Grade 6 of the 12-year general education school. Authors: N. Mira (team leader), I. Saingerel, D. Sarnai; editor P. Bayanjargal. Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports of Mongolia, Ulaanbaatar; first published 2015 (bibliographic note on the imprint page), this copy the **4th printing, 2019**, printed by Ochirpress LLC in a run of **37,000 copies**, with Asian Development Bank project support. **ISBN 978-99962-74-22-0** as printed on the imprint page; the back cover's printed ISBN line reads 99962-74-22-5 while its barcode digits read …4220 — both recorded as printed. Working copy 58,474,541 bytes, 139 PDF pp; printed pages 1–136, **printed page = PDF page − 1**. The PDF is image-only (no text layer): the book was read page by page from 100-DPI page renders, and the citation check was run against those renders, three pages re-checked at 150 DPI. Front matter: cover and title (pp.1–2), contents (p.3: Content Map p.4 · Grammar Reference p.118 · Dictionary p.129), and a four-page **Content Map** (pp.4–7) that tables every unit's Listening/Speaking/Reading/Writing/**Use of English** objectives — the year's grammar spine is readable off it whole. Every unit opens with a coded Learning Objectives page (L/S/R/W/UE codes) and closes with a **Self-Check** (Vocabulary · Use of English · Listening and Speaking · Reading and Writing) ending in an "I can … / Well / OK / Not well" self-rating box. The year's anchor pupil is **Telmen** (spelt Telmen and Telmen in different units, as printed), with Anu, Namuun, Och, Maral, James and others recurring.

**The year's spine, in order:** present simple of *to be* + present simple + demonstratives (U1) → prepositions of place + present continuous + imperatives (U2) → numbers 1–100, even/odd, ordinals, in/on for dates, there is/are (U3) → possessive 's + frequency adverbs + possessive adjectives/pronouns (U4) → comparatives + superlatives + irregular plurals (U5) → past simple + and/but/or + -ly adverbs (U6) → telling the time + prepositions of time on/in/at + So do I / Nor do I (U7) → countable/uncountable + some/any + quantity nouns + will for intention + would like (U8) → weather adjectives + when clauses + should/shouldn't (U9) → past simple and will revisited + question words (U10) — then a bilingual **Grammar Reference** (pp.118–127), a **Numbers** table (p.128), and an English–Mongolian **Dictionary** (pp.129–136).
