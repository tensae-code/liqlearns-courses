## Class 1 — Marigold (Textbook in English for Class I)

NCERT, first edition February 2006; this copy the
2020-21 printing; ISBN 81-7450-478-8; price ₹65 as
printed; printed at Amit Printing Press, Mathura;
cover, layout and illustrations Henu Mehtani. Built on
the National Curriculum Framework 2005. Chairperson,
advisory committee at primary level: Anita Rampal;
chief advisor R. Lalitha Eapen (EFLU, Hyderabad).
Read in full, page by page, 2026-10-26, from the
Internet Archive's copy of NCERT's own chapter files
(prelims + Units 1–10; NCERT's site will not serve this
network). 122 printed pages in all.

SHAPE: TEN units (pp.1–120) plus a closing PICTURE
DICTIONARY (pp.121–122). Every unit is the same
machine: one POEM, one STORY, then the apparatus —
"New words," "Let's read" (two model sentences), and
activity strands (Reading is fun / Let's talk / Let's
share / Let's act / Let's draw / Let's do / Let's
write / Say aloud / Sing) — and every unit ENDS WITH
ITS OWN TEACHER'S PAGES inside the same chapter file:
listening, pronunciation (rhyming sets), exposure to
language (sight words), speaking (role play in four
colour groups), writing/fine motor, raising awareness,
and a materials list. No formal grammar anywhere: the
teacher pages state the method — mother tongue allowed
at the start, DO NOT correct mistakes, concept order
"from near to far," writing is the LAST milestone.

The letters are taught as a slow strand across the
whole book, never as one alphabet lesson: a–c written
in U1, a–f said aloud in U2, CVC word-building from U3
(bag/hen/tap/cap/tin/cot/ant/pen/fox/axe with the
missing vowel filled), join a–z in U4, writing in
groups a–r by U5, s–z by U6; CAPITALS introduced in
U7 and finished W–Z in U10. Numbers: 1–10 in U1,
11–20 in U3's teacher pages. The Picture Dictionary
(p.121–122) pairs each letter with one word and a
picture, letter first, no "for": A apple, B balloon,
C cap, D dog, E elephant, F fish, G grapes,
H helicopter, I igloo, J jug, K kite, L lamp, M moon,
N nest, O orange, P peacock, Q quilt, R rose, S sheep,
T tomato, U umbrella, V violin, W whale, X Xmas tree,
Y yak, Z zebra.

## Class 2 — Marigold Book Two (Textbook in English for Class II)

NCERT, first edition February 2007; this copy the
2020-21 printing; ISBN 81-7450-670-5; price ₹65 as
printed; printed at Good Luck Publishers Ltd.,
Saharanpur; cover, layout and illustrations Henu
Mehtani; same committee leads as Class 1 (Anita
Rampal; chief advisor R. Lalitha Eapen). Read in
full, page by page, 2026-10-26, from the Internet
Archive's copy of NCERT's own chapter files (the
chapter PDFs are image scans; read from the files'
own text layer). 176 printed pages in all.

SHAPE: TEN units (pp.1–174) plus a closing PICTURE
DICTIONARY (pp.175–176) — this year the dictionary
is an alphabet RHYME. Same unit machine as Class 1
(poem + story + New words + Reading is fun + talk/
share/act/write strands + Teacher's Pages at each
unit's end), with three growth steps: (1) most units
now carry EXTRA poems or pieces beyond the pair —
short rhymes dropped between the activities; (2)
CORRECTION WORK begins — the child fixes printed
mistakes (missing capitals, missing full stops) in
sentences the book shows wrong; (3) real language
points are now taught inside the activities, still
unnamed: plurals, opposites, pronouns, a/an/the,
question words, before/after, prepositions. The
Teacher's Pages add: running hand (joined writing,
pencil not lifted till the word ends), the tripod
grip, and "if the child is inclined to left-handed
writing, do not push the child to be a right-handed
writer."
