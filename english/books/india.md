# India — English books read for LiqLearns

India's set: the NCERT books (National Council of
Educational Research and Training, the central
government's own publisher) — Marigold for Classes 1–5,
Honeysuckle 6, Honeycomb 7, Honeydew 8, Beehive 9, First
Flight 10, Hornbill 11, Flamingo 12. Twelve books.
Read basics-only: what each book teaches and where —
no transcription.

## Class 1 — Marigold (Textbook in English for Class I)

NCERT, first edition February 2006; this copy the
2020-21 printing; ISBN 81-7450-478-8. Read from NCERT's
own chapter files (prelims + Units 1–10, 132 pp. in all).

Shape: TEN units, each one POEM + one STORY, picture-led,
closing with a Picture Dictionary (pp.121–122). Every
unit works the same way: the poem and story, a "New
words" list, "Reading is fun" questions, and talk / do /
write activities. No formal grammar at Class 1 — first
words, short sentences, and the world close to the child.

- U1 A Happy Child (poem) · Three Little Pigs (story)
- U2 After a Bath · The Bubble, the Straw, and the Shoe
- U3 One Little Kitten · Lalu and Peelu
- U4 Once I Saw a Little Bird · Mittu and the Yellow Mango
- U5 Merry-Go-Round · Circle
- U6 If I Were an Apple · Our Tree
- U7 A Kite · Sundari
- U8 A Little Turtle · The Tiger and the Mosquito
- U9 Clouds · Anandi's Rainbow
- U10 Flying-Man · The Tailor and his Friend
