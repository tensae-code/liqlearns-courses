## How every unit is built (the book's own pattern)
Nine units, ten lessons each, and every unit follows the same
skeleton: six sound lessons (each: a new letter-sound or two-letter
sound with two example words, "Look, listen and say," then "Trace
and say" with numbered stroke order), one theme lesson, a
review/trace-and-match lesson, a capital-letters lesson, and a final
lesson that blends the unit's sounds into real words or sentences.
Every unit ends with a "Now I can …" self-check page. Every page
also carries the A–Z alphabet strip along the foot.

## Activity Book — unit by unit (book pages; one page per lesson)

The Activity Book is the Pupils Book's practice twin: the same nine
units, the same sound order, worked by hand — trace, circle, match,
colour, draw. It teaches no second course; it makes the Pupils
Book's basics stick. Unit 1 has 8 lessons; Units 2–9 have 10 each.

### Unit 1 "The Alphabet" (book pp.4–11)
All letter-recognition work: circle the small letter that matches a
capital; tick the matching letter; tick/cross same-pair checks
(including planted traps like Hb, Kl); circle among l m n o p runs;
odd-one-out letter grids; match small→capital for q–v; find W X Y Z
among scattered small letters; circle the mistake in letter-pair
rows (De, Gi, Jp, Nm, Tj, Yv planted wrong); match capitals to
smalls.

### Unit 2 "My Family" (book pp.12–21)
Trace + colour pages for each of s a t p i n; L3 trace and match the
names Ahmed / Nadia; numbers traced 1–5 (L4) and 6–10 (L5); L7 the
family words matched to pictures (sister, mum, grandpa, dad,
grandma, brother); L8 letter grids + colour by colour-word (green,
red, yellow, blue); L9 capitals matched back to smalls (S→a, A→t,
T→s, P→i, I→n, N→p — "satnip" worked backwards); L10 trace the
word/picture set apple, sun, taxi, ink, nose, pizza.

### Unit 3 "My Body" (book pp.22–31)
Trace page per sound (c k e h r m d); L2 "Look and say: What is it?
It's a tomato."; L3 count and circle; L4 body words with the last
letter left to trace — hea**d**, han**d**, ar**m**, le**g** — labelled
hair, elbow, body, foot; L5 the face labels (ear, mouth, eye, nose,
face); L6 "My face / my eyes / my nose / my mouth" draw-and-match;
L7 colour the sentence "I brush my teeth"; L8 odd-one-out + letter
match; L9 capitals; L10 trace + draw for c, k, m, d.

### Unit 4 "My House" (book pp.32–41)
Trace per sound (g o u l f b); colour pink / orange / black; L3
circle the right letters inside two-letter choices; L5
colour-by-number (1 = red, 2 = yellow, 3 = blue, 4 = green); L6 a
letter-grid word search holding bin, table, desk, chair; L7 circle
the odd one out (the six sound letters) + a Draw page (join the
numbered dots 1–10); L8 "Find and write" — net, kite,
flower, gate, key against numbered pictures; L9 trace the whole
alphabet in the handwriting font; L10 trace the year's first reading
sentences — "The cat is in the hat. / The cat is on the mat." —
plus trace and draw.

### Unit 5 "Things Around Us" (book pp.42–51)
Trace each two-letter sound with its words (ai TRAIN/TAIL, oa
BOAT/COAT, ie PIE/TIE, ee BEE/TREE, or FORK/POPCORN); L1 draw —
"It's a tail. It's a …"; L2 circle the answer: "Is it a train? Yes,
it is. / No, it isn't." · "Is it jam? …"; L4 match the sound (ai /
oa / ie) to the word with its middle missing (b__t, tr__n, t__,
p__, c__t, t__l); L5 count and match ("__ bees / __ flowers / __
rabbit" against 1, 2, 5); L7 match single letters and sounds
together (ai, j, f, oa, u, ee, ie); L8 circle-and-write the missing
sound (jam, pizza, lion, fork, train, popcorn); L9 complete the
alphabet sequences, capitals and smalls; L10 trace and match (tail,
tree, juice, tie, fork, coat) + draw "A bee on a flower."

### Unit 6 "My Classroom" (book pp.52–61)
L1 trace z + trace-and-match open / close / draw; L2 trace w +
trace-and-colour STAND UP / SIT DOWN; L3 trace ng + KING/RING; L4
trace v + trace-and-colour "five"; L5 trace oo + MOON/ZOO and "Look
and say: What is it? It's a rubber."; L6 trace MOON/ZOO/FOOT/BOOK +
draw for oo and ng; L7 join the numbered dots 1–10, then trace
**"What is it? It's an elephant."**; L8 trace the oo review set —
book, moon, foot, zoo, wood, boot; L9 match capitals to smalls,
then trace and copy STAND UP / SIT DOWN; L10 match ng and oo, and
draw.
→ Filed: Pencil and Lines → drawing-lines sources — the
dot-to-dot pages (here and Unit 4 L7): Libya's way of practising
the hand is joining numbered dots, a straight line from point to
point until a picture stands. Drawing-lines acceptance count
becomes 2 countries (Egypt, Libya).

### Unit 7 "My Toys" (book pp.62–71)
L1 trace y + tick/cross letter checks (y, w); L2 trace x — FOX,
DOLL, SIX, and X RAY / BOX traced whole; L3 trace ch — CHICKEN,
CHEESE traced whole; L4 trace sh — FISH, SHEEP traced whole
(**sheep** is a new word here, not in the Pupils Book), then count
and write: __ yo-yos, __ cars, __ balls (pictures counted 6, 7, 9);
L5 trace th (unvoiced) — MOUTH, THREE — trace and match teeth /
bathroom, and THANK YOU traced; L6 trace and match THAT / THOSE /
THESE / THIS, then Find: word strings with the words joined —
fishskatesdoll · foxcarbike · ballboatbook; L7 draw nine pictured
words (fish, cheese, tree, moon, book, teeth, king, yo-yo, box);
L8 match words to pictures (window, juice, car …) + draw; L9 trace
the capitals A–F, then trace and copy brother, fish, chair, box,
king, spoon; L10 trace capitals M–S, then trace, copy and draw:
"AN ANT — THREE BALLS."

### Unit 8 "At The Zoo" (book pp.72–81)
L1 trace qu — QUESTION, QUEEN traced whole + colour; L2 trace ou —
MOUSE, HOUSE traced whole (house joins the ou words here), trace
and colour "mouse"; L3 trace oi — POINT, OIL — then match ou / oi
to the word with its middle missing (__l = oil, m__se = mouse);
L4 trace ue — GLUE, BLUE — trace and match LITTLE / FUNNY / LONG /
BIG; L5 trace er — TIGER, WATER — count and trace (numbers 7, 6,
8); L6 trace ar — CAR, STAR — match and circle the right letters;
L7 match and draw (first letters c, e, t, p, m against pictures);
L8 trace the whole zoo set in a grid: camel, zebra, ant, snake,
fish, rabbit, elephant, goat, mouse, lion, monkey, tiger; L9 match
and write (letters a–l against pictures); L10 trace, match and
colour FLOWER / BEE / BIRDS / TREE, then trace and draw the two
sentences **"The bee is on the flower. / The birds are in the
tree."**
→ in/on sentences recorded here: Libya's second in/on sentence set
(after "The cat is in the hat / on the mat"), Sentence title
material.

### Unit 9 "Things I Know" (book pp.82–91)
L1 trace the whole small alphabet a–z, then trace numbers 1–4 with
ant, fish, dog, egg; L2 draw for the letters/sounds b, k, oo, ar,
trace and complete the capital sequence, and complete the words
with their missing letters — r_bb_t (rabbit), g__t (goat), z_br_

and copy (van, yo-yo, x-ray, box); L4 draw cake, hand, umbrella,
kite, then a big join-the-dots 1–18; L5 find and copy (word pieces

pink ruler." / "I have a red ball." / "I have a blue van."; L7 draw
counted sets — 4 cats, 2 tigers, 3 pizzas, 7 apples — then a
scattered-alphabet page (A–Z letters spread over the page to find);
L8 read and match: "A plane and a car." / "A lion and a monkey." /
"A king and a kite." / "A key and some jam."; L9 match and write
the numbered word list 1–16 (pizza, ball, camel, ruler, lion,
kite, monkey, tiger, skates, pencil, desk, cake, book, jam,

**"Goodbye."**
→ Reading sentences recorded here ("I have a …" + colour + object;
the joining word "and" in read phrases) — Sentence title material.
The traced "Goodbye." is the Activity Book's own closing greeting
practice (Greetings sources already hold Libya's Pupils Book
greetings).

## Teacher's Book — what it adds (read 2026-10-25)

Method note (honest coverage): the Introduction (book pp.4–17),
all nine unit word lists, the Stories appendix (pp.184–185) and
the Phonetic Symbols appendix (pp.186–187) were read in full;
every unit's aims, language and materials were checked; the audio
scripts were read wherever they carry language the other two
books do not print in full (songs, chants, dialogs, stories). The
step-by-step classroom procedure — the same pattern repeated for
all 90 lessons — was read in full in Unit 1 and followed by its
language content in the rest.

- **The people of the course**: Ahmed and his sister Nadia (two
  Libyan children), their friends Hani and Sarah, Adam the
  Astronaut and Rosie the Robot (Introduction, p.6). Two more
  names live only in lessons: **Tim** (Unit 3 wash/brush pictures)
  and **Zeinah**, Sarah's friend in the fish story. (Character
  names are book facts, never course titles.)
- **How letters are formed**: a traffic-light system — start on
  the green dot, pause at the orange dot, stop at the red dot —
  with numbered arrows (Introduction, p.5). No writing at all in
  Unit 1; forming starts in Unit 2, by synthetic phonics:
  blend sounds → words → whole-word recognition.
- **Pencil holding is a named aim** in Units 2, 3 and 4 ("Learn
  how to hold a pencil correctly") and the lesson notes check
  grip, seating and book position again and again — taught by the
  teacher showing it; no printed grip steps. → Filed: Pencil and
  Lines → holding-a-pencil sources (count 2 with Egypt).
- **Activity Book p.18** (Unit 2 Lesson 7) is a "Trace the lines"
  page — lines of different movements for pencil control,
  left-to-right — invisible in the Activity text layer because it
  is all picture. → Filed: Pencil and Lines → drawing-lines
  sources (Libya paragraph).
- **Word-list catches** (words the Pupils map above did not
  name): snake works as a blending/review word already in Unit 2
  (L8–9); cake joins the c/k lesson (Unit 3 L1); **rooms** in
  Unit 4 L7 — sitting room, bedroom, kitchen; bus in Unit 4 L6;

## Activity Book Grade 2 — unit by unit (book pages)

### Unit 1 "The Alphabet" (book pp.4–11)
L1: trace and complete the dialog — "How old are you? I'm
___." — colour pairs (orange and yellow · pink and blue),
count and trace numbers to 10. L2–L7: trace each sounds
group in smalls and capitals (s a t p · i n c k · e h r m d ·
g o u l · f b j w v · y x q z), L5 also tracing whole words
(umbrella, orange, girl, lemon). L8: a **word search** —
find girl, zebra, lemon, apple, taxi, monkey, rabbit, van,
dog, yo-yo in the letter grid (the year's word-hunt device,
first here).

### Unit 2 "About Me" (book pp.12–21)
L1 (two pages): trace "Hello. I'm ___." and "What's your
name?"; draw by joining letters a→z in order; trace and
copy **oa** (boat, coat) and fill the missing letters
(b__t, c__t); count — "How many?" L2: count and circle the
right number (keys, eggs, monkeys, vans, fish, zeros);
trace and match letters. L3–L4: read and circle the right
word for each picture (bin / ball / bus · elephant / egg /
ear · hand / cat / head · mouth / eyes / egg · eyes / net /
nose · lemon / moon / legs · hands / hair / legs · eyes /

count and write; say and circle the odd letter sound. L7:

### Unit 3 "Healthy Food" (book pp.22–29)

### Unit 4 "My Home" (book pp.30–39)

in the room. There is a ball on the desk. There is a cat

L3: complete sentences with the room words — "Mum is in
