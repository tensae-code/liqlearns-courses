## How every unit is built (the book's own pattern)
Nine units, ten lessons each, and every unit follows the same
skeleton: six sound lessons (each: a new letter-sound or two-letter
sound with two example words, "Look, listen and say," then "Trace
and say" with numbered stroke order), one theme lesson, a
review/trace-and-match lesson, a capital-letters lesson, and a final
lesson that blends the unit's sounds into real words or sentences.
Every unit ends with a "Now I can …" self-check page. Every page
also carries the A–Z alphabet strip along the foot.

## Pupils Book — unit by unit (book pages; contents pages read in full)

### Unit 1 "The Alphabet" (book pp.7–16)
The whole alphabet first, in A–Z song order: L1 the whole alphabet
(book p.8); L2 a–g + "Good morning / Good afternoon" (p.9); L3 h–k;
L4 l–p; L5 q–v; L6 w–z; L7–8 review. One word per letter: apple,
ball, car, duck, elephant, flower, gate, hand, insect, jam, kite,
lemon, monkey, nose, orange, plane, queen, rocket, sun, tomato,
umbrella, violin, watch, x-ray, yo-yo, zebra.
→ Filed: Letter → the-alphabet (A–Z order way); Greetings (Good
morning / Good afternoon).

### Unit 2 "My Family" (book pp.17–28)
Sounds **s, a, t, p, i, n** — one per lesson, two words each:
s (sister, son), a (apple, ant), t (taxi, tomato), p (plane, pizza),
i (ink, insect), n (net, nose). Riding on the sound lessons: L1
"Hello. Goodbye." · L2 Colours — blue, green, red, yellow · L3
"What's your name? I'm …" (I'm Nadia. I'm Ahmed.) · L4 Numbers
1–5 (with a Sing page) · L5 Numbers 6–10 · L6 "How many brothers do
you have? Three." · L7 the family set — dad, mum, sister, brother,
grandma, grandpa — sung: "He's my dad. She's my mum." · L8 trace and
match the six sounds · L9 capital letters ("satnip" — the six small
letters matched to their capitals) · L10 **blending**: the six
letters spelled out and blended — s-i-t → sit, p-a-t → pat, t-i-n →
tin, p-i-n → pin. "Now I can …": say greetings, say colours, ask and
answer about names, count to ten, say family words, make some
words.
→ Filed: the-alphabet (sounds way, group 1); Greetings (Hello/
Goodbye, name exchange). Family set, colours 1st set, numbers 1–10
recorded here for the Word/Sentence/Maths titles.

### Unit 3 "My Body" (book pp.29–40)
Sounds **c, k, e, h, r, m, d**: c (car, cat), k (key, kite) — the two
/k/ letters taught in one lesson · e (egg, elbow) + "What is it?
It's a … / What are they? They are …" · h (hand, hair) + "How

head, arm, body, leg · m (mouth, moon) + ear, eye, mouth, nose,
face · d (dog, duck) + "My nose. My eyes." · L7 actions: "I wash my
face/hands. I brush my teeth/hair." · L10 blending: hit, ten, hen,
and the first a–e words: make, cake, racket, packet.
→ Body set, "What is it? It's a …", wash/brush sentences recorded
here for the Word and Sentence titles.

### Unit 4 "My House" (book pp.41–52)

### Unit 9 "Things I Know …" (book pp.101–112) — the review unit
L1–2 word-building reviews (tiger, queen, watch, monkey, bus, duck;
ink, cat, ant, son, net, red, zoo, foot) · L3 rhyming words · L4
story — **"Sarah's Little Fish"** (a listening story; the text
layer carries its two spoken lines: "I'm 6." / "It's my little
fish.") · L5 talk about differences (listen and tick) · L6 "I have a
pen." → "I have a pen and a pencil." (a memory game) · L7 "How
many …?" find and count · L8 "What is it? / What can you see?" ·
L9 Draw: My bedroom · L10 the closing dialog, traced and answered:
"Hello. — What's your name? I'm ___. — **How old are you? I'm ___**
— Goodbye." + Sing. "Now I can …": write words, match rhyming
words, listen to and follow a story, match letters and pictures,
find the difference, talk about what I have, talk about pictures,
draw and talk about my bedroom, write my name and age.
→ Filed: Greetings sources — the age exchange (first book in the
courses to teach asking age inside the greeting).

## Basics filed from this book
1. **Letter → the-alphabet**: Unit 1's A–Z way (one word per
   letter) and Libya's full sounds line — 42 sounds in Jolly
   Phonics group order (Units 2–8), incl. both oo sounds and both th
   sounds as separate lessons. Count stays 2 countries (Egypt,
   Libya).
2. **Greetings → hello-and-your-name**: Good morning / Good
   afternoon (U1), Hello/Goodbye + name exchange (U2), and the age
   exchange "How old are you? I'm …" (U9). Count 3 with Nicaragua.

   colours (10, with sound spellings), numbers 1–10, body, house,
   classroom (+ instructions), toys, zoo animals, the question
