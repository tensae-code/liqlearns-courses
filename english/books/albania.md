### The guide's organising system for Chapters 31–40

Each chapter unit in the guide follows the same template:

1. An **overview table**: Topic; Learning outcomes ("The student: …"); Grammar focus; Main vocabulary (a word list); **Language structures** — a set of complete example sentences printed in full (the guide's largest body of actually-printed student-facing language); **Interactive methods** — a named list of the platform activity types the chapter contains (memory games, guessing, songs, readings, Personal Coach, etc.), described in one line each, never reproduced.
2. For Chapters 31–37 only, a **Sample lesson plan**: topic and duration (45 minutes; Chapter 31 is 45+45), its own shorter outcomes list, "Language elements", resources/materials, cross-curricular links, an **Akelius destination** URL on `languages.akelius.com` for that chapter, and a step-by-step Methodology and activities sequence (warm-up, presentation/guided practice, activities, wrap-up/reflection).
3. **Worksheets**, printed in full and ready to photocopy: 2 each for Chapters 31, 32, 33, 34 and 36; 1 each for Chapters 35 and 37; and for Chapters 38–40 — which have **no sample lesson plan** — sets of comprehension-question and true/false worksheets keyed to named platform reading/listening sections (4 sheets for Chapter 38, 4 for Chapter 39, and for Chapter 40 two sheets plus seven further "More exercises" for its Recipe section).

### Chapter 31 — Body parts — overview printed pp. 10–11 (PDF 13–14); sample plan printed pp. 12–13 (PDF 15–16); worksheets printed pp. 14–15 (PDF 17–18)

- **Outcomes (as listed):** name common body parts including facial features; describe physical features in full sentences; follow basic physical instructions ("Touch your toes"); describe movement with verbs (jump, stretch, fall, move); ask/answer about body parts ("Where does it hurt?"); role-play exercise/description dialogues.
- **Grammar focus (as named):** adverbs and adjectives, forming adverbs from adjectives (adjective + *-ly*); revision of simple present and present continuous; revision of possessive adjectives.
- **Main vocabulary:** body-part nouns (head, arm, foot, leg, ears, mouth, lips, neck, tongue, back, chest, hips, shoulder, stomach, ankle, knee, elbow, toe, wrist), movement verbs (fall, jump, move, stretch), and descriptors (still, beard, glasses, curly, beautiful, look).
- **Printed student content (i):** the vocabulary list and a full Language structures set (identification sentences, present-continuous actions, imperatives "Shake your head!", and the adjective/adverb contrast pair — a slow animal walking slowly; eating popcorn quietly).
- **Platform-only (ii):** Bingo, memory games, shell-sentences, guessing and Personal Coach activities, and the song "Body parts" — named with one-line descriptions only.
- **Sample plan:** topic "Body parts", duration 45+45 (the only double-length plan in the book); language elements "Giving instructions; Asking and answering wh- questions"; cross-curricular link Biology; an Akelius destination URL for Chapter 31; warm-up on a platform animation, guided platform tasks, a "Simon says" game, and writing from Worksheet 2.

## 5. Student-content grading

The scheme used throughout this filing: **(a)** student content reproduced in full; **(b)** student content printed as excerpts, lists or example sets; **(c)** student content named or described but not printed; **(d)** teacher methodology or course description with no student content. Graded separately:

- **Front matter (printed pp. 1–9 (PDF 4–12)): (d), with (b) elements.** The Introduction, blended-learning sections and Tips are methodology and platform description addressed to teachers. The printed reference matter inside them — the Explanation of symbols chart (printed p. 4 (PDF 7)), the CEFR A2 global-scale descriptors and the A2 topic lists (printed pp. 5–6 (PDF 8–9)) — is printed in full as lists, grade (b) as lists; none of it is student lesson content.
- **Chapter overviews (printed pp. 10–63 (PDF 13–66)): (b).** For each of Chapters 31–40 the guide prints the learning outcomes, the "Main vocabulary" list, the "Grammar focus" line and a complete set of example sentences ("Language structures"). These are real student-facing language, printed in the guide — but they are the course's word lists and model sentences, not the course's lessons: no lecture text, dialogue, reading passage or exercise from the platform is reproduced in an overview.
- **Sample lesson plans (Chapters 31–37 only; printed pp. 12–46 (PDF 15–49)): (d).** Methodology throughout — warm-ups, presentation steps, monitoring and feedback moves — written for the teacher. The student language inside them (suggested role-play phrases, board examples, discussion questions) is fragmentary and incidental to the teaching script; it does not amount to a reproduction of any platform lesson.
- **Interactive methods (in every overview): (c).** The platform's lectures, games, songs, videos, readings, art/science/architecture sections and Personal Coach exercises are named and described in one line each, chapter by chapter, and **never reproduced**. This includes every reading the later worksheets depend on: "Tommy's accident" (Chapter 35, printed pp. 33–34 (PDF 36–37)), "Petra" and "The Dead Sea" (Chapter 38, printed p. 49 (PDF 52)), "Space travel" and "Exploring the planets" (Chapter 39, printed pp. 55–56 (PDF 58–59)), and the Chapter 40 Recipe story (printed p. 63 (PDF 66)).
- **Worksheets (printed pp. 14–69 (PDF 17–72)): (a) for the sheets themselves; (c) for the platform readings several of them question.** Every worksheet is printed complete and ready to photocopy — the artefact is fully present, grade (a). But the comprehension and true/false sets of Chapters 38, 39 and 40 (printed pp. 50–65 (PDF 53–68)) question readings and a listening story whose texts are nowhere printed, so as evidence of *those readings'* student content they grade (c): questions without the text they question.

**Overall grade: (b)** — as stated in the header. The guide's student content is printed excerpts and lists (vocabulary lists, grammar-focus lines, model-sentence sets, complete worksheets), not a reproduction of the student course: **no (a) reproduction of any platform lesson exists anywhere in the book**, and the platform course itself — the actual student side — is present only at grade (c), named and described. The (a) worksheets do not lift the overall grade, because they are teacher-made photocopiables printed in a teacher's guide, not the platform lessons the guide serves.
