## Grammar / vocabulary spine (Grade 3)
Greetings + name/age questions with be (Ch.1A) → I like/love +
activity verb phrases (Ch.1B) → family nouns, he/she/they and
Who-questions (Ch.2A) → be from + Indonesian place names
(Ch.2B) → present continuous for current activities, all
persons (Ch.3A) → in + room (Ch.3B) → there is/are + Is there…?
(Ch.4A) → prepositions of place + place adjectives (Ch.4B) →
this/that, in/on/under, How many…?, numbers to 50 (Ch.5A) →
whose, possessive 's, his/her, colours (Ch.5B) → job nouns and
What is he like? / What does he look like? + adjectives
(Ch.6). No past tense, no future form, no alphabet/phonics
strand anywhere in the book — English starts here as whole
spoken phrases backed by audio; writing is copying and
gap-filling only.

## Honest negatives (Grade 3 student book)
- No letter names, phonics or spelling work of any kind;
  reading is whole-phrase recognition of taught expressions.
- Numbers stop at 50, and 31–39 are never printed (the ladder
  jumps 30 → 40 → 50 on p.121).
- The alphabet song and days/months are absent (contrast the
  China G3 book, which teaches letters from Unit 1).
- Two internal inconsistencies: the front matter (p.vii) says
  Bara's family is from Bima, but Ch.2's teaching and story
  put Bara from Sumba throughout (pp.50, 52); and the mother
  is from Bandung in the chant and story (pp.49, 52) but from
  Cianjur in the front matter (p.vii) — the teacher guide's
  answer key sides with Cianjur (see the guide's section).
- Chapter title drift: Ch.3 is "My Family's Activities at
  Home" in the Contents but "My Family Time" in the running
  heads; Ch.6 is "My Teacher" in the Contents, "My Teachers"
  in the body.

  for the same panel.
- Enrichment links per chapter (buku.kemdikbud.go.id/s/
  ing3kaya1–5) are named in the guide; they are teacher
  enrichment pages, not pupil material.

## Series verdict (Grade 3 pair)
A complete, tightly-matched pair: the guide adds nothing to
the pupil language spine beyond the two transcript items
above, but it is the only place the audio layer, the answer
keys, the Fase B attainment framing and the Oral Story
Sharing lesson model exist in print. For course-building the
student book carries the spine; the guide is the authority
for what the audio teaches and for settling the student
book's two origin inconsistencies (one each way).

## Grammar / vocabulary spine (Grade 4)
Simple-present routines with time adverbials (always, usually,
around) and clock time (Ch.1) → food nouns, What's for…?,
Can I have…? (Ch.2A) → taste/texture adjectives and because
(Ch.2B) → May I borrow…? permission (Ch.3A) → shapes +
possessive 's for parts (Ch.3B) → present continuous extended
to school activities and places (Ch.4) → can/cannot with
sense verbs and ball actions, imperatives, feelings with
negated be (Ch.5) → days, subjects, months, ordinal dates and
on + date (Ch.6). Against Grade 3: the phrase-only approach
starts to grow sentence patterns — guided paragraphs with
sequence words (Ch.1), model descriptive texts (Ch.2, Ch.3),
and one prose story read for detail (Ch.3B).

## Honest negatives (Grade 4 student book)
- Still no alphabet, phonics or spelling strand; still no
  past tense or future form taught — the single was/will be
  pair lives inside one chant (p.139) and is never presented
  as language to learn.
- Clock time stops at the :15/:30/:45 pattern read as
  five-fifteen etc.; no quarter to/past, no 24-hour time.
- Ordinals are given only from 11th to 31st (plus first in
  "the first group" in game instructions); 1st–10th are
  nowhere listed — the book assumes the Grade 3 numbers or
  skips them.
- The date table (p.144) prints two suffix errors as models:
  "21th" and "31th" in the numeral row (the word row below
  correctly has Twenty-first and Thirty-First).
- The birthday worksheet (p.149) likewise prints "Bara 31th"
  as its worked example.
- Picture captions drift: Picture 5.1 is captioned "Art
  Class" in a PE chapter (the Daftar Gambar repeats it), and
  Picture 2.4 / Picture 3.x cross-references in the text do
  not always match the Daftar Gambar numbering.

## Series verdicts (partial — only the read books judged)

- **Primary pair read so far (Grades 3–4):** one continuous
  series with one cast and one lesson machine. Grade 3 builds
  spoken phrase frames topic by topic (self, family, home,
  school, classroom, teachers) and smuggles in the present
  continuous and there is/are as frames, never as grammar;
  Grade 4 starts converting frames into sentence patterns —
  usually/always + routines and clock time, because-reasons,
  May I…? permission, can/cannot — and gives pupils their
  first guided paragraphs and one prose story. The series'
  hard limits at this point: no phonics or alphabet work at
  all, no past or future forms taught, writing = copying,
  gap-filling and frame-completion. Both books carry small
  internal inconsistencies (origin facts in Grade 3; date
  suffixes and a glossary misprint in Grade 4), each filed in
  its section.
- **Not yet judged:** Grades 5–6 (the Fase B pair's second
  half), the whole secondary run (Grades 7–12), and the six
  mirror-row student books, whose editions cannot even be
  named until the library copies are opened. A series-final
  verdict for Indonesia is owed when the set is read.
