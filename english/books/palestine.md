## Grade 1, Part 1 (Semester 1) — Student's Book & Workbook 1A

MoEHE & Macmillan Publishers, 2011 ("New Edition");
ISBN 9780230409873. 64 pp. Each unit is six "Periods"
(lessons), and every unit closes with a parents' page
("In Unit N children learned…"). NINE units, then a
Picture Dictionary and — last of all — The Alphabet:

- U1 Hello! — greeting people, saying your name (hello,
  hi, goodbye, good morning, good afternoon)
- U2 Let's eat! — food; numbers 1, 2, 3
- U3 Animals — numbers 4, 5, 6
- U4 My body — numbers 7, 8, 9, 10
- U5 Revision (of Units 1–4)
- U6 My classroom — "What's this? It's a pencil."
- U7 My family — "Who's this? This is my brother."
- U8 Let's drink!
- U9 Revision (of Units 6–8)
- Picture Dictionary · The Alphabet (p.62 — the letters
  come at the END of Part 1, after a semester of talk)

Palestine starts like Bangladesh: conversation first,
letters last — the alphabet is not this book's opening.
