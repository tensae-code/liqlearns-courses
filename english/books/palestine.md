## Grade 1, Part 1 (Semester 1) — Student's Book & Workbook 1A

English for Palestine, Ministry of Education &
Higher Education (State of Palestine) with Macmillan.
Cover: "STUDENT'S BOOK & WORKBOOK 1A — NEW EDITION."
Authorship & Curriculum Supervisory Committee:
Ali Manassra (General Supervision), Othman Diab Amer
(Coordinator), Reem Ayoush, Dr Samir M. Rammal
(Birzeit University), Dr Hazem Y. Najjar (Bethlehem
University) and eight further ministry members; the
original-edition committee was headed by Najjar with
Dr Salem Aweiss (Birzeit) and Dr Omar Abu Al-Hummos
(Al-Quds). Text by Wendy Arnold; designed by Melissa
Swan; illustrated by Ruth Palmer; publishing
management Hyphen S.A.; printed and bound in
Palestine by A R Hijjawi & Sons; © Macmillan
Publishers 2011, first published 2011. ISBN as
printed in the Part 2 imprint: 978-0-230-40987-3;
the amended imprint bound at the end of this Part 1
file prints ISBN 978-0-2304-1355-9 — both recorded
as printed. 64 pp.; printed page = PDF page.
Downloaded from www.englishforpalestine.com (the
footer on every page).

Shape of the book. NINE units, a Picture Dictionary
(pp.58–61) and The Alphabet (pp.62–63). Every
teaching unit runs SIX Periods (lessons) in a fixed
rhythm: Period 1 presents the new words and the
unit's dialogue by listening (Listen and find /
Listen and say / Say); Period 2 is the writing
period — tracing and copying the unit's numbers or
letters, find-and-circle; Period 3 drills the
dialogue pattern; Period 4 carries the unit's SONG
plus tracing/matching; Period 5 is listening,
counting and colouring work; Period 6 is workbook
tasks ending in a game (Play) and the PARENTS' PAGE —
"In Unit N children learned: …" — printed at the
foot of Period 6. The two Revision units run THREE
Periods each. The cast throughout: Tala, Sami,
Salwa, Amir, Wafa, and the picture-dictionary names
Pip and Polly.

The letters do NOT open the book. Conversation
comes first (Units 1–5 carry no letters at all —
only traced NUMBERS), and letters arrive inside
Units 6–8, a few at a time, in this order: a, c
(Unit 6) · e, o (Unit 7) · d, g, q (Unit 8) — each
traced, copied, found and circled, and matched
against the letters taught before. The full A–Z is
printed only at the END of the book (pp.62–63),
each letter with one word: a apple, b bag, c cat,
d dog, e egg, f fig, g goat, h hand, i insect,
j juice, k kitten, l lion, m melon, n nut, o olive,
p pencil, q quiet, r rabbit, s skate, t teacher,
u umbrella, v van, w water, x box, y yo-yo, z zebra
(the word list is repeated identically in Part 2).

Unit by unit (Contents pp.3–4 give each unit's
context and language; the filings follow the book):
- U1 Hello! (p.4) — greeting people, saying your
  name. Words: hello, hi, goodbye, bye, good morning,
  good afternoon. Pattern: "What's your name? My
  name's Tala." Tracing in this unit is shapes and
  lines only; the parents' page: children learned
  to say hello, to ask someone's name, to give their
  own name.
- U2 Let's eat! (p.10) — food: nut, olive, melon,
  date, egg, bread, cheese; NUMBERS 1, 2, 3 — traced
  and copied ("1 1 11 1 / 2 2 22 2 / 3 3 33 3"),
  counted and written through the unit.
- U3 Animals (p.16) — insect, goat, rabbit, cat,
  kitten, lion, zebra, dog; numbers 4, 5, 6 traced
  and copied; counting joins and songs.
- U4 My body (p.22) — head, hand, finger, leg, face,
  nose, mouth; numbers 7, 8, 9, 10 traced and copied;
  "Listen and touch" — the body words are taught on
  the child's own body.
- U5 Revision (p.28) — of Units 1–4, in three
  Periods: listening and counting, tracing and
  copying 1–10, and the book's first written
  ADDITION — picture sums with "+ =" (p.31).
- U6 My classroom (p.34) — board, teacher, pencil,
  book, bag, desk. First pattern question: "What's
  this? It's a pencil / It's a bag." (p.36). LETTERS
  a and c open here (trace and copy "aaa / ccc,"
  find-and-circle among a/c strings, p.35).
- U7 My family (p.40) — Mum, Dad, sister, brother,
  baby, me. Pattern: "Who's this? This is Tala /
  my brother / my mum / the baby" (pp.42–44).
  Letters e and o (p.41); the unit's song sits in
  Period 2 here.
- U8 Let's drink! (p.46) — water, milk, tea, coffee,
  apple juice, orange juice; "What's this? It's
  water / It's apple juice." Letters d, g, q (p.47).
- U9 Revision (p.52) — of Units 6–8, three Periods:
  the two patterns side by side ("What's this? It's
  a book." / "Who's this? This is my dad." /
  "What's this? It's a zebra," p.54); write with the
  seven letters a c d e g o q; letter-counting sums
  at the close. SLIP recorded as printed: the
  parents' page says "In Unit 9 children revised
  the language learned in Units 5–8" (p.57) — the
  Contents and the unit itself say Units 6–8, and
  Unit 5 is itself a revision unit.

Picture Dictionary (pp.58–61): every word of the
part drawn and listed alphabetically under its
first letter (a: apple juice, Amir; b: baby, bag,
board, bread, brother, bye; …) with a tick box
beside each, then the numbers 1–10 (p.61).

## Grade 1, Part 2 (Semester 2) — Pupil's Book 1B

Same series, committee and printing (imprint
p.64: ISBN 978-0-230-40987-3, Text © Macmillan
Publishers Limited 2011, "Written by Wendy
Arnold," printed and bound in Palestine by
A R Hijjawi & Sons; the impression line runs to
2019). Cover title differs from Part 1 as printed:
"PUPIL's Book 1B." 64 pp.; printed page = PDF page.
Units continue 10–18 in the same six-Period /
three-Period-revision shape, closing again with the
Picture Dictionary (pp.58–61) and The Alphabet
(pp.62–63, the identical word list). The letter
teaching completes the alphabet in the same
scattered order: i, l, t (Unit 11) · b, h, p
(Unit 12) · m, n, r (Unit 13) · v, w, x (Unit 15) ·
j, u, y (Unit 16) · f, k, s, z (Unit 17) — with
Unit 10's Period 6 copying the Part 1 set (a c /
e o / d g q) as revision. All 26 letters are covered
by Unit 17.

- U10 Let's play! (p.4) — toys: ball, doll, yo-yo,
  kite, balloon, skates. Pattern: "Where's my ball /
  kite / doll / yo-yo? Here! There! Thank you."
  Reading opens: Period 6 "Read. Find and write"
  (5 kites, and the toy words).
- U11 Transport (p.10) — car, bus, bike, van, taxi.
  Pattern: "How many taxis? Five taxis." — counting
  combined objects ("How many taxis and bikes?"),
  written addition again; letters i, l, t traced
  and the first whole-word writing ("v_an, _ike,
  _axi, _ar, _us," p.15).
- U12 Colours (p.16) — red, green, white, blue,
  black, yellow, with umbrella as the carrier word.
  Pattern: "What colour? Red and black." Reading
  and colouring join ("blue car, yellow taxi, white
  van, red bus, black bike, green car," p.21).
  Letters b, h, p.
- U13 My clothes (p.22) — t-shirt, shoes, shorts,
  jeans, dress, skirt, described by colour: "a red
  skirt, blue jeans"; read-and-colour with two-colour
  clothes ("yellow and green t-shirt," p.25).
  Letters m, n, r.
- U14 Revision (p.28) — of Units 10–13: colours
  written whole ("w_hite, _reen, _ed, _ellow, _lue,
  _lack"), all four question patterns matched to
  their answers in one exercise — "How many? Four.
  / What colour? Red. / What's this? It's a zebra.
  / Where's my ball? Here! / Who's this? This is
  Mum." (p.33).
- U15 My bedroom (p.34) — bed, table, chair, box,
  window, door. Pattern: "Where's the kite? It's in
  the box. / Where's the box? It's under the
  window." — IN / ON / UNDER taught as the position
  set, read-and-circle at the close ("The cat is in
  the box," p.38). Letters v, w, x.
- U16 In my country (p.40) — things that grow in
  Palestine: orange, lemon, grape, apple, banana,
  fig, olive. A/AN appears in the pattern: "What's
  this? It's a fig. It's an olive." and the first
  plural question "What are these? They're grapes."
  Size: "It's big / small," then "It's very big /
  very small" (pp.42–44). Letters j, u, y.
- U17 My friends (p.46) — introducing and
  describing a friend: "This is my friend … He's /
  She's tall." — I'M / HE'S / SHE'S with tall and
  short ("I'm short. He's tall," p.46). SIMPLE
  INSTRUCTIONS as the unit's second language:
  "Stand up. Sit down. Jump up and down." — listened
  to, done, read and matched, and written (p.48–49).
  Letters f, k, s, z complete the alphabet. New
  names in the dictionary: Pippa, Peter.
- U18 Revision (p.52) — of Units 15–17: positions
  ("Where's the cat? It's under the desk," "The
  kite is under the table"), size ("It's a big
  ball / a small umbrella"), bedroom words traced
  with first letters given ("b_ed, _able, _air,
  _ox, _indow, _oor"), and a closing full dialogue
  that replays the whole year — "Good morning.
  Hello. What's your name? My name's Jamila. Who's
  this? This is my baby brother. Goodbye. Bye."
  (p.56; Jamila is a new name, appearing only here).

Picture Dictionary (pp.58–61): the year's whole
vocabulary alphabetically with tick boxes, adding
Part 2's words (banana, big, black, blue, box,
bus, car, chair, doll, door, dress…) and the three
instructions as entries (in, on, under with their
dots; jump up and down; stand up; sit down).
