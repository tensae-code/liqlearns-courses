**In hand and read here:** 6 — the early-primary books only. 566 PDF pages.

The badges, Contents pages and production slugs printed inside the books settle each file's true identity; the corrections are listed under each book and gathered at the end of this file.

**The series.** Every in-hand book is a **Student's Book + Workbook combo binding** ("COMBO" in each production slug): the Student's Book body, then Phonics Practice / Word List / Picture Dictionary / Audio Track Lists / Objectives back matter, then the term's Workbook bound in. The three *We Can!* levels are by **Yoko Matsuka and Glenn McDougall** (McGraw Hill; series consultant Mayumi Tabuchi): *We Can!* © 2009, Saudi adaptation © 2024 (2023 printings) / © 2025 (2025 printings), Ministry of Education 2023–1445 / 2026–1448. **An ISBN is printed in every *We Can!* book** — the first filing's "no ISBN anywhere" claim was wrong. Grade 4 is a different series entirely, *Top Goal 1* (see Book 6). **No teacher's book exists in Saudi Arabia's record**, so there are no companion verdicts to give; every held book is a student's combo.

**How the books are organised.** Each *We Can!* term book has the same skeleton: an Introduction or opening units, 8 printed pages per unit, then the back matter and the bound-in Workbook (one Workbook goal page per Student's Book goal). The books print their own **Contents** and a multi-page **Syllabus** (Unit and Topic / Talk Time / Words / Phonics / Grammar) in the front matter — the accounts below follow those maps, spot-verified on the body pages named.

**What the in-hand set teaches (verified).** *We Can! 1* (Grades 1 Terms 1+3 in hand): the alphabet and letter sounds, greetings and name/age exchanges, body, family, classroom objects, colours and shapes, feelings, animals, days and weather, numbers 1–10 then 11–30 and tens to 100. *We Can! 2* (Grade 2 Terms 1+3): feelings and clothes, actions and the **present progressive**, house and prepositions of place, counting to 100, days and clock time, *can/can't*, weather, jobs with *does/doesn't*, *there is/are*. *We Can! 3* (Grade 3, full-year combo, partially scanned): possessives, comparatives and superlatives, the present simple, the **past tense** (irregular forms in Term 2, regular *-ed* and *was/were* in Term 3), *should/shouldn't*, directions, and *going to* for future plans. Top Goal 1 Part 2 (Grade 4): verb + *-ing* / to-infinitive, *could/couldn't*, *should*, relative pronouns, comparatives/superlatives, *be going to*.

**Honest negatives for the in-hand set.** No conditionals, no passive voice, no reported speech and no present perfect in any form are taught in any of the six books. No examination preparation and no printed answer key appears in any held student's-book or workbook page. The past tense is taught **only in *We Can! 3*** (from Term 2) and in no *We Can! 1* or *We Can! 2* book in hand, where past forms do not appear as teaching at all. Nothing can be said from this set about Saudi Grades 5–12: those books are link-only (see "Still owed").
