**What the in-hand set teaches, in one paragraph.** Grades 1–3 (*We Can!*) are oral-first phonics courses: a full letter-sound ladder (single letters, then *sh/ch/th/ph/wh*, silent *e*, *ar/er/ir/or/ur*, long vowels), vocabulary by theme, sentence frames practised in chants and short dialogues, one "I Can" reading passage per unit, and a picture dictionary per book. Grade 4 (*Top Goal 1*, Part 2 only in hand) is where named grammar begins: verb + to-infinitive / verb + *-ing*, *could/couldn't* for past ability, *should* for advice, relative pronouns *that/who*, comparatives and superlatives as one system, and *be going to* for the future. **No conditionals, no passive, no reported speech, no present perfect system, and no examination preparation appear in any of the six books**, and no student's book prints an answer key.
