**Read in full + citation-checked 4 November 2026 (286 anchors tested across the 13 books: 270 passed as cited, 16 corrected in place, 0 not found; 1,449 PDF pages).** Yemen's English record holds exactly 13 books, all in hand: for each of Grades 7–12 a Pupil's Book and a Workbook of one course — *English Course for Yemen*, Books 1–6 — plus a single teacher's book, Teacher's Book 3 (Grade 9). All 13 files were pulled from the secured library and byte- and MD5-verified against the record before reading (facts in the sections), and every cover was checked against its slot — every slot holds exactly the book its record names.

**The course.** The held set is one course end to end. Teacher's Book 3's Introduction identifies it as the **Crescent English Course** in its Yemen adaptation (first published in 1977 for Arab schools; the guide calls its level "Crescent 3" throughout, and its cover footer credits Oxford University Press's "English language Teaching for Arb World" line [as printed]). Teacher's Book 3 is an earlier, different printing of its level: the ministry's 2011–1432 retype. **No ISBN is printed in any of the 13 books**, and **no pupil's book prints its school grade anywhere** — every grade in this file rests on the series position (Book N = Grade N+6), exactly as the record shelves them.

**Companions.** Every companion volume checked is a **TRUE COMPANION**: all six workbooks key to their pupil's books by the pupil's book's own section numbers and constant page references that land (independently spot-verified in both directions at the citation check), and Teacher's Book 3 keys Step-for-Step to the held Pupil's Book 3 and Workbook 3 — the only difference is its earlier printing, with content and pagination unaffected. **The grammar spine across the six grades.** Book 1 (Grade 7) is foundations only: present simple, present continuous, past simple, *have got*, *can*, *there is/are* — no future form, no passive, no conditional, no reported speech, no present perfect, no comparison of any kind. Book 2 (Grade 8) adds the full past simple, the past continuous, *will/won't*, *would like* and *have to*, and superlatives — still no comparatives, and the same five structures absent. Book 3 (Grade 9) is where the course crosses its big lines: comparatives, the present perfect, the past perfect in narrative, the passive (present and past simple), relative clauses, and conditionals **Type 1 and Type 2** — but reported speech never arrives and Type 3 never does. Book 4 (Grade 10) mostly uses and reviews: its pupil's book confines the passive and **reported statements** to single review charts, while its workbook carries the explicit teaching (past perfect, reported statements with backshift, comparison, the passive, quantities, and the course's one dictionary-skills study); conditionals are used from its first unit but never systematised, and reported questions and commands are never presented. Book 5 (Grade 11) re-presents Types 1–2, extends the passive (present-perfect, modal and future passive), and adds *used to*, question tags and non-defining relative clauses, alongside its Arts and Science readers — still no reported speech, no Type 3, no perfect continuous. Book 6 (Grade 12) is a revision-and-use year: it presents the **present perfect continuous** (Language review 5, printed p.20 — the only place in the whole held set where that form is taught), recycles the passive and the perfect tenses, and builds its top end on modal deduction (present and past) — while conditionals and reported speech are **never taught as structures** in the final book either, and no future form is ever presented.

**What Yemen's held set never teaches.** **No examination preparation exists in any of the 13 books** — no past papers, model answers, mark schemes, unit tests or revision-for-exam units anywhere, including the Grade 12 certificate-year pair (independently confirmed for both Book 6 volumes by a page-by-page check: examinations appear only as story furniture). **No answer key exists in any pupil's book or workbook**; keys exist only embedded in Teacher's Book 3's step notes, and the teacher's books for the other five grades are not held. **Conditional Type 3 and mixed conditionals are never taught in any book. Reported speech is taught only as the Grade 10 reported-statements chart and workbook study** — reported questions and reported commands are never taught anywhere, and Books 1–3, 5 and 6 never teach reported speech at all. The **present perfect continuous is presented only in the final book** (Grade 12). There is **no phonics programme**: letter–sound grouping appears only in Grade 7's Unit 3, and the course's only pronunciation teaching (word stress, question intonation, minimal pairs) sits inside Workbook 6. There are **no tapescripts for most of the course's cassette listening** — the pupil's books print none, the workbooks print none, and Teacher's Book 3 prints scripts only for cassette-only material at its own level. Arabic support in the pupil-facing books is effectively nil (two prayer-time matching exercises in Workbook 4 and one Arabic-proverb translation task in Workbook 6 are the only exceptions).
