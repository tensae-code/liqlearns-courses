# Video script — Asking About Size and Measure

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands between a tiny dog and a huge dog.]
"How BIG is the dog? This dog is small. This dog is very big!"

[A long river winds past, then a tall tree.]
"How LONG is the river? How TALL is the tree?"

[The mascot lifts a heavy bag onto a scale.]
"How much does it weigh? Ten kilos! Heavy!"
