## True or false

1. "How tall is the river?" is the best question for a river. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a river is long, not tall: "How long is the river?"

2. "How much does it weigh?" asks about size. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it asks about weight, not size.

3. "It weighs five kilos" answers a question about weight. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — kilos measure weight.
