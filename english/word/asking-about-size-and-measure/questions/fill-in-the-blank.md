## Fill in the blank

1. ___ tall is the door? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: How** — the size questions start with How.

2. The table is two metres ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: long** — length answers end in long (or tall for height).

3. How much does it ___? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: weigh** — the weight question is "How much does it weigh?"

4. The bag ___ ten kilos. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: weighs** — "It weighs ten kilos."
