## Multiple choice

1. You want to know the size of a box. You ask: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) How tall is it?  b) How big is it?  c) How much is it?
   **Answer: b) How big is it?** — big asks about size in general.

2. For a river, the right question is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) How long is the river?  b) How tall is the river?  c) How heavy is the river?
   **Answer: a)** — long asks about length: rivers, roads, ropes.

3. For a tree, the right question is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) How long is the tree?  b) How big is the tree weigh?  c) How tall is the tree?
   **Answer: c)** — tall asks about height: trees, buildings, people.

4. You want to know a bag's weight. You ask: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) How much does it weigh?  b) How long does it weigh?  c) How tall is it weigh?
   **Answer: a)** — "How much does it weigh?" asks about weight.

5. "How much does the book weigh?" (Sub lesson 2)
   a) It is two metres long.  b) It weighs one kilo.  c) It is very tall.
   **Answer: b)** — weight is answered with a weight: kilos.
