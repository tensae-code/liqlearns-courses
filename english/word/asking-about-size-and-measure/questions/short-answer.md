## Short answer

1. Ask about the height of a building. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: How tall is the building?** — tall for height.

2. Answer in a full sentence: How long is your book? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: (a true measure sentence)** — e.g. "My book is thirty centimetres long."

3. Ask about the weight of a bag of rice. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: How much does the bag of rice weigh?** — the weight question.
