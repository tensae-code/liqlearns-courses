# Hands-on — Asking About Size and Measure

Hands teach these words best. Do these with real things, not only
pictures.

## Measure it, ask it

Take a ruler or a tape. Measure the table, the door and your own
book. For each one, ask the question out loud first — "How long
is the table?" — then measure, then answer in a full sentence:
"It is two metres long."

## Lift and guess

Collect five things: a book, a stone, a bag, a pot, a shoe. Lift
each one and guess its weight: "I think it weighs two kilos."
Then weigh them if you can, and say the true answers: "The stone
weighs three kilos."
