# Short Answer — Table Manners and Social Etiquette

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name three manners of the table. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any three of: wait until everyone is served; sit up; elbows off the table; chew with your mouth closed; never talk with your mouth full; never reach across a plate.
2. Why do we not talk with our mouths full? (Sub lesson 1)
   — It is unpleasant for the others to see and hear, and hard to understand; finish the bite first, then speak.
3. Say the polite way to ask for the water. (Sub lesson 2)
   — "Please pass the water." (or "Could you pass the water, please?")
4. A guest answers an offer of more food with "No, thank you." Is this rude? Why? (Sub lesson 2)
   — No — it is polite: the answer says no and still thanks the host.
