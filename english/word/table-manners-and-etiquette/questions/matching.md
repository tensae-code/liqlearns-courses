# Matching — Table Manners and Social Etiquette

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match the moment to the manner or the phrase.

1. The food is served, but the host has not started (Sub lesson 1)
2. Your mouth is full (Sub lesson 1)
3. You need the bread, far away on the table (Sub lesson 2)
4. You must leave the table for a moment (Sub lesson 2)
5. The host offers you more rice (Sub lesson 2)
6. The meal ends and you speak to the cook (Sub lesson 2)

a) "Excuse me."
b) Wait — do not start yet.
c) "Thank you — it was delicious."
d) "Please pass the bread."
e) "Yes, please." / "No, thank you."
f) Do not talk — finish the bite first.

Answers: 1-b, 2-f, 3-d, 4-a, 5-e, 6-c
