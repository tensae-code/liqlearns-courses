# Lesson Plan — Table Manners and Social Etiquette

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** how to behave, and what to say, when you eat with other people — the manners of the table and the polite phrases that go with them.

## Sub lessons

1. **Manners at the table.** *Can-do: Show the manners of a shared meal: wait until everyone is served before you start, sit up at the table, keep your elbows off it, chew with your mouth closed, never talk with your mouth full, and never reach across another person's plate — ask for things instead.*
2. **The phrases of the table.** *Can-do: Use the etiquette phrases of a meal: ask politely ("Please pass the salt"), excuse yourself ("Excuse me"), thank the cook ("Thank you — it was delicious"), offer food to the others before yourself, and answer an offer politely, with a yes or a no and a thank-you.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Table manners and etiquette phrases for eating with others (item 73): the behaviour of the table and the polite words that carry it, from Book 1's Module 3 Lesson 8 (printed pp.114–117).
- The same book lesson teaches *can / can't* for what is acceptable and *Can I…?* for permission (item 74, stocked in can-for-ability and modal-verbs) — the permission phrases of the table belong to that same meal.
