# Hands-On — Table Manners and Social Etiquette

1. **Set the table.** Lay a real table (or draw one): plates, cups, cutlery — and take your seat the way the lesson teaches.
2. **Pass it, don't reach.** Practise in pairs: one asks politely for the salt, the bread, the water; the other passes it. Then swap.
3. **The polite meal.** Role-play a whole meal in a small group: offers, answers, an excuse-me, and a thank-you to the cook at the end.
4. **Spot the mistake.** One learner acts out a table mistake (talking with a full mouth, reaching across); the others name it and say the manner that fixes it.
