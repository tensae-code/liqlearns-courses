# Levels — Table Manners and Social Etiquette

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Manners at the table | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Show the manners of a shared meal: wait until everyone is served before you start, sit up at the table, keep your elbows off it, chew with your mouth closed, never talk with your mouth full, and never reach across another person's plate — ask for things instead. |
| Sub lesson 2 — The phrases of the table | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Use the etiquette phrases of a meal: ask politely ("Please pass the salt"), excuse yourself ("Excuse me"), thank the cook ("Thank you — it was delicious"), offer food to the others before yourself, and answer an offer politely, with a yes or a no and a thank-you. |
