# Compound words — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — words
built from whole words — splitting first, then building, then
reading new compounds by their parts, the order the books teach it
in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: split the
books' compounds — football, hourglass, sundial, cupboard,
seaside, blackboard — into their two whole words, and pass
the alone-test on each part.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Books' Sets and Formation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join
word cards into true compounds (the second word is the
thing, the first word tells what kind) and spell them
joined or hyphenated the way the books print them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading by Parts
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a
compound you have never seen by its parts and say what it
must mean, and form compound plurals by Vietnam's rules.
