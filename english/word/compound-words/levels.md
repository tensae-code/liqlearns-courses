# Compound words — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — words built from whole words — set
by the pattern across the countries' books: splitting first, then
building, then reading new compounds by their parts.

## Level 1 — splitting
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: split the
books' compounds — football, hourglass, sundial, cupboard,
seaside, blackboard — into their two whole words, and pass
the alone-test on each part.

## Level 2 — building
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join
word cards into true compounds (the second word is the
thing, the first word tells what kind) and spell them
joined or hyphenated the way the books print them.

## Level 3 — reading by parts, and plurals
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a
compound you have never seen by its parts and say what it
must mean, and form compound plurals by Vietnam's rules.
