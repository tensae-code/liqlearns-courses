# Video script — Compound words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two words walk in. ONE word walks out!

**FOOT**… **BALL**… slide them together… **FOOTBALL!**
**SUN**… **DIAL**… **SUNDIAL** — the sun tells the time!
**HOUR**… **GLASS**… **HOURGLASS** — sand runs through!

A **BLACKBOARD** is a board that is black.
A **WATERFALL** is water that falls.
A **TOOTHBRUSH** is a brush for teeth!

Every part can stand alone — foot can, ball can.
That is a compound!

Now you. Split it: CUPBOARD.
Cup… board! You found the words inside!
That is compound words — in English.
