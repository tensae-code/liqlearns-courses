# Compound words — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Split HOURGLASS into its two words.
**Answer:** hour + glass (Sudan, SMILE Book 7, Unit 1).

**2. (Level 2)** Join SUN and DIAL. What does the compound
mean?
**Answer:** sundial — a dial the sun works on, for telling
the time.

**3. (Level 3)** Read by parts: what is a BLACKBOARD?
**Answer:** a board that is black (Vietnam's adjective +
noun type).

**4. (Level 3)** Name the kind of compound noun a swimming
pool is, in Vietnam's types.
**Answer:** verb-ing + noun — a pool for swimming.
