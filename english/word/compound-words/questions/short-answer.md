# Compound words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words · (Level 2)
-> Sub lesson 2 — The Books' Sets and Formation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3
— Reading by Parts.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Split HOURGLASS into its two words.
**Answer:** hour + glass (Sudan, SMILE Book 7, Unit 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Join SUN and DIAL. What does the compound
mean?
**Answer:** sundial — a dial the sun works on, for telling
the time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Read by parts: what is a BLACKBOARD?
**Answer:** a board that is black (Vietnam's adjective +
noun type).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the kind of compound noun a swimming
pool is, in Vietnam's types.
**Answer:** verb-ing + noun — a pool for swimming.
