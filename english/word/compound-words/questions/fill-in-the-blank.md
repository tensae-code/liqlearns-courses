# Compound words — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words · (Level 2)
-> Sub lesson 2 — The Books' Sets and Formation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3
— Reading by Parts.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hourglass · waterfall · toothbrush · seaside ·
greenhouse

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sand runs through an ______ to measure
time.
**Answer: hourglass** — hour + glass (Sudan's set).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Water that falls over rocks is a ______.
**Answer: waterfall** — read by its parts: water + fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** You clean your teeth with a ______.
**Answer: toothbrush** — tooth + brush.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Barbados prints the land beside the sea
as sea-______.
**Answer: seaside** — printed "sea-side," as printed.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Plants grow warm in a ______ — a house of
glass.
**Answer: greenhouse** — green + house.
