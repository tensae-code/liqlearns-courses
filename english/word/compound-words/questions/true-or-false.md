# Compound words — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words · (Level 2)
-> Sub lesson 2 — The Books' Sets and Formation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3
— Reading by Parts.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every part of a compound word can stand
alone as a word.
**True** — that is the alone-test; parts that cannot stand
alone (un-, -ness) belong to word building.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a compound, the FIRST word is the thing
itself.
**False** — the SECOND word is the thing (a sundial is a
kind of dial); the first word tells what kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Albania's doctor chapter names compound
nouns as a grammar focus.
**True** (A2 Chapter 35, printed p.32).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The plural of swimming pool is swimming
pools, with -s on the second word.
**True** — Vietnam's plural rules (Grade 11, Unit 10).
