Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words · (Level 3)
-> Sub lesson 3 — Reading by Parts.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — compound and its two whole words.
1. football — **foot + ball** (Sudan)
2. hourglass — **hour + glass** (Sudan)
3. sundial — **sun + dial** (Sudan)
4. cupboard — **cup + board** (Barbados, as printed)
5. blackboard — **black + board**
6. toothbrush — **tooth + brush**

is.
1. waterfall — **water that falls**
2. greenhouse — **a glass house for plants**
3. swimming pool — **a pool for swimming** (Vietnam's

4. sundial — **a dial the sun works on**
