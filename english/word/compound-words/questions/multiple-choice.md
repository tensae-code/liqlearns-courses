# Compound words — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Splitting Compound Words · (Level 2)
-> Sub lesson 2 — The Books' Sets and Formation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3
— Reading by Parts.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** FOOTBALL splits into ______.
a) foot + ball  b) foo + tball  c) footb + all
**Answer: a) foot + ball** — Sudan's own example (SMILE
Book 7, Unit 1, Lesson 5).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which part can stand alone as a word?
a) un-  b) -ness  c) sun
**Answer: c) sun** — the alone-test: compound parts are
whole words; un- and -ness are word-building parts.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A dial the sun works on is a ______.
a) sundial  b) dialglass  c) sunball
**Answer: a) sundial** — sun + dial (Sudan's set).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Barbados's syllabus prints cupboard as
______.
a) cup-board  b) cup board  c) cupboardd
**Answer: a) cup-board** — hyphenated, as printed (Class 1
syllabus).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The plural of blackboard is ______.
a) blacksboard  b) blackboards  c) blackboardes
**Answer: b) blackboards** — the -s goes on the thing word,
the second word (Vietnam's plural rules, Grade 11,
Unit 10).
