# Video script — British and American English

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

English lives in two big houses. London. New York.

Same language. Different name tags!

In America, pants. In Britain, trousers. Careful — this word tricks people!

A flat in London. An apartment in New York. Same home!

Holidays in Britain. Vacation in America.

British chips are American French fries. Hot and long!

And the spelling changes too. Colour — color. Metre — meter.

Neither is wrong. Just know BOTH. Then nobody can trick you.
