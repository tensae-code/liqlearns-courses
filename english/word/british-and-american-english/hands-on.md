# Hands-on — British and American English

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The quiz, live.** Run Vietnam's matching quiz on a partner: five British words, five American partners, shuffled. Then swap roles and add jumper/sweater.
2. **Wardrobe sort.** Take one page from a British book and one from an American book (or websites). List every word or spelling that betrays its side.
3. **Menu rescue.** Write a café menu twice — once fully British (chips, crisps, aubergine), once fully American (French fries, chips, eggplant) — so no traveller is surprised.
4. **Spelling families.** Collect ten -re/-our/-ise words and write their American twins; then read both lists aloud.
