**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An American's 'pants' are a Briton's — a) underwear b) trousers c) shoes **Answer: b) trousers** — Vietnam G9 U9's opening trap — in Britain 'pants' alone means underwear

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** British 'chips', hot and served with fish, are American — a) chips b) crisps c) French fries **Answer: c) French fries** — the quiz pair (Vietnam, pp.92–101); American chips are British crisps

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which spelling is American? a) colour b) color c) centre **Answer: b) color** — the -our/-or family: British colour, American color (Nepal G8 U7 Extra bit)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A text says 'vacation', 'apartment' and 'soccer'. It was dressed in — a) London b) New York c) neither can be told **Answer: b) New York** — all three are the American partners in Vietnam's quiz set
