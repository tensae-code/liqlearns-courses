# British and American English — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** A British ______ is an American sweater. **Answer: jumper** — one of Vietnam's five quiz pairs

**2. (Level 1)** The game Britain calls football, America calls ______. **Answer: soccer** — the fifth pair in Vietnam's quiz set

**3. (Level 2)** British 'favour' loses its 'u' in America: ______. **Answer: favor** — the -our/-or spelling family (Nepal G8 U7)
