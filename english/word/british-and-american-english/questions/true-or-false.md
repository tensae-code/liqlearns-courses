# British and American English — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** American English is wrong English. **Answer: False** — neither side is wrong; the skill is recognising the wardrobe (Vietnam's World Englishes unit)

**2. (Level 2)** 'Chips' names the same food in Britain and America. **Answer: False** — British chips = American French fries; American chips = British crisps — the same word, different food

**3. (Level 3)** Real books always keep the two spelling systems perfectly apart. **Answer: False** — Nepal's own Sports Day list mixes 'Meters' and 'Metres' on one page (printed p.80)
