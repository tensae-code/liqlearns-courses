# British and American English — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
RANKS inside this one lesson — pass each rank to climb.

## Level 1 — the famous pairs
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give both sides of Vietnam's five quiz pairs without a miss

## Level 2 — spelling at sight
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort written words into British and American by their spelling families (-re/-er, -our/-or, -ise/-ize)

## Level 3 — reading the wardrobe
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the side a fresh short text was written on, from its words and spellings, and translate its trickiest pair for a reader from the other side
