# Video script — The body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Touch your **head**! Touch your **nose**!
This is my **face**. This is my **mouth**.
Wiggle your **fingers** — one, two, three, four, five!
Stamp your **leg**!

Now the magic five.
I can **see** with my **eyes**. Look — the moon!
I can **hear** with my **ears**. Listen — music!
I can **smell** with my **nose**. A flower!
I can **taste** with my **tongue**. Ice cream!
I can **touch** with my **hands**. Soft!

See, hear, smell, taste, touch —
five senses, every day!

Now you. Touch the part. Say the name.
That is your body — in English.
