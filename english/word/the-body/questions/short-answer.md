# The body — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Touched Seven · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Parts at Work · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Whole Body Set.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Palestine's seven body words.
**Answer:** head, hand, finger, leg, face, nose, mouth
(Grade 1 Part 1, p.22).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Finish the five: I can see with my ___.
I can hear with my ___. I can smell with my ___. I can taste
with my ___. I can touch with my ___.
**Answer:** eyes · ears · nose · tongue · hands (Egypt,
p.76).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Omar has an apple. Which part does he taste
it with?
**Answer:** his tongue — the dialog's own line (Egypt, p.75).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three more body parts from Egypt's
letter pages.
**Answer:** hair (p.56), neck (p.47), elbow (p.72).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A boy is on the phone. Which sense is he
using, and with which part?
**Answer:** hearing, with his ear (Egypt, p.78).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the five senses in Egypt's closing
order.
**Answer:** see, hear, smell, taste, touch (Egypt, p.75).
