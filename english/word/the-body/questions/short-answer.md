# The body — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Palestine's seven body words.
**Answer:** head, hand, finger, leg, face, nose, mouth
(Grade 1 Part 1, p.22).

**2. (Level 2)** Finish the five: I can see with my ___.
I can hear with my ___. I can smell with my ___. I can taste
with my ___. I can touch with my ___.
**Answer:** eyes · ears · nose · tongue · hands (Egypt,
p.76).

**3. (Level 2)** Omar has an apple. Which part does he taste
it with?
**Answer:** his tongue — the dialog's own line (Egypt, p.75).

**4. (Level 3)** Name three more body parts from Egypt's
letter pages.
**Answer:** hair (p.56), neck (p.47), elbow (p.72).

**5. (Level 3)** A boy is on the phone. Which sense is he
using, and with which part?
**Answer:** hearing, with his ear (Egypt, p.78).

**6. (Level 3)** Say the five senses in Egypt's closing
order.
**Answer:** see, hear, smell, taste, touch (Egypt, p.75).
