# The body — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Touched Seven · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Parts at Work · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Whole Body Set.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** We taste with our ears.
**False** — we taste with the tongue. We hear with the ears
(Egypt, p.76).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** We can smell a flower with our nose.
**True** — "I can smell it with my nose. It smells nice!"
(Egypt, p.75).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The mouth and the nose are on the face.
**True** — Palestine's set puts them there (p.22).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A finger is bigger than a hand.
**False** — fingers are the hand's five small parts.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** We have two noses.
**False** — one nose. We have two eyes, two ears, two hands,
two legs — and ten fingers.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The moon is something we see with our eyes.
**True** — Egypt's own matching (moon → eye, p.78).
