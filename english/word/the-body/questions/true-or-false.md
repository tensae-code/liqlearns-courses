# The body — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 2)** We taste with our ears.
**False** — we taste with the tongue. We hear with the ears
(Egypt, p.76).

**2. (Level 2)** We can smell a flower with our nose.
**True** — "I can smell it with my nose. It smells nice!"
(Egypt, p.75).

**3. (Level 1)** The mouth and the nose are on the face.
**True** — Palestine's set puts them there (p.22).

**4. (Level 1)** A finger is bigger than a hand.
**False** — fingers are the hand's five small parts.

**5. (Level 3)** We have two noses.
**False** — one nose. We have two eyes, two ears, two hands,
two legs — and ten fingers.

**6. (Level 2)** The moon is something we see with our eyes.
**True** — Egypt's own matching (moon → eye, p.78).
