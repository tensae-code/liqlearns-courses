# The body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "Listen and touch": the word is **nose**. You
touch —
a) your mouth  b) the middle of your face  c) your leg
**Answer: b)** — Palestine's set (Grade 1 Part 1, p.22).

**2. (Level 1)** Your fingers are part of your ______.
a) leg  b) head  c) hand
**Answer: c) hand** (Palestine's set).

**3. (Level 2)** "I can hear with my ______."
a) eyes  b) ears  c) nose
**Answer: b) ears** — Egypt's sentences (Primary 1 Term 1,
p.76).

**4. (Level 2)** You smell a flower with your ______.
a) tongue  b) hands  c) nose
**Answer: c) nose** (Egypt, pp.76–78).

**5. (Level 2)** Ice cream belongs to which sense pair?
a) taste — tongue  b) see — eyes  c) touch — hands
**Answer: a) taste — tongue** (Egypt, p.78).

**6. (Level 3)** The bend in the middle of your arm is your
______.
a) knee  b) elbow  c) neck
**Answer: b) elbow** (Egypt's Ee words, p.72).

**7. (Level 3)** Which row counts the body right?
a) two noses, one eye  b) two ears, ten fingers  c) one hand,
three legs
**Answer: b)** — pairs and singles the books' sets stand on.
