# The body — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** eyes · ears · nose · tongue · hands · finger

**1. (Level 2)** I can see with my ______.
**Answer: eyes.**

**2. (Level 2)** I can hear with my ______.
**Answer: ears.**

**3. (Level 2)** I can smell with my ______.
**Answer: nose.**

**4. (Level 2)** I can taste with my ______.
**Answer: tongue.**

**5. (Level 2)** I can touch with my ______.
**Answer: hands.**
(1–5 are Egypt's five sense sentences, p.76.)

**6. (Level 1)** A hand has five small parts. One of them is
a ______.
**Answer: finger** (Palestine's set, p.22).
