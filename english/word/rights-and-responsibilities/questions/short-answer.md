# Short Answer — Rights and Responsibilities

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Say one right of a child, with a verb. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Example: "Children have the right to learn." (also: to play, to be heard, to live in safety)
2. What changes between "I have the right" and "she has the right"? (Sub lesson 1)
   — One person takes has; more than one takes have.
3. Name three rights said with nouns. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Any three of: the right to education, the right to health, the right to a name, the right to protection, the right to food and clean water.
4. Say one right both ways — with a verb and with a noun. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "Children have the right to learn." / "Children have the right to education."
