# Levels — Rights and Responsibilities

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Have the right to + verb | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Say what people may do, with the pattern have the right to + verb: children have the right to learn and to play, every person has the right to speak, to be heard and to live in safety. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Have the right to + noun | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Name the rights as things every person holds — the right to education, the right to health, the right to a name, the right to protection, the right to food and clean water — and say that rights come with responsibilities: mine end where yours begin. |
