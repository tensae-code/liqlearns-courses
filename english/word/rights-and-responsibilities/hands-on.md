# Hands-On — Rights and Responsibilities

1. **Rights wall.** Write five rights from the lesson on cards — one card each — and pin them up: education, health, a name, protection, food and clean water.
2. **Say it both ways.** Take a card and say the right two ways: with a verb ("Children have the right to learn") and with a noun ("Children have the right to education").
3. **Whose job?** For each right on the wall, name who must protect it: the family, the school, the town, the country.
4. **Rights and responsibilities.** Match each right with the responsibility that walks with it: the right to be heard — the responsibility to listen.
