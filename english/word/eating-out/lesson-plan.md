# Lesson Plan — Eating Out

**One thing:** the words for eating out — the places, the people and the things on the table — and how a meal out runs, from sitting down to paying.

## Sub lessons

1. Restaurants and cafés — the places and the people
2. Eating out — sitting down, ordering, paying

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The places: a restaurant serves full meals; a café serves drinks and light food.
- The people: the customer eats; the waiter (man) or waitress (woman) serves; the cook makes the food.
- The things: a menu lists the food and prices; you sit at a table; the bill is what you pay at the end.
- The run of a meal out: sit down → read the menu → order → eat → ask for the bill → pay.
