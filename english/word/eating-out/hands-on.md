# Hands-On — Eating Out

Things you do with your hands and voice. No special materials needed.

1. **Menu making.** Fold a paper in two. Outside, write your café's name. Inside, write three drinks and three foods with prices. That is a real menu — open it and read it aloud.
2. **Waiter, customer.** Play the meal with a friend or family member. The waiter brings the menu, takes the order, brings pretend food, and brings the bill. The customer orders and pays with pretend money. Then change places.
3. **Place spotting.** On your next walk, count the restaurants and cafés you pass. Say each one in English as you pass: "A restaurant." "A café."
4. **Bill maths.** Write a bill for two customers from your menu: one drink and one food each. Add the prices and write the total on the bill.
5. **My table.** Set a table at home for a meal out: plate, cup, spoon, fork. Point at each thing and say the whole run of the meal: sit down, menu, order, eat, bill, pay.
