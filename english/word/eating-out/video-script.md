# Video Script — Eating Out

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The eating-out words appear on screen as they are said.

[Picture: two doors — a big restaurant, a small café]

Two places to eat! A restaurant — big meals. A café — drinks and cakes. Which one do you like?

[Picture: inside a restaurant — tables, a waiter, people eating]

Look inside. The people eating are customers. The man bringing food is the waiter. The woman is the waitress. And the cook? The cook is cooking — you cannot see him!

[Picture: hands holding a menu]

First, the menu! The menu tells you the food — and the prices. Read… choose…

[Picture: a customer talking to the waiter]

Now order! "I would like the soup, please."

[Picture: eating, smiling; then a small paper — the bill]

Eat… enjoy… And at the end — the bill! You pay the bill. Thank you, waiter!

Now tell me about YOUR meal out!
