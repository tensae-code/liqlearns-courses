# Levels — Eating Out

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Restaurants and cafés — the places and the people | Tell a restaurant from a café, and name the people (customer, waiter, waitress, cook) and the menu. |
| 2. Eating out — sitting down, ordering, paying | Follow the run of a meal out and use the words for each step, ending with the bill. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can talk about a meal in a restaurant or café from start to finish.
