# Short Answer — Beach and Seaside Words

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the place words of the lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Beach, lagoon, cliff, waves, mountain (and the activity: swimming).
2. Why is a lagoon a good place for children to swim? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Its water is calm and shallow, held apart from the open sea and its big waves.
3. Suggest a beach for big-wave fun, and say why. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "Try the open beach beyond the cliffs — its waves are huge."
4. Describe a beach you know in two sentences, using three lesson words. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "Our beach is not touristy. The waves are small, and the view of the mountain is breathtaking."
