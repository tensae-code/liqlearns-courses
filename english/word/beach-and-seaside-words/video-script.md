# Video Script — Beach and Seaside Words

Super-simple lines, one picture per line.

[Picture: a wide beach, small waves rolling in]
"The beach! The waves come in, one, two, three."

[Picture: a calm lagoon, a child swimming safely]
"A lagoon — calm water. Perfect for swimming."

[Picture: a high cliff above the sea, a person looking down, amazed]
"A cliff! And the view — breathtaking!"
