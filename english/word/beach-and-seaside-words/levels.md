# Levels — Beach and Seaside Words

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Seaside words | First step into the lesson. Can-do: The place words: beach, lagoon, cliff, waves, mountain, swimming — and the judging words: breathtaking (so beautiful it stops your breath) and touristy (full of tourists). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Choosing a beach | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Use the words in advice: a local suggests beaches — Cabarete and Playa Dorada in the book — to a tourist who wants calm water; saying what each place is like and which fits. |
