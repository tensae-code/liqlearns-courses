# Lesson Plan — Beach and Seaside Words

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the words for the seaside — beach, lagoon, cliff, waves, breathtaking, touristy — and asking for and giving beach advice.

## Sub lessons

1. **Seaside words.** *Can-do: The place words: beach, lagoon, cliff, waves, mountain, swimming — and the judging words: breathtaking (so beautiful it stops your breath) and touristy (full of tourists).*
2. **Choosing a beach.** *Can-do: Use the words in advice: a local suggests beaches — Cabarete and Playa Dorada in the book — to a tourist who wants calm water; saying what each place is like and which fits.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Travel and nature vocabulary in a suggestions dialogue (item 239): advice, beach, touristy, mountain, breathtaking, lagoon, cliff, waves, swimming — a local suggests Cabarete and Playa Dorada to a tourist who wants calm water.
