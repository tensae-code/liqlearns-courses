# Lesson Plan — Art Words

**One thing:** the words for the kinds of art, and for the places where people keep and show art.

## Sub lessons

1. What is art? — the kinds of art
2. Museums and places of interest

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Art is what people make to be looked at, listened to and thought about; "What is art?" is itself a question with many answers.
- The kinds of art: painting, drawing, sculpture, photograph, music, dance, theatre, film.
- The places: a museum keeps old and important things; a gallery shows paintings and photographs; an exhibition is a show of art works for a time.
- Saying what you like with a reason: "I like this painting because…"
