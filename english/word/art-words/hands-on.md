# Hands-On — Art Words

Things you do with your hands and eyes. No special materials needed.

1. **Kind hunt.** Walk through your home or school. Find one example of each kind you can: a painting or drawing, a photograph, something shaped like a sculpture. Name each one in English as you touch it.
2. **Twenty-second gallery.** Put three of your own pictures or objects in a row on a table. That is your exhibition. Walk a friend or family member along it and name every work.
3. **Like it + because.** Stand before each work in your exhibition and finish the sentence out loud: "I like this one because…" One reason each time — colour, shape, the story it tells.
4. **Museum label.** Museums put a small card beside every work. Make a card for one work at home: its kind, who made it, and one sentence about it. Put the card beside the work.
5. **Photo or drawing?** Take one photograph of a place of interest near you — or draw it if you have no camera. Say which kind your work is, and why the place is a place of interest.
