# Video Script — Art Words

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The art words appear on screen as they are said.

[Picture: a wall with a painting, a drawing and a photograph]

Look at this wall. A painting. A drawing. A photograph. Three pictures — three kinds of art!

[Picture: a stone horse]

And this? You can walk around it. It is a sculpture. Sculpture is art in stone, wood or metal.

[Picture: people dancing; a stage; a cinema screen]

Music is art for your ears. Dance is art with your body. Theatre is a story with real people. A film is a story on a screen.

[Picture: a big museum building, people walking in]

Where does art live? In a museum! A museum keeps old and important things. A gallery shows paintings. An exhibition is a show — art together, for a little time.

[Picture: a child looking at a bright painting]

Do you like this painting? Say it — and say why! "I like this painting because the colours are bright."

Now you try. What is art? YOU tell me!
