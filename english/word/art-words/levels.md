# Levels — Art Words

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What is art? — the kinds of art | Name the kinds of art (painting, drawing, sculpture, photograph, music, dance, theatre, film) and say which kind a work is. |
| 2. Museums and places of interest | Name the places where art is kept and shown (museum, gallery, exhibition) and say what they like with a reason. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can talk about kinds of art and about visiting a museum or gallery.
