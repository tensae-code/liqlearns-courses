# Video script — Films and Movie Genres

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A cinema screen lights up; six film posters appear one by one.]

"Films come in kinds."

"A comedy makes you laugh. A horror film makes you scared."

"Action is fast. Drama is serious. Cartoons are drawn."

"Science fiction takes you to space. Which kind do you like?"
