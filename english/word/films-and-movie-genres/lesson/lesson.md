# Films and Movie Genres

## Do it first

Think of a film you love. Is it funny? Scary? Full of fights and chases? About space? You already know its kind — this lesson gives you the English names for the kinds of films.

## Sub lesson 1 — The genres

Films come in kinds, and each kind has a name. A **comedy** makes you laugh. A **drama** is serious — real people's lives, real feelings. A **horror** film makes you scared. An **action** film is fast — fights, chases, danger. **Science fiction** takes you to space and the future. **Cartoons** are drawn films — no actors, only pictures that move.

Say them with me: comedy — drama — horror — action — science fiction — cartoons. When you know how a film makes you feel, you know its genre: funny, serious, scary, fast, space, drawn.

*Difficulty: Six genre names, each tied to the feeling it gives.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Saying what you like

Now the genres become yours. Say what you like: "I like comedies — they make me laugh." Say what you love: "I love action films." And say what is not for you: "I don't like horror films. They are too scary."

Ask about others the same way: "Do you like science fiction?" "What kind of films do you like?" Six genre names, and you can talk about every film you have ever seen.

*Difficulty: The genre words at work in likes and dislikes.*

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion your favourite kind of film and one film of that kind. Ask them theirs.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write three film genres and one film you know for each.
2. Ask someone at home which kind of film they like best. Write their answer.
3. Invent a film title for each genre — a funny one, a scary one, a space one.
