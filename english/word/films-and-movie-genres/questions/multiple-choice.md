## Multiple choice

1. A film that makes you laugh is a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) horror film  b) comedy  c) drama
   **Answer: b) comedy.**

2. A scary film is a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cartoon  b) horror film  c) comedy
   **Answer: b) horror film.**

3. A film about space and the future is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) science fiction  b) drama  c) action
   **Answer: a) science fiction.**

4. Cartoons are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) drawn films  b) true stories  c) news films
   **Answer: a) drawn films.**

5. "I like action films" says… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a dislike  b) a like  c) a question
   **Answer: b) a like.**
