# Lesson plan — Films and Movie Genres

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Films and Movie Genres
**Lesson:** films-and-movie-genres — the kinds of films — comedy, drama, horror, action, science fiction and cartoons — and how to say which ones you like
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name the film genres and say which kinds of films they like.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 9 book opens its media teaching with the types of films, named as a set. No lesson in the course housed the film-genre set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **The genres**
2. **Saying what you like**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "The genres": name the kinds of films: comedy, drama, horror, action, science fiction and cartoons
- after sub lesson "Saying what you like": say which genres you like and which you do not

## What you need
Nothing special.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — the learner meets the words in a doing task before any explaining.
2. **ADD IMAGE** — a picture for the lesson's world, when the picture pass reaches this lesson.
3. **Sub lesson 1 — The genres.**
4. **Sub lesson 2 — Saying what you like.**
5. **ADD VIDEO** — the filmed run of this lesson, when the video pass reaches it.

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
Tie each genre to one film or story the learner knows; the feeling names the genre — funny, sad, scary, fast, space, drawn.
