# Hands-on — What Things Are Made Of

Hands teach these words best. Do these with real things, not only
pictures.

## Touch and tell

Collect five things: one wooden, one glass, one metal, one
stone, one plastic. Touch each one. Say its name and its
material in a full sentence: "This spoon is made of metal."
"This cup is made of glass."

## The street walk

Walk down your street with a partner. At every house, say what
the walls are made of, what the roof is made of, and what the
fence is made of. Ten houses, then swap — your partner says, you
check.
