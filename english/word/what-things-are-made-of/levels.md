# Levels — What Things Are Made Of

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson
(restructure 2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching
order. The old layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below
are the lesson's own parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Materials and their adjective forms | The building materials named in the book's unit — wood, brick (bricks), concrete, granite — with the wider everyday set (stone, glass, metal) worked the same way, and the material adjectives: wooden and the same-word adjectives (a brick house, a concrete bridge, a granite building). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Saying what a thing is made of — be made of | The frame "be made of + material": "a wooden house — made of wood"; saying what houses, bridges and everyday things are made of; asking "What is it made of?" and answering. |
