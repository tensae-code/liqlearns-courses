# Video script — What Things Are Made Of

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot knocks on a wooden door, then on a stone wall.]
"Wood! Stone! What is it made of?"

[A wooden house, a brick house, a concrete bridge and a granite
building appear one by one.] "A wooden house — made of wood! A
brick house — made of bricks! A concrete bridge — made of
concrete! A granite building — made of granite!"

[The mascot holds up a glass cup and a metal key.] "Glass!
Metal! What is YOUR house made of?"
