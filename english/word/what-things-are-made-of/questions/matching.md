## Matching

Match the thing to the material it is made of. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. a window pane — c
2. a wooden chair — a
3. a big city bridge — e
4. a monument — b
5. a house wall — d

a) wood
b) granite
c) glass
d) brick
e) concrete

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (panes are glass), 2–a (wooden = made of wood), 3–e (concrete for big bridges), 4–b (granite for monuments), 5–d (walls of bricks).
