## Fill in the blank

1. A ___ house is made of wood. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: wooden** — wood becomes "wooden" in front of a name.

2. The walls of the old house are made of ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: brick / bricks** — brick walls are made of bricks.

3. The big bridge is made of ___. It starts soft and wet and dries like stone. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: concrete** — concrete is the material that dries hard like stone.

4. "What is it made ___?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: of** — the frame is "be made of."

5. The monument is made of ___, a very hard, shiny stone. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: granite** — granite is the hard stone of big buildings and monuments.
