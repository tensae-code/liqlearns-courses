## True or false

1. A window pane is made of wood. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a window pane is made of glass.

2. "Wooden" is the adjective form of "wood." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — a wooden house is a house made of wood.

3. In the sentence "The house is made of wooden," the material word is correct. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — in the full sentence the material takes its plain name: "made of wood."

4. Concrete starts soft and wet and dries hard. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — that is how concrete works.
