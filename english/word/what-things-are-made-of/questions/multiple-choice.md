## Multiple choice

1. Which material comes from trees? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) granite  b) wood  c) concrete
   **Answer: b) wood** — wood is the material that comes from trees.

2. "A ___ house" — which word fills the gap for a house made of wood? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) wooden  b) woodly  c) woods
   **Answer: a) wooden** — wood becomes "wooden" in front of a thing's name.

3. Bricks are made by… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cutting trees  b) baking clay blocks  c) melting glass
   **Answer: b) baking clay blocks** — bricks are baked clay blocks, laid one on top of another.

4. Which sentence is right? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The bridge is made of concrete.  b) The bridge is made from concretes.  c) The bridge made of concrete is.
   **Answer: a)** — the frame is "be made of + material": "is made of concrete."

5. "What is the window pane made of?" (Sub lesson 2)
   a) It is made of wood.  b) It is made of glass.  c) It is made of brick.
   **Answer: b) glass** — a window pane is made of glass.

6. A wooden house — said in a full sentence: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The house is made of wooden.  b) The house is made of wood.  c) The house made wooden of.
   **Answer: b)** — in the full sentence the material takes its plain name: wood, not wooden.
