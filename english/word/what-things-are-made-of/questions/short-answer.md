## Short answer

1. What is a school desk usually made of? Answer with the full frame. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: It is made of wood (or metal).** — any true material in the "be made of" frame.

2. Name the material: it is baked in blocks and walls are built from it, one on top of another. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: brick (bricks).**

3. Your friend asks about your chair: "What is it made of?" Answer for a wooden chair. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: It is made of wood.**

4. Say "a house made of stone" the short way, with the material in front. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: a stone house.**
