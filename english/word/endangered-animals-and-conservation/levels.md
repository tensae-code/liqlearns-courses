# Levels — Endangered Animals and Conservation

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Words for wild life at risk | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the danger set: endangered species, habitat loss, poaching, deforestation, pollution — and the answer set: conservation, organisation, protected area, fact sheet. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Finding out and presenting | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Run the research project: choose an endangered animal, find out about it (collaborative jigsaw reading), complete a fact sheet, and make a poster or visual presentation of the case. |
