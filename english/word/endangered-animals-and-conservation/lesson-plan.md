# Lesson Plan — Endangered Animals and Conservation

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the words for wild life at risk — endangered species, habitat loss, poaching, deforestation, pollution, conservation, protected areas — and presenting a researched case.

## Sub lessons

1. **Words for wild life at risk.** *Can-do: Name the danger set: endangered species, habitat loss, poaching, deforestation, pollution — and the answer set: conservation, organisation, protected area, fact sheet.*
2. **Finding out and presenting.** *Can-do: Run the research project: choose an endangered animal, find out about it (collaborative jigsaw reading), complete a fact sheet, and make a poster or visual presentation of the case.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Endangered animals and the environment project (item 423): the words endangered species, organisation, habitat loss, conservation, poaching, protected area, deforestation, fact sheet, pollution; learners choose an endangered animal, find out about it (jigsaw reading), complete a fact sheet and present a poster.
