# Short Answer — Endangered Animals and Conservation

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the four danger words. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Habitat loss, poaching, deforestation, pollution.
2. Why is habitat loss dangerous for a species? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Without its home — its food, shelter and breeding places — the species cannot survive.
3. Name the project's four steps. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Choose an endangered animal; find out about it (jigsaw reading); complete a fact sheet; present a poster or visual presentation.
4. What four parts belong on the fact sheet? (Sub lesson 2)
   — The animal's name, its habitat, why it is endangered, and what is being done to help.
