# Verbs — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Doing words, what a verb is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The test, what did they do · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — He, she, it adds -s.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is a verb?
a) school  b) swim  c) happy  d) book
**Answer: b) swim** — it tells what someone does. School and
book name things; happy describes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word tells what a person does?
a) teacher  b) quickly  c) eat  d) red
**Answer: c) eat.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Yaa came," the verb is —
a) Yaa  b) came
**Answer: b) came** — What did Yaa do? She came. (Ghana's own
example sentence, Basic 2.)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The bird sang," what did the bird do?
a) bird  b) sang  c) the
**Answer: b) sang** — sang is the doing word.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** "I ride. He ______."
a) ride  b) rides  c) riding
**Answer: b) rides** — for he, she, or it, we add -s (Egypt's
rule, p.86).

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which one is right?
a) She take pictures.  b) She takes pictures.  c) She takess
pictures.
**Answer: b)** — the book's own model pair: "I take pictures.
My friend rides the camel" (Egypt, p.86).

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all verbs?
a) kick, hit, run  b) ball, goal, net  c) happy, fast, big
**Answer: a)** — Egypt's sports doing words. Ball, goal, and
net name things; happy, fast, big describe.
