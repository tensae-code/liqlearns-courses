# Verbs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a verb from the box.

**Box:** kicked · swims · ate · rides · sleeps · reads

**1. (Level 1)** Musa ______ the sandwich.
**Answer: ate.**

**2. (Level 1)** Hana ______ the ball into the goal.
**Answer: kicked** (Egypt's sports unit).

**3. (Level 2)** The cat ______ all day on the chair.
**Answer: sleeps.**

**4. (Level 2)** My brother ______ a story at night.
**Answer: reads.**

**5. (Level 3)** I ride a bicycle. My friend ______ a camel.
**Answer: rides** — he/she/it adds -s (Egypt's own model pair,
p.86).

**6. (Level 3)** I swim on Fridays. She ______ on Fridays too.
**Answer: swims.**
