# Verbs — matching

Match each word to verb or not a verb.
(Egypt's Final Review sorts nouns against verbs, pp.106–108;
Libya sorts mixed words into columns, Grade 7 WorkBook 7.7.)

**Set 1 (Level 1)**
1. kick — **verb** (Egypt's sports unit)
2. ball — **not a verb** (a thing — a noun)
3. swim — **verb**
4. happy — **not a verb** (a describing word)
5. jump — **verb**
6. school — **not a verb** (a place — a noun)

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — match each sentence to its verb.
1. "Ofori danced." — **danced**
2. "The bird sang." — **sang**
3. "Musa ate." — **ate** (Ghana's own examples)
4. "Yaa came." — **came**
5. "The bus stopped." — **stopped**
6. "Hana kicked the ball." — **kicked**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — match each start to its right ending.
1. I ride. → He — **rides**
2. I take pictures. → She — **takes pictures**
3. I play. → It — **plays**
4. I swim. → He — **swims**
