# Verbs — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What is a verb?
**Answer:** a doing word — a word that tells what a person or
a thing does (Bangladesh: "a word that expresses an action").

**2. (Level 1)** Name one verb for something you did today.
**Answer:** any true doing word — ate, ran, read, slept…

**3. (Level 2)** In "Ofori danced," ask the test question and
answer it.
**Answer:** What did Ofori do? — danced. Danced is the verb
(Ghana's test, Basic 2).

**4. (Level 2)** Build the smallest whole sentence with the
name Musa and the doing word ate.
**Answer:** "Musa ate." — (Name) + (Doing word), Ghana's own
example.

**5. (Level 3)** Change for she: "I swim. I play."
**Answer:** She swims. She plays.

**6. (Level 3)** Say Egypt's rule in your own words.
**Answer:** when the doer is he, she, or it, add -s to the
verb (Primary 2 Term 2, p.86).
