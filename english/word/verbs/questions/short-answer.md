# Verbs — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Doing words, what a verb is · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The test, what did they do · Sub lesson 3 — The smallest sentence, name plus doing word · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — He, she, it adds -s.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a verb?
**Answer:** a doing word — a word that tells what a person or
a thing does (Bangladesh: "a word that expresses an action").

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one verb for something you did today.
**Answer:** any true doing word — ate, ran, read, slept…

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Ofori danced," ask the test question and
answer it.
**Answer:** What did Ofori do? — danced. Danced is the verb
(Ghana's test, Basic 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Build the smallest whole sentence with the
name Musa and the doing word ate.
**Answer:** "Musa ate." — (Name) + (Doing word), Ghana's own
example.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Change for she: "I swim. I play."
**Answer:** She swims. She plays.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Say Egypt's rule in your own words.
**Answer:** when the doer is he, she, or it, add -s to the
verb (Primary 2 Term 2, p.86).
