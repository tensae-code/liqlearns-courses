# Verbs — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — a doing word — set by the pattern
across the countries' books: the doing words themselves first,
then finding the verb inside a sentence with the books' test,
then the one change the early books make to it.

## Level 1 — doing words
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given a mixed
list of words, pick out every doing word — and leave the naming
words and describing words out.

## Level 2 — the verb in a sentence
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: in a short
sentence, find the verb with the test — "What did … do?" — and
build a whole sentence in the books' smallest shape, (Name) +
(Doing word): "Yaa came."

## Level 3 — he, she, it adds -s
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: change a verb
for he, she, or it without a miss — "I ride → he rides," "I take
pictures → she takes pictures" — and say Egypt's rule in your
own words.
(The tenses — present, past, going to — are a different thing
and are tested at the Sentence stage, not here.)
