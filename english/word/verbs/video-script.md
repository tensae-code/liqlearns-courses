# Video script — Verbs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me.

I **run**. Run! What am I doing? Running.
Run is a **verb**. A verb tells what you do.

I **jump**. Jump is a verb.
I **eat**. Eat is a verb.
I **sleep**. Sleep is a verb.

Now a name and a verb: **Musa ate.**
Who? Musa. What did Musa do? He ate.
Two words. A whole sentence!

One trick. Watch:
**I** swim. **He** swims!
**I** ride. **She** rides!
He — she — it: the verb gets a little **s**.

Now you. Stand up. Run! Jump! Swim — like this!
Say the verb. Do the verb.
That is a verb.
