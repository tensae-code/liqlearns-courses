# Lesson Plan — Fractions and Percentages

**One thing:** the English words for parts of a whole — the fractions (half, quarter, three-fifths, five-eighths) and percentages (75%, said "seventy-five per cent").

## Sub lessons

1. Fractions — half, quarter, and the -fifths / -eighths words
2. Percentages — per cent, and saying 75%

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A fraction names a part of one whole thing: one whole cut into equal parts.
- **Half** — one whole cut into 2 equal parts; one part is a half (1/2). Two halves make the whole.
- **Quarter** — one whole cut into 4 equal parts; one part is a quarter (1/4). Four quarters make the whole.
- Bigger fractions are read in a pattern: the top number is a counting word, the bottom number is an ordering word with **-s** on the end when the top number is more than one: **three-fifths** (3/5), **five-eighths** (5/8).
- A **percentage** is a fraction counted out of 100. The sign **%** stands for **per cent** — "out of every hundred".
- **75%** is said "seventy-five per cent" — 75 out of every 100.
