# Video Script — Fractions and Percentages

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The words appear on screen as they are said.

[Picture: one whole cake on a table]

One cake. One whole cake.

[Picture: the cake cut into 2 equal parts; one part lifted]

Cut it in two! One part is… a half! A half.

[Picture: the cake cut into 4 equal parts; one part lifted]

Cut it in four! One part is… a quarter! A quarter.

[Picture: a bar cut into 5 equal parts, 3 parts coloured]

Five parts. Colour three. Three-fifths! Three-fifths.

[Picture: a bar cut into 8 equal parts, 5 parts coloured]

Eight parts. Colour five. Five-eighths! Five-eighths.

[Picture: the % sign big on screen, then a tank of water filled to the 75 mark]

And this sign? % — per cent! Out of every hundred. Seventy-five per cent. The tank is seventy-five per cent full!
