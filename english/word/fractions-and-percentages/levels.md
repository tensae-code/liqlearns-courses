# Levels — Fractions and Percentages

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Fractions — half, quarter, three-fifths, five-eighths | Name a half (1/2) and a quarter (1/4), and read 3/5 as "three-fifths" and 5/8 as "five-eighths" using the pattern: counting word on top, ordering word with -s underneath. |
| 2. Percentages — per cent, 75% | Say what % and "per cent" mean (out of every hundred) and read 75% aloud as "seventy-five per cent". |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can say the English names for the common fractions and read a percentage aloud.
