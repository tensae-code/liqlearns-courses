# Hands-On — Fractions and Percentages

Things you do with paper, a pencil and your hands. No special materials needed.

1. **Fold and name.** Fold a piece of paper into 2 equal parts. Hold up one part and say: "A half." Fold another piece into 4 equal parts. Hold up one and say: "A quarter."
2. **Colour and say.** Draw a bar, cut it into 5 equal parts with lines, colour 3 parts, and say: "Three-fifths are coloured." Draw another bar in 8 parts, colour 5, and say: "Five-eighths are coloured."
3. **Hundred steps.** Imagine a rope of 100 steps. Walk or point along 75 of them and say: "Seventy-five per cent of the rope."
4. **Ask at home.** Show someone at home the sign % and ask: "What does per cent mean?" Tell them the answer: out of every hundred. Then ask them to say 75% in words.
5. **Half or quarter?** A partner folds a paper and holds up one part without showing the folds. Guess and say: "A half!" or "A quarter!" Then unfold together and check.
