# Lesson Plan — Daily Routines

**One thing:** the phrases for the two ends of the day — waking up and going to bed — and the words that say when: early and at midnight.

## Sub lessons

1. Waking up — wake up, wake up early
2. Going to bed — go to bed early, go to bed at midnight

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- **Wake up** is what you do when sleep ends and your eyes open: "I wake up."
- **Wake up early** — early means before the usual time; waking up early gives the day a head start.
- **Go to bed** is what you do when the day ends and you go to sleep: "I go to bed."
- **Go to bed early** — going to bed early helps you wake up early.
- **Go to bed at midnight** — midnight is the middle of the night, 12 o'clock at night; going to bed at midnight is very late.
- The two ends join up: when you go to bed decides when you can wake up.
