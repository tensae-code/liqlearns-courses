# Levels — Daily Routines

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Waking up — wake up, wake up early | Say "I wake up" and "I wake up early", and say what early means. |
| 2. Going to bed — go to bed early, go to bed at midnight | Say "I go to bed early" and "I go to bed at midnight", say what midnight is, and join the two ends of the day: going to bed early helps you wake up early. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can say the phrases for both ends of their day and when they do them.
