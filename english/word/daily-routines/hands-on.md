# Hands-On — Daily Routines

Things you do with your body and your day. No special materials needed.

1. **Mime the ends.** Act out the day in order: pretend to do your day's work, then mime going to bed, sleeping, and waking up with a big stretch. Say each phrase as you do it: "I go to bed." "I wake up."
2. **Early or late?** A partner says a time word — **early** or **at midnight**. You mime the right one: yawning at midnight, or springing up bright and early.
3. **My two sentences.** Say your own day out loud in two sentences: "I wake up early." "I go to bed early." Change the ending words until both sentences are true for you.
4. **Ask at home.** Ask two people at home: "Do you wake up early?" and "Do you go to bed at midnight?" Report their answers: "He wakes up early." "She does not go to bed at midnight."
5. **The early-bed test.** For three nights, go to bed early. Each morning, say how waking up felt: "Today I woke up early. It was easy / it was hard."
