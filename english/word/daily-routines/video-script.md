# Video Script — Daily Routines

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The phrases appear on screen as they are said.

[Picture: a child asleep in bed, moon at the window]

Sleep… sleep… The day is finished. I go to bed.

[Picture: the clock showing very late; the child still awake]

Oh no — it is midnight! Twelve o'clock at night. I go to bed at midnight. Very late!

[Picture: the child in bed early, smiling, book closed]

Tonight is different. I go to bed early. Early! Before the usual time.

[Picture: morning sun; the child opening their eyes and stretching]

The sun is up. My eyes open. I wake up! I wake up early!

[Picture: the child dressed and ready, smiling]

Go to bed early — wake up early. The two ends of the day hold hands!
