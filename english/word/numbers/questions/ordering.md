# Numbers — ordering

Put each set in counting order. (This question type exists
for this lesson because counting IS order.)

**1. (Level 1)** 5 · 2 · 8 · 1 · 10
**Answer:** 1, 2, 5, 8, 10.

**2. (Level 1)** nine · three · seven · one · five
**Answer:** one, three, five, seven, nine.

**3. (Level 2)** 14 · 11 · 20 · 17 · 12
**Answer:** 11, 12, 14, 17, 20.

**4. (Level 2)** nineteen · thirteen · eleven · sixteen ·
twelve
**Answer:** eleven, twelve, thirteen, sixteen, nineteen.

**5. (Level 3)** 60 · 100 · 30 · 90 · 10 · 50
**Answer:** 10, 30, 50, 60, 90, 100 — the tens line.

**6. (Level 3)** Count DOWN, the check the books' tracing
builds toward: 20 → 1 in words, said aloud. First three and
last three only.
**Answer:** twenty, nineteen, eighteen … three, two, one.
