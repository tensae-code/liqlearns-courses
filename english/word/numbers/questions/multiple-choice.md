# Numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "How many pencils are there?" (picture: 3
pencils) — "There are ______ pencils."
a) 2  b) 3  c) 5
**Answer: b) 3** — Egypt's own exercise shape (KG2, p.55).

**2. (Level 1)** The word for 7 is ______.
a) six  b) seven  c) eleven
**Answer: b) seven.**

**3. (Level 2)** Which number refuses the -teen pattern?
a) thirteen  b) fifteen  c) twelve
**Answer: c) twelve** — eleven and twelve stand as themselves
in the 11–20 line (Egypt, Primary 1 Term 2, Unit 2).

**4. (Level 2)** "There are ______ stars." (picture: 15
stars)
a) fifty  b) fifteen  c) five
**Answer: b) fifteen** — the counting sentence Egypt files
for 11–20.

**5. (Level 3)** Counting by tens, after 40 comes ______.
a) 41  b) fifty  c) fourteen
**Answer: b) fifty** (Egypt, Primary 3: count by 10s to 100).

**6. (Level 3)** A voice says the jump landing "thirty." The
number is —
a) 13  b) 30  c) 3
**Answer: b) 30** — -ty is a tens landing; -teen is the
one-by-one line.

**7. (Level 3)** 100 in words is ______.
a) one hundred  b) ten tens  c) hundred one
**Answer: a) one hundred.**
