# Numbers — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Numbers 1 to 10 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Numbers 11 to 20 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Numbers by Tens to 100. Sub lessons 4 — Numbers Past 100: Hundreds, Thousands, a Million — and 5 — Ordinal Numbers: First to One Hundredth — were added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule; they have no old depth group.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "How many pencils are there?" (picture: 3
pencils) — "There are ______ pencils."
a) 2  b) 3  c) 5
**Answer: b) 3** — Egypt's own exercise shape (KG2, p.55).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The word for 7 is ______.
a) six  b) seven  c) eleven
**Answer: b) seven.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which number refuses the -teen pattern?
a) thirteen  b) fifteen  c) twelve
**Answer: c) twelve** — eleven and twelve stand as themselves
in the 11–20 line (Egypt, Primary 1 Term 2, Unit 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "There are ______ stars." (picture: 15
stars)
a) fifty  b) fifteen  c) five
**Answer: b) fifteen** — the counting sentence Egypt files
for 11–20.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Counting by tens, after 40 comes ______.
a) 41  b) fifty  c) fourteen
**Answer: b) fifty** (Egypt, Primary 3: count by 10s to 100).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A voice says the jump landing "thirty." The
number is —
a) 13  b) 30  c) 3
**Answer: b) 30** — -ty is a tens landing; -teen is the
one-by-one line.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 100 in words is ______.
a) one hundred  b) ten tens  c) hundred one
**Answer: a) one hundred.**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** 514 in words is ______.
a) five hundred fourteen  b) five hundred and fourteen
c) five fourteen hundred
**Answer: b) five hundred and fourteen** — Nepal's Grade 3
book writes three-figure numbers with "and" (Unit 3).

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** "Thirteen thousand, six hundred and
seventeen" is ______.
a) 13,617  b) 13,067  c) 130,617
**Answer: a) 13,617** — Greece's Grade 7 workbook matches
big numbers whole (Appendix Task 46, printed p.138).

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** In Jordan's big-number set, after a
hundred thousand comes ______.
a) a thousand  b) a million  c) ten thousand
**Answer: b) a million** (Grade 5, Unit 3's own set).

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** The ordinal for 5 is ______.
a) fiveth  b) fifth  c) fiveteen
**Answer: b) fifth** — fifth changes its shape; it is not
counting word + th.

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** 100th in words is ______.
a) hundred  b) one hundred  c) one hundredth
**Answer: c) one hundredth** — the end of Afghanistan's
whole list, 1st to 100th (Grade 7, Unit 15, Lesson 3).
