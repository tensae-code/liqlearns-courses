# Numbers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** three · seven · twelve · fifteen · forty · hundred

**1. (Level 1)** 3 = ______
**Answer: three.**

**2. (Level 1)** 7 = ______
**Answer: seven.**

**3. (Level 2)** 12 = ______
**Answer: twelve.**

**4. (Level 2)** "There are ______ stars." (15 in the
picture)
**Answer: fifteen.**

**5. (Level 3)** Counting by tens: 10, 20, 30, ______…
**Answer: forty.**

**6. (Level 3)** 100 = one ______.
**Answer: hundred.**
