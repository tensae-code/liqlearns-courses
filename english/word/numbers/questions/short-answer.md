# Numbers — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Numbers 1 to 10 · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Numbers 11 to 20 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Numbers by Tens to 100.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write 1–10 as words.
**Answer:** one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight,
nine, ten.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's book counts: 4 leaves, 1 sun, 5
apples. How many things in all? (Count them — the adding
itself is Maths-title work; the count is the answer here.)
**Answer:** ten things counted (4 + 1 + 5), KG2 p.55's own
objects.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write 11–20 as words.
**Answer:** eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen,
sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Answer whole: How many chairs are there in
your room right now?
**Answer:** "There are ___ chairs" — the learner's real count
(Egypt's shape).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the tens line to 100 as words.
**Answer:** ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty,
seventy, eighty, ninety, one hundred.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A date says the 21st page of the count:
write 21, 22, 23 in words.
**Answer:** twenty-one, twenty-two, twenty-three — the tens
joined to the ones you own.
