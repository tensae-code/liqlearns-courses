# Lesson plan — Culture and Living-Abroad Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Culture and Living-Abroad Words
**Lesson:** culture-and-living-abroad-words — the words of
living in a new place: accent, fluent, accustomed, expatriate —
and the people words: fellow countrymen, stubborn
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World
**The one thing:** the word set of culture and living abroad,
learned whole, the way the book's unit presents it.
**This lesson's own level:** Word lesson: the new-place words
first, then the people words.

## Why this lesson exists

No lesson housed this word set. Palestine's Grade 12 book's
"Different places, different ways" unit teaches culture and
living-abroad words — accent, accustomed, expatriate, fluent,
fellow countrymen, stubborn (Unit 11, printed pp.112–121).
The 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep read the book for the
first time and marked this part NEW LESSON NEEDED. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), a teaching item with no
lesson gets a new lesson built; this is that lesson. Its only
source is the Palestine book named in lesson/sources.md.

## Sub lessons

1. Living in a new place — accent, fluent, accustomed, expatriate
2. People words — fellow countrymen, stubborn

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is forced first — this lesson stands alone: it starts
from "a person moves to a new country" and teaches every word
it uses.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Talk about a new place: a person's accent, being fluent,
  getting accustomed, being an expatriate.
- Use the people words: fellow countrymen, stubborn.

## What you need
A story of someone who moved — a family member, a neighbour,
or yourself.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — describe a person who moved to a new
   country before anyone teaches the words (lesson/lesson.md).
2. ADD IMAGE: a traveller at an airport, two flags, a person
   practising new words.
3. Work through sub lesson 1: the new-place words.
4. Work through sub lesson 2: the people words.
5. ADD VIDEO (short): a newcomer learning the new place — the
   words grow big as they land: ACCENT… FLUENT… ACCUSTOMED…

## Add-ons
- Interview someone who has lived abroad: ask about their
  accent, when they became fluent, what they got accustomed to.
- Write three lines: "If I lived abroad, I would miss…"

## If the learner is stuck
Cut back to two words — fluent and accustomed — used about
learning English itself until they are sure. Add the rest one
at a time.

## Teacher note
The book teaches this set inside one unit about different
places and different ways of living, and this lesson keeps
that shape: every word is met inside the move to a new place.
Only the words the book's placements name are taught here.
