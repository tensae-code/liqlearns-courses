# Video script — Culture and Living-Abroad Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot lands with a small bag in a new town.] "New place!
My ACCENT is from home."

[The mascot talks easily with a shopkeeper.] "Now I am
FLUENT!"

[The mascot eats a strange new food and smiles.] "I am
ACCUSTOMED to it now!"
