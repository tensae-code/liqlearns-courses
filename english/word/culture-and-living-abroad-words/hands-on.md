# Hands-on — Culture and Living-Abroad Words

These words are learned by telling a real move. Do these out
loud, with a partner.

## The newcomer

One of you is new in town. The other asks: "Where are you
from?" "Are you fluent in our language?" "What are you
accustomed to now?" Answer in whole sentences.

## From home

Tell your partner about people from your country living
abroad — your fellow countrymen. Where do they live? What did
they get accustomed to?
