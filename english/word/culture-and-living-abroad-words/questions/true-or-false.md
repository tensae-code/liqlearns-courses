## True or false

1. Your accent shows where you are from. (Sub lesson 1)
   **True.**
2. A fluent speaker stops at every word. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — a fluent speaker speaks well and easily.
3. An expatriate lives in their own country. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — an expatriate lives in a country that is not their own.
4. If you are accustomed to a thing, it feels strange and new. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — it has become normal for you.
5. A stubborn person changes their mind easily. (Sub lesson 2)
   **False** — a stubborn person does not change their mind or way.
