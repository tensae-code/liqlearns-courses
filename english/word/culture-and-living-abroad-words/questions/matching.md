## Matching

Match the word to what it means. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. accent — d
2. fluent — a
3. accustomed — f
4. expatriate — b
5. fellow countrymen — e
6. stubborn — c

a) speaking a language well and easily
b) a person living outside their own country
c) not changing your mind or your way
d) the way of speaking that shows where you are from
e) people from your own country
f) used to a thing; it became normal

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–d (the accent carries home in it), 2–a (fluent = well and easily), 3–f (accustomed = it became normal), 4–b (an expatriate lives abroad), 5–e (fellow countrymen are from your country), 6–c (stubborn = will not be moved).
