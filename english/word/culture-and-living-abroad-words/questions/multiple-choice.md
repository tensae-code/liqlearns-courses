## Multiple choice

1. Your accent is ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) your way of speaking that shows where you are from  b) your passport  c) your bag
   **Answer: a) your way of speaking that shows where you are from.**

2. A fluent speaker ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) speaks a language well and easily  b) speaks very loudly  c) speaks only at home
   **Answer: a) speaks a language well and easily.**

3. "He got accustomed to the cold" means ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) he loves winter sports  b) the cold was strange, then it became normal for him  c) he bought a coat
   **Answer: b) the cold was strange, then it became normal for him.**

4. An expatriate is a person who ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) travels for one day  b) teaches languages  c) lives in a country that is not their own
   **Answer: c) lives in a country that is not their own.**

5. Your fellow countrymen are ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) people from your own country  b) your teachers  c) people you meet on a bus
   **Answer: a) people from your own country.**

6. A stubborn person ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) changes their mind every hour  b) does not change their mind or their way  c) never speaks
   **Answer: b) does not change their mind or their way.**
