## Short answer

1. What does fluent mean? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Speaking a language well and easily, without stopping to hunt for words.
2. Write one sentence with accustomed about something that became normal for you. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** any true sentence, e.g. "I am accustomed to the noise now."
3. Who are your fellow countrymen when you are abroad? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The people from your own country.
4. Write one sentence about a stubborn person, without a name. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** any sentence showing someone not changing their mind or way, e.g. "She is stubborn — she will not change her plan."
