# Hands-on — Shapes

Hands teach shapes best. Do these with real things, not only pictures.

## Trace and draw

Trace each shape in the air with your finger, then draw it on paper. Say the name while you draw — the hand learns the shape and the mouth learns the word at the same time.

## Shape hunt at home

Find a circle (a plate), a rectangle (a book), a square (a tile) and a triangle (a folded napkin). Hold each one up and say its shape name.
