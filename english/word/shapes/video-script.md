# Video script — Shapes

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The eight shapes in a row, no words. The mascot points at the square.] “Shapes! This one is a square. Four sides, all the same.”

[Triangle, rectangle, rhombus appear one by one.] “Triangle — three sides. Rectangle — two long, two short. Rhombus — a tipped-over square!”

[Night sky: circle sun, heart, star, crescent moon.] “Circle — round. Heart — for love. Star — five points. Crescent — the thin moon.”

[A table of mixed shapes; the mascot counts circles.] “How many circles are there? One… two… three! There are three circles.”
