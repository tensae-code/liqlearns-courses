## Fill in the blank

1. A ___ has three sides. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: triangle** — A triangle has three sides.

2. A ___ has four sides, all the same. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: square** — All four sides are the same.

3. A ___ has two long sides and two short sides. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: rectangle** — Long, short, long, short.

4. A ___ is round. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: circle** — Round like a ball's face.

5. We draw a ___ for love. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: heart** — The heart means love.

6. The thin moon is a ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: crescent** — The crescent moon.

7. “How many ___ are there?” — “There are six.” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: triangles** (any shape name is right) — We ask “how many” about a shape, then count.
