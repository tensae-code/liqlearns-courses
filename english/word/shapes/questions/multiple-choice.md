## Multiple choice

1. Which shape has three sides? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) square  b) triangle  c) circle
   **Answer: b) triangle** — a triangle has three sides.

2. Which shape has four sides, all the same? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) square  b) crescent  c) heart
   **Answer: a) square** — a square's four sides are all the same.

3. A rectangle has… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) three sides  b) two long sides and two short sides  c) no sides
   **Answer: b) two long sides and two short sides** — that is what makes it a rectangle, not a square.

4. A rhombus looks like… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a ball  b) a square tipped over  c) the moon
   **Answer: b) a square tipped over** — like a diamond standing on its corner.

5. Which shape is round like the sun? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) circle  b) star  c) triangle
   **Answer: a) circle** — a circle is round.

6. Which shape do we draw for love? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) crescent  b) heart  c) rectangle
   **Answer: b) heart** — the heart is the shape of love.

7. A star has… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) five points  b) four sides  c) three sides
   **Answer: a) five points** — count them: one, two, three, four, five.

8. The thin moon is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) square  b) crescent  c) rhombus
   **Answer: b) crescent** — a crescent is the thin, curved moon.

9. “How many circles are there?” — you count one, two, three, four. The answer is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) There are three circles.  b) There are four circles.  c) There is one circle.
   **Answer: b) There are four circles** — you counted four.

10. To count shapes well, you should… (Sub lesson 3)
    a) guess fast  b) touch each shape once and count slowly  c) count the same shape twice
    **Answer: b) touch each shape once and count slowly** — that is the whole trick.
