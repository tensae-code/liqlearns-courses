## Short answer

1. How many sides does a triangle have? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Three (3).** — One, two, three sides.

2. How many sides does a square have? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Four (4), all the same.** — That is what makes it a square.

3. Which shape is a square tipped over like a diamond? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: The rhombus.** — It stands on its corner.

4. Name the round shape. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: The circle.** — Round like the sun.

5. Which shape has five points and shines at night? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: The star.** — Five points.

6. What do we call the thin moon shape? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: A crescent.** — A curved sliver of light.

7. Look: ○ ○. How many circles are there? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: There are two circles.** — Count: one, two.

8. Look: ☆ ☆ ☆ ☆ ☆. How many stars are there? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: There are five stars.** — Count each star once.
