## True or false

1. A triangle has four sides. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a triangle has three sides.

2. A square has four sides, all the same. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — that is a square.

3. A rectangle and a square are exactly the same shape. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a rectangle has two long sides and two short sides; a square's sides are all the same.

4. A circle has straight sides. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a circle is round; it has no straight sides.

5. A star has five points. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — count the points: five.

6. A crescent is a square. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a crescent is the thin moon.

7. When we count shapes, we touch each shape once. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — touch once, count slowly, miss nothing.
