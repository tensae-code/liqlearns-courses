## Matching

Match the shape name to what it looks like. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. square — c
2. triangle — a
3. rectangle — d
4. rhombus — b
5. circle — e
6. heart — f
7. star — g
8. crescent — h

a) three sides
b) a square tipped over like a diamond
c) four sides, all the same
d) two long sides and two short sides
e) round like the sun
f) the shape we draw for love
g) five points, shining at night
h) the thin moon

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (four equal sides), 2–a (three sides), 3–d (long and short sides), 4–b (tipped-over square), 5–e (round), 6–f (love), 7–g (five points), 8–h (thin moon).
