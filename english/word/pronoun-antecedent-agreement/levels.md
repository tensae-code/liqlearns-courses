# Levels — Pronoun–Antecedent Agreement

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The first rules: what stands between, and compounds | Apply the agreement rules for intervening phrases, indefinite-pronoun antecedents, and-compounds and or/nor proximity. |
| 2. The special cases: collectives, titles and number traps | Apply the rules for collective nouns, titles, plural-form/singular-meaning words, every and many a, and 'the number of' against 'a number of'. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: making a pronoun agree with its antecedent — the noun it stands for — under the book's nine rules: intervening phrases, indefinite antecedents, compounds, proximity, collectives, titles, and the special number cases.
