# Video Script — Pronoun–Antecedent Agreement

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a pronoun arrow flying past a crowd of words to land on one noun]

A pronoun must MATCH its noun! The words in between? They cannot stop it!

[Picture: two children holding hands under one umbrella labelled THEIR]

Two joined by AND? Plural! Their!

[Picture: two doors labelled OR — the pronoun walks to the nearer door]

With OR… the NEARER one rules!

[Picture: one book with a grand title, holding a small sign: IT]

And a title is ONE book. Always singular!
