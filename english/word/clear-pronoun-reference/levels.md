# Levels — Clear Pronoun Reference

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Spotting vague and ambiguous reference | Spot the sentences where a pronoun's antecedent is vague (nowhere stated) or ambiguous (two or more nouns could claim it). |
| 2. Fixing faulty reference | Fix a faulty pronoun by repeating the noun, restructuring the sentence, or quoting the words directly. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: making every pronoun point at exactly one antecedent — spotting vague or ambiguous reference in sentences, and fixing it so a reader never has to guess who or what a pronoun means.
