# Hands-On — Clear Pronoun Reference

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Fault hunt.** From the book's kind of practice: write six sentences — three ambiguous, three vague — without marking them. A partner spots and labels each fault.
2. **Fix race.** Take the same six faulty sentences. Each partner fixes them independently, then compare: which fix reads clearest at first sight?
3. **Own-writing audit.** Read one page of your own writing aloud to a partner. Every time they raise a hand — their 'who?' pause — mark the sentence and fix it on the spot.
