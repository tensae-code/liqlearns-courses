# Video Script — Spelling Patterns

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: the word BUTTERFLY clapped into three chunks: BUT-TER-FLY]

Every word has beats! Clap the syllables, then spell chunk by chunk.

[Picture: RUN growing a second N as -ING arrives: RUNNING]

Running! The ending comes, the consonant doubles. Patterns have rules — learn the rule, spell the word!

[Picture: HAT growing an E and stretching its A: HATE]

Silent e does a big job quietly. Hat — hate! The e says nothing and changes everything.

[Picture: a long word cracking into pieces like a chocolate bar]

Long words break! Spell piece by piece — no word is too long.
