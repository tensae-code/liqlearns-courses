# Levels — Spelling Patterns

One lesson, 3 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Spelling foundations | Use a study technique for learning spelling words, divide words into syllables, spell high-frequency sight words, and spell by sound — initial, middle and final consonants, short and long vowels and their letter combinations, blends and digraphs. |
| 2. Word parts and spelling patterns | Spell with root words, prefixes and suffixes; add inflectional endings with and without spelling changes; and handle double consonants, r-controlled vowels, diphthongs, silent consonants, silent e, compound words, contractions and homophones — and alphabetise a list of words. |
| 3. Spelling longer words | Break regular and irregular complex polysyllabic words into manageable units, use word families, roots and origins and visual patterns to spell them, and proofread spelling with a dictionary. |

A learner who finishes all 3 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: the pattern system inside English spelling — how sounds map to letters, how roots, prefixes, suffixes and endings spell, and how long words break into spellable pieces — the system a speller checks a word against.
