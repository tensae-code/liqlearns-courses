# Lesson Plan — Spelling Patterns

**One thing:** the pattern system inside English spelling — how sounds map to letters, how roots, prefixes, suffixes and endings spell, and how long words break into spellable pieces — the system a speller checks a word against.

## Sub lessons

1. Spelling foundations
2. Word parts and spelling patterns
3. Spelling longer words

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Spelling foundations: a study technique; syllables; sight words; sound–letter spelling — consonants, short/long vowels, blends, digraphs (Primary Language Arts Scope and Sequence, objectives 3.31–3.38, printed pp.35–36).
- Word parts and patterns: roots, prefixes, suffixes; alphabetising; inflectional endings; double consonants; r-controlled vowels; diphthongs; digraphs; silent consonants; silent e; compounds; contractions; homophones (objectives 3.39–3.50, printed pp.35–36).
- Secondary spelling: sound-symbol relationships and phonological patterns; synonyms/antonyms/hyponyms/homonym-family spellings; affix meanings and spellings; word families, roots and origins; inflectional endings; polysyllabic words broken into units; visual patterns; silent letters; proofreading with dictionaries (Secondary Guidelines, objectives 7.75–7.92, printed pp.78–79).
