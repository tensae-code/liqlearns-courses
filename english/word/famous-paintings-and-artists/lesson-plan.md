# Lesson Plan — Famous Paintings and Artists

**One thing:** the names of the world's most famous painters and their most famous paintings — and the two Liechtenstein artists who stand in the same set.

## Sub lessons

1. The painters and their paintings — the unit's 24-work card set: Georg Malin: Z Dice; Martin Frommelt: untitled; Leonardo da Vinci: Mona Lisa; Vincent van Gogh: three works; Pablo Picasso: Les Demoiselles d'Avignon; Roy Lichtenstein: Masterpiece; Friedensreich Hundertwasser: Sunflower and Circles; Jackson Pollock: Convergence; Wassily Kandinsky: White Point; Gustav Klimt: The Tree of Life; Sandro Botticelli: The Birth of Venus; Andy Warhol: Marilyn; Paul Cézanne: Harlequin; Franz Marc: The Little Blue Horse; James McNeill Whistler; Boris Kustodiev: At Home; Pahari School (Chamba): The Beginning of Spring
2. Talking about a famous painting — name the painting and the artist, say what you can see in it, name its colours, and say what you think of it, by the unit's model description

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- **Two artists of the set are Liechtenstein's own:** **Georg Malin** (his work in the set is **Z Dice**) and **Martin Frommelt** (his work is **untitled** — it has no name). A small country stands in the same card set as the world's most famous names.
- **The world names:** **Leonardo da Vinci** painted the **Mona Lisa**, the most famous painting in the world. **Vincent van Gogh** has three works in the set. **Pablo Picasso** painted **Les Demoiselles d'Avignon**. **Roy Lichtenstein** — note the spelling: the American artist's name is spelled almost like the country Liechtenstein, but it is a different name — painted **Masterpiece**. **Friedensreich Hundertwasser** painted **Sunflower and Circles**. **Jackson Pollock** painted **Convergence**. **Wassily Kandinsky** painted **White Point** (the work is also known as Composition 8). **Gustav Klimt** painted **The Tree of Life**. **Sandro Botticelli** painted **The Birth of Venus**. **Andy Warhol** painted **Marilyn**. **Paul Cézanne** painted **Harlequin**. **Franz Marc** painted **The Little Blue Horse**. **James McNeill Whistler** stands in the set with his painting. **Boris Kustodiev** painted **At Home**. And the oldest work in the set is not by one man but by a school of painters: the **Pahari School** of Chamba painted **The Beginning of Spring**.
- **Talking about a painting:** first name the painting and its artist. Then say what you can see in it. Then name the colours — bright, light, dark, dull. Then give your opinion: "I like the painting because it is colourful."
