# Video Script — Famous Paintings and Artists

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each painting appears on screen as it is named.

[Picture: a gallery wall of famous paintings]

Look! Paintings! Famous paintings — from all over the world.

[Picture: the Mona Lisa]

This is the Mona Lisa. Leonardo da Vinci painted her. She is smiling — a little smile.

[Picture: sunflowers in bright yellow and orange]

Sunflowers! Bright, bright yellow! Vincent van Gogh loved sunflowers.

[Picture: a small dice sculpture-painting, then a green Alpine valley]

And look — this one is from a small country. Liechtenstein! Georg Malin made the Z Dice. Martin Frommelt painted here too. Small country — big art!

[Picture: a child pointing at a painting and talking]

Now you try. Say the name. Say the artist. Say what you see. Do you like it? I like it — it is colourful!
