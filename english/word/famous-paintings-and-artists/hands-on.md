# Hands-On — Famous Paintings and Artists

Things you do with pictures, cards and your voice. No special materials needed.

1. **Artist–painting pairs.** Write the artists' names on cards and the paintings' names on other cards. Shuffle, and match each painting to its painter. Say the pair out loud: "The Mona Lisa — Leonardo da Vinci."
2. **My gallery.** Draw or print five paintings from the lesson and hang them in a row. Walk a friend along your gallery and introduce each one: name, artist, one thing you can see.
3. **Copy a master.** Choose one painting from the set and copy it in your own colours. Under your copy, write the real artist's name and your name.
4. **Two from home.** Find out about Georg Malin and Martin Frommelt — the two Liechtenstein artists. Say one sentence about each.
5. **My opinion.** Pick the painting you like best and say three sentences: what it is called, who painted it, and why you like it.
