# Levels — Famous Paintings and Artists

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The painters and their paintings | Name the artists of the set and the painting that goes with each — including Liechtenstein's own Georg Malin and Martin Frommelt. |
| 2. Talking about a famous painting | Talk about any painting in the set in order: its name, its artist, what you can see, its colours, and your opinion of it. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can walk through a gallery of the world's most famous paintings and say who painted each one and what they think of it.
