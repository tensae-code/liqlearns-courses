## Multiple choice

1. Jocks are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the sporty ones  b) the teachers  c) the cooks
   **Answer: a).**

2. Geeks and nerds are names for learners who are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) deep in study and their interests  b) always late  c) new at school
   **Answer: a).**

3. A clique is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a school club with open doors  b) a tight circle of friends that keeps others out  c) a class
   **Answer: b).**

4. Your peers are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) your teachers  b) the people of your own age around you  c) your parents
   **Answer: b).**
