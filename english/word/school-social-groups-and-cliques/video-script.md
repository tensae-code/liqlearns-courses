# Video script — School Social Groups and Cliques

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A schoolyard seen from above; circles of friends form.]

“Jocks. Geeks. Nerds. Every school has its map.”

[One tight circle closes.] “A clique keeps others out.”

[The mascot taps the map.] “Learn the names — and what the labels hide.”
