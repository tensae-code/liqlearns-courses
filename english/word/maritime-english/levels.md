# Levels — Maritime English

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** name the main parts of a ship and
  the seafarers' jobs, and say what the ship's particulars
  (length, draught, tonnage) tell.
- **Climb:** add the safety set — equipment, emergency steps,
  the three signals Mayday, Pan Pan and Securité — and the
  radio basics: the spelling alphabet and numbers said digit
  by digit.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** use the standard
  phrases whole — message markers, wheel orders, positions
  and bearings — and read the working language of cargo,
  ports, dangerous goods and inspections.
