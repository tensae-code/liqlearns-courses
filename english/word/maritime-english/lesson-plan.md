# Lesson Plan — Maritime English

Unit 7 — Words for the World. Planned shape: four sub lessons —
first the ship itself and the people who sail her, then safety
and emergencies, then the radio talk of the sea with its
standard phrases, and last the working world of cargo and
ports. The lesson is built from Greece's maritime English
set: the two Maritime English volumes written for the
Merchant Navy Academies and the IMO Standard Marine
Communication Phrases, the worldwide standard wording every
seafarer is trained to use. It is specialist stock, like the
working-words lesson beside it in this unit, and it stands
alone for a learner who opens it directly: each sub lesson
teaches its own part whole.

## Sub lessons

1. **The ship and the seafarer** — the parts of a ship, the
   people who sail her and their jobs, and a ship's
   particulars (size, draught, tonnage).
2. **Safety and emergencies** — safety equipment, what to do
   in an emergency, the distress signals, and asking for
   medical help at sea.
3. **Radio talk: the standard phrases** — the spelling
   alphabet, numbers said the sea way, message markers
   (Mayday, Pan Pan, Securité), wheel orders, positions and
   bearings.
4. **Work, cargo and ports** — the work of the ship: cargo
   handling, preparing for sea, arriving in port, navigation
   aids, dangerous goods, pollution rules and port
   inspections.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
