# True or False — Maritime English

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. Port means the ship's left side when facing the bow. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

2. Mayday may be used for any small problem on board, to get attention quickly. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — Mayday is only for grave and immediate danger to the ship or to life.

3. A Securité message warns about safety — for example, weather or a danger to navigation. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

4. On the radio, "repeat" is the standard way to ask someone to say a message again. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — the standard phrase is "Say again"; "repeat" belongs to gunfire.

5. A ship's course is measured in degrees. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True**

6. A ship may discharge oil into the sea if she is far from land. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — marine pollution is forbidden; spills must be prevented and reported.
