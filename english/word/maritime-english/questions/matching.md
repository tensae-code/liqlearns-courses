# Matching — Maritime English

Match each item to its meaning.

1. Match the ship's part to where it is. (Sub lesson 1)
   - 1. "bow" → the front of the ship
   - 2. "stern" → the back of the ship
   - 3. "bridge" → where the ship is steered from
   - 4. "hull" → the body of the ship

2. Match the signal to when it is used. (Sub lesson 2)
   - 1. "Mayday" → grave and immediate danger
   - 2. "Pan Pan" → an urgent message about a serious problem
   - 3. "Securité" → a safety warning, such as weather
   - 4. "medical assistance" → asking a doctor ashore for advice about a patient

3. Match the radio word to what it does. (Sub lesson 3)
   - 1. "Say again" → asks the other side to repeat
   - 2. "I say again" → repeats something important
   - 3. "Over" → I have finished speaking; answer me
   - 4. "Out" → the conversation is finished

4. Match the port word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 4)
   - 1. "berth" → the place where the ship ties up
   - 2. "tug" → a small strong boat that helps the ship move in harbour
   - 3. "pilot" → the local expert who guides the ship in and out
   - 4. "cargo" → the goods the ship carries
