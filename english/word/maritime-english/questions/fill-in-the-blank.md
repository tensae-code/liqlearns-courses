# Fill in the Blank — Maritime English

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. The front of the ship is the ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: bow**

2. The officer on duty on the bridge is the officer of the ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: watch**

3. A ____ keeps a person floating in the water. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: lifejacket**

4. The urgency signal — serious trouble, but no immediate danger to life — is ____ ____. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Pan Pan**

5. In the spelling alphabet, the word for the letter N is ____. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: November**

6. When you want the other station to repeat a message, you say "____ again." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Say**

7. Cargo coming off the ship in port is being ____. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: discharged**

8. Hiring a ship, or space in one, to carry cargo is called ____. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: chartering**
