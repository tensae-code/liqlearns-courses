# Short Answer — Maritime English

Answer in a few words.

1. Name the ship's two sides and say which is which when facing the bow. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Port (left) and starboard (right).

2. A crew member falls seriously ill at sea and the ship needs a doctor's advice by radio. What assistance does the ship request? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Medical assistance.

3. Spell the name KIM the sea way. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** Kilo, India, Mike.

4. What two things does a position report give when it is made from a known mark? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** The bearing from the mark and the distance from it.

5. Name two things Port State Control inspectors check. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** Any two of: the ship's certificates, her safety equipment, her crew.
