# Multiple Choice — Maritime English

Choose the one right answer.

1. Which side of the ship is starboard? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) The front
   - B) The right side, facing the bow
   - C) The left side, facing the bow
   - D) The back
   **Answer: B**

2. What does a ship's draught tell? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) How fast she can sail
   - B) How many crew she carries
   - C) How deep she sits in the water
   - D) How old she is
   **Answer: C**

3. Your ship is in grave and immediate danger. Which call do you make? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Securité
   - B) Pan Pan
   - C) Mayday
   - D) Over and out
   **Answer: C**

4. What is a lifeboat for? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Carrying cargo to shore
   - B) Carrying people away from a ship that must be abandoned
   - C) Fishing
   - D) Guiding the ship into port
   **Answer: B**

5. How is the number 125 said on a ship's radio? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) One hundred twenty-five
   - B) One twenty-five
   - C) One two five
   - D) Twelve five
   **Answer: C**

6. The helmsman hears "Midships." What does it mean? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) Stop the engines
   - B) Put the wheel back to the centre
   - C) Turn fully to port
   - D) Drop the anchor
   **Answer: B**

7. What does a pilot do when he comes aboard in port? (Sub lesson 4)
   - A) He takes command of the ship for the whole voyage
   - B) He inspects the cargo for the buyers
   - C) He pays the port charges
   - D) He guides the ship in and out of the harbour with his local knowledge
   **Answer: D**

8. What happens at a Port State Control inspection? (Sub lesson 4)
   - A) Officials check the ship's certificates, safety equipment and crew
   - B) The cargo is sold
   - C) The ship is repainted
   - D) The crew take their examinations
   **Answer: A**
