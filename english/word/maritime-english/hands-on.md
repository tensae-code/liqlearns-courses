# Hands-On — Maritime English

Things to do away from the page.

- **Name the ship.** Draw a ship from the side and label her:
  bow, stern, port side, starboard side, deck, bridge, engine
  room, funnel, anchor. Say each label aloud as you write it.
- **Spell it the sea way.** Spell your own name and your
  town's name with the IMO spelling alphabet (Alfa, Bravo,
  Charlie…). Then spell a partner's name while they write the
  letters down — the way a radio operator checks a name.
- **Signal drill.** With a partner, act out the three
  signals: one of you calls Mayday for a ship in grave danger,
  Pan Pan for an urgent problem that is not yet danger to
  life, and Securité for a safety warning — the other says
  what each signal means before answering.
