# Hands-on — Words and How They Change

- **Origin hunt.** Each learner picks five common words and finds their origin notes in a dictionary. Class tally: which languages lent the most words?
- **Timeline wall.** Build the four-stage timeline on the wall; each learner adds one dated fact or example word to the right stage.
- **Living change.** Interview an older speaker about a word whose meaning has shifted in their lifetime; report the old and new meanings to the class.
