# Video script — Words and How They Change

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Story of English
- Beat 1: A road unrolls; four signposts rise along it: CELTIC, OLD ENGLISH, MIDDLE ENGLISH, MODERN ENGLISH. The paving changes style at each sign.
- Beat 2: One sentence appears four times in four old hands, each more readable. The presenter reads the last aloud: the language changed — the sentence survived.

## Sub lesson 2 — Borrowed Words
- Beat 1: An old French court; the server calls "Tennez!" as he serves. The word flies over the net and lands, reshaped, as TENNIS on a modern court.
- Beat 2: A market stall of words in many scripts, all being packed into one English dictionary. The presenter: English borrows — and keeps what it borrows.
