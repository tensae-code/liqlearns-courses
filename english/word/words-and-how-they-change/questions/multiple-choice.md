# Multiple choice — Words and How They Change

1. Which order gives the story of English correctly? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Modern English, Old English, Celtic, Middle English
   - B. Celtic, Old English, Middle English, Modern English
   - C. Old English, Celtic, Modern English, Middle English
   - D. Celtic, Middle English, Old English, Modern English
   Answer: B

2. In Sāmoa's book, Middle English is dated… (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. 500–800
   - B. 800–1000
   - C. 1200–1500
   - D. 1700–1900
   Answer: C

3. The word "tennis" carries inside it the French word… (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. "Tenez"
   - B. "Tennez"
   - C. "Tennis"
   - D. "Tente"
   Answer: B

4. What does "tennez" show about English? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. English never changes
   - B. English borrows words from other languages and makes them its own
   - C. Tennis was invented in England
   - D. French words keep their French spelling in English
   Answer: B
