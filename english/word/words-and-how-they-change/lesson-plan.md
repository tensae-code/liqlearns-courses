# Words and How They Change — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 43, Words in Depth

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a long road with four signposts along it — CELTIC, OLD ENGLISH, MIDDLE ENGLISH, MODERN ENGLISH — and the road's paving stones changing style at each sign. *The learners read the signs in order and say what is changing along the road.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of the same short sentence written four times in four old-fashioned hands, each version a little more readable than the last. *The learners try to read the oldest version, then the newest, and say what made the newest easier.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a tennis ball with a speech bubble saying TENNEZ! in old French letters, and an arrow from the bubble to the modern word TENNIS. *The learners say where the word tennis was hiding before it was English.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a market stall where words in different scripts and alphabets are being handed across the counter into an English dictionary. *The learners name two languages they know English has borrowed words from.*
