## Sub lesson 1 — The Story of English
- Depth: an outline history of the English language — Celtic beginnings, Old English, Middle English (given as 1200–1500), and Modern English emerging before Shakespeare (Samoa, *English Year 13*, printed pp.27–28).

## Sub lesson 2 — Borrowed Words
- Depth: loan words — how English takes words from other languages and makes them its own, worked through "tennis", which carries the French "tennez" inside it (Samoa, *English Year 13*, printed p.31).
