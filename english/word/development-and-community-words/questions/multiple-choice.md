## Multiple choice

1. Goal 1 is to eradicate extreme… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) homework  b) poverty and hunger  c) winter
   **Answer: b) poverty and hunger.**

2. Goal 2 says every child should have… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a phone  b) education  c) a car
   **Answer: b) education** — education for all.

3. Gender equality (Goal 3) means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) girls and women get the same chances  b) only boys decide  c) no school for girls
   **Answer: a).**

4. Goal 6 fights… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) bad weather  b) HIV/AIDS, malaria and other diseases  c) noise
   **Answer: b).**

5. Goal 7 cares for… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the environment  b) examinations  c) costumes
   **Answer: a)** — environmental sustainability.
