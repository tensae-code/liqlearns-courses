# Lesson plan — Development and Community Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Development and Community Words
**Lesson:** development-and-community-words — the Millennium Development Goals in words — ending poverty and hunger, school for every child, equal chances for girls and women, healthy children and mothers, fighting disease, caring for the earth, and countries working together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name the eight Millennium Development Goals and the words each one carries.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's series returns to the Millennium Development Goals three times: Goals 1–3 in Grade 9, the goals again in Grade 10's passive lesson, and Goals 4–8 in Grade 11 — the goals are the books' own chosen frame for the world's work. No lesson in the course housed the development-goals set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Goals for everyone**
2. **Health, earth and partnership**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Goals for everyone": name Goals 1–3: end poverty and hunger, education for all, equal chances for women and men
- after sub lesson "Health, earth and partnership": name Goals 4–8: child health, mothers' health, fighting disease, a living earth, and countries in partnership

## What you need
Nothing special.

## If the learner is stuck
One goal at a time, each tied to a face: a hungry child, a girl out of school, a mother at a clinic. The words hold when the faces do.
