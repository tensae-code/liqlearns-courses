# Video script — Development and Community Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Eight doors in a row; each opens on a small scene — a meal, a school, a clinic, a tree.]

"The world made eight promises."

"No hunger. School for every child. Equal chances."

"Healthy children. Safe mothers. Diseases fought. A living earth. Countries together."

"Eight goals. One promise."
