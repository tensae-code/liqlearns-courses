# Development and Community Words

## Do it first

Think of your own community. What does it need most — food, a school, a clinic, clean water? The world once wrote down eight goals for every community. This lesson teaches their names.

## Sub lesson 1 — Goals for everyone

The world set itself eight goals — the **Millennium Development Goals**. The first three are for everyone, everywhere. **Goal 1: eradicate extreme poverty and hunger** — no one starving, no one with nothing. **Goal 2: education for all** — every child in school. **Goal 3: gender equality** — girls and women with the same chances as boys and men, and more women with a voice in decisions.

Poverty, hunger, school for all, equal chances: the first words of the world's promise.

*Difficulty: Three goals, each with its key words.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Health, earth and partnership

The other five goals finish the promise. **Goal 4: reduce child mortality** — fewer children dying young. **Goal 5: improve maternal health** — mothers kept safe. **Goal 6: combat HIV/AIDS, malaria and other diseases.** **Goal 7: environmental sustainability** — the earth cared for, not used up. **Goal 8: a global partnership** — countries working together, with rich countries helping poorer ones.

Eight goals, one promise: a world where no one is left outside.

*Difficulty: Five more goals — the set completes.*

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion which of the eight goals your community needs most, and why.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the eight goals in your own short words, one line each.
2. Pick one goal and write three things your town could do for it.
3. Ask someone at home which goal matters most to them. Write their answer and their reason.
