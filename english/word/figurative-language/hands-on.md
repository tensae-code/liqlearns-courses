# Hands-on — Figurative Language

- **Flag hunt.** In a page of any story, find one simile and mark its comparing word; then find one metaphor and say what two things it fuses.
- **Tool swap.** Take a plain description paragraph and add exactly one tool per sentence — personification, alliteration, onomatopoeia — then read both versions aloud and compare.
- **Figure cards.** Make a card per tool: the name, the test that identifies it, and one example of your own. Test a partner with the cards.
