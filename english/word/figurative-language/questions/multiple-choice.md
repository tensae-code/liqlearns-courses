# Multiple choice — Figurative Language

1. Which sentence holds a simile? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. The moon was a silver coin.
   - B. Their new friend was as ripe as a pawpaw.
   - C. The crocuses peered at the sky.
   - D. The rain went bang on the roof.
   Answer: B

2. How does a metaphor differ from a simile? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. A metaphor is always longer
   - B. A metaphor pictures one thing as BEING another, with no comparing word
   - C. A metaphor only appears in poems
   - D. A metaphor uses "like" or "as"
   Answer: B

3. "The crocuses peered at the sky" is an example of… (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Onomatopoeia
   - B. Alliteration
   - C. Personification
   - D. A simile
   Answer: C

4. Which word is onomatopoeia? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Table
   - B. Splash
   - C. Quickly
   - D. Garden
   Answer: B
