# Fill in the blank — Figurative Language

1. A ______ pictures one thing as similar to another, using "like", "as" or "than". (Sub lesson 1) — simile
2. A ______ pictures one thing as being another. (Sub lesson 1) — metaphor
3. Giving human qualities to a flower or the wind is called ______. (Sub lesson 2) — personification
4. Words that sound like what they mean, such as "splash", show ______. (Sub lesson 2) — onomatopoeia
