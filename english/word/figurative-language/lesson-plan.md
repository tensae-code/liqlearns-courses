# Figurative Language — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 43, Words in Depth

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a pawpaw cut open, golden and ripe, beside a smiling new friend, with the words AS RIPE AS A PAWPAW under both. *The learners say what two things are being compared and which small word does the comparing.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of a night sky where the moon is drawn as a silver boat sailing on clouds, with no comparing word anywhere. *The learners say what the moon is pictured AS, and how this picture differs from the pawpaw one.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of crocus flowers with small curious faces peering up at the sky. *The learners say what human thing the flowers are doing, and what that way of writing is called.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a page with three sound words in big letters — SPLASH, HUM, BANG — each beside a small drawing of the thing that makes the sound. *The learners read the words aloud and say why they sound like what they mean.*

## Sub lessons
1. Simile and Metaphor
2. Personification, Alliteration and Onomatopoeia

Gate pool for each sub lesson: the whole question set of the sub lesson before it — every question file of this lesson carries (Sub lesson N) markers, and the gate draws from the previous sub lesson's items, at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts.
