# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simile and Metaphor
- Depth: similes defined as figurative language — picturing one thing as similar to another with "like", "as" or "than" (Samoa, *English Year 13*, printed p.21); metaphors — picturing one thing as being another (printed p.22); and the senior creative-writing techniques named as one set (Samoa, *English Year 12*, printed p.26). Fiji's separate simile and metaphor lessons go deeper on each figure alone; this lesson holds the family together as Sāmoa teaches it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Personification, Alliteration and Onomatopoeia
- Depth: personification in poetry — human qualities given to things, as in "The crocuses peered at the sky" (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 1*, printed p.54); alliteration spotted and judged in poetry lines (printed p.60); onomatopoeia named inside the creative-writing techniques set (Samoa, *English Year 12*, printed p.26).
