# Video script — Figurative Language

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simile and Metaphor
- Beat 1: A ripe pawpaw glows beside a smiling face; the words AS RIPE AS A PAWPAW appear, and the word AS pulses. The presenter: the small word is the simile's flag.
- Beat 2: The moon over the sea becomes a silver coin, no comparing word anywhere. The presenter: no flag this time — the moon doesn't resemble the coin, in the sentence it IS the coin. That is a metaphor.

## Sub lesson 2 — Personification, Alliteration and Onomatopoeia
- Beat 1: Crocuses lift small faces and peer at the sky. The presenter: flowers can't peer — personification loans them our qualities.
- Beat 2: Three sound words land with their sounds: SPLASH, HUM, BANG. The presenter: words that sound like what they mean — onomatopoeia; and listen for alliteration, the same opening sound repeated.
