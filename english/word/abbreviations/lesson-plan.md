# Lesson Plan — Abbreviations

**One thing:** an abbreviation is a shortened form of a word or name — a few letters standing for the full form — and writing the full form out.

## Sub lessons

1. Shortened forms — what an abbreviation is; a few letters standing for a whole word or name; meeting them in reading
2. Writing them in full — reading an abbreviation by its full form; writing the full form in careful writing so nobody has to guess

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Writers shorten words and names to save space and time: the shortened form is called an **abbreviation**.
- The short form stands for the full form: Mr. stands for Mister, Dr. for Doctor, Rd. for Road, Jan. for January, Mon. for Monday.
- One short form can stand for more than one full form: St. can be Street or Saint — the sentence around it tells you which.
- When you read an abbreviation aloud, you say the full form, not the letters: "Dr. Bello" is read "Doctor Bello".
- In careful writing, write the word in full. The full form leaves nobody guessing what the short form meant.
