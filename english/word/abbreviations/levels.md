# Levels — Abbreviations

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Shortened forms | Say what an abbreviation is, match common short forms to the words and names they stand for, and tell when one short form can stand for two different full forms. |
| 2. Writing them in full | Read any abbreviation aloud by its full form, and write the full form out in careful writing so the reader never has to guess. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can meet a shortened form anywhere, know what it stands for, say it properly, and write the full form when the writing matters.
