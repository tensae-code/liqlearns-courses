# Video Script — Abbreviations

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each short form appears on screen as it is said, then opens into its full form.

[Picture: a road sign reading "Palm Rd."]

Look at the sign. "Rd." — the word is cut short! Rd. stands for Road. A cut-short word is called an abbreviation.

[Picture: "Dr." opening into "Doctor", "Jan." opening into "January"]

Dr. stands for Doctor. Jan. stands for January. The short form stands for the full form!

[Picture: the words "St. Mary's Road" and "St. Mary" side by side]

Careful! St. can mean Street… and St. can mean Saint. The words around it tell you which one.

[Picture: a child reading aloud, the words "Doctor Bello" in a speech bubble]

When you read out loud, say the full form. Not "Dr." — say "Doctor"!

[Picture: a neat letter with "January" and "Road" written out in full]

And when your writing matters, write the word in full. January. Road. Nobody has to guess!
