# Short Answer — Abbreviations

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What is an abbreviation? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A shortened form of a word or name: a few letters standing for the full form.
2. Name two places where shortened forms are used to save space. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any two of: road signs, addresses, notes, lists, timetables.
3. Write "Dr." and "Mr." in full. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Doctor; Mister.
4. You read "Born in Jan." in a book. What do you say aloud? (Sub lesson 2)
   — "Born in January" — the full form, not the letters of the short form.
5. Why is it safer to write the full form in careful writing? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Because the reader does not have to stop and guess what the short form stands for; the full form can only be read one way.
