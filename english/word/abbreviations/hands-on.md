# Hands-On — Abbreviations

Things you do with real signs, real addresses and a pen. No special materials needed.

1. **Short-form hunt.** Walk to the nearest road sign, shop sign or school notice. Write down every shortened form you find, and write its full form beside it.
2. **Read it whole.** Take a note or letter that uses short forms. Read it aloud twice: first saying the letters you see, then saying the full forms. Which reading sounds like real speech?
3. **The St. test.** Find "St." used in the world around you — a street name, a school name, a church name. Decide from the words around it: Street or Saint? Show your working to a partner.
4. **Write it in full.** Copy a short address or note that is full of shortened forms, and rewrite the whole thing with every word written in full.
5. **Make a pair card.** On one side of a card write a short form (Dr., Rd., Jan., Mon., Mr.); on the other side its full form. Trade cards with a partner and test each other both ways.
