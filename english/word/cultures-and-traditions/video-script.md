# Video script — Cultures and Traditions

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Pictures of dancers, a theatre, a street market appear one by one.]

“Every country has its peoples. Every people has its ways.”

[The words pura vida appear.] “In Costa Rica they say: pura vida! Pure life.”

[A small group inside a big crowd is circled.] “A small world inside the big one — that is a subculture.”

[Arrows travel between two towns.] “And when ways travel? That is diffusion. Cultures meet — and they change.”
