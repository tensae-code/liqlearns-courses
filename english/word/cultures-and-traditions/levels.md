# Levels — Cultures and Traditions

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| The peoples of a country | the first peoples of Costa Rica today — the Boruca, the Chorotegans, the Maleku — and a country's traditions and identity (quedar bien, la hora tica, pura vida) |
| Kinds of culture | high culture vs popular culture, and the smaller worlds inside a culture: subculture and counterculture (the hipster's story) |
| Cultures meet and change | cultural diffusion and globalization — how ways of living spread — cultural lag, and what technology does to culture |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
