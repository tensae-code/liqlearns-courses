## Multiple choice

1. The Boruca are known in the book as… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the smallest people  b) the people who were never conquered  c) a people of Nicoya
   **Answer: b).**

2. “La hora tica” means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Tico time — arriving relaxed and late  b) a famous clock  c) dinner time
   **Answer: a).**

3. A subculture is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a group with its own style inside a bigger culture  b) the culture of a whole country  c) an old tradition
   **Answer: a).**

4. Cultural diffusion is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) a law  b) the spread of ways of living from one group to another  c) a kind of dance
   **Answer: b).**
