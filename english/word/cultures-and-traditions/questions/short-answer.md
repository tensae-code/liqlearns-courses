## Short answer

1. Name two of Costa Rica's indigenous peoples from this lesson. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any two of the Boruca, the Chorotegans, the Maleku (also named in the book: Huetar, Bribri, Cabécar, Brunca, Ngöbe, Teribe).**

2. What does “qué chiva” mean? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: how great / how cool.**

3. How is a counterculture different from a subculture? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: a subculture lives its own style inside the main culture; a counterculture pushes against the main culture's ways.**

4. Give one example of cultural diffusion from your own life. (Sub lesson 3) **Answer: any food, word, music or style that travelled from another group/country (learner's own).**
