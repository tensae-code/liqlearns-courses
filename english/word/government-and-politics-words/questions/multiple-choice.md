## Multiple choice

1. A democracy is a system where… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a king is born to rule  b) the people choose their rulers in elections  c) one man holds all the power
   **Answer: b).**

2. A republic is a country… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) with an elected head and no king or queen  b) ruled by a parliament only  c) with no government
   **Answer: a).**

3. An election is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a law  b) a party meeting  c) the choosing of rulers by votes
   **Answer: c).**

4. A minister is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a voter  b) a member of the government with one job  c) the head of a republic
   **Answer: b).**
