## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)

1. democracy
2. monarchy
3. dictatorship
4. parliament
5. government
6. constitution

a) one person holds all the power and cannot be changed by the people
b) the group of people who rule a country
c) a country ruled by a king or queen
d) the people's chosen assembly that makes the laws
e) a country's highest set of laws
f) the people choose their rulers in elections

**Answers:** 1–f, 2–c, 3–a, 4–d, 5–b, 6–e.
