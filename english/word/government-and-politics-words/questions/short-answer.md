## Short answer

1. What is the difference between a monarchy and a republic? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A monarchy is ruled by a king or queen; a republic has an elected head — a president — and no king or queen.

2. Name the assembly that makes a country's laws, and the group that rules the country. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The parliament makes the laws; the government rules the country.

3. Say how a president in a democracy gets his job, in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The people choose him in an election — he wins the voters' votes.
