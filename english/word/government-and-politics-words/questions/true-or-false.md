## True or false

1. In a dictatorship, the people can change their ruler in an election. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** In a dictatorship one person holds all the power and the people cannot change him.

2. A parliament makes a country's laws. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. A voter is a person who votes in an election. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
