# Lesson plan — Government and Politics Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Government and Politics Words
**Lesson:** government-and-politics-words — the words for how a country is ruled: the systems (democracy, republic, monarchy), the rulers and assemblies, and the elections that choose them
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name and tell apart the systems — democracy, republic, monarchy, dictatorship — and say what makes each one what it is, and use the ruling set — government, parliament, president, minister, election, vote, party — to say who rules and how they got there.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Armenia's Grade 11 book gives its Political Systems unit a vocabulary of its own — words and phrases for different political systems — before its listenings on the US and Armenian systems. No lesson in the course housed this set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## If the learner is stuck
