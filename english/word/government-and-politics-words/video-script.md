# Video script — Government and Politics Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A ballot box; a crown; a parliament chamber; a president's desk.]

"Countries are ruled in different ways. In a democracy, the people choose their rulers."

"A republic has a president. A monarchy has a king or queen. In a dictatorship, one man holds all the power."

"An election is the choosing. A vote is your mark. And a parliament makes the laws."
