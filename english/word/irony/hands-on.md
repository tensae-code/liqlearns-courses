# Hands-On — Irony

1. **Irony hunt.** In pairs, hunt the day's talk and news for ironies; present the best one with both sides named.
2. **Surface and truth.** Cards with ironic sentences; learners match each to its true meaning card.
3. **The audience knows.** Act a tiny scene twice: once plain, once after the class is told the secret first — feel the dramatic irony change it.
4. **Peer check.** Swap irony explanations with a partner and mark: surface named? truth named? clear?
