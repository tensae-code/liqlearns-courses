# Video Script — Irony

Super-simple lines, one picture per line.

[Picture: rain pouring, a soaked boy smiling and giving a thumbs up]
""Wonderful weather!" He means the opposite — that is irony."

[Picture: a cat sitting proudly beside a sign that says NO CATS ALLOWED]
"A cat, under a no-cats sign. The picture says the opposite of the words."

[Picture: an audience watching a stage; the audience can see a trapdoor the actor cannot]
"Dramatic irony: we know. He does not. Watch out!"
