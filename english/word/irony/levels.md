# Levels — Irony

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — When words mean the opposite | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Spot irony in a worked paragraph: identify the irony and explain the meaning underneath — what is said on the surface, and what is really meant. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Dramatic irony | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Understand and find dramatic irony: the audience knows something a character does not, and watches the character walk towards it — the lesson's reading (a Hawthorne excerpt) carries it. |
