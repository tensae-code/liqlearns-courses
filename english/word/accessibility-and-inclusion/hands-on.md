# Hands-on — Accessibility and Inclusion

1. **Audit your place.** Walk through your school or street with the book's eyes: doors, steps, ramps, signs, toilets, paths. List what helps everyone get in and take part, and what blocks someone.
2. **Start a K-L-W chart.** Write what you Know about inclusion, what you Want to know, and — after the lesson's reading — what you Learned.
