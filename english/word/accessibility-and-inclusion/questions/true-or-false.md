## True or false

1. **A ramp can be used by everyone, not only wheelchair users.** (Sub lesson 1) — **True**
2. **Accessibility is only about buildings.** (Sub lesson 1) — **False** (it covers services and information too)
3. **In a K-L-W chart, the last column is filled in first.** (Sub lesson 2) — **False** (Learned is completed last)
4. **Inclusion means everyone takes part, not watching from the edge.** (Sub lesson 2) — **True**
