# Multiple choice questions — Accessibility and Inclusion

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).
The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions —
no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Multiple choice

1. **What does accessibility mean?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Only new buildings need it
   - b) A place or service can be reached and used by everyone
   - c) Moving very fast
   - **Answer: b) A place or service can be reached and used by everyone**

2. **What does the Grade 9 book send learners to audit?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A shop's prices
   - b) Their own neighbourhood and school
   - c) A football match
   - **Answer: b) Their own neighbourhood and school**

3. **For a wheelchair user, a step is like…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A wall
   - b) A door
   - c) A game
   - **Answer: a) A wall**

4. **What do the letters in a K-L-W chart stand for?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Know, Want to know, Learned
   - b) Kick, Walk, Leap
   - c) Keep, Write, Listen
   - **Answer: a) Know, Want to know, Learned**

5. **An inclusion advocate is a person who…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Sells tickets
   - b) Works to bring shut-out people in
   - c) Builds roads
   - **Answer: b) Works to bring shut-out people in**
