(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).

## Short answer

1. **Name one thing that helps and one thing that blocks in a place you know.** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Any real pair from the learner's audit (for example: the ramp at the door helps; the narrow doorway blocks).
2. **What is the difference between accessibility and inclusion, as the book teaches them?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** Accessibility makes places, services and information reachable and usable by everyone; inclusion makes everyone belong and take part.
3. **What does the L column of a K-L-W chart hold?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** What I learned, written at the end.
