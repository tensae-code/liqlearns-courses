## Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word from the box. The box holds one word for each gap.

**Box:** accessibility, audit, inclusion, advocate

1. ______ means everyone can reach and use a place or service. **Answer:** accessibility (Sub lesson 1)
2. To ______ a place is to check it item by item. **Answer:** audit (Sub lesson 1)
3. ______ means everyone is in and taking part. **Answer:** inclusion (Sub lesson 2)
4. An inclusion ______ works to bring shut-out people in. **Answer:** advocate (Sub lesson 2)
