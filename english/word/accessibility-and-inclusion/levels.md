# Levels — Accessibility and Inclusion

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it moves from a definition to fieldwork — the learner audits a real place — and then to organising the whole topic in a chart. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — What accessibility means | The definition and the audit of home ground. |
| 2 — Inclusion — everyone in | The chart method and the advocate's example. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
