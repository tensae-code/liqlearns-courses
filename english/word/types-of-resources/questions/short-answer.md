# Short Answer — Types of Resources

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the three kinds of resources. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Human, natural and capital resources.
2. Give one example of each kind on a farm. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Human: the farmer's labour and skill. Natural: the land and rain. Capital: the plough or tractor.
3. Read a school through its resources: one of each kind. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Human: the teachers. Natural: the water and trees. Capital: the school building and desks.
4. What is a colmado? (Sub lesson 2)
   — A small neighbourhood store (in the Dominican Republic).
