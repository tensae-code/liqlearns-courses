# Lesson Plan — Types of Resources

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the three kinds of resources a community lives on — human, natural and capital — and how to name and sort them.

## Sub lessons

1. **Three kinds of resources.** *Can-do: Sort the world's useful things into three kinds: human resources (people's labour, skills and knowledge), natural resources (from nature, used in natural form) and capital resources (man-made tools, machines and buildings).*
2. **Resources in my community.** *Can-do: Read a community through its resources: the colmado (neighbourhood store) and the people, land and tools around it — who gives the labour, what nature gives, what was built.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Resources in the community (item 296): human resources (people who provide labour, skills and knowledge), natural resources (from nature, used in natural form) and capital resources (man-made tools, machines and buildings), with colmados as neighbourhood stores.
