# Levels — Types of Resources

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of resources | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Sort the world's useful things into three kinds: human resources (people's labour, skills and knowledge), natural resources (from nature, used in natural form) and capital resources (man-made tools, machines and buildings). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Resources in my community | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Read a community through its resources: the colmado (neighbourhood store) and the people, land and tools around it — who gives the labour, what nature gives, what was built. |
