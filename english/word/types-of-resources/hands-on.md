# Hands-On — Types of Resources

1. **Sort the cards.** Picture cards (river, doctor, plough, forest, teacher, school…) sorted into the three kinds, then defended aloud.
2. **Community walk.** Walk the school grounds and list every resource you can see, kind by kind.
3. **Build a colmado.** In groups, plan a small shop: name its human, natural and capital resources.
4. **Resource interview.** Ask an adult what resources their job uses; report the three kinds to the class.
