# Hands-on — Sports

The lesson in your real body, not only on the page.

## The mime round
With a partner: mime a sport — kick, throw, swim, ride.
Your partner names the sport AND its partner: "Play
football!" "Go swimming!" Five mimes, then swap. A wrong
partner costs the mime a repeat.

## Your week in sports
Write your real week: every sport or game you played,
with its day. Read it aloud in whole pairs: "On Saturday
I play football. I go swimming on…"

## The action hunt
Take one sport you love. List its action verbs in
English — for football: kick, pass, run, catch (the
keeper!). Act each verb as you say it.

## The fit interview
Ask a real person Mongolia's question: "What sport do
you do to keep fit?" Write their answer whole — sport,
partner, how often — and read it back to them.
