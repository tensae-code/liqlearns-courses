# Sports — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sports · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Play, Do and Go.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is in Mexico's sports set?
a) skateboarding  b) baking  c) sewing
**Answer: a) skateboarding** — running, cycling,
swimming, skateboarding, volleyball, football, walking,
yoga, dancing (Mexico, Project 6, p.60).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In football, you ______ the ball with
your foot.
a) kick  b) paddle  c) bat
**Answer: a) kick** — Mongolia's action verbs (English
IX, Unit 3).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "I ______ basketball twice a week."
a) play  b) do  c) go
**Answer: a) play** — ball games take play (Mongolia's
PLAY/DO/GO discovery).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "She ______ swimming every day."
a) plays  b) does  c) goes
**Answer: c) goes** — the pair is go + the -ing sport:
"I go swimming," "She goes swimming" (Mongolia's
PLAY/DO/GO discovery).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He ______ judo on Saturdays."
a) plays  b) does  c) goes
**Answer: b) does** — martial arts take do: do judo,
do squats (Mongolia's discovery).
