# Sports — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sports · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Sports in Action · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Play, Do and Go.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five sports from Mexico's list.
**Answer:** any five of running, cycling, swimming,
skateboarding, volleyball, football, walking, yoga,
dancing (Project 6, p.60).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three action verbs for basketball,
from Mongolia's pictured-sports verb set.
**Answer:** any three of throw, pass, dribble, bounce,
block, catch (the full set: throw, carry, kick, pass,
dribble, tackle, pitch, bat, jump, swim, paddle, bounce,
block, catch — English IX, Unit 3).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a lifter, in Mongolia's
sportspeople table?
**Answer:** a person who lifts weights in the gym
(English IX, Unit 3).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer Mongolia's interview question for
yourself: "What sport do you do to keep fit?"
**Answer:** a true answer using play/do/go correctly —
"I do… / I play… / I go …ing…" (Unit 3, p.35).
