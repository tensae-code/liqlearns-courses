# Sports — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Sports · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Play, Do and Go.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** play · do · go · kick · swim · catch

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In football you ______ the ball.
**Answer: kick.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In basketball you throw and ______
the ball.
**Answer: catch.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** I ______ football with my friends.
**Answer: play.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** I ______ judo on Saturdays.
**Answer: do.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** I ______ swimming every day.
**Answer: go** — "I go swimming" (Mongolia's shape;
go + the -ing sport).
