# Sports — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** play · do · go · kick · swim · catch

**1. (Level 1)** In football you ______ the ball.
**Answer: kick.**

**2. (Level 1)** In basketball you throw and ______
the ball.
**Answer: catch.**

**3. (Level 3)** I ______ football with my friends.
**Answer: play.**

**4. (Level 3)** I ______ judo on Saturdays.
**Answer: do.**

**5. (Level 3)** I ______ swimming every day.
**Answer: go** — "I go swimming" (Mongolia's shape;
go + the -ing sport).
