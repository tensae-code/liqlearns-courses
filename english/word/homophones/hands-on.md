# Hands-on — Homophones

1. **Hear it, choose it.** One of you reads a sentence aloud with a homophone hidden in it — "Their boat is over there." The other writes it down, choosing each spelling by the meaning, then explains the choices.

2. **Trap hunt.** Swap a piece of writing. Each of you hunts the other's trap pairs — their/there/they're, your/you're, its/it's, to/too/two — and marks any wrong choice with the meaning it needed.
