## Fill in the blank

1. Words that sound the same but differ in meaning and spelling are called ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: homophones.**

2. "I can ______ the sea" — the word for what your eyes do is spelled with two e's and no a. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: see.**

3. "______ are going home" — the contraction of "they are" takes the apostrophe. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: They're.**

4. Choose by _______, not by sound: that is the whole method for writing homophones right. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: meaning.**
