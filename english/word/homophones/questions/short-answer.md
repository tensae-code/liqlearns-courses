## Short answer

1. What are homophones? Give one pair as your example. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: words that sound the same but have different meanings and spellings — for example see (with your eyes) and sea (the ocean).**

2. Why can't you trust your ear to choose between them? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: the ear hears one sound for both words; only the meaning in the sentence tells them apart.**

3. What is the working method for choosing the right homophone when you write? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: say the meaning you want first, pick the spelling that carries that meaning, then check the known trap pairs in what you wrote.**
