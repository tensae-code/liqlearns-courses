## Multiple choice

1. Homophones are words that… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) sound the same but mean different things  b) mean the same but sound different  c) are spelled the same and mean the same
   **Answer: a).**

2. Which pair are homophones? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cat / dog  b) see / sea  c) run / running
   **Answer: b).**

3. Your ear alone cannot choose between homophones because… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) they are silent  b) they share one sound — only the meaning separates them  c) they are too long
   **Answer: b).**

4. "______ boat is over ______." The right spellings, in order, are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Their / there  b) There / their  c) They're / their
   **Answer: a).**

5. A spell-checker will not catch a wrong homophone because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) it is switched off  b) every homophone is a real word, spelled rightly for its own meaning  c) homophones are too rare
   **Answer: b).**
