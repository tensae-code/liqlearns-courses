## True or false

1. Homophones are spelled the same way. (Sub lesson 1) **False — they sound the same; their spellings differ.**

2. "Hear" and "here" are homophones. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. A spell-checker always catches a wrong homophone. (Sub lesson 2) **False — the wrong choice is still a real, rightly-spelled word, for its own meaning.**

4. Their, there and they're are three different words sharing one sound. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
