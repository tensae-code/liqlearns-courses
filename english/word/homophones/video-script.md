# Video script — Homophones

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot looks out at the sea, then points at its own eyes.]

"See — sea! My eyes see, and that is the sea. Same sound!"

[Two word cards appear: SEE and SEA.] "Two words. One sound. Different spelling, different meaning."

"When you write, your reader cannot hear you. So choose by the meaning — not by the sound."
