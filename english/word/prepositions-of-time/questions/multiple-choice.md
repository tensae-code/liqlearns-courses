## Multiple choice

1. I get up ___ 7 o'clock. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) in  b) on  c) at
   **Answer: c) at** — clock times take at.

2. We sing ___ Carnival. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) at  b) in  c) on
   **Answer: a) at** — holiday names take at.

3. I play football ___ Saturday. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) at  b) on  c) in
   **Answer: b) on** — days take on.

4. The party is ___ 5 May. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) in  b) at  c) on
   **Answer: c) on** — dates take on, like days.

5. My birthday is ___ June. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) on  b) in  c) at
   **Answer: b) in** — months take in.

6. School starts ___ 2025. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) in  b) on  c) at
   **Answer: a) in** — years take in.

7. I read ___ the evening. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) at  b) on  c) in
   **Answer: c) in** — parts of the day take in — but night takes at.

8. We travelled ___ August, ___ Monday, ___ 6 o'clock. (Sub lesson 4)
   a) on, in, at  b) in, on, at  c) at, on, in
   **Answer: b) in, on, at** — month → in; day → on; clock time → at.
