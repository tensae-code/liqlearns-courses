## True or false

1. We say “at Monday”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — days take on: on Monday.

2. We say “at 7 o'clock”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — clock times take at.

3. We say “in Christmas”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — holidays take at: at Christmas.

4. We say “on June”. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — months take in: in June.

5. We say “in the morning”. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — parts of the day take in.

6. We say “in night”. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — night is the odd one: at night.

7. We say “on 5 May”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — dates take on.

8. One sentence can hold at, on and in together. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — “We left on Monday, in June, at 6 o'clock.”
