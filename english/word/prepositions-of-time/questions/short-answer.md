## Short answer

1. Which word goes with clock times — at, on or in? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: At** — at 7 o'clock, at noon.

2. Which word goes with “Christmas”? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: At** — at Christmas; holidays take at.

3. Which word goes with days of the week? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: On** — on Monday, on Saturday.

4. Which word goes with a date like 25 December? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: On** — on 25 December; dates behave like days.

5. Which word goes with months and years? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: In** — in June, in 2024.

6. Which word goes with “winter” and with “the morning”? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: In** — in winter, in the morning.

7. Finish: “The festival was ___ night.” (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: at** — night is the odd one: at night.

8. Finish the sentence with all three: “We left ___ June, ___ Sunday, ___ noon.” (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: in June, on Sunday, at noon** — month → in; day → on; clock point → at.
