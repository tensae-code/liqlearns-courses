## Fill in the blank

1. Lunch is ___ 1 o'clock. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: at** — clock times take at.

2. We rest ___ Christmas. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: at** — holidays take at.

3. The test is ___ Friday. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: on** — days take on.

4. He was born ___ 3 March. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: on** — dates take on.

5. Flowers come ___ spring. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: in** — seasons take in.

6. I study ___ the afternoon. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: in** — parts of the day take in.

7. The shop opens ___ Monday ___ 9 o'clock. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: on; at** — day → on; clock time → at.

8. It snowed ___ winter, ___ January, ___ night. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: in; in; at** — season → in; month → in; night → at.
