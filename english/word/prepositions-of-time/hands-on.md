# Hands-on — Prepositions of Time

A real clock and a real calendar teach this lesson best.

## Point and say

Point at a time on the clock — your partner says “at … o'clock”. Point at a day on the calendar — “on …”. Point at a month — “in …”. Faster and faster, like a game.

## My week out loud

Tell your whole week with time words: when you get up (at), your best day (on), your birthday month (in). Your partner counts how many at/on/in you used right.
