# Video script — Prepositions of Time

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A clock spins to 7:00.] “AT 7 o'clock! At is for clock times — and holidays: at Christmas!”

[A calendar page: MONDAY circled.] “ON Monday! On is for days — and dates: on 5 May!”

[Calendar pages flip: JUNE, then a year, then snow.] “IN June! IN 2024! IN winter! In is for the long times — and in the morning, in the evening.”

[All three pictures together.] “Clock? At. Day? On. Longer? In. ‘We left on Monday, in June, at 6 o'clock!’”
