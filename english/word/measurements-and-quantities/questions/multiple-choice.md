## Multiple choice

1. Weight is measured in… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) metres  b) kilos  c) spoonfuls
   **Answer: b) kilos.**

2. Height is measured in… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) metres  b) grams  c) bottles
   **Answer: a) metres.**

3. Capacity tells… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) how tall a thing is  b) how much a thing holds  c) how heavy a thing is
   **Answer: b).**

4. A milligram is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a whole gram  b) a thousandth of a gram  c) a bottle
   **Answer: b).**

5. "Twice a day" means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) two times every day  b) two bottles  c) every two days
   **Answer: a).**
