## Short answer

1. Name the four classes of measure in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Weight, height, length and capacity.

2. In what units are medicines weighed? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Grams and milligrams.

3. How is a liquid medicine taken, by the label? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** By spoonfuls, a set number of times a day.
