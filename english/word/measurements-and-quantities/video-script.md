# Video script — Measurements and Quantities

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A scale, a measuring tape and a bottle appear one by one.]

"How heavy? How tall? How long? How much?"

"Kilos for weight. Metres for height and length."

"And at the pharmacy: grams, milligrams, spoonfuls — once a day, twice a day."

"Measure carefully. It matters."
