# Video script — Birthday Customs Around the World

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A cake with candles; around it, flags of many countries.]

“A birthday! But how do they mark it — there? And there?”

[The mascot shrugs happily.] “Customs differ. That is the fun of it.”

“Read how the world marks a birthday.”
