## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. custom
2. mark (a birthday)
3. candle
4. celebrate

a) to do something special for a day
b) a usual way of doing things in a place
c) to keep a special day in the usual way
d) a small wax stick that burns with a flame

**Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-d, 4-a.**
