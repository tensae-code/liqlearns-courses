## Multiple choice

1. A custom is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a law  b) a people's usual way of doing a thing  c) a birthday cake
   **Answer: b).**

2. The Grade 9 reading is about… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) birthday customs around the world  b) wedding customs  c) school rules
   **Answer: a).**

3. The book tests the reading by asking you to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) sing  b) correct wrong sentences about the customs  c) draw a cake
   **Answer: b).**

4. When customs differ, the lesson says… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) one is right and the rest wrong  b) none is “the right one” — they differ  c) ignore them
   **Answer: b).**
