# Levels — Community and Conflict Words

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson at word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|

| The way out | use dialogue, tolerance and resolution — talking, bearing differences, and solving the conflict |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
