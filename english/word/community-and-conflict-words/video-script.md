# Video script — Community and Conflict Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two figures face each other, arms crossed; a speech bubble opens between them.]

"Trouble has names. Violence. Bullying. Conflict."

"And the way out has names too."

"Dialogue — talk and listen. Tolerance — bear our differences. Resolution — the problem, solved."
