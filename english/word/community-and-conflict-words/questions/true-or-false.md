## True or false

1. Bullying happens only with hands. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** It can be with words, or by leaving someone out.

2. Tolerance means agreeing with everyone about everything. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False.** It means bearing differences without hate.
