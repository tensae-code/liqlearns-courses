## Multiple choice

1. Hurting someone again and again, with hands or words, is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) dialogue  b) bullying  c) tolerance
   **Answer: b) bullying.**

2. Talking and listening until both sides are heard is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) violence  b) resolution  c) dialogue
   **Answer: c) dialogue.**

3. Bearing people who are different from you, without hate, is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) tolerance  b) bullying  c) conflict
   **Answer: a) tolerance.**

4. The solving of a conflict, its worked-out end, is its… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) start  b) resolution  c) name
   **Answer: b) resolution.**
