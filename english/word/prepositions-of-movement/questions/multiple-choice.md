# Multiple choice — Prepositions of Movement

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Through and around · Sub lesson 2 — Into and out of · Sub lesson 3 — The movement words on a walk. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Walking ___ the forest" — the walker enters among the trees and comes out the far side.
a) around  b) through  c) out of  d) into-rest
**Answer: b) through** — the chapter's own example: a crossing that passes inside and out again. (Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 38, printed p.48.)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sentence tells a circle?
a) We walked through the lake.  b) We walked around the lake.  c) We walked out of the lake.  d) We walked into the lake and stayed in it.
**Answer: b)** — around keeps the outside and comes back to the start.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The swimmer dives ___ the water."
a) out of  b) around  c) into  d) through-rest
**Answer: c) into** — the dive is a movement whose end is the inside of the water.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "She climbs ___ the water and dries in the sun."
a) into  b) out of  c) around  d) in
**Answer: b) out of** — she leaves the inside for the outside; out of is the facing partner of into.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The path goes ___ the forest, then ___ a small lake, and ends at a river."
a) around; through  b) through; around  c) out of; into-rest  d) in; in
**Answer: b) through; around** — the path crosses the forest's inside, then circles the lake.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A bird flies out of the trees, circles the field, and lands in the grass. In order, its words are —
a) into; through; out of  b) out of; around; into  c) around; out of; in-rest  d) through; into; around
**Answer: b) out of; around; into** — leaving the trees, circling the field, entering the grass.
