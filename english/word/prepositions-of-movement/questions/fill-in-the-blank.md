# Fill in the blank — Prepositions of Movement

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Through and around · Sub lesson 2 — Into and out of · Sub lesson 3 — The movement words on a walk. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Fill each gap with through, around, into or out of.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We are walking ___ the forest, in among the trees.
**Answer: through** (the chapter's own example — Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 38, printed p.48)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The road runs ___ the village, keeping outside it in a wide circle.
**Answer: around**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** He steps ___ the water slowly — it is cold!
**Answer: into**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cat jumps ___ the box and runs away.
**Answer: out of**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The hikers went ___ the forest, ___ the lake, and then swam ___ the river and climbed ___ it.
**Answer: through; around; into; out of**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The bird flew ___ its nest in the tree, circled ___ the tree twice, and dropped ___ the grass.
**Answer: out of; around; into**
