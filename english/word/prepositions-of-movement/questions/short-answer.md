# Short answer — Prepositions of Movement

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — Through and around · Sub lesson 2 — Into and out of · Sub lesson 3 — The movement words on a walk. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "walking through the forest," where does the walker come out?
**Answer: the far side of the forest** — through crosses the inside and exits. (Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 38, printed p.48.)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A walker goes around a lake. Where does the walk end?
**Answer: back at the start** — around is a circle that never enters.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two movement words face each other as a pair?
**Answer: into and out of** — in by movement, and back out again.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What small word must not be dropped from "out of"?
**Answer: of** — out of is one chunk in two parts.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A path crosses a forest, circles a lake, and a swimmer enters a river and climbs out. Name the four words in order.
**Answer: through; around; into; out of.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the choosing test for the four movement words.
**Answer: ask what the moving thing does — crosses an inside: through; circles: around; enters: into; leaves an inside: out of.**
