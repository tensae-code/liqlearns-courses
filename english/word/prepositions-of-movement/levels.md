# Prepositions of Movement — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and
Sub Lessons"). The lesson was built new in the restructure
era (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so
there are no old depth layers to map: the sub lessons below
are its depths as built, cut at the set's own joints — the
crossing pair, the facing pair, and the four at work
together. The questions live in the type files; this file
is the by-sub-lesson view.

Pass marks: the book names the focus and gives its example
but states no pass mark for it, so no mark is invented —
each sub lesson's pass mark is stated as the observable
thing the learner can do.

## Sub lesson 1 — Through and around
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use through for a crossing that passes inside a thing and out the other side ("walking through the forest" — the chapter's own example), and around for a circle or surrounding path, and tell the two paths apart.

## Sub lesson 2 — Into and out of
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use into for movement to the inside and out of for movement back outside, as one facing pair, keeping the "of" in out of.

## Sub lesson 3 — The movement words on a walk
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose correctly among through, around, into and out of in new nature sentences, and narrate a short walk using all four.
