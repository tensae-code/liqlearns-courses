# Video script — Prepositions of Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Walk with me.
I walk **through** the forest.
In among the trees — and out the other side. **Through!**

Now the lake.
I walk **around** the lake.
A big circle. I come back to the start. **Around!**

The water!
I dive **into** the water. Splash!
I climb **out of** the water. Drip, drip.

Four walks, four words.
Crossing inside? **Through.**
A circle? **Around.**
Going in? **Into.**
Coming out? **Out of.**

Now you walk. Say the words!
