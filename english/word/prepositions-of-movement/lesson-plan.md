# Lesson plan — Prepositions of Movement

**Course:** English · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Prepositions of Movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Word Kinds (CURRICULUM.md) —
a sweep addition beside the unit's word-kind lessons.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the four movement
words — **through**, **around**, **into**, **out of** — the
small words that tell how a thing moves through the world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Verbs (a movement
word is found by the movement it rides on) and beside
Prepositions In and Into, which teaches the into of this
set against in. It needs nothing else.
**Why this lesson exists:** Albania's Akelius A2 teacher's
guide names prepositions of movement — around, into, out
of, through — as the grammar focus of its nature chapter
(Chapter 38, printed p.48), with the chapter's own example,
"walking through the forest." The 2026-11-08
re-verification sweep re-read the guide page by page and
rebuilt its placements from the PDF itself (41 teaching
items; 26 already in the course); this focus was one of the
15 missing items, and no lesson housed it — one movement
sentence is quoted inside nature-and-the-environment, but
the named focus was stocked nowhere. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the focus gets its
own lesson.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Through and around** — through crosses the inside of a thing, from one side to the other (walking through the forest); around goes in a circle, or on all sides of a thing (walking around the lake).
2. **Into and out of** — into moves to the inside (into the forest, into the water); out of moves from the inside to the outside (out of the forest, out of the water) — the pair that faces each other.
3. **The movement words on a walk** — choosing among the four on a nature walk: the path goes through the forest, around the lake, into the water and out of it again.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Verbs; Prepositions In and Into for
  the into of this set. Recommended, never forced — the
  lesson stands alone.
- See also: giving-directions — its place words answer
  "where is it?"; this lesson's words answer "how does it
  move?", a different question.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Use **through** for a crossing that passes inside a
   thing and out the other side: "We walk through the
   forest."
2. Use **around** for a circle or a surrounding path:
   "We walk around the lake."
3. Use **into** and **out of** as the facing pair: in by
   movement, and back out again.
4. Choose among the four movement words in new nature
   sentences and say why.

## What you need
A chair or a box to walk around, a doorway or two chairs to
walk between (the "through"), and room to walk. Nothing
else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner walks through the doorway,
   then around a chair, saying each word as the body does
   it — the four words are four walks (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — through and around; into and out
   of; then all four on one walk (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF at the start of the explanation: a picture of a
     walker passing between two trees, 1–2 seconds, the word
     "through" under it — then the same walker circling a
     small lake with "around" under it — the student says
     each word as its picture plays.
   - ADD IMAGE under the facing pair: a picture of a swimmer
     at a lake's edge, one arrow diving in marked "into the
     water" and one arrow climbing out marked "out of the
     water" — the student says which arrow each word rides.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the four words in
   your own words, then narrate each other's walks
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the
     four walks in the super-simple voice (made later, when
     videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice,
   short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching —
   served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md (the four-walks course).
6. **Do It Yourself** — narrate a real walk of yours with
   all four words, said out loud (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes through and around → draw the two paths: through
  cuts across the middle and comes out the far side; around
  stays outside and comes back to the start.
- Drops the "of" in out of → out of is one word in two
  pieces; practise it as a single chunk: "out-of-the-water."
- This lesson's neighbour: Prepositions In and Into — into
  against its resting partner in.
