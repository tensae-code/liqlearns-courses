# Levels — Pronoun Case

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The three cases | Name the three cases of the personal pronouns and give the full set for each case. |
| 2. Choosing the correct case | Choose the correct case for a pronoun in a sentence by the job it is doing there. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the three cases of the personal pronouns — subjective, objective and possessive — and choosing the correct case for the job a pronoun is doing in a sentence.
