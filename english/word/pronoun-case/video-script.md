# Video Script — Pronoun Case

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: three children holding signs: I, ME, MY]

One person — three shapes! I… me… my. Why? Because the JOB changes!

[Picture: the child with the I sign throwing a ball]

I — does the action!

[Picture: the child with the ME sign catching the ball]

Me — receives it! And after words like TO and FOR, me again!

[Picture: the child with the MY sign hugging a book]

My — owns it! Three cases. Three jobs. Choose by the job!
