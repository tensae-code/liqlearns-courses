# Video Script — Kinds of Pronouns

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a hand pointing at a leaf close by, then at a tree far away]

This! That! Some words POINT. This — near. That — far. Pointing pronouns!

[Picture: a big question mark over a child's head]

Who? What? Which? Some words ASK. Asking pronouns!

[Picture: a shadowy figure leaving bread, face hidden]

Someone came… but WHO? We do not know! Indefinite — not named!

[Picture: two sentence cards joined by a bridge word WHO]

And some words JOIN: the man… WHO came. Joining pronouns — the whole family!
