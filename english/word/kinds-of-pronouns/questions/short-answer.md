# Short Answer — Kinds of Pronouns

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Give one sentence where 'that' is a pronoun and one where it is an adjective. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Pronoun: 'That is the last leaf.' Adjective: 'That leaf still hangs.' (With a noun beside it, it accompanies; alone, it stands in the place.)

2. What makes an indefinite pronoun indefinite? (Sub lesson 2)
   — It refers to people or things without naming exactly which ones — someone, anyone, each, many.

3. In 'the balls that the bears swallowed', what does 'that' join? (Sub lesson 2)
   — It joins the describing clause 'that the bears swallowed' to its noun 'the balls'.

4. Why does the REMEMBER box insist on the pronoun/adjective line? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Because the same words (this, that, many, which) do both jobs; only the test — standing alone in the noun's place vs accompanying a noun — tells which job a word is doing in a given sentence.
