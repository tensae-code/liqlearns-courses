   a) Interrogative pronouns
   b) Demonstrative pronouns ✓
   c) Relative pronouns

2. Which word asks as an interrogative pronoun? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) That
   b) Who ✓
   c) Someone

3. In "this leaf is painted", the word 'this' is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A pronoun standing alone

4. "Someone left bread for them." Someone is: (Sub lesson 2)

5. "The cobbler who freed the hand" — 'who' here is: (Sub lesson 2)

   d) Not a pronoun at all
