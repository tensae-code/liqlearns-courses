## Teaching points

- Kinds of pronouns beyond the personal set: demonstrative, interrogative, indefinite, relative — each defined (Lesson 2, Task 8 REMEMBER box, pp.307–308).
- The pronoun-versus-adjective distinction: a pronoun stands in the noun's place; an adjective accompanies the noun (p.308).
