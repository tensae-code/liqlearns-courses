# Hands-On — Kinds of Pronouns

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Point and name.** Around the room: point at things near and far and make four sentences — two with demonstratives as pronouns, two as adjectives. A partner labels each.
2. **Question round.** Write five questions using who, whom, whose, what, which. Read them aloud; your partner answers and names the pronoun kind in each.
3. **Kind hunt.** In one page of a story, find one pronoun of each of the four kinds and copy the sentences into a four-box chart, with the kind named and the test applied.
