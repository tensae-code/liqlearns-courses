# Levels — Kinds of Pronouns

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Pointing and asking pronouns | Define and use demonstrative pronouns (this, that, these, those) and interrogative pronouns (who, what, which…). |
| 2. Indefinite and relative pronouns | Define and use indefinite pronouns (someone, anyone, each, many…) and relative pronouns (who, which, that joining a clause), keeping the pronoun/adjective test in hand. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: the pronoun kinds beyond the personal set — demonstrative, interrogative, indefinite and relative pronouns — each defined, and the line between a pronoun and an adjective doing similar work.
