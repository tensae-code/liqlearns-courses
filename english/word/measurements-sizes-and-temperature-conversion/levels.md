# Levels — Measurements, Sizes and Temperature Conversion

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson: what
each sub lesson holds, in teaching order. The old layer wording
is not used; the sub lessons below are the lesson's own parts,
exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Two systems side by side | Imperial and metric measures set side by side for the three jobs: length (inch/foot/yard/mile against centimetre/metre/kilometre), volume (pint/quart/gallon — UK and US gallons differing — against the litre), weight (ounce/pound/stone against gram/kilogram). From the "Useful Numbers" appendix of Iceland's *Spotlight 9*, printed pp.126–127. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Sizes that travel | Men's and women's clothing and shoe sizes for Iceland, the UK and the USA printed as tables: the same person carries three different numbers, so the table for the country is always read. Same appendix, printed pp.126–127. |
| Sub lesson 3 — Temperature in two scales | Celsius and Fahrenheit and the two conversion formulas (°F = °C × 9/5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9), checked against the freezing and boiling points of water. Same appendix, printed pp.126–127. |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In
this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous
one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's
whole question set as the pool).
