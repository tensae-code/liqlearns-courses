# Video script — Measurements, Sizes and Temperature Conversion

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Two rulers appear side by side — one in inches, one in
centimetres.] "The world measures in TWO systems. Inches
here — centimetres there. Both are right!"

[A shoe appears with three size tags: 42, 8, 9.] "And a shoe?
In one country it is 42. In another, 8. In another, 9. Same
shoe! Read the table for the country."

[A thermometer appears: 0 and 32 at the bottom, 100 and 212
at the top.] "Water freezes: ZERO Celsius — THIRTY-TWO
Fahrenheit. Two scales, one cold!"

[Back to the mascot.] "Two systems. Read the table. Change
the numbers. You can do it!"
