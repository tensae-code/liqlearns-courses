# Hands-on — Measurements, Sizes and Temperature Conversion

## Label hunt

Find labels at home or in class: a shoe, a shirt, a bottle, a
bag of rice. For every number you find, say: what does it
measure (length, volume, weight, size, temperature)? Which
system or country does the number belong to? Sort the labels
into two piles — imperial and metric.

## Weather swap

In pairs: one partner reads out a temperature in Celsius —
"a cold morning, 5 degrees" — and the other changes it to
Fahrenheit with the formula, step by step, out loud. Then
swap, with a Fahrenheit temperature changed back. Check every
answer against the two fixed points: freezing 0°C = 32°F,
boiling 100°C = 212°F.
