## Multiple choice

1. Which list is all imperial? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) metre, litre, kilo  b) inch, pint, pound  c) centimetre, gram, litre
   **Answer: b) inch, pint, pound.**

2. A UK gallon and a US gallon are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) exactly the same  b) different sizes  c) both metric
   **Answer: b) different sizes** — the British gallon holds more.

3. Your shoe is size 42 in Iceland. In the UK it will be… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) also 42, always  b) a different number — read the size table  c) size 0
   **Answer: b)** — each country counts sizes from its own starting point.

4. Water boils at 100°C. In Fahrenheit that is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) 100°F  b) 180°F  c) 212°F
   **Answer: c) 212°F** — 100 × 9/5 = 180, + 32 = 212.

5. 0°C in Fahrenheit is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) 0°F  b) 32°F  c) 50°F
   **Answer: b) 32°F** — the freezing point of water on both scales.
