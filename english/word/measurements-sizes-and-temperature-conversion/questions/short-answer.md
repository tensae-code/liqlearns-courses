## Short answer

1. Name the three jobs the two systems measure side by side in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Length, volume (how much liquid a thing holds), and weight.

2. Why must you read a size table when you buy shoes or clothes from another country? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Because the same person carries a different size number in each country — Iceland, the UK and the USA each count from their own starting point, so a number carried across a border will not fit.

3. Write the formula that changes Celsius into Fahrenheit. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.

4. A day is 25°C. How warm is that in Fahrenheit? Show the steps. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** 25 × 9/5 = 45; 45 + 32 = 77°F.
