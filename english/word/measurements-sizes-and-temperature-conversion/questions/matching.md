## Matching

Match the part to its job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. The inch — b
2. The litre — d
3. The stone — a
4. A shoe size — e
5. Fahrenheit — c

a) An imperial unit of weight
b) An imperial unit of length
c) The temperature scale used in the USA
d) A metric unit of volume
e) A number that changes from country to country

**Answers:** 1–b (imperial length), 2–d (metric volume), 3–a (imperial weight — fourteen pounds), 4–e (sizes travel), 5–c (the imperial temperature scale).
