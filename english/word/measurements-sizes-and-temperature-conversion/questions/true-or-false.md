## True or false

1. The inch, the foot and the mile belong to the metric system. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — they are imperial; the metric length units are the centimetre, metre and kilometre.

2. A UK gallon and a US gallon hold exactly the same amount. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the British gallon holds more; the book prints them as two different measures.

3. The book prints men's and women's clothing and shoe sizes for Iceland, the UK and the USA. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — the "Useful Numbers" appendix sets the three countries' tables side by side.

4. Water freezes at the same number on both temperature scales. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — 0°C but 32°F.

5. To change Fahrenheit back to Celsius, first take away 32. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
