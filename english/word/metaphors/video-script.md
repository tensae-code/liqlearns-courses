# Video script — Metaphors

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot fans itself in a hot, sunny classroom scene.]

"Phew! This classroom is an oven! Is there an oven here? No!"

[A big rock appears; a family leans against it, safe.] "Grandmother is the family's rock. Not stone — strong. Everyone leans on her."

"That is a metaphor. It does not say LIKE. It says IS — and you feel the point."
