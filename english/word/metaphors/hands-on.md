# Hands-on — Metaphors

1. **Spot it.** Read a short text together. Every time a metaphor appears, tap the table: say the two things and the shared point the metaphor carries.

2. **Turn it.** One of you says a simile — "the sea was like a mirror". The other turns it into a metaphor — "the sea was a mirror" — and says which one feels stronger, and why.
