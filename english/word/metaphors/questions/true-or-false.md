## True or false

1. A metaphor uses the words like or as. (Sub lesson 1) **False — those markers belong to the simile; the metaphor states the thing as the other.**

2. "The classroom was an oven" claims there is a real oven in the room. (Sub lesson 1) **False — it carries the shared point, the heat.**

3. Fiji's Year 6 book teaches metaphors in the unit right after similes. (Sub lesson 2) **True — Unit 20 follows Unit 19.**

4. A metaphor is the bolder figure because it commits to the comparison. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
