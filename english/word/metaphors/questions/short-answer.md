## Short answer

1. What is a metaphor? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a figure that names one thing with the name of another because the two share a point — stated as fact, with no like or as.**

2. In "the city never sleeps — the town is a watchman", what is the shared point? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: the town is awake and active all night, like a watchman who never closes his eyes.**

3. How do you tell a metaphor from a simile in a text you are reading? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: look at the wording — like or as on show means simile; the thing stated to BE the other means metaphor.**
