## Matching

Match each saying to its figure. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. The classroom was an oven.
2. The classroom was like an oven.
3. Grandmother is the family's rock.
4. He swam like a fish.
5. The town was a sleeping giant.

a) simile  b) metaphor

**Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-b, 4-a, 5-b.**
