## Multiple choice

1. A metaphor… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) compares with the word like  b) states one thing as another thing  c) lists the parts of a thing
   **Answer: b).**

2. In "the classroom was an oven", the shared point is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cooking  b) the heat  c) the door
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which of these is a metaphor? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) He is as strong as an ox.  b) He is an ox of a man — nothing moves him.  c) He likes oxen.
   **Answer: b).**

4. "The runner was the wind itself" is a metaphor because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) it uses the word wind  b) the runner is stated to BE the other thing, with no like or as  c) it is about running
   **Answer: b).**

5. The wording test between the two figures is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) long or short  b) like/as on show (simile), or the thing stated as the other (metaphor)  c) poem or story
   **Answer: b).**
