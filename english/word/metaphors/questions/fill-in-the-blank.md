## Fill in the blank

1. A ______ names one thing with the name of another, because the two share a point. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: metaphor.**

2. "Her grandmother is the family's ______" — the metaphor's word for strength everyone leans on. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: rock.**

3. A metaphor has no ______ and no as — the comparison is stated as fact. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: like.**

4. "The runner was ______ the wind" would be a simile; "the runner was the wind itself" is a metaphor. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: like.**
