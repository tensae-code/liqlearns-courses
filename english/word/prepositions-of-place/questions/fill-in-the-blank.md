## Fill in the blank

1. The cat is ___ the chair. (It sits on top.) (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: on** — on = touching the top.

2. The fish is ___ the water. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: in** — in = inside.

3. The ball is ___ the table. (The table is over it.) (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: under** — under = below.

4. The kite flies ___ in the sky. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: up** — up = high.

5. The leaves fall ___ to the ground. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: down** — down = low.

6. The garden is ___ the house. (At its back.) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: behind** — behind = at the back.

7. The school is ___ the park. (At its side, close.) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: next to** — next to = at the side, close.

8. Stand ___ me so I can see you. (Before me, facing me.) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: in front of** — in front of = before a thing.
