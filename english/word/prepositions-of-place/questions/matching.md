## Matching

Match the place word to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. on — b
2. in — a
3. under — c
4. up — e
5. down — d
6. in front of — g
7. next to — f
8. behind — h

a) inside
b) touching the top
c) below, with something over it
d) low
e) high
f) at the side, close
g) before a thing, facing it
h) at the back of a thing
