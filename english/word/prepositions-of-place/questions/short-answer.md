## Short answer

1. The book touches the top of the table. Which place word? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: On** — “The book is on the table.”

2. The pen is inside the bag. Which place word? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: In** — “The pen is in the bag.”

3. The dog sleeps below the table. Which place word? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Under** — “The dog is under the table.”

4. Say one sentence with “up”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “The bird is up in the tree.”** (any true up sentence) — up means high.

5. Say one sentence with “down”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “The cat is down on the floor.”** (any true down sentence) — down means low.

6. The teacher stands before the class, facing it. Which place words? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: In front of** — “The teacher is in front of the class.”

7. Your friend sits at your side, close. Which place words? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Next to** — “My friend is next to me.”

8. The sun goes at the back of the mountain and hides. Which place word? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Behind** — “The sun is behind the mountain.”
