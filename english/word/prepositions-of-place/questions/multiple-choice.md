## Multiple choice

1. The book is ___ the table. It touches the top. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) in  b) on  c) under
   **Answer: b) on** — on means touching the top.

2. The cat is ___ the box. The box holds it all around. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) in  b) on  c) up
   **Answer: a) in** — in means inside.

3. The shoes are ___ the bed. The bed is over them. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) on  b) in  c) under
   **Answer: c) under** — under means below, with something over it.

4. The bird is ___ in the tree. It is high. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) down  b) up  c) next to
   **Answer: b) up** — up means high.

5. The ball came ___ to the floor. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) up  b) behind  c) down
   **Answer: c) down** — down means low.

6. The dog is ___ the house — before it, facing it. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) behind  b) in front of  c) under
   **Answer: b) in front of** — in front of means before a thing.

7. The cat is ___ the dog — at its side, close. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) next to  b) in  c) up
   **Answer: a) next to** — next to means at the side, close.

8. The hen is ___ the tree — at its back, hidden by it. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) on  b) in front of  c) behind
   **Answer: c) behind** — behind means at the back.
