## True or false

1. “In” means touching the top of something. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — that is “on”; “in” means inside.

2. “Under” means below, with something over it. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the book is under the chair.

3. “Up” means low. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — up means high; down means low.

4. A bird down on the ground is low. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — down on the ground is low.

5. “Next to” means far away. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — next to means at the side, close.

6. “Behind” means at the back of something. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the hen is behind the tree.

7. “In front of” and “behind” mean the same. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — they are opposites: before it, and at its back.
