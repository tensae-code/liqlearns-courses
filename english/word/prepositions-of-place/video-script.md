# Video script — Prepositions of Place

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A cat on a table.] “The cat is ON the table.” [The cat in a box.] “The cat is IN the box.” [The cat under a chair.] “The cat is UNDER the chair.”

[A bird flies up, then down.] “UP — high! DOWN — low!”

[A dog, a house, a tree.] “The dog is IN FRONT OF the house. The cat is NEXT TO the dog. The hen is BEHIND the tree. Six place words — you can say where everything is!”
