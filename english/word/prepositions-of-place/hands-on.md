# Hands-on — Prepositions of Place

Place words are learned by moving real things. Use a book, a box and a chair.

## Move and say

Put the book ON the table, IN the box, UNDER the chair. Say the full sentence every time: “The book is under the chair.” Then a partner moves it and you say where it went.

## Hide and describe

Hide a toy somewhere in the room. Describe only its place with the fuller set: “It is behind the box, next to the chair.” Your partner finds it from the words alone.
