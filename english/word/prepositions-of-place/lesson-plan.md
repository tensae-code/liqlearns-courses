# Lesson plan — Prepositions of Place

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Prepositions of Place
**Lesson:** prepositions-of-place — saying where a thing is: on, in, under, up, down, in front of, next to, behind
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Word Kinds (with the other preposition lessons)
**The one thing:** The little words that say where a thing is.
**This lesson's own level:** Word lesson: use on, in, under, up, down, in front of, next to and behind to say where things are.

## Why this lesson exists

The prepositions a book teaches split three ways in Bolivia's set: the Grade 3 book teaches on, in, under, up and down with house and pet sentences; the teacher compendium's Year 1 teaches the fuller set — in front of, next to, behind, under, in, on. Neither strand had a lesson of its own: the course's preposition lessons took the Albania strands (in/into; movement). This lesson is built from the Bolivia placements under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06).

## Sub lessons

1. On, in, under
2. Up and down
3. The fuller set — in front of, next to, behind

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. It is a sister of Prepositions In and Into and Prepositions of Movement; a learner may start at any of them — each stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Say where a thing is with on, in and under.
- Use up and down for high and low places.
- Use in front of, next to and behind for the fuller set.

## What you need
A book, a box or bag, and a chair. Real things beat pictures for place words.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: a cat in three panels — ON the table, IN the box, UNDER the chair. The learner says where the cat is in each panel before any word is shown.
2. Teach sub lesson 1 with the real book, box and chair: put the book ON, IN, UNDER and say the sentence each time.
3. ADD GIF (1–2 seconds): a bird flies UP to the roof, then DOWN to the ground. Sub lesson 2 with real stairs or a step if you have them.
4. ADD IMAGE: a row of animals — the dog IN FRONT OF the house, the cat NEXT TO the dog, the hen BEHIND the tree. Sub lesson 3: the learner hides a toy and says where it is with the fuller set.

## Add-ons
- Hide-and-tell: one learner hides a pencil; the other asks and guesses with place words until it is found.
- Teacher's trick: put the book in a silly place (under the chair!) — the learner must say the true sentence, laughing is allowed.

## If the learner is stuck
Keep only on/in/under and play with real things until those three are sure; up/down and the fuller set are small steps after that.

## Teacher note
The Grade 3 book teaches these words inside its house unit (its house description is stocked in The Home); the compendium teaches the fuller set in its Year 1 places spread (its places are stocked in The Town). This lesson teaches the place words themselves, from both sources.
