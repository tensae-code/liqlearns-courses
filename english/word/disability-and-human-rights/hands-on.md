# Hands-on — Disability and Human Rights

1. **Help or hindrance?** Cards name places (a shop with steps only, a school with a ramp, a bus with a low floor). Decide together: can a disabled person use it? Say why, with the lesson's words.

2. **Rights sentences.** In pairs, finish three sentences: "A blind person is allowed to …", "Every citizen has the right to …", "Equality means …" Read them to the class.
