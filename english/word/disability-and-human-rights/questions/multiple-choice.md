## Multiple choice

1. A blind person is someone who… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cannot hear  b) cannot see  c) cannot walk
   **Answer: b).**

2. A guide dog is trained to help… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a blind person move safely  b) a farmer  c) a policeman
   **Answer: a).**

3. Equality means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the same rights for everyone  b) the same job for everyone  c) no rights at all
   **Answer: a).**

4. A citizen is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a visitor for one day  b) a person who belongs to a country, with its rights  c) a city building
   **Answer: b).**
