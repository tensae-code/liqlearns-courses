# Video script — Disability and Human Rights

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A man walks with a guide dog behind the mascot.]

"This man is blind. He cannot see. His dog helps him — a guide dog."

"He goes to work, like you and me. Every citizen has rights. Equality means: the same rights for everyone."

[The mascot nods.] "A disability does not take away a person's rights."
