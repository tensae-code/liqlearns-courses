# Identity and Brand Words

## Do it first

Look at two shirts: one plain, one with a famous name on it.
Many people pay more for the named one. Try to talk about why.
Try to say:

- "The name is part of who they are — part of their ___."
- "Their friends — their ___ — wear the same name."
- "The name has a strong ___ — it pulls people to buy it."

The words you were missing — identity, peers, appeal — are
this lesson: the words of who I am and what I buy.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who I am

Your **identity** is who you are — the things that make you
you. Your **image** is how you look to other people — the
picture of you that they see.

Your **peers** are the people of your own age and group around
you. To **conform** is to do what the group does, to be like
them: "He **conforms** — he wears what his peers wear."

Say it whole: "My clothes are part of my **identity**. My
**peers** care about their **image**. I do not always
**conform**."

*Difficulty: first steps — say each word inside your own life, never alone.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Brands and buying

A brand's **appeal** is the pull that makes people want it:
"The shoe's **appeal** is its name."

**Commerce** is buying and selling — business. **Consumption**
is using things up — buying and using goods: "High
**consumption** means people buy and use a lot."

**Loyalty** is staying with one thing: a loyal buyer buys the
same brand again and again — brand **loyalty**.
**Perceptions** are the ways people see and understand things:
"People's **perceptions** of the brand changed."
**Psychology** is the study of the mind — how people think and
feel: "Adverts use **psychology** — they work on your mind."

Write three sentences: one about a brand's appeal, one about
your own loyalty (or no loyalty) to a brand, one about how
adverts use psychology.

*Difficulty: building — the buying words join the self words in one whole talk.*

## Say it to your companion

Explain to your companion why a famous name can cost more
than a plain one, using identity, peers, image and appeal.
Your companion then says where they conform — and where they
do not.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write four sentences about yourself with identity, image,
   peers and conform.
2. Choose one brand. Write what its appeal is, who buys it,
   and whether you are loyal to it.
3. Write two sentences about consumption in your town: what
   people buy and use a lot of.
