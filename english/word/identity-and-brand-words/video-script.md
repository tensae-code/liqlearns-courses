# Video script — Identity and Brand Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Two shirts appear — one plain, one with a big name.] "Same
shirt! But this name has APPEAL!"

[The mascot points at himself.] "My clothes are part of my
IDENTITY — who I am!"

[A row of the same shoes appears.] "LOYALTY! They buy the same
brand again and again!"
