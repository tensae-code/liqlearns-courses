# Lesson plan — Identity and Brand Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Identity and Brand Words
**Lesson:** identity-and-brand-words — who I am: identity, image,
peers, conform — and the words of brands and buying: appeal,
commerce, consumption, loyalty, perceptions, psychology
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World
**The one thing:** the word set of identity and brands, learned
whole, the way the book's unit presents it.
**This lesson's own level:** Word lesson: the self words first,
then the brand words.

## Why this lesson exists

No lesson housed this word set. Palestine's Grade 12 book's
"Who am I?" unit teaches identity and brand words — appeal,
commerce, conform, consumption, identity, image, loyalty,
peers, perceptions, psychology (Unit 10, printed pp.102–111).
The 2026-11-08 re-verification sweep read the book for the
first time and marked this part NEW LESSON NEEDED. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), a teaching item with no
lesson gets a new lesson built; this is that lesson. Its only
source is the Palestine book named in lesson/sources.md.

## Sub lessons

1. Who I am — identity, image, peers, conform
2. Brands and buying — appeal, commerce, consumption, loyalty, perceptions, psychology

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is forced first — this lesson stands alone: it starts
from "who am I, and why do I buy what I buy?" and teaches every
word it uses.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Talk about the self: identity, image, peers, conform.
- Talk about brands and buying: appeal, commerce, consumption,
  loyalty, perceptions, psychology.

## What you need
Two or three real brand names and adverts to talk about.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — talk about why people wear one brand
   before anyone teaches the words (lesson/lesson.md).
2. ADD IMAGE: two shirts, one with a famous name, one plain;
   a group of friends dressed the same way.
3. Work through sub lesson 1: the self words.
4. Work through sub lesson 2: the brand words.
5. ADD VIDEO (short): an advert — every time a brand word is
   used, the word grows big on screen: LOYALTY… APPEAL…

## Add-ons
- Brand hunt: name five brands your class uses and say what
  their appeal is.
- Class talk: "Do peers change your image?" — three sentences
  each.

## If the learner is stuck
Cut back to two words — identity and loyalty — used about the
learner's own life until they are sure. Add the rest one at a
time.

## Teacher note
The book teaches this set inside one unit about identity and
brands, and this lesson keeps that shape: the self words and
the buying words are two halves of one question — who am I,
and what do I choose? Only the words the book's placements
name are taught here.
