## Multiple choice

1. Your identity is ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) your best friend  b) who you are  c) your school bag
   **Answer: b) who you are** — the things that make you you.

2. Your peers are ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) your teachers  b) people of your own age and group  c) your parents
   **Answer: b) people of your own age and group.**

3. To conform is to ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) do what the group does  b) work alone  c) pay more
   **Answer: a) do what the group does.**

4. A brand's appeal is ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) its price tag  b) the pull that makes people want it  c) its colour only
   **Answer: b) the pull that makes people want it.**

5. Brand loyalty means ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) buying a new brand every day  b) never buying anything  c) staying with one brand, buying it again and again
   **Answer: c) staying with one brand.**

6. Psychology is the study of ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the mind  b) old buildings  c) the sea
   **Answer: a) the mind** — how people think and feel.
