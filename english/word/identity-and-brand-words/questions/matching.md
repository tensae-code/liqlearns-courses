## Matching

Match the word to what it means. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. identity — c
2. image — a
3. conform — f
4. commerce — b
5. consumption — d
6. perceptions — e

a) how you look to other people
b) buying and selling; business
c) who you are
d) buying and using goods
e) the ways people see and understand things
f) to do what the group does

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (identity = who you are), 2–a (your image is the picture others see), 3–f (conform = follow the group), 4–b (commerce is business), 5–d (consumption is buying and using up), 6–e (perceptions are ways of seeing).
