## Short answer

1. What is your image — how do other people see you? Write one sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** any true sentence about how others see the learner.
2. What does consumption mean? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Buying and using goods — using things up.
3. Write one sentence about commerce in your town. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** any true sentence about buying and selling, e.g. "The market is the centre of commerce in our town."
4. Do your peers change what you wear? Answer with conform in a whole sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** any true sentence using conform/conforms, e.g. "I conform — I wear what my peers wear" or "I do not conform."
