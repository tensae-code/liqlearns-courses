# Hands-on — Identity and Brand Words

These words are learned by talking about real brands and real
choices. Do these out loud, with a partner.

## The two shirts

Show two things — one with a famous name, one plain. Take
turns: say what the named one's appeal is, and whether your
peers would choose it. Use identity, image and peers.

## Loyal or not?

Name a brand you buy again and again — or say you have no
loyalty. Your partner asks why, and you answer with loyalty,
consumption or psychology in a whole sentence.
