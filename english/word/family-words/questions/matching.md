# Family words — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Your Own Row and Your Parents ·
(Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Whole Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Wide Tree.

Match each person to their place in the family.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — Palestine's set and their rows.
1. Mum — **your mother**
2. Dad — **your father**
3. brother — **your row, a boy**
4. sister — **your row, a girl**
5. baby — **the smallest**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — the wide tree.
1. grandfather — **your father's or mother's father**
2. grandmother — **your father's or mother's mother**
3. uncle — **your parent's brother**
4. aunt — **your parent's sister**
5. cousin — **your uncle's or aunt's child**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)** — one row down and married in.
1. nephew — **your brother's or sister's son**
2. niece — **your brother's or sister's daughter**
3. mother-in-law — **your husband's or wife's mother**
