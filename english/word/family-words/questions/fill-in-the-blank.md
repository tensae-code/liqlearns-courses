# Family words — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Your Own Row and Your Parents ·
(Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Whole Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Wide Tree.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** grandfather · grandmother · uncle · aunt ·
cousin · niece

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This is my ______, Bill.
**Answer: grandfather** (North Macedonia's model, p.4).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** My father's sister is my ______.
**Answer: aunt.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** My mother's brother is my ______.
**Answer: uncle.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** My aunt's son is my ______.
**Answer: cousin.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** My brother's daughter is my ______.
**Answer: niece** (Mongolia, English IX, p.8).
