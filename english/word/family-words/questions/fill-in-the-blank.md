# Family words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** grandfather · grandmother · uncle · aunt ·
cousin · niece

**1. (Level 1)** This is my ______, Bill.
**Answer: grandfather** (North Macedonia's model, p.4).

**2. (Level 2)** My father's sister is my ______.
**Answer: aunt.**

**3. (Level 2)** My mother's brother is my ______.
**Answer: uncle.**

**4. (Level 2)** My aunt's son is my ______.
**Answer: cousin.**

**5. (Level 3)** My brother's daughter is my ______.
**Answer: niece** (Mongolia, English IX, p.8).
