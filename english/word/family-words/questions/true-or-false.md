# Family words — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Your Own Row and Your Parents ·
(Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Whole Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Wide Tree.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Palestine's book, "Who's this?" can be
answered "This is my mum."
**True** — the book's own pattern (pp.42–44).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cousin is in your parents' row of the
tree.
**False** — a cousin stands in YOUR row; your parents'
row holds uncle and aunt.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** North Macedonia's book teaches only the
formal words father and mother.
**False** — it teaches mummy and daddy beside them, in
the song, the captions and the checkpoint (pp.5, 8, 11).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A nephew is your brother's or sister's
son.
**True** — Rwanda's crossword and Mongolia's sets both
place him there.
