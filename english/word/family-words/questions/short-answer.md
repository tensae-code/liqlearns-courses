# Family words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Your Own Row and Your Parents ·
(Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Whole Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Wide Tree.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Palestine's family set.
**Answer:** Mum, Dad, sister, brother, baby, me
(Grade 1 Part 1, Unit 7, p.40).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name North Macedonia's six photograph
words.
**Answer:** father, grandfather, mother, sister,
grandmother, brother (printed p.4).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the family's short names for
father and mother in North Macedonia's song?
**Answer:** daddy and mummy (printed pp.5, 8, 11).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Your uncle's children are your…?
**Answer:** cousins — your own row, another house
(Rwanda, Pupil's Book 6, pp.64–65).
