# Family words — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Your Own Row and Your Parents ·
(Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Whole Tree · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Wide Tree.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Who's this?" — "This is my ______."
(Palestine's pattern)
a) brother  b) table  c) baby-car
**Answer: a) brother** — "This is Tala / my brother / my
mum / the baby" (Palestine, pp.42–44).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Your mother's mother is your ______.
a) aunt  b) grandmother  c) sister
**Answer: b) grandmother** — the top row of the tree.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** North Macedonia's model says: "This is
my ______, Bill."
a) grandfather  b) cousin  c) nephew
**Answer: a) grandfather** — the book's own model frame
(printed p.4).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Your father's brother is your ______.
a) nephew  b) uncle  c) cousin
**Answer: b) uncle** — the parents' row, beside your
father (Rwanda's tree, pp.64–65).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Your sister's daughter is your ______.
a) niece  b) aunt  c) mother-in-law
**Answer: a) niece** — one row down from your sister
(Mongolia's relatives sentences, English IX, p.8).
