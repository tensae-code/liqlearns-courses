# Video script — Family words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is my family!

This is my **grandfather**. This is my **grandmother**.
This is my **father** — my daddy!
This is my **mother** — my mummy!
This is my **brother**. This is my **sister**.
And this is the baby. And this is ME!

Who's this? This is my uncle.
Who's this? This is my aunt.
And my **cousin** — we play together!

A big tree. Grandfather and grandmother at the top.
Me at the bottom. Everyone has a name.

Now you. Show me YOUR family.
Who's this? Who's this?
Say the names — in English.
