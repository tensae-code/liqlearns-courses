# Hands-on — Family words

The lesson in your real family, not only on the page.

## Draw your tree
Draw your family tree on the biggest paper you have:
grandparents at the top, you near the bottom. Write each
person's kinship word under their picture — grandfather,
aunt, cousin — not only their name.

## The photograph round
Take a family photograph (or your drawing). Point at each
person and introduce them aloud: "This is my grandmother."
Go round the whole photograph twice; the second time,
faster.

## Who is missing?
With a partner: show your tree with one label covered.
Your partner asks "Who's this?" and guesses the covered
word from its place on the tree. Five covers, then swap.

## The in-law hunt
If your family has them, find your uncle by marriage,
your aunt by marriage, a cousin's child. Say their words:
uncle, aunt, nephew, niece. If your family is small today,
use a friend's big family story instead.
