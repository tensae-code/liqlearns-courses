## Short answer

1. What do you do with a book in a library? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: you borrow it, read it, and bring it back.**

2. Write one line paying tribute to libraries. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any respectful line, e.g. "I pay tribute to libraries: they enlighten everyone who reads."**

3. How do libraries carry civilisation? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: they keep a people's learning and stories and pass them to the next generation.**
