## Fill in the blank

1. A place that holds books for everyone is a ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: library.**

2. I ______ a book from the library and bring it back next week. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: borrow.**

3. Books ______ their readers — they give light to the mind. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: enlighten.**

4. We pay ______ to libraries for what they give us. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: tribute.**
