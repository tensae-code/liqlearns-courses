## Multiple choice

1. In a library, you usually… a book. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) buy  b) borrow  c) burn
   **Answer: b).**

2. Literacy means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) being able to read and write  b) owning many books  c) a kind of library
   **Answer: a).**

3. "Enlighten" means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) to make a room brighter with lamps only  b) to give light to the mind  c) to close a book
   **Answer: b).**

4. To pay tribute is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) pay money  b) say with respect what something has given  c) borrow a book
   **Answer: b).**
