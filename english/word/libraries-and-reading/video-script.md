# Video script — Libraries and Reading

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Shelves of books appear behind the mascot.]

"So many books! This is a library. You do not buy the books here — you borrow them, read them, bring them back."

"Reading makes people strong. A country that reads, grows."

[The mascot takes a book.] "Come — pick one."
