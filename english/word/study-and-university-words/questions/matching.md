## Matching

Match the word to what it means. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. foundation course — b
2. field — d
3. optional — a
4. participate — e
5. like-minded — c
6. routine — f

a) you may choose it or not
b) a course that gets you ready
c) thinking the same way as you
d) your chosen part of study
e) to take part
f) the daily order of things you do

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–b (the ready-making course), 2–d (your field is your chosen part), 3–a (optional = your choice), 4–e (participate = take part), 5–c (like-minded = same way of thinking), 6–f (a routine is the daily order).
