## True or false

1. A foundation course comes after the main study ends. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — it comes before, to get you ready.
2. Your field is the part of study you choose. (Sub lesson 1)
   **True.**
3. An optional subject is one you must take. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — you may choose it or not choose it.
4. To participate means to watch and say nothing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **False** — it means to take part.
5. A routine is the order of things you do every day. (Sub lesson 2)
   **True.**
