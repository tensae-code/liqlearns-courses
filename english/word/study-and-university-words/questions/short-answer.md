## Short answer

1. What is a foundation course? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A course that gets you ready for university study, taken before the main study.
2. Write one sentence with optional about a subject at your school. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** any true sentence, e.g. "Art is optional at my school."
3. What does like-minded mean? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Thinking in the same way as you; liking the same things.
4. Write two lines of a student's routine. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** any two daily steps in order, e.g. "She gets up at six. She studies before class."
