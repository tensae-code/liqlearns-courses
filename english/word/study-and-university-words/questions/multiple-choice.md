## Multiple choice

1. A course that gets you ready for university study is a ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) routine  b) foundation course  c) field
   **Answer: b) foundation course** — it comes before the main study.

2. "Her ___ is medicine" — the part of study she chose. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) field  b) routine  c) optional
   **Answer: a) field.**

3. An optional subject is one you ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) must take  b) may choose or not choose  c) take every day
   **Answer: b) may choose or not choose.**

4. To participate is to ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) take part  b) sleep  c) pay money
   **Answer: a) take part.**

5. Like-minded people are people who ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) live in one house  b) think in the same way as you  c) are the same age
   **Answer: b) think in the same way as you.**

6. A routine is ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) one hard exam  b) the things you do every day, in the same order  c) a new friend
   **Answer: b) the things you do every day, in the same order.**
