# Lesson plan — Study and University Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Study and University Words
**Lesson:** study-and-university-words — the words of starting
university: foundation course, field, optional — and of student
life: participate, like-minded, routine
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World
**The one thing:** the word set of university life, learned
whole, the way the book's opening unit presents it.
**This lesson's own level:** Word lesson: the starting words
first, then the student-life words.

## Why this lesson exists

No lesson housed the university word set. Palestine's Grade 12
book opens with "A new start" — university life — and teaches
the words foundation course, participate, like-minded, optional,
routine and field inside it (Unit 1, printed pp.4–13). The
2026-11-08 re-verification sweep read the book for the first
time and marked this part NEW LESSON NEEDED. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), a teaching item with no
lesson gets a new lesson built; this is that lesson. Its only
source is the Palestine book named in lesson/sources.md.

## Sub lessons

1. Starting at university — foundation course, field, optional
2. Student life — participate, like-minded, routine

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is forced first — this lesson stands alone: it starts
from "a student starts at university" and teaches every word
it uses.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Name the start of university study: a foundation course, a
  field, an optional subject.
- Talk about student life: participate, like-minded people, a
  daily routine.

## What you need
A picture of a university, or a student you know to talk about.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — describe a student's first day before
   anyone teaches the words (lesson/lesson.md).
2. ADD IMAGE: a university gate, a class list with one subject
   marked "you may choose it", students talking in a group.
3. Work through sub lesson 1: the starting words.
4. Work through sub lesson 2: the student-life words.
5. ADD VIDEO (short): a student's day from morning to night —
   the words grow big as they land: FOUNDATION COURSE…
   ROUTINE…

## Add-ons
- Interview a real student: ask about their field, their
  routine, and one optional subject.
- Write your own dream start: "My field is… My routine is…"

## If the learner is stuck
Cut back to two words — field and routine — used about the
learner's own school life until they are sure. Add the rest one
at a time.

## Teacher note
The book teaches this set inside its opening unit's university
reading, and this lesson keeps that shape: the words are met
inside student life, never as a bare list. Only the words the
book's placements name are taught here.
