# Hands-on — Study and University Words

University words are learned by using them about a real
student. Do these out loud, with a partner.

## The new student

One of you is a new student. The other asks: "What is your
field?" "Do you take a foundation course?" "Which subject is
optional for you?" Answer in whole sentences.

## My routine

Tell your partner your routine for tomorrow, step by step.
Your partner tells theirs. Say one way your routines are the
same — you are like-minded about that part of the day.
