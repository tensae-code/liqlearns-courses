# Matching — Measurements

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the unit to what it measures.
   1. metre
   2. kilo
   3. litre
   a. liquids
   b. length
   c. weight
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the question to its answer.
   1. How tall is he?
   2. How heavy is the bag?
   3. How far is the town?
   a. Five kilos.
   b. Three kilometres.
   c. One metre seventy.
   **Answer: 1-c, 2-a, 3-b** (Sub lesson 2)
