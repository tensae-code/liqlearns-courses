# Multiple choice — Measurements

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. How many centimetres are in a metre?
   A) 10
   B) 100
   C) 1,000
   D) 12
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Which unit measures a bottle of water?
   A) kilo
   B) litre
   C) metre
   D) kilometre
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. A bag of potatoes weighing five units is measured in:
   A) litres
   B) centimetres
   C) kilos
   D) metres
   **Answer: C** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Choose the correct answer sentence.
   A) It is two metres long.
   B) It is two litres long.
   C) It is two kilos tall.
   D) It long two metres is.
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 2)

5. "How far is the town?" asks about:
   A) weight
   B) distance
   C) price
   D) time
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. "The bag weighs five kilos" — "weighs" tells us:
   A) the colour
   B) the weight
   C) the length
   D) the owner
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
