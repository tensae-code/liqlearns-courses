# Short answer — Measurements

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Which is bigger: a centimetre or a kilometre?
   **Answer:** A kilometre — it is 1,000 metres; a centimetre is a hundredth of a metre. (Sub lesson 1)

2. Name the unit: "a door is about two ___ high".
   **Answer:** metres. (Sub lesson 1)

3. Ask about the weight of a bag, in a full question.
   **Answer:** How heavy is the bag? (Sub lesson 2)

4. Answer in a full sentence: your book is 25 cm long.
   **Answer:** My book is twenty-five centimetres long. (Sub lesson 2)
