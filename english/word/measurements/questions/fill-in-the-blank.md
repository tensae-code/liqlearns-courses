# Fill in the blank — Measurements

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. There are 1,000 grams in a _____. (kilo / litre)
   **Answer: kilo** (Sub lesson 1)

2. A pen is about fifteen _____ long. (centimetres / kilometres)
   **Answer: centimetres** (Sub lesson 1)

3. _____ long is your book? (How / What colour)
   **Answer: How** (Sub lesson 2)

4. The town is three _____ away. (kilometres / litres)
   **Answer: kilometres** (Sub lesson 2)
