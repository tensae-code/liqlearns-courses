# Video Script — Life Stages

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each stage appears on screen as it is said.

[Picture: a mother-to-be smiling, a clinic sign behind her]

She is pregnant. The baby is coming soon. She goes to the antenatal clinic for a checkup.

[Picture: a newborn baby wrapped in a blanket]

The baby is born! A newborn! He drinks milk. He wears a nappy. He sleeps in his pram.

[Picture: a toddler taking first steps; a schoolchild with a bag; a teenager with friends]

Now a toddler — walk, walk! Now a schoolchild. Now a teenager. Growing up, up, up!

[Picture: a young graduate in a gown throwing a cap; a wedding pair; an old man smiling in a garden]

A graduate! Then work. Then marriage — a family. And one day… retire. A pensioner, resting in the garden.

[Picture: flowers laid gently by a grave]

And at the end of a long life, we pass away. We say goodbye at the funeral. We bring flowers — a wreath. We remember.
