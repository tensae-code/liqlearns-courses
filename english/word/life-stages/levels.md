# Levels — Life Stages

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Birth and babies | Use the words for the start of a life: pregnant/pregnancy, antenatal clinic, going into labour, giving birth, newborn, breast fed / bottle fed, nappy, birth certificate, baptism/christening, pram, pushchair, adopting a child. |
| 2. Growing up and growing old | Name every stage from infant to pensioner in order, with the reader's ages, and use the grown-up life words: graduate, work/job, marriage/married/partner, family/parent, housewife/househusband, retire/pensioner. |
| 3. Death and dying | Use the words for the end of a life: die/pass away/dead/death, the deceased, undertaker, coffin, funeral/burial, grave/buried, mausoleum, wreath, will, executor, heir(s), inheritance, orphan. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons can talk about a whole life in English, from the antenatal clinic to the grave.
