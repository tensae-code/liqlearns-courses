# Lesson Plan — Life Stages

**One thing:** the words for the stages of a human life — being born and being a baby, growing up and growing old, and dying and death.

## Sub lessons

1. Birth and babies — pregnant/pregnancy, antenatal clinic and checkups, going into labour, giving birth, newborn, breast fed / bottle fed, nappy, birth certificate and registering a birth, baptism/christening, pram, pushchair, adopting a child
2. Growing up and growing old — baby/infant, toddler, preschooler/child/kid, schoolchild/schoolgirl/schoolboy, preteen, teenager/teen, adult, then graduate, work/job, marriage/married/partner, family/parent, housewife/househusband, retire/pensioner
3. Death and dying — die/pass away/dead/death, the deceased, undertaker, coffin, funeral/burial, grave/buried, mausoleum, wreath, will, executor, heir(s), inheritance, orphan

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- **Birth and babies:** before a baby is born, the mother is **pregnant** (the time is her **pregnancy**); she goes to the **antenatal** (prenatal) clinic for checkups. When the baby is coming, she is **going into labour**; then she **gives birth**. The new baby is a **newborn**; it is **breast fed** or **bottle fed** and wears a **nappy**. Its birth is registered and it gets a **birth certificate**. A **baptism/christening** names and blesses the child in church. The baby rides in a **pram** or a **pushchair**. A family can also **adopt a child** — take a child into their family as their own.
- **The stages of growing up:** **baby/infant** (1–12 months) → **toddler** (1–3) → **preschooler / child / kid** (3–5) → **schoolchild / schoolgirl / schoolboy** (5–10) → **preteen** (11–12) → **teenager / teen** (13–19) → **adult** — a man or woman from 18.
- **Grown-up life:** a person may **graduate** (a **graduate** has finished a degree; a **postgraduate** studies further), go to **work** and have a **job**; **marriage** — two people are **married**, each is the other's **partner**; they may have a **family** and become a **parent**. A **housewife** or **househusband** runs the home. At 65 a worker may **retire** and become a **pensioner**.
- **Death and dying:** **die** is the verb, **dead** the adjective, **death** the noun; **pass away** is the gentle way to say die. The dead person is **the deceased**. The **undertaker** arranges the **funeral** and the **burial**; the body lies in a **coffin** and is **buried** in a **grave**; a **mausoleum** is a building for the dead; people bring a **wreath** of flowers. Before death a person writes a **will**; the **executor** carries it out, and the **heir(s)** receive the **inheritance**. A child whose parents have died is an **orphan**.
