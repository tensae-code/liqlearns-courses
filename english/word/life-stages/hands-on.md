# Hands-On — Life Stages

Things you do with your family, your pictures and your voice. No special materials needed.

1. **The ladder line.** Stand in a line with friends or family, youngest to oldest. Each person says their stage word out loud: "I am a schoolchild." "I am an adult." "I am a pensioner."
2. **Photo walk.** Find family photos (or draw them) for as many stages as you can: a newborn, a toddler, a schoolchild, a teenager, a bride or groom, a grandparent. Name each stage and one more word from its sub lesson.
3. **Baby words hunt.** Look at a real baby's things — or pictures of them — and name them: nappy, pram, pushchair, bottle. Say one sentence for each.
4. **My life line.** Draw a line and mark it: born — toddler — schoolchild — teenager — adult — retire. Write where you are now, and one hope for a later stage.
5. **A kind paragraph.** Write four kind sentences about someone old who has passed away, using pass away, funeral, wreath and one word from the will family (will, executor, heir, inheritance).
