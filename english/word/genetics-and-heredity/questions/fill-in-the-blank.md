## Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word from the box. The box holds one word for each gap.

**Box:** heredity, transmission, population, variants

1. ______ is traits passing from parents to children. **Answer:** heredity (Sub lesson 1)
2. ______ genetics follows traits from parent to child. **Answer:** transmission (Sub lesson 1)
3. ______ genetics watches traits across whole groups of people. **Answer:** population (Sub lesson 1)
4. Reading the virus's instructions let scientists spot new ______. **Answer:** variants (Sub lesson 2)
