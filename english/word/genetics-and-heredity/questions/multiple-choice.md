## Multiple choice

1. **What does genetics study?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) How traits are passed on and how they vary
   - b) The weather
   - c) How to cook
   - **Answer: a) How traits are passed on and how they vary**

2. **Which branch follows traits from parent to child?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Population genetics
   - b) Transmission genetics
   - c) Behaviour genetics
   - **Answer: b) Transmission genetics**

3. **Which branch studies the instructions inside the cell?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Molecular genetics
   - b) Quantitative genetics
   - c) Transmission genetics
   - **Answer: a) Molecular genetics**

4. **Population genetics watches traits across…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) One family
   - b) Whole populations
   - c) One cell
   - **Answer: b) Whole populations**

5. **How did genetic science help with Covid-19?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) It read and tracked the virus's instructions, spotting variants
   - b) It closed the schools
   - c) It named the virus
   - **Answer: a) It read and tracked the virus's instructions, spotting variants**
