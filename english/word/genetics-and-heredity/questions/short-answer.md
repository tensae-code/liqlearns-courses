## Short answer

1. **What is heredity?** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** The passing of traits from parents to children.
2. **Name three branches of genetics from the book.** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Any three of: transmission, molecular, population, psychological, quantitative, behaviour.
