This lesson is built at one level of its own: it names a field's branches first, then argues the open questions the field leaves. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts).
