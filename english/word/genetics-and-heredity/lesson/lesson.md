## Sub lesson 1 — What genetics studies
**Genetics** is the science that studies how living things pass their traits on — heredity — and how those traits vary. Uruguay's Grade 12 book opens the field by its branches. **Transmission genetics** follows traits from parent to child. **Molecular genetics** studies the instructions themselves, in the cell. **Population genetics** watches traits across whole populations. And the field reaches further: **psychological genetics**, **quantitative genetics** and **behaviour genetics** ask how inheritance touches mind, measure and behaviour.

The class researches the branches online, brainstorms sideways to widen the picture, matches pictures to the branch each one belongs to, and presents its findings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Genetics questions everyone asks
The book ends the topic with the questions the whole room wants answered: **What should everyone know about genetics?** — and **what does genetics have to do with Covid-19?** A virus's instructions, read and tracked by genetic science, are how new variants were spotted and followed around the world; the class argues its answers with the branch research in hand.

[Picture 0003]
*Difficulty: Open questions, argued from the research — there is no single right answer to memorise.*
