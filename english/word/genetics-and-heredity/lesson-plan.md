# Lesson plan — Genetics and Heredity

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Genetics and Heredity
**Lesson:** genetics-and-heredity — the branches of genetics — what each one studies, from how traits pass down to genetics and Covid-19
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** name the branches of genetics, say what each one studies, and argue what everyone should know about genetics.

**This lesson's own level:** built from two sub lessons that open in order.

## Why this lesson exists
Uruguay's Grade 12 English book teaches genetics inside its Being Human unit — the branches researched, pictured and presented, ending in open discussion questions. The course held body and health words but nothing on genetics itself — so this lesson is built, from Uruguay's book alone. It teaches the names and questions of the field; the science itself belongs to the science course.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sub lesson 1 — What genetics studies.** Uruguay's Grade 12 book maps the fields and branches of genetics — transmission, molecular, population, and the branches that meet psychology, quantity and behaviour — and matches pictures to each branch.
2. **Sub lesson 2 — Genetics questions everyone asks.** The class presents its research and debates the questions the book ends on: what everyone should know about genetics, and what genetics had to do with Covid-19.

Sub lesson 2 opens only when the prove-it check on sub lesson 1 is passed.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed first. Health words sits nearby for the body words — a soft hint, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
Name the book's branches of genetics, match each branch to the questions it asks, present one branch to the class, and argue what everyone should know about genetics.

## What you need
Nothing to bring: the learner's own family likenesses are the opening example of heredity.
