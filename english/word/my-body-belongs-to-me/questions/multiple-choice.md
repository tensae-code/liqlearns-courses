## Multiple choice

1. Your body belongs to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) your teacher  b) you  c) your friends
   **Answer: b).**

2. If you do not want a hug, you may… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) say no  b) say nothing and cry  c) run away forever
   **Answer: a).**

3. Your private parts are the parts covered by… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) your hat  b) your shoes  c) your underwear
   **Answer: c).**

4. If someone touches your private parts, you must… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) keep it a secret  b) tell a trusted grown-up  c) forget it
   **Answer: b).**

5. If the first grown-up does not listen, you should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) stop telling  b) keep telling until someone listens  c) tell nobody ever
   **Answer: b).**
