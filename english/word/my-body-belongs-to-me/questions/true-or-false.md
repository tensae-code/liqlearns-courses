## True or false

1. A child may say no to a touch they do not want. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. A secret about touching private parts must always be kept. (Sub lesson 2) **False — that secret must be told.**

3. It is the child's fault if someone touches their private parts. (Sub lesson 2) **False — it is never the child's fault.**

4. Grown-ups must listen when a child says no about their own body. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**
