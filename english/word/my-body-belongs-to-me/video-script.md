# Video script — My Body Belongs to Me

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot puts a hand on his own shoulder.]

"This body is mine. Say it with me: my body belongs to me."

[He holds up a hand, palm out.] "If I do not want a touch, I can say no. You can say no too."

"The parts under your underwear are private. Nobody may touch them."

"If anybody tries — tell a grown-up you trust. Tell, and keep telling, until someone listens. Telling is always right."
