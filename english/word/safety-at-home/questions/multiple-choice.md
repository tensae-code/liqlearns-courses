## Multiple choice

1. Which of these is a danger at home? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a chair  b) a candle burning by a curtain  c) a book
   **Answer: b).**

2. A cleaning bottle is dangerous because… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) it is poison  b) it is heavy  c) it is blue
   **Answer: a).**

3. Spilled water on the floor is dangerous because… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) it is wet  b) someone can slip and fall  c) it is cold
   **Answer: b).**

4. When you find something dangerous, you should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) touch it to check  b) hide it  c) not touch it and call a grown-up
   **Answer: c).**

5. "It is dangerous because it can cut" explains a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) knife  b) pillow  c) cup of water
   **Answer: a).**
