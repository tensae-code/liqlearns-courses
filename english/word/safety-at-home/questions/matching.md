## Matching

Match the danger to why it is dangerous. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. a boiling pot
2. a knife
3. a cleaning bottle
4. spilled water
5. a candle

a) it can cut you
b) it can burn you
c) you can slip and fall
d) it is poison
e) it can start a fire

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–a, 3–d, 4–c, 5–e.
