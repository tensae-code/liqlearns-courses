## Short answer

1. Name three dangers you might find in a kitchen. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any three — e.g. a burning stove, a knife, a boiling pot.**

2. A bottle of cleaning liquid stands next to the juice. Why is that dangerous? Say it in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: it is dangerous because a child could drink it thinking it is juice, and it is poison.**
