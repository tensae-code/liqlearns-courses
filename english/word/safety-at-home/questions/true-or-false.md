## True or false

1. Matches are safe toys for children. (Sub lesson 1) **False — matches are a fire danger.**

2. Medicines can look like sweets but are poison to a child who eats the wrong one. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. You should touch a dangerous thing to move it away quickly. (Sub lesson 2) **False — do not touch it; call a grown-up.**

4. Saying why something is dangerous can warn someone before they get hurt. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
