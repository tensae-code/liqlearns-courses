# Video script — Staying Safe at Home

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A kitchen picture appears behind the mascot.]

"Look at this kitchen. Can you see the danger?"

[A candle near a curtain glows.] "A candle by the curtain. Fire can spread. That is danger."

[A knife on a low table glows.] "And a knife where small hands can reach it. Sharp things can cut."

"When you see danger at home, do not touch it. Call a grown-up. That keeps everyone safe."
