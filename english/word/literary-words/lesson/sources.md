**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Egypt, Ghana, Jordan, Sierra Leone, Bangladesh).** Egypt names the five kinds in a full literature unit and studies a bound-in novel by chapters. Ghana's JHS curriculum analyses literature by plot, setting and characters and stocks the device set (curriculum evidence, worded as such). Sierra Leone teaches the simile/metaphor test outright and adds irony, euphemism and repetition. Jordan stocks film genres and a set poem about loving words themselves. Bangladesh names the literary kinds (satire, tragedy, comedy) off its senior book's opening folk tale and reads literature by theme — dreams, peace — in the same book (added 2026-11-08 by the re-verification sweep).
