# Video script — Literary words

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Stories and poems have their own words. Learn them!

Five kinds! Novels. Poetry. Drama. Folktales. Essays.

Inside the kinds — genres! A thriller. A comedy. A mystery.

Now the tricks of the writers.

She is LIKE a rose. That is a simile. Look — like, as!

She IS a rose. That is a metaphor. No like. No as.

The river smiles. Rivers cannot smile! That is personification.

Plot — what happens. Setting — where and when. Character — who. Theme — what it is really about.

Now you can talk about books like a reader.
