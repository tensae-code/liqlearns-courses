# Hands-on — Literary words

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Shelf game.** Name ten works you know — stories, poems, plays, films. Sort each by kind and genre, and defend the hard ones aloud.
2. **Figure hunt.** Take one poem or story page. Hunt one simile, one metaphor, one personification; write each with its name and what it makes you see.
3. **Pretty words.** Jordan's game: collect your five prettiest English words — words you love for their sound — and say why each pleases you, as the poem 'Pretty Words' does.
4. **Chapter questions.** Write three chapter questions about a book you know, Egypt-style: one on plot, one on a character, one on the theme.
