# Literary words — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Egypt's five kinds of literature. **Answer: novels, poetry, drama, folktales, essays** — Unit 11, Lesson 1 takes them one by one

**2. (Level 2)** 'Her voice was honey.' Simile or metaphor — and how do you know? **Answer: metaphor — there is no 'like' or 'as'; the thing simply IS the other thing** — Sierra Leone L25's own test

**3. (Level 3)** Jordan's list names the film genre of a life-story film. What is it called? **Answer: a biopic** — from the G12 film-genres speaking set (S2, U7, p.23)
