# Literary words — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Kinds of Literature · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Figures of Speech · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Talking About Books.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of Egypt's five kinds of literature? a) novels b) drama c) dictionaries d) essays **Answer: c) dictionaries** — the five are novels, poetry, drama, folktales, essays (Egypt Sec 11 T1, U11 L1)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Her voice was like honey" is — a) a metaphor b) a simile c) personification **Answer: b) a simile** — the flag-word 'like' is flying (Sierra Leone L25's test)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The moon watched us home" is — a) a simile b) a metaphor c) personification **Answer: c) personification** — watching is human life given to a thing

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The theme of a story is — a) its longest chapter b) what it is really about, underneath the events c) its setting **Answer: b) what it is really about, underneath the events** — plot is what happens; theme is what it means (Ghana's analysis set, curriculum evidence)
