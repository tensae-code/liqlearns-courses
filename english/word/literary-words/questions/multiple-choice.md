# Literary words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which is NOT one of Egypt's five kinds of literature? a) novels b) drama c) dictionaries d) essays **Answer: c) dictionaries** — the five are novels, poetry, drama, folktales, essays (Egypt Sec 11 T1, U11 L1)

**2. (Level 1)** "Her voice was like honey" is — a) a metaphor b) a simile c) personification **Answer: b) a simile** — the flag-word 'like' is flying (Sierra Leone L25's test)

**3. (Level 2)** "The moon watched us home" is — a) a simile b) a metaphor c) personification **Answer: c) personification** — watching is human life given to a thing

**4. (Level 3)** The theme of a story is — a) its longest chapter b) what it is really about, underneath the events c) its setting **Answer: b) what it is really about, underneath the events** — plot is what happens; theme is what it means (Ghana's analysis set, curriculum evidence)
