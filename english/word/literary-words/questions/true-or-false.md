# Literary words — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Kinds of Literature · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Figures of Speech · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Talking About Books.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A simile must use the word 'like' or 'as'. **Answer: True** — that flag is exactly what Sierra Leone's L25 test hunts; without it, the figure is a metaphor

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A folktale is one of Egypt's five kinds of literature. **Answer: True** — novels, poetry, drama, folktales, essays (U11 L1)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A genre is the same thing as a kind of literature. **Answer: False** — a genre is a family INSIDE a kind — thriller and comedy live inside the novel/film kinds (Jordan's genre set, p.23)
