# Literary words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** Where and when a story happens is its ______. **Answer: setting** — one of Ghana's analysis words (curriculum evidence)

**2. (Level 1)** What happens in a story, in order, is its ______. **Answer: plot** — plot, setting, characters — the story-words

**3. (Level 2)** ______ gives human life to things that are not human — 'the river smiles'. **Answer: Personification** — one of Ghana's named devices (curriculum evidence)
