# Video script — Word relationships

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words have friends. Words have opposites. Listen!

**Buy** and **purchase** — the SAME meaning!
Friends! **Synonyms!**
**Hot** and **cold** — OPPOSITE meanings!
**Antonyms!**

Now hear this: **to**… **two**. Same sound!
Different words! **Homophones!**
T-O walks to school. T-W-O is the number 2.

And this word — **mouse**.
A mouse eats cheese. A mouse clicks the computer!
ONE word, TWO meanings! A **homonym**!

Same, opposite, same sound, two meanings.
Now you. Give me a friend for BIG.
Give me the opposite. That is word relationships — in
English.
