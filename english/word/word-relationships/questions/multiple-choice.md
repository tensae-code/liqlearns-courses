# Word relationships — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Same and Opposite · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Same Sound · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — One Word, Many Meanings.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A synonym of BUY, from Tanzania's pairs,
is ______.
a) sell  b) purchase  c) search
**Answer: b) purchase** — buy/purchase, the reader's
same-meaning set (Units Ten–Eleven).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The antonym of HOT is ______.
a) warm  b) cold  c) heat
**Answer: b) cold** — the hot/cold pair Tanzania's reader
works with.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "I walk ______ school." (movement word)
a) two  b) too  c) to
**Answer: c) to** — to walks toward; two is the number
(Vietnam's homophone set, Grade 12, Unit 6).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ house is big." (the house belongs
to them)
a) There  b) Their  c) To
**Answer: b) Their** — there/their, Vietnam's homophones.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The mouse ate the cheese" and "Click the
mouse" — mouse here is a ______.
a) synonym  b) homonym  c) prefix
**Answer: b) homonym** — one word, two meanings;
Tanzania's Mr Mouse unit plays the same trap.
