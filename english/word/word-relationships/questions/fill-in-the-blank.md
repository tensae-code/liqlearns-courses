# Word relationships — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Same and Opposite · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Same Sound · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — One Word, Many Meanings.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** till · aloud · antonym · synonym · dislike

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wait until five — wait ______ five means
the same.
**Answer: till** — until/till, Tanzania's pair.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hate and ______ are synonyms in Tanzania's
reader.
**Answer: dislike.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading ______ means reading so people can
hear you — not silent reading.
**Answer: aloud** — allowed/aloud, Vietnam's homophones.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Big and small: small is the ______ of big.
**Answer: antonym.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Purchase is a ______ of buy.
**Answer: synonym.**
