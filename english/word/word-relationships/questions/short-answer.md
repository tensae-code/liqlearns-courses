# Word relationships — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

pairs.

**2. (Level 1)** Give the antonym of FIRST.

**3. (Level 2)** Spell the homophone that is a number:
to, two or too?

**4. (Level 3)** Write two meanings of MOUSE.

pointing part — the pair Tanzania's Mr Mouse story and
