**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Barbados,
Tanzania, South Africa, Vietnam, China).** Barbados names all four
relationships in its primary syllabi. Tanzania's evidence
rests on ONE primary reader — Reading (English), Standard
One, Pupil's Book Two — read in full; the other recorded
Tanzania books are link-only and contribute nothing here;
that one reader carries synonyms as its most explicit
teaching, homophone pairs in its first unit, and the
mouse/mouth trap in its last. South Africa defines and
distinguishes homophones and homonyms in its Grade 5 Home
Language Workbook 2. Vietnam carries homophones as a named
pronunciation strand and the closest/opposite question
shapes in every review. China carries the web and the
sort: word webs in Grades 5 and 6, word families and
opposites, and sorting words into categories (stocked
2026-11-08 by the re-verification sweep). Other closed countries join as
their books are read (see READING-QUEUE.md).
