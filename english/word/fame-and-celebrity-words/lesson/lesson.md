# Fame and Celebrity Words

## Do it first

Open any magazine cover in your mind: a famous face, a headline about their love life. What is all that talk called? This lesson teaches the words of fame and celebrity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fame words

Grade 9's celebrity set starts with the ground words: a **celebrity** is a famous person; **popularity** is being liked and known by many people; **fame** is the state of being known far and wide.

*Difficulty: Three ground words for one bright world.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Gossip and celebrity news

Where fame goes, talk follows. **Gossip** is talk about other people's private lives. Celebrity news follows **breakups** — famous couples splitting — and the **latest couples** forming. And fame has a price some pay to keep their look: **plastic surgery**, operations that change how a person looks.

*Difficulty: The shadow side of the bright world — gossip and breakups named plainly.*

## Say it to your companion

Your companion gives a word — gossip! You say what it means and make a headline with it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the five fame words and what each means.
2. Write one celebrity-news headline using “breakup” or “latest couple”.
3. Write one sentence: is popularity always a good thing? Say why.
