# Video script — Fame and Celebrity Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A camera flash pops; a magazine cover appears.]

“A celebrity! Everyone knows the face.”

[Whispering speech bubbles multiply.] “And the talk that follows? Gossip.”

“Learn the words of fame — the bright side, and the shadow.”
