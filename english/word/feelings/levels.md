# Feelings — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the feeling words — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: naming the feelings first,
then saying them of yourself, then causes and the past.

## Level 1 — name the feelings
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name
happy, sad, tired, hungry, thirsty, hot, cold, bored,
excited, surprised, nervous, afraid from faces and from
Bolivia's list of seventeen, without a miss.

## Level 2 — say how you feel
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer
"How are you feeling?" whole — "I am… / I feel…" — match
feelings to faces (Mongolia's six: sleepy, sad, excited,
happy, angry, surprised), and run the make-me chain:
"A clown in the circus always makes me happy."

## Level 3 — causes, pairs and the past
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give a
feeling its cause with because, hold Albania's -ed/-ing
pair (I feel bored / the movie is boring), and say a past
feeling in Mexico's frame — "I was happy with my
friends" — naming joy, sadness, fear and pride from its
dictionary list.
