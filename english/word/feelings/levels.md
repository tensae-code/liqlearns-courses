# Feelings — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
feeling words — naming the feelings first, then saying them of
yourself, then causes and the past, the order the books teach it
in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Feelings
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name
happy, sad, tired, hungry, thirsty, hot, cold, bored,
excited, surprised, nervous, afraid from faces and from
Bolivia's list of seventeen, without a miss.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saying How You Feel
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer
"How are you feeling?" whole — "I am… / I feel…" — match
feelings to faces (Mongolia's six: sleepy, sad, excited,
happy, angry, surprised), and run the make-me chain:
"A clown in the circus always makes me happy."

## Sub lesson 3 — Causes, Pairs and the Past
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give a
feeling its cause with because, hold Albania's -ed/-ing
pair (I feel bored / the movie is boring), and say a past
feeling in Mexico's frame — "I was happy with my
friends" — naming joy, sadness, fear and pride from its
dictionary list.
