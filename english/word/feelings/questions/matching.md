# Feelings — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Feelings · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Saying How You Feel · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causes,
Pairs and the Past.

Match each feeling to its face or cause.

1. hungry — **wants food**
2. thirsty — **wants drink**
3. tired — **wants sleep**
4. hot — **wants shade and water**
5. afraid — **wants safety**
