# Feelings — matching

Match each feeling to its face or cause.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — feelings and bodies.
1. hungry — **wants food**
2. thirsty — **wants drink**
3. tired — **wants sleep**
4. hot — **wants shade and water**
5. afraid — **wants safety**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — Mongolia's faces.
1. sleepy — **eyes closing**
2. excited — **eyes wide, smiling big**
3. angry — **brows down, mouth tight**
4. surprised — **mouth open, brows up**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — Albania's pairs: the feeler and
the cause.
1. I feel annoyed — **He is annoying**
2. I feel bored — **This movie is boring**
3. I feel excited — **The game is exciting**
