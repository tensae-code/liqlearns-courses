# Feelings — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Feelings · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Saying How You Feel · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causes,
Pairs and the Past.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name six feelings from Bolivia's list.
**Answer:** any six of bored, cold, hot, confused,
disappointed, tired, hungry, thirsty, surprised,
nervous, happy, sad, upset, excited, afraid, brave,
amused (Year 2, Topic 7).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are you feeling now? Answer whole.
**Answer:** "I am… / I feel…" with a true feeling word.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes you happy? Answer in
Mongolia's frame.
**Answer:** "…always makes me happy" / "…makes me happy"
(English VIII, Unit 3, p.38).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four of Mexico's dictionary
feelings.
**Answer:** any four of joy, sadness, fear, surprise,
frustration, guilt, jealousy, anxiety, melancholy,
shame, pride (Project 14).
