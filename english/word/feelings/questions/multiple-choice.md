# Feelings — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Feelings · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Saying How You Feel · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causes,
Pairs and the Past.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You slept badly and your eyes are heavy.
You are ______.
a) thirsty  b) tired  c) brave
**Answer: b) tired** — in Bolivia's seventeen and
Mongolia's face set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is NOT in Bolivia's feelings
list of seventeen?
a) amused  b) disappointed  c) sleepy
**Answer: c) sleepy** — Bolivia's list runs bored, cold,
hot, confused, disappointed, tired, hungry, thirsty,
surprised, nervous, happy, sad, upset, excited, afraid,
brave, amused; sleepy is Mongolia's face word.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "A clown in the circus always makes me
______."
a) happy  b) a happy  c) happiness-hat
**Answer: a) happy** — Mongolia's chain-game sentence
(English VIII, Unit 3).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I feel ______. This movie is ______."
a) boring / bored  b) bored / boring  c) bore / bores
**Answer: b) bored / boring** — Albania's pair: the
person feels -ed, the thing is -ing (Ch.36).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mexico: "I ______ happy with my friends"
(yesterday).
a) was  b) am  c) be
**Answer: a) was** — was/were taught through emotions
(Project 14, p.126).
