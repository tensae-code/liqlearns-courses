# Feelings — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** happy · tired · hungry · thirsty · afraid ·
surprised

**1. (Level 1)** I want to eat. I am ______.
**Answer: hungry.**

**2. (Level 1)** I want to drink. I am ______.
**Answer: thirsty.**

**3. (Level 2)** My best friend always makes me ______.
**Answer: happy** (Mongolia's discovery sentence, p.38).

**4. (Level 2)** Long weekdays make us ______.
**Answer: tired** — in Mongolia's frame: "Long weekdays
make us busy" (English VIII, Unit 3, p.38); tired is the
true completion here.

**5. (Level 3)** I was ______ with my friends yesterday.
**Answer: happy** (Mexico's model, Project 14, p.126).
