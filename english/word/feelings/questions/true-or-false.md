# Feelings — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Hungry and thirsty are feelings in
Bolivia's list.
**True** — both stand in the seventeen (Year 2, Topic 7).

**2. (Level 2)** In Mongolia's game, a clown makes the
speaker sad.
**False** — "A clown in the circus always makes me
happy" (English VIII, Unit 3).

**3. (Level 3)** "I feel boring" is the right way to say
the movie makes YOU feel it, in Albania's teaching.
**False** — the person feels BORED; the movie is boring
(Ch.36's -ed/-ing sets).

**4. (Level 3)** Albania's book prints the word for calm
correctly everywhere.
**False** — its vocabulary list misprints "clam"; its
own structure sentence spells "calm" right ("She is
always calm," p.39). The lesson teaches calm.
