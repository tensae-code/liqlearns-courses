# Video script — Feelings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How are you feeling?

I am **happy**! I am **excited**!
I am **tired**. I am **sleepy**.
I am **hungry**! I am **thirsty**!
I am **sad**. I am **surprised**!

A clown makes me happy.
Long days make me tired.
Don't worry. Be **calm**. Be **brave**.

Yesterday I was happy with my friends.

Now you. Show me a face.
Happy? Sad? Tired? Surprised?
Say how you feel — in English.
