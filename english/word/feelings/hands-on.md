# Hands-on — Feelings

The lesson on your real face, not only on the page.

## The mirror round
Look in a mirror (or at your companion). Make each
feeling and say it as you make it: "I am surprised."
"I am tired." Six faces minimum — Mongolia's six first:
sleepy, sad, excited, happy, angry, surprised.

## The day chart
Three times today — morning, midday, evening — stop and
write your feeling in English: "I am hungry." At night,
read the day back and add one past sentence: "I was
happy at…"

## The chain game
With a partner, play Mongolia's chain: one names a cause
— "A clown in the circus…" — the other finishes:
"…always makes me happy." Five causes each, then swap.

## Feelings detective
Watch people (family, a street, a programme). Guess each
person's feeling and say it quietly in English with its
because: "She is tired because…" Albania's worksheet
asks the same of paintings; yours are live.
