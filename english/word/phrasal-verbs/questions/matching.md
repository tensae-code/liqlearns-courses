Match each phrasal verb to its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "count on"
**Answer: depend on / trust** — from Cambodia's friendship set (Grade 12, Unit 1) — a friend you can count on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "come across"
**Answer: meet or find by chance** — in Bolivia's inseparable table (Year 6, §1.3) — its object follows the whole phrase.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "live up to"
**Answer: reach what is expected** — a three-word chain (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 9): live up to expectations.
