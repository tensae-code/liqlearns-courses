# Phrasal verbs — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Use "give up" in a true sentence about a habit.
**Answer: He gave up smoking last year. (any true use: stop doing)** — give up sits in Cambodia's friendship set (Grade 12, Unit 1).

**2. (Level 2)** Rewrite with the pronoun: "Turn off the lights." (them)
**Answer: Turn them off.** — separable verb + pronoun = the pronoun goes between, the law of Bolivia's Year 5 lesson.

**3. (Level 3)** Say one thing you keep up with, in a full sentence.
**Answer: I keep up with the news. (object after the whole chain)** — keep up with is inseparable in Bolivia's table and a three-word chain in Vietnam's list.
