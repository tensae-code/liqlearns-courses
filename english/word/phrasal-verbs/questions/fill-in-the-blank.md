# Phrasal verbs — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right phrasal verb or placement.

**1. (Level 1)** We ______ of milk — there is none left. (run …)
**Answer: ran out** — run out of = have none left, from Vietnam's Grade 9 Remember box (Unit 1).

**2. (Level 2)** Pick up your bag. = Pick ______ up. (noun both ways)
**Answer: your bag (Pick your bag up)** — a noun object may stand after the pair or between its halves; only the pronoun is forced between.

**3. (Level 3)** She puts ______ with the noise because she loves the city.
**Answer: up** — put up with — tolerate; a three-word chain from Vietnam's Grade 12 list.
