# Phrasal verbs — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A phrasal verb's meaning is just its verb's meaning plus its particle's meaning.
**Answer: False** — the pair carries its own meaning — look after is not look + after; learn the pair as one word.

**2. (Level 2)** "She looks the children after" is acceptable English.
**Answer: False** — look after is non-separable (Bolivia's Year 6 table): She looks after the children — the object follows the whole phrase.

**3. (Level 3)** Every country's book in this set teaches separable and non-separable phrasal verbs.
**Answer: False** — Bolivia alone teaches that grammar in the whole read set; the other four countries witness the meaning sets only.
