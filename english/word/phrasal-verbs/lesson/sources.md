Iran joins with phrasal verbs as a See Also grammar in its Grade 11 book (stocked 2026-11-09 by the re-verification sweep merge). Two sourcing
facts are stated as plainly as the brief requires.
First: Bolivia is the ONLY source in the whole read set
that teaches the separable / non-separable grammar (and
the transitive / intransitive rules beneath it) — the
grammar half of this lesson stands on Bolivia alone,
and the lesson says so; the other four countries
witness the meaning sets. Second, two Bolivia print
slips are recorded, not copied: its separable example
line prints "Rosa fill out the test" (missing the verb
ending), and its separable definition phrases the split
as separation "by the subject that the phrasal verb is
referring to" — the lesson teaches the standard
object-placement law (object between verb and particle;
a pronoun must go between), which is what Bolivia's own
worked examples ("I'll put my jacket on", "Hang up your
jacket") show.
