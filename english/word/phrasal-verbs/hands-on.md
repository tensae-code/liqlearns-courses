# Hands-on — Phrasal verbs

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Mime the set.** Cards carry the filed sets —
   clothes, travel, friendship. One partner mimes, the
   other must name the phrasal verb before the mime
   ends; then both say a true sentence with it.
2. **Slide the pronoun.** Sentences with noun objects
   ("Put on your jacket") are read aloud; the learner
   must re-say each with a pronoun slid into the only
   place it may go ("Put it on"), and say "never" to
   the split of a non-separable ("Look after it" —
   never "look it after").
3. **The morning chain.** Each partner narrates their
   real morning using at least six phrasal verbs (woke
   up, got up, put on, had… ) — wake up, get dressed —
   the partner ticks each one and challenges any pair
   read as two separate words.
4. **Chain talk.** Three-word chains on cards (look
   forward to, put up with, keep up with). Each partner
   must make a true sentence with the object after the
   whole chain: "I look forward to Friday."
