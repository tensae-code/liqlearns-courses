# The home — the lesson

Course: English · Title: Word · Lesson: The home
Built from: Egypt, English Language Primary 1 Term 2, Unit 4
"My House" — Libya, English for Libya Grade 1 (the house unit)
and Grade 2 (the labelled house) — Palestine, English for
Palestine Grade 1 Part 2, Unit 15 "My bedroom."

## Do it first
Take a walk through your own home in your head. Start at the
door. Go into every room, one by one. In your own language you
know every room's name, and you know what each room is FOR.
Now we learn the English names — because a home is the first
part of the world every learner can name whole.

## Then the explanation
Egypt's book teaches the home as **five rooms**, with a
sentence for what you do in each one. Learn the five with
their sentences, and you own the set:

- **bedroom** — "I sleep in the bedroom."
- **bathroom** — "I brush my teeth in the bathroom."
- **living room** — "I watch TV in the living room."
- **dining room** — "I eat in the dining room."
- **kitchen** — "My mother cooks in the kitchen."

Libya's Grade 1 book teaches three of the same rooms —
**sitting room** (its name for the living room), **bedroom**,
**kitchen** — and adds the **garden** outside. Two names for
one room is normal in English; this course keeps both, the way
the books do.

Now the things that live in the rooms. Egypt's house unit
names them: **bed, closet, sink, toilet, fridge, oven, couch,
TV, table, chairs**. Libya's house adds **wardrobe, shelf,
picture, lamp** — and Palestine's bedroom lesson gives the
first four a child needs: **bed, table, chair, box**. Say them
with their rooms: the **oven** is in the kitchen. The **bed**
is in the bedroom. The **sink** and the **toilet** are in the
bathroom. The **couch** and the **TV** are in the living room.

And the question the books drill until it is easy: **"What do
you do in the …?"** — "What do you do in the living room?" "I
watch TV in the living room." "What do you do in the bedroom?"
"I sleep in the bedroom." Ask it about any room; answer with
the room's doing word: sleep, cook, eat, brush my teeth,
watch TV.

## Say it to your companion
Take your companion on a tour of your home, room by room, in
English: "This is the kitchen. My mother cooks in the kitchen.
This is my bedroom. I sleep in the bedroom." Then swap: your
companion tours, you ask the question — "What do you do in
the bathroom?" — and they answer in a whole sentence.

## Do It Yourself
1. Draw your home — every room as a box. Write each room's
   English name inside its box.
2. Under each room, write its sentence: what you do there.
3. Write three things that live in your bedroom, with their
   names: bed, lamp…
4. Ask the question about your own home five times, once per
   room, and answer out loud each time.
