# Hands-on — The home

The lesson in your real home, not only on the page.

## The room walk
Walk through your home slowly. At every room, stop at the
door, say its English name out loud, and say its sentence:
"Kitchen. My mother cooks in the kitchen." Every room, every
time you pass it today. By the third walk, the names come
before you think.

## Label the house
Write each room's name on a small card — BEDROOM, KITCHEN,
BATHROOM, LIVING ROOM, DINING ROOM — and tape each card to its
room's door or wall for a day. Read the card every time you
enter. Tomorrow, take the cards down and say the names from
memory as you walk.

## Things hunt
Take one room. Name everything in it you can, in English:
"bed — lamp — shelf — book — chair." Five things minimum per
room. The thing you cannot name is your new word to find in
the lesson's list.

## The tour game
With a partner: one guides, one follows. The guide stops at
each room and asks: "What do you do in this room?" The other
answers in a whole sentence. Five rooms, then swap roles.
