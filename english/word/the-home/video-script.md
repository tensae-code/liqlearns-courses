# Video script — The home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome to my home! Come in!

This is the **living room**. I watch TV in the living room.
This is the **kitchen**. My mother cooks in the kitchen.
Mmm — an oven! A fridge!

This is the **dining room**. I eat in the dining room.
This is the **bathroom**. I brush my teeth in the bathroom.
And this — this is my **bedroom**. I sleep in the bedroom.
My bed! My lamp!

Five rooms. Five names.
Bedroom — sleep!
Kitchen — cook!
Bathroom — brush your teeth!

Now you. Walk in YOUR home.
Name the room. Say what you do.
That is your home — in English.
