# The home — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Five Rooms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Things in the Rooms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Rooms in
Sentences.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five rooms Egypt's book teaches.
**Answer:** bedroom, living room, kitchen, dining room,
bathroom (Primary 1 Term 2, pp.67–68).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do you do in the dining room?
**Answer:** I eat (in the dining room) — Egypt's sentence.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things that live in a bedroom.
**Answer:** any three of bed, lamp, shelf, wardrobe, closet,
table, chair, picture, box (Libya's and Palestine's sets).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which room has a fridge?
**Answer:** the kitchen (Egypt's house set).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer in a whole sentence: What do you do in
the bathroom?
**Answer:** I brush my teeth in the bathroom (Egypt, p.67).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer about YOUR home: What do you do in the
kitchen?
**Answer:** any true sentence in the shape "I ___ in the
kitchen."
