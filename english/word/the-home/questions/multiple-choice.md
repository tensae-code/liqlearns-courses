# The home — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Five Rooms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Things in the Rooms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Rooms in
Sentences.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** I sleep in the ______.
a) kitchen  b) bedroom  c) bathroom
**Answer: b) bedroom** — Egypt's own sentence (Primary 1
Term 2, p.67).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** My mother cooks in the ______.
a) dining room  b) living room  c) kitchen
**Answer: c) kitchen** (Egypt, p.67).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** I brush my teeth in the ______.
a) bathroom  b) bedroom  c) garden
**Answer: a) bathroom** (Egypt, p.67).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Libya's book calls the living room the ______.
a) dining room  b) sitting room  c) bed room
**Answer: b) sitting room** (Libya, Grade 1 Unit 4).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The oven lives in the ______.
a) bathroom  b) kitchen  c) bedroom
**Answer: b) kitchen** — "There's an oven in the kitchen"
(Egypt, Unit 4).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The sink and the toilet are in the ______.
a) bathroom  b) dining room  c) living room
**Answer: a) bathroom** (Egypt's house set).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "What do you do in the living room?" — "I

a) sleep  b) watch TV  c) cook
**Answer: b) watch TV** — the book's own drilled pair (Egypt,
