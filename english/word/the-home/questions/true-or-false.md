# The home — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** We cook in the kitchen.
**True** — Egypt's sentence: "My mother cooks in the
kitchen."

**2. (Level 1)** We sleep in the bathroom.
**False** — we sleep in the bedroom. In the bathroom we brush
our teeth.

**3. (Level 1)** The sitting room and the living room are the
same room with two names.
**True** — Libya says sitting room; Egypt says living room.
Both names are in the books.

**4. (Level 2)** The toilet is in the kitchen.
**False** — the toilet is in the bathroom. The oven and the
fridge are in the kitchen.

**5. (Level 2)** A wardrobe is something that lives in a
bedroom.
**True** — Libya's "What's in your bedroom?" set (Grade 2,
p.46).

**6. (Level 3)** "What do you do in the living room?" — "I
watch TV in the living room" is a right answer.
**True** — the book's own drilled pair (Egypt, p.70).
