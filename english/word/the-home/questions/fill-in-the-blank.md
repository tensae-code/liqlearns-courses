# The home — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** bedroom · bathroom · kitchen · dining room · living
room · oven

**1. (Level 1)** I sleep in the ______.
**Answer: bedroom.**

**2. (Level 1)** I eat in the ______.
**Answer: dining room.**

**3. (Level 1)** I watch TV in the ______.
**Answer: living room.**

**4. (Level 1)** I brush my teeth in the ______.
**Answer: bathroom.**

**5. (Level 1)** My mother cooks in the ______.
**Answer: kitchen.**
(All five are Egypt's own room sentences, pp.67–68.)

**6. (Level 2)** There's an ______ in the kitchen.
**Answer: oven** (Egypt, Unit 4).
