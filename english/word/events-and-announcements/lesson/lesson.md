# Events and Announcements

## Do it first

A poster on a school wall says: BOOK FAIR — FRIDAY. A voice on the radio says: a storm is coming. A shop window says: DISCOUNT — TODAY ONLY. Events fill our weeks, and every event has its word. Learn the words today, and you will understand every announcement you meet.

## Sub lesson 1 — Words for events

*Very simple. Can-do: Name the events: conference, book fair, festival, quiz, exhibition, tournament — and the announcement words that travel with them: storm (an event in the news) and discount (an event in a shop).*

Meet the event words, one by one:

A **conference** — people meet to talk about one big subject. A **book fair** — books are shown and sold, like a market for reading. A **festival** — days of music, food and celebration. A **quiz** — a game of questions with a winner. An **exhibition** — things are put out for people to look at: paintings, pets, school projects. A **tournament** — games played round by round until one winner stands.

Two more announcement words: a **storm** — the weather event the news announces so people can get ready; and a **discount** — a shop event: the price drops for a short time.

Try it now: which event would you announce for your school — a book fair, a quiz or a tournament? Say its name and one sentence about it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Announcing an event

*Simple. Can-do: Say that an event exists and when: There is a… / There will be… — the announcement shape the books use for festivals, fairs and tournaments.*

An announcement has a job: to say **what** will happen, **where**, and **when**. English announces with *there*: "There is a book fair on Friday." "There will be a tournament next week." "There was a festival last year."

Build yours in three parts: There is / There will be + the event + the time or place. "There will be an exhibition of our paintings in the school hall on Saturday."

Try it now: announce the event you chose in Sub lesson 1 — say the whole announcement aloud, with its day and place.

## Say it to your companion

Read this lesson aloud to your companion. Then announce three events — one real, one you wish for, one from the news words (a storm or a discount) — each with its day.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write a poster announcement for a school book fair, with the day and place.
2. Listen for announcements today — on the radio, at school, in a shop — and name each event word you hear.
3. Make a quiz announcement: what is the quiz about, when is it, who can play?
4. Draw a shop window with a DISCOUNT announcement and say the prices before and after.
