# Short Answer — Events and Announcements

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name three event words and one thing that happens at each. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Example: book fair — books are sold; quiz — questions are asked; tournament — games are played to find one winner.
2. What is the difference between a festival and a conference? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A festival is days of celebration (music, food); a conference is a meeting to talk about one big subject.
3. Announce a school exhibition with what, where and when. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "There will be an exhibition of our paintings in the school hall on Saturday."
4. Why does the news announce a storm before it comes? (Sub lesson 2)
   — So people can get ready and stay safe.
