# Levels — Events and Announcements

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Words for events | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the events: conference, book fair, festival, quiz, exhibition, tournament — and the announcement words that travel with them: storm (an event in the news) and discount (an event in a shop). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Announcing an event | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Say that an event exists and when: There is a… / There will be… — the announcement shape the books use for festivals, fairs and tournaments. |
