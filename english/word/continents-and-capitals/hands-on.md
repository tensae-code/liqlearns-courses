# Hands-on — Continents and Capitals

The map is learned by pointing. Do these out loud, with a
partner.

## Point and name

One partner points at a continent on the world map. The other
names it and names one country on it: "Africa — and this is
Rwanda."

## Neighbours

Point at Rwanda. Name its four neighbours one by one — Uganda,
Tanzania, Burundi, the Democratic Republic of Congo — then
Rwanda's capital: Kigali. Change places and do it again.
