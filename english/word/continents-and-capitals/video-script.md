# Video script — Continents and Capitals

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands by a world map.] "The world is made of big
lands. We call them… CONTINENTS!"

[The map colours Africa.] "AFRICA! Our continent!"

[The map zooms to East Africa; Rwanda grows big.] "RWANDA! And
its capital city — KIGALI!"

[A blue line runs north from East Africa to the sea.] "THE
NILE! The longest river in the world — six thousand, six
hundred and seventy kilometres!"

[Back to the mascot by the map.] "Continents. Countries.
Capitals. You can read the world map!"
