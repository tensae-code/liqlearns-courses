Book error carried as the placements carry it: the P5 book's
topic heading prints "Naming Continets" for Continents
(printed p.165) — the word is taught corrected in this lesson,
never as printed.

This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-08 re-verification
sweep of Rwanda's English books. The sweep re-read all 11 held
books (347 teaching items placed; 244 already in the course,
103 missing) and marked both parts NEW LESSON NEEDED. The P4
book's rivers-of-the-world part stocks
nature-and-the-environment and its famous-structures part
stocks the world-landmarks lesson in this same merge; the
world-geography parts had no home anywhere. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06) the parts get their own
lesson.
