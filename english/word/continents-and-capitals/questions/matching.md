## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Continent — b
2. Capital — d
3. Neighbour — a
4. The Nile — e
5. Kigali — c

a) A country that stands beside another country
b) One of the world's very big lands
c) The capital city of Rwanda
d) A country's chief city, where its government sits
e) The longest river in the world, 6,670 km long

**Answers:** 1–b (a continent is a very big land), 2–d (a capital is a
country's chief city), 3–a (a neighbour stands beside another
country), 4–e (the Nile is the longest river in the world), 5–c
(Kigali is Rwanda's capital).
