## Fill in the blank

1. "Asia is the ___ continent in the world." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: biggest** — Asia is bigger than every other continent.

2. "Australia is a continent and a ___ at the same time." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: country** — one country fills the whole continent of Australia.

3. "Kigali is the ___ of Rwanda." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: capital** — Kigali is Rwanda's chief city.

4. "Uganda, Tanzania and Burundi are ___ of Rwanda." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: neighbours** — they are countries that stand beside Rwanda in East Africa.

5. "The Nile is ___ kilometres long." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: 6,670** — the Nile, at 6,670 km, is the longest river in the world.
