## Multiple choice

1. "A very big land, like Africa or Asia, is called a ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) capital  b) continent  c) country
   **Answer: b) continent** — a continent is one of the world's very big lands.

2. "Rwanda is a country on the continent of ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Europe  b) Australia  c) Africa
   **Answer: c) Africa** — Rwanda is an African country.

3. "The biggest continent in the world is ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Asia  b) Antarctica  c) Europe
   **Answer: a) Asia** — Asia is the biggest of the continents.

4. "The chief city of a country, where its government sits, is its ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) river  b) capital  c) neighbour
   **Answer: b) capital** — every country has a capital city.

5. "The capital of Rwanda is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Giza  b) Dubai  c) Kigali
   **Answer: c) Kigali** — Kigali is Rwanda's capital.

6. "The longest river in the world is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the Nile  b) the Congo  c) the Thames
   **Answer: a) the Nile** — the Nile is 6,670 kilometres long, the longest river in the world.
