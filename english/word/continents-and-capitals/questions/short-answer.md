## Short answer

1. What is a continent? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** One of the world's very big lands — Africa, Europe, Asia, North America, South America, Australia, Antarctica.

2. Which continent is covered in ice, at the bottom of the world? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Antarctica.

3. What is a capital? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** A country's chief city, where its government sits.

4. Name two of Rwanda's East African neighbours. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of Uganda, Tanzania, Burundi and the Democratic Republic of Congo.

5. How long is the Nile? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** 6,670 kilometres.
