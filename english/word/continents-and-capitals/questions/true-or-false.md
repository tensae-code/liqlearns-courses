## True or false

1. A continent is a very small piece of land. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a continent is a very big land; Africa and Asia are continents.

2. Antarctica is a cold continent covered in ice. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. Every country has a capital city. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

4. The Nile is the longest river in the world. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — it is 6,670 kilometres long.

5. Kigali is a neighbour of Rwanda. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — Kigali is Rwanda's capital city; its neighbours are countries like Uganda and Tanzania.
