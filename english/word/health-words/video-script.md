# Video script — Health words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oh no. I feel sick.

My head! **I have a headache.**
My throat! **I have a sore throat.**
Cough, cough! **I have a cough.**

I go to the doctor.
The doctor asks: **"What is wrong?"**
I say: **"I have a fever."**

The doctor says: **"You should rest."**
**"You should drink water."**
**"You shouldn't go out today."**

Rest. Water. Sleep.
And soon — **get well soon!**

Now you. Show me: headache!
Show me: cough!
Say it to the doctor. That is health — in English.
