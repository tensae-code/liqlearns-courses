# Hands-on — Health words

The lesson on your own body, not only on the page.

## Mime and name
Stand up. Mime a symptom silently — hand on head, hand on
stomach, a cough, holding one tooth. A partner names it in
English: "Headache!" Then the full sentence: "You have a
headache." Five symptoms, then swap.

## The advice cards
Write six problem cards: a headache · a cough · a fever ·
a sore throat · a hurt knee · staying up late. Shuffle.
Draw one, read the problem aloud, and give the advice in
one should or shouldn't sentence before you turn the card
over. Albania's worksheet, played as a game.

## The doctor's room, for real
With a partner and two chairs, play the whole visit: knock,
sit, "What is wrong?", the symptom sentence, one advice
sentence, "Get well soon." Use a different illness each
round — food poisoning, a cough, malaria, a cold — the four
Rwanda's book names.

## My healthy day
Saudi Arabia's list as a day plan: write your day with
should — get up early, drink a lot of water, eat three
meals — and one shouldn't for the evening. Read it aloud
at breakfast tomorrow.
