# Health words — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Symptoms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — At the Doctor · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Advice with Should.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fever means your temperature is very
high.
**True** — Albania's structures: "Your temperature is very
high. I will check if you have a fever."

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sore throat is a pain in your foot.
**False** — the throat is in your neck; a sore throat hurts
when you swallow.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Rwanda's dialogue, the patient is ill
with food poisoning, and learners then play the same talk
with a cough, typhoid or malaria.
**True** (Rwanda, P.5, pp.135–136).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "You shouldn't rest" is good advice for a
friend with a cold.
**False** — Bolivia's suggestion for a cold is the opposite:
stay in bed, rest.
