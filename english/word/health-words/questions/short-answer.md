# Health words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Symptoms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — At the Doctor · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Advice with Should.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three symptoms from Albania's doctor
chapter.
**Answer:** any three of headache, fever, cough, cold,
stomachache, sneeze (A2 Chapter 35, printed p.32).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the doctor ask first?
**Answer:** "What is wrong?" — Rwanda's dialogue opening
(P.5, pp.135–136).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say the kind closing words of a doctor's
visit.
**Answer:** "Get well soon" (Albania's list) — "Take care"
is also in the books.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one should sentence for a friend with
a sore throat.
**Answer:** any true advice in the shape "You should …" —
for example "You should rest" / "You should go to the
doctor" (Albania; Bolivia).
