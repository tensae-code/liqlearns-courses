# Health words — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Symptoms · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Advice with Should.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — the -ache family: symptom and place.
1. headache — **the head**
2. stomachache — **the stomach**
3. toothache — **one tooth**
4. sore throat — **the throat**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 3)** — problem and advice, the way the books
pair them.
1. a cold — **You should stay in bed** (Bolivia, Year 4)
2. a stomachache — **You should drink a chamomile mate**
   (Bolivia, Year 4)
3. too much junk food — **You shouldn't eat it** (Saudi
   Arabia, Grade 3, Unit 10)
4. staying up late — **You shouldn't do it** (Saudi Arabia,
   Grade 3, Unit 10)

   (Albania's advice worksheet, printed p.37)
