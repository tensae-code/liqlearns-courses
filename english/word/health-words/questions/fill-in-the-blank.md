# Health words — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Symptoms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — At the Doctor · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Advice with Should.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** headache · fever · should · shouldn't · well

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** I have a ______. My head hurts.
**Answer: headache.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Your temperature is very high. You have a
______.
**Answer: fever** (Albania, Chapter 35: "I will check if
you have a fever").

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You ______ go to the doctor. You are very
sick.
**Answer: should** (Albania's advice sentence).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** I ______ eat too much junk food.
**Answer: shouldn't** (Saudi Arabia, Grade 3, Unit 10).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "I don't feel ______ so I will go to the
doctor."
**Answer: well** (Albania, Chapter 35 structures).
