# Health words — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Symptoms · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — At the Doctor · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Advice with Should.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your head hurts. You have a ______.
a) toothache  b) headache  c) cough
**Answer: b) headache** — Albania's symptom list (A2
Chapter 35, printed p.32).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I have a ______." — you cough and cough.
a) cough  b) fever  c) cold
**Answer: a) cough** — the books' symptom sentence shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The doctor asks the patient: "______"
a) Get well soon.  b) What is wrong?  c) I feel sick.
**Answer: b) What is wrong?** — the opening line of
Rwanda's doctor–patient dialogue (P.5, pp.135–136).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Magezi, Rwanda's patient, is ill with ______.
a) a headache  b) food poisoning  c) a cold
**Answer: b) food poisoning** (Rwanda, P.5, pp.135–136).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Your friend has a cold. The best advice:
a) You should stay in bed.  b) You should run fast.
c) You shouldn't rest.
**Answer: a) You should stay in bed** — Bolivia's health
suggestions (Year 4, Topic 4): a cold → stay in bed.
