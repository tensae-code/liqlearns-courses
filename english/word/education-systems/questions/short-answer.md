## Short answer

1. Name the parts of Costa Rica's education system in order. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: the cycles, then the Bachillerato and FARO tests, then the universities.**

2. What do the FARO tests do? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: they measure what learners know.**

3. What is a specialized university? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: a university narrowed to a field or profession.**
