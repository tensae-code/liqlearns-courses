# Education Systems

## Do it first

How many stages does your schooling have, and what test stands at the end? This lesson reads a whole education system — Costa Rica's — and names its parts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The school road — cycles and tests

How is a country's schooling built? Grade 10 reads **Costa Rica's education system**: school runs in **cycles**, stage after stage; at the top of school stand the leaving examinations — the **Bachillerato** — and the **FARO tests** that measure what learners know.

An education *system* is the whole machine: the stages, the tests, and the doors each stage opens.

*Difficulty: A whole country's school machine, named part by part.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Universities and higher study

After the cycles come the universities. The reading names Costa Rica's **four public universities** and its **specialized universities** — the top of the system, where study narrows to a profession.

*Difficulty: The top floor of the machine — who runs it and what it is for.*

## Say it to your companion

Ask your companion: how is schooling built in Costa Rica? They answer with the parts — cycles, Bachillerato, FARO tests, universities. Then swap.
