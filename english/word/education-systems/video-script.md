# Video script — Education Systems

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A road climbs steps, each step labelled.]

“School is a road. Cycles — step, step, step.”

[At the top, a gate.] “The big tests: the Bachillerato. The FARO tests.”

[A wide building beyond.] “And then — the universities.”
