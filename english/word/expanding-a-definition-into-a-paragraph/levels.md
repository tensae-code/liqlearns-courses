# Levels — Expanding a Definition into a Paragraph

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Expanding by example and by comparison | Expand a definition into a paragraph by giving an example, and by comparing and contrasting the thing with its neighbours. |
| 2. Expanding by describing a process | Expand a definition into a paragraph by describing the process the thing is or does, step by step. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: growing a one-sentence definition into a full paragraph in three ways — by giving an example, by comparing and contrasting, and by describing a process.
