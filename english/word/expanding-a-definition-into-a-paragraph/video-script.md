# Video Script — Expanding a Definition into a Paragraph

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: one sentence on a page: a definition of SPEED]

Here is a definition. One sentence. It is right… but small.

[Picture: the page growing — a runner, a bus, a river under the sentence]

Give an example! Show it at work. The definition grows!

[Picture: a snail, a walker and a runner in a line under the sentence]

Or put it beside its neighbours. Slower… faster… There! Now you feel its edges.

[Picture: footprints step by step across the page]

Or follow it step by step, like footprints. Three ways to grow one definition into a paragraph!
