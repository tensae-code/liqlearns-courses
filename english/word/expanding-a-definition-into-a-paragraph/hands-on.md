# Hands-On — Expanding a Definition into a Paragraph

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Three ways, one word.** Take one word from your science or geography study. Write its sentence definition, then three short paragraphs: one by example, one by comparison, one by process. Staple them together as a set.
2. **Way-spotting.** In a textbook you own, find a paragraph that defines something. Decide which of the three ways it uses — or whether it mixes ways — and mark the sentences that do the work.
3. **Partner test.** Read only your example paragraph to a partner, without the word. Can they name the word from the example alone? If not, sharpen the example.
