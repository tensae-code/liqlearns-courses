# Lesson plan — State and Constitution Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** State and Constitution Words
**Lesson:** state-and-constitution-words — the words of a country's own house: its constitution and its opening words, its national symbols, its leaders — president, governor, mayor — and being prepared when the earth shakes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name the constitution, the national symbols and the country's leaders, and say what preparedness means when an emergency comes.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 11 book teaches the state itself as English vocabulary: the constitution's preamble, the national symbols, the ladder of leaders, and — because Venezuela knows earthquakes — preparedness. No lesson in the course housed this set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **The constitution and its symbols**
2. **Leaders and being prepared**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "The constitution and its symbols": name the constitution, its preamble and the national symbols
- after sub lesson "Leaders and being prepared": name the ladder of leaders — president, governor, mayor — and say what earthquake preparedness asks
