## Short answer

1. Name two national symbols. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any two of: the flag, the anthem, the coat of arms.

2. Name the three leaders in their steps, biggest place first. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** President (nation), governor (state/region), mayor (town/city).

3. What does preparedness mean in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Knowing what to do — where to go, what to carry, how to stay calm — in an earthquake or emergency.
