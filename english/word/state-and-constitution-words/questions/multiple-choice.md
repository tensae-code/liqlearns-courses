## Multiple choice

1. A country's book of rules is its… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) anthem  b) constitution  c) preamble only
   **Answer: b) constitution.**

2. The preamble is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the constitution's opening words  b) a flag  c) a town leader
   **Answer: a).**

3. A mayor leads… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the nation  b) a state  c) a town or city
   **Answer: c) a town or city.**

4. A governor leads… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a state or region  b) one school  c) the nation
   **Answer: a) a state or region.**
