# Video script — State and Constitution Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A flag rises; a big book opens beneath it.]

"A country writes its rules in one book: the constitution."

"It opens with a preamble. It flies a flag. It sings an anthem."

"A president leads the nation. A governor, a state. A mayor, a town."

"And a wise country is prepared — ready when the earth shakes."
