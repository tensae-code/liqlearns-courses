# Hands-on — World Landmarks

Landmarks are learned by placing them. Do these out loud, with
a partner.

## Point and name

One partner points at a country on the world map — the United
Kingdom, Russia, Egypt, China, Brazil, the United Arab
Emirates. The other names its landmark and says what it is:
"Egypt — the Giza Pyramids. They are very old stone tombs."

## Our landmark

Choose the most famous place in your own country together.
Present it the lesson's way: its name, what it is, and where it
stands. Then tell your partner why people visit it.
