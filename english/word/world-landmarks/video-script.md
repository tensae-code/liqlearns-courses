# Video script — World Landmarks

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands by a world map.] "Some buildings are famous
all over the world!"

[The map flies to London; a clock tower appears.] "BIG BEN! A
great clock tower — in London!"

[The map flies to Egypt; three stone triangles appear by a
river.] "THE GIZA PYRAMIDS! Very, very old — in Egypt!"

[The map flies to China; a long wall runs over hills.] "THE
GREAT WALL! It runs and runs — in China!"

[The map flies to Dubai; a tower rises past the clouds.] "THE
BURJ KHALIFA! The tallest building in the world!"

[Back to the mascot by the map, all six names around him.]
"Six landmarks. Six countries. Now you know them!"
