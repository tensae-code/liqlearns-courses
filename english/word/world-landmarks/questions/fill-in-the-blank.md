## Fill in the blank

1. "St Basil's is a church with many coloured ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: domes** — its round tops look like onions.

2. "The Great Wall is a very long ___ in China." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: wall** — it runs over hills and mountains for thousands of kilometres.

3. "The Giza Pyramids stand near the river ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Nile** — the pyramids stand at Giza, near the Nile.

4. "Christ the Redeemer stands on a ___ over Rio de Janeiro." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: mountain** — the big statue watches over the city from the mountain.

5. "The Burj Khalifa is the ___ building in the world." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: tallest** — no building in the world is taller.
