## True or false

1. Big Ben is a clock tower in London. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. The Giza Pyramids are in China. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the Giza Pyramids are in Egypt; the Great Wall is in China.

3. St Basil's has many coloured domes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

4. Christ the Redeemer is a statue in Brazil. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

5. The Burj Khalifa is the tallest building in the world. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
