## Multiple choice

1. "Big Ben is a famous ___ in London." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) clock tower  b) statue  c) wall
   **Answer: a) clock tower** — Big Ben is the great clock tower of London.

2. "The Giza Pyramids stand in ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) China  b) Egypt  c) Russia
   **Answer: b) Egypt** — the pyramids stand at Giza, in Egypt, near the Nile.

3. "St Basil's, with its coloured domes, is in ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Moscow  b) Dubai  c) Rio de Janeiro
   **Answer: a) Moscow** — St Basil's is the church with coloured domes in Moscow.

4. "The Great Wall runs over hills and mountains in ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Brazil  b) the United Kingdom  c) China
   **Answer: c) China** — the Great Wall is China's very long wall.

5. "Christ the Redeemer is a big statue in ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Brazil  b) Egypt  c) Russia
   **Answer: a) Brazil** — the statue stands over Rio de Janeiro, in Brazil.

6. "The Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world, is in ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) London  b) Dubai  c) Moscow
   **Answer: b) Dubai** — the Burj Khalifa stands in Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates.
