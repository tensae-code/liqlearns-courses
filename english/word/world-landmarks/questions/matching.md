## Matching

Match the landmark to its country. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Big Ben — c
2. The Great Wall — a
3. The Giza Pyramids — e
4. Christ the Redeemer — b
5. The Burj Khalifa — d

a) China
b) Brazil
c) The United Kingdom
d) The United Arab Emirates
e) Egypt

**Answers:** 1–c (Big Ben — the United Kingdom), 2–a (the Great Wall —
China), 3–e (the Giza Pyramids — Egypt), 4–b (Christ the Redeemer —
Brazil), 5–d (the Burj Khalifa — the United Arab Emirates).
