## Short answer

1. What is a landmark? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A famous building or structure that everyone knows.

2. What is Big Ben? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The great clock tower in London.

3. What are the Giza Pyramids? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Very old stone triangles in Egypt, built as tombs for kings.

4. Which landmark is the tallest building in the world, and where is it? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The Burj Khalifa, in Dubai (in the United Arab Emirates).

5. Name the landmark that stands over Rio de Janeiro, and say what it is. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Christ the Redeemer — a very big statue of Christ with its arms open wide.
