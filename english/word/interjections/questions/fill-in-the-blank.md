## Fill in the blank

1. "An interjection shows a sudden ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: feeling** — joy, surprise, pain, sorrow.

2. "'___!' We won the match!" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Hurrah** — the joy word.

3. "'___!' That thorn hurt." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Ouch** — the pain word.

4. "A strong interjection takes an ___ mark." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: exclamation.**

5. "An interjection dropped inside a sentence is set off by ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: commas.**
