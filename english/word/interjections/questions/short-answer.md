## Short answer

1. Name an interjection for surprise and one for sorrow. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Surprise: Wow! / Oh! Sorrow: Alas!

2. Why does an interjection stand apart from its sentence? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** It is a burst of feeling thrown into the speech, not a working part of the sentence's grammar.

3. Write a sentence with an interjection dropped inside it. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any sentence with the interjection inside, set off by commas — e.g. "The boat is, alas, already gone."

4. When does an interjection take a comma instead of an exclamation mark? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** When the feeling is soft rather than a full burst — "Well, I think so."
