## Multiple choice

1. What is an interjection? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A long explaining word  b) A word thrown into speech to show a sudden feeling  c) A naming word
   **Answer: b)** — one word, one burst of feeling.

2. Which word shows pain? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Hurrah!  b) Ouch!  c) Wow!
   **Answer: b) Ouch!**

3. Which mark usually follows a strong interjection? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A question mark  b) A full stop only  c) An exclamation mark
   **Answer: c) An exclamation mark** — !

4. In "It is, alas, too late", the interjection is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Dropped inside the sentence, set off by commas  b) Missing  c) The whole sentence
   **Answer: a)** — an interjection can live inside a sentence, set off by commas.

5. How many interjections should a page of writing carry? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) One on every line  b) None ever  c) A sparing few, where the feeling truly bursts
   **Answer: c)** — a burst on every line stops sounding like feeling.
