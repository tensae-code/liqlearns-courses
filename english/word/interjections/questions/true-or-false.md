## True or false

1. An interjection is joined tightly to the grammar of its sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it stands apart; the sentence goes on without it.

2. "Wow!" shows surprise. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. An interjection is usually one word. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

4. Every sentence should begin with an interjection. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — use them sparingly, where the feeling is real.

5. A soft interjection can take a comma instead of an exclamation mark. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — "Well, I think so."
