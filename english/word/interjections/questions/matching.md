## Matching

Match the interjection to its feeling. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Hurrah! — c
2. Ouch! — a
3. Wow! — e
4. Alas! — b
5. Hush! — d

a) Pain
b) Sorrow
c) Joy
d) A call for quiet
e) Surprise

**Answers:** 1–c (joy), 2–a (pain), 3–e (surprise), 4–b (sorrow),
5–d (a call for quiet).
