# Hands-on — Interjections

## Feeling cards

Cards read: "You won the prize!" — "You stepped on a thorn"
— "Your friend is back!" — "The rain took the roof." Answer
with the interjection only, in the voice of the feeling. The
group must name the feeling from your word alone.

## One burst only

Each learner writes a five-line story with exactly ONE
interjection in it, placed where the feeling truly bursts.
Read them aloud; the class votes on whose single burst lands
hardest — and why.
