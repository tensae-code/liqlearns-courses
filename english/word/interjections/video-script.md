# Video script — Interjections

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A book drops — clatter! The mascot jumps.] "OH!"

[He holds up the word.] "A word that BURSTS OUT is an…
INTERJECTION!"

[Joy, surprise, pain flash by.] "HURRAH! WOW! OUCH!"

[Back to the mascot, one finger up.] "One burst is enough.
Use it where the feeling is REAL!"
