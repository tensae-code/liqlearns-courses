# Hands-On — Writing the Date

Things you do with real letters, real dates and a pen. No special materials needed.

1. **Two ways.** Write today's date on paper twice: the British way (day – month – year) and the American way (month – day – year). Say each one aloud as you write it.
2. **Comma catch.** Write a sentence about your birth with the date inside it. Read it slowly and tap the desk at each comma: one before the date's year, one after it.
3. **Format hunt.** Find three real documents — a letter, an invitation, an invoice or an official paper. For each one: which date format is used, and is it the format that belongs in that kind of document?
4. **The trick date.** Write 04/07/2026 on a card. Ask a partner: "What day is this?" Then show them both readings — 4 July (British) and April 7 (American) — and write the month in words to fix it.
5. **One style letter.** Write a short formal letter (five lines is enough) with the date at the top, fully written out. Check the whole letter: one style only, no mixing.
