# Levels — Writing the Date

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Writing a date inside a sentence | Write a date inside a sentence with its commas in the right places, write a date in full, tell ordinal from cardinal numbers, and keep one style through a whole document. |
| 2. British and American formats | Write the same date in the British day-month-year order and the American month-day-year order, move between the fully written-out and the short numeric formats, and say which format fits an invitation, a memo, an invoice or an official document. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can write any date correctly in a formal letter or a sentence, in either system, and never mix the two.
