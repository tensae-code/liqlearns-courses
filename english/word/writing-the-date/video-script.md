# Video Script — Writing the Date

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each date appears on screen as it is said.

[Picture: the top corner of a formal letter, with a date written out in full]

Look — a letter. At the top is the date. It is written out in full. Not just numbers!

[Picture: a sentence with a date inside it, the two commas glowing]

A date inside a sentence sits between commas. "She was born on May 3, 2010, in Paramaribo." Comma… date… comma!

[Picture: two flags side by side — the British way and the American way]

British way: day, month, year — 3 May 2026. American way: month, day, year — May 3, 2026. Same day! Two orders!

[Picture: the short numbers 03/05/2026 with a big question mark]

Careful! Short numbers can trick you. 03/05 — is it 3 May, or March 5? When it matters, write the month in words!

[Picture: an invitation with a beautiful written-out date]

Invitations and formal letters: write it out in full. Invoices: short numbers are fine. Pick one style — and keep it!
