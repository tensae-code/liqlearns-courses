# Video Script — Immigration and Living Abroad

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each scene appears as it is said.

[Picture: a family with bags at an airport, waving goodbye]

This family is leaving their country. They will live in a new country. This is immigration.

[Picture: the family in a snowy street, looking at signs they cannot read]

A new country is hard at first. New language. New rules. New weather! This is called adjusting.

[Picture: a closed door with a "no" sign, then the same door opening]

Some people are not fair to newcomers. That is discrimination. It is a problem — and we name it.

[Picture: the family a year later — at work, at school, sharing their food and music with neighbours]

And later? They work. They learn. They give new food, new music, new strength to their new country. Both sides are the true story.
