# Lesson Plan — Immigration and Living Abroad

**One thing:** immigration as a topic — the problems that come with it (adjusting to a new country, discrimination), and life abroad: the experiences and expectations of the people who live there, and what they contribute to their new country.

## Sub lessons

1. Leaving and arriving — immigration and its problems: adjustment problems, discrimination
2. Living abroad — experiences, expectations and contributions of people living in another country

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Immigration = moving to another country to live there. The book treats it as the travel theme of the second secondary year — travel that does not come back.
- The problems the programme names: **adjustment problems** (settling into a new language, new rules, new ways) and **discrimination** (being treated unfairly for where you come from).
- The programme's ground: Haitians living in the USA and Canada — their **experiences** (what life there is really like), their **expectations** (what they hope for), and their **contributions** (what they give to American life).
- The topic is taught for discussion: identify the problems, discuss them, and set them beside what immigrants bring.
