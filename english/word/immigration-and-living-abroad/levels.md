# Levels — Immigration and Living Abroad

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Leaving and arriving | Say what immigration is and name and explain its two big problems: adjustment problems and discrimination. |
| 2. Living abroad | Talk about life abroad in the programme's three parts — experiences, expectations and contributions — with the Haitians of the USA and Canada as the standing example. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can discuss immigration whole: why people leave, what is hard when they arrive, and what they build where they land.
