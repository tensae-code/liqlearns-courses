# Video script — History and Independence Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[An old map of South America; flags rise over it one by one.]

"This land was ruled from far away. A colony."

"Then people rose — a movement. Heroes led them."

"Bolívar — the Liberator. The lands were freed: liberation."

"And republics were born."
