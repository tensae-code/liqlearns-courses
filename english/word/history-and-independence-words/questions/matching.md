## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 2)

1. colony
2. movement
3. liberation
4. republic
5. hero

a) a land ruled from far away
b) a country ruled by its own people
c) many people acting together
d) the freeing of a land
e) a person remembered for a great deed

**Answers:** 1–a, 2–c, 3–d, 4–b, 5–e.
