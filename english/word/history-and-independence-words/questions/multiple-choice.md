## Multiple choice

1. Simón Bolívar is remembered as… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the Liberator  b) a poet only  c) a king
   **Answer: a) the Liberator.**

2. A land ruled from far away is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) republic  b) colony  c) movement
   **Answer: b) colony.**

3. When a movement wins and the land is freed, that is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) liberation  b) a census  c) a colony
   **Answer: a) liberation.**

4. A country ruled by its own people is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) colony  b) republic  c) province of another land
   **Answer: b) republic.**
