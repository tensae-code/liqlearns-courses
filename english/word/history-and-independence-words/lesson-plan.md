# Lesson plan — History and Independence Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** History and Independence Words
**Lesson:** history-and-independence-words — the heroes and movements of Latin American independence — the people who fought, the lands they freed, and the words their story carries
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name Latin America's independence heroes and movements and tell, in short, what they did.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 11 book turns to history: the heroes and movements that freed Latin America, taught as an English word set. No lesson in the course housed the independence set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Heroes of independence**
2. **Movements that freed countries**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Heroes of independence": name the heroes of Latin American independence and what each is remembered for
- after sub lesson "Movements that freed countries": talk about independence movements: colonies, liberation and the new republics

## What you need
Nothing special — a map of Latin America helps if one is near.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — the learner meets the words in a doing task before any explaining.
2. **ADD IMAGE** — a picture for the lesson's world, when the picture pass reaches this lesson.
3. **Sub lesson 1 — Heroes of independence.**
4. **Sub lesson 2 — Movements that freed countries.**
5. **ADD VIDEO** — the filmed run of this lesson, when the video pass reaches it.

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
One hero, one land, one deed at a time; the four story-words come after the faces.
