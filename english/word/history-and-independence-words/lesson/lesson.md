# History and Independence Words

## Do it first

Every free country was once freed by somebody. Who freed yours? What were they called? This lesson teaches the English words of Latin America's independence — its heroes and its movements.

## Sub lesson 1 — Heroes of independence

Latin America was freed by named people. **Simón Bolívar** — the Liberator, who freed the north of South America, Venezuela among its lands. Around him stand the other **heroes** of the **independence** movements — generals, thinkers and fighters whose names their countries still carry on streets, plazas and money.

A hero in this lesson is a history word: a person, a fight, a freed land. Learn the names with their lands.

*Difficulty: Names tied to their lands and deeds.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Movements that freed countries

A land ruled from far away is a **colony**. Its people rise in a **movement** — many acting together. When the movement wins, the land is **liberated** — freed — and becomes a **republic**, ruled by its own people.

Colony, movement, liberation, republic: four words, and the whole story of a continent fits inside them.

*Difficulty: Four story-words that carry the whole arc.*

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion about one independence hero: their name, their land, and what they did.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write one hero's name, land and deed in two or three sentences.
2. Write the four story-words with one line each.
3. Ask someone at home to name a hero of your country's history. Write the name and one deed in English.
