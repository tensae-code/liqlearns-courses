**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (China).**

This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-08
re-verification sweep of China's English books. The sweep
re-read all 9 held books page by page (462 teaching items;
224 already in the course) and rebuilt the placements from
the PDFs themselves; these five parts — the three word
sets of China's opening primary units — had no lesson of
their own. school-subjects houses the subjects, the-town
houses a town's rooms; the school's own things were
homeless. Under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06)
they get their own lesson.

Searchable tags: the books' own unit names for these parts
are "Hello!" (Grade 3 Volume 1, Unit 1), "My classroom"
(Grade 4 Volume 1, Unit 1) and "My school" (Grade 4 Volume
2, Unit 1) — kept here as tags, not as the lesson's title,
which names the thing itself.
