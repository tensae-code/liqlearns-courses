# Hands-on — School and Classroom Things

Hands teach these words best. Do these with real things, not only
pictures.

## The bag game

Empty your school bag on the table. Name every thing as it comes
out: "A ruler. A pencil. An eraser…" Then close your eyes. Your
partner takes one thing away. Open your eyes — what is missing?
Name it in a whole sentence: "I have a pen. I have a pencil. Where
is my eraser?"

## Classroom hunt

Stand in a real classroom. Your partner names a thing —
"Blackboard!" — and you run to it, touch it, and name it back:
"Blackboard!" Ten things, then swap. The last three rounds, name
the thing AND say where it is: "The fan is on the wall."

## The school walk

Walk around your school with a partner. At every room, stop and
name it together: "The library." "The gym." "The music room." Go
up the stairs and name the floors: "The first floor. The second
floor." Finish outside: "The playground. The garden."
