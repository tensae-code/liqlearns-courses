## Fill in the blank

1. You rub out a pencil line with an ______. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: eraser** — the eraser rubs out pencil lines, and it takes "an".

2. I have a ______ for drawing straight lines. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: ruler** — the ruler draws straight lines and measures.

3. The teacher writes on the ______. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: blackboard** — the big writing board of the classroom.

4. Open the ______ and let the light in. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: window** — the window lets the light in.

5. We play outside in the ______. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: playground** — the school's open place for play.

6. We draw and paint in the ______ room. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: art** — the art room is for drawing and painting.
