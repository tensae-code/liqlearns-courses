## True or false

1. An eraser is one of the school things you carry. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — it is in the bag with the pencil, pen and ruler.

2. A bag is a room in the school. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a bag is a thing you carry; the school's rooms are the library, the gym and the rest of sub lesson 3's set.

3. The blackboard is in the classroom. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — it is the classroom's big writing board.

4. A fan is one of the school's rooms. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a fan is a thing in the classroom; it moves the air when the room is hot.

5. The library is a room in the school. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the library is the school's room of books.

6. The gym is where you borrow books. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — you borrow books in the library; the gym is the big room for sport and exercise.
