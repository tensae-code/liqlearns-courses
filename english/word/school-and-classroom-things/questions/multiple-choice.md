## Multiple choice

1. You draw a straight line with a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) crayon  b) ruler  c) eraser
   **Answer: b) ruler** — a ruler is the flat stick for straight lines and measuring.

2. "I have ___ eraser." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a  b) an  c) the bags
   **Answer: b) an** — eraser starts with a vowel sound, so it takes "an".

3. Your pencils and pens live in a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) pencil box  b) book  c) bag only, never a box
   **Answer: a) pencil box** — the pencil box is where pencils and pens live.

4. The big board the teacher writes on is the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) window  b) floor  c) blackboard
   **Answer: c) blackboard** — the blackboard is the classroom's writing board.

5. The teacher's table is the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) teacher's desk  b) wall  c) fan
   **Answer: a) teacher's desk** — the desk that is the teacher's.

6. Light comes into the classroom through the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) door only when it is shut  b) window  c) picture
   **Answer: b) window** — the window lets the light in.

7. You read and borrow books in the… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) gym  b) garden  c) library
   **Answer: c) library** — the library is the school's room of books.

8. You sing and play music in the… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) music room  b) art room  c) teachers' office
   **Answer: a) music room** — the room for singing and music.

9. Up the stairs from the first floor is the… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) playground  b) second floor  c) computer room
   **Answer: b) second floor** — the floor above the first floor.
