## Matching

Match the school thing to what it does. (Sub lesson 1)

1. ruler — b
2. pencil — d
3. eraser — a
4. crayon — e
5. pen — c
6. pencil box — f

a) rubs out pencil lines
b) draws straight lines and measures
c) writes in ink
d) writes and draws, and can be rubbed out
e) a coloured stick for drawing and colouring
f) where pencils and pens live

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–b (straight lines), 2–d (pencil work rubs out), 3–a (rubbing out), 4–e (colour), 5–c (ink), 6–f (the pencils' home).

Match the classroom thing or school room to its place or job. (Sub lessons 2–3)

1. blackboard — g
2. window — c
3. teacher's desk — a
4. fan — e
5. library — b
6. gym — h
7. music room — d
8. playground — f

a) the teacher's table in the classroom
b) the room of books — read and borrow there
c) lets the light into the classroom
d) sing and play music there
e) moves the air when the room is hot
f) the open place outside for play
g) the big board the teacher writes on
h) the big room for sport and exercise

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–g (the writing board), 2–c (light), 3–a (the teacher's), 4–e (air), 5–b (books), 6–h (sport), 7–d (music), 8–f (play outside).
