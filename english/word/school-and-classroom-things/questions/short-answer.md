## Short answer

1. Name three things you carry in your school bag. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any three of: ruler, pencil, eraser, crayon, bag, pen, pencil box, book — the eight school things of the set.

2. Say one whole sentence about a thing you have, starting "I have…" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** "I have a pen." (Any true sentence with a/an and one of the eight things — "an" only for eraser.)

3. Name two things in a classroom that are part of the room itself — not things you carry. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: window, blackboard, light, door, wall, floor — the classroom's own parts.

4. What is the teacher's desk? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The desk (table) in the classroom that is the teacher's.

5. Which room of the school is full of computers? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** The computer room.

6. Name the school's two outdoor places from this lesson. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** The playground and the garden.
