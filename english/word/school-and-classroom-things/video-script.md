# Video script — School and Classroom Things

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot empties a school bag on the table, one thing at a
time.] "School things! A ruler. A pencil. An eraser. A crayon. A
pen. A pencil box. A book. And the bag! I have a pencil. I have an
eraser. What do you have?"

[A classroom picture; the mascot points at each thing.] "In the
classroom! The blackboard. A window. A door. A light. A picture.
The wall. The floor. The teacher's desk. A computer. A fan."

[A walk through a school; each room named as the camera arrives.]
"Rooms in the school! The library. The gym. The music room. The
art room. The computer room. The teachers' office. The
playground. The garden. Up, up — the second floor!"
