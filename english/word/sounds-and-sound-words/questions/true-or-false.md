## True or false

1. A bee buzzes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — buzzing is the bee's sound.

2. My feet go ring. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — feet go boom; a phone goes ring.

3. We clap with our feet. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — we clap with our hands; our feet go boom.

4. A drum goes bang. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — one loud hit on a drum is a bang.

5. A phone goes boom. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a phone goes ring; boom is the feet's sound.
