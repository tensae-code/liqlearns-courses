## Short answer

1. What sound do your hands make? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: A clap.** — Hands clap.

2. What goes boom? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: My feet.** — "My feet go boom!"

3. What goes ring? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: A phone.** — "My phone goes ring!" (A bell rings too.)

4. Say the bee's sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "A bee buzzes."** — buzz is the bee's sound word.

5. A drum is hit once, loudly. What is the sound word? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Bang.** — A drum goes bang.
