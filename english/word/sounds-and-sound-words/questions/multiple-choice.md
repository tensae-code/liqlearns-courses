## Multiple choice

1. The sound your hands make when they hit each other is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) buzz  b) clap  c) boom
   **Answer: b) clap** — hands clap.

2. "My feet go ___!" (Sub lesson 1)
   a) boom  b) ring  c) buzz
   **Answer: a) boom** — feet stamp: boom, boom!

3. A phone makes the sound… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) bang  b) clap  c) ring
   **Answer: c) ring** — a phone goes ring (a bell rings too).

4. One loud, sudden hit on a drum goes… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) buzz  b) bang  c) ring
   **Answer: b) bang** — a drum goes bang.

5. A bee makes the sound… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) buzz  b) boom  c) clap
   **Answer: a) buzz** — a bee buzzes.

6. "___ your hands!" (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Buzz  b) Ring  c) Clap
   **Answer: c) Clap** — we clap our hands.

7. Which sentence is right? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) A bee rings.  b) A bee buzzes.  c) A bee booms.
   **Answer: b) A bee buzzes** — buzzing is the bee's sound.

8. "My phone goes ___! Someone is calling." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) ring  b) clap  c) bang
   **Answer: a) ring** — phones ring.

9. Which pair goes together? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) drum — buzz  b) feet — clap  c) drum — bang
   **Answer: c) drum — bang** — a drum goes bang.

10. You stamp your feet hard on the floor. The sound word is… (Sub lesson 2)
    a) buzz  b) boom  c) ring
    **Answer: b) boom** — "My feet go boom!"
