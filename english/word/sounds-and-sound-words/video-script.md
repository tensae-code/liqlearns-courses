# Video script — Sounds and Sound Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the sounds named here are heard as he speaks.

[The mascot claps his hands.] “Clap! My hands go clap!”

[He stamps his feet twice.] “Boom! My feet go boom!”

[A phone rings beside him; he points at it.] “Ring! My phone goes ring!”

[He taps a big drum once.] “Bang! My drum goes bang!”

[A bee buzzes past in a loop; he follows it with his eyes.] “Buzz! A bee buzzes.”

[All five makers in a row: hands, feet, phone, drum, bee.] “Clap, boom, ring, bang, buzz. Five sound words!”
