# Hands-on — Sounds and Sound Words

Ears teach sounds best. Do these with real sounds, not only pictures.

## Make the sounds

Clap your hands. Tap a cup or a pot for a bang. Hum like a bee for a buzz. Say each word while you make its sound — the ear learns the sound and the mouth learns the word at the same time.

## Sound hunt at home

Close your eyes while a partner makes a sound in the room — a clap, a tap, a hum. Guess what made it, and answer with the sound word: "That is a bang!" Then change places.
