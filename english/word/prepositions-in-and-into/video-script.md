# Video script — Prepositions In and Into

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the ball.
The ball is **in** the goal.
It is not moving. It is inside. **In.**

Now — I throw it!
I throw the ball **into** the hoop!
It moves — outside, then inside. **Into!**

Kick the ball — **into** the goal!
The ball flies. It lands inside. **Into.**

And now it rests.
The ball is **in** the goal again.

Your turn. Moving inside? **Into.**
Already inside? **In.**
Say it with the ball!
