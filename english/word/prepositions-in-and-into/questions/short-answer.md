# Short answer — Prepositions In and Into

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — In, already inside · Sub lesson 2 — Into, moving inside · Sub lesson 3 — In or into, the choosing test. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "The ball is in the goal," is the ball moving or resting?
**Answer: resting** — in tells where a thing already is.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does "in" tell us about a thing?
**Answer: where it is — inside, and staying there.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Kick the ball into the goal," where does the ball end?
**Answer: inside the goal** — into walks the ball from outside to inside. (Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 33, printed pp.21–22.)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does "into" add to "in"?
**Answer: the movement** — into is in with the journey to the inside left in it.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence says: "She puts the ball ___ the hoop." Moving or already there — and so which word?
**Answer: moving — into.** The hand carries the ball inside as she puts it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the choosing test in your own words.
**Answer: ask the verb — if the thing is moving to the inside, use into; if it is already inside, use in.**
