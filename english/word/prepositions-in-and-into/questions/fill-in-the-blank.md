# Fill in the blank — Prepositions In and Into

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — In, already inside · Sub lesson 2 — Into, moving inside · Sub lesson 3 — In or into, the choosing test. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Fill each gap with in or into.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ball is ___ the goal. (It rests there.)
**Answer: in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hoop holds the ball ___ it after the throw.
**Answer: in**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Throw the ball ___ the hoop!
**Answer: into** (the chapter's own example — Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 33, printed p.21)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** He kicked the ball ___ the goal.
**Answer: into**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** She drops the paper ball ___ the box, and there it lies ___ the box.
**Answer: into; in** — first the movement, then the resting place.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The player jumps ___ the marked square and then stands ___ it, waiting.
**Answer: into; in** — the jump is a movement ending inside; the standing is resting inside.
