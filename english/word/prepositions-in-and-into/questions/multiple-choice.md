# Multiple choice — Prepositions In and Into

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — In, already inside · Sub lesson 2 — Into, moving inside · Sub lesson 3 — In or into, the choosing test. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Albania re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ball is resting inside the goal. "The ball is ___ the goal."
a) into  b) in  c) through  d) out of
**Answer: b) in** — the ball is already inside and not moving; in is the word for position.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sentence uses "in" the right way?
a) She threw the ball in the hoop as it flew.  b) The ball sits in the hoop.  c) Kick the ball in the goal — it is flying.  d) He jumps in the air into the box.
**Answer: b)** — sitting is position, not movement, so in.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Throw the ball ___ the hoop!"
a) in  b) into  c) on rest  d) inside-rest
**Answer: b) into** — the throw moves the ball from outside to inside. (The chapter's own example: Albania, Akelius A2 Guide, Chapter 33, printed pp.21–22.)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which verb goes with "into" in the sports chapter's examples?
a) rests  b) sits  c) kick  d) is
**Answer: c) kick** — "kick the ball into the goal": the kick moves the ball inside.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The player kicks the ball ___ the goal, and now it rests there." The first gap takes —
a) in, because it rests  b) into, because the kick moves it  c) in, because all balls take in  d) into, because it rests
**Answer: b) into** — the choosing test asks the verb: the kick is a movement ending inside.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "After the goal, the ball lies ___ the goal." The word is —
a) into, because goals move  b) into, because it flew before  c) in, because it is already inside and still  d) through, because it is still
**Answer: c) in** — what the ball did before does not matter; now it rests inside, so in.
