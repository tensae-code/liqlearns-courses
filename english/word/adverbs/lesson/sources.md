**Acceptance count: taught in 17 countries (Bangladesh, Egypt,
Ghana, Libya, China, Indonesia, Iran, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Greece, Kazakhstan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, South Sudan, Nepal).**
Bangladesh carries the definition and the mime
game; Egypt carries the making rule (-ly, y → i) and the
frequency ladder; Libya carries the adjective-to-adverb move
inside a story, with the rebels fast and well; Ghana carries
the first naming and the order the kinds arrive in; China
carries the frequency set as a survey system; Indonesia
carries the full frequency set as a Language Focus and the
manner set with its non--ly rebels; Iran carries the adverb
as a named word kind — manner, the -ly making, the irregulars
— and a second printed frequency ladder; Sierra Leone carries
the adjective-to-adverb change as two lessons of its own and
the function stated plainly. Jordan carries the frequency set twice — Grade 4 with its once/twice/three-times phrases, Grade 8 with ordering work in the Workbook — and the manner set with well among the -ly words; Greece carries the frequency set twice — the five-word set in Grade 4, and the printed percentage ladder with sentence position in Grade 5 — and comparison of adverbs in Grade 6; Kazakhstan carries the frequency words on daily routines and manner adverbs a term later in its Grade 4 Express book, frequency, degree and manner named together in Grade 5 Excel, and adverbs of time, frequency and manner as a named set in Grade 4 Smiles; Iraq carries both kinds in one intermediate book — the frequency set with its position rule in Grade 7 book's Unit 1, and the manner box with the -ly making, y → i and the irregulars fast and well in Unit 3 — with -ly adverbs hunted in a story in Grade 4; Ethiopia carries kinds of adverbs and degrees of adverbs in Grade 6, adverbs of frequency in Grade 9 (taught, then recapped a unit later), adverbs of time and frequency and adverbs of manner in Grade 10, and adverbs and their categories in Grade 11; South Africa carries adverbs answering how/when/where in Grade 3 HL, manner, degree and place with the adjective/adverb contrast in Grade 4 HL, the manner/place/time classification in Grade 5 HL, and the four-kind sort — manner, place, time, frequency — in Grade 6 HL; Syria carries the frequency scale in full — always / usually / often / sometimes / never — inside Grade 3 routines, the frequency words again in Grade 4, and adverbs of manner as a named point in Grade 5; South Sudan carries adverbs of time and comparing adverbs in Primary 4, adverbs of duration and adverbs of manner as Secondary 1's two closing language practices, and adverbs of frequency twice over in Secondary 2; Other
closed countries join as their books are read (see
READING-QUEUE.md).
Nepal joined 2026-11-03: it carries the frequency set in Grade 4 (Unit 5), adjective/adverb formation and the position of adverbs in Grade 11 (Unit 2), and adverbs as a named grammar — frequency adverbs above all — in Grade 12 (Unit 7).
