# Video script — Adverbs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me walk.
Just walk. Nothing special.

Now I walk — **slowly**. Slooowly.
Now I walk — **quickly**! Quick, quick, quick!

The verb is the same: walk.
The new word tells HOW. Slowly. Quickly.
That word is an **adverb**.

She sings — **loudly**!
He writes — **carefully**.
The adverb rides behind the verb.

Making them is easy. Take an adjective: quick.
Add **-ly**: quickly!
Happy? The y runs away — happ**ily**!

Two rebels. Fast stays **fast** — he drives fast!
And good turns into — **well**. She sings well!

Now you. Run — slowly! Sing — loudly! Write — carefully!
Say the how-word. Do it that way.
That is an adverb.
