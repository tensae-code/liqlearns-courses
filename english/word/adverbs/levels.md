# Adverbs — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — a word that describes a verb —
set by the pattern across the countries' books: the how-words
first, then making them from adjectives, then the how-often
ladder.

## Level 1 — how-words
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pair verbs with
their how-words (run quickly, write carefully), pick the adverb
out of a mixed list, and find it after its verb in a sentence
with the test — "How did they do it?"

## Level 2 — making them, and the rebels
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: make an adverb
from any adjective without a miss — add -ly; y changes to i
(happy → happily) — and handle the two rebels the books name:
fast stays fast, good becomes well. Tell an adjective from an
adverb in a pair (a beautiful song / she sang beautifully).

## Level 3 — how often
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer "How
often?" on the ladder — always, usually, sometimes, never —
and place the words in true sentences about your own day, the
way Egypt's frequency ladder teaches them.
(The when / where / degree kinds are named in the later books —
Ghana B4–B6, Egypt's Preparatory box — and are tested when
those levels are built, not here.)
