# Adverbs — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How-words, adverbs that tell how · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The two rebels, fast and well (item 6 fills the rebel "well").

Fill each blank with an adverb from the box.

**Box:** quickly · slowly · loudly · quietly · carefully · well

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The class sings ______ in the morning.
**Answer: loudly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Write your name ______. Do not hurry.
**Answer: carefully.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The baby sleeps. Walk ______.
**Answer: quietly.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The bus came late, so we ran ______.
**Answer: quickly.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The old man walks ______ with his stick.
**Answer: slowly.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** She is a good singer. She sings ______.
**Answer: well** — the rebel: good does not take -ly (Libya,
Grade 8).
(All six pairs are Bangladesh's mime-game sets, Class 5,
p.27: run/write/sing/draw × quickly/slowly/loudly/quietly.)
