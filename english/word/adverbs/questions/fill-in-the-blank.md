# Adverbs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with an adverb from the box.

**Box:** quickly · slowly · loudly · quietly · carefully · well

**1. (Level 1)** The class sings ______ in the morning.
**Answer: loudly.**

**2. (Level 1)** Write your name ______. Do not hurry.
**Answer: carefully.**

**3. (Level 1)** The baby sleeps. Walk ______.
**Answer: quietly.**

**4. (Level 1)** The bus came late, so we ran ______.
**Answer: quickly.**

**5. (Level 1)** The old man walks ______ with his stick.
**Answer: slowly.**

**6. (Level 2)** She is a good singer. She sings ______.
**Answer: well** — the rebel: good does not take -ly (Libya,
Grade 8).
(All six pairs are Bangladesh's mime-game sets, Class 5,
p.27: run/write/sing/draw × quickly/slowly/loudly/quietly.)
