# Adverbs — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How-words, adverbs that tell how · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Making adverbs, add -ly, except item 6 (the rebel good → well), which is Sub lesson 3 — The two rebels, fast and well · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — How often, the frequency ladder.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is an adverb?
a) quick  b) quickly  c) school  d) song
**Answer: b) quickly** — it tells how an action is done. Quick
describes a thing; school and song name.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "He walked quickly to the church," the
adverb is —
a) he  b) walked  c) quickly  d) church
**Answer: c) quickly** — How did he walk? Quickly. (Ghana's own
example, Basic 3.)

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bangladesh's book says an adverb usually comes
—
a) before the noun  b) after the verb  c) at the start of the
book
**Answer: b) after the verb** (Class 5, p.27).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Make the adverb: careful → ______
a) carefuly  b) carefully  c) carefuler
**Answer: b) carefully** — add -ly (Egypt, p.50).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Happy → ______
a) happyly  b) happily  c) happyer
**Answer: b) happily** — y changes to i before -ly (Egypt,
p.50; Libya, Grade 8).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "She is a good singer. She sings ______."
a) good  b) goodly  c) well
**Answer: c) well** — Libya's rebel pair (Grade 8).

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Which word answers "How often?"
a) quickly  b) always  c) carefully
**Answer: b) always** — the frequency ladder (Egypt, Primary 5
Term 1, p.12).
