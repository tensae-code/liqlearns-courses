# Adverbs — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How-words, adverbs that tell how, except item 2 (finding the adverb in a new sentence), which is Sub lesson 4 — The test, how did they do it · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Making adverbs, add -ly, except item 5 (the rebel fast), which is Sub lesson 3 — The two rebels, fast and well · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — How often, the frequency ladder.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An adverb tells us how we do something.
**True** — Egypt's own words (Primary 5 Term 2, p.50).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In "Mercy ate slowly," the adverb is "ate."
**False** — ate is the verb. How did Mercy eat? Slowly —
slowly is the adverb (Ghana, Basic 5).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We make most adverbs by adding -ly to the
adjective.
**True** — quick → quickly, careful → carefully (Egypt,
p.50; Libya, Grade 8).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Happy" becomes "happyly."
**False** — the y changes to i: happily (Egypt's rule).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He drives fastly" is right.
**False** — fast does not change: "He drives fast" (Libya's
rebel, Grade 8).

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** "Never" tells how often something happens.
**True** — never sits at the bottom of the frequency ladder:
not even once (Egypt, Primary 5 Term 1, p.12).
