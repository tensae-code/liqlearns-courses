# Adverbs — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** An adverb tells us how we do something.
**True** — Egypt's own words (Primary 5 Term 2, p.50).

**2. (Level 1)** In "Mercy ate slowly," the adverb is "ate."
**False** — ate is the verb. How did Mercy eat? Slowly —
slowly is the adverb (Ghana, Basic 5).

**3. (Level 2)** We make most adverbs by adding -ly to the
adjective.
**True** — quick → quickly, careful → carefully (Egypt,
p.50; Libya, Grade 8).

**4. (Level 2)** "Happy" becomes "happyly."
**False** — the y changes to i: happily (Egypt's rule).

**5. (Level 2)** "He drives fastly" is right.
**False** — fast does not change: "He drives fast" (Libya's
rebel, Grade 8).

**6. (Level 3)** "Never" tells how often something happens.
**True** — never sits at the bottom of the frequency ladder:
not even once (Egypt, Primary 5 Term 1, p.12).
