# Adverbs — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How-words, adverbs that tell how, except item 2 (asking the test question), which is Sub lesson 4 — The test, how did they do it · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Making adverbs, add -ly, except item 5 (the rebel fast), which is Sub lesson 3 — The two rebels, fast and well · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — How often, the frequency ladder.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an adverb?
**Answer:** a word that describes a verb — it tells how an
action is done (Bangladesh: "Adverbs say how an action is
done," Class 5, p.27).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In "She danced beautifully," ask the test
question and answer it.
**Answer:** How did she dance? — beautifully. Beautifully is
the adverb (Ghana's example, Basic 3).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Make adverbs from: slow, loud, angry.
**Answer:** slowly, loudly, angrily (angry drops its y for i —
Egypt's rule, p.50).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Libya's story pair: "The bird's song was
beautiful. The bird sang ______." Which word goes in the
blank, and which lesson's word is "beautiful" here?
**Answer:** beautifully. Beautiful is the adjective — it
describes the song; beautifully is the adverb — it describes
the singing (Grade 8).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He has a fast car. He drives ______." 
**Answer:** fast — fast does not change (Libya, Grade 8).

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Put these how-often words on the ladder, top
to bottom: sometimes, always, never, usually.
**Answer:** always — usually — sometimes — never (Egypt's
frequency ladder, Primary 5 Term 1, p.12).
