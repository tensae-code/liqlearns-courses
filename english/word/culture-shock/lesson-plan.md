# Lesson Plan — Culture Shock

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** culture shock — the strange feelings of living in a new culture — its stages (honeymoon, crisis, recovery) and the language of getting through it.

## Sub lessons

1. **The stages of culture shock.** *Can-do: Name the stages in the book's text: the honeymoon (everything new is exciting), the crisis (everything new is hard — discrimination named in the same reading), and the recovery that follows.*
2. **Living through it.** *Can-do: Tell and hear the real journey: a migrant's listening account (Berkly, to the USA), the word set of the move — move, stable, willpower, realise, push oneself — and the judgements empathise, sympathy and critical thinking it asks for.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Culture shock (items 434, 435): a reading on the stages of culture shock (honeymoon, crisis, recovery; discrimination in the same reading) and a listening account by a named migrant, Berkly, who moved to the USA — with the words move, stable, willpower, realise, push oneself, empathise, sympathy and critical thinking.
