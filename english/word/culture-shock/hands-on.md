# Hands-On — Culture Shock

1. **Stage sort.** Diary cards from an imaginary migrant sorted into honeymoon, crisis, recovery.
2. **Berkly retell.** After listening-style reading of a migrant account, retell the journey in pairs with the engine words.
3. **Welcome team.** Design a first-week welcome plan for a newcomer to your school — empathy in action.
4. **Custom or unkindness?** Small scenes judged with critical thinking: new custom, discrimination, or misunderstanding?
