# Levels — Culture Shock

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The stages of culture shock | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the stages in the book's text: the honeymoon (everything new is exciting), the crisis (everything new is hard — discrimination named in the same reading), and the recovery that follows. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Living through it | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Tell and hear the real journey: a migrant's listening account (Berkly, to the USA), the word set of the move — move, stable, willpower, realise, push oneself — and the judgements empathise, sympathy and critical thinking it asks for. |
