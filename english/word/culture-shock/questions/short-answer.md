# Short Answer — Culture Shock

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the three stages of culture shock. (Sub lesson 1)
   — The honeymoon, the crisis, and the recovery.
2. Which stage is this: "Nobody laughs at my jokes here, and I miss home"? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The crisis.
3. Use realise in a migrant sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "In the second month, Ana realised the neighbours' loud greetings were kindness, not anger."
4. How can a class empathise with a new student from another country? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Listen to their story, imagine their honeymoon and crisis, learn a few of their words, and help with the school's ways — kindly and without pity.
