# Video script — Toys and Games

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A toy box opens beside the mascot.] "Toys! A BALL! A DOLL! A KITE! Name them with me!"

[The mascot hugs a kite.] "My FAVOURITE toy is my kite. It flies HIGH! What is YOUR favourite toy?"

[A friend holds a board game.] "Sharing is playing too: MAY I BORROW the board game, please? — Yes, of course!"

[The mascot gives the game back.] "Thank you! Ask kindly, play together, give it back."
