# Hands-on — Toys and Games

Real toys are the practice material here.

## Show and tell

Bring one toy (or draw one). Say its name, one thing it does, and whether it is your favourite: "This is a kite. It flies. It is my favourite toy."

## The borrow market

Put three games on a table. Each learner must borrow one — with "May I borrow…?" — play one turn, return it with "Thank you for the game!", and borrow another.
