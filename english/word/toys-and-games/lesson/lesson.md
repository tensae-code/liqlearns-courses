# Toys and Games

## Do it first

Look around: what toys can you see, or picture in your mind? A ball? A doll? Try to name three in English before we start. In this lesson you will learn the toy words, how to talk about your favourite toy, and how to ask a friend to borrow a game.

## Sub lesson 1 — Toy words — naming what we play with

Toys are the things we play with. Learn their names by pointing:

a **ball**, a **doll**, a **kite**, a **toy train**, a **teddy bear**, a **board game**, a **puzzle**, a **car**.

Indonesia's Grade 4 book teaches these inside its toys chapter — words first, by looking and naming. A **game** is play with rules: a board game has a board, pieces, and turns.

Point and name: which of these can fly? Which one rolls? Which one do you hug?

## Sub lesson 2 — My favourite toy

Everyone has a favourite. Say it whole: "**My favourite toy is** my kite. It flies high." "Her favourite toy is a doll."

Give one more sentence — what it does, or why you love it: "My favourite toy is my ball. I play with it every day."

Indonesia's chapter moves from naming to the favourite — the same step this lesson takes, and the same favourite frame as Likes and Hobbies, here for things you can hold.

Say yours now: favourite toy + one more sentence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Borrowing a game — May I borrow…?

Games are for sharing. Ask politely: "**May I borrow** the board game, please?" Kind answers: "Yes, of course." "Sure — here you are." And when it comes back: "Thank you!"

In Indonesia's Grade 4 chapter, this asking lives in the toys chapter itself — borrowing is part of play, not a separate politeness lesson.

Practise the whole exchange with your partner: ask, receive, thank, return.

## Say it to your companion

Show your companion (or picture) your favourite toy. Name three toys you can see in the picture or room, say which is your favourite and why, then ask to borrow your companion's game — politely.

## Do It Yourself

Write four sentences: two naming toys ("This is a…"), one about your favourite toy, and one polite borrowing ("May I borrow…?"). Draw your favourite toy and label three of its parts in English.
