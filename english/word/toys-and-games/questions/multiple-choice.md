## Multiple choice

1. Which toy can fly in the wind on a string? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a doll  b) a kite  c) a puzzle
   **Answer: b) a kite** — a kite flies on a string.

2. A board game is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a game played on a board, with pieces and turns  b) a kind of ball  c) a toy car
   **Answer: a)** — boards, pieces, turns: play with rules.

3. "This is a ___ bear." (the soft toy you hug) (Sub lesson 1)
   a) teddy  b) train  c) board
   **Answer: a) teddy** — a teddy bear is the hugging toy.

4. "My ___ toy is my train." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) every  b) favourite  c) borrow
   **Answer: b) favourite** — favourite names the one you love most.

5. A full favourite sentence is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Favourite ball."  b) "My favourite toy is my ball. I play with it every day."  c) "Ball play."
   **Answer: b)** — favourite sentence + one more sentence about it.

6. The polite way to ask for a friend's game: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Give me the game!"  b) "May I borrow the board game, please?"  c) Take it quietly
   **Answer: b)** — May I borrow…? asks kindly.

7. A kind answer to "May I borrow…?" is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "No, never!"  b) "Yes, of course — here you are."  c) Silence
   **Answer: b)** — sharing answers are kind.
