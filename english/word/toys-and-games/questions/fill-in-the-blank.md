## Fill in the blank

1. A ___ flies in the wind on a string. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: kite** — toy words by what they do.

2. We play a board ___ with pieces and turns. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: game** — a game is play with rules.

3. My favourite ___ is my doll. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: toy** — my favourite toy is…

4. Her favourite toy is a ball. She ___ with it every day. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: plays** — the second sentence says what she does with it.

5. ___ I borrow the board game, please? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: May** — May I borrow…? is the polite ask.

6. "Yes, of course — here you ___." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: are** — the kind giving answer.
