## Matching

Match the toy to what it does. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. a kite — a
2. a ball — b
3. a teddy bear — c
4. a board game — d
5. My favourite toy is my doll. — e
6. May I borrow the puzzle, please? — f

a) flies on a string
b) rolls and bounces
c) you hug it
d) played with pieces and turns
e) naming a favourite
f) asking to borrow

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (a kite flies), 2–b (a ball rolls), 3–c (a teddy is hugged), 4–d (boards, pieces, turns), 5–e (favourite talk), 6–f (May I borrow…? asks).
