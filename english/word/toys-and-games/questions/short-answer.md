## Short answer

1. Name three toys. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "A ball, a doll and a kite."** (any three toy words) — the naming set.

2. Which toy rolls? Which toy can you hug? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "A ball rolls. I can hug a teddy bear."** — toys known by what they do.

3. Say your favourite toy and one thing about it. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "My favourite toy is my train. It is red and long."** — favourite + one more sentence.

4. Ask to borrow a puzzle politely. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "May I borrow the puzzle, please?"** — May I borrow…?

5. Your friend lent you a game and you give it back. What do you say? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "Thank you for the game!"** — borrowing ends with thanks.
