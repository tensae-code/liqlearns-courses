## True or false

1. A kite is played on a board. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a kite flies on a string; boards are for board games.

2. A game is play with rules. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — turns and rules make it a game.

3. "My favourite toy is…" names the toy you like most. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — favourite = the top one.

4. One word — "Ball." — is a full favourite sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — say it whole: "My favourite toy is my ball."

5. "May I borrow…?" is a polite way to ask. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — May I…? asks kindly.

6. Taking a game without asking is borrowing. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — borrowing asks first and gives back.
