# Video script — Happiness and Wellbeing

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A man walks to work, smiling, greeting neighbours.]

“Six hours with people. Seven hours of sleep.”

[Fruit, a football, a helping hand appear.] “Good food. Play. Helping.”

[The mascot smiles.] “The world's happiest person — and his habits are ordinary.”
