# Lesson Plan — Flags and National Symbols

**One thing:** a flag is a symbol of a country, and every part of it — its stripes, its colours, its star — stands for something the country is proud of; and because of that, people honour the flag.

**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (with the other word sets)

## Sub lessons

1. What a flag stands for
2. Honouring the flag

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A flag is a national symbol: a sign of a whole country, and its parts are not decoration — each part stands for something (Grade 1, *Flag Day*).
- Liberia's flag, worked part by part: the red stripes stand for bravery — "being brave"; the white stripes stand for goodness; the star stands for Liberia as the first free country in Africa (Grade 1, *Flag Day*, printed pp.9–13).
- The way to read any flag: name its parts, then ask what each part stands for (Grade 1, *Flag Day*).
- Honouring the flag: people stand for it and put a hand over the heart (Grade 1, *Flag Day*, printed pp.9–13).
- The flag gathers a country's people: one people, working together (Grade 1, *Flag Day*).
