# Levels — Flags and National Symbols

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. What a flag stands for | Say that a flag is a symbol of a country, name a flag's parts, and say what each part stands for — worked on Liberia's flag: red stripes for bravery, white stripes for goodness, the star for the first free country in Africa. |
| 2. Honouring the flag | Show how people honour a flag (standing, a hand over the heart) and say why: the flag stands for the country and gathers its people as one people, working together. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can look at any country's flag and ask the right question — what are its parts, and what does each part stand for?
