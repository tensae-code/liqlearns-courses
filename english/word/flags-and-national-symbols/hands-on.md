# Hands-On — Flags and National Symbols

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, crayons or coloured pencils.

1. **Draw your flag.** Draw the flag of your country as well as you can — its colours, its stripes, its star or picture. Under each part, write what it stands for if you know it. If you do not know, write the question: "What does this part stand for?" — and ask a teacher or a parent.
2. **Draw Liberia's flag.** Red and white stripes and one star. Label the parts: "red — bravery", "white — goodness", "star — the first free country in Africa".
3. **Honour the flag.** Stand up straight. Put your hand over your heart. Stay still and quiet for ten slow counts, the way people honour a flag.
4. **One people.** With a partner, each hold one side of your drawn flag. Say together: "One people, working together." Then each say one job you can do together — carry water, clean the classroom, plant a tree.
