# Video Script — Flags and National Symbols

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each part of the flag is pointed at as it is named.

[Picture: a waving flag with red and white stripes and one star]

This is a flag. A flag is a symbol. It stands for a whole country.

[Picture: a finger on the red stripes]

See the red stripes. Red stands for bravery. Bravery means being brave.

[Picture: a finger on the white stripes]

See the white stripes. White stands for goodness.

[Picture: a finger on the star]

See the star. This is Liberia's flag. The star stands for Liberia — the first free country in Africa.

[Picture: a child standing with a hand over the heart, looking at the flag]

Now we honour the flag. We stand. We put a hand over the heart. One country. One people. Working together.
