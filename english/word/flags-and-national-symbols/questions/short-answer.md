# Short Answer — Flags and National Symbols

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. In your own words, what is a flag? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A symbol — a sign that stands for a whole country and all its people.
2. Name the two stripe colours of Liberia's flag and what each stands for. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Red stands for bravery (being brave); white stands for goodness.
3. What does the star on Liberia's flag stand for? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Liberia as the first free country in Africa.
4. What two things do people do with their body to honour the flag? (Sub lesson 2)
   — They stand, and they put a hand over the heart.
5. Why does a flag matter to a country's people? (Sub lesson 2)
   — It stands for their country, and it gathers them as one people, working together.
6. Pick any part of your own country's flag. Say what the part is — and if you know it, what it stands for. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Any one part named (a colour, a stripe, a star, a picture); a meaning earned by asking or looking it up is the full answer — the lesson's point is to ask what each part stands for.
