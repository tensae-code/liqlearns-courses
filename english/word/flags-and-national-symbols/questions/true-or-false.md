# True or False — Flags and National Symbols

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A flag is a symbol of a country. (Sub lesson 1) — True
2. The parts of a flag stand for nothing at all. (Sub lesson 1) — False
3. In Liberia's flag, the red stripes stand for goodness. (Sub lesson 1) — False (red stands for bravery; white stands for goodness)
4. The star on Liberia's flag stands for Liberia, the first free country in Africa. (Sub lesson 1) — True
5. People honour the flag by standing with a hand over the heart. (Sub lesson 2) — True
6. A flag splits a country's people into enemies. (Sub lesson 2) — False (it gathers them as one people, working together)
