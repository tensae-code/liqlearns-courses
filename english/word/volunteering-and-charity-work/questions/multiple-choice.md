## Multiple choice

1. Volunteering is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) work you do to help, without pay  b) a job with a big salary  c) homework
   **Answer: a).**

2. Blood donation means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) selling blood  b) giving some of your blood to a hospital to save lives  c) a blood test for school
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which is a volunteering action for the environment? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) gathering litter  b) dropping litter  c) buying trees
   **Answer: a).**

4. Charity helps people in… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) uniforms  b) poverty  c) examinations
   **Answer: b).**
