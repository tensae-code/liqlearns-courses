# Video script — Volunteering and Charity Work

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Children plant a small tree behind the mascot.]

"Look — they are helping, and nobody pays them. That is volunteering!"

"Volunteers plant trees. They gather litter. They give blood — that is blood donation."

[The mascot smiles.] "Helping is a word you can DO. What will you do?"
