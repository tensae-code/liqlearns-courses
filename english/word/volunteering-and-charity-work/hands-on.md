# Hands-on — Volunteering and Charity Work

1. **Match the help.** Cards name needs (a dirty park, an old man crossing the road, a hospital needing blood). Match each need to a volunteering action from the lesson, and say the sentence: "Volunteers gather litter."

2. **Our class project.** In pairs, plan one real helping job for your school or street using two lesson words. Present it in two sentences: what you will do, and who it helps.
