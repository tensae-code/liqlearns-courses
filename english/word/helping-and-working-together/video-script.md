# Video Script — Helping and Working Together

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each habit is shown as it is named.

[Picture: two children carrying one big box together]

The box is big. One child cannot carry it. Two children carry it together! Helping each other makes the work easy.

[Picture: a tall child and a short child playing together]

She is tall. He is short. They are different — and they are friends. Different is fine. We care for everyone.

[Picture: a child holding a broken pencil, saying sorry]

Oh no! The pencil is broken. "I'm sorry." Saying sorry is honest. And honest is good.

[Picture: children sharing one plate of fruit]

One plate, many friends. Share! Sharing is caring.
