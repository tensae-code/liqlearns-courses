# Short Answer — Helping and Working Together

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. A big box is too heavy for one child. What can two children do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Carry it together: help each other and work together.
2. Name two ways two classmates can be different. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Any two of: tall/short, fast/slow, different foods, different languages at home, different ways of living.
3. What should you say when you break something that is not yours? (Sub lesson 3)
   — "I'm sorry" — and help fix it if you can.
4. How do you spell "honesty"? (Sub lesson 3)
   — H-O-N-E-S-T-Y.
5. What does sharing show? (Sub lesson 3)
   — Caring: sharing is caring.
