# Levels — Helping and Working Together

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Helping each other | Join in with others, offer help and work together on a shared job. |
| 2. Different and the same | Say how people can be different (short/tall, different ways of living) and treat everyone with care and respect. |
| 3. Honest, sorry, sharing | Tell the truth, say "I'm sorry" when something goes wrong, and share and care in class. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons has the words and habits of a good classmate: help, respect, honesty, sorry and sharing.
