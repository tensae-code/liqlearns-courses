# Hands-On — Helping and Working Together

Things you do with real people in a real classroom or home. No special materials needed.

1. **Carry it together.** Find something a little too big or heavy for one person — a box, a pile of books. Carry it with a partner. Say: "We are working together."
2. **Spot the difference, stay friends.** With a partner, find three ways you are different (tall/short, hair, favourite food) and three ways you are the same. Tell each other both lists kindly.
3. **The sorry practice.** Act a small accident with a partner — a bumped desk, a dropped book. The one who did it says "I'm sorry," and the other answers kindly. Swap roles.
4. **Share it out.** Take one thing that can be shared — fruit, crayons, a ball. Share it out fairly with your group and count what each person gets.
5. **Honest letters.** Spell H-O-N-E-S-T-Y aloud with an action for each letter, then say the rule: "Honest means: don't tell a lie."
