# Lesson Plan — Helping and Working Together

**One thing:** the words and habits of a good classmate — helping each other and working together, treating people kindly when they are different from you, and being honest, saying sorry and sharing.

## Sub lessons

1. Helping each other
2. Different and the same
3. Honest, sorry, sharing

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Helping and working together: approaching others and working with others — co-operation (Prep, Term 4 Week 8).
- People are different and the same: short and tall, different ways of living — care and respect for everyone (Prep, Term 4 Week 3).
- Honesty — H-O-N-E-S-T-Y, don't tell a lie; saying "I'm sorry"; sharing and caring (Prep, Term 4 Week 4).
