## Short answer

1. Name the three big words of the first sub lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Inequality, poverty, society.

2. What can close a job door even to a person who is able? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Having no qualification — the paper or skill the job asks for.

3. Say one sentence about pay using "inequality". (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any true sentence with the word used rightly, e.g. "There is inequality when the same work is paid differently."
