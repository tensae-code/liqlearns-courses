## Multiple choice

1. Things not equal — pay, chances, treatment — is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) society  b) inequality  c) a qualification
   **Answer: b) inequality.**

2. Having too little to live on is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) poverty  b) a salary  c) partnership
   **Answer: a) poverty.**

3. A qualification is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a day off  b) a paper or skill that proves you can do a job  c) a pay envelope
   **Answer: b).**

4. Society means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) one family  b) all the people living together  c) one workplace
   **Answer: b).**
