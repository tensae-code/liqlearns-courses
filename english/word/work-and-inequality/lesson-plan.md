# Lesson plan — Work and Inequality

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Work and Inequality
**Lesson:** work-and-inequality — the words for unequal pay and unequal chances — inequality, poverty, society — and the qualifications that open the door to a job
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can talk about unequal salaries and unequal chances at work, and name what a qualification does.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 11 book teaches unequal salaries head-on: the vocabulary of inequality and poverty, and the way qualifications decide who may enter which job. No lesson in the course housed this set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Words for unequal pay**
2. **Jobs and qualifications**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. jobs and job-interviews stand near it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Words for unequal pay": use inequality, poverty and society to talk about salaries that are not equal
- after sub lesson "Jobs and qualifications": name qualifications and how they open — or close — the door to a job

## What you need
Nothing special.

1. **Do it first** — the learner meets the words in a doing task before any explaining.

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
Anchor the words in one story of two workers; big social words stick to small true scenes.
