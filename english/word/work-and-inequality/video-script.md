# Video script — Work and Inequality

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two pay envelopes side by side; one is thick, one is thin.]

"Same work. Different pay."

"That gap has a name: inequality."

"And a paper can open a door: a qualification."

"Words let us say what is fair — and what is not."
