# Lesson Plan — Expanded Definitions of Words

**Unit:** Unit 8 — Using the Dictionary

**One thing:** building a definition that is fuller than a dictionary gloss — by working a word through organisers, and by the formula term + class + distinguishing characteristics, at sentence level and as a whole paragraph.

## Sub lessons

1. Building an expanded definition
2. The sentence formula and the definition paragraph

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- An expanded definition is fuller than a dictionary gloss: it grows the gloss until the word is fully placed (Lesson 1, Task 4, pp.277–278).
- Vocabulary organisers grow a definition: Trash Can Thesaurus, Shades of Meaning, Tricky Word, Magical Word (p.277).
- The sentence formula: term + class + distinguishing characteristics (Lesson 5, Task 4, p.355).
- The extended definition as a paragraph method, worked on a poem's hard words (Lesson 3, Task 4, p.318).
