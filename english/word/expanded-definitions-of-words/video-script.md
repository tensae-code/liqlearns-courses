# Video Script — Expanded Definitions of Words

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a dictionary page with one short line under the word STORM]

Look at this line. It is short. It tells you a little. But can you SEE the storm? Not yet!

[Picture: the same word with arrows to a line of words: breeze, wind, gale, storm, hurricane]

Now we line the words up. Weak… stronger… stronger… STORM! Now the word has a place.

[Picture: three boxes labelled TERM, CLASS, WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT]

A full definition has three parts. The word. Its family. And what makes it different. Say it with me: term, class, different!

[Picture: a paragraph growing under the three boxes]

And the same three parts can grow… into a whole paragraph. That is an expanded definition!
