# Short Answer — Expanded Definitions of Words

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Why is a dictionary gloss not always enough for a writer? (Sub lesson 1)
   — It is kept short on purpose — enough to recognise the word, but it does not fully place the word among its neighbours or mark what sets it apart; writing and speaking often need that fuller placing.

2. What does the Magical Word organiser do? (Sub lesson 1)
   — It follows the word into real sentences and watches what the word does there, so the definition is built from the word in use.

3. Write the three parts of the sentence formula, in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Term (the word itself) + class (the family it belongs to) + distinguishing characteristics (what sets it apart inside that family).

4. A learner defines 'a drought is a long period with little or no rain'. Which formula parts are present? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Term (drought) and distinguishing characteristics (long, little or no rain); the class (a period of weather / a spell of dry weather) is only implied — a full formula names it.
