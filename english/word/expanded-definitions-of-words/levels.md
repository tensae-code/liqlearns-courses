# Levels — Expanded Definitions of Words

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Building an expanded definition | Work a word through vocabulary organisers (Trash Can Thesaurus, Shades of Meaning, Tricky Word, Magical Word) to build a definition fuller than a dictionary gloss. |
| 2. The sentence formula and the definition paragraph | Expand a word into a sentence definition by the formula term + class + distinguishing characteristics, and write the extended definition as a paragraph. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: building a definition that is fuller than a dictionary gloss — by working a word through organisers, and by the formula term + class + distinguishing characteristics, at sentence level and as a whole paragraph.
