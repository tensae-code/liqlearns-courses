# Hands-On — Expanded Definitions of Words

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Grow a gloss.** Pick a word from your current reading. Copy its dictionary gloss, then run the word through Shades of Meaning and Tricky Word on paper. Write the expanded definition and read both versions to a partner: which one lets them picture the word's exact place?
2. **Formula drill.** With a partner, take turns: one names a word (volcano, harbour, whisper, drought…), the other says a sentence definition by the formula — term, class, distinguishing characteristics — without writing. Swap every word.
3. **Definition paragraph.** Choose the hardest word from a text you are studying and write its extended definition as a paragraph: the formula sentence first, then the supporting detail the book's method adds.
