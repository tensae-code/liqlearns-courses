# Video script — Choosing your register

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Same message. Different clothes!

To a friend: 'Give me the book.' Easy words.

To an office: 'I would like to request the book.' Formal words!

That choice has a name. Register.

At home, your own dialect. Warm. Yours.

At school, at work — standard English opens the doors. Switch! Both are yours.

And be soft when you must. 'It seems…' 'Perhaps…' That is hedging.

Not sure of the name? 'Stuff.' 'Things.' Vague words — on purpose!

Choose the clothes. Then speak.
