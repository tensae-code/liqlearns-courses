**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Barbados, Jordan, South Africa, Uzbekistan).** South Africa names register in a workbook question and works it through dialogues. Uzbekistan teaches formal/informal style through invitations, pair words and a deliberately mixed letter to fix. Barbados rules the dialect question in its syllabus: discriminate by place and circumstance, and build the switch (syllabus evidence, worded as such). Jordan stocks the two soft tools — hedging as a writing skill and vague language as a named set.
