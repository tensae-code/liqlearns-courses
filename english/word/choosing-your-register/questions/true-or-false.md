# Choosing your register — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Home dialect is broken English that school should remove. **Answer: False** — Barbados's syllabus defines dialect's role and builds switching; dialect is a code of its own, not an error (syllabus evidence)

**2. (Level 2)** A formal letter may freely mix 'guy' and 'inform' in one paragraph. **Answer: False** — Uzbekistan's Review plants exactly that mix as the error to spot and correct (p.85)

**3. (Level 3)** Hedging a claim you cannot fully prove is more honest than stating it flatly. **Answer: True** — a hedge marks your real certainty; the flat over-claim is the dishonest move (Jordan, pp.24–25)
