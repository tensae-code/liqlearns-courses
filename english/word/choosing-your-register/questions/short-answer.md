# Choosing your register — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Give the formal partner of 'book' (as in: book a table), from Uzbekistan's pairs. **Answer: arrange** — ask for/inform · find out/be knowledgeable about · guy/man · book/arrange (EM9, p.80)

**2. (Level 2)** What is the name for moving between home dialect and standard English as the place demands? **Answer: code-switching** — Barbados's rationale names the ability to switch codes as the skill (syllabus evidence)

**3. (Level 3)** Name two vague-language words from Jordan's set and one honest job they do. **Answer: any two of: stuff, things, sort of, kind of — e.g. when the exact item does not matter** — Jordan G12 S2 U7, pp.16–17
