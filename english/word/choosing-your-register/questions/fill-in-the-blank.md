# Choosing your register — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** The way of speaking a moment demands is called its ______. **Answer: register** — South Africa's workbook question names it (p.53)

**2. (Level 2)** A ______ invitation follows formal style even though it invites you to a celebration. **Answer: formal (wedding)** — Uzbekistan sets the formal wedding invitation beside the party letter (p.78)

**3. (Level 3)** Softening a claim to match your evidence is called ______. **Answer: hedging** — Jordan's Active Writing skill (pp.24–25)
