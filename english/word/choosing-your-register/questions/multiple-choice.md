# Choosing your register — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Formal and Informal · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Register for Place and Circumstance · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Hedging and Vague Language.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a formal letter, 'I am writing to ______ a copy of the form' takes — a) ask for b) inform you and ask for c) request **Answer: c) request** — the formal suit of 'ask for' — Uzbekistan's pair-word drill (EM9 U7, p.80)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'How would the register (or way of speaking)…' is the question of — a) Egypt's book b) South Africa's Grade 6 workbook c) a dictionary **Answer: b) South Africa's Grade 6 workbook** — printed p.53, where a policeman's speech is set against a boy's

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Barbados's objective says dialect and standard must be discriminated — a) by age b) according to place and circumstance c) by spelling **Answer: b) according to place and circumstance** — the syllabus's own words (syllabus evidence)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 'It is believed that the road will flood' is an example of — a) vague language b) hedging c) dialect **Answer: b) hedging** — the claim is softened to the strength of its evidence (Jordan G12, pp.24–25)
