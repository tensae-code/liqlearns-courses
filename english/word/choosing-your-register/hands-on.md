# Hands-on — Choosing your register

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Three ways.** Take one real request. Write it three ways — friend, teacher, office — and read all three aloud. The meaning must not move; only the clothes.
2. **Register spotting.** South Africa's drill: take a dialogue (a policeman and a boy, as in the workbook) and mark every line for its register — then rewrite the boy's lines in a teacher-facing register.
3. **Invitation judge.** Uzbekistan's task, live: here are a party letter and a formal wedding invitation — judge each line: does its style fit its moment? Fix the one that doesn't.
4. **Hedge the headline.** Take five hard claims ('This will definitely…'). Hedge each one honestly with seems / perhaps / it is believed that — Jordan's editor-letter tools.
