# Days and months — matching

Match each word to its place, the way the books set them.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — after/before on the circle (Egypt,
pp.6–7).
1. after Sunday — **Monday**
2. after Monday — **Tuesday**
3. before Saturday — **Friday**
4. before Thursday — **Wednesday**
5. after Friday — **Saturday** (the circle closes)

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — month and number (Bangladesh, Class 3).
1. January — **first**
2. March — **third**
3. June — **sixth**
4. September — **ninth**
5. December — **twelfth**
6. May — **fifth**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — the rhyme's counts (Bangladesh, Class 4).
1. September — **30 days**
2. April — **30 days**
3. January — **31 days**
4. February — **28, and 29 in a leap year**
5. a year — **365 days** (366 in a leap year)
