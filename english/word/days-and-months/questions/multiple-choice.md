# Days and months — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** How many days are there in a week?
a) 5  b) 7  c) 12
**Answer: b) 7** — Egypt's first question (Primary 1 Term 2,
p.6).

**2. (Level 1)** What day comes after Monday?
a) Sunday  b) Tuesday  c) Wednesday
**Answer: b) Tuesday** (Egypt's pair work, p.7).

**3. (Level 1)** What day comes before Saturday (in Egypt's
Saturday-first list)?
a) Friday  b) Sunday  c) Thursday
**Answer: a) Friday** (Egypt, p.7).

**4. (Level 2)** January is the ______ month of the year.
a) twelfth  b) first  c) second
**Answer: b) first** (Bangladesh, Class 3, pp.61–64).

**5. (Level 2)** The names of months begin with a ______.
a) small letter  b) number  c) capital letter
**Answer: c) capital letter** — the books' grammar focus
(Bangladesh, Class 3).

**6. (Level 2)** Which month is the ninth?
a) October  b) August  c) September
**Answer: c) September.**

**7. (Level 3)** February has 29 days in a ______.
a) leap year  b) school year  c) new year
**Answer: a) leap year** — the rhyme's last line (Bangladesh,
Class 4, pp.62–63).
