**4. (Sub lesson 2)** January is the ______ month of the year.
a) twelfth  b) first  c) second
**Answer: b) first** (Bangladesh, Class 3, pp.61–64).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The names of months begin with a ______.
a) small letter  b) number  c) capital letter
**Answer: c) capital letter** — the books' grammar focus
(Bangladesh, Class 3).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which month is the ninth?
a) October  b) August  c) September
**Answer: c) September.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** February has 29 days in a ______.

**Answer: a) leap year** — the rhyme's last line (Bangladesh,
Class 4, pp.62–63).
