# Days and months — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box. (Days and months
take capital letters — the books' rule — so the box gives
them dressed.)

**Box:** Tuesday · Friday · February · first · twelfth ·
thirty-one

**1. (Level 1)** After Monday comes ______.
**Answer: Tuesday.**

**2. (Level 1)** Before Saturday comes ______.
**Answer: Friday.**
(1–2 are Egypt's pair work, p.7.)

**3. (Level 2)** ______ is the second month of the year.
**Answer: February.**

**4. (Level 2)** January is the ______ month of the year.
**Answer: first.**

**5. (Level 2)** December is the ______ month of the year.
**Answer: twelfth.**
(3–5 are Bangladesh's sentence ladder, Class 3, pp.61–64.)

**6. (Level 3)** All the rest have ______ days — except
February.
**Answer: thirty-one** (the rhyme, Class 4, pp.62–63).
