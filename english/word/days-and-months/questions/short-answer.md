# Days and months — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Say the seven days in Egypt's order.
**Answer:** Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday,
Thursday, Friday (Primary 1 Term 2, p.6).

**2. (Level 1)** What day comes after Wednesday? Before
Friday?
**Answer:** Thursday — Thursday (the circle, Egypt pp.6–8).

**3. (Level 2)** Say the twelve months in order.
**Answer:** January, February, March, April, May, June, July,
August, September, October, November, December.

**4. (Level 2)** Finish the book's sentence: "December is
the ______ month of the year."
**Answer:** twelfth (Bangladesh, Class 3).

**5. (Level 3)** From the rhyme: which months have thirty
days?
**Answer:** September, April, June, November (Bangladesh,
Class 4 poster, pp.62–63).

**6. (Level 3)** How many days in a year? In a leap year?
**Answer:** 365 — 366 (Bangladesh's written answers, U31).
