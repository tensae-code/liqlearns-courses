# Days and months — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Seven Days · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Twelve Months · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Calendar Machine.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the seven days in Egypt's order.
**Answer:** Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday,
Thursday, Friday (Primary 1 Term 2, p.6).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What day comes after Wednesday? Before
Friday?
**Answer:** Thursday — Thursday (the circle, Egypt pp.6–8).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say the twelve months in order.
**Answer:** January, February, March, April, May, June, July,
August, September, October, November, December.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Finish the book's sentence: "December is
the ______ month of the year."
**Answer:** twelfth (Bangladesh, Class 3).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** From the rhyme: which months have thirty
days?
**Answer:** September, April, June, November (Bangladesh,
Class 4 poster, pp.62–63).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** How many days in a year? In a leap year?
**Answer:** 365 — 366 (Bangladesh's written answers, U31).
