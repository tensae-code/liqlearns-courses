# Video script — Days and months

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Seven days in a week! Count them:

**Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday,
Wednesday, Thursday, Friday!**

What day is it today? Today is a GOOD day.
What day comes after Monday? Tuesday!
Before Saturday? Friday!

Now the year — twelve months:
**January, February, March, April,
May, June, July, August,
September, October, November, December!**

January is the FIRST month.
December is the TWELFTH month.

And the rhyme — sing it!
Thirty days in September,
April, June, and November!

Now you. Name the days. Name the months.
That is the calendar — in English.
