# Adjectives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with an adjective from the box.

**Box:** fresh · dirty · tall · hungry · old · happy

**1. (Level 1)** A ______ mango tastes sweet and good.
**Answer: fresh** (Bangladesh's pair).

**2. (Level 1)** Wash that ______ shirt before you wear it.
**Answer: dirty.**

**3. (Level 1)** The ______ tree touches the sky.
**Answer: tall** (Libya's pair).

**4. (Level 2)** The ______ wolf looks for food all day.
**Answer: hungry** (Libya's story pair).

**5. (Level 2)** My grandfather rides an ______ bike.
**Answer: old** (Bangladesh's pair: a new bike — an old bike).

**6. (Level 3)** The cat is ______. It plays all day.
**Answer: happy** — the adjective in its second home, after
"is" (Bangladesh's happy cat).
