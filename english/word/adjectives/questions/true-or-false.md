# Adjectives — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Describing words in pairs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The test, what is it like · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The second home, after is.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An adjective is a word that describes a noun.
**True** — Egypt's statement (Primary 4 Term 2, p.67).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "a small fire," the adjective is "fire."
**False** — fire names the thing. Small tells what it is like
(Libya's story pair).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "Adjectives and nouns go together."
**True** — Libya's own rule (Grade 5, "Let's check!" box).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The brave bird sang," the word "sang" is
the adjective.
**False** — sang is the doing word. Brave describes the bird.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The shirt is clean" and "a clean shirt" use
the same adjective in two places.
**True** — before the noun, and after "is": the two homes.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "A mango fresh" is the right way to build the
pair.
**False** — in the pair the describing word rides in front:
a fresh mango. After the noun it needs "is": the mango is
fresh.
