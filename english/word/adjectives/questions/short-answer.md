# Adjectives — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** What is an adjective?
**Answer:** a describing word — a word that tells us more
about a noun: what it looks, feels, sounds, or smells like
(Egypt, Primary 4 Term 2, p.67).

**2. (Level 1)** Libya's book asks: "Are they big, small,
happy, sad?" What are those four words?
**Answer:** adjectives — describing words (Grade 5, "Let's
check!" box).

**3. (Level 2)** In "a brave bird," ask the test question and
answer it.
**Answer:** What is the bird like? — brave. Brave is the
adjective (Libya's story pair).

**4. (Level 2)** Find the adjective: "She wore a red dress to
the party."
**Answer:** red — it describes the dress.

**5. (Level 3)** Turn it around after "is": "a dirty shirt."
**Answer:** The shirt is dirty (Bangladesh's pair, Class 4).

**6. (Level 3)** Name one adjective for the weather today, in
a whole sentence.
**Answer:** any true one in the shape "It is ___" — hot,
cold, sunny… (Ghana's weather use, Basic 2).
