# Adjectives — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Describing words in pairs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The test, what is it like, except item 5 (what an adjective describes), which is Sub lesson 1's definition · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The second home, after is.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is an adjective?
a) mango  b) fresh  c) eat  d) quickly
**Answer: b) fresh** — it describes the mango. Mango names;
eat does; quickly describes a doing word.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "a tall tree," the describing word is —
a) a  b) tall  c) tree
**Answer: b) tall** (Libya's own pair, Grade 5).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair is built the right way?
a) a mango fresh  b) a fresh mango  c) fresh a mango
**Answer: b)** — the describing word rides in front of its
noun (Bangladesh's pictured pairs, Class 4).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The hungry wolf ran," the adjective is —
a) wolf  b) ran  c) hungry
**Answer: c) hungry** — What is the wolf like? Hungry. (Libya's
story pair.) Wolf names; ran does.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an adjective describe?
a) a verb  b) a noun  c) itself
**Answer: b) a noun** — Egypt's statement: adjectives are
words that describe nouns (Primary 4 Term 2, p.67).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence puts the adjective in its
second home?
a) A clean shirt hangs there.  b) The shirt is clean.
c) Clean the shirt.
**Answer: b)** — after "is," the describing word still
describes the shirt. In c), clean is a doing word.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "It is ______." (weather, Ghana's example)
a) cold  b) coldly  c) a cold
**Answer: a) cold** — "It is cold."
