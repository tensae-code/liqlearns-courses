# Video script — Adjectives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look. A mango.
Just — a mango. You know nothing about it.

Now — a **fresh** mango! Mmm.
A **bad** mango. Yuck!

Fresh. Bad. These words paint the mango.
A painting word is an **adjective**.

A **clean** shirt. A **dirty** shirt.
A **new** bike. An **old** bike.
A **happy** cat. An **angry** cat!

The adjective holds the noun's hand.
What is it like? Fresh! Dirty! Old!
That word — that is the adjective.

And look — it can move!
A fresh mango. The mango **is** fresh.
A tall tree. The tree **is** tall.

Now you. Point at something.
Say its name. Now paint it!
Big? Small? Red? Happy?
Say the pair. That is an adjective.
