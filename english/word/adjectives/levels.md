# Adjectives — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — a describing word — the pairs first, then finding the adjective by its noun, then its second home after "is".

Mapping from the old depth layers: the three old layers map one to one — old Level 1 (describing words in pairs) = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 (find it by its noun) = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 (the second home: after "is") = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Describing words in pairs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build and recognise adjective + noun pairs — a fresh mango, a tall tree — and pick the describing word out of a mixed list. (Old Level 1's pass mark.)

## Sub lesson 2 — The test, what is it like
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: in a short sentence, find the noun, ask "What is it like?", and name the adjective that answers — and say why the other words are not adjectives. (Old Level 2's pass mark.)

## Sub lesson 3 — The second home, after is
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: turn pairs around both ways without a miss — "a clean shirt ↔ the shirt is clean" — and use an adjective after is/are for weather, people, and things, the way Ghana's and Egypt's books do. (Comparing — bigger, biggest — is a different thing, Egypt's own step in Primary 4 Term 1; it is tested in its own lesson, not here.) (Old Level 3's pass mark.)
