# Adjectives — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — a describing word — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the pairs first, then finding
the adjective by its noun, then its second home after "is."

## Level 1 — describing words in pairs
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build and
recognise adjective + noun pairs — a fresh mango, a tall tree —
and pick the describing word out of a mixed list.

## Level 2 — find it by its noun
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: in a short
sentence, find the noun, ask "What is it like?", and name the
adjective that answers — and say why the other words are not
adjectives.

## Level 3 — the second home: after "is"
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: turn pairs
around both ways without a miss — "a clean shirt ↔ the shirt is
clean" — and use an adjective after is/are for weather, people,
and things, the way Ghana's and Egypt's books do.
(Comparing — bigger, biggest — is a different thing, Egypt's own
step in Primary 4 Term 1; it is tested in its own lesson, not
here.)
