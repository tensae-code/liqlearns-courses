# Hands-on — Travel Words

Travel words are learned by planning a real trip. Do these out
loud, with a partner.

## Our town tour

Plan a tour of your town together: five stops, with the time you
arrive and the time you leave at each stop. Take turns saying
the plan: "We arrive at the market at nine. We leave at ten.
Next, we travel to the park."

## Tomorrow's plan

Tell your partner three things you will do tomorrow, using
tomorrow, before and next: "Tomorrow I will get up before
seven. Next, I will…"
