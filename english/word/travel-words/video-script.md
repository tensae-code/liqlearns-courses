# Video script — Travel Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands by a small bus with a map.] "We TRAVEL by
bus!"

[The bus stops; the mascot steps down and waves.] "We ARRIVE!
We are here!"

[The mascot floats on his back on blue water, smiling.] "I can
FLOAT! The water holds me!"
