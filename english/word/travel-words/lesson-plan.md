# Lesson plan — Travel Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Travel Words
**Lesson:** travel-words — the words a tour runs on: tour, travel,
arrive, meet, leave, next, tomorrow — and the words around them:
ruin, before, float, near, our
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World
**The one thing:** the word set of going on a tour and talking
about the trip, learned whole, the way the book's tour unit
presents them.
**This lesson's own level:** Word lesson: the moving words of the
tour first, then the describing words around them.

## Why this lesson exists

No lesson housed the travel word set. Palestine's Grade 5 book —
the Blended Learning Module 4, the only Grade 5 English book in
hand — opens with a tour unit: a "Best of Palestine" seven-day
tour that teaches next, tour, arrive, meet, travel, ruin, before,
float, leave, near, tomorrow, our (Unit 1, printed pp.2–4). The
2026-11-08 re-verification sweep read the book page by page for
the first time and marked this part NEW LESSON NEEDED. Under the
everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06), a teaching item with no
lesson gets a new lesson built; this is that lesson. Its only
source is the Palestine book named in lesson/sources.md.

## Sub lessons

1. Going on a tour — tour, travel, arrive, meet, leave, next, tomorrow
2. Words the tour needs — ruin, before, float, near, our

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is forced first — this lesson stands alone: it starts
from "we are going on a trip" and teaches every word it uses.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Name the moves of a trip: travel, arrive, meet, leave.
- Say what comes next and when: next, tomorrow, before.
- Use the tour's describing words: tour, ruin, float, near, our.

## What you need
A map of your own country and a plan for a trip of one day.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — plan a one-day tour of your town out loud
   before anyone teaches the words (lesson/lesson.md).
2. ADD IMAGE: a tour bus, a map with seven stops, a traveller
   floating on water. The learner names what they can before the
   words are taught.
3. Work through sub lesson 1: the moving words, each said inside
   the tour's own day plan.
4. Work through sub lesson 2: the words around the tour.
5. ADD VIDEO (short): a day of the tour, minute by minute —
   every time a travel word is used, the word grows big on
   screen: ARRIVE… MEET… LEAVE…

## Add-ons
- Plan the class's own "Best of our town" tour for one day and
  present it with the lesson's words.
- Diary line: write what the class will do tomorrow, next, and
  before lunch, using the words.

## If the learner is stuck
Cut back to four words — travel, arrive, meet, leave — acted out
with toy cars or drawings until they are sure. Add the rest one
at a time.

## Teacher note
The book teaches this set inside one real tour — the words are
never a bare list in the book, and this lesson keeps that shape:
every word is met inside the trip. The Grade 5 book is a blended
module, a condensed extract of a main book that is not in hand;
the set is taught exactly as the module presents it.
