## Multiple choice

1. "We ___ by bus to the city." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) travel  b) float  c) ruin
   **Answer: a) travel** — travel is moving from place to place.

2. "The bus stops. We ___ at the town!" (Sub lesson 1)
   a) leave  b) arrive  c) meet
   **Answer: b) arrive** — arrive means you get there.

3. "We will ___ Sara at the gate." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) meet  b) float  c) near
   **Answer: a) meet** — meet is coming together with a person.

4. "We eat ___ we leave." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) next  b) before  c) our
   **Answer: b) before** — before means earlier than.

5. "At the Dead Sea, you can ___ on the water." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) travel  b) arrive  c) float
   **Answer: c) float** — float is staying on top of the water.

6. "The sea is ___ our town — only ten minutes away." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) near  b) ruin  c) tomorrow
   **Answer: a) near** — near means not far.
