## Short answer

1. Your tour arrives at a town at nine and leaves at five. Write the two sentences with arrive and leave. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** "We arrive at the town at nine. We leave at five." (any true order of the two sentences)
2. What is a ruin? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** An old place broken by time — old walls, an old castle.
3. The water holds you up and you do not go down. Which word is it? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** float.
4. Write one sentence with near about a place close to your home. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** any true sentence with near, e.g. "The shop is near my home."
