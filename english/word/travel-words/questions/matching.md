## Matching

Match the word to what it means. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. tour — c
2. arrive — a
3. leave — d
4. ruin — b
5. tomorrow — e
6. our — f

a) to get to a place
b) an old place broken by time
c) a trip to see places
d) to go away from a place
e) the day after today
f) belonging to us

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (a tour is the trip itself), 2–a (arrive = get there), 3–d (leave = go away), 4–b (a ruin is the broken old place), 5–e (tomorrow follows today), 6–f (our = belonging to us).
