## True or false

1. "Arrive" means to go away from a place. (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — arrive means to get there; leave means to go away.
2. "Tomorrow" is the day after today. (Sub lesson 1)
   **True.**
3. A ruin is a new building. (Sub lesson 2)
   **False** — a ruin is an old place broken by time.
4. When you float, the water holds you up. (Sub lesson 2)
   **True.**
5. "Our" means belonging to us. (Sub lesson 2)
   **True.**
