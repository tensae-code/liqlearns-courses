# Hands-on — Music Words

Hear it, mime it, name it.

## Genre charades

One learner mimes listening to or playing a genre — wild rock, smooth jazz, marching classical. The others shout the genre name in English.

## Air band

Form an air band: each learner mimes an instrument. The class must name every instrument before the “song” ends: “She plays the violin! He plays the drums!”
