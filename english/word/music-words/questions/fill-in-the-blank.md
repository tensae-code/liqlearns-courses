## Fill in the blank

1. ___ is popular music everybody knows. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Pop** — pop = popular.

2. ___ is the music of orchestras, written long ago. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Classical** — classical music is orchestral.

3. The ___ has six strings and is played with the fingers or a pick. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: guitar** — the guitar is a string instrument.

4. The ___ is big, black and white keys, played sitting down. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: piano** — the piano is a key instrument.

5. You blow the ___ and the ___. (two wind instruments) (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: flute; trumpet** (also saxophone, clarinet, trombone).

6. My favourite ___ is jazz. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: music** — “My favourite music is jazz.”

7. A song's words are called its ___. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: lyrics** — songs have lyrics; instrumental music does not.
