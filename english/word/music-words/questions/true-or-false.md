## True or false

1. Jazz is a music genre. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — jazz is one of the eleven genres in the book.

2. Reggae comes from Jamaica. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the relaxed Jamaican beat.

3. The violin is a wind instrument. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the violin has strings.

4. You hit the drums. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — drums are percussion: you hit them.

5. The flute is played with strings. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — you blow the flute; it is a wind instrument.

6. Classical music played by an orchestra has lyrics. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — instrumental music has melodies, not lyrics (the book's own slip, taught corrected).

7. “Genre” means a kind of music. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — rock, pop and jazz are genres.
