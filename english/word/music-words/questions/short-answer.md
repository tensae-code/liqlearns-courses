## Short answer

1. Name three music genres. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: rock, pop, jazz** (any three of the eleven) — genres are kinds of music.

2. Which genre rides words on a strong beat? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Hip hop / rap.** — words riding the beat.

3. Name three string instruments. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: guitar, violin, cello** (also bass guitar, harp, ukulele).

4. Name two wind instruments. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: flute, trumpet** (also saxophone, clarinet, trombone).

5. Say your favourite music in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “My favourite music is (genre).”** — the book's favourite-music frame.

6. Say which instrument you would like to play. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I would like to play the (instrument).”** — would like + the instrument.

7. What are the words of a song called? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Lyrics.** — and instrumental music has none.
