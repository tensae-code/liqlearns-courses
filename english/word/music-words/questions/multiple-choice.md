## Multiple choice

1. Loud guitars and drums, a band sound: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) jazz  b) rock  c) classical
   **Answer: b) rock** — rock is the loud guitar-and-drums genre.

2. The relaxed beat from Jamaica: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) reggae  b) blues  c) pop
   **Answer: a) reggae** — reggae comes from Jamaica.

3. Music made with machines and computers: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) folk music  b) electronic  c) country
   **Answer: b) electronic** — electronic music is machine-made.

4. The music of a people, passed down over time: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) hip hop  b) folk music  c) rap
   **Answer: b) folk music** — folk music is a people's own music.

5. Which instrument has strings? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) flute  b) drums  c) violin
   **Answer: c) violin** — the violin is a string instrument.

6. Which instrument do you blow? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) trumpet  b) piano  c) cello
   **Answer: a) trumpet** — the trumpet is a wind instrument.

7. Which instrument do you hit? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) harp  b) drums  c) saxophone
   **Answer: b) drums** — drums are hit.

8. Music played only by instruments has… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) lyrics  b) no lyrics — melodies instead  c) words
   **Answer: b) no lyrics** — lyrics are the words of a song; instrumental music has melodies.
