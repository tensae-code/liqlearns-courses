# Video script — Music Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[Four quick scenes: a rock band, a jazz saxophone, a folk singer, an orchestra.] “Rock! Jazz! Folk music! Classical! Kinds of music are called GENRES.”

[Instruments appear in groups.] “Strings: guitar, violin, cello! Wind: flute, trumpet, saxophone! And the drums — you hit them!”

[The mascot with headphones, smiling.] “My favourite music is folk music. What's YOUR favourite? What would you like to play?”
