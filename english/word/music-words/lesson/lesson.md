# Music Words

## Do it first

What music do you love? Hum a little of it. Is it fast or slow, loud or soft? Every kind of music has a name in English, and every instrument too. In this lesson you learn the words — so you can talk about the music in your life.

## Sub lesson 1 — Music genres

A **genre** is a kind of music. Bolivia's book names eleven:

**rock** — loud guitars and drums. **pop** — popular songs everybody knows. **jazz** — clever, swinging music, often with saxophone and trumpet. **electronic** — music made with machines and computers. **hip hop** and **rap** — words riding a strong beat. **reggae** — the relaxed beat from Jamaica. **blues** — deep, feeling music. **folk music** — the music of a people, passed down. **country** — stories sung with guitars. **classical** — the music of orchestras, written long ago and still played.

Say the ones you know. Which have you heard at home?

## Sub lesson 2 — Musical instruments

The book's instrument set, grouped by how they make sound:

**Strings:** guitar, bass guitar, violin, cello, harp, ukulele.

**Keys:** piano, keyboard, accordion.

**Wind:** flute, trumpet, saxophone, clarinet, trombone.

**Hit:** drums.

Point and name: which do you blow? Which have strings? Which do you hit? The groups help the words stick.

## Sub lesson 3 — Talking about favourite music

Now use the words about yourself, as the book does:

“My favourite music is folk music.” “I love rock. My favourite artist is…” “I would like to play the guitar.” “He plays the drums in a band.”

One care point the book itself gets wrong: it says classical music's “lyrics touch the heart” — but music played only by instruments has **no lyrics** (lyrics are the words of a song). Songs have lyrics; instrument music has melodies.

Tell me: your favourite genre, your favourite artist, and the instrument you would choose.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: “My favourite music is… My favourite artist is… I would like to play the…” Ask them the same three things and remember their answers — then tell me about THEM: “Her favourite music is jazz.”

## Do It Yourself

Make your music page: write the genres you like in order (1 = most loved), name two artists, and draw the instrument you would like to play with its English name under it. Show it to someone at home and teach them three of the words.
