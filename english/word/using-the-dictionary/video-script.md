# Video script — Using the dictionary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words, words, everywhere. Where is YOUR word?

A dictionary is a city in **letter order**.
A… B… C… every word in its line.
First letter first. Tie? The NEXT letter decides.
**Dark, deep, doctor.** See? D-A, D-E, D-O.

Open the book. Look UP — two **guide words**!
They fence the page. Is your word inside the fence?
Yes? Read down. No? Turn the pages!

And here it is — the **entry**:
how to spell it, how to say it, what it means,
noun or verb — and an example in *italics*.

Careful! One word can mean three things.
**Dry**: not wet. Little rain. Not interesting.
Pick the one that fits!

Now you. Take a word. Find it fast.
That is the dictionary — YOUR tool.
