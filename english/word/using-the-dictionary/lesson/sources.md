**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Bangladesh, India,
Mongolia, China).** Bangladesh carries the full skill — order, guide
words, the entry, many meanings; India carries the first
form, a picture dictionary in every year's book; Mongolia
carries the working tool, its own book's dictionary; China
carries the habit — A–Z lists with page references at the
back of its books, and dictionary use taught inside its
Grade 9 learning-to-learn unit. Other
finished countries join as their books are read (see
READING-QUEUE.md).
