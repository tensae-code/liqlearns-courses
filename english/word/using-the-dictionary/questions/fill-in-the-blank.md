# Using the dictionary — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** alphabetical · guide · italics · meaning · synonym ·
antonym

**1. (Level 1)** Dictionary words stand in ______
order.
**Answer: alphabetical.**

**2. (Level 2)** The two words at the top of the page are
the ______ words.
**Answer: guide.**

**3. (Level 3)** Example sentences in an entry are printed
in ______.
**Answer: italics.**

**4. (Level 3)** A ______ means the same or almost the same
as the word.
**Answer: synonym.**

**5. (Level 3)** An ______ means the opposite.
**Answer: antonym.**

**6. (Level 3)** A word may have more than one ______ —
'dry' has three.
**Answer: meaning.**
(All from Bangladesh, Class 8, Unit Four, pp.36–41.)
