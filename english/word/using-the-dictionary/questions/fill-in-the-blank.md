# Using the dictionary — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Dictionary Letter Order · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Dictionary Guide Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Dictionary Entries.
Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** alphabetical · guide · italics · meaning · synonym ·
antonym

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dictionary words stand in ______
order.
**Answer: alphabetical.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The two words at the top of the page are
the ______ words.
**Answer: guide.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Example sentences in an entry are printed
in ______.
**Answer: italics.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A ______ means the same or almost the same
as the word.
**Answer: synonym.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An ______ means the opposite.
**Answer: antonym.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A word may have more than one ______ —
'dry' has three.
**Answer: meaning.**
