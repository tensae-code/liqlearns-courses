# Using the dictionary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which word stands FIRST in a dictionary?
a) deep  b) dark  c) doctor
**Answer: b) dark** — all start with d; the second letter
decides: a before e before o (Bangladesh, Class 8, p.39).

**2. (Level 1)** Words in a dictionary stand in ______ order.
a) size  b) alphabetical  c) story
**Answer: b) alphabetical** (Bangladesh, p.39).

**3. (Level 2)** The two words at the top of a dictionary page
are called ______.
a) fence posts  b) guide words  c) titles
**Answer: b) guide words** — the first and last word on the
page (Bangladesh, pp.39–40).

**4. (Level 2)** Your word is 'teapot'. The page's guide words
are 'tea' and 'technique'. Does 'teapot' sleep on that page?
a) yes  b) no — it comes before 'tea'  c) no — after
'technique'
**Answer: a) yes** — tea < teapot < technique by the letters
(Bangladesh's own drill, pp.39–40).

**5. (Level 3)** In an entry, the example sentence is printed
in ______.
a) bold  b) italics  c) capitals
**Answer: b) italics** (Bangladesh, pp.36–38).

**6. (Level 3)** A verb stands in an entry in its ______,
with its past forms beside it.
a) simple present tense  b) -ing form  c) plural
**Answer: a) simple present tense** (Bangladesh, pp.36–38).

**7. (Level 3)** 'Dry' in "a dry day, no rain for weeks"
means —
a) not wet  b) with very little rain  c) not interesting
**Answer: b)** — one of its three meanings; pick the one that
fits (Bangladesh, pp.40–41).
