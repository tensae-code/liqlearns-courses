# Using the dictionary — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Put in dictionary order: egg, apple, dog,
cat, ball.
**Answer:** apple, ball, cat, dog, egg.

**2. (Level 1)** Why does 'deep' stand before 'doctor'?
**Answer:** the first letters tie (d); the second letter
decides — e before o (Bangladesh, p.39).

**3. (Level 3)** Name four things a dictionary entry gives.
**Answer:** any four of spelling, pronunciation, meaning,
word class, examples (in italics), synonyms, antonyms
(Bangladesh, pp.36–38).

**4. (Level 3)** What is a synonym? An antonym?
**Answer:** a synonym means the same or almost the same; an
antonym means the opposite (Bangladesh, pp.40–41).

**5. (Level 3)** Give the three meanings of 'dry'.
**Answer:** not wet; with very little rain; not interesting
(Bangladesh, pp.40–41).

**6. (Level 2)** Your word starts with 'm'. The open page's
guide words are 'tea' and 'technique'. Do you turn forward
or back?
**Answer:** back — m stands long before t; guide words tell
you before you read a page (Bangladesh, pp.39–40).
