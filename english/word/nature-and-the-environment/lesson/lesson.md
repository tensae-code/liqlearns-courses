# Nature and the environment — the lesson

Course: English · Title: Word · Lesson: Nature and the environment
Built from: Albania, English A2 (Akelius) Teacher's Guide,
Chapter 38 "Nature" — Mexico, Grade 7 (1° de Secundaria),
Project 18 — Mongolia, English VIII (Grade 8), Unit 8
"Saving the Earth," and English VII (Grade 7), Unit 7
"Environment" — Saudi Arabia, We Can! 2 (Grade 2), Unit 4
"Beautiful Nature."

A soft hint before you start: if you know the Animals
lesson, this one will be easier — the animals come back
here, in the places they live. You do not need it first.
Every place below is taught whole.

## Do it first
Look out of a window, or remember your last walk outside
the town. What did you see that nobody built? A hill? Trees?
Water? The sky? You already have these words in your own
language. Now we learn the English names — the words for
the world that was there before us.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Nature Places
**The places.** Albania's nature chapter teaches the set
as one list, and it is the list every book here works with:

**forest · grass · hill · mountain · lake · river · stream ·
rock · sea · sun · moon · star · sunrise · sunset**

Say them with the chapter's own sentences: "They are
walking **through the forest**." "We're crossing **the
lake** in a boat." "Grass can grow in gardens, parks and
forests." Saudi Arabia's youngest book teaches the same
world by pointing: "Look! It's a snake. I love the
**mountains**."

> a picture of a mountain with a lake under it and the sun
> rising behind it — the student names the four things the
> picture shows: mountain, lake, sun, sunrise.

## Sub lesson 2 — Habitats
**Habitats — where animals live.** A habitat is an animal's
home place. Albania's sentence is the pattern: "**Bears can
live in the forest and in the mountains.**" Mongolia's
Grade 7 environment unit matches the nature words to
pictures the same way — wild animals, a forest, mountains,
a desert, birds, a river, the ocean, a lake, a waterfall, a
glacier — and asks where each animal lives. Learn the
pattern once: animal + can live in + place. A fish can live
in a river. A camel can live in the desert. A bird can live
in a tree.

## Sub lesson 3 — Problems and Solutions
**Problems and solutions.** Mongolia's Grade 8 unit "Saving
the Earth" builds the environment words as phrases and
sorts them into two boxes, and the sorting IS the lesson:

- **Problems:** climate change, air pollution, a water
  shortage — the things that hurt the earth.
- **Solutions:** wind power, organic farming — the things
  people do to help.

Mexico's Grade 7 project carries the fullest problem set in
any book here: **pollution, acid rain, climate change,
drought** (no rain for a long time), **flood** (too much
water), **litter** (rubbish on the ground) — and on the
solution side, **biodegradable** (it rots away safely) and
**renewable energy** (power that never runs out, like wind
power). Albania's chapter ends at the same place: caring
for nature, and our job in keeping it alive.

## Say it to your companion
Describe the land around your home. Say three nature things
that are there: "There is a river. There are hills. The sun
is hot." Name one animal and where it lives: "Birds can
live in the trees." Then one problem and one solution for
your place: "There is litter. We can recycle." Your
companion listens and repeats your land back to you — then
you swap.

## Do It Yourself
1. Draw your land — the country around your home. Label
   five nature words in English.
2. Write three habitat sentences: animal + can live in +
   place.
3. Write two problem words and two solution words from the
   lesson, and one sentence with each problem.
4. Watch a sunrise or a sunset and say the word as it
   happens — sunrise in the morning, sunset in the evening.
