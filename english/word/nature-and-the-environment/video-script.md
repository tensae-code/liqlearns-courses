# Video script — Nature and the environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Come outside. Look!

The **sun** is up. **Sunrise!**
Here is a **hill**. Here is a **forest**.
Trees, trees, trees — and **grass** under my feet.

A **stream**! Small water, running.
A **lake**! Big water, still. A boat crosses it.
And far away — a **mountain**, with a **rock** on top.

Who lives here?
A **bear** can live in the forest.
A fish can live in the **river**.
This is their home. It is our home too.

So we take care. No **litter**. We **recycle**.
That is nature — in English.
