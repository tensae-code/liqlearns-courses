# Hands-on — Nature and the environment

The lesson outside, where the words live.

## The window list
Stand at a window or a door. Name everything natural you
can see, in English, and count your words: sky, sun, grass,
tree, bird… Ten is the target. The thing you cannot name is
your new word to find in the lesson's list.

## The habitat walk
Walk a short way — a garden, a park, a road out of town.
Every time you see an animal (a real one, or its home — a
nest, a hole, a web), stop and say the sentence: "A bird
can live in a tree." Five animals, five homes.

## Problem spotter, solution spotter
On the same walk, hunt with your eyes: one problem (litter
on the ground, smoke, dry land) — say the problem word
aloud. Then one thing people do right (a tree planted, a
clean stream) — say a solution word. Problems in one hand,
solutions in the other, like Mongolia's two boxes.

## My land page
Draw the land you walked. Label it in English — forest,
river, hill, sun — and write under it one problem sentence
and one solution sentence about this real place.
