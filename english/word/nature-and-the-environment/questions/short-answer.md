# Nature and the environment — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name four nature places from Albania's
list.
**Answer:** any four of forest, hill, mountain, lake,
river, stream, sea (Chapter 38, printed p.48).

**2. (Level 2)** Where can a bear live?
**Answer:** In the forest and in the mountains — Albania's
own sentence.

**3. (Level 2)** Name two homes from Mongolia's Grade 7
picture set that are water.
**Answer:** any two of river, ocean, lake, waterfall
(English VII, Unit 7, p.94).

**4. (Level 3)** Name one problem and one solution from
this lesson.
**Answer:** problem — any of pollution, climate change,
drought, flood, litter; solution — any of recycle, wind
power, renewable energy, organic farming.
