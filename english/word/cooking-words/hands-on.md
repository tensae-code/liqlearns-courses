# Hands-on — Cooking Words

The kitchen is the classroom. Adult help for anything hot or sharp.

## Mime the action

Cards or calls: “Whisk!” “Knead!” “Peel!” Each learner mimes the action; the others name it. Wrong guesses keep the game alive.

## Cook one thing

Make the simplest real dish you can — a salad, eggs, toast. Say every cooking word aloud as you do it: “I slice the tomato. I toast the bread.” Real food, real words.
