## Fill in the blank

1. ___ the meat over the fire on a rack. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Grill** — grilling cooks over fire on a rack.

2. ___ the cake in the oven for 30 minutes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Bake** — cakes are baked.

3. ___ the eggs gently in hot water. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Poach** — poaching is gentle hot-water cooking.

4. ___ the onion into small cubes. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Dice** — dice = small cubes.

5. ___ the soup round with a spoon. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Stir** — stirring moves food round.

6. ___ the dough with your hands. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Knead** — knead the dough.

7. ___ salt to the soup and ___ hot. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Add; serve** — add an action word; serve is how recipes end.
