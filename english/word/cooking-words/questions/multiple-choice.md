## Multiple choice

1. Cooking bread or cake in the oven is to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) bake  b) poach  c) steam
   **Answer: a) bake** — baking is the oven method for bread and cakes.

2. Cooking meat slowly over fire and smoke is to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) toast  b) barbecue  c) whisk
   **Answer: b) barbecue** — barbecue = slow cooking over fire and smoke.

3. Cooking in the vapour of boiling water is to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) grill  b) steam  c) carve
   **Answer: b) steam** — steam cooks in water's vapour.

4. Cooking fast in a hot pan, moving all the time: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) stir-fry  b) stew  c) gratin
   **Answer: a) stir-fry** — stir-fry is fast and always moving.

5. Cooking long and slow in liquid is to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) toast  b) stew  c) broil
   **Answer: b) stew** — a stew cooks long in liquid.

6. Cutting into little cubes is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) dice  b) drain  c) knead
   **Answer: a) dice** — dice = cut in little cubes.

7. Taking the skin off a potato is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) pour  b) peel  c) grease
   **Answer: b) peel** — peel = take the skin off.

8. Working dough with your hands is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) knead  b) dissolve  c) slice
   **Answer: a) knead** — kneading is the dough work.

9. The verb for finding how heavy something is: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) weight  b) weigh  c) waiter
   **Answer: b) weigh** — weigh is the verb; weight is the noun (the book prints “Weight” as the verb — taught corrected).

10. “___ the potatoes, then ___ them for twenty minutes.” (Sub lesson 3)
    a) Peel / steam  b) Grill / whisk  c) Weigh / toast
    **Answer: a) Peel / steam** — an action, then a water method.
