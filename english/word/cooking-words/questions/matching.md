## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. grill — a
2. roast — b
3. simmer — c
4. stir-fry — d
5. slice — e
6. mince — f
7. drain — g
8. whisk — h

a) cook over fire on a rack
b) cook meat or vegetables in the oven
c) cook just below boiling
d) cook fast in a hot pan, moving all the time
e) cut in flat pieces
f) cut very, very small
g) pour the water away
h) beat fast with a whisk
