## Short answer

1. Name three oven methods. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: bake, roast, broil** (also gratin for a golden top) — oven methods use dry heat.

2. Name two water methods. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: steam, poach** (also simmer, stew) — water methods cook in or over water.

3. What do you do to eggs when you scramble them? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Stir them as they set in the pan.** — scrambling is stirring eggs while they cook.

4. Name three cutting actions. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: slice, dice, chop** (also mince, carve, peel for skin) — cutting words differ by size and shape.

5. Which action pours the cooking water away? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Drain.** — drain = pour the water away, keep the food.

6. Which two actions beat with speed? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Beat and whisk.** — whisk uses the whisk tool.

7. Give two cooking instructions for making toast with eggs. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “Toast the bread. Scramble the eggs.”** (any two true instructions) — a method word in each.
