## True or false

1. To bake is to cook in the oven. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — bread and cakes are baked.

2. To steam is to cook over fire on a rack. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — that is to grill; steam cooks in water's vapour.

3. To simmer is to cook just below boiling. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — a simmer is the gentlest boil.

4. To dice means to pour water away. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — that is to drain; dice means cut in little cubes.

5. To peel means to take the skin off. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — peel a potato, peel an orange.

6. “Weight” is the correct verb for finding how heavy food is. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the verb is weigh; weight is the noun (the book's slip, taught corrected).

7. A recipe uses both method words and action words. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — actions say what you do; methods say how it cooks.
