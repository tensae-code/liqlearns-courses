# Video script — Cooking Words

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[Three dishes: grilled meat, a cake, a soup.] “GRILLED! BAKED! STEAMED! Methods are HOW food meets the heat!”

[Hands slicing, stirring, pouring.] “Slice! Stir! Pour! Whisk! Actions are what the cook's hands DO.”

[A finished dish.] “Peel the potatoes. Slice them. Steam them. Add salt. Now YOU can read a recipe in English!”
