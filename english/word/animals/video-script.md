# Video script — Animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Come to the farm!

Look at the **cow**! The cow is **big**.
Look at the **duck**! The duck is **small**.
The **horse** — big! The **chicken** — small!
I love farm animals!

At home: a **cat**. A **dog**. A **goat**.
In the wild: a **lion**! A **zebra**! An **elephant**!

Where does the crow live? In a **nest**.
What does the cow eat? **Grass**.
What does the tiger eat? **Meat**!

Now you. Name the animal.
Big or small? Where does it live? What does it eat?
That is an animal — in English.
