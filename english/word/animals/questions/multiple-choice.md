# Animals — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Farm and Home Animals · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Wild Set · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Animals in
Sentences.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The cow is ______."
a) small  b) big  c) a fruit
**Answer: b) big** — Egypt's own sentence (Primary 1 Term 1,
p.86).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which animal belongs on the farm?
a) turtle  b) cat  c) chicken
**Answer: c) chicken** — Egypt's circle-the-farm-animals work
sets bird, cat, dog, and turtle outside the gate (p.87).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A baby cat is a ______.
a) kitten  b) puppy  c) cub
**Answer: a) kitten** (Palestine's set, Grade 1 Part 1, p.16).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set is all wild animals?
a) cow, duck, goat  b) lion, zebra, tiger  c) cat, dog, rabbit
**Answer: b)** — Palestine's and Bangladesh's wild names.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The crow lives in a ______."
a) tree hole  b) nest  c) house
**Answer: b) nest** — Bangladesh's true/false set (Class 2,
pp.86–87).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "The cow eats ______."
a) meat  b) insects  c) grass
**Answer: c) grass** (Bangladesh's strips, pp.88–89).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is true?
a) The tiger eats grass.  b) The tiger eats meat.  c) The
tiger eats nuts.
**Answer: b)** (Bangladesh's table).
