# Animals — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Farm and Home Animals · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Wild Set · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Animals in
Sentences.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Egypt's farm four.
**Answer:** cow, duck, horse, chicken (Primary 1 Term 1,
p.86).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the size sentences for the horse and the
duck.
**Answer:** "The horse is big." "The duck is small."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five wild animals from the books' sets.
**Answer:** any five of lion, zebra, elephant, monkey, tiger,
deer, parrot, crow (Palestine p.16; Libya's list;
Bangladesh's table).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do we get from the chicken? From the
cow?
**Answer:** the egg from the chicken; cheese and meat (and
milk) from the cow (Egypt, Review 2, p.123).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Build the sentence: monkey — tree.
**Answer:** "The monkey lives in a tree" (Bangladesh,
pp.86–87).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Build the sentence: parrot — eats.
**Answer:** "The parrot eats fruits" (Bangladesh's strips,
pp.88–89).
