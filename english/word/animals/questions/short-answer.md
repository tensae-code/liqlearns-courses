# Animals — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Egypt's farm four.
**Answer:** cow, duck, horse, chicken (Primary 1 Term 1,
p.86).

**2. (Level 1)** Say the size sentences for the horse and the
duck.
**Answer:** "The horse is big." "The duck is small."

**3. (Level 2)** Name five wild animals from the books' sets.
**Answer:** any five of lion, zebra, elephant, monkey, tiger,
deer, parrot, crow (Palestine p.16; Libya's list;
Bangladesh's table).

**4. (Level 2)** What do we get from the chicken? From the
cow?
**Answer:** the egg from the chicken; cheese and meat (and
milk) from the cow (Egypt, Review 2, p.123).

**5. (Level 3)** Build the sentence: monkey — tree.
**Answer:** "The monkey lives in a tree" (Bangladesh,
pp.86–87).

**6. (Level 3)** Build the sentence: parrot — eats.
**Answer:** "The parrot eats fruits" (Bangladesh's strips,
pp.88–89).
