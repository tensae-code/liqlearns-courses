# Animals — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** cow · duck · nest · grass · meat · zebra

**1. (Level 1)** The ______ is big. (the farm animal that
gives milk)
**Answer: cow** (Egypt, p.86).

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ is small. (the farm bird that
swims)
**Answer: duck** (Egypt, p.86).

**3. (Level 3)** The crow lives in a ______.
**Answer: nest** (Bangladesh, pp.86–87).

**4. (Level 3)** The cow eats ______.
**Answer: grass.**

**5. (Level 3)** The tiger eats ______.
**Answer: meat.**
(4–5 are Bangladesh's animal-to-food strips, pp.88–89.)

**6. (Level 2)** The ______ has black and white stripes.
**Answer: zebra** (Palestine's set, p.16; Libya's list).
