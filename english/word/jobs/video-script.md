# Video script — Jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What do you do? I'm a pupil!

This is a **teacher**. A teacher teaches!
This is a **doctor**. A doctor helps sick people.
This is a **farmer**. A farmer grows food.
This is a **baker**. A baker bakes bread. Mmm!
This is a **driver**. A driver drives a bus.

What does she do? She's a nurse.
What does he do? He's a cook. He cooks!

Jobs everywhere! Teacher — doctor — farmer — baker!

Now you. What do YOU do?
What does your mother do? Your father?
Say the jobs — in English.
