# Jobs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** teacher · farmer · baker · driver · nurse ·
reporter

**1. (Level 1)** A ______ teaches children.
**Answer: teacher.**

**2. (Level 1)** A ______ bakes bread.
**Answer: baker** — the doing sentence the lesson is
built on.

**3. (Level 2)** What does she do? She's a ______ in a
hospital.
**Answer: nurse.**

**4. (Level 2)** A ______ grows food on a farm.
**Answer: farmer.**

**5. (Level 3)** A ______ reports the news.
**Answer: reporter** (Vietnam, Tiếng Anh 5, Unit 5).

**6. (Level 3)** A taxi ______ drives a taxi.
**Answer: driver** (Rwanda's taxi driver, P4, p.59).
