# Jobs — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Cambodia's six grown-up jobs.
**Answer:** seller, teacher, farmer, doctor, policeman,
housewife (Grade 4, Unit 6).

**2. (Level 2)** What do your parents do? Answer for your
real family.
**Answer:** whole true sentences — "My mother is a… My
father is a…" (Cambodia's question, Unit 6).

**3. (Level 2)** Name four jobs from Rwanda's P4 list
that Cambodia does not teach.
**Answer:** any four of nurse, carpenter, shopkeeper,
chef, secretary, taxi driver, mechanic, actress, police
officer (Rwanda P4, pp.59–61).

**4. (Level 3)** What would you like to be in the future?
Answer in Vietnam's frame.
**Answer:** "I'd like to be a…" — any job word (Tiếng
Anh 5, Unit 5).
