# Jobs — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Asking About Other People · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Doings
and Workplaces.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "What do you do?" — "I'm a ______."
a) pupil  b) hospital  c) yesterday
**Answer: a) pupil** — Cambodia's own opening pair
(Grade 4, Unit 6, p.72).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who grows food on a farm?
a) a doctor  b) a farmer  c) a singer
**Answer: b) a farmer** — in every country's set
(Cambodia; Rwanda; Vietnam).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "What does she do?" — "She's a ______."
a) teacher  b) teaches  c) school
**Answer: a) teacher** — the job word, not the doing
word (Cambodia, Grade 4, Unit 6).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vietnam: "What's his job?" — "He's a
______."
a) driver  b) drives  c) bus
**Answer: a) driver** — Tiếng Anh 3, Unit 12's pattern.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "Where does a nurse work?" — "At a
______."
a) farm  b) nursing home  c) factory
**Answer: b) nursing home** — Vietnam's workplaces:
factory, farm, hospital, nursing home (Tiếng Anh 4,
Unit 12).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A ______ reports the news.
a) gardener  b) reporter  c) writer
**Answer: b) reporter** — Vietnam's job activities:
grow flowers, report the news, teach children, write
stories (Tiếng Anh 5, Unit 5).
