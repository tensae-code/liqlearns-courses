# Jobs — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Asking About Other People · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Doings
and Workplaces.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Cambodia's book, a pupil answers
"What do you do?" with "I'm a pupil."
**True** — the unit's opening pair (Grade 4, p.72).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "What does she do?" — "She's a teaches"
is a right answer.
**False** — the answer takes the job word: "She's a
teacher."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rwanda's job list has both cook's
partners: chef and shopkeeper.
**True** — chef and shopkeeper are both in P4's set
(pp.59–61).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In Vietnam's books, a farmer works at a
hospital.
**False** — a farmer works ON a farm; the hospital is
the doctor's and nurse's place (Tiếng Anh 4, Unit 12).
