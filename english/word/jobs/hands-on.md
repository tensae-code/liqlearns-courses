# Hands-on — Jobs

The lesson on your real street, not only on the page.

## The street jobs walk
Walk a street you know. At every workplace, stop and say
the job in English: "A seller. A driver. A mechanic."
Five jobs minimum. The worker you cannot name is your new
word to find in the lesson's list.

## The family jobs chart
Write your family's jobs in two columns: the person, the
job. Read the chart aloud in whole answers: "My mother is
a seller. My father is a farmer."

## The doing mime
With a partner: one mimes a job's doing — baking, driving,
teaching — the other names the job and says its sentence:
"A baker bakes bread." Five mimes, then swap.

## The job I would like
Draw yourself in the job you would like. Write Vietnam's
pair under it: "What would you like to be in the future?
I'd like to be a…" and "Because I'd like to…"
