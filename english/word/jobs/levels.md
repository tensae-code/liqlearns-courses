# Jobs — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the job words — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: naming the jobs first,
then the questions, then doings and workplaces.

## Level 1 — name the jobs
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name
teacher, doctor, nurse, farmer, seller, policeman, driver,
cook and pupil on pictures, and answer "What do you do?"
— "I'm a pupil" (Cambodia).

## Level 2 — ask about other people
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask and
answer for he and she — "What does she do?" "She's a
teacher." "Is she a teacher/seller?" — and for your
parents: "What do your parents do?" (Cambodia, Grade 4,
Unit 6).

## Level 3 — doings and workplaces
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give
each job its doing in the books' shape — "A baker bakes
bread," "He reports the news" — and its workplace:
"Where does he work? He works at a hospital / on a farm"
(Vietnam), including the future frame "What would you
like to be? I'd like to be a…"
