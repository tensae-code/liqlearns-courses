# Jobs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
job words — naming the jobs first, then the questions, then doings
and workplaces, the order the books teach it in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Jobs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name
teacher, doctor, nurse, farmer, seller, policeman, driver,
cook and pupil on pictures, and answer "What do you do?"
— "I'm a pupil" (Cambodia).

## Sub lesson 2 — Asking About Other People
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask and
answer for he and she — "What does she do?" "She's a
teacher." "Is she a teacher/seller?" — and for your
parents: "What do your parents do?" (Cambodia, Grade 4,
Unit 6).

## Sub lesson 3 — Doings and Workplaces
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give
each job its doing in the books' shape — "A baker bakes
bread," "He reports the news" — and its workplace:
"Where does he work? He works at a hospital / on a farm"
(Vietnam), including the future frame "What would you
like to be? I'd like to be a…"
