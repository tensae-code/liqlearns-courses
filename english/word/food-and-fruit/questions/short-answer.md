# Food and fruit — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Meals and First Foods · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Fruits and Likes · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Food in Whole
Answers.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three meals.
**Answer:** breakfast, lunch, dinner (Egypt, p.15).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three foods from Egypt's breakfast
plate.
**Answer:** any three of egg, cheese, beans, milk — and bread
from Ali's answer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five fruits from Palestine's and
Bangladesh's sets.
**Answer:** any five of orange, lemon, grape, apple, banana,
fig, olive, mango, guava, papaya.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Answer whole: Do you like guava? (Answer for
yourself — the no answer must name what you like instead.)
**Answer:** "Yes, I do." or "No, I don't. I like ___."
(Bangladesh, p.39.)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer in a whole sentence: What do you eat
for breakfast?
**Answer:** "I eat ___ and ___." — the learner's real
breakfast (Egypt's shape, pp.15–16).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do fruits and vegetables stand high on
Bangladesh's pyramid lesson?
**Answer:** they "have vitamins and help eyes" (Class 5,
pp.23–27).
