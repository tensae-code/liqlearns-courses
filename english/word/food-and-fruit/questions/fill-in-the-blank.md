# Food and fruit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** breakfast · lunch · dinner · milk · mango · grapes

**1. (Level 1)** We eat ______ in the morning.
**Answer: breakfast.**

**2. (Level 1)** We eat ______ in the middle of the day.
**Answer: lunch.**

**3. (Level 1)** We eat ______ in the evening.
**Answer: dinner.**

**4. (Level 1)** The breakfast plate has egg, cheese, beans,
and a glass of ______.
**Answer: milk** (Egypt, p.15).

**5. (Level 2)** "No, I don't. I like ______."
**Answer: mango** — Bangladesh's own model answer (Class 2,
p.39).

**6. (Level 2)** "What are these?" — "They're ______."
**Answer: grapes** (Palestine, p.40).
