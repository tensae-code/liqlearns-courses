# Food and fruit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The morning meal is ______.
a) lunch  b) dinner  c) breakfast
**Answer: c) breakfast** (Egypt, Primary 1 Term 2, p.15).

**2. (Level 1)** Egypt's breakfast plate holds egg, cheese,
beans, and a glass of ______.
a) juice  b) milk  c) water
**Answer: b) milk** (Egypt, p.15).

**3. (Level 1)** "What do you eat for breakfast?" — "I eat
______ and eggs."
a) bread  b) rice  c) fish
**Answer: a) bread** — Ali's own answer (Egypt, pp.15–16).

**4. (Level 2)** "What's this?" (one small green fruit that
grows in Palestine) — "It's a ______."
a) grapes  b) fig  c) olive
**Answer: b) fig** — Palestine's own pair: "It's a fig. It's
an olive" (Grade 1 Part 2, p.40).

**5. (Level 2)** "What are these?" — "They're ______."
a) grape  b) a grape  c) grapes
**Answer: c) grapes** — the book's first plural question
(Palestine, p.40).

**6. (Level 2)** "Do you like guava?" — the full yes is —
a) "Yes, I like."  b) "Yes, I do."  c) "Yes, I am."
**Answer: b) "Yes, I do."** (Bangladesh, Class 2, p.39 — the
book prints "Yes, I like" as its model in one place; the
exchange's taught shape is "Yes, I do. / No, I don't.")

**7. (Level 3)** On Bangladesh's Food Pyramid, the wide base
floor is ______.
a) sweets  b) grains  c) milk
**Answer: b) grains** — fruits and vegetables stand on the
floor above (Class 5, pp.23–27).
