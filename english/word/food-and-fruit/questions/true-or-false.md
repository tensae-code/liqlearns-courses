# Food and fruit — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Meals and First Foods · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Fruits and Likes · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Food in Whole
Answers.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breakfast is the evening meal.
**False** — breakfast is the morning meal. Dinner is the
evening meal (Egypt, p.15).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ali eats bread and eggs for breakfast.
**True** — his own answer in Egypt's book (pp.15–16).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We say "It's an olive" but "It's a fig."
**True** — an before a vowel sound; Palestine's own pair
(p.40).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "They're grapes" is the answer to "What's
this?" pointing at one grape.
**False** — "They're…" answers "What are these?" about many.
One grape: "It's a grape" (Palestine, p.40).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Guava, papaya, and mango are fruits in
Bangladesh's book.
**True** — its fruit labels: a guava, a banana, an orange, a
papaya; "I like mango" (Class 2, p.39).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** On the Food Pyramid, sweets stand at the
wide base.
**False** — grains stand at the base; fruits and vegetables
above them (Bangladesh, Class 5).
