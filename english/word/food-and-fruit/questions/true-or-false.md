# Food and fruit — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Breakfast is the evening meal.
**False** — breakfast is the morning meal. Dinner is the
evening meal (Egypt, p.15).

**2. (Level 1)** Ali eats bread and eggs for breakfast.
**True** — his own answer in Egypt's book (pp.15–16).

**3. (Level 2)** We say "It's an olive" but "It's a fig."
**True** — an before a vowel sound; Palestine's own pair
(p.40).

**4. (Level 2)** "They're grapes" is the answer to "What's
this?" pointing at one grape.
**False** — "They're…" answers "What are these?" about many.
One grape: "It's a grape" (Palestine, p.40).

**5. (Level 2)** Guava, papaya, and mango are fruits in
Bangladesh's book.
**True** — its fruit labels: a guava, a banana, an orange, a
papaya; "I like mango" (Class 2, p.39).

**6. (Level 3)** On the Food Pyramid, sweets stand at the
wide base.
**False** — grains stand at the base; fruits and vegetables
above them (Bangladesh, Class 5).
