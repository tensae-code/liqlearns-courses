# Video script — Food and fruit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morning! Time for **breakfast**.
An **egg**. **Bread**. **Cheese**. A glass of **milk**.
What do you eat for breakfast? I eat bread and eggs!

Middle of the day — **lunch**.
Evening — **dinner**. Breakfast, lunch, dinner!

Now the market. What's this?
It's an **orange**! It's a **fig**!
It's a **banana**. An **apple**. A **mango**!
And these? They're **grapes**!

Do you like guava? Yes, I do!
Do you like fish? No, I don't. I like mango!

Now you. Name the food. Name the fruit.
Say what you like.
That is food — in English.
