**1. (Sub lesson 1)** deep · dark · doctor

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** door · doctor · dog
**Answer: doctor, dog, door** — second letters tie (o); third letters decide: c, g, o.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** test · terrace · term · terrible
**Answer: term, terrace, terrible, test** — third letters: r (term, terrace, terrible) before s (test); inside the r's, the fourth letter puts term (m) first; terrace and terrible tie to the fifth letter: a before i.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** terror · terrify · territory · terrible · terrace
**Answer: terrace, terrible, terrify, territory, terror** — fifth letters: a (terrace), then the i words, then o (terror); inside the i words the sixth letter decides: b (terrible), f (terrify), t (territory). India's page family, ordered by the rule the lesson teaches.
