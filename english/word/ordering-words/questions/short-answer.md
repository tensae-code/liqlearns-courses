# Ordering words — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Put in dictionary order: zoo, apple, mango, ball, tea.
**Answer: apple, ball, mango, tea, zoo** — first letters settle this set; no tie survives the first letter.

**2. (Level 2)** India's Class 9 section is called "How to look at an index". What does an index order, and by what rule?
**Answer: it orders topics (subjects), by the same deep letter order a dictionary uses for words** — Beehive, Class 9, Unit 9, worked on a real reproduced subject index.

**3. (Level 3)** Name four parts or gifts of a dictionary entry as India's Class 6 page works them.
**Answer: any four of: the headword in its ordered place; its meanings (all of them — ghastly holds two); the exact word for a job (term for a part of the school year, test for an examination); the word family grouped on the page (the terr- words); words that may follow the word (five that may follow "that")** — Honeysuckle, Class 6, Unit 5.
