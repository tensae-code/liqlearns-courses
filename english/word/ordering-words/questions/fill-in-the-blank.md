# Ordering words — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** When the first letters tie, the ______ letter decides; when those tie, the ______ decides.
**Answer: second; third** — and then the fourth, the fifth: the rule never stops (Barbados's ladder, Classes 1–3).

**2. (Level 2)** Rwanda's P.5 Unit 3 teaches "Using Tables of Contents and ______" — the contents finds a part of the book; the other finds a topic anywhere in it.
**Answer: Indexes** — P.5 Learner's Book, Unit 3, printed p.69, practised on specimens at printed pp.69–71.

**3. (Level 3)** Rwanda's dictionary tasks print the saying of a word beside it as an IPA ______.
**Answer: transcription** — P.5 Learner's Book, Unit 1, printed pp.18–19, and Test 2.2, printed p.50.
