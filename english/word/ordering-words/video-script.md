# Video script — Ordering words

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Five words. All start with D.
DARK. DEEP. DOCTOR. DOG. DOOR.
The first letter cannot help us. They all tie!

[Picture: five word cards, all starting with D]

So look DEEPER. The SECOND letter.
A… E… O… O… O…
DARK first. DEEP next.
But doctor, dog, door still tie!

Look deeper again. The THIRD letter.
Doctor: C. Dog: G. Door: O.
C… G… O. There they stand!

[Picture: the cards settling into line, letters popping]

Deeper and deeper. Second letter. Third. Fourth.
One letter always breaks the tie.
That is how a dictionary stands. That is how an index stands.

And when you LAND on your word — look!
The entry gives its meanings. Sometimes TWO.
Read them all. Pick the one that fits.

[Picture: a dictionary page, the word TERM standing big]

Now you. Take five words that tie.
Order them deep. Say the letter that decided.
You can order anything now.
