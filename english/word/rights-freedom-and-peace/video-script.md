# Video script — Rights, Freedom and Peace

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Music notes float behind the mascot; a song is playing.]

"Listen — a song of freedom! Songs can carry big words."

"Rights: what every person may have and do. Freedom: being free. Peace: no fighting, no war."

[The mascot sings one line softly.] "Now the words are yours, too."
