## Multiple choice

1. "Rights" are… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the things every person is allowed to have and do  b) presents  c) rules for one game
   **Answer: a).**

2. Freedom means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) being busy  b) being free — not held, stopped or ruled unfairly  c) being rich
   **Answer: b).**

3. Peace is the opposite of… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) music  b) war and fighting  c) silence
   **Answer: b).**

4. A "song of freedom" is sung by people who… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) want rights, freedom and peace  b) want to sleep  c) are selling a business
   **Answer: a).**
