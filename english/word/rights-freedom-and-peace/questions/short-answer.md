## Short answer

1. Name two rights every person has. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any two of: the right to learn, to speak, to be safe (human rights).**

2. What are the singers of a freedom song asking for? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: rights, freedom and peace.**

3. Write one sentence with "peace". (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any true sentence, e.g. "We want peace in our country."**
