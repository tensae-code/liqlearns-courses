# Video script — Colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look! Colours everywhere!

**Red.** A red bus.
**Green.** A green car.
**Blue.** A blue car.
**Yellow.** A yellow taxi.
**Black.** A black bike.
**White.** A white van.

What colour? Red!
What colour? Blue!

Now the rainbow. Red — orange — yellow — green — blue —
indigo — violet. Seven colours!

Mix them! Red and yellow make **orange**.
Yellow and blue make **green**.
Blue and red make **violet**.

Now you. Look around you.
What colour is this? What colour is that?
Name the colours — in English.
