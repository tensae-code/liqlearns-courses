# Colours — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** red · green · white · blue · black · yellow

**1. (Level 1)** A ______ car. (Palestine's line gives this
car two colours in its list — blue and green; either is
right here if it matches the book.)
**Answer: blue** (or green) — "blue car … green car"
(Palestine, p.21).

**2. (Level 1)** A ______ taxi.
**Answer: yellow** — "yellow taxi" (p.21).

**3. (Level 1)** A ______ bike.
**Answer: black** — "black bike" (p.21).

**4. (Level 2)** "What colour?" — "______ and black."
**Answer: Red** — the book's own pattern answer (p.16).

**5. (Level 3)** Yellow + blue = ______.
**Answer: green** (Mongolia's mixing chart, p.72).
