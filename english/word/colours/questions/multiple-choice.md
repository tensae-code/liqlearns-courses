# Colours — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The First Six Colours · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Rainbow Seven and Colours on Things · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Mixing Colours.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "What colour is the bus in Palestine's
colouring line?" — The bus is ______.
a) blue  b) red  c) green
**Answer: b) red** — "red bus" (Palestine, Grade 1 Part 2,
p.21).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Palestine's book teaches six first colours.
Which one is NOT one of the six?
a) yellow  b) black  c) violet
**Answer: c) violet** — the six are red, green, white, blue,
black, yellow (p.16); violet joins with the rainbow set.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The taxi in the colouring line is ______.
a) yellow  b) white  c) black
**Answer: a) yellow** — "yellow taxi" (Palestine, p.21).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Red + yellow = ______ (Mongolia's chart).
a) green  b) violet  c) orange
**Answer: c) orange** — the mixing chart (Mongolia, English
X, p.72).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which three are the primary colours?
a) orange, green, violet  b) red, yellow, blue
c) black, white, gray
**Answer: b) red, yellow, blue** — Mongolia's colour text;
black, white and gray are the neutral colours.
