# Colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "What colour is the bus in Palestine's
colouring line?" — The bus is ______.
a) blue  b) red  c) green
**Answer: b) red** — "red bus" (Palestine, Grade 1 Part 2,
p.21).

**2. (Level 1)** Palestine's book teaches six first colours.
Which one is NOT one of the six?
a) yellow  b) black  c) violet
**Answer: c) violet** — the six are red, green, white, blue,
black, yellow (p.16); violet joins with the rainbow set.

**3. (Level 2)** The taxi in the colouring line is ______.
a) yellow  b) white  c) black
**Answer: a) yellow** — "yellow taxi" (Palestine, p.21).

**4. (Level 3)** Red + yellow = ______ (Mongolia's chart).
a) green  b) violet  c) orange
**Answer: c) orange** — the mixing chart (Mongolia, English
X, p.72).

**5. (Level 3)** Which three are the primary colours?
a) orange, green, violet  b) red, yellow, blue
c) black, white, gray
**Answer: b) red, yellow, blue** — Mongolia's colour text;
black, white and gray are the neutral colours.
