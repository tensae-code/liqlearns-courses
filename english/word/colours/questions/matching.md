# Colours — matching

Match each thing to its colour, the way Palestine's book
pairs them (Grade 1 Part 2, p.21).

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — things and their colours.
1. bus — **red**
2. taxi — **yellow**
3. van — **white**
4. bike — **black**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — Mongolia's mixes.
1. red + yellow — **orange**
2. yellow + blue — **green**
3. blue + red — **violet**
