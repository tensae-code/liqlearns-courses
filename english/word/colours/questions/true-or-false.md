# Colours — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The First Six Colours · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Rainbow Seven and Colours on Things · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Mixing Colours.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yellow is one of Palestine's six first
colours.
**True** — red, green, white, blue, black, yellow (p.16).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Palestine's colouring line, the bus is
blue.
**False** — the bus is red; the car is blue (p.21).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigo is one of the rainbow seven in
Mongolia's unit.
**True** — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo,
violet (English X, p.66).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Black, white and gray are primary colours.
**False** — they are neutral colours; the primaries are
red, yellow and blue (Mongolia, Self-Check colour text).
