# Colours — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Palestine's six first colours.
**Answer:** red, green, white, blue, black, yellow
(Grade 1 Part 2, Unit 12, p.16).

**2. (Level 1)** What colour is the van in the colouring
line?
**Answer:** white — "white van" (p.21).

**3. (Level 2)** Write the rainbow seven in order.
**Answer:** red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo,
violet (Mongolia, English X, Unit 5, p.66).

**4. (Level 3)** Blue + red makes which colour?
**Answer:** violet (Mongolia's mixing chart, p.72).
