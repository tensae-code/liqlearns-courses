# Colours — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The First Six Colours · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Rainbow Seven and Colours on Things · (Level 3) -> Sub
lesson 3 — Mixing Colours.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Palestine's six first colours.
**Answer:** red, green, white, blue, black, yellow
(Grade 1 Part 2, Unit 12, p.16).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What colour is the van in the colouring
line?
**Answer:** white — "white van" (p.21).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the rainbow seven in order.
**Answer:** red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo,
violet (Mongolia, English X, Unit 5, p.66).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Blue + red makes which colour?
**Answer:** violet (Mongolia's mixing chart, p.72).
