# Hands-On — Optical Illusions

Things you do with paper, your hands and your eyes. No special materials needed.

1. **Duck or rabbit?** Draw the Duck or Rabbit picture (or find it) and show it to three people. Ask each one: "What do you see?" Turn the picture and ask again.
2. **A hole in your hand.** Roll a sheet of paper into a tube. Hold it to one eye and look through it. Put your other hand flat beside the tube, halfway along it, and look with both eyes open — a hole appears in your hand.
3. **Another finger.** Hold a finger up close in front of your eyes and look past it at something far away. Your one finger becomes two ghost fingers.
4. **Measure the tables.** Draw two tables that look different in size — then measure them with a ruler and show they are the same.
5. **Present one illusion.** Choose one illusion from the lesson, draw it big, and present it: show it, let everyone look, ask "What do you see?", then explain the trick.
