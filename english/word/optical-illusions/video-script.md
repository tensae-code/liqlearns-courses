# Video Script — Optical Illusions

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each illusion appears on screen as it is named.

[Picture: a duck's head drawn in lines]

Look! A duck! … Wait. Turn it. Now a rabbit! One picture — two animals! This is an optical illusion. It fools your eyes!

[Picture: two tables side by side, one looking longer]

This table is big. This table is small. No! Measure! They are the same! Your eyes fooled you!

[Picture: circles of snakes that seem to turn]

Snakes! They are moving! … No. Stop. Look closely. They do not move. It is a picture. Your eyes fooled you again!

[Picture: a child holding up a paper tube to one eye, looking at their hand]

Now you try! Make a hole in your hand — with no hole! Show a friend. "What do you see?" Fooled you!
