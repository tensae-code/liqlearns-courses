# Matching — Optical Illusions

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match the illusion to what it does.

1. Duck or Rabbit (Sub lesson 1)
2. Cat and Mouse (Sub lesson 1)
3. young-or-old woman (Sub lesson 1)
4. Table illusion (Sub lesson 2)
5. Fraser Spiral (Sub lesson 2)
6. Rotating Snakes (Sub lesson 2)
7. hole in your hand (Sub lesson 2)

a) two tables that look different but measure the same
b) a woman who is young looking away and old looking down
c) circles that look like a spiral
d) a head that is a duck one way and a rabbit the other
e) a hole that appears in your palm but is not there
f) a mouse hidden inside a cat picture
g) circles of snakes that seem to move but hold still

Answers: 1-d, 2-f, 3-b, 4-a, 5-c, 6-g, 7-e
