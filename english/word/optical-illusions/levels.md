# Levels — Optical Illusions

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Two pictures in one | Look at the Duck or Rabbit, the Cat and Mouse and the young-or-old woman, say both things each picture can be, and ask and answer "What do you see?" |
| 2. Fooling size, shape and movement | Name the unit's size, shape and movement illusions, say how each one fools the eye, do the hole-in-your-hand and another-finger tricks, and present one chosen illusion to the class. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can show a friend a picture that fools the eye — and explain the trick.
