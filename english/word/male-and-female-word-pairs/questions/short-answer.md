## Short answer

1. What is the difference between a masculine word and a feminine word? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A masculine word names a male; a feminine word names a female.

2. Sort these into masculine and feminine: wife, father, sister, groomsmen. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Masculine: father, groomsmen. Feminine: wife, sister.

3. Write the pair: the male word for the man at a wedding, and the female word for the woman. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** bridegroom (masculine) — bride (feminine).

4. A sentence says "The bridegroom's sister smiled." Which words are pair words, and which side is each? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** bridegroom — masculine; sister — feminine.
