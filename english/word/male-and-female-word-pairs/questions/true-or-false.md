## True or false

1. "Aunt" is a feminine word. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. "Brother" names a female. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — brother is masculine; it names a male.

3. The feminine word of a pair is made by adding letters to the masculine word. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — each side of the pair is its own word: bride and bridegroom are two different words.

4. "Groomsmen" and "bridesmaids" are a pair. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

5. Choosing the right word of a pair tells the reader whether the person is male or female. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
