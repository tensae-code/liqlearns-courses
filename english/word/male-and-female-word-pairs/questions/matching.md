## Matching

Match the masculine word to its feminine pair. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. brother — c
2. father — a
3. bridegroom — d
4. groomsmen — b

a) aunt
b) bridesmaids
c) girl
d) bride

**Answers:** 1–c (brother/girl), 2–a (father/aunt), 3–d (bridegroom/bride), 4–b (groomsmen/bridesmaids) — the pairs as the book gives them.
