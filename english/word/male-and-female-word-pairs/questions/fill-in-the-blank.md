## Fill in the blank

1. "Words that name males are called ___ words." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: masculine.**

2. "Words that name females are called ___ words." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: feminine.**

3. "The feminine pair of 'brother' in the book's set is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: girl.**

4. "At the wedding, the bride stood with her ___ (the women who stand with a bride)." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: bridesmaids.**

5. "The masculine pair of 'bride' is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: bridegroom.**
