# Levels — Male and Female Word Pairs

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson (restructure 2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching order. The old layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below are the lesson's own parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Male words and female words | The two sets: masculine words name males (brother, father, bridegroom, groomsmen), feminine words name females (girl, aunt, bride, bridesmaids, wife, sister) — the word set of the book's wedding unit. Guyana, *Fun With Language Book 3*, Unit 17, printed pp.80–85. |
| Sub lesson 2 — The pairs | The pairs matched as the book gives them (brother/girl, father/aunt, bridegroom/bride, groomsmen/bridesmaids) and used in sentences about people. Guyana, *Fun With Language Book 3*, Unit 17, printed pp.84–85. |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
