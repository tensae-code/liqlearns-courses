# Lesson Plan — Male and Female Word Pairs

**One thing:** some words come in pairs — a **masculine** word that names a male and a **feminine** word that names a female: brother/girl, father/aunt, bridegroom/bride.

## Sub lessons

1. Male words and female words — masculine words name males, feminine words name females
2. The pairs — matching each male word with its female pair and using the pairs in sentences

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Words for people can name a male or a female. The male word is called **masculine**; the female word is called **feminine**.
- The pairs the book works with, from its wedding unit: brother/girl, father/aunt, bridegroom/bride, groomsmen/bridesmaids, and the wedding words wife and sister on the female side.
- The pair words are not the male word changed — each side has its own word: a bridegroom is not a "male bride"; he has his own word.
- Choosing the right word of a pair tells the reader at once whether the person is male or female.
