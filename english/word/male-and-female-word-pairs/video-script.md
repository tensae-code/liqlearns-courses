# Video script — Male and Female Word Pairs

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A bride and a bridegroom appear side by side.] "Look! A BRIDE — a woman. A BRIDEGROOM — a man. Two words, a PAIR!"

[The word cards "brother" and "girl" appear.] "BROTHER — male. GIRL — female. Words for males are MASCULINE. Words for females are FEMININE."

[More pairs flip in: father/aunt, groomsmen/bridesmaids.] "FATHER and AUNT. GROOMSMEN and BRIDESMAIDS. Every pair has two own words!"

[Back to the mascot.] "Say the word — and we know: male or female. That is the pair's job!"
