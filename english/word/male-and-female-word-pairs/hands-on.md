# Hands-on — Male and Female Word Pairs

## Sort the sets

Write these words on slips of paper: brother, bride, father, wife, groomsmen, girl, sister, bridesmaids, aunt, bridegroom. Sort them into two piles — masculine and feminine — then pair each slip with its partner from the other pile. Check your pairs against the lesson.

## Pairs in a story

Take a story you know with people in it. List every people word in it, mark each one M (masculine) or F (feminine), and write the pair word for each one where the story does not give it.
