# Lesson plan — Languages and Peoples

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Languages and Peoples
**Lesson:** languages-and-peoples — the languages people speak — in Venezuela and in the world — and the peoples who speak them, including Venezuela's indigenous peoples
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name languages and the peoples who speak them, and say which languages are spoken in their own country.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 10 book teaches languages and nationalities through the peoples who speak them — the languages of Venezuela and the world, and Venezuela's own indigenous peoples by name. The course's countries-and-nationalities lesson carries flags and origins; this people-and-language set had no home, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Languages of the world**
2. **Peoples of Venezuela**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. If the learner knows countries-and-nationalities first, this lesson will feel easier — they might try it first.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Languages of the world": name the world's languages beside the peoples and countries they belong to
- after sub lesson "Peoples of Venezuela": name Venezuela's indigenous peoples and the languages they keep

## What you need
Nothing special — a world map helps if one is near.

Build from the learner's own languages outward: home first, then the country, then the world.
