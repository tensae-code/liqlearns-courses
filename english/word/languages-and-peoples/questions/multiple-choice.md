## Multiple choice

1. A people's language is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) only words  b) a people's voice  c) a flag
   **Answer: b)** — the lesson ties each language to the people who speak it.

2. Spanish is the main language of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Brazil  b) Venezuela  c) France
   **Answer: b) Venezuela.**

3. The Wayuu and the Warao are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) languages of Europe  b) indigenous peoples of Venezuela  c) cities
   **Answer: b)** — the book names Venezuela's indigenous peoples.

4. Brazil's language is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Spanish  b) French  c) Portuguese
   **Answer: c) Portuguese.**
