## Short answer

1. Name two languages of the world. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any two of: Spanish, English, Portuguese, French (or another true language).

2. Name two indigenous peoples of Venezuela. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: Wayuu, Warao, Pemón, Yanomami.

3. What does every language name, besides words? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A people — the people who speak it.
