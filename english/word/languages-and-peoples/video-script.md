# Video script — Languages and Peoples

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A map of the world; each country lights up with the sound of its language.]

"Every people speaks. Every language has a home."

"Spanish here. English there. Portuguese, French — and many more."

"And the first peoples of a land keep their own languages. Say their names with respect."
