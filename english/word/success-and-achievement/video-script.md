# Video script — Success and Achievement

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A light bulb flickers, then shines.]

“Edison kept going. That is perseverance.”

[Eight words appear one by one.] “Passion. Work. Good. Focus. Push. Serve. Ideas. Persist.”

[The mascot nods.] “Eight secrets. The last one holds the rest: persist.”
