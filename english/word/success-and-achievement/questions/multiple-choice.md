## Multiple choice

1. Perseverance means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) giving up early  b) keeping going when a thing is hard  c) being lucky
   **Answer: b).**

2. Which of these is one of the eight secrets? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) sleep  b) persist  c) wait
   **Answer: b).**

3. “Tireless” means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) never working  b) not getting tired; keeping on  c) sleepy
   **Answer: b).**

4. Helen Keller showed perseverance by… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) flying a plane  b) learning to read and write without sight or hearing  c) winning races
   **Answer: b).**
