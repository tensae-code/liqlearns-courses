# Levels — Success and Achievement

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Success words | the Grade 11 success box — perseverance, determination, goals, objectives, tireless, humility, goal setting, planning (spelled right — the book misprints three of them) |
| Perseverance — not giving up | the perseverance text: what it means, the people who showed it — Edison, Harriet Tubman, Helen Keller, Stevie Wonder — and ways to show it yourself |
| Eight secrets of success | Richard St. John's TED talk, set as a video task in the book: passion, work, good, focus, push, serve, ideas, persist |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
