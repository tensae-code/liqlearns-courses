# Video script — Describing people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is my friend. Let me tell you about her!

**What does she look like?** Ask my eyes!
She is **tall**. She has **long hair**.
She has brown eyes. She is **beautiful**.

**What is she like?** Ask my heart!
She is **kind**. She is **funny**.
She is **generous** — she gives and gives.
She is my friend!

Two questions. Two answers.
Eyes: look like. Heart: be like.

Now you. Your friend! What does he look like?
What is he like?
That is describing people — in English.
