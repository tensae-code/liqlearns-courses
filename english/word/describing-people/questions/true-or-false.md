# Describing people — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Appearance Words · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Personality Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Two
Questions.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** "What is she like?" asks what food she
likes.
**False** — it asks about her personality: kind? funny?
shy? (Cambodia, Unit 22.)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bald is an appearance word.
**True** — Cambodia's appearance set, with beard and
moustache.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Bolivia's sorting, selfish and greedy
stand with the good adjectives.
**False** — they stand with the bad set, against kind and
generous (Year 3, Topic 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "She looks like her mother" describes her
personality.
**False** — look like is the eyes' frame: same appearance
(Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 12).
