# Describing people — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Appearance Words · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Personality Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Two
Questions.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three appearance words from Rwanda's
set.
**Answer:** any three of tall, short, fat, thin, slim,
overweight, attractive, handsome, beautiful, ugly (P4,
Unit 2).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three personality words from
Rwanda's Primary 4 set.
**Answer:** any three of confident, humble, friendly,
fearful, funny, generous, honest, intelligent, obedient,
kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask about a man's appearance in one
question.
**Answer:** "What does he look like?" (Cambodia's unit
title question.)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask about the same man's personality.
**Answer:** "What is he like?" — the second title question.
