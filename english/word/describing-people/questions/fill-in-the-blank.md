# Describing people — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** tall · generous · beard · shy · looks

**1. (Level 1)** She's ______. Isn't she?
**Answer: tall** — Rwanda's own model sentence (P4, p.27).

**2. (Level 1)** He has a ______ on his face — hair on his
chin.
**Answer: beard** (Cambodia's appearance set).

**3. (Level 2)** A ______ person gives freely and helps
others.
**Answer: generous** (Bolivia's matched meaning).

**4. (Level 2)** She is ______: she does not like talking
to new people.
**Answer: shy** (Cambodia's personality set).

**5. (Level 3)** She ______ happy today. (look + adjective)
**Answer: looks** — Nepal's frame: look + adjective.
