# Describing people — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Appearance Words · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Personality Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Two
Questions.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "What does she look like?" — "She is
______ and has long hair."
a) kind  b) tall  c) generous
**Answer: b) tall** — look like asks about appearance
(Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 22).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "What is he like?" — "He is ______."
a) bald  b) friendly  c) short
**Answer: b) friendly** — be like asks about personality
(Cambodia's personality set: friendly, shy, lazy, patient).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Reliable means ______.
a) slow to act  b) can be trusted  c) gives freely
**Answer: b) can be trusted** — Bolivia's definition
matching (Year 3, Topic 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A man with no hair is ______.
a) bald  b) slim  c) handsome
**Answer: a) bald** — Cambodia's appearance set.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He looks ______ his father." (same face,
same family look)
a) like  b) likes  c) look
**Answer: a) like** — look like + a person (Nepal, Grade 7,
Unit 12).
