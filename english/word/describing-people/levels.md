# Describing people — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — describing a person — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the eyes' words first, then
the heart's words, then the two questions kept apart.

## Level 1 — appearance
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe
what a person looks like — tall, short, slim, long hair,
a beard, bald — in whole sentences, without a miss.

## Level 2 — personality
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe
what a person is like — kind, friendly, shy, generous,
honest, lazy — and sort the books' adjectives into good
and bad the way Bolivia's book does.

## Level 3 — the two questions
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"What does she look like?" for appearance and "What is he
like?" for personality, answer each with its own set, and
use look / look like / be like in the right frame.
