# Describing people — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
describing a person — the eyes' words first, then the heart's
words, then the two questions kept apart, the order the books
teach it in.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Appearance Words
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe
what a person looks like — tall, short, slim, long hair,
a beard, bald — in whole sentences, without a miss.

## Sub lesson 2 — Personality Words
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe
what a person is like — kind, friendly, shy, generous,
honest, lazy — and sort the books' adjectives into good
and bad the way Bolivia's book does.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Two Questions
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"What does she look like?" for appearance and "What is he
like?" for personality, answer each with its own set, and
use look / look like / be like in the right frame.
