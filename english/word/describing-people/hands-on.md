# Hands-on — Describing people

The lesson on real people, kindly, not only on the page.

## Guess who
In a pair or a group: one describes a person everyone knows
— no name, eyes first, then heart. The others guess. The
describer must use at least three appearance words and
three personality words before the name can be guessed.

## Photo talk
Take one photo with people in it. Describe each person in
two sentences: one for the eyes, one for the heart. Write
the sentences under the photo. Kind words for real people —
the class rule from the lesson plan.

## The two-question interview
Interview a partner: ask "What do you look like?" — no,
wait: ask about a person your partner loves. "What does
your mother look like?" "What is she like?" Write their
answers in two columns, EYES and HEART, and read the
columns back. Then swap chairs.

## Sort the set
Write Bolivia's adjectives on twelve slips. Sort them into
GOOD and BAD piles, then match six to their meanings:
reliable — can be trusted; generous — gives freely;
hesitant — slow to act. Check against the lesson.
