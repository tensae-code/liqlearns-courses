# Hands-on — Idioms and collocations

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with real
texts and real people, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Idiom hunt.** Hunt one page of any book for a fixed phrase. Write the phrase whole, its whole meaning, and one sentence of your own using it — the hunt Egypt's books set.
2. **make/do/have sort.** On cards, write twenty partnerships without their verbs (a cake, a favour, the washing-up, a phone call, a headache…). Sort them under MAKE, DO, HAVE, then check against Libya's sets.
3. **Proverb in plain words.** Take 'A friend in need is a friend indeed.' Say it in the plainest words you can, then tell of one real moment it describes.
4. **Money idioms.** Match Uzbekistan's set — money doesn't grow on trees, money talks, spend money like water — to plain meanings, then use one about a real purchase.
