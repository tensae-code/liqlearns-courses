# Idioms and collocations — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** You can always work out an idiom's meaning from its separate words. **Answer: False** — Egypt's tip says the opposite: the meaning can't be deduced from its words

**2. (Level 2)** 'Play volleyball' and 'ride a bike' are collocations — habitual word partnerships. **Answer: True** — Libya G7 matches play/ride/go/make to their partners as wholes (U3.6)

**3. (Level 3)** There is a grammar rule that decides between make and do for every phrase. **Answer: False** — no rule decides; the partnership is learned pair by pair — collocations must be memorised (Sierra Leone)
