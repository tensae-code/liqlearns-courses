# Idioms and collocations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**1. (Level 1)** You ______ the washing-up, but you ______ a cake. **Answer: do — make** — Libya's Grade 5 workbook collocations, in their own seats

**2. (Level 2)** Collocations follow no rule, so they must be ______. **Answer: memorised** — Sierra Leone SSS2 states it plainly (L27–28)

**3. (Level 2)** 'Money doesn't grow on ______' warns that money is not free or endless. **Answer: trees** — Uzbekistan EM9 money set (U4, p.44)
