# Idioms and collocations — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Explain the proverb 'a friend in need is a friend indeed' in plain words. **Answer: a friend who helps you when you are in trouble is a true friend** — Sierra Leone's Idioms and Proverbs lesson is built on this saying (L123)

**2. (Level 2)** Complete the partnerships: ___ a phone call; ___ a headache. **Answer: make a phone call; have a headache** — Libya G12's make/do/have worked row (p.44)

**3. (Level 3)** Uzbekistan's set: what does 'spend money like water' mean? **Answer: to spend money freely, as if it could never run out** — one of the EM9 money-idiom matches (U4, p.44)
