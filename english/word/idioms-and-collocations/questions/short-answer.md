# Idioms and collocations — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Idioms as Whole Phrases · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Collocations and Their Partnerships · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Using Idioms and Collocations.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the proverb 'a friend in need is a friend indeed' in plain words. **Answer: a friend who helps you when you are in trouble is a true friend** — Sierra Leone's Idioms and Proverbs lesson is built on this saying (L123)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Complete the partnerships: ___ a phone call; ___ a headache. **Answer: make a phone call; have a headache** — Libya G12's make/do/have worked row (p.44)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Uzbekistan's set: what does 'spend money like water' mean? **Answer: to spend money freely, as if it could never run out** — one of the EM9 money-idiom matches (U4, p.44)
