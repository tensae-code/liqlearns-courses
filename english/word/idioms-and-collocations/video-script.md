# Video script — Idioms and collocations

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some words travel together. Always.

An idiom is a group of words with ONE meaning.

It is raining cats and dogs. No cats. No dogs. Only heavy rain!

Do not translate word by word. Learn the whole phrase.

A proverb is a short saying with a lesson. A friend in need is a friend indeed.

And some words just live together. Make a cake. Do the washing-up. Have a headache.

No rule. Learn the partners together. Like friends.
