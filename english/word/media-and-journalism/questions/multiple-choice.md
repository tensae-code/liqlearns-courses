## Multiple choice

1. The big line that names a story is the… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) section  b) headline  c) photograph
   **Answer: b) headline.**

2. The press means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) radio stations  b) newspapers and magazines  c) television channels
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which medium carries voices through the air, with no pictures? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) television  b) the press  c) radio
   **Answer: c) radio.**

4. The book calls the media the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) first power only  b) third, fourth and fifth power  c) last resort
   **Answer: b)** — after the powers that govern, the powers that inform.
