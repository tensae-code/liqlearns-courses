## Short answer

1. Name three parts of a newspaper. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any three of: front page, headline, sections, articles, photographs.

2. Name the three media of communication. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The press, radio and television.

3. Why are the media a power? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Who informs shapes what people know.
