# Video script — Media and Journalism

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A newspaper opens; its parts light up one by one.]

"A newspaper has parts. A front page. A headline. Sections. Articles."

"And news travels three ways: the press, the radio, television."

"Who tells the news shapes what people know. That is power."
