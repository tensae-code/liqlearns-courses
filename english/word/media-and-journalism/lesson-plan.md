# Lesson plan — Media and Journalism

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Media and Journalism
**Lesson:** media-and-journalism — the parts of a newspaper and the media of communication — press, radio and television — and the power they hold
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name the parts of a newspaper and the media, and say what power the media hold.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 11 book gives the media its own lesson: the parts of a newspaper named one by one, and press, radio and television weighed as a power in the country. No lesson in the course housed the media set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **The newspaper**
2. **The media and their power**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "The newspaper": name the parts of a newspaper and what each part does
- after sub lesson "The media and their power": name press, radio and television as the media of communication, and the power the book gives them

## What you need
A real newspaper helps the first sub lesson, if one is near.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — the learner meets the words in a doing task before any explaining.
2. **ADD IMAGE** — a picture for the lesson's world, when the picture pass reaches this lesson.
3. **Sub lesson 1 — The newspaper.**
4. **Sub lesson 2 — The media and their power.**
5. **ADD VIDEO** — the filmed run of this lesson, when the video pass reaches it.

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
Open a real paper and name its parts on the page itself; the media words follow the same way — one device, one name.
