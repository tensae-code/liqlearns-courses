# Lesson plan — Clothes

**Course:** English · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Clothes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the clothes words —
shirt, trousers, dress, skirt, shoes and their partners —
with the wearing pattern: what you usually wear against
what you are wearing today, and what things are made of.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Colours (clothes
are described by colour) and Level 1 reading. It needs
nothing else. If you know the colour words already, this
lesson will be easier for you — you might try Colours
first. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the clothes set: shirt, T-shirt, trousers, jeans,
   dress, skirt, shorts, shoes, boots, scarf, belt, hat.
2. Say what someone is wearing: "He is wearing a yellow
   shirt" — and what you usually wear against today.
3. Describe clothes by colour, the books' way: "a red
   skirt, blue jeans."
4. Say what a thing is made of at Level 3: cotton, silk,
   wool, leather, linen, nylon — "His tie is made of silk."

## What you need
The clothes you are wearing. That is the whole picture set.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner names everything they are
   wearing, in their own language; the lesson then gives
   the English names (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the clothes set; the wearing
   pattern; colours on clothes; materials (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the start of the explanation: a picture
     of a child in a shirt, trousers and shoes, with a
     scarf and a hat beside them — the student points and
     names five clothes before reading on.
   - ADD GIF in the wearing part: a picture of a person
     putting on a shirt, then shoes, then a scarf, 1–2
     seconds each — the student says "I am wearing…"
     for each as it goes on.
   - ADD AUDIO in the usually/today part: a voice saying
     what it usually wears, then what it is wearing
     today — the student answers for themselves before
     the voice does.
3. **Say it to your companion** — describe your companion's
   clothes, then your own usual clothes (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed —
     the getting-dressed report in the super-simple voice
     (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — draw yourself twice: usually, and
   today; label every item (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE — a picture of a dressed child with scarf and hat
  beside → the student points and names five clothes.
- GIF — a picture of a person putting on shirt, shoes,
  scarf → the student says "I am wearing…" for each.
- AUDIO — usually-wear / wearing-today sentences read
  aloud → the student answers for themselves first.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the
  student dresses along with it in words.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes trousers and shorts → length is the difference:
  trousers go to the shoes, shorts stop at the knees.
  Point at real ones.
- Says "I wearing a shirt" → the wearing pattern needs
  its partner: "I AM wearing…" (Cambodia's unit is built
  on the full shape).
- This lesson's next door: Weather and seasons — what you
  wear when it is snowy (Mongolia's question joins the two
  lessons).
