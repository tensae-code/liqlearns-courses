# Clothes — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Clothes · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Wearing Sentences and Colours · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 —
Materials.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five clothes words.
**Answer:** any five of shirt, T-shirt, sweater, trousers,
jeans, shorts, skirt, dress, shoes, boots, scarf, belt,
hat, tie.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are you wearing today? Answer whole.
**Answer:** "I am wearing…" with true clothes words
(Cambodia's shape, Grade 7, Unit 15).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you usually wear to school?
**Answer:** "Usually I wear…" with true clothes words —
the plain shape, against today's -ing.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three materials from Mongolia's list.
**Answer:** any three of cotton, linen, silk, nylon,
satin, leather, lace, velvet, rayon, wool, brocade,
polyester (English IX, p.53; English X, p.72).
