# Clothes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** shirt · trousers · dress · shoes · scarf · silk

**1. (Level 1)** She is wearing a yellow ______.
**Answer: dress.**

**2. (Level 1)** He is wearing a ______ and boots — it is
cold today.
**Answer: scarf.**

**3. (Level 2)** Usually I wear a T-shirt. Today I am
wearing a ______.
**Answer: shirt** (Cambodia's contrast, Unit 15).

**4. (Level 2)** My ______ are blue. (the legs, long)
**Answer: trousers.**

**5. (Level 3)** His tie is made of ______.
**Answer: silk** (Mongolia, English X, p.74).
