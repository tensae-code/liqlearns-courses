Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** shirt · trousers · dress · shoes · scarf · silk

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She is wearing a yellow ______.
**Answer: dress.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** He is wearing a ______ and boots — it is
cold today.

wearing a ______.
