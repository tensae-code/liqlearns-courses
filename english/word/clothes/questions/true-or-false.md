# Clothes — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A skirt and a dress are the same thing.
**False** — a skirt covers the legs only; a dress covers
the body and the legs together.

**2. (Level 2)** "Today I am wearing…" and "Usually I
wear…" say the same thing.
**False** — Cambodia's unit is built on their difference:
usually is the habit, wearing today is now (Unit 15).

**3. (Level 2)** In Bolivia's book, the cold highlands wear
colourful clothes and the hot valleys wear white.
**True** — Year 1, Topic 6, student p.173.

**4. (Level 3)** We say "made from" when we can still see
the material, like silk in a tie.
**False** — that is made OF. Made from is for when the
material has changed, like ketchup from tomatoes
(Mongolia, English IX, p.53).
