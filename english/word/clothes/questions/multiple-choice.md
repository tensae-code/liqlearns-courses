# Clothes — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Clothes · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Wearing Sentences and Colours · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 —
Materials.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You wear them on your feet. They are
______.
a) trousers  b) shoes  c) scarves
**Answer: b) shoes** — the set's feet words are shoes and
boots.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Blue ______" — Palestine's colouring pair
for the legs.
a) jeans  b) dress  c) hat
**Answer: a) jeans** — "a red skirt, blue jeans."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Today I ______ a shirt."
a) am wearing  b) usually wears  c) wear yesterday
**Answer: a) am wearing** — today takes the -ing shape
(Cambodia, Grade 7, Unit 15).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which describing word is in Mongolia's
clothes set?
a) striped  b) delicious  c) cloudy
**Answer: a) striped** — pretty, patterned, multicoloured,
neat, loose, casual, smart, striped, tight, plain
(Mongolia, English IX, p.50).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "His tie is made of ______."
a) silk  b) ketchup  c) glass
**Answer: a) silk** — Mongolia's own example (English X,
p.74).
