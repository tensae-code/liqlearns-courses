# Clothes — matching

Match each cloth to where it is worn.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — clothes and body parts.
1. shoes — **feet**
2. hat — **head**
3. scarf — **neck**
4. trousers — **legs**
5. shirt — **body (top)**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — materials and things (Mongolia's
examples).
1. silk — **a tie** ("His tie is made of silk")
2. glass — **a vase** ("This vase is made of glass")
3. tomatoes — **ketchup** (made from, not made of)
