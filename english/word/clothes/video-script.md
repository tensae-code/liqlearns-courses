# Video script — Clothes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at me! What am I wearing?

A **shirt**. **Trousers**. **Shoes**.
A red shirt! Blue trousers!

She is wearing a **dress**. A yellow dress.
He is wearing a **scarf**. It is cold today!

Usually I wear a T-shirt.
Today I am wearing a shirt. A shirt and jeans.

Cotton! Wool! This scarf is made of wool.

Now you. Look down. What are YOU wearing?
Name it all — in English.
