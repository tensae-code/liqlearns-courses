# Hands-on — Clothes

The lesson on your own body, not only on the page.

## Name what you wear
Stand up. Touch each thing you are wearing and say its
English name: "Shirt. Trousers. Shoes." Every morning for
three days, as you dress, name each thing as you put it on.

## The washing-line sort
Take five real clothes. Lay them in a line. Say each name
and its colour: "a red skirt," "blue jeans." Then close
your eyes while a partner moves one — open them and say
which one moved, by name and colour.

## Usually and today
Draw two people: one is you on a usual day, one is you
today. Label every item on both. Say the two sentences:
"Usually I wear…" "Today I am wearing…"

## Made of what?
Find three things at home made of cotton, wool, leather or
wood. Say each one's material sentence: "This shirt is
made of cotton."
