# Video script — Religions and Festivals Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A church, a mosque and a temple at a distance; candles; a table set for a festival.]

"All over the world, people believe, and pray, and worship — in churches, mosques, temples and synagogues."

"Faith is strong belief. Holy means set apart for God."

"And every faith marks its year with festivals — holy days of prayer, songs and food."
