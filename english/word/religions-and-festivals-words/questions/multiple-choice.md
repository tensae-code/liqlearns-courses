## Multiple choice

1. Faith is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a building  b) strong belief and trust  c) a festival
   **Answer: b).**

2. To pray is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) to speak words to God  b) to celebrate with food  c) to build a temple
   **Answer: a).**

3. A holy day is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) any day of rest  b) a market day  c) a day set apart by a religion
   **Answer: c).**

4. People celebrate a festival to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) mark a special day with joy  b) end a religion  c) close the places of worship
   **Answer: a).**
