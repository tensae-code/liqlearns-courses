## Short answer

1. Name three places where people worship together. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any three of: church, mosque, temple, synagogue.

2. What is a prayer? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Words spoken to God.

3. Say what a religious celebration marks, in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** It marks a special day of the faith — often remembering a story — with prayer, songs, food and gathering.
