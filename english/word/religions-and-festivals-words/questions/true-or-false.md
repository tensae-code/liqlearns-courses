## True or false

1. A synagogue is a place where Christians worship. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** A synagogue is where Jewish people worship; Christians worship in a church.

2. A ceremony is the set of actions done in the right way for a special day. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

3. Holy means ordinary and everyday. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** Holy means special to a religion — set apart for God.
