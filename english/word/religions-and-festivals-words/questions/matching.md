## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)

1. religion
2. holy
3. prayer
4. worship
5. church
6. mosque

a) words spoken to God
b) a people's way of believing in and worshipping God, or gods
c) the place where Muslims worship together
d) special to a religion — set apart for God
e) to show love and honour to God
f) the place where Christians worship together

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–d, 3–a, 4–e, 5–f, 6–c.
