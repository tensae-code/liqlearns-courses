# Lesson plan — Religions and Festivals Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Religions and Festivals Words
**Lesson:** religions-and-festivals-words — the words for the world's religions and their holy days: faith, belief and prayer, the places of worship, and the celebrations that mark the year
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can use the religion set — religion, faith, believe, holy, prayer, pray — and name the places where people worship, and talk about religious celebrations — festival, celebrate, holy day, ceremony — and say what a celebration marks.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Armenia's Grade 12 book teaches world religions and religious celebrations around the world as readings in its Religion topic. No lesson in the course housed this set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **World religions — the words**
2. **Religious celebrations**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

Nothing is needed before this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "World religions — the words": use the religion set — religion, faith, believe, holy, prayer, pray — and name the places where people worship
- after sub lesson "Religious celebrations": talk about religious celebrations — festival, celebrate, holy day, ceremony — and say what a celebration marks

## If the learner is stuck
Start from a celebration the learner knows — a holy day in their own town — and hang the set on it: what is believed, where people gather, what the day marks.
