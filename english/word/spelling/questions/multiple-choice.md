# Multiple Choice — Spelling

Choose the one right answer.

1. What is invented spelling? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Making up words that do not exist
   - B) Writing a word from the sounds you hear in it — spelling under construction
   - C) Copying without looking
   - D) Spelling with numbers
   **Answer: B**

2. The first step in spelling a word by ear is: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Guessing its letters
   - B) Saying the word slowly and stretching it
   - C) Closing your eyes
   - D) Counting its letters
   **Answer: B**

3. A child writes *sed* for *said*. This shows: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) A dull ear
   - B) A sharp ear — the sounds are carried truly; only the standard pattern is missing
   - C) Cheating
   - D) Nothing at all
   **Answer: B**

4. If you are unsure of a letter while writing, you should: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Stop writing the sentence
   - B) Leave a blank and give up
   - C) Write it as it sounds and check it after
   - D) Change to an easier word always
   **Answer: C**

5. Your by-ear spelling works as invented spelling if: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) It is long
   - B) It says the word when read aloud
   - C) It uses every letter once
   - D) It looks like a picture
   **Answer: B**

6. Standard spelling is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) The spelling one teacher prefers
   - B) The spelling everyone agrees to use, so every reader can read every writer
   - C) The longest spelling
   - D) Spelling by ear only
   **Answer: B**

7. *jumpt* becomes *jumped* because: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Longer is always right
   - B) The -ed ending spells that final sound by the pattern's rule
   - C) The letter p is silent
   - D) It is a sight word
   **Answer: B**

8. Words like *was*, *said*, *one* and *two* must be: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Avoided forever
   - B) Sounded out every time
   - C) Remembered as sight spellings, because they break the sound rules
   - D) Written in capitals
   **Answer: C**

9. Checking a spelling is done against: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) The ear, twice
   - B) Patterns, word families and remembered spellings
   - C) The word's colour
   - D) A friend's guess
   **Answer: B**

10. Ghana's curriculum names "Use invented spelling" at: (Sub lesson 1)
    - A) Basic 1
    - B) Basic 4, in the Using Writing Conventions strand
    - C) JHS 3 only
    - D) It never names it
    **Answer: B**
