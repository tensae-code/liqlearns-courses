# Short Answer — Spelling

Answer in a few words.

1. What are the three moves of spelling by ear? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Say the word slowly and stretched; hear its sounds in order; write a letter or pattern for each sound.

2. Why is invented spelling not "wrong spelling"? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Because it carries the word's sounds truly — it is spelling under construction, missing only the standard pattern.

3. Give the standard spelling of the by-ear word *wos*. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Was.

4. Name two words that break the sound rules and must be remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: was, said, one, two.

5. Against what three things do you check a spelling? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Patterns, word families, and remembered sight spellings.
