# Fill in the Blank — Spelling

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. Invented spelling is spelling under ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: construction**

2. Stretch the word and listen for its ____ in order. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: sounds**

3. *Fish* ends with the two-letter pattern ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: sh**

4. A word written by ear can be ____; a word never written is a sentence lost. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: fixed**

5. *Was* and *said* must be carried as ____ spellings. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: sight**
