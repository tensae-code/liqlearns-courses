# True or False — Spelling

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. Invented spelling is a shameful mistake that good writers never make. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it is the honest first spelling every writer uses, named in Ghana's curriculum.

2. If your written word says the word when read aloud, it works as invented spelling. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. You should stop mid-sentence whenever you are unsure of a letter. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — write it as it sounds and check it after.

4. *Said* checked by sound alone looks right as *sed*. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

5. Some words break the sound rules and must simply be remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**
