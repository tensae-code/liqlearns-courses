# Levels — Spelling

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** spell any word by ear — say it
  slowly, hear its sounds in order, write a letter or pattern
  for each sound (invented spelling).
- **Climb:** read back the word you built and judge whether it
  says the word aloud.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** check by-ear
  spellings against known patterns and word families, fix them
  to standard spelling, and carry the rule-breaking words
  (was, said, one, two) as remembered sight spellings.
