# Lesson Plan — Spelling

Unit 6 — Word Kinds. Planned shape: two sub lessons — first
write it as it sounds (invented spelling: building a word
from the sounds you hear, the honest first spelling every
writer uses), then from invented spelling to standard
spelling (checking a word against the sound patterns the
course teaches, and fixing it). The lesson is built from
Ghana's NaCCA Basic 4–6 curriculum, whose Using Writing
Conventions strand carries the Spelling standard at Basic 4
named plainly: "Use invented spelling." The course's phonics
lessons teach the sound patterns this lesson leans on
(two-letter-sounds, word-building); this lesson teaches the
act of spelling itself — getting a word onto the page, then
getting it right.

## Sub lessons

1. **Write it as it sounds** — invented spelling: say the
   word slowly, hear its sounds in order, write a letter or
   letter-pattern for each sound.
2. **From invented spelling to standard spelling** — check
   the word you built against known patterns and word
   families; fix what the patterns say is wrong; learn the
   words whose spelling must simply be remembered.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
