# Hands-On — Spelling

Things to do away from the page.

- **Stretch and write.** Take five words from your day. Say
  each slowly, stretched, and write what you hear — then
  check each one and write the standard spelling beside it.
- **Sight-spelling wall.** Keep a small list of the words
  that break the rules (was, said, one, two, because). Add
  each new one you meet; read the list aloud once a week.
