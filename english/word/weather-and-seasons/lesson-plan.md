# Lesson plan — Weather and seasons

**Course:** English · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Weather and seasons
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the weather words and
the four seasons — sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, hot,
cold — with the books' own questions: "What's the weather
like today?" and "What was the weather like yesterday?"
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Level 1 reading and
the Nouns lesson. It needs nothing else. If you know the
days and months already, this lesson will be easier for
you — you might try Days and months first. Recommended,
never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Today's Weather** — the weather words and "What's the weather
   like today?" (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **The Four Seasons** — spring, summer, autumn/fall and winter,
   matched to their weather and compared. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Yesterday and Strong Weather** — "What was the weather like
   yesterday?" with it was, and the strong-weather set; the
   today/yesterday question pair stands here because its new step
   is the past question. (Was depth Level 3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking
is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set
is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the weather set: sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy,
   snowy, stormy, hot, cold, warm, cool, dry, wet.
2. Name the four seasons: spring, summer, autumn (fall),
   winter.
3. Ask and answer for today: "What's the weather like
   today?" — "It's cloudy and windy."
4. Ask and answer for the past, Rwanda's shape: "What was
   the weather like yesterday?" — "It was …"
5. Name the strong-weather words at Level 3: flood,
   lightning, thunder, storm names (cyclone, hurricane,
   typhoon, tornado), heat wave, drought, wildfire.

## What you need
A window to look out of. Today's sky is the first picture.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner looks out of the window
   and says today's weather in their own language; the
   lesson then gives the English words (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the weather set; the four seasons;
   today against yesterday; strong weather
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the start of the explanation: a picture
     of the same street in four weathers — sunny, rainy,
     snowy, windy — the student names each weather before
     reading on.
   - ADD GIF in the seasons part: a picture of one tree
     changing through spring, summer, autumn, winter, 1–2
     seconds each — the student says each season as it
     changes.
   - ADD AUDIO in the question part: a voice asking
     "What's the weather like today?" and "What was the
     weather like yesterday?" — the student answers for
     their own place before the voice does.
3. **Say it to your companion** — give a weather report for
   today, yesterday, and your favourite season
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed —
     the weather report in the super-simple voice (made
     later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — a four-season picture of one place,
   each season labelled with its weather (lesson/lesson.md).

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes weather and season → a season is the long stretch,
  weather is what today does inside it: "In winter, today
  is snowy."
- Says "It was sunny today" for now → today takes IS,
  yesterday takes WAS: Rwanda's pair (pp.48–50) is the fix.
- Albania's list prints "lightening" — a book error. The
  word is taught corrected: **lightning**. Never copy the
  misprint.
- This lesson's next door: Clothes — what you wear for
  each weather (Rwanda matches clothes to weather).
