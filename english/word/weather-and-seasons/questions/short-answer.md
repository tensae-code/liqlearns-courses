# Weather and seasons — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Today's Weather · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Four Seasons · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Yesterday and
Strong Weather.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four weather words from Bolivia's
anagram set.
**Answer:** any four of sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy,
snowy, windy, hot, cold (Bolivia, Year 2, Topic 3).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the weather like today, where you
are? Answer in the books' shape.
**Answer:** "It's …" with a true weather word (Cambodia's
shape, Grade 5, p.115).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four seasons.
**Answer:** spring, summer, autumn (fall), winter.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Cambodia's flood lesson says what floods
can do. Finish it: "The floods can destroy the ______."
**Answer:** roads (Cambodia, Grade 5, pp.126–129).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What was the weather like yesterday, where
you are? Answer in Rwanda's shape.
**Answer:** "It was …" with a true weather word (Rwanda
P4, pp.49–50).
