# Weather and seasons — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** sunny · rainy · snowy · windy · spring · summer ·
lightning · flood

**1. (Level 1)** It's cloudy and ______.
**Answer: windy** (Cambodia, Grade 5, p.115).

**2. (Level 1)** "What do you wear when it is ______?"
**Answer: snowy** — Mongolia's question (English VI,
Unit 9).

**3. (Level 2)** ______ is the hottest time of the year.
**Answer: Summer** (Albania, Ch.32, Language structures).

**4. (Level 2)** After winter comes ______.
**Answer: spring.**

**5. (Level 3)** Thunder and ______ come with a storm.
**Answer: lightning** — Albania's list misprints it
"lightening"; the word is taught corrected.
