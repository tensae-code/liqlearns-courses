# Weather and seasons — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Today's Weather · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Four Seasons · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Yesterday and
Strong Weather.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** sunny · rainy · snowy · windy · spring · summer ·
lightning · flood

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It's cloudy and ______.
**Answer: windy** (Cambodia, Grade 5, p.115).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "What do you wear when it is ______?"
**Answer: snowy** — Mongolia's question (English VI,
Unit 9).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is the hottest time of the year.
**Answer: Summer** (Albania, Ch.32, Language structures).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After winter comes ______.
**Answer: spring.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Thunder and ______ come with a storm.
**Answer: lightning** — Albania's list misprints it
"lightening"; the word is taught corrected.
