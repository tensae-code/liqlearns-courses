# Weather and seasons — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Today's Weather · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Four Seasons · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Yesterday and
Strong Weather.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "What's the weather like today?" — "It's
______ and windy."
a) cloudy  b) table  c) yesterday
**Answer: a) cloudy** — Cambodia's own drilled pair
(Grade 5, p.115).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is a season?
a) rainy  b) winter  c) thunder
**Answer: b) winter** — the four seasons are spring,
summer, autumn/fall, winter (Albania, Ch.32).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Albania's guide names the autumn season
______.
a) fall  b) harvest  c) leaf-time
**Answer: a) fall** — the American name, printed in
Chapter 32's vocabulary list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Today is ______ than yesterday."
a) colder  b) coldest  c) coldly
**Answer: a) colder** — Albania's comparative example
(Ch.32).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "What was the weather like yesterday?" —
"It ______ sunny."
a) is  b) was  c) does
**Answer: b) was** — Rwanda's past pair (P4, pp.49–50).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which one is a storm name from India's
book?
a) drizzle  b) typhoon  c) rainbow
**Answer: b) typhoon** — cyclone, gale, typhoon, tornado,
hurricane, whirlwind (India, Class 10, Unit 1).
