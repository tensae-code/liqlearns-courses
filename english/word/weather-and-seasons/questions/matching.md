# Weather and seasons — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Today's Weather · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Four Seasons · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Yesterday and
Strong Weather.

Match each weather word to what you see or wear.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — weather and sky.
1. sunny — **the sun shines**
2. rainy — **rain falls**
3. snowy — **snow falls**
4. windy — **the wind blows**
5. cloudy — **clouds cover the sky**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — seasons and their weather (Albania's
and Bolivia's sets).
1. spring — **flowers and new leaves**
2. summer — **the hottest time of the year**
3. autumn / fall — **leaves fall**
4. winter — **cold and snow**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)** — strong weather and what it does.
1. flood — **water covers the roads** (Cambodia)
2. drought — **no rain for a long time** (Ecuador)
3. heat wave — **very hot days together** (Ecuador)
4. lightning — **the flash with thunder** (taught
   corrected; Albania prints "lightening")
