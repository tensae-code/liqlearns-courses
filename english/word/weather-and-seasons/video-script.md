# Video script — Weather and seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look out of the window. What's the weather like today?

**Sunny!** The sun is shining.
**Cloudy.** **Windy.** **Rainy** — rain, rain!
**Snowy.** White and cold.

It's cloudy and windy today.

Four seasons. **Spring** — flowers.
**Summer** — hot! The hottest time of the year.
**Autumn** — leaves fall. **Winter** — cold, snow.

And yesterday? What was the weather like yesterday?
It was sunny. It was hot.

Now you. Look out of YOUR window.
Tell me today. Tell me yesterday.
That is the weather — in English.
