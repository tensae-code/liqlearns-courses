# Hands-on — Weather and seasons

The lesson in your real sky, not only on the page.

## The seven-day weather diary
For seven days, write one line each morning: the date, and
"It's …" with today's weather word. On the eighth morning,
read the week back with WAS: "Yesterday it was rainy."

## The season tree
Draw one tree four times — spring, summer, autumn, winter.
Leaves on, leaves off, flowers, snow: label each drawing
with its season and one weather word.

## Dress for the weather
Lay out clothes for four weathers: sunny and hot; rainy;
snowy; windy and cold. Name each weather, then say one
thing you wear in it. Rwanda's books match clothes to
weather the same way.

## The forecast game
With a partner: one is the weather reporter for tomorrow,
the other asks "What's the weather like today?" and "What
was the weather like yesterday?" Answer both, then swap.
