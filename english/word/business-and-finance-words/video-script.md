# Video script — Business and Finance Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A small shop appears behind the mascot.]

"A shop is a business. Big businesses have their own words."

"People invest money. They buy stocks in a corporation. When prices go up and up, that is inflation."

[The mascot holds up a sign: SOLD.] "And one day, an owner may sell the business. Now you know the words for all of it."
