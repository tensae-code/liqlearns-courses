# Hands-on — Business and Finance Words

1. **Read the change.** Cards show business news lines ("Profits rose to £250m / by £50m"). Learners read each aloud and say: did it rise or fall, to how much, by how much?

2. **Sell or keep?** In pairs, one learner owns a small business and lists two reasons to sell it; the other asks one question using a lesson word (invest, stocks, firm). Swap roles.
