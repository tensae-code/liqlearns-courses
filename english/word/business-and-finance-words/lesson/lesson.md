# Business and Finance Words

## Do it first

Tunisia's Perform to Learn book gives its Economics lessons real business English: the financial market, inflation, business changes, selling a business. This lesson gathers those words into one place — the words you need to read business news and talk about money at work.

## Sub lesson 1 — Money in business

- **economist** — a person who studies money and business for a country.
- **stocks** — parts of a company that people buy and sell.
- **bond** — a paper a company or government sells to borrow money.
- **corporation** — a big company.
- **invest** — to put money into something to make more money.
- **firm** — a company or business.
- **inflation** — prices rising over time, so money buys less.

*Difficulty: Seven words from the financial-market lesson and the inflation text.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Changes, inflation and selling a business

Business news reports change with **to** and **by**:

- "Profits **rose to** £250m" — the new level is £250m.
- "Profits **rose by** £50m" — the change itself is £50m.

And the last Economics lesson asks why an owner sells a business — the reasons (age, a new plan, money troubles) and the preparations (knowing its value, telling the workers). Inflation belongs here too: the book's inflation text follows what rising prices do to a family's life.

*Difficulty: Two small words — to and by — that change what a number means, and the words for selling up.*

## Say it to your companion

Read your companion a headline: "Sales fell to 900 by 100." They must say the new level and the size of the fall. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the seven words with a short meaning for each.
2. Write two headlines: one with "rose to", one with "rose by" — different numbers, so the difference shows.
3. Write two reasons a person might sell their business.
