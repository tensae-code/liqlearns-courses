## Multiple choice

1. To invest means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) to spend all your money  b) to put money into something to make more money  c) to borrow a book
   **Answer: b).**

2. Inflation is when… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) prices fall  b) prices rise over time, so money buys less  c) a shop closes
   **Answer: b).**

3. "Profits rose to £250m" means the new level is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) £250m  b) £50m  c) unknown
   **Answer: a).**

4. "Profits rose by £50m" — the £50m is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the new level  b) the size of the change  c) a tax
   **Answer: b).**
