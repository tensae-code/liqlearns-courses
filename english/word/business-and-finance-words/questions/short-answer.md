## Short answer

1. What is the difference between "rose to £250m" and "rose by £50m"? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: "rose to" gives the new level (£250m); "rose by" gives the size of the change (£50m).**

2. Name two things a person does before selling a business, from the lesson. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any two of: knowing its value, telling the workers, preparing the papers/reasons.**

3. What does an economist study? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: money and business (for a country).**
