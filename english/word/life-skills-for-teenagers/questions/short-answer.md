# Short Answer — Life Skills for Teenagers

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name four needs and two wants. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Needs: food, water, clothing, shelter. Wants: jewellery, a car (or the newest shoes).
2. Why do needs come first in a budget? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because needs keep you alive and safe; wants can wait until the needs are covered.
3. Order these: a maths test tomorrow, a film, helping grandmother, lunch. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example order: lunch (body first), the maths test study, helping grandmother, the film last — urgent and important before pleasure.
4. Name the six cooking words of the lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Teaspoon, pan, boil, cool, ingredients, preheat.
