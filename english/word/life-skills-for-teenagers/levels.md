# Levels — Life Skills for Teenagers

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Money skills | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Budget: list income and outgo, follow the budget once made; needs first (food, water, clothing, shelter) and wants second (jewellery, a car) — the needs-versus-wants line, drawn and defended. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Running your days | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Assess the situation before acting, prioritise your commitments (what must be done first), and cook: the kitchen words of the book's recipes — teaspoon, pan, boil, cool, ingredients, preheat — and making a microwave cake. |
