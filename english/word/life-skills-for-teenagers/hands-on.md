# Hands-On — Life Skills for Teenagers

1. **Budget table.** In groups, plan a family week's budget on a fixed sum; present and defend the needs line.
2. **Needs-wants debate.** Pairs argue the hard cases: is a phone a need? Is a bicycle? Use assess and prioritise.
3. **Cook the cake.** Follow the microwave cake recipe, naming each step's word as you do it (adult watching the hot parts).
4. **Day in order.** Commitment cards for one busy day sorted as a class into do-first, do-later, drop.
