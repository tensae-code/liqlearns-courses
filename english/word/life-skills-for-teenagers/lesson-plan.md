# Lesson Plan — Life Skills for Teenagers

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the everyday skills a teenager runs a life with — making and following a budget, telling real needs from wants, cooking, and assessing and prioritising your commitments.

## Sub lessons

1. **Money skills.** *Can-do: Budget: list income and outgo, follow the budget once made; needs first (food, water, clothing, shelter) and wants second (jewellery, a car) — the needs-versus-wants line, drawn and defended.*
2. **Running your days.** *Can-do: Assess the situation before acting, prioritise your commitments (what must be done first), and cook: the kitchen words of the book's recipes — teaspoon, pan, boil, cool, ingredients, preheat — and making a microwave cake.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Life skills for teenagers (item 441): make and follow a budget, assess the situation, prioritise the commitments, the needs-versus-wants matching (food, water, clothing, shelter against jewellery and a car), and cooking with the recipe words teaspoon, pan, boil, cool, ingredients, preheat and the microwave cake.
