# Levels — Housework and Chores

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The jobs in the house | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the housework jobs: make the bed, wash the dishes, sweep and mop the floor, take out the trash, clean the windows, water the plants, iron the clothes — and the care jobs: take care of someone, feed the dog. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Helping at home | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Talk about a family's Saturday of chores — cleaning, sweeping and cooking together before visitors come — with can/can't for what each person can do and must not do. |
