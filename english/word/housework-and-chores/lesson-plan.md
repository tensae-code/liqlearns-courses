# Lesson Plan — Housework and Chores

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the words for the jobs that keep a house running — and the habit of helping at home as a team.

## Sub lessons

1. **The jobs in the house.** *Can-do: Name the housework jobs: make the bed, wash the dishes, sweep and mop the floor, take out the trash, clean the windows, water the plants, iron the clothes — and the care jobs: take care of someone, feed the dog.*
2. **Helping at home.** *Can-do: Talk about a family's Saturday of chores — cleaning, sweeping and cooking together before visitors come — with can/can't for what each person can do and must not do.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- The chore set (items 28, 249): make the bed, wash/do the dishes, sweep the floor, mop the floor, take out the trash, clean the windows, water the plants, iron the clothes; care jobs: take care of someone, feed the dog; alongside the free-time words (play an instrument, text a friend, watch tutorials).
- The family-teamwork dialogue (item 264): Saturday-morning chores before the cousins arrive — cleaning, sweeping, cooking — with can/can't for abilities and prohibitions in community challenges.
