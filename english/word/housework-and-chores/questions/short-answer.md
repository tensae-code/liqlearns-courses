# Short Answer — Housework and Chores

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name four housework jobs. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any four of: make the bed, wash the dishes, sweep the floor, mop the floor, take out the trash, clean the windows, water the plants, iron the clothes.
2. What is the difference between sweep the floor and mop the floor? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Sweeping pushes the dirt away with a broom; mopping washes the floor with water afterwards.
3. Say one thing you can do at home and one thing you can't do yet. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "I can wash the dishes. I can't iron the clothes yet."
4. Why does the family in the story clean together? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Because the cousins are coming to visit — and teamwork makes the big job easy.
