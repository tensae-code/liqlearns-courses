# Hands-On — Housework and Chores

1. **Chore charades.** Mime a chore — sweeping, ironing, washing dishes. Your partner names the job in English.
2. **The Saturday plan.** In a group, plan a house cleaning before visitors arrive: list the jobs and give one to each person with can.
3. **Label the house.** Walk through a real or drawn house and stick a word card on each job's place: bed, dishes, floor, trash, windows, plants, clothes.
4. **Feed and care.** Show how you feed a pet or care for a plant, saying each step aloud.
