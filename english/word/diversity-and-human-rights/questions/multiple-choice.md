## Multiple choice

1. Cultural diversity means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) everyone is the same  b) many ways of life in one place  c) one language only
   **Answer: b).**

2. Which is a basic need in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a car  b) shelter  c) a costume
   **Answer: b) shelter** — with food and health, one of the three.

3. Human rights are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) things anyone may take away  b) things no one may take away  c) only for adults
   **Answer: b).**

4. Origins, religions and beliefs are parts of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) diversity  b) weather  c) sport
   **Answer: a) diversity.**
