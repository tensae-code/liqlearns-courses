## Short answer

1. Name the three basic needs. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Food, health and shelter.

2. Name two parts of cultural diversity. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any two of: origins, religions, beliefs (also foods, music, festivals).

3. What is missing when a person has no food? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** A right — the basic need of food.
