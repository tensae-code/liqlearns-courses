# Video script — Diversity and Human Rights

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A street fills with different faces, foods, clothes and music.]

"One country. Many peoples."

"Different origins. Different beliefs. One home."

"And every person needs the same three things: food, health, and a safe place to live."

"Those are rights. No one may take them away."
