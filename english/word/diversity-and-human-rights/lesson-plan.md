# Lesson plan — Diversity and Human Rights

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Diversity and Human Rights
**Lesson:** diversity-and-human-rights — cultural diversity — the many origins, religions and beliefs of one country — and human rights: the basic needs every person must have, food, health and shelter
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can talk about cultural diversity and name human rights through the basic needs: food, health and shelter.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 9 book teaches cultural diversity as its own topic — origins, religions and beliefs — and its Grade 11 book teaches human rights through the basic needs. No lesson in the course housed the diversity-and-rights set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Many cultures, one country**
2. **Rights and basic needs**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. community-and-conflict-words stands near it — tolerance is the bridge between them.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Many cultures, one country": talk about cultural diversity: origins, religions and beliefs living together
- after sub lesson "Rights and basic needs": name human rights through the basic needs: food, health and shelter
