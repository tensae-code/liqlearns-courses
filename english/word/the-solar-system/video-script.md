# Video script — The Solar System

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands under a starry sky.] "Look up! Space is full
of worlds!"

[A big sun appears, and a small planet flies past.] "MERCURY!
The closest to the sun!"

[The flight reaches a blue-green planet.] "EARTH! Our home —
the third planet!"

[The flight passes a giant planet.] "JUPITER! The biggest
planet of all!"

[The moon rises; sunlight bounces off it down to the mascot.]
"And the moon? The moon has no light. MOONLIGHT is sunlight,
bounced off the moon!"
