# Hands-on — The Solar System

Space is learned by placing worlds. Do these out loud, with a
partner.

## Planet parade

Eight learners stand in a line as the planets, in their order
from the sun: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, Neptune. Each says their planet's name and one thing
about it. A ninth learner stands at the end as Eris, the
largest dwarf planet.

## Moonlight test

In a dark room, one partner holds a torch — the sun. The other
holds a ball — the moon — so the torchlight falls on it. Look
at the ball's lit side: the ball shines with the torch's light.
That is moonlight: sunlight reflected off the moon.
