## True or false

1. Earth is the third planet from the sun. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. Pluto is the ninth planet of the solar system. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — Pluto was made a dwarf planet; the solar system has eight planets.

3. Eris is a dwarf planet. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — and it is the largest of the dwarf planets.

4. The moon makes its own light. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — moonlight is sunlight reflected off the moon.

5. Jupiter is the biggest planet. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
