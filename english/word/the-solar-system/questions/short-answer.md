## Short answer

1. Name the eight planets in their order from the sun. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.

2. Which planet is closest to the sun, and which is the farthest of the eight? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Mercury is the closest; Neptune is the farthest.

3. What happened to Pluto? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** It was made a dwarf planet — it is no longer counted as the ninth planet.

4. What does a planet's diameter measure? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The distance straight through the planet's middle — how big across it is.

5. Where does moonlight come from? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** It is sunlight reflected off the moon — the moon makes no light of its own.
