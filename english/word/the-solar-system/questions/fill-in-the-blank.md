## Fill in the blank

1. "All of space and everything in it is called the ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: universe** — the stars, the sun and our Earth are all part of the universe.

2. "The solar system has ___ planets." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: eight** — Mercury to Neptune; Pluto is a dwarf planet now.

3. "___ is the largest dwarf planet." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Eris** — Eris is bigger than every other dwarf planet.

4. "The planets stand ___ of kilometres from the sun." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: millions** — space's distances are counted in millions of kilometres.

5. "Moonlight is sunlight ___ off the moon." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: reflected** — the moon throws the sun's light down to us.
