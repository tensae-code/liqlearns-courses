## Multiple choice

1. "The sun and its family of planets are called ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the universe  b) the solar system  c) the moonlight
   **Answer: b) the solar system** — the planets travelling around the sun make the solar system.

2. "Our planet, Earth, is ___ from the sun." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the first  b) the eighth  c) the third
   **Answer: c) the third** — Mercury, Venus, then Earth.

3. "The largest dwarf planet is ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Eris  b) Pluto  c) Neptune
   **Answer: a) Eris** — Eris is the largest of the dwarf planets.

4. "Pluto is now counted as ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a star  b) a dwarf planet  c) the ninth planet
   **Answer: b) a dwarf planet** — Pluto was made a dwarf planet; the solar system has eight planets.

5. "The planet with the largest diameter is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Mars  b) Mercury  c) Jupiter
   **Answer: c) Jupiter** — Jupiter is the biggest planet of all.

6. "Moonlight is ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) light the moon makes itself  b) sunlight reflected off the moon  c) light from the stars
   **Answer: b) sunlight reflected off the moon** — the moon makes no light of its own.
