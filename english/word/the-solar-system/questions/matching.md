## Matching

Match the world to its fact. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Mercury — d
2. Earth — a
3. Jupiter — e
4. Eris — b
5. Pluto — c

a) Our planet, the third from the sun
b) The largest dwarf planet
c) Made a dwarf planet; once called the ninth planet
d) The planet closest to the sun
e) The biggest planet of all

**Answers:** 1–d (Mercury is closest to the sun), 2–a (Earth is our
planet, third from the sun), 3–e (Jupiter is the biggest planet),
4–b (Eris is the largest dwarf planet), 5–c (Pluto was made a dwarf
planet).
