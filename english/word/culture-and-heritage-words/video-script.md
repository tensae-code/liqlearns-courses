# Video script — Culture and Heritage Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A table with special food; an old song; a young dancer learning an old dance.]

"Every people has its own way of life. That is its culture."

"A tradition is a custom that has lived a long time. A value is what a people holds right. A belief is what it holds true."

"And heritage is what the old hand down to the young — dances, crafts, stories. To preserve it is to keep it alive."
