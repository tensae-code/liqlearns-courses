## True or false

1. A custom and a tradition are exactly the same thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** A custom is one usual way of doing a thing; a tradition is a custom that has lived long and is handed down.

2. Heritage is only old buildings. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False.** Heritage is everything a people receives from those before them — dances, stories, crafts and songs as well as buildings.

3. Folk songs are the ordinary people's own songs. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**
