## Multiple choice

1. A tradition is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) something a people has done for a long time and keeps doing  b) a new fashion  c) a kind of food
   **Answer: a).**

2. A custom is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a tax paid at a border  b) one usual way of doing a thing  c) a ceremony
   **Answer: b).**

3. Heritage is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) everything a people receives from those before them  b) a new building  c) a folk song only
   **Answer: a).**

4. To preserve a dance means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) to watch it once  b) to change it completely  c) to keep it safe so it does not disappear
   **Answer: c).**
