## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)

1. culture
2. tradition
3. custom
4. value
5. belief
6. ceremony

a) something a people holds to be true
b) a special set of actions for a special day
c) the whole way of life of a people
d) something a people holds to be important and right
e) something done for a long time and handed down
f) one usual way of doing a thing

**Answers:** 1–c, 2–e, 3–f, 4–d, 5–a, 6–b.
