## Short answer

1. What is the difference between a custom and a tradition? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A custom is one usual way of doing a thing; a tradition is a custom that has lived for a long time and is handed down.

2. Name two things that can be a people's heritage. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: old buildings, dances, stories, crafts, songs.

3. Say one value of your own people, in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any true sentence naming a value the learner's people holds important and right — for example, respect for old people.
