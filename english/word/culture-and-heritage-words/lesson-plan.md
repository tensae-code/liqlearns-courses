# Lesson plan — Culture and Heritage Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Culture and Heritage Words
**Lesson:** culture-and-heritage-words — the words for what a people believes, keeps and hands down: culture, tradition, custom, value, belief, heritage
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can use the culture set — culture, tradition, custom, value, belief, folk, ceremony, and talk about heritage — the things a people keeps and hands down — and say what preserving them means.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Armenia's Grade 9 book opens its Culture unit with the words of culture itself — cultural values and traditions — before it reads about UNESCO and the Kochari dance. No lesson in the course housed this set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Culture words — values and traditions**
2. **Heritage — what a people keeps**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

Nothing is needed before this lesson.

- after sub lesson "Culture words — values and traditions": use the culture set — culture, tradition, custom, value, belief, folk, ceremony
- after sub lesson "Heritage — what a people keeps": talk about heritage — the things a people keeps and hands down — and say what preserving them means

## If the learner is stuck
Start from the learner's own home: one food, one song, one day of the year that their family keeps — each one is a custom, and the set hangs on it.

## Teacher note
The set is Armenia's Grade 9 Student's Book, Unit 2 (Culture), printed pp.28–29 (see lesson/sources.md): the unit's opening vocabulary of culture, cultural values and traditions. The same unit's UNESCO and Kochari readings are stocked in answering-from-the-text, and its folklore proverbs in literary-words and idioms-and-collocations.
