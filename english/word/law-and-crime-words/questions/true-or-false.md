## True or false

1. The jury gives the sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** The jury decides what is true; the judge gives the sentence.

2. To charge someone is to accuse them formally, in the law's own way. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. Crippen escaped because his ship was faster than the radio message. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False.** The radio message was faster, and the police were waiting for him.
