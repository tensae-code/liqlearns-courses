## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)

1. lawyer
2. judge
3. jury
4. trial
5. sentence
6. court

a) the meeting where a case is heard and decided
b) the person trained in the law who speaks for one side
c) the punishment given to a guilty person
d) the place where a trial happens
e) the person who leads the court and keeps the trial fair
f) the group of ordinary people who listen and decide what is true

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–e, 3–f, 4–a, 5–c, 6–d.
