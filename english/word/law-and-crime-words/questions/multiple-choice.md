## Multiple choice

1. To arrest someone means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) to set them free  b) for the police to take them and hold them  c) to punish them
   **Answer: b).**

2. A person who did NOT do the crime is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) guilty  b) charged  c) innocent
   **Answer: c) innocent.**

3. Doctor Crippen was famous because he was… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the first judge  b) the first criminal caught by radio  c) the first lawyer
   **Answer: b).**

4. The reader "Young people and the law" is about… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) how ships use radio  b) famous trials  c) what the law asks of young people
   **Answer: c).**
