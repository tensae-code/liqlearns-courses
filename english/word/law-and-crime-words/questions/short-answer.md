## Short answer

1. Name the three steps that come before a trial, in order. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Arrest, accuse, charge.

2. What is the difference between guilty and innocent? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Guilty means the person did the crime; innocent means he did not.

3. How was Doctor Crippen caught? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** A message by radio reached the other side of the sea before his ship did, and the police were waiting when it arrived.
