# Video script — Law and Crime Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A courthouse door opens; a judge's bench, an empty jury box.]

"When someone breaks the law, the police arrest them."

"They accuse them of a crime, and charge them. Then comes the trial, in a court."

"A judge leads the court. A jury listens. A lawyer speaks for each side."

"Guilty — he did it. Innocent — he did not. And if he is guilty, the judge gives the sentence."
