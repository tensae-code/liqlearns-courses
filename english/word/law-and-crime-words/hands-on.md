# Hands-on — Law and Crime Words

1. **The case in steps.** Cards for arrest, accuse, charge, trial, guilty, innocent, sentence; put them in the order a case moves, then say one line for each card.

2. **Your own court.** With a partner, act a tiny trial: one is the judge, one the lawyer, one the accused. The judge ends it: guilty or innocent — and the sentence.
