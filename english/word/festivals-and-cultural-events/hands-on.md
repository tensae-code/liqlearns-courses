# Hands-on — Festivals and Cultural Events

1. **Festival calendar.** With a partner, list the months and place one festival in each month you can — the book's festivals first, then your own town's.

2. **Wish and compliment round.** Take turns: one names a festival, the other gives a wish or a compliment that fits it.

3. **Invite me.** One partner invites the other to a local festival, with the day and the place; the other accepts or asks one question.
