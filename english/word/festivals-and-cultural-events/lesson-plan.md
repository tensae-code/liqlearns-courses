# Lesson plan — Festivals and Cultural Events

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Festivals and Cultural Events
**Lesson:** festivals-and-cultural-events — the festivals of the year — Christmas and New Year, Carnival, Holy Week, Mother's Day and the festivals of your own place — with the wishes, compliments and invitations they carry
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can name the year's festivals and use the words each one carries: wishes and compliments at Christmas and New Year, comparisons at Carnival, past accounts at Holy Week, describing on Mother's Day, and invitations to a local festival.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 7 book gives festivals a whole lesson of their own — "Let's celebrate!" — walking the year festival by festival, each with its own English work. No lesson in the course housed the festival set, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **The festivals of the year**
2. **Talking at festivals**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. If the learner already knows the months of the year (days-and-months) and how to compare (stronger-comparisons), the second sub lesson will feel easier — they might try those first.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "The festivals of the year": name the year's big festivals and say when they come
- after sub lesson "Talking at festivals": give wishes and compliments, compare festivals, tell what happened at one, and invite someone to a local festival

## What you need
Nothing special — a calendar of the year helps if one is near.

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
Go back to the learner's own festival and build out from it: their celebration first, then the book's year. The words stick to a day they already love.

## Teacher note
The set and its pairings are Venezuela's Grade 7 lesson's own (see lesson/sources.md): Christmas and New Year carry wishes and compliments, Carnival carries comparisons, Holy Week carries accounts of a visit told in the past, Mother's Day carries describing, and regional festivals carry invitations and directions. Teacher's-book evidence only — no student's book is held for Venezuela.
