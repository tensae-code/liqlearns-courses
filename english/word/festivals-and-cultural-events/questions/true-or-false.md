## True or false

1. The book teaches festivals as one list with no work of their own. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** Each festival carries its own English work — wishes, comparisons, accounts, describing, invitations.

2. Holy Week is told in the past in this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

3. Regional festivals are only in big cities. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** The book's strand is regional and local festivals — every place has its own.
