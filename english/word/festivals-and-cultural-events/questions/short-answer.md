## Short answer

1. Name two festivals the book teaches. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any two of: Christmas, New Year, Carnival, Holy Week, Mother's Day.

2. What two things do people give each other at Christmas and New Year in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Wishes and compliments.

3. What does the Mother's Day strand practise? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Describing — saying what someone is like.

4. Name one thing the regional-festivals strand teaches. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Invitations (or directions to the festival).
