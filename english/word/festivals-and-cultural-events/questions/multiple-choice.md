## Multiple choice

1. Which festival comes at the end of the year, with wishes and compliments? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Carnival  b) Christmas  c) Holy Week
   **Answer: b) Christmas** — the book pairs Christmas and New Year with wishes and compliments.

2. Which festival does the book pair with comparisons? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Carnival  b) Mother's Day  c) Holy Week
   **Answer: a) Carnival** — Carnival is where the lesson works comparisons.

3. Holy Week accounts in the book are told… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) in the future  b) in the past  c) as orders
   **Answer: b) in the past** — the Holy Week strand tells what happened on a visit.

4. Which sentence is an invitation to a festival? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "The festival was long."  b) "Come to our festival on Saturday!"  c) "Festivals are days."
   **Answer: b)** — it invites someone to come.

5. A compliment is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a kind thing you say about someone  b) a date  c) a costume
   **Answer: a)** — compliments are the kind words of Christmas and New Year in this lesson.
