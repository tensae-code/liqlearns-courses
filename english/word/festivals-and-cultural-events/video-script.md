# Video script — Festivals and Cultural Events

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A calendar of the year turns its pages; each festival lights up as it is named.]

"A year has special days. Days for family. Days for music. Days for food."

"Christmas. New Year. Carnival. Holy Week. Mother's Day."

"At Christmas we say good wishes. At Carnival we dance and compare costumes."

"Every town has its own festival too. Come! I invite you."
