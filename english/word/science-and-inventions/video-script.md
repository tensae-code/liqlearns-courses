# Video script — Science and Inventions

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A laboratory; a scientist lifts a small dish to the light.]

“Cloning. A new science — at the Roslin Institute.”

[The mascot holds a pencil over a page.] “Read the facts first.”

“Then argue — with reasons.”
