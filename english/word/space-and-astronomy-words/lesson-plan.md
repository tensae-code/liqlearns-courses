# Lesson plan — Space and Astronomy Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Space and Astronomy Words
**Lesson:** space-and-astronomy-words — the words for space and for studying it: the universe and its bodies, the events in the sky, and the people and tools that watch them
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World

**The one thing:** the learner can use the space set — universe, galaxy, star, orbit, gravity, eclipse, comet — to name what is in the sky and what happens there, and talk about studying the universe — astronomer, telescope, observatory, observe, discover — and about Armenia's own part in that study.

**This lesson's own level:** word level — it stands in Unit 7 (Words for the World) with the other word sets.

## Why this lesson exists
Armenia's Grade 11 book gives its Space unit a vocabulary of its own — space-themed words for analysing astronomical occurrences, with Armenians' contributions to the study of the universe. No lesson in the course housed this set (the-solar-system houses the planets themselves), so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## If the learner is stuck
Start from the night sky the learner can see: name the moon, a star, a planet first — then reach for the words for what cannot be seen without help.
