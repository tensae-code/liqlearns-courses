## Short answer

1. What is the difference between a star and a planet? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** A star makes its own light; a planet goes round a star and shines with the star's light.

2. Name the person, the tool and the building of astronomy. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The astronomer, the telescope, and the observatory.

3. What does an astronomer do at an observatory? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Observes the sky with telescopes — watches carefully, records what is there, and sometimes discovers things never seen before.
