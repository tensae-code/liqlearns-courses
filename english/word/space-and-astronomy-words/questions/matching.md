## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)

1. galaxy
2. star
3. planet
4. orbit
5. gravity
6. comet

a) the path a body follows round another body
b) a huge family of stars
c) a body of ice and dust that grows a bright tail near the sun
d) a great ball of hot gas that makes its own light
e) the pull that holds the planets in their paths
f) a large body that goes round a star

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–d, 3–f, 4–a, 5–e, 6–c.
