## Multiple choice

1. The universe is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) our galaxy only  b) everything that exists  c) the space above one country
   **Answer: b).**

2. An eclipse is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) when one body moves into another's shadow  b) a new star being born  c) a comet's tail
   **Answer: a).**

3. An astronomer is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a person who travels into space  b) a tool for watching the sky  c) a scientist who studies the universe
   **Answer: c).**

4. Astronomers work in a building called… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) an observatory  b) a spacecraft  c) an orbit
   **Answer: a).**
