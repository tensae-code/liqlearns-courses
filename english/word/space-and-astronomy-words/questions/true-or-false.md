## True or false

1. The sun is a planet. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False.** The sun is a star; planets go round it and shine with its light.

2. Gravity holds the planets in their orbits. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. To discover a star means to make a new one. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False.** To discover is to find something that was always there but never seen before.
