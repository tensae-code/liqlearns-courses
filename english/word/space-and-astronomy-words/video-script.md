# Video script — Space and Astronomy Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A night sky; a galaxy turning slowly; a comet's tail; a telescope on a mountain.]

"Everything that exists — that is the universe. Our galaxy is the Milky Way. Our sun is a star."

"A planet moves in its orbit, held by gravity. An eclipse is a shadow in the sky."

"And astronomers watch it all — with telescopes, from observatories."
