# Transport — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** bus · train · plane · boat · bike · foot

**1. (Level 1)** "How many taxis and ______?" (Palestine
counts them together)
**Answer: bikes** (p.10).

**2. (Level 2)** A ______ travels on rails.
**Answer: train.**

**3. (Level 2)** A ______ travels on water.
**Answer: boat.**

**4. (Level 3)** I travel by ______ to school every day.
(any vehicle from the box)
**Answer: bus / train / bike** — a true vehicle word
(Rwanda's travel-by shape, p.110).

**5. (Level 3)** I travel on ______ when I walk.
**Answer: foot** (Rwanda P5, p.110).
