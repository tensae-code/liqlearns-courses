# Transport — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Street Five · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Full Set, Sorted · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Travelling.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Palestine's five transport words.
**Answer:** car, bus, bike, van, taxi (p.10).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two transports for water, two for
air.
**Answer:** water — boat, ferry (canoe also right);
air — plane, helicopter (jet also right).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do you travel to school? Answer
whole, in Rwanda's shape.
**Answer:** "I travel by…" with a vehicle, or "I travel
on foot" (P5, p.110).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In Mongolia's unusual-transport reading,
what carries people in Madeira, downhill?
**Answer:** the Monte Toboggan (English VIII, Unit 6,
p.86).
