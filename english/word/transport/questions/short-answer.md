# Transport — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Name Palestine's five transport words.
**Answer:** car, bus, bike, van, taxi (p.10).

**2. (Level 2)** Name two transports for water, two for
air.
**Answer:** water — boat, ferry (canoe also right);
air — plane, helicopter (jet also right).

**3. (Level 3)** How do you travel to school? Answer
whole, in Rwanda's shape.
**Answer:** "I travel by…" with a vehicle, or "I travel
on foot" (P5, p.110).

**4. (Level 3)** In Mongolia's unusual-transport reading,
what carries people in Madeira, downhill?
**Answer:** the Monte Toboggan (English VIII, Unit 6,
p.86).
