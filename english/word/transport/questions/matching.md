# Transport — matching

Match each transport to where it travels.

**Set 1 (Level 2)** — land, water, air.
1. train — **land (railway)**
2. ferry — **water**
3. helicopter — **air**
4. lorry — **land (road)**
5. boat — **water**
6. plane — **air**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — travel by (Rwanda's shapes).
1. to school by road — **travel by bus**
2. across the sea — **travel by ferry**
3. walking — **travel on foot**
4. to a far city in the sky — **travel by plane**
