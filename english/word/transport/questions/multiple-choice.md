# Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Palestine's book teaches five transport
words. Which one is NOT one of the five?
a) car  b) taxi  c) train
**Answer: c) train** — the five are car, bus, bike, van,
taxi (Grade 1 Part 2, p.10); the train joins later.

**2. (Level 1)** "How many taxis?" — "______ taxis."
a) Five  b) By  c) Foot
**Answer: a) Five** — Palestine's own pattern (p.10).

**3. (Level 2)** Which one travels on water?
a) lorry  b) ferry  c) motorcycle
**Answer: b) ferry** — the sea/land/air sort (Rwanda P5,
pp.109–111; Mongolia, English VIII, Unit 5).

**4. (Level 2)** Which one travels by air?
a) helicopter  b) ambulance  c) canoe
**Answer: a) helicopter** — air: plane, helicopter
(Mongolia's chart adds jet and rocket).

**5. (Level 3)** "She travels ______ foot."
a) by  b) on  c) with
**Answer: b) on** — "travel by" + vehicle, but "travel
on foot" (Rwanda P5, p.110).
