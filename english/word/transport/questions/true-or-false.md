# Transport — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A taxi is one of Palestine's five
transport words.
**True** — car, bus, bike, van, taxi (p.10).

**2. (Level 2)** A ferry travels by air.
**False** — a ferry travels on water; planes and
helicopters travel by air.

**3. (Level 2)** Bolivia's chart sorts transport by
water, land and air.
**True** — Year 2, Topic 2, §3.4.

**4. (Level 3)** We say "travel by foot" for walking.
**False** — walking is "travel ON foot"; by goes with
vehicles (Rwanda P5, p.110).
