# Transport — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Street Five · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Full Set, Sorted · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Travelling.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A taxi is one of Palestine's five
transport words.
**True** — car, bus, bike, van, taxi (p.10).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ferry travels by air.
**False** — a ferry travels on water; planes and
helicopters travel by air.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bolivia's chart sorts transport by
water, land and air.
**True** — Year 2, Topic 2, §3.4.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** We say "travel by foot" for walking.
**False** — walking is "travel ON foot"; by goes with
vehicles (Rwanda P5, p.110).
