# Lesson plan — Transport

**Course:** English · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Transport
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the transport words
— car, bus, train, plane, boat, bike and their partners —
sorted the books' way, by land, by water and by air, with
the travel pattern: travel by bus, travel on foot.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Level 1 reading
and the Nouns lesson. It needs nothing else. If you know
The town already, this lesson will be easier for you —
you might try it first. Recommended, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The Street Five** — car, bus, bike, van and taxi, counted in
   Palestine's pattern. (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **The Full Set, Sorted** — the full set sorted by land, water
   and air. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Travelling** — "travel by…" for every vehicle, "travel on
   foot" for walking, and the books' comparisons. (Was depth Level
   3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking
is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set
is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Nouns (Level 6) and The town (this
  level). Recommended, never forced.
- Comparing transport (faster, cheaper) is NOT this
  lesson; the comparisons ride at Level 3 only as the
  books' own examples. This lesson names and sorts.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Palestine's first five: car, bus, bike, van, taxi.
2. Name the full set: train, plane, helicopter, lorry,
   motorcycle, ambulance, boat, ferry, horse.
3. Sort transport by land, water and air, the books' way.
4. Say how people travel: "travel by bus," "travel by
   train," "travel on foot."

## What you need
A street to watch, or pictures of vehicles. Your own
journeys today are the material.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner lists every way they
   have travelled this week, in their own language; the
   lesson then gives the English names (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the first five; the full set;
   the land/water/air sort; travel by (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the start of the explanation: a picture
     of a street with a car, a bus, a bike, a van and a
     taxi — the student points and names each one before
     reading on (Palestine's five).
   - ADD GIF in the sorting part: a picture of vehicles
     moving into three groups — land, water, air — the
     student says each vehicle's name and group as it
     moves.
   - ADD AUDIO in the travel part: a voice asking "How
     do you travel to school?" with by-bus, by-train and
     on-foot answers — the student answers for their own
     journey before the voice does.
3. **Say it to your companion** — ask each other how you
   came today and how you would cross water and sky
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed —
     the journey report in the super-simple voice (made
     later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — draw your journey to school with
   every vehicle in it, labelled (lesson/lesson.md).

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE — a picture of a street with car, bus, bike, van,
  taxi → the student points and names each one.
- GIF — a picture of vehicles sorting into land, water,

- AUDIO — "How do you travel…?" questions read aloud →

- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the
  student reports along with it.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes lorry and van → size and job: a lorry carries
  heavy loads far; a van is the smaller box on wheels.
  (American books say truck for lorry — both are in the
  books; either is a right answer.)
- Says "travel by foot" → the one exception is taught as
  a pair: by bus, by car — but ON foot (Rwanda, p.110).
- This lesson's next door: The town — the places these
  vehicles go between.
