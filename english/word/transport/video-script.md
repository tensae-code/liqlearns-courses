# Video script — Transport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the street! A **car**! A **bus**!
A **bike**! A **van**! A **taxi**!

How many taxis? Five taxis!

On the land: car, bus, train, lorry.
On the water: a **boat**! A **ferry**!
In the air: a **plane**! A **helicopter**!

I travel by bus. I travel by train.
And you? You travel **on foot**!

Now you. How do you travel?
Say it — in English.
