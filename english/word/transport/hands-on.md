# Hands-on — Transport

The lesson on your real street, not only on the page.

## The street count
Stand where you can see a street for five minutes. Count
and write every vehicle in English: cars, buses, bikes,
taxis, lorries. Finish with Palestine's question about
your own page: "How many…?"

## The three journeys
Think of three journeys: to school, across a river or
sea, and to a far city. Say which transport takes you on
each, and why: "I travel by bus. I travel by ferry. I
travel by plane."

## Land, water, air
Draw three boxes: LAND — WATER — AIR. Write or draw every
transport word you know into its box. The box you cannot
fill is your new words to find in the lesson's list.

## The walk report
Walk somewhere short today. Come back and report it whole:
"I travelled on foot." Then report a partner's week: one
journey by vehicle, one on foot.
