# Levels — Mishaps and Accidents

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The mishap words | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the mishaps: trip over, pass out, get a flat tire, run into someone, spill a drink, fall down in public, burn food, have your possessions stolen. |
| Sub lesson 2 — A bad-luck story | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Chain the mishaps into a story that keeps going wrong — Victor's day: burnt food, spilt coffee, a tripping dog, a flat tire, stolen cards and money — until a friend helps. |
