# Short Answer — Mishaps and Accidents

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name four mishaps from the lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any four of: trip over, pass out, get a flat tire, run into someone, spill a drink, fall down in public, burn food, have your possessions stolen.
2. What does fall down in public mean? (Sub lesson 1)
   — To fall where everyone can see you.
3. Tell Victor's day in three mishaps, in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: first he burns the food and spills his coffee; then he trips over his dog and gets a flat tire; finally his cards and money are stolen — but he runs into Omar, who helps.
4. Write one chain sentence with then. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "I spilt my tea, then I tripped over the cat."
