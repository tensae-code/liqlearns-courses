# Hands-On — Mishaps and Accidents

1. **Mishap mime.** Cards with the eight mishaps; one learner mimes, the group names the mishap.
2. **The chain story.** The class builds one long bad-luck story together, each learner adding one mishap with then/next.
3. **Bad day diary.** Write a diary page for the worst day ever — at least four mishaps.
4. **Help desk.** In pairs: one tells a mishap, the other answers with help and advice.
