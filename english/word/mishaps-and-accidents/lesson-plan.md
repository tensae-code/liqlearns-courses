# Lesson Plan — Mishaps and Accidents

**Unit 7 — Words for the World.**

**One thing:** the words for the small disasters of a day — trip over, pass out, get a flat tire, run into someone, spill a drink, fall down, burn food — and how to tell the story of them.

## Sub lessons

1. **The mishap words.** *Can-do: Name the mishaps: trip over, pass out, get a flat tire, run into someone, spill a drink, fall down in public, burn food, have your possessions stolen.*
2. **A bad-luck story.** *Can-do: Chain the mishaps into a story that keeps going wrong — Victor's day: burnt food, spilt coffee, a tripping dog, a flat tire, stolen cards and money — until a friend helps.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Mishap vocabulary practised in a story (item 247): trip over, pass out, get a flat tire, run into someone, spill a drink, fall down in public, burn food, steal possessions — in Victor's chain-of-bad-luck story.
