# Nouns — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a noun from the box.

**Box:** school · dad · car · Nile · cat · library

**1. (Level 1)** My ______ drives me to school in the morning.
**Answer: dad** (a person).

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ is fast and red.
**Answer: car** (a thing).

**3. (Level 2)** We read books at the ______.
**Answer: library** (a place — Egypt's city unit).

**4. (Level 2)** The ______ sleeps on the chair.
**Answer: cat** (a living thing).

**5. (Level 2)** Children learn at ______.
**Answer: school** (a place).

**6. (Level 3)** The ______ is a long river in Egypt.
**Answer: Nile** — a special name, one only, so it starts with
a capital.
