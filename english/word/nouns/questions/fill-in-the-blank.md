Fill each blank with a noun from the box.

**Box:** school · dad · car · Nile · cat · library

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** My ______ drives me to school in the morning.
**Answer: dad** (a person).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ is fast and red.
**Answer: car** (a thing).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We read books at the ______.
**Answer: library** (a place — Egypt's city unit).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ sleeps on the chair.
**Answer: cat** (a living thing).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Children learn at ______.
**Answer: school** (a place).

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The ______ is a long river in Egypt.
**Answer: Nile** — a special name, one only, so it starts with
a capital.
