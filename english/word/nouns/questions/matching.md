# Nouns — matching

Match each word to its sort: person, place, or thing.
(The books' own work — Egypt's Final Review sorts nouns into
person / place / thing, pp.106–108; Libya sorts mixed words into
columns, Grade 7 WorkBook 7.7.)

**Set 1 (Level 2)**
1. dad — **person**
2. library — **place**
3. bicycle — **thing**
4. teacher — **person**
5. park — **place**
6. plane — **thing**

**Set 2 (Level 2)**
1. doctor — **person**
2. supermarket — **place**
3. book — **thing**
4. cat — **thing** (a living thing)
5. street — **place**
6. friend — **person**

**Set 3 (Level 4)** — match each word to noun or not a noun.
1. castle — **noun** (a thing; Libya's sort list)
2. drove — **not a noun** (a doing word)
3. Tripoli — **noun** (a place — and a special name, capital T)
4. cooked — **not a noun** (a doing word)
5. pot — **noun** (a thing)
6. she — **not a noun** (a word that stands in for a noun —
   the Pronouns lesson's word)
