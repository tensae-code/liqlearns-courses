# Video script — Social Problems

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A busy street appears behind the mascot.]

"A town can have problems — problems for everybody. We call them social problems."

"Too many people in one place: overpopulation. Not enough food: a food shortage. Fighting and harm: violence."

[The mascot points at the learner.] "First we name the problem. Then we can talk about help."
