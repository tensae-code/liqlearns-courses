## Short answer

1. Name three social problems from the lesson. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any three of: violence, anti-social behaviour, youth problems, overpopulation, food shortage, being overweight, poverty.**

2. Write one sentence naming a social problem and one thing that would help. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any true pair, e.g. "A food shortage means not enough food; growing more food would help."**

3. What are "youth problems"? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: the problems of young people as a group.**
