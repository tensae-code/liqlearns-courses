## Multiple choice

1. Overpopulation means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) too many people in one place  b) too few people  c) a big family
   **Answer: a).**

2. A food shortage is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a big meal  b) not enough food for everyone  c) a new restaurant
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which of these is anti-social behaviour? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) helping a neighbour  b) damaging other people's things  c) gathering litter
   **Answer: b).**

4. A social problem is a problem for… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) one person only  b) a whole town or society  c) one family only
   **Answer: b).**
