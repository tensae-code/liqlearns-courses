# Video script — The town

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Let's walk around town!

Look — a **school**! Children learn at school.
You feel sick? The **hospital**. I go to the hospital.
That's a **shop**. We buy things at the shop!

And the **street**! What do you see on the street?
A **truck**! A **bus**! A **car**!

A big **building**! And here — the **library**.
Books live at the library.
The **park**! We play at the park.

Eight places. Eight names.
Hospital — sick. School — learn. Shop — buy.

Now you. Walk YOUR street.
Name the places. Say what they are for.
That is your town — in English.
