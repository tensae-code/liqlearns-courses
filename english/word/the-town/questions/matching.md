# The town — matching

Match each place to what it is for, the way the books pair
them.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — Egypt's first set.
1. hospital — **go when you feel sick**
2. school — **children learn**
3. shop — **buy things**
4. street — **the car, truck, and bus go there**
5. building — **a big house of rooms**
6. bus — **carries many people on the street**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — the wider town.
1. library — **borrow books**
2. post office — **send letters**
3. supermarket — **buy food (the big shop)**
4. park — **play**
5. zoo — **see animals**
6. beach — **swim and dig in the sand**
