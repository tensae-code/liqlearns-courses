# The town — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Where do you go when you feel sick?
a) the school  b) the hospital  c) the shop
**Answer: b) the hospital** — the dialog's own question
(Egypt, Primary 1 Term 2, p.87).

**2. (Level 1)** "What do you see on the street?" — "I see a
______."
a) truck  b) kitchen  c) bed
**Answer: a) truck** — truck, bus, and car go on the street
(Egypt, p.87).

**3. (Level 1)** We can buy things at the ______.
a) street  b) building  c) shop
**Answer: c) shop** — "That's a shop. We can buy things
there" (Egypt, p.87).

**4. (Level 1)** Children learn at the ______.
a) school  b) hospital  c) park
**Answer: a) school.**

**5. (Level 2)** You borrow books at the ______.
a) post office  b) library  c) supermarket
**Answer: b) library** (Egypt's city set, Primary 2 Term 2).

**6. (Level 2)** The big food shop is the ______.
a) supermarket  b) zoo  c) club
**Answer: a) supermarket** (Egypt's city set).

**7. (Level 2)** You see animals at the ______.
a) beach  b) zoo  c) street
**Answer: b) zoo** — "I see animals in the zoo" (Egypt, Unit 5
Scope).
