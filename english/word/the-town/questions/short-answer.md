# The town — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Eight Town Words · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Wider Town · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Town and the
Village.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Egypt's eight town words.
**Answer:** hospital, school, shop, building, street, car,
truck, bus (Primary 1 Term 2, p.86).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a building?
**Answer:** any big house of rooms — a school, a hospital,
and a shop are all buildings (the dialog's "that big building
over there," p.87).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three more town places from the later
books.
**Answer:** any three of library, post office, supermarket,
park, zoo, club, city (Egypt's city set; Unit 5 Scope).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bangladesh's book pairs two lessons: Oyshi's
______ life and Osman's ______ life.
**Answer:** city — village (Class 3, pp.72–80).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer in a whole sentence: Where do you go
when you feel sick?
**Answer:** I go to the hospital (Egypt, p.87).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer about YOUR street: What do you see on
the street?
**Answer:** any three true things in the shape "I see a ___,
a ___, and a ___."
