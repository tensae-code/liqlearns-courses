# The town — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Eight Town Words · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Wider Town · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Town and the
Village.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We go to the hospital when we feel sick.
**True** — the dialog's own answer (Egypt, p.87).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** We buy things at the school.
**False** — we buy things at the shop. At school, children
learn.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A hospital is a building.
**True** — a building is any big house of rooms; the hospital,
the school, and the shop are all buildings.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The supermarket is a small shop.
**False** — the supermarket is the BIG food shop (Egypt's
city set).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** We send letters at the post office.
**True** (Egypt's city set, Primary 2 Term 2).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "What do you see on the street?" — "I see a
bed" is a right answer.
**False** — beds live in bedrooms. On the street we see the
car, the truck, and the bus (Egypt, p.87).
