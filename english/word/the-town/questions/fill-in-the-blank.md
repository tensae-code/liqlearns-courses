# The town — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** hospital · school · shop · street · library · park

**1. (Level 1)** I go to the ______ when I feel sick.
**Answer: hospital.**

**2. (Level 1)** Children learn at ______.
**Answer: school.**

**3. (Level 1)** We can buy things at the ______.
**Answer: shop.**

**4. (Level 1)** I see a truck, a bus, and a car on the
______.
**Answer: street.**
(All four are the Sara and Ali dialog's own lines, Egypt,
p.87.)

**5. (Level 2)** I borrow books at the ______.
**Answer: library.**

**6. (Level 2)** We play at the ______.
**Answer: park.**
