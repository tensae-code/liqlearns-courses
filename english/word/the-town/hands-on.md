# Hands-on — The town

The lesson on a real street, not only on the page.

## The street walk
Walk a real street with a partner or a family member. At every
place you pass, stop, point, and say its English name and its
job out loud: "A shop — we buy things there." Eight places
minimum. The place you cannot name is your new word to find in
the lesson's lists.

## Draw the town
On a big sheet, draw your street the way Egypt's book pictures
it: the road across the middle, the places along it. Draw the
car, the bus, the truck on the street. Label everything in
English. Your drawing should hold all eight words of the first
set.

## The job game
One person names a need — "I feel sick." "I want a book." "I
want bread." "I want to play." — the other runs (or points on
the drawing) to the place that answers it and says the whole
sentence: "I go to the hospital." Five needs each, then swap.

## Photo labels
If you have photos of your town — on a phone or printed — write
each place's English name under its photo. Put the set where
you will see it tomorrow and read it once more.
