Mongolia, Laos, China, Indonesia, Iran, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Greece, Kazakhstan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, South Sudan, Nepal, Armenia, Vietnam, Cambodia, Pakistan, Sudan, Kuwait).** Egypt

carries a second town set located with near / next to /

neighbourhood on one map, with directions and the position
set; Iran carries the city described and located whole, and
the widest pictured places set in this lesson; Sierra Leone
carries the community word-web and directions by landmarks.

Iraq carries a whole My town unit in Grade 3 — six town places on a street scene, the town described with there is / there isn't and directed over a map — and describing places as its own box in Grade 9 book; Ethiopia carries only the describing side of this lesson — town against country in its Grade 7 reading "Living in Rural and Urban Areas: Advantages and Disadvantages" with speaking on where one lives, and the big city described in its Grade 9 reading "Life in a Big City" — no town-places word set, locating frame or directions work is filed for Ethiopia, and none is claimed here; Syria carries a places-to-go set and a then-and-now My Town unit in Grade 3, and a whole Places in My Town unit with a Giving Directions unit beside it in Grade 4 — Syria, unlike Ethiopia here, files the word set, the locating prepositions and the directions work together; Nepal carries the town through directions: asking the way and five direction-writing tasks to town places in Grade 3 (keyed by its true companion guide), map directions to the ATM booth, museum, hospital and post office in Grade 6, and street-map locating and direction-giving in Grade 9; Kuwait carries neighbourhood places — police station, post office, clinic, barber, garage, supermarket — with opposite and nearby in Grade 2, and shops in the older course's Grade 2 activity book, cited for its own teaching only.
