# Video script — Pronouns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is **Ahmed**. Ahmed is my friend.
Ahmed lives near me. Ahmed plays football.

Ahmed, Ahmed, Ahmed! Too much Ahmed!

Small words can carry the name.
**He** lives near me. **He** plays football.
He = Ahmed. One small word!

Talk about yourself? **I**.
Talk to a friend? **You**.
A boy or a man? **He**.
A girl or a woman? **She**.
An animal or a thing? **It**.
You and your friends together? **We**.
Many people? **They**.

Seven small words. They stand in for names.

And whose is it?
**My** book. **Your** book.
The book is **mine**!

Now you. Point at the boy — he!
Point at the girl — she!
Point at the dog — it!
That is a pronoun.
