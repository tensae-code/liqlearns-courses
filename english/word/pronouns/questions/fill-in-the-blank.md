# Pronouns — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a pronoun from the box.

**Box:** I · you · he · she · it · we · they

**1. (Level 1)** ___ am a student. (yourself)
**Answer: I.**

**2. (Level 1)** My father is a doctor. ___ works at the
hospital.
**Answer: he** — a man.

**3. (Level 1)** The cat is small. ___ sleeps all day.
**Answer: it** — an animal.

**4. (Level 1)** My sister is kind. ___ helps me every day.
**Answer: she** — a girl or a woman.

**5. (Level 2)** You and I are friends. ___ play together.
**Answer: we** — yourself and others together.

**6. (Level 2)** The boys are late. ___ run to school.
**Answer: they** — more than one person.
(All definitions are Egypt's own, Primary 1 Term 2, p.27.)
