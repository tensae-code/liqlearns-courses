# Pronouns — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The seven person words · (Level 2) -> by what the items ask: items 5–6 choose the person word itself by who is meant, so they are Sub lesson 1 — The seven person words (no repeated name is swapped in them).

Fill each blank with a pronoun from the box.

**Box:** I · you · he · she · it · we · they

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ___ am a student. (yourself)
**Answer: I.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** My father is a doctor. ___ works at the
hospital.
**Answer: he** — a man.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The cat is small. ___ sleeps all day.
**Answer: it** — an animal.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** My sister is kind. ___ helps me every day.
**Answer: she** — a girl or a woman.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** You and I are friends. ___ play together.
**Answer: we** — yourself and others together.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** The boys are late. ___ run to school.
**Answer: they** — more than one person.
