# Pronouns — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The seven person words · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The swap, replacing a repeated name · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Whose-forms, my and mine.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a pronoun?
**Answer:** a word that stands in for a noun — a word we use
in place of a name (Bangladesh: "used to replace or refer to
a noun in a sentence," Class 3).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's book calls the pronouns "Words We Use
Instead of ___."
**Answer:** Names (Primary 1 Term 2, p.27).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Give Egypt's definition for "they."
**Answer:** "When you talk about more than one person, animal,
or thing" (p.27).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rewrite the light way: "Ahmed lost Ahmed's
bag. Ahmed looked for the bag all day."
**Answer:** Ahmed lost his bag. He looked for it all day.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pronoun stands in: "The children are
hungry. ___ want lunch."
**Answer:** They.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the difference between "my" and "mine."
**Answer:** my rides with its noun (my book); mine stands
alone (the book is mine).
