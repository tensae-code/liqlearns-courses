# Pronouns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "When you talk about a girl or a woman" —
the word is —
a) he  b) she  c) it  d) they
**Answer: b) she** — Egypt's own definition (Primary 1 Term 2,
p.27).

**2. (Level 1)** "When you talk about an animal or a thing" —
a) we  b) you  c) it
**Answer: c) it** (Egypt, p.27).

**3. (Level 1)** You and your friends together: "___ are
happy."
a) They  b) We  c) It
**Answer: b) We** — yourself and others together (Egypt,
p.27).

**4. (Level 2)** "Mariam won the game. ___ was very happy."
a) He  b) It  c) She
**Answer: c) She** — Mariam is a girl; the pronoun stands in
for her name (Libya's swap work, 7.5).

**5. (Level 2)** "The dog ran away. ___ ran to the park."
a) It  b) They  c) We
**Answer: a) It** — an animal takes it.

**6. (Level 2)** Which sentence is the light way?
a) Omar reads Omar's book every day.
b) Omar reads his book every day.
**Answer: b)** — his stands in for Omar's; the name is said
once.

**7. (Level 3)** "This pen is ___." (It belongs to you,
standing alone.)
a) your  b) yours  c) you
**Answer: b) yours** — mine, yours, his, hers, theirs stand
alone (Ghana, B4–6).

**8. (Level 3)** "___ book is on the desk." (the book belongs
to me)
a) Mine  b) My  c) I
**Answer: b) My** — my rides with its noun; mine stands alone
(Egypt's box, Primary 2 Term 1, p.51).
