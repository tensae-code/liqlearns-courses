# Pronouns — matching

Match each of Egypt's plain definitions to its word.
(Primary 1 Term 2, p.27 — "Words We Use Instead of Names.")

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — the book's own definitions.
1. When you talk about yourself. — **I**
2. When you talk to one person or to a group. — **you**
3. When you talk about a boy or a man. — **he**
4. When you talk about a girl or a woman. — **she**
5. When you talk about an animal or a thing. — **it**
6. When you talk about yourself and others together. — **we**
7. When you talk about more than one person, animal, or
   thing. — **they**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — match each name to its stand-in.
1. Mariam — **she**
2. Omar — **he**
3. the dog — **it**
4. the children — **they**
5. you and I — **we**
6. the book — **it**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — match each whose-form to its job.
1. my — **rides with a noun: my pen**
2. mine — **stands alone: the pen is mine**
3. your — **rides with a noun: your bag**
4. yours — **stands alone: the bag is yours**
5. her — **rides with a noun: her desk**
6. hers — **stands alone: the desk is hers**
