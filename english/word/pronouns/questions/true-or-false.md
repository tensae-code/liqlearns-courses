# Pronouns — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The seven person words · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The swap, replacing a repeated name · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Whose-forms, my and mine.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pronoun is a word we use in place of a
noun.
**True** — Libya's own words (Grade 7 WorkBook, 7.7);
Bangladesh's definition says the same.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "It" is the word for a boy or a man.
**False** — it is for an animal or a thing. A boy or a man
takes he (Egypt, p.27).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "We" means yourself and others together.
**True** — Egypt's own definition (p.27).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Ahmed he went home" is the right way to use
a pronoun.
**False** — the pronoun replaces the name; one worker per
job. "Ahmed went home," then "He went home" — never both
together in the same job slot.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The birds flew away. They flew over the
river," the word "they" stands for the birds.
**True** — more than one animal takes they.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "The bag is my" is right.
**False** — standing alone takes mine: "The bag is mine."
My rides with its noun: "my bag" (Ghana's forms; Egypt's box,
Primary 2 Term 1, p.51).
