# Video Script — Symbolism and Allegory

Super-simple lines, one picture per line.

[Picture: a dove carrying an olive branch]
"A dove — not just a bird. It stands for peace. That is a symbol."

[Picture: farm animals around a barn, pigs in the farmhouse window]
"Animal Farm: a farm that is really a country. An allegory!"

[Picture: giant insect shape in a bedroom doorway, a frightened family]
"A man, changed into an insect — the symbol of feeling small and hated."
