# Levels — Symbolism and Allegory

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Symbols that stand for ideas | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name what symbols stand for: in Kafka's The Metamorphosis the vermin form, the father's apple, the sister's violin and the three lodgers each carry a meaning past themselves; in the lesson's poem, connotation does the same work in single words. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Allegory — a story inside a story | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Read allegory whole: Orwell's Animal Farm, where the farm stands for a state and its revolution, and Wells's The Time Machine, where the future's two peoples stand for the classes of the author's own day. |
