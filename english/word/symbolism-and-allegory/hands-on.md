# Hands-On — Symbolism and Allegory

1. **Symbol gallery.** Draw a symbol and hide its meaning; the class guesses the idea before you reveal it.
2. **Decode the farm.** In groups, build the Animal Farm key: character by character, who do they stand for?
3. **Allegory in one page.** Invent a tiny allegory — animals in a schoolyard standing for a real school problem — and read it aloud.
4. **Connotation sort.** Sort feeling-words into shining and darkening piles for a poem about morning.
