# Short Answer — Symbolism and Allegory

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name three symbols from The Metamorphosis and one meaning each. (Sub lesson 1)
   — The vermin form (feeling small, hated, a burden); the apple (the father's rejection); the sister's violin (beauty/humanity); the three lodgers (cold respectability). Any three.
2. What does a sunrise often symbolise? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A new beginning, hope.
3. How do you read an allegory? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Ask of every big thing in the story: who or what does this stand for in the real world?
4. Who do the Eloi stand for in The Time Machine? (Sub lesson 2)
   — The soft, gentle upper class who live above ground on others' labour.
