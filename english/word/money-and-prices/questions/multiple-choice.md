# Money and prices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "An orange costs 900 ______."
a) Riels  b) kilos  c) dozen
**Answer: a) Riels** — Cambodia's money (Grade 5, Chapter 4,
Unit 6, pp.86–89).

**2. (Level 1)** For many things, the question is: "How much
______ the clothes?"
a) is  b) are  c) does
**Answer: b) are** — Cambodia's singular/plural pair
(Unit 7, pp.90–93).

**3. (Level 2)** The money used in 19 countries since 1999,
in Sudan's book, is the ______.
a) yen  b) pound  c) euro
**Answer: c) euro** (Sudan, Grade 8, Unit 7, p.49).

**4. (Level 3)** Money goes OUT of my hand to you. I ______
you 10.
a) borrow  b) lend  c) save
**Answer: b) lend** — Uzbekistan's money set (English
Matters 9, Unit 4).

**5. (Level 3)** The money your job pays you is your ______.
a) cheque  b) salary  c) coin
**Answer: b) salary** (English Matters 9, Unit 4).
