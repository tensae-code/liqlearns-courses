# Money and prices — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Asking the Price · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Currencies and Near Prices · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Money Set.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "How much are the clothes?" is the right
question for many things.
**True** — Cambodia's pair: is for one, are for many
(Unit 7, pp.90–93).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About and around make a price exact.
**False** — they do the opposite: they soften a price that
is not exact (Sudan, Unit 7, p.49).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lend and borrow mean the same thing.
**False** — one coin, two directions: lend goes out of my
hand; borrow comes into my hand.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In Cambodia's book, one book is 2,100
Riels.
**True** (Grade 5, Chapter 4, Unit 7).
