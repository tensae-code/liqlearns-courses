# Money and prices — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Asking the Price · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — Currencies and Near Prices · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The
Money Set.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** costs · borrow · about · note · save

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One pencil ______ 1,200 Riels.
**Answer: costs** (Cambodia, Grade 5, Unit 7).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Can I ______ 10 from you? I will give it
back.
**Answer: borrow** — money coming INTO my hand.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** It costs ______ 10 pounds. I am not sure
of the exact price.
**Answer: about** — Sudan's softening word (Unit 7, p.49).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A coin is round metal money; a ______ is
paper money.
**Answer: note** (Uzbekistan's set).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** I do not spend all my money. I ______ some
every month.
**Answer: save.**
