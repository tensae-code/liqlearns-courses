# Money and prices — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** costs · borrow · about · note · save

**1. (Level 1)** One pencil ______ 1,200 Riels.
**Answer: costs** (Cambodia, Grade 5, Unit 7).

**2. (Level 3)** Can I ______ 10 from you? I will give it
back.
**Answer: borrow** — money coming INTO my hand.

**3. (Level 2)** It costs ______ 10 pounds. I am not sure
of the exact price.
**Answer: about** — Sudan's softening word (Unit 7, p.49).

**4. (Level 3)** A coin is round metal money; a ______ is
paper money.
**Answer: note** (Uzbekistan's set).

**5. (Level 3)** I do not spend all my money. I ______ some
every month.
**Answer: save.**
