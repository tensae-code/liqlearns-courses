# Money and prices — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Ask the price of one orange, with cost.
**Answer:** "How much does an orange cost?" — Cambodia's
question (Unit 6).

**2. (Level 1)** How much does the orange cost, in
Cambodia's book?
**Answer:** 900 Riels.

**3. (Level 2)** Name three currencies from Sudan's lesson.
**Answer:** pound, yen, euro (Grade 8, Unit 7, p.49).

**4. (Level 3)** Name four words from Uzbekistan's money
set.
**Answer:** any four of coin, note, exchange, cheque,
account, income, salary, save, lend, borrow (English
Matters 9, Unit 4).
