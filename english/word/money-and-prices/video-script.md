# Video script — Money and prices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome to my market! Come and buy!

An orange. **How much does an orange cost?**
**An orange costs 900 Riels.**
A pencil. **How much is a pencil?**
**One pencil costs 1,200 Riels.**

One thing — **how much IS it?**
Many things — **how much ARE they?**

This is a **coin**. This is a **note**.
This money is the **pound**. This money is the **euro**.

I **lend** you 10 — out of my hand!
You **borrow** 10 — into your hand!
And this money? I **save** it. Good money. Safe money.

Now you. Ask me: how much?
That is money — in English.
