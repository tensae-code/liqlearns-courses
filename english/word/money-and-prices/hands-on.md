# Hands-on — Money and prices

The lesson with real things and real prices, not only on
the page.

## The home market
Put five things from your home in a row. Make a price card
for each — a number and a money word. A partner walks your
stall, asks the price of every thing out loud, and writes
the answers. All five asked and answered, then swap stalls.

## Coin in the hand
With one coin (or a drawn one): pass it back and forth and
say the sentence that fits the direction, every pass — "I
lend you 10." "I borrow 10 from you." Ten passes; the hands
teach the pair faster than the head can.

## Real prices
Find three real prices today — a price called out at a
market, a price on a packet, a fare. Say each one in a full
English sentence at home: "The rice costs about …" About
and around are allowed; Sudan's book uses them too.

## My money words
Write your own money page: your country's money name, the
coins and notes it has, and three sentences — one save, one
lend, one borrow — about your own life.
