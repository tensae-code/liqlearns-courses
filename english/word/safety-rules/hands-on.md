# Hands-on — Safety Rules

1. **Walk and name.** Walk through a home or classroom together. At each danger — heat, height, sharp things, water, the road outside — stop and say the safety rule for it out loud.

2. **What happens next?** One of you starts an accident story with one line ("The cup is at the edge of the table…"). The other finishes it with going to, then tells what rule would have stopped it.
