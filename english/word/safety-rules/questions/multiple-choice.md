## Multiple choice

1. Which word makes a safety rule strongest — it says the thing must not happen at any time? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) sometimes  b) never  c) maybe
   **Answer: b).**

2. A thing that can hurt you is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) careful  b) dangerous  c) low
   **Answer: b).**

3. "The glass is at the edge of the table — it is ______ fall." The prediction form is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) going to  b) never  c) yesterday
   **Answer: a).**

4. In Eritrea's books, safety is taught… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) only in Grade 3  b) in a Grade 3 unit and again in Grade 4, on danger and accidents  c) in no book
   **Answer: b).**
