## Short answer

1. Give one safety rule for the kitchen, using never. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any true rule in the shape, e.g. "Never touch a hot pot without a cloth."**

2. What three things does an accident account need? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: what happened (the past), what was dangerous (the danger word), and the advice or rule for next time.**

3. Which two Eritrea books teach safety, and in which units? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the Grade 3 book, Unit 18, and the Grade 4 book, Unit 11.**
