## Matching

Match each danger to its safety rule. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. fire
2. a knife in a child's hand
3. a high wall
4. a glass at the table's edge

a) Never run with it — carry it point-down, or not at all.
b) Never play with it; it burns fast and spreads.
c) Move it to the middle before it falls.
d) Do not climb it; a fall from high up breaks bones.

**Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c.**
