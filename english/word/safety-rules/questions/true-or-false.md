## True or false

1. A safety rule names the dangerous thing and the safe action in one short line. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. "Never" in a safety rule means "only on Sundays". (Sub lesson 1) **False — it means at no time, ever.**

3. Predicting an accident — saying what is going to happen — is part of safety talk. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. When an accident happens, there is nothing useful to say about it. (Sub lesson 2) **False — telling what happened, what was dangerous, and the rule for next time is how accidents teach.**
