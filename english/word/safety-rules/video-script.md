# Video script — Safety Rules

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A pot steams at the very edge of a table.]

"Careful! Hot! The pot is at the edge. It is going to fall!"

[The mascot holds up a flat hand — stop.] "Some words keep us safe. DANGER. NEVER. Never play with fire. Never run with a knife."

"Say the rule. Remember the rule. The rule keeps you safe."
