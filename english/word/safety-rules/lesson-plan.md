# Lesson plan — Safety Rules

Unit 7 (Words for the World) — the language of staying safe: the danger words, the rules built with never and careful, and how to talk about accidents and what happens next. Eritrea's Grade 3 book gives safety its own unit; its Grade 4 book returns to danger and accidents.

## Sub lessons

1. Danger words and safety rules
2. Accidents — what happened, and what happens next

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## How this lesson sits with safety-at-home
safety-at-home (Unit 7) teaches the youngest learners to spot a danger in a picture of a home and say why it is dangerous. This lesson teaches the rules language itself — danger, careful, never — and the talk around accidents. The two stand side by side; neither replaces the other.
