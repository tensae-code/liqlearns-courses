# Multiple choice questions — Languages and Language Learning

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).

## Multiple choice

1. **What does CLE stand for in Uruguay's language centres?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Centros de Lenguas Extranjeras
   - b) Central Library of English
   - c) Children's Language Exams
   - **Answer: a) Centros de Lenguas Extranjeras**

2. **What does the Inglés Sin Límites programme do?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) It translates books into Spanish
   - b) It carries English teaching to rural schools
   - c) It tests teachers
   - **Answer: b) It carries English teaching to rural schools**

3. **Uruguayan Sign Language is…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Spanish spelled with the fingers
   - b) A full language of the hands
   - c) A set of classroom signals
   - **Answer: b) A full language of the hands**

4. **How many languages does the Grade 12 book say “thank you” in?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Six
   - b) Ten
   - c) Sixteen
   - **Answer: c) Sixteen**

5. **Guaraní and Quechua are in the list because…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) They are South America's own languages
   - b) They are easy to spell
   - c) They are new languages
   - **Answer: a) They are South America's own languages**
