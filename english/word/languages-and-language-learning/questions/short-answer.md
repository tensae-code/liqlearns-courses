## Short answer

1. **Who runs the CLE language centres?** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** ANEP, Uruguay's national education administration.
2. **Name two languages from the Grade 12 book's “thank you” set that are South America's own.** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** Guaraní and Quechua.
3. **Why does the book ask learners to judge beliefs about language learning?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** Because how you believe a language is learned changes how you learn it.
