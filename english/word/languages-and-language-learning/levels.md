# Levels — Languages and Language Learning

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it moves from one country's language map to the whole world's, and from naming languages to learning one. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — Languages in Uruguay — and learning them there | The CLE centre languages, sign language, and the rural English programme. |
| 2 — Languages of the world — and learning a new one | Sixteen ways to say thank you, the benefits, and which learning beliefs hold up. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
