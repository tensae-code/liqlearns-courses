This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-10 expansion read of Uruguay's English books: the read found languages and language learning stocked nowhere in the course as a lesson of its own (Languages and peoples names languages; it does not teach programmes or learning); its diff marks the topics “NEW LESSON NEEDED: languages of Uruguay and language-learning programmes” and “NEW LESSON NEEDED: languages of the world and language learning”, stocked here as one lesson because both halves teach the one thing — the languages people learn, and how they learn them.
