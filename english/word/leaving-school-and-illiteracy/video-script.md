# Video script — Leaving School and Illiteracy

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[An empty school desk appears behind the mascot.]

"Some children leave school early. They stop learning. Why?"

"Work. Money. A long road to school. And a person who cannot read or write is illiterate. Illiteracy makes life hard."

[The mascot opens a book.] "But learning can start again — at any age."
