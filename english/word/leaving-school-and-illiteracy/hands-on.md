# Hands-on — Leaving School and Illiteracy

1. **Reasons poll.** Like the book's poll: in small groups, list reasons pupils leave school early, then poll the class and count the answers. Say the top reason in one English sentence.

2. **Hard or easy?** Cards name everyday jobs (reading a bus sign, filling a form, reading a medicine label). Decide together: easy, or hard for an illiterate person? Say why.
