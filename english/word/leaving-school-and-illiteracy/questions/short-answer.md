## Short answer

1. Name two reasons pupils leave school early. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any two of: work and money, family needs, distance, finding school hard.**

2. Name two difficulties illiterate people face. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any two of: forms they cannot fill, signs/labels they cannot read, jobs they cannot take.**

3. What is the difference between "illiterate" and "illiteracy"? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: "illiterate" describes the person; "illiteracy" names the state or problem.**
