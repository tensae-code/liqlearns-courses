## Multiple choice

1. An illiterate person is someone who… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) cannot read or write  b) does not like books  c) left school last year
   **Answer: a).**

2. A truant is a pupil who… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) loves school  b) stays away from school without permission  c) teaches the class
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which is a reason pupils leave school early, from the book's poll? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) work and money  b) too many holidays  c) easy lessons
   **Answer: a).**

4. How can an illiterate adult learn again? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) by joining a reading course  b) by waiting  c) it is impossible
   **Answer: a).**
