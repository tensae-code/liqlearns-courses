## True or false

1. A truant has permission to stay away from school. (Sub lesson 1) **False — a truant stays away without permission.**

2. Illiteracy makes everyday life harder. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

3. An illiterate adult can never learn to read. (Sub lesson 2) **False — reading courses exist for adults.**

4. Leaving school early is always the pupil's own choice. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the poll gives reasons like work, money and family needs.**
