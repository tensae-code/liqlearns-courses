## Fill in the blank

1. To stop going to school before finishing is to ______ school early. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: leave.**

2. A pupil who stays away without permission is a ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: truant.**

3. Not being able to read or write is ______. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: illiteracy.**

4. An adult can join a reading ______ to learn again. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: course.**
