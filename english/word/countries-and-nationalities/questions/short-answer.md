## Short answer

1. Name the six countries in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Japan, Peru, Mexico, France, the USA, Bolivia.** — the compendium's set.

2. Which two are in North America? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Mexico and the USA.** — both in North America.

3. What is the nationality word for Peru? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Peruvian.** — Peru → Peruvian (-ian).

4. What is the nationality word for the USA? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: American.** — USA → American (-an).

5. Ask someone where they are from. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “Where are you from?”** — the from-question.

6. Answer for yourself with both forms. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I'm from (country). I'm (nationality).”** — country and people-word together sound complete.

7. Say where a French person is from. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “She/He is from France.”** — French → from France.
