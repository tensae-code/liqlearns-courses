## Fill in the blank

1. Tokyo is in ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Japan** — Tokyo is Japan's capital city.

2. Paris is in ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: France** — Paris is France's capital.

3. Japan → ___ (nationality). (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Japanese** — the -ese ending.

4. Mexico → ___ (nationality). (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Mexican** — the -an ending.

5. Bolivia → ___ (nationality). (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Bolivian** — the -ian ending.

6. “Where are you ___?” — “I'm from Peru.” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: from** — the question word is from.

7. “They are from Japan. They are ___.” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Japanese** — nationality words do not change for plural.
