## Matching

Match the country to its nationality word. (Sub lesson 2)

1. Japan — a
2. Peru — b
3. Mexico — c
4. France — d
5. the USA — e
6. Bolivia — f

a) Japanese
b) Peruvian
c) Mexican
d) French
e) American
f) Bolivian

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (-ese), 2–b (-ian), 3–c (-an), 4–d (its own word), 5–e (-an), 6–f (-ian). Learn them as pairs, exactly as the book teaches them.
