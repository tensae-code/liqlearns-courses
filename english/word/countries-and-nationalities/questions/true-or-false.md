## True or false

1. Peru is in South America. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — Peru is Bolivia's South American neighbour.

2. France is in Asia. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — France is in Europe.

3. The nationality for Japan is “Japanian”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it is Japanese.

4. The nationality for France is “French”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — French is its own word.

5. Nationality words change when we talk about many people. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — “They are Japanese” — the word stays the same.

6. “I'm from Bolivian” is correct. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — from takes the country: “I'm from Bolivia. I'm Bolivian.”
