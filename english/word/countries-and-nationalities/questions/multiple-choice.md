## Multiple choice

1. Which is a country in Asia? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Japan  b) France  c) Peru
   **Answer: a) Japan** — Japan is the Asian country in this set.

2. Which country is Bolivia's neighbour in South America? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Mexico  b) Peru  c) France
   **Answer: b) Peru** — Peru neighbours Bolivia.

3. The USA is in… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Europe  b) North America  c) Asia
   **Answer: b) North America** — the United States of America is in North America.

4. A person from Japan is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Japanian  b) Japanese  c) Japans
   **Answer: b) Japanese** — Japan → Japanese (-ese).

5. A person from France is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Francian  b) Frenchese  c) French
   **Answer: c) French** — France → French; its own word.

6. A person from Mexico is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Mexican  b) Mexicish  c) Mexical
   **Answer: a) Mexican** — Mexico → Mexican (-an).

7. “Where are you from?” — “I'm ___ Peru.” (Sub lesson 3)
   a) of  b) from  c) nationality
   **Answer: b) from** — from + country answers the question.

8. “She is from Bolivia.” She is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Bolivia  b) Bolivian  c) Bolivese
   **Answer: b) Bolivian** — Bolivia → Bolivian (-ian).
