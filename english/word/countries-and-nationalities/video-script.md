# Video script — Countries and Nationalities

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A world map, six countries lighting up.] “Japan! Peru! Mexico! France! The USA! Bolivia! Every country has TWO words.”

[Flags rise with people-words.] “From Japan — JAPANESE! From Peru — PERUVIAN! From France — FRENCH!”

[Two travellers meet.] “Where are you from? I'm from Bolivia. I'm Bolivian! Now you can introduce anyone on Earth.”
