# Hands-on — Countries and Nationalities

A map, real or drawn, is the whole classroom for this lesson.

## Point and pair

One learner points at a country on the map; the other says the pair: “Mexico — Mexican.” Wrong pair? Point again and try again, kindly.

## Travellers' party

Each learner takes a secret country card. Mingle and ask: “Where are you from?” Answer in character: “I'm from Japan. I'm Japanese.” Find your same-country partner by asking.
