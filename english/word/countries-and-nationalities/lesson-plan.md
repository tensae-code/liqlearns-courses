# Lesson plan — Countries and Nationalities

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Countries and Nationalities
**Lesson:** countries-and-nationalities — country names and the words for their people: Japan–Japanese, Peru–Peruvian
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (with the other word sets)
**The one thing:** Country names paired with their nationality words, and asking and answering “Where are you from?”
**This lesson's own level:** Word-set lesson: six country–nationality pairs from the compendium's Year 1 set, with the from-question they serve.

## Why this lesson exists

Bolivia's teacher compendium (Year 1) teaches a countries-and-nationalities set — Japan/Japanese, Peru/Peruvian, Mexico/Mexican, France/French, USA/American, Bolivia/Bolivian — and no course lesson carried the paired set. (The Grade 7 student book's own countries teaching is stocked in Hello and Your Name, where its dialogue lives.) This lesson is built from the compendium placement under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06); the source is teacher-compilation evidence and is cited as such.

## Sub lessons

1. Countries
2. Nationalities
3. “Where are you from?”

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. Hello and Your Name carries the introduction dialogues these words live in; each lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Name the six countries: Japan, Peru, Mexico, France, the USA, Bolivia.
- Pair each country with its nationality word: Japanese, Peruvian, Mexican, French, American, Bolivian.
- Ask “Where are you from?” and answer with country and nationality.

## What you need
A world map or globe, if one is near.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: a world map with six countries coloured — Japan, Peru, Mexico, France, the USA, Bolivia. The learner points and guesses their English names first.
2. Country round (sub lesson 1): name each coloured country; find your own country on the map and say its English name.
3. ADD GIF (1–2 seconds): a flag rises over Japan and the word JAPANESE grows beside it. Sub lesson 2 pairs each flag with its people-word.
4. Sub lesson 3 is the conversation itself: two travellers meet — “Where are you from? I'm from Peru. I'm Peruvian.”

## Add-ons
- Flag points: one learner points at a country; the other must say country AND nationality before the hand moves.
- Travellers' party: everyone takes a country card and mingles, asking “Where are you from?” in English only.

## If the learner is stuck
Six pairs only — learn them as pairs (country–people) from the first minute, never as two separate lists.

## Teacher note
The set is small because the book's set is small: six pairs, exactly as the compendium teaches them. More countries live in Hello and Your Name and across the course; this lesson keeps the compendium's own set whole.
