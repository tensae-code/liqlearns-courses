# Hands-on — Working words

The lesson at a real desk, not only on the page.

## Run the desk
A table, a chair, a paper form, a key (any key). Play the
whole guest road with a partner: phone booking, arrival,
registration, the key handed over with its sentence. The
desk worker may not leave the desk's English — the guest
may ask anything.

## The message pad
Phone play: one partner calls (speaking to the wall is
fine), the desk answers, finds the person "engaged," and
takes a message on a real pad: caller, for whom, message,
call back. Read the message back exactly — the trade's
first skill is accuracy.

## Label the letter
Take any real business letter or print one. Label every
part in the margin — address, date, greeting, subject,
body, close, signature — the Office Clerks book's opening
two pages, done on real paper.

## Board-basis menu
Write the four ways a hotel feeds a guest on four cards —
half-board, full-board, bed and breakfast, self-catering —
and under each, draw the meals it includes. Test a partner:
they hold up a card, you say the meals.
