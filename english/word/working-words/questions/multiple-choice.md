# Working words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You phone a hotel and book a room for next
month. You make a ______.
a) reception  b) reservation  c) brochure
**Answer: b) reservation** — the booking, made before
arrival (Hotel and Tourist Trade, Unit 2).

**2. (Level 1)** The money you pay first, before you
arrive, is the ______.
a) balance  b) rate  c) deposit
**Answer: c) deposit** — "a deposit now and the balance two
weeks before arrival" (Unit 2, pp.37–50).

**3. (Level 2)** In a business letter, the line that tells
what the letter is about is the ______.
a) signature  b) subject line  c) greeting
**Answer: b) subject line** — Office Clerks, "Things to
keep in mind," printed pp.9–10.

**4. (Level 3)** The line is busy; the desk says the person
is ______.
a) engaged  b) confirmed  c) registered
**Answer: a) engaged** — the Hotel book's telephone strand
(Unit 2).

**5. (Level 3)** Bed, breakfast and one main meal is
______.
a) full-board  b) half-board  c) self-catering
**Answer: b) half-board** — the board-basis set (Unit 4,
pp.67–82).
