# Working words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Hotel Desk · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Office · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Telephone and the
Trade.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four fields on the hotel registration
form.
**Answer:** any four of name, nationality, address,
passport number, room, arrival, nights, departure (Hotel
book, Unit 3, pp.51–66).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four letter-kinds of the Office
Clerks export unit.
**Answer:** inquiry, order, acknowledgement, complaint
(Unit 3, pp.153–211).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say two telephone phrasal verbs from the
Hotel book.
**Answer:** any two of put through, get through, ring up,
hold on, cut off, hang up, call back (Unit 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which country teaches this whole lesson's
stock?
**Answer:** Greece — its EPAL vocational books (Hotel and
Tourist Trade; Office Clerks). This is the specialist
lesson.
