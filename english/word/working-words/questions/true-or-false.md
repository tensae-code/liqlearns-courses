# Working words — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Hotel Desk · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Office · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Telephone and the
Trade.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A reservation is made at reception when
you arrive.
**False** — a reservation is the booking, made BEFORE
arrival; reception is where you register when you come
(Units 2–3).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A business letter's parts run: address,
date, greeting, subject, body, close, signature.
**True** — the Office Clerks book's opening anatomy
(printed pp.9–10).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Hold on" and "hang up" mean the same on
the phone.
**False** — hold on means WAIT on the line; hang up ends
the call. The Hotel book lists both (Unit 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Working words is a general lesson for
every learner.
**False** — it is this level's SPECIALIST lesson: hotels,
offices and tourism, from Greece's vocational books.
