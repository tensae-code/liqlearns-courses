# Working words — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Hotel Desk · (Level 2) -> Sub
lesson 2 — The Office · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Telephone and the
Trade.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** reception · key · memo · brochure · call back

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On arrival, the guest goes to ______ to
register.
**Answer: reception** (Hotel and Tourist Trade, Unit 3).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Here is your ______." The guest can now
open the room.
**Answer: key.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A short office note sent inside the firm
is a ______.
**Answer: memo** — Office Clerks, Unit 2's channel choice.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The travel agent offers to post a ______ —
the holiday book with pictures and prices.
**Answer: brochure** (Unit 1).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He is out. Please ______ later."
**Answer: call back** — the telephone phrasal verbs
(Unit 2).
