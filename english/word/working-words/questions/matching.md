# Working words — matching

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — the guest's road, in order (Hotel
book, Units 1–3).
1. enquiry — **asking about a holiday**
2. reservation — **booking the rooms**
3. deposit — **first money paid**
4. registration — **signing in at reception**
5. key — **opening your room**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — office word and its job (Office
Clerks).
1. inquiry — **asking a firm about goods**
2. order — **telling a firm to send goods**
3. complaint — **writing that something is wrong**
4. memo — **a short note inside the office**
5. signature — **your name at the letter's foot**
