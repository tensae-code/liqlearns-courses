# Video script — Working words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Good morning! Welcome to the hotel!

You want a room? This is a **reservation**.
Two rooms, ten nights. The **rate** is with breakfast.
A **deposit** now, the **balance** later. Confirmed!

Now you are here. This is **reception**.
Please register — name, passport, dates.
The **porter** takes your bags.
**Here is your key.** Enjoy your stay!

Ring ring! The phone: **"Hold on, please."**
**"I will put you through."**
Engaged? **"Please call back."**

Hotel words, office words, travel words.
This is the specialist shelf — take it if the work is
yours.
That is working words — in English.
