# Social Media

## Do it first

Open any social feed in your mind. Someone posts a funny picture; a thousand people share it in an hour. What just happened — and what is it called? This lesson teaches the words of social media, from both of Costa Rica's senior books.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social media words

Costa Rica's books teach social media as a word set. From Grade 10: **go viral** (spread everywhere fast), a **troll** (a person who posts to upset others), a **hater**, a **vlogger** (video blogger), a **meme** (a picture or idea that spreads), a **mash-up**, the **blogosphere** (all the blogs together), **clickbait** (a headline made to pull clicks); and the doing words: **log in, share, spam**.

Grade 11's table of 30 terms adds: **app, blog, campaign, chat, cloud computing, comment, crowdfunding, cyberbullying, download, ebook, feed, hashtag, like, podcast, selfie, trending, upload, viral** — each with its Spanish meaning and an example sentence.

*Difficulty: Thirty working words — most learners know the things; here they learn the names.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Short forms — internet acronyms

People online write short. Grade 11 teaches the acronyms: **LOL** (laughing out loud), **ICYMI** (in case you missed it), **TL;DR** (too long; didn't read), **FOMO** (fear of missing out), **PRT** (please retweet), **FBF** (flashback Friday), **TBT** (throwback Thursday).

*Difficulty: Seven letter-shorts to read on sight.*

## Sub lesson 3 — Talking about social media

Words are for talking. Grade 10's discussion questions ask: what is good about social networking, and what is bad? Should children be online? Can a screen full of “friends” still leave a person lonely? Is meeting people online a good way to make friends?

There are no book answers here — the lesson gives you the words and the questions; the opinions are yours.

*Difficulty: Giving your own view, with reasons, on questions the book itself asks.*

## Say it to your companion

Your companion says an acronym — FOMO! You say what it means and use it in a sentence. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write five social media words and what each means.
2. Write three acronyms and their full forms.
3. Write two good things and two bad things about social media — the Grade 10 book's own question.
