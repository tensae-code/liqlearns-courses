# Video script — Social Media

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A picture flies from phone to phone, faster and faster.]

“It spread everywhere! It went viral.”

[A small laughing face with letters.] “L-O-L. Laughing out loud.”

[The mascot holds up a question card.] “Good or bad — what do you think? Now you have the words to say it.”
