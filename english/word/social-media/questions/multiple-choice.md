## Multiple choice

1. When a post “goes viral”, it… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) is deleted  b) spreads everywhere fast  c) is printed
   **Answer: b).**

2. A troll is a person who… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) posts to upset others  b) fixes computers  c) takes selfies
   **Answer: a).**

3. FOMO means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) fear of missing out  b) friends on my own  c) follow me online
   **Answer: a).**

4. Grade 10's discussion asks about… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) exam marks  b) pros and cons of social networking  c) sports
   **Answer: b).**
