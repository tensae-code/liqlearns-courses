## Fill in the blank

1. A picture or idea that spreads online is a ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: meme.**

2. You ______ a file when you take it from the internet onto your device. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: download.**

3. TL;DR means “too long; didn't ______”. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: read.**

4. A headline made only to pull clicks is ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: clickbait.**
