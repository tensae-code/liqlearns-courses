# Levels — Social Media

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Social media words | the working words of social media — viral, meme, troll, vlogger, hashtag, feed, share, upload, download, cyberbullying — from the books' two vocabulary sets |
| Short forms — internet acronyms | LOL, ICYMI, TL;DR, FOMO, PRT, FBF, TBT — the letter-shorts of internet talk |
| Talking about social media | the discussion questions of Grade 10 — pros and cons, children online, loneliness, meeting people |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
