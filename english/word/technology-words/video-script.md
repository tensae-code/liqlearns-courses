# Video script — Technology words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is my computer. Wake up, computer!

This is the **monitor** — it shows me things.
This is the **keyboard** — I put words in.
This is the **mouse** — click! It points.
And the **printer** — paper comes out!

**Plug in. Turn on. Connect!**
Now the internet. **Visit a website.**
**Surf** — page to page to page!
Photo goes UP — **upload!**
Song comes DOWN — **download!**
**Send a message** — hello, friend!

And who is this? A **chatbot**. It talks back!
That is **artificial intelligence** — AI.

Now you. Point and name: monitor! keyboard! mouse!
That is technology — in English.
