# Technology words — the lesson

Course: English · Title: Word · Lesson: Technology words
Built from: Bolivia, Year 2, Topic 6 (computer hardware and
software) and Year 5, Topics 7–8 ("Surviving on the
internet"; internet activities) — Vietnam, Tiếng Anh 12
(Grade 12), Unit 6 "Artificial intelligence" — Sudan, SPINE
5 (Grade 11), Chapter Ten "Modern Technology," and SMILE
Book 7 (Grade 7), Unit 4 "Technology."

A soft hint before you start: if you know the Verbs lesson,
this one will be easier — half of it is doing words. You do
not need it first. Every word below is taught whole, in its
own sentence.

## Do it first
Look at the phone or computer nearest you. Touch the screen.
Touch the keys or buttons. Follow the charger to the wall.
Every part you just touched has an English name, and every
thing you do with the machine — turn it on, send a message,
save a photo — has English words too. Those names and those
doings are this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Computer Parts
**The parts.** Bolivia's Year 2 book numbers the parts of a
computer 1 to 6, with what each one does. Learn them as the
book gives them:

1. **drive** — reads CD/DVD discs
2. **monitor** — the screen; an output device (it shows you
   things)
3. **keyboard** — the principal input device (you put words
   in with it)
4. **mouse** — the pointing device
5. **modem / router** — brings the network signal
6. **printer** — prints documents on paper

The same topic teaches the word pairs that ride with them:
**hardware** (the parts you can touch) and **software**
(the programmes inside). And the first computer actions:
**scan** a document, **upload** a photo, **print** a
document.

> a picture of a desk with a computer, a keyboard, a mouse
> and a printer on it — the student names the four things
> the picture shows, in English.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Technology Actions
**The doing words.** Bolivia's action set is four verbs:
**plug in, turn on, turn off, connect**. Its Year 5 book
goes onto the internet itself, and the activities list is
the heart of this level:

- **visit a website**
- **surf the internet** — move from page to page
- **upload a file** — send it up, from you to the internet
- **download an application** — bring it down, to your
  machine
- **send a message**

Upload and download are a pair: up is away from you, down
is to you. The same book's classroom task keeps the pair
honest: describe the steps to **upload** a task to the
Classroom platform — the steps go up, because the task
leaves your hands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gadgets and the Newest Words
**Gadgets.** Sudan's Grade 7 technology unit names the
small machines: **sat-nav, headphones, camera, webcam,
mobile** — and Sudan's senior book gives the whole field
its chapter title: **Modern Technology** (SPINE 5, Chapter
Ten). A gadget is a small useful machine; the set above is
the books' own.

**The newest words.** Vietnam's Grade 12 book teaches the
words of now, in its Artificial Intelligence unit:
**artificial intelligence** (a machine that learns and
answers — AI for short), **chatbot** (a programme you talk
to, that talks back), **programme**, **analyse** (study
something carefully, part by part), **advanced**. Its
reading, "Tech News," is about AI at school — digital
portfolios and learning tools — so the newest words are
already school words.

## Say it to your companion
Give the orders, one by one: "Plug in the computer. Turn it
on. Connect to the internet. Visit a website. Upload a
photo. Send a message. Turn it off." Your companion mimes
each order on a real or imagined machine and says the doing
word back. Then swap — and this time your companion orders,
and adds one gadget: "Put on the headphones."

## Do It Yourself
1. Draw a computer on a desk. Label the six parts from
   Bolivia's list.
2. Write the steps to upload a task, in order: first…,
   then…, using upload, connect, send.
3. Write two sentences with download and two with upload —
   one true about your own phone.
4. Name five gadgets in your home, in English.
