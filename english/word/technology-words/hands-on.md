# Hands-on — Technology words

The lesson on a real machine, not only on the page.

## Touch and name
On a real computer or phone, touch each part and say its
name as you touch: monitor (screen), keyboard, mouse,
printer, drive, modem. No machine? Mime the desk — hands
where each part would sit.

## The order game
A partner gives you orders from the lesson — plug in, turn
on, connect, upload, download, turn off — and you mime each
one big and clear. Six orders without a miss, then swap.
Bolivia's action verbs, played.

## Up or down?
Say ten things you do with a phone. After each one, your
partner calls "up!" or "down!": sending a photo to the
class page — up (upload). Bringing a new game — down
(download). Reading a message — neither; it is already
here. The arrow is the meaning.

## Gadget hunt
Walk your home and name every gadget you find, in English:
mobile, headphones, camera… Sudan's set first, then any
others you can name. Write the list; the one you cannot
name is your new word.
