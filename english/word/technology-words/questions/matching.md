# Technology words — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Computer Parts · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Technology Actions.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — Bolivia's six parts and what they do
(Year 2, Topic 6, p.177).
1. drive — **reads CD/DVD discs**
2. monitor — **output device; shows you things**
3. keyboard — **principal input device**
4. mouse — **pointing device**
5. modem/router — **brings the network signal**
6. printer — **prints documents**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — the action and its direction.
1. upload a file — **up: from you to the internet**
2. download an application — **down: to your machine**
3. turn on — **start the machine**
4. turn off — **stop the machine**
5. plug in — **put the plug in the wall**
