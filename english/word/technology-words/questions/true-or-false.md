# Technology words — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Hardware is the parts you can touch;
software is the programmes inside.
**True** — Bolivia's Topic 6 pair (Year 2, p.177).

**2. (Level 2)** "Download a photo to the class page" is the
right way to say sending your photo up.
**False** — sending up is upload. Download brings things
down to you (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 8).

**3. (Level 3)** Vietnam's Grade 12 AI unit teaches the
words chatbot and artificial intelligence.
**True** (Grade 12, Unit 6, pp.76–87).

**4. (Level 3)** Sudan's senior technology chapter is
called "Modern Technology."
**True** — SPINE 5, Chapter Ten, opener printed p.139.
