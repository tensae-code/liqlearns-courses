# Technology words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The part you type on is the ______.
a) monitor  b) keyboard  c) printer
**Answer: b) keyboard** — Bolivia's list calls it "the
principal input device" (Year 2, Topic 6, p.177).

**2. (Level 1)** The pointing device is the ______.
a) mouse  b) drive  c) modem
**Answer: a) mouse** — part 4 of Bolivia's numbered list.

**3. (Level 2)** You send a photo from your phone up to the
class page. You ______ it.
a) download  b) upload  c) print
**Answer: b) upload** — Bolivia's collocation "upload a
photo" (Year 2, Topic 6).

**4. (Level 3)** A programme you talk to, that talks back,
is a ______.
a) chatbot  b) sat-nav  c) webcam
**Answer: a) chatbot** — Vietnam's AI unit (Grade 12,
Unit 6, pp.76–87).

**5. (Level 3)** Which is one of Sudan's gadgets?
a) sat-nav  b) keyboard  c) router
**Answer: a) sat-nav** — SMILE Book 7, Unit 4, "Technology
Everywhere" (pp.25–32).
