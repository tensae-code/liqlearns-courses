# Technology words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Computer Parts · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 2 — The Technology Actions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gadgets
and the Newest Words.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six parts of a computer in
Bolivia's list.
**Answer:** drive, monitor, keyboard, mouse, modem/router,
printer (Year 2, Topic 6, p.177).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three internet activities from
Bolivia's Year 5 list.
**Answer:** any three of visit a website, surf the
internet, upload a file, download an application, send a
message (Topic 8, p.221).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a chatbot do?
**Answer:** It is a programme you talk to that talks back —
Vietnam's AI unit word (Grade 12, Unit 6).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two gadgets from Sudan's technology
unit.
**Answer:** any two of sat-nav, headphones, camera, webcam,
mobile (SMILE Book 7, Unit 4).
