**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ shows you things; it is an
output device.

**Answer: printer** (Bolivia's list, part 6).

Classroom platform.
**Answer: upload** (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 8).

**Answer: connect** — one of Bolivia's four action verbs.
