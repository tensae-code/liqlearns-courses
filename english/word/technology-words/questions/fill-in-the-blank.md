# Technology words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** monitor · printer · download · upload · connect

**1. (Level 1)** The ______ shows you things; it is an
output device.
**Answer: monitor** (Bolivia's list, part 2).

**2. (Level 1)** The ______ prints documents on paper.
**Answer: printer** (Bolivia's list, part 6).

**3. (Level 2)** ______ an application to bring it down to
your machine.
**Answer: download** (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 8, p.221).

**4. (Level 2)** Describe the steps to ______ a task to the
Classroom platform.
**Answer: upload** (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 8).

**5. (Level 2)** First ______ the printer, then print.
**Answer: connect** — one of Bolivia's four action verbs.
