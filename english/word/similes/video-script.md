# Video script — Similes

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A runner flashes past behind the mascot, impossibly fast.]

"He ran like the wind! Is he wind? No!"

[A lion appears on one side, a boy standing brave on the other.] "As brave as a lion. The boy is not a lion. He shares one thing with the lion: he is brave."

"That is a simile. It compares two things with like, or as — and it helps you see."
