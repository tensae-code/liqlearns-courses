## Multiple choice

1. A simile compares two things using… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the words like or as  b) a full stop  c) a question mark
   **Answer: a).**

2. In "as busy as a bee", the one shared point is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) flying  b) being busy  c) making honey
   **Answer: b).**

3. Which of these is a simile? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The wind is strong today.  b) She sang like a bird.  c) The bird sang at dawn.
   **Answer: b).**

4. A writer plants a simile in a story to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) make the reader see something fast and exactly  b) fill the page  c) end the story
   **Answer: a).**

5. In Fiji's Year 6 book, similes are the grammar focus of… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the first unit  b) the last narrative unit, Unit 19  c) the dictionary section
   **Answer: b).**
