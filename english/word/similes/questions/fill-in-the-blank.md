## Fill in the blank

1. A simile compares two unlike things with the word ______ or the word as. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: like.**

2. "As brave ______ a lion" — the simile marker that completes it is the same word twice. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: as.**

3. Every simile carries one shared ______: the point the two things have in common. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: point.**

4. When a plain word would not make the picture — not "very fast" but "fast like a river in flood" — a writer reaches for a ______. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: simile.**
