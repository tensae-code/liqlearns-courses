## True or false

1. A simile says two things are exactly the same thing. (Sub lesson 1) **False — it says they share one point; like or as keeps the comparison open.**

2. "As busy as a bee" is a simile. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. Similes are only for poems, never for stories. (Sub lesson 2) **False — Fiji's Year 6 book teaches them inside a narrative unit.**

4. A good simile makes the reader see the thing faster and more exactly. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
