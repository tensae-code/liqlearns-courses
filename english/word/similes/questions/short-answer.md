## Short answer

1. What is a simile? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a comparison of two unlike things with like or as, carrying one shared point.**

2. In "the baby slept like a log", what are the two things compared, and what is the point? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: the baby and a log; the shared point is sleeping soundly, without moving or waking.**

3. Why does a simile say its comparison openly, with like or as on show? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: so the reader knows it is a comparison — the two things are not claimed to be the same thing, only alike in one point.**
