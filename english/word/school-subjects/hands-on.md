# Hands-on — School subjects

The lesson in your real school week, not only on the page.

## Your timetable, in English
Copy your real timetable (or last year's, or your child's)
and rewrite every subject in English. Read one full day
aloud: day, subject, subject, subject.

## The favourite vote
Ask five people: "What's your favourite subject?" Write
each answer with its because. Read the results back as a
little survey, the way Mongolia's book surveys a London
school: "Three like Science. One likes Art a lot."

## Subject detective
Take one subject. Find three English words that live
only in it — for Science: plant, water, grow; for Maths:
plus, equals, half. Say the subject and its three words
as a set.

## The because round
With a partner: one names a subject, the other must
answer with a like or don't-like AND a because, in three
seconds. Ten subjects, then swap. Slow answers replay
the round.
