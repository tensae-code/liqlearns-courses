# School subjects — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Subjects · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 3 — The Timetable · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 2 — Likings with
Reasons.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set is Mexico's list?
a) Spanish, History, Mathematics, Biology…
b) Cooking, Driving, Fishing, Mining…
c) Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday…
**Answer: a)** — Mexico's ten: Spanish, History,
Mathematics, Biology, Geography, Physical Education,
Arts, Technology, Civics and Ethics, English (p.23).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The numbers subject is ______.
a) Geography  b) Mathematics  c) Music
**Answer: b) Mathematics** — Maths in Uzbekistan's
wordsearch and Rwanda's timetable.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda: "How many English lessons are
there?" — "There ______ three."
a) is  b) are  c) be
**Answer: b) are** — "How many… are there? There are…"
(Rwanda P4, Unit 1).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "I like Science ______ it is
interesting."
a) because  b) but yesterday  c) by bus
**Answer: a) because** — Rwanda's liking frame (P4,
p.9).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Mongolia's London survey, which
subject is liked a lot by the most students?
a) ICT  b) History  c) Foreign Language
**Answer: a) ICT** — 78.6% "like a lot," against History
39.3% and Foreign Language 32.3% (English X, p.14).
