# School subjects — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Subjects · (Level 3) ->
Sub lesson 2 — Likings with Reasons.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Mexico's ten subjects.
**Answer:** Spanish, History, Mathematics, Biology,
Geography, Physical Education, Arts, Technology, Civics
and Ethics, English (Grade 7, Project 2, p.23).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Rwanda's eight subjects.
**Answer:** Science, Mathematics, Social Studies,
Kinyarwanda, French, Music, Computer, Physical Education
(P4, Unit 1, pp.6–7).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What's your favourite subject? Answer
with a because.
**Answer:** "I like… because it is…" — any subject with
one of Rwanda's reasons: interesting, easy, fantastic,
splendid (or an honest don't-like with difficult, boring,
tough).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Anand (Mongolia's text) has six classes
a day. Which subject is the most interesting for him?
**Answer:** physics (English VIII, Unit 3, pp.37–38).
