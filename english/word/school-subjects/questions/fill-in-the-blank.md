# School subjects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** Mathematics · Science · History · Geography ·
Music · because

**1. (Level 1)** ______ is the subject of long ago —
kings, wars and old times.
**Answer: History.**

**2. (Level 1)** ______ is the subject of maps and
places.
**Answer: Geography.**

**3. (Level 1)** In ______, we study plants, animals
and how things work.
**Answer: Science.**

**4. (Level 2)** ______ is my favourite — I love
numbers.
**Answer: Mathematics.**

**5. (Level 3)** I like Art ______ it is fantastic.
**Answer: because** (Rwanda's frame, P4, p.9).
