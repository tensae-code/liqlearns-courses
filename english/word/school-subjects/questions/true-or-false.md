# School subjects — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Naming the Subjects · (Level 2) ->
Sub lesson 3 — The Timetable · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 2 — Likings with
Reasons.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical Education is a subject in both
Mexico's and Rwanda's lists.
**True** — Mexico Project 2, p.23; Rwanda P4, pp.6–7.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda's timetable work uses quantity
words like a lot, a few and many.
**True** — the determiners of quantity worked on the
timetable (P4, pp.13–15).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "I don't like Maths because it is easy"
uses Rwanda's frame correctly AND says a usual reason.
**False (as a pair)** — the frame is right, but Rwanda's
don't-like reasons are the hard ones: difficult, boring,
tough. "Easy" belongs with like.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Mongolia's survey, History was liked
a lot by more students than ICT.
**False** — ICT 78.6% "like a lot"; History 39.3%
(English X, p.14).
