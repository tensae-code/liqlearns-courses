# School subjects — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Physical Education is a subject in both
Mexico's and Rwanda's lists.
**True** — Mexico Project 2, p.23; Rwanda P4, pp.6–7.

**2. (Level 2)** Rwanda's timetable work uses quantity
words like a lot, a few and many.
**True** — the determiners of quantity worked on the
timetable (P4, pp.13–15).

**3. (Level 3)** "I don't like Maths because it is easy"
uses Rwanda's frame correctly AND says a usual reason.
**False (as a pair)** — the frame is right, but Rwanda's
don't-like reasons are the hard ones: difficult, boring,
tough. "Easy" belongs with like.

**4. (Level 3)** In Mongolia's survey, History was liked
a lot by more students than ICT.
**False** — ICT 78.6% "like a lot"; History 39.3%
(English X, p.14).
