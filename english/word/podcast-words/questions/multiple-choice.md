## Multiple choice

1. One show in a podcast series is an… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) episode  b) edit  c) ad
   **Answer: a) episode** — an episode is one show in the series.

2. The person you invite to talk on your podcast is the… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) lead  b) guest  c) file
   **Answer: b) guest** — the guest is the invited talker.

3. The short opening that says “here we are!” is the… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) intro  b) length  c) news
   **Answer: a) intro** — the intro opens the episode.

4. What a podcast is about is its… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) title  b) topic  c) checklist
   **Answer: b) topic** — the topic is the subject; the title is its name.

5. How long an episode runs is its… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) lead  b) length  c) notes
   **Answer: b) length** — length tells the running time.

6. To speak your podcast into a phone is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) download it  b) record it  c) share it
   **Answer: b) record it** — record = capture the sound.

7. To cut mistakes out after recording is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) edit  b) listen  c) guest
   **Answer: a) edit** — editing cleans the recording.

8. The list you tick off so nothing is forgotten is the… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) episode  b) checklist  c) intro
   **Answer: b) checklist** — the planner's checklist keeps you honest.
