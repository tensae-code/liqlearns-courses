## Fill in the blank

1. This is episode one of our ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: podcast** — a podcast is the whole show series.

2. Our ___ today is a famous singer. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: guest** — the invited person is the guest.

3. The ___ of this episode is “Music of Bolivia”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: topic** (title is also possible for a name) — the topic is what it is about.

4. The episode's ___ is twenty minutes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: length** — length = how long.

5. First we ___ the show, then we ___ the mistakes out. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: record; edit** — record first, edit after.

6. ___ the file to your phone and ___ it with your friends. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Download; share** — download to get it, share to spread it.

7. Tick every box on the ___ before you share. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: checklist** — the planner's checklist.
