## True or false

1. An episode is a whole podcast series. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — an episode is ONE show in the series.

2. A guest is a person invited to talk. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — guests talk; hosts invite.

3. The intro comes at the end of an episode. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the intro is the opening.

4. You should edit before you record. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — record first, then edit what you recorded.

5. A checklist helps you forget nothing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — tick every box before sharing.

6. To share a podcast means to keep it secret. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — to share means to let others get it.
