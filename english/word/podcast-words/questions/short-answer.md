## Short answer

1. What is the name of a podcast called? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Its title.** — every podcast has a title.

2. What do we call the first line that pulls listeners in? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: The lead.** — the lead opens strong.

3. Where does the sound of an episode live, as a thing on a computer? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: In a file.** — the episode is a sound file.

4. What are the small advertisement pieces inside a podcast called? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Ads.** — advertisements, shortened.

5. Name the three making steps after planning, in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Record, edit, share** (download sits with sharing) — record it, clean it, put it out.

6. What do people do with a podcast when it is ready? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: They download it and listen to it.** — download, then listen.

7. What must be ready before you record? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Your notes (and your lead and guest).** — the planner comes first.
