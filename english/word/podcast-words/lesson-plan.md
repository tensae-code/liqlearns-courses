# Lesson plan — Podcast Words

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Podcast Words
**Lesson:** podcast-words — the words for making a podcast: episode, guest, intro, record, edit, share
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Words for the World (with the other word sets)
**The one thing:** The vocabulary of a podcast and the planner for making one: title, topic, guest, intro, length, checklist, ads.
**This lesson's own level:** Word-set lesson: seventeen podcast words in two groups — what a podcast is made of, and the steps of making one.

## Why this lesson exists

Bolivia's teacher compendium (Year 3) carries a starred spread, “Creating a podcast”, with its own planner and vocabulary, and no course lesson carried podcast words. This lesson is built from that placement under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06). The source is teacher-compilation evidence and is cited as such.

## Sub lessons

1. The words of a podcast
2. Making one — the planner

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. It sits with the Unit 7 word sets and beside Technology Words; each stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Name the parts of a podcast: episode, guest, intro, topic, title, news, notes, lead.
- Name the making steps: record, edit, download, share, listen — and plan with a checklist, length, file and ads.

## What you need
A phone or any recorder, if you have one; paper for the planner if not.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: a microphone, headphones and a phone with a play button. The learner guesses what show-words they know before the set is taught.
2. ADD IMAGE: a podcast page with its parts labelled — title, episode list, a guest's name, the intro. Sub lesson 1 walks the page word by word.
3. ADD VIDEO (short): the planner filling in, line by line — TITLE, TOPIC, GUEST, LENGTH, CHECKLIST. Sub lesson 2: the learner plans a real one-minute podcast about their own town.

## Add-ons
- Planner race: pairs fill a podcast planner for a silly topic (“The News for Cats”) — first complete planner wins.
- Record a 30-second intro with a phone: title, topic, guest — real words in real use.

## If the learner is stuck
Teach the two groups on two different days if needed; the planner matters more than the word list — a learner who can plan a podcast owns the words.
