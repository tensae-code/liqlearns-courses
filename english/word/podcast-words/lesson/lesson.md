# Podcast Words

## Do it first

Have you ever listened to a podcast — a radio show you choose yourself, on a phone, whenever you like? Could you make one? Bolivia's teacher book has a whole page for creating one, with its own special words. Learn the words first — then plan a podcast of your own.

## Sub lesson 1 — The words of a podcast

A **podcast** is a show made of sound that people download and listen to when they want.

Its parts: an **episode** is one show in the series. The **title** is its name. The **topic** is what it is about. The **intro** is the short opening that says “here we are!”. A **guest** is the person you invite to talk. The **lead** is the opening line that pulls listeners in. **News** and **notes** are what you prepare to talk about. The **length** is how long the episode runs. The sound lives in a **file**, and small **ads** (advertisements) may sit inside it. And **listen** is what the whole world does with it.

Say them in one breath: episode, guest, intro, topic, title, lead, length, file.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making one — the planner

The book gives a **planner** — a checklist for making an episode:

1. Choose the **title** and the **topic**.
2. Book your **guest**.
3. Write your **notes** and your **lead**.
4. **Record** — speak it into a recorder or phone.
5. **Edit** — cut the mistakes, keep the good parts.
6. **Download** and **share** — put the file where people can get it, and tell them.
7. People **listen**.

The **checklist** keeps you honest: every box ticked before you share.

Plan yours now: a podcast about your town, your school, or your favourite music (Music Words will help). One minute is enough for episode one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion your podcast plan: “My podcast is called… The topic is… My guest is… It is (length) long.” They must ask you one question about it — and you answer from your notes.

## Do It Yourself

Fill the planner on paper for a real episode: title, topic, guest, intro line (your lead), length, and the checklist (notes ready? recorded? edited? shared?). If you have a phone, record the intro only — thirty seconds — and listen to yourself.
