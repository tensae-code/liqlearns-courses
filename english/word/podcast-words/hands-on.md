# Hands-on — Podcast Words

Words stick when you use them to make a real thing, however small.

## Plan a podcast

On paper, fill the planner: TITLE / TOPIC / GUEST / LEAD (your opening line) / LENGTH / CHECKLIST. Silly topics are allowed and encouraged — the words are the same.

## Record the intro

With a phone or recorder, record just the intro: “Hello! This is (title). Today our topic is… and our guest is…” Listen to it once. That recording is your episode zero.
