# Telling the time — matching

Match each time to its words, the way the books say them.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — o'clock.
1. 6:00 — **six o'clock**
2. 9:00 — **nine o'clock**
3. 12:00 (day) — **twelve o'clock, midday**
4. 12:00 (night) — **twelve o'clock, midnight**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — Mita's day (Bangladesh, pp.28–29).
1. gets up — **six o'clock**
2. breakfast — **seven**
3. walks to school — **nine**
4. visits grandmother — **three**
5. returns home — **five**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — Mongolia's minutes (Grade 6, p.78).
1. 7:30 — **half past seven**
2. 8:15 — **quarter past eight**
3. 10:10 — **ten past ten**
4. 8:45 — **quarter to nine**
5. 3:10 — **ten past three**

**Set 4 (Level 2)** — the day's halves (Bangladesh's rule).
1. 5 in the morning — **a.m.**
2. 5 in the evening — **p.m.**
3. 11 at night — **p.m.**
4. 11 in the morning — **a.m.**
