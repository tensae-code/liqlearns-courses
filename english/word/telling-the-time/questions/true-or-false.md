# Telling the time — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** The long hand is the hour hand.
**False** — the SHORT hand is the hour hand; the long hand is
the minute hand (Bangladesh, pp.26–27).

**2. (Level 2)** 12 o'clock at night is called 12 o'clock
midday.
**False** — at night it is 12 o'clock MIDNIGHT; midday is the
day's middle (Bangladesh, p.27).

**3. (Level 2)** We say "School begins at 7 a.m."
**True** — AT stands with times (Bangladesh's language
focus, U14).

**4. (Level 3)** Half past seven is 7:15.
**False** — half = 30 minutes: half past seven is 7:30.
7:15 is quarter past seven (Mongolia, p.78).

**5. (Level 3)** Ten past ten is 10:10.
**True** — Mongolia's own example (p.78).

**6. (Level 3)** Quarter to nine is later than half past
eight.
**True** — quarter to nine is 8:45; half past eight is 8:30.
The to half counts down to the next hour.
