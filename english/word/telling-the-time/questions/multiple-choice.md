# Telling the time — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — O'Clock Times · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Day in Times · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Minutes, Past and To.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The short hand on a clock is the ______.
a) minute hand  b) hour hand  c) second hand
**Answer: b) hour hand** — "the short, red hand is the hour
hand; the long, blue hand is the minute hand" (Bangladesh,
Class 4, pp.26–27).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "What's the time?" — the long hand stands at
12 and the short hand at 6. "It's ______."
a) six o'clock  b) twelve o'clock  c) six minutes
**Answer: a) six o'clock.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From midnight until midday we use ______.
a) p.m.  b) a.m.  c) o'clock
**Answer: b) a.m.** — the book's rule, word for word
(Bangladesh, p.27).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mita walks to school at ______.
a) seven  b) nine  c) three
**Answer: b) nine** — her day: up at six, breakfast at seven,
school at nine (Bangladesh, pp.28–29).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "School begins ______ 7 a.m."
a) on  b) in  c) at
**Answer: c) at** — the book's language focus: AT with times.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A quarter of an hour is ______ minutes.
a) 30  b) 15  c) 5
**Answer: b) 15** — Mongolia's rule: Quarter = 15 minutes,
Half = 30 (Grade 6, p.78).

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** 8:45 said the past/to way is ______.
a) quarter past eight  b) quarter to nine  c) half past
eight
**Answer: b) quarter to nine** — the to half counts to the
NEXT hour.
