# Telling the time — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** o'clock · a.m. · p.m. · at · half · quarter

**1. (Level 1)** It's six ______.
**Answer: o'clock.**

**2. (Level 2)** From midday until midnight, we use ______.
**Answer: p.m.** (Bangladesh's rule, p.27).

**3. (Level 2)** Breakfast at 7 is 7 ______.
**Answer: a.m.**

**4. (Level 2)** I go to school ______ 8 a.m.
**Answer: at** (Egypt's routines; Bangladesh's focus).

**5. (Level 3)** ______ past seven is 7:30.
**Answer: half.**

**6. (Level 3)** ______ past eight is 8:15; ______ to nine
is 8:45.
**Answer: quarter — quarter** (Mongolia's quarter = 15
minutes, p.78).
