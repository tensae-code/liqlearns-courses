# Telling the time — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — O'Clock Times · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Day in Times · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Minutes, Past and To.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ask the time, in English. Then answer it for
a clock showing 4:00.
**Answer:** "What's the time?" — "It's four o'clock."

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give 12 o'clock its two names.
**Answer:** 12 o'clock midday; 12 o'clock midnight
(Bangladesh, pp.26–27).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell Mita's day in its five times.
**Answer:** gets up at six o'clock; breakfast at seven;
walks to school at nine; grandmother at three; home at five
(Bangladesh, pp.28–29).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Answer about YOU, whole: What time do you
wake up?
**Answer:** "I wake up at ___ a.m." — Egypt's own Q&A shape
(Primary 3, p.25).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Tsogt gets up at half past seven. Write the
time in figures.
**Answer:** 7:30 (Mongolia, Grade 6, p.78).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Say 3:10 and 10:10 the Mongolian book's way.
**Answer:** ten past three; ten past ten (p.78's own pair).
