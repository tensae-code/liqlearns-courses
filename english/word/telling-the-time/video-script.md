# Video script — Telling the time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What's the time? Look at the clock!

The SHORT hand tells the hour.
The LONG hand runs — the minutes.
Hand up at twelve? **O'clock!**
It's **six o'clock**! Time to get up!

It's **seven o'clock** — breakfast!
It's **nine o'clock** — school!

The sun is high — **twelve o'clock, midday**.
**Three o'clock** — grandmother's house.
**Five o'clock** — home!

Half past seven — **7:30**.
Quarter past eight — the clock is smiling.
Ten past ten! Quarter to nine!

Midnight comes — **twelve o'clock** again.
A day of times, all told.

Now you. What time do YOU wake up?
Say it whole. That is YOUR day — in English.
