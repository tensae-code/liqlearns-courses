## True or false

1. A website may ask for your school as one of the five asks. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. Only unfriendly messages are dangerous; friendly ones are always safe. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the five asks can sound friendly.**

3. Telling a grown-up about a strange message gets you in trouble. (Sub lesson 2) **False — telling is how you stay safe.**

4. It is safe to meet an online friend if they sound nice. (Sub lesson 2) **False — never meet an online friend alone; tell a grown-up.**
