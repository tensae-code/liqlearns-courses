## Short answer

1. Name the five things a website or online friend may ask for. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: your address, your name, your school, a photo, and a meeting.**

2. A friendly message in your game asks for your address. What do you do? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: do not type it; close it and tell a grown-up you trust.**

3. Why is telling a grown-up the safe answer for every one of the five asks? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: because a grown-up you trust can see the message, stop the stranger, and keep you safe — telling is not getting in trouble.**
