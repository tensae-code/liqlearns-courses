## Multiple choice

1. A website asks for your home address. What is it asking for? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) one of the five private things  b) your favourite colour  c) a game score
   **Answer: a).**

2. Which of these is one of the five asks? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "What is your school called?"  b) "Do you like football?"  c) "What time is it?"
   **Answer: a).**

3. An online friend says, "Send me a photo of you." You should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) send your best photo  b) not send it, and tell a grown-up  c) send a photo of your friend instead
   **Answer: b).**

4. A game offers you a prize if you type your full name and address. You should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) type them quickly before the prize is gone  b) type only your name  c) not type them, and tell a grown-up
   **Answer: c).**

5. An online friend invites you to meet at the park, just the two of you. You should… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) go, but be quick  b) not go, and tell a grown-up  c) go with your little brother
   **Answer: b).**
