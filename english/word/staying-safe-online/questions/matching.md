## Matching

Match the message to its safe answer. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. "Tell me where you live and I will send your prize."
2. "Send me a picture of you."
3. "Come and meet me at the gate after school."
4. "Which school do you go to?"

a) Do not send it — tell a grown-up.
b) Do not type it — tell a grown-up.
c) Do not say it — tell a grown-up.
d) Do not go — tell a grown-up.

**Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c.**
