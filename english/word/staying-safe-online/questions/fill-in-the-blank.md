## Fill in the blank

1. A website or online friend may ask for your address, your name, your ______, a photo, or a meeting. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: school.**

2. The five asks are: address, name, school, ______ and meeting. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: photo.**

3. Do not give your ______ to people on a screen. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: address (also right: name / school).**

4. When a strange message asks for one of the five things, tell a ______. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: grown-up.**
