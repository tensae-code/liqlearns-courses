# Video script — Staying Safe Online

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A phone screen appears behind the mascot, with a friendly message bubble.]

"A message! It says: tell me your address. Hmm. Do we tell?"

[The bubble shakes its head.] "No. Your address is yours."

[A second bubble asks for a photo.] "A photo? No. We do not send photos to people on a screen."

"Five things we never give: our address, our name, our school, a photo — and we never go to meet an online friend."

[The mascot points up.] "What do we do? We tell a grown-up. Telling keeps us safe."
