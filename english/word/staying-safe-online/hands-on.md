# Hands-on — Staying Safe Online

1. **Spot the ask.** A partner reads out friendly messages — some ask for one of the five things, some are ordinary chat. For each one, say "safe" or "that's an ask!" and name which of the five it is.

2. **The safe answer.** Act it out: one learner is a website asking for an address, a photo, or a meeting; the other must answer with the safe answer and mime showing the message to a grown-up.
