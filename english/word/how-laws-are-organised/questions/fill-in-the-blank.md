## Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word from the box. The box holds one word for each gap.

**Box:** constitution, treaties, decrees, pyramid

1. The rules stack up like a ______. **Answer:** pyramid (Sub lesson 1)
2. The ______ sits at the very top. **Answer:** constitution (Sub lesson 1)
3. International ______ are promises a country signs with other countries. **Answer:** treaties (Sub lesson 1)
