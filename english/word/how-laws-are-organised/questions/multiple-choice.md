## Multiple choice

1. **What stands at the very top of Kelsen's pyramid?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A decree
   - b) The Constitution
   - c) An administrative act
   - **Answer: b) The Constitution**

2. **What comes directly below the Constitution?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) International treaties
   - b) Administrative acts
   - c) School rules
   - **Answer: a) International treaties**

3. **National law is made by…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) One official at a desk
   - b) The country's parliament
   - c) Another country
   - **Answer: b) The country's parliament**

4. **A decree disagrees with a law. Which one wins?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) The decree
   - b) The law
   - c) Neither — they are equal
   - **Answer: b) The law**

5. **Where do administrative acts sit on the pyramid?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) At the top
   - b) In the middle
   - c) At the bottom
   - **Answer: c) At the bottom**
