## Short answer

1. **Name the five steps of Kelsen's pyramid, from the top.** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Constitution, international treaties, national law, decrees, administrative acts.
2. **A law disagrees with the Constitution. Which wins?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** The Constitution.
3. **Why does the pyramid's order matter?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** So that the rights and rules at the top cannot be beaten by the everyday rules at the bottom.
