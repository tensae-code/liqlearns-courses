# Levels — How Laws Are Organised

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it teaches one fixed diagram — Kelsen's pyramid — and then the single rule the diagram exists to teach. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — The top of the pyramid | The pyramid's top three steps, in order. |
| 2 — The lower steps — and why the order matters | The two lower steps, and the rule that a lower rule loses to a higher one. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
