# Video script — How Laws Are Organised

[The mascot faces the camera. Beside it, five flat stones are stacked into a pyramid, the biggest at the bottom.]

"A country has thousands of rules."
"They are not all equal."
"They stack up, like a pyramid."
"The Constitution sits at the top."
"Every rule below must obey the rules above."
"If two rules fight, the higher one wins."
"Can you build the pyramid?"
