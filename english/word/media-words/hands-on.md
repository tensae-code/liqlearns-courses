# Hands-on — Media Words

Words stick when you use them on the real thing, however small.

## Sort a real day of television

Take a TV guide, a channel page or a TV app. List the programmes you find and sort them into the eight kinds: game show, quiz show, current affairs, cookery, talent show, chat show, wildlife documentary, lifestyle show. Count them — which kind does your television show most?

## Your media in three boxes

On paper, draw the three channels: newspapers and magazines / television and radio / computers and the internet. Write each media word in its box, then add one true sentence about yourself in each box, using the collocations: read an article, watch an advert, listen to a programme, go on a social networking site, upload a video.
