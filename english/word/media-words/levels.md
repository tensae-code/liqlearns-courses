# Levels — Media Words

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson (restructure
2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching order. The old
layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below are the lesson's own
parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Media words by channel | The media set sorted by the book's three channels: newspapers and magazines (article, front page, headline, journalist, advert), television and radio (channel, programme, presenter, celebrity, drama, video), computers and the internet (blog, email, homepage, social networking site, spam) — with the verb collocations (read an article, watch an advert, write a blog/an email, listen to a programme, use/go on a social networking site, upload a video) and true-statement writing about your own media use. |
| Sub lesson 2 — TV programme kinds | The programme genres: game shows, quiz shows, current affairs programmes, cookery programmes, talent shows, chat shows, wildlife documentaries, lifestyle shows — naming the kind of a programme and your own favourite kind. |

Pass mark: the source states no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
