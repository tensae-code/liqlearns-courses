## Fill in the blank

1. The first page of a newspaper is the ___ page. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: front** — the front page carries the headline.

2. You read an ___ in a magazine. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: article** — articles are read; programmes are watched or listened to.

3. The person who runs a show and speaks to the guests is the ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: presenter** — the presenter presents the programme.

4. She writes her pieces on her own page on the internet — she writes a ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: blog** — a blog is a person's own writing page online.

5. In a ___ show, singers and dancers perform and the best one wins. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: talent** — a talent show shows people's talents.

6. A ___ programme teaches you how to cook a dish. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: cookery** — cookery programmes are about cooking.
