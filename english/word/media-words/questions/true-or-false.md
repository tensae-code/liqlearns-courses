## True or false

1. A headline is the big title on the front page of a newspaper. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the headline sits at the top of the front page.

2. Spam is an email from a good friend. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — spam is email you never asked for.

3. You listen to a programme on the radio. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the book's collocation is "listen to" with programmes.

4. A celebrity is a famous person. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — celebrities are the famous people in the media.

5. In a cookery programme, people answer questions to win prizes. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — that is a quiz show; a cookery programme shows cooking.

6. A wildlife documentary is a true film about wild animals. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — documentaries are true films; this kind is about wildlife.
