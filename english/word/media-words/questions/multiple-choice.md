## Multiple choice

1. A piece of writing in a newspaper or magazine is an… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) article  b) advert  c) channel
   **Answer: a) article** — an article is the written piece; an advert tries to sell something.

2. The big title on the front page of a newspaper is the… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) spam  b) headline  c) homepage
   **Answer: b) headline** — the headline is the big title on the front page.

3. The person who writes the reports for a newspaper is a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) presenter  b) celebrity  c) journalist
   **Answer: c) journalist** — a journalist writes reports; a presenter runs a show.

4. Email you never asked for is called… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) spam  b) a blog  c) a drama
   **Answer: a) spam** — spam is junk email.

5. To put a video up for others to see is to… it. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) read  b) upload  c) watch
   **Answer: b) upload** — you upload a video; then others can watch it.

6. A programme where people answer questions to win a prize is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) cookery programme  b) quiz show  c) chat show
   **Answer: b) quiz show** — quiz shows ask questions; game shows play games.

7. A true film about wild animals is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) wildlife documentary  b) talent show  c) lifestyle show
   **Answer: a) wildlife documentary** — a documentary is a true film; this one is about wildlife.

8. A programme about the news and the big questions of today is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) game show  b) drama  c) current affairs programme
   **Answer: c) current affairs programme** — current affairs covers today's news and questions.
