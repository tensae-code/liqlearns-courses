## Short answer

1. Which verb goes with "an article" — read, watch or upload? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Read.** — you read an article.

2. What do we call a letter sent by computer? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: An email.** — email is the computer letter.

3. Name one thing you can do on a social networking site, using the book's verbs. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Use it / go on it (and meet people and share there).** — the book's collocations are "use" and "go on" a social networking site.

4. In which kind of programme does a presenter talk with guests? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: A chat show.** — the talking-with-guests kind is the chat show.

5. Name two kinds of TV programme where people can win something. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: A game show and a quiz show (a talent show also chooses a winner).** — game shows play games, quiz shows answer questions.
