# Multiple choice questions — Citizens' Rights and the Constitution

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).
The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions —
no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Multiple choice

1. **Which document names Uruguay's five basic rights?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) A school rule book
   - b) The Constitution, Article 7
   - c) A bus timetable
   - **Answer: b) The Constitution, Article 7**

2. **Which of these is one of the five rights of Article 7?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Honour
   - b) Holidays
   - c) Homework
   - **Answer: a) Honour**

3. **The rights section of Uruguay's Constitution runs from Article 7 to which article?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Article 20
   - b) Article 50
   - c) Article 72
   - **Answer: c) Article 72**

4. **Human Rights Day, December 10, is one of the milestones in which book's research task?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) The Grade 9 book
   - b) The Grade 11 book
   - c) The Grade 7 book
   - **Answer: b) The Grade 11 book**

5. **“Justice delayed is justice denied” is used in the books as…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) A debate motion
   - b) A song title
   - c) A spelling rule
   - **Answer: a) A debate motion**
