## Short answer

1. **Name the five rights of Article 7.** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Life, freedom, honour, property and work.
2. **Which article of Uruguay's Constitution names the five rights?** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** Article 7.
3. **In the debate motion “Justice delayed is justice denied”, what does the defending team argue?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** That a right which arrives too late is a right refused.
