# Levels — Citizens' Rights and the Constitution

This lesson is built at one level of its own: it teaches a fixed, named list (Article 7's five rights) and then the record and argument built on it. It is layered by sub lesson, not by the course's old four broad levels.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1 — The rights the constitution gives you | The five rights of Article 7, heard and named. |
| 2 — Rights in Uruguay's history and the world | The milestones, the debate and the world comparison. |

There is no pass mark written in this file: mastery is shown in the lesson's own prove-it checks (at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson just finished, mixed with earlier parts). Sub lesson 2 opens only when the check on sub lesson 1 is passed; a failed check means review, then a fresh set — never a relock.
