# Video script — The Five Senses

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot points to its eyes, ears, nose, mouth, and paws, one by one.]

"Eyes — I see! Ears — I hear! Nose — I smell! Tongue — I taste! Skin — I feel! Five senses!"

[A lemon appears.] "What does it taste like? Hmm — sour! What does it look like? Yellow!"

"Tell me: how does the world come to you? Through five doors!"
