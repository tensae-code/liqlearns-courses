# Hands-on — The Five Senses

1. **One sense at a time.** Blindfold one learner (with their agreement). Hand them things to name by touch alone, then by smell alone, then by taste alone (only safe, known foods). The class says which sense did the work each time.

2. **Sense walk.** Walk a short way together and stop three times. At each stop, each learner says one thing for a different sense: I see…, I hear…, I smell….
