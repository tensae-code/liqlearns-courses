## True or false

1. There are five senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. You smell with your ears. (Sub lesson 1) **False — you smell with your nose; the ears are for hearing.**

3. "It tastes sweet" answers the question "What does it taste like?". (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. A thing can only be described through one sense. (Sub lesson 2) **False — describing a thing through all five senses describes it whole.**
