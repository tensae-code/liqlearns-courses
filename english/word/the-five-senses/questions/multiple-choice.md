## Multiple choice

1. You taste with your… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) ears  b) tongue  c) skin
   **Answer: b).**

2. How many senses are there? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) three  b) four  c) five
   **Answer: c).**

3. "What does the soup taste like?" — a true answer is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) It tastes salty.  b) It looks at me.  c) It hears well.
   **Answer: a).**

4. The stone is cold and smooth — which sense reports this? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) smell  b) touch — it feels cold and smooth  c) sight
   **Answer: b).**
