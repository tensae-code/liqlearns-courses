## Short answer

1. Name the five senses and the body part of each. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), taste (tongue), touch (skin).**

2. Ask what a lemon tastes like, and answer truly. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: "What does it taste like? — It tastes sour."**

3. Which Eritrea book and unit teach the five senses? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the Grade 4 book, Unit 5.**
