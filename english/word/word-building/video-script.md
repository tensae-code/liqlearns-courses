# Video script — Word building

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome to the word workshop! We BUILD words here.

This word is **HAPPY**. Happy is the **root** — the meaning
lives here.
Put **UN-** in front… **UNHAPPY**! Not happy!
A front part is a **prefix**.

Put **-NESS** at the back… **HAPPINESS**!
A back part is a **suffix**.

**RE-**read — read again!
**CARE-ful** — full of care!
**CARE-less** — no care at all!

Clap the beats: tea-cher — two!
Un-hap-pi-ness — four!

Front part, root, back part. You are a builder now.
Take a word apart. Build a new one.
That is word building — in English.
