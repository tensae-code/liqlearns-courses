# Hands-on — Word building

The lesson with word parts in your hands, not only on the
page.

## Blocks on the table
Write roots on cards — HAPPY, CARE, READ, HELP, TEACH — and
parts on smaller cards: UN-, RE-, DIS-, -FUL, -LESS,
-NESS, -ER. Slide parts onto roots and read every word you
build aloud. A build counts only if it is a true word and
you can say what it means.

## The chin count
Hand under chin. Say the lesson's words slowly — happy,
teacher, unhappy, happiness, understand — and count the
chin drops. Then clap the same words. The drops and the
claps must agree: those are the syllables.

## Break it to read it
Take three long words from any book you are reading. For
each: circle the prefix, underline the root, box the
suffix, clap the beats, then say the whole word. Long words
are built words — that is the whole trick.

## Opposite machine
A partner says a word: fair! kind! honest! You answer with
its built opposite — unfair, unkind, dishonest — before a
count of five. Barbados's own pair (un-fair → unfair)
starts the game.
