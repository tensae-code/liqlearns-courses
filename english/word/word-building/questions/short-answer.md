# Word building — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Take "careless" apart: root and suffix.
**Answer:** care (root) + -less (suffix).

**2. (Level 2)** Build a word meaning "read again" with
re-.
**Answer:** reread (Cambodia's set).

**3. (Level 2)** Name three suffixes from Cambodia's list.
**Answer:** any three of -ful, -ment, -ian, -or.

**4. (Level 3)** Guess from its parts: what does "fearless"
mean?
**Answer:** with no fear — fear (root) + -less (without).
