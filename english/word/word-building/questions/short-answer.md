# Word building — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Parts of a Word · (Level
2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Building with the Sets · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 —
Syllables and New Words.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Take "careless" apart: root and suffix.
**Answer:** care (root) + -less (suffix).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Build a word meaning "read again" with
re-.
**Answer:** reread (Cambodia's set).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three suffixes from Cambodia's list.
**Answer:** any three of -ful, -ment, -ian, -or.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Guess from its parts: what does "fearless"
mean?
**Answer:** with no fear — fear (root) + -less (without).
