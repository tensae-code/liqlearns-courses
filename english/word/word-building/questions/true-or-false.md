# Word building — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A suffix stands at the front of a word.
**False** — a suffix stands at the BACK. The front part is
the prefix.

**2. (Level 1)** Un- can stand alone as a word.
**False** — un- is a part, not a word; it must join a root
(unfair, unhappy). Whole words that join are compounds —
the next lesson.

**3. (Level 2)** Malaysia's Year 6 evidence for -tion/-sion
comes from its Scheme of Work.
**True** — mapped evidence: the Scheme of Work plans the
lessons; it is not a pupil's-book read.

**4. (Level 3)** "Unhappiness" has four syllables.
**True** — un-hap-pi-ness, counted by claps (Nepal's
counting work).
