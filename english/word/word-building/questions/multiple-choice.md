# Word building — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Parts of a Word · (Level
2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Building with the Sets · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 —
Syllables and New Words.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In UNHAPPY, the part UN- is a ______.
a) suffix  b) prefix  c) root
**Answer: b) prefix** — it stands at the front (Barbados,
Class 1 syllabus).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The root of a word carries its ______.
a) beats  b) main meaning  c) last letter
**Answer: b) main meaning** — Barbados's Class 2 syllabus
calls the root the "'meaning' unit."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** RE- in reread means ______.
a) not  b) wrongly  c) again
**Answer: c) again** — Cambodia's prefix set (Grade 12,
Unit 5).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Teach + -er makes a word of a new kind:
teacher is a ______.
a) verb  b) noun  c) adjective
**Answer: b) noun** — a suffix can change the kind; teach
is the verb, teacher the person-noun.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How many syllables in "happiness"?
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer: b) 3** — hap-pi-ness, counted by clapping, the
way Nepal's Grade 8 work counts them.
