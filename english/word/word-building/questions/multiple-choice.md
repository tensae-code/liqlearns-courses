# Word building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In UNHAPPY, the part UN- is a ______.
a) suffix  b) prefix  c) root
**Answer: b) prefix** — it stands at the front (Barbados,
Class 1 syllabus).

**2. (Level 1)** The root of a word carries its ______.
a) beats  b) main meaning  c) last letter
**Answer: b) main meaning** — Barbados's Class 2 syllabus
calls the root the "'meaning' unit."

**3. (Level 2)** RE- in reread means ______.
a) not  b) wrongly  c) again
**Answer: c) again** — Cambodia's prefix set (Grade 12,
Unit 5).

**4. (Level 2)** Teach + -er makes a word of a new kind:
teacher is a ______.
a) verb  b) noun  c) adjective
**Answer: b) noun** — a suffix can change the kind; teach
is the verb, teacher the person-noun.

**5. (Level 3)** How many syllables in "happiness"?
a) 2  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer: b) 3** — hap-pi-ness, counted by clapping, the
way Nepal's Grade 8 work counts them.
