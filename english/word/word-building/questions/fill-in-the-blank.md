# Word building — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** un- · -less · mis- · -ment · root

**1. (Level 2)** ______happy means not happy.
**Answer: un-** (Barbados's pair shape: un-fair → unfair).

**2. (Level 2)** Care______ means with no care at all.
**Answer: -less** — South Africa's circled set (un-, re-,
-ful, -less, -ness).

**3. (Level 2)** ______understand means understand wrongly.
**Answer: mis-** (Cambodia's prefix set).

**4. (Level 2)** Agree______ is the act of agreeing.
**Answer: -ment** (Cambodia's suffix set).

**5. (Level 1)** The ______ is the middle part that carries
the meaning.
**Answer: root.**
