# Word building — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Three Parts of a Word · (Level
2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Building with the Sets.

Fill each blank with a word from the box.

**Box:** un- · -less · mis- · -ment · root

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** ______happy means not happy.
**Answer: un-** (Barbados's pair shape: un-fair → unfair).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Care______ means with no care at all.
**Answer: -less** — South Africa's circled set (un-, re-,
-ful, -less, -ness).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______understand means understand wrongly.
**Answer: mis-** (Cambodia's prefix set).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agree______ is the act of agreeing.
**Answer: -ment** (Cambodia's suffix set).

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ is the middle part that carries
the meaning.
**Answer: root.**
