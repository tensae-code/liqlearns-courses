# Word building — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the
sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub
lessons as: (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Building with the Sets.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 2)** — Cambodia's prefixes and what they do
(Grade 12, Unit 5).
1. re- — **again** (reread)
2. un- — **not** (unhappy)
3. dis- — **not / the opposite** (dishonest)
4. mis- — **wrongly** (misunderstand)
5. pre- — **before** (pre-read)
6. under- — **too little** (undercooked)

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — suffixes and the words they build.
1. -ful — **careful: full of care**
2. -less — **careless: with no care**
3. -ness — **happiness: the state of being happy**
4. -er — **teacher: the person who teaches**
5. -ment — **agreement: the act of agreeing**
