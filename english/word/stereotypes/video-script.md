# Video script — Stereotypes

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Speech bubbles appear beside the mascot: "Girls can't…" "Boys don't…" "Old people can't…"]

"You have heard words like these. All girls. All boys. All old people."

"That fixed idea about a whole group has a name: a stereotype."

[The bubbles fade; one person stands in front of him.] "But look — here is one person. She is herself, not an idea."

"Let people show you who they are. Do not let a fixed belief answer first."
