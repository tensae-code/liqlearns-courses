## True or false

1. Everyone in a group is the same. (Sub lesson 1) **False — that sameness is the stereotype's claim, and it is not true.**

2. A stereotype can stop you from seeing the real person in front of you. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. One different person is enough to break an "all of them" claim. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. Stereotypes are only about girls and boys. (Sub lesson 1) **False — they can be about any group: ages, places, peoples, jobs.**
