## Short answer

1. Say what a stereotype is, in your own words. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a fixed belief that everyone in a group is the same.**

2. Give one stereotype and the real person or fact that breaks it. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any named stereotype with one true counterexample.**
