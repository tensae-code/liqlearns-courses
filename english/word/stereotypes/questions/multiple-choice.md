## Multiple choice

1. A stereotype is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a kind of story  b) a fixed belief that everyone in a group is the same  c) a true fact about one person
   **Answer: b).**

2. "Fixed" means the belief… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) does not move, even when the person in front of you is different  b) is always true  c) is new
   **Answer: a).**

3. A claim about ALL of a group falls when… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) it is said loudly  b) one person in the group is different  c) it is written down
   **Answer: b).**

4. The honest way to meet a person is to… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) let a fixed belief answer first  b) let the person show you who they are  c) ask their group
   **Answer: b).**

5. "Girls can't fix cars" is an example of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a stereotype  b) a riddle  c) a report
   **Answer: a).**
