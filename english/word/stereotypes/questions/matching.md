## Matching

Match each sentence to what it is. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. "All old people can't learn new things."
2. "My grandmother is learning a new language."
3. "Boys don't cry."
4. "This girl fixed our car."
5. "Let people show you who they are."

a) a real person breaking a claim about girls
b) the cure for a stereotype
c) a stereotype about old people
d) a real person breaking a claim about old people
e) a stereotype about boys

**Answers:** 1–c, 2–d, 3–e, 4–a, 5–b.
