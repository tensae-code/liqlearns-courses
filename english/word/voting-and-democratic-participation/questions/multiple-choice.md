# Multiple choice questions — Voting and Democratic Participation

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).
The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions —
no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Multiple choice

1. **Which three words sit around “voting” in the Grade 9 book's bubble map?** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Government, legislature, elections
   - b) King, queen, castle
   - c) School, home, park
   - **Answer: a) Government, legislature, elections**

2. **In Uruguay, voting is…** (Sub lesson 1)
   - a) Only for landowners
   - b) A right and a duty
   - c) Forbidden for students
   - **Answer: b) A right and a duty**

3. **Democracy began in…** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Ancient Greece
   - b) Uruguay
   - c) The space age
   - **Answer: a) Ancient Greece**

4. **Uruguay elects its leaders for terms of how many years?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) Two years
   - b) Four years
   - c) Five years
   - **Answer: c) Five years**

5. **In a dictatorship, who holds the power?** (Sub lesson 2)
   - a) The voters
   - b) One ruler
   - c) A parliament
   - **Answer: b) One ruler**
