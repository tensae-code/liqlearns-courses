## Matching

Match each system to who holds the power. (Sub lesson 2)
1. Democracy → the people, through their votes
2. Monarchy → a king or queen
3. Constitutional monarchy → a monarch sharing the country with a constitution
4. Dictatorship → one ruler, and votes do not choose

Match each bubble-map word to its meaning. (Sub lesson 1)
1. Government → the body that runs the country
2. Legislature → the body the winners sit in
3. Elections → the counting of all the votes
