# Short answer questions — Voting and Democratic Participation

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N).

no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

## Short answer

1. **What is a vote?** (Sub lesson 1) — **Answer:** One person's counted choice of who should govern.
2. **Where did democracy begin, and what does its name mean?** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** It began in ancient Greece; the name means rule by the people.
3. **Give one discussion question the Grade 12 book asks about voting.** (Sub lesson 2) — **Answer:** Should voting be mandatory? (Also accepted: why some young people are not interested in voting; what about gender quotas.)
