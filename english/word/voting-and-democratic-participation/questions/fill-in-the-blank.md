## Fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word from the box. The box holds one word for each gap.

**Box:** elections, legislature, democracy, dictatorship

1. The counting of all the votes is called ______. **Answer:** elections (Sub lesson 1)
2. The winners of an election go to sit in the ______. **Answer:** legislature (Sub lesson 1)
3. Rule by the people is called ______. **Answer:** democracy (Sub lesson 2)
4. Rule by one person who holds all the power is a ______. **Answer:** dictatorship (Sub lesson 2)
