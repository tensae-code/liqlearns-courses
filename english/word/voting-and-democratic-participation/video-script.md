# Video script — Voting and Democratic Participation

[The mascot faces the camera. Beside it stands a clear box with a slot in the top, and a paper card in the mascot's paw.]

"This card is a vote."
"One person. One card. One choice."
"Millions of cards choose a government."
"That is an election."
"But who may vote? How often? And what if you don't?"
"Today we find out."
