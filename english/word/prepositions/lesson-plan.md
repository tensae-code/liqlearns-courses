# Lesson Plan — Prepositions

Unit 6 — Word Kinds. Planned shape: four sub lessons — first
what a preposition does (it ties a word to the rest of the
sentence and tells position), then place and direction, then
time (days and dates), and last the many meanings of the busy
prepositions (by, for, about, between, against) used inside
whole sentences. The lesson is built from Ghana's NaCCA
curricula, which run a prepositions strand from Basic 1 to
Basic 6 — position at Basic 1, days and dates at Basic 2,
directions at Basic 3, and "a variety of meanings" at Basics
4–6 — with the Upper Primary Level 3 Distance Learning guide
teaching by, for, about, between and against by name. The
course's four specific preposition lessons
(prepositions-in-and-into, prepositions-of-movement,
prepositions-of-place, prepositions-of-time) hang off this
one: this is the general lesson they stand on.

## Sub lessons

1. **What prepositions do** — a preposition ties a word to the
   rest of the sentence; the position family: in, on, under,
   between.
2. **Place and direction** — saying where things are and which
   way they move.
3. **Time: days and dates** — prepositions with days, dates
   and times of day.
4. **Many meanings** — by, for, about, against and the rest,
   each carrying more than one meaning, used in whole
   sentences.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
