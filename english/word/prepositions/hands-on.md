# Hands-On — Prepositions

Things to do away from the page.

- **Hide and tell.** Hide a small object and tell a partner
  where it is using only a preposition sentence ("It is under
  something soft"). They guess; then swap.
- **Day diary.** Write tomorrow's plan in three sentences —
  one with on and a day, one with in and a part of the day,
  one with at and a clock time.
