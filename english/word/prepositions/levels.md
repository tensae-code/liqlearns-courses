# Levels — Prepositions

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** use in, on, under and between to
  say where a thing is.
- **Climb:** add direction (to, into, over, across) and the
  time family (on for days and dates, in for months and parts
  of the day, at for clock times).
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** read the busy
  prepositions — by, for, about, between, against — with
  their several meanings, and choose the meaning the sentence
  is using.
