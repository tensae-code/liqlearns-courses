# Multiple Choice — Prepositions

Choose the one right answer.

1. What does a preposition do? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) It names a person or thing
   - B) It ties a word to the rest of the sentence, often telling where it is
   - C) It ends every sentence
   - D) It shows an action is finished
   **Answer: B**

2. "The books are ___ the box." Choose the preposition that means inside. (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) on
   - B) under
   - C) in
   - D) between
   **Answer: C**

3. Which sentence uses **between** correctly? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) The school is between the market and the river.
   - B) The school is between the market.
   - C) Between the school is the market.
   - D) The school between is the market the river.
   **Answer: A**

4. "The bird flew ___ the wall." The movement word that fits is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) over
   - B) in
   - C) at
   - D) of
   **Answer: A**

5. Which pair is right? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Place: into; direction: in
   - B) Place: in; direction: into
   - C) Both always mean movement
   - D) Neither tells place or movement
   **Answer: B**

6. "The market opens ___ Saturday." (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) in
   - B) at
   - C) on
   - D) under
   **Answer: C**

7. "School closes ___ December." (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) on
   - B) in
   - C) at
   - D) by
   **Answer: B**

8. "The lesson starts ___ nine o'clock." (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) on
   - B) in
   - C) at
   - D) between
   **Answer: C**

9. In "a story **by** Ama", the word by tells: (Sub lesson 4)
   - A) the place of the story
   - B) who made the story
   - C) the price of the story
   - D) the length of the story
   **Answer: B**

10. In "He leaned his bicycle **against** the fence", against tells: (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) a gift
    - B) a day of the week
    - C) touching and leaning on something
    - D) a number, nearly
    **Answer: C**
