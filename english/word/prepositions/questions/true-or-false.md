# True or False — Prepositions

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. A preposition can stand alone with no noun after it and still tie the sentence together. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a preposition needs the noun it ties to the sentence.

2. "On" is the preposition for days and dates: on Monday, on the fifth of May. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True**

3. "In" is used for clock times: in nine o'clock. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — clock times take at: at nine o'clock.

4. One preposition can carry more than one meaning. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — by alone can mean who did it, how, or beside.

5. About can mean nearly, as in "about twenty people". (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True**
