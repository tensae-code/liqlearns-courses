# Fill in the Blank — Prepositions

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. The cat is sleeping ____ the table. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: under**

2. Put the pen ____ the bag. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: in**

3. They ran ____ the field to the other side. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: across**

4. Come ____ the room, please — walk in. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: into**

5. We play football ____ school, when lessons are done. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: after**

6. This gift is ____ you. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: for**
