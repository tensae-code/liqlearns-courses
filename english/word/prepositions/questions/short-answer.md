# Short Answer — Prepositions

Answer in a few words.

1. Name the four position prepositions taught first in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** In, on, under and between.

2. What is the difference between in and into? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** In tells place (where a thing is); into tells movement towards the inside.

3. Which preposition goes with a day of the week? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** On — on Monday.

4. Which preposition goes with a month? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** In — in June.

5. Give two meanings of the preposition by. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** Any two of: who did it (a story by Ama); how (travel by bus); beside (sit by me).

6. Give two meanings of the preposition about. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** The subject (a book about farming); nearly (about twenty people).
