This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-08
re-verification sweep of Mongolia's English books. The sweep
re-read the held Grades 5–10 books and read English XI and
English XII page by page for the first time (690 teaching
items placed; 425 already in the course, 265 missing — the
whole gap was the two senior books) and marked this part NEW
LESSON NEEDED. The course's preposition lessons covered place,
time and movement only; no lesson housed the prepositions that
belong to a word itself. Under the everything-goes-in ruling
(2026-11-06) the part gets its own lesson.

Searchable tags: the books' own names for these parts are the
Unit 8 grammar section of *English XI* (prepositions after
adjectives) and the Unit 1 language section of *English XII*
(prepositional phrases) — kept here as tags, not as the
lesson's title, which names the thing itself.
