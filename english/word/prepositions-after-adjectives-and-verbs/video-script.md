# Video script — Prepositions After Adjectives and Verbs

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot kicks a ball into a goal.] "I am good AT football!
Good — AT!"

[Two very different coats side by side; the mascot points from
one to the other.] "Different FROM! This coat is different from
that coat."

[The mascot puts a hand to his ear, then points at a radio.] "I
listen TO music. Listen — TO!"
