## Short answer

1. Finish the sentence about yourself: "I am good at ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: any true skill in the pair "good at"** — e.g. "I am good at drawing."

2. What is your town or city famous for? Use the pair. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "… is famous for …"** with a true thing.

3. Name one thing you listen to, in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "I listen to …"** — the pair "listen to" must be whole.

4. Your school must choose a day for a trip. Say it with the noun pair. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "The school made a decision on the day"** — or any true sentence with "a decision on."
