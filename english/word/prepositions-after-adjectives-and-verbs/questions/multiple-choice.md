## Multiple choice

1. "She is good ___ singing." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) at  b) from  c) on
   **Answer: a) at** — for a skill, the pair is "good at."

2. "My town is famous ___ its lake." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) from  b) for  c) to
   **Answer: b) for** — the pair is "famous for."

3. "I listen ___ the radio in the morning." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) — (nothing)  b) at  c) to
   **Answer: c) to** — the pair is "listen to"; the "to" belongs to it.

4. "Running is good ___ your health." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) at  b) for  c) from
   **Answer: b) for** — for a benefit, the pair is "good for."

5. "They made a decision ___ the new road." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) on  b) at  c) from
   **Answer: a) on** — the noun "decision" carries "on": a decision on.

6. "Be careful — she is aware ___ the problem." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) at  b) of  c) to
   **Answer: b) of** — the pair is "aware of."
