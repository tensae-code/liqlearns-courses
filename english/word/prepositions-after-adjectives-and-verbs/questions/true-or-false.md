## True or false

1. "I am good at football" and "I am good for football" mean the same thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — "good at" is for a skill; "good for" is for a benefit.

2. "Listen music" is a correct sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the pair is "listen to": "Listen to music."

3. "Different from" is a pair learned as one thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the adjective "different" carries "from."

4. You can always guess a word's preposition from its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer: False** — the books present the pairs as things to learn whole; "good" alone takes at, for and to.
