## Fill in the blank

1. His bag is different ___ mine. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: from** — the pair is "different from."

2. She is good ___ her little brother — she always helps him. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: to** — for kindness, the pair is "good to."

3. Wear a hat and stay safe ___ the sun. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: from** — the pair is "safe from."

4. We listen ___ music after school. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: to** — "listen to" is one pair.

5. The school made a decision ___ the trip. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: on** — "a decision on" is the pair.
