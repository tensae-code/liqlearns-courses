# Hands-on — Prepositions After Adjectives and Verbs

Pairs are learned by using them about real people. Do these out
loud, with a partner.

## The good-at survey

Ask five people: "What are you good at?" Write each answer as a
full sentence: "Mina is good at singing." Then ask what is good
for them, and who is good to them — one sentence for each pair.

## Famous for

Stand with your partner and take turns: one names a place or a
person, the other must say what they are famous for, in a full
sentence with the pair whole: "Our town is famous for its
market."
