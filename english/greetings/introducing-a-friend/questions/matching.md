# Introducing a friend — matching

Matching questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
Join each line to who says it.

1. Who's this?            a) The introducer, about her friend
2. This is my friend, Sarah  b) The one meeting someone new
3. Hello, Sarah           c) The one asking about the new person

## Answers
