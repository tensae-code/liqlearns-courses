# How are you? — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: two friends meeting]

Hello!
Now a warm question.
How are you?

[Picture: the sun shining, a smiling child]

I'm very well, thanks!
That is a good day.

[Picture: a child with a small smile]

I'm fine.
That is a normal day.

[Picture: a child sitting down, tired]

I'm not very well.
That is a hard day.
It is okay to say it.

[Picture: the two friends, the question going back]

And how are you?
Now you ask it back.
Now you try it. Ask me: How are you?
