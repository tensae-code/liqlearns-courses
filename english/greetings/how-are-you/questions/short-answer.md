# How are you? — short answer

Short-answer questions only. Answer in a word or a short sentence.

## Level 1
1. Ask your companion how they are. Write the question.
2. Answer "How are you?" for a normal day.
3. Answer "How are you?" for a hard day, honestly.

## Level 2
1. Your friend answers "I'm fine." Ask the question back to them,
   the polite way.
