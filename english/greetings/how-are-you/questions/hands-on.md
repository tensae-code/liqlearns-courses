# How are you? — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: three real answers
You need: three people today (or your companion, three times).
1. Ask each person: "How are you?"
2. Write down the answer each one gives.
3. Count: how many said very well, how many fine, how many not
   very well? Real people, real days — that is the lesson landing.

## Project 2: teach your companion
1. Ask your companion "How are you?" and answer for them with the
   three answers, one by one.
2. Explain in your own words why the question is asked back.
