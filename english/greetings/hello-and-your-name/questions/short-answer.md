# Greetings — short answer

Short-answer questions only. Answer in a word or a short sentence —
a complete sentence where the lesson taught one.

## Level 1
1. What is your name? Answer with the whole sentence: "My name is ___."
2. Say the first word of a greeting — the word that opens it.
3. Say the word that closes a greeting when you leave.
4. What do you say right after you hear someone's name for the first time?

## Level 2
1. Greet your companion and introduce yourself in three sentences:
   open it, give your name, close it warmly.
2. Your friend says "I'm Adam!" — answer him the way Hana answered Maya.
