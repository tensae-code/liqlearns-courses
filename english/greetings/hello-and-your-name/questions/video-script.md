# Greetings — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: two children meeting at a school gate]

This is Maya.
This is Hana.
They meet.

[Picture: Maya waving]

Maya says: Hello!
Hello is the first word.
Hello opens the talk.

[Picture: the two children talking]

Hana says: What's your name?
Maya says: My name is Maya.
Hana says: I'm Hana. Nice to meet you!

[Picture: the two children shaking hands]

See their hands?
They shake hands.
Nice to meet you — and shake hands.

[Picture: Maya walking away, waving]

Now Maya goes.
She says: Goodbye!
Goodbye is the last word.
Goodbye closes the talk.

Hello opens. Goodbye closes.
Now you try it. Say: Hello!
