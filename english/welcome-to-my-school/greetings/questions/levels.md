# Greetings — questions by level

Practice sessions by level, tied to the learner's skill number
(starts at 100 and moves as they learn). The questions themselves live
in the type files (multiple-choice.md, short-answer.md,
fill-in-the-blank.md, true-or-false.md, matching.md) — this file is the
by-level practice view, it does not copy them.

## Level 1 — the basics
Run: all Level 1 questions from every type file, in this order —
multiple choice, matching, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, short answer.
Pass mark to open Level 2: the learner can do the full greeting shape
(open — names — close) out loud without help.

## Level 2 — use it with a person
Run: the Level 2 short-answer questions — a real greeting, spoken,
with a complete sentence for the name.
