# How old are you? — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: a birthday cake with 6 candles]

This is Sarah.
Sarah is six.
How old are you, Sarah?
I'm 6.

[Picture: the same child, a cake with 7 candles]

Now a year went by.
I'm 7.
The number grew. The sentence stayed.

[Picture: a boy holding up his hands]

This is Omar.
How old are you, Omar?
I'm 8.

[Picture: the words, big]

How old are you?
I'm ___.
Now you try it. How old are you?
