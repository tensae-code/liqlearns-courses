# Two-letter sounds — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. ch says one sound, not c and h separately. (True / False)
2. oo says the same sound in "spoon" and in "foot". (True / False)
3. q can travel alone in these books' English words. (True / False)

## Answers
1-True, 2-False (long in spoon, short in foot), 3-False (q travels with u)
