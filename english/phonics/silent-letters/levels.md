# Silent letters — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — letters seen but not said — set by
the pattern across the countries' books: the word itself first
(Ecuador's and India's picked-out words), then the family
(Kuwait's four spelling families), then reading whole lists clean
and catching the traps.

## Level 1 — the word
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what
a silent letter is, and read single words with the quiet
letter kept quiet — knife, comb, walk, hour, wrinkle —
the words Ecuador's cards and India's lists pick out.

## Level 2 — the family
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort
and read by family — kn-, wr-, -mb, -lk, -gh, silent h —
and say Kuwait's frame for each: a letter you see, but
you don't say.

## Level 3 — clean reading and the traps
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a
mixed list aloud with no sounded silent letter, pick
five words and use each in a spoken sentence (Ecuador's
closing task, printed p.41), and catch the trap:
knowledge belongs to the kn- family, not the silent-b
family, whatever the card it was printed on says.
