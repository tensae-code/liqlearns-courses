# Video script — Silent letters

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Look at this word. K-N-I-F-E.
Now listen. "Knife."
Where is the K? You can SEE it. You cannot HEAR it.
The K is quiet. It says nothing.

[Picture: the word KNIFE, the K in pale grey]

Some letters are quiet like that.
A letter you see, but you don't say.

K is quiet at the start. Knife. Knight. Knee.
B is quiet at the end. Comb. Lamb. Thumb.
Say "comb" — no B!
L is quiet in walk, talk, chalk.
G and H are quiet in bright, through, night.
H is quiet in hour. Honest.
W is quiet in write. Wrong. Wrinkle.

[Picture: five word cards, one grey letter on each]

Careful! One trap.
KNOWLEDGE. It starts with K.
The K is quiet — not the B. There is no B!

Now you try.
Read the cards. Keep the quiet letters quiet.
Knife. Comb. Walk. Bright. Hour.
Good. Your eyes see all the letters.
Your mouth skips the quiet ones.
