# Silent letters — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or letter.

**1. (Level 1)** Kuwait's frame says: "A letter you ______, but you don't ______."
**Answer: see; say** — the rhyme-frame that opens every silent-letter family in Skyline 5B.

**2. (Level 1)** In "hour" and "honest", the silent letter is ______.
**Answer: h** — Ecuador's Silent 'H' cards, printed pp.38–39; India's h- group adds honour and heir.

**3. (Level 2)** The words through, bright and bought all hide the silent pair ______.
**Answer: gh** — Kuwait's -gh family, Skyline 5B, Unit 8.

**4. (Level 3)** "Wrinkle" is said without its first letter: the ______ is silent, as India's Class 3 book states.
**Answer: w** — wr- says only the r: wrinkle, write, wrong, wreck.
