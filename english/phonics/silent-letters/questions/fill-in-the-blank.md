# Silent letters — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a silent letter is, and Sub lesson 7 — Silent h and the rest (split item by item) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 6 — The -gh family · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The wr- family.

Fill each gap with the right word or letter.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait's frame says: "A letter you ______, but you don't ______."
**Answer: see; say** — the rhyme-frame that opens every silent-letter family in Skyline 5B.

**2. (Sub lesson 7)** In "hour" and "honest", the silent letter is ______.
**Answer: h** — Ecuador's Silent 'H' cards, printed pp.38–39; India's h- group adds honour and heir.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** The words through, bright and bought all hide the silent pair ______.
**Answer: gh** — Kuwait's -gh family, Skyline 5B, Unit 8.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Wrinkle" is said without its first letter: the ______ is silent, as India's Class 3 book states.
**Answer: w** — wr- says only the r: wrinkle, write, wrong, wreck.
