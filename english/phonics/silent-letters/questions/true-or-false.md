# Silent letters — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — The kn- family (item 1's knight also carries the silent gh of Sub lesson 6) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 5 — The -lk family, and Sub lesson 6 — The -gh family (split item by item) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The -mb family (the knowledge trap).

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In "knight", you pronounce the k, the g and the h.
**Answer: False** — none of the three is said: the k is silent (kn-) and the gh is silent; the word is said "night" (Ecuador, printed pp.35–36).

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** In "walk, talk, chalk", the l is silent.
**Answer: True** — Kuwait's -lk family (Skyline 5B, Unit 7); India states the same for talk in Class 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Every word with the letters gh says the gh aloud.
**Answer: False** — in bright, through, bought, night, high and straight the gh is written but not said (Kuwait, Unit 8; India's -gh- group).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ecuador's Worksheet 6 files "knowledge" under silent B, and that filing is right.
**Answer: False** — the word contains no b; its silent letter is the initial k, so it belongs to the kn- family. The book's slip is recorded in the sources and taught corrected.
