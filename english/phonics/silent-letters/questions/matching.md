# Silent letters — matching

Match each word to the letter that stays quiet in it, the way the books group them.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: knee — a) silent w  b) silent k  c) silent b
**Answer: b) silent k** — the kn- family: knee, knife, knot, know (Ecuador, p.36; India, Class 5 grouping).

**2. (Level 1)** Match: thumb — a) silent h  b) silent l  c) silent b
**Answer: c) silent b** — the -mb family: thumb, comb, lamb, climb (Kuwait, Unit 6; Ecuador, p.37).

**3. (Level 2)** Match: write — a) silent w  b) silent t  c) silent gh
**Answer: a) silent w** — the wr- family: write, wrist, wrinkle, wrong (Ecuador, pp.39–40).

**4. (Level 2)** Match: chalk — a) silent k  b) silent l  c) silent c
**Answer: b) silent l** — the -lk family: walk, talk, chalk (Kuwait, Unit 7).

**5. (Level 3)** Match: neighbour — a) silent b  b) silent gh  c) silent w
**Answer: b) silent gh** — India's -gh- group: neighbour, daughter, fight, straight (Class 5 filing).
