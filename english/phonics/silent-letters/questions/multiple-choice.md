# Silent letters — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — What a silent letter is, and Sub lesson 2 — The kn- family (split item by item) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — The -mb family, and Sub lesson 5 — The -lk family (split item by item) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The -mb family (the knowledge trap is taught corrected inside the -mb part).

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A silent letter is —
a) a letter missing from the word  b) a letter you can see in the word but do not pronounce  c) a letter at the end of every word
**Answer: b) a letter you can see in the word but do not pronounce** — Ecuador's definition, Worksheet 6, printed p.35.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In "knife", the silent letter is —
a) n  b) f  c) k
**Answer: c) k** — kn- at the start of a word says only the n (Ecuador, printed p.36).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Which word hides its silent letter at the END, after m?
a) knee  b) lamb  c) write
**Answer: b) lamb** — the -mb family: lamb, climb, thumb (Kuwait, Skyline 5B, Unit 6).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** "Walk, talk, chalk" — the silent letter in all three is —
a) a  b) k  c) l
**Answer: c) l** — Kuwait's -lk family (Unit 7); India's Class 3 book states the same fact for talk.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** "Knowledge" belongs to which family?
a) silent b  b) kn- (silent k)  c) silent h
**Answer: b) kn- (silent k)** — the word has no b at all; Ecuador's card files it under B by mistake, and this lesson teaches it corrected.
