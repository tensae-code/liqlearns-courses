## Multiple choice

1. A sight word is a word you… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) sound out letter by letter every time  b) know whole, at a glance  c) skip when reading
   **Answer: b).**

2. Which of these is in the Grade 1 book's FIRST sight-word set? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) book  b) where  c) they
   **Answer: a).**

3. Which word cannot be sounded out in the usual letter way, so it must be learned by sight? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cat  b) the  c) run
   **Answer: b).**

4. In Eritrea's Grade 2 book, the sight-word sets… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) stop after one set  b) keep coming through the book  c) are only for teachers
   **Answer: b).**
