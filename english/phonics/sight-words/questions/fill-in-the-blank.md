## Fill in the blank

1. Words you know whole, at a glance, are called ______ words. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: sight.**

2. The first set in the Grade 1 book is: book, and, you, ______, little. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: can.**

3. "______ and help me, please." — one word from the second set opens the sentence. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: Come.**

4. The fifth Grade 1 set is: to, what, ______, make, look. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: where.**
