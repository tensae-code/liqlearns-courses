## Short answer

1. Why are some words learned by sight instead of by sounding out? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: they come up on every page, and some of them (like the, what, come) do not follow the letter sounds, so sounding them out does not work.**

2. Name the five words of the Grade 1 book's first sight-word set. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: book, and, you, can, little.**

3. How does the Grade 1 book work each set? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the words are read, matched and written — met as whole words, not sounded out.**
