## True or false

1. Every word in English can be read by sounding out its letters. (Sub lesson 1) **False — words like the and what must be learned whole, by sight.**

2. Eritrea's Grade 1 book teaches sight words in sets, with reading, matching and writing. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. Once you finish the Grade 1 sets, no book asks for more sight words. (Sub lesson 2) **False — the Grade 2 book keeps adding sets through the year.**

4. A sight word is learned when you can take it in stride inside a sentence without stopping. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
