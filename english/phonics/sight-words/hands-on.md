# Hands-on — Sight Words

1. **Flash and read.** Write the lesson's sight words on cards. Show one for a single breath — the learner reads it whole or puts it on the practice pile. No sounding out allowed; that is the game.

2. **Hunt them.** Open any page the learner can read. The learner finds and touches every sight word from this lesson on the page, saying each one as it is found.
