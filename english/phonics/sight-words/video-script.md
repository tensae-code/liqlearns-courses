# Video script — Sight Words

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The word THE in big letters.]

"Look: THE. Can we sound it out? T… h… e… No! This word we just KNOW."

[The mascot points at the word, eyes wide.] "See it. Say it. THE. Words like this are sight words. We learn them whole — like a face we know!"

[Five little word cards fan out: book, and, you, can, little.] "Book. And. You. Can. Little. Now we can read: I can go! You can come!"
