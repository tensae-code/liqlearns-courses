# Levels — Songs

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** listen to a song attentively —
  hear its words, follow its build, and sing it with
  understanding.
- **Climb:** find the line that returns (the chorus) and hear
  how the song is put together across more than one listening.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** name a song's
  values and state its central message in one sentence,
  judged from the whole song.
