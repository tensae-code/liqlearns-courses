# Lesson Plan — Songs

Unit 3 — Sounds Together. Planned shape: two sub lessons —
first listening to songs (hearing a song attentively and
singing it with understanding), then what songs carry (the
values and central messages inside a song). The lesson is
built from Ghana's NaCCA Basic 4–6 curriculum, whose
oral-language strand gives Songs its own standard at Basic 4
("Identify and discuss values in songs") and Basic 5
("explain the central messages in songs"). It stands beside
the rhymes lesson in this unit: rhymes teaches the little
poems; this lesson teaches the songs — longer, sung whole,
and carrying a message.

## Sub lessons

1. **Listening to songs** — hear a song attentively; sing it
   with understanding, not by noise alone.
2. **What songs carry** — the values in a song and its
   central message: what the song teaches and what it wants
   you to feel and do.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
