# Fill in the Blank — Songs

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. The tune carries the words; the words carry the ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: meaning** (also accepted: message)

2. The line of a song that returns again and again is the ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: chorus**

3. Singing with ____ means knowing what the lines you sing are saying. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: understanding**

4. The good things a song praises are its ____. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: values**

5. A song's central ____ is the one big thing it says. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: message**
