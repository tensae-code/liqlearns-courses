# Matching — Songs

Match each song description to what it carries.

1. Match the song to its value or message. (Sub lesson 2)
   - 1. A song praising a mother who cares for her children → the value of love and care
   - 2. A song warning that greed leaves a man friendless → the value of generosity, taught by its opposite
   - 3. A song about rising early to work the farm → the value of hard work
   - 4. A song whose chorus says "our home is worth protecting" → a central message about love of home
