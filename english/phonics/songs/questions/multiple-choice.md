# Multiple Choice — Songs

Choose the one right answer.

1. A song is not only a tune because: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) It is always long
   - B) Its words carry a meaning and a message
   - C) It is written in a book
   - D) It has a chorus
   **Answer: B**

2. Listening to a song attentively means: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Hearing the words, not only the tune
   - B) Singing louder than everyone
   - C) Listening once only
   - D) Ignoring the words
   **Answer: A**

3. The part of a song that returns again and again is: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) The title
   - B) The chorus
   - C) The last verse only
   - D) The drum beat
   **Answer: B**

4. Why listen to a song more than once? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) To make it longer
   - B) Because the second hearing catches what the first missed
   - C) Because once is forbidden
   - D) To change its words
   **Answer: B**

5. "Sing with understanding" means: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) Sing without knowing what the lines say
   - B) Know what the lines you sing are saying
   - C) Sing in another language only
   - D) Sing the tune with no words
   **Answer: B**

6. A song's values are: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Its price in the market
   - B) The good things it praises or the bad things it warns against
   - C) The number of verses
   - D) The instruments it uses
   **Answer: B**

7. A song's central message is: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Its loudest line
   - B) The one big thing it says — the whole song shrunk to a sentence
   - C) Its first word
   - D) The singer's name
   **Answer: B**

8. A song about a farmer who rises early and works steadily most likely praises: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) Sleeping late
   - B) Hard work and patience
   - C) Travelling
   - D) Rain
   **Answer: B**

9. To find a song's message you should judge by: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) One bright line only
   - B) The whole song
   - C) The tune alone
   - D) The singer's clothes
   **Answer: B**

10. A song that shows a liar ending alone teaches the value of: (Sub lesson 2)
    - A) Honesty, taught by its opposite
    - B) Farming
    - C) Speed
    - D) Silence
    **Answer: A**
