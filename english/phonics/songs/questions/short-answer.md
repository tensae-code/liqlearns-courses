# Short Answer — Songs

Answer in a few words.

1. What two things do you listen for in a song besides its tune? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Its words (what the lines say) and its build — what returns, what it is about.

2. Why should you not let a catchy tune carry careless words past you? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Because singing is saying: if you would not say the line in plain speech, the tune should not say it for you.

3. Name two values a song can carry. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: hard work, kindness, honesty, patience, love of home and country.

4. What three questions help you find a song's message? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** What does it praise? What does it warn against? If everyone did what it says, what would change?

5. State the central message of a song you know, in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any one-sentence statement that the whole of the named song supports.
