# True or False — Songs

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. If the tune is catchy, the words do not matter. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the words carry the meaning; understanding comes first.

2. The chorus usually holds the song's heart. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. One bright line is enough to judge a song's whole message. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — judge by the whole song.

4. A song can teach a value by showing its opposite, like a liar ending alone. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

5. Every song's central message can be stated in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**
