# Video script — Loud Sounds and Soft Sounds

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A drum appears beside the mascot; he hits it once.]

"BOOM. That was loud."

[A bell rings.] "DING. Loud too."

[The mascot puts a finger to his lips and whispers.] "And this… is soft."

[Sounds of rain on leaves.] "Rain on leaves — soft."

"Loud sounds carry far. Soft sounds stay near. Your ears know both."
