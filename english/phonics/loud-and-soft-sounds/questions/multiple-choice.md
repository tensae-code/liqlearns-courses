## Multiple choice

1. A drum makes a… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) soft sound  b) loud sound  c) no sound
   **Answer: b).**

2. Thunder is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) loud  b) soft  c) silent
   **Answer: a).**

3. A whisper is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) loud sound  b) soft sound  c) song
   **Answer: b).**

4. A soft sound is heard best… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) from far away  b) when you are near and listening  c) never
   **Answer: b).**

5. Sorting sounds into loud and soft is a job for your… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) eyes  b) feet  c) ears
   **Answer: c).**
