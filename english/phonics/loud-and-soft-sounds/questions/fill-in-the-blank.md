## Fill in the blank

1. A sound that carries far is ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: loud.**

2. A sound you hear only up close is ______. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: soft.**

3. A dog barking makes a ______ sound. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: loud.**

4. When you ______ your name, you make a soft sound. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: whisper.**
