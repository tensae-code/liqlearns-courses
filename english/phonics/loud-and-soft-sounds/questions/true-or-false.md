## True or false

1. A loud sound can wake you up. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. A cat's purr is a loud sound. (Sub lesson 2) **False — a purr is soft.**

3. The same voice can make a loud sound and a soft sound. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. Rain on leaves is louder than thunder. (Sub lesson 1) **False.**
