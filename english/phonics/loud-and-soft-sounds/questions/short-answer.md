## Short answer

1. Name two things that make loud sounds. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any two — e.g. a drum, a bell, thunder, a barking dog.**

2. Name two things that make soft sounds. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any two — e.g. a whisper, a cat's purr, rain on leaves, a turning page.**
