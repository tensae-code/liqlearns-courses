# Video Script — Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each word is pointed at as it is read, syllable by syllable.

[Picture: the word TABLE split into two boxes: TA — BLE]

Ta-ble. The a is open, so the a says its name. Table!

[Picture: the words CANDLE and APPLE, the -le endings circled]

Can-dle. Ap-ple. Words can end in -le. Count the syllables: two!

[Picture: the words ACTION and ELEPHANT]

Ac-tion — ti says sh! Ele-phant — ph says f! Two tricks, two words. You can read them now.
