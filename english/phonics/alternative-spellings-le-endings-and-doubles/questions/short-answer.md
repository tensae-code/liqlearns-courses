# Short Answer — Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Write table in two syllable boxes. Why does the a say its name? (Sub lesson 1)
   — TA / BLE. The first syllable ends on the a — it is open — so the a says its long sound.
2. Name two be- words from the workbook list. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any two of: begin, began, before, became, behind, below, between, beside, beyond, being.
3. Why does this course keep the al/all list at 9 words? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because the workbook prints only 9 words in that list and leaves row 10 blank; nothing is added from anywhere else.
4. Split fiction and telephone into syllables. (Sub lesson 2)
   — FIC-tion (ti says sh); TEL-e-phone (ph says f).
5. What job do the double letters do in rabbit and letter? (Sub lesson 2)
   — They keep the vowel before them short.
