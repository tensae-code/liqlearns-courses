# Hands-On — Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, a pencil, and the word lists in the lesson.

1. **Clap the syllables.** Read the -le words aloud and clap once per syllable: CAN-dle (2), EAG-le (2), PUZ-zle (2). Write each word one syllable in each box.
2. **Double or single?** A partner says a word (rabbit, summer, letter, paper). Write it. Check: does a double consonant guard a short vowel?
3. **Spot the trick.** Read action, fiction, elephant, photo. For each, say the trick: ti as sh, or ph as f.
4. **Write the week.** Pick ten words — five from each sub lesson — and write each one syllable by syllable in boxes, then write four sentences using four of the words, the way the spelling workbook does it.
