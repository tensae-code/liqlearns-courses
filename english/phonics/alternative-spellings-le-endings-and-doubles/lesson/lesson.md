# Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

## Do it first

Read these words slowly: **candle, rabbit, action, elephant**. Each one hides a spelling trick: candle ends in -le, rabbit doubles its b, action's ti says sh, and elephant's ph says f. From Class III's last weeks, the spelling workbook also writes these words **one syllable per box** — that is how we will meet them here.

## Sub lesson 1 — Open-syllable a, be- words, -ey endings, al/all and wa for wor

*Difficulty: first step — five families that finish the a spellings.*

- **a as long a in open syllables** — basic, label, table, paper, apron, basin, danger, major, vacant, chamber. The syllable ends on the a, so the a says its name.
- **be- words** — begin, began, before, became, behind, below, between, beside, beyond, being.
- **-ey and y endings** — key, money, monkey, donkey, honey, valley, hockey, kidney, turkey, parsley; and chimney, barley, alley, pulley, plenty, twenty, family, really, body, angry.
- **al / all** — almost, already, also, always, water, install, walnut, almighty, enthrall (the book prints 9 words; row 10 is blank — we keep the list as printed).
- **wa for the wor sound** — dwarf, swarm, warm, warn, wart, award, quarter, reward, towards, wardrobe.

Try it now: read table, paper, begin, money, always, warm aloud. Name the family of each before you read the next.

## Sub lesson 2 — -le endings, double consonants, ti as sh and ph as f

*Difficulty: builds on Sub lesson 1 — longer words now, split into syllables.*

- **-le endings** — grumble, crumble, candle, handle, needle, jungle, eagle, sparkle, simple, beetle; bubble, puddle, apple, little, cattle, pebble, huddle, bottle, settle, puzzle. The last syllable is consonant + le: CAN-dle, AP-ple.
- **Double consonants** — sudden, ladder, hidden, rabbit, cubby, ribbon, stagger, nugget, soccer, occur; tunnel, sunny, summer, common, hammer, happen, support, puppet, letter, better. The double letter keeps the vowel before it short.
- **ti as sh** — function, lotion, pollution, fiction, action, devotion, fraction, mention, section, emotion.
- **ph as f** — elephant, alphabet, photo, orphan, nephew, telephone, dolphin, trophy, triumph, typhoon.

Try it now: split candle, rabbit, fiction, elephant into syllables aloud — one clap per syllable — then write each word one syllable in each box, the way the workbook does from this point on.
