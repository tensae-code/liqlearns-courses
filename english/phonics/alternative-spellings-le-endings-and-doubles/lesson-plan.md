# Lesson Plan — Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

**One thing:** the last spelling families of the Class III programme: the open-syllable a, be- words, -ey endings, al/all and wa for wor; then the -le endings, double consonants, ti as sh and ph as f — written syllable by syllable.

## Sub lessons

1. Open-syllable a, be- words, -ey endings, al/all and wa for wor
2. -le endings, double consonants, ti as sh and ph as f

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- a says its long sound in open syllables: basic, label, table, paper (Class III Phonics Workbook, weeks 25–36).
- The be- family (begin, began, before, became…) and the -ey/y endings (key, money / chimney, family) are spelling families of their own.
- al/all rounds the a (almost, always, water) and wa says wor (warm, warn, quarter).
- Words end in consonant + -le (candle, apple, little), double consonants guard short vowels (rabbit, letter), ti says sh (action, fiction) and ph says f (elephant, photo).
