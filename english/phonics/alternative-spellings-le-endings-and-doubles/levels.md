# Levels — Alternative Spellings — -le Endings, Double Consonants, ti and ph

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Open-syllable a, be- words, -ey endings, al/all and wa for wor | Read and spell the open-a list, the be- family, the -ey endings, and hear al/all and wa-for-wor. |
| 2. -le endings, double consonants, ti as sh and ph as f | Split -le words into syllables, double consonants after short vowels, and read ti as sh and ph as f. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons holds the whole last block of the Class III spelling programme.
