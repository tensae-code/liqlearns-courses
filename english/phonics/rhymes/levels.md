# Levels — Rhymes

Same as every lesson in this course: the lesson is taught once,
whole, in lesson/lesson.md, and its level is recorded here. This
lesson has no separate level files; the difficulty of each sub
lesson is marked at its end in the lesson text.

**This lesson's own depth record** — this file records how deep
this lesson goes and how far its learners can climb.

- **Floor (every learner):** hear where a rhyme's line-ends
  match, and join in with a familiar rhyme or song.
- **Climb:** recite rhymes and tongue-twisters whole, with
  rhythm, expression and actions, clearly before fast.
- **Ceiling (as far as this lesson goes):** interpret a rhyme
  in your own words, say what rhymes and songs do for us, and
  compose a short rhyme that both matches in sound and makes
  sense.
