# Fill in the Blank — Rhymes

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. Lines that end in matching sounds are called ____. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: rhymes**

2. Rhymes move in ____, like clapping. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: beats** (also accepted: rhythm)

3. A ____-twister is a line built to trip the tongue. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: tongue**

4. Say a tongue-twister ____ first, then a little faster each time. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: slowly** (also accepted: clearly)

5. To compose a rhyme, the second line's end must match the first line's ____. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: sound**

6. A made rhyme must both match in sound and make ____. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: sense**
