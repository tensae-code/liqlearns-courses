# Short Answer — Rhymes

Answer in a few words.

1. In "Humpty Dumpty sat on a wall, / Humpty Dumpty had a great fall", which two words rhyme? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Wall and fall.

2. Name two things you listen for in a rhyme. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Any two of: the rhythm or beat; the matching sounds at the ends of lines; the feeling or expression.

3. How should a tongue-twister be practised? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Slowly and clearly first, then a little faster each time.

4. What do the actions of a rhyme do for the learner? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** They help memory — hands help you hold the rhyme.

5. Say in your own words what "Rain, rain, go away" asks for. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** It asks the rain to stop (so the children can play).

6. Give one benefit of songs and rhymes that Ghana's curriculum asks learners to talk about. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** Any one of: they help memory; they carry language enjoyably; they bring people together.
