# True or False — Rhymes

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. Words rhyme when they look alike in spelling, even if they sound different. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — rhyming is about sound, not spelling.

2. Joining in while you listen to a rhyme is part of learning it. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. The fastest saying of a tongue-twister is always the best. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — clear first; speed comes after.

4. Actions can help you remember a rhyme. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

5. A line that rhymes but says nothing is a good made rhyme. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — a made rhyme must make sense too.
