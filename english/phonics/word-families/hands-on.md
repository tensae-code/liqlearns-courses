# Word families — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: build a family street
You need: paper, your pencil.
1. Draw five houses and label them with the endings of one vowel
   set (e.g. short a: -an, -ap, -at).
2. Write each book word in its house.
3. Read the whole street to your companion, house by house.

## Project 2: the sorting game
1. Write these words on small pieces of paper: pan, jet, dig,
   cap, wet, fig, bun, hop.
2. Shuffle, then sort them into their families against the clock.
3. Say each family out loud as a rhyme when its pile is done.
