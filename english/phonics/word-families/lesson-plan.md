# Lesson plan — Word families

**Course:** English · **Title:** Phonics · **Lesson:** Word families
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4, "First Words" —
after Blends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** words that share the same
ending — same letters, same sound — so knowing one opens its whole
family: cat, mat, hat.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## Before this lesson
- Blending sounds and Blends: the family is where blending starts
  paying rent. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read a new family word by changing only the first letter of a
   known one.
2. Name the book's fifteen families by their vowel: short a (-an,
   -ap, -at) · short e (-et, -en) · short i (-ig, -in, -ip) ·
   short o (-og, -op, -ot) · short u (-ug, -un, -ut).
3. Sort mixed words into their family boxes.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — say cat; change only the first letter: mat;
   again: hat — two words read almost for free (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE at the top of the lesson: a picture of three houses
     on one street with the same roof marked -AT, and the words CAT,
     MAT, HAT on the doors — the student reads the street and says
     what all three houses share.
2. **The explanation** — families family by family, the way Egypt's
   book prints them (a dot under each letter, a blending arrow), the
   rhyming chant, and the sorting work (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD AUDIO: a rhyming chant of one family — "pan, man, can" —
     sung or said with a beat — the student chants along, then
     chants a second family the same way.
   - ADD IMAGE under the sorting work: a picture of five boxes, one
     for each short vowel, with family words waiting outside them —
     the student says which box each word goes into, and why.
3. **Say it to your companion** — grow the -at family from cat,
   changing only the first letter, twice; explain why the new words
   were almost free.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed —
     families grown letter by letter in the super-simple voice
     (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the -an, -ap and -at families from
   memory and check them; say a rhyming line with one family's words
   three times; sort pan, jet, dig, cap, wet, fig into families.

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE — a picture of three -AT houses, CAT, MAT, HAT on the doors
  → the student reads the street and names what the houses share.
- AUDIO — a rhyming chant of one family → the student chants along,
  then chants another family.
- IMAGE — a picture of five vowel boxes with words waiting → the
  student says where each word goes, and why.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student grows
  each family along with it.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the
plan is the build sheet, so nothing the lesson needs gets forgotten.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads every family word letter by letter → cover all but the
  ending: read the ending once, then only the first letter changes.
- Mixes two families (-at words in the -an box) → the ending's sound
  is the family's name; say the ending out loud before sorting.
- Egypt Primary 2 Term 1 re-teaches these vowel sounds as word sets
  a year up and adds the long vowels — filed in this lesson's
  sources.
