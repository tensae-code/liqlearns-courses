# The three sounds of -ed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The -ed in "helped" is said —
a) /ɪd/  b) /t/  c) /d/
**Answer: b) /t/** — helped stands in Bolivia's /t/ list (Year 3, Topic 4, student p.219); p does not buzz.

**2. (Level 1)** The -ed in "wanted" is said —
a) /ɪd/  b) /t/  c) /d/
**Answer: a) /ɪd/** — after t, -ed takes a whole new syllable (Bolivia's /ɪd/ list: wanted, visited, needed, started).

**3. (Level 2)** The -ed in "cleaned" is said /d/ because —
a) cleaned is a long word  b) the sound before -ed (n) is voiced — it buzzes  c) all -ed words are /d/
**Answer: b) the sound before -ed (n) is voiced — it buzzes** — the choosing rule: voiced sound or vowel → /d/ (Bolivia's /d/ list: damaged, called, cleaned, loved…).

**4. (Level 2)** Which word's -ed adds a whole new syllable?
a) washed  b) played  c) needed
**Answer: c) needed** — needed ends in d before the -ed, so the -ed is /ɪd/; washed is /t/ and played is /d/, neither adds a beat.

**5. (Level 3)** In "a learn-ed pundit" (India), the -ed is said —
a) /t/  b) not at all  c) in full — "learn-id", rhyming with created and waited
**Answer: c) in full — "learn-id", rhyming with created and waited** — India's describing-word rule, Marigold Book Four.
