# The three sounds of -ed — matching

Match each verb to the sound its -ed makes, the way Bolivia's lists sort them.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: fixed — a) /ɪd/  b) /d/  c) /t/
**Answer: c) /t/** — x ends in the voiceless /ks/; fixed stands in Bolivia's /t/ list (student p.219).

**2. (Level 1)** Match: loved — a) /d/  b) /t/  c) /ɪd/
**Answer: a) /d/** — v buzzes; loved stands in Bolivia's /d/ list.

**3. (Level 2)** Match: started — a) /t/  b) /ɪd/  c) /d/
**Answer: b) /ɪd/** — started ends in t before the -ed (Bolivia's /ɪd/ list: wanted, visited, needed, started).

**4. (Level 2)** Match: exercised — a) /ɪd/  b) /t/  c) /d/
**Answer: c) /d/** — Bolivia's Year 5 review sorts exercised with played and listened under /d/.

**5. (Level 3)** Match: shopped — a) /t/  b) /d/  c) /ɪd/
**Answer: a) /t/** — p does not buzz; Bolivia's Year 5 review lists shopped with liked and washed under /t/.
