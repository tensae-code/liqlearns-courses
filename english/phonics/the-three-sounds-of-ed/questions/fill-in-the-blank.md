# The three sounds of -ed — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The three lists of -ed · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The choosing rule for -ed · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — The describing-word -ed.

Fill each gap with the right sound: /t/, /d/ or /ɪd/.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** washed — the -ed is said ______.
**Answer: /t/** — sh is voiceless; washed stands in Bolivia's /t/ list in both its presentations (Year 3, p.219; Year 5 review).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** visited — the -ed is said ______.
**Answer: /ɪd/** — visited ends in t before the -ed, so the -ed takes its own syllable (Bolivia, p.219).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** enjoyed — the -ed is said ______.
**Answer: /d/** — enjoyed ends in a vowel sound, and vowels take /d/ (Bolivia's /d/ list).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** In India's describing words — accepted, irritated, completed, challenged — the ticked ending sound is ______.
**Answer: /ɪd/ (the "id" sound)** — Marigold Book Four's ticked set, rhyming with created and waited.
