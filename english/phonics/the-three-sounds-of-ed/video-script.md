# Video script — The three sounds of -ed

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Look. HELPED. PLAYED. WANTED.
All three end the same. E-D.
But listen! They do NOT sound the same.

[Picture: three words, each -ed in a different colour]

HELPED. The end is a small T. Help-t.
PLAYED. The end is a small D. Play-d.
WANTED. The end is a whole new beat. Want-id.

Three sounds. One spelling.

Who chooses? The sound BEFORE the -ed chooses.
Touch your throat. Say SSS. No buzz. Say ZZZ. Buzz!

After T or D? The -ed needs its own beat. WANTED. NEEDED.
After a quiet sound — P, K, S, SH? A small T. HELPED. LOOKED. WASHED.
After a buzz sound, or a vowel? A small D. LOVED. CLEANED. PLAYED.

[Picture: three baskets — T, D, ID — with words falling in]

Now you try.
WATCHED… T basket!
CALLED… D basket!
VISITED… ID basket!

One spelling. Three sounds.
Your ear knows the way.
