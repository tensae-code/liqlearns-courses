**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Bolivia, India, Barbados, Nicaragua, Pakistan,
Palestine, Sudan).**
Bolivia stocks the thing itself, twice: the three lists
at Year 3 Topic 4 (student p.219) and the same sorting
again in its Year 5 review. India stocks the corner
case — the full-said -ed of describing words (a
learn-ed pundit) and a ticked /ɪd/ set. Barbados stocks
the -ed suffix in its Classes 1–2 word-building lists,
syllabus evidence for practice only; no three-sounds
presentation is claimed from Barbados. Bolivia's
"breathed" stands in its /t/ list as printed and is
taught corrected (its -ed is /d/), never as a /t/
example. Counts grow as other closed countries' books
are read.
