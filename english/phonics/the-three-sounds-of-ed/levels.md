## Sub lesson 1 — The three lists of -ed
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read
Bolivia's three lists aloud — helped /t/, played /d/,
wanted /ɪd/ — and hear that only the /ɪd/ family adds a
whole syllable.

## Sub lesson 2 — The choosing rule for -ed
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state
and use the rule — after t or d, /ɪd/; after a voiceless
sound, /t/; after a voiced sound or a vowel, /d/. (Sorting
unseen regular past verbs into the three families without a
miss — old Level 3's first half — is this rule applied.)

## Sub lesson 3 — The buzz test
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: prove
the rule with the throat buzz test on new words — if the
verb's last sound buzzes, the -ed is /d/; if it does not buzz,
the -ed is /t/; t and d endings take /ɪd/ (old Level 2's
second half).

## Sub lesson 4 — The three sounds come back

listened, exercised · /t/: liked, washed, shopped · /ɪd/:
