# Hands-on — The three sounds of -ed

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done out
loud, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Bolivia's lists, read clean.** Read the three lists
   aloud (Year 3, Topic 4): /t/ — helped, looked, washed,
   watched, kissed, danced, fixed · /d/ — damaged,
   called, cleaned, offered, loved, amazed, enjoyed,
   followed, used · /ɪd/ — wanted, visited, needed,
   started. Your companion ticks each word only when its
   ending sounds right.
2. **The buzz test.** Two fingers on your throat. Say
   each pair and feel for the buzz on the sound before
   -ed: looked (no buzz → /t/) · loved (buzz → /d/) ·
   washed (no buzz → /t/) · cleaned (buzz → /d/) ·
   played (vowel → /d/) · wanted (t before it → /ɪd/).
   The throat decides before the ear does.
3. **Card sort, live.** Write twelve regular past verbs
   from any book page on slips — mix the families. Deal
   them face down. Each player turns one over, says the
   word aloud, and puts it in the /t/, /d/ or /ɪd/ pile,
   saying why ("…because it ends in a buzz"). A wrong
   pile goes back in the deck.
4. **India's corner case.** Read these the full way, the
   way Marigold Class 4 teaches the describing word: a
   learn-ed pundit ("learn-id"), rhyming with created
   and waited — then tick the words that end in the /ɪd/
   sound: accepted, irritated, completed, challenged
   (India's own ticked set).
