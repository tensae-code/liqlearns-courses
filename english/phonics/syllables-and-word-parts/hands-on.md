# Hands-on — Syllables and Word Parts

## Clap my name

Each learner says their name slowly and claps its beats. The
group counts the claps and answers: "Three!" Names are pinned
on the wall in beat order — one-beat names, two-beat names,
three-beat names.

## Beat shop

One learner "sells" a word by clapping it, without saying it.
The buyer must guess the word from the claps alone — or ask
for the first beat said aloud. Change places.
