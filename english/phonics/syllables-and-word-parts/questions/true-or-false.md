## True or false

1. Every word has exactly one syllable. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — "cat" has one, "mango" has two, "banana" has three.

2. You clap once for each letter of a word. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — you clap once for each beat (syllable), not each letter.

3. "Teacher" has two syllables. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — teach-er.

4. Long words use a different way of counting than short words. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the way never changes: say it slowly, clap the jumps, count.

5. Your own name has syllables you can clap. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
