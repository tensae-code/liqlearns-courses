## Multiple choice

1. What is a syllable? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A letter  b) One beat of a word  c) A whole sentence
   **Answer: b) One beat of a word** — "cat" has one; "mango" has two.

2. How many syllables does "pencil" have? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) One  b) Two  c) Three
   **Answer: b) Two** — pen-cil: two beats, two claps.

3. How do you count a word's syllables? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Count its letters  b) Clap once for every beat as you say it  c) Count its words
   **Answer: b)** — the hands count the voice's jumps.

4. How many syllables does "banana" have? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Two  b) Three  c) Four
   **Answer: b) Three** — ba-na-na.

5. How many syllables does "elephant" have? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Three  b) Two  c) Five
   **Answer: a) Three** — el-e-phant.
