## Fill in the blank

1. "One beat of a word is called a ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: syllable** — every word is made of one or more.

2. "'Cat' has ___ syllable." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: one** — one beat, one clap.

3. "You count syllables by ___ once for every beat." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: clapping** — the plan's own method.

4. "'Ba-na-na' has ___ syllables." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: three.**

5. "A hand under your ___ helps you feel the beats: it drops once per beat." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: chin.**
