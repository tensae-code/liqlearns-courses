## Matching

Match the word to its syllable count. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. cat — b
2. mango — d
3. banana — a
4. elephant — a
5. Freetown — d

a) 3 syllables
b) 1 syllable
c) 4 syllables
d) 2 syllables

**Answers:** 1–b (cat: one beat), 2–d (man-go: two), 3–a (ba-na-na:
three), 4–a (el-e-phant: three), 5–d (Free-town: two).
