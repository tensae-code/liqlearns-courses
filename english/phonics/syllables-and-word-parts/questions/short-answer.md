## Short answer

1. How many syllables does "river" have? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Two — riv-er.

2. What do your hands do while you count syllables? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Clap once for every beat of the word.

3. How many syllables does "Sierra Leone" have? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Four — Sier-ra Le-one.

4. Say a word with three syllables and clap it. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any three-beat word, clapped in three — e.g. banana (ba-na-na) or elephant (el-e-phant).
