# Video script — Syllables and Word Parts

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot says a word and claps once.] "CAT. One beat!"

[He claps twice.] "MAN-GO. Two beats!"

[Three claps, slower.] "BA-NA-NA. Three beats!"

[He claps a long word, counting on his fingers.] "Every word
has BEATS. They are called… SYLLABLES!"

[Back to the mascot, clapping.] "Clap the word. Count the
beats. You can hear a word's parts!"
