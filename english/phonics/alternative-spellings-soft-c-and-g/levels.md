# Levels — Alternative Spellings — Soft c and g, -dge, -tch

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Soft c — c as s in -ce, final -se, and s as z in -se | Read and spell the -ce and -se word families, and hear whether a final -se says s or z. |
| 2. Soft g and the -dge / -tch endings | Read and spell -ge words with g as j, and use -dge and -tch after short vowels. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can read and spell the soft-c and soft-g word families wherever they meet them.
