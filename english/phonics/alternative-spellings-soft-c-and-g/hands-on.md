# Hands-On — Alternative Spellings — Soft c and g, -dge, -tch

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, a pencil, and the word lists in the lesson.

1. **Buzz or hiss.** Read the -se words aloud one by one (horse, please, mouse, cheese, nurse, noise). Put a hand on your throat: buzz goes in one pile, hiss in the other.
2. **Build the families.** Write -ce, -se, -ge, -dge, -tch on five cards. A partner reads a word from the lesson; lay it under its ending.
3. **Guard the vowel.** Write juge and mach on paper. Fix them the workbook way: judge, match. Say why the guard letter is there.
4. **Write the week.** Pick ten words — two from each family — and write each one sound by sound, one sound in each box, then write four sentences using four of the words, the way the spelling workbook does it.
