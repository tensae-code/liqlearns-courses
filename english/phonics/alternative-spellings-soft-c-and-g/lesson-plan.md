# Lesson Plan — Alternative Spellings — Soft c and g, -dge, -tch

**One thing:** the spellings that give c and g their soft sounds — c as s in -ce, final -se (and s as z in -se), g as j in -ge, and the endings -dge and -tch.

## Sub lessons

1. Soft c — c as s in -ce, final -se, and s as z in -se
2. Soft g and the -dge / -tch endings

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- c as s before e: face, place, space, dance, peace, since, race, fence, chance, rice (Class III Phonics Workbook, weeks 7–16).
- Final -se carries the s sound (horse, mouse, goose, house, loose, nurse…) and sometimes the z sound (surprise, please, cheese, praise, noise…).
- g as j before e: large, change, page, stage, huge, cage, hinge, range, tinge, plunge.
- After a short vowel, the j sound is written -dge (bridge, judge, hedge) and the ch sound -tch (match, catch, stitch).
