# Short Answer — Alternative Spellings — Soft c and g, -dge, -tch

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Why does rice end in -ce and not -se or -ss? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because c before e carries the s sound in this word family, the way the workbook lists it: face, place, space, dance, peace, since, race, fence, chance, rice.
2. Say horse and please aloud. What is the difference in the final sound? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Horse ends with a hissing s; please ends with a buzzing z.
3. Write two -ge words from the workbook list. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Any two of: large, change, page, stage, huge, cage, hinge, range, tinge, plunge.
4. Why does judge need the d? (Sub lesson 2)
   — After a short vowel, the j sound is written -dge; without the d the u would not stay short.
5. Write the -tch word that means "to take hold of something moving". (Sub lesson 2)
   — catch.
