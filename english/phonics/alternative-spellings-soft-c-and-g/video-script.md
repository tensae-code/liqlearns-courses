# Video Script — Alternative Spellings — Soft c and g, -dge, -tch

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each word is pointed at as it is read.

[Picture: the word FACE in big letters]

Face. The c says s! C before e is soft. Face, place, dance.

[Picture: the words HORSE, then PLEASE, with a hand on the throat for the buzz]

Horse — the s hisses. Please — the s buzzes, like z! Feel your throat. Buzz means z.

[Picture: the words LARGE, BRIDGE, MATCH]

Large. The g says j. Bridge — we write d-g-e. Match — we write t-c-h. Short vowel? Add the guard letter!
