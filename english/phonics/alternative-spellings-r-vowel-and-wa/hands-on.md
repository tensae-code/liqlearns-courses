# Hands-On — Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, a pencil, and the word lists in the lesson.

1. **Sort the families.** Write ear, eer, oar, ore, oor/our on six cards. A partner reads a word from Sub lesson 1; lay it under its family card. Watch out for ear as er — it gets its own pile.
2. **Same letters, two sounds.** Read ear, hear, fear, then earth, learn, heard. Mark which pile each belongs to and say the sound aloud.
3. **The w change.** Read was, want, wash aloud. Now read sat, sand, sack. Say what the w did to the a.
4. **Write the week.** Pick ten words — five from each sub lesson — and write each one sound by sound, one sound in each box, then write four sentences using four of the words, the way the spelling workbook does it.
