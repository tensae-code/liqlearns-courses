# Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

## Do it first

Read these words: **ear, hear, earth**. The same three letters — e, a, r — say one sound in ear and hear, and a different sound in earth. And read **was**: the a does not say a at all. English vowels change their sound around r and after w. This lesson teaches the workbook's families for those changes, one list at a time.

## Sub lesson 1 — ear/eer, oar/ore, oor/our and ear as er

*Difficulty: first step — the r-vowel families, one spelling at a time.*

- **ear** — ear, tear, hear, year, near, clear, fear
- **eer** — cheer, deer, steer (the same sound as ear, another spelling)
- **oar** — oar, boar, roar, soar, board, hoarse, hoard, coarse (the book prints only these 8 words; rows 9–10 are left blank — we keep the list as the book prints it)
- **ore** — sore, score, shore, more, store, snore, core, chore, before, explore
- **oor / our** — poor, door, floor, indoor, court, pour, four, fourth, your, fourteen
- **ear as er** — earth, learn, earn, search, pearl, yearn, earthworm, research, early, heard

Read each list aloud. Inside one list the spelling and the sound stay together. Between lists, compare: hear (ear) against heard (ear as er) — same letters, new sound. That contrast is why the workbook teaches them in neighbouring weeks.

Try it now: read ear, cheer, board, shore, door, earth aloud, and name the spelling family of each.

## Sub lesson 2 — a as ar, and a as o after w

*Difficulty: builds on Sub lesson 1 — now the letter a itself changes around r and after w.*

The **a as ar** list: **ask, last, grass, path, fast, class, past, mask, basket, after**. In these words a says the long, open ar sound — no r is written, but you can hear one.

After **w**, a changes again — it says **o**: **wash, want, what, was, squash, swap, swamp, watch, wasp, quality**. The w reaches forward and rounds the a.

Try it now: read ask, grass, after, then wash, want, was. In the first set you hear ar; in the second, o. Write each word sound by sound, one sound in a box, the workbook way.
