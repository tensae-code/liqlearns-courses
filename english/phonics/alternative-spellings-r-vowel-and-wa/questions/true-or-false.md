(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

2. The letters ear always say the same sound. (Sub lesson 1) — False
3. Door and floor are spelled with oor. (Sub lesson 1) — True
4. In ask, last and grass, the a says the ar sound. (Sub lesson 2) — True
5. After w, the letter a keeps its usual short sound. (Sub lesson 2) — False
6. What and was both show a saying o after w. (Sub lesson 2) — True
