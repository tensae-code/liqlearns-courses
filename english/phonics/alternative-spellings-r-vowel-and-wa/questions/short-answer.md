# Short Answer — Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the two sounds the letters ear carry in this lesson, with one word for each. (Sub lesson 1)
   — The ear sound (ear, hear, fear) and the er sound (earth, learn, heard).
2. Why does this course keep the oar list at 8 words? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because the workbook prints only 8 words in that list and leaves rows 9–10 blank; nothing is added from anywhere else.
3. Which spelling family do pour and fourteen belong to? (Sub lesson 1)
   — oor/our.
4. Read ask and was aloud. What does the a say in each? (Sub lesson 2)
   — ask: the ar sound. was: o.
5. Write two words from the a-as-o-after-w list. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Any two of: wash, want, what, was, squash, swap, swamp, watch, wasp, quality.
