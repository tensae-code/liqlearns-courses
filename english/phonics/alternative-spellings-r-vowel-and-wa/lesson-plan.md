# Lesson Plan — Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

**One thing:** the spellings for the r-vowel sounds — ear/eer, oar/ore, oor/our, ear as er — and the a spellings after w and before r: a as ar, and a as o after w.

## Sub lessons

1. ear/eer, oar/ore, oor/our and ear as er
2. a as ar, and a as o after w

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- ear and eer carry the same sound two ways (ear, hear, year / cheer, deer, steer) (Class III Phonics Workbook, weeks 17–24).
- oar, ore and oor/our are three spellings around the or sound (board, shore / door, pour).
- ear can also say er (earth, learn, earn, search, pearl).
- After w, the letter a changes: it says ar in fast-class words' cousins — the workbook's a-as-ar list (ask, last, grass, path) — and o in wash, want, what, was.
