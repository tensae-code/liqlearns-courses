# Video Script — Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each word is pointed at as it is read.

[Picture: the words EAR and HEAR in big letters]

Ear. Hear. Same sound, same spelling: e-a-r.

[Picture: the word EARTH beside them]

Earth! Same letters — new sound. Ear says er in earth. English is tricky!

[Picture: the words WASH, WANT, WAS]

After w, a changes. Wash. Want. Was. The a says o!
