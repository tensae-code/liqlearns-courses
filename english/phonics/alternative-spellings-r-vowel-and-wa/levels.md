# Levels — Alternative Spellings — R-Vowel and W-A Spellings

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. ear/eer, oar/ore, oor/our and ear as er | Choose the right spelling family for the r-vowel sounds and read the workbook's lists. |
| 2. a as ar, and a as o after w | Read and spell the a-as-ar list (ask, last, grass, path) and the w-a list (wash, want, what, was). |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can read the r-vowel and w-a word families wherever they meet them.
