# Lesson plan — Blends

**Course:** English · **Title:** Phonics · **Lesson:** Blends
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — First Words —
the first lesson of the unit (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two letters at the start of a
word where you hear BOTH sounds, sliding together — sk in skip,
cl in clock.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The s-set blends** — sk (skip, sky) · sn (snail, snake) · st (strong, star) · sw (swing, swim) — two sounds at a word's start, both heard, sliding together; the opening lines name both sets before the s-set is practised, and are kept here whole. (Was part of depth Level 1 — the basics.)
2. **The l-set blends** — cl (clock) · bl (black) · fl (flag) · sl (slide) — and the book drops each blend inside whole words, then sentences. (Was part of depth Level 1 — the basics.)
3. **The cover test** — cover the second letter: in a blend the first sound is still there in the word — s is really in "skip"; in a two-letter sound like ch it is not. (Was part of depth Level 1 — the basics.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Blending sounds (sliding single sounds together) and Two-letter
  sounds (the neighbour this lesson is tested against).
  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the s-set blends with their book words: sk (skip, sky) ·
   sn (snail, snake) · st (strong, star) · sw (swing, swim).
2. Say the l-set blends with their book words: cl (clock) ·
   bl (black) · fl (flag) · sl (slide).
3. Read a blend word slowly, sound by sound, then smooth.
4. Use the cover test to prove a blend is not a two-letter sound.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A finger to cover letters with.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — say s, then k, then with no gap: sk, the start
   of "skip"; the mouth keeps both sounds (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD AUDIO at the top of the lesson: s, k, then sk slow and
     smooth, then the word "skip" — the student says it along and
     checks both sounds are still there.
2. **The explanation** — Egypt's two sets, printed pink inside the
   whole words (Primary 1 Term 2, Units 2 and 5), and the cover test
   against ch (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE under the sets: a picture of the book's way — whole
     words with their first two letters in pink (SKIP, SNAIL, CLOCK,
     FLAG) — the student points at the pink part and says its two
     sounds.
   - ADD GIF: a picture that moves — the letters s and k sliding
     together but both staying on screen, side by side, as the word
     SKIP forms — the student says both sounds while they slide,
     the way both stay heard in a blend.
3. **Say it to your companion** — say the eight blends, each with one
   book word; explain the cover test.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed —
     the blends sounded out word by word in the super-simple voice
     (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false, matching — served at the
   learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — say each blend word slowly then smooth;
   write the s-set in one line and the l-set in another; find the
   blends in "snake" and "slide."

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- AUDIO — s, k, sk, "skip" → the student says along and checks both
  sounds survive.
- IMAGE — a picture of whole words with their blends in pink, the
  book's way → the student points at the pink and says its sounds.
- GIF — a picture that moves: the two letters slide together and both
  stay → the student says both sounds as they slide.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student sounds
  out each word along with it.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the
plan is the build sheet, so nothing the lesson needs gets forgotten.

## If the learner is stuck
- Drops the first sound ("kip" for skip) → slow right down: s-k,
  s-k, sk; the keeping of both sounds is the whole lesson.
- Calls sk a two-letter sound → cover the k: the s is still really
  in "skip." In ch, covering the h leaves nothing of the word's
  sound. That is the difference.
