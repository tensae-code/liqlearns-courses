**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Egypt, China, Iran, Jordan).**
China joins with blends taught two at a time on Let's spell
pages in Grade 5 (cl/pl, gr/br) and sorted again in the
end-of-primary grouping review (Grade 6 Volume 2). Iran joins
with blends carried inside its Grade 8 spelling sets — eight
opening blends across four lessons, each taught by how it is
said (no added /e/), and checked with the digraphs at Review 3.
Jordan joins with blends as the whole Grade 3 phonics strand —
fourteen opening blends across five unit-closing phonics
lessons — and the three-letter str/spr/scr in Grade 7.
Counts grow as other closed countries' books are read.

### Egypt — Primary 3 Term 1 (read 2026-10-25) — three-letter blends
Phonics Focus "Three-Consonant Clusters" (p.105): "Words that begin
with three consonants" — **spr-** (spring, spray), **spl-** (splash,
split), **str-** (string, street), **scr-** (screen, scratch). The
blend grows from two letters to three, still at the START of the
word — this lesson's own thing, one step deeper. (Egypt Primary 2
Term 2's END blends, -mp/-ft/-lt/-nd/-sk, stay a separate lesson,
as recorded there.) Same country, deeper layer.

## Egypt, Primary 3 Term 2 (filed 2026-10-25 — book fully read, EGYPT-BOOK-MAP.md)

- **The definition itself, stated by a book at last**: "A
  consonant blend is when two or more consonants are joined
  together, and you can still hear each sound." (Unit 1 phonics,
  p.11) — the same rule this lesson teaches by ear from Egypt
  Primary 1, now named in print at Primary 3.
- **sl / st / gr / tr** sets (p.11), each with the book's own
  how-it-sounds line ("Say /s/ + /l/ quickly: 'sl'"):
  sl — slide, slow, slip, sleep · st — star, stop, step, stone,
  stand · gr — green, grow, grass, grab · tr — tree, train, trip,
  truck, trap.
- Book's own naming: "Consonant Blends 'sl', 'st', 'gr', 'tr'"
  (Scope and Sequence, p.4).

## Egypt — Primary 4 Term 1 (read 2026-10-25)
- **The fr / dr / pl families** (U3 Pronunciation, p.49), with the
  blend definition restated in the book's shortest form yet —
  joining two or more sounds together to read a word:
  fr — frown, fruit · dr — drum, draw · pl — plane, plant.
Same country, deeper layer — the country count does not change.

## Egypt — English Primary 5 Term 1 (2026/2027) — added 2026-10-25

- **Consonant clusters — the general statement** (U1 L3
  Pronunciation, p.16): a consonant cluster is a sequence of
  two or more consonant sounds without vowels between them
  (str- in strong, bl- in black), and clusters can appear at
  the BEGINNING, MIDDLE, or END of words (examples: tree,
  branches, crocodiles, spring). This is the definition the
  Primary 4 blend lessons were building toward — P4 taught
  blends set by set (start blends, then end blends kept
  apart); P5 names the whole family in one rule.
