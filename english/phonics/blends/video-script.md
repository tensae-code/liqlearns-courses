# Blends — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: the letters s and k]

s. k.
Two sounds. Both are real.

[Picture: the letters sliding together over a skipping rope]

Push them close. s-k… sk!
Skip!
You can hear them both. s and k.

[Picture: a snake: sn]

sn! Snake.
s and n. Both there.

[Picture: ch crossed gently]

But ch? Cover the h — c is not in "chair" at all.
That is a different lesson. That pair says one sound.
Now you try it. s and t — what do they start? Star!
