# Blends — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: slow, then smooth
1. Write the eight book words big: skip, sky, snail, snake,
   strong, star, swing, swim (s-set) — clock, black, flag, slide
   (l-set).
2. Say each one in two beats — first the blend's two sounds, then
   the whole word.
3. Read the list to your companion once slow, once at talking
   speed.
