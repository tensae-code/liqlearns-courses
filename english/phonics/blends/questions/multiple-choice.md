# Blends — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. In "skip", the blend at the start is:
   a) si  b) sk  c) ki  d) ip
2. Which word starts with the blend sn?
   a) star  b) sky  c) snake  d) slide
3. In a blend, what do you hear?
   a) Only the first sound  b) Only the second sound  c) Both sounds  d) No sounds

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-c
