# Short Answer — Tongue Twisters

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Say “Black bug bit a big black bear” slowly. Which sound do you hear again and again? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The /bl/ sound — in “black”, twice.
2. Say “Six silly snakes sneak in the room” slowly. Which sound keeps coming? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The /s/ sound — in six, silly, snakes, sneak.
3. Your friend tries a twister fast and the sounds tangle. What should they do? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Go back to slow, fix the sound that fell, and climb up again little by little.
4. How do you know a fast twister was said well? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Every sound stayed clear — a listener can hear every word.
