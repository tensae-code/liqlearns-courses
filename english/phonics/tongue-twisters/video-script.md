# Video Script — Tongue Twisters

Super simple English. Slow at first. Smile. The saying of the twister is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a child listening to a teacher, mouth closed, ears big]

Listen first. A tongue twister is a tricky line. Hear the whole line.

[Picture: the same child saying the line very slowly, each word big]

Now say it slow. “Black… bug… bit… a… big… black… bear.” Slow is strong.

[Picture: the child saying it faster, smiling, words still whole]

A little faster! And faster! Every sound is still there.

[Picture: the child laughing, tongue in a knot drawn playfully above]

Oops! The tongue trips! Go back. Slow down. Start again. That is the game.
