# Hands-On — Tongue Twisters

Things you do out loud, with real people. No materials needed.

1. **The slow climb.** In a group, say “Black bug bit a big black bear” together — once very slowly, then faster and faster. Stop the moment the sounds tangle, and start the climb again.
2. **Catch the sound.** One person performs a twister fast. The others listen for the tricky sound — /bl/ or /s/ — and hold up a finger every time they hear it. Count together at the end.
3. **Teach a twister.** Each person teaches the group one twister the slow way: hear it, say it slowly, then climb.
4. **Beat your speed.** Say one twister clearly three times in a row. Then try to beat it — faster, but still clear. If a sound falls, it does not count.
