# Levels — Tongue Twisters

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Slow and clear | Hear a tongue twister, break it into its sounds and say it slowly, sound by sound, without dropping a sound. |
| 2. Faster, but still clear | Speed the twister up little by little and perform it fast while every sound stays clear — and slow down again the moment the sounds tangle. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can perform a tongue twister clearly at speed — the pronunciation game the books play.
