# Lesson Plan — Tongue Twisters

**One thing:** tongue twisters — short tricky lines you say slowly first, then faster and faster, keeping every sound clear to the end.

## Sub lessons

1. Slow and clear
2. Faster, but still clear

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Tongue twisters as pronunciation play: listening to a twister, repeating it and performing it with clear pronunciation (ECE Level 2, Term 3).
- The method the books teach: say the twister slowly first, then fast (ECE Level 1 Term 2, topic 0.1.6).
- The tricky sounds the books name: /bl/ and /s/ — “Black bug bit a big black bear”; “Six silly snakes sneak in the room…”
