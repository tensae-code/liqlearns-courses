countries: Egypt, Libya, Laos, China, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, Nepal, Pakistan

Every part of this lesson traces to its book and page. No part enters
without its tag. The blending material lived inside the alphabet
lesson's sources (and its level-2 pass mark) until 2026-10-25, when
it was split out under his rule: one lesson explains one thing only —
blending sounds into words is a different thing from knowing the
letters.

**Acceptance count: taught in 12 countries (Egypt, Libya, Laos, China, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, Nepal, Pakistan).**
China adds the alphabet stocked as five phonics sets rather than
letter by letter (Grade 3 Volume 1), a complete short-vowel cycle
inside one volume (Grade 3 Volume 2), and end-of-primary review
stock that sorts the learned sounds into groups (Grade 6 Volume 2).
Sierra Leone adds blending named as the skill itself: a
"Combining Letter Sounds" block in Primary 1, and decoding —
sound out and blend — as the closing lesson of Primary 2's
letter sequence. Jordan adds CVC building and blending as the
closing phonics of both Grade 1 semesters, with the teacher's
book naming the blending routine itself (isolated phonemes
blended into a word).
Iraq joined 2026-11-03 with blending as a named Unit 5
objective in its Grade 1 Teacher's Book — finger-blending
on words the children already know, and cluster blending
in Unit 6; the work is the teacher's, honestly recorded:
Iraq's primary pupil books carry no phonics programme.
Ethiopia joined 2026-11-03 with blending inside its
Grade 1 book itself: Unit 4 blends arm, van, pen, pin,
sun, sock, worm and web, Unit 5 blends the animal names
and names reading by blending, and its Grade 1 Teacher's
Guide blends mat, bad, far and sit in Unit 1.
Syria joined 2026-11-03 in part only: its Grade 2 book
names one letter-blending activity — "Find and say" in
Unit 22 (printed pp.70–72) — over the sound-matching
groundwork of its Grade 1 Phonics Corners (initial
sounds, printed p.30; final sounds, printed p.46); no
CVC word-building system is recorded for Syria, and none
is claimed here.
Counts grow as other closed countries' books are read.
Nepal joined 2026-11-03 on its Grade 1 teacher's guide alone, recorded exactly as filed: the guide teaches blending inside its own programme — blending/segmenting activities in its opening activity bank (printed pp.1–49) and blending checkpoints at Lessons 25, 36, 47 and 58 of its Alphabet sequence (printed pp.97–278) — but it is the guide to a different, unheld workbook, and the held Nepal student's books teach no blending and no systematic phonics at all.

**Egypt Primary 2 Term 1 added 2026-10-25** (same country, the
first-sound layer): *English — Primary 2 — Term 1* (MoE Egypt with
the British Council, © 2025; ISBN 978-633-8475-44-4; %PDF,
39,918,874 B, 104 pp, image-only; full read in
english/EGYPT-BOOK-MAP.md). Unit 3's Phonics Focus is **Initial
Consonant Sounds** (pp.37–38), with the book's own tip: "Listen
carefully to the first sound of each word. Say it out loud and feel
how your mouth moves." The work: circle the word that starts with
the same first sound as the action word (sit → sun as the worked
example; read → ruler; clap → classmate; clean → classroom; draw →
door), then a pair game — say an action word, your partner answers
with new words starting with the same sound.

**Sierra Leone added 2026-11-03** — three lesson-plan books
stock this lesson: **Books:** *Lesson plans for PRIMARY
Language ARTS*, Primary 1 Term 2, Primary 2 Term 1 and Primary
2 Term 2 — Sierra Leone, Ministry of Education, Science and
Technology (teacher lesson-plan books; no publication year
printed). Filed in english/books/sierra-leone.md (Sierra Leone
— FULLY READ + CITATION CHECK PASSED 2026-11-03). Sierra Leone
builds toward blending in steps: Primary 1 Term 2 carries a
whole teaching block named "Combining Letter Sounds" (Lessons
71–75), Lesson 90 distinguishes letters from words and builds
words from letters, and Lesson 105 works the sounds at the
start and end of words (initial/final sounds) as a standalone
phonemic-awareness lesson. Primary 2 Term 1 has the child use
the sounds of two letters together (Lesson 47, Hh and Ii) and
match sounds to letters and words (Lesson 49). The sequence
ends, in Primary 2 Term 2, Lesson 120, on the skill named
outright: **decoding — decode (sound out and blend) unfamiliar
words** — the term and the Class 2 letter sequence close on
blending, not on a new text. Where Laos blends with letter
tiles in the hand, Sierra Leone names the move itself.
