# Blending sounds — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. Which set of letters can blend into the word "sat"?
   a) q, v, z  b) s, a, t  c) x, y, j  d) o, u, w
2. Blend p-a-t. The word is:
   a) tap  b) tip  c) pat  d) pan
3. When you blend, what closes?
   a) The book  b) The gaps between the sounds  c) Your eyes  d) The door

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-b
