# Video script — Phonetics

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot's paw rests on its own throat; a drawing of the throat and mouth glows beside it.]

"Say SSSSS. Air hissing — no buzz. Now ZZZZZ. Feel it? The buzz switched ON!"

[Two words appear: PAT — BAT.] "One tiny sound changed… the whole word changed! A sound that changes meaning is called a PHONEME."

"Phonetics studies the sounds we make: breath, voice — and the shapes our mouths make."
