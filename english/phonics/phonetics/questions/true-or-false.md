## True or false

1. Speech sounds are shaped by the tongue, lips and teeth. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. *sss* and *zzz* are made exactly the same way. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the hiss is the same, but *zzz* adds voice: the vocal cords buzz.**

3. Every small difference in sound is a different phoneme. (Sub lesson 2) **False — only a difference that changes a word's meaning is a different phoneme.**

4. Fiji's Year 13 book teaches phonetics inside its Language and Linguistics unit. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
