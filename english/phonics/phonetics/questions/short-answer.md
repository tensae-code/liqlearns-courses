## Short answer

1. Name the three workings that make every speech sound. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: breath from the lungs; voice or no voice from the vocal cords; and a shape made by the tongue, lips and teeth.**

2. How can your fingers prove the difference between *sss* and *zzz*? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: rest them on the throat — *zzz* buzzes (the cords vibrate) and *sss* does not.**

3. What is a phoneme, and how do you test for one? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: a sound-difference that changes meaning; test with a minimal pair — change one sound, and if the word's meaning changes, the two sounds are different phonemes.**
