## Fill in the blank

1. The throat, mouth and nose together, where speech sounds are shaped, are called the vocal ______. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: tract.**

2. The vocal ______ in the throat can buzz the breath into voice, or let it pass silent. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: cords.**

3. English owns about ______ phonemes — its fixed set of meaning-changing sounds. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: forty-four (44).**

4. The ______-pair test finds phonemes: change one sound, and if the meaning changes, you crossed a boundary. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: minimal.**
