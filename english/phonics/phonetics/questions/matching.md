## Matching

Match the sound pair to what changed. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. *sss* → *zzz*
2. *fff* → *vvv*
3. *ship* → *sheep*
4. *cap* → *cup*
5. *pat* → *bat*

a) the vowel phoneme changed
b) the voice switched on — same hiss, now buzzing
c) the voice switched on — same lip-teeth shape
d) the vowel phoneme changed
e) the first phoneme changed: /p/ to /b/

**Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d, 5-e.**
