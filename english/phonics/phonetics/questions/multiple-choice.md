## Multiple choice

1. Phonetics is the study of… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) spelling rules  b) speech sounds — how they are made  c) paragraph shapes
   **Answer: b).**

2. Speech begins as… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) breath pushed up from the lungs  b) ink on a page  c) a picture
   **Answer: a).**

3. The difference between *sss* and *zzz* is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the tongue's place  b) voice — the vocal cords buzz for *zzz* and not for *sss*  c) loudness only
   **Answer: b).**

4. A phoneme is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) any noise  b) a sound-difference that changes a word's meaning  c) a letter of the alphabet
   **Answer: b).**

5. *Pat* and *bat* prove /p/ and /b/ are different phonemes because… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) they are spelled alike  b) changing the one sound changes the whole word's meaning  c) they rhyme
   **Answer: b).**
