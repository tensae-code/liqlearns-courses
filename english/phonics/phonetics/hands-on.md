# Hands-on — Phonetics

1. **Feel the machine.** Fingers on throats, say pairs aloud: *sss/zzz*, *fff/vvv*, *pat/bat*. For each pair, say what changed — the buzz, the shape, or both.

2. **The minimal-pair hunt.** Together, hunt minimal pairs in a reading book: two words differing by one sound. Write each pair and mark the two phonemes. Ten pairs wins the hunt.
