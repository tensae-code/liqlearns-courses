# Video Script — Poems and Songs for Speaking

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Clap to the beat as you speak.

[Picture: a class that has just finished a lesson, the teacher holding up a poem card and a song card]

You learned a topic. Good! Now the topic has a poem. And the topic has a song. What do we do with them?

[Picture: children listening, heads up, hands still]

First — listen. Listen to the poem. Listen to the song. Hear the words. Feel the beat.

[Picture: a child standing, saying a poem, touching her head and her hand as the words name them]

Now say the poem. Say ALL of it. Loud and clear. Up and down goes your voice. Do the actions!

[Picture: the class singing together, clapping on the beat]

Now sing the song. Sing ALL of it. Clap, clap — keep the beat! Every word clear.

[Picture: the class clapping and smiling at the end of the song]

Listen. Recite. Sing. The poem and the song make the words stay!
