# Levels — Poems and Songs for Speaking

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Listening to the poem and the song | Hear the short poem and the song that ride on the topic just taught, follow their words and rhythm, and join in on the repeated parts. |
| 2. Reciting the poem | Say a short poem whole, out loud, with correct pronunciation, good intonation, the rhythm kept, and the actions/gestures done with the words. |
| 3. Singing the song | Sing a song whole, in rhythm — clapping or stamping to the beat — with every word clear to the end. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons can take the poem and the song of any new topic and perform them: listen first, recite the poem whole, sing the song whole — the topic spoken again in verse, with the sounds, the voice and the beat all in place.
