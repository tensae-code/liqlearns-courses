# Hands-On — Poems and Songs for Speaking

Things you do with your voice and your hands. You need a short poem and a short song on a topic you are learning.

1. **Listen twice.** Play or say the poem and the song. First time: only listen, and tap the beat quietly on your knee. Second time: join in on every part that repeats. Name afterwards: which words from the topic did you hear inside the verse?
2. **Recite it whole.** Stand and recite the poem from the first line to the last — no stopping, no skipping the hard line. A partner listens with a checklist: every word's sounds correct? Voice rising and falling? Beat kept? Actions done with the words? Fix the line the checklist caught, then recite whole again.
3. **Sing it on the beat.** Sing the song whole while clapping to the rhythm. If clapping makes a word disappear, the song is too fast: slow down, speak that line once, then sing it again at the slower beat. Finish with the whole class clapping and singing together.
4. **Verse for a new topic.** In pairs, take a topic you learned this week and find or make a four-line poem for it. Recite your poem to another pair, and sing theirs back to them. The pair that can perform both verses whole has finished the activity.
