# Matching — Poems and Songs for Speaking

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match each thing to what it means in this lesson.

1. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The poem of the topic → the short verse that rides on the topic just taught
   b) The song of the topic → the sung piece that rides on the same topic
   c) Listening first → hearing the verse whole before performing it
   d) Joining in → saying or singing the repeated parts along with the verse

2. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Pronunciation → making every word's sounds correctly
   b) Intonation → the voice rising and falling with the meaning
   c) Rhythm → the beat of the poem, kept from line to line
   d) Actions → the gestures done with the words as you say them

3. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Singing whole → the song from the first verse to the end, no stopping
   b) Clapping to the rhythm → hands keeping the beat while the song goes on
   c) Clear words → every word spoken on its note, none mumbled
   d) Slowing down → what you do when a word disappears inside the tune
