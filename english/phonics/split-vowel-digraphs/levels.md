# Levels — Split Vowel Digraphs (Magic E)

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The split digraphs one at a time — a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e, u_e | Read and spell words with each split digraph, and say how the final e changes the vowel sound (plan → plane). |
| 2. Split digraphs mixed in harder words | Meet split digraphs mixed together at harder words (amaze, inside, alone, delete) and read and spell them without sorting first. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can read any magic-e word they meet and spell the ones they need.
