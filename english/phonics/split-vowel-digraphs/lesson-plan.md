# Lesson Plan — Split Vowel Digraphs (Magic E)

**One thing:** the split vowel digraph — a vowel and a final e with one consonant between them (a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e, u_e) — which makes the vowel say its long sound: plan → plane, hop → hope, cub → cube.

## Sub lessons

1. The split digraphs one at a time — a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e, u_e
2. Split digraphs mixed in harder words

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- A split digraph is one sound written in two parts: the vowel letter and the e at the end, split by one consonant (Class II Phonics Workbook, weeks 12–17).
- The short/long pairs open the pattern: plan/plane, hid/hide, hop/hope, cub/cube, pet/Pete (Class II Phonics Workbook).
- One week each in the workbook: a_e (made, snake, late, cake, take…), e_e with ee (these, theme, eve, delete, extreme), i_e (ride, slide, life, while…), o_e (rode, broke, woke, home…), u_e (cute, use, tune, dune…).
- At Class III the same spellings return mixed, at harder words (amaze, inside, alone, cube, delete) (Class III Phonics Workbook, weeks 1–6).
