# Hands-On — Split Vowel Digraphs (Magic E)

Things you do with real things around you. You need paper, a pencil, and letter cards or bottle tops with letters on them.

1. **Make the pair.** Build a short word with your letters: hop. Say it. Now add e at the end: hope. Say the new word. Do the same with plan, hid, cub, mad, cut.
2. **Sort the lists.** Write these words on slips: made, snake, ride, home, cute, these, slide, note, tube, game. Sort them into five piles — a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e, u_e — by their spelling.
3. **Read and write.** A partner reads a word from Sub lesson 2 (amaze, inside, alone, rude, cube, delete). Write it sound by sound, one sound in each box. Check the split digraph: is there exactly one consonant between the vowel and the e?
4. **Hunt the pattern.** Open any book you have. Find three magic-e words on one page. Write them down and read them aloud, saying the long vowel sound in each.
