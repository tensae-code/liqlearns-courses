# Video Script — Split Vowel Digraphs (Magic E)

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each word is pointed at as it is read.

[Picture: the word HOP in big letters]

Hop. Short o. Hop.

[Picture: an E walks in and stands after the P — the word is now HOPE]

Here comes E! E says nothing. But look — now it is HOPE. The o says its name: O. Hope!

[Picture: five word pairs appearing one by one — plan/plane, hid/hide, cub/cube, pet/Pete, mad/made]

Plan — plane. Hid — hide. Cub — cube. The little e changes the sound. Every time!

[Picture: the harder words — amaze, inside, alone, delete]

Big words work the same way. A-MAZE. In-SIDE. De-LETE. Find the vowel. Find the e. The vowel says its name.
