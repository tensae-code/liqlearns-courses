# Stress and intonation — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** A word has as many ______ as it has vowel sounds, and only ______ of them is stressed.
**Answer: syllables; one** — India's rule (Honeydew, Class 8, Unit 7), restated by Sierra Leone, SSS2 L48.

**2. (Level 2)** In "sofa", "about" and "ago", the quiet first syllable is the ______, written ______.
**Answer: schwa; ə** — Ecuador's practice cards, Superior Worksheet 6, printed p.40.

**3. (Level 2)** Content words are stressed; ______ words — pronouns, prepositions, articles, conjunctions, auxiliaries — are not, unless you clarify or correct.
**Answer: structure** — Sierra Leone, SSS3 L20's split, with its exception stated in the same lesson.

**4. (Level 3)** A question tag said with a ______ tune expects agreement; said with a ______ tune, it is really asking.
**Answer: falling; rising** — Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 10 (intonation in question tags), summarised again in Grade 12, Unit 10.
