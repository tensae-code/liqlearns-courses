# Stress and intonation — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Word stress · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Sentence stress, and Sub lesson 3 — The schwa (split item by item) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The tune at the end.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "banana", the stressed syllable is —
a) the first (BA-na-na)  b) the second (ba-NA-na)  c) the third (ba-na-NA)
**Answer: b) the second (ba-NA-na)** — Ecuador's own worked example: "buh-NAH-nuh", the two quiet a's shrinking to schwas (Superior Worksheet 6, p.40).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** India's book marks a stressed syllable with —
a) an underline only  b) the ´ mark (´manner)  c) a capital letter
**Answer: b) the ´ mark (´manner)** — Honeydew, Class 8, Unit 7: the stressed syllable is the more prominent one, marked ´.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which words in a sentence are normally stressed (Sierra Leone, SSS3 L20)?
a) pronouns, articles, prepositions  b) conjunctions and auxiliaries  c) nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs
**Answer: c) nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs** — the content words carry the stress; structure words shrink, except to clarify or correct.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The schwa sound is —
a) a loud, long "ah"  b) a quick, relaxed "uh" (ə) in unstressed syllables  c) the sound of the letter w
**Answer: b) a quick, relaxed "uh" (ə) in unstressed syllables** — Ecuador, Superior Worksheet 6, printed p.40.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** "Would you like some tea?" — an invitation — takes which tune in Vietnam's system?
a) plain falling  b) plain rising  c) fall-rise
**Answer: c) fall-rise** — Grade 11, Unit 8: invitations, suggestions and polite requests take the fall-rise tune.
