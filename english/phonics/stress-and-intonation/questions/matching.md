# Stress and intonation — matching

Match each item to its stress or tune, the way the books teach them.

**1. (Level 1)** Match: camel (India's worked set) — a) stress on the first syllable (´camel)  b) stress on the second syllable  c) no stress at all
**Answer: a) stress on the first syllable (´camel)** — Honeydew, Class 8, Unit 7's set: camel, balloon, decent, opinion, ´manner.

**2. (Level 1)** Match: balloon (India's worked set) — a) stress on the first syllable  b) stress on the second syllable (bal´loon)  c) both syllables stressed
**Answer: b) stress on the second syllable (bal´loon)** — the ´ falls on -loon; one word, one stressed syllable.

**3. (Level 2)** Match: the word "of" inside a fast sentence — a) said in full, "ov"  b) shrunk to its weak form with a schwa  c) stressed louder than the nouns
**Answer: b) shrunk to its weak form with a schwa** — Jordan practises the weak form of of by name (Grade 8, Unit 3); structure words are unstressed (Sierra Leone, SSS3 L20).

**4. (Level 3)** Match: a choice question ("Tea or coffee?") — a) falls on both choices  b) rises on the first choice and falls on the last  c) rises at the very end only
**Answer: b) rises on the first choice and falls on the last** — Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 9: intonation in choice questions.

**5. (Level 3)** Match: a command ("Open the window.") — a) falling tune  b) rising tune  c) fall-rise tune
**Answer: a) falling tune** — Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 6 groups statements, commands and lists under the falling pattern.
