# Video script — Stress and intonation

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Say this word. BA-NA-NA.
Three beats. But one beat is BIG.
Ba-NA-na! The middle one stands up.
Every long word has one big beat.

[Picture: the word BANANA, the NA standing tall]

´Manner. CA-mel. Bal-LOON.
Tap the table. One loud tap. The rest are soft.

Now a sentence.
"I WANT the RED BOOK."
The big words stand up. Want. Red. Book.
The little words shrink. The… a… of…
They shrink to a tiny sound. UH. The schwa.
"Buh-NAH-nuh." Sof-UH. About — uh-BOUT.

[Picture: a sentence with the big words in tall letters]

Now the tune at the end.
A statement falls down. "It is raining." Down.
A yes-or-no question climbs up. "Is it raining?" Up!
A Wh- question falls. "Where is my book?" Down.

[Picture: a line falling, then a line rising]

Push ONE word hard, and you fix a mistake.
"Not my SISTER. My AUNT!"
The pushed word does the work.

Now you try. Tap the big beats.
Shrink the little words.
End with the right tune.
That is the music of English.
