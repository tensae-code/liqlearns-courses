# Hands-on — Dictation

## Speaker and writer

In pairs: the speaker says five words, one at a time, twice
each. The writer writes, then checks on the second saying.
The speaker confirms or corrects. Change places after five.

## Sentence ladder

Start with three words: "Rain fell hard." When both partners
write and check it clean, climb: four words, five words, a
whole sentence with a capital and a full stop. The ladder
stops where the checking starts finding gaps — that rung is
your working rung.
