## Matching

Match the dictation move to what it does. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Hear it whole — c
2. Write exactly — a
3. Second hearing — e
4. Word-by-word compare — b
5. Fix above the line — d

a) Turn the sounds you heard into letters, capitals and stops in place
b) Check every word: none added, none lost, spelling right
c) Listen to the full word or sentence before writing anything
d) Cross out with one line and write the correction over it
e) The speaker says it again so you can check

**Answers:** 1–c (hear whole first), 2–a (write exactly), 3–e (the
second saying is for checking), 4–b (compare word by word), 5–d (fix
visibly).
