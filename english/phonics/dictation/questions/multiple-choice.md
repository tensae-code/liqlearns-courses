## Multiple choice

1. What is the one rule of dictation? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Write what you think the speaker meant  b) Write exactly what you hear  c) Write as fast as you can
   **Answer: b) Write exactly what you hear.**

2. When should you start writing a sentence in dictation? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) At the first sound  b) Before the speaker starts  c) After hearing the whole sentence
   **Answer: c) After hearing the whole sentence.**

3. What clothes does a dictated sentence keep? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A capital letter, spaces between words, a full stop  b) Pictures  c) Numbers
   **Answer: a)** — the sentence is written whole, as a sentence.

4. When do you check your dictation? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Never  b) When the speaker says it again  c) A week later
   **Answer: b) When the speaker says it again** — you compare word by word.

5. How do you fix a wrong word in dictation? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Rub it out so nobody sees  b) Cross it out with one line and write the fix above it  c) Leave it
   **Answer: b)** — the difference stays visible; the difference is the lesson.
