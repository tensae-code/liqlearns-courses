## Short answer

1. What are the three checks of dictation's second half? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Every word there (none added, none lost); every spelling matching the sounds; capital at the start and full stop at the end.

2. Why do you listen to the whole sentence before writing? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** So the writing follows the sentence itself, not a guess from its first sounds.

3. Why is the first try crossed out, not rubbed out? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** So the difference between what you wrote and what was said stays visible — that difference is what dictation teaches from.

4. What do you do if a dictated sentence is too long to hold? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Cut back to a few words and build up — three words first, then more.
