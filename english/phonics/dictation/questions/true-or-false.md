## True or false

1. In dictation you may add words the speaker did not say. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — write what you hear, exactly: none added, none lost.

2. Hearing the whole sentence first makes the writing better. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. The checking half of dictation is where much of its teaching lives. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

4. You should rub out your first try so the fix looks clean. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — cross out with one line; the visible difference is the lesson.

5. A full stop belongs at the end of a dictated sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**
