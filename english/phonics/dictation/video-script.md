# Video script — Dictation

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A speech bubble appears; a pencil writes under it.] "I
SPEAK. You WRITE what you hear!"

[The bubble again, slower.] "Hear it WHOLE first."

[A written sentence appears with a word crossed out and
fixed above it.] "Then hear it AGAIN — and CHECK!"

[Back to the mascot, pencil up.] "Listen. Write. Check.
That is DICTATION!"
