# Levels — The Three Sounds of the -s Endings

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The three sounds | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Hear that the -s ending is not one sound but three: /s/ as in books, /z/ as in plays, and /ɪz/ as in watches — the /ɪz/ sound adds a whole syllable. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the right sound | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Pick the sound by the sound before it: quiet (voiceless) sounds take /s/, voiced sounds take /z/, and s/z/sh/ch sounds take /ɪz/ — in plural nouns and in third-person verbs alike. |
