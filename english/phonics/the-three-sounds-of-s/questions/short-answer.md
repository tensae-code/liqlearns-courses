# Short Answer — The Three Sounds of the -s Endings

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the three sounds of the -s endings. (Sub lesson 1)
   — /s/ (a hiss), /z/ (a buzz) and /ɪz/ (an extra syllable).
2. Why does watches have more beats than watch? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because after a hissing sound (ch) the ending must be /ɪz/, which adds a whole syllable.
3. Sort: cups, doors, classes — by their ending sound. (Sub lesson 2)
   — cups /s/; doors /z/; classes /ɪz/.
4. Say a he/she sentence and name its ending's sound. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "She sings" — sings ends with /z/, after the voiced sound /ŋ/.
