# Hands-On — The Three Sounds of the -s Endings

1. **Sound sort.** Word cards on the table: sort them into three piles — hiss, buzz, extra beat — then read each pile aloud.
2. **Throat test.** Fingers on your throat: say the word's last sound. Buzzing throat → /z/ ending; quiet throat → /s/.
3. **Extra-beat clap.** Clap the syllables of watches, buses, classes — one extra clap for the /ɪz/.
4. **Verb round.** In a circle, each learner says a he/she sentence ("He runs. She wishes.") and the group names the ending's sound.
