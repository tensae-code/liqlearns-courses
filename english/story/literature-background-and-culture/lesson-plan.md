# Lesson Plan — Literature, Background and Culture

**One thing:** reading a work through the life and the culture behind it — how a writer's personal and historical background shaped the work, the unique cultural aspects inside it (in the founding curriculum, Bahamian and Caribbean aspects), and how works compare with each other and connect to your own life.

## Sub lessons

1. The writer behind the work
2. Culture inside a work
3. Comparing works, connecting to life

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- How a writer's personal background and historical background influenced the work (Secondary Language Arts Curriculum Guidelines, objectives 5.19–5.26, printed pp.65–66).
- Unique Bahamian and Caribbean cultural aspects in a work; comparing cultural perspectives (objectives 5.19–5.26, printed pp.65–66).
- Comparing works by the same writer; comparing works studied with other literary works and with other media forms; connections between a literary work and one's own life (objectives 5.19–5.26, printed pp.65–66).
