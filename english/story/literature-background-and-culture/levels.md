# Levels — Literature, Background and Culture

One lesson, 3 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The writer behind the work | Show how a writer's personal background and historical background influenced a work. |
| 2. Culture inside a work | Identify the unique cultural aspects inside a work — in the founding curriculum, Bahamian and Caribbean aspects — and compare cultural perspectives between works. |
| 3. Comparing works, connecting to life | Compare works by the same writer, compare studied works with other literary works and with other media forms, and make connections between a literary work and your own life. |

A learner who finishes all 3 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: reading a work through the life and the culture behind it — how a writer's personal and historical background shaped the work, the unique cultural aspects inside it (in the founding curriculum, Bahamian and Caribbean aspects), and how works compare with each other and connect to your own life.
