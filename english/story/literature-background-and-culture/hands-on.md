# Hands-On — Literature, Background and Culture

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Two lines of background.** For your class writer: one line of personal background in the work, one line of historical background. Defend both from the text.
2. **Culture fingerprints.** List a work's cultural aspects. Star the three that could belong to no other place, and say why.
3. **Theme across cultures.** Pairs take one theme in two works from two cultures and present how each culture turns it.
4. **Book to film.** Compare one scene in a work and in its film or song form: what survived, what was lost, what was gained?
