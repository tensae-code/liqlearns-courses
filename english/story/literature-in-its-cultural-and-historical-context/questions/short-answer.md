# Short Answer — Literature in Its Cultural and Historical Context

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Why can't a key phrase always be swapped for a flat modern word? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because the key phrase carries its culture and history into the work — setting, custom, belief, power, memory — and the flat word leaves that world behind.
2. Name the three contexts a work is placed in. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Social, cultural and historical.
3. Give one way context can change a reading. (Sub lesson 2)
   — A character's choice judged foolish today may be the only choice her world allowed (any one sound example).
