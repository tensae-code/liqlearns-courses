# Lesson Plan — Literature in Its Cultural and Historical Context

**One thing:** a literary work belongs to a time and a culture: some of its key words and phrases carry that world inside them, and the work opens fully only when you place it in its appropriate social, cultural and historical context.

## Sub lessons

1. Words that carry a culture
2. Placing a work in its context

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Key words and phrases of cultural and historical significance (Secondary Language Arts Curriculum Guidelines, objectives 4.14–4.16, printed p.58).
- The significance of key words and phrases in a literary work (objectives 4.14–4.16, printed p.58).
- Placing a literary work in its appropriate social, cultural and historical context (objectives 4.14–4.16, printed p.58).
