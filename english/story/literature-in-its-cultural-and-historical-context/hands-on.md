# Hands-On — Literature in Its Cultural and Historical Context

Things you do with real people, real pages and real screens. No special materials needed beyond what is named.

1. **Key-word hunt.** In pairs, take one page of a work from another time or culture. Circle the carrier words; write each one's world in one line.
2. **Context card.** Make a context card for your class work: social, cultural, historical — three lines. Pin it where the class reads.
3. **Judge twice.** Pick a character's hard choice. Judge it by today's rules, then by the work's own world. Did your judgement move?
4. **Swap test.** Replace a key phrase with a flat modern phrase. Read the sentence aloud both ways and name what was lost.
