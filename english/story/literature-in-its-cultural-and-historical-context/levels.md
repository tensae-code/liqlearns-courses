# Levels — Literature in Its Cultural and Historical Context

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Words that carry a culture | Identify key words and phrases of cultural and historical significance in a work, and explain the significance of key words and phrases inside that work. |
| 2. Placing a work in its context | Place a literary work in its appropriate social, cultural and historical context and say what the placing explains about the work. |

A learner who finishes all 2 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: a literary work belongs to a time and a culture: some of its key words and phrases carry that world inside them, and the work opens fully only when you place it in its appropriate social, cultural and historical context.
