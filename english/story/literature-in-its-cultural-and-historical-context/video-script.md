# Video Script — Literature in Its Cultural and Historical Context

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: an old word in a story glowing, a small world opening inside it — a festival, a market, a harbour]

Some words carry a whole world! Stop at the key word. Ask: what world does this word hold?

[Picture: a book standing on a timeline, houses and clothes of its time rising around it]

Place the work in its time! How did people live? What did they believe? What had just happened?

[Picture: a reader reading the same page twice, the second time with the scene around the page filled in]

Read it twice. Once as it stands — once standing in its own time. The second reading is bigger!
