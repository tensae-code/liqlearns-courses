# Video script — Telling a story back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You heard a story. Now — tell it BACK!

Whole! From start to end.
In ORDER! The same road.
In YOUR words! Not the book's words!

Big stones — KEEP them!
The crow FINDS the jar. The water RISES.
Small stones — you may drop them.
The jar's colour? Drop it!

Bangladesh gives you a WORD BOX to start.
Ghana tells it in a CHAIN — hand to hand!
And try it as the crow: "I was thirsty. I found a jar…"

Nothing invented! That is telling BACK.

Now you. Close the book. Tell it.
That is a retelling — in English.
