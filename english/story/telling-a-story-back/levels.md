## Sub lesson 1 — The Retelling's Three Laws
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell a short story whole and in order — with the word box if needed — missing no load-bearing stone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Two Kinds of Stones
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: shorten a retelling to half and to two sentences, dropping only small stones, and catch a dropped load-bearing stone in another's telling on first hearing.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Books' Helps
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell with the books' helps — the word box's load-bearing words supplied, the chain's next-happening telling in a circle, the Herring Bone frame's spine and ribs — the furniture's names given, the sentences your own.

## Sub lesson 4 — The First-Person Turn and the Import Law
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell in the first person as a character, then compare the retelling against the original item by item — imports named, order defended, words demonstrably your own.
