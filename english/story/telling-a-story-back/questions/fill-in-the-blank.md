# Telling a story back — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** whole · order · word box · chain · load-bearing ·
import

**1. (Level 1)** Retell it ______: from the first happening
to the landing.
**Answer: whole.**

**2. (Level 1)** Retell it in ______: the happenings' road
unchanged.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Level 1)** Bangladesh's starter help is the ______:
the story's hard words given.
**Answer: word box.**

**4. (Level 2)** A dropped ______ stone breaks what follows.
**Answer: load-bearing.**

**5. (Level 2)** Ghana's circle telling is the ______.
**Answer: chain.**

**6. (Level 3)** A detail the teller added that the story
never had is an ______.
**Answer: import.**
