# Telling a story back — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Retelling's Three Laws (items 1–2) and Sub lesson 3 — The Books' Helps (item 3, the word box) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Two Kinds of Stones (item 4) and Sub lesson 3 (item 5, the chain) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The First-Person Turn and the Import Law (item 6).

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** whole · order · word box · chain · load-bearing ·
import

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell it ______: from the first happening
to the landing.
**Answer: whole.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell it in ______: the happenings' road
unchanged.
**Answer: order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bangladesh's starter help is the ______:
the story's hard words given.
**Answer: word box.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A dropped ______ stone breaks what follows.
**Answer: load-bearing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ghana's circle telling is the ______.
**Answer: chain.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** A detail the teller added that the story
never had is an ______.
**Answer: import.**
