# Hands-on — Studying Themes in Literature

1. **Find the theme.** Take a play or story you have both read. Each of you names one theme as a full sentence and proves it with two scenes. Your partner challenges: does the work really return to it?

2. **The pull.** Take two themes that pull against each other — love and obligation. Act or retell one scene twice: once showing the first theme winning, once the second. Talk about what the work itself does with the pull.
