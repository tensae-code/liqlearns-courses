# Levels — Studying Themes in Literature

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Finding a work's themes | naming a theme as a full sentence and grounding it, from the Theme study sections of Fiji's *Two Plays* book (teenage pregnancy; obedience to parents; racial tolerance) |
| Weighing themes against each other | holding pulling themes over the same scenes fairly — racial prejudice; inter-marriage; love vs obligation — the second play's study |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
