# Video script — Studying Themes in Literature

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two actors on a small stage behind the mascot: a young couple, their families turned away.]

"The play is finished. Now the deep work begins: what is it ABOUT?"

[Three cards rise: LOVE — DUTY — PREJUDICE.] "Not just love. Love against duty. Duty against prejudice. The themes pull against each other!"

"A theme is a big idea the work keeps returning to. We name it in a full sentence — and we prove it with scenes."
