## Fill in the blank

1. A ______ is a big idea a work keeps returning to, worked out through what its people do. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: theme.**

2. Name a theme as a full ______, not a topic word — then hold it against the scenes. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: sentence.**

3. In "Seras Choice", love is weighed against ______: the two pull against each other in the same scenes. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: obligation.**

4. Sera and Anil marry across the racial ______ — and prejudice is the theme their marriage tests. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: divide.**
