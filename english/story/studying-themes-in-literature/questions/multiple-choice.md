## Multiple choice

1. A theme of a work is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a big idea the work keeps returning to, worked out through its people  b) the title of its first chapter  c) its longest scene
   **Answer: a).**

2. Which is a theme sentence, not a topic word? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "marriage"  b) "the play studies what obedience to parents costs"  c) "Act Two"
   **Answer: b).**

3. The Theme study of "A Child for Iva" sets these themes… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) teenage pregnancy; obedience to parents; racial tolerance  b) rugby; weather; farming  c) spelling; punctuation; handwriting
   **Answer: a).**

4. In "Seras Choice", the themes pull against each other. They are… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) day and night  b) racial prejudice; inter-marriage; love vs obligation  c) sea and land
   **Answer: b).**

5. Weighing themes fairly means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) picking a winner and ignoring the rest  b) stating each theme, pointing at its scenes, and saying how the work sets them against each other  c) counting the pages
   **Answer: b).**
