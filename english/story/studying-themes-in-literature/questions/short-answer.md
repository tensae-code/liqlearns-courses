## Short answer

1. How do you find a work's themes? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: look where the work returns — the choices that keep being forced and the consequences that keep arriving — then name the theme in a full sentence and hold it against the scenes.**

2. What happens in "A Child for Iva" that carries the theme of obedience to parents? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: Iva loves Epeli but becomes pregnant by Atama; the two elope to Suva and are disowned — the cost of disobedience is the theme's proof.**

3. Why is weighing themes harder than finding them? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: the themes pull against each other inside the same scenes, and each must be stated fairly with its own evidence — picking a winner and ignoring the rest is not weighing.**
