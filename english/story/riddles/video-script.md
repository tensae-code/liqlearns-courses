# Video script — Riddles

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The mascot covers his eyes with one paw.]

"I will give you clues. You guess the answer."

"I am tall. I eat leaves from the tops of trees. My neck is very, very long. What am I?"

[A pause; then a giraffe appears.] "A giraffe!"

"Now the zebra. I wear black and white stripes, and I look like a horse. What am I?"

[The zebra appears.] "Yes! A zebra. Now make a riddle for me."
