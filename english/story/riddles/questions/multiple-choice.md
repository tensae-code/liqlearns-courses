## Multiple choice

1. "I am tall, I eat leaves from the treetops, my neck is very long." The answer is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a zebra  b) a giraffe  c) an elephant
   **Answer: b).**

2. "I wear black and white stripes and look like a horse." The answer is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a zebra  b) a cow  c) a rhino
   **Answer: a).**

3. "My nose is a long trunk." The speaker is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a bird  b) a house  c) an elephant
   **Answer: c).**

4. A riddle hides its answer and gives… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) the name first  b) clues  c) nothing
   **Answer: b).**

5. When you make a riddle, you end with… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) the answer  b) "What am I?"  c) your name
   **Answer: b).**
