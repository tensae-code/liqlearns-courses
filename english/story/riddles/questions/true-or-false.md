## True or false

1. A riddle gives its answer away at the start. (Sub lesson 1) **False — the answer is hidden; the clues lead to it.**

2. The best clue is the one that fits only one thing. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. When making a riddle, you should say the answer's name in the first clue. (Sub lesson 2) **False — the name stays secret to the end.**

4. Every riddle clue is a describing sentence. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
