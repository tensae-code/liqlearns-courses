## Short answer

1. Write one clue for a dog without saying its name. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any true describing clue — e.g. "I bark and wag my tail when I am happy."**

2. Why should a riddle's last clue be the one that fits only one thing? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: because then the guesser can be sure — the earlier clues narrow the field, the last clue settles it.**
