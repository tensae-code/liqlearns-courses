# Video script — Poetry writing

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

A poem is small. But it is built, like a house.
Today you build three small houses.

First, the words of the trade.
A line is one row. Lines stack into a stanza.
Rhyme: two line-ends sing the same sound.
Rhythm: the beat your voice walks to.
Refrain: the line that comes back, like a chorus.

House one: the bio poem. A person, in lines.
Name. Who they are. What they love. What they fear.
What they hope. A life, standing up in verse.

House two: the colour poem. One colour only.
What does red look like? Sound like? Taste like?
Feel like? Each sense, one line. The colour becomes a world.

House three: the limerick. Five lines. Funny.
Lines one, two, five: long, and they rhyme together.
Lines three, four: short, and they rhyme together.
A-A-B-B-A. Clap it: da-da-DA, da-da-DA… short-short… DA.

Rhyme or no rhyme? Your choice. Poets write both ways.
Goal. Theme. Pictures in words. Sound. Then revise.
Build your three houses now.
