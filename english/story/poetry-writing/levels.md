# Poetry writing — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — writing to a poem's shape — set by
the pattern across the countries' books: the terms and the steps
first, then the three named shapes, then the choices a poet makes
on purpose.

## Level 1 — terms and steps
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use the
writer's core terms correctly (line, stanza, rhyme,
rhythm, refrain, persona, imagery) and run a poem through
the writing steps — goal, theme, imagery, sound, revise.

## Level 2 — the named shapes
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a
bio poem and a colour poem to their frames, and a limerick
of five lines rhyming AABBA with its long-short heartbeat.

## Level 3 — the poet's choices
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose
rhyme or free verse deliberately, place a refrain and a
comparison where they earn their keep, and judge a draft
against the shapes and steps.
