# Poetry writing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Poetry Terms and Writing Steps · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Named Poetry Shapes · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Poet's Choices.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A poem that does not rhyme is not a poem.
**Answer: False** — South Sudan sets rhyme against free
verse and has pupils write in BOTH forms; Egypt calls
rhyme optional.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A limerick's two short lines are lines 1
and 5. **Answer: False** — the short pair is lines 3 and
4; lines 1, 2 and 5 are the long rhyming three.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The persona of a poem is always the poet
themselves. **Answer: False** — the persona is the voice
speaking the poem; a poet may lend it to anyone or anything.
