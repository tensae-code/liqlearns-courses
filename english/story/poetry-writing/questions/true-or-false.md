# Poetry writing — true or false

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Level 1)** A poem that does not rhyme is not a poem.
**Answer: False** — South Sudan sets rhyme against free
verse and has pupils write in BOTH forms; Egypt calls
rhyme optional.

**2. (Level 2)** A limerick's two short lines are lines 1
and 5. **Answer: False** — the short pair is lines 3 and
4; lines 1, 2 and 5 are the long rhyming three.

**3. (Level 3)** The persona of a poem is always the poet
themselves. **Answer: False** — the persona is the voice
speaking the poem; a poet may lend it to anyone or anything.
