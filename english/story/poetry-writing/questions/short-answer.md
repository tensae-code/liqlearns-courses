Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name South Sudan's five how-to-write-a-poem
steps. **Answer: goal — theme — imagery — rhyme/rhythm —
revise** — Primary 7, Unit 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your limerick's lines rhyme ABABA and run
long-long-long-short-long. Name its two faults.
**Answer: the rhyme plan (must be AABBA) and the heartbeat
(lines 3–4 short, not line 4 only)** — rebuild from the
rhyme words, chosen first.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three poem structures South
Sudan's Unit 7 names. **Answer: lyric, narrative,
dramatic** — with stanzas as their shared building unit,
pp.83–102.
