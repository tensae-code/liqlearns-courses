# Poetry writing — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or phrase.

**1. (Level 1)** In South Sudan's glossary, the beat the
voice walks to is the poem's ______. **Answer: rhythm**
— beside rhyme, refrain, line, stanza and the rest.

**2. (Level 2)** Barbados's grid begins bio poems, colour
poems and limericks at Class ______. **Answer: 3** —
Scope and Sequence, syllabus evidence only.

**3. (Level 2)** Ghana's line counts grow by year: four
lines at Basic 4, ______ at Basic 5, eight at Basic 6.
**Answer: six (6) lines** — the curriculum's own ladder.

**4. (Level 3)** Egypt's four-line poem task must carry
one adjective, one ______ and one feeling word.
**Answer: comparison** — the three required ingredients,
Secondary 12, Unit 11, Lesson 5.
