# Poetry writing — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Refrain — **Answer: the line that comes
back, like a chorus** — South Sudan's glossary term,
a writer's tool.

**2. (Level 1)** Persona — **Answer: the voice speaking
the poem — not always the poet** — one of the Unit 7
glossary terms.

**3. (Level 2)** Colour poem — **Answer: one colour
walked through the senses — looks, sounds, tastes,
smells, feels** — named in Barbados's Scope and Sequence.

**4. (Level 2)** Acrostic, sonnet, haiku — **Answer:
poems by type, composed and performed at Ghana's Basic 8**
— curriculum evidence only, Strand 5, p.67.

**5. (Level 3)** Dirges, lullabies, war and praise songs —
**Answer: African poems named by their function at
Ghana's Basic 7** — curriculum evidence only, p.35;
poems sorted by the work they do in the world.
