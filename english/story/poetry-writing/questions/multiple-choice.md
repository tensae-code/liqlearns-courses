# Poetry writing — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Poetry Terms and Writing Steps · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Named Poetry Shapes · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Poet's Choices.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stanza is — a) one word b) lines
stacked together with a gap around them c) the poem's
title **Answer: b)** — lines are the bricks; stanzas are
the stacked groups (South Sudan's glossary).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** South Sudan's how-to-write-a-poem steps
run — a) print, sell, sign b) goal, theme, imagery,
rhyme/rhythm, revise c) read, copy, memorise
**Answer: b)** — Primary 7, Unit 2: purpose first,
revision last.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A limerick has — a) eight lines b) five
lines, rhyming AABBA c) no rhyme at all **Answer: b)** —
long, long, short, short, long; one of Barbados's Class 3
forms.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A bio poem's job is — a) to report the
weather b) to stand a person up in lines — who they are,
love, fear, hope c) to hide the person **Answer: b)** —
the frame itself is the shape; no rhyme is owed.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Egypt's poem steps say rhyme is —
a) forbidden b) compulsory c) optional **Answer: c)** —
choose a topic; feelings and images; short lines; rhyme
optional (Secondary 12, Unit 11, Lesson 5).
