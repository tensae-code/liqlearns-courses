# Hands-on — Poetry writing

A real writing task — three poems, one of each shape.

## Task 1 — bio poem (frame; a real person inside)
[First name]
[Two words: who they are — dancer, brother, dreamer…]
Lover of [three things, one per line or in one line]
Who feels [a joy] / Who fears [a fear]
Who hopes [a hope for the future]
[Last name, or the name they are called by]

## Task 2 — colour poem (frame; one colour inside)
[Colour] is [a thing it looks like].
[Colour] sounds like [a sound].
[Colour] tastes like [a taste] and smells like [a smell].
[Colour] feels like [a touch].
[Colour] is [a place, a day or a feeling it belongs to].

## Task 3 — limerick (frame; beats and rhymes first)
Choose your three A-rhyme words first, then your two
B-rhyme words. Build the lines backwards from them.
Line 1 (long, ends A): There was a [person] from [place]…
Line 2 (long, ends A): …
Line 3 (short, ends B): …
Line 4 (short, ends B): …
Line 5 (long, ends A): …and the joke lands here.

## Check
- Clap each limerick line: long, long, short, short, long?
- Read the bio poem to its person: do they recognise
  themselves?
- Mark one refrain or one comparison somewhere across the
  three poems — placed on purpose, named in the margin.
