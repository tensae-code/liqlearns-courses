# Video Script — Realism and Naturalism

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a story book turning away from a crown and a dragon toward a kitchen, a boat, a snowy road]

No more kings and dragons! Show life AS IT IS. This is REALISM.

[Picture: the snowy road growing colder; arrows labelled BIRTH, PLACE, POVERTY pressing on a small figure]

But go further: what MADE this man? His blood, his place, his hunger…

[Picture: a thermometer at sixty below beside a tiny fire]

Sixty below zero does not argue. It DECIDES. That is NATURALISM.

[Picture: two labels pinned on one story: OBSERVES / ARGUES]

Realism observes. Naturalism argues. Now you can read both!
