# Hands-On — Realism and Naturalism

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Movement spotting.** The teacher reads three short passages (or summaries). Learners call 'realist' or 'naturalist' for each and name the evidence in the passage.
2. **Force map.** For "To Build a Fire", draw the man's force map: cold, inexperience, instinct, choice — sized by how much each decides the ending.
3. **Two readings.** Take one story and write two three-sentence readings: a realist's (what is observed) and a naturalist's (what forces argue). Compare the difference aloud.
