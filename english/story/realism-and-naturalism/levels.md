# Levels — Realism and Naturalism

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Realism: life as it is | Say what Realism is: the movement that shows ordinary life as it truly is, in detailed, believable telling. |
| 2. Naturalism: shaped by heredity and environment | Define Naturalism with the book's own terms, and read "To Build a Fire" through it. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: two literary movements — Realism, showing life as it is, and Naturalism (1880s–1930s), using detailed realism to suggest that social conditions, heredity and environment shape human character.
