# Short Answer — Realism and Naturalism

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What did Realism turn away from, and toward? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Away from romance, heroes and the inflated; toward ordinary life — common people, real work, true speech — closely and believably observed.

2. Give the book's definition of Naturalism. (Sub lesson 2)
   — The 1880s–1930s movement using detailed realism to suggest that social conditions, heredity and environment shape (determine) human character.

3. How does naturalism differ from realism, in one line? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Realism observes life as it is; naturalism argues a thesis about it — that forces of heredity, environment and condition shape what a person becomes and does.

4. Ask the naturalist question of "The Story of Keesh". (Sub lesson 2)
   — What forces made Keesh — a dead father's shame, hunger, the council's unfair division — and where does the story let him choose against them (his headcraft, his fairness as head man)?
