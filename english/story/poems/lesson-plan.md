# Lesson Plan — Poems

**One thing:** the set poems of a school anthology — reading a book's poetry section poem by poem: who wrote each poem, what it is about, and the ideas the class studies in it.

## Sub lessons

1. Poems in the Classes IV–VI anthologies
2. Poems in the Classes VII–IX anthologies
3. Poems in the Classes X–XII anthologies

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Every DSE Reading & Literature anthology from Class IV up closes with a poetry section; the poems are named study texts, with poets named (Classes IV–XII anthologies).
- The early sets are short and close to the child's world: family thanks, water, litter, courage, liking yourself (Classes IV–VI).
- The middle sets add the classic narrative and reflective poems: Lochinvar, Annabel Lee, Stopping by Woods, The Road Not Taken, If— (Classes VII–IX).
- The senior sets study the canon in earnest: Dover Beach, My Last Duchess, Digging, Ulysses — and the course teaches Keats, not the contents page's "P. B. Shelley", as the poet of La Belle Dame sans Merci (Class XII; book error, taught corrected).
