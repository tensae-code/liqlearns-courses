# Hands-On — Poems

Things you do with real things around you. You need one of the anthologies if you can get it, or any book of poems, plus paper and a pencil.

1. **Find the section.** Open an anthology and find where the poetry section starts. Write down its first and last poem and their poets.
2. **Poet hunt.** From Sub lesson 2, write the names Frost, Poe and Kipling. Match each to one poem in the lists and say what class book prints it.
3. **Correct the book.** Find a contents page (or use the Class XII one described in the lesson) and check one credit against what you know. Write the correction the way this course does: the fact, taught corrected, with the error named.
4. **One poem, three sentences.** Choose any set poem from the lesson. Write three sentences: who wrote it, what it is about, and one thing the class studies in it (a refrain, a rhyming pattern, a figure like personification).
