# Levels — Poems

One lesson, three sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Poems in the Classes IV–VI anthologies | Name the set poems of the three junior anthologies and their poets, and say what each poem is about. |
| 2. Poems in the Classes VII–IX anthologies | Name the set poems of the middle anthologies — ballad, reflective and classic poems — and their poets. |
| 3. Poems in the Classes X–XII anthologies | Name the senior set poems and their poets, including the corrected Keats attribution. |

A learner who finishes all three sub lessons knows the whole poetry spine of Bhutan's school anthologies, book by book.
