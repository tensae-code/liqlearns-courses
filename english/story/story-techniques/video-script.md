# Video script — Story techniques

Super-simple voice. Read slow.

---

You know the story shape. People. Place. Happenings.
Now the tricks that make it sing.

Trick one: jump back. Flashback.
The old man sees a red scarf. Suddenly we are fifty years
back, and he is young. Then we return. Now we understand him.

Trick two: point forward. Foreshadowing.
A small hint, early. A locked drawer. A strange noise.
Later, it blooms. The reader says: it was there all along!

Trick three: suspense. Make the reader worry.
Danger is coming. The clock is moving. Do not tell yet.
Worry is a hook. The reader must turn the page.

Trick four: the mystery. A detective. Clues — real clues,
two or three. And one red herring, one false clue,
to send the reader the wrong way. At the end, the answer
must fit the real clues. No cheating.

Trick five: show the person. STEAL.
Speech. Thoughts. Effect on others. Actions. Looks.
Five windows into one character.

Last: the ending. Choose it. Happy, sad, a twist, a door
left open. Then a coda — one small last piece that says
what it all meant.

Shape you know. Craft you now hold. Write.
