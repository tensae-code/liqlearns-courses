Sierra Leone, Sudan, Venezuela, Yemen).** Yemen joins with writing a story from sentence prompts (Grade 9) and from an introduction and pictures (Grade 12) (stocked 2026-11-11 by the re-verification sweep merge). India joins with story technique worked in Classes 9, 11 and 12 — humour removed and restored, creative writing taught, and a humorous piece in the author's style (stocked 2026-11-09 by the India re-verification sweep merge). Ghana's curriculum
names the time moves and the character channels; South
Sudan practises suspense, coincidence and flashback as
named story styles; Indonesia works character by STEAL,
tables the four-stage frame with its coda, and sets the
ending as a choice among four; Egypt boxes the mystery's
clue-craft — real clues, one red herring, a solution
that fits. The plan's "nine ending kinds" for Indonesia
could not be confirmed in the Indonesia filing and is
not counted or taught here; the confirmed ending stock
above stands in its place. Indonesia's Grade 11 books add
narrative types by time shape and fairy-tale creation
(stocked 2026-11-09 by the re-verification sweep merge). Mexico's Grade 7 book sorts literary genres in a discussion table and compares the types of writing — songs, stories, poems, plays and novels — in one diagram (stocked 2026-11-09 by the Mexico re-verification sweep merge).
