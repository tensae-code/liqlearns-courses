# Story techniques — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the craft laid over the story's
basic shape — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
time moves first, then suspense and the mystery's clue-craft,
then characterisation and endings.

## Level 1 — the time moves
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell
flashback from foreshadowing from flash forward, and say
what backstory is — Ghana Basic 9's four ways to manipulate
time, each named and pointed the right way.

## Level 2 — suspense and the mystery
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build
suspense the books' way and lay a mystery to Egypt's Tip
Box — a mystery, a detective, 2–3 real clues, 1 red
herring, and a solution that fits the clues.

## Level 3 — character and endings
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: show a
character by STEAL (all five channels) and close a story
deliberately — a chosen ending kind with a coda that says
what the story meant or how life went on.
