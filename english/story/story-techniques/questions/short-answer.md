# Story techniques — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Story Time Moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Suspense and the Mystery · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Character and Endings.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your story must show why the old keeper
fears the sea, in a scene from his youth. Which move?
**Answer: a flashback (fed by backstory)** — jump back,
show the storm, return; Ghana's time set.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Egypt's four mystery steps in order.
**Answer: think of a mystery — create a detective — add
clues and red herrings — solve it so the answer fits the
clues** — the Tip Box, Secondary 12, Unit 9, Lesson 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A story ends with the problem solved but
the reader cannot tell what it meant. What is missing?
**Answer: the coda** — the small closing stage that says
what changed or was learned (Indonesia Grade 9, Table 3.2).
