# Story techniques — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Story Time Moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Suspense and the Mystery · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Character and Endings.

Say true or false, in the strict format.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreshadowing and flashback are the same
move. **Answer: False** — the flashback jumps back;
foreshadowing plants a forward hint without jumping.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A mystery's solution may arrive from
nowhere, as long as it surprises. **Answer: False** —
Egypt's law: solve it so the answer FITS the clues;
surprise rides on fairness, not instead of it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Telling the reader "she was brave" is
the strongest characterisation. **Answer: False** —
STEAL and Ghana's channels agree: show her by speech,
thoughts, effect, actions and looks, and let the reader gather it.
