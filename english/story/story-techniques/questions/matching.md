# Story techniques — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Story Time Moves · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Suspense and the Mystery · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Character and Endings.

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Backstory — **Answer: what happened
before the story opens, fed in when the reader needs it**
— Ghana's time set, Basic 9.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Flash forward — **Answer: a jump ahead
to a later scene** — the forward twin of the flashback.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Red herring — **Answer: the one false
clue, planted to mislead** — Egypt's Tip Box counts
exactly one beside 2–3 real clues.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Suspense — **Answer: the stretched gap
between the reader's worry and the answer** — named a
story style in South Sudan Primary 8.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Coda — **Answer: the small stage after
the resolution — what changed, what was learned, how
life went on** — Indonesia Grade 9, Table 3.2, p.198.
