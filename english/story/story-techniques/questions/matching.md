# Story techniques — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** Backstory — **Answer: what happened
before the story opens, fed in when the reader needs it**
— Ghana's time set, Basic 9.

**2. (Level 1)** Flash forward — **Answer: a jump ahead
to a later scene** — the forward twin of the flashback.

**3. (Level 2)** Red herring — **Answer: the one false
clue, planted to mislead** — Egypt's Tip Box counts
exactly one beside 2–3 real clues.

**4. (Level 2)** Suspense — **Answer: the stretched gap
between the reader's worry and the answer** — named a
story style in South Sudan Primary 8.

**5. (Level 3)** Coda — **Answer: the small stage after
the resolution — what changed, what was learned, how
life went on** — Indonesia Grade 9, Table 3.2, p.198.
