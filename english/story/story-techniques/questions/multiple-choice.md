# Story techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A flashback is — a) a hint of what is
coming b) a jump back to an earlier scene, then a return
c) the story's final line **Answer: b)** — one of Ghana
Basic 9's time moves; it travels backwards, foreshadowing
points forward.

**2. (Level 1)** Foreshadowing works by — a) planting a
small hint early that a later event makes bloom
b) stopping the story c) changing the narrator
**Answer: a)** — no jump at all; the sign is simply there
from the start.

**3. (Level 2)** Egypt's Mystery Story Tip Box allows —
a) 2–3 real clues and 1 red herring b) no clues at all
c) ten red herrings and no detective **Answer: a)** —
and the solution must fit the real clues.

**4. (Level 2)** South Sudan Primary 8 names these story
styles — a) suspense, coincidence, flashback, stories
told through letters b) only happy stories c) news and
reports **Answer: a)** — Unit 5, pp.62–74, practised as
styles on purpose.

**5. (Level 3)** In STEAL, the E stands for — a) Ending
b) Effect on others c) Excitement **Answer: b)** —
Speech, Thoughts, Effect, Actions, Looks (Indonesia
Grade 11).
