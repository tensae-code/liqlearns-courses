# Hands-On — TWIST Poem Analysis

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **TWIST relay.** Five learners each take one letter and analyse the same short poem in turn, building on the previous letters' evidence. The class judges whether the theme was earned.
2. **Evidence check.** Swap TWIST analyses. Underline every claim that has no quoted word under it. Return for repair.
3. **Topic or theme?** The teacher reads ten one-line statements about a poem; learners call 'topic' or 'theme' and turn the topics into themes aloud.
