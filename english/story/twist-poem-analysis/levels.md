# Levels — TWIST Poem Analysis

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. T, W, I — tone, word choice, imagery | Analyse a poem's tone, its word choice, and its imagery, with evidence from the poem's lines. |
| 2. S, T — style and theme | Analyse a poem's style (how it is made — form, sound, structure) and its theme (what it says about life), completing the TWIST. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: TWIST — a five-part method for analysing a poem: Tone, Word choice, Imagery, Style, Theme — worked through in order, so a whole poem gets read closely instead of glanced at.
