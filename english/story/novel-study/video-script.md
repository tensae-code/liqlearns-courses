# Video script — Novel Study

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A very thick book appears behind the mascot, its pages turning one by one.]

"A whole novel! Twenty-six chapters. How do we study a book this big?"

[Three cards appear: SUMMARY — WORDS — QUESTIONS.] "Chapter by chapter, the same work: tell what happened. Learn the hard words. Answer from the chapter."

"And at the end, step back. What is this book really about? Loyalty. Hard work. Every journey begins with a single step."
