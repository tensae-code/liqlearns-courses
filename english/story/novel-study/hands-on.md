# Hands-on — Novel Study

1. **The chapter work.** Take one chapter of a novel you are studying. Together, do the study book's three parts: a summary in order, its hard words learned in place, and three comprehension questions answered from the chapter itself.

2. **Prove the theme.** One of you states a theme of the novel in one sentence. The other must prove it with two scenes from different chapters — no theme stands without its scenes.
