## Multiple choice

1. The chapter work in Fiji's Year 9 study book has three parts for every chapter… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) summary, vocabulary set, comprehension questions  b) songs, games, drawings  c) tests, marks, reports
   **Answer: a).**

2. The study book is for Jogindar Singh Kanwal's novel… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The Morning  b) Two Plays  c) A Dream Fulfilled
   **Answer: a).**

3. Comprehension questions on a chapter should be answered… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) from the talk about the chapter  b) from the chapter itself  c) from the chapter's title only
   **Answer: b).**

4. A theme of a novel is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) its longest word  b) what the whole book works out through its people — proved by scenes  c) its first chapter
   **Answer: b).**

5. Which of these is named as a theme of *The Morning*? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) loyalty and friendship  b) winning at rugby  c) building roads
   **Answer: a).**
