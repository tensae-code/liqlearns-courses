## Short answer

1. What three things does the Year 9 study book set for every chapter of *The Morning*? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: a summary of the chapter, a vocabulary set of its hard words, and comprehension questions answered from the chapter.**

2. What set-novel work do Fiji's Year 11 and Year 13 books add? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: predictions before reading, response journals, and compare-and-contrast work (Year 11 Unit 1); a Focus on Novel with themes — love; choices (Year 13 Unit 4).**

3. Name the four themes the Brief Notes give for *The Morning*. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: responsibility; hard work and honesty; every journey begins with a single step; loyalty and friendship.**
