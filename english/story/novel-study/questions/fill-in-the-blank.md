## Fill in the blank

1. *The Morning* has ______ chapters, and the study book works through every one the same way. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: 26 (twenty-six).**

2. Never read a chapter ______: summarise it, take its words, answer from it — then go on. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: naked.**

3. "Every ______ begins with a single step" is one of *The Morning*'s named themes. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: journey.**

4. A theme is proved by pointing at the ______ where a character's choices carry it. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: chapters (also right: scenes).**
