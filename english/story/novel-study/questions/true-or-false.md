## True or false

1. The Year 9 study book covers only the first few chapters of the novel. (Sub lesson 1) **False — it works through all 26 chapters.**

2. A vocabulary set takes a chapter's hard words and learns them in place. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. A theme is just a topic word, like "ships" or "school". (Sub lesson 2) **False — a theme is what the whole book works out through its people, and it is proved by scenes.**

4. A character is studied across the whole arc of the book, not in one scene only. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
