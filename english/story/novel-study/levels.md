# Levels — Novel Study

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson as noted in its plan.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Studying a novel chapter by chapter | the three-part chapter work of Fiji's Year 9 study book for *The Morning* — summary, vocabulary set, comprehension questions — with the set-novel work of Years 11 and 13 |
| Themes and characters across the whole book | naming and proving a novel's themes and following its characters across the whole arc, from the Year 9 Brief Notes and the Year 13 novel focus |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
