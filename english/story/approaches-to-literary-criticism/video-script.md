# Video Script — Approaches to Literary Criticism

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a story book seen through three coloured glasses in turn]

Same story — three pairs of glasses! Each one shows a different face.

[Picture: blue glasses — the book's gears and structure glowing]

Blue glasses: HOW is it built?

[Picture: green glasses — a heart and a warning sign glowing in the story]

Green glasses: what does it TEACH?

[Picture: red glasses — a scale with the rich on one side, the workers on the other]

Red glasses: who has the POWER… and who does the work? Choose your lens — then read!
