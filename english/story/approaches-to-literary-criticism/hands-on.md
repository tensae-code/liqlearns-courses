# Hands-On — Approaches to Literary Criticism

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Three chairs.** Read one short text as a class. Three groups each take a lens and present a two-minute reading. The class lists what each lens saw that the others missed.
2. **Lens swap.** Take a critique paragraph written through one lens and rewrite it through another, keeping the same text evidence rules. Compare what changed.
3. **Which lens wrote this?** The teacher reads five one-sentence readings of a known story; learners name the lens behind each and defend the call.
