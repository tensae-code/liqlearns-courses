# Levels — Approaches to Literary Criticism

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The formalist and moralist lenses | Read a work through the formalist lens — its form and craft — and through the moralist lens — its values, lessons and effects on readers. |
| 2. The Marxist lens: power and struggle | Read a work through the Marxist lens: who holds power, who labours, who gets what — and how the work shows the struggle between them. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: reading a literary work through a chosen lens — the formalist lens (the work's own craft), the moralist lens (its values and lessons), and the Marxist lens (power and struggle) — and saying what each lens shows.
