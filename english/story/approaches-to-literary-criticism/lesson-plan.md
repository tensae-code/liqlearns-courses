# Lesson Plan — Approaches to Literary Criticism

**Unit:** Unit 41 — Stories and Poems in Detail

**One thing:** reading a literary work through a chosen lens — the formalist lens (the work's own craft), the moralist lens (its values and lessons), and the Marxist lens (power and struggle) — and saying what each lens shows.

## Sub lessons

1. The formalist and moralist lenses
2. The Marxist lens: power and struggle

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Approaches to literary criticism are named in the book's objectives: the formalist/structuralist and moralist lenses (Lesson 1 objectives, p.273).
- The Marxist critique — the critique focused on power struggle — is applied in the critique work (Lesson 2, pp.297, 308–310).
