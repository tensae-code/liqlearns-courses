**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Libya, Pakistan)** — the
two countries whose schoolbooks bind a complete book-length
text into themselves: Libya binds complete GRADED readers;
Pakistan binds one complete novella in the author's own
words. That is why the curriculum names these books as the
road-markers. The habits taught (ladder at book

lessons grown, cross-referenced in the lesson plan rather
than re-sourced. Other closed countries join as their
