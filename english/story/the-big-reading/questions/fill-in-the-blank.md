# The big reading — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** graded · retold · level · chapter · staircase ·
original

**1. (Level 1)** A ______ reader keeps the whole story and
sizes the English to the learner.
**Answer: graded.**

**2. (Level 1)** Crusoe and Treasure Island stand in Libya's
book ____ by Peter Viney.
**Answer: retold.**

**3. (Level 1)** The page test finds your honest ______.
**Answer: level.**

**4. (Level 2)** No new ______ until the last is told back
in two sentences.
**Answer: chapter.**

**5. (Level 3)** The books climb like a ______: stand on your
true step.
**Answer: staircase.**

**6. (Level 3)** At the top waits the ______, unretold.
**Answer: original** — Defoe himself, at the Novel title.
