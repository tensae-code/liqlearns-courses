# The big reading — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** A graded reader is —
a) a summary of a famous book  b) a whole real story retold
in English sized to the learner's level  c) a book of
grammar exercises
**Answer: b)** — the dress is graded; the tale is whole.

**2. (Level 1)** Libya's Grade 11 Literary book binds in —
a) one reader  b) two complete readers: Robinson Crusoe
(from p.150) and Treasure Island (from p.178), both retold
by Peter Viney  c) none
**Answer: b)** — Garnet, 2019, as the book's Readers
section stands.

**3. (Level 1)** The page test for your level: a middle page
with —
a) no unknown words at all  b) a handful of unknown words
while the story still runs  c) unknown words in every line
**Answer: b)** — a) is a lazy Sunday; c) is a step too high,
for later.

**4. (Level 2)** At book scale, the dictionary is for —
a) every unknown word  b) words that block the story twice;
the ladder (read on, infer, break) runs the road  c)
nothing — never open it in a reader
**Answer: b)** — Unit 2's ladder, grown.

**5. (Level 2)** The two-line law says —
a) read only two lines a day  b) no new chapter until the
last is told back in two sentences  c) write two lines of
the author's words
**Answer: b)** — Telling a story back as the chapter check.

**6. (Level 3)** You step up a level when —
a) one book felt easy once  b) two books at a level read
easy, tellings-back clean  c) the year ends
**Answer: b)** — evidence, not pride or the calendar.
