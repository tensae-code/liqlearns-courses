# Video script — The big reading

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list) — the lesson's VIDEO slot is
the animated-map telling of Crusoe; this script is its spoken
spine. Made later, when videos are made.

A WHOLE book. In English. By YOU!

A real story — Robinson Crusoe! Treasure Island! —
retold in YOUR English.
