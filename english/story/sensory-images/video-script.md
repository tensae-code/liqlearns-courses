# Video Script — Sensory Images (Imagery)

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to the thing exactly as the words say.

[Picture: a story page with a storm scene; a child reading, eyes wide]

A story can make you FEEL the rain. How? With pictures made of words!

[Picture: the words THUNDER, WET GRASS, DARK BARN lifting off the page as pictures]

Words that you can hear… words that you can feel… These are sensory images!

[Picture: five small icons — eye, ear, nose, tongue, hand]

Seeing. Hearing. Smelling. Tasting. Touching. Five senses — five kinds of pictures!

[Picture: the child standing inside the story's storm, smiling]

Hold the images, and you stand INSIDE the story. That is fellow feeling!
