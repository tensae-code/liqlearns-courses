# Hands-On — Sensory Images (Imagery)

Things you do with real books, real texts and real talk.

1. **Sense hunt.** Take one page of any story. With five coloured pencils (one per sense), underline its sensory images by sense. Which colour wins?
2. **Image swap.** Rewrite a flat sentence ("The morning was cold and unpleasant") three ways, each led by a different sense. Read them aloud; vote on which morning you can feel.
3. **Two moments.** From a story you are reading, copy the images of its happiest moment and its most frightening moment into two columns. Write one sentence on what the images alone tell you.
