# Lesson Plan — Sensory Images (Imagery)

**Unit:** Unit 41 — Stories and Poems in Detail

**One thing:** imagery — the pictures a text paints through the senses: finding sensory images in a story, and saying what they make a reader see, hear, smell, taste and feel.

## Sub lessons

1. Finding sensory images in a text
2. What sensory images do

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Sensory images are identified in a read story and named by the sense they wake (Lesson 1, Task 5, pp.285–286).
- The "Fellow Feeling" activity: images are how a reader enters and feels a story (p.286).
