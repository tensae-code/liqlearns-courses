# Levels — Sensory Images (Imagery)

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Finding sensory images in a text | Identify sensory images in a read text and name the sense each one speaks to. |
| 2. What sensory images do | Say how sensory images carry a story's feelings and help a reader compare its elements — the book's "Fellow Feeling" work. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons owns this lesson whole: imagery — the pictures a text paints through the senses: finding sensory images in a story, and saying what they make a reader see, hear, smell, taste and feel.
