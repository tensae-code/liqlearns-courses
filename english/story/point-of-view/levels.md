# Levels — Point of View

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Who is telling the story | First step into the lesson. Can-do: Name the narrator: first person (the narrator is inside the story, saying I), second person (the narrator speaks to you), third person (the narrator stands outside, saying he/she/they). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Limited and omniscient | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Judge how much the narrator knows: limited — one character's eyes and thoughts; omniscient — the all-knowing narrator who sees every heart — and defend the identification with evidence. |
