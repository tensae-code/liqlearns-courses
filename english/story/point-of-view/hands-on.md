# Hands-On — Point of View

1. **Pronoun hunt.** In a page of any story, circle every I, you, he, she — then name the point of view.
2. **Whose eyes?** The teacher reads short passages; learners hold up cards: first, second, third-limited, third-omniscient.
3. **Rewrite relay.** Pass a scene around the class; each writer turns it to a new point of view.
4. **Evidence debate.** Two teams argue the point of view of the class novel — every claim needs a quotation.
