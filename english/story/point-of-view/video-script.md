# Video Script — Point of View

Super-simple lines, one picture per line.

[Picture: a girl with a bow telling her own story, a speech bubble with I]
""I did it myself!" First person — the teller is in the story."

[Picture: a finger pointing out of the page at the viewer, the word YOU]
""You open the door…" Second person — the teller talks to you!"

[Picture: a wide view of a whole village from above, every house open like a doll's house]
"Third person, all-knowing: the teller sees every heart in the village."
