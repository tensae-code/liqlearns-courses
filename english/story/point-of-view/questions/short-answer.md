# Short Answer — Point of View

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the three persons of point of view. (Sub lesson 1)
   — First person (I), second person (you), third person (he/she/they).
2. Name the point of view: "She locked the door and listened." (Sub lesson 1)
   — Third person.
3. What is the difference between limited and omniscient? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Limited sees through one character's eyes and thoughts; omniscient knows every heart, and the past and future.
4. Rewrite in first person: "He opened the gate." (Sub lesson 2)
   — "I opened the gate."
