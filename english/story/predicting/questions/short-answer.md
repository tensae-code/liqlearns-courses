# Short answer — Predicting What Happens Next

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. A story opens with a girl finding a key. Give one clue and what it points to.
   **Answer:** The key — something (a door, a box) will be opened. (Sub lesson 1)

2. Why is a prediction not just a wish?
   **Answer:** Because it is built on clues the story planted, not on what you want to happen. (Sub lesson 1)

3. Write a full prediction: "I think … will … because …" about the girl and the key.
   **Answer:** Any prediction in the shape, e.g. "I think the girl will find a hidden door because the key was left under her door for her to find." (Sub lesson 2)

4. Your prediction was wrong. What do you do?
   **Answer:** Change it — test it against the new pages and predict again from the new clues. (Sub lesson 2)
