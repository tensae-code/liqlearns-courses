# Fill in the blank — Predicting What Happens Next

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Stories show things early because they will _____ later. (matter / disappear always)
   **Answer: matter** (Sub lesson 1)

2. A _____ character usually acts bravely — what you know about the people is a clue.
   **Answer: brave** (Sub lesson 1)

3. I think the girl will open the door _____ the key was left for her.
   **Answer: because** (Sub lesson 2)

4. When the story says otherwise, a good reader _____ the prediction. (changes / hides)
   **Answer: changes** (Sub lesson 2)
