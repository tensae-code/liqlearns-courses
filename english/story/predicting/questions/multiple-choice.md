# Multiple choice — Predicting What Happens Next

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. A prediction in reading is:
   A) a random wish
   B) a guess built on clues
   C) the end of the book
   D) a summary
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Which is a clue?
   A) the page number
   B) a storm cloud shown early in the story
   C) the book's price
   D) your name
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. If a story shows a locked door early, it probably:
   A) will matter later
   B) is a printing mistake
   C) ends the story
   D) is about weather
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Complete the prediction shape: "I think she will run home ___ it is getting dark."
   A) because
   B) but no reason
   C) yesterday
   D) and the end
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 2)

5. Your clues are weak. The honest word is:
   A) I'm sure
   B) maybe
   C) certainly
   D) never
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. After predicting, a good reader:
   A) stops reading
   B) reads on and checks the prediction
   C) tears out the page
   D) forgets the story
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
