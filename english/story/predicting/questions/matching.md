# Matching — Predicting What Happens Next

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the clue to what it points to.
   1. a small key under a door
   2. black clouds over the sea
   3. a boy hiding a letter
   a. a storm or trouble at sea
   b. something will be opened
   c. a secret will come out
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the word to the strength of the prediction.
   1. maybe
   2. probably
   3. I'm sure
   a. weak — few clues
   b. strong — many clues
   c. strongest — the clues are clear
   **Answer: 1-a, 2-b, 3-c** (Sub lesson 2)
