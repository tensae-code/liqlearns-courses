# The story — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the
story's shape — finding the furniture first, then the coat and
the border, then shaping stories of your own.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 covered two natural jumps, so it splits at the
jump — its coat (the past) is Sub lesson 2 and its border work
is Sub lesson 3, and its pass mark splits the same way below
(its order mark — restoring a shuffled story to its
happening-order — sits in Sub lesson 1, where the happenings'
order is taught); old Level 3 = Sub lesson 4.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Story's Furniture
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the people, the place, and the happenings of any short story, sort story from non-story on sight, and restore a shuffled story to its true happening-order.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Story's Coat — the Past
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep a story's telling in the past.

## Sub lesson 3 — Story or Essay — the Border
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold the story/essay border (argue vs happen) on any text.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Trouble Seed
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a story from bare furniture — a who that wants something, a place that holds them, happenings that resist and yield — in past-tense sentences under signposts, and say what each piece is doing.
