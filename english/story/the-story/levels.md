# The story — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the story's shape — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: finding the furniture first,
then the order and the coat, then shaping stories of your own.

## Level 1 — the furniture
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the people,
the place, and the happenings of any short story, and sort story
from non-story on sight.

## Level 2 — order and coat
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: restore a
shuffled story to its true happening-order, keep its telling in
the past, and hold the story/essay border (argue vs happen).

## Level 3 — shaping
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build a story
from bare furniture — a who that wants something, a place that
holds them, happenings that resist and yield — in past-tense
sentences under signposts, and say what each piece is doing.
