**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Bangladesh,
Egypt, Ghana, China, Indonesia, Sierra Leone).** Ghana names the narrative mode; Bangladesh
teaches stories from its earliest classes with the fables;
Egypt reads whole stories through the secondary years;
China names the story's three parts outright (setting,
characters, plot) and binds complete play scripts to
perform. Indonesia tables the narrative's own stages
(Orientation, Complication, Resolution — and a Coda at
Grade 9) on the stories themselves and re-teaches the
definition whole; Sierra Leone names characters, setting
and plot from Primary 4–5 and restates the shared elements
(plot, theme, characters, setting, conflict, point of
view) in the SSS3 creative-writing lesson. Other closed countries join as their books are read (see
READING-QUEUE.md).
