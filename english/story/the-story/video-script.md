# Video script — The story

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is a story? Three things!

ONE — PEOPLE! Someone it happens to.
A girl! A king! A thirsty CROW!
TWO — A PLACE! Where it stands.
A dry land. An island. A school.
THREE — THINGS HAPPENING!
In ORDER! In the PAST!

She FOUND a jar. She DROPPED stones.
The water ROSE. She DRANK!

No person? Not a story.
Points in order? That is an ESSAY.
Happenings to someone — THAT is a story!

And give them a WANT. Trouble makes it live!

Now you. Find the three things in YOUR story.
That is a story — in English.
