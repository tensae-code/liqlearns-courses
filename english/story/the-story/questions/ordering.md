Stand the happenings in their true story order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The crow: She drank. / She found a jar
with a little water. / She dropped stones, one by one. /
The water rose.
**Answer:** She found a jar with a little water. — She
dropped stones, one by one. — The water rose. — She drank.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A morning: He ate breakfast. / He woke.
He washed and dressed. / He left for school.
**Answer:** He woke. — He washed and dressed. — He ate
breakfast. — He left for school.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lost goat: The family ate in peace. /
The goat wandered into the hills. / Sami followed its
tracks at dawn. / He found it in a thorn bush.
**Answer:** The goat wandered into the hills. — Sami
followed its tracks at dawn. — He found it in a thorn
bush. — The family ate in peace.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Order AND repair the coat: She will open
the shop. / A thief has taken the key last night. / She
stands at the door at seven.

night. At seven, she stood at her shop door. She could not
open it." — past throughout, trouble first (the order a
