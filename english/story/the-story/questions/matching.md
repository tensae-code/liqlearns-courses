# The story — matching

Match each text to its kind, and each piece to its work.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — story or not?
1. "First the bell rang, then the teacher entered, then we
   opened our books." (no one it happens to is felt — a bare
   sequence) — **not yet a story: happenings in search of a
   who**
2. "Meron hated the bell. It stole her mornings. One day
   she hid it in the well bucket…" — **a story: a who, a
   want, happenings**
3. "Three reasons the well matters: water, health, life."
   — **an essay skeleton: points, no happenings**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — the four modes (Ghana).
1. tells what happened — **narrative**
2. explains a thing — **expository**
3. paints a thing in words — **description**
4. argues a side — **argument**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — piece and work.
1. the people — **who it all happens to**
2. the place — **where the story stands**
3. the happenings in order — **the spine**
4. the want/trouble — **the fire**
