# The story — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word the lesson uses.

**Box:** people · place · happenings · narrative · past ·
want

**1. (Level 1)** A story needs ______, a ______, and things
______ — in order. *(three blanks, box words)*
**Answer: people — place — happenings.**

**2. (Level 2)** Ghana names the story's writing mode
______.
**Answer: narrative.**

**3. (Level 2)** The story's tense-home is the ______.
**Answer: past.**

**4. (Level 3)** The trouble seed: the person has a ______
or meets a wrong.
**Answer: want.**

**5. (Level 1)** The ______ is where the story stands.
**Answer: place.**

**6. (Level 3)** A list of ______ with nobody they happen
to is a timetable, not a story.
**Answer: happenings.**
