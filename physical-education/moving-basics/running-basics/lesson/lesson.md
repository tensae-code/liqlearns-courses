# Running Basics

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Run on the spot. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

[Picture 13501 — children running on the spot with arms out like wings]

[GIF 13502 — children running on the spot, then opening the arms like wings]

## Sub lesson 1 — Running on the spot

Run on the spot. Your feet move fast but you stay in your place. Lift your knees and swing your arms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Running like an eagle

Run with your arms spread wide, like an eagle's wings in flight. Keep running while your arms stay wide.

## Sub lesson 3 — Somsoma shuttle running

Run back and forth in the group shuttle run the book calls somsoma running. Run to the end, turn, and run back.

## Sub lesson 4 — Running in place for your heart (teacher guide — evidence)

Run in place with your knees lifting, in a steady rhythm. Two children running in place is the teacher guide's own picture for heart and lungs work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
