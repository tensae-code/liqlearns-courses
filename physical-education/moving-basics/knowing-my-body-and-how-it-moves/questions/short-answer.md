# Knowing My Body and How It Moves — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three big parts does the Grade 1 lesson move?
**Answer: Head, trunk and limbs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the body answer, in the Grade 2 lesson?
**Answer: Sensory stimuli.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What fast answer to a stimulus does the Grade 3 lesson name?
**Answer: The reflex-arc motor response.**
