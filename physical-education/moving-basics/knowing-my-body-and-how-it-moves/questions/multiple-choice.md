# Knowing My Body and How It Moves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Knowing my body and how it moves is about…
a) Body parts, bones and joints, and the movements the body can make  b) Running a race  c) Throwing a ball far
**Answer: a) Body parts, bones and joints, and the movements the body can make**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's Grade 1 guide starts with…
a) Body schema and body image, with the senses guiding movement  b) A league table  c) A swimming stroke
**Answer: a) Body schema and body image, with the senses guiding movement**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 2 guide's joint movements include…
a) Sideways, circular, twisting, rotation and flexion–extension  b) Only running  c) Only jumping
**Answer: a) Sideways, circular, twisting, rotation and flexion–extension**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 3 guide teaches moving the body…
a) Whole and by segments  b) Only sitting still  c) Only with a ball
**Answer: a) Whole and by segments**
