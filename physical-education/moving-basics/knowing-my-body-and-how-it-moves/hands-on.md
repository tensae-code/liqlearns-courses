# Hands-on — Knowing My Body and How It Moves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Move it part by part

Move only your head. Only your trunk. Only one arm, only one leg — the Grade 1 guide's own starting moves. Name each part as it moves.

## Joint movements

At one joint at a time, show the Grade 2 movements: sideways, circular, twisting, rotation, flexion–extension. Say each name as you do it.

## Whole and by segments

Move your whole body in one jump-and-turn. Then repeat it segment by segment — head, trunk, arms, legs — the Grade 3 way.
