# Lesson plan — Knowing My Body and How It Moves

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Knowing My Body and How It Moves
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Knowing my body and how it moves: body parts, bones and joints, and the movements the body can make.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Knowing my body** — the El Salvador guide: body schema and body image, the senses guiding movement, moving head, trunk and limbs.
2. **Knowing and moving my body** — the El Salvador guide: body structure and joint movement — sideways, circular, twisting, rotation, flexion–extension — and answering sensory stimuli.
3. **Moving my body whole and by parts** — the El Salvador guide: moving the body whole and by segments; the reflex-arc motor response to stimuli.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Walking in Patterns; Body and Space Awareness. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what knowing my body means: body parts, and the movements the body can make.
2. Name the Grade 1 starting point: body schema and body image, the senses guiding movement, moving head, trunk and limbs.
3. Name the joint movements the Grade 2 guide teaches: sideways, circular, twisting, rotation, flexion–extension.
4. Say what the Grade 3 guide adds: moving whole and by segments, and the reflex-arc motor response to stimuli.

## What you need
Nothing — your own body and a space to move in, as the guide teaches it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Body schema and body image blur → the schema is your body's map for moving; the image is the picture you hold of your body.
- The joint movements blur → bend and straighten is flexion–extension; turning is rotation; going round is circular; wringing is twisting.
