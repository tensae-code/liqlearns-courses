# Knowing My Body and How It Moves

## Do it first

Stand up. Move your head. Move your trunk. Move one arm, then one leg. You have just used the thing this lesson teaches — knowing your body and how it moves — before anyone explains it. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing my body

**Knowing my body** starts with the **body schema** — your own map of your body — and your **body image**, the picture you hold of it. El Salvador's Grade 1 guide opens here (Unit 1 Lesson 1, *Conociendo mi cuerpo*): the **senses guide movement**, and the child practises **moving the head, the trunk and the limbs** — the big parts every later movement is built from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Knowing and moving my body

In Grade 2 (Unit 1 Lesson 1, *Conociendo y moviendo mi cuerpo*) the guide goes deeper: the **structure of the body** and how the **joints move** — **sideways, circular, twisting, rotation, and flexion–extension** — and the body **answering sensory stimuli**: you sense something, and you move in answer.

## Sub lesson 3 — Moving my body whole and by parts

In Grade 3 (Unit 1 Lesson 1, *Muevo mi cuerpo globalmente y por partes*) the child moves the body **whole** and **by segments** — all of it together, then part by part — and meets the **reflex-arc motor response**: a stimulus comes in, and the body answers with movement, fast and without stopping to think.
