# Video script — Knowing My Body and How It Moves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is your body. Head. Trunk. Arms. Legs.
Your senses guide you as you move.
Joints bend, straighten, twist and turn.
Move it whole — then part by part.
That is **knowing my body and how it moves**.
