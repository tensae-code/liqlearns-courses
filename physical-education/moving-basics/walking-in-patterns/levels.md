# Walking in Patterns — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Walking hand in hand
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — walking hand in hand in pairs and in a group.

## Sub lesson 2 — Walking the lines and shapes
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — walking on a straight line, a rectangle, a circle and a zigzag line.

## Sub lesson 3 — Walking while rolling a ball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — rolling a ball by hand while walking.

## Sub lesson 4 — Walking the line in balance (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — walking heel to toe along a straight line with arms out.

## Sub lesson 7 — Selame Selame — greet, pass the cloth, change places (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — moving around a circle, greeting and changing places on the signal while a cloth passes hand to hand.
