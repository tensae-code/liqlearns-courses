# Walking in Patterns — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Walking hand in hand — a
2. Walking the lines and shapes — b
3. Walking while rolling a ball — c
4. Walking the line in balance (teacher guide — evidence) — d
5. Selame Selame — greet, pass the cloth, change places (teacher guide — evidence) — e

What each teaches:
a) walking hand in hand in pairs and in a group
b) walking on a straight line, a rectangle, a circle and a zigzag line
c) rolling a ball by hand while walking
d) walking heel to toe along a straight line with arms out
e) moving around a circle, greeting and changing places on the signal while a cloth passes hand to hand

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
