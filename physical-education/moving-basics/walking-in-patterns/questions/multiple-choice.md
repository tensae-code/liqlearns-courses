# Walking in Patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the part "Walking hand in hand" teach?
a) walking on a straight line, a rectangle, a circle and a zigzag line  b) walking hand in hand in pairs and in a group  c) rolling a ball by hand while walking
