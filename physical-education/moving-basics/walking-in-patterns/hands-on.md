# Hands-on — Walking in Patterns

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: walking hand in hand, walking the lines and shapes, walking while rolling a ball, walking the line in balance (teacher guide — evidence).

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.


## The Grade 8–10 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Walking tall — Grade 8 (teacher guide — evidence), Walking drills that stretch you as you go — Grade 9 (teacher guide — evidence). Stop if anything hurts; use a mat, a spotter and soft or appropriate balls where the part says so.
