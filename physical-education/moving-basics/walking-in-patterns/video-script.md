# Video script — Walking in Patterns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Walking hand in hand. Walk hand in hand with a partner, then in a group.

Walking the lines and shapes. Walk on a straight line.

Walking while rolling a ball. Walk and roll a ball by hand at the same time.

Walking the line in balance (teacher guide — evidence). Walk heel to toe along a straight line, arms out, and do not step off the line.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

Walking tall — Grade 8 (teacher guide — evidence). Walk tall, the way the Grade 8 guide teaches it.

Walking drills that stretch you as you go — Grade 9 (teacher guide — evidence). These walking drills are dynamic stretching — you stretch by moving.
