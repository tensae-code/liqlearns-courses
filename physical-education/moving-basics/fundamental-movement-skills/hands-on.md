# Hands-on — Fundamental Movement Skills

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise it the Fiji way

Work through this lesson's parts in order, doing each one as Fiji's *PE is Fun* books teach it: Chapter 2 Body Movement; Chapter 1: Fundamental Motor Skills. Practise each part slowly first, then at full speed, and teach one part to a partner.
