# Drill, Marching and Formations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Spanish title of the Grade 1 lesson that teaches organising in a group?
**Answer: ¡Qué fácil organizarnos en grupo!**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two small groupings the lesson teaches before rows and columns.
**Answer: Pairs and trios.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which formation goes all the way round?
**Answer: The circle.**
