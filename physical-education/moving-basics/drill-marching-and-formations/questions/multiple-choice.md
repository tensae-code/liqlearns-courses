# Drill, Marching and Formations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A formation is…
a) A shape the whole group makes together  b) A race  c) A ball skill
**Answer: a) A shape the whole group makes together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's Grade 1 guide teaches organising into…
a) Pairs, trios, rows, columns and circles  b) Only lines  c) Only teams of eleven
**Answer: a) Pairs, trios, rows, columns and circles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A column is…
a) Children standing one behind another  b) Children in a ring  c) Two children only
**Answer: a) Children standing one behind another**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A row is…
a) Children standing side by side  b) Children sitting down  c) A circle of children
**Answer: a) Children standing side by side**
