# Drill, Marching and Formations

## Do it first

Stand with a partner. Now stand in a row. Now in a circle. You have just made three formations — the thing this lesson teaches. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Organising in a group

El Salvador's Grade 1 guide (Unit 1 Lesson 7, *¡Qué fácil organizarnos en grupo!*) teaches the youngest children to **organise themselves in a group**: into **pairs** and **trios**, and into **rows, columns and circles**. Every formation is a shape the whole group makes together — the ground all later drill and marching stands on.

## Say it to your companion
