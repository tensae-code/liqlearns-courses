# Hands-on — Drill, Marching and Formations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the shapes

On the call, the group forms: pairs — trios — a row — a column — a circle. The Grade 1 guide's own organisation task, done fast and quietly.

## Change the shape

Start in a circle. On the next call, become a row without anyone being told where to stand. Then a column. The group organises itself.
