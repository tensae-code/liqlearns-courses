# Video script — Drill, Marching and Formations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two together — a pair. Three — a trio.
Side by side — a row. One behind another — a column.
All the way round — a circle.
The group makes the shape together.
That is **drill, marching and formations**.
