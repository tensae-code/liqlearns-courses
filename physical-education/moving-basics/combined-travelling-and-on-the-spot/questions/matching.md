# Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence) — a
2. Combined travelling movements — b
3. Combined movements on the spot — c
4. Travelling and on-the-spot, chained together — d
5. First movement skills — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence) — e
6. Moving in place — bend, stretch, keep your balance (teacher guide — evidence) — f
7. Moving my body well — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence) — g
8. Moving in my own space — the Grade 2 in-place skills (teacher guide — evidence) — h
9. What fundamental movement skills are — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence) — i
10. How far can our team step? Hunter and Hunted (teacher guide — evidence) — j

What each teaches:
a) three families — in place, travelling, and with objects
b) travelling skills chained — through a ring, ball between the legs, sack jump and run out
c) on-the-spot combinations — holding a stretched leg and reaching to touch it
d) one chain — travel, work on the spot, travel again
e) fundamental movement skills are the base laid in childhood — control of body position and health basics built by joining movements in varied activities
f) bending forward to touch the toes and moving body parts without travelling, keeping balance
g) fundamental skills using the whole body — moving fast with a direction change without falling, moving in a small space, and working with partners
h) moving the hands, feet and head slowly and with control inside a small marked space, without travelling
i) the most-used capacity in daily life — its fruits are strong body parts and social/psychological growth
j) team distance stepping from the last learner's mark, and the hunting game where the caught join the hunters

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e · 6–f · 7–g · 8–h · 9–i · 10–j
