# Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: three families — in place, travelling, and with objects.
**Answer: True**
