# Video script — Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence). There are three families of movement: movements done in place, movements that carry the body from place to place, and movements done with objects.

Combined travelling movements. Chain travelling skills together: pass through a ring, pass a ball backwards between the legs in a line, jump into a sack, jump forward in it, step out and run.

Combined movements on the spot. Stay in your place and combine: bend one leg and hold the stretched leg with both hands; reach sideways to touch a stretched leg; run between numbers on the ground, put your foot on each number, and return.

Travelling and on-the-spot, chained together. Chain the two families in one sequence: travel, stop and work on the spot, then travel again.

First movement skills — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence). Fundamental movement skills — መሠረታዊ የእንቅስቃሴ ክህሎት — are the base laid in childhood. Control of your body position, and the basics of health, are built by joining these movements in varied sport and social activities. The chapter's promises are three: show movement skills in varied places, perform movements in place, and move the body's parts in varied directions. Every later part of this lesson grows from this base.

Moving in place — bend, stretch, keep your balance (teacher guide — evidence). Some movements never leave your spot. Bend your body forward and down to touch your toes, then come back up slowly. Move one body part at a time — an arm, a leg, your head — while your feet stay planted, and keep your balance the whole time. The guide's picture shows children stretching toward the ground in a park: straight legs, long arms, slow and controlled.

Moving my body well — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence). In Grade 2 the same chapter opens wider: fundamental skills use the whole body's capacity — walking, running, jumping and movements with objects. The chapter's three skills are movement in place, movement that travels, and movement with objects. Its promises: move fast with a change of direction without falling, move in place inside a small space, and use your body parts together with partners.

Moving in my own space — the Grade 2 in-place skills (teacher guide — evidence). In-place movement skill — በቦታ ላይ በመሆን የመንቀሳቀስ ክህሎት. Stand inside a small marked space and move the small parts of your body: hands, feet, head. Everything is slow and controlled, and nothing travels — your spot is your whole field. This is how a crowded class can all move at once, safely.

What fundamental movement skills are — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence). መሠረታዊ የእንቅስቃሴ ክህሎት — fundamental movement skills. You use them every day: at home, at school, with your friends. They make your body strong. They help you grow with other people.

How far can our team step? Hunter and Hunted (teacher guide — evidence). ለርቀት ጠብ ጠብ ማለት — step as far as you can. Stop. Your friend starts where you stopped. Step by step, the team goes far. Then: አዳኝ እና ታዳኝ ጨዋታ — Hunter and Hunted! Hunters hunt. Others run, inside the field. Caught? Join the hunters!

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
