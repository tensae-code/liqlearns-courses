# Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — three families — in place, travelling, and with objects.

## Sub lesson 2 — Combined travelling movements
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — travelling skills chained — through a ring, ball between the legs, sack jump and run out.

## Sub lesson 3 — Combined movements on the spot
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — on-the-spot combinations — holding a stretched leg and reaching to touch it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Travelling and on-the-spot, chained together
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — one chain — travel, work on the spot, travel again.


## Sub lesson 5 — What fundamental movement skills are — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner says what a fundamental movement skill is, names where it is used (home, school, social activities), and names its two fruits — strong body parts and social/psychological growth.

## Sub lesson 6 — How far can our team step? Hunter and Hunted (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner steps in the team chain starting exactly on the last learner's mark, and plays Hunter and Hunted inside the marked field, joining the hunters when caught.

## Sub lesson 7 — First movement skills — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — fundamental movement skills are the base laid in childhood — control of body position and health basics built by joining movements in varied activities.

## Sub lesson 8 — Moving in place — bend, stretch, keep your balance (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — bending forward to touch the toes and moving body parts without travelling, keeping balance.

## Sub lesson 9 — Moving my body well — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — fundamental skills using the whole body — moving fast with a direction change without falling, moving in a small space, and working with partners.

## Sub lesson 10 — Moving in my own space — the Grade 2 in-place skills (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — moving the hands, feet and head slowly and with control inside a small marked space, without travelling.
