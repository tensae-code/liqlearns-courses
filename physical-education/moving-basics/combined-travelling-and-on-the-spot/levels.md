# Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — three families — in place, travelling, and with objects.

## Sub lesson 2 — Combined travelling movements
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — travelling skills chained — through a ring, ball between the legs, sack jump and run out.

## Sub lesson 3 — Combined movements on the spot
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — on-the-spot combinations — holding a stretched leg and reaching to touch it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Travelling and on-the-spot, chained together
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — one chain — travel, work on the spot, travel again.
