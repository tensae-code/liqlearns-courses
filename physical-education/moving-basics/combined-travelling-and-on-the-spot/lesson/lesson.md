# Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: There are three families of movement: movements done in place, movements that carry the body from place to place, and movements done with objects. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of movement (teacher guide — evidence)

There are three families of movement: movements done in place, movements that carry the body from place to place, and movements done with objects. Show a movement without leaving your spot — that is movement in place.

## Sub lesson 2 — Combined travelling movements

Chain travelling skills together: pass through a ring, pass a ball backwards between the legs in a line, jump into a sack, jump forward in it, step out and run.

## Sub lesson 3 — Combined movements on the spot

Stay in your place and combine: bend one leg and hold the stretched leg with both hands; reach sideways to touch a stretched leg; run between numbers on the ground, put your foot on each number, and return.

## Sub lesson 4 — Travelling and on-the-spot, chained together

Chain the two families in one sequence: travel, stop and work on the spot, then travel again. In Grade 4 this grows to rope jumping while walking, and holding a ball in two hands while jumping along a rectangle line beside a partner.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
