# Jumping and Hopping — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "Jumping on two feet" teaches: jumping on two feet — crouch, spring, land on both feet.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "Jumping on two feet", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "The one-two jump pattern and the rings" teaches: a one-two jump pattern and two-foot jumps inside zigzag rings.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The one-two jump pattern and the rings", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "Hopping on one foot" teaches: hopping on one foot, then landing on two feet or inside a circle.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "Hopping on one foot", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In this lesson, "Jumping jacks and line jumps (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: the jumping jack — jump open, jump closed — and forward-back jumps over a line.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In "Jumping jacks and line jumps (teacher guide — evidence)", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** In this lesson, "Jumping the long rope together (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: two children turning a long rope while two jump together in the middle, in rhythm.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In "Jumping the long rope together (teacher guide — evidence)", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
