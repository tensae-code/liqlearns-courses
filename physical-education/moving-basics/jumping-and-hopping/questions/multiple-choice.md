# Jumping and Hopping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the part "Jumping on two feet" teach?
a) a one-two jump pattern and two-foot jumps inside zigzag rings  b) jumping on two feet — crouch, spring, land on both feet  c) hopping on one foot, then landing on two feet or inside a circle
