# Jumping and Hopping

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Jump up on two feet, like a kangaroo: crouch, spring up, and land on both feet. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jumping on two feet

Jump up on two feet, like a kangaroo: crouch, spring up, and land on both feet. Jump facing a partner. Jump up freely with your group.

## Sub lesson 2 — The one-two jump pattern and the rings

Jump in a one-two, one-two pattern. Jump on two feet inside a zigzag of rings, from ring to ring.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hopping on one foot

Hop on one foot. Hop to touch a handkerchief. Hop on one foot and land on two feet. Hop and land inside a circle.

## Sub lesson 4 — Jumping jacks and line jumps (teacher guide — evidence)

Jump and open your legs while your arms rise sideways over your head; jump and close again — the jumping jack. Then jump forward and backward over a line on the ground, landing on two feet, again and again without stopping.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
