# Video script — Jumping and Hopping

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Jumping on two feet. Jump up on two feet, like a kangaroo: crouch, spring up, and land on both feet.

The one-two jump pattern and the rings. Jump in a one-two, one-two pattern.

Hopping on one foot. Hop on one foot.

Jumping jacks and line jumps (teacher guide — evidence). Jump and open your legs while your arms rise sideways over your head; jump and close again — the jumping jack.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
