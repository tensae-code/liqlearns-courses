# Jumping and Hopping — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jumping on two feet
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — jumping on two feet — crouch, spring, land on both feet.

## Sub lesson 2 — The one-two jump pattern and the rings
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — a one-two jump pattern and two-foot jumps inside zigzag rings.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hopping on one foot
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — hopping on one foot, then landing on two feet or inside a circle.

## Sub lesson 4 — Jumping jacks and line jumps (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the jumping jack — jump open, jump closed — and forward-back jumps over a line.
