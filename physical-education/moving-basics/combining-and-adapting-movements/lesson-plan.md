# Lesson plan — Combining and Adapting Movements

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Combining and Adapting Movements
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Combining and adapting movements: joining skills into sequences and adapting them in complex, real and changing situations. — taught here through Indonesia's way: its Kurikulum Merdeka PJOK books for Grades 7–12.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Combinations and sequences of movement** — Indonesia's Grade 9 book teaches combinations and sequences of movement across sports — basketball catch-and-shoot, football dribble-pass, long jump, rhythmic and floor gymnastics, pencak silat, kasti, relay and freestyle swimming — and its teacher guide teaches how to teach them.
2. **Complex advanced movements** — The Grade 9 book teaches complex advanced movements such as trick shots and attacking and defending movement patterns.
3. **Applying movement in real situations** — The Grade 9 book teaches applying movement in real game situations — attacking play, rotating defence and evading in kasti.
4. **Body control and movement accuracy** — The Grade 9 book teaches body control and accuracy of movement.
5. **Adapting to challenging movement situations** — Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches adapting movement skills to challenging situations — running in varied conditions, throw-catch games and volleyball play — and its teacher guide teaches how to teach refining movement skills.
6. **Movement skills in complex situations** — Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches movement skills under complex, pressured situations — basketball under pressure, long jump and floor gymnastics.
7. **Innovating movement solutions** — The Grade 12 book teaches innovating movement solutions in changing situations — handball, badminton and aerobic gymnastics.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
