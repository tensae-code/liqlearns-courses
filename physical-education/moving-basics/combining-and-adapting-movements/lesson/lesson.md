# Combining and Adapting Movements

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Combinations and sequences of movement

Indonesia's Grade 9 book teaches combinations and sequences of movement across sports — basketball catch-and-shoot, football dribble-pass, long jump, rhythmic and floor gymnastics, pencak silat, kasti, relay and freestyle swimming — and its teacher guide teaches how to teach them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Complex advanced movements

The Grade 9 book teaches complex advanced movements such as trick shots and attacking and defending movement patterns.

## Sub lesson 3 — Applying movement in real situations

The Grade 9 book teaches applying movement in real game situations — attacking play, rotating defence and evading in kasti.

## Sub lesson 4 — Body control and movement accuracy

The Grade 9 book teaches body control and accuracy of movement.

## Sub lesson 5 — Adapting to challenging movement situations

Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches adapting movement skills to challenging situations — running in varied conditions, throw-catch games and volleyball play — and its teacher guide teaches how to teach refining movement skills.

## Sub lesson 6 — Movement skills in complex situations

Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches movement skills under complex, pressured situations — basketball under pressure, long jump and floor gymnastics.

## Sub lesson 7 — Innovating movement solutions

The Grade 12 book teaches innovating movement solutions in changing situations — handball, badminton and aerobic gymnastics.
