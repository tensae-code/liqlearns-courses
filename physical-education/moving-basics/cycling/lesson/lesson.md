## Sub lesson 1 — Cycling

North Macedonia's Grade 9 book teaches cycling: road cycling on asphalt (the Tour de France and Giro d'Italia named) with light narrow-tyred bicycles, and off-road/ridged-terrain cycling in nature, hills, mountains, parks and forests; cycling as transport and recreation.
