# Video script — How We Learn Movement Skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How do we learn a movement skill?
Move efficiently — like a swimmer's arm recovering cleanly over the water.
Use equipment that helps the movement.
And listen to feedback: from inside, from outside, and from the feel of your own body.
That is **how we learn movement skills**.
