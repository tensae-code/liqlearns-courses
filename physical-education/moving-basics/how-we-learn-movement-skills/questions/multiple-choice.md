# How We Learn Movement Skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's guide, the swimmer's arm recovery is an example of…
a) Biomechanical efficiency  b) A fitness test  c) A club meeting
**Answer: a) Biomechanical efficiency**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a factor affecting movement and performance in the guide?
a) Effective equipment  b) The weather forecast  c) The colour of the hall
**Answer: a) Effective equipment**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's three kinds of feedback in skill learning are…
a) Intrinsic, extrinsic and kinaesthetic  b) Written, oral and drawn  c) Fast, slow and stopped
**Answer: a) Intrinsic, extrinsic and kinaesthetic**
