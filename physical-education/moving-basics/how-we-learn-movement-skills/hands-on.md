# Hands-on — How We Learn Movement Skills

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Check a movement for wasted effort

Pick a simple movement you know — walking across the room, miming a swim stroke. Do it once loosely, then once trying to waste no effort: no extra swings, no extra steps. Say what changed. That difference is biomechanical efficiency, the guide's first factor.

## Sort the feedback

While a partner (or you yourself) performs a simple skill, collect one example of each feedback kind the guide names: intrinsic (what the mover senses), extrinsic (a word or result from outside) and kinaesthetic (the feel in the moving body). Write the three examples down, labelled.
