# Hands-on — Skipping, Galloping and Leaping

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the three travelling steps

On a clear, safe stretch of ground, practise each step in turn: skip with step-hops on alternate feet, gallop with one foot leading and the other brought rapidly up to it, and leap with a long step forward and a forward lean — the way Belize's Lower Division curriculum teaches them.
