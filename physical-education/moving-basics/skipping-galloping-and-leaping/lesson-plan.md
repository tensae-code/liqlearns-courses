# Lesson plan — Skipping, Galloping and Leaping

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Skipping, Galloping and Leaping
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Skipping, galloping and leaping: the travelling steps that carry you across the ground. — taught here through Belize's way: its Primary Curriculum Physical Education teacher curriculum books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Skipping** — Skipping — a series of step-hops on alternate feet, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 4), revisited in Grade 2 with music's contribution to movement (printed p. 45).
2. **Galloping** — Galloping — one foot leads and the other is brought rapidly up to it, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 5), revisited in Grade 2 (printed p. 46).
3. **Leaping** — Leaping — a slight jump with a long step forward and a forward lean, as Belize's Grade 2 book teaches it (printed p. 47), revisited in Grade 3 with a lean of about ten degrees (printed p. 96).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
