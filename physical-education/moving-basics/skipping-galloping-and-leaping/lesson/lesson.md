# Skipping, Galloping and Leaping

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Skipping

Skipping — a series of step-hops on alternate feet, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 4), revisited in Grade 2 with music's contribution to movement (printed p. 45).

## Sub lesson 2 — Galloping

Galloping — one foot leads and the other is brought rapidly up to it, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 5), revisited in Grade 2 (printed p. 46).

## Sub lesson 3 — Leaping

Leaping — a slight jump with a long step forward and a forward lean, as Belize's Grade 2 book teaches it (printed p. 47), revisited in Grade 3 with a lean of about ten degrees (printed p. 96).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — Skipping, galloping and leaping: the travelling steps that carry you across the ground. — in your own words, the way Belize's books teach it.
