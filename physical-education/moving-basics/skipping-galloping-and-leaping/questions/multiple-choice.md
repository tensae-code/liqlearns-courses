# Skipping, Galloping and Leaping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skipping — a series of step-hops on alternate feet, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 4), revisited in Grade 2 with music's contribution to movement (printed p. 45).
a) True as the books teach it  b) A swimming stroke  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Galloping — one foot leads and the other is brought rapidly up to it, as Belize's Grade 1 book teaches it (printed p. 5), revisited in Grade 2 (printed p. 46).
a) True as the books teach it  b) A swimming stroke  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leaping — a slight jump with a long step forward and a forward lean, as Belize's Grade 2 book teaches it (printed p. 47), revisited in Grade 3 with a lean of about ten degrees (printed p. 96).
a) True as the books teach it  b) A swimming stroke  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**
