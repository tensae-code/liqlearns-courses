# Swimming Basics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Turkmenistan book, swimming in the water is taught as part of…
a) All-round physical development  b) Examination technique  c) Team selection
**Answer: a) All-round physical development**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book places swimming inside…
a) Its exercise sets  b) Its glossary  c) Its closing tests
**Answer: a) Its exercise sets**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's section Suwda ýüzmegiň ähmiýeti is about…
a) The importance of swimming  b) The history of pools  c) Swimming races
**Answer: a) The importance of swimming**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Swimming matters, in the book's framing, because…
a) It is part of developing the whole body  b) Only athletes need it  c) It replaces all other exercise
**Answer: a) It is part of developing the whole body**
