# Swimming Basics — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the Turkmenistan book place swimming?
**Answer: Inside its exercise sets for all-round physical development (Part VIII §§8.14–8.15).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Turkmen title of the swimming section?
**Answer: Suwda ýüzmek (Swimming in the Water).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does swimming matter, as the book teaches it?
**Answer: It is part of all-round physical development — the development of the whole body.**
