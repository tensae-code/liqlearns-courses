# Swimming Basics

## Do it first

Can you swim? Whether you can or not, say what you think swimming does for the body — which parts of you work when you swim? Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Swimming in the water

Turkmenistan's *Bedenterbiýe* teaches **swimming in the water** (Suwda ýüzmek) inside its exercise sets for **all-round physical development** — the sets that develop the whole body, alongside the exercises for the feet and the leg muscles. Swimming takes its place there because moving through water works the whole body at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — The importance of swimming

The book gives swimming its own importance section (Suwda ýüzmegiň ähmiýeti): swimming **matters** — it is part of all-round physical development, the development of the whole person that the whole *Bedenterbiýe* course is built to give. That is why swimming belongs in everyone's physical education, not only in the training of athletes.
