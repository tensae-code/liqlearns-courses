# Lesson plan — Swimming Basics

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Swimming Basics
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Swimming basics: first steps in the water — getting in, floating, moving through water and playing in it, and why everyone should learn to swim.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Swimming in the water** — the Turkmenistan book: swimming as part of all-round physical development (Suwda ýüzmek).
2. **The importance of swimming** — the Turkmenistan book: why swimming matters (Suwda ýüzmegiň ähmiýeti).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Water Safety and Rescue; Walking in Patterns. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what swimming in the water is, as the Turkmenistan book teaches it: a part of all-round physical development.
2. Say why swimming matters — why it belongs in everyone's physical development.
3. Name swimming's place in the book's system: one of the exercise sets that develop the whole body.

## What you need
Nothing for the knowledge parts; any water practice is done only in safe, supervised water, as the Water Safety and Rescue lesson teaches — never alone in deep water.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner says whether they can swim and what they think swimming does for the body, before any explaining (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson to a companion in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).

5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swimming feels like a sport only → anchor it in the book's place for it: one of the all-round development exercise sets, for everybody.
- Why it matters blurs → it develops the whole body at once, in the water.
