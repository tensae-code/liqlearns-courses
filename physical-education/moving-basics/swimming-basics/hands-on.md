# Hands-on — Swimming Basics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. All land practice is dry practice; water practice only in safe, supervised water.

## The whole-body check — on land

Stand tall and name, one by one, the parts of you that work when you swim: arms, legs, back, chest, breathing. The Turkmenistan book places swimming among the all-round development exercises — check that claim against your own list: swimming works the whole body at once.

## Say why it matters

Tell a partner, in two sentences, why swimming matters — once as the book teaches it (part of all-round physical development), once in your own words. Your partner checks the book's reason is in your answer.
