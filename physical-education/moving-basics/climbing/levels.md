# Climbing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Climbing the A-frame ladder
Pass mark: say and show climbing the A-frame ladder: up one foot at a time, up alternating feet, and down again (only with a helper).

## Sub lesson 2 — Clambering over obstacles and the ladder
Pass mark: say and show clambering: over an obstacle and over the A-frame ladder, then combining climbing and clambering on a course.
