# Hands-on — Climbing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Climb with a helper

With a helper standing beside the ladder, climb up one foot at a time and down again, the way the lesson describes. Then climb alternating feet. Never climb without the helper.

## Clamber the course

Clamber over a low obstacle and over the ladder in one course, combining climbing and clambering as the book's second lesson teaches.
