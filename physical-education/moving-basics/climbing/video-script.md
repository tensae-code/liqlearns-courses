# Video script — Climbing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climbing — up the ladder and over the frame, the way Vietnam's Grade 5 book teaches it.
Up the A-frame ladder — one foot at a time, then alternating feet — and down again, always with a helper.
Then clambering — over an obstacle and over the ladder — and both together on a course.
That is **climbing**.
