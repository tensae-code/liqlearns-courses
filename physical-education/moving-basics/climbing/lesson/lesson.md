# Climbing

## Do it first

Look at a ladder or a climbing frame. Going up one foot at a time is only the start — this lesson teaches climbing and clambering the way Vietnam's Grade 5 book teaches them, always with a helper.

## Sub lesson 1 — Climbing the A-frame ladder

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 5 GDTC 5* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **climbing the A-frame ladder: up one foot at a time, up alternating feet, and down again (only with a helper)**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clambering over obstacles and the ladder

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 5 GDTC 5* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **clambering: over an obstacle and over the A-frame ladder, then combining climbing and clambering on a course**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches, in the books' own order, the way this lesson taught you.
