# Climbing — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climbing the A-frame ladder is practised…
a) Only with a helper  b) Alone, as fast as possible  c) With your eyes closed
**Answer: a) Only with a helper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first ladder pattern is…
a) Up one foot at a time  b) Up two feet together  c) Up backwards
**Answer: a) Up one foot at a time**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clambering means…
a) Going over an obstacle and over the ladder  b) Hanging still from the ladder  c) Climbing with a rope only
**Answer: a) Going over an obstacle and over the ladder**
