# Hands-on — Controlling My Body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Still and moving

Hold your left arm still. Move your head, trunk and right arm. Swap sides — the Grade 1 guide's own control task.

## Two sides, two jobs

Pat your head with one hand while the other hand circles your stomach; walk while clapping a different rhythm — the Grade 2 task of different movements at the same time.

## Both sides with implements

Hold, move, hit, catch, throw and drive a ball, hoop or baton with your left side, then your right — the Grade 3 bilateral task.
