# Controlling My Body — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Controlling and stopping movements
Pass mark: say that controlling movement and holding one body segment still while others move, as El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Different movements at the same time
Pass mark: say that moving left and right arms, hands, legs and feet differently at the same time, as El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bilateral efficiency
Pass mark: say that holding, moving, hitting, catching, throwing and driving implements — balls, hoops, batons, wheels, rackets — with both hands and feet, as El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Independence of every part
Pass mark: say that independence of lower limbs from upper limbs in physical activity, as El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide teaches it.
