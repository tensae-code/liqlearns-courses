# Controlling My Body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Controlling my body means…
a) Moving body parts independently, and controlling and stopping movements  b) Always moving everything at once  c) Never stopping
**Answer: a) Moving body parts independently, and controlling and stopping movements**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 lesson teaches holding…
a) One body segment still while others move  b) A ball with two hands only  c) A rope without moving
**Answer: a) One body segment still while others move**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Grade 2 lesson, left and right arms, hands, legs and feet…
a) Move differently at the same time  b) Must always copy each other  c) Stay frozen
**Answer: a) Move differently at the same time**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Bilateral efficiency in Grade 3 means working implements…
a) With both hands and feet  b) With the favourite hand only  c) Without touching them
**Answer: a) With both hands and feet**
