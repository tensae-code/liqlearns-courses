# Controlling My Body — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** What independence does the Grade 3 Lesson 6 teach?
**Answer: Independence of the lower limbs from the upper limbs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two implements the Grade 3 bilateral lesson names.
**Answer: Any two of: balls, hoops, batons, wheels, rackets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides starting a movement, what two things does control include?
**Answer: Stopping the movement, and holding one segment still while others move.**
