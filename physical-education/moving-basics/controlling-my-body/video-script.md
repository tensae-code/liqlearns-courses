# Video script — Controlling My Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Move one part. Hold the rest still.
Left does one thing. Right does another.
Both hands. Both feet. Every implement.
Legs and arms — each doing its own work.
That is **controlling my body**.
