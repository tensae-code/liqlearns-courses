# Pencak Silat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pencak silat punches is:
a) Straight and circular punches  b) Something the books do not teach  c) Chosen by the referee
**Answer: a) Straight and circular punches**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pencak silat kicks and sweeps is:
a) The T-kick, the back kick and sweeps  b) Something the books do not teach  c) Chosen by the referee
**Answer: a) The T-kick, the back kick and sweeps**
