# Lesson plan — Pencak Silat

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Pencak Silat
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pencak silat: Indonesia's martial art — punches, kicks and sweeps. — taught here through Indonesia's way: its Kurikulum Merdeka PJOK books for Grades 7–12.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Pencak silat punches** — Indonesia's Grade 12 teacher guide teaches pencak silat punches — straight and circular punches.
2. **Pencak silat kicks and sweeps** — The guide teaches pencak silat kicks and sweeps — the T-kick, the back kick and sweeps.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the book teaches in pencak silat punches, as filed.
2. Say what the book teaches in pencak silat kicks and sweeps, as filed.

## What you need
Nothing is bought for this lesson — paper and a pencil for the hands-on tasks, and a safe space to move.
