# Camping and Survival Outdoors

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bloque IV Secuencia 2

Bloque IV Secuencia 2 — survival in the natural environment: the survival kit, choosing a safe camp site, improvised shelters (lean-to, fallen tree, A-frame), bedding, and making and keeping a fire

## Sub lesson 2 — Bloque IV Secuencia 1

Bloque IV Secuencia 1 — camp craft: knots and lashings (nudos y amarres) with rope and round wood, drawing a sketch map (croquis) of a place, and fire, fireplaces and campfires (fuego, fogones y fogatas)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — Camping and survival outdoors: camps, shelters, fire, camp equipment, outdoor trips and caring for nature while you are out. — in your own words, the way Honduras's books teach it.
