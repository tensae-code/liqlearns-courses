# Camping and Survival Outdoors — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bloque IV Secuencia 2
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: survival in the natural environment: the survival kit, choosing a safe camp site, improvised shelters (lean-to, fallen tree, A-frame), bedding, and making and keeping a fire.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bloque IV Secuencia 1
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: camp craft: knots and lashings (nudos y amarres) with rope and round wood, drawing a sketch map (croquis) of a place, and fire, fireplaces and campfires (fuego, fogones y fogatas).
