# Hands-on — Camping and Survival Outdoors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Choose a camp site and make a shelter

With your class or family, choose a safe camp site the way Honduras's Grade 8 book teaches it, lay out the survival kit, and build an improvised shelter — a lean-to, a fallen-tree shelter or an A-frame — then practise the camp craft of the Grade 7 guide: knots and lashings with rope, a sketch map (croquis) of the place, and a safe campfire place.
