# Camping and Survival Outdoors — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Bloque IV Secuencia 2 — survival in the natural environment: the survival kit, choosing a safe camp site, improvised shelters (lean-to, fallen tree, A-frame), bedding, and making and keeping a fire**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Bloque IV Secuencia 1 — camp craft: knots and lashings (nudos y amarres) with rope and round wood, drawing a sketch map (croquis) of a place, and fire, fireplaces and campfires (fuego, fogones y fogatas)**
