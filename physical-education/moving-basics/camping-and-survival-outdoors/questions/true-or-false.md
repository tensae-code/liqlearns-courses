# Camping and Survival Outdoors — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Survival in the natural environment: the survival kit, choosing a safe camp site, improvised shelters (lean-to, fallen tree, A-frame), bedding, and making and keeping a fire.
**Answer: True**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Camp craft: knots and lashings (nudos y amarres) with rope and round wood, drawing a sketch map (croquis) of a place, and fire, fireplaces and campfires (fuego, fogones y fogatas).
**Answer: True**
