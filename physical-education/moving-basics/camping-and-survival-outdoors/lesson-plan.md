# Lesson plan — Camping and Survival Outdoors

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Camping and Survival Outdoors
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Camping and survival outdoors: camps, shelters, fire, camp equipment, outdoor trips and caring for nature while you are out. — taught here through Honduras's way: its Telebásica books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Bloque IV Secuencia 2** — Bloque IV Secuencia 2 — survival in the natural environment: the survival kit, choosing a safe camp site, improvised shelters (lean-to, fallen tree, A-frame), bedding, and making and keeping a fire
2. **Bloque IV Secuencia 1** — Bloque IV Secuencia 1 — camp craft: knots and lashings (nudos y amarres) with rope and round wood, drawing a sketch map (croquis) of a place, and fire, fireplaces and campfires (fuego, fogones y fogatas)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
