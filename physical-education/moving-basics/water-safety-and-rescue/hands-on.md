# Hands-on — Water Safety and Rescue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. All practice is dry practice, on land.

## Reach and throw — on land

Lay a line on the ground for the pool edge. A partner lies beyond it as the swimmer in trouble. From behind the line: first reach with a long object, then throw a float aid — the Zambia module improvises with sealed bottles as float aids. Say the order aloud as you work: Reach–Throw–Don't Go. Never cross the line yourself.

## The pool-rules walk

Walk — do not run — a short course while calling the pool rules in order: shower before entering; no spitting or urination in the water; walk, never run; know the depth; do not swim when sick.
