# Lesson plan — Water Safety and Rescue

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Water Safety and Rescue
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Water safety and rescue: staying safe in pools, lakes and rivers, survival swimming, rescue steps, resuscitation and pool hygiene.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## Sub lessons
1. **Water safety** — the Zambia module: preventing drowning in pools, lakes and rivers — lifeguard rescue with a float aid without entering the water, Reach–Throw–Don't Go, CPR, rip currents, pool safety and waterborne illnesses.
2. **The pool — hygiene and safe behaviour** — the Zambia module: shower before entering, no spitting or urination in the water, walking not running, depth awareness, and not swimming when sick.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Walking in Patterns; Running Basics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how drowning is prevented in pools, lakes and rivers.
2. Show the rescue order: Reach–Throw–Don't Go, and a lifeguard rescue with a float aid without entering the water.
3. Name CPR as the resuscitation step, and say what a rip current is a danger of.
4. Keep a pool clean and safe: shower before entering, never spit or urinate in the water, walk — never run — and know the depth.
5. Say why you do not swim when sick, and name waterborne illnesses as a pool-safety danger.

## What you need
Nothing for the knowledge parts; the hands-on rescue practice is done dry, on land, with a float aid or a sealed bottle as the module improvises it — never by entering deep water unsupervised.

## If the learner is stuck
- The rescue order blurs → say it as three short words: Reach. Throw. Don't Go.
- Pool hygiene blurs → clean body in, clean water kept: shower first, nothing of yours into the water.
