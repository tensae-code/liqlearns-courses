# Video script — Water Safety and Rescue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water can kill. So we learn its rules first.
Reach. Throw. Don't go.
Help from the side, with a float — do not jump in after them.
Shower before the pool. Walk, never run. Know the depth.
That is **water safety and rescue**.
