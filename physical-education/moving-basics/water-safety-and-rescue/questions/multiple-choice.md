# Water Safety and Rescue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rescue order the module teaches is…
a) Reach–Throw–Don't Go  b) Go–Swim–Pull  c) Shout–Jump–Dive
**Answer: a) Reach–Throw–Don't Go**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A lifeguard rescue with a float aid is done…
a) Without entering the water  b) By diving in headfirst  c) Alone, in deep water
**Answer: a) Without entering the water**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** CPR is…
a) The resuscitation step  b) A swimming stroke  c) A kind of float
**Answer: a) The resuscitation step**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before entering a pool you should…
a) Shower  b) Run  c) Spit in the water
**Answer: a) Shower**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Around a pool you should…
a) Walk, not run  b) Run, not walk  c) Dive from the edge anywhere
**Answer: a) Walk, not run**
