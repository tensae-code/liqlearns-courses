# Water Safety and Rescue — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three places the module's water-safety topic covers.
**Answer: Pools, lakes and rivers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one danger the module names besides drowning itself.
**Answer: Rip currents (or: waterborne illnesses).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must you know the depth of the water?
**Answer: Depth awareness — so you know how deep the water is before you are in it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When should you not swim?
**Answer: When you are sick.**
