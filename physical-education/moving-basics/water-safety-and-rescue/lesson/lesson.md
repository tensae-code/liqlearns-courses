# Water Safety and Rescue

## Do it first

Someone is in trouble in the water. Three actions are on the table: reach to them, throw them something that floats, go in after them. Put them in the safe order — and decide which one you should *not* do. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water safety

Zambia's module teaches **water safety** as **preventing drowning in pools, lakes and rivers**. The rescue it teaches keeps the rescuer out of the water: a **lifeguard rescue with a float aid without entering the water**, summed up in the order **Reach–Throw–Don't Go** — reach to the person, throw them a float, and do not go in after them.

The module names **CPR** — the resuscitation step for a person brought out of the water — and the dangers to know: **rip currents**, **pool safety**, and **waterborne illnesses**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The pool — hygiene and safe behaviour

The module's pool rules are plain. **Shower before entering** the water. **No spitting or urination in the water.** **Walking, not running**, around the pool. **Depth awareness** — know how deep the water is before you are in it. And **not swimming when sick**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the rescue order — Reach–Throw–Don't Go — and why the rescuer stays out of the water, then list the five pool rules from Sub lesson 2.
