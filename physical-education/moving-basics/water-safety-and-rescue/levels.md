# Water Safety and Rescue — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water safety
Pass mark: say how drowning is prevented in pools, lakes and rivers; give the Reach–Throw–Don't Go order and the float-aid rescue without entering the water; name CPR, rip currents and waterborne illnesses as the dangers and answers the module names.

## Sub lesson 2 — The pool — hygiene and safe behaviour
Pass mark: list the pool rules — shower before entering, no spitting or urination in the water, walk do not run, know the depth, and do not swim when sick.
