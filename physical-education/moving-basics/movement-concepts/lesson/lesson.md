# Movement Concepts

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Time, force and flow in rhythmic performance

In Ball Rhythmics, time, force and flow are named and graded (slow/slower/slowest, light/lighter/lightest, smoothness of movement) as the qualities every rhythmic performance is judged by (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 6, printed pp. 22–23).

## Sub lesson 2 — Time, force and flow in a race and a sack race

Time, force and flow read from a 400-metre race story (lanes, pacing, who finishes where and why) and observed in the sack race's direction and speed (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 3.1.1, printed p. 349).
