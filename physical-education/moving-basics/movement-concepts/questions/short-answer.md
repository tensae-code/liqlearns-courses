# Movement Concepts — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three movement qualities and how each is graded.
**Answer: In Ball Rhythmics, time, force and flow are named and graded (slow/slower/slowest, light/lighter/lightest, smoothness of movement) as the qualities every rhythmic performance is judged by (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 6, printed pp. 22–23).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say where time, force and flow are observed in the Grade 2 book.
**Answer: Time, force and flow read from a 400-metre race story (lanes, pacing, who finishes where and why) and observed in the sack race's direction and speed (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 3.1.1, printed p. 349).**
