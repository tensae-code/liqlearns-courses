# Movement Concepts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are time, force and flow in movement?
a) Qualities of movement — how fast, how strong, and how smooth  b) Three ball games  c) Three dance costumes
**Answer: a) Qualities of movement — how fast, how strong, and how smooth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the Grade 2 book observe time, force and flow?
a) In a 400-metre race story and in the sack race  b) Only in a song  c) Only in sitting still
**Answer: a) In a 400-metre race story and in the sack race**
