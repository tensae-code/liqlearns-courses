# Sliding and Changing Direction — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Sliding sideways — a
2. Changing direction on shapes — b
3. Changing direction at speed, combined — c
4. Turning at the cones (teacher guide — evidence) — d
5. Change direction, roll to the target, obstacle walking (teacher guide — evidence) — e

What each teaches:
a) sliding sideways — step, close, step — left and right
b) running the marked numbers, letters and rectangle and changing direction at the corners
c) running fast, combined and correctly, changing direction without stopping
d) sprinting between cones and turning sharply at each cone
e) combined movement in one flow — direction change at a signal, a roll to a target, and walking an obstacle line

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
