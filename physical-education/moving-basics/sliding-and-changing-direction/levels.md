# Sliding and Changing Direction — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sliding sideways
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sliding sideways — step, close, step — left and right.

## Sub lesson 2 — Changing direction on shapes
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — running the marked numbers, letters and rectangle and changing direction at the corners.

## Sub lesson 3 — Changing direction at speed, combined
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — running fast, combined and correctly, changing direction without stopping.

## Sub lesson 4 — Turning at the cones (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sprinting between cones and turning sharply at each cone.

## Sub lesson 5 — Change direction, roll to the target, obstacle walking (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner changes direction at a signal without losing balance, steps and rolls to a target on a mat, and walks a full obstacle line without knocking an obstacle.
