# Sliding and Changing Direction

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Slide sideways to the left and to the right: step, close, step. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sliding sideways

Slide sideways to the left and to the right: step, close, step. Your feet do not cross. You travel, but you keep facing the same way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Changing direction on shapes

Run fast over shapes marked on the ground — the numbers 5 and 7, the letters ha, ba and ra, and a rectangle — and change direction sharply at their corners.

## Sub lesson 3 — Changing direction at speed, combined

Run fast and change direction sharply without stopping. In Grade 3 the basic skills are done fast, correctly, and combined, while changing direction, working together in a group.

## Sub lesson 4 — Turning at the cones (teacher guide — evidence)

Sprint to the first cone, turn, sprint to the next cone, turn, and sprint on, following the T-shaped cone course in the Grade 4 teacher guide.

## Sub lesson 5 — Change direction, roll to the target, obstacle walking (teacher guide — evidence)

This is የተቀናጀ መሠረታዊ የእንቅስቃሴ ክህሎት — combined fundamental movement skill: joining movements into one flowing action, without stopping between them.

**Change direction at the signal — አቅጣጫን በመቀየር ጠብ ጠብ ማለት.** Run. At the signal, change direction sharply — and do not lose your balance. Your feet turn, your body follows, and you run on. A learner who cannot run does this same movement from a wheelchair (Wheelchair), with the teacher's help — the direction change is the skill, however you travel.

[Picture 34902 — changing direction sharply at a signal]

**Roll to the target — ጠብ ጠብ በማለት ዒላማ ያለው ግልቢያ.** Step and dab toward your target marker, then roll — ግልቢያ — to it, on a mat, and finish standing at the target. The steps carry you near; the roll lands you there.

[GIF 34904 — step, step, roll to the target]

**Obstacle walking — በመስናክል መራመድ.** Walk the obstacle line: obstacles are laid in a row (cones in the middle), and you step over and around each one (በመስናክል) without knocking them, keeping your balance from the first to the last.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
