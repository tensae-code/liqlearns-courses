# Hands-on — Sliding and Changing Direction

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: sliding sideways, changing direction on shapes, changing direction at speed, combined, turning at the cones (teacher guide — evidence).

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.

## The Grade 3 teacher-guide part

**Signal turns.** A partner claps or calls; the runner changes direction at every signal, keeping balance. Swap the caller. A learner using a wheelchair turns the chair sharply at the signal with a helper nearby — same skill, same game.

**The obstacle line.** Lay cones, stones or bags in a row. Walk it stepping over and around each one. Then roll to a target flag on a mat at the end of a short stepping run.
