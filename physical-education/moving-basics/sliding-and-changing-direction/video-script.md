# Video script — Sliding and Changing Direction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Sliding sideways. Slide sideways to the left and to the right: step, close, step.

Changing direction on shapes. Run fast over shapes marked on the ground — the numbers 5 and 7, the letters ha, ba and ra, and a rectangle — and change direction sharply at their corners.

Changing direction at speed, combined. Run fast and change direction sharply without stopping.

Turning at the cones (teacher guide — evidence). Sprint to the first cone, turn, sprint to the next cone, turn, and sprint on, following the T-shaped cone course in the Grade 4 teacher guide.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
