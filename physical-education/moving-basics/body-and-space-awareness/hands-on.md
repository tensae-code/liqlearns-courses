# Hands-on — Body and Space Awareness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Command voices — find your way

A partner gives the commands, as Mozambique's manual teaches orientation: turn left, turn right, step forward, step back, stop. Move only on the command, and stop the moment the command says stop. Then swap: you give the commands, your partner finds their way.

## My space, our space

Stand in your own personal space — arms out, turn slowly, that circle is yours. Then walk the shared space with the whole group: keep moving, keep out of everyone else's circle, and never bump.
