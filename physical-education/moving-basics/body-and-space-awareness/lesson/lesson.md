# Body and Space Awareness

## Do it first

Stand up. Point to your left. Point to your right. Take one step forward, one step back. You have just used the thing this lesson teaches — before anyone explains it. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding your way in space

**Body and space awareness** is knowing **where you are in space**: your **left** and your **right**, the **directions** you can move in, your **personal space** — the space that is yours — and the **shared space** everyone moves in together.

Mozambique's Grade 1 manual teaches this as **Orientação** — **spatial orientation exercises** — inside its **organisation and control exercises**, the same family as its formations. The exercises are done **to command voices**: the teacher commands, and the children find their way in space on the command.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four parts of body and space awareness — left and right, directions, personal space, shared space — and how Mozambique's manual teaches orientation: exercises done to command voices.
