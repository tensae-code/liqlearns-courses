# Lesson plan — Body and Space Awareness

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Moving Basics · **Lesson:** Body and Space Awareness
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Moving Basics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Body and space awareness: where I am in space — left and right, directions, personal space and shared space.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Finding your way in space** — the Mozambique manual: spatial orientation exercises (Orientação), done to command voices, inside the organisation and control exercises.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Walking in Patterns; Running Basics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what body and space awareness is: knowing where you are in space.
2. Name the parts of it: left and right, directions, personal space and shared space.
3. Say how Mozambique's Grade 1 manual teaches it: as Orientação — spatial orientation exercises done to command voices.
4. Say which family those exercises belong to: the organisation and control exercises.

## What you need
Nothing — a space to move in and a voice giving the commands, as the manual teaches it.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner points left, points right, and steps into their own space before any explaining (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Left and right blur → raise the hand you write with and name it, then name the other.
- Personal and shared space blur → your own bubble is personal space; the ground everyone moves on together is shared space.
