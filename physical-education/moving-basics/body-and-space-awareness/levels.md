# Body and Space Awareness — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding your way in space
Pass mark: say that body and space awareness is knowing where you are in space — left and right, directions, personal space and shared space — and that Mozambique's Grade 1 manual teaches it as Orientação, spatial orientation exercises done to command voices, inside the organisation and control exercises.
