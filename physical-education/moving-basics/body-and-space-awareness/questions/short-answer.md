# Body and Space Awareness — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four parts of body and space awareness.
**Answer: Left and right, directions, personal space and shared space.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Portuguese name the Mozambique manual gives the spatial orientation exercises?
**Answer: Orientação.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which family of exercises does Orientação belong to in the manual?
**Answer: The organisation and control exercises (Exercícios de organização e controlo).**
