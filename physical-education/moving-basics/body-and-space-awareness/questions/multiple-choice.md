# Body and Space Awareness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Body and space awareness is…
a) Knowing where you are in space  b) Running as fast as you can  c) Throwing a ball far
**Answer: a) Knowing where you are in space**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mozambique's Grade 1 manual teaches spatial orientation as…
a) Orientação, inside the organisation and control exercises  b) A ball game  c) A swimming lesson
**Answer: a) Orientação, inside the organisation and control exercises**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The orientation exercises are done to…
a) Command voices  b) Music only  c) A whistle league table
**Answer: a) Command voices**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal space is…
a) The space that is yours  b) The whole playground  c) The teacher's desk
**Answer: a) The space that is yours**
