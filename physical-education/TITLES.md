# Physical Education course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Physical Education is one world course: the moving body is the
same everywhere, and each country's way of teaching it shows
inside as examples. The first stocked part is built from
Ethiopia's Health and Physical Education books — the strand of
those books that teaches movement, games and sport. The path
climbs from the first patterns a small child can copy to the
trained skills of the older student.

**Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics →
Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and
Dances → Sport and Its Story**

That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" path points
down this ladder).

The titles, in ladder order:

1. **Moving Basics** — the first patterns: walking in patterns,
   sliding, running basics, moving to a beat.
2. **Fitness and Stretching** — what fitness is, heart-and-breathing
   fitness, stretching and flexibility, staying safe while
   exercising.
3. **Gymnastics** — positions, rolls and supports, apparatus work,
   rhythmic movement.
4. **Athletics** — sprinting and the start commands, timed
   running, the long jump, throwing.
5. **Ball Games** — passing inside and outside the foot, the
   forearm pass, setting, small-sided games that put the skills
   together.
6. **Games to Play** — the books' group games: games for
   self-control, cooperation, communication, awareness, careful
   thinking and self-leadership.
7. **Traditional Games and Dances** — Ethiopia's own games and
   dances, locality by locality, each named with its home area.
8. **Sport and Its Story** — what physical education is and where
   it came from, the Olympic Games ancient and modern, Ethiopia's
   famous athletes.
