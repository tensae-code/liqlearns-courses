# Physical Education course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Physical Education is one world course, organised by **levels,
not grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path climbs from the first movement
patterns to trained skills (see TITLES.md): **Moving Basics →
Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games →
Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its
Story**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part from
Ethiopia's Health and Physical Education books — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## Unit 1 — Moving Basics

1. **Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements** (`moving-basics/combined-travelling-and-on-the-spot`) — joining travelling movements together, joining on-the-spot movements together, and chaining the two families in one sequence.
2. **Jumping and Hopping** (`moving-basics/jumping-and-hopping`) — jumping on two feet, hopping on one foot, and landing safely.
3. **Running Basics** (`moving-basics/running-basics`) — running on the spot, running with wide arms, and shuttle running back and forth.
4. **Sliding and Changing Direction** (`moving-basics/sliding-and-changing-direction`) — sliding sideways, and running fast while changing direction at markers and shapes.
5. **Walking in Patterns** (`moving-basics/walking-in-patterns`) — walking in patterns — in pairs and groups, and along lines and shapes on the ground — without breaking the hand-hold.

## Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching

1. **Agility — Rising, Running and Quick Changes** (`fitness-and-stretching/agility`) — agility — rising quickly from the ground and running, and quick line drills with sharp turns.
2. **Heart and Breathing Fitness** (`fitness-and-stretching/heart-and-breathing-fitness`) — what heart and breathing fitness is, and the steady movements that build it — so you can walk and run without tiring.
3. **Muscle Strength and Endurance** (`fitness-and-stretching/muscle-strength-and-endurance`) — what muscle endurance is, and the movements that make legs, arms and stomach muscles work for a long time without tiring.
4. **Stretching and Flexibility** (`fitness-and-stretching/stretching-and-flexibility`) — stretching and flexibility — what they are, why they prevent injury, and the stretches for neck, waist, arms and legs.

## Unit 3 — Gymnastics

1. **Balance and Calisthenics** (`gymnastics/balance-and-calisthenics`) — holding your balance without falling, and calisthenics sets for arms, shoulders and legs.
2. **Counted Gymnastics Sequences** (`gymnastics/counted-gymnastics-sequences`) — gymnastics done to counts — waist, arm and leg series performed position by position on the numbers.
3. **Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports** (`gymnastics/gymnastics-basics-rolls-and-supports`) — what gymnastics is, the basic positions, and rolls and supports done safely on a mat.
4. **Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement** (`gymnastics/moving-to-a-beat`) — moving in sequence to a beat — simple sequences first, then continuous, progressive, with equipment, and finally creating your own.

## Unit 4 — Athletics

1. **Sprinting and Hurdle Running** (`athletics/sprinting-and-hurdle-running`) — starting fast on a signal, sprinting in running games, and clearing low hurdles without breaking stride.

## Unit 5 — Ball Games

1. **Ball Handling on the Spot** (`ball-games/ball-handling-on-the-spot`) — passing, throwing, catching and bouncing a ball without travelling — the on-the-spot ball skills.
2. **Kicking, Passing and Dribbling** (`ball-games/kicking-passing-and-dribbling`) — kicking and passing with the feet, controlling a ball close, dribbling in a zigzag, and the first net-ball skills.
