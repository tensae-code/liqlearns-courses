# Physical Education course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Physical Education is one world course, organised by **levels,
not grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path climbs from the first movement
patterns to trained skills (see TITLES.md): **Moving Basics →
Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games →
Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its
Story**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part from
Ethiopia's Health and Physical Education books — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## Unit 1 — Moving Basics

1. **Combined Travelling and On-the-Spot Movements** (`moving-basics/combined-travelling-and-on-the-spot`) — joining travelling movements together, joining on-the-spot movements together, and chaining the two families in one sequence.
2. **Jumping and Hopping** (`moving-basics/jumping-and-hopping`) — jumping on two feet, hopping on one foot, and landing safely.
3. **Running Basics** (`moving-basics/running-basics`) — running on the spot, running with wide arms, and shuttle running back and forth.
4. **Sliding and Changing Direction** (`moving-basics/sliding-and-changing-direction`) — sliding sideways, and running fast while changing direction at markers and shapes.
5. **Walking in Patterns** (`moving-basics/walking-in-patterns`) — walking in patterns — in pairs and groups, and along lines and shapes on the ground — without breaking the hand-hold.
6. **Water Safety and Rescue** (`moving-basics/water-safety-and-rescue`) — staying safe in pools, lakes and rivers, rescue steps, resuscitation and pool hygiene — preventing drowning, lifeguard rescue with a float aid without entering the water, Reach–Throw–Don't Go, CPR, rip currents, and the pool rules: shower before entering, walking not running, depth awareness, not swimming when sick (Zambia's Form 1 teacher module, evidence only).
7. **Body and Space Awareness** (`moving-basics/body-and-space-awareness`) — where I am in space — left and right, directions, personal space and shared space — taught in Mozambique's Grade 1 manual as Orientação, spatial orientation exercises done to command voices, inside the organisation and control exercises (Mozambique's Grade 1 teacher manual, evidence only).
8. **Swimming Basics** (`moving-basics/swimming-basics`) — swimming basics: first steps in the water — getting in, floating, moving through water and playing in it, and why everyone should learn to swim — taught in Turkmenistan's Bedenterbiýe as swimming in the water (Suwda ýüzmek) and the importance of swimming (Suwda ýüzmegiň ähmiýeti), part of all-round physical development (Turkmenistan's student book).
9. **How We Learn Movement Skills** (`moving-basics/how-we-learn-movement-skills`) — how we learn movement skills: the factors affecting movement and performance — biomechanical efficiency (the swimmer's arm recovery) and effective equipment — and feedback in skill learning: intrinsic, extrinsic and kinaesthetic (Samoa's Years 11–12 teacher guide, evidence only).
10. **Correct Posture** (`moving-basics/correct-posture`) — correct posture: holding the body well standing, sitting, walking, lifting and in sport — and recognising and correcting posture faults — taught in Pakistan's Grade 6 book as Posture, correct and faulty posture, inside the skeletal system and muscles chapter (Pakistan's student book).
11. **Ice Skating** (`moving-basics/ice-skating`) — ice skating: the sport and its technique — taught in China's Senior High book as ice skating (滑冰) (China's student book).
12. **Roller Skating** (`moving-basics/roller-skating`) — roller skating: choosing skates and protective gear, safe falling, standing up and basic technique — taught in China's Senior High book as roller skating (轮滑): safe falling and standing up, and basic technique (China's student book).
13. **Knowing My Body and How It Moves** (`moving-basics/knowing-my-body-and-how-it-moves`) — knowing my body and how it moves: body parts, bones and joints, and the movements the body can make — taught in El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide as Conociendo mi cuerpo (Grade 1: body schema and body image, the senses guiding movement), Conociendo y moviendo mi cuerpo (Grade 2: body structure and joint movement) and Muevo mi cuerpo globalmente y por partes (Grade 3: moving whole and by segments, the reflex-arc response) (El Salvador's teacher guide, evidence only).
14. **Controlling My Body** (`moving-basics/controlling-my-body`) — controlling my body: moving body parts independently, controlling and stopping movements, and turning and spinning — taught in El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide as holding one body segment still while others move (Grade 1), moving left and right sides differently at the same time (Grade 2), and bilateral efficiency with implements and independence of lower limbs from upper limbs (Grade 3) (El Salvador's teacher guide, evidence only).
15. **Drill, Marching and Formations** (`moving-basics/drill-marching-and-formations`) — drill, marching and formations: falling in, attention and at-ease, turns, marching, and standing in rows, lines and circles — taught in El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide as ¡Qué fácil organizarnos en grupo! (Grade 1): organising into pairs, trios, rows, columns and circles (El Salvador's teacher guide, evidence only).
16. **Movement Concepts** (`moving-basics/movement-concepts`) — movement concepts: body, space, effort (time, force and flow) and relationships in movement — taught in the Philippines' Grade 3 book as the movement targets of Ball Rhythmics (time, force and flow named and graded) and in its Grade 2 book as Oras, Lakas at Daloy, read from a 400-metre race story and the sack race (Philippines' student books).

## Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching

1. **Agility — Rising, Running and Quick Changes** (`fitness-and-stretching/agility`) — agility — rising quickly from the ground and running, and quick line drills with sharp turns.
2. **Heart and Breathing Fitness** (`fitness-and-stretching/heart-and-breathing-fitness`) — what heart and breathing fitness is, and the steady movements that build it — so you can walk and run without tiring.
3. **How Training Works** (`fitness-and-stretching/how-training-works`) — how training works — the four rules of training, and the methods you choose by goal.
4. **Muscle Strength and Endurance** (`fitness-and-stretching/muscle-strength-and-endurance`) — what muscle endurance is, and the movements that make legs, arms and stomach muscles work for a long time without tiring.
5. **Stretching and Flexibility** (`fitness-and-stretching/stretching-and-flexibility`) — stretching and flexibility — what they are, why they prevent injury, and the stretches for neck, waist, arms and legs.
6. **Staying Active for Life** (`fitness-and-stretching/staying-active-for-life`) — staying active for life: regular participation, activity outdoors and in the community, and keeping fitness for the long term — taught in Tajikistan's Grades 1–4 book as Your Sports Corner, setting up your own sports corner at home with a skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises (Tajikistan's student book).
7. **The Body in Exercise** (`fitness-and-stretching/the-body-in-exercise`) — the body in exercise: bones, joints, muscles and the body systems that produce movement — taught in the United Kingdom's GCSE book as the structure and functions of the musculo-skeletal system (United Kingdom's student book).
8. **Biomechanics in Sport** (`fitness-and-stretching/biomechanics-in-sport`) — biomechanics in sport: forces, levers, planes of movement, projectile flight, and analysing and correcting technique — taught in the United Kingdom's GCSE book as lever systems, mechanical advantage, and planes and axes of movement (United Kingdom's student book).
9. **Testing Your Fitness** (`fitness-and-stretching/testing-your-fitness`) — testing your fitness: the standard fitness tests, measuring the body, and reading your results — taught in the United Kingdom's GCSE book as fitness testing, its protocols and using its data (United Kingdom's student book).
10. **Sports Nutrition and Hydration** (`fitness-and-stretching/sports-nutrition-and-hydration`) — sports nutrition and hydration: what to eat and drink for exercise, before, during and after competition — taught in the United Kingdom's GCSE book as energy use, diet, nutrition and hydration: energy balance, a balanced diet, and water and electrolytes for performers (United Kingdom's student book).
11. **Warming Up and Cooling Down** (`fitness-and-stretching/warming-up-and-cooling-down`) — warming up and cooling down: preparing the body for exercise, and relaxing and returning to calm afterwards — taught in Pakistan's Grade 10 book as Warm-Up and Warm-Down (Cool-Down) Activities for Sports (Pakistan's student book).
12. **Planning Your Own Training** (`fitness-and-stretching/planning-your-own-training`) — planning your own training: fitness plans, FITT, training zones and cycles, and designing training from your goals and data — taught in Pakistan's Grade 11 book as the Fitness Plan (Pakistan's student book).
13. **Adapted Physical Education** (`fitness-and-stretching/adapted-physical-education`) — adapted physical education: adjusting activity so every child — including children with disabilities — can take part — taught in Afghanistan's Grade 10 guide as PE for children with disabilities: who needs adapted PE, and how activity is adjusted for children with intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, and children who are withdrawn or afraid of activity (Afghanistan's Grade 10 teacher guide, evidence only).
14. **Rope Skipping** (`fitness-and-stretching/rope-skipping`) — rope skipping: swinging and jumping the rope — basic, fancy and complex rope skills, alone and in groups — taught in China's Senior High book as fancy rope skipping (花样跳绳): solo and group patterned skipping (China's student book).

## Unit 3 — Gymnastics

1. **Balance and Calisthenics** (`gymnastics/balance-and-calisthenics`) — holding your balance without falling, and calisthenics sets for arms, shoulders and legs.
2. **Counted Gymnastics Sequences** (`gymnastics/counted-gymnastics-sequences`) — gymnastics done to counts — waist, arm and leg series performed position by position on the numbers.
3. **Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports** (`gymnastics/gymnastics-basics-rolls-and-supports`) — what gymnastics is, the basic positions, and rolls and supports done safely on a mat.
4. **Gymnastics with Apparatus** (`gymnastics/gymnastics-with-apparatus`) — gymnastics on apparatus — the box and the bars — always with a helper, always landing in control.
5. **Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement** (`gymnastics/moving-to-a-beat`) — moving in sequence to a beat — simple sequences first, then continuous, progressive, with equipment, and finally creating your own.
6. **Gymnastics — Rules and Competition Types** (`gymnastics/gymnastics-rules-and-competition-types`) — the basic laws of gymnastics and its competition families — artistic, rhythmic and aerobics — as the Grade 8 book teaches them.
7. **Expressing with the Body** (`gymnastics/expressing-with-the-body`) — expressing with the body: communicating feelings and ideas through body, face and mime, and dramatising situations — taught in El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide as Me comunico con mi cuerpo (Grade 1: gestures and movement representing animals, natural things and feelings), Mi cuerpo se comunica (Grade 2: body language in everyday communication) and Comunicación con todo mi cuerpo (Grade 3: gesture and movement with verbal expression) (El Salvador's teacher guide, evidence only).

## Unit 4 — Athletics
