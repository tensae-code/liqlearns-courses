# The Rules of Football

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rules of the football game

North Macedonia's Grade 9 book teaches the first law of football: only the goalkeeper may touch the ball with the hands; the other players may use any part of the body except the arms and hands.

## Sub lesson 2 — The rules of football: pitch and team

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches the pitch (90–120 m long and 45–90 m wide, the goal 7.32 × 2.44 m, the goal and penalty areas and the 9.15 m penalty arc, the ball of 68–71 cm and 396–453 g) and the team of eleven (goalkeeper; defenders; midfielders; wings; striker and second striker), at most five substitutions through the centre line, two halves of 45 minutes with a 15-minute break, the coin toss and kick-offs from the centre.
