# The Rules of Football — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In football, who may touch the ball with the hands?
a) Only the goalkeeper  b) Any player  c) No one at all
**Answer: a) Only the goalkeeper**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A football team on the pitch has:
a) Eleven players  b) Seven players  c) Five players
**Answer: a) Eleven players**
