# Hands-on — Introducing Racket Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Palm, then racket

Bounce a ball with your palm ten times, as the Grade 1 book starts. Then take the small racket and bounce it ten more times, and finish by hitting to a wall. Your partner counts the clean bounces.

## The bat bounce

Hold the table-tennis bat the way the Grade 2 book shows, and bounce the ball on the table, keeping it low and steady. Your partner checks the grip.
