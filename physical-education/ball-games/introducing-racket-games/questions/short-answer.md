# Introducing Racket Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the order of the tennis first skills?
**Answer: Bounce the ball with the palm, then with a small racket, then hit to a wall or partner.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the Grade 2 book teach first for table tennis?
**Answer: Holding the bat, and bouncing the ball on the table.**
