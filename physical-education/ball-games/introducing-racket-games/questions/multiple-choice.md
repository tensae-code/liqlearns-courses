# Introducing Racket Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 book's tennis first skills begin with…
a) Bouncing a ball with the palm  b) A full match  c) Serving over a net
**Answer: a) Bouncing a ball with the palm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After the palm, the learner uses…
a) A small racket  b) A bat only  c) Their foot
**Answer: a) A small racket**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first racket hits go to…
a) A wall or a partner  b) The highest window  c) No target at all
**Answer: a) A wall or a partner**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 2 book's first table-tennis skill is…
a) Holding the bat and bouncing the ball on the table  b) Smashing  c) Spinning serves
**Answer: a) Holding the bat and bouncing the ball on the table**
