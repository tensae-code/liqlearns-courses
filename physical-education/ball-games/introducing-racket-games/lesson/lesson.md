# Introducing Racket Games

## Do it first

Bounce a ball and catch it, five times without dropping it. That control is where every racket game starts — now read how DR Congo's books begin them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tennis first skills

The Grade 1 book's tennis first skills start **without a racket: bouncing a ball with the palm**. Only then comes the **small racket**, and the first hits go **to a wall or a partner**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Table-tennis first skills

Inside its mini-volley lesson, the Grade 2 book gives the **first table-tennis skills: holding the bat, and bouncing the ball on the table**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the order the books teach the first racket skills in, and why the palm comes before the racket.
