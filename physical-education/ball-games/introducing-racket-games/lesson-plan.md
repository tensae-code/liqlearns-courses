# Lesson plan — Introducing Racket Games

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Introducing Racket Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Introducing racket games: first skills with a racket and bat — bouncing, hitting to a wall or partner, and first rallies.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Tennis first skills** — bouncing a ball with the palm, then with a small racket, and hitting to a wall or partner.
2. **Table-tennis first skills** — holding the bat and bouncing the ball on the table.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ball Handling on the Spot. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Show the first tennis skills in the books' order: palm first, then the small racket.
2. Say where the first racket hits are aimed — to a wall or a partner.
3. Show how the table-tennis bat is held and bounce the ball on the table.

## What you need
A ball, a small racket and a table-tennis bat with a table, as the books' first skills use them.

## If the learner is stuck
- The ball will not stay bouncing → go back to the book's first step: bounce it with the palm before picking up the racket.
- The bat feels wrong → check the grip first, then bounce the ball on the table only, as the Grade 2 book starts it.
