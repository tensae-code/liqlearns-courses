# Video script — Playing the Team Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Small ball games — Grade 5: putting the skills together. Small games (ትንንሽ ጨዋታዎች) are the proving ground: small-sided games that call for all the skills together — the forearm pass and the finger pass, setting, passing with the inside and outside of the foot, controlling with the chest, and dribbling.

Team games — Grade 6: volleyball, football, basketball, handball. Now play the team games themselves: a volleyball game, a football game, a basketball game and a handball game.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
