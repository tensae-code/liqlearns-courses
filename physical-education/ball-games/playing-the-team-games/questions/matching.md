# Playing the Team Games — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Small ball games — Grade 5: putting the skills together — a
2. Team games — Grade 6: volleyball, football, basketball, handball — b

What each teaches:
a) small-sided games that use all of the ball skills together — forearm and finger passes, setting, foot passes, chest control and dribbling — as the proving ground of the skills
b) playing the full team games — a volleyball game, a football game, a basketball game and a handball game — applying the skills learned in a real game

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b
