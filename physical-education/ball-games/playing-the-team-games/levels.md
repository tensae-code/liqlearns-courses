# Playing the Team Games — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Small ball games — Grade 5: putting the skills together
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — small-sided games that use all of the ball skills together — forearm and finger passes, setting, foot passes, chest control and dribbling — as the proving ground of the skills.

## Sub lesson 2 — Team games — Grade 6: volleyball, football, basketball, handball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — playing the full team games — a volleyball game, a football game, a basketball game and a handball game — applying the skills learned in a real game.

## Sub lesson 3 — Football formations and team strategy — Grade 10

## Sub lesson 4 — Small-sided games — Grade 9: 2 against 1, 4 against 4, 5 against 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — small-sided games — grade 9: 2 against 1, 4 against 4, 5 against 5 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 5 — Handball team defence and attack — Grade 10
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — handball team defence and attack — grade 10 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 6 — Football rules — the guide's own pass (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner states the guide's football frame — FIFA's rules govern everywhere; the international pitch of 110 m × 75 m as printed; eleven players; two halves of 45 minutes — and explains the ball in and out of play, scoring, offside, the throw-in and the corner kick, naming Pierluigi Collina as the model official.
