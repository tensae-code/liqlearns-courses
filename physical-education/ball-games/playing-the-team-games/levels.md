# Playing the Team Games — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Small ball games — Grade 5: putting the skills together
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — small-sided games that use all of the ball skills together — forearm and finger passes, setting, foot passes, chest control and dribbling — as the proving ground of the skills.

## Sub lesson 2 — Team games — Grade 6: volleyball, football, basketball, handball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — playing the full team games — a volleyball game, a football game, a basketball game and a handball game — applying the skills learned in a real game.
