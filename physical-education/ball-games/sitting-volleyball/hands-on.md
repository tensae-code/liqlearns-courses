# Hands-on — Sitting Volleyball

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sitting Volleyball at Senior One

Do the part as Rwanda's books teach it: Sitting volleyball — techniques of playing volleyball seated on the floor (Unit 10), as Rwanda's Senior One PE Teacher's Guide S1 teaches it (printed p. 147). Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.

## Sitting Volleyball at Senior Two

Do the part as Rwanda's books teach it: Sitting volleyball: techniques and tactics (Unit 10), as Rwanda's Senior Two PE Teacher's Guide 2 teaches it (printed p. 143). Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.
