# Baseball 5 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Baseball 5 is…
a) The five-a-side street version of baseball  b) A swimming game  c) A board game
**Answer: a) The five-a-side street version of baseball**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a Baseball 5 fundamental the programme teaches?
a) Fielding grounders  b) Serving underarm  c) Kicking with the instep
**Answer: a) Fielding grounders**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pelota a la mano and las cuatro esquinas are…
a) Cuba's popular street forms of baseball, named as Baseball 5's roots  b) Two Cuban dances  c) Two athletics events
**Answer: a) Cuba's popular street forms of baseball, named as Baseball 5's roots**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Grade 5, Baseball 5 stands in for full baseball because…
a) It is playable anywhere with one rubber ball  b) It needs a full stadium  c) It is played in water
**Answer: a) It is playable anywhere with one rubber ball**
