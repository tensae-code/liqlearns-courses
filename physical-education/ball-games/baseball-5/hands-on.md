# Hands-on — Baseball 5

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play the fundamentals

With one rubber ball and a safe space: practise the defensive stance and grip, throw and receive with a partner, field a rolled grounder, and run from home to first after a hit — the fundamentals Cuba's Grade 5 programme teaches, in its own order.

## Name the roots

Say the two street games the programme names as Baseball 5's roots — pelota a la mano and las cuatro esquinas — and say what makes Baseball 5 playable anywhere.
