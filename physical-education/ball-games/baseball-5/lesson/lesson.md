## Sub lesson 1 — Baseball 5 fundamentals

Cuba's Grade 5 programme teaches Baseball 5 fundamentals: basic defensive stance, grip, throws and receiving, fielding grounders, hitting the ball (with run-up variants), and running home to first; its Grade 5 teacher book gives the full teaching treatment of those fundamentals through games and imitative exercises.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its street-game roots

Cuba's programme names the unit's roots: pelota a la mano and las cuatro esquinas — Cuba's popular street forms of baseball. Baseball 5 stands in for full baseball in Grade 5 because it is playable anywhere with one rubber ball.
