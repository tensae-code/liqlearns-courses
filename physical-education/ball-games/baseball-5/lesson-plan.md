# Lesson plan — Baseball 5

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Baseball 5
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Baseball 5: the five-a-side street version of baseball — its fundamentals and how it is played. — taught here through Cuba's way: its MINED national programmes and teacher Orientaciones Metodológicas.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Baseball 5 fundamentals** — Cuba's Grade 5 programme teaches Baseball 5 fundamentals: basic defensive stance, grip, throws and receiving, fielding grounders, hitting the ball (with run-up variants), and running home to first; its Grade 5 teacher book gives the full teaching treatment of those fundamentals through games and imitative exercises.
2. **Its street-game roots** — Cuba's programme names the unit's roots: pelota a la mano and las cuatro esquinas — Cuba's popular street forms of baseball. Baseball 5 stands in for full baseball in Grade 5 because it is playable anywhere with one rubber ball.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Baseball 5 fundamentals: stance, grip, throws and receiving, fielding grounders, hitting, running home to first.
2. Name pelota a la mano and las cuatro esquinas as the street-game roots.
3. Say why Baseball 5 stands in for full baseball in Grade 5: playable anywhere with one rubber ball.

## What you need
One rubber ball and a safe space to move — Baseball 5 is played with one rubber ball, as the programme records it.
