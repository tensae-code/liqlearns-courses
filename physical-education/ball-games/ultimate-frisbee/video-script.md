# Video script — Ultimate Frisbee

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A frisbee. Two teams. And no referee.
The players keep the rules themselves — that is Ultimate.
Timor-Leste's teachers carry its rules and its mini-games in their guide.
Learn it small, in the mini-games. Then play the whole game.
That is **Ultimate Frisbee**.
