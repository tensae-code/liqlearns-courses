# Lesson plan — Ultimate Frisbee

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Ultimate Frisbee
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ultimate Frisbee: the self-refereed team frisbee game — its rules and mini-games.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Ultimate Frisbee — the game and its rules** — the Timor-Leste guide: the self-refereed team frisbee game, taught in the guide's seven-sport appendix with its basic rules — self-refereed: the players themselves keep the rules, with no referee.
2. **Ultimate Frisbee mini-games** — the Timor-Leste guide: the mini-games the appendix gives for learning the game.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ball Handling on the Spot; Playing the Team Games. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Ultimate Frisbee is: a team frisbee game the players referee themselves.
2. Say what self-refereed means: there is no referee — the players keep the rules themselves.
3. Say where Timor-Leste teaches it: in the Grade 7 teacher guide's seven-sport appendix, with basic rules and mini-games for each sport.
4. Play the game's mini-games as the guide gives them, as the way into the full game.

## What you need
A frisbee and open ground, as the guide's appendix sets the game up.
