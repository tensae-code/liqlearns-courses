# Hands-on — Ultimate Frisbee

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mini-games first

Play Ultimate Frisbee the way Timor-Leste's guide builds it: start with the appendix's mini-games — the small games that teach the team frisbee game piece by piece — before the full game.

## Referee yourselves

In every mini-game, practise the game's own way: no referee. When a call is needed, the players make it themselves, honestly and out loud — that is what self-refereed means, practised, not just said.
