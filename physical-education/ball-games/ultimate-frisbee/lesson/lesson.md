# Ultimate Frisbee

## Do it first

A team game played with a frisbee — and no referee at all. Who keeps the rules? Who settles a dispute? Say your answers, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ultimate Frisbee — the game and its rules

Timor-Leste's Grade 7 teacher guide teaches **Ultimate Frisbee** in its **seven-sport appendix** — the reference library of sports the class can choose from — alongside football/futsal, volleyball, handball, basketball, kickball and human football. For each sport the appendix gives the **basic rules and mini-games**.

Ultimate Frisbee is **the self-refereed team frisbee game**: two teams play with a frisbee, and there is **no referee** — the players themselves keep the rules. That self-refereeing is the game's own mark: honesty is not added to the game, it is how the game is played. Timor-Leste adopted it into its PE through this appendix, as one of the seven sports a class can choose for its period tournament cycle.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ultimate Frisbee mini-games

The guide does not throw learners straight into the full game: its appendix gives **mini-games** for Ultimate Frisbee — small games that teach the big game piece by piece, self-refereeing practised from the start. The mini-games come first; the full game grows out of them.
