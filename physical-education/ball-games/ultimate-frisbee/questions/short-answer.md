# Ultimate Frisbee — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Ultimate Frisbee?
**Answer: The self-refereed team frisbee game.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who keeps the rules in Ultimate Frisbee?
**Answer: The players themselves — the game has no referee.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the mini-games for?
**Answer: They teach the full game piece by piece, before it is played whole.**
