# Ultimate Frisbee — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ultimate Frisbee is…
a) The self-refereed team frisbee game  b) A swimming game  c) A board game
**Answer: a) The self-refereed team frisbee game**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-refereed means…
a) The players themselves keep the rules — there is no referee  b) A referee watches from far away  c) Nobody follows any rules
**Answer: a) The players themselves keep the rules — there is no referee**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Timor-Leste teaches Ultimate Frisbee in…
a) The Grade 7 teacher guide's seven-sport appendix  b) A swimming appendix  c) The student book's stretching guide
**Answer: a) The Grade 7 teacher guide's seven-sport appendix**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The mini-games come…
a) Before the full game, to teach it piece by piece  b) Only after the full game is mastered  c) Instead of any game at all
**Answer: a) Before the full game, to teach it piece by piece**
