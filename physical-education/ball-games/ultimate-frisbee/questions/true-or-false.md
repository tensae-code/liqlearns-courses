# Ultimate Frisbee — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ultimate Frisbee is played with a referee who settles every call.
**Answer: False** — it is self-refereed: the players keep the rules themselves.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ultimate Frisbee is one of the seven sports in the Timor-Leste guide's appendix.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide gives mini-games for Ultimate Frisbee.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson rests on a pupil's book from Timor-Leste.
**Answer: False** — it rests on the Grade 7 teacher guide, filed as evidence only.
