# Volleyball Spiking and Blocking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two serves does the guide teach?
a) The simple underhand serve and the overhand "hammer" serve  b) Only a throw-in  c) The jump throw
**Answer: a) The simple underhand serve and the overhand "hammer" serve**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How should a spiker land, as the guide teaches it?
a) Safely on both feet  b) On one foot  c) Falling backwards
**Answer: a) Safely on both feet**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must a blocker read before jumping?
a) The spiker  b) The referee  c) The scoreboard
**Answer: a) The spiker**
