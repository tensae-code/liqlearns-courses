# Hands-on — Volleyball Spiking and Blocking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Serve, spike, block

Practise the guide's three net skills in turn: serve at a target with the underhand and overhand serves, take approach steps and jump to hit a held ball down, landing on both feet, then jump at the net with both hands up as a block, alone and in a two-player block.
