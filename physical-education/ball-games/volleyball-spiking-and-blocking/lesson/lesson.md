# Volleyball Spiking and Blocking

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Serving — the serve as an attacking weapon

The Grade 7 guide teaches the serve as an attacking weapon: the simple underhand serve and the overhand "hammer" serve, step by step, with target drills.

## Sub lesson 2 — Spiking — hitting the ball down

The spike: approach steps, jump, hitting the ball down, and landing safely on both feet — as the Grade 7 guide teaches it step by step.

## Sub lesson 3 — Blocking at the net

Blocking at the net: reading the spiker, jumping with both hands over the net, the two-player block, and block training drills — as the Grade 7 guide teaches it.
