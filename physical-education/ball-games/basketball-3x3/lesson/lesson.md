## Sub lesson 1 — Basketball 3x3

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches basketball 3x3: the urban half-court game, official at the 2010 Youth Olympics and Olympic at Tokyo 2020 — one hoop, teams of three plus one substitute, the check-ball start, clearing the ball beyond the arc after each change of possession, a 12-second shot clock, shots worth one and two points, and a game of 10 minutes or first to 21.
