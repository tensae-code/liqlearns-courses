# Basketball 3x3 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Basketball 3x3 is played:
a) On a half-court with one hoop, three players a side plus one substitute  b) On a full court with five a side  c) With two hoops and no substitutes
**Answer: a) On a half-court with one hoop, three players a side plus one substitute**
