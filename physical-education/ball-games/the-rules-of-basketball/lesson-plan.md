# Lesson plan — The Rules of Basketball

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** The Rules of Basketball
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the rules that run a basketball game — court and quarters, timed violations, fouls and free throws, scoring values, and the Grade 10 depth: eligibility, roles, the coach's team, teams of twelve, uniforms, throw-ins, time-outs and substitutions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the basketball skills lessons (Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw); depth is ordered Grade 9 first, then the Grade 10 pass as the deeper sub lessons (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The playing area, the equipment and the quarters** — the marked court, the backboard and basket, 4 quarters of 10 minutes, intervals, tied score and overtime.
2. **Timed violations, fouls and free throws** — the 3-second, 5-second and free-throw-line rules; fouls, free throws for a fouled shooter, the technical foul; referees and the yearly re-reading of the rules.
3. **Scoring and the international court (teacher guide — evidence)** — most points wins; 2 points inside the three-point line, 3 outside, 1 for a free throw; the 28 m × 15 m international court.
4. **Who may play, the players' roles and the coach's team — Grade 10** — eligibility, players' roles and contact fouls, the head coach's professional staff, and the game-clock signal with red perimeter lighting.
5. **Teams of twelve, uniforms, and how the game is run (teacher guide — evidence)** — twelve players including the captain, coach and assistant coach as delegates; uniforms in team colours, numbers 0–99; quarters, goal, throw-in, time-out and substitution.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw. Recommended, never forced — a learner may jump in here directly.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the court's parts and its international size, and say how the game is timed — quarters, intervals, overtime.
2. Spot and name the 3-second, 5-second and free-throw-line violations in a live mini game.
3. Score any basket or free throw correctly: 2, 3 or 1 points.
4. Say who is eligible to play, what the captain's title means, and who stands behind the head coach.
5. Run a game's chain in order: team, uniform, quarter, throw-in, time-out, substitution — and say what ends a quarter when the backboard's red light is lit.

## What you need
A court or marked space with a hoop, backboard or target, and a basketball. Modest sports clothes. A partner or a small group for the mini-game parts.

## If the learner is stuck
- The seconds rules blur → act them one at a time in a mini game, counting aloud: 3 in the area, 5 holding the ball, and the shooter's feet at the line.
- Scoring values mix up → use the court's own picture: point at the arc, point inside it, point at the free-throw line, and say 3, 2, 1 in that order.
- The Grade 10 chain feels long → walk it as a story: first the team arrives in uniform, then the quarters run, then the throw-ins, time-outs and substitutions happen inside them.
