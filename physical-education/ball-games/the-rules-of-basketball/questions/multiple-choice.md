# The Rules of Basketball — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A basketball game is played in…
a) 4 quarters of 10 minutes  b) 2 halves of 45 minutes  c) 3 periods of 20 minutes
**Answer: a) 4 quarters of 10 minutes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** If the score is tied at the end of the fourth quarter…
a) Overtime is played until one team is ahead  b) The game is a draw and stops  c) Both teams lose
**Answer: a) Overtime is played until one team is ahead**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 3-second rule says a player may not…
a) Stay 3 seconds in the restricted area while their team has the ball  b) Hold the ball at all  c) Cross the centre line
**Answer: a) Stay 3 seconds in the restricted area while their team has the ball**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A shooter who is fouled is awarded…
a) Free throws  b) A throw-in at centre court  c) Two points at once
**Answer: a) Free throws**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Players, coaches and referees must re-read the rules every year because…
a) The rules change  b) The ball changes colour  c) The court is repainted
**Answer: a) The rules change**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A basket from outside the three-point line is worth…
a) 3 points  b) 2 points  c) 1 point
**Answer: a) 3 points**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The court for international competitions measures…
a) 28 m by 15 m  b) 110 m by 75 m  c) 40 m by 20 m
**Answer: a) 28 m by 15 m**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** A player may take part in a game only if they are…
a) Authorised for their team under the regulations  b) The tallest on court  c) Wearing number 1
**Answer: a) Authorised for their team under the regulations**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** Where the backboard has red perimeter lighting, a quarter ends…
a) When the light is lit — the light governs  b) Whenever a player calls out  c) After the next basket
**Answer: a) When the light is lit — the light governs**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** The head coach's staff is…
a) A team of professionals — such as a nutritionist, a physician and an assistant coach — sharing the duties of player development  b) One person doing everything alone  c) The twelve players themselves
**Answer: a) A team of professionals — such as a nutritionist, a physician and an assistant coach — sharing the duties of player development**

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** A basketball team has at most…
a) Twelve players, including the team captain  b) Six players  c) Twenty players
**Answer: a) Twelve players, including the team captain**

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** Shirt numbers run from…
a) 0 to 99  b) 1 to 11  c) 10 to 20
**Answer: a) 0 to 99**

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** A time-out is…
a) A break a team may call during the game  b) The end of the game  c) A foul by the captain
**Answer: a) A break a team may call during the game**
