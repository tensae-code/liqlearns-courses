# The Rules of Basketball — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The backboard is part of basketball's equipment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A closely guarded player holding the ball must play it within 5 seconds.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A free-throw shooter may cross the free-throw line while the shot is in the air.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Each successful free throw is worth 2 points.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The uniform is the team's identity on the court, and teams choose their own colours.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Substitution means changing a player for a team-mate during the game.
**Answer: True**
