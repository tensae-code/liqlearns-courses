# Hands-on — The Rules of Basketball

The lesson on a real court, not only your eyes.

## Walk and name the court
Walk the court and name every line and area you cross — centre line, key, free-throw line, three-point arc — and pace out the 28 m by 15 m size along the edges with a partner.

## Violation-spotting mini game
Play a short mini game. One watcher stands out with a tally sheet and calls every 3-second, 5-second and free-throw-line violation they spot, naming the rule each time. Swap the watcher every few minutes — players, coaches and referees re-read the rules every year, and spotting is how you learn them.

## Score the game
Keep the score for the mini game on paper: 2 for a basket inside the arc, 3 from outside, 1 for each free throw. Read the running total aloud after every score.

## If it is hard
Play half-court with fewer players and stop after every violation to walk the rule slowly — where the player stood, what the count was, what should have happened — before playing on.
