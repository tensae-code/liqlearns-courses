# The Rules of Basketball — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Depth is ordered Grade 9 first, then the Grade 10 pass as the deeper sub lessons.

## Sub lesson 1 — The playing area, the equipment and the quarters
Pass mark: the learner names the court and backboard equipment and states the game's timing — 4 quarters of 10 minutes, intervals, and overtime when the score is tied.

## Sub lesson 2 — Timed violations, fouls and free throws
Pass mark: the learner names the 3-second, 5-second and free-throw-line-crossing violations, says what a fouled shooter is awarded, and spots violations in a mini game.

## Sub lesson 3 — Scoring and the international court (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner values any score correctly — 2 points inside the three-point line, 3 outside, 1 for a free throw — and gives the international court size, 28 m × 15 m.

## Sub lesson 4 — Who may play, the players' roles and the coach's team — Grade 10
Pass mark: the learner explains eligibility, the players' individual roles, the head coach's shared professional staff, and that the game-clock signal — or the backboard's lit red perimeter light — ends a quarter, an overtime or the game.

## Sub lesson 5 — Teams of twelve, uniforms, and how the game is run (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner states the team of at most twelve players including the captain, the uniform as the team's identity with numbers 0–99, and runs the chain in order: quarter, goal, throw-in, time-out, substitution.
