# Video script — The Rules of Basketball

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then say it with me.

Four quarters. Ten minutes each. Tied at the end? We play overtime.

Inside the big arc: two points. Outside it: three points. A free throw: one point.
The court is twenty-eight metres by fifteen.

Count with me. Three seconds in the area — move out. Five seconds holding the ball — play it.
A fouled shooter shoots free throws. Nobody crosses the line while the ball flies.

Twelve players in a team. The captain leads and speaks.
Shirts numbered nought to ninety-nine.
The clock ends the quarter — and the red light on the backboard, when it is lit, has the last word.

Now you. Name the court. Score the shots. Spot the violations.
