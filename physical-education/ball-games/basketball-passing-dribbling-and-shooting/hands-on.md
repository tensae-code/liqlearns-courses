# Hands-on — Basketball Passing, Dribbling and Shooting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pass, beat, shoot

Work through the guide's skills with a partner: chest passes in a line and a circle, a long baseball pass down the court, a feint and pivot past a marker, then shots with backspin and dribbles — control dribbling beside the body, speed dribbling in front.
