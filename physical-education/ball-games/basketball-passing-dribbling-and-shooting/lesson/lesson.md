# Basketball Passing, Dribbling and Shooting

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Passing — the two-hand chest pass and passing while running

The Grade 10 guide teaches passing: the two-hand chest pass and passing while running, taught through line, circle, square and star passing drills and the figure-of-eight.

## Sub lesson 2 — The baseball pass — the long one-handed pass

The baseball pass is the long one-handed pass used for fast breaks and long outlets: its technique, the high arc that beats defenders, its key points and the four common errors — as the Grade 10 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Beating a defender — feinting and pivoting

Beating a defender: change of direction with and without the ball (feinting past a marker) and pivoting — inside and outside turns on a fixed foot, standing and after the dribble — as the Grade 10 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Shooting and dribbling points

Shooting and dribbling points from the Grade 10 guide: finger spacing and backspin in the shot, the best release angles (38–45°, steeper for the layup), the joint angles of the release — and control dribbling beside the body versus speed dribbling in front.
