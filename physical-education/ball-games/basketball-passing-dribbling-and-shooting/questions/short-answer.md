# Basketball Passing, Dribbling and Shooting — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one passing drill shape the guide uses.
**Answer: Line, circle, square or star passing drills, or the figure-of-eight.**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What shape does the baseball pass take to beat defenders?
**Answer: A high arc.**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two kinds of pivot turn does the guide teach?
**Answer: Inside and outside turns.**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What spin does the guide teach in the shot?
**Answer: Backspin.**
