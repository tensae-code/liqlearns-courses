# Basketball Passing, Dribbling and Shooting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pass does the guide teach first?
a) The two-hand chest pass  b) A behind-the-back pass  c) A throw-in
**Answer: a) The two-hand chest pass**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the baseball pass used for?
a) Fast breaks and long outlets  b) Free throws  c) Starting the game
**Answer: a) Fast breaks and long outlets**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is feinting past a marker?
a) A change of direction that beats a defender  b) A foul  c) A kind of shot
**Answer: a) A change of direction that beats a defender**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What release angles does the guide give as best for the shot?
a) 38–45°  b) 10–15°  c) 90°
**Answer: a) 38–45°**
