## Sub lesson 1 — Futsal

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches futsal: indoor five-a-side football created by Juan Carlos Ceriani at the Montevideo YMCA in the 1930s, recognised by FIFA and UEFA — the court of 25–42 × 16–25 m with 3 × 2 m goals and 6 m and 10 m penalty marks, universal players and the pivot, two halves of 20 minutes with the clock stopped, one timeout per half, unlimited flying substitutions, and accumulated fouls leading to 10 m penalties.
