# Ball Handling on the Spot — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the part "Passing in a circle" teach?
a) throwing a ball above the head and catching it, and bouncing a ball in place  b) passing a ball sideways in a circle, left to right and right to left  c) self toss-and-catch, two-hand chest pass, and kicking a stationary ball
**Answer: b) passing a ball sideways in a circle, left to right and right to left**
