# Ball Handling on the Spot — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Passing in a circle — a
2. Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot — b
3. Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence) — c
4. Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence) — d
5. Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5 — e

What each teaches:
a) passing a ball sideways in a circle, left to right and right to left
b) throwing a ball above the head and catching it, and bouncing a ball in place
c) self toss-and-catch, two-hand chest pass, and kicking a stationary ball
d) throwing up and catching with two hands, then partner throw and catch
e) general ball handling and control, and receiving and passing with the chest — cushioning the ball softly so it drops to the feet

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
