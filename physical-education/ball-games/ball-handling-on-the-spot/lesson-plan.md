# Lesson plan — Ball Handling on the Spot

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Ball Handling on the Spot
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** passing, throwing, catching and bouncing a ball without travelling — the on-the-spot ball skills.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 1–4 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Passing in a circle** — passing a ball sideways in a circle, left to right and right to left.
2. **Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot** — throwing a ball above the head and catching it, and bouncing a ball in place.
3. **Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence)** — self toss-and-catch, two-hand chest pass, and kicking a stationary ball.
4. **Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence)** — throwing up and catching with two hands, then partner throw and catch.
5. **Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5** — general ball handling and control, and receiving and passing with the chest — cushioning the ball softly so it drops to the feet.
6. **Skills with objects — throw, catch, kick (teacher guide — evidence)** — working with partners and objects — throwing and catching a ball, and kicking.
7. **Ball control with hands and feet together (teacher guide — evidence)** — catching a thrown ball by hand and trapping a rolling ball with the sole of the foot, in one partner practice.
8. **Balls into the basket, and heel kicks (teacher guide — evidence)** — throwing small balls one by one into a basket, and jogging in place kicking the heels up to the buttocks.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Open flat ground, a mat for any roll or floor work, and the equipment named in the parts (a ball, a rope, rings or cones where the part says so). Modest sports clothes. A partner or a small group for the partner parts.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
