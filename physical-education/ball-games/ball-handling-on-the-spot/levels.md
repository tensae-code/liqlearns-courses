# Ball Handling on the Spot — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Passing in a circle
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — passing a ball sideways in a circle, left to right and right to left.

## Sub lesson 2 — Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — throwing a ball above the head and catching it, and bouncing a ball in place.

## Sub lesson 3 — Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — self toss-and-catch, two-hand chest pass, and kicking a stationary ball.

## Sub lesson 4 — Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — throwing up and catching with two hands, then partner throw and catch.

## Sub lesson 5 — Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — general ball handling and control, and receiving and passing with the chest — cushioning the ball softly so it drops to the feet.
