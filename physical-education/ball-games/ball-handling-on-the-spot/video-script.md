# Video script — Ball Handling on the Spot

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Passing in a circle. Stand in a circle and pass a ball left to right and right to left to a partner.

Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot. Throw a ball above your head and catch it on the spot.

Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence). Toss a ball upward and catch it yourself, again and again.

Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence). Throw a ball upward and catch it with two hands.

Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5. Ball handling (የኳስ አያያዝ) is general ball mastery: bring a moving ball under control with the first touch.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
