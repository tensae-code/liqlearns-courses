# Video script — Ball Handling on the Spot

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Passing in a circle. Stand in a circle and pass a ball left to right and right to left to a partner.

Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot. Throw a ball above your head and catch it on the spot.

Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence). Toss a ball upward and catch it yourself, again and again.

Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence). Throw a ball upward and catch it with two hands.

Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5. Ball handling (የኳስ አያያዝ) is general ball mastery: bring a moving ball under control with the first touch.

Skills with objects — throw, catch, kick (teacher guide — evidence). Now the objects join in. Work with a partner: throw a ball and catch the return, then kick a ball to your partner and control the one that comes back. Hands and feet take turns on the same ball — throwing, catching and kicking in one flowing practice by the lake in the guide's picture.

Ball control with hands and feet together (teacher guide — evidence). One ball, two kinds of control. Catch a thrown ball with your hands — then stop a rolling ball with the sole of your foot, trapping it still. Hand catch, foot trap, hand catch, foot trap: the guide's picture (ስዕል 3.3 — ኳስ ተወርውሮ በእጅና በእግር ነጥሮ ሲያዝ, a ball thrown and caught by hand and trapped with the foot) shows the pair of skills side by side, and partners practise them together.

Balls into the basket, and heel kicks (teacher guide — evidence). Two in-place tasks. Balls into the basket — ኳስ በቅርጫት: three to five small balls wait in a basket (ቅርጫት); step forward and throw them one by one into the basket, then gather them back for the next learner. Heel kicks — መቀመጫን በእግር መምታት (the guide's own gloss: Kick Buttock): jog on the spot, kicking your heels up behind to touch your buttocks, arms bent, left leg, right leg, in turn.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
