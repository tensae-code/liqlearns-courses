# Ball Handling on the Spot

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Stand in a circle and pass a ball left to right and right to left to a partner. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Passing in a circle

Stand in a circle and pass a ball left to right and right to left to a partner. Move a ball down, left and right. Your feet stay on the spot; your hands and eyes do the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Throw up and catch; bouncing on the spot

Throw a ball above your head and catch it on the spot. Bounce small balls in place, with control, at waist height.

## Sub lesson 3 — Toss, chest pass and still-ball kick (teacher guide — evidence)

Toss a ball upward and catch it yourself, again and again. Throw a two-hand chest throw to a partner and catch the return. Kick a stationary ball — stand beside it, swing and kick, keeping the ball controlled.

## Sub lesson 4 — Throw it up and catch it with two hands (teacher guide — evidence)

Throw a ball upward and catch it with two hands. Then throw to a partner and catch the return, and try bounce-catch variations.

## Sub lesson 5 — Ball handling and chest control — Grade 5

Ball handling (የኳስ አያያዝ) is general ball mastery: bring a moving ball under control with the first touch. Receiving and passing with the chest (በደረት ትይዩ): meet the ball chest-parallel, cushion it so it drops softly off the chest to your feet, then pass; games played by passing with the chest practise the same skill in play.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
