# Hands-on — Volleyball Footwork and Ready Positions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Show the three stances

On your partner's call — high, medium or low — take that ready position. Your partner checks the stance matches the height the ball would come at.

## Step four ways

Practise the four basic steps across the court: plain step, sliding step, cross step and wide straddle step. Your partner names each step before you move.
