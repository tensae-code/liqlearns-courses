# Volleyball Footwork and Ready Positions — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three ready positions — high, medium and low
Pass mark: say and show the three ready positions of volleyball: high, medium and low, and when each one is used.

## Sub lesson 2 — The basic steps — plain, sliding, cross and wide straddle
Pass mark: say and show the basic steps: the plain step, the sliding step, the cross step and the wide straddle step.
