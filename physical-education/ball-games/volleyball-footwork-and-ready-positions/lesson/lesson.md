# Volleyball Footwork and Ready Positions

## Do it first

Stand ready for a ball that could come high, at your chest, or down at your knees. Each height asks for a different stance — this lesson teaches the three stances and the steps that move you between them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three ready positions — high, medium and low

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 10 GDTC 10 Volleyball* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **the three ready positions of volleyball: high, medium and low, and when each one is used**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The basic steps — plain, sliding, cross and wide straddle

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 10 GDTC 10 Volleyball* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **the basic steps: the plain step, the sliding step, the cross step and the wide straddle step**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.
