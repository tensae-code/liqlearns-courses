# Volleyball Footwork and Ready Positions — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many ready positions does this lesson teach?
a) Three — high, medium and low  b) One  c) Five
**Answer: a) Three — high, medium and low**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The low ready position is used when the ball comes…
a) Low, down toward your knees  b) High above your head  c) From behind you
**Answer: a) Low, down toward your knees**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the basic steps?
a) The sliding step  b) The skipping step  c) The marching step
**Answer: a) The sliding step**
