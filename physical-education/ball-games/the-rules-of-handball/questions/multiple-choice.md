# The Rules of Handball — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The handball goals and court markings are painted…
a) In two contrasting colour bands  b) All in one colour  c) Only when visitors come
**Answer: a) In two contrasting colour bands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The goal's net is attached so that…
a) A ball thrown into the goal stays in the goal  b) The keeper can hide behind it  c) The ball bounces back out
**Answer: a) A ball thrown into the goal stays in the goal**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A substitute may enter the court…
a) At any time, repeatedly — once the replaced player has left  b) Only at half time  c) While the replaced player is still on court
**Answer: a) At any time, repeatedly — once the replaced player has left**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A time-out is obligatory when…
a) A player receives a 2-minute suspension or is disqualified  b) A goal is scored  c) The ball goes out
**Answer: a) A player receives a 2-minute suspension or is disqualified**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Personal punishments fall hardest on fouls…
a) Aimed at the opponent's body  b) Against your own team  c) That nobody saw
**Answer: a) Aimed at the opponent's body**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's own rules pass, students learn the court's size by…
a) Working in pairs and drawing the court  b) Guessing it  c) Copying the football pitch
**Answer: a) Working in pairs and drawing the court**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The basic skills named with the rules are…
a) Passing, shooting, dribbling and goalkeeping  b) Serving, setting and spiking  c) Batting and bowling
**Answer: a) Passing, shooting, dribbling and goalkeeping**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Rules exist for…
a) The safety of the players, the integrity of the game and the fairest possible competition  b) Making the game slower  c) The referee's entertainment
**Answer: a) The safety of the players, the integrity of the game and the fairest possible competition**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** In defence, the goalkeeper may touch the ball…
a) With any part of the body  b) With the hands only  c) With the feet only
**Answer: a) With any part of the body**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** A goal stands when…
a) The referee confirms it  b) The scorer shouts loudly  c) The keeper agrees
**Answer: a) The referee confirms it**

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** The throw-in is awarded by the referee when…
a) A rule is broken or the ball leaves play  b) A team asks nicely  c) The first half ends
**Answer: a) A rule is broken or the ball leaves play**
