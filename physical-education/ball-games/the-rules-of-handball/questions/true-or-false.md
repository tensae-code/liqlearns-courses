# The Rules of Handball — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A handball goal is fitted with a net so a scored ball stays in the goal.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A substitute may run on before the player they replace has left the court.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At a 2-minute suspension, the time-out is a choice the teams may skip.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide teaches the handball rules under the international (IHF) rules of handball.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** To win, a team must score more goals than the opponent without breaking the rules.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The deep rules pass is applied by playing small-sided games.
**Answer: True**
