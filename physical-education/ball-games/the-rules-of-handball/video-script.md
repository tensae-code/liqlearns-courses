# Video script — The Rules of Handball

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then say it with me.

The court is marked in two colours, so every line is clear.
The goal has a net. A scored ball stays in. No arguing.
Out first, then in — that is how a substitute enters. Any time. Again and again, but never early.

A player is suspended for two minutes? The time-out must be called. It is not a choice.
Fouls aimed at the body cost the player most. Aim at the ball. Aim at the goal.

The keeper may save with any part of the body — hands, legs, body.
A goal stands when the referee confirms it.
The ball leaves play, or a rule is broken? Throw-in. The referee awards it.

Rules keep us safe. Rules keep the game honest. Rules keep it fair.

Now you. Draw the court. Bring your substitute on. Award the throw-in — and say why.
