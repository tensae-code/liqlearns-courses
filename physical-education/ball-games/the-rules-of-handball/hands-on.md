# Hands-on — The Rules of Handball

The lesson on a real court, not only your eyes.

## Draw the court together
In pairs, measure and draw the court — goals, goal areas, centre line — on the ground with chalk or on paper at scale, the way the guide's own teaching does it. Walk your drawing and name each part.

## Substitutes at the bench line
Run a rolling game where the only job is the change: a player leaves the court completely, calls their substitute, and only then does the substitute enter. A change made early is replayed slowly until it is clean.

## Small-sided games with spoken throw-ins
Play a small-sided game where everyone keeps goal, scores and takes throw-ins in turn. Every throw-in is awarded aloud with its reason, and every goal waits for the referee's confirmation before play restarts.

## If it is hard
Play on a half court with fewer players, soft throws only at goal, and stop after every punishment to say together what was fouled and what it costs — before playing on.
