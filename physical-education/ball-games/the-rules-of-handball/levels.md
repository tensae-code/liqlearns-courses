# The Rules of Handball — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The Grade 9 student pass opens the depth; the two guide passes deepen it as labelled evidence; the Grade 10 student pass is cross-cited in Playing the Team Games and is not repeated here.

## Sub lesson 1 — The court, the goals and the ball
Pass mark: the learner draws and explains the court, the goals and markings in two contrasting colour bands, the net that keeps a scored ball in the goal, the goal area and the ball.

## Sub lesson 2 — The team, playing time, the obligatory time-out and punishments
Pass mark: the learner brings a substitute on legally, states that a time-out is obligatory at a 2-minute suspension or a disqualification, and explains personal punishments — hardest for fouls aimed at the opponent's body.

## Sub lesson 3 — The rules in the guide's own pass: court, time, ball, team and fouls (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner draws the court in a pair, and names the guide's rules order — court, playing time, ball, team, fouls — and the four basic skills: passing, shooting, dribbling and goalkeeping.

## Sub lesson 4 — The goalkeeper, scoring and the throw-in (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner says why rules exist (safety, integrity, fairest competition), keeps goal with any part of the body, states that a goal stands when the referee confirms it, and awards a throw-in with its reason — a rule broken, or the ball out of play.
