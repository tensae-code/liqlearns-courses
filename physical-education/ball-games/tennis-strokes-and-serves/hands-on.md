# Hands-on — Tennis Strokes and Serves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Grips, strokes, serves

Practise the guide's strokes in order: hold the racket in each of the four grips, play forehands and backhands (one-handed and two-handed) from the ready position with shuffle and crossover steps, then serves with the shoulder turn, volleys close to the net, a smash and a soft drop shot.
