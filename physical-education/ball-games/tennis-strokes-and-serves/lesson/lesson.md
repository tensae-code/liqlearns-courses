# Tennis Strokes and Serves

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tennis groundstrokes — grips, footwork, forehand and backhand

The Grade 10 guide teaches the groundstrokes: the four grips (Continental/European, Eastern, semi-Western, Western), the ready position, shuffle and crossover footwork, the forehand as the foundation stroke, and the one-handed and two-handed backhand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tennis serve, volley and touch shots

The guide teaches the serve, volley and touch shots: serve mechanics (shoulder turn and forearm rotation), moving in to volley, the overhead smash, and the drop shot played softly just over the net.
