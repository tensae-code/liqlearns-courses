# Video script — Basketball Rebounding and Boxing Out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Basketball rebounding and boxing out — winning the ball when a shot misses.
First box out — turn into the attacker and seal them off with your back and hips.
Then take the rebound — the sealed-off attacker cannot reach it.
That is **basketball rebounding and boxing out**.
