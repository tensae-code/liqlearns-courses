# Hands-on — Basketball Rebounding and Boxing Out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Box out a partner

Stand between your partner and an imaginary hoop. On the call, turn into your partner and seal them off with your back and hips, the way the lesson describes. Your partner checks that you turned first and sealed with back and hips, not with your arms.

## Seal and take

Your partner tosses a ball up against a wall or toward a hoop. Box your partner out as it goes up, then step to where it falls and take it with both hands.
