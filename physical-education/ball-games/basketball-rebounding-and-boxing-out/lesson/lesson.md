# Basketball Rebounding and Boxing Out

## Do it first

Shoot a ball at a hoop and watch where it bounces. The player who is standing in the right place when it comes down gets the next play — this lesson teaches how that place is won.

## Sub lesson 1 — Boxing out — turning into the attacker and sealing them off

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 12 GDTC 12 Basketball* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **turning into the attacker, sealing them off with back and hips**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Taking the rebound

Vietnam's *Vietnam Grade 12 GDTC 12 Basketball* student book teaches this as its own lesson: **then taking the rebound once the attacker is sealed off**. The teaching is cited to the book and printed page in lesson/sources.md, and this lesson names it as its subject without adding to it.
