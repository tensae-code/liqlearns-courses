# Basketball Rebounding and Boxing Out — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boxing out starts by…
a) Turning into the attacker  b) Running away from the basket  c) Calling for a time-out
**Answer: a) Turning into the attacker**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The attacker is sealed off with…
a) Your back and hips  b) Your elbows  c) The ball
**Answer: a) Your back and hips**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After the attacker is sealed off, the defender…
a) Takes the rebound  b) Leaves the court  c) Shoots from the centre line
**Answer: a) Takes the rebound**
