# Refereeing and Fair Play — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pupils taking the whistle
Pass mark: say that pupils themselves take the whistle and referee the team games, as the Pédagogie Générale book teaches.

## Sub lesson 2 — Impartiality and fair play
Pass mark: say that the referee judges impartially — no favour to either side — and that fair play and sportsmanship govern how everyone takes the decisions.
