# Refereeing and Fair Play

## Do it first

Two players both say the ball was theirs. Who decides — and what makes that decision fair? Say your answer, then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pupils taking the whistle

DR Congo's trainee-teacher book teaches refereeing as something **pupils do themselves: taking the whistle** and running the team games.

## Sub lesson 2 — Impartiality and fair play

The same teaching sets the referee's standard: **impartiality — judging without favour** — and **fair play** from everyone who plays.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what a fair pupil referee does, as the book teaches it.
