# Refereeing and Fair Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's teaching, who takes the whistle?
a) The pupils themselves  b) Only visiting adults  c) Nobody
**Answer: a) The pupils themselves**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Impartiality means the referee…
a) Judges without favour to either side  b) Helps their friends' team  c) Never blows the whistle
**Answer: a) Judges without favour to either side**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fair play asks players to…
a) Accept decisions and play fairly, win or lose  b) Argue every call  c) Play only when winning
**Answer: a) Accept decisions and play fairly, win or lose**
