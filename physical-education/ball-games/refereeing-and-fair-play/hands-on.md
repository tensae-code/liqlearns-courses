# Hands-on — Refereeing and Fair Play

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take the whistle

Referee a short team game with the whistle, as the book teaches pupils to do. A partner watches and notes every decision you gave and whether both sides were judged the same.

## The fair-play check

After the game, name one moment of fair play you saw and one decision that was hard to take impartially. Say what you did about it.
