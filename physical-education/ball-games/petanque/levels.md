# Petanque — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The story of petanque — a leading Lao sport
Pass mark: say why petanque matters in Laos and how the Grade 12 guide tells its history and importance, as the scheme of work names it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Holding, pointing and shooting the boule
Pass mark: say how the basic petanque throws are taught: how to hold the boule, point it toward the target and shoot.

## Sub lesson 3 — The rules of petanque
Pass mark: say that the game is played by its printed rules, as the Grade 12 guide teaches them in its rules lesson.
