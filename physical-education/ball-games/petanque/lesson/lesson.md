# Petanque

## Do it first

Name one game or dance from your own childhood that everybody knew without a book. Games and dances handed down like that are this lesson's subject — now read how Laos's books teach their own.

## Sub lesson 1 — The story of petanque — a leading Lao sport

Laos's Grade 12 (M7) teacher guide teaches petanque as one of its two elective sports, opening with the game's **history and importance**: the story of petanque, named in the guide as a leading Lao sport, and why it matters. The lesson is cited to the guide's year scheme of work (printed p. 9), which lists every lesson in teaching order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Holding, pointing and shooting the boule

The guide's second petanque lesson teaches the **basic petanque throwing techniques**: how to hold, point and shoot the boule. Pointing is the throw that finishes close to the target; shooting is the throw that moves an opponent's boule.

## Sub lesson 3 — The rules of petanque

The third lesson teaches the **rules of petanque** — the laws of the game as the guide prints them. The game is played by those rules, and this lesson names them as its subject without adding to them.
