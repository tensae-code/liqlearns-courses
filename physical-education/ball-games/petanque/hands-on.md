# Hands-on — Petanque

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Point the boule

Hold a boule the way the lesson describes and point it toward a target ball on flat ground. Your partner watches where it lands and says whether it finished nearer or farther than the last throw.

## Shoot the boule

Practise the shooting throw at a partner's boule placed on the ground. Your partner checks that the throw follows the lesson's basic technique before each try.
