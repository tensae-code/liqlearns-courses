# Petanque — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three petanque lessons does the Grade 12 guide teach, in order?
**Answer: The history and importance of petanque, the basic petanque throwing techniques, and the rules of petanque.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the two basic throws this lesson names?
**Answer: Pointing — throwing to finish close to the target — and shooting — throwing to move an opponent's boule.**
