# Petanque — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which book teaches petanque in this lesson?
a) Laos's Grade 12 PE teacher guide  b) A Grade 1 guide  c) No book — it is not taught
**Answer: a) Laos's Grade 12 PE teacher guide**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In petanque, pointing means…
a) Throwing the boule to finish close to the target  b) Pointing at the scoreboard  c) Choosing teams
**Answer: a) Throwing the boule to finish close to the target**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shooting in petanque is the throw that…
a) Tries to move an opponent's boule  b) Starts the lesson  c) Ends the school day
**Answer: a) Tries to move an opponent's boule**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the Grade 12 guide order its petanque teaching?
a) History and importance, then basic throwing techniques, then the rules  b) Rules only  c) It does not teach petanque
**Answer: a) History and importance, then basic throwing techniques, then the rules**
