# Using Tactics in a Match — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A match tactic plan is:
a) Drawn up from what tactics are  b) Made up after the match  c) Chosen by the referee
**Answer: a) Drawn up from what tactics are**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Applying tactics during a match means:
a) Observing play and adjusting  b) Ignoring the other team  c) Never changing anything
**Answer: a) Observing play and adjusting**
