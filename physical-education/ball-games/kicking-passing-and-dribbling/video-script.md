# Video script — Kicking, Passing and Dribbling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Kicking and passing a ball. Kick a ball standing still.

Ball control with the feet. Dribble a ball tied on a string, tapping it with the front of your foot.

Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets. Combine equipment skills with travelling and stationary moves.

Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence). Dribble a ball with your feet along the ground — control it with the inside of your foot, keep it close, then strike it toward the goal or target.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
