# Video script — Kicking, Passing and Dribbling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Kicking and passing a ball. Kick a ball standing still.

Ball control with the feet. Dribble a ball tied on a string, tapping it with the front of your foot.

Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets. Combine equipment skills with travelling and stationary moves.

Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence). Dribble a ball with your feet along the ground — control it with the inside of your foot, keep it close, then strike it toward the goal or target.

Passing with the inside and outside of the foot — Grade 5. Pass and receive with the inside of the foot at graded distances — 4 metres, 6 metres, 8 metres, up to 10 metres: ankle locked, ball along the ground to your partner.
Football dribbling — Grade 6. Dribbling (ኳስ መንዳት) moves the ball with you, close to the feet.
Kicking and controlling — Grade 7. Kick with the inside of the foot, and with the front of the foot, from a correct stance.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

Shooting at goal — Grade 8. Shoot at goal in stages, exactly as the book builds it.

Chest control, first touch and heading — Grade 9. Control brings the ball under your command.

Tackling, thigh control and goalkeeping — Grade 10. Three advanced football skills.
