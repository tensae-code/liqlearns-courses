# Kicking, Passing and Dribbling — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kicking and passing a ball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — kicking still, kicking after steps, passing, and receiving and controlling a pass.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ball control with the feet
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — tapping a string ball with the front of the foot, and kicking and pushing hanging balls.

## Sub lesson 3 — Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — zigzag dribbling between cones, target throwing, and ring skills.

## Sub lesson 4 — Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — inside-of-the-foot dribbling kept close, striking to a target, and setting over a net.
