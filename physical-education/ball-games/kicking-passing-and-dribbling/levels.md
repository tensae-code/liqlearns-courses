# Kicking, Passing and Dribbling — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kicking and passing a ball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — kicking still, kicking after steps, passing, and receiving and controlling a pass.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ball control with the feet
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — tapping a string ball with the front of the foot, and kicking and pushing hanging balls.

## Sub lesson 3 — Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — zigzag dribbling between cones, target throwing, and ring skills.

## Sub lesson 4 — Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — inside-of-the-foot dribbling kept close, striking to a target, and setting over a net.

## Sub lesson 5 — Passing with the inside and outside of the foot — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — passing and receiving with the inside of the foot at 4, 6, 8 and up to 10 metres, with the outside of the foot, and one-touch circle play using both.

## Sub lesson 6 — Football dribbling — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — dribbling with the inside and the outside of the foot — 20 metres to a partner, between cones, then small team football games using the dribble.

## Sub lesson 7 — Kicking and controlling — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — kicking with the inside and the front of the foot, controlling with the sole against a wall at 7, 5 and 3 metres, and controlling with the front of the foot at 10 metres.

## Sub lesson 8 — Shooting at goal — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — shooting at goal — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 9 — Chest control, first touch and heading — Grade 9
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — chest control, first touch and heading — grade 9 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 10 — Tackling, thigh control and goalkeeping — Grade 10
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — tackling, thigh control and goalkeeping — grade 10 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
