# Kicking, Passing and Dribbling

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Kick a ball standing still. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kicking and passing a ball

Kick a ball standing still. Kick forward after one or two steps. Pass a ball to a partner. Receive a pass and control it — trap it still.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ball control with the feet

Dribble a ball tied on a string, tapping it with the front of your foot. In pairs, kick a thrown ball in the air at close range. Kick a hanging ball standing still, and lie on your back and push a hanging ball upward with your feet.

## Sub lesson 3 — Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets

Combine equipment skills with travelling and stationary moves. Dribble a ball while moving in different directions and in a zigzag between cones, keeping it close. Throw a ball at a target. Turn step-rings by hand, go in and out of a ring, turn a ring at your waist, pass a ball round your waist and between your legs, and kick a ball between cones.

## Sub lesson 4 — Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence)

Dribble a ball with your feet along the ground — control it with the inside of your foot, keep it close, then strike it toward the goal or target. In net ball, move and run with the ball and set and dig it with your hands over the net.

## Sub lesson 5 — Passing with the inside and outside of the foot — Grade 5

Pass and receive with the inside of the foot at graded distances — 4 metres, 6 metres, 8 metres, up to 10 metres: ankle locked, ball along the ground to your partner. Then the outside-of-foot pass, the counterpart skill, most useful once your ball skill has grown. Put both together in one-touch circle play: stand in a circle and play the ball with the inside and the outside of the foot in one touch, without stopping it.

## Sub lesson 6 — Football dribbling — Grade 6

Dribbling (ኳስ መንዳት) moves the ball with you, close to the feet. Dribble with the inside of the foot and with the outside of the foot; dribble 20 metres to a partner; dribble between cones, keeping the ball close at every turn; then play small team football games that use the dribble to beat space, not people.

## Sub lesson 7 — Kicking and controlling — Grade 7

Kick with the inside of the foot, and with the front of the foot, from a correct stance. Control the ball with the sole (በእግር መዳፍ): eye on the ball, trap it still under the sole — practise against a wall at 7 metres, then 5 metres, then 3 metres. Control with the front of the foot from 10 metres: meet the ball gently and take its speed away with the first touch.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
