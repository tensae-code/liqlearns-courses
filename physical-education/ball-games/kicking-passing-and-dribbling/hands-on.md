# Hands-on — Kicking, Passing and Dribbling

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: kicking and passing a ball, ball control with the feet, equipment skills — balls, rings and targets, football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence).

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.

## The Grade 5–7 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Passing with the inside and outside of the foot, Football dribbling, Kicking and controlling. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat and a partner or teacher for rolls, bars and balances.


## The Grade 8–10 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Shooting at goal — Grade 8, Chest control, first touch and heading — Grade 9, Tackling, thigh control and goalkeeping — Grade 10. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat, a spotter and soft or appropriate balls where the part says so.
