# Lesson plan — Kicking, Passing and Dribbling

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Kicking, Passing and Dribbling
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kicking and passing with the feet, controlling a ball close, dribbling in a zigzag, and the first net-ball skills.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 1–4 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Kicking and passing a ball** — kicking still, kicking after steps, passing, and receiving and controlling a pass.
2. **Ball control with the feet** — tapping a string ball with the front of the foot, and kicking and pushing hanging balls.
3. **Equipment skills — balls, rings and targets** — zigzag dribbling between cones, target throwing, and ring skills.
4. **Football dribbling and net ball (teacher guide — evidence)** — inside-of-the-foot dribbling kept close, striking to a target, and setting over a net.
5. **Passing with the inside and outside of the foot — Grade 5** — passing and receiving with the inside of the foot at 4, 6, 8 and up to 10 metres, with the outside of the foot, and one-touch circle play using both.
6. **Football dribbling — Grade 6** — dribbling with the inside and the outside of the foot — 20 metres to a partner, between cones, then small team football games using the dribble.
7. **Kicking and controlling — Grade 7** — kicking with the inside and the front of the foot, controlling with the sole against a wall at 7, 5 and 3 metres, and controlling with the front of the foot at 10 metres.
11. **Kicking a ball, and up-and-down steps (teacher guide — evidence)** — standing beside a ball and kicking it toward a partner or target with feet planted in turn, and stepping up onto and down from a low place in turn.
12. **Passing the football; hit the target (teacher guide — evidence)** — passing a football along the ground between partners over five metres, receiving with one foot, and throwing or kicking at a set target to score.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
