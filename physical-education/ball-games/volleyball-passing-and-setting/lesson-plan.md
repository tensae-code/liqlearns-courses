# Lesson plan — Volleyball Passing and Setting

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Volleyball Passing and Setting
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the volleyball family's hands — the forearm pass, the finger pass, setting for a team-mate, and the underarm serves that start the rally.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 5–7 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The forearm pass — Grade 5** — receiving and passing with the forearm (ክንድ) — a flat platform that rebounds the ball up to a partner — then games using the forearm.
2. **The finger pass — Grade 5** — receiving and passing with the fingers — holding the ball in two hands, throwing it above the head and tapping it up repeatedly with the fingertips.
3. **Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5** — setting (ለደፈቃ ኳስ ማመቻቸት) — placing the ball for a team-mate's dig, including the backwards finger set, from the middle of a circle to each player in turn.
4. **Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6** — the volleyball underarm serve (ልግ) — the easiest serve, used by beginners — from behind the line over the net to a partner 3–6 metres away, and receiving the serve on the forearms.
5. **Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7** — the side underarm serve (ኳስን በጎን ከታች ወደ ላይ መለጋት) to a partner and over the net, and forearm reception (በክንድ ልግን መቀበል) from a stance with the feet shoulder-width and the knees bent.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
