# Video script — Volleyball Passing and Setting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

The forearm pass — Grade 5. The forearm pass is volleyball's receiving skill: join the forearms into one flat platform, meet the ball on the platform, and rebound it up to a partner.

The finger pass — Grade 5. The finger pass plays the ball overhead: hold the ball in two hands, throw it above your head, and tap it up again and again with the fingers — the fingertips touch the ball when you push it up above the head.

Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5. Setting places the ball where a team-mate can play it.

Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6. The underarm serve (ልግ) is the easiest serve, the one beginners use: stand behind the line, swing the arm underarm, and serve over the net to a partner standing 3 to 6 metres away.

Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7. The side underarm serve (ኳስን በጎን ከታች ወደ ላይ መለጋት) turns the body side-on: take the grip and stance, serve to a partner without holding the ball in the air first, then serve over the net.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

The overhead serve — Grade 8. The overhead serve (ከላይ ወደታች መለጋት) is the serve that marks a complete volleyball player.

The spike — Grade 9. Spiking is volleyball's main way to score, and it is built in four stages: the approach to the net, the take-off, the body movement in the air, and the hitting action.

Blocking — Grade 10. Blocking is the defence at the net.
