# Volleyball Passing and Setting — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. The forearm pass — Grade 5 — a
2. The finger pass — Grade 5 — b
3. Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5 — c
4. Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6 — d
5. Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7 — e

What each teaches:
a) receiving and passing with the forearm (ክንድ) — a flat platform that rebounds the ball up to a partner — then games using the forearm
b) receiving and passing with the fingers — holding the ball in two hands, throwing it above the head and tapping it up repeatedly with the fingertips
c) setting (ለደፈቃ ኳስ ማመቻቸት) — placing the ball for a team-mate's dig, including the backwards finger set, from the middle of a circle to each player in turn
d) the volleyball underarm serve (ልግ) — the easiest serve, used by beginners — from behind the line over the net to a partner 3–6 metres away, and receiving the serve on the forearms
e) the side underarm serve (ኳስን በጎን ከታች ወደ ላይ መለጋት) to a partner and over the net, and forearm reception (በክንድ ልግን መቀበል) from a stance with the feet shoulder-width and the knees bent

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
