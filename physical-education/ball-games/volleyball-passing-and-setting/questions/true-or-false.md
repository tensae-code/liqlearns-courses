# Volleyball Passing and Setting — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "The forearm pass — Grade 5" teaches: receiving and passing with the forearm (ክንድ) — a flat platform that rebounds the ball up to a partner — then games using the forearm.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "The forearm pass — Grade 5", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "The finger pass — Grade 5" teaches: receiving and passing with the fingers — holding the ball in two hands, throwing it above the head and tapping it up repeatedly with the fingertips.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The finger pass — Grade 5", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5" teaches: setting (ለደፈቃ ኳስ ማመቻቸት) — placing the ball for a team-mate's dig, including the backwards finger set, from the middle of a circle to each player in turn.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In this lesson, "Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6" teaches: the volleyball underarm serve (ልግ) — the easiest serve, used by beginners — from behind the line over the net to a partner 3–6 metres away, and receiving the serve on the forearms.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In "Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** In this lesson, "Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7" teaches: the side underarm serve (ኳስን በጎን ከታች ወደ ላይ መለጋት) to a partner and over the net, and forearm reception (በክንድ ልግን መቀበል) from a stance with the feet shoulder-width and the knees bent.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In "Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
