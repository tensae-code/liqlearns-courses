# Volleyball Passing and Setting — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — The forearm pass — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — receiving and passing with the forearm (ክንድ) — a flat platform that rebounds the ball up to a partner — then games using the forearm.

## Sub lesson 2 — The finger pass — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — receiving and passing with the fingers — holding the ball in two hands, throwing it above the head and tapping it up repeatedly with the fingertips.

## Sub lesson 3 — Setting the ball for a team-mate — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — setting (ለደፈቃ ኳስ ማመቻቸት) — placing the ball for a team-mate's dig, including the backwards finger set, from the middle of a circle to each player in turn.

## Sub lesson 4 — Underarm serve and forearm receive — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the volleyball underarm serve (ልግ) — the easiest serve, used by beginners — from behind the line over the net to a partner 3–6 metres away, and receiving the serve on the forearms.

## Sub lesson 5 — Side underarm serve and receiving stance — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the side underarm serve (ኳስን በጎን ከታች ወደ ላይ መለጋት) to a partner and over the net, and forearm reception (በክንድ ልግን መቀበል) from a stance with the feet shoulder-width and the knees bent.
