# Hands-on — Kickball

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mini-games first

Play kickball the way Timor-Leste's guide builds it: start with the appendix's mini-games — the small games that teach the kick-and-run game piece by piece — before the full game.

## Name it as you play

Each time a player kicks and sets off running, the class calls the game's name together: kick — and run. The name is the game's whole idea.
