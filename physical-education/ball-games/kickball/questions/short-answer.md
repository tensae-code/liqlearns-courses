# Kickball — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is kickball?
**Answer: The North-American kick-and-run ball game.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two other sports in the guide's seven-sport appendix.
**Answer: Any two of: football/futsal, volleyball, handball, basketball, ultimate frisbee, human football.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the mini-games for?
**Answer: They teach the full game piece by piece, before it is played whole.**
