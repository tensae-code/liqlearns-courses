# Kickball

## Do it first

A ball game where you kick the ball and then run. What would its rules have to settle — where you kick from, where you run to, and how the other team stops you? Say your answers, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kickball — the game and its rules

Timor-Leste's Grade 7 teacher guide teaches **kickball** in its **seven-sport appendix** — the reference library of sports the class can choose from — alongside football/futsal, volleyball, handball, basketball, ultimate frisbee and human football. For each sport the appendix gives the **basic rules and mini-games**.

Kickball is **the North-American kick-and-run ball game**: the player kicks the ball and runs, and the game is built on that kick and that run. Timor-Leste adopted it into its PE through this appendix, as one of the seven sports a class can choose for its period tournament cycle.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kickball mini-games

The guide does not throw learners straight into the full game: its appendix gives **mini-games** for kickball — small games that teach the big game piece by piece. The mini-games come first; the full game grows out of them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what kickball is, where Timor-Leste teaches it, and why the mini-games come before the full game.
