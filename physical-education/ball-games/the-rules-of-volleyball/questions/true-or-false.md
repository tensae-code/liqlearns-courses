# The Rules of Volleyball — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The net's mesh squares are 10 cm and black.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All twelve players of a team play on the court at the same time.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A player found standing out of the rotational order is a fault.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A ball played under the net and not recovered is out.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The block contact counts as one of the team's three hits.
**Answer: False**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Referees lead the match, and their decision settles a rally.
**Answer: True**
