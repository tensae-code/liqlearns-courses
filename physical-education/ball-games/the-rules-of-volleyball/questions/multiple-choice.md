# The Rules of Volleyball — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The volleyball court is…
a) Rectangular and symmetrical  b) Round  c) A different size for each team
**Answer: a) Rectangular and symmetrical**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The free zone is…
a) The clear space around the court where a wide ball may be chased  b) The space under the net  c) Where the substitutes sit
**Answer: a) The clear space around the court where a wide ball may be chased**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The net is…
a) 1 m wide and 9.50–10 m long, of 10 cm black mesh  b) 2 m wide and 5 m long  c) As long as the players like
**Answer: a) 1 m wide and 9.50–10 m long, of 10 cm black mesh**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A volleyball team may have up to…
a) 12 players  b) 6 players  c) 20 players
**Answer: a) 12 players**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How many players of one team are on the court at one time?
a) Always six  b) All twelve  c) Five
**Answer: a) Always six**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The starting line-up fixes…
a) The rotational order for the set  b) The score  c) The colour of the ball
**Answer: a) The rotational order for the set**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** When a team wins the serve back from the other team, its players…
a) Rotate — each moves round one position  b) Stay exactly where they are  c) All move to the net
**Answer: a) Rotate — each moves round one position**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A rally continues until…
a) The ball touches the ground, goes out, or a team fails to return it properly  b) Someone is tired  c) The referee leaves
**Answer: a) The ball touches the ground, goes out, or a team fails to return it properly**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** A ball that lands outside the court's lines is…
a) Out, and the rally ends  b) Still in play  c) Worth two points
**Answer: a) Out, and the rally ends**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these is a facility, not equipment?
a) The court and its posts — the lasting, built parts  b) The ball  c) The whistle a player brings
**Answer: a) The court and its posts — the lasting, built parts**

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** A team may hit the ball how many times to return it over the net?
a) Three times — in addition to the block contact  b) Once only  c) As many times as it likes
**Answer: a) Three times — in addition to the block contact**

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The five major skills that frame the game are…
a) Service, pass, set, spike and block  b) Running, jumping and throwing  c) Dribbling, shooting and tackling
**Answer: a) Service, pass, set, spike and block**
