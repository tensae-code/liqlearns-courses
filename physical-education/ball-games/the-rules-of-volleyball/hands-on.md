# Hands-on — The Rules of Volleyball

The lesson on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Walk the court
Walk the court's edge, then the free zone around it. Stand at the net and pace its length with a partner; say its width and length aloud at the posts.

## Rotate for real
Six players stand in the starting order. Serve once from the back corner. Win the serve back in a short rally, then rotate — everyone moves round exactly one position. Repeat until each player has served once.

## Call it true
Play short rallies. The player nearest each landing calls "in" or "out" at once and points where the ball landed. A wrong call is replayed slowly, together.

## If it is hard
Draw the court small on paper first and move six counters through one rotation with your finger, naming each position, before doing it with bodies on the real court.
