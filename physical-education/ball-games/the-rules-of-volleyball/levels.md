# The Rules of Volleyball — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The Grade 9 pass is taught as its own depth here; the Grade 10 pass (interruptions and the libero) lives in Volleyball Passing and Setting and is cross-referenced, not repeated.

## Sub lesson 1 — The court, the free zone and the net
Pass mark: the learner draws and explains the rectangular, symmetrical court, the free zone around it, and the net — 1 m wide, 9.50–10 m long, 10 cm black mesh — on its posts.

## Sub lesson 2 — Twelve players, six on court, and the rotational order
Pass mark: the learner states up to 12 players a team and always six on court, explains that the starting line-up fixes the rotational order for the set, and rotates one position correctly.

## Sub lesson 3 — How a rally is played, and when the ball is out
Pass mark: the learner follows a rally's playing actions in order and calls the ball out correctly when it lands out or cannot be recovered.

## Sub lesson 4 — Facilities, equipment and the referees (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner tells a facility from equipment with an example of each, says what the referees do, and states that a team may hit the ball three times in addition to the block contact to return it.
