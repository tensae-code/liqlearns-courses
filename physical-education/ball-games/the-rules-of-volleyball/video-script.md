# Video script — The Rules of Volleyball

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then say it with me.

The court is a rectangle. The same on both sides.
Around it, clear space. That is the free zone.
Across the middle, the net. One metre wide. Nine and a half to ten metres long.

A team has twelve players. Six are on the court.
The first six fix the order. When we win the serve back, everyone moves round one place.

The rally goes on until the ball touches the ground, goes out, or is not returned properly.
Three hits to send it back — and the block touch does not count as one of the three.

Referees lead the match. Their call settles the rally.

Now you. Name the court. Name the net. Show me the rotation.
