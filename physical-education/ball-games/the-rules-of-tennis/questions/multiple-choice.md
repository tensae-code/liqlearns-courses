# The Rules of Tennis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What court dimensions does the guide give?
a) Dimensions for singles and for doubles  b) Only one size for all games  c) No dimensions are given
**Answer: a) Dimensions for singles and for doubles**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In what units is tennis scored, as the guide teaches it?
a) Points, games and sets  b) Goals and corners  c) Rounds only
**Answer: a) Points, games and sets**
