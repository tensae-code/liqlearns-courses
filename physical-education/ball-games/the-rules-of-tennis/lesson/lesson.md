# The Rules of Tennis

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tennis court and equipment

The Grade 10 guide sets out the tennis court and equipment: racket weights and the ball's size and weight, court dimensions for singles and doubles, and the net.

## Sub lesson 2 — Scoring, the serve, foot faults and lets

The guide teaches scoring in points, games and sets, how the serve works, foot faults and lets — the rules a tennis match runs by.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — The rules of tennis: court, equipment, scoring, the serve and how a match runs. — in your own words, the way Afghanistan's books teach it.
