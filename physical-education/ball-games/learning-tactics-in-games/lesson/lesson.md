# Learning Tactics in Games

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Learning tactics through games

Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches learning tactics through games — handball 3v3, throw-catch and kasti lead-up games — and its Grade 7 teacher guide teaches how to teach tactics and space through small-sided games.

## Sub lesson 2 — Space in team games

The Grade 7 book teaches the concept of space in team sports such as football.

## Sub lesson 3 — Space in individual sports

The Grade 7 book teaches space and angles in individual sports — pencak silat movement and the shot-put release angle.

## Sub lesson 4 — Creating, using and defending space

The Grade 7 book teaches creating, using and defending space in game activities, including limited-space basketball.

## Sub lesson 5 — Moving with and without the ball

The Grade 7 book teaches on-the-ball and off-the-ball movement, such as blocking movement in sepak takraw.

## Sub lesson 6 — Choosing zone or man-to-man defence

The Grade 7 book teaches choosing zone defence or man-to-man defence, with zone formations analysed.

## Sub lesson 7 — Learning movement inside games

The Grade 7 book teaches learning movement inside games and small-sided games — attacking in football and defending in volleyball.

## Sub lesson 8 — Game principles and movement concepts

Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches game principles joined to movement concepts.

## Sub lesson 9 — Understanding tactical skills

The Grade 10 book teaches understanding tactical skills in games, and its teacher guide teaches how to teach tactical skills and awareness.

## Sub lesson 10 — Developing tactical awareness

The Grade 10 book teaches developing tactical awareness.

## Sub lesson 11 — Choosing the difficulty level of tactical skills

The Grade 10 book teaches choosing the difficulty level of tactical skills.
