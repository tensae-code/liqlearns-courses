# Lesson plan — Learning Tactics in Games

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Ball Games · **Lesson:** Learning Tactics in Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ball Games (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Learning tactics in games: game principles, tactical awareness, using space, and movement with and without the ball. — taught here through Indonesia's way: its Kurikulum Merdeka PJOK books for Grades 7–12.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Learning tactics through games** — Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches learning tactics through games — handball 3v3, throw-catch and kasti lead-up games — and its Grade 7 teacher guide teaches how to teach tactics and space through small-sided games.
2. **Space in team games** — The Grade 7 book teaches the concept of space in team sports such as football.
3. **Space in individual sports** — The Grade 7 book teaches space and angles in individual sports — pencak silat movement and the shot-put release angle.
4. **Creating, using and defending space** — The Grade 7 book teaches creating, using and defending space in game activities, including limited-space basketball.
5. **Moving with and without the ball** — The Grade 7 book teaches on-the-ball and off-the-ball movement, such as blocking movement in sepak takraw.
6. **Choosing zone or man-to-man defence** — The Grade 7 book teaches choosing zone defence or man-to-man defence, with zone formations analysed.
7. **Learning movement inside games** — The Grade 7 book teaches learning movement inside games and small-sided games — attacking in football and defending in volleyball.
8. **Game principles and movement concepts** — Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches game principles joined to movement concepts.
9. **Understanding tactical skills** — The Grade 10 book teaches understanding tactical skills in games, and its teacher guide teaches how to teach tactical skills and awareness.
10. **Developing tactical awareness** — The Grade 10 book teaches developing tactical awareness.
11. **Choosing the difficulty level of tactical skills** — The Grade 10 book teaches choosing the difficulty level of tactical skills.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
