# Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Dribbling and bounce-passing — Grade 5 — a
2. Basketball low dribble — Grade 6 — b
3. High dribble and dribbling games — Grade 6 — c
4. Basketball standing shot — Grade 7 — d
5. Handball dribble and jump throw — Grade 7 — e

What each teaches:
a) dribbling and bouncing the ball (በማንጠር) while moving, and the dribbling games that practise it
b) the low dribble — below the knee, to shield the ball from opponents and escape pressure — with figure-8 drills between the legs
c) the high dribble, dribbling between obstacles, and dribbling games in letter shapes — moving sideways, forwards and backwards
d) the standing shot at the basket (በመቆም ወደ ቅርጫት መወርወር) — grip, stance, and shooting without jumping — then game-form practice
e) handball dribbling with the ball held at the waist in both hands, and the jump-throw scoring technique

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
