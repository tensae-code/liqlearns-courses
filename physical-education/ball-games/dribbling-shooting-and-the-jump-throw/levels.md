# Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dribbling and bounce-passing — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — dribbling and bouncing the ball (በማንጠር) while moving, and the dribbling games that practise it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Basketball low dribble — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the low dribble — below the knee, to shield the ball from opponents and escape pressure — with figure-8 drills between the legs.

## Sub lesson 3 — High dribble and dribbling games — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the high dribble, dribbling between obstacles, and dribbling games in letter shapes — moving sideways, forwards and backwards.

## Sub lesson 4 — Basketball standing shot — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the standing shot at the basket (በመቆም ወደ ቅርጫት መወርወር) — grip, stance, and shooting without jumping — then game-form practice.

## Sub lesson 5 — Handball dribble and jump throw — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — handball dribbling with the ball held at the waist in both hands, and the jump-throw scoring technique.

## Sub lesson 6 — The basketball jump shot — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the basketball jump shot — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 7 — Lay-ups — Grade 9 underhand, Grade 10 extended-arm
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — lay-ups — grade 9 underhand, grade 10 extended-arm (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 8 — Handball — the dive throw, goalkeeping and passing — Grades 8–10
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — handball — the dive throw, goalkeeping and passing — grades 8–10 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
