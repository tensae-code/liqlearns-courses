# Hands-on — Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: Dribbling and bounce-passing, Basketball low dribble, High dribble and dribbling games, Basketball standing shot, Handball dribble and jump throw.

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.


## The Grade 8–10 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: The basketball jump shot — Grade 8, Lay-ups — Grade 9 underhand, Grade 10 extended-arm, Handball — the dive throw, goalkeeping and passing — Grades 8–10. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat, a spotter and soft or appropriate balls where the part says so.
