# Video script — Dribbling, Shooting and the Jump Throw

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Dribbling and bounce-passing — Grade 5. Dribbling and bounce-passing (በማንጠር) keep the ball moving under your hand.

Basketball low dribble — Grade 6. Basketball dribbling (ኳስ ማንጠር) pushes the ball to the ground continuously, under control, the body slightly bent at the waist, ready to move in any direction.

High dribble and dribbling games — Grade 6. The high dribble bounces the ball higher, for moving fast in open space.

Basketball standing shot — Grade 7. First the grip: hold the ball correctly in the hands.

Handball dribble and jump throw — Grade 7. Handball has been played for a long time, in the world and in Ethiopia.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

The basketball jump shot — Grade 8. Stand with feet slightly apart and knees slightly bent.

Lay-ups — Grade 9 underhand, Grade 10 extended-arm. The lay-up is basketball's most essential shot — you lay the ball up near the basket with one hand, off the backboard and in, for two points.

Handball — the dive throw, goalkeeping and passing — Grades 8–10. Handball joins running, jumping, catching and throwing.
