# Lesson plan — Athletics Competitions

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Athletics · **Lesson:** Athletics Competitions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Athletics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Athletics competitions: the stadium, the events programme, and putting your skills together in individual competition. — taught here through North Macedonia's Grade 11 book, which teaches every sport as technique, tactics, full rules and history.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Athletics competitions** — North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches athletics competitions: the stadium (400 m oval track around the field events area) and the full competition programme — short, middle and long track races, hurdles, steeplechase, relays (4×100 m, 4×400 m), race walking, the jumps and throws, and the decathlon and heptathlon — with discipline equipment, photo-finish timing, and three attempts in the vertical jumps.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
