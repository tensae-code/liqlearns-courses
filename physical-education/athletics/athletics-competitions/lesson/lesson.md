## Sub lesson 1 — Athletics competitions

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches athletics competitions: the stadium (400 m oval track around the field events area) and the full competition programme — short, middle and long track races, hurdles, steeplechase, relays (4×100 m, 4×400 m), race walking, the jumps and throws, and the decathlon and heptathlon — with discipline equipment, photo-finish timing, and three attempts in the vertical jumps.
