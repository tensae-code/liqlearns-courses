# Jumping for Distance and Height — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Long jump — Grade 5: run-up, take-off, flight, landing — a
2. Triple jump — Grade 6: hop, step, jump — b
3. High jump beginnings (teacher guide — evidence) — c
4. High jump — Grade 7: the scissors jump — d

What each teaches:
a) the long jump in its real parts — a fast run-up (መንደርደር) to the take-off line, take-off on one foot, arms and legs working in the air, and a two-foot landing
b) the triple jump — hop (land on the same leg), step (land on the opposite leg), jump — built from standing one-leg jumps
c) the high jump's parts — run-up, take-off on one foot, clearing the obstacle, landing — practised over a rope at 50–70 centimetres
d) the scissors jump (የመቀስ ዝላይ) — first standing, then from three run-up steps at speed, clearing the bar with the legs scissoring

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d
