# Jumping for Distance and Height — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "Long jump — Grade 5: run-up, take-off, flight, landing" teaches: the long jump in its real parts — a fast run-up (መንደርደር) to the take-off line, take-off on one foot, arms and legs working in the air, and a two-foot landing.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "Long jump — Grade 5: run-up, take-off, flight, landing", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "Triple jump — Grade 6: hop, step, jump" teaches: the triple jump — hop (land on the same leg), step (land on the opposite leg), jump — built from standing one-leg jumps.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Triple jump — Grade 6: hop, step, jump", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "High jump beginnings (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: the high jump's parts — run-up, take-off on one foot, clearing the obstacle, landing — practised over a rope at 50–70 centimetres.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "High jump beginnings (teacher guide — evidence)", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In this lesson, "High jump — Grade 7: the scissors jump" teaches: the scissors jump (የመቀስ ዝላይ) — first standing, then from three run-up steps at speed, clearing the bar with the legs scissoring.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In "High jump — Grade 7: the scissors jump", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
