# Video script — Jumping for Distance and Height

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Long jump — Grade 5: run-up, take-off, flight, landing. The long jump is taught in its real parts.

Triple jump — Grade 6: hop, step, jump. The triple jump (የስሉስ ዝላይ) is hop, step, jump.

High jump beginnings (teacher guide — evidence). Jumping, like running and throwing, is a natural movement, used to pass obstacles, and the skill feeds many sports.

High jump — Grade 7: the scissors jump. High-jump movements help many sports and daily life.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

High jump — the straddle — Grade 8. Jumpers clear the bar in different styles — the Fosbury flop is among them — and the style taught at this grade is the straddle (ስትራድል): run in, take off, and cross the bar with your body parallel over it, one leg over after the other, landing on the mats.

Long jump — Grade 10. The long jump has four phases: the approach run, the take-off, the flight, and the landing.

Triple jump — Grade 10. The triple jump is three jumps in one: hop, step, jump.
