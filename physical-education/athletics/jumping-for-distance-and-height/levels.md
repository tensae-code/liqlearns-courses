# Jumping for Distance and Height — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Long jump — Grade 5: run-up, take-off, flight, landing
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the long jump in its real parts — a fast run-up (መንደርደር) to the take-off line, take-off on one foot, arms and legs working in the air, and a two-foot landing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Triple jump — Grade 6: hop, step, jump
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the triple jump — hop (land on the same leg), step (land on the opposite leg), jump — built from standing one-leg jumps.

## Sub lesson 3 — High jump beginnings (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the high jump's parts — run-up, take-off on one foot, clearing the obstacle, landing — practised over a rope at 50–70 centimetres.

## Sub lesson 4 — High jump — Grade 7: the scissors jump
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the scissors jump (የመቀስ ዝላይ) — first standing, then from three run-up steps at speed, clearing the bar with the legs scissoring.
