Watch me. Then do it with me.

Throwing a small ball and a stick — Grade 5. Throwing a small ball for distance develops your throwing skill — throw overarm: step, turn, throw, and follow through, sending the ball far into the throwing area.

Ring and javelin-style throwing — Grade 6. The ring (quoits) throw: hold the ring on spread fingers in an open palm, swing the arm and turn before the throw, the way a discus turns.

Shot put — Grade 7: the five stages. The shot put has five stages: 1.

Javelin — Grade 7. The javelin (ጦር) is an individual event needing upper-body and arm strength, like the shot.

Discus — Grade 7. The discus stages: 1.
