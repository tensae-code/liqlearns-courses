# Throwing for Distance — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Throwing a small ball and a stick — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — overarm throwing of a small ball for distance, and the standing stick throw — step, turn, throw, follow through, into a clear area.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ring and javelin-style throwing — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — ring throwing from spread fingers in an open palm with a pre-throw turn, and javelin-style throwing — run-up, gathering the run's force, the spear pulled back then thrown forward past the line.

## Sub lesson 3 — Shot put — Grade 7: the five stages
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the shot put in five stages — grip and stance, pre-throw arm movement, glide or turn, throw, and recovery with balance.

## Sub lesson 4 — Javelin — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the javelin throw — grip, run-up, throw, and a balanced finish without crossing the line; a throw that does not stick point-first shows the technique was wrong.

## Sub lesson 5 — Discus — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the discus throw in its stages — grip and basic stance, pre-throw arm swing, turn, throw — beginning with the standing throw.
