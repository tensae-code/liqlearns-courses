# Throwing for Distance — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Throwing a small ball and a stick — Grade 5 — a
2. Ring and javelin-style throwing — Grade 6 — b
3. Shot put — Grade 7: the five stages — c
4. Javelin — Grade 7 — d
5. Discus — Grade 7 — e

What each teaches:
a) overarm throwing of a small ball for distance, and the standing stick throw — step, turn, throw, follow through, into a clear area
b) ring throwing from spread fingers in an open palm with a pre-throw turn, and javelin-style throwing — run-up, gathering the run's force, the spear pulled back then thrown forward past the line
c) the shot put in five stages — grip and stance, pre-throw arm movement, glide or turn, throw, and recovery with balance
d) the javelin throw — grip, run-up, throw, and a balanced finish without crossing the line; a throw that does not stick point-first shows the technique was wrong
e) the discus throw in its stages — grip and basic stance, pre-throw arm swing, turn, throw — beginning with the standing throw

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
