# Throwing for Distance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "Throwing a small ball and a stick — Grade 5" teaches: overarm throwing of a small ball for distance, and the standing stick throw — step, turn, throw, follow through, into a clear area.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "Throwing a small ball and a stick — Grade 5", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Ring and javelin-style throwing — Grade 6", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
