# Outdoor and Adventure Activities

## Do it first

Walk your school grounds or a nearby open space and name everything you could move through, over or towards. That open-air ground is this lesson's classroom — now read how Turkey's books teach in it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Field and nature sports — the specialised skills

Turkey's Grade 10 teacher guide teaches the specialised movement skills of field and nature sports (Alan/Doğa Sporları), with **cycling and archery prominent**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Field and nature sports — tactics and strategy

The same guide's next part builds **tactics and strategy in field and nature sports** — choosing how to use the skills out in the field.

## Sub lesson 3 — Exploring your surroundings and nature by moving

In the primary teacher guide, grades 3 and 4 each carry a theme of **exploring by moving**: grade 3 explores the surroundings (Hareket Ederek Çevremi Keşfediyorum), grade 4 explores nature itself (Hareket Ederek Doğayı Keşfediyorum).

## Sub lesson 4 — Open-air and adventure activities

The middle-school teacher guide's grade 7 Theme 3 is **Açık Alan ve Macera Etkinlikleri — open-air and adventure activities**, the spiral's adventure step.
