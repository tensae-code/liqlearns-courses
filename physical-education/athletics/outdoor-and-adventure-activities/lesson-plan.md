# Lesson plan — Outdoor and Adventure Activities

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Athletics · **Lesson:** Outdoor and Adventure Activities
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Athletics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Outdoor and adventure activities: open-air, field and nature sports, their skills and tactics.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Field and nature sports — the specialised skills**
2. **Field and nature sports — tactics and strategy**
3. **Exploring your surroundings and nature by moving**
4. **Open-air and adventure activities**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Timed and Distance Running. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the field and nature sports the books teach, with cycling and archery first.
2. Say how the books order the learning: specialised skills first, then tactics and strategy.
3. Say how the youngest learners meet the outdoors: exploring surroundings, then nature, by moving.

## What you need
Safe outdoor space, as the books' field and nature activities use it; a bicycle and archery set only where the school's own programme provides them, as the Grade 10 guide teaches them.

## If the learner is stuck
- The outdoors feels like free play → go back to the books' order: the Grade 10 guide teaches specialised skills first, tactics after — name the skill before you play. → You cannot name a field sport → start from the two the guide names as prominent: cycling and archery.
