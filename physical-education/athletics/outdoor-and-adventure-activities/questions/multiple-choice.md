# Outdoor and Adventure Activities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 10 guide's field and nature sports part names which two sports as prominent?
a) Cycling and archery  b) Football and basketball  c) Swimming and skiing
**Answer: a) Cycling and archery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the books' order, the specialised skills of field and nature sports come…
a) Before tactics and strategy  b) After tactics and strategy  c) Instead of any practice
**Answer: a) Before tactics and strategy**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The tactics and strategy part of the Grade 10 guide is about…
a) Choosing how to use the skills in the field  b) Memorising rule books  c) Indoor fitness tests
**Answer: a) Choosing how to use the skills in the field**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the primary guide, grade 3's exploring theme explores…
a) The surroundings, by moving  b) Only the classroom  c) Nothing — it rests
**Answer: a) The surroundings, by moving**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 7 Theme 3 in the middle-school guide is…
a) Open-air and adventure activities  b) A written exam  c) A swimming unit
**Answer: a) Open-air and adventure activities**
