# Hands-on — Outdoor and Adventure Activities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Explore by moving

Walk a route around your grounds the way the primary guide's themes do it: explore your surroundings as you move, naming what you find. Your partner walks it after you and names one thing you missed.

## Skills before tactics

Pick one field sport from the lesson. First show its basic skill slowly, as the Grade 10 guide's skills part orders it; then say out loud one tactic you would choose in the field, as its tactics part orders it. Your partner checks the order: skills first, tactics second.
