# Baton Relay Racing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — The baton relay — a team race
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the baton relay as a prized Olympic and world-championship team event — 4 × 100 metres, or 4 × 50 / 4 × 25 metres where space is short.

## Sub lesson 2 — The upsweep changeover
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the upsweep changeover (ከታች ወደ ላይ) — the baton passed from below upward into the waiting hand.

## Sub lesson 3 — The downsweep changeover
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the downsweep changeover — the baton pressed down into the waiting hand — and full competition changeovers.

## Sub lesson 4 — Relay exchanges in motion — Grade 9
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — relay exchanges in motion — grade 9 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
