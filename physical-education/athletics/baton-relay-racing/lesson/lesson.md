## Sub lesson 1 — The baton relay — a team race

The baton relay is a team race: four runners each run a leg, passing the baton inside the changeover zone. It is a prized event at the Olympic Games and the World Championships. Where space is short, run 4 × 50 metres or 4 × 25 metres instead of 4 × 100 metres. The race is won or lost at the changeover — practise it standing, then moving, then in full competition.

## Sub lesson 2 — The upsweep changeover

Upsweep (ከታች ወደ ላይ) means from below upward: the incoming runner sweeps the baton up into the outgoing runner's waiting hand, palm down and ready behind them. The outgoing runner does not look back — they trust the call and the hand. Practise it standing, then walking, then running inside the zone.

## Sub lesson 3 — The downsweep changeover

Downsweep is the second technique: the incoming runner presses the baton down into the outgoing runner's upturned palm. Learn both techniques, choose the one your team does best, and use it the same way every time. Finish with full competition changeovers at race speed inside the zone.
