# Video script — Baton Relay Racing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

The baton relay — a team race. The baton relay is a team race: four runners each run a leg, passing the baton inside the changeover zone.

The upsweep changeover. Upsweep (ከታች ወደ ላይ) means from below upward: the incoming runner sweeps the baton up into the outgoing runner's waiting hand, palm down and ready behind them.

The downsweep changeover. Downsweep is the second technique: the incoming runner presses the baton down into the outgoing runner's upturned palm.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

Relay exchanges in motion — Grade 9. A relay team has four runners and three exchanges, and the fourth runner crosses the finish.
