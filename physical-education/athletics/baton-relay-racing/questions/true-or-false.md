# Baton Relay Racing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "The baton relay — a team race" teaches: the baton relay as a prized Olympic and world-championship team event — 4 × 100 metres, or 4 × 50 / 4 × 25 metres where space is short.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "The baton relay — a team race", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "The upsweep changeover" teaches: the upsweep changeover (ከታች ወደ ላይ) — the baton passed from below upward into the waiting hand.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The upsweep changeover", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "The downsweep changeover" teaches: the downsweep changeover — the baton pressed down into the waiting hand — and full competition changeovers.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "The downsweep changeover", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
