# Baton Relay Racing — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. The baton relay — a team race — a
2. The upsweep changeover — b
3. The downsweep changeover — c
4. Relay exchanges in motion — Grade 9 — d
5. The first relay — out, back, hand over; copy the broom game — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence) — e

What each teaches:
a) the baton relay as a prized Olympic and world-championship team event — 4 × 100 metres, or 4 × 50 / 4 × 25 metres where space is short
b) the upsweep changeover (ከታች ወደ ላይ) — the baton passed from below upward into the waiting hand
c) the downsweep changeover — the baton pressed down into the waiting hand — and full competition changeovers
d) four runners, three exchanges, the baton passed inside the 20 m passing zone
e) the shuttle relay — out to the marker and back, hand over — and copying the broom-sweeping game in rows

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
