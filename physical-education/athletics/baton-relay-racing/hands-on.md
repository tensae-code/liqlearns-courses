# Hands-on — Baton Relay Racing

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: The baton relay, The upsweep changeover, The downsweep changeover.

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.


## The Grade 8–10 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Relay exchanges in motion — Grade 9. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat, a spotter and soft or appropriate balls where the part says so.

## The Grade 3 teacher-guide part
Do the new part in order, slowly first: the shuttle relay — out to a marker and back, handing over to the next runner — then the broom game: one learner shows a sweeping movement with a broom or stick, and the rows copy it exactly. Stop if anything hurts.
