# Video script — Sprinting and Hurdle Running

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Running games and the sprint start (teacher guide — evidence). In running games, start from a signal, run in a group so everyone finishes, and lift your knees high in turn while running in place.

Over the low hurdles (teacher guide — evidence). Run a line of four low obstacles from a standing start, clearing each one without breaking stride, again and again.

Skipping, sideways and backwards travel (teacher guide — evidence). Skip forward and sideways along the track line for 20 metres.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
