# Sprinting and Hurdle Running — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Running games and the sprint start (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — starting on a signal and running in a group so everyone finishes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Over the low hurdles (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — clearing four low hurdles in a run without breaking stride.

## Sub lesson 3 — Skipping, sideways and backwards travel (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — skipping forward and sideways for 20 metres, side gallop and careful backward travel.

## Sub lesson 4 — Sprinting and the start commands — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the sprint start — on your marks (በቦታህ) at the line, set in the starting block (መነሻ ሳንቃ), and drive out on the signal; racing 30–50 metres and 60–70 metres.

## Sub lesson 5 — Sprint training — Grade 6: high knees and butt kicks
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — running form drills — high knees and butt kicks — and comparing short-distance and middle-distance technique.

## Sub lesson 6 — Hurdles — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — hurdles — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 7 — Sprint start, drive and finish — Grade 9
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sprint start, drive and finish — grade 9 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
