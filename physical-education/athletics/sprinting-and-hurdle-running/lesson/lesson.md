# Sprinting and Hurdle Running

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: In running games, start from a signal, run in a group so everyone finishes, and lift your knees high in turn while running in place. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Running games and the sprint start (teacher guide — evidence)

In running games, start from a signal, run in a group so everyone finishes, and lift your knees high in turn while running in place. The start comes on the signal — be ready, then go.

## Sub lesson 2 — Over the low hurdles (teacher guide — evidence)

Run a line of four low obstacles from a standing start, clearing each one without breaking stride, again and again.

## Sub lesson 3 — Skipping, sideways and backwards travel (teacher guide — evidence)

Skip forward and sideways along the track line for 20 metres. Travel sideways in a side gallop, and travel backwards carefully along a line. These travelling forms build the speed and control sprinting needs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
