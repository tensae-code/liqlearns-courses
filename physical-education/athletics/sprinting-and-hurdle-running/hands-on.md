# Hands-on — Sprinting and Hurdle Running

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: running games and the sprint start (teacher guide — evidence), over the low hurdles (teacher guide — evidence), skipping, sideways and backwards travel (teacher guide — evidence).

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.

## The Grade 5–7 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Sprinting and the start commands, Sprint training. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat and a partner or teacher for rolls, bars and balances.
