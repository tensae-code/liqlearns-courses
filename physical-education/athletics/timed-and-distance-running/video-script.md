# Video script — Timed and Distance Running

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Timed running — Grade 5: beating your own time. Three markers stand at 5-metre spacing.

Running to the clock — middle distance (teacher guide — evidence). Middle-distance running keeps an even pace to the clock.

Distance running — Grade 6: 1500 and 2000 metres. Build distance by mixing paces.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
