a) timed runs over 20 metres past cones set 5 metres apart, and the 20–40 metre speed run — the aim is improving your time over the distance  b) middle-distance running to the clock — running slowly and steadily for 5–6 minutes, then 6–8 minutes in pairs and threes, correcting each other's form  c) an alternating-pace 1500 metres (500 m medium pace with 20 m marching breaks) and a medium-pace 2000 metre run
