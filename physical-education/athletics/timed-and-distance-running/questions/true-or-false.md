# Timed and Distance Running — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "Timed running — Grade 5: beating your own time" teaches: timed runs over 20 metres past cones set 5 metres apart, and the 20–40 metre speed run — the aim is improving your time over the distance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "Timed running — Grade 5: beating your own time", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "Running to the clock — middle distance (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: middle-distance running to the clock — running slowly and steadily for 5–6 minutes, then 6–8 minutes in pairs and threes, correcting each other's form.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Running to the clock — middle distance (teacher guide — evidence)", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "Distance running — Grade 6: 1500 and 2000 metres" teaches: an alternating-pace 1500 metres (500 m medium pace with 20 m marching breaks) and a medium-pace 2000 metre run.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "Distance running — Grade 6: 1500 and 2000 metres", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
