# Timed and Distance Running — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Timed running — Grade 5: beating your own time
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — timed runs over 20 metres past cones set 5 metres apart, and the 20–40 metre speed run — the aim is improving your time over the distance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Running to the clock — middle distance (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — middle-distance running to the clock — running slowly and steadily for 5–6 minutes, then 6–8 minutes in pairs and threes, correcting each other's form.

## Sub lesson 3 — Distance running — Grade 6: 1500 and 2000 metres
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — an alternating-pace 1500 metres (500 m medium pace with 20 m marching breaks) and a medium-pace 2000 metre run.
