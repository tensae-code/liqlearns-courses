# Hands-on — Skiing for Beginners

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. All practice is on snow with a teacher, in a safe place.

## The sliding step, checked on the ground

Without skis, walk the pattern the guide names first: slide one foot forward along the ground, then the other — the sliding step's shape. Then the alternating stride: opposite arm and leg together, as in walking, longer and gliding. Name each one as you do it.

## Down, turn, climb — say the set

Point and say, in order: descending in a stance, the step turn, climbing "ladder" style. A partner checks your order against the lesson. On snow, these are done only where the teacher sets them.
