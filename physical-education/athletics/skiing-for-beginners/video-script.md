# Video script — Skiing for Beginners

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Slide — one ski, then the other. Down in a stance. Turn by stepping. Climb like a ladder.
Then the whole distance, and free skiing at the end.
That is **skiing for beginners**.
