# Skiing for Beginners

## Do it first

Two long skis, snow under them, and you have never skied. What do you think the hardest part would be — moving forward, going down, or getting up a slope? Guess first — then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — First steps on skis

Kazakhstan's Grade 4 guide gives its third quarter to **ski training** (лыжная подготовка), and it starts where a beginner starts: the **sliding step** — gliding forward on the skis — and the **alternating stride**, the stride that carries the skier along the track.

## Sub lesson 2 — Going down, turning and climbing

The same beginner set teaches the three slope skills: **descending in a stance**, coming down held in the skiing stance; the **step turn**, turning by stepping the skis round; and **climbing "ladder" style**, stepping up the slope sideways like the rungs of a ladder.

## Sub lesson 3 — Skiing the distance

The quarter builds to distance: **covering 2–3 km** on skis, and **free skiing** — skiing on your own once the steps are yours. The guide pairs the practice with **safety theory** and **drill on skis**, so the beginner learns how to be safe on snow together with how to move on it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the whole beginner set in order: sliding step, alternating stride, descending in a stance, step turn, ladder climbing, 2–3 km, free skiing — and the two things that go with it: safety theory and drill on skis.
