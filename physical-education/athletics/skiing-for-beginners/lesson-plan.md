# Lesson plan — Skiing for Beginners

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Athletics · **Lesson:** Skiing for Beginners
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Athletics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Skiing for beginners: the sliding step, strides, descents, turns and climbing on skis.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **First steps on skis** — the Kazakhstan guide: the sliding step and the alternating stride.
2. **Going down, turning and climbing** — the Kazakhstan guide: descending in a stance, the step turn, and climbing "ladder" style.
3. **Skiing the distance** — the Kazakhstan guide: covering 2–3 km and free skiing, with safety theory and drill on skis.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Running Basics; Timed and Distance Running. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the beginner's first steps on skis as Kazakhstan's guide teaches them: the sliding step and the alternating stride.
2. Say how a beginner goes down, turns and climbs: descending in a stance, the step turn, climbing "ladder" style.
3. Say what the quarter builds to: covering 2–3 km and free skiing — and that safety theory and drill on skis go with it.

## What you need
Skis on snow, in a safe place with a teacher, as the guide's own lessons provide; the knowledge parts need nothing. Ski practice is never done alone on steep ground.

## If the learner is stuck
- The steps blur → order them as the guide does: slide first, then stride; down in a stance, turn by stepping, climb like a ladder.
- The distance feels big → it is the quarter's build-up, reached step by step, ending in free skiing.
