# Skiing for Beginners — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A beginner's first step on skis, as the guide teaches it, is…
a) The sliding step  b) The jump turn  c) Free skiing
**Answer: a) The sliding step**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The stride that carries the skier along the track is…
a) The alternating stride  b) The ladder climb  c) The stance
**Answer: a) The alternating stride**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Climbing "ladder" style means…
a) Stepping up the slope sideways, like ladder rungs  b) Climbing a real ladder  c) Sliding down fast
**Answer: a) Stepping up the slope sideways, like ladder rungs**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The ski quarter builds to covering…
a) 2–3 km  b) 100 m  c) 20 km
**Answer: a) 2–3 km**
