# Skiing for Beginners — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two first steps of ski training in the guide.
**Answer: The sliding step and the alternating stride.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three slope skills in the beginner set.
**Answer: Descending in a stance, the step turn, and climbing "ladder" style.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the quarter end in, after the 2–3 km distance?
**Answer: Free skiing.**
