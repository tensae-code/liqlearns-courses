# Hands-on — Ballet Basics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Try the positions

Stand and try ballet's basic positions one by one, the way the guide's expressive-movement chapter introduces them — slowly, to a rhythm.

## Build a tiny routine

Join three of the basic positions into a short free routine of your own, in the spirit of the chapter's sample free ballet routine, and perform it for someone.
