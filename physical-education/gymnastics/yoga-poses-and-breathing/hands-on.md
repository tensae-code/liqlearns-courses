# Hands-on — Yoga Poses and Breathing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do yoga outdoors

Outside, on a safe flat place, do the postures Honduras's Grade 9 book names after animals and natural things — Mountain, Tree, Dog, Cat, Cow and Fish — and then the Sun Salutation (Saludo al Sol), moving slowly and breathing calmly, the way the book teaches yoga in nature.
