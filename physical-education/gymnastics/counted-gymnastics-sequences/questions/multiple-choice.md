# Counted Gymnastics Sequences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the part "Counted waist series" teach?
a) numbered sequences of 1 to 10 positions for arms, waist and legs, moved on the counts  b) hands-on-waist waist bending and stretching in a counted series  c) sitting still and watching only
**Answer: b) hands-on-waist waist bending and stretching in a counted series**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is done in "Counted waist series"?
a) numbered sequences of 1 to 10 positions for arms, waist and legs, moved on the counts  b) sitting still and watching only  c) hands-on-waist waist bending and stretching in a counted series
**Answer: c) hands-on-waist waist bending and stretching in a counted series**
