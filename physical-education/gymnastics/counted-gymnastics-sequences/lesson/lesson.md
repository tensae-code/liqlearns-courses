# Counted Gymnastics Sequences

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: With hands on your waist, bend and stretch your waist to different directions in a counted series — one position for each count. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Counted waist series

With hands on your waist, bend and stretch your waist to different directions in a counted series — one position for each count. Leg-movement series are counted the same way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Full counted sequences, position by position

Perform full counted sequences printed as numbered position grids — 1 to 10 positions in a sequence — for arms, waist and legs. Move to the next position exactly on its count.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
