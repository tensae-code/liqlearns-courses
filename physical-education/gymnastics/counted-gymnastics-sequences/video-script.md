# Video script — Counted Gymnastics Sequences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Counted waist series. With hands on your waist, bend and stretch your waist to different directions in a counted series — one position for each count.

Full counted sequences, position by position. Perform full counted sequences printed as numbered position grids — 1 to 10 positions in a sequence — for arms, waist and legs.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
