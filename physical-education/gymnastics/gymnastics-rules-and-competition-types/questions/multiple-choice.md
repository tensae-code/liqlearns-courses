# Gymnastics — Rules and Competition Types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The basic laws of gymnastics are…
a) different for every school  b) the rules that govern how the movements are performed and competed, the same for everyone  c) only for teachers
**Answer: b) the rules that govern how the movements are performed and competed, the same for everyone**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the laws matter for a competition?
a) They turn the same movements into a fair contest judged the same way everywhere  b) They make the movements easier  c) They choose the music
**Answer: a) They turn the same movements into a fair contest judged the same way everywhere**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Artistic gymnastics is…
a) the apparatus kind, its men's events pictured at the front of the chapter  b) performed only with a ribbon  c) a running event
**Answer: a) the apparatus kind, its men's events pictured at the front of the chapter**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rhythmic gymnastics at the Olympics is performed…
a) by mixed teams  b) for women only  c) only by children
**Answer: b) for women only**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sports aerobics belongs, in this lesson's map, to…
a) the Olympic running events  b) the wider family at the world championships  c) the ball games
**Answer: b) the wider family at the world championships**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's map has three parts. They are…
a) laws; the Olympic kinds; the championship family  b) running, jumping, throwing  c) home, school, stadium
**Answer: a) laws; the Olympic kinds; the championship family**
