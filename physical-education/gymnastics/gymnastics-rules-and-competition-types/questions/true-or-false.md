# Gymnastics — Rules and Competition Types — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gymnastics movements in competition stand under basic laws.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Artistic and rhythmic gymnastics are the same kind under two names.
**Answer: False** — artistic is the apparatus kind; rhythmic is the routine kind, performed for women only.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rhythmic gymnastics is performed for women only.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sports aerobics is one of the kinds at the world championships.
**Answer: True**
