# Hands-on — Gymnastics — Rules and Competition Types

The lesson with your eyes and your voice, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Sort the kinds

Watch or picture six gymnastics movements — from class, a picture strip, or the lessons beside this one. Sort each one: artistic, rhythmic, or sports aerobics? Say the clue that decided you.

## Judge by the laws

With a partner: one performs a simple movement from the Gymnastics lessons (a balance, a roll) twice — once carefully, once carelessly. The other says, as a judge under the basic laws would, what differed. Swap.

## If it is hard

Use the picture only: point to the artistic side, point to the rhythmic side, and say one sentence about each.
