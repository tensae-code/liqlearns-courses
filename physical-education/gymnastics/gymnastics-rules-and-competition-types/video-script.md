The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Gymnastics has laws. The same laws for everyone. That is what makes it a competition.

At the Olympics: artistic gymnastics — strength on apparatus. And rhythmic gymnastics — the routine, for women only.

At the world championships, the family is wider. Sports aerobics is there too.

Laws underneath. Kinds on top. That is the map.
