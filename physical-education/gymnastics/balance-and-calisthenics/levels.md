# Balance and Calisthenics — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What balance is
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — controlling balance so you do not fall — one-leg balances forward and back.

## Sub lesson 2 — Calisthenics — arms and shoulders
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — hands on shoulders, stretch side/up/forward, circle forward and backward.

## Sub lesson 3 — Calisthenics — legs
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — seated alternate leg raises and hand-and-knee supports.

## Sub lesson 4 — Combined gymnastics movements
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — same-side arm and leg together, and crouch-jump-straighten in the air.
