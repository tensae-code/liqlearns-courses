# Video script — Balance and Calisthenics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

What balance is. Balance is controlling your body's balance when standing, lying, walking and running.

Calisthenics — arms and shoulders. Put your hands on your shoulders, then stretch your arms to the side, up and forward, and fold them back.

Calisthenics — legs. Sit and raise your two legs alternately, in rhythm.

Combined gymnastics movements. Raise one arm with the same-side leg and arm moving together.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
