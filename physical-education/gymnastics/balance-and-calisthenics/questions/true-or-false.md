# Balance and Calisthenics — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, "What balance is" teaches: controlling balance so you do not fall — one-leg balances forward and back.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "What balance is", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, "Calisthenics — arms and shoulders" teaches: hands on shoulders, stretch side/up/forward, circle forward and backward.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Calisthenics — arms and shoulders", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, "Calisthenics — legs" teaches: seated alternate leg raises and hand-and-knee supports.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In "Calisthenics — legs", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In this lesson, "Combined gymnastics movements" teaches: same-side arm and leg together, and crouch-jump-straighten in the air.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In "Combined gymnastics movements", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.


**9. (Sub lesson 5)** In this lesson, "Free gymnastics and balance — Grade 5" teaches: free gymnastics without apparatus, with keeping balance as a named element — and form checked, not just done: the hand turned fully to the opposite leg.

**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
