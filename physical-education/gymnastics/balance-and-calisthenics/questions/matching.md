# Balance and Calisthenics — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. What balance is — a
2. Calisthenics — arms and shoulders — b
3. Calisthenics — legs — c
4. Combined gymnastics movements — d
5. Free gymnastics and balance — Grade 5 — e

What each teaches:
a) controlling balance so you do not fall — one-leg balances forward and back
b) hands on shoulders, stretch side/up/forward, circle forward and backward
c) seated alternate leg raises and hand-and-knee supports
d) same-side arm and leg together, and crouch-jump-straighten in the air
e) free gymnastics without apparatus, with keeping balance as a named element — and form checked, not just done: the hand turned fully to the opposite leg

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e
