# Lesson plan — Gymnastics with Apparatus

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Gymnastics · **Lesson:** Gymnastics with Apparatus
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Gymnastics (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** gymnastics on apparatus — the box and the bars — always with a helper, always landing in control.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 5–7 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Apparatus gymnastics — Grade 5: box and bar** — jumping up onto a box and down, hanging and swinging on a horizontal bar by the hands, and rolling forward over a bar while resting on the stomach — with apparatus safety and spotting.
2. **Single bar — Grade 6: roll-over and swing-through** — apparatus work always with a helper (በረዳት): hanging backward roll-over on the single bar, and swinging through between the hands backward — higher-skill moves needing great help and care.
3. **Uneven bars — Grade 6** — the uneven bars — hanging from the taller bar, moving between the tall and the short bars, swinging and dismounting to the ground.
4. **Bar work — Grade 7: pull-up, swing and traverse** — the pull-up method on the bar, swinging forward and backward hanging from a single bar, and travelling hand over hand along a high bar from one end to the other.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Open flat ground, a mat for any roll or floor work, and the equipment named in the parts (a ball, a rope, rings or cones where the part says so). Modest sports clothes. A partner or a small group for the partner parts.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
