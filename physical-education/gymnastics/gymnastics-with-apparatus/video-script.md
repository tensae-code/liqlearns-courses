# Video script — Gymnastics with Apparatus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Apparatus gymnastics — Grade 5: box and bar. Apparatus gymnastics (የመሳሪያ ጅምናስቲክስ) works on equipment.

Single bar — Grade 6: roll-over and swing-through. Apparatus gymnastics is tumbling and flexibility work done with apparatus, always with a helper (በረዳት).

Uneven bars — Grade 6. On the uneven bars: hang from the taller bar; move between the tall bar and the short bar; swing, and dismount to the ground, landing in control on the mat.

Bar work — Grade 7: pull-up, swing and traverse. Three bar skills: the pull-up method, pulling up on the bar; swinging forward and backward while hanging from a single bar, in control; and travelling hand over hand along a high bar, from one end to the other, without letting go.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
