# Gymnastics with Apparatus — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Apparatus gymnastics — Grade 5: box and bar — a
2. Single bar — Grade 6: roll-over and swing-through — b
3. Uneven bars — Grade 6 — c
4. Bar work — Grade 7: pull-up, swing and traverse — d

What each teaches:
a) jumping up onto a box and down, hanging and swinging on a horizontal bar by the hands, and rolling forward over a bar while resting on the stomach — with apparatus safety and spotting
b) apparatus work always with a helper (በረዳት): hanging backward roll-over on the single bar, and swinging through between the hands backward — higher-skill moves needing great help and care
c) the uneven bars — hanging from the taller bar, moving between the tall and the short bars, swinging and dismounting to the ground
d) the pull-up method on the bar, swinging forward and backward hanging from a single bar, and travelling hand over hand along a high bar from one end to the other

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d
