# Gymnastics with Apparatus — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Apparatus gymnastics — Grade 5: box and bar
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — jumping up onto a box and down, hanging and swinging on a horizontal bar by the hands, and rolling forward over a bar while resting on the stomach — with apparatus safety and spotting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Single bar — Grade 6: roll-over and swing-through
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — apparatus work always with a helper (በረዳት): hanging backward roll-over on the single bar, and swinging through between the hands backward — higher-skill moves needing great help and care.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uneven bars — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the uneven bars — hanging from the taller bar, moving between the tall and the short bars, swinging and dismounting to the ground.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bar work — Grade 7: pull-up, swing and traverse
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the pull-up method on the bar, swinging forward and backward hanging from a single bar, and travelling hand over hand along a high bar from one end to the other.

## Sub lesson 5 — Parallel bars — the support swing — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — parallel bars — the support swing — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
