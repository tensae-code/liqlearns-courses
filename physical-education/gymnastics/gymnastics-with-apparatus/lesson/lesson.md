# Gymnastics with Apparatus

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Apparatus gymnastics (የመሳሪያ ጅምናስቲክስ) works on equipment. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Apparatus gymnastics — Grade 5: box and bar

Apparatus gymnastics (የመሳሪያ ጅምናስቲክስ) works on equipment. Jump up onto a box and down again, landing soft. On the horizontal bar: first jump to grip the bar, hang by the hands, and swing. Roll forward over a single horizontal bar while resting on the stomach. A teacher or spotter checks the apparatus first and stands where they can help — every time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Single bar — Grade 6: roll-over and swing-through

Apparatus gymnastics is tumbling and flexibility work done with apparatus, always with a helper (በረዳት). On the single bar: hang and roll over backward around the bar; and swing through between your own hands backward. The book flags these as higher-skill moves that need great help and care — no helper, no attempt.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uneven bars — Grade 6

On the uneven bars: hang from the taller bar; move between the tall bar and the short bar; swing, and dismount to the ground, landing in control on the mat. A helper watches every move, and the bars are checked before anyone hangs.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bar work — Grade 7: pull-up, swing and traverse

Three bar skills: the pull-up method, pulling up on the bar; swinging forward and backward while hanging from a single bar, in control; and travelling hand over hand along a high bar, from one end to the other, without letting go. Land every dismount on the mat, knees soft.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
