# Expressing with the Body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expressing with the body means communicating through…
a) Body, face and mime  b) Only written words  c) A whistle
**Answer: a) Body, face and mime**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 guide represents…
a) Animals, natural things and feelings  b) League positions  c) Swimming strokes
**Answer: a) Animals, natural things and feelings**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 2 takes body communication into…
a) Body language in everyday communication  b) Only the stage  c) Only sport
**Answer: a) Body language in everyday communication**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 3 joins gesture and body movement with…
a) Verbal expression  b) Silence  c) Written homework
**Answer: a) Verbal expression**
