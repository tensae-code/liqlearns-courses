# Hands-on — Expressing with the Body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Represent it

Show an animal, a natural thing (a tree in wind, rain falling), and a feeling — no words. Your partner names each one: the Grade 1 task.

## Everyday body language

With a partner, hold a whole everyday conversation — greeting, asking, agreeing, refusing — in body language only. Then say what each gesture meant.

## Body and voice together

Tell a tiny story twice: words only, then words with gesture and movement. Notice what the body added.
