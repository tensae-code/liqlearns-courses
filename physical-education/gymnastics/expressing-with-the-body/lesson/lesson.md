# Expressing with the Body

## Do it first

Show a happy face without words. Now show, with your whole body, a cat stretching. You have just communicated with your body — the thing this lesson teaches. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — I communicate with my body

El Salvador's Grade 1 guide opens its third unit here (Unit 3 Lesson 1, *Me comunico con mi cuerpo*): the child **communicates with gestures and movement**, **representing animals, natural things and feelings** — the body speaks before words do.

## Sub lesson 2 — My body communicates

In Grade 2 (Unit 3 Lesson 1, *Mi cuerpo se comunica*) the same line widens to everyday life: **body language in everyday communication** — the gestures and postures people use with each other every day, read and answered without a word.

## Sub lesson 3 — Communication with my whole body

In Grade 3 (Unit 3 Lesson 1, *Comunicación con todo mi cuerpo*) the two channels join: **gesture and body movement together with verbal expression** — body and voice saying it together, each making the other clearer.

## Say it to your companion
