# Lesson plan — Expressing with the Body

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Gymnastics · **Lesson:** Expressing with the Body
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Gymnastics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Expressing with the body: communicating feelings and ideas through body, face and mime, and dramatising situations.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **I communicate with my body** — the El Salvador guide: communicating with gestures and movement, representing animals, natural things and feelings.
2. **My body communicates** — the El Salvador guide: body language in everyday communication.
3. **Communication with my whole body** — the El Salvador guide: gesture and body movement together with verbal expression.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement; Balance and Calisthenics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what expressing with the body is: communicating feelings and ideas through body, face and mime.
2. Name what the Grade 1 guide represents: animals, natural things and feelings.
3. Say where Grade 2 takes it: body language in everyday communication.
4. Say what Grade 3 joins together: gesture and body movement with verbal expression.

## What you need
Nothing — your own body and face, and a space to move in, as the guide teaches it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mime feels embarrassing → start with animals — everyone looks the same kind of silly, and the guide starts there too.
- Body and voice fight each other → let the body show it first, then add the words — the Grade 3 order.
