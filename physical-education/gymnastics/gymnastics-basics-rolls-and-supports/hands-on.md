# Hands-on — Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: what gymnastics is, positions and rolls, rolls and steps, harder, rolls and supports in pairs.

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.

## The Grade 5–7 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Forward rolls from a forehead stand and a handstand, Free gymnastics. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat and a partner or teacher for rolls, bars and balances.


## The Grade 8–10 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: The dive roll — Grade 8, The handstand family — Grade 8. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat, a spotter and soft or appropriate balls where the part says so.

## The Grade 3 movement pair
On a mat: five star jumps — open wide, close, land soft. Then turn left and turn right with your arms raised, three times each way, slowly. Stop if you feel dizzy.

## Teacher-guide parts (added depth)
- **Rolling, jumping and turning — the Grade 1 guide's second body parts (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — forward-roll preparation tucked on a mat, forward-and-back steps, crouch-stand jump sequences, and pivot turns left and right in place. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
