# Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Gymnastics is a family of movements — rolls, balances and jumps — and its movements give good things to the body. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — What gymnastics is

Gymnastics is a family of movements — rolls, balances and jumps — and its movements give good things to the body. This is the knowledge lesson that opens the gymnastics chapter in Grade 3.

## Sub lesson 2 — Positions and rolls

Stand, sit, and lie on your stomach and on your back. Roll sideways. Roll left and right hugging your legs. Rock forward and back, touching the ground with your back. Slide down from a small height, and jump up from a crouch. Always work on a mat.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rolls and steps, harder

Sit holding your knees and roll left and right. Roll with arms and legs stretched upward. Step up onto two small stepped boxes and come down. Kneel, fold one leg back and stretch it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Rolls and supports in pairs

Do a forward roll and a backward roll. Kneel, fall forward onto your hands and lie on your chest. Roll forward and backward two by two, and roll forward over a partner's back. Pairs climb on and off a horizontal bar and move left and right on it. Use a mat and a spotter for safety.

## Sub lesson 5 — Forward rolls from a forehead stand and a handstand — Grade 6

The rolls grow harder: now the forward roll begins from a forehead stand, and then from a handstand — the body weight is carried on the forehead and the hands, or on the hands alone, before the roll. A helper (spotter) stands beside you for every one of these rolls, as the book requires. Tuck tight, roll straight, and stand up in control.

## Sub lesson 6 — Free gymnastics — Grade 7: headstand, head spring and cartwheel

The headstand: make a triangle on the mat with your forehead and both hands, then raise the legs one by one until they rest on the elbows, and grow the hold. The head spring (በግንባር መስፈንጠር): from a run-up, one leg forward, and spring from the head and hands. The cartwheel (በእጅ ወደጎን መሽከርከር): turn sideways hand to hand, like the spokes of a wheel. A spotter works beside you for all three, and mastery tasks close the practice.

## Sub lesson 7 — The dive roll — Grade 8

Free gymnastics is done with no equipment. Build the dive roll in its order: first practise forward-rolling on a rolled mat, then the long dive roll itself — run, dive forward over the rolled mat, roll through on your shoulders and stand. Use a mat, a clear space, and a teacher or partner watching. Never dive toward anyone.

## Sub lesson 8 — The handstand family — Grade 8

Three linked skills, each prepared in pairs and on a rolled mat, with a spotter beside you. The handstand: hands on the mat, body straight and extended, balance held. The handstand forward roll: from the handstand, tuck and roll forward out of it. The handspring (በእጅ መስፈንጠር): forward — keep the speed of your run-up into the next movement, hands down, push and fly over — and backward, prepared carefully with your partner spotting. Stop and rest whenever a shape feels unsafe.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
