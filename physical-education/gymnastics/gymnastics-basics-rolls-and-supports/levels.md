# Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What gymnastics is
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — gymnastics — rolls, balances and jumps that benefit the body.

## Sub lesson 2 — Positions and rolls
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — standing, sitting and lying positions, side rolls and leg-hug rolls on a mat.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rolls and steps, harder
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — knee-hug rolls, stretched rolls, and stepping up and down boxes.

## Sub lesson 4 — Rolls and supports in pairs
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — forward and backward rolls, pair rolls, and bar work with a mat and spotter.

## Sub lesson 5 — Forward rolls from a forehead stand and a handstand — Grade 6
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — forward rolls that begin from a forehead stand and from a handstand, always with a helper spotting.

## Sub lesson 6 — Free gymnastics — Grade 7: headstand, head spring and cartwheel
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the headstand built on a triangle of forehead and hands, the head spring, and the cartwheel — with spotting.

## Sub lesson 7 — The dive roll — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the dive roll — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 8 — The handstand family — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the handstand family — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
