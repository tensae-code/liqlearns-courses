# Video script — Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

What gymnastics is. Gymnastics is a family of movements — rolls, balances and jumps — and its movements give good things to the body.

Positions and rolls. Stand, sit, and lie on your stomach and on your back.

Rolls and steps, harder. Sit holding your knees and roll left and right.

Rolls and supports in pairs. Do a forward roll and a backward roll.

Forward rolls from a forehead stand and a handstand — Grade 6. The rolls grow harder: now the forward roll begins from a forehead stand, and then from a handstand — the body weight is carried on the forehead and the hands, or on the hands alone, before the roll.
Free gymnastics — Grade 7: headstand, head spring and cartwheel. The headstand: make a triangle on the mat with your forehead and both hands, then raise the legs one by one until they rest on the elbows, and grow the hold.

Rolling, jumping and turning — the Grade 1 guide's second body parts (teacher guide — evidence). Rolling and turning the body — ማገልበጥ. On a mat, tuck into a small squat and prepare the forward roll: chin in, back round, hands down. Then move forward and backward in place, stepping in rhythm. Then the standing jump sequence: crouch, stand, crouch — down and up like a spring. Last, turn in place: pivot to the left, pivot to the right (ወደ ግራና ቀኝ መዞር), on the spot, in control. Always on a mat, always with room around you.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

The dive roll — Grade 8. Free gymnastics is done with no equipment.

The handstand family — Grade 8. Three linked skills, each prepared in pairs and on a rolled mat, with a spotter beside you.

Grade 3 movements — the star jump and turning left and right. Jump on the mat. Open your arms and legs wide like a star. Close them and land soft. Now turn left with your arms up. Come back. Turn right. Stay tall.
