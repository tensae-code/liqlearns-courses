# Video script — Gymnastics Basics — Positions, Rolls and Supports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

What gymnastics is. Gymnastics is a family of movements — rolls, balances and jumps — and its movements give good things to the body.

Positions and rolls. Stand, sit, and lie on your stomach and on your back.

Rolls and steps, harder. Sit holding your knees and roll left and right.

Rolls and supports in pairs. Do a forward roll and a backward roll.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
