# Lesson plan — Movement Stories

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Gymnastics · **Lesson:** Movement Stories
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Gymnastics (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Movement stories: listening to a story and acting its movements out. — taught here through Jordan's way: its NCCD student books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Little Hero** — Unit 6 Movement Stories, Lesson 1: The Little Hero — listening to a story and acting its movements with basic motor skills — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 31.
2. **The Little Astronaut** — Unit 6, Lesson 2: The Little Astronaut — acting a space story; moving in space without bumping into classmates — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 33.
3. **A Trip to the Farm** — Unit 6, Lesson 3: A Trip to the Farm — acting farm-story movement tasks that need strength and effort — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 35.
4. **A Race in the Forest** — Unit 6, Lesson 4: A Race in the Forest — acting an animal-race story using varied speeds — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 37.
5. **The World of Professions** — Unit 6, Lesson 5: The World of Professions — acting the movements of different jobs; movement tasks that build physical fitness — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 39.
6. **Individual Expressive Movements** — Unit 5, Lesson 2: Individual Expressive Movements — performing varied movements in time with chants, alone — Jordan Grade 3 PE Term 2, printed p. 26.
7. **Group Expressive Movements** — Unit 5, Lesson 3: Group Expressive Movements — performing movements with classmates in time with chants, e.g. the shadow game — Jordan Grade 3 PE Term 2, printed p. 28.
8. **Expressive Movements (1)** — Unit 5, Lesson 6: Expressive Movements (1) — movements matched to a sports chant about the national Olympic committee — Jordan Grade 3 PE Term 2, printed p. 34.
9. **Expressive Movements (2)** — Unit 5, Lesson 7: Expressive Movements (2) — movements matched to a national chant — Jordan Grade 3 PE Term 2, printed p. 37.
