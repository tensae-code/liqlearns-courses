# Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Move in sequence to a beat, at low intensity. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simple sequences to a beat

Move in sequence to a beat, at low intensity. Clap overhead with right and left palms in turn, counting 1-2-3-4. Sit, beat the ground with both hands, then clap overhead. Face a partner hand in hand and fold and stretch the arms. March on the spot and touch foot to foot.

## Sub lesson 2 — Continuous sequences to music

Keep moving without stopping, at higher intensity, to music: stretch arm and leg to the side and return, hold your waist and stretch to the side, twist to different directions, jump on two feet forward and back, and jump left and right with feet together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Progressive sequences and equipment to music

Build a sequence step by step — first step, added step, full sequence — keeping the beat. Then time equipment work to the music: the ball, hoop or rope movement lands exactly on the beat.

## Sub lesson 4 — Equipment to music, and creating your own

Match the beat to the equipment: when you dribble, the ball's bounce is timed to the beat you hear; jump through circles on two feet to the music. Then create: creativity is making a new thing — invent your own beat movements, practise them, show them, and name your movement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
