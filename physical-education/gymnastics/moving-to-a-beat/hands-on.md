# Hands-on — Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Do the parts in order
Do each part of the lesson in order, slowly first: simple sequences to a beat, continuous sequences to music, progressive sequences and equipment to music, equipment to music, and creating your own.

## Teach it on
Pick one part. Name it, show it slowly, and teach it to a partner. Your partner does it; you watch for safety and for the right shape.

## Count and check
Do the main movement ten times, counting aloud. Stop if anything hurts. Rest, breathe, and say what the movement trained.

## If it is hard
Do the youngest part only, on the spot, at walking speed, beside a partner or a wall for balance. Smaller and slower is still the lesson.

## The Grade 5–7 parts
Do the new parts in order, slowly first: Rhythmic gymnastics, Rhythmic gymnastics. Stop if anything hurts; use a mat and a partner or teacher for rolls, bars and balances.

## Teacher-guide parts (added depth)
- **Movements that flow together — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — sequential movement — joining single movements with music and games so the movement is never broken. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Marching to the beat, and the school song (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — marching in place to a beat, arms swinging, singing the marching song "እንሒድ ትምህርት ቤት" (Let's go to school). Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **The circle game — round dance in a ring (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — circle-formation play — a round dance game held by the whole ring, each move answering the one before. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Movements in a chain — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — unbroken movement chains to music — passing smoothly from one movement to the next and keeping the beat. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Move and freeze; side bends (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — moving and freezing on a colour signal, and side bending right and left with the arms overhead, in rhythm. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Counting catches; knee drives (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — throwing and catching in pairs while counting the catches together, and driving alternate knees high in rhythm while travelling forward. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **The circuit course; slow and controlled movements (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — a three-station circuit two metres apart, and four slow controlled arm, leg and turn movements done to command. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.

- **Dance, aerobics and movement chains — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — join three simple movements without a break, to a clapped beat. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **To the beat: in-place rhythm and arm-leg coordination (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — step in place, swing the arms, turn the trunk, then opposite arm-and-leg together. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Follow the rhythm leader; bounce on the beat (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — one learner leads, everyone copies; bounce a ball on a clapped beat; pass balls around a circle in rhythm. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Moving with a partner; Selame Selame and clap-and-bounce (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — the Selame Selame circle with greetings and partner changes, then clap-bounce-clap with a small ball. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Movement chains and cultural dance — Grade 4 (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — name the three kinds, then perform a short group chain to a beat. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Stepping in sequence to the drum (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — march a repeated step pattern to a drum or claps, turning at the phrase end. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Making my own movement chain; pair rope jumping (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — build a chain of your own, then jump one rope with a partner, in time. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **Long rope with clapping; skipping that travels (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — enter, jump and leave a turning long rope while others clap; skip travelling forward and backward. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
- **One chain, whole group: cultural dance movement (teacher guide — evidence):** do it slowly first — the whole group performs one cultural-dance step together, to music or a drum, everyone on the same step. Stop if anything hurts; work beside a partner or teacher.
