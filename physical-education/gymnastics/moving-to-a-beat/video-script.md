# Video script — Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Simple sequences to a beat. Move in sequence to a beat, at low intensity.

Continuous sequences to music. Keep moving without stopping, at higher intensity, to music: stretch arm and leg to the side and return, hold your waist and stretch to the side, twist to different directions, jump on two feet forward and back, and jump left and right with feet together.

Progressive sequences and equipment to music. Build a sequence step by step — first step, added step, full sequence — keeping the beat.

Equipment to music, and creating your own. Match the beat to the equipment: when you dribble, the ball's bounce is timed to the beat you hear; jump through circles on two feet to the music.

Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 5: a hoop to music. Rhythmic gymnastics (ሪትሚክ ጅምናስቲክስ) is a creative individual performance form.
Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 6: ball, hoop and rope. Rhythmic gymnastics now works with three tools: a ball, a hoop (ቀለበት), and a rope or ribbon.

Movements that flow together — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence). A young child's movement should not be broken or interrupted. Sequential movement — ተከታታይነት ያለው የእንቅስቃሴ ክህሎት — joins single movements together with music and children's games, so one movement flows into the next. The chapter has two parts: moving the body parts in sequence, and whole sequential movement. Its promise: join movements inside children's games and in social and cultural activities, without stopping.

Marching to the beat, and the school song (teacher guide — evidence). March in place to a steady beat, arms swinging — the guide's picture (ስዕል 2.1) names it አርምጪ, marching. While you march, the class sings the printed song "እንሒድ ትምህርት ቤት" — "Let's go to school". The song is a marching song: its beat carries the steps, and it teaches marching together, in time, on the way to school.

The circle game — round dance in a ring (teacher guide — evidence). Stand in a circle and hold the game together. A circle formation is sequential group movement: the ring turns, the play passes from child to child around the ring, and nobody's part comes alone — each move answers the one before it. Keep the circle round, keep the rhythm, keep everyone in.

Movements in a chain — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence). Sequential movements — ተከታታይነት ያላቸው እንቅስቃሴዎች — are unbroken chains: one movement follows another, to music and rhythm, without stopping. The Grade 2 chapter's parts are rhythmic sequential movement, changing sequential movement, and growth tasks that build step by step. Its promises: perform sequential movement to music, pass smoothly from one movement to the next, and keep the beat all the way through.

Move and freeze; side bends (teacher guide — evidence). Rhythmic repetition — መደበኛ የሆነ የምት ድግግሞሽ: the same movement again and again, to a beat. The Green-Green game — አረንጓዴ, አረንጓዴ: at the colour signal you move; at the stop signal you freeze in place, holding your body still and controlled — then free play with the same game. And side bends — ወደ ጎን መታጠፍ: both arms up, bend your trunk to the right with the left arm over, come back to the middle, bend to the left — in rhythm.

Counting catches; knee drives (teacher guide — evidence). Rhythmic flow — ፍሰት ያለው የምት አማራጭ: choosing movements that fit a changing rhythm. The Number game — የቁጥር ጨዋታ: stand in two lines one metre apart; throw a ball so your partner catches it, and count the throws and catches together — the number is the score you build as a pair. Knee drives — ጉልበት ማንሳት (the guide's gloss: Knee-drive): lift one knee high, set it down, lift the other, in rhythm, travelling forward as you go.

The circuit course; slow and controlled movements (teacher guide — evidence). Progressive rhythm — ዕድገት ያለው የምት ድረጀ: tasks that grow harder in stages, each built on the last. The guide names the three kinds: መደበኛ (regular), ፍሰት ያለው (flowing) and ዕድገት ያለው (progressive) sequential movement. The Circuit — ዑደታዊ ጨዋታ: stations stand two metres apart; do the set movement at the first station, move to the second, then the third, arms and legs working together in balance. And the four control movements — አራቱ 'መ' በቁጥጥር: arms to the sides and down, legs opening and closing with the arms, turning the body on the spot — all slow, accurate, and to command.

Dance, aerobics and movement chains — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence). ዳንስ — dance! አየሮዊ እንቅስቃሴ — aerobics! ጅምናስቲክ — gymnastics! One movement… then the next… no break. The beat carries you.

To the beat: in-place rhythm and arm-leg coordination (teacher guide — evidence). Step in place — one, two — arms with the beat. Turn your trunk: left… right. Now: right arm, LEFT leg. Left arm, RIGHT leg. Together, on the beat.

Follow the rhythm leader; bounce on the beat (teacher guide — evidence). Watch the leader. Copy — exactly, in time. Bounce the ball: bounce… bounce… on the beat. Pass the balls around — dropped one? Pick it up. The rhythm goes on.

Moving with a partner; Selame Selame and clap-and-bounce (teacher guide — evidence). ሰላሜ ሰላሜ! Around the circle — greet your partner — change! Now: clap… bounce… clap… bounce. One joined song of movement.

Movement chains and cultural dance — Grade 4 (teacher guide — evidence). A chain of movements, to a beat: rope sequences, the game's own rhythm, cultural movement. Show it. Create it. Perform it — together.

Stepping in sequence to the drum (teacher guide — evidence). ክበሮ — the drum speaks. Step… step… arms swinging. The pattern repeats — exactly. Turn at the end of the phrase.

Making my own movement chain; pair rope jumping (teacher guide — evidence). Your turn to create: join your movements into a chain. Then two partners, one rope — jump as one, in time with the chant.

Long rope with clapping; skipping that travels (teacher guide — evidence). The long rope turns. The class claps. Enter… jump… leave! Now your own rope: skip forward, skip backward — twenty metres, turning at the ends.

One chain, whole group: cultural dance movement (teacher guide — evidence). Everyone. The same step. The same beat. Many dancers — one chain.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
