# Video script — Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Simple sequences to a beat. Move in sequence to a beat, at low intensity.

Continuous sequences to music. Keep moving without stopping, at higher intensity, to music: stretch arm and leg to the side and return, hold your waist and stretch to the side, twist to different directions, jump on two feet forward and back, and jump left and right with feet together.

Progressive sequences and equipment to music. Build a sequence step by step — first step, added step, full sequence — keeping the beat.

Equipment to music, and creating your own. Match the beat to the equipment: when you dribble, the ball's bounce is timed to the beat you hear; jump through circles on two feet to the music.

Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 5: a hoop to music. Rhythmic gymnastics (ሪትሚክ ጅምናስቲክስ) is a creative individual performance form.
Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 6: ball, hoop and rope. Rhythmic gymnastics now works with three tools: a ball, a hoop (ቀለበት), and a rope or ribbon.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
