# Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Simple sequences to a beat
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — clapping overhead counting 1-2-3-4, marching on the spot, foot to foot.

## Sub lesson 2 — Continuous sequences to music
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — continuous sequences — side stretches, twists and two-foot jumps without stopping.

## Sub lesson 3 — Progressive sequences and equipment to music
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sequences that build step by step, and equipment movements landing on the beat.

## Sub lesson 4 — Equipment to music, and creating your own
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — timing the dribble bounce to the beat, and inventing your own rhythmic movement.

## Sub lesson 5 — Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 5: a hoop to music
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — rhythmic gymnastics as a creative individual performance — moving a ring or hoop in time with different music beats.

## Sub lesson 6 — Rhythmic gymnastics — Grade 6: ball, hoop and rope
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — rhythmic gymnastics with a ball, with a hoop and with a rope — training attention and body-part coordination, performed alone and in groups.

## Sub lesson 7 — Movements that flow together — the Grade 1 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sequential movement — joining single movements with music and games so the movement is never broken.

## Sub lesson 8 — Marching to the beat, and the school song (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — marching in place to a beat, arms swinging, singing the marching song "እንሒድ ትምህርት ቤት" (Let's go to school).

## Sub lesson 9 — The circle game — round dance in a ring (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — circle-formation play — a round dance game held by the whole ring, each move answering the one before.

## Sub lesson 10 — Movements in a chain — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — unbroken movement chains to music — passing smoothly from one movement to the next and keeping the beat.

## Sub lesson 11 — Move and freeze; side bends (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — moving and freezing on a colour signal, and side bending right and left with the arms overhead, in rhythm.

## Sub lesson 12 — Counting catches; knee drives (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — throwing and catching in pairs while counting the catches together, and driving alternate knees high in rhythm while travelling forward.

## Sub lesson 13 — The circuit course; slow and controlled movements (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — a three-station circuit two metres apart, and four slow controlled arm, leg and turn movements done to command.


## Sub lesson 14 — Dance, aerobics and movement chains — Grade 3 (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner defines sequential movement and joins three simple movements without a break, to a beat.

## Sub lesson 15 — To the beat: in-place rhythm and arm-leg coordination (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner builds a sequence step by step — body part, join two, join more — and performs the opposite arm-and-leg pattern on the beat.

## Sub lesson 16 — Follow the rhythm leader; bounce on the beat (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner copies a leader's rhythm movements exactly and in time, bounces a ball on the beat, and keeps a ball circulation going after a drop.

## Sub lesson 17 — Moving with a partner; Selame Selame and clap-and-bounce (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner plays Selame Selame in the circle with greeting and partner changes in time, and repeats the clap-bounce-clap sequence without breaking it.

## Sub lesson 18 — Movement chains and cultural dance — Grade 4 (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner names the chapter's three kinds of sequential movement and performs a short chain in a group.

## Sub lesson 19 — Stepping in sequence to the drum (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner marches a step pattern to the drum exactly, arms swinging, and turns at the phrase end.

## Sub lesson 20 — Making my own movement chain; pair rope jumping (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner creates a short chain from known movements and jumps one rope together with a partner, in time.

## Sub lesson 21 — Long rope with clapping; skipping that travels (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner enters, jumps and leaves a turning long rope to clapped rhythm, and skips travelling forward and backward across 20 metres.

## Sub lesson 22 — One chain, whole group: cultural dance movement (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the whole group performs one cultural-dance chain together, every learner keeping the same step on the beat.
