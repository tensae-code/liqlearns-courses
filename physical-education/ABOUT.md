# About the Physical Education course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part is built
from Ethiopia's own Health and Physical Education books for
Grades 1–10, read in full, page by page: the movement, games and
sport strand of those books. The health-knowledge strand of the
same books stands as its own course, Health Education. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## The books (Ethiopia, Health and Physical Education)

Book entries are named in full in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. The set in hand for this build:

- Student textbooks Grades 1–10 — complete, read in full.
  Grades 1–8 are the Amharic-medium books; Grades 9–10 are the
  English federal 2023 GEQIP-E series.
- Teacher guides — used as evidence only, never counted as
  pupil-taught. The G1–G8 guide scans held are truncated
  (roughly their first halves; G8 is missing printed pp. 51–104+
  including Athletics, Gymnastics and Ball skills); lessons
  resting on them say so in their sources. The G9 guide is
  complete; G10 is substantively complete (only the final
  procedure of §8.3 is cut, at printed p.92).

**Acceptance reckoning:** this course's first part is taught in
1 country (Ethiopia) from its own books, except the lessons whose
sources state they stand on teacher-guide evidence only — those
are counted nowhere, and say so.

what the books themselves say in their front matter — the

### Teacher guides, Grades 1–4 — introductions, preface and shape

- **Grade 1 guide — introduction, printed pp. iii–v.** Book:
  Grade 1 Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የጤናና
  ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ ትምህርት የመምህር መምሪያ መጽሐፍ, አንድኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa
  City Administration Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. The
  introduction (መግቢያ) says Health and Physical Education in the
  new curriculum trains movement skills, rhythmic activities,
  gymnastics and Ethiopian traditional games so children grow
  healthy in body and mind. It notes, on printed p. iv, that the
  teacher's subject knowledge is tested before teaching, in a
  30% theory / 70% practice split. It also gives the guide's own
  picture of Grade 1 children (printed pp. iv–v): first-graders
  cannot sit still, everything they use is high-value to them,
  and playthings must not be taken from them or thrown away —
  the child-development basis the guide gives for its lessons.
- **Grade 2 guide — the book's own shape.** Book: Grade 2 Health
  and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የጤናና ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ ትምህርት
  የመምህር መምሪያ, ሁለተኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa City Administration
  Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. The guide has the same 6-chapter
  architecture as Grade 1, but the teaching unit inside each
  sub-unit is the numbered task (ተግባር), each with its own period
  count and a fixed procedure (የአሰራር ቅደም ተከተል): learners first
  move freely, then the teacher demonstrates, then learners
  practise in rows or groups, then a closing tidy-up — cool-down
  and putting equipment away. Every sub-unit opens with the
  outcomes box, a materials list (recurring kit: pictures, ball,
  basket, mats, rope, whistle, small balls, cloth), teaching
  points, the tasks, assessment questions with printed answers,
  and a chapter summary with summary assessment answers.
  Assessment load is stated in the introduction: 70% practical /
  30% theory. Period counts in the part of the guide held:
  Chapter 1 = 23, Chapter 2 = 12, Chapter 3 = 24. The held scan
  is truncated (printed pp. 1–41 of 85); Chapters 4–6, the
  glossary and the references are missing from the file held.
- **Grade 2 guide — introduction, printed pp. v–8.** The same
  book's introduction says that at this level Health and Physical
  Education is learned through play — knowledge and skills are
  built by doing. Teachers must know learners' physical condition
  and give special support in practical work. The introduction
  explains the guide's own structure to the teacher (outcomes,
  materials, teaching points, prior-period assessment answers,
  chapter summary, summary questions), states the assessment
  split — 70% practical / 30% theory — and requires the teacher's
  own fitness and preparation.
- **Grade 3 guide — introduction, front matter.** Book: Grade 3
  Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የጤናና ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ
  ትምህርት የመምህር መምሪያ መጽሐፍ, ሦስተኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa City
  Administration Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. The introduction
  explains this guide's six chapters and their furniture: each
  chapter's teaching outcomes, materials, teaching points,
  assessment questions and answers, summary and summary questions
  are set out for the teacher. It also requires that learners
  with special educational needs be given additional support in
  every activity.
- **Grade 4 guide — preface, front matter.** Book: Grade 4 Health
  and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የጤናና ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ ትምህርት
  መጽሐፍ, አራተኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa City Administration Education
  Bureau, © 2015 E.C. The front matter carries a preface
  (ምስጋና), not a separate introduction chapter beyond the preface
  and contents. The bureau's preface says the guide was prepared
  to help teachers deliver the new-curriculum Health and Physical
  Education subject, thanks the writers, editors and assessors
  named in the book, and asks teachers to use the guide fully.

### Teacher guides, Grades 5–7 — introductions and shape

- **Grade 5 guide — introduction, printed p. iii.** Book: Grade 5
  Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የጤናና ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ
  ትምህርት የመምህር መምሪያ መጽሐፍ, አምስተኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa City
  Administration Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. The introduction
  (መግቢያ) frames education as a country's development engine and
  Health and Physical Education as a full education field, not a
  break. It names the grade's content: ዘመናዊ የጤናና ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ
  ትምህርት፣ ሰውነት ማጎልመሻ በማህበራዊና ሥነ-ልቦናዊ ትምህርት፣ ጤናና የአካል ብቃት
  እንቅስቃሌ፣ አትሌቲክስ፣ ጅምናስቲክ፣ በኳስ ጨዋታዎች መሠረታዊ የማቀበልና የመቀበል ክህሎት
  እና በአዲስ አበባ ደረጃ የሚገኙ ባህላዊ እንቅስቃሌዎችና ጨዋታዎች. It then gives a
  class-time ruling, stated here in words so it can be found:
  fitness and aerobic work and skills are to be taught mixed, at
  roughly **40% theory / 60% practice** — about 40% of class time
  goes to theory-type activity and about 60% to practice.
- **Grade 6 guide — introduction, printed pp. I–II.** Book:
  Grade 6 Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide (የሰውነት
  ማጎልመሻ ትምህርት የመምህር መምሪያ መጽሐፍ, ስድስተኛ ክፍል), Addis Ababa City
  Administration Education Bureau, © 2014 E.C. The introduction
  (መግቢያ) presents education as a country's development task and
  names Health and Physical Education as one of the education
  fields that develop the whole person — body, mind, psychology
  and social relationship. It lists the grade's seven-chapter
  content, and it repeats the Grade 5 class-time ruling: at this
  level about **40%** of class time goes to theory-type activity
  and about **60%** to practice, mixed.
- **Grade 7 guide — introduction, printed p. iii.** Book: Grade 7
  Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide (English), Addis
  Ababa City Administration Education Bureau, © 2014 E.C. The
  introduction says physical education at this grade gives a
  systematic progression of movement experiences, and that the
  guide exists to help the teacher implement the syllabus —
  contents, safety precautions, teaching strategies, resources.
  It gives student-centred learning as the way, because "students
  learn better when they take part in their own learning through
  physical activities". It then states the inclusion duty in
  full: teachers are expected to motivate students with physical
  impairment or health problems to participate actively in
  physical education class, to create a safe learning
  environment for them, and to organise special and adapted
  activities for them in advance. It names the assessment areas:
  attendance and dressing, social, psychological and emotional
  development, skill, and theory.
- **Grade 7 guide — the book's own shape.** The same book's three
  held chapters carry period counts of 12, 14 and 16 —
  Chapter 1, Concepts of PE and sport, 12 periods; Chapter 2,
  Social and emotional development, 14 periods; Chapter 3,
  Physical fitness, 16 periods. Every topic carries a learning
  competency, a materials list, teaching and learning strategies,
  the content itself, and "Answer for Activity N" keys; the book
  also carries chapter assessment notes (project work,
  paper-and-pencil tests), chapter summaries and review-question
  answers, and it references figures and pictures/videos as
  materials. The inclusion duty above is stated up front, in the
  introduction, before any chapter begins. The held file holds
  Chapters 1–3 only (printed pp. 1–45); chapters beyond Chapter 3
  are missing from the file held.
