# About the Physical Education course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part is built
from Ethiopia's own Health and Physical Education books for
Grades 1–10, read in full, page by page: the movement, games and
sport strand of those books. The health-knowledge strand of the
same books stands as its own course, Health Education. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## The books (Ethiopia, Health and Physical Education)

Book entries are named in full in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. The set in hand for this build:

- Student textbooks Grades 1–10 — complete, read in full.
  Grades 1–8 are the Amharic-medium books; Grades 9–10 are the
  English federal 2023 GEQIP-E series.
- Teacher guides — used as evidence only, never counted as
  pupil-taught. The G1–G8 guide scans held are truncated
  (roughly their first halves; G8 is missing printed pp. 51–104+
  including Athletics, Gymnastics and Ball skills); lessons
  resting on them say so in their sources. The G9 guide is
  complete; G10 is substantively complete (only the final
  procedure of §8.3 is cut, at printed p.92).

**Acceptance reckoning:** this course's first part is taught in
1 country (Ethiopia) from its own books, except the lessons whose
sources state they stand on teacher-guide evidence only — those
are counted nowhere, and say so.
