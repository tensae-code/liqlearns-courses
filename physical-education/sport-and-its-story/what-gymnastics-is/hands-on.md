# Hands-on — What Gymnastics Is and Why We Do It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three shapes

On a mat: roll sideways, balance on one leg for a slow count of five, jump and stretch. Feel the three families of the chapter in your own body.

## Sort the strip

Look at the lesson's picture strip. Name each movement and its family — roll, balance, jump. Add a fourth from memory: a position.

## Kinds hunt

Watch or picture three gymnastics performances and sort each: free, apparatus or rhythmic. Name the equipment — or its absence — that decided your sort.
