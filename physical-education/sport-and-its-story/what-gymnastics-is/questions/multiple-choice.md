# What Gymnastics Is and Why We Do It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is gymnastics, in these books?
a) The body's basic trained movements — positions, rolls, balances, jumps and turns — done in order with control  b) A ball game  c) A kind of running race only
**Answer: a) The body's basic trained movements — positions, rolls, balances, jumps and turns — done in order with control**

**2. ** What benefit does the balance teaching state?
a) Not falling and breaking a bone  b) Running faster than everyone  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Not falling and breaking a bone**

**3. ** What comes after the basic gymnastics movements in the chapter?
a) Number gymnastics — sequences done to counts  b) The end of the subject  c) Swimming
**Answer: a) Number gymnastics — sequences done to counts**

**4. ** Which of these is one of the gymnastics families in these books?
a) Balances  b) Kicking games  c) Rope skipping races
**Answer: a) Balances**

**5.** The Grade 5 book's gymnastics chapter opens with…
a) A concept lesson: what gymnastics is  b) A race  c) A ball game
**Answer: a) A concept lesson: what gymnastics is**

**6.** Rhythmic gymnastics joins movement with…
a) A hoop (ቀለበት) and different music beats  b) A ball and a net  c) A rope and a bell
**Answer: a) A hoop (ቀለበት) and different music beats**

**7.** The two kinds the Grade 6 book keeps are…
a) Free gymnastics and apparatus gymnastics  b) Fast and slow gymnastics  c) Boys' and girls' gymnastics
**Answer: a) Free gymnastics and apparatus gymnastics**

**8.** The Grade 7 book opens its gymnastics chapter with…
a) History and research tasks  b) A competition  c) New apparatus
**Answer: a) History and research tasks**
