# Lesson plan — What Gymnastics Is and Why We Do It

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** What Gymnastics Is and Why We Do It
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: … → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the concept lesson that opens the gymnastics line: what gymnastics is, and what its movements give the body — the first knowledge section in the gymnastics line of these books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own movement. The gymnastics movement lessons are in the Gymnastics title, next door.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: none — this is the knowledge opener for the Gymnastics title beside it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what gymnastics is, in the book's framing: the basic movements — positions, rolls, balances, jumps — trained in order, on mats, for a healthy body and mind.
2. Name what gymnastics movements give the body, from the chapter that follows: balance that keeps you from falling, arm, shoulder and chest strength, and control of the whole body.
3. Say what comes next in the chapter: basic gymnastics movements, then counted gymnastics sequences.

## What you need
A mat if you try the movements named — this lesson itself is knowledge; the trying belongs to the Gymnastics lessons.
