# Video script — What Gymnastics Is and Why We Do It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Roll! Balance! Jump and stretch!
Three movements — one family. Its name: **gymnastics** — ጅምናስቲክስ.
Positions. Rolls. Balances. Jumps and turns. In order. With control. On the mat.
Why? A body that governs itself. Balance that keeps you from falling. Arms and shoulders made strong.
First this knowledge. Then the movements. Then gymnastics to COUNTS.
The door is open. The Gymnastics lessons are next door.
Free — no apparatus. Apparatus — box and bar. Rhythmic — a hoop, moving with the music.
And behind them all, a history: gymnastics is a subject, spread through schools.
