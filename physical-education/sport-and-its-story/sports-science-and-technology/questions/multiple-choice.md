# Sports Science and Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoa's primary curriculum teaches sports science and technology as…
a) A substrand inside movement work  b) A separate examination only  c) A school holiday activity
**Answer: a) A substrand inside movement work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is part of that substrand?
a) Body measurements and care of sports equipment  b) Cooking lunch  c) Map reading only
**Answer: a) Body measurements and care of sports equipment**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the senior guide, TV and video are used to…
a) Analyse sport and recreation performance  b) Replace all practice  c) Keep score in a league
**Answer: a) Analyse sport and recreation performance**
