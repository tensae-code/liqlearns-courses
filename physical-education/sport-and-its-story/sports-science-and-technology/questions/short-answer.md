# Sports Science and Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three parts of Samoa's sports science and technology substrand.
**Answer: Any three of: exploring movement with media/technology; sports wear and equipment that enhance movement; body measurements; modified equipment; investigating sports facilities; care of sports equipment; the stages of human movement.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the senior guide use visual aids?
**Answer: TV and video are used to analyse sport and recreation performance.**
