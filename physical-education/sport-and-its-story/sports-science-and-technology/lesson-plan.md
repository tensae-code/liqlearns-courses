# Lesson plan — Sports Science and Technology

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Sports Science and Technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sports science and technology: how science and technology analyse and improve sport — Samoa's curriculum strand of exploring movement with media and technology, sports wear and equipment, body measurements, modified equipment, sports facilities and equipment care, and the senior guide's use of visual aids to analyse performance.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Exploring movement with science and technology** — the primary curriculum's sports science and technology substrand, Years 1–8, with its seven named parts.
2. **Sports science in action** — the senior guide's sports-science pass: visual aids (TV and video) analysing performance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE; Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name parts of Samoa's primary sports science and technology substrand: media/technology exploration, enhancing wear and equipment, body measurements, modified equipment, facilities, equipment care, stages of human movement.
2. Say what sports science does in the senior guide: it enhances physical activities.
3. Give the guide's analysis tool: visual aids — TV and video — used to analyse performance.

## What you need
No equipment is needed. If a phone or camera is at hand, it can stand in for the book's visual aids in the hands-on task; if not, the task is done from memory and description.

## If the learner is stuck
- The substrand list is long → group it: tools (media, wear, equipment), the body (measurements, stages of movement), and places and care (facilities, looking after equipment).
- Sports science sounds like a laboratory → anchor it in the senior guide: a TV/video replay you study to improve is sports science in action.
