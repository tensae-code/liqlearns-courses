# Hands-on — Sports Science and Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Care-of-equipment check

Take one piece of sports equipment you use. Inspect it the way Samoa's substrand teaches — as equipment to be cared for: is it clean, whole, safe and stored properly? List one care action it needs, or record that it needs none.

## Analyse a movement from a replay (or a memory)

Watch a short replay of any movement if you have a device; if not, replay one movement in your mind three times. Write two observations a scientist would make — one about the body (a measurement or a stage of the movement) and one about the equipment or wear — following the senior guide's visual-aid analysis.
