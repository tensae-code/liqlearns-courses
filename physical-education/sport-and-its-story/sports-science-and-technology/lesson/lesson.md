# Sports Science and Technology

## Do it first

Name one way a tool or a measurement has ever helped you move better — a replay you watched, shoes that gripped, a distance you measured. Say it first — then read on and check yourself against Samoa's two books.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exploring movement with science and technology

Samoa's Years 1–8 curriculum carries a whole substrand, **Sports science and technology**, inside its movement work. Its parts, as the statement lists them: **exploring movement with media/technology**; **sports wear and equipment that enhance movement**; **body measurements**; **modified equipment**; **investigating sports facilities**; **care of sports equipment**; and **the stages of human movement**. Science and technology are not outside sport here — they are one of the ways movement itself is studied.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sports science in action

Samoa's Years 11–12 teacher guide takes the same idea to senior level in **Sports science in action**: **the role of sports science in enhancing physical activities**, including **using visual aids (TV and video) to analyse sport and recreation performance** — watching the movement back, with science, to make it better.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the primary substrand's parts, as Samoa's statement lists them, and how the senior guide puts sports science into action with TV and video analysis.
