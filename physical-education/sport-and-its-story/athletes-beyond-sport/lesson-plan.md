# Lesson plan — Athletes Beyond Sport — What Champions Give Back

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Athletes Beyond Sport — What Champions Give Back
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what famous athletes do beyond racing: the jobs, businesses, hospitals, training centres and public work the books record for Abebe Bikila, Tirunesh Dibaba, Haile Gebreslasie, Derartu Tulu and Mengistu Worku — fame turned into work for others.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia's Famous Athletes in this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Kenenisa's and the athletes' wider roles — the Grade 6 tasks** — the Grade 6 book asks, for Kenenisa Bekele and Meseret Defar alike, about the athlete's investment and social participation beyond promoting Ethiopia.
2. **Bikila and Dibaba — what their fame gave back (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 6 teacher guide: Bikila's Adidas agreement and the athletes he inspired; Dibaba's 300+ jobs, her Kaliti hospital and her training centre.
3. **Athletes invest, employ and serve — the Grade 7 account** — the Grade 7 book: most Olympic athletes do charity and help work; athletes invest fame and money in hotels, trade, farming, industry, import/export and sports centres, creating jobs.
4. **Sport legends at work — Haile, Derartu, Mengistu (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 7 teacher guide's legends lesson, taught by its own stated method — job creation, social and political work over race results: Haile Gebreslasie, Derartu Tulu and Mengistu Worku.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ethiopia's Famous Athletes — know the champions' races before their works. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell what Abebe Bikila's fame brought Ethiopia, and what Tirunesh Dibaba built: jobs for more than 300 workers, a hospital in her name in Kaliti, and a training centre for young athletes (teacher guide — evidence).
2. Name Haile Gebreslasie's and Derartu Tulu's work beyond sport: businesses and jobs, federation presidencies, goodwill ambassadorships.
3. Say the Grade 7 guide's method in its own point: teach the legends' job creation and social and political work, not only their race results.
4. Carry out the books' research task: a local sportsperson's charity work, or a national athlete's investments and jobs created.

## If the learner is stuck
- Only race results can be found → ask the guide's question instead: jobs, charity, public roles. Search for those words with the name.
- Numbers like "thousands of jobs" feel abstract → compare with a workplace the learner knows, as the DIY does.
