# Athletes Beyond Sport — What Champions Give Back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 6 book's tasks on Kenenisa ask about his…
a) Investment and social participation  b) Shoe size  c) Favourite food
**Answer: a) Investment and social participation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The hospital named for Tirunesh Dibaba stands in…
a) Kaliti sub-city  b) Bekoji  c) Rome
**Answer: a) Kaliti sub-city**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dibaba created jobs for more than…
a) 300 workers  b) 3 workers  c) 30,000 workers
**Answer: a) 300 workers**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which field does the Grade 7 book list for athletes' investment?
a) Farming  b) Lotteries  c) Cinema acting only
**Answer: a) Farming**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 7 guide tells teachers to focus on the legends'…
a) Job creation, social and political work  b) Race times only  c) Medal counts only
**Answer: a) Job creation, social and political work**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Haile Gebreslasie founded…
a) The Great Ethiopian Run  b) The Olympic Games  c) Saint-George club
**Answer: a) The Great Ethiopian Run**
