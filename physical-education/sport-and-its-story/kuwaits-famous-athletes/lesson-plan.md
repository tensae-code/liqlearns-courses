# Lesson plan — Kuwait's Famous Athletes

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Kuwait's Famous Athletes
**Place in the curriculum:** Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kuwait's famous athletes: the champions of Kuwait held up as role models — taught here through Kuwait's way: the "Heroes of My Homeland" pages that close the folk-heritage teaching of both girls' guides.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Heroes of My Homeland — Kuwait's champions as role models** — Both Kuwait girls' guides close their folk-heritage teaching with "Heroes of My Homeland": Kuwaiti sports champions held up as role models for the pupils — in the primary guide inside its Kuwaiti folk-heritage chapter, and in the middle guide as the chapter's appendix. The placements name no individual champion; the lesson teaches the framing exactly as the guides give it: Kuwait's own champions, presented to Kuwaiti pupils as the models to follow.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
