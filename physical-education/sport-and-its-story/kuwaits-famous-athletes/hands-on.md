# Hands-on — Kuwait's Famous Athletes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name a hero of your homeland

Name one Kuwaiti sports champion you know of, and write two sentences: what they achieved, and why a pupil might take them as a role model — the way the guides hold up the heroes of the homeland.

## Ask at home

Ask someone at home which Kuwaiti champion they remember best, and why.
