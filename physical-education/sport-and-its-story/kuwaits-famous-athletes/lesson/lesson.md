## Sub lesson 1 — Heroes of My Homeland — Kuwait's champions as role models

Both Kuwait girls' guides close their folk-heritage teaching with "Heroes of My Homeland": Kuwaiti sports champions held up as role models for the pupils — in the primary guide inside its Kuwaiti folk-heritage chapter, and in the middle guide as the chapter's appendix. The placements name no individual champion; the lesson teaches the framing exactly as the guides give it: Kuwait's own champions, presented to Kuwaiti pupils as the models to follow.
