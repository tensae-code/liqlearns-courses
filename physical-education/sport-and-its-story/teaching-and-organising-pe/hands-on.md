# Hands-on — Teaching and Organising PE

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan a lesson on paper

Write a Plano de Aula the Mozambique way: first the objective — what the lesson is for. Then the parts of the lesson in order, with a timing for each part. Build every part from the recreative-formative method: a recreational game, a song, or a dance — no part is a lecture.

## Teach it in play

Take one part of your plan and teach it to a partner exactly as the plan says: the game, song or dance itself does the teaching. Afterwards, say which part of the plan you were in, and how long it took.
