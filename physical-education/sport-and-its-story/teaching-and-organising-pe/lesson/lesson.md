## Sub lesson 1 — The PE lesson and the recreative-formative method

Mozambique's Grade 1 manual opens its PE part with **A aula de Educação Física** — **the PE lesson** itself: how a Grade 1 PE lesson is built. A lesson starts from its **objectives** — what the lesson is for — and is written down as a **Plano de Aula**, a **lesson plan**: the plan's **parts**, in order, each with its **timing**.

The manual's recommended way of teaching is the **Método recreativo-formativo** — the **recreative-formative method**: teaching **through recreational games, songs and dances**. The play is not a break from the lesson; in Mozambique's first cycle, the play *is* the lesson — every lesson is built from it.
