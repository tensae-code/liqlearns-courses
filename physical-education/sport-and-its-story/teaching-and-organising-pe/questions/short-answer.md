# Teaching and Organising PE — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does a Plano de Aula give for each part of the lesson?
**Answer: The part itself, in order, and its timing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Portuguese name of the manual's recommended method?
**Answer: Método recreativo-formativo (the recreative-formative method).**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three things every lesson is built from under that method.
**Answer: Recreational games, songs and dances.**
