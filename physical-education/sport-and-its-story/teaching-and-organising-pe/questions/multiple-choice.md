# Teaching and Organising PE — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Mozambique Grade 1 PE lesson is written down as…
a) A Plano de Aula — a lesson plan with parts and timing  b) A match fixture list  c) A shopping list
**Answer: a) A Plano de Aula — a lesson plan with parts and timing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A lesson plan starts from…
a) Its objectives — what the lesson is for  b) The final whistle  c) The score
**Answer: a) Its objectives — what the lesson is for**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The manual's recommended method is…
a) The recreative-formative method  b) Learning by lecture only  c) No teaching at all
**Answer: a) The recreative-formative method**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The recreative-formative method teaches through…
a) Recreational games, songs and dances  b) Written exams  c) Sitting still
**Answer: a) Recreational games, songs and dances**
