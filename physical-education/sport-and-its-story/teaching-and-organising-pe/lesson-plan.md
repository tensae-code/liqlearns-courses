# Lesson plan — Teaching and Organising PE

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Teaching and Organising PE
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Teaching and organising PE: how PE lessons and programmes are built, taught and assessed, and the challenges PE faces.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The PE lesson and the recreative-formative method** — the Mozambique manual: how a Grade 1 PE lesson is built — its objectives, the lesson plan's parts and timing — and teaching through recreational games, songs and dances.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Physical Education Is and Where It Came From; What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a Mozambique Grade 1 PE lesson is built: objectives first, then the lesson plan's parts, each with its timing.
2. Name the manual's recommended method: the recreative-formative method (Método recreativo-formativo).
3. Say what that method builds every lesson from: recreational games, songs and dances.
4. Say where this teaching sits in the manual: A aula de Educação Física — the PE lesson — with its Plano de Aula, the lesson plan.

## What you need
Nothing — this is the knowledge of how a PE lesson is put together; the hands-on task plans a lesson on paper.

## If the learner is stuck
- The method's name blurs → say it in two words: recreative (play) and formative (it forms the child) — play that teaches.
- Plan and method blur → the plan is the paper (parts and timing); the method is the way of teaching inside it (games, songs and dances).
