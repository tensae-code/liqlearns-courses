# Hands-on — Sport in Turkey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell the story in order

Tell your partner Turkey's sport story in the books' three parts — history, Atatürk, policies — in that order, one sentence each. Your partner checks the order against the lesson.

## People and events

Name the two things the Grade 5 book pairs with the history of sport among the Turks — its key people and its key events — and give one example of each kind from your own country's sport story. Your partner listens for both kinds.
