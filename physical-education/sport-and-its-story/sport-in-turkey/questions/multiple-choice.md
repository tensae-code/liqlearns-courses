# Sport in Turkey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 5 sports-school book teaches the history of sport among the Turks together with…
a) Its key people and events  b) Only stadium addresses  c) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Its key people and events**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 9 guide's closing sport-culture part is…
a) Atatürk ve Spor — Atatürk and sport  b) Swimming technique  c) A fitness test
**Answer: a) Atatürk ve Spor — Atatürk and sport**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 10 guide's policy part asks how Turkish sport was shaped by…
a) National sport policies  b) Foreign leagues  c) Weather
**Answer: a) National sport policies**
