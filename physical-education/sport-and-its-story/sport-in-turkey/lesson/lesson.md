# Sport in Turkey

## Do it first

Name one sports hero from your own country and one event everybody remembers. A country's sport story is made of such names — now read how Turkey's books tell theirs.

## Sub lesson 1 — The history of sport in Turkey

The Grade 5 sports-school book teaches **Türklerde Sporun Tarihi Gelişimi — the history of sport among the Turks** — with **the key people and events of Turkish sport history**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Atatürk and sport

The Grade 9 teacher guide closes its sport-culture theme with **Atatürk ve Spor — Atatürk's place in Turkish sport**, Turkey's own national sport story.

## Sub lesson 3 — National sport policies and Turkish sport

The Grade 10 teacher guide teaches **how national sport policies shaped Turkish sport** (Türk Sporunun Gelişiminde Millî Spor Politikalarının Etkisi).
