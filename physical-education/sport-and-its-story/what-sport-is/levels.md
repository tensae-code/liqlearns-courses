# What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sport defined — competitive play with its own rules
Pass mark: define sport and name its three marks: rules, organisation, competition.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sport's kinds and what sport does (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name five kinds of sport and two things sport does for people and country.

## Sub lesson 3 — PE and sport — same or different?
Pass mark: sort six activities into PE, sport or both, and defend each sort with the definitions.
