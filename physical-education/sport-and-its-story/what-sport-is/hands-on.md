# Hands-on — What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sorting game

Cards name activities — a class warm-up, a cup final, a stretching break, an Olympic race. The class sorts them PE / sport / both and must defend each card with a definition.

## Rules make sport

Play a chasing game with no rules for two minutes — then add three rules and play again. What changed? That change is the difference between play and sport.
