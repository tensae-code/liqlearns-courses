# What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sport sits inside physical education.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An activity with no rules and no organisation is already a sport.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** PE is instruction in exercises and games, especially in schools.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** PE and sport are exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A game with no law or rule of its own still counts as sport, in the guide's summary.
**Answer: False**
