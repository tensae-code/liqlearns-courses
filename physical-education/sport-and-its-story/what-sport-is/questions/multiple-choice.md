# What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sport is competitive play with…
a) National and world rules and its own organisation  b) No rules at all  c) Only one player
**Answer: a) National and world rules and its own organisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In sport, what is decided?
a) A winner and a loser  b) The weather  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) A winner and a loser**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is named as a kind of sport in the guide?
a) Swimming (የውሃ ዋና)  b) Cooking  c) Singing
**Answer: a) Swimming (የውሃ ዋና)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does sport do for free time?
a) Uses it healthily  b) Wastes it  c) Deletes it
**Answer: a) Uses it healthily**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** PE, in the guide's words, is solely for…
a) Physical and health fitness  b) Winning cups  c) Selling tickets
**Answer: a) Physical and health fitness**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Sport instruction may be given for a competitive purpose; PE is…
a) Not necessarily aimed at competition  b) Always a final  c) Only for adults
**Answer: a) Not necessarily aimed at competition**
