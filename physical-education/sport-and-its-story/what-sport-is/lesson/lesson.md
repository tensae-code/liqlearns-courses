# What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE

## Do it first

Football. A warm-up in class. A marathon race. Copying a partner's movement. Swimming races. Stretching. Which of these are *sport* — and which are physical education? This lesson draws the line the books draw.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sport defined — competitive play with its own rules

**Sport** is **competitive play with national and world rules and its own organisation**. In sport, a **winner and a loser are decided**. And sport **sits inside PE**: football, athletics and gymnastics are named as the sports inside the wider subject.

Play becomes sport when three things arrive together: **rules, organisation and competition**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sport's kinds and what sport does (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide gathers the definitions and settles one: **sport is competitive play done through physical activity, having its own rules and order**.

Its kinds are named: **የአገር ኳስ** football, **የመረብ ኳስ** volleyball, **የእጅ ኳስ** handball, **የቅርጫት ኳስ** basketball, throwing games, **የውሃ ዋና** swimming, **የብስክሌት ውድድር** cycling races and running races.

What does sport do? A person who does sport **keeps growing**; it **uses free time healthily**; it produces **athletes who represent the country**; and it **creates jobs**.

## Sub lesson 3 — PE and sport — same or different?

The Grade 7 book opens with the puzzle: PE and sport are **often confused for each other** — they are **different, but closely linked**, and the book compares them by **participants, venue, aim and organisation**.

The teacher guide settles it. **PE** (in Charles A. Butcher's 1964 definition, as the guide quotes it) is "an integral part of the total education process" aiming at physically, mentally, socially and emotionally fit citizens through planned physical activities — **instruction in exercises and games, especially in schools; it is solely for physical and health fitness, and not necessarily aimed at competition**. **Sport** is an activity requiring **physical prowess or skill**, organised by **rules and regulations**, requiring a high degree of skill, and **competitive**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one-line difference: PE is an educational process for fitness; sport is competitive play under rules. Give one example of each.
