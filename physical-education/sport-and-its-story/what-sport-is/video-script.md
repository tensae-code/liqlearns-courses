# Video script — What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Play is free. Add rules. Add organisation. Add a winner — and play becomes SPORT.
Football! Volleyball! Swimming! Cycling! Sport sits INSIDE physical education.
But PE itself? PE is learning — for fitness, for health — competition or not.
PE teaches. Sport competes. Both build the person.
