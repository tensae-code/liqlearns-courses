# The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — 776 BC to Athens 1896 — the founding story
Pass mark: tell the story in order: 776 BC Olympia — 1894 the IOC — 1896 Athens.

## Sub lesson 2 — The flag, the rings and the motto
Pass mark: draw the flag with the rings in their colours and say the motto in Latin and in English.

## Sub lesson 3 — The three bodies that run the Games (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name the three bodies and say in one line what each one is.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Olympic principles — peace, friendship, unity
Pass mark: name the six principles and explain non-discrimination in your own words.
