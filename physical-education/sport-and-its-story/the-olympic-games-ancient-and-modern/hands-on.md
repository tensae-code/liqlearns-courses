# Hands-on — The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A class opening ceremony

The class holds its own small opening: the drawn flag is carried in, the motto is read aloud in Latin and English, and the games begin only after a handshake line.

## Rings hunt

On any five flags at school or at home, find the ring colours: the book says every nation's flag colour appears in at least one ring. Test the claim on the flags you can find.
