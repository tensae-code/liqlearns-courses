# The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ancient Games honoured Zeus.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The IOC was founded after the first modern Games were held.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Olympic flag's cloth is white.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Solidarity means bringing the world together for a peaceful world through sport.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Ethiopia missed Montreal 1976, Los Angeles 1984 and Seoul 1988 for political reasons.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide says Ethiopia's champions developed a winning mentality in other African athletes.
**Answer: True**
