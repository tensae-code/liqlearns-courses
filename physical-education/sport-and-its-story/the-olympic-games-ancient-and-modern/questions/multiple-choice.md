# The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ancient Games began in…
a) 776 BC  b) 1896  c) 1914
**Answer: a) 776 BC**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The name "Olympic" comes from…
a) Olympia, the place  b) A Greek king  c) The five rings
**Answer: a) Olympia, the place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first modern Olympic competition was held in…
a) Athens, 1896  b) Paris, 1900  c) Rome, 1960
**Answer: a) Athens, 1896**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The five rings stand for…
a) The five participating continents  b) Five sports  c) Five judges
**Answer: a) The five participating continents**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Olympic motto means…
a) Faster, Higher, Stronger  b) Play, Win, Rest  c) Peace, Land, Sea
**Answer: a) Faster, Higher, Stronger**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is NOT one of the movement's three bodies?
a) The village council  b) The IOC  c) The NOCs
**Answer: a) The village council**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Non-discrimination means…
a) Everyone may practise sport without discrimination  b) Only champions may play  c) Nations play separately forever
**Answer: a) Everyone may practise sport without discrimination**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The Games are held every…
a) Four years  b) Year  c) Ten years
**Answer: a) Four years**
