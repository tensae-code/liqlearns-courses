# How Athletics Grew — A Short History

## Do it first

Before there were stadiums, there were these: run, jump, throw. Every child does them; every ancient festival crowned them. How did those three movements become the huge sporting world of today?

## The long road of athletics

The Grade 7 book opens its athletics chapter with history: **modern sports grew over a long process into today's huge commercial industries**. Athletics stands at the head of that road — the sport of the oldest movements, carried from ancient festivals into the modern stadium and the modern sporting world.

The historical development is the frame for everything the Athletics title teaches with the body: relay, shot, javelin, discus and high jump all have their roots in this long growth.

## Athletics — the base sport (teacher guide — evidence)

The Grade 5 teacher guide says why athletics comes first: **athletics is the base sport — running, jumping and throwing — from which other sports grow**. Running is a natural movement done everywhere, and so are jumping and throwing: people have always run, jumped over obstacles and thrown, long before any rule book.

The base sport is the root; the other sports are the branches.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three base movements and one way the sporting world of today differs from the ancient festivals.
