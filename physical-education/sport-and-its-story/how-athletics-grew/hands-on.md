# Hands-on — How Athletics Grew — A Short History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-movement festival

The class holds a small festival of the three base movements only — a short run, a standing jump, a safe soft-ball throw — in the ancient order: run, jump, throw.

## Three tellings on one timeline

Draw one timeline and mark the guide's three tellings on it: the federation's founding era with Africa already contributing, the 1960s rise of African prominence, and Bikila's two marathon crowns — Rome barefoot, Tokyo in shoes. Read the timeline aloud to a partner, world telling first, then Africa, then Ethiopia.
