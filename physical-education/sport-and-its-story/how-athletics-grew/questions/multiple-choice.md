# How Athletics Grew — A Short History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Modern sports grew into today's huge commercial industries over…
a) A long process  b) One year  c) A single meeting
**Answer: a) A long process**

**2.** Athletics is called the base sport because other sports grow from…
a) Running, jumping and throwing  b) Swimming and cycling  c) Reading and writing
**Answer: a) Running, jumping and throwing**

**3.** The base movements are…
a) Natural movements done everywhere  b) Invented last year  c) Only for professionals
**Answer: a) Natural movements done everywhere**

**4.** Maria Mutola of Mozambique was Olympic champion and three-time IAAF World Champion at…
a) 800 m  b) 100 m  c) The marathon
**Answer: a) 800 m**

**5.** The Confederation of African Athletics (CAA) was established in…
a) 1973, with its headquarters in Dakar  b) 1896, in Athens  c) 1999, in Rome
**Answer: a) 1973, with its headquarters in Dakar**

**6.** At Barcelona 1992, Derartu Tulu became…
a) The first black African woman to win Olympic gold  b) A swimming champion  c) A referee
**Answer: a) The first black African woman to win Olympic gold**

**7.** In the Grade 10 guide's telling, IAAF stands for…
a) International Amateur Athletics Federation  b) International African Athletics Fund  c) Independent Athletics Association of France
**Answer: a) International Amateur Athletics Federation**

**8.** In the Grade 10 guide's telling, African athletes' prominence in athletics grew from…
a) The 1960s  b) The 1890s  c) The 2020s
**Answer: a) The 1960s**

**9.** In the Grade 10 guide's Ethiopian telling, Abebe Bikila in Tokyo…
a) Retained his marathon title, wearing shoes this time  b) Won his first marathon barefoot  c) Carried the flag but did not run
**Answer: a) Retained his marathon title, wearing shoes this time**
