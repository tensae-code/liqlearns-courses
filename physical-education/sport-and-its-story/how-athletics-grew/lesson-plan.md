# Lesson plan — How Athletics Grew — A Short History

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** How Athletics Grew — A Short History
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how athletics grew: modern sports developed over a long process into today's huge commercial industries — and athletics, running, jumping and throwing, is the base sport from which other sports grow.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What Sport Is in this title; the Athletics title's skill lessons grow out of it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Sport Is — and How It Differs from PE. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell athletics' growth as the Grade 7 book frames it: a long process from old movement to modern sport to huge commercial industry.
2. Say why athletics is called the base sport, in the Grade 5 guide's words: running, jumping and throwing are the natural movements other sports grow from.
