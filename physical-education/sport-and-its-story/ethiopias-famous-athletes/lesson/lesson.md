# Ethiopia's Famous Athletes

## Do it first

Name an Ethiopian athlete. Now another. How many can the class name together? Ethiopia's runners have raised the country's flag at African Championships, World Championships and Olympic Games. This lesson profiles them as the books do — and sets you researching the rest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meselech Melkamu and Sileshi Sihine — the Grade 5 profiles

**Athlete Meselech Melkamu — አትሌት መሰለች መልካሙ.** A women's long-distance runner, born **19 Miyazia 1977 E.C.** in **Debre Markos** town.

**Athlete Sileshi Sihine — አትሌት ስለሽ ስሂን.** A men's long-distance runner, born **21 Tir 1975 E.C.** in **Sheno** town, Oromia Region.

The book's tasks: research both athletes' **full participation and results**; identify the pictured famous athletes and their events (Fig. 1.3, "famous runners"); and, as a project, **profile two more Ethiopian sportspersons** of your own choosing. (The birth dates are Ethiopian-calendar dates, exactly as printed.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Kenenisa Bekele and Gezahegne Abera (teacher guide — evidence)

**Kenenisa Bekele — ቀነኒሳ በቀለ.** Born **1974 E.C.** in **Oromia Region, Arsi Zone** (the guide's own dates and places). Through the sport club system he became a world athlete in the **5,000 m and 10,000 m**, winning at world level — the guide tells his rise from school running to the top.

**Gezahegne Abera — ገዛኸኝ አበራ.** Born **May 15, 1970**, in **Eteya, Arsi**. He began in school and club races in Ethiopia and abroad, won the **Sydney 2000 Olympic marathon gold** for Ethiopia, and won the **Fukuoka marathon in 1999 and again in 2001 and 2002** — at 32 he was still winning world marathons.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kenenisa again, Meseret Defar and Coach Sewnet Bishaw

**Kenenisa Bekele**, in the Grade 6 book's telling: born in **Bekoji, Arsi, 13 June 1982**, famed for his **finishing speed** in long-distance running. The book's task: list his events and prizes.

**Meseret Defar**: born in **Addis Ababa, 19 November 1983**; a long-distance runner mainly in the **3000 m and 5000 m**.

**Coach Sewnet Bishaw**: born in **Bure, West Gojjam, 1952**; among Ethiopia's most successful **football** coaches. The book's task: list his results coaching the national team.

Notice: the two books give Kenenisa's birth in two forms — Ethiopian-calendar in the guide, a June 1982 date in the Grade 6 book. Both are the books' own tellings; this lesson keeps both and says so.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ethiopia's Olympic heroes

The Grade 7 book lines up Ethiopia's Olympic heroes:

- **Mamo Wolde** — Mexico **1968 marathon gold** and 10,000 m silver; Munich 1972 marathon bronze.
- **Fatuma Roba** — Atlanta **1996 marathon gold**.
- **Miruts Yifter** — Munich 1972 10,000 m bronze; Moscow **1980 double gold** in the 5000 m and 10,000 m.
- **Tirunesh Dibaba** — Athens 2004 5000 m bronze; Beijing **2008 double gold**; London 2012 10,000 m gold and 5000 m bronze.
- **Almaz Ayana** — Rio **2016 10,000 m gold**.
