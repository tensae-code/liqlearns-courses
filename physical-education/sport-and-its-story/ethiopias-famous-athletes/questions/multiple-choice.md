# Ethiopia's Famous Athletes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Meselech Melkamu is a…
a) Women's long-distance runner  b) Football coach  c) Swimmer
**Answer: a) Women's long-distance runner**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sileshi Sihine was born in…
a) Sheno town, Oromia Region  b) Addis Ababa  c) Gondar
**Answer: a) Sheno town, Oromia Region**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gezahegne Abera won Olympic marathon gold at…
a) Sydney 2000  b) Athens 1896  c) Rio 2016
**Answer: a) Sydney 2000**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kenenisa Bekele became a world athlete in the…
a) 5,000 m and 10,000 m  b) 100 m only  c) Marathon only
**Answer: a) 5,000 m and 10,000 m**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Meseret Defar runs mainly the…
a) 3000 m and 5000 m  b) 100 m hurdles  c) 800 m
**Answer: a) 3000 m and 5000 m**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Sewnet Bishaw is famous as a…
a) Football coach  b) Marathon runner  c) Boxer
**Answer: a) Football coach**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Miruts Yifter's double gold came at…
a) Moscow 1980  b) Mexico 1968  c) London 2012
**Answer: a) Moscow 1980**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Almaz Ayana won 10,000 m gold at…
a) Rio 2016  b) Atlanta 1996  c) Beijing 2008
**Answer: a) Rio 2016**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 8 athletes section is built in two parts: victories, then…
a) Their works  b) Their shoes  c) Their diets
**Answer: a) Their works**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Athletes' works in the book include…
a) Charity, investment and development roles  b) Only racing  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Charity, investment and development roles**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Yidnekachew Tessema served as CAF president for…
a) 15 years  b) 1 year  c) 50 years
**Answer: a) 15 years**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Geremew Denboba was the first person to…
a) Carry Ethiopia's flag at the Olympic Games  b) Win the marathon  c) Found CAF
**Answer: a) Carry Ethiopia's flag at the Olympic Games**

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** Meseret Defar championed, as UNFPA ambassador…
a) Women, youth and HIV/AIDS prevention  b) Stadium building  c) Ticket sales
**Answer: a) Women, youth and HIV/AIDS prevention**

**14. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set names the athletes the guide's written answer calls Ethiopia's fruit of this field?
a) Abebe Bikila, Derartu Tulu, Haile Gebrselassie, Kenenisa Bekele and Tirunesh Dibaba  b) three referees and a judge  c) no one — the field has produced nobody
**Answer: a) Abebe Bikila, Derartu Tulu, Haile Gebrselassie, Kenenisa Bekele and Tirunesh Dibaba**
