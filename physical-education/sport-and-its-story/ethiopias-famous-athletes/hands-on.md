# Hands-on — Ethiopia's Famous Athletes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The research wall

Each learner pins one athlete profile — name, birthplace, event, victories, source — on the class wall. Duplicates must add a new fact.

## Finishing speed

Kenenisa is famed for his finishing speed: over a short run, practise finishing the last stretch faster than the first — speed held in reserve, then spent.
