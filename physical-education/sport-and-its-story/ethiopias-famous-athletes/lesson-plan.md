# Lesson plan — Ethiopia's Famous Athletes

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Ethiopia's Famous Athletes
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's famous athletes as the books profile them — Meselech Melkamu and Sileshi Sihine, Kenenisa Bekele, Meseret Defar, Coach Sewnet Bishaw, and the Olympic heroes Mamo Wolde, Fatuma Roba, Miruts Yifter, Tirunesh Dibaba and Almaz Ayana — with the research tasks the books set on each.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern in this title.

GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Meselech Melkamu and Sileshi Sihine — the Grade 5 profiles** — the Grade 5 book profiles two long-distance runners for research: Meselech Melkamu (አትሌት መሰለች መልካሙ) of Debre Markos and Sileshi Sihine (አትሌት ስለሽ ስሂን) of Sheno.
2. **Kenenisa Bekele and Gezahegne Abera (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 teacher guide's two profiles: Kenenisa Bekele (ቀነኒሳ በቀለ) of Arsi in the 5,000 m and 10,000 m, and Gezahegne Abera (ገዛኸኝ አበራ), Sydney 2000 Olympic marathon champion.
3. **Kenenisa again, Meseret Defar and Coach Sewnet Bishaw** — the Grade 6 book's profiles: Kenenisa Bekele of Bekoji, Arsi (born 13 June 1982), famed for his finishing speed; Meseret Defar of Addis Ababa in the 3000 m and 5000 m; and football coach Sewnet Bishaw of Bure, West Gojjam.
4. **Ethiopia's Olympic heroes** — the Grade 7 book's Olympic heroes and their victories: Mamo Wolde, Fatuma Roba, Miruts Yifter, Tirunesh Dibaba and Almaz Ayana.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern — several of these athletes are Olympic heroes. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Profile Meselech Melkamu and Sileshi Sihine as the Grade 5 book gives them: event, birth year and birthplace.
2. Tell Kenenisa Bekele's story in the books' two tellings, and name Meseret Defar's events and Coach Sewnet Bishaw's work.
3. Match the Olympic heroes to their victories: Mamo Wolde, Fatuma Roba, Miruts Yifter, Tirunesh Dibaba, Almaz Ayana.
4. Carry out the books' research tasks: an athlete's full results, and a profile of two more Ethiopian sportspersons.

## What you need
A notebook for the research tasks. Pictures from newspapers or the radio's sports reports help, where available.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names and dates blur → learn each athlete as a triple: name–place–victory; three hooks hold better than a list.
- A research source cannot be found → the radio's sports reports and newspapers count; name your source on the profile either way.
