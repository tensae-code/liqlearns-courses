# Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical literacy includes understanding, not only skill.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Confidence is not part of physical literacy.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Physical activity matters only while you are at school.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Staying active helps a person keep their motivation, confidence, competence and understanding.
**Answer: True**
