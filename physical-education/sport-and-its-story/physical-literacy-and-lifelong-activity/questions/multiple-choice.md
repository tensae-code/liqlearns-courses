# Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical literacy is…
a) Motivation, confidence, competence and understanding for lifelong activity  b) Only being good at one sport  c) Only reading about sport
**Answer: a) Motivation, confidence, competence and understanding for lifelong activity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many parts of physical literacy does the guide name here?
a) Four  b) Two  c) Ten
**Answer: a) Four**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In physical literacy, competence means…
a) Being able to move well  b) Owning equipment  c) Winning every game
**Answer: a) Being able to move well**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does physical activity matter, in the guide's framing?
a) It builds and keeps the physical literacy that keeps a person active for life  b) It matters only in school  c) It matters only for athletes
**Answer: a) It builds and keeps the physical literacy that keeps a person active for life**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Namibia's PE4Life way, sport and activity are used openly as a tool against…
a) Lifestyle diseases  b) School holidays  c) Team circles
**Answer: a) Lifestyle diseases**
