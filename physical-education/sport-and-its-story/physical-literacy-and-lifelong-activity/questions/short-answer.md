# Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four parts of physical literacy.
**Answer: Motivation, confidence, competence and understanding.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is physical literacy for?
**Answer: For lifelong activity — being active for life.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does physical activity matter?
**Answer: It builds and keeps physical literacy, so a person stays active for life.**
