# Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity

## Do it first

Name one physical activity you feel confident doing, and one you avoid. What makes the difference between the two? Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Physical literacy

Namibia's PE4Life guide teaches **physical literacy** as the foundation of a lifetime of activity: a physically literate person has the **motivation**, the **confidence**, the **competence** and the **understanding** to be active for life. Motivation is wanting to move; confidence is daring to try; competence is being able to move well; understanding is knowing why activity matters and how to value it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why physical activity matters

The guide pairs physical literacy with **the importance of physical activity**: activity matters because it is how physical literacy is built and kept — the person who stays active keeps the motivation, confidence, competence and understanding that keep them active. In the PE4Life way, this is aimed openly at a healthy life: sport and activity are used as a tool against Namibia's lifestyle diseases.
