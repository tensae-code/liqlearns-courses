# Hands-on — Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your four parts

Write the four parts of physical literacy — motivation, confidence, competence, understanding — and beside each one, name one activity where you are strong in that part and one where you are still growing.

## One activity for life

Pick one physical activity you could still be doing as an old person. Say which of the four parts you most need to build now so that future stays open, and do that activity once this week.
