# Video script — Physical Literacy and Lifelong Activity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Want to move. Dare to move. Can move well. Know why you move.
That is physical literacy — motivation, confidence, competence and understanding.
Build it, and activity is not a school subject. It is for life.
That is **physical literacy and lifelong activity**.
