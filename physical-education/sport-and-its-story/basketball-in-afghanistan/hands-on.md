# Hands-on — Basketball in Afghanistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell the story in order

Put the guide's story in order on paper: the game arrives (1317 HS), grows through Kabul University and the schools, girls' and women's teams play, the national team tours (from 1353 HS), the federation is founded and Afghanistan joins FIBA (1357–1363 HS). Tell it to a companion in that order.
