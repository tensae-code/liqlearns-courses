# Basketball in Afghanistan

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The game arrives and grows — Bala Hissar, Kabul University and the schools

Basketball arrives in Afghanistan in 1317 HS (1938/39) with a foreign coach at Bala Hissar, and grows through Kabul University and the schools — as the Grade 10 guide tells the game's Afghan story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Girls' and women's teams, the national team and FIBA

The guide records girls' and women's teams — at Malalai Lycee and Kabul University — the national team's tours from 1353 HS, the founding of the federation, and Afghanistan joining FIBA (1357–1363 HS).
