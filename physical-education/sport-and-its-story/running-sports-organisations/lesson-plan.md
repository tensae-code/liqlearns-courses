# Lesson plan — Running Sports Organisations

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Running Sports Organisations
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Running sports organisations — sports administration: how a sports club is started, planned and run.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **How to start a sports club** — the Namibia guide: sports management and planning — how to start a sports club.
2. **Running a sports club** — the Namibia guide: sports management and planning — how to run a sports club once it is started.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Sport and Society; Teaching and Organising PE. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that starting and running a sports club is sports management and planning.
2. Say what the Namibia guide's session teaches: how to start a sports club, and how to run one.
3. Name the paired topic that is NOT part of this lesson: the session's financial-literacy healthy-lifestyle theme, a life-skill topic outside PE scope.
