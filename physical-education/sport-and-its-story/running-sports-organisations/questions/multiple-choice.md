# Running Sports Organisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Namibia guide's session teaches sports management and planning through…
a) How to start and run a sports club  b) How to win a match  c) How to sew a kit
**Answer: a) How to start and run a sports club**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sports club is…
a) A sports organisation  b) A kind of ball  c) A single match
**Answer: a) A sports organisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Running a sports club means…
a) The continuing planning and management that keep its sessions happening  b) Running around the field  c) Closing the club down
**Answer: a) The continuing planning and management that keep its sessions happening**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The whole job of starting and running a club is called…
a) Sports management and planning  b) Warming up  c) Officiating
**Answer: a) Sports management and planning**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The session's paired healthy-lifestyle theme, financial literacy, is…
a) A life-skill topic outside this PE lesson  b) The PE topic of the session  c) A ball skill
**Answer: a) A life-skill topic outside this PE lesson**
