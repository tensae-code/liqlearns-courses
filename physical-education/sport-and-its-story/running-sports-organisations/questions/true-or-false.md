# Running Sports Organisations — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sports club is a sports organisation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sports club appears by itself, with no planning.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Starting a club is the whole job — nothing remains to run afterwards.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sports management and planning covers both starting and running a club.
**Answer: True**
