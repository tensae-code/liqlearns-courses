# Hands-on — Running Sports Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Start a club on paper

Plan a sports club that does not exist yet: give it a name and a sport, and write the first three things that must be planned before its first session can happen.

## Keep it running

For the same club, write what must be planned again every week or every month to keep it running — and who in the club would do each job.
