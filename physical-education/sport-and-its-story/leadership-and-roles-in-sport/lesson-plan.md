# Lesson plan — Leadership and Roles in Sport

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Leadership and Roles in Sport
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Leadership and roles in sport: captains, coaches, referees and organisers, and leading and collaborating in a team. — taught here through Indonesia's way: its Kurikulum Merdeka PJOK books for Grades 7–12.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Roles in sport** — Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches the roles in sport — captain, coach, referee and the Sport Education Model's pupil roles.
2. **From strategy to leadership** — Indonesia's Grade 9 book teaches moving from strategy to leadership in PE learning.
3. **Leadership in PJOK lessons** — The Grade 9 book teaches leadership in PJOK lessons, and its teacher guide teaches how to teach leadership and collaboration.
4. **Applying leadership and collaboration** — The Grade 9 book teaches applying leadership and collaboration in class activity.
5. **Decision-making, leadership and teamwork** — Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches decision-making in teamwork, leadership skills and collaboration in physical activity.
6. **Developing your leadership style** — Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches developing your leadership style, emotional intelligence and team dynamics in activity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
