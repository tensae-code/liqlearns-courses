# Leadership and Roles in Sport

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Roles in sport

Indonesia's Grade 7 book teaches the roles in sport — captain, coach, referee and the Sport Education Model's pupil roles.

## Sub lesson 2 — From strategy to leadership

Indonesia's Grade 9 book teaches moving from strategy to leadership in PE learning.

## Sub lesson 3 — Leadership in PJOK lessons

The Grade 9 book teaches leadership in PJOK lessons, and its teacher guide teaches how to teach leadership and collaboration.

## Sub lesson 4 — Applying leadership and collaboration

The Grade 9 book teaches applying leadership and collaboration in class activity.

## Sub lesson 5 — Decision-making, leadership and teamwork

Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches decision-making in teamwork, leadership skills and collaboration in physical activity.

## Sub lesson 6 — Developing your leadership style

Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches developing your leadership style, emotional intelligence and team dynamics in activity.
