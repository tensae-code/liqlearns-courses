# Hands-on — What Physical Education Is and Where It Came From

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four growths in one game

Play any game from this course for ten minutes. Afterwards the class sorts what happened into the four growths: what did the body gain, the mind, the friendships, the skill?

## The four-words poster

In four panels — health, body, building, education — draw one picture for each word. The set is the subject's name, explained.
