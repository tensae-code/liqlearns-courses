# What Physical Education Is and Where It Came From — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical education develops…
a) The whole person  b) Only the legs  c) Only examination marks
**Answer: a) The whole person**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In ancient history, which place holds the biggest place in PE?
a) Greece  b) Rome  c) Egypt
**Answer: a) Greece**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Modern" PE history begins at…
a) The end of the 16th / start of the 17th century  b) The year 2000  c) Ancient Greece
**Answer: a) The end of the 16th / start of the 17th century**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The four words of the subject are…
a) Health, body, building, education  b) Run, jump, throw, catch  c) Read, write, count, draw
**Answer: a) Health, body, building, education**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Motor and skill development means…
a) Nerves and muscles working together in skilled movement  b) Driving a car  c) Watching sport
**Answer: a) Nerves and muscles working together in skilled movement**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which is NOT one of the four purposes?
a) Cooking development  b) Mental development  c) Social-relations development
**Answer: a) Cooking development**
