# Lesson plan — What Physical Education Is and Where It Came From

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** What Physical Education Is and Where It Came From
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what physical education is — an education field that develops the whole person through movement — and where it came from: ancient Greece, and the road from the feudal age to the modern school subject.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Sport and Its Story title's knowledge line; every movement lesson in this course stands on it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **PE defined — and ancient Greece** — the Grade 5 book's opening section: PE as an education field developing the whole person, and Greece's big place in its ancient history.
2. **"Modern" PE — from the feudal age to today** — the Grade 5 book's history line: modern PE runs from the feudal-to-capitalist transition at the end of the 16th / start of the 17th century to today, still being revised to fit pupils.
3. **Four words, four growths (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 teacher guide defines the subject by its four words — ጤና health, ሰውነት body, ማጎልመሻ building, ትምህርት education — and names the four growths HPE gives.
4. **The four purposes — the Grade 6 pass** — the Grade 6 student book's four aims and the teacher guide's four purposes, each defined: physical, mental, social-relations, and nerve-and-skill development.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — this is an opening lesson of its title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define physical education in the Grade 5 book's words, and name what learning it serves: health, work ability, mental development and effectiveness.
2. Tell PE's history in the book's order: ancient Greece first, then "modern" PE from the end of the 16th / start of the 17th century to today.
3. Name the four words of the subject — health, body, building, education — and the four growths it gives (teacher guide — evidence).
4. Name the four purposes of PE from the Grade 6 books: physical, mental, social, and nerve-and-skill.

## What you need
Nothing — a knowledge lesson. A notebook helps for the history line.

## If the learner is stuck
- History dates slip → anchor them to the story's three stops (Greece, the transition, today), not to memorised years alone.
- Four words and four growths get mixed → words first (the name), growths second (the fruit); say them in that order.
