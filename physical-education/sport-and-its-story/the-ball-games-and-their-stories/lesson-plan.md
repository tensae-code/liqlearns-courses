# Lesson plan — The Ball Games and Their Stories — Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Handball

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** The Ball Games and Their Stories — Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Handball
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Sport and Its Story (Physical Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the histories of the four team ball games, each in the federal books' three scopes — world, Africa, Ethiopia — from ancient games to Ethiopia's own first clubs, federations and titles.
**This lesson's own level:** it follows the athletics history in the Sport and Its Story title; the skills of these games live in the Ball Games title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Football's story — world, Africa, Ethiopia** — the Grade 9 student book's §4.1 with its teacher guide: FIFA 1904, Khartoum 1957, and Ethiopia's 1962 title under Mengistu Worku.
2. **Volleyball's story — Mintonette to Ethiopia 1943** — the Grade 9 student book's §5.1 with its teacher guide: Morgan 1895, Egypt 1915, Ethiopia 1943 and the 1954 federation.
3. **Basketball's story — peach baskets to Addis Ababa 1950** — the Grade 9 student book's §6.1 with its teacher guide: Naismith's Springfield game, peach baskets until 1906, the orange ball, and Ethiopia's 1949 FIBA membership.
4. **Handball's story — ancient games to the IHF** — the Grade 9 student book's §7.1 with its teacher guide: Urania and Harpaston, the 1946 IHF, CAHB 1973, and Ethiopia 1960 E.C.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title — recommended, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell football's story in three scopes, ending with Ethiopia's 1962 championship and its scorer.
2. Tell volleyball's story: its inventor, first name, and its road through Egypt to Ethiopia.
3. Tell basketball's story: peach baskets, the orange ball, and Ethiopia's long tradition.
4. Tell handball's story: its ancestor games, its federation, and its Ethiopian arrival.
