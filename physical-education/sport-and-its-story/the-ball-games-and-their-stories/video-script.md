# Video script — The Ball Games and Their Stories — Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Handball

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order.

---

Narrator: Four games, four journeys. Each story walks the same road: the world, Africa, Ethiopia.
Narrator: Football crowns Ethiopia in 1962. Volleyball begins as Mintonette. Basketball begins with peach baskets. Handball begins in ancient games.
