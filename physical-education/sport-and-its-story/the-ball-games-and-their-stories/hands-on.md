# Hands-on — The Ball Games and Their Stories — Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Handball

One line of framing: these activities use real things — the field, the street, the classroom and home.

## Three-scope timelines
Each group takes one game and draws its world–Africa–Ethiopia timeline on one long paper strip. Pin the four strips in a row.

## Which came first?
Deal date cards (1863, 1895, 1904, 1943, 1946, 1949, 1957, 1962, 1973) and order them as a class, naming the event on each.
