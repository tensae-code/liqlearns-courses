# The Ball Games and Their Stories — Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Handball — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** FIFA was founded on…
a) 21 May 1904  b) 1 January 2000  c) 8 March 1896
**Answer: a) 21 May 1904**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's only Africa Cup title came in…
a) 1962, with goal-scorer Mengistu Worku  b) 1957  c) 2020
**Answer: a) 1962, with goal-scorer Mengistu Worku**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Volleyball was invented in 1895 by…
a) William G. Morgan  b) James Naismith  c) Pierre de Coubertin
**Answer: a) William G. Morgan**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Volleyball's first name was…
a) Mintonette  b) Harpastum  c) Hazena
**Answer: a) Mintonette**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Basketball's first baskets were…
a) Peach baskets  b) Metal hoops  c) Nets on posts
**Answer: a) Peach baskets**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia joined FIBA in…
a) 1949  b) 1906  c) 1964
**Answer: a) 1949**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The IHF was founded (under its present name) in…
a) 1946, Copenhagen  b) 1895, Holyoke  c) 1972, Munich
**Answer: a) 1946, Copenhagen**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Handball was introduced in Ethiopia in…
a) 1960 E.C., by Addis Ababa University instructors  b) 1927, at a school  c) 2000
**Answer: a) 1960 E.C., by Addis Ababa University instructors**
