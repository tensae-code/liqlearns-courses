# Azerbaijan's Sport Story

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Olympic Successes of Azerbaijani Athletes

Azerbaijan's Grade 8 book tells the Olympic successes of Azerbaijani athletes — the country's own Olympic story, inside its opening theory part.

## Sub lesson 2 — Azerbaijan — the Country That Held the First European Games

Azerbaijan's Grade 9 book presents Azerbaijan as the country that held the first European Games — its own sport story, taught as national content.

## Sub lesson 3 — Azerbaijani Athletes at the Rio Olympics

Azerbaijan's Grade 10 book opens with its athletes at the XXXI Summer Olympic Games in Rio de Janeiro — the nation's own athletes held up to its pupils.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — Azerbaijan's sport story: its Olympic successes, its athletes at the Rio Games, and holding the first European Games. — in your own words, the way Azerbaijan's books teach it.
