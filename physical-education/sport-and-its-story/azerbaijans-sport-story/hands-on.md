# Hands-on — Azerbaijan's Sport Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell Azerbaijan's sport story

Write three short cards — one for Azerbaijan's Olympic successes, one for the first European Games, and one for its athletes at the Rio 2016 Games — and arrange them in the order Azerbaijan's books teach them (Grades 8, 9 and 10). Then tell the story aloud to a partner.
