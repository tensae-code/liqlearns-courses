# Sport Psychology

## Do it first

Remember a time your mind changed how your body performed — nerves before a game, or confidence that carried you. What was happening in your mind? Say it first — then read on and check yourself against Samoa's guide.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mind and performance

Samoa's Years 11–12 teacher guide teaches **sports psychology** as examinable knowledge. Its first pass covers the mind that performs: **personality**, **motivation**, **goal setting**, and **anxiety and arousal** — how worked-up the mind and body are, and what that does to performance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Calming and motivating yourself

The guide's second pass is about steering that mind: **relaxation and visualisation**, **stress management**, and the question underneath all of it — **what motivates students to take part in regular physical activity**. Psychology here is practical: calm the stress, picture the performance, and understand your own reasons for taking part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the guide's sports-psychology topics — personality, motivation, goal setting, anxiety and arousal — and its calming tools: relaxation, visualisation and stress management.
