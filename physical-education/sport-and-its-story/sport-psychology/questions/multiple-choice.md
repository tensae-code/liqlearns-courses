# Sport Psychology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these does Samoa's guide teach inside sports psychology?
a) Goal setting  b) Club minutes  c) The sit-and-reach test
**Answer: a) Goal setting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Anxiety and arousal in the guide are about…
a) The mind and body being worked up, and what that does to performance  b) The size of the stadium  c) The rules of netball
**Answer: a) The mind and body being worked up, and what that does to performance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's calming tools include…
a) Relaxation and visualisation  b) Extra examinations  c) Louder music
**Answer: a) Relaxation and visualisation**
