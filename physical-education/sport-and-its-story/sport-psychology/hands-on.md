# Hands-on — Sport Psychology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Set one goal the guide's way

Write one goal for a physical activity you take part in, following the guide's goal-setting topic: make it one goal, yours, and checkable (you will know whether you reached it). Keep it where you will see it before your next session.

## Relax and visualise

Sit quietly for one minute. Relax your shoulders and breathing, then visualise — picture in detail — yourself performing one movement well, from start to finish, as the guide's relaxation and visualisation topic teaches. Afterwards, write one sentence on what you pictured and one on how your stress level felt before and after.
