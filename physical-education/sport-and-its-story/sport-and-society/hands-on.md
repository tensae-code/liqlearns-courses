# Hands-on — Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs

One line of framing: these activities use real things — the field, the street, the classroom and home.

## The professions walk
In pairs, pick one of the five PE professions and prepare a one-minute 'day in my work' talk. The class guesses the profession.

## Coverage audit
Take one day's sport coverage (paper, radio or phone). Count stories about men's, women's, disability and local sport. Compare with the books' named gap.
