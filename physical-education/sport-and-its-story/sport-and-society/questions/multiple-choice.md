# Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Health, in the guide's definition, is…
a) Complete physical, mental and social well-being  b) Only the absence of disease  c) Muscle size
**Answer: a) Complete physical, mental and social well-being**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide says children's activity is displaced by…
a) Television, internet and video gaming  b) Farming  c) Walking to school
**Answer: a) Television, internet and video gaming**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The student book's surgeon needs, through a long operation…
a) Hand–eye coordination, muscle strength and stamina  b) Only a scalpel  c) Nothing from PE
**Answer: a) Hand–eye coordination, muscle strength and stamina**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Planning a team's diet for its physical work is the job of the…
a) Dietician  b) Referee  c) Commentator
**Answer: a) Dietician**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Fatuma Roba was the first Ethiopian woman to…
a) Win the Olympic marathon  b) Referee a World Cup  c) Found FIFA
**Answer: a) Win the Olympic marathon**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** VAR's role is to ensure…
a) No clearly wrong decision stands  b) Every match is drawn  c) The crowd decides
**Answer: a) No clearly wrong decision stands**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The three media types are…
a) Print, broadcasting and internet  b) Only radio  c) Posters only
**Answer: a) Print, broadcasting and internet**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** UNESCO's Charter Article 1 makes participation in sport…
a) A fundamental right for all  b) A prize for champions  c) A job for officials
**Answer: a) A fundamental right for all**
