# Lesson plan — Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Sport and Its Story (Physical Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how sport lives in society: what PE promises every job, how sport builds an economy, who gets to play, the media and technology that carry and judge it, and the values sport and religion and politics bring to it.
**This lesson's own level:** it follows the famous-athletes and Olympic lessons in the Sport and Its Story title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What Health, PE and Sport mean — and why PE matters** — the Grade 8 teacher guide's Chapter 1 opener and §1.1, with the Grade 8 student book's benefits diagram: the three definitions, the sedentary-life warning, and PE's six importances.
2. **PE helps every job — and the jobs that grow out of PE** — the Grade 8 student book's §1.2 (the surgeon example) and the Grade 8 teacher guide's §1.2: the five closely-related professions.
3. **Women and girls in sport — and technology that judges fairly** — the Grade 9 student book's §§1.2–1.3 with its teacher guide: the barriers, Ethiopia's firsts, Article 35(1), and VAR, goal-line technology and the photo finish.
4. **Media, religion and politics — sport in the whole of life** — the Grade 10 student book's §§1.1–1.3 with its teacher guide: the three media types, 'Sport for all!', and sports diplomacy.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title — recommended, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define health, PE and sport in the guide's words, and name the sedentary-life danger PE answers.
2. Explain, with the surgeon example, how PE serves any profession, and name the five PE professions.
3. Name the barriers women face, Ethiopia's pioneering sportswomen, and three technologies that make decisions fairer.
4. Name the three media types and their three jobs, state the 'Sport for all!' right, and explain sports diplomacy in one line.
