# Video script — Sport and Society — Money, Media, Gender, Technology and Jobs

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order.

---

Narrator: Health is well-being — body, mind and people together. PE is learning through movement. Sport is the ruled contest.
Narrator: A surgeon stands for hours because PE built the body that stands. Every job leans on it.
Narrator: Print, broadcast, internet — media carry sport to everyone. Sport for all: that is the right.
