# Sport for Everyone — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the social model place disability?
**Answer: In the attitudes, barriers and arrangements around the person.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two social barriers the book names.
**Answer: Stereotypes and prejudice.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** State the right this lesson ends on.
**Answer: Disabled people's right to equal sport.**
