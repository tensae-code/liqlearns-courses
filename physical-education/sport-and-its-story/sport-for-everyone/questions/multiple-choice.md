# Sport for Everyone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cyprus book teaches what disability is in the framing of…
a) The World Health Organization (WHO)  b) A single sports club  c) No authority at all
**Answer: a) The World Health Organization (WHO)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the medical model, disability is placed…
a) In the person's body  b) In people's attitudes  c) In the weather
**Answer: a) In the person's body**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the social model, disability is placed…
a) In the attitudes and barriers around the person  b) Only in hospitals  c) Nowhere
**Answer: a) In the attitudes and barriers around the person**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A fixed idea about what disabled people can and cannot do is…
a) A stereotype  b) A training plan  c) A competition rule
**Answer: a) A stereotype**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Disabled people have the right to…
a) Equal sport  b) Watch only  c) No sport at all
**Answer: a) Equal sport**
