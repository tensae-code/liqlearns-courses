# Hands-on — Sport for Everyone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the barriers

Walk your school or sports ground as an inspector. List every barrier a disabled athlete would meet — steps, narrow doors, attitudes in words you have heard. Sort your list: which barriers are in the place, and which are in people's thinking?

## Rewrite the stereotype

Take one stereotype about disabled people and sport. Write what it claims, why it is prejudice and not fact, and the true sentence that should stand in its place.
