# Hands-on — What Physical Fitness Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sitting day

Keep a one-day tally: chalk marks for every long sitting, every walk, every run, every carry. At day's end, read your own body's day and name the fitness parts it used.

## Four parts, four stations

Set four stations — heart (march in place and feel the pulse), muscle (wall push), stretch (reach), agility (side steps). Move round and name each part at its station.
