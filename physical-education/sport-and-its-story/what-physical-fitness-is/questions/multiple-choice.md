# What Physical Fitness Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which list names the four components of fitness?
a) Heart and blood-vessel working, muscle strength, stretching/flexibility, agility  b) Reading, writing, counting, drawing  c) Speed only
**Answer: a) Heart and blood-vessel working, muscle strength, stretching/flexibility, agility**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Why must fitness activity be built into children's play?
a) Children spend much of the school day sitting in class  b) Children never sit down  c) Play is not allowed otherwise
**Answer: a) Children spend much of the school day sitting in class**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Which of these is one of the printed benefits of fitness activity?
a) Healthy blood vessels  b) Longer school sitting  c) Skipping play
**Answer: a) Healthy blood vessels**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Which benefit is about the body's shape?
a) A well-proportioned body  b) A protected heart  c) Healthy blood vessels
**Answer: a) A well-proportioned body**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Fitness, in the Grade 5 book, is the ability to…
a) Carry out daily tasks competently  b) Win every race  c) Avoid all work
**Answer: a) Carry out daily tasks competently**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Doing daily tasks efficiently without tiring describes…
a) Fitness  b) Doping  c) A warm-up
**Answer: a) Fitness**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Health-related fitness…
a) Keeps the body healthy and supports daily work  b) Prepares a competitor for events  c) Is only for athletes
**Answer: a) Keeps the body healthy and supports daily work**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Skill-related fitness prepares…
a) A competitor for events  b) A cook for market  c) A pupil for examinations only
**Answer: a) A competitor for events**
