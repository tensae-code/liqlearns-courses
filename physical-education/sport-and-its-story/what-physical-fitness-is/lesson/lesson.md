# What Physical Fitness Is

## Do it first

Today your body sat in class for hours. It also walked, carried, ran, played. Some of that made it stronger; some of it just wore the day away. What is the *capacity* underneath all of it — the thing that decides how much your body can do? It has a name, and it has four parts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four parts of fitness (teacher guide — evidence)

The Grade 1 teacher guide opens its fitness chapter with the definition this whole course builds on: **physical fitness is the body's capacity foundation** for health and physical education. Its components are named:

- **የልብና የደም ስር አሰራር** — the heart and blood-vessel working;
- **የጡንቻ ብርታት** — muscle strength;
- **መዘርጋትና መተጣጠፍ** — stretching and flexibility;
- **ቅልጥፍና** — agility.

And the guide gives the reason fitness is taught through play at all: **children spend much of the school day sitting in class**, so fitness activity must be **built into their play**.

## Sub lesson 2 — What fitness protects (teacher guide — evidence)

The Grade 3 teacher guide opens its fitness chapter by saying what fitness activity does: it **protects the child's health — ጤና**. By doing varied fitness activities at school and elsewhere, the body's systems are strengthened. The benefits are printed:

- **ጤናው የተጠበቀ ልብ ይኖራቸዋል** — a protected, healthy heart;
- **ጤናማ ሳንባና የትንፋሽ አካላት** — healthy lungs and breathing organs;
- **ጤናማ የደም ስሮች** — healthy blood vessels;
- a **well-proportioned body**;
- **healthy body weight**.

That is the story the fitness lessons tell with movement. This lesson is its first page: fitness is not a game added to the day — it is the body being protected, part by part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four parts of fitness, and then the five things fitness protects. Which part do you think your own play trains most?

## Do It Yourself

1. Watch your school day: write down every time your body sits for long, and every time it moves. Where could fitness be built into the play?
2. Draw the four parts of fitness as four pictures: heart, muscle, stretch, agility.
3. Ask a friend: which of the five benefits matters most to you? Say yours too.
