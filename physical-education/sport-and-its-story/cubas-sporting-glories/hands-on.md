# Hands-on — Cuba's Sporting Glories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find a glory

Name one leading figure of Cuban sport — local or national — and say which sport they belong to, the way Cuba's programmes frame the glories in each sport.

## Say why they are held up

Write two sentences: what did this figure do, and why does the programme hold such figures up for pupils to know and admire?
