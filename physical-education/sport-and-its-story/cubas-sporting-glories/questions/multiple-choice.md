# Cuba's Sporting Glories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The "glorias deportivas" are…
a) The sporting glories and leading figures of the Cuban Sports Movement  b) A Cuban dance  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) The sporting glories and leading figures of the Cuban Sports Movement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This objective appears in…
a) Every grade's programme objectives  b) Only Grade 11  c) Only Grade 2
**Answer: a) Every grade's programme objectives**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 5 names the glories of…
a) Basketball, handball, baseball and Cuban sport  b) Swimming only  c) Chess only
**Answer: a) Basketball, handball, baseball and Cuban sport**
