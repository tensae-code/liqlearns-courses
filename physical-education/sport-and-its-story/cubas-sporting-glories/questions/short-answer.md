# Cuba's Sporting Glories — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose glories does every grade's objective name?
**Answer: The sporting glories and leading figures of the Cuban Sports Movement, local and national.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Grade 11 frame the objective?
**Answer: Respect and admiration for the sporting glories and leading figures in each sport.**
