# Lesson plan — Cuba's Sporting Glories

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Cuba's Sporting Glories
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cuba's sporting glories: the leading figures and heroes of Cuban sport. — taught here through Cuba's way: its MINED national programmes and teacher Orientaciones Metodológicas.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Knowing Cuba's sporting glories** — Every grade of Cuba's programme carries the same objective: knowing and admiring the sporting glories and leading figures of the Cuban Sports Movement, local and national — in Grade 5 named for basketball, handball, baseball and Cuban sport, in Grade 8 as recognition of the glories and their influence on pupils' development, and in Grade 11 as respect and admiration for the glories in each sport.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what every grade's objectives include: knowing the sporting glories of Cuban sport.
2. Name the sports Grade 5 names for its glories: basketball, handball, baseball and Cuban sport.
3. Say how Grade 11 frames it: respect and admiration for the glories in each sport.
