## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing Cuba's sporting glories

Every grade of Cuba's programme carries the same objective: knowing and admiring the sporting glories and leading figures of the Cuban Sports Movement, local and national — in Grade 5 named for basketball, handball, baseball and Cuban sport, in Grade 8 as recognition of the glories and their influence on pupils' development, and in Grade 11 as respect and admiration for the glories in each sport.
