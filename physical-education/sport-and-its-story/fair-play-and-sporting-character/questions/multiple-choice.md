# Fair Play and Sporting Character — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book's third chapter open with?
a) Sporting spirit  b) Match tactics  c) Fitness testing
**Answer: a) Sporting spirit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sports morality in the book means:
a) Rules, respect and honesty in competition  b) Winning at any cost  c) Training every day
**Answer: a) Rules, respect and honesty in competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is part of sporting character as the book teaches it?
a) Perseverance, teamwork and responsibility  b) Skipping practice  c) Hiding mistakes
**Answer: a) Perseverance, teamwork and responsibility**
