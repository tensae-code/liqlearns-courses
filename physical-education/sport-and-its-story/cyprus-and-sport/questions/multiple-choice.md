# Cyprus and Sport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cyprus's sporting story begins in 1892 with…
a) The Limassol Gymnastic Association  b) The Cyprus Sports Organisation  c) The first modern Olympics
**Answer: a) The Limassol Gymnastic Association**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Pancyprian Games were held in…
a) 1896  b) 1892  c) 1969
**Answer: a) 1896**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cyprus Sports Organisation was founded in…
a) 1969  b) 1896  c) 1892
**Answer: a) 1969**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sports does the book list as practised in Cyprus?
a) The Olympic sports  b) Only one village game  c) No sports at all
**Answer: a) The Olympic sports**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cyprus's disability-sport federations include…
a) Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf federations  b) Only a football federation  c) None
**Answer: a) Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf federations**
