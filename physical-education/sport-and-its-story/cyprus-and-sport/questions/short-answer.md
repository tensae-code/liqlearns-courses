# Cyprus and Sport — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What started in Limassol in 1892?
**Answer: The Limassol Gymnastic Association — Cyprus's first gymnastic association.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In what year were the first Pancyprian Games held?
**Answer: 1896.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Cyprus's three disability-sport federations.
**Answer: The Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf federations.**
