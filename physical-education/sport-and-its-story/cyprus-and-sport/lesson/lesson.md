# Cyprus and Sport

## Do it first

Three dates: 1892, 1896, 1969. One island: Cyprus. Put the dates in order and guess what each one started. Then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — From 1892 to the Cyprus Sports Organisation

Cyprus's sporting story, as its own book tells it, begins in **1892** with the **Limassol Gymnastic Association** — the island's first gymnastic association. Four years later, in **1896**, came the **first Pancyprian Games** — games for the whole island.

The story runs forward to **1969**, when the **Cyprus Sports Organisation** was founded — the body that organises sport for the whole island.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cyprus's Olympic sports and its disability-sport federations

The book closes with Cyprus in the Olympic world: the **Olympic sports practised in Cyprus**, and the federations that carry disability sport on the island — its **Paralympic** federation, its **Special Olympics** federation and its **Deaf** federation. Cyprus's own sporting story is thus told whole: from one town's association to the island's place in Olympic and disability sport.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion Cyprus's three anchor dates — 1892, 1896, 1969 — and what each one started, then name the three disability-sport federations.
