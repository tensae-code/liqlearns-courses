# Cyprus and Sport — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — From 1892 to the Cyprus Sports Organisation
Pass mark: date the Limassol Gymnastic Association (1892), the first Pancyprian Games (1896) and the Cyprus Sports Organisation (1969).

## Sub lesson 2 — Cyprus's Olympic sports and its disability-sport federations
Pass mark: say the Olympic sports are practised in Cyprus, and name the Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf federations.
