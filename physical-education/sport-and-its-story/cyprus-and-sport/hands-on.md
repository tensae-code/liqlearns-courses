# Hands-on — Cyprus and Sport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the timeline

Cards carry the dates 1892, 1896 and 1969, and cards carry what each date started. Match them, order them, and say each match aloud in a full sentence.

## Your island's sporting story

Cyprus's story starts with one association in one town. What was the first organised sport in your own town or area? Find who started it and when, and add it to the timeline.
