# Video script — Cyprus and Sport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

1892. A gymnastic association in Limassol.
1896. The first Pancyprian Games.
1969. The Cyprus Sports Organisation.
From a single association to a whole island of Olympic sport —
and to federations for Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf athletes.
That is **Cyprus and sport**.
