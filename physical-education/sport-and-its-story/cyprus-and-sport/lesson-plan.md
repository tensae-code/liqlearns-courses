# Lesson plan — Cyprus and Sport

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Sport and Its Story · **Lesson:** Cyprus and Sport
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 8 — Sport and Its Story (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cyprus and sport — how sport grew on the island, from the first gymnastic association and Pancyprian Games to today.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## Sub lessons
1. **From 1892 to the Cyprus Sports Organisation** — the Cyprus book: sport's growth on the island from the 1892 Limassol Gymnastic Association and the first Pancyprian Games of 1896, to the Cyprus Sports Organisation (1969).
2. **Cyprus's Olympic sports and its disability-sport federations** — the Cyprus book: the Olympic sports practised in Cyprus and its Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf federations.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Olympic Games, Ancient and Modern; Sport for Everyone. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Date Cyprus's sporting start: the Limassol Gymnastic Association in 1892 and the first Pancyprian Games in 1896.
2. Name the Cyprus Sports Organisation and its founding year, 1969.
3. Say that the Olympic sports are practised in Cyprus.
4. Name Cyprus's three disability-sport federations: Paralympic, Special Olympics and Deaf.

## What you need
Nothing — a knowledge lesson.
