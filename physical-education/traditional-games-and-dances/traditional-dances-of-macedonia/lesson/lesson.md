## Sub lesson 1 — Macedonian folk dances (народни ора)

North Macedonia's Grade 9 book teaches the Macedonian folk dances (народни ора): performing three ora of the pupils' and teacher's choice (named: Lesnoto, Teškoto, Kalajdžisko, Nevestinsko, Tresenica, Egejsko); how to hold hands and move in space, body carriage and steps; ritual and festive ora, danced in a semicircle, a closed circle, a snake form or in pairs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Macedonian folk dances (народни ора) — the gymnasium telling

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches the oro as cultural heritage handed down the generations, danced at feasts, weddings and gatherings, in open or closed chains moving to the right, dancers holding hands, shoulders or belts to music from gajda, tapan, zurla and kaval; its movements (turning, kneeling, hopping, shaking) run in slow and fast rhythms, above all the asymmetric Balkan metres 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 7/16 and 13/16; the best known ora include Pravoto, Pajduško, Eleno mome and Čupurlika.
