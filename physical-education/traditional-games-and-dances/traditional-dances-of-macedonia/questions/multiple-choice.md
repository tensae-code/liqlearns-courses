# Traditional Dances of Macedonia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An oro is danced:
a) Holding hands in a semicircle, a closed circle, a snake form or in pairs  b) Alone, without music  c) Only sitting down
**Answer: a) Holding hands in a semicircle, a closed circle, a snake form or in pairs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The gymnasium book gives the oro's commonest asymmetric metres as:
a) 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 7/16 and 13/16  b) Only 4/4  c) Only 3/4
**Answer: a) 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 7/16 and 13/16**
