# Traditional Dances of Macedonia — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which book and pages teach the Grade 9 ora?
**Answer: North Macedonia Grade 9 HPE, printed pp. 61–62.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two instruments the gymnasium book names for oro music.
**Answer: Any two of: gajda, tapan, zurla and kaval.**
