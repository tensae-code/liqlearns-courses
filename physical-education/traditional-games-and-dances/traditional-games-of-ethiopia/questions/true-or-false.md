# Traditional Games of Ethiopia — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Written laws let the same traditional game be played the same way everywhere.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Gena, breaking a game's laws does no harm, says the book's task.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gebeta variants are named by stone count: one-stone, two-stone, three-stone.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Horse racing stands in the book as a ruled traditional sport.
**Answer: True**
