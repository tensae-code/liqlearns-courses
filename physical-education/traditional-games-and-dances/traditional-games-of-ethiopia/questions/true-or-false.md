# Traditional Games of Ethiopia — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Written laws let the same traditional game be played the same way everywhere.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In Gena, breaking a game's laws does no harm, says the book's task.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Gebeta variants are named by stone count: one-stone, two-stone, three-stone.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Horse racing stands in the book as a ruled traditional sport.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** The book teaches non-injurious kinds of wrestling.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** Making the Korbo stick and ring is set as a task in the book.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** Bub's stations total 25, counting the start station.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 9)** The book lists archery's equipment in detail.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional sport game was there before you and will be played after you.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Gena's value, in the book's own tasks, includes productivity, friendship and culture.
**Answer: True**
