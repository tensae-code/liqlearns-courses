# Traditional Games of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How many games does the book picture as given laws by the Federation?
a) Five  b) Two  c) Ten
**Answer: a) Five**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's Gena tasks ask about its value for…
a) Productivity, friendship and culture  b) Examinations  c) Selling tickets
**Answer: a) Productivity, friendship and culture**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** On the 12-hole gebeta board, each player has…
a) Six houses  b) Twelve houses  c) Three houses
**Answer: a) Six houses**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The 18-hole gebeta is called…
a) ስሉስ (selus)  b) ቆርቦ (Korbo)  c) ቡብ (Bub)
**Answer: a) ስሉስ (selus)**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Gugs is…
a) The horse game  b) A board game  c) A dance
**Answer: a) The horse game**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** The book's instruction for Shah is…
a) Play it with your friends  b) Watch it only  c) Never play it
**Answer: a) Play it with your friends**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Traditional wrestling's equipment includes…
a) A bell, a whistle and a results board  b) A net and posts  c) Starting blocks
**Answer: a) A bell, a whistle and a results board**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The taught wrestling hold is…
a) Holding the waist with one hand  b) Pulling hair  c) Tripping from behind
**Answer: a) Holding the waist with one hand**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** The second Korbo player scores by…
a) Spearing the ring's middle to stop it  b) Catching the ring by hand  c) Kicking the ring
**Answer: a) Spearing the ring's middle to stop it**

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** The spearer moves inside…
a) The throwing zone  b) The goal area  c) A circle of stones
**Answer: a) The throwing zone**

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** The Bub board has…
a) 24 stone stations plus one start station  b) 10 stations  c) 100 stations
**Answer: a) 24 stone stations plus one start station**

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** At the centre of the Bub board is…
a) A diamond shape  b) A goal  c) A net
**Answer: a) A diamond shape**

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** In the book's framing, archery is practised…
a) At a target, supervised  b) Aimed at players  c) Indoors at windows
**Answer: a) At a target, supervised**

**14. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional sport game is…
a) a game invented for one class  b) a game handed down through a community's generations, carrying its identity  c) a game played only in stadiums
**Answer: b) a game handed down through a community's generations, carrying its identity**

**15. (Sub lesson 1)** The opening task of the games chapter is to…
a) name the traditional games known where you live  b) memorise a rule book  c) choose a captain
**Answer: a) name the traditional games known where you live**

**16. (Sub lesson 4)** In the 12-hole Gebeta, each player moves the stones from…
a) their own six houses, in turn  b) any hole at any time  c) the middle hole only
**Answer: a) their own six houses, in turn**
