# Traditional Games of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many games does the book picture as given laws by the Federation?
a) Five  b) Two  c) Ten
**Answer: a) Five**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's Gena tasks ask about its value for…
a) Productivity, friendship and culture  b) Examinations  c) Selling tickets
**Answer: a) Productivity, friendship and culture**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the 12-hole gebeta board, each player has…
a) Six houses  b) Twelve houses  c) Three houses
**Answer: a) Six houses**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The 18-hole gebeta is called…
a) ስሉስ (selus)  b) ቆርቦ (Korbo)  c) ቡብ (Bub)
**Answer: a) ስሉስ (selus)**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Gugs is…
a) The horse game  b) A board game  c) A dance
**Answer: a) The horse game**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The book's instruction for Shah is…
a) Play it with your friends  b) Watch it only  c) Never play it
**Answer: a) Play it with your friends**
