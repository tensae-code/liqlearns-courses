# Traditional Games of Ethiopia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The federated games — the five with laws
Pass mark: name the five games and say one harm of breaking a game's laws.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gena — the rules pass
Pass mark: answer four of the book's Gena tasks from teaching or local knowledge, and name the ones still open.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gebeta — the rules pass: twelve holes and eighteen
Pass mark: sow a full turn on the 12-hole board correctly and name the 18-hole form.

## Sub lesson 4 — Horse racing and Gugs, and the Shah game
Pass mark: name Gugs as the horse game and play Shah with friends by its local form.

## Sub lesson 5 — Traditional wrestling — laws and practice
Pass mark: name wrestling's three equipment items and show the one-hand waist hold as the book's non-injurious form.

## Sub lesson 6 — Korbo — the ring and the spear
Pass mark: describe Korbo's two roles (roller and spearer) and where the spearer stands and moves.

## Sub lesson 7 — Bub — the board of twenty-five stations
Pass mark: draw the Bub board: 24 stone stations plus the start station, with the diamond at the centre.

## Sub lesson 8 — Archery — the traditional bow game
Pass mark: state archery's safety framing (target only, supervised) and name where its laws and equipment list sit in the book.
