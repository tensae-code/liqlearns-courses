# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Twelve and eighteen

Play a turn on the 12-hole board, then look at the 18-hole (ስሉስ) board: what changes when the board grows? More houses, longer sowing, deeper thinking.

## The Gena task set

In groups, take the book's Gena tasks one by one — festivals, field, kinds of play, value — and present what your group could answer and what stayed open.
