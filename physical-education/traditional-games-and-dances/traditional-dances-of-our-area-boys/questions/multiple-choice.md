# Traditional Dances of Our Area (Boys) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the opening picture of the Grade 1 boys' dances?
a) Buhe Belu — boys dancing with sticks  b) A football match  c) A swimming race
**Answer: a) Buhe Belu — boys dancing with sticks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What do the dancers in the Buhe Belu picture hold?
a) Sticks  b) Balls  c) Books
**Answer: a) Sticks**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** How does the Grade 2 book treat the boys' dances?
a) The same frame as the girls' — the locality's dances, danced and studied  b) As a written test only  c) It leaves them out
**Answer: a) The same frame as the girls' — the locality's dances, danced and studied**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What three things does the presenter of a boys' local dance give?
a) Its dance, danced together, and the body part it works most  b) Only its name in writing  c) A test about it
**Answer: a) Its dance, danced together, and the body part it works most**
