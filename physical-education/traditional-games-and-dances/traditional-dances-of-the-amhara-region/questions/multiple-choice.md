# Traditional Dances of the Amhara Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gondar dance belongs to which region's survey?
a) The Amhara Region  b) The Oromia Region  c) No region — it is a school dance
**Answer: a) The Amhara Region**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's task for every pictured dance is to name…
a) The body parts it develops  b) The fastest dancer  c) The song's author
**Answer: a) The body parts it develops**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The North Shewa dance's pictured movement is…
a) The shoulder movement in rhythm  b) Jumping over a rope  c) Throwing a ball
**Answer: a) The shoulder movement in rhythm**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair of dances forms the Grade 6 book's second regional set?
a) Wollo and Wag Himra  b) Gondar and North Shewa  c) Korbo and Bub
**Answer: a) Wollo and Wag Himra**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book want the body-parts question answered?
a) In practice — by dancing  b) By guessing  c) By copying a friend's answer
**Answer: a) In practice — by dancing**
