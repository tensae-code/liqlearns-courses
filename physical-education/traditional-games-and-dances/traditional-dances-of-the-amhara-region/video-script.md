# Video script — Traditional Dances of the Amhara Region

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

One region — many dances!
Gondar! Shoulders moving in traditional dress.
North Shewa! The shoulders keep the beat.
Wollo! Wag Himra! Every home has its dance.
Dance first. Then answer: which body parts did it grow?
