# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of the Amhara Region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance, then answer

For each dance: dance it first, in a group, then sit and answer the book's task from your own body — which parts worked, above and below the waist.

## Shoulder rhythm practice

A steady clapped beat. Shoulders up, down, circling — exactly on the beat, one minute, rest, again. The beat does not speed up for anyone.
