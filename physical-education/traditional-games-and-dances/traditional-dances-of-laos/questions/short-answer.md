# Traditional Dances of Laos — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three named Lao folk dances taught in the guides.
**Answer: Any three of: Kan Bin; Yat Phaen Bin; Daen Kap Seua; Songkran; Ngan Kin Thang; Leu Lai; Thang Viset; Yat Duang; Ka Chap Lak; Yaeo Luk Ka Kai; Lam Yan Khwa; Pa Kin Lao; Chat Tum; Yat Nam; Rak Sa San Tung; Ka Don; Mae Yat Bon.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the primary guides organise their year around dance?
**Answer: In four strands — formation drill, gymnastics postures, gymnastics with music, and dance/games (fon) — with named folk dances taught one at a time.**
