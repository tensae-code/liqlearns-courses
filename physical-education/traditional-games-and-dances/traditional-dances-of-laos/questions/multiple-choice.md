# Traditional Dances of Laos — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does "fon" name in the Lao guides?
a) The Lao folk dances  b) A ball game  c) A swimming stroke
**Answer: a) The Lao folk dances**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Songkran folk dance is taught for which occasion?
a) The new-year festival  b) A football match  c) An exam
**Answer: a) The new-year festival**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which guide teaches the folk dance Kan Bin?
a) The Grade 4 PE & Handicraft teacher guide, 2009  b) The Grade 12 guide  c) The Grade 9 guide
**Answer: a) The Grade 4 PE & Handicraft teacher guide, 2009**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Sending and receiving" is what kind of dance in these guides?
a) A named Lao partner dance  b) A solo sprint  c) A rope climb
**Answer: a) A named Lao partner dance**
