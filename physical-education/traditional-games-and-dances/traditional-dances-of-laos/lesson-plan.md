# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Laos

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Laos
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional dances of Laos: the Lao folk dances (fon), taught step by step.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The fon strand in the primary guides**
2. **Named dances — festivals, places and animals**
3. **Partner, circle and formation dances**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show: say how the 2007–2009 primary guides teach folk dance: one named fon dance at a time, step by step, inside the drill / gymnastics / music-gymnastics / folk-dance pattern of the series.
2. Say and show: name dances the guides teach by name — Kan Bin, Yat Phaen Bin, Daen Kap Seua, Songkran (the new-year festival dance), Ngan Kin Thang, Leu Lai, Thang Viset, Yat Duang, Ka Chap Lak, Yaeo Luk Ka Kai, Lam Yan Khwa, Pa Kin Lao, Chat Tum, Yat Nam, Rak Sa San Tung, Ka Don and Mae Yat Bon — as their placements name them.
3. Say and show: name the partner and formation dances — sending and receiving, receiving and sending, dancing around the post, circling the post, going around the post, guarding the victory post, the long-route folk dance, jumping over the rope, the duck circle dance and the cloth tied at the waist — as their placements name them.

## What you need
Open space to move in a line and a circle, and music where the dance is taught to music, as the dances are danced, not only read about.
