# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Laos

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance the circle

Form a circle and practise a circle dance's steps slowly, the way the guides teach a fon dance step by step. Your partner counts the steps and says when the circle keeps its shape.

## Send and receive

With a partner, practise the sending-and-receiving movement of the partner dances named in this lesson, facing each other, then turn and repeat.
