# Video script — Traditional Dances of Laos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Laos teaches its own dances by name — the fon.
One dance at a time, step by step, in the primary guides.
Dances for festivals — Songkran, the new-year dance.
Partner dances — sending and receiving; circle dances — around the post.
That is **traditional dances of Laos**.
