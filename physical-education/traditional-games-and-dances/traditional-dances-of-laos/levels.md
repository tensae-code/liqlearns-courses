# Traditional Dances of Laos — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The fon strand in the primary guides
Pass mark: say how the 2007–2009 primary guides teach folk dance: one named fon dance at a time, step by step, inside the drill / gymnastics / music-gymnastics / folk-dance pattern of the series.

## Sub lesson 2 — Named dances — festivals, places and animals
Pass mark: name dances the guides teach by name — Kan Bin, Yat Phaen Bin, Daen Kap Seua, Songkran (the new-year festival dance), Ngan Kin Thang, Leu Lai, Thang Viset, Yat Duang, Ka Chap Lak, Yaeo Luk Ka Kai, Lam Yan Khwa, Pa Kin Lao, Chat Tum, Yat Nam, Rak Sa San Tung, Ka Don and Mae Yat Bon — as their placements name them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Partner, circle and formation dances
Pass mark: name the partner and formation dances — sending and receiving, receiving and sending, dancing around the post, circling the post, going around the post, guarding the victory post, the long-route folk dance, jumping over the rope, the duck circle dance and the cloth tied at the waist — as their placements name them.
