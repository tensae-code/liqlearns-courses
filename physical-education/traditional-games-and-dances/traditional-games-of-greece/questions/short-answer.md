# Traditional Games of Greece — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is skipping rope called a game of the whole world, in the book?
**Answer: It is an old traditional game children all over the world still play.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is Varelakia played?
**Answer: Children bend in a line holding their knees, head tucked in, and the last one jumps over them one by one.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things do children do with the elastic band?
**Answer: Stretch it into geometric shapes and play jumping patterns over it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What did children carry in the Chelidona custom, and what did they sing?
**Answer: A flower-decked wooden swallow, and the spring carols (chelidonismata).**
