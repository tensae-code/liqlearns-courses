# Traditional Games of Greece — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skipping rope has a skipping song in the Grades 1-2 book.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gyro Gyro Oloi is shown in Savvidis' 1905 painting and still played today.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Varelakia, the last child jumps over the bent line one by one.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The elastic band is stretched into geometric shapes in the elastic games.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In Perperouna, the girl dressed in leaves and branches was sprinkled with water.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The Rogatsia dancers dance for the good of the year.
**Answer: True**
