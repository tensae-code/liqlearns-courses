# Traditional Games of Greece — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grades 1-2 book, skipping rope is…
a) An old traditional game children all over the world still play  b) A new Olympic sport  c) A game for one child alone
**Answer: a) An old traditional game children all over the world still play**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the skipping game, two children…
a) Turn the rope while the rest jump  b) Hold the skipping song book  c) Count from the side only
**Answer: a) Turn the rope while the rest jump**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a skipping way the Grades 3-4 book practises?
a) Skipping on the spot and while travelling  b) Skipping under water  c) Skipping with eyes closed only
**Answer: a) Skipping on the spot and while travelling**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gyro Gyro Oloi is…
a) The Greek circle singing game  b) A leapfrog game  c) An elastic game
**Answer: a) The Greek circle singing game**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Varelakia, the children in the line…
a) Bend holding their knees, head tucked in  b) Stand on one leg  c) Sit in a circle
**Answer: a) Bend holding their knees, head tucked in**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The elastic games are played with…
a) A tied elastic band  b) A long rope only  c) A hoop
**Answer: a) A tied elastic band**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The Chelidona swallow was carried from house to house on…
a) 1 March  b) 1 January  c) Easter Sunday
**Answer: a) 1 March**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Perperouna is the custom of singing for…
a) Rain  b) Snow  c) A good harvest of olives only
**Answer: a) Rain**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** The Rogatsia is a carnival custom from…
a) Macedonia  b) Crete  c) Pontus
**Answer: a) Macedonia**
