# Video script — Traditional Games of Greece

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two turn the rope, and the rest jump — children everywhere play this game.
Round in a circle singing, over the bent backs one by one, over the elastic in patterns.
In spring the swallow goes from house to house; in dry days the village sings for rain.
That is **traditional games of Greece**.
