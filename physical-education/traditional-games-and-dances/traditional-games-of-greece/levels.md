# Traditional Games of Greece — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Skipping rope — the world's game
Pass mark: say that skipping rope is an old traditional game children all over the world still play, with two turning the rope and the rest jumping, and name ways the older book practises it (on the spot, travelling, partner and group games).

## Sub lesson 2 — Circle and leapfrog games
Pass mark: describe Gyro Gyro Oloi as the Greek circle singing game and Varelakia as leapfrog over a line of children bent holding their knees, heads tucked in.

## Sub lesson 3 — Elastic games
Pass mark: say that the elastic games use a tied elastic band, stretched into geometric shapes, with jumping patterns played over it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Seasonal customs
Pass mark: tell Chelidona (1 March, the flower-decked wooden swallow and the spring carols), Perperouna (singing for rain, sprinkled with water) and Rogatsia (the Macedonian carnival custom, dancing for the good of the year).
