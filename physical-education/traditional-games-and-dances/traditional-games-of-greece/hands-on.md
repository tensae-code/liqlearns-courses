# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Greece

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Turn and jump

With two partners turning a long rope, take your turn jumping while the rope turns, the way the Grades 1-2 book sets the game. Count your jumps aloud with the group.

## The elastic shapes

Stretch a tied elastic band between two holders into a geometric shape, as the Grades 3-4 book does, and play a jumping pattern over it. Your partner checks the shape and the pattern against the lesson.
