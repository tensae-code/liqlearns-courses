# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Sri Lanka

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sri Lankan folk games — Grade 7

Do the part as Sri Lanka's books teach it: Ch.3 Folk games — named Sri Lankan folk games (Pancha, Nerenchi, Olinda, Coppara Coppara Pipingya, Iniweta Peralima, Mewara keliya, Pora Pol Gasima and others) with how to play several of them, as Sri Lanka's Grade 7 student book teaches it (printed p. 28). Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.
