# Dancesport — Ballroom and Latin Dancing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dancesport (ลีลาศ) in the Thailand book has two types. They are…
a) Ballroom and Latin American  b) Folk and classical  c) Solo and group
**Answer: a) Ballroom and Latin American**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before dancing, the book teaches the basics first. Which set is the book's?
a) Dance lines, body positions, how to hold a partner, and leading  b) Costumes and music only  c) Jumps and lifts
**Answer: a) Dance lines, body positions, how to hold a partner, and leading**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The waltz belongs to which family in the book?
a) Ballroom  b) Latin American  c) Thai folk play
**Answer: a) Ballroom**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cha-cha-cha belongs to which family in the book?
a) Latin American  b) Ballroom  c) Neither — it is a game
**Answer: a) Latin American**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book treat dancesport?
a) As a sport — with competition rules, judging and manners for dancers and spectators  b) As free play with no rules  c) As a written exam only
**Answer: a) As a sport — with competition rules, judging and manners for dancers and spectators**
