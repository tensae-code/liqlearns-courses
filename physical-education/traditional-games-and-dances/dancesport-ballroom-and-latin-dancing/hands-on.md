# Hands-on — Dancesport — Ballroom and Latin Dancing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hold and lead

With a partner, take the hold the book teaches for dancesport and practise leading your partner across a clear floor space, keeping the body positions the book names. Your partner checks the hold and the leading against the lesson — do not add steps the book does not give.

## Two rhythms

Say the two rhythms aloud — waltz for ballroom, cha-cha-cha for Latin American — and step each one's beat in place. Your partner checks each rhythm against the lesson.
