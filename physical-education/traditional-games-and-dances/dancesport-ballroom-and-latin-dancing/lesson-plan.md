# Lesson plan — Dancesport — Ballroom and Latin Dancing

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Dancesport — Ballroom and Latin Dancing

## Sub lessons
1. **ลีลาศ basics** — the Thailand Grade 12 book: the history of dancesport, its types (ballroom and Latin American), dance lines, body positions and how to hold a partner, leading, and preparing to take part.
2. **Dancing the waltz and the cha-cha-cha** — ballroom dancing in waltz rhythm and Latin American dancing in cha-cha-cha rhythm.
3. **Dancesport as a sport** — the benefits and beauty of ลีลาศ, good manners for dancers and spectators, competition rules, how to watch a competition, judging, and using your own ability to raise your team's potential.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Moving to a Beat — Rhythmic Movement. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what dancesport (ลีลาศ) is in the book: ballroom and Latin American dancing learned as a competitive sport.
2. Name the two dances the book teaches: the waltz (ballroom) and the cha-cha-cha (Latin American).
3. Say what a dancer holds and leads with: dance lines, body positions, how to hold a partner, and leading.
4. Say how dancesport is watched and judged: good manners for dancers and spectators, competition rules, and judging.

## What you need
A partner and a clear floor space, as the book's dancesport lessons use them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Dancesport feels like "just dancing" → anchor it in the book: ลีลาศ is taught as a sport, with competition rules, judging and manners for dancers and spectators.
