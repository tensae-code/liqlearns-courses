## Sub lesson 1 — ลีลาศ basics

Thailand's Grade 12 book ends its PE course with **ลีลาศ — dancesport**. The book teaches its **history**, and its **types**: **ballroom** dancing and **Latin American** dancing. Before any dance is danced, the book teaches the basics a dancer stands on: **dance lines**, **body positions** and **how to hold a partner**, **leading**, and **preparing to take part**. This is Thailand's own way: its senior PE specialism is not a team game but dancesport.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dancing the waltz and the cha-cha-cha

The book then teaches two dances, one from each family: **ballroom dancing in waltz rhythm**, and **Latin American dancing in cha-cha-cha rhythm**. The waltz carries the ballroom side; the cha-cha-cha carries the Latin American side.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dancesport as a sport

ลีลาศ is taught as a **sport**, not only as dancing. The book teaches its **benefits and beauty**, the **good manners** expected of dancers and of spectators, its **competition rules**, **how to watch a competition**, and **judging** — and how to use your own ability to **raise your team's potential**.
