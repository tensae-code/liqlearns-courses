# Traditional Games of DR Congo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The wrestling initiation games begin with…
a) Greeting and respect  b) A race  c) A whistle start
**Answer: a) Greeting and respect**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the Grade 1 opposition games?
a) Scarf-pull  b) Chess  c) Sack race
**Answer: a) Scarf-pull**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Marelle (Kebo), the player first…
a) Throws a puck  b) Claps a song  c) Runs the court
**Answer: a) Throws a puck**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** While hopping the Kebo court, you must not…
a) Touch the lines  b) Breathe  c) Change feet ever
**Answer: a) Touch the lines**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Zango is played with…
a) Clapping and song  b) A ball and goals  c) A rope
**Answer: a) Clapping and song**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the older book, Nzango is adapted for…
a) Rhythm and leg power  b) Swimming  c) Throwing distance
**Answer: a) Rhythm and leg power**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 1 book adapts local games from…
a) Kivu, Tshopo and other provinces  b) Other continents only  c) No named places
**Answer: a) Kivu, Tshopo and other provinces**
