# Traditional Games of DR Congo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the wrestling initiation kept safe in the Grade 2 book?
**Answer: Standing opposition only — unbalancing and scarf-grab under respect rules.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is Marelle (Kebo) played?
**Answer: Throw a puck, then hop the court without touching the lines, keeping balance.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Zango train?
**Answer: Rhythmic foot-and-hand agility, with clapping and song.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which local games does the Grade 1 book adapt?
**Answer: Spinning-top and running games from Kivu, Tshopo and other provinces.**
