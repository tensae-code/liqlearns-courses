# Traditional Games of DR Congo — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wrestling initiation games
Pass mark: describe the standing opposition games — scarf-pull, push games, unbalancing and scarf-grab — and say that greeting and respect come first.

## Sub lesson 2 — Marelle (Kebo)
Pass mark: say that the player throws a puck, then hops the court without touching the lines, keeping balance.

## Sub lesson 3 — Zango and Nzango
Pass mark: describe Zango as rhythmic foot-and-hand agility sequences with clapping and song, and Nzango as played for rhythm and leg power.

## Sub lesson 4 — Adapting other local games
Pass mark: name the spinning-top and running games from Kivu, Tshopo and other provinces that the Grade 1 book adapts for its class.
