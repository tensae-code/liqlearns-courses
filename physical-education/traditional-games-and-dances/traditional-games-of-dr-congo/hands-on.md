# Hands-on — Traditional Games of DR Congo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Kebo court

Chalk a marelle court, throw your puck, and hop the course without touching the lines, the way the books teach it. Your partner watches the lines and your balance.

## Scarf-pull with respect

With a partner, greet first, then play the standing scarf-pull game: feet planted, pull the scarf from your partner's waistband without pushing them over. Stop the moment the greeting's respect is lost.
