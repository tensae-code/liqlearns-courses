# Lapta and Shuttlecock Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three lapta forms in the Tajikistan book.
**Answer: "Bita", "Stick-Knocker" and "Knock Out of the Circle".**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two shuttlecock games in the book.
**Answer: Round lapta with a shuttlecock and "Hit the Shuttlecock Up" (also: returning the shuttlecock with rackets).**
