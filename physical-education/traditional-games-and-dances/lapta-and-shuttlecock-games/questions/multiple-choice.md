# Lapta and Shuttlecock Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lapta is…
a) A bat game  b) A swimming game  c) A board game
**Answer: a) A bat game**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a lapta form in the Tajikistan book?
a) "Knock Out of the Circle"  b) "Score a Goal"  c) "Ball in the Field"
**Answer: a) "Knock Out of the Circle"**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Hit the Shuttlecock Up" is…
a) A shuttlecock game  b) A lapta form with a bat only  c) A snow game
**Answer: a) A shuttlecock game**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the shuttlecock games, players return the shuttlecock with…
a) Rackets  b) Their feet only  c) A hoop
**Answer: a) Rackets**
