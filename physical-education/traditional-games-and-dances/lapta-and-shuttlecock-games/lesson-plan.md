# Lesson plan — Lapta and Shuttlecock Games

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Lapta and Shuttlecock Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Lapta and shuttlecock games: the Russian bat game lapta and the shuttlecock games played with it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## Sub lessons
1. **Lapta** — the Tajikistan book: the bat game lapta — "Bita", "Stick-Knocker", "Knock Out of the Circle".
2. **Shuttlecock games** — the Tajikistan book: round lapta with a shuttlecock, "Hit the Shuttlecock Up" and returning the shuttlecock with rackets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Kicking, Passing and Dribbling. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name lapta as a bat game and name its forms the book teaches: "Bita", "Stick-Knocker", "Knock Out of the Circle".
2. Name the shuttlecock games: round lapta with a shuttlecock, "Hit the Shuttlecock Up" and returning the shuttlecock with rackets.
3. Say that both families are taught together in the Tajikistan book.

## What you need
A bat and ball for lapta, a shuttlecock and rackets for the shuttlecock games — only as the book's games use them, in a clear space.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lapta and shuttlecock blur together → separate them by equipment: lapta is the bat game; the shuttlecock games keep the shuttlecock up and return it with rackets.
- Names feel hard → learn them as the book lists them, one family at a time.
