# Lapta and Shuttlecock Games

## Do it first

What bat-and-ball games do you know? And have you ever kept a shuttlecock in the air? Say what you know first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lapta

Tajikistan's *Fizicheskaya Kultura* teaches **lapta**, the bat game, in its forms **"Bita", "Stick-Knocker" and "Knock Out of the Circle"**. Lapta is the Russian bat game the registry names for this lesson: a striking game played with a bat.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shuttlecock games

The same section teaches the **shuttlecock games**: **round lapta with a shuttlecock, "Hit the Shuttlecock Up" and returning the shuttlecock with rackets**. Where lapta strikes a ball with a bat, the shuttlecock games keep the shuttlecock up and send it back.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the lapta forms and the shuttlecock games the Tajikistan book names, one family at a time.
