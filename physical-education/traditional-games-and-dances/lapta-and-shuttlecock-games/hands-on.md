# Hands-on — Lapta and Shuttlecock Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the games

Say the lapta forms aloud — "Bita", "Stick-Knocker", "Knock Out of the Circle" — then the shuttlecock games — round lapta with a shuttlecock, "Hit the Shuttlecock Up", returning the shuttlecock with rackets. Your partner checks each name against the lesson.

## Keep it up

With a shuttlecock, practise the book's idea in "Hit the Shuttlecock Up": hit the shuttlecock up and count how many hits you and a partner can keep going. Stop and reset safely when it falls.
