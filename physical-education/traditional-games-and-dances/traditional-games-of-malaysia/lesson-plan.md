# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Malaysia

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Malaysia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Malaysia: Malaysian traditional games such as Gelap Cerah, Aci Sep, Tiang Empat, Hoki Terbang and Usung Balak. — taught here through Malaysia's way: its KSSM PJPK books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan** — Unit 7 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — jengkek ligan, a Malaysian traditional hopping game played in recreation time — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 106.
2. **Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan** — Unit 7 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — bola tuju kaki, a Malaysian traditional target-ball game played with the feet — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 106.
3. **Permainan Tradisional** — Unit 7 Permainan Tradisional — Gelap Cerah, a Malaysian traditional chase game — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 112.
4. **Permainan Tradisional** — Unit 7 Permainan Tradisional — Aci Sep, a Malaysian traditional chase-and-tag game — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 114.
5. **Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan** — Unit 5 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — Aci Bulat, a Malaysian traditional game (pupils make flash cards teaching it) — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 124.
6. **Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan** — Unit 5 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — Tiang Empat ("four posts"), a Malaysian traditional game — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 124.
7. **Hoki Terbang** — Unit 5 Hoki Terbang — Hoki Terbang ("flying hockey"), a Malaysian traditional game: two teams move actively to find space, attack together to score and defend their goal, playable with wooden sticks on any open ground — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 92.
8. **Usung Balak** — Unit 5 Usung Balak — Usung Balak, a Malaysian traditional team game: carrying a log together in a relay to a cone and back; a dropped log eliminates the team — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 96.
