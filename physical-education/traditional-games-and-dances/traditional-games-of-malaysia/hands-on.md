# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Malaysia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise traditional games of malaysia

Practise the skills in this lesson slowly and safely, in a clear space, the way Malaysia's books teach them — traditional games of Malaysia: Malaysian traditional games such as Gelap Cerah, Aci Sep, Tiang Empat, Hoki Terbang and Usung Balak.
