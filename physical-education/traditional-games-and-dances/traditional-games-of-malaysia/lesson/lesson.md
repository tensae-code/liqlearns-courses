Unit 7 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — jengkek ligan, a Malaysian traditional hopping game played in recreation time — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 106.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan

Unit 7 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — bola tuju kaki, a Malaysian traditional target-ball game played with the feet — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 106.

## Sub lesson 3 — Permainan Tradisional

Unit 7 Permainan Tradisional — Gelap Cerah, a Malaysian traditional chase game — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 112.

## Sub lesson 4 — Permainan Tradisional

Unit 7 Permainan Tradisional — Aci Sep, a Malaysian traditional chase-and-tag game — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 114.

## Sub lesson 5 — Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan

Unit 5 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — Aci Bulat, a Malaysian traditional game (pupils make flash cards teaching it) — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 124.

## Sub lesson 6 — Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan

Unit 5 Rekreasi dan Kesenggangan — Tiang Empat ("four posts"), a Malaysian traditional game — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 124.

## Sub lesson 7 — Hoki Terbang

Unit 5 Hoki Terbang — Hoki Terbang ("flying hockey"), a Malaysian traditional game: two teams move actively to find space, attack together to score and defend their goal, playable with wooden sticks on any open ground — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 92.

## Sub lesson 8 — Usung Balak

Unit 5 Usung Balak — Usung Balak, a Malaysian traditional team game: carrying a log together in a relay to a cone and back; a dropped log eliminates the team — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 96.
