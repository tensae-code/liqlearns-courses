# Traditional Dances of Our Zone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which dance name does the book give for the men's festival dances?
a) Hoya Hoye  b) Buhe Belu  c) Abeba Ayesh Wey
**Answer: a) Hoya Hoye**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the book frame dances as developing?
a) Communication, coordination and fitness  b) Only speed  c) Silence
**Answer: a) Communication, coordination and fitness**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What are the two victory dances the book pictures?
a) A development victory dance and a sport victory dance  b) A wedding dance and a sleep dance  c) Only one kind exists
**Answer: a) A development victory dance and a sport victory dance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The development victory dance named in the book is —
a) ዱብ ዱብ አለ ("Dub Dub Ale")  b) Hoya Hoye  c) Buhe Belu
**Answer: a) ዱብ ዱብ አለ ("Dub Dub Ale")**
