# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Our Zone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Occasion hunt

From elders and family, collect one dance for each occasion — wedding, festival, work, victory — with its name and home. Present the set of four.

## Victory dance

After a real class game or finished work task, perform the victory dance that belongs to it — sport victory for the game, development victory for the work.
