# Samoan Cultural Dance and Games — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Samoa's curriculum express cultural practices in movement?
**Answer: Through dance — work actions such as preparing coconut cream shown in dance movements.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is lape?
**Answer: A Samoan hand game named in Samoa's Years 1–8 curriculum.**
