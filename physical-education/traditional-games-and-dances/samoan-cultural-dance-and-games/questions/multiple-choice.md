# Samoan Cultural Dance and Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's curriculum, cultural practices in movement are expressed through…
a) Dance  b) Examinations  c) Silent reading
**Answer: a) Dance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's dance example shows which work action?
a) Preparing coconut cream  b) Repairing a car  c) Building a road
**Answer: a) Preparing coconut cream**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lape is…
a) A Samoan hand game  b) A fitness test  c) A swimming stroke
**Answer: a) A Samoan hand game**
