# Hands-on — Samoan Cultural Dance and Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work action into dance movement

Choose one work action (the book's example is preparing coconut cream — or take another work action you know). Show it first as plain work, then as dance movement: bigger, rhythmic, repeated in time. Perform it for a partner and let them name the action.

## Name the game

Write down, from memory of the lesson: the name of the Samoan hand game in Samoa's curriculum (lape), and one sentence on where the curriculum places it — inside the Year 3 cultural-practices movement work, alongside the cultural dance. Do not add rules the book does not give.
