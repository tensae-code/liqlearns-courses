# Samoan Cultural Dance and Games

## Do it first

Mime one everyday work action — stirring, carrying, preparing food — without saying what it is. Could a partner guess it from the movement alone? That is the seed of Samoa's cultural dance. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Samoan cultural dance

Samoa's curriculum statement puts **cultural practices inside the movement strand**: in Year 3, learners express cultural practices **through movement in dance** — the dance shows the work of Samoan life, such as **preparing coconut cream and other work actions**, turned into movement. The dance is the culture, carried by the body.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lape — the Samoan hand game

The same Year 3 work names **lape** as a **Samoan hand game** — Samoa's own game, carried in the curriculum alongside the cultural dance. The statement names the game as part of Samoa's cultural practices in movement; it does not print the game's rules in the placed pages, so this lesson names lape as the book names it and invents no rules for it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how Samoa's curriculum expresses cultural practices in dance, with its coconut-cream example, and name the Samoan hand game it carries alongside: lape.
