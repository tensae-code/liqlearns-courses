# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Kuwait

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Kuwait
**Place in the curriculum:** Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** traditional dances of Kuwait: Kuwaiti folk arts and dances, and movement formations for national occasions — taught here through Kuwait's way: a full folk-heritage chapter in each girls' guide.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Al-Dazza and Al-Saff — the folk dances of the primary guide** — The primary girls' guide's folk-heritage chapter teaches Kuwaiti folk arts: Al-Dazza, the fast-rhythm women's folk dance, and the Bedouin women's art Al-Saff, the row dance — their rhythmic movement performance, formations and heritage instruments.
2. **Al-Samri — the women's folk art of the middle guide** — The middle girls' guide's folk-heritage chapter teaches Al-Samri, the women's folk art: its story, its movement performance, group formations and instruments.
3. **Movement formations for national occasions** — Both guides teach national occasions with movement formations: the Kuwaiti National Day (25 February) and Liberation Day (26 February) celebrations and the Kuwaiti folk occasions — and the primary guide adds the Gargee'an mid-Ramadan children's festival movement performance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Kuwait's folk dances as the guides teach them: Al-Dazza, Al-Saff and Al-Samri.
2. Say which occasions are taught with movement formations: National Day, Liberation Day, the folk occasions and Gargee'an.
