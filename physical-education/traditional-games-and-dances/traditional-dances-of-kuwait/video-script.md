# Video script — Traditional Dances of Kuwait

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait's folk arts are danced, not just named.
Al-Dazza moves fast; Al-Saff dances in a row; Al-Samri tells its story.
And on National Day the whole class forms up and moves together.
Those are the **traditional dances of Kuwait**.
