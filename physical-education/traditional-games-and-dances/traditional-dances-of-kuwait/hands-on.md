# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Kuwait

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance in a row

Try Al-Saff's way: stand in a row with companions and move together in rhythm, the way the primary guide's folk-heritage chapter teaches the row dance.

## Form up for the day

With a group, make a movement formation for a celebration day — the kind both guides teach for National Day and Liberation Day — and perform it together.
