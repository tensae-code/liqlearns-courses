# Traditional Dances of Kuwait

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Al-Dazza and Al-Saff — the folk dances of the primary guide

The primary girls' guide's folk-heritage chapter teaches Kuwaiti folk arts: Al-Dazza, the fast-rhythm women's folk dance, and the Bedouin women's art Al-Saff, the row dance — their rhythmic movement performance, formations and heritage instruments.

## Sub lesson 2 — Al-Samri — the women's folk art of the middle guide

The middle girls' guide's folk-heritage chapter teaches Al-Samri, the women's folk art: its story, its movement performance, group formations and instruments.

## Sub lesson 3 — Movement formations for national occasions

Both guides teach national occasions with movement formations: the Kuwaiti National Day (25 February) and Liberation Day (26 February) celebrations and the Kuwaiti folk occasions — and the primary guide adds the Gargee'an mid-Ramadan children's festival movement performance.
