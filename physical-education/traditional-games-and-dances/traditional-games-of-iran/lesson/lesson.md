# Traditional Games of Iran

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Module 1, play part

Iran's book teaches module 1, play part — old Iranian games named and discussed as cultural heritage: bazi-ye vasati (dodgeball), gorgam-be-hava and haft-sang (seven stones).

## Sub lesson 2 — Module 1 "Planning traditional games and sports"

Iran's book teaches module 1 "Planning traditional games and sports" — selecting, adapting and running Iran's traditional games and sports as programmes.

## Sub lesson 3 — Module 1, Unit 2 "Introducing Iran's native and local games"

Iran's book teaches module 1, Unit 2 "Introducing Iran's native and local games" — a catalogue of Iran's regional games, their rules and where they are played.

## Sub lesson 4 — Module 1 framing "Planning traditional games and sports"

Iran's book teaches module 1 framing "Planning traditional games and sports" — the module's planning task over Units 1–2.
