# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Iran

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise it the book's way

Practise the skill slowly and safely, the way Iran's book teaches it — Module 1, play part — old Iranian games named and discussed as cultural heritage: bazi-ye vasati (dodgeball), gorgam-be-hava and haft-sang (seven stones).
