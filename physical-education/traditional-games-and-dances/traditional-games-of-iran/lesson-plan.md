# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Iran

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Iran
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Iran: Iran's native and local games, their rules, and planning and running them. — taught here through Iran's way: its official ministry books for the technical-vocational Physical Education field.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Traditional Games of Iran — Module 1, play part** — Iran's book teaches module 1, play part — old Iranian games named and discussed as cultural heritage: bazi-ye vasati (dodgeball), gorgam-be-hava and haft-sang (seven stones).
2. **Traditional Games of Iran — Module 1 "Planning traditional games and sports"** — Iran's book teaches module 1 "Planning traditional games and sports" — selecting, adapting and running Iran's traditional games and sports as programmes.
3. **Traditional Games of Iran — Module 1, Unit 2 "Introducing Iran's native and local games"** — Iran's book teaches module 1, Unit 2 "Introducing Iran's native and local games" — a catalogue of Iran's regional games, their rules and where they are played.
4. **Traditional Games of Iran — Module 1 framing "Planning traditional games and sports"** — Iran's book teaches module 1 framing "Planning traditional games and sports" — the module's planning task over Units 1–2.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
