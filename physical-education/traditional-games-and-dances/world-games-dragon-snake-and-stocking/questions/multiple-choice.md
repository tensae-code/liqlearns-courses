# World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book gives how many world games in full for practice?
a) Two  b) Ten  c) None
**Answer: a) Two**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The dragon is formed by players…
a) Holding the shoulders or waist of the one in front  b) Standing apart  c) Sitting in a circle
**Answer: a) Holding the shoulders or waist of the one in front**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The line wins when it…
a) Circles and traps the leader inside  b) Runs away  c) Drops hands
**Answer: a) Circles and traps the leader inside**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In Stocking, the rope…
a) Rises from low down to knee height and beyond  b) Stays on the ground  c) Is held by one person
**Answer: a) Rises from low down to knee height and beyond**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** While the jumper jumps, the others…
a) Sing or dance traditional dances round her  b) Sit silently  c) Leave
**Answer: a) Sing or dance traditional dances round her**
