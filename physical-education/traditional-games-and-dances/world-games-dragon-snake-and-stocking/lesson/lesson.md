# World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking

## Do it first

Name a game from another country that you have heard of. What country is it from? Keep your answer — this lesson teaches two world games in full, the way your own country's games were taught.

## Sub lesson 1 — World traditional games — every country has its own

Ethiopia's games are not the only traditional games. **Many countries have traditional games of their own**, and the Grade 8 book gives **two world games in full for practice**: the Dragon Snake and the Stocking game. They are taught exactly as Ethiopia's games are taught — set-up, play, and how the game is won and lost.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dragon Snake (የድራጎን እባብ) — Vietnam / China

A **leader** is chosen. The other players **line up holding the shoulders or waist of the one in front, forming the dragon**. The leader **runs to catch or separate the last player** — the tail. The **dragon's head blocks with linked arms**. If **the line circles and traps the leader inside, the line wins**; the **leader wins by touching the last player or breaking the line**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Stocking (ስቶኪንግ) — the rising rope game

**Stocking (ስቶኪንግ)** is an **African girls' traditional game found worldwide** — a rope game, not just skipping: **two players hold the rope's ends at the sides while one jumps in the middle, the rope rising from low down up to knee height and beyond** — the game **works the body from feet to neck**. The others **sing or dance traditional dances round her** as she jumps.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the Dragon Snake line and mark the leader, the head and the tail.
2. Set a rope low and practise Stocking jumps, raising it a little each round.
3. Teach one of the two games to a younger pupil, rules first.
