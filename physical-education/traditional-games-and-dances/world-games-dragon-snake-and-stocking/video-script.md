# Video script — World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order.

---

Narrator: Every country has its own games. Today: a dragon from Vietnam and China, and a rising rope the whole world plays.
Narrator: Hold the one in front. Protect the tail. If the dragon traps its leader, the dragon wins.
Narrator: Two hold the rope. One jumps in the middle. Higher, higher — feet to neck.
