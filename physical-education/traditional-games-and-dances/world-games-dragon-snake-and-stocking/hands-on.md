# Hands-on — World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking

One line of framing: these activities use real things — the field, the street, the classroom and home.

## Play both games
Play Dragon Snake in a line of at least six, then Stocking with a rope held low. Swap roles so everyone leads, jumps and holds.

## Make the game cards
Write each game on a card: name, home, players, how it is won. Keep the cards with the class games box.
