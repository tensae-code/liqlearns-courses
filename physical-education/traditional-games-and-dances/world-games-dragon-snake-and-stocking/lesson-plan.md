# Lesson plan — World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** World Games — Dragon Snake and Stocking
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Traditional Games and Dances (Physical Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two world traditional games in full: Vietnam/China's Dragon Snake and the worldwide African girls' Stocking rope game — how each is set up, won and lost.
**This lesson's own level:** it extends the Traditional Games and Dances title beyond Ethiopia, as the Grade 8 book itself does.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **World traditional games — every country has its own** — the Grade 8 student book's §7.2 opening: many countries have traditional games of their own; two are given in full for practice.
2. **Dragon Snake (የድራጎን እባብ) — Vietnam / China** — the Grade 8 student book's §7.2.1: the dragon line, its head blocking with linked arms, the leader hunting the tail.
3. **Stocking (ስቶኪንግ) — the rising rope game** — the Grade 8 student book's §7.2.2: two holders, one jumper, the rope rising from low to knee height and beyond, singing and dancing around her.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title — recommended, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a world traditional game is and name the two games this lesson teaches in full.
2. Play Dragon Snake: form the dragon, protect the tail, and state both ways the game is decided.
3. Play Stocking: hold, jump and raise the rope in its order, feet to neck.

## What you need
A field or yard, and one long rope for Stocking. No other equipment.

## If the learner is stuck
- The dragon breaks apart → hands stay holding shoulders or waist; the head leads the turn, the line follows.
- The rope trips the jumper → start it low; it rises only as jumps succeed.
