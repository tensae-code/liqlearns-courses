# Hands-on — Kho-Kho

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play a round of kho-kho

With your class or family, set out the sitting chasers the way Nepal's Grade 7 book teaches it, and play a round: one chaser gives kho, the runner dodges and runs around the poles, and everyone follows the rules from the Grade 8 book.
