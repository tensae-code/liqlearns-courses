## Sub lesson 1 — Tug of war (risman kashi) — a traditional Afghan game for holidays

Tug of war — risman kashi — is a traditional Afghan game played for holidays, as the Grade 7 guide teaches it. Two equal teams pull a rope of 20 m or more.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pulling across the centre line — strength and coordination

Each team pulls to drag the other team across the centre line. The game is played to build strength and coordination.
