# Tug of War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How long is the rope in risman kashi, as the guide teaches it?
a) 20 m or more  b) 2 m  c) 5 m
**Answer: a) 20 m or more**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a team win the pull in risman kashi?
a) By dragging the other team across the centre line  b) By letting go of the rope  c) By pulling the rope off the ground
**Answer: a) By dragging the other team across the centre line**
