# Lesson plan — Traditional Sports of China

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Sports of China
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional sports of China: China's traditional sports culture and its ethnic folk sports — board-shoe and stilt racing, dragon-boat racing, pearl ball and diabolo. — taught here through China's way: its PEP Senior-High PE & Health compulsory volume.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **China's traditional sports culture** — China's Senior High book teaches China's traditional sports culture: its deep cultural meaning, its body-and-mind harmony, and its rich variety of forms.
2. **China's ethnic folk sports** — The book teaches competitive and performance folk sports: board-shoe racing, stilt racing, flower-cannon scramble, pearl ball, shuttlecock kicking, diabolo and dragon-boat racing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
