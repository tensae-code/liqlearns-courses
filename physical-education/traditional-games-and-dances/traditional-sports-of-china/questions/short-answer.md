# Traditional Sports of China — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does China's traditional sports culture join in harmony?
**Answer: Body and mind.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of the book's ethnic folk sports.
**Answer: Board-shoe racing, stilt racing, flower-cannon scramble, pearl ball, shuttlecock kicking, diabolo, dragon-boat racing (any two).**
