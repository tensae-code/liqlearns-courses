# Traditional Sports of China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** China's traditional sports culture stresses harmony of:
a) Body and mind  b) Speed and strength only  c) Winning and prizes
**Answer: a) Body and mind**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the folk sports the book names?
a) Dragon-boat racing  b) Ice hockey  c) Cricket
**Answer: a) Dragon-boat racing**
