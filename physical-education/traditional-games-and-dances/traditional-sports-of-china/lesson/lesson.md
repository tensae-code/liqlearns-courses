# Traditional Sports of China

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — China's traditional sports culture

China's Senior High book teaches China's traditional sports culture: its deep cultural meaning, its body-and-mind harmony, and its rich variety of forms.

## Sub lesson 2 — China's ethnic folk sports

The book teaches competitive and performance folk sports: board-shoe racing, stilt racing, flower-cannon scramble, pearl ball, shuttlecock kicking, diabolo and dragon-boat racing.
