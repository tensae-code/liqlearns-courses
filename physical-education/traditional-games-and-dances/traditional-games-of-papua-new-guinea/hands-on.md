# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Papua New Guinea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Traditional leisure and recreational games — Grade 5

Do the part as Papua New Guinea's guides teach it: Lesson-title tables, Leisure and Recreation unit: traditional leisure games, traditional recreational games, favourite recreational game. Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.

## Sekapu, string games and hopscotch — Lower Primary

Do the part as Papua New Guinea's guides teach it: Strand: Physical Activity: traditional games used to teach ball-handling skills, and local games named in the leisure outcomes — sekapu (hand/stone games), string games, hopscotch. Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.
