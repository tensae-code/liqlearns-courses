# Traditional Dances of Our Area (Girls) — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The book gives one national set of dances that every class must dance.
**Answer: False** — the dances are deliberately the pupils' own locality's.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** For each pictured dance, pupils say which body part it moves most.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Grade 2 dances are also the pupils' own locality's dances.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Grade 2 dances are the girls' dances of their own locality, not a national set.
**Answer: True**
