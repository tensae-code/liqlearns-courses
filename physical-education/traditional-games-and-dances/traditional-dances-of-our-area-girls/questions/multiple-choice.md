# Traditional Dances of Our Area (Girls) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the book ask you to do with the pictured dances?
a) Name which are done in your area, dance them, and say which body part each moves most  b) Copy a dance from another country  c) Sit and watch only
**Answer: a) Name which are done in your area, dance them, and say which body part each moves most**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** With which dance does the Grade 1 series open?
a) Abeba Ayesh Wey  b) Hoya Hoye  c) Buhe Belu
**Answer: a) Abeba Ayesh Wey**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** In Grade 2, what does the task ask about each dance?
a) Which body part it works most  b) How much it costs  c) Who invented it
**Answer: a) Which body part it works most**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does the Grade 2 task add to the study of each dance?
a) Which body part the dance works most — the dance as the area's own physical training  b) The dance's price  c) The dance's inventor
**Answer: a) Which body part the dance works most — the dance as the area's own physical training**
