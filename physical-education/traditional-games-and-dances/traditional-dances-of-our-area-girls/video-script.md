# Video script — Traditional Dances of Our Area (Girls)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A dance of YOUR area. Not far away — yours.
It opens: **Abeba Ayesh Wey** — አበባ አዬሽ ወይ.
Which of these dances live in your area? Name them!
Now dance — with your classmates, the way your area dances.
Stop. Which body part moved most? Shoulders? Neck? Feet? Say it!
Again! This time, feel that part work.
