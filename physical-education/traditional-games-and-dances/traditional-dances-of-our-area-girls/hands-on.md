# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Our Area (Girls)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask and bring

Interview a grandmother, mother or elder dancer: what is the dance called, when is it danced, which body part moves most? Bring the answers — and the dance — to class.

## Dance and study

Dance the area's dance together. Stop. Everyone points to the body part that worked most. Dance again, feeling that part work.
