# Hands-on — What Traditional Dance and Games Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bring one from home

Each learner brings one traditional game or dance from their family or area — name, home, occasion — and the class lists them together. Nothing is invented: what nobody knows stays an open question for the elders.

## Festival map

On one page, list the festivals of your area through the year and write beside each the dance or game that belongs to it.
