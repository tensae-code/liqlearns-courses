# What Traditional Dance and Games Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional dance expresses what?
a) The community's identity  b) The teacher's favourite steps  c) Nothing — it is only exercise
**Answer: a) The community's identity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the books' words, culture is…
a) A society's identity — its dress and the rest of its way of life  b) Only its food  c) Only its language
**Answer: a) A society's identity — its dress and the rest of its way of life**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which body has given laws to a set of Ethiopian traditional games?
a) The Ethiopian Cultural Sport Federation  b) The Olympic Committee  c) The school office
**Answer: a) The Ethiopian Cultural Sport Federation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the five federated games?
a) Gebeta  b) Basketball  c) Hurdles
**Answer: a) Gebeta**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What do written laws do for a traditional game?
a) Protect it, so it is played the same way in every place  b) End it  c) Make it a school subject only
**Answer: a) Protect it, so it is played the same way in every place**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional dances are performed especially at…
a) Festivals  b) Examinations  c) Market closing time
**Answer: a) Festivals**
