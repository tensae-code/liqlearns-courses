# What Traditional Dance and Games Are — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A traditional dance belongs to one community's own way of life.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional game stops being traditional when it is given written laws.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gena and Gebeta are among the games the Federation has given laws.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture means only a society's dress.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional games stand inside the same culture framing as traditional dance.
**Answer: True**
