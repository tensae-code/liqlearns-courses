# Lesson plan — What Traditional Dance and Games Are

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** What Traditional Dance and Games Are
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what a traditional dance and a traditional game are: a community's own movement, carrying its identity — its dress, its festivals and its way of life.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the Traditional Games and Dances title at region level; it stands on the area, district and zone lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What traditional dance is** — the concept lessons of Grades 5–7: dance as part of a community's identity, performed at festivals inside its cultural and environmental setting.
2. **What traditional games are — and the games with laws** — the concept lessons on traditional games, and the Grade 7 book's pictured set of the games the Ethiopian Cultural Sport Federation has given laws: Korbo, horse racing, Gena, Gebeta and Bub.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Traditional Dances of Our Zone and Traditional Games of Our Zone — this lesson names what those surveys were collecting. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a traditional dance is: a community's cultural movement, performed at festivals, expressing its identity.
2. Say what culture means in the books' words: a society's identity — its dress and the rest of its way of life.
3. Say what traditional games are and name the games the Ethiopian Cultural Sport Federation has given laws.
4. Tell a traditional game from a modern sport game by its home, its occasion and its rules.

## What you need
Nothing but the class itself — and, for the last part, an elder or family member who knows a game from home.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot name a game from home → that is the homework, not a failure: ask an elder and bring the answer next time.
- Mixes traditional games with modern sport → sort by home and occasion: learned at home and festivals, or taught in school with a rule book?
