# Video script — What Traditional Dance and Games Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A festival! Drums, dress, a whole community moving together.
That is traditional dance — a community's identity, moving.
And the games? Played for generations. Some now have written laws: Korbo, Gena, Gebeta, horse racing, Bub.
Name yours. Every area has its own.
