# African Games — Bayoka, Mbube Mbube and Mamba — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bayoka — the snake race (Zambia / Zaire)
Pass mark: wriggle a full obstacle course as a joined snake without breaking once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mbube, Mbube — the lion and the impala (South Africa)
Pass mark: sing a lion closer and farther correctly — louder and faster near, softer and slower far.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mamba — the last one caught (across Africa)
Pass mark: play a full round of Mamba and say how the snake grows.
