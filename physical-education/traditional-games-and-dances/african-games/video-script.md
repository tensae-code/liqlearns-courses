# Video script — African Games — Bayoka, Mbube Mbube and Mamba

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Africa plays! Three games, three cards.
Bayoka! Sit, hold waists — wriggle, snake, wriggle! Stay joined!
Mbube, mbube… louder — the lion is CLOSE. Softer… it strays.
Mamba! Caught? Join the snake! The last one free wins!
