# Hands-on — African Games — Bayoka, Mbube Mbube and Mamba

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the Bayoka course

Set chairs, boxes or tyres as the obstacle course the book names. Two snakes race it in turn; a broken snake restarts from the last obstacle.

## The singing circle rehearsal

Without blindfolds: one learner walks with eyes closed between two classmates who guide only by singing louder or softer. Ears first, blindfolds after.
