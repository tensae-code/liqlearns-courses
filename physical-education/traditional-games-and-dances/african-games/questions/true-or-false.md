# African Games — Bayoka, Mbube Mbube and Mamba — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bayoka is played seated, holding waists.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Mbube Mbube, the lion and the impala can both see.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If the lion makes no catch in 1–2 minutes, a new lion is chosen.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Caught players in Mamba leave the game and sit out.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** A Bayoka round lasts 5–10 minutes.
**Answer: True**
