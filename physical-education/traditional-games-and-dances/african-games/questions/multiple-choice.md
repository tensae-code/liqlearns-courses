# African Games — Bayoka, Mbube Mbube and Mamba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bayoka teams must have…
a) 12 or more players  b) Exactly 3 players  c) Only 1 player
**Answer: a) 12 or more players**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Bayoka, the snake must…
a) Stay joined  b) Stand up to run  c) Let go at obstacles
**Answer: a) Stay joined**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The circle's song in Mbube Mbube goes faster and louder when…
a) The lion nears the impala  b) The lion strays away  c) The round ends
**Answer: a) The lion nears the impala**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How long is a round of Mbube Mbube?
a) 2–3 minutes  b) One hour  c) All day
**Answer: a) 2–3 minutes**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mamba's aim is to be…
a) The last player caught by the snake  b) The first caught  c) The snake's head
**Answer: a) The last player caught by the snake**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What equipment does Mamba need?
a) None  b) Two blindfolds  c) Chairs and tyres
**Answer: a) None**
