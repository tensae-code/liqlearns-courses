# Traditional Games of Mozambique — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six traditional games and dances the manual lists.
**Answer: Neca, As estátuas, Ao fom fim, O pião, Este é um cumprimento and Jogo do silêncio.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Neca played with?
**Answer: Small stones.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Portuguese heading of this part of the manual?
**Answer: Jogos e danças tradicionais (traditional games and dances).**
