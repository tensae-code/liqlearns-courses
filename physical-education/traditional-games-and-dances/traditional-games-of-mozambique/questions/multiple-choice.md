# Traditional Games of Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neca is played with…
a) Small stones  b) A football  c) A rope
**Answer: a) Small stones**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** O pião is…
a) The spinning top  b) A song  c) A formation
**Answer: a) The spinning top**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** O pião is known…
a) All over the country  b) In one classroom only  c) Nowhere anymore
**Answer: a) All over the country**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is in Mozambique's manual's traditional set?
a) Jogo do silêncio  b) Chess  c) Basketball
**Answer: a) Jogo do silêncio**
