# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Mozambique

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Neca with small stones

Play Neca the way Mozambique's manual names it: a game played with small stones. Use a handful of small stones on clear ground, and play it the way the class knows it — the manual names the game and its stones, and the playing lives with the players.

## Spin O pião

Spin the top — O pião. Watch how long it stands spinning, the top known all over Mozambique. Take turns: who keeps it spinning longest without touching it?
