## Sub lessons
1. **Mozambique's own traditional games and dances** — the Mozambique manual: Neca (played with small stones), As estátuas, Ao fom fim, O pião (the spinning top, known all over the country), Este é um cumprimento, Jogo do silêncio.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Traditional Games of Ethiopia. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Mozambique's traditional games and dances as its manual lists them: Neca, As estátuas, Ao fom fim, O pião, Este é um cumprimento, Jogo do silêncio.
2. Say what Neca is played with: small stones.
3. Say what O pião is, and how widely it is known: a spinning top, known all over the country.
4. Say where these games live in the manual: Jogos e danças tradicionais — its traditional games and dances.

## What you need
Small stones for Neca and a spinning top for O pião, as the manual names them; the other games are brought and taught the way the class knows them, as the manual's own way directs.
