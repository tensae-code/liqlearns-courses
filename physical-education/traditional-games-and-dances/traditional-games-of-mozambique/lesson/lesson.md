# Traditional Games of Mozambique

## Do it first

Every country keeps games its children have played for a long time. Name one from your own place. Then read on and meet Mozambique's own set.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mozambique's own traditional games and dances

Mozambique's Grade 1 manual closes its PE part with **Jogos e danças tradicionais** — its **traditional games and dances**. The set it names is: **Neca**, played with **small stones**; **As estátuas**; **Ao fom fim**; **O pião**, the **spinning top**, **known all over the country**; **Este é um cumprimento**; and **Jogo do silêncio**.

These are Mozambique's own way in this course: games and dances handed down across generations, taught in the manual as content in their own right — and, in the manual's own method, children teach each other the dances they know. The games are taught here only as the manual names them; no rules have been invented for any game the manual leaves with its players.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the six names — Neca, As estátuas, Ao fom fim, O pião, Este é um cumprimento, Jogo do silêncio — and the two anchors: Neca's small stones, and O pião the spinning top known all over the country.
