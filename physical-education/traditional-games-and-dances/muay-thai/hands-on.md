# Hands-on — Muay Thai

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take the guard

Stand in the guard stance the book names — การจดมวย — and hold it steady. Your partner checks your stance against the lesson — practise the stance as form only, as the book's sporting-spirit rule requires.

## Name the strikes

Say each technique aloud with its Thai name — the swinging punch and the uppercut (การชกหมัดเหวี่ยง/เสย), elbow strikes (การใช้ศอกตี) — and show each one's shape slowly in the air, without contact. Your partner checks each name against the lesson.
