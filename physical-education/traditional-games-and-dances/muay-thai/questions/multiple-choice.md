# Muay Thai — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Thailand book, Muay Thai is taught inside…
a) กีฬาไทย — Thai sports  b) กีฬาสากล — international sports  c) The games chapter only
**Answer: a) กีฬาไทย — Thai sports**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** การจดมวย is…
a) The guard stance  b) The uppercut  c) An elbow strike
**Answer: a) The guard stance**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The two punches the book teaches are…
a) The swinging punch and the uppercut  b) The jab and the hook only  c) No punches — elbows only
**Answer: a) The swinging punch and the uppercut**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** การใช้ศอกตี means…
a) Elbow strikes  b) The guard stance  c) Sporting spirit
**Answer: a) Elbow strikes**
