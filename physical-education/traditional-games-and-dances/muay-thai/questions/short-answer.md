# Muay Thai — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Muay Thai's guard stance in Thai.
**Answer: การจดมวย.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Under what one rule are Thai sports and international sports taught together in the book?
**Answer: One rule of sporting spirit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the elbow technique the book teaches, in Thai.
**Answer: การใช้ศอกตี — elbow strikes.**
