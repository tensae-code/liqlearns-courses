# Muay Thai — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Muay Thai is Thailand's own martial art in the book.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches Muay Thai inside international sports (กีฬาสากล).
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The uppercut is one of the punches the book teaches.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Thai sports and international sports sit under one rule of sporting spirit in the book.
