## Sub lesson 1 — Thai sports (กีฬาไทย) — Muay Thai technique

Thailand's Grade 5 book teaches sport as two families side by side: **กีฬาไทย — Thai sports** — and **กีฬาสากล — international sports** — under **one rule of sporting spirit**. Inside Thai sports, the book teaches **Muay Thai** technique in detail. A fighter begins from the **guard stance (การจดมวย)**. The punches are the **swinging punch** and the **uppercut (การชกหมัดเหวี่ยง/เสย)**. And Muay Thai strikes with the elbow too: **elbow strikes (การใช้ศอกตี)**. This is Thailand's own way, carried in its own textbook: its own martial art, taught to its own schoolchildren as a sport with spirit, not as fighting.

## Say it to your companion
