# Hands-on — Traditional Games of the Amhara Region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jumping relay of open places

In groups of four, take turns to jump from a set place and hand the jump over in a relay, covering a long distance — every member must take part, as the Grade 5 cooperation games also rule.

## Gebeta stones

With stones and hollows scratched in the earth, play gebeta by the orders used in your locality. A player who knows different orders teaches theirs; both are recorded.
