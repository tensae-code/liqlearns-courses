# Traditional Games of the Amhara Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Suzi game develops which movement?
a) Jumping  b) Swimming  c) Throwing
**Answer: a) Jumping**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ball-throwing-out is played by…
a) Two opposing teams  b) One player alone  c) Only teachers
**Answer: a) Two opposing teams**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gebeta is played with…
a) Hollows and stones on a board  b) A net and a ball  c) Curved sticks
**Answer: a) Hollows and stones on a board**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gena was played by shepherds…
a) From Tahsas to Tir, after the harvest, in the afternoon  b) At night in winter  c) Before the harvest at dawn
**Answer: a) From Tahsas to Tir, after the harvest, in the afternoon**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Horse racing's course is prepared at…
a) 400 or 600 metres  b) 50 metres  c) 10 kilometres
**Answer: a) 400 or 600 metres**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** At most how many riders race at once?
a) Six  b) Twenty  c) Two
**Answer: a) Six**
