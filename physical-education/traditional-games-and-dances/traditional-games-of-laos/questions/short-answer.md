# Traditional Games of Laos — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three animal chase games taught in the Lao guides.
**Answer: Any three of: the bear carries rice; snatch the monkey's tail; the tiger chases the pig; the tiger and the lion; Mr Tiger; catch the rabbit; catch the bird; catch the fish; the cat catches the mouse; the rabbit and the hunter.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What kind of game is "pass and receive the treasure"?
**Answer: A named Lao passing game, taught in the Grade 2 teacher guide of 2020.**
