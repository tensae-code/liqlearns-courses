# Traditional Games of Laos — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which grade's 2021 guide teaches "the bear carries rice"?
a) Grade 3  b) Grade 12  c) Grade 9
**Answer: a) Grade 3**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Nang Mon hides the cloth" is what kind of game?
a) A hiding-and-chasing playground game  b) A swimming game  c) A board game
**Answer: a) A hiding-and-chasing playground game**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In which book is "kick the ball at the target" taught?
a) The Grade 4 PE & Handicraft teacher guide, 2009  b) The Grade 12 guide  c) The Grade 6 guide
**Answer: a) The Grade 4 PE & Handicraft teacher guide, 2009**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The tortoise lays eggs game appears in how many of the Lao guides stocked here?
a) Four  b) One  c) None
**Answer: a) Four**
