# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Laos

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Laos
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Laos: the named Lao playground and chase games — animal games, circle games, dance-games and ball games.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Animal chase games**
2. **Circle, water, weather and hiding games**
3. **Target, race and ball games**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show: name the animal chase games the Lao guides teach — the bear carries rice, snatch the monkey's tail, the tiger chases the pig, the tiger and the lion, Mr Tiger, catch the rabbit, catch the bird, catch the fish, the cat catches the mouse, and the rabbit and the hunter — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.
2. Say and show: name the circle and hiding games — Nang Mon hides the cloth, flowing water and whirlpool, lightning thunder and rain, pass and receive the treasure, the rice-field dance, the tortoise lays eggs, and monkeys snatch the ball — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.
3. Say and show: name the target, race and ball games — kick the ball at the target, zigzag running, race to snatch a chair, change places, who reaches first, the running hand relay, jumping over the waves, little birds pecking, and herding the ducks into the pen — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.

## What you need
Open play space, and for the ball games a ball and a target, as the games are played, not only read about.
