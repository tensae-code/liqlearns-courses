# Video script — Traditional Games of Laos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every country hands its games down — Laos's guides teach theirs by name.
Animal chases — the tiger chases the pig, catch the rabbit, catch the bird.
Circle games — Nang Mon hides the cloth, flowing water and whirlpool.
Races and targets — snatch the chair, kick the ball at the target, who reaches first.
That is **traditional games of Laos**.
