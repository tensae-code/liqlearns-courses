# Traditional Games of Laos — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Animal chase games
Pass mark: name the animal chase games the Lao guides teach — the bear carries rice, snatch the monkey's tail, the tiger chases the pig, the tiger and the lion, Mr Tiger, catch the rabbit, catch the bird, catch the fish, the cat catches the mouse, and the rabbit and the hunter — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Circle, water, weather and hiding games
Pass mark: name the circle and hiding games — Nang Mon hides the cloth, flowing water and whirlpool, lightning thunder and rain, pass and receive the treasure, the rice-field dance, the tortoise lays eggs, and monkeys snatch the ball — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Target, race and ball games
Pass mark: name the target, race and ball games — kick the ball at the target, zigzag running, race to snatch a chair, change places, who reaches first, the running hand relay, jumping over the waves, little birds pecking, and herding the ducks into the pen — and say what kind of game each one is, as its placement names it.
