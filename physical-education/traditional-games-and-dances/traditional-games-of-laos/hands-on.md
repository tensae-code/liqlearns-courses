# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Laos

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play a chase game

Choose one animal chase game from this lesson and play it in a group the way its placement describes it — chasers, runners and safe places as the game names them. Your partner names the game and its kind afterwards.

## Play a circle game

Play one circle game from this lesson — hiding the cloth, flowing water and whirlpool, or passing the treasure — in a ring. Then swap roles and play again.
