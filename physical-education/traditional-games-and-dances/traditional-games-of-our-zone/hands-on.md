# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Our Zone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stick and circle

Mark the circle and the striking place. Practise the genna strike at a still ball until the ball rolls true; then play in turns, scoring balls in.

## The thinking board

Set a dama board (drawn on paper, stones for pieces). Play one game slowly, saying each move's reason aloud — thinking, attention, decision, patience, practised out loud.
