# Traditional Games of Our Zone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In genna, what must the struck ball do?
a) Go into a circle  b) Fly over a roof  c) Stay at your feet
**Answer: a) Go into a circle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the spinning-target game, what is thrown, and at what?
a) A stick (ዘንግ), at a spinning round object, from a set distance  b) A ball, at a person  c) A stone, anywhere
**Answer: a) A stick (ዘንግ), at a spinning round object, from a set distance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does the book say dama improves?
a) Thinking, attention, decision-making and patience  b) Only running speed  c) Singing
**Answer: a) Thinking, attention, decision-making and patience**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Besides dama, what kind of games close the zone survey?
a) Team running and chasing games  b) Swimming games  c) Card tricks
**Answer: a) Team running and chasing games**
