# Traditional Games of Our Zone — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The book gives the wrestling-type game a proper name.
**Answer: False** — it describes the game without naming it — and this lesson invents none.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** The spinning target can be made of tin, wood, carton or plastic.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Dama's pieces include the foot-soldier and the tank-soldier.
**Answer: True**
