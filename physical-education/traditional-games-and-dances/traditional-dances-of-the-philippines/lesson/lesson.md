# Traditional Dances of the Philippines

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kunday-Kunday basics

The Tagalog dance Kunday-Kunday: the kunday hand movement (like kumintang, wrist rotating), arms in lateral and reverse-T positions, and the change step and touch/point step (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 3, printed p. 10).

## Sub lesson 2 — Enjoying Kunday-Kunday

Performing the complete Kunday-Kunday dance with a partner, Figures I–IV, in folk costume, to its own music (Francisca Reyes Aquino collection) (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 4, printed p. 14).

## Sub lesson 3 — Philippine folk dance steps

Social/folk dancing in the Grade 2 book: the change step and heel-and-toe change step, and performing a chosen folk dance (Kamayan, Alahoy or Apat-apat) (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 3.3.2, printed p. 358).

## Sub lesson 4 — Alitaptap — the native folk dance

The native folk dance Alitaptap danced with a partner to its music, prepared for a culminating performance in improvised costume (balintawak and tapis; barong Tagalog and white trousers) (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 4.3.1, printed p. 375).
