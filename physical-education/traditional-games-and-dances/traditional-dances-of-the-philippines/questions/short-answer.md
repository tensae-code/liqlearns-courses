# Traditional Dances of the Philippines — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the kunday hand movement and one dance step.
**Answer: The Tagalog dance Kunday-Kunday: the kunday hand movement (like kumintang, wrist rotating), arms in lateral and reverse-T positions, and the change step and touch/point step (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 3, printed p. 10).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say how the complete dance is performed.
**Answer: Performing the complete Kunday-Kunday dance with a partner, Figures I–IV, in folk costume, to its own music (Francisca Reyes Aquino collection) (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 4, printed p. 14).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two change steps.
**Answer: Social/folk dancing in the Grade 2 book: the change step and heel-and-toe change step, and performing a chosen folk dance (Kamayan, Alahoy or Apat-apat) (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 3.3.2, printed p. 358).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Say how Alitaptap is prepared and performed.
**Answer: The native folk dance Alitaptap danced with a partner to its music, prepared for a culminating performance in improvised costume (balintawak and tapis; barong Tagalog and white trousers) (Grade 2 PE LM, Aralin 4.3.1, printed p. 375).**
