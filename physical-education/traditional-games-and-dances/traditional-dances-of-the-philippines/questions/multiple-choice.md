# Traditional Dances of the Philippines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the kunday hand movement like?
a) Like kumintang, with the wrist rotating  b) A clap  c) A fist pump
**Answer: a) Like kumintang, with the wrist rotating**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the complete Kunday-Kunday performed?
a) With a partner, Figures I–IV, in folk costume  b) Alone, sitting down  c) As a race
**Answer: a) With a partner, Figures I–IV, in folk costume**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which steps does the Grade 2 book teach for folk dancing?
a) The change step and the heel-and-toe change step  b) The cartwheel  c) The sprint start
**Answer: a) The change step and the heel-and-toe change step**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How is Alitaptap performed?
a) With a partner, to its music, in improvised costume  b) Alone in silence  c) On stilts
**Answer: a) With a partner, to its music, in improvised costume**
