## Sub lesson 1 — Active games of the ancestors of the Tajik people

Tajikistan's *Fizicheskaya Kultura* closes with the **active games of the ancestors of the Tajik people** — the historically formed active games of Tajik ancestors. Among them is the pushing game **"Kiro tela dinem?" (Whom shall we push?)**, for **6–40 school-age players**. These games are Tajikistan's own way, carried in its own textbook: the games its people formed over history, still taught to its youngest schoolchildren.
