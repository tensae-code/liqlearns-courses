## Sub lessons
1. **Active games of the ancestors of the Tajik people** — the Tajikistan book: the historically formed active games of Tajik ancestors, including the pushing game "Kiro tela dinem?" (Whom shall we push?), for 6–40 school-age players.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Games for Playing Together. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the traditional games of Tajikistan are in the book: the active games of the Tajik ancestors.
2. Name the pushing game "Kiro tela dinem?" (Whom shall we push?) and its player range, 6–40 school-age players.
3. Say where these games sit in the book: they close the book, after the active games.

## What you need
A group of players — the named game is for 6–40 school-age players — and a clear space, as the book's games use them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Ancestors feels distant → anchor it in the book: these are the active games formed over Tajik history, handed down to today's schoolchildren.
- The game's name is hard → say it as the book gives it: "Kiro tela dinem?" — Whom shall we push?
