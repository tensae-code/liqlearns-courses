# Traditional Games of Tajikistan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose active games close the Tajikistan book?
**Answer: The Tajik ancestors' — the historically formed active games of the Tajik people.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the pushing game and its player range.
**Answer: "Kiro tela dinem?" (Whom shall we push?), for 6–40 school-age players.**
