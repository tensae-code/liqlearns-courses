# Traditional Games of Tajikistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional games of Tajikistan in the book are…
a) The active games of the Tajik ancestors  b) Imported board games  c) Swimming games only
**Answer: a) The active games of the Tajik ancestors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Kiro tela dinem?" means…
a) Whom shall we push?  b) Score a goal  c) Hit the shuttlecock up
**Answer: a) Whom shall we push?**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pushing game is for…
a) 6–40 school-age players  b) 2 players only  c) Adults only
**Answer: a) 6–40 school-age players**
