# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Tajikistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the game

Say the pushing game's name aloud — "Kiro tela dinem?" — and its meaning, Whom shall we push?, and its player range from the book: 6–40 school-age players. Your partner checks each fact against the lesson.

## Games from before

Ask someone at home what active games were played when they were at school. Compare one answer with what this lesson says about games handed down from the ancestors — do not add rules the book does not give.
