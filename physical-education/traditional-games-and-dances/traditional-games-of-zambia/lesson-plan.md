# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Zambia

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Zambia
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Zambia: indigenous Zambian games of the learners' own communities.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Research and perform your own community's games** — the Zambia module: learners research and perform the indigenous Zambian traditional games of their own communities.
2. **The named games and why they matter** — the Zambia module: chain tag (chasing), Sheep-Sheep Come All (breakthrough), musical chairs (rhythmic), Who Am I? (knowledge) and manipulative/calculative games — and why traditional games matter.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Traditional Games of Ethiopia. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Zambia's module asks learners to do with traditional games: research them and perform them.
2. Name the chasing game: chain tag.
3. Name the breakthrough game: Sheep-Sheep Come All.
4. Match musical chairs to rhythmic games and Who Am I? to knowledge games.
5. Name the fifth family the module names: manipulative/calculative games.

## What you need
Nothing — the games are the learners' own communities' games, brought by the class as the module directs.

## If the learner is stuck
- The families blur → anchor each one to its game: chasing = chain tag, breakthrough = Sheep-Sheep Come All, rhythmic = musical chairs, knowledge = Who Am I?
- No game comes to mind → ask at home: what did the older people play as children? That is the research the module sets.
