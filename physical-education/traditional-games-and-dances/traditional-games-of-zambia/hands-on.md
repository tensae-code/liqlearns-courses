# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Zambia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Research one game

Ask at home or in your community: what game did people play here when they were children? Learn its name, who plays it, and how it starts. Bring it back to the class — that bringing-back is the research the Zambia module sets.

## Perform it

Teach the game you found to a small group and play it together. Afterwards, say which of the module's families it sits closest to: chasing, breakthrough, rhythmic, knowledge, or manipulative/calculative.
