# Video script — Traditional Games of Zambia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every community has its games. Zambia's learners go and find theirs.
Chain tag — chase. Sheep-Sheep Come All — break through.
Musical chairs — move to the rhythm. Who Am I? — know and guess.
Research them. Perform them. Keep them alive.
That is **traditional games of Zambia**.
