# Traditional Games of Zambia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zambia's module asks learners to do what with traditional games?
a) Research and perform the games of their own communities  b) Only read about foreign games  c) Stop playing them
**Answer: a) Research and perform the games of their own communities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Chain tag is the module's example of…
a) A chasing game  b) A knowledge game  c) A rhythmic game
**Answer: a) A chasing game**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sheep-Sheep Come All is…
a) A breakthrough game  b) A chasing game  c) A calculative game
**Answer: a) A breakthrough game**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who Am I? is…
a) A knowledge game  b) A rhythmic game  c) A breakthrough game
**Answer: a) A knowledge game**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Musical chairs is the module's example of…
a) A rhythmic game  b) A knowledge game  c) A chasing game
**Answer: a) A rhythmic game**
