# Traditional Games of Zambia — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose traditional games does the module send learners to research?
**Answer: The indigenous Zambian games of the learners' own communities.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the fifth game family the module names, besides chasing, breakthrough, rhythmic and knowledge.
**Answer: Manipulative/calculative games.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the rhythmic game the module names.
**Answer: Musical chairs.**
