# Traditional Games of Myanmar — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Htok-See-Toe — the line-crossing tag game
Pass mark: say how Myanmar's traditional tag game is played: the court zones, the runners who cross them and the taggers who stop them, by the game's full rules as the Grade 6 and Grade 7 books print them.

## Sub lesson 2 — "What Time Is It, Great Jasmine Flower?"
Pass mark: say how the Grade 2 game runs: the class asks the time and crosses the flower's ground, and when the call turns, the flower chases.

## Sub lesson 3 — White Rope, Black Rope
Pass mark: say how the Grade 2 rope game is played: two rope teams crouch in the lanes, and at the caller's word — white or black — one side chases the other home.
