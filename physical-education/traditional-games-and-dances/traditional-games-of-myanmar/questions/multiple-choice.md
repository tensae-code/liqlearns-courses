# Traditional Games of Myanmar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Htok-See-Toe, the two sides are…
a) Runners who cross the court zones and taggers who guard the lines  b) Two goalkeepers  c) A referee and a scorer
**Answer: a) Runners who cross the court zones and taggers who guard the lines**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which books teach Htok-See-Toe with its full rules?
a) Myanmar's Grade 6 and Grade 7 PE books  b) Only a Grade 1 book  c) No book — it is learned only outdoors
**Answer: a) Myanmar's Grade 6 and Grade 7 PE books**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "What Time Is It, Great Jasmine Flower?", the class crosses the flower's ground…
a) While the call is safe, and runs when the call turns and the flower chases  b) Only backwards  c) Sitting down
**Answer: a) While the call is safe, and runs when the call turns and the flower chases**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In White Rope, Black Rope, the chase starts when…
a) The caller calls the word — white or black  b) The teacher blows a whistle three times  c) A coin lands on heads
**Answer: a) The caller calls the word — white or black**
