# Traditional Games of Myanmar — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Htok-See-Toe is Myanmar's traditional line-crossing tag game, taught with its court zones and full rules.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Jasmine Flower game, the flower never chases anyone.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In White Rope, Black Rope, the two teams crouch in the lanes and wait for the caller's word.
**Answer: True**
