# Traditional Games of Myanmar — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the runners in Htok-See-Toe have to do?
**Answer: Cross the court's zones while the taggers guard the lines and try to stop them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What question does the class ask in the Jasmine Flower game?
**Answer: "What time is it, Great Jasmine Flower?"**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does White Rope, Black Rope start its chase?
**Answer: The caller calls white or black, and one side chases the other home.**
