# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Myanmar

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Myanmar

## Sub lessons

2. **"What Time Is It, Great Jasmine Flower?"**
3. **White Rope, Black Rope**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how Htok-See-Toe is set up and played: its court zones, its runners and its taggers.
2. Say how the Jasmine Flower game runs: the class asks the time, crosses the flower's ground, and runs when the call turns.
3. Say how White Rope, Black Rope is played: two rope teams crouch in the lanes, and at the caller's word one side chases the other home.

## What you need
Open play space with marked lines and lanes, as the games are played, not only read about.

## If the learner is stuck
- You cannot say how Htok-See-Toe works → start from its three parts as the books give them: the court zones, the runners who cross, and the taggers who guard the lines. → The Jasmine Flower call will not stick → say the game's own question out loud — "What time is it, Great Jasmine Flower?" — the crossing is safe until the call turns, and then the flower chases.
