# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Myanmar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cross the zones

Mark a court in zones the way the books describe Htok-See-Toe: runners on one side, taggers on the lines. Play a round — runners cross zone by zone, taggers guard their lines. Your partner names who crossed cleanly and who was tagged, and where.

## Ask the flower the time

Play the Jasmine Flower game in a group: ask "What time is it, Great Jasmine Flower?", cross the flower's ground on the safe calls, and run when the call turns and the flower chases. Then swap the flower and play again.

## White! Black!

Crouch in two rope teams in the lanes and play White Rope, Black Rope: at the caller's word, one side chases the other home. Your partner calls the words and checks that nobody moved before the call.
