# Video script — Traditional Games of Myanmar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every country hands its games down — Myanmar's books teach theirs by name.
Htok-See-Toe — runners cross the court's zones while the taggers guard the lines.
"What time is it, Great Jasmine Flower?" — cross while the call is safe, run when it turns.
White Rope, Black Rope — crouch in the lanes, and chase at the caller's word.
That is **traditional games of Myanmar**.
