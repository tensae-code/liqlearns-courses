## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 6 Traditional Games: Indian Games

Chapter 6 Traditional Games: Indian Games — the games of Fiji's Indian community from ancient India: Kancha (marbles), Kho Kho with its full rules and officials, Poshampa (hands-bridge song game), Pachisi with cowrie shells, Kith-Kith (hopscotch), DhopKhel (11-a-side ball court game) and Pallanguli (seed-and-pit board game)

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 3: Traditional Games of India

Chapter 3: Traditional Games of India — Bambaram (wooden spinning top, played as Bongaralu Aata in Telangana), Mallakhamb (ancient pole, hanging and rope gymnastics) and Gella-Chutt (Tripura's king-and-ghar team game)
