## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 6 Traditional Games: Indian Games
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: Chapter 6 Traditional Games: Indian Games — the games of Fiji's Indian community from ancient India: Kancha (marbles), Kho Kho with its full rules and officials, Poshampa (hands-bridge song game), Pachisi with cowrie shells, Kith-Kith (hopscotch), DhopKhel (11-a-side ball court game) and Pallanguli (seed-and-pit board game).
