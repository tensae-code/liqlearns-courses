# Traditional Games of the Philippines — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say what a player does in Tumbang Preso.
**Answer: The Filipino target game Tumbang Preso: knocking down the can and racing to retrieve your slipper while dodging the "IT", testing agility, accuracy, speed, honesty and self-discipline (with the Crazy Can lead-up game) (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 8, printed p. 28).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say what kadang are and how the race is won.
**Answer: Racing 50 metres on kadang (coconut shells with rope straps, used as stilts) in a team relay; the first team across without falling wins (Grade 2 MAPEH LM, Waray edition, Leksyon 8, printed p. 224).**
