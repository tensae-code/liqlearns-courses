# Traditional Games of the Philippines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Tumbang Preso, what does the player knock down?
a) A can  b) A hoop  c) A cone tower
**Answer: a) A can**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are kadang?
a) Coconut shells with rope straps, used as stilts  b) Wooden balls  c) Bamboo poles
**Answer: a) Coconut shells with rope straps, used as stilts**
