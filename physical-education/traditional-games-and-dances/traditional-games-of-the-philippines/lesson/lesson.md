# Traditional Games of the Philippines

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tumbang Preso

The Filipino target game Tumbang Preso: knocking down the can and racing to retrieve your slipper while dodging the "IT", testing agility, accuracy, speed, honesty and self-discipline (with the Crazy Can lead-up game) (Grade 3 MAPEH LM Unit 3, Lesson 8, printed p. 28).

## Sub lesson 2 — Kadang-kadang — the stilt race

Racing 50 metres on kadang (coconut shells with rope straps, used as stilts) in a team relay; the first team across without falling wins (Grade 2 MAPEH LM, Waray edition, Leksyon 8, printed p. 224).
