# Wushu and Taijiquan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Xingshen Quan form is in which style?
a) Changquan  b) Taijiquan  c) It is a ball game, not a form
**Answer: a) Changquan**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Taijiquan beginner form in the book is:
a) The Eight Methods and Five Steps  b) The Xingshen Quan form  c) A racing form
**Answer: a) The Eight Methods and Five Steps**
