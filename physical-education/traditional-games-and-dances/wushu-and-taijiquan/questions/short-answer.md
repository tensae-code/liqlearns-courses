# Wushu and Taijiquan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two parts of the Xingshen Quan form does the book name?
**Answer: Its hand forms and its stances.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the Taijiquan form for beginners called in the book?
**Answer: The Eight Methods and Five Steps.**
