# Lesson plan — Wushu and Taijiquan

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Wushu and Taijiquan
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Wushu and Taijiquan: Chinese martial arts — the Xingshen Quan form and the Taijiquan Eight Methods and Five Steps. — taught here through China's way: its PEP Senior-High PE & Health compulsory volume.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Wushu and the Xingshen Quan form** — China's Senior High book teaches what wushu is, and the Xingshen Quan form in the Changquan style, with its hand forms and stances.
2. **Taijiquan — Eight Methods and Five Steps** — The book teaches Taijiquan: the Eight Methods and Five Steps beginner form.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the book teaches in wushu and the xingshen quan form, as filed.
2. Say what the book teaches in taijiquan — eight methods and five steps, as filed.
