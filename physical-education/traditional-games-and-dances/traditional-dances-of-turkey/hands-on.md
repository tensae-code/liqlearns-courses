# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Turkey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Features, then steps

Choose one dance from the lesson. First say its features and traditional dress, as the books teach them; then show its first steps. Your partner names the dance from your steps alone.

## Spoon rhythm

Take two wooden spoons the way the Kaşık dances use them and click a steady rhythm while stepping in place, as the Grade 2 book's spoon dances do. Your partner keeps the beat honest.
