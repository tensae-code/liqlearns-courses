# Traditional Dances of Turkey — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two things the Horon theme teaches before the steps are performed.
**Answer: Its general features and its traditional dress.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two things do the books teach about folk dances overall?
**Answer: Their types (with traditional dress) and the basic movements inside them.**
