# Traditional Dances of Turkey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The folk-dance teaching in the books covers the types of dances together with…
a) Their traditional dress  b) Their ticket prices  c) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Their traditional dress**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Horon is the folk dance of which region?
a) The Black Sea region  b) The Aegean  c) Thrace
**Answer: a) The Black Sea region**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 1 book teaches which folk dance?
a) Halay  b) Zeybek  c) Karşılama
**Answer: a) Halay**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Zeybek, the grade 7 dance, is described as…
a) The slow, proud Aegean dance  b) A fast Black Sea dance  c) A spoon dance
**Answer: a) The slow, proud Aegean dance**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In the Kaşık dances, the dancers click…
a) Wooden spoons  b) Castanets  c) Drumsticks on a drum
**Answer: a) Wooden spoons**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Karşılama is the dance-game tradition of…
a) Thrace  b) The Black Sea  c) The Aegean
**Answer: a) Thrace**
