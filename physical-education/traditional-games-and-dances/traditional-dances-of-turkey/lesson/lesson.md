# Traditional Dances of Turkey

## Do it first

Clap a steady beat and step in place to it. Every folk dance in this lesson is built on a beat a whole village can hold — now read how Turkey's books teach theirs, dance by dance.

## Sub lesson 1 — Turkish folk dances — types and basic movements

The books first teach **the types of Turkish folk dances, with their traditional dress** (Grade 3), and **the basic movements inside folk dances** (Grades 6 and 7).

## Sub lesson 2 — Horon — the Black Sea dance

**Horon**, the folk dance of the Black Sea region, is a whole theme at grades 5 and 6: its **general features and traditional dress**, then **the Horon steps**, learned and performed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Halay

The Grade 1 book already teaches a folk dance: **Halay Öğreniyorum — learning the Halay**: its definition and performing the Halay steps.

## Sub lesson 4 — Zeybek — the Aegean dance

By grade 7 the folk-dance theme is **Zeybek, the slow, proud Aegean dance**: its general features and traditional dress, then the Zeybek steps.

## Sub lesson 5 — Kaşık — the spoon dances

Grade 2 closes with the **Kaşık (spoon) dances**: children dance **clicking wooden spoons** — what the dances are, their traditional dress, and performing the steps.

## Sub lesson 6 — Karşılama — dance-games of Thrace

The Grade 3 book closes with **Karşılama Oyunları — the Karşılama dance-games of Thrace**, getting to know them after the folk-dance types.
