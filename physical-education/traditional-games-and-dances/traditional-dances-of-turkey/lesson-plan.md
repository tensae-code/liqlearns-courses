# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Turkey

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Turkey
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional dances of Turkey: the folk dances — Horon, Halay, Zeybek, Kaşık spoon dances and Karşılama — their features, dress and steps.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Turkey's folk dances the books teach: Horon, Halay, Zeybek, Kaşık and Karşılama.
2. Say each dance's home or mark: Horon the Black Sea, Zeybek the Aegean, Karşılama Thrace, Kaşık the clicking spoons.
3. Say the order the books use: features and dress first, then the steps performed.

## What you need
Open floor space and music, as the dances are danced; two wooden spoons for the Kaşık steps, as the Grade 2 book dances them.

## If the learner is stuck
- The dances blur together → anchor each to its mark: Horon–Black Sea, Zeybek–Aegean, Karşılama–Thrace, Kaşık–spoons, Halay–grade 1's first dance. → You know the name but not the order → the books always teach features and dress before steps; say the features first.
