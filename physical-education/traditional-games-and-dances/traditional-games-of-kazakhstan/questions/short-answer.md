# Traditional Games of Kazakhstan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the eight outdoor games the guide lists.
**Answer: "Keep the Ball in the Air", "Pioneerball", "Hunters and Ducks", "Above the Ground", "Tag with Ribbons", "Defence of the Fortifications", "Ball to the Captain" and "Don't Give the Ball to the Leader".**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Russian name for the outdoor games in the guide?
**Answer: Подвижные игры.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Kazakhstan's guide organise its PE year?
**Answer: By quarterly plan-graphs (план-график), with games in every quarter.**
