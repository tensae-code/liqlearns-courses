# Traditional Games of Kazakhstan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is one of Kazakhstan's named outdoor games?
a) Hunters and Ducks  b) Chess  c) Basketball
**Answer: a) Hunters and Ducks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's word for these outdoor games is…
a) Подвижные игры  b) ОРУ  c) План-график
**Answer: a) Подвижные игры**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The named outdoor games appear in the guide's year…
a) In every quarter  b) In one week only  c) Never — they are not in the plans
**Answer: a) In every quarter**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a ball game in Kazakhstan's named set?
a) Ball to the Captain  b) Tag with Ribbons  c) Above the Ground
**Answer: a) Ball to the Captain**
