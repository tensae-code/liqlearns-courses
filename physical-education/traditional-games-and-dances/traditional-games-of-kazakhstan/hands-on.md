# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name and sort the set

Say the eight names aloud — "Keep the Ball in the Air", "Pioneerball", "Hunters and Ducks", "Above the Ground", "Tag with Ribbons", "Defence of the Fortifications", "Ball to the Captain" and "Don't Give the Ball to the Leader". Sort them the way they play: which are ball games, which are chasing games, which defend a place? The guide names the set; sorting it is your own work.

## Keep the Ball in the Air

Play the set's first named game the way its name directs: in a circle, keep a ball in the air together for as long as you can without it touching the ground. Count the touches out loud. The guide names the game; the playing lives with the players.
