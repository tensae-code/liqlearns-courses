# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Kazakhstan

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Kazakhstan
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Kazakhstan: the named Kazakh outdoor games played through the school year.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Kazakhstan's named outdoor games** — the Kazakhstan Grade 4 guide: the named outdoor games (подвижные игры) played in every quarter of the school year — "Keep the Ball in the Air", "Pioneerball", "Hunters and Ducks", "Above the Ground", "Tag with Ribbons", "Defence of the Fortifications", "Ball to the Captain" and "Don't Give the Ball to the Leader".

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are; Traditional Games of Ethiopia. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Kazakhstan's named outdoor games as its Grade 4 guide lists them: "Keep the Ball in the Air", "Pioneerball", "Hunters and Ducks", "Above the Ground", "Tag with Ribbons", "Defence of the Fortifications", "Ball to the Captain" and "Don't Give the Ball to the Leader".
2. Say where these games sit in Kazakhstan's school year: outdoor games (подвижные игры) run in every quarter, alongside the quarter's lead strand.
3. Say what kind of book names them: a teacher guide — the games are filed here as evidence of Kazakhstan's own way, exactly as the guide's plan-graphs name them.

## What you need
Open ground and a ball for the ball games in the set, as the guide's own lessons provide them; the games are played the way the class's teacher leads them — this lesson names the set, it does not invent rules the guide's excerpt does not print.
