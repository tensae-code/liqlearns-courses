# Traditional Games of Kazakhstan

## Do it first

Every country keeps games its children play outside, year after year. Name one from your own place. Then read on and meet Kazakhstan's own named set.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan's named outdoor games

Kazakhstan's Grade 4 *Physical Culture* guide runs its year from quarterly plan-graphs, and in **every quarter** the plan names its **outdoor games** (подвижные игры). The named set is: **"Keep the Ball in the Air"**, **"Pioneerball"**, **"Hunters and Ducks"**, **"Above the Ground"**, **"Tag with Ribbons"**, **"Defence of the Fortifications"**, **"Ball to the Captain"** and **"Don't Give the Ball to the Leader"**.

These are Kazakhstan's own way in this course: the games sit inside the school year itself — beside the athletics, gymnastics and ski quarters — named in the guide's plans as content in their own right. The games are taught here only as the guide's excerpt names them; no rules have been invented for any game the excerpt leaves with its players.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the eight names, and the one fact that holds them together: they are the outdoor games (подвижные игры) named in every quarter of Kazakhstan's Grade 4 year.
