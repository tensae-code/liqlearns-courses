# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Cuba

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play a rhythmic game together

In a small group, play a rhythmic game: move and play to a steady beat together, the way Cuba's Grade 2 programme frames its rhythmic and traditional games — as the vehicle for expression and group norms.

## Organise it like the teacher book

Give your game a name, say how it is organised, and agree its rules before you play — the teacher book works its named games out with organisation and rules.
