# Traditional Games of Cuba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cuba's Grade 2 programme places rhythmic and traditional games inside…
a) The Rhythmic Activities unit  b) The athletics unit  c) The swimming unit
**Answer: a) The Rhythmic Activities unit**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the programme, the games are the vehicle for…
a) Expression and group norms  b) Winning prizes  c) Individual records
**Answer: a) Expression and group norms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 2 teacher book gives the games with…
a) Organisation and rules  b) Only pictures  c) No names
**Answer: a) Organisation and rules**
