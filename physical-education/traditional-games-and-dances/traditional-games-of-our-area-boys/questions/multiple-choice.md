# Traditional Games of Our Area (Boys) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the task of this lesson?
a) Bring the boys' games of your area, play them, explain which body part each works most  b) Memorise a named game from the book  c) Only watch games
**Answer: a) Bring the boys' games of your area, play them, explain which body part each works most**

**2. ** Where do the boys' games of this lesson come from?
a) The pupils' own area, brought by the class  b) A named list in the book  c) A shop
**Answer: a) The pupils' own area, brought by the class**

**3. ** What four things does the presentation frame ask for?
a) Name, home area, rules, demonstration  b) Only the name  c) A drawing only
**Answer: a) Name, home area, rules, demonstration**
