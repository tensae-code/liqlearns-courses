The Grade 1 student textbook is complete (see the book line above); nothing in this lesson rests on a partial scan of it.

The books deliberately leave these dances and games to the pupils' own locality: the placements record only the names actually printed and the task the book sets (bring the dance or game of your area, perform it, say which body part it works most). This lesson keeps that frame exactly — it never invents steps or rules the placements do not give.

This lesson rests on a task-only placement: §6.2.2 is a task with no pictured or named game, exactly as the placement records.
