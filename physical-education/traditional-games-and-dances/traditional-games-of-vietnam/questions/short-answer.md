# Traditional Games of Vietnam — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three traditional Vietnamese games taught in this lesson.
**Answer: Any three of: Mèo và chim sẻ (cat and sparrows), Bịt mắt bắt dê (blindfold catching the goat), Dung dăng dung dẻ (the singing circle game), Kéo lưới bắt cá (dragnet fishing), Mèo đuổi chuột (cat chases mouse), Bỏ khăn (drop the handkerchief), Kéo cưa lừa xẻ (the sawing-song game), Chọi gà (cockfighting), Lộn cầu vồng (rainbow somersault), Đếm sao (counting stars).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Bịt mắt bắt dê, how does the blindfolded player find the "goat"?
**Answer: Guided by classmates' directions.**
