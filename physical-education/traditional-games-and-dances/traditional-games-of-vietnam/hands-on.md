# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Vietnam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play the blindfold game

Play Bịt mắt bắt dê: one player is blindfolded and tries to catch the "goat", guided by classmates' directions, as the Grade 1 and 2 books teach it.

## Play a circle game

Form a circle and play Bỏ khăn, drop-the-handkerchief, or Dung dăng dung dẻ, the singing circle game, as the books teach them.
