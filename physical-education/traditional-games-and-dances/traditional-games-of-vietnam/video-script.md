# Video script — Traditional Games of Vietnam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Traditional games of Vietnam — the games Vietnamese children play, taught in the Grade 1 to 3 books.
Chase games, circle games and singing games — the cat and the sparrows, the blindfold goat game, the dragnet in the pool.
Every game is played the way the books teach it.
Those are the **traditional games of Vietnam**.
