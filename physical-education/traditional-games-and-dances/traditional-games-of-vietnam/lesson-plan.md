# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Vietnam

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Vietnam
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Vietnam: the chase and playground games Vietnamese children play.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show: say and show the cat-and-sparrows chase game (also the warm-up game in Chủ đề 3 Bài 3).
2. Say and show: say and show blindfold catching the "goat", guided by classmates' directions.
3. Say and show: say and show the traditional singing circle game (repeated as warm-up in Bài 5).
4. Say and show: say and show dragnet fishing: the "net" of children catches the "fish" in the pool (repeated p. 90).
5. Say and show: say and show blindfold catching the "goat".
6. Say and show: say and show cat chases mouse through the circle of joined hands.
7. Say and show: say and show drop-the-handkerchief circle chase game.
8. Say and show: say and show the traditional sawing-song partner game.
9. Say and show: say and show cockfighting: hopping on one leg, arms crossed, bumping opponents out.
10. Say and show: say and show rainbow somersault: partners grip hands and flip over in turn.
11. Say and show: say and show cat chases mouse.
12. Say and show: say and show the sawing-song game played sitting in the pool.
13. Say and show: say and show counting stars: the traditional singing circle game ("một ông sao sáng…").

## What you need
An open space to run and form circles in, and a blindfold for the blindfold games, as the games are played, not only read about.
