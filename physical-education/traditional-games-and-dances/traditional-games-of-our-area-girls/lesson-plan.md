# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Our Area (Girls)

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Our Area (Girls)
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: … → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the girls' traditional games of the pupils' own local area — the book names none and pictures none: the class brings them, plays them, and explains which body part each works most.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the games lessons of Games to Play. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the dances lessons of this title, its neighbours. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Bring a girls' traditional game of their area to class.
2. Play it with classmates by its own rules.
3. Explain which body part the game works most.

## What you need
Whatever your area's games use — stones, a rope, a scarf — brought by the players. Nothing bought.

## If the learner is stuck
- No game is known → the search goes home: grandmothers, mothers, older sisters are the book's library for this lesson.
- Two areas in one class → both games are played; the lesson's frame holds every area's game equally.
