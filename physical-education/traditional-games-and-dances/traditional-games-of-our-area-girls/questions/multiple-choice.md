# Traditional Games of Our Area (Girls) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does the book ask pupils to do in this lesson?
a) Bring the girls' games of their area, play them, and explain which body part each works most  b) Learn a game named in the book  c) Write an essay about games
**Answer: a) Bring the girls' games of their area, play them, and explain which body part each works most**

**2. ** Why does the book name no game here?
a) The games are deliberately the pupils' own locality's  b) The page was lost  c) There are no girls' games
**Answer: a) The games are deliberately the pupils' own locality's**

**3. ** Who holds the games this lesson studies?
a) The pupils' own area — its players and elders  b) The book's pictures  c) Another country
**Answer: a) The pupils' own area — its players and elders**
