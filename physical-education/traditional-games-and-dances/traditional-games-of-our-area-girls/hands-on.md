# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Our Area (Girls)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The home interview

Interview an elder about a girls' game of your area: name, players, rules, the body part it works. Record it in writing — your class is building the record the book left open.

## Present and play

Present your game with the script frame — name, home, rules, demonstration — then play a full round with the class.
