# Traditional Dances of Honduras

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Bloque I Secuencia 3 "Las danzas que nos identifican"

Bloque I Secuencia 3 "Las danzas que nos identifican" — Honduran folk dance: the Xique base step, the official classification of Honduran folk dances, and dancing the Guapango del Río (steps, displacements and holds), with Polca la Rosa and Guapango Chorotega named

## Sub lesson 2 — Bloque I Secuencia 3

Bloque I Secuencia 3 — the step families of Honduran folk dance: the Xique (punteado, rascado, zapateado, cruzado), polcas and valses, and how Honduran folk dances are classified

## Sub lesson 3 — Bloque I Secuencia 3

Bloque I Secuencia 3 — the official classification of Honduran folk dances with named examples of each class: indigenous (Los Guancascos, Moros y Cristianos), indigenous-root (La Cadena, La Tusa), criolla-root (Polca la Rosa, Guapango Chorotega), criolla imitation (La Galopa, El Zopilote), colonial (La Varsoviana, La Mazurca) and Garífuna (El Dugú, Yancunú, Júngu Júngu)

## Sub lesson 4 — Bloque I Secuencia 2

Bloque I Secuencia 2 — the dances and songs of the Garífuna people: La Punta (danced at wakes and in celebration), La Parranda (the serenade), Guaraní, the women's Abeimajani and the men's Aramajani, and the Dugú and Chugú ancestor rites
