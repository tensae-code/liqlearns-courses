# Traditional Dances of Honduras — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Honduran folk dance: the Xique base step, the official classification of Honduran folk dances, and dancing the Guapango del Río (steps, displacements and holds), with Polca la Rosa and Guapango Chorotega named.
**Answer: True**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The step families of Honduran folk dance: the Xique (punteado, rascado, zapateado, cruzado), polcas and valses, and how Honduran folk dances are classified.
**Answer: True**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The official classification of Honduran folk dances with named examples of each class: indigenous (Los Guancascos, Moros y Cristianos), indigenous-root (La Cadena, La Tusa), criolla-root (Polca la Rosa, Guapango Chorotega), criolla imitation (La Galopa, El Zopilote), colonial (La Varsoviana, La Mazurca) and Garífuna (El Dugú, Yancunú, Júngu Júngu).
**Answer: True**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The dances and songs of the Garífuna people: La Punta (danced at wakes and in celebration), La Parranda (the serenade), Guaraní, the women's Abeimajani and the men's Aramajani, and the Dugú and Chugú ancestor rites.
**Answer: True**
