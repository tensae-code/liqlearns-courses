# Traditional Dances of Honduras — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bloque I Secuencia 3 "Las danzas que nos identifican"
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: Honduran folk dance: the Xique base step, the official classification of Honduran folk dances, and dancing the Guapango del Río (steps, displacements and holds), with Polca la Rosa and Guapango Chorotega named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bloque I Secuencia 3
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: the step families of Honduran folk dance: the Xique (punteado, rascado, zapateado, cruzado), polcas and valses, and how Honduran folk dances are classified.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bloque I Secuencia 3
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: the official classification of Honduran folk dances with named examples of each class: indigenous (Los Guancascos, Moros y Cristianos), indigenous-root (La Cadena, La Tusa), criolla-root (Polca la Rosa, Guapango Chorotega), criolla imitation (La Galopa, El Zopilote), colonial (La Varsoviana, La Mazurca) and Garífuna (El Dugú, Yancunú, Júngu Júngu).

## Sub lesson 4 — Bloque I Secuencia 2
Pass mark: Say what this part teaches: the dances and songs of the Garífuna people: La Punta (danced at wakes and in celebration), La Parranda (the serenade), Guaraní, the women's Abeimajani and the men's Aramajani, and the Dugú and Chugú ancestor rites.
