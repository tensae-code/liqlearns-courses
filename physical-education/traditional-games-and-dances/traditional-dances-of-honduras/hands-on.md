# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Honduras

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dance a Honduran folk dance

With your class or family, learn the Xique base step the way Honduras's books teach it — punteado, rascado, zapateado and cruzado — and dance a short piece of the Guapango, with its steps, displacements and holds, naming which class of Honduran folk dance it belongs to.
