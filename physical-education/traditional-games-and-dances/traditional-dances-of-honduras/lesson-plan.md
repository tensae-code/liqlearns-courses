# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Honduras

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Honduras
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional dances of Honduras: Honduran folk dance — the Xique and Guapango, the Garífuna dances Punta and Parranda, and how the dances are classified. — taught here through Honduras's way: its Telebásica books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Bloque I Secuencia 3 "Las danzas que nos identifican"** — Bloque I Secuencia 3 "Las danzas que nos identifican" — Honduran folk dance: the Xique base step, the official classification of Honduran folk dances, and dancing the Guapango del Río (steps, displacements and holds), with Polca la Rosa and Guapango Chorotega named
2. **Bloque I Secuencia 3** — Bloque I Secuencia 3 — the step families of Honduran folk dance: the Xique (punteado, rascado, zapateado, cruzado), polcas and valses, and how Honduran folk dances are classified
3. **Bloque I Secuencia 3** — Bloque I Secuencia 3 — the official classification of Honduran folk dances with named examples of each class: indigenous (Los Guancascos, Moros y Cristianos), indigenous-root (La Cadena, La Tusa), criolla-root (Polca la Rosa, Guapango Chorotega), criolla imitation (La Galopa, El Zopilote), colonial (La Varsoviana, La Mazurca) and Garífuna (El Dugú, Yancunú, Júngu Júngu)
4. **Bloque I Secuencia 2** — Bloque I Secuencia 2 — the dances and songs of the Garífuna people: La Punta (danced at wakes and in celebration), La Parranda (the serenade), Guaraní, the women's Abeimajani and the men's Aramajani, and the Dugú and Chugú ancestor rites
