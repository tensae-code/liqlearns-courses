# Buzkashi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do the chapandaz fight to lift in buzkashi?
a) A goat or calf carcass  b) A ball  c) A flag
**Answer: a) A goat or calf carcass**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a point won in buzkashi?
a) By carrying the carcass past a flag and dropping it in the scoring circle  b) By throwing the carcass over a net  c) By riding the fastest lap
**Answer: a) By carrying the carcass past a flag and dropping it in the scoring circle**
