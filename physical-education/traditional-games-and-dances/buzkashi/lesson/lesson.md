## Sub lesson 1 — Buzkashi, Afghanistan's national game — the riders and the carcass

Buzkashi is Afghanistan's national game. Mounted riders, called chapandaz, fight to lift a goat or calf carcass — that contest to lift and hold the carcass is the heart of the game, as the Grade 7 guide teaches it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The field, the teams and how a point is won

The riders carry the carcass past a flag and drop it in the scoring circle — that is how a point is won. The guide sets out the field and the teams the game is played with.
