# Traditional Dances of Malaysia

## Sub lesson 1 — Pergerakan Berirama

Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Joget, a Malay traditional dance: its locomotor and non-locomotor skills and movement concepts, performed to music — Malaysia Grade 7 PJPK Tingkatan 1, printed p. 12.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pergerakan Berirama

Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Sumazau, traditional dance of the Kadazan-Dusun people of Sabah: its steps and movement concepts, compared with the Joget — Malaysia Grade 7 PJPK Tingkatan 1, printed p. 12.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pergerakan Berirama

Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Inang, a Malay traditional dance danced in male-female pairs: on-the-spot steps, forward-and-back steps, ragam "C" (sudi), ragam sudi and ragam "L" patterns at slow (2×4) and fast (4×4) masri tempo, assembled into a full dance sequence — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 26.

## Sub lesson 4 — Pergerakan Berirama

Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Zapin, a Malay traditional dance of Johor — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 14.

## Sub lesson 5 — Pergerakan Berirama

Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Ngajat, traditional dance of the Iban people of Sarawak — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 17.

## Sub lesson 6 — Tarian Sewang

Unit 1 Tarian Sewang — the Sewang, traditional dance of Malaysia's Orang Asli (indigenous) people: its ragam steps classified by locomotor/non-locomotor skills and movement concepts, and its aesthetic values — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 4.

## Sub lesson 7 — Tarian Singa

Unit 1 Tarian Singa — the lion dance of Malaysia's Chinese community: its movements, the teamwork and communication the two dancers need, and creating a themed lion-dance performance — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 11.

## Sub lesson 8 — Tarian Bhangra

Unit 1 Tarian Bhangra — the Bhangra, traditional dance of Malaysia's Punjabi community, performed to music — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 4.

## Sub lesson 9 — Tarian Farapeirra

Unit 1 Tarian Farapeirra — the Farapeirra, traditional dance of Melaka's Portuguese (Kristang) community — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 7.
