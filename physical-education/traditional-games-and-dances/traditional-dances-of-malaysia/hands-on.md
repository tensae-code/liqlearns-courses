# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Malaysia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise traditional dances of malaysia

Practise the skills in this lesson slowly and safely, in a clear space, the way Malaysia's books teach them — traditional dances of Malaysia: the Joget, Inang, Zapin, Sumazau, Ngajat, Sewang, lion dance, Bhangra and Farapeirra.
