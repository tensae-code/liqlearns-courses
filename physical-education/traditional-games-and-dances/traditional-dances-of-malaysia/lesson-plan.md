# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Malaysia

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Malaysia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional dances of Malaysia: the Joget, Inang, Zapin, Sumazau, Ngajat, Sewang, lion dance, Bhangra and Farapeirra. — taught here through Malaysia's way: its KSSM PJPK books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Pergerakan Berirama** — Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Joget, a Malay traditional dance: its locomotor and non-locomotor skills and movement concepts, performed to music — Malaysia Grade 7 PJPK Tingkatan 1, printed p. 12.
2. **Pergerakan Berirama** — Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Sumazau, traditional dance of the Kadazan-Dusun people of Sabah: its steps and movement concepts, compared with the Joget — Malaysia Grade 7 PJPK Tingkatan 1, printed p. 12.
3. **Pergerakan Berirama** — Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Inang, a Malay traditional dance danced in male-female pairs: on-the-spot steps, forward-and-back steps, ragam "C" (sudi), ragam sudi and ragam "L" patterns at slow (2×4) and fast (4×4) masri tempo, assembled into a full dance sequence — Malaysia Grade 8 PJPK Tingkatan 2, printed p. 26.
4. **Pergerakan Berirama** — Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Zapin, a Malay traditional dance of Johor — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 14.
5. **Pergerakan Berirama** — Unit 2 Pergerakan Berirama — the Ngajat, traditional dance of the Iban people of Sarawak — Malaysia Grade 9 PJPK Tingkatan 3, printed p. 17.
6. **Tarian Sewang** — Unit 1 Tarian Sewang — the Sewang, traditional dance of Malaysia's Orang Asli (indigenous) people: its ragam steps classified by locomotor/non-locomotor skills and movement concepts, and its aesthetic values — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 4.
7. **Tarian Singa** — Unit 1 Tarian Singa — the lion dance of Malaysia's Chinese community: its movements, the teamwork and communication the two dancers need, and creating a themed lion-dance performance — Malaysia Grade 10 PJPK Tingkatan 4, printed p. 11.
8. **Tarian Bhangra** — Unit 1 Tarian Bhangra — the Bhangra, traditional dance of Malaysia's Punjabi community, performed to music — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 4.
9. **Tarian Farapeirra** — Unit 1 Tarian Farapeirra — the Farapeirra, traditional dance of Melaka's Portuguese (Kristang) community — Malaysia Grade 11 PJPK Tingkatan 5, printed p. 7.
