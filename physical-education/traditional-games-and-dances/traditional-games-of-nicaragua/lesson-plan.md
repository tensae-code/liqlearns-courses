# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Nicaragua

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Nicaragua
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Traditional games of Nicaragua: Nicaragua's traditional games and rounds (rondas), with their rules. — taught here through Nicaragua's way: its MINED manuals and books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Nicaragua's traditional games — El Pañuelo, Macho parado and the sack race** — Unidad IV, Juegos Tradicionales — Nicaragua's own traditional games with full rules: El Pañuelo/la pañoleta, Macho parado, Bola peleada, Salto la cuerda, Encostalados (sack race), Quita y ponga
2. **Traditional games and rounds (rondas)** — Unidad 4, Juegos tradicionales y rondas — traditional games and rounds (rondas) of Nicaragua
3. **Classified traditional games — stilt races and the ribbon race** — Unidad Juegos Tradicionales — traditional and popular games classified (strength games: lifting and dragging/traction; throwing games: distance and precision), stilt races (carreras de zancos — speed, relay, obstacle), ribbon race (carrera de cintas)
4. **El Bolero and the recreational traditional games** — Unidad V, Juegos Infantiles Recreativos: Juegos tradicionales a practicar — El Bolero (wooden cup-and-ball toy, eye-hand coordination, individual and competition forms), El pañuelo, La carrera de sacos

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
