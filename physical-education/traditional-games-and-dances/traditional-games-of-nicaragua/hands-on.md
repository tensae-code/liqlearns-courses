# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Nicaragua

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play a Nicaraguan traditional game

With your class or family, play one of Nicaragua's traditional games the way the books teach it — the Encostalados sack race or El Pañuelo — first hearing the rules, then playing, the way the Grade 2 manual teaches a traditional game: explain, set the rules, demonstrate, play, praise.
