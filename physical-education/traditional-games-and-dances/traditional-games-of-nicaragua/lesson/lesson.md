# Traditional Games of Nicaragua

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Nicaragua's traditional games — El Pañuelo, Macho parado and the sack race

Unidad IV, Juegos Tradicionales — Nicaragua's own traditional games with full rules: El Pañuelo/la pañoleta, Macho parado, Bola peleada, Salto la cuerda, Encostalados (sack race), Quita y ponga

## Sub lesson 2 — Traditional games and rounds (rondas)

Unidad 4, Juegos tradicionales y rondas — traditional games and rounds (rondas) of Nicaragua

## Sub lesson 3 — Classified traditional games — stilt races and the ribbon race

Unidad Juegos Tradicionales — traditional and popular games classified (strength games: lifting and dragging/traction; throwing games: distance and precision), stilt races (carreras de zancos — speed, relay, obstacle), ribbon race (carrera de cintas)

## Sub lesson 4 — El Bolero and the recreational traditional games

Unidad V, Juegos Infantiles Recreativos: Juegos tradicionales a practicar — El Bolero (wooden cup-and-ball toy, eye-hand coordination, individual and competition forms), El pañuelo, La carrera de sacos
