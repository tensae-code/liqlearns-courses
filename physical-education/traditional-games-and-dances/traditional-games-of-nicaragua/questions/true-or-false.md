# Traditional Games of Nicaragua — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Pañuelo and the Encostalados sack race are named traditional games of Nicaragua in the Grade 2 manual.
**Answer: True**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nicaragua's Grade 3 manual teaches traditional games together with rounds (rondas).
**Answer: True**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nicaragua's Grade 5 book classifies traditional games into strength games and throwing games, and teaches stilt races the children build themselves.
**Answer: True**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** El Bolero, the wooden cup-and-ball toy, is played in individual and competition forms in Nicaragua's Grade 6 book.
**Answer: True**
