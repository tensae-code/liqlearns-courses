# Traditional Games of Nicaragua — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Unidad IV, Juegos Tradicionales — Nicaragua's own traditional games with full rules: El Pañuelo/la pañoleta, Macho parado, Bola peleada, Salto la cuerda, Encostalados (sack race), Quita y ponga**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Unidad 4, Juegos tradicionales y rondas — traditional games and rounds (rondas) of Nicaragua**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Unidad Juegos Tradicionales — traditional and popular games classified (strength games: lifting and dragging/traction; throwing games: distance and precision), stilt races (carreras de zancos — speed, relay, obstacle), ribbon race (carrera de cintas)**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Say what this part teaches.
**Answer: Unidad V, Juegos Infantiles Recreativos: Juegos tradicionales a practicar — El Bolero (wooden cup-and-ball toy, eye-hand coordination, individual and competition forms), El pañuelo, La carrera de sacos**
