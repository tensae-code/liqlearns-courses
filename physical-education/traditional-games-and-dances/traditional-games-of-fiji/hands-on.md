# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Fiji

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise it the Fiji way

Work through this lesson's parts in order, doing each one as Fiji's *PE is Fun* books teach it: Chapter 5 Traditional Games: team and chasing games; Chapter 5 Traditional Games: target games; Chapter 8 Traditional Games: field and reed games; Chapter 8 Traditional Games: lemon games; Chapter 6 Traditional Games: I-Taukei Games; Chapter 3: Traditional Games; Chapter 3: Traditional Fijian Games. Practise each part slowly first, then at full speed, and teach one part to a partner.
