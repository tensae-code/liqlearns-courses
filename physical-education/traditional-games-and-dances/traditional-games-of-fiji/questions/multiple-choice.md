# Traditional Games of Fiji — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chapter 5 Traditional Games: team and chasing games — Veibuka (beach goal-touch game, "seva" in Lau), Veisole (hidden carried player guessed by touch), Veileveleveti (tree-climbing leg-pull game), Veitara (tag with a "tibi" safe sign) and Veisabiliga (hand-slap chase game)
a) True as the books teach it  b) A swimming stroke  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**

**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**
