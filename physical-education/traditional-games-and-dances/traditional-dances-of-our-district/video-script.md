# Video script — Traditional Dances of Our District

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ring widens! From our area — to our **district**! ወረዳ
Neck! Shoulder! Waist! Arm! Leg! The whole body dances here.
A stick — ዘንግ. The props have names. The dances have homes.
At the threshing floor, the work begins — and the work becomes praise.
**Fukera!** ፉከራ — the shoulders carry the praise!
Your district dances. Learn its names.
