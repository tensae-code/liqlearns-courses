# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Our District

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thresh and praise

With sheaves, sticks, or empty hands, work a threshing rhythm together — then let the shoulders turn the work rhythm into the fukera's praise movement.

## District map of dances

On paper, draw your area inside your district. Mark each dance your class has collected with its village. The survey, made visible.
