# Traditional Dances of Our District — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Grade 3, the dance survey widens from our area to what?
a) The district (ወረዳ)  b) Another country  c) The whole world
**Answer: a) The district (ወረዳ)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which of these does the book name as a dance prop?
a) ዘንግ — a stick  b) A telephone  c) A bicycle
**Answer: a) ዘንግ — a stick**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What work does the pictured fukera accompany?
a) Threshing grain  b) Building a house  c) Fishing
**Answer: a) Threshing grain**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The fukera in the book is whose dance?
a) A men's work praise-dance  b) A girls' wedding dance  c) A children's game
**Answer: a) A men's work praise-dance**
