## Sub lesson 1 — El Salvador's school games

The annexes of El Salvador's Grades 1–3 guide (Anexos 3–5, *Juegos lúdicos para Primer, Segundo y Tercer Grado*) hold a **bank of Salvadoran school games for each grade**. The guide names them: **Pañuelo de colores**, **Te toco… te sigo**, **Ladrón librado en parejas**, **Pelota por el túnel**, **Relevos locos**, **Pelota al espacio** — and the per-grade banks around them. These are El Salvador's own way: the games its own children play, collected by its own ministry guide as content. The games are taught as the guide gives them; no rules have been invented for any game the placements leave game-dependent.
