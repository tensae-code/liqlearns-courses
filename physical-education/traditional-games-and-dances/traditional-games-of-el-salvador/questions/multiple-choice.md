# Traditional Games of El Salvador — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson holds…
a) El Salvador's own school games  b) Only Olympic sports  c) Only board games
**Answer: a) El Salvador's own school games**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The game banks sit in the guide's…
a) Anexos 3–5, one bank per grade  b) First chapter  c) Cover page
**Answer: a) Anexos 3–5, one bank per grade**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a named Salvadoran school game in the guide?
a) Pañuelo de colores  b) Chess  c) Cricket
**Answer: a) Pañuelo de colores**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Pelota por el túnel means the game of the ball through the…
a) Tunnel  b) Net  c) Hoop
**Answer: a) Tunnel**
