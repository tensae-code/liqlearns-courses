# Traditional Games of El Salvador — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Spanish name of the annex game banks?
**Answer: Juegos lúdicos (para Primer, Segundo y Tercer Grado).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two games the guide names.
**Answer: Any two of: Pañuelo de colores, Te toco… te sigo, Ladrón librado en parejas, Pelota por el túnel, Relevos locos, Pelota al espacio.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** For which grades are the banks made?
**Answer: Primer, Segundo y Tercer Grado — Grades 1, 2 and 3.**
