# Hands-on — Traditional Games of El Salvador

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The per-grade banks

Open the bank for your grade (Anexos 3–5) and play one game from it exactly as the guide gives it.

## Name and play

Pick one named game — Pañuelo de colores, Pelota por el túnel, Relevos locos, Pelota al espacio — and play it with the whole class, the Salvadoran school way.
