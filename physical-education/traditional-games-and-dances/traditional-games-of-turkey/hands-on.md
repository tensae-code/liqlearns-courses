# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Turkey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play one the traditional way

Play one traditional children's game the way the books place it: learned from the group, by its own handed-down rules. Your partner watches and names one rule that nobody wrote down but everybody followed.

## Heritage check

Say the middle-school theme name — Kültürel Birikimlerimiz ve Oyun — and tell your partner what it means and which grades carry it (6 and 7). Your partner checks you against the lesson.
