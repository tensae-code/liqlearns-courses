# Traditional Games of Turkey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The named part that returns at grades 2, 5, 6 and 7 in the music-school books is…
a) Turkey's traditional children's games  b) Competitive swimming  c) Chess openings
**Answer: a) Turkey's traditional children's games**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 book carries the traditional games as…
a) Musical traditional children's games  b) Silent reading  c) A written test
**Answer: a) Musical traditional children's games**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The middle-school theme name Kültürel Birikimlerimiz ve Oyun means…
a) Our cultural heritage and games  b) Our school and its garden  c) Our team and its coach
**Answer: a) Our cultural heritage and games**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At which middle-school grades does the cultural-heritage theme appear?
a) Grades 6 and 7  b) Only grade 8  c) Only grade 5
**Answer: a) Grades 6 and 7**
