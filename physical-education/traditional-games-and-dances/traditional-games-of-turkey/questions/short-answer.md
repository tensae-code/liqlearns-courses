# Traditional Games of Turkey — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Turkish name of the traditional children's games part?
**Answer: Geleneksel Çocuk Oyunları.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the middle-school books name Turkey's game heritage?
**Answer: As a theme: Kültürel Birikimlerimiz ve Oyun — our cultural heritage and games.**
