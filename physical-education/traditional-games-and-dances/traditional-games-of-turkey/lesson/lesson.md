# Traditional Games of Turkey

## Do it first

Name one game from your own childhood that older children taught you, with no book and no teacher. Games handed down like that are this lesson's subject — now read how Turkey's books teach their own.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional children's games

Across the music-school books, one named part returns at grades 2, 5, 6 and 7: **Geleneksel Çocuk Oyunları — Turkey's traditional children's games**, played and compared; the Grade 1 book carries them as **musical traditional children's games**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Our cultural heritage and games

The middle-school books name the same heritage as a theme — **Kültürel Birikimlerimiz ve Oyun (our cultural heritage and games)** — at grades 6 and 7, and the primary guide carries it at grade 3 as **our cultural heritage and traditional games**.
