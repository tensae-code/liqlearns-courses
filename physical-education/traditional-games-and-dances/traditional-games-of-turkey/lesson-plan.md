# Lesson plan — Traditional Games of Turkey

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Games of Turkey

**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## If the learner is stuck
- You cannot name a traditional game → start from what the books name: the part is called Geleneksel Çocuk Oyunları, Turkey's traditional children's games — name the family first, then one game you know from home. → The Turkish theme name will not stick → split it: Kültürel Birikimlerimiz = our cultural heritage; Oyun = game/play.
