# Hands-on — Elle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Elle at Grade 9

Do the part as Sri Lanka's books teach it: Ch. 4 — Elle, Sri Lanka's own bat-and-ball team game: play, skills and its place among international games, as Sri Lanka's Grade 9 Sinhala/Tamil student book teaches it (printed p. 29). Work through it once slowly, then once at full speed, naming each step as you go.
