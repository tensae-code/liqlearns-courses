# Traditional Dances of Greece — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dance begins at home
Pass mark: say that dance begins at home, with babies danced in arms to lullaby "tachtarismata".

## Sub lesson 2 — Festivals, holds and costumes
Pass mark: name the festival occasions (feasts, weddings, christenings), two ways dancers hold each other, and how children join the dance.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dance in myth, story and ancient Greece
Pass mark: retell the Kouretes dance for the baby Zeus and say how the ancient Greeks honoured dance, as the book gives it (Plato: the person who cannot dance is uneducated).

## Sub lesson 4 — Dance travels through time and place
Pass mark: say why Greece has many local dances — its varied terrain made or adopted different dances in each place — and that Panhellenic dances are few.

## Sub lesson 5 — The dance band, improvisation and mimetic dance
Pass mark: say that most Greek traditional dances leave room for the lead dancer to improvise, that mimetic dances mime work and everyday life, and what a zygia is.

## Sub lesson 6 — The named dances, dance by dance
Pass mark: name zonaradikos, tsamikos, syrtos-kalamatianos, tik, enteka and pentozalis with the region or hold the book gives each.
