# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Greece

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Join the end of the circle

Stand in a line or circle with your group, holding each other one of the ways the book names — by the palms, arms crossed, or from the shoulders. Practise joining at the end of the line, the way the Grades 1-2 book shows children joining the festival dance.

## Name that dance

Your partner names a dance from Sub lesson 6 — zonaradikos, tsamikos, syrtos-kalamatianos, tik, enteka, pentozalis — and you say the region or hold the book gives it. Swap roles.
