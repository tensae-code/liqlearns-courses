# Traditional Dances of Greece — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grades 1-2 book, dance begins…
a) At home, with babies danced in arms  b) On a theatre stage  c) In a sports stadium
**Answer: a) At home, with babies danced in arms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lullabies babies are danced to are called…
a) Tachtarismata  b) Panigiria  c) Zygies
**Answer: a) Tachtarismata**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Panigiria are…
a) Village festivals at feasts, weddings and christenings  b) Dance examinations  c) Costume shops
**Answer: a) Village festivals at feasts, weddings and christenings**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the ways dancers hold each other, in Theofilos' painting?
a) From the shoulders  b) By the ankles  c) Back to back only
**Answer: a) From the shoulders**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How do children join the festival dance, in the book?
a) At the end of the dance circle  b) They may not join  c) Only on stage
**Answer: a) At the end of the dance circle**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did the Kouretes dance outside the cave?
a) So Kronos would not hear the baby Zeus cry  b) To win a prize  c) To open the Games
**Answer: a) So Kronos would not hear the baby Zeus cry**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What did Plato say, in the book?
a) The person who cannot dance is uneducated  b) Dance is only for festivals  c) Dance began in Rome
**Answer: a) The person who cannot dance is uneducated**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Why does Greece have many local dances?
a) Its varied terrain made or adopted different dances in each place  b) Every village was ordered to invent one  c) They were imported last century
**Answer: a) Its varied terrain made or adopted different dances in each place**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** A zygia is…
a) Two instruments together  b) A dance costume  c) A festival square
**Answer: a) Two instruments together**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Mimetic dances mime…
a) Work and everyday life  b) Sea voyages only  c) Nothing — they are abstract
**Answer: a) Work and everyday life**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** The zonaradikos is…
a) The Thracian belt-hold dance  b) A Cretan war dance  c) A Pontic single-file dance
**Answer: a) The Thracian belt-hold dance**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** The pentozalis is…
a) The Cretan "five steps" war dance  b) Greece's best-known circle dance  c) The "eleven" dance
**Answer: a) The Cretan "five steps" war dance**

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** Greece's best-known circle dance, in the book, is…
a) Syrtos - kalamatianos  b) Tik  c) Enteka
**Answer: a) Syrtos - kalamatianos**
