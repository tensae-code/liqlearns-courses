# Traditional Dances of Greece — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the youngest book, traditional dance is taught through a grandmother's photo album.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At a panigiri, everyone dances in the square.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Children must wait until they are adults to join the dance circle.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Kouretes danced so Kronos would not hear the baby Zeus cry.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Panhellenic dances are many and local dances are few, in the Gymnasium book.
**Answer: False**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Most Greek traditional dances leave room for the lead dancer to improvise.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** The tik is danced in single file and comes from Pontus, in the book.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The enteka is the "eleven" dance of western Macedonia.
**Answer: True**
