# Traditional Dances of Greece — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does dance begin, in the Grades 1-2 book?
**Answer: At home — babies are danced in arms with lullaby "tachtarismata".**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two ways dancers hold each other.
**Answer: Any two of: by the palms, arms crossed, from the shoulders, palms with elbows bent.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose life did dance save in the myth the book tells?
**Answer: The baby Zeus — the Kouretes danced and beat their swords so Kronos would not hear him cry.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Why are Greek local dances many?
**Answer: Greece's varied terrain made or adopted different dances in each place.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What is a kompania?
**Answer: The dance band that came after the 19th century — clarinet, violin, laouto, defi and santouri.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which dance is the Thracian belt-hold dance?
**Answer: The zonaradikos.**
