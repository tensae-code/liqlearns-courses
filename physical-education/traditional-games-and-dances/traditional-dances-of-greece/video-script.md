# Video script — Traditional Dances of Greece

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Greece, dance begins at home — a baby danced in arms.
At the festival, everyone dances in the square, and the children join the end of the circle.
Zonaradikos, tsamikos, syrtos, pentozalis — every place keeps its own dance.
That is **traditional dances of Greece**.
