# Video script — Traditional Games of Thailand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Every people keeps its own old games.
Thailand's folk play — การละเล่นพื้นเมือง — is played by its own rules, agreed by its players.
Play them Thailand's way, and they stay alive.
That is **traditional games of Thailand**.
