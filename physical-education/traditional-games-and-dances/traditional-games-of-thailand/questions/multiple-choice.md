# Traditional Games of Thailand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** การละเล่นพื้นเมือง in the Thailand book is…
a) Thai folk/traditional play  b) Imported board games  c) Swimming races
**Answer: a) Thai folk/traditional play**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is Thai folk play played, in the book?
a) By its own rules and agreements  b) With no rules at all  c) Only by teachers
**Answer: a) By its own rules and agreements**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 3 book, folk play is…
a) A named chapter of the movement unit  b) Not mentioned  c) An exam appendix
**Answer: a) A named chapter of the movement unit**
