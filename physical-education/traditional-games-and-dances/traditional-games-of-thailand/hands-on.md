# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Thailand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it Thailand's way

Say the name aloud — การละเล่นพื้นเมือง — and its meaning, Thai folk play, and how it is played: by its own rules and agreements. Your partner checks each fact against the lesson.

## Games from home

Ask someone at home what folk games were played when they were at school. Compare one answer with what this lesson says about folk play — do not add rules the book does not give.
