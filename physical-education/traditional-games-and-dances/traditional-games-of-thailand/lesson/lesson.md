# Traditional Games of Thailand

## Do it first

What games belong to your own place — games your grandparents played as children? Say one — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thai folk play (การละเล่นพื้นเมือง)

Thailand's Grade 3 book gives Thai folk play its own named chapter: **การละเล่นพื้นเมือง — Thai folk/traditional play**. It sits inside the book's movement unit, beside the chapters on moving the body and on games that develop movement. The book's rule for it is its own: folk play is **played by its own rules and agreements** — the rules the play itself carries, agreed by the children who play it. This is Thailand's own way, carried in its own textbook: its people's play, taught as content in its own right.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what การละเล่นพื้นเมือง is and how it is played, as the book gives them.
