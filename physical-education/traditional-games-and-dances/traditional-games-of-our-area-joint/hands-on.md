# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Our Area (Joint)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The unbroken chain

Build the chain and move: walking, turning, gently quickening. If it breaks, stop, rejoin, and say what broke it.

## Community sentence at work

Play Ayajibbo–Jibbo (or your area's joint game). Afterwards, test the book's sentence: what did we work on together? What problem did we solve together? What result did we reach together?
