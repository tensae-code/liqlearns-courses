# Traditional Games of Our Area (Joint) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does Enchaewet Lijoch mean?
a) Let's play, children  b) Go home now  c) Sit down
**Answer: a) Let's play, children**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the chain game, what must the children do?
a) Hold each other in a chain and move as one  b) Let go and run alone  c) Stand still
**Answer: a) Hold each other in a chain and move as one**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which joint game is pictured as children holding each other?
a) The chain game — እርስበርስ ተያይዘን  b) Qilmosh  c) Dama
**Answer: a) The chain game — እርስበርስ ተያይዘን**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does the Grade 2 book say games are?
a) Where a community works together, solves problems together, and reaches results together  b) A way to be alone  c) Only exercise
**Answer: a) Where a community works together, solves problems together, and reaches results together**
