# Video script — Traditional Games of Kuwait

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait's girls played these games in the sandy lanes.
Hopscotch squares chalked on the ground, a cap dropped in secret, seven stones to knock down.
Each game has its type, its rules and its song.
Those are the **traditional games of Kuwait**.
