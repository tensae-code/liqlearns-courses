# Traditional Games of Kuwait — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 6)** What does Shawt Shawt Ya Batta teach through its role-play?
**Answer: Kuwaiti customs and values.**

**2. (Sub lesson 9)** From how far away is the cloth ball thrown in Al-Anbar?
**Answer: From the "shish" line, seven metres away.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What must the chosen player do in Taq Taq Taqiya?
**Answer: Notice the dropped cap or cloth and chase the dropper round the circle.**
