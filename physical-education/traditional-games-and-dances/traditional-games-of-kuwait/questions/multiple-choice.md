# Traditional Games of Kuwait — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Al-Hila is…
**Answer: a) Hopscotch through chalked squares  b) A ring-guessing game  c) A blindfold game**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Taq Taq Taqiya, the dropped thing is…
**Answer: a) A cap or cloth  b) A ring  c) A cloth ball**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tabaq Hanna Tabaq Mash is a game of…
**Answer: a) Strength — partners lifting each other back to back  b) Guessing  c) Hopping**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Ghazala Bazala is…
**Answer: a) The choosing song that picks who plays which part  b) A target game  c) A chase game**

**5. (Sub lesson 9)** In Al-Anbar you knock down…
**Answer: a) A stack of seven stones  b) Seven pins on a table  c) A tower of books**

**6. (Sub lesson 10)** Al-Muhaibis hides…
**Answer: a) A small ring in a closed fist  b) A cap behind a player  c) A ball under a cloth**
