# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Kuwait

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chalk Al-Hila

Chalk six to eight squares on the ground, find a small marker, and play Al-Hila: hop on one foot through the squares, tossing and picking up the marker, the way the guide teaches it.

## Drop the cap

Play Taq Taq Taqiya with a group: sit in a circle, one player walks behind singing and secretly drops a cap or cloth — the chosen player must notice and chase.
