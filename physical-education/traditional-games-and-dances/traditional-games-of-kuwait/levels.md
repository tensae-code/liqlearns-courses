# Traditional Games of Kuwait — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Al-Hila — hopscotch
Pass mark: say how Al-Hila is played: hopping on one foot through six to eight chalked squares, tossing and picking up the rabaza marker.

## Sub lesson 2 — Taq Taq Taqiya
Pass mark: say how Taq Taq Taqiya is played: a cap or cloth secretly dropped behind a seated player, who must notice and chase the dropper round the circle.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tabaq Hanna Tabaq Mash
Pass mark: say how Tabaq Hanna Tabaq Mash is played: partners back to back with locked arms, lifting each other in turns while chanting.

## Sub lesson 4 — Haybala wa Haybala
Pass mark: say how Haybala wa Haybala is played: a singing circle whose leader jumps to a girl at the food cue, and the whole circle turns at "turn, my sisters".

## Sub lesson 5 — Ghazala Bazala — the choosing song
Pass mark: say what Ghazala Bazala is: the choosing song that picks who plays which part, sung in a half-circle with pointing.

## Sub lesson 6 — Shawt Shawt Ya Batta
Pass mark: say what Shawt Shawt Ya Batta is: a role-play singing game about a mother, her daughter and the boy who wants to join, teaching Kuwaiti customs and values.

## Sub lesson 7 — Ana Al-Dheeb Bakalkum — the wolf game
Pass mark: say how Ana Al-Dheeb Bakalkum is played: the mother guards her line of daughters while the wolf tries to snatch one.

## Sub lesson 8 — Amakor Tah Bil-Tannour — blindfold tag
Pass mark: say how Amakor Tah Bil-Tannour is played: the blindfolded Amakor tries to catch the players who touch him and dodge away chanting.

## Sub lesson 9 — Al-Anbar — the seven stones
Pass mark: say how Al-Anbar is played: knock down the stack of seven stones with a cloth ball from the shish line seven metres away, then rebuild it.

## Sub lesson 10 — Al-Muhaibis — the ring game
Pass mark: say how Al-Muhaibis is played: one team hides a ring in a closed fist and the other team guesses which hand holds it.
