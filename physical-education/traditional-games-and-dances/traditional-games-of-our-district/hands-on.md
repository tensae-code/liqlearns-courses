# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Our District

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five pebbles

Find five small round pebbles of equal size. Practise the Qilmosh toss and gather alone, then play in turns with a partner, counting clean turns.

## Mark and throw

Mark Zagol's small circles on the ground, measure the set distance with feet, and throw in turns. Everyone throws from the same mark.
