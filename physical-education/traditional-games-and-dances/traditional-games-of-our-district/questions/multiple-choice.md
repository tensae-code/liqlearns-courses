# Traditional Games of Our District — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** How many pebbles does Qilmosh use?
a) Five small round pebbles  b) Two pebbles  c) Twenty pebbles
**Answer: a) Five small round pebbles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Qilmosh is written in Amharic as —
a) የጠጠር ቅልሞሽ  b) የዳማ ጨዋታ  c) ገበጣ
**Answer: a) የጠጠር ቅልሞሽ**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** In Zagol, what is thrown, and from where?
a) A piece (key), thrown from a set distance at marked small circles  b) A ball, from anywhere  c) A stone, at a person
**Answer: a) A piece (key), thrown from a set distance at marked small circles**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Which game also appeared in the Grade 1 decision games?
a) Yaekob  b) Dama  c) Genna
**Answer: a) Yaekob**
