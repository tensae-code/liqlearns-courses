Traditional games of Bhutan — the games Bhutanese children play, taught in the Class I and Class IV books.
Hopscotch with a bean bag balanced on your head, the listening-and-chase game "What Time Is It, Charo?", and the Zeko ring game.
Every game is played the way the books teach it.
Those are the **traditional games of Bhutan**.
