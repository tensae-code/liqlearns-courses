## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Traditional Dance and Games Are. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show: say and show hopscotch played with hopping and sliding technique points and balance variations (bean bag on head/hand/shoulder).
2. Say and show: say and show the Bhutanese listening-and-chase game: children step forward by the called hour until "lunch time" starts the chase (charo = friend).
3. Say and show: say and show the Bhutanese ring game: throw the zeko (ring) over a pole from 1, 2 or 3 metres for points, with team roles of thrower, retriever, scorer and pole guard.

## What you need
An open space to play in, a bean bag for the hopscotch balance variations, and a ring and a pole for the Zeko, as the games are played, not only read about.
