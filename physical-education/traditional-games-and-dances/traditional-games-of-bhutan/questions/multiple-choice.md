# Traditional Games of Bhutan — multiple choice

Choose the right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Fun with Hopscotch, what do players do?
a) Hopscotch played with hopping and sliding technique points and balance variations (bean bag on head/hand/shoulder)  b) Players swim lengths of a pool  c) Players serve a shuttlecock over a net
**Answer: a) Hopscotch played with hopping and sliding technique points and balance variations (bean bag on head/hand/shoulder)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In What Time Is It, Charo?, what happens?
a) Children step forward by the called hour until "lunch time" starts the chase  b) Players swim lengths of a pool  c) Players serve a shuttlecock over a net
**Answer: a) Children step forward by the called hour until "lunch time" starts the chase**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In The Zeko, what do players do?
a) Throw the zeko (ring) over a pole from 1, 2 or 3 metres for points  b) Players swim lengths of a pool  c) Players serve a shuttlecock over a net
**Answer: a) Throw the zeko (ring) over a pole from 1, 2 or 3 metres for points**
