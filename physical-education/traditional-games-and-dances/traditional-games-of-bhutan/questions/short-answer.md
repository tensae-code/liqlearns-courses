# Traditional Games of Bhutan — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three traditional Bhutanese games taught in this lesson.
**Answer: Fun with Hopscotch, What Time Is It, Charo?, and The Zeko.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does "charo" mean in What Time Is It, Charo?
**Answer: Charo means friend.**
