# Traditional Games of Bhutan — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In What Time Is It, Charo?, the chase starts when "lunch time" is called.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fun with Hopscotch is a swimming game.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In The Zeko, the team roles are thrower, retriever, scorer and pole guard.
**Answer: True**
