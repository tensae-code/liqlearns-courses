# Traditional Games of Bhutan — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fun with Hopscotch
Pass mark: say and show hopscotch played with hopping and sliding technique points and balance variations (bean bag on head/hand/shoulder).

## Sub lesson 2 — What Time Is It, Charo?
Pass mark: say and show the Bhutanese listening-and-chase game: children step forward by the called hour until "lunch time" starts the chase (charo = friend).

## Sub lesson 3 — The Zeko
Pass mark: say and show the Bhutanese ring game: throw the zeko (ring) over a pole from 1, 2 or 3 metres for points, with team roles of thrower, retriever, scorer and pole guard.
