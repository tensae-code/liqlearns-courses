# Hands-on — Traditional Games of Bhutan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play hopscotch the Bhutanese way

Play Fun with Hopscotch: hop and slide through the court with the book's technique points, then try the balance variations — a bean bag on your head, hand or shoulder — as the Class I book teaches it.

## Play the Zeko

Play The Zeko: throw the ring over the pole from 1, 2 and 3 metres for points, taking turns at the team roles — thrower, retriever, scorer and pole guard — as the Class IV book teaches it.
