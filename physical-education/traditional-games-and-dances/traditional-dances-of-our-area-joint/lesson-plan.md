# Lesson plan — Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint)

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Traditional Games and Dances · **Lesson:** Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint)
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 7 — Traditional Games and Dances (CURRICULUM.md path: … → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the dances girls and boys dance together — the mixed circle dances of the local area, and the partner-turning dance of the teacher guide with its Embushe Gela chant.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the girls' and boys' dance lessons next door in this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Grade 1 joint dances** — pictures of mixed traditional dancing; the task: identify, bring, dance jointly, explain.
2. **The Grade 2 joint dances** — a picture of girls and boys dancing traditional dances together, with the chapter task.
3. **Turning with a partner — the Embushe Gela dance (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 1 teacher guide: pairs hold hands and turn/dance together; the traditional circle/partner dance chant "Embushe Gela" (summarised, not transcribed); pair turning games build social bonds.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Traditional Dances of Our Area (Girls) and (Boys). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Identify, bring and dance the joint dances of their area, and explain them.
2. Dance a joint circle dance together, keeping the step and the rhythm.
3. Turn with a partner, holding hands, in the partner dance the teacher guide teaches.

## What you need
A circle of dancers and your area's music or drum. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Circle breaks rhythm → shrink the circle and slow the beat; one step, repeated, until the circle is one body.
- Partner turns tangle → practise the turn walking slowly first, hands held, before any rhythm is added.
