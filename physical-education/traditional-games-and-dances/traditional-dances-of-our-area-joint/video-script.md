# Video script — Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everyone in the circle! Girls and boys — together!
One step. One rhythm. One circle.
Identify it. Bring it. Dance it — jointly. Explain it!
Now take hands. Turn with your partner. Hold — trust — turn as one!
**Embushe Gela** — እምቡሼ ገላ — the chant calls, the pairs turn.
A circle that dances together stays together.
