# Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the Grade 1 task ask for the joint dances?
a) Identify them, bring them, dance jointly, explain them  b) Only watch them  c) Dance them alone
**Answer: a) Identify them, bring them, dance jointly, explain them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Who dances in a joint traditional dance?
a) Girls and boys together  b) Only teachers  c) One child alone
**Answer: a) Girls and boys together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does the Grade 2 book picture for joint dances?
a) Girls and boys dancing traditional dances together  b) One child dancing alone  c) A written list only
**Answer: a) Girls and boys dancing traditional dances together**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What comes with the Grade 2 joint-dance picture?
a) The chapter task on the joint dancing  b) A written exam  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) The chapter task on the joint dancing**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does the guide say pair turning games build?
a) Social bonds  b) Walls between children  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Social bonds**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** What must partners do so a pair turn does not break?
a) Hold hands, trust, and move as one  b) Let go at the turn  c) Close their eyes
**Answer: a) Hold hands, trust, and move as one**
