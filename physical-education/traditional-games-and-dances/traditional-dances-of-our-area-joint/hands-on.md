# Hands-on — Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One circle

Form the full-class circle and dance the area's joint dance. Change direction when the music changes. The circle must not break.

## Hold and turn

In pairs, hold hands and turn: quarter turn, half turn, full turn. Feel what the guide names — the social bond of moving as one.
