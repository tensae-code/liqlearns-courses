# Traditional Dances of Our Area (Joint) — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Grade 1 joint dances
Pass mark: dance one joint dance in a full circle, keeping the step for a whole round, and explain it in one sentence.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Grade 2 joint dances
Pass mark: join the Grade 2 joint dance picture's dance — or your area's own — for a full round without breaking the circle.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turning with a partner — the Embushe Gela dance (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: hold hands with a partner and turn together through a full circle of turning without letting go.
