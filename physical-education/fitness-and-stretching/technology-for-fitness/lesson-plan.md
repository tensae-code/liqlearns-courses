# Lesson plan — Technology for Fitness

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Technology for Fitness
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Technology for fitness: apps, wearables and heart-rate tracking, and judging your fitness with them. — taught here through Indonesia's way: its Kurikulum Merdeka PJOK books for Grades 7–12.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Technology that supports fitness** — Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches technology that supports fitness — apps, wearables and heart-rate tracking.
2. **Evaluating fitness-support technology** — Indonesia's Grade 11 book teaches evaluating fitness-support technology.
3. **Evaluating your own fitness with technology** — Indonesia's Grade 12 book teaches evaluating your own fitness with technology.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
