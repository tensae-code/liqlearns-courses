## Sub lesson 1 — PE for children with disabilities — who needs adapted PE

The Grade 10 guide gives PE a full chapter on including disabled children: who needs adapted PE, so that every child can take part in physical education.

## Sub lesson 2 — How activity is adjusted — the guide's groups of children

The guide teaches how activity is adjusted for children with intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, and children who are withdrawn or afraid of activity.
