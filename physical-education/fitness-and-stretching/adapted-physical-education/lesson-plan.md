# Lesson plan — Adapted Physical Education

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Adapted Physical Education
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Adapted physical education: adjusting activity so every child — including children with disabilities — can take part. — taught here through Afghanistan's way: Afghanistan's Grade 10 teaching guide (Pashto).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **PE for children with disabilities — who needs adapted PE** — The Grade 10 guide gives PE a full chapter on including disabled children: who needs adapted PE, so that every child can take part in physical education.
2. **How activity is adjusted — the guide's groups of children** — The guide teaches how activity is adjusted for children with intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, and children who are withdrawn or afraid of activity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
