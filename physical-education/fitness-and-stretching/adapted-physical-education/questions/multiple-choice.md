# Adapted Physical Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does adapted PE make possible?
a) Every child, including children with disabilities, can take part  b) Only the strongest children play  c) PE is cancelled
**Answer: a) Every child, including children with disabilities, can take part**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For which children does the guide teach adjustments?
a) Children with intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, and withdrawn or fearful children  b) Only injured players  c) Only beginners
**Answer: a) Children with intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, and withdrawn or fearful children**
