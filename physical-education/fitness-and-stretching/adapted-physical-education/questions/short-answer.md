# Adapted Physical Education — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is adapted PE for?
**Answer: Children with disabilities — every child who needs activity adjusted so they can take part.**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one group of children the guide teaches adjustments for.
**Answer: Any one of: intellectual disability, postural and bone problems, hearing and sight impairment, hyperactivity, children withdrawn or afraid of activity.**
