# Video script — Agility — Rising, Running and Quick Changes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

What physical fitness is (teacher guide — evidence). Physical fitness has parts: heart and blood-vessel working, muscle strength, stretching and flexibility, and agility.

Rising and running. Sit with legs stretched — rise and run.

Line drills. Run, touch a line, and return.

Agility — Grade 5: obstacles and group runs. Agility at this grade means changing direction fast and accurately while keeping your balance.
Line-touch races and cone weaves (teacher guide — evidence). The line-touch race: lines are set at 5 metres, then 4 metres, then 3 metres; sprint from the start, touch each line with your hand, and sprint back; in the second round, run around all the markers in one flow.
Agility — Grade 6: starting positions and direction changes. Agility is changing the body's direction quickly and accurately, and it needs the body and the mind working together.
Speed work — Grade 7: high knees, pursuit and the three sprint phases. High-knee running (ጉልበትን ከፍ በማድረግ መሮጥ) lifts the knees high as you run.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
