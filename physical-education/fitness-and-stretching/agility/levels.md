# Agility — Rising, Running and Quick Changes — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What physical fitness is (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — fitness parts — heart and vessels, muscle strength, stretching/flexibility and agility.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rising and running
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — rising quickly from sitting or lying and running.

## Sub lesson 3 — Line drills
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sprinting to touch a line, turning and sprinting back.

## Sub lesson 4 — Agility — Grade 5: obstacles and group runs
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — running between obstacles keeping your balance, and group circle running games where called numbers run.

## Sub lesson 5 — Line-touch races and cone weaves (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the line-touch race over lines at 5, 4 and 3 metres, and weaving in and out of a line of cones at speed without touching them.

## Sub lesson 6 — Agility — Grade 6: starting positions and direction changes
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — sprint starts from lying on the chest and from kneeling on one knee over 30 metres, fast one- and two-leg jumps over 10 metres, and a four-direction marker course — forward, sideways, backward, round.

## Sub lesson 7 — Speed work — Grade 7: high knees, pursuit and the three sprint phases
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — high-knee running and pursuit runs, and the sprint's three phases — drive, transition, and top speed — over 20, 30 and 40 metre runs.

## Sub lesson 8 — Speed and the rules of sprinting technique — Grade 8
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — speed and the rules of sprinting technique — grade 8 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 9 — Fartlek — speeding up and easing off — Grade 10 (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — fartlek — speeding up and easing off — grade 10 (teacher guide — evidence) (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
