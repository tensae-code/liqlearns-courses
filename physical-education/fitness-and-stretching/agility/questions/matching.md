# Agility — Rising, Running and Quick Changes — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. What physical fitness is (teacher guide — evidence) — a
2. Rising and running — b
3. Line drills — c
4. Agility — Grade 5: obstacles and group runs — d
5. Line-touch races and cone weaves (teacher guide — evidence) — e
6. Agility — Grade 6: starting positions and direction changes — f
7. Speed work — Grade 7: high knees, pursuit and the three sprint phases — g

What each teaches:
a) fitness parts — heart and vessels, muscle strength, stretching/flexibility and agility
b) rising quickly from sitting or lying and running
c) sprinting to touch a line, turning and sprinting back
d) running between obstacles keeping your balance, and group circle running games where called numbers run
e) the line-touch race over lines at 5, 4 and 3 metres, and weaving in and out of a line of cones at speed without touching them
f) sprint starts from lying on the chest and from kneeling on one knee over 30 metres, fast one- and two-leg jumps over 10 metres, and a four-direction marker course — forward, sideways, backward, round
g) high-knee running and pursuit runs, and the sprint's three phases — drive, transition, and top speed — over 20, 30 and 40 metre runs

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e · 6–f · 7–g
