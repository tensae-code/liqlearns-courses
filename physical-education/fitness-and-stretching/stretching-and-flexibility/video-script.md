Watch me. Then do it with me.

Stretching and bending on the spot. Bend forward to touch your toes and return.

What stretching and flexibility are. Stretching is increasing the muscles' elasticity.

Stretching together and with a ball. Sit and raise your hands and return.

Side leans and waist stretches. Raise both arms up and lean right and left.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
