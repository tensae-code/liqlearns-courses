# Video script — Stretching and Flexibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

Stretching and bending on the spot. Bend forward to touch your toes and return.

What stretching and flexibility are. Stretching is increasing the muscles' elasticity.

Stretching together and with a ball. Sit and raise your hands and return.

Side leans and waist stretches. Raise both arms up and lean right and left.

Stretching and flexibility — Grade 5. Flexibility work is done with the joints warmed.
Flexibility — Grade 6 stretches. Bend and reach with arm raises.
Flexibility — Grade 7: sit-and-reach and the six-stretch circuit. Flexibility is the ability to move the joints and use the muscles through their full range of motion.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
