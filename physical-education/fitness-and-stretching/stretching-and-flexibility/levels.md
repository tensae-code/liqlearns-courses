# Stretching and Flexibility — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stretching and bending on the spot
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — bending forward to touch the toes, leaning each way, and stretching overhead.

## Sub lesson 2 — What stretching and flexibility are
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — stretching = the muscles' elasticity; flexibility = the joints' range of movement; benefit = preventing injury.

## Sub lesson 3 — Stretching together and with a ball
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — circle bending together holding hands, and passing a ball backwards overhead.

## Sub lesson 4 — Side leans and waist stretches
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — arms-up side leans, waist forward-and-back stretches, and leg stretching to reduce injury.

## Sub lesson 5 — Stretching and flexibility — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — leaning left and right, bringing the knee to the chest, and the bridge, with joints warmed first.

## Sub lesson 6 — Flexibility — Grade 6 stretches
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — bend-and-reach arm raises, the seated toe-touch, side bends, and the wide-stance side lunge held 10 seconds; plus the seated inner-thigh and hip-and-thigh stretches.

## Sub lesson 7 — Flexibility — Grade 7: sit-and-reach and the six-stretch circuit
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the sit-and-reach for the lower back and back of the thigh, and a six-stretch circuit done on the breath — runners stretch, standing side stretch, forward hang, low lunge arch, seated back twist, bound angle.

## Sub lesson 8 — Flexibility — Grade 8: static and dynamic

## Sub lesson 9 — Dynamic and static stretching — Grade 9
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — dynamic and static stretching — grade 9 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 10 — Flexibility — Grade 10: warm first, never force
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — flexibility — grade 10: warm first, never force (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 11 — Flexibility — the Grade 5 guide: range of motion and the seated toe-touch (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner defines flexibility as the joints' ability to move through their natural range of motion, says flexibility work is done with the joints warmed, and performs the seated toe-touch as the guide sets it — legs straight, reach slowly, hold, return, no bouncing; both legs, then each leg.
