# Stretching and Flexibility — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. Stretching and bending on the spot — a
2. What stretching and flexibility are — b
3. Stretching together and with a ball — c
4. Side leans and waist stretches — d
5. Stretching and flexibility — Grade 5 — e
6. Flexibility — Grade 6 stretches — f
7. Flexibility — Grade 7: sit-and-reach and the six-stretch circuit — g

What each teaches:
a) bending forward to touch the toes, leaning each way, and stretching overhead
b) stretching = the muscles' elasticity; flexibility = the joints' range of movement; benefit = preventing injury
c) circle bending together holding hands, and passing a ball backwards overhead
d) arms-up side leans, waist forward-and-back stretches, and leg stretching to reduce injury
e) leaning left and right, bringing the knee to the chest, and the bridge, with joints warmed first
f) bend-and-reach arm raises, the seated toe-touch, side bends, and the wide-stance side lunge held 10 seconds; plus the seated inner-thigh and hip-and-thigh stretches
g) the sit-and-reach for the lower back and back of the thigh, and a six-stretch circuit done on the breath — runners stretch, standing side stretch, forward hang, low lunge arch, seated back twist, bound angle

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e · 6–f · 7–g
