# Stretching and Flexibility

## Do it first

Try it now, before you read: Bend forward to touch your toes and return. Do it slowly once. Then read on and learn how the books teach it, step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stretching and bending on the spot

Bend forward to touch your toes and return. Lean forward, back, left and right. Bend forward and touch the ground with your palms. Lie down and raise alternate arms. Raise both arms joined overhead and stretch. Sit with legs stretched and pull forward. These movements above the waist develop arm, shoulder, waist and chest muscles.

## Sub lesson 2 — What stretching and flexibility are

Stretching is increasing the muscles' elasticity. Flexibility is the joints' range of movement. Their benefit is preventing injury on the field. Stretch with neck tilts right and left, standing side-stretches, both arms stretched overhead, the sheberek forward pull, leaning forward and back, waist twists, and a seated forward pull to the toes.

## Sub lesson 3 — Stretching together and with a ball

Sit and raise your hands and return. Stand in a circle facing outward, hold hands, bend and return together. Sit supported on your back and lift your legs. Pass a ball backwards overhead with both hands to a partner.

## Sub lesson 4 — Side leans and waist stretches

Raise both arms up and lean right and left. Stretch the waist forward and back, with alternate hands supporting from behind. Stretch the legs — this reduces injury and load when the legs work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
