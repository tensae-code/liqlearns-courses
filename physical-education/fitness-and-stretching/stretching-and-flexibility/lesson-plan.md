# Lesson plan — Stretching and Flexibility

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Stretching and Flexibility
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** stretching and flexibility — what they are, why they prevent injury, and the stretches for neck, waist, arms and legs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 1–4 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Stretching and bending on the spot** — bending forward to touch the toes, leaning each way, and stretching overhead.
2. **What stretching and flexibility are** — stretching = the muscles' elasticity; flexibility = the joints' range of movement; benefit = preventing injury.
3. **Stretching together and with a ball** — circle bending together holding hands, and passing a ball backwards overhead.
4. **Side leans and waist stretches** — arms-up side leans, waist forward-and-back stretches, and leg stretching to reduce injury.
5. **Stretching and flexibility — Grade 5** — leaning left and right, bringing the knee to the chest, and the bridge, with joints warmed first.
6. **Flexibility — Grade 6 stretches** — bend-and-reach arm raises, the seated toe-touch, side bends, and the wide-stance side lunge held 10 seconds; plus the seated inner-thigh and hip-and-thigh stretches.
7. **Flexibility — Grade 7: sit-and-reach and the six-stretch circuit** — the sit-and-reach for the lower back and back of the thigh, and a six-stretch circuit done on the breath — runners stretch, standing side stretch, forward hang, low lunge arch, seated back twist, bound angle.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
