# Lesson plan — Heart and Breathing Fitness

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Heart and Breathing Fitness
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what heart and breathing fitness is, and the steady movements that build it — so you can walk and run without tiring.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 1–4 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What heart and breathing fitness is** — the heart and lungs working for a long time at low intensity without tiring.
2. **Heart and breathing fitness grows** — endurance — working for a long time — trained with swings, partner holds and basket throws.
3. **Fitness that protects the heart and breathing** — fitness that protects against heart and breathing-system diseases, trained with group rope skipping and jumping jacks.
4. **The six-minute run and steady stepping** — the six-minute run, somsoma heel-touching and steady step-ups.
5. **The step-up test and counting the heart (teacher guide — evidence)** — the step-up test on a 20–30 cm step, with the heartbeat counted before and after.
6. **Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 5 movements** — walking, rope jumping, jumping jacks and the side-jump lunge that build the heart and breathing.
7. **Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 6: pulse, skipping and stepping** — taking the pulse at the wrist or neck (15 seconds × 4), travelling and group skipping, knee-lift running and step-ups on a 30–40 cm box.
8. **Cardio fitness — Grade 7: jacks, hop squats and ropes** — cardiovascular endurance work — jumping jacks, in-and-out hopping squats and rope jumping on the balls of the feet, at least a minute each with 30 seconds' rest.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
