Watch me. Then do it with me.

What heart and breathing fitness is. Heart and breathing fitness is the heart and lungs' ability to work for a long time at low intensity.

Heart and breathing fitness grows. Endurance means resisting a load and working for a long time.

Fitness that protects the heart and breathing. This fitness protects against diseases that come with the heart and breathing system.

The six-minute run and steady stepping. This work has great importance for preventing bodily pain and unnecessary body weight.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
