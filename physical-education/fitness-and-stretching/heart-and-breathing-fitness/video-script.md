# Video script — Heart and Breathing Fitness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then do it with me.

What heart and breathing fitness is. Heart and breathing fitness is the heart and lungs' ability to work for a long time at low intensity.

Heart and breathing fitness grows. Endurance means resisting a load and working for a long time.

Fitness that protects the heart and breathing. This fitness protects against diseases that come with the heart and breathing system.

The six-minute run and steady stepping. This work has great importance for preventing bodily pain and unnecessary body weight.

The step-up test and counting the heart (teacher guide — evidence). Step up and down on a high step, 20 to 30 centimetres high, in rhythm — first for 40 seconds at one pace, then for one full minute, then for two minutes without stopping, resting between rounds (Step-up).
Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 5 movements. Four movements build the heart and breathing.
Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 6: pulse, skipping and stepping. First learn your pulse: feel it at the wrist with three fingers, or at the neck; count for 15 seconds and multiply by 4 — that is your heartbeats in one minute.
Cardio fitness — Grade 7: jacks, hop squats and ropes. Cardiovascular endurance is the ability of the heart, blood vessels, blood and breathing system to supply fuel and oxygen to the muscles, and of the muscles to use that fuel to keep exercising.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.

The Grade 8 cardio set — zig-zag, skipping, squat jumps, aerobics. Four set practices build the heart and breathing at this grade.

Designing cardio training — Grade 9: FITT, jogging and the step-kick. Now you design the training.

The pure aerobic list — Grade 10. The Grade 10 book lists the pure aerobic activities: walking, jogging, running, cycling, swimming, aerobics, rowing, stair climbing, hiking, cross-country skiing and many dances.
