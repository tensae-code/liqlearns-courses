# Heart and Breathing Fitness — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What heart and breathing fitness is
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the heart and lungs working for a long time at low intensity without tiring.

## Sub lesson 2 — Heart and breathing fitness grows
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — endurance — working for a long time — trained with swings, partner holds and basket throws.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fitness that protects the heart and breathing
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — fitness that protects against heart and breathing-system diseases, trained with group rope skipping and jumping jacks.

## Sub lesson 4 — The six-minute run and steady stepping
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the six-minute run, somsoma heel-touching and steady step-ups.

## Sub lesson 5 — The step-up test and counting the heart (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the step-up test on a 20–30 cm step, with the heartbeat counted before and after.

## Sub lesson 6 — Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 5 movements
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — walking, rope jumping, jumping jacks and the side-jump lunge that build the heart and breathing.

## Sub lesson 7 — Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 6: pulse, skipping and stepping
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — taking the pulse at the wrist or neck (15 seconds × 4), travelling and group skipping, knee-lift running and step-ups on a 30–40 cm box.

## Sub lesson 8 — Cardio fitness — Grade 7: jacks, hop squats and ropes
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — cardiovascular endurance work — jumping jacks, in-and-out hopping squats and rope jumping on the balls of the feet, at least a minute each with 30 seconds' rest.

## Sub lesson 9 — The Grade 8 cardio set — zig-zag, skipping, squat jumps, aerobics
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the grade 8 cardio set — zig-zag, skipping, squat jumps, aerobics (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 10 — Designing cardio training — Grade 9: FITT, jogging and the step-kick
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — designing cardio training — grade 9: fitt, jogging and the step-kick (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lesson 11 — The pure aerobic list — Grade 10
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the pure aerobic list — grade 10 (Grades 8–10 depth; see lesson/sources.md).
