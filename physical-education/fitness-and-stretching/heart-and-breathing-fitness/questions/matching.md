# Heart and Breathing Fitness — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. What heart and breathing fitness is — a
2. Heart and breathing fitness grows — b
3. Fitness that protects the heart and breathing — c
4. The six-minute run and steady stepping — d
5. The step-up test and counting the heart (teacher guide — evidence) — e
6. Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 5 movements — f
7. Heart and breathing fitness — Grade 6: pulse, skipping and stepping — g
8. Cardio fitness — Grade 7: jacks, hop squats and ropes — h
9. Rope jumping for fitness — your own rope, and the long rope (teacher guide — evidence) — i

What each teaches:
a) the heart and lungs working for a long time at low intensity without tiring
b) endurance — working for a long time — trained with swings, partner holds and basket throws
c) fitness that protects against heart and breathing-system diseases, trained with group rope skipping and jumping jacks
d) the six-minute run, somsoma heel-touching and steady step-ups
e) the step-up test on a 20–30 cm step, with the heartbeat counted before and after
f) walking, rope jumping, jumping jacks and the side-jump lunge that build the heart and breathing
g) taking the pulse at the wrist or neck (15 seconds × 4), travelling and group skipping, knee-lift running and step-ups on a 30–40 cm box
h) cardiovascular endurance work — jumping jacks, in-and-out hopping squats and rope jumping on the balls of the feet, at least a minute each with 30 seconds' rest
i) solo rope turns counted in fives, and entering a turning long rope, jumping three to five times and leaving in turn without stopping the rope
