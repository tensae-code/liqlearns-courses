# Hands-on — Testing Your Fitness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow a protocol

Choose one simple fitness measure you can do safely — for example timing how long you can balance on one leg. Write the protocol first: exactly how it will be done, the same way every time. Then carry it out exactly as written.

## Use the data

Write your result down. Say in one sentence what it tells you about your fitness, and what you would test again after training to see a change.
