# Testing Your Fitness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fitness test's protocol is…
a) The set way the test must be carried out  b) A guess at the result  c) A prize for the winner
**Answer: a) The set way the test must be carried out**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fitness-test data is used to…
a) Read your results and know where your fitness stands  b) Skip training  c) Choose a team kit
**Answer: a) Read your results and know where your fitness stands**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The standard fitness tests are for…
a) Measuring the components of fitness  b) Entertaining spectators  c) Replacing all training
**Answer: a) Measuring the components of fitness**
