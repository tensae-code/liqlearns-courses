# Testing Your Fitness

## Do it first

How would you find out how fit you are — not guess, but measure? Say one thing you could test and how. Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The standard fitness tests and their protocols

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the standard fitness tests and the protocols — the set way each test must be carried out** (Component 1 Topic 3.2 (second part)).

## Sub lesson 2 — Using fitness-test data

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **using the data a fitness test gives you — reading your results** (Component 1 Topic 3.2 (second part)).
