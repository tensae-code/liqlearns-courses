# Physical Work and Chores — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work — work as effort exerted by the body to make and maintain things; cleaning (sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping, washing) as physical activity that also builds self-esteem, as Belize's Grade 4 book teaches it (printed pp. 4–5).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work — effort of the body in cleaning work: sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping, washing; keeping the environment clean, as Belize's Grade 5 book teaches it (printed pp. 22–23).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work — work as bodily effort; sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping and washing as valued physical activity, as Belize's Grade 6 book teaches it (printed pp. 42–43).
**Answer: True**
