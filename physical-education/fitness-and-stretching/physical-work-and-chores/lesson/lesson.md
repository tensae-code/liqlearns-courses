# Physical Work and Chores

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Work in Grade 4

Work — work as effort exerted by the body to make and maintain things; cleaning (sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping, washing) as physical activity that also builds self-esteem, as Belize's Grade 4 book teaches it (printed pp. 4–5).

## Sub lesson 2 — Work in Grade 5

Work — effort of the body in cleaning work: sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping, washing; keeping the environment clean, as Belize's Grade 5 book teaches it (printed pp. 22–23).

## Sub lesson 3 — Work in Grade 6

Work — work as bodily effort; sweeping, wiping, mopping, chopping and washing as valued physical activity, as Belize's Grade 6 book teaches it (printed pp. 42–43).
