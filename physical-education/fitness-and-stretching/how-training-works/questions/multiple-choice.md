# How Training Works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which rule says: train the energy systems and muscles the way your sport uses them?
a) the offside rule  b) only resting, never training  c) specificity
**Answer: c) specificity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fitness that is lost when you stop training shows which rule?
a) only resting, never training  b) reversibility  c) the offside rule
**Answer: b) reversibility**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Growing the load gradually so the body adapts — and avoiding injury — is…
a) progressive overload  b) the offside rule  c) only resting, never training
**Answer: a) progressive overload**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Anaerobic activity works…
a) the offside rule  b) only resting, never training  c) without oxygen — high intensity for a short period, using stored energy
**Answer: c) without oxygen — high intensity for a short period, using stored energy**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In a circuit, you usually work each station for…
a) only resting, never training  b) 30–60 seconds, as many repetitions as you can  c) the offside rule
**Answer: b) 30–60 seconds, as many repetitions as you can**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A circuit station using large muscle groups mainly trains…
a) aerobic work for the heart and breathing system  b) the offside rule  c) only resting, never training
**Answer: a) aerobic work for the heart and breathing system**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** How many training methods does the Grade 10 guide name?
a) the offside rule  b) only resting, never training  c) seven
**Answer: c) seven**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** For heart-and-breathing fitness, the guide points to…
a) only resting, never training  b) continuous, fartlek or interval training  c) the offside rule
**Answer: b) continuous, fartlek or interval training**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Fartlek is named the aerobic exercise best for…
a) cardiovascular development  b) the offside rule  c) only resting, never training
**Answer: a) cardiovascular development**
