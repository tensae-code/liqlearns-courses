# How Training Works — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Specificity means training the muscles and energy systems the way your sport uses them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fitness can be stored — once you are fit, you stay fit without training.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Circuit stations are ordered so muscle groups alternate.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Anaerobic means slow, steady work with plenty of oxygen.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 10 guide names seven training methods.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Fartlek is a strength exercise done only with weights.
**Answer: False**
