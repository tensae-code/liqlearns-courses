# Video script — How Training Works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch me. Then plan it with me.

Two kinds of fitness and the four rules of training. Train the way your sport uses your muscles. Grow the load little by little. Fitness cannot be stored — stop, and it fades. Every body is different, so every plan is personal.

Ways to train. Aerobic works with oxygen, steady and long. Anaerobic is hard and short. A circuit goes station to station, muscle groups taking turns.

The seven methods. Continuous. Interval. Fartlek. Circuit. Resistance. Flexibility. Plyometric. Pick by your goal.

Now you. Say the four rules. Pick one method. Start small.
