# Hands-on — How Training Works

The lesson with your body, on real ground, not only your eyes.

## Plan it on paper
Write a one-week plan: your goal, your method, and a load that grows a little each day — progressive overload in real ink.

## Run a mini circuit
Four stations, 30 seconds each: marching on the spot, wall push-ups, step-ups, and a held stretch. Move in order; note which stations felt aerobic and which felt anaerobic.

## Test reversibility honestly
After a rest week, repeat one station and feel what faded. That feeling is the rule — plan so it does not happen again.

## If it is hard
Two stations and 15 seconds is still a circuit. Keep the order, keep the rule names, and grow from there.
