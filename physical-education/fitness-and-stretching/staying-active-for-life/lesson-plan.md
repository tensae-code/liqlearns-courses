# Lesson plan — Staying Active for Life

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Staying Active for Life
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Staying active for life: regular participation, activity outdoors and in the community, and keeping fitness for the long term — taught here through Tajikistan's own way: setting up your own sports corner at home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Your sports corner** — the Tajikistan book: setting up your own sports corner at home with a skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Heart and Breathing Fitness; Muscle Strength and Endurance. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a home sports corner is, as the Tajikistan book teaches it.
2. Name what goes in it: a skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises.
3. Say why it matters for staying active for life: activity you can do at home, every day.

## What you need
A small clear space at home; a skipping rope if you have one. Nothing is bought for this lesson — use what you have, as the book's corner does.

## If the learner is stuck
- No sports corner at home → start with the space, not the equipment: a clear space for morning exercises is the corner's heart.
- Staying active feels like only lessons → anchor it in the book: the corner is for every day at home.
