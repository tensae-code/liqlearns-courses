# Staying Active for Life

## Do it first

Where could you be active at home, every day — not only in a PE lesson? Say a place and one thing you could do there. Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Your sports corner

Tajikistan's *Fizicheskaya Kultura* teaches **your sports corner**: setting up your own sports corner at home with a **skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises**. The corner is Tajikistan's way of staying active for life — activity lives at home, in a place that is yours, ready every morning, not only when a lesson or a team gathers.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what goes in a home sports corner, as the Tajikistan book teaches it, and why a corner at home helps you stay active for life.
