# Hands-on — Staying Active for Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan your corner

Find a small clear space at home. List what you already have that the Tajikistan book names for a sports corner — a skipping rope, gymnastic equipment — and mark the space for morning exercises. Do not buy anything; plan with what you have.

## Morning-exercise space check

Stand in your planned space and check you can move your arms and legs without hitting anything. If you cannot, clear or move the space until you can.
