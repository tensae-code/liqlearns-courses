# Staying Active for Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Tajikistan book, a home sports corner is set up with…
a) A skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises  b) A television and a sofa  c) Examination books only
**Answer: a) A skipping rope, gymnastic equipment and space for morning exercises**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sports corner is at…
a) Home  b) Only at school  c) Only at a stadium
**Answer: a) Home**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Morning exercises in the corner are for…
a) Starting the day actively, every day  b) Only competition days  c) Never
**Answer: a) Starting the day actively, every day**
