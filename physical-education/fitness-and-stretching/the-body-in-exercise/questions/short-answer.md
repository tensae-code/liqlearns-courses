# The Body in Exercise — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts of the musculo-skeletal system.
**Answer: Bones, joints and muscles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the parts of the musculo-skeletal system produce movement?
**Answer: The bones form the frame, the joints let the frame bend, and the muscles move the bones at the joints — together they produce movement.**
