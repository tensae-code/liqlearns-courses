# The Body in Exercise — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The musculo-skeletal system includes bones, joints and muscles.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Muscles produce movement entirely on their own, without bones or joints.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Joints are part of the musculo-skeletal system.
**Answer: True**
