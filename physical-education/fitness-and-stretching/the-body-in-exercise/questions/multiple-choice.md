# The Body in Exercise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The musculo-skeletal system is made of…
a) Bones, joints and muscles  b) Only muscles  c) Only bones
**Answer: a) Bones, joints and muscles**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Movement is produced by…
a) Bones, joints and muscles working together  b) Muscles alone, with no bones  c) The skin
**Answer: a) Bones, joints and muscles working together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A joint is…
a) Where bones meet and movement becomes possible  b) A kind of muscle  c) A bone in the leg
**Answer: a) Where bones meet and movement becomes possible**
