# Hands-on — The Body in Exercise

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts as you move

Bend and straighten one arm slowly. Say out loud, in the book's own order: the bones that form the arm, the joint where it bends, and the muscles that produce the movement.

## Find three joints

Find three places on your own body where bones meet and movement happens. Name each one as a joint of the musculo-skeletal system.
