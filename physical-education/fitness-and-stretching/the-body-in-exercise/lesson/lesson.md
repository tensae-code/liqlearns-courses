# The Body in Exercise

## Do it first

Point to a place where your body bends, and a muscle you can feel working when you move it. Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bones and the skeleton

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the bones of the musculo-skeletal system and what the skeleton does** (Component 1 Topic 1.1).

## Sub lesson 2 — Joints

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the joints — where bones meet and movement becomes possible** (Component 1 Topic 1.1).

## Sub lesson 3 — Muscles and how they produce movement

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the muscles and how bones, joints and muscles together produce movement** (Component 1 Topic 1.1).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — bones, joints, muscles and how they produce movement — the musculo-skeletal system the United Kingdom GCSE book opens with — in your own words, the way the United Kingdom book teaches it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the hands-on tasks in hands-on.md.
2. Write one sentence: what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
3. Teach it to someone at home, the way this lesson taught you.
