# The Body in Exercise — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bones and the skeleton
Pass mark: say, in the book's own terms, the bones of the musculo-skeletal system and what the skeleton does.

## Sub lesson 2 — Joints
Pass mark: say, in the book's own terms, the joints — where bones meet and movement becomes possible.

## Sub lesson 3 — Muscles and how they produce movement
Pass mark: say, in the book's own terms, the muscles and how bones, joints and muscles together produce movement.
