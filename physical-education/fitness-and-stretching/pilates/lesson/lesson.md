## Sub lesson 1 — Pilates

North Macedonia's Grade 11 book teaches Pilates: Joseph Pilates' system of controlled, precise, coordinated movement with correct breathing — some 50 repeatable exercises, on the mat with body weight or on the reformer, built on breathing, cervical alignment, stabilisation of the ribs and shoulders, pelvic mobility and activating the deep transversus abdominis, with health benefits for posture, balance, coordination and injury prevention.
