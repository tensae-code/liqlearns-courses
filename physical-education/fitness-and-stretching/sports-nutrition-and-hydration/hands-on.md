# Hands-on — Sports Nutrition and Hydration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balance the day

Write down, roughly, the energy you took in today (food and drink) and the energy you used (activity). Say in one sentence whether the two were in balance, as the book teaches energy balance.

## Plan the water

For your next active session, plan when you will drink water — before, during and after — and say why electrolytes matter when you sweat.
