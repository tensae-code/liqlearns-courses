# Sports Nutrition and Hydration — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is energy balance?
**Answer: The balance between the energy a performer takes in from food and drink and the energy the body uses.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts of the book's nutrition topic.
**Answer: Energy use and energy balance; diet and nutrition — a balanced diet; and hydration — water and electrolytes.**
