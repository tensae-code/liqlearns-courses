# Sports Nutrition and Hydration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Energy balance is…
a) Energy taken in balanced against energy used  b) Eating nothing before sport  c) Drinking only water for a week
**Answer: a) Energy taken in balanced against energy used**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A balanced diet gives a performer…
a) The nutrition the body needs for activity  b) Only one kind of food  c) Nothing important
**Answer: a) The nutrition the body needs for activity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Electrolytes matter for performers because…
a) They are lost in sweat and must be replaced with water  b) They are a kind of training  c) They replace sleep
**Answer: a) They are lost in sweat and must be replaced with water**
