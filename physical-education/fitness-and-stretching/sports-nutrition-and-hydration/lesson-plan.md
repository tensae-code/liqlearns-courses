# Lesson plan — Sports Nutrition and Hydration

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Sports Nutrition and Hydration
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sports nutrition and hydration: what to eat and drink for exercise, before, during and after competition — taught here through the United Kingdom's GCSE way: energy use, diet, nutrition and hydration for performers — energy balance, a balanced diet, and water and electrolytes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Energy use and energy balance** — the United Kingdom book: how the body uses energy, and energy balance — energy in against energy out.
2. **A balanced diet for performers** — the United Kingdom book: diet and nutrition — what a balanced diet gives a performer.
3. **Hydration — water and electrolytes** — the United Kingdom book: water and electrolytes for performers — why drinking matters in sport.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Heart and Breathing Fitness; How Training Works. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what energy balance is, as the book teaches it.
2. Say why a performer needs a balanced diet.
3. Say why water and electrolytes matter for performers.

## What you need
Nothing is bought for this lesson — paper and a pencil for the hands-on tasks, and a safe space to move.
