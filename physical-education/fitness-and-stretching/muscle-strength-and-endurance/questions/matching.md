# Muscle Strength and Endurance — matching

Match each part name to what it teaches.

1. What muscle endurance is — a
2. Muscle strength work — b
3. Slow and continuous strength work — c
4. Forward jumps and seated leg raises — d
5. Muscle strength by body part — Grade 5 — e
6. Push-ups, side hops and curl-ups (teacher guide — evidence) — f
7. Muscle strength — Grade 6 sets — g
8. Muscular endurance — Grade 7 — h

What each teaches:
a) leg, arm and stomach muscles working a long time at low load — used for writing, drawing and clapping
b) partner squats, ball jumps and frog jumps
c) slow, continuous arm raises level with the shoulders
d) two-foot forward-and-back jumps and seated leg raises in turn
e) strength work organised by body part — above the waist, stomach and back, and below the waist
f) the knee push-up in sets (10 × 3 rounds = 30), the full push-up, two-foot side hops, and the curl-up (3 × 10 = 30)
g) push-ups, walking on the hands, pull-ups on a single bar, sit-up variants, trunk raises held to 30 seconds, the rabbit jump, the wall-sit and box step-ups
h) muscular endurance as stamina — push-ups 4 × 10, squat jumps 3 × 5, and modified pull-ups on a low bar with the feet grounded, 6–10 reps

**Answer key:** 1–a · 2–b · 3–c · 4–d · 5–e · 6–f · 7–g · 8–h
