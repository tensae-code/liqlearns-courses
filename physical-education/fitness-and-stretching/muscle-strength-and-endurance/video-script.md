Watch me. Then do it with me.

What muscle endurance is. Muscle endurance is the leg, arm and stomach muscles working for a long time at a low load without tiring.

Muscle strength work. Build strength with alternating leg lifts touched by the hand, partner squat-and-rise holding a ball behind the back (sheberek), jumping up holding a ball and throwing it, back-to-back partner squats, and frog jumps — crouch, spring, land in a crouch, like a frog.

Slow and continuous strength work. Stand and stretch both arms sideways, level with the shoulders, again and again.

Forward jumps and seated leg raises. Stand and move both arms in different directions, again and again.

Now you. Do it slowly. Do it safely. Name it while you do it.
