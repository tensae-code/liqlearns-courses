# Muscle Strength and Endurance — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest.

## Sub lesson 1 — What muscle endurance is
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — leg, arm and stomach muscles working a long time at low load — used for writing, drawing and clapping.

## Sub lesson 2 — Muscle strength work
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — partner squats, ball jumps and frog jumps.

## Sub lesson 3 — Slow and continuous strength work
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — slow, continuous arm raises level with the shoulders.

## Sub lesson 4 — Forward jumps and seated leg raises
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — two-foot forward-and-back jumps and seated leg raises in turn.

## Sub lesson 5 — Muscle strength by body part — Grade 5
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — strength work organised by body part — above the waist, stomach and back, and below the waist.

## Sub lesson 6 — Push-ups, side hops and curl-ups (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the knee push-up in sets (10 × 3 rounds = 30), the full push-up, two-foot side hops, and the curl-up (3 × 10 = 30).

## Sub lesson 7 — Muscle strength — Grade 6 sets
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — push-ups, walking on the hands, pull-ups on a single bar, sit-up variants, trunk raises held to 30 seconds, the rabbit jump, the wall-sit and box step-ups.

## Sub lesson 8 — Muscular endurance — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — muscular endurance as stamina — push-ups 4 × 10, squat jumps 3 × 5, and modified pull-ups on a low bar with the feet grounded, 6–10 reps.
