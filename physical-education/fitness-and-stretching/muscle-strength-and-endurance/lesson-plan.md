# Lesson plan — Muscle Strength and Endurance

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Fitness and Stretching · **Lesson:** Muscle Strength and Endurance
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fitness and Stretching (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what muscle endurance is, and the movements that make legs, arms and stomach muscles work for a long time without tiring.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own body and the movements they already do in play; the parts climb from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest book's teaching in this band (Grades 1–4 books; see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What muscle endurance is** — leg, arm and stomach muscles working a long time at low load — used for writing, drawing and clapping.
2. **Muscle strength work** — partner squats, ball jumps and frog jumps.
3. **Slow and continuous strength work** — slow, continuous arm raises level with the shoulders.
4. **Forward jumps and seated leg raises** — two-foot forward-and-back jumps and seated leg raises in turn.
5. **Muscle strength by body part — Grade 5** — strength work organised by body part — above the waist, stomach and back, and below the waist.
6. **Push-ups, side hops and curl-ups (teacher guide — evidence)** — the knee push-up in sets (10 × 3 rounds = 30), the full push-up, two-foot side hops, and the curl-up (3 × 10 = 30).
7. **Muscle strength — Grade 6 sets** — push-ups, walking on the hands, pull-ups on a single bar, sit-up variants, trunk raises held to 30 seconds, the rabbit jump, the wall-sit and box step-ups.
8. **Muscular endurance — Grade 7** — muscular endurance as stamina — push-ups 4 × 10, squat jumps 3 × 5, and modified pull-ups on a low bar with the feet grounded, 6–10 reps.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The movement breaks down → go back to the youngest part and do it slowly, on the spot, before travelling or adding equipment.
- A roll or balance feels unsafe → stop, use a mat, work beside a partner or teacher, and never copy a movement that hurts.
- The learner cannot name the movement → say the name aloud together while doing it slowly; the name and the movement are learned together.
