# Muscle Strength and Endurance

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What muscle endurance is

Muscle endurance is the leg, arm and stomach muscles working for a long time at a low load without tiring. You use it for writing with pen and pencil, drawing and clapping. Train it with jumping up holding the waist, arm side-stretches, hand-in-hand two-foot jumping, and standing on one leg holding hands with the other leg stretched back.

## Sub lesson 2 — Muscle strength work

Build strength with alternating leg lifts touched by the hand, partner squat-and-rise holding a ball behind the back (sheberek), jumping up holding a ball and throwing it, back-to-back partner squats, and frog jumps — crouch, spring, land in a crouch, like a frog.

## Sub lesson 3 — Slow and continuous strength work

Stand and stretch both arms sideways, level with the shoulders, again and again. Do strength work slowly and continuously to get the change. Add seated leg stretch-and-fold in the air.

## Sub lesson 4 — Forward jumps and seated leg raises

Stand and move both arms in different directions, again and again. Jump forward and back on both feet from one spot, and jump forward progressively. Sit with legs stretched and raise them in turn.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the name of each movement in this lesson, and what it trains. Your companion does each movement slowly while you name it. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do each part of the lesson once, slowly and safely, in order.
2. Do the first part again — this time at your full, careful speed.
3. Teach one part to a friend or a family member: name it, show it, and watch them do it.
4. Say, in one sentence, what this lesson trained in your body.
