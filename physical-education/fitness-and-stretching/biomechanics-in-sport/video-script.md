# Video script — Biomechanics in Sport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every sporting movement can be analysed.
Your joints and bones work as levers — three classes of them.
And every movement runs along a plane, around an axis.
That is **biomechanics in sport**.
