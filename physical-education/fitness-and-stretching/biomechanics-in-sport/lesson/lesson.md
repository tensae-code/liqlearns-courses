# Biomechanics in Sport

## Do it first

Lift something with one arm and watch where the effort is. Is your arm working like a lever? Guess first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lever systems

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the three lever classes in the body and mechanical advantage in sport** (Component 1 Topic 2.1).

## Sub lesson 2 — Planes and axes of movement

The United Kingdom's GCSE book teaches **the planes and axes along which sporting movements happen** (Component 1 Topic 2.2).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — how movement works mechanically — the lever systems in the body, and the planes and axes along which sporting movements happen — in your own words, the way the United Kingdom book teaches it.
