# Hands-on — Biomechanics in Sport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the lever

Do a slow arm curl with a light object. Say which part of your arm is acting as the lever, in the book's terms, and where the effort is applied.

## Name the plane

Watch a partner make one sporting movement — a jump straight up, a step sideways, a turn. Say, as the book teaches, along which plane and around which axis the movement happens; if you are not sure of the names yet, describe the direction and the line it turns around.
