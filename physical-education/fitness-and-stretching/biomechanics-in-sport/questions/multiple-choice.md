# Biomechanics in Sport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many classes of lever does the book teach in the body?
a) Three  b) Two  c) Five
**Answer: a) Three**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mechanical advantage in sport is about…
a) How a lever system helps the body move a load  b) Wearing better shoes  c) Playing at home
**Answer: a) How a lever system helps the body move a load**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sporting movements happen…
a) Along planes and around axes  b) Only in straight lines  c) Without any axis
**Answer: a) Along planes and around axes**
