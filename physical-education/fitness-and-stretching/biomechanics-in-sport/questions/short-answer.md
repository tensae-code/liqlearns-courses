# Biomechanics in Sport — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a lever system in the body?
**Answer: A system in which bones and joints work as levers so muscles can produce movement — the book teaches three lever classes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What two ideas does the book use to describe where a sporting movement happens?
**Answer: The plane the movement runs along and the axis it turns around.**
