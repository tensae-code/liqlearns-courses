# Games for Communication and Cooperation — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** A cast that nobody answers catches nothing in the Fishing game.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Fishing game is the founding game of the communication and cooperation family.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Fishing game appears in both the Grade 3 and the Grade 4 books.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the ball-inside-the-circle game, the circle must talk to work as a team.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** The Gebeta game is named in the guide among signal and turn games.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The jumping relay covers a long distance by handing the jump over.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** In the overhead relay, the ball is passed overhead backwards down the line.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** A pupil who does not applaud the winners sits out the next game.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** In Turn Back, letting go of hands is the fastest way to finish.
**Answer: False**

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** The book says the rope tug gives a wide chance to know people and build communication.
**Answer: True**

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** In Ball in a Box the passing repeats until everyone has run.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 10)** In Abarosh, the teams are matched, and the winners meet the next team.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 11)** Growing communication and cooperation in games improves them in the rest of life.
**Answer: True**
