# Games for Communication and Cooperation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What two things does the Fishing game run on?
a) Communication and cooperation  b) Silence and hiding  c) Running and jumping only
**Answer: a) Communication and cooperation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What must the circle do to be a team in the ball-inside-the-circle game?
a) Talk — who has it, who wants it, who is free  b) Stay silent  c) Leave the circle
**Answer: a) Talk — who has it, who wants it, who is free**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is giving notice (ማስጠን)?
a) Giving a clear signal before a change so others can get ready  b) Changing the game secretly  c) Stopping the game
**Answer: a) Giving a clear signal before a change so others can get ready**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) ** After how many rounds does the beat change in the four-beat game?
a) 5 rounds  b) 1 round  c) Never
**Answer: a) 5 rounds**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** Which of these is a signal the guide names for giving notice?
a) A whistle, a count, or a call  b) A secret look only  c) Silence
**Answer: a) A whistle, a count, or a call**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In the jumping relay, each jumper starts…
a) Where the last jumper landed  b) Back at the first line  c) Anywhere they like
**Answer: a) Where the last jumper landed**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The relay's cooperation rule is…
a) Every group member must take part  b) Only the best jumper jumps  c) Two members may rest
**Answer: a) Every group member must take part**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** In the baton relay, the stick is…
a) Handed over, never thrown  b) Thrown forward  c) Kicked along
**Answer: a) Handed over, never thrown**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Cooperation, in the Grade 6 book, includes…
a) Clear communication, listening, owning mistakes, respecting diversity  b) Silence and speed  c) Winning alone
**Answer: a) Clear communication, listening, owning mistakes, respecting diversity**

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** The bottle target game is won by…
a) Most knock-downs in 3 minutes  b) The loudest shouting  c) The longest throws
**Answer: a) Most knock-downs in 3 minutes**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** After the bottle game, the losers must…
a) Applaud and embrace the winners  b) Leave at once  c) Demand a replay
**Answer: a) Applaud and embrace the winners**

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** Which is one of fair play's five principles?
a) Have everyone participate  b) Win by any means  c) Question every official
**Answer: a) Have everyone participate**

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** Compassion in games is shown by…
a) Cheering for your team, not against the other team  b) Mocking the losers  c) Hiding the ball
**Answer: a) Cheering for your team, not against the other team**
