# Games for Communication and Cooperation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Fishing game (Grade 3)
Pass mark: play the Fishing game in a group, casting and answering in turn.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Fishing game and the ball in the circle (Grade 4)
Pass mark: play the Fishing game and the ball-inside-the-circle game, calling and answering on every play.

## Sub lesson 3 — Signals before changes and the four-beat clapping game (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: give a clear signal before changing a game, and clap the four-beat pattern in rows, joining exactly on the beat for 5 rounds.

## Sub lesson 4 — The jumping relay — cooperation in fours
Pass mark: complete a four-person jumping relay where every member jumps and the hand-over is clean.

## Sub lesson 5 — Cooperation games — hand tug, baton relay, overhead relay
Pass mark: hand a baton over while running without throwing it, and pass the overhead relay ball backwards down a full line.

## Sub lesson 6 — The bottle target — communication, awareness and applause
Pass mark: play the 3-minute game and complete the sportsmanship ending: applause and embrace, whichever side you are on.

## Sub lesson 7 — Fair play, compassion and getting along (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: list fair play's five principles and complete a Turn Back circle out and back without letting go.

## Sub lesson 8 — Pair Hunting and the Rope Tug — the Grade 8 social games
Pass mark: play Pair Hunting to its printed scoring (last uncaught pupil wins) and set a 3-against-3 rope tug with one shared pull signal.

## Sub lesson 9 — Ball in a Box and Special Basketball — the Grade 8 cooperation games
Pass mark: run Ball in a Box until every pupil has run, and split a class into four equal teams for Special Basketball.
