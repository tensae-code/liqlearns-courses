# Video script — Games for Communication and Cooperation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The fisher casts! Who answers? Who catches?
This game cannot be played alone. It runs on voices.
Call! Answer! That is communication.
A ball inside a circle — who has it? Who is free? TALK, circle!
Before the game changes — a signal! Whistle! Count! Call! Get ready!
Clap — clap — clap — clap. Two rows. One beat. Join exactly ON it.
Five rounds — the beat CHANGES! Hear it! Follow — together!
Four jumpers, one long distance — every member jumps!
Hand to hand — the baton is NEVER thrown.
Down goes the bottle! Now — applaud the winners. Embrace them.
Respect the rules. Respect the opponents. Everyone plays. That is fair play.
