# Hands-on — Games for Communication and Cooperation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cast and answer

Play the Fishing game, then swap every role. Afterwards each player names the best call they heard and the best answer they gave.

## Two rows, one beat

Two rows face each other. One clap starts. Join on the beat. Five rounds, then the leader changes the beat mid-pattern — the rows must follow without stopping.

## The full-line overhead relay

Lines of six or more race the overhead relay round a cone 30 m away. A dropped ball returns to the player who dropped it — owning mistakes is in the definition.

## Turn Back circles

Circles of 5, 7 and 9 attempt Turn Back. The class records which size was hardest and why — communication, not strength, is the usual answer.
