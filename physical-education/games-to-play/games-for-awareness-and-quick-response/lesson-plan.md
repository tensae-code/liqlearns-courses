# Lesson plan — Games for Awareness and Quick Response

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Games for Awareness and Quick Response
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 3 book's new games family — awareness (ንቃተ ህሊና): hearing, feeling and responding the instant the moment comes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the other Games to Play lessons; its blindfold games need a guiding partner and a marked circle, always.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Grade 3 games — covered eyes, the name call, Thief and Police** — finding a ball with the eyes covered; entering the circle and catching when a name is called; the Thief and Police game; dribbling a ball inside a circle to touch a partner.
2. **The Grade 4 games — the inside/outside command game** — the book states the aim in words — a fast response to movements and stimuli: the "outside–inside / inside–outside" command game, and dribbling a ball by foot inside a circle to touch the children in it, two teams of equal number, skill and size.
3. **Watch and listen — attention games on changing calls (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 4 teacher guide's attention practice: dribbling, catching and throwing in a row on the teacher's changing calls, and the number game with its printed counting pattern.

4. **Social awareness games — Ball in the Air, Five Rounds, outside–inside** — the Grade 5 book's games for social interaction and awareness: Ball in the Air (ኳስ በአየር), Five Rounds (አምስት ዙር) and the two-circles outside–inside command game.
5. **Running while passing, and the number command** — the Grade 6 book's social-awareness games: teams running while passing a ball in the air only, and numbered players racing to the ball when their number is called.
6. **Attention and observation games (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 guide's attention and observation training — on-command movements, one-leg balance counting to 5, tiptoe with eyes closed, and the called-stations run — with the Grade 6 guide's interest and attention games: the middle-interceptor, traffic-light cards and the small-ball net game.
7. **Attention control and mindfulness (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 7 teacher guide: attention control — focusing on task-relevant information and resisting distractors — and mindfulness, awareness of the present moment, with reflective thinking.

8. **Spot the Difference and the Learning Logo — watching people closely (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 8 teacher guide's §2.2: the Spot the Difference leadership drill and the Learning Logo bench scenario.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Games for Quick Decisions — awareness grows out of fast deciding. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Find a ball with eyes covered, guided safely inside a marked circle.
2. Dash into the circle and catch the ball when their name is called.
3. Answer the inside/outside command game instantly, the right way.
4. Do exactly the called action — dribble, hold, throw — on changing calls, and answer the number game without error.

5. Play Five Rounds and the number command with full awareness — ball tracked, number answered at once.
6. Name attention control and mindfulness in the guide's words and show one practice of each.

## What you need
A ball, a cloth to cover eyes, a marked circle, and a guiding partner for every covered-eyes player. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Dashes before the call → awareness includes waiting: practise with calls mixed among other names; readiness is not rushing.
- Confuses inside/outside → say the words while pointing, then while moving slowly, then at speed.
- Frightened with covered eyes → they guide first, the space shrinks, and a trusted partner's voice leads.
