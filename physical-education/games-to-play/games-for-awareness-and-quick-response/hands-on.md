# Hands-on — Games for Awareness and Quick Response

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ears and feet

Play the name-call game and the inside/outside command game back to back. Notice: both are won by listening before moving.

## The covered-eyes search

In a small marked circle, with a guide's voice only, find a ball that is tapped on the ground to make a sound. Then find a silent ball by touch alone.

## Called stations at home

Four stones numbered 1, 2, 4, 5 in a yard. A caller names three; the runner runs only to those. Swap roles; the caller must watch like a judge.

## Red and green

Two coloured cards or leaves. A leader flashes them at random while the class moves: red freezes every body mid-step; green releases the run.

## Reading people

In pairs: one partner shows a feeling with the face and body only — no words. The other names the feeling and one kind thing to do about it. Swap. Then say the same thing in words, and notice how the two kinds of communication work together.
