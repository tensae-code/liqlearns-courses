# Games for Awareness and Quick Response — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the awareness family build, besides awareness itself?
a) Decision-making  b) Sleeping  c) Reading
**Answer: a) Decision-making**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the name-call game, when do you enter the circle?
a) When your name is called  b) Whenever you like  c) Never
**Answer: a) When your name is called**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Thief and Police, what must each player do?
a) Watch the other player closely  b) Sleep  c) Leave the circle
**Answer: a) Watch the other player closely**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What aim does the book state for this family?
a) A fast response to movements and stimuli  b) A long rest  c) Quiet reading
**Answer: a) A fast response to movements and stimuli**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the command game, what happens if you hear the called words wrong?
a) You move the wrong way  b) You win at once  c) Nothing happens
**Answer: a) You move the wrong way**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** In the dribble-hold-throw game, what makes it an attention game?
a) The teacher's calls change, and you must do exactly the called action  b) Nothing changes  c) You choose any action
**Answer: a) The teacher's calls change, and you must do exactly the called action**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) ** What happens in the number game when a mistake is made?
a) The count restarts  b) The game ends forever  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) The count restarts**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Five Rounds, the whole class passes fast to…
a) Finish the round first  b) Hold the ball longest  c) Sit down first
**Answer: a) Finish the round first**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** In the outside–inside game, how many group names are called at once?
a) Two  b) One  c) Five
**Answer: a) Two**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In running-while-passing, if the ball touches the ground the team…
a) Restarts  b) Wins  c) Changes the ball
**Answer: a) Restarts**

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** In the number command, you race when…
a) Your number is called  b) Any number is called  c) The whistle blows twice
**Answer: a) Your number is called**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** In the called-stations game, you run…
a) Only to the named stations  b) To every station  c) To the nearest station
**Answer: a) Only to the named stations**

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** A red traffic-light card means…
a) Stop  b) Run  c) Jump
**Answer: a) Stop**

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** Attention control is focusing on…
a) Task-relevant information, resisting distractors  b) Everything at once  c) Only your own feelings
**Answer: a) Task-relevant information, resisting distractors**

**15. (Sub lesson 7)** Mindfulness is focusing awareness on…
a) The present moment  b) Yesterday's game  c) Next year's prize
**Answer: a) The present moment**

**16. (Sub lesson 8)** In Spot the Difference, while one line is turned away, the other line…
a) Visibly changes things about themselves  b) Leaves the field  c) Sits down
**Answer: a) Visibly changes things about themselves**

**17. (Sub lesson 8)** In the Learning Logo scenario, you are on the bench because…
a) You feel ill, and your team trails 3–0  b) You won already  c) The game ended
**Answer: a) You feel ill, and your team trails 3–0**

**18. (Sub lesson 5)** Social relations and awareness mean helping, tolerance, understanding others' feelings — and…
a) empathy  b) winning alone  c) ignoring the team
**Answer: a) empathy** — feeling with others is one of the four parts of the definition.

**19. (Sub lesson 8)** Social awareness is the ability to…
a) run the fastest  b) take the perspective of and empathise with others, including those from diverse backgrounds and cultures  c) memorise the rules
**Answer: b) take the perspective of and empathise with others, including those from diverse backgrounds and cultures**

**20. (Sub lesson 8)** Which of these is nonverbal communication?
a) stating the rules in words  b) a facial expression or a signal that teammates read  c) writing the score down
**Answer: b) a facial expression or a signal that teammates read** — nonverbal communication expresses feeling and thought without words.

**21. (Sub lesson 8)** Which list holds only leadership skills to learn?
a) communication, motivation, delegating, trustworthiness  b) sitting out, guessing, hiding mistakes  c) running, jumping, throwing
**Answer: a) communication, motivation, delegating, trustworthiness** — the leader's skills also include positivity, creativity, feedback, responsibility, commitment and flexibility.

**22. (Sub lesson 6) ** The guide's written answer says the attention activities work with…
a) the mind, the hands, the legs and the whole body  b) only the eyes  c) only a whistle
**Answer: a) the mind, the hands, the legs and the whole body**
