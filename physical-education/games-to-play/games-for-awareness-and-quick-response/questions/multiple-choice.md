# Games for Awareness and Quick Response — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the awareness family build, besides awareness itself?
a) Decision-making  b) Sleeping  c) Reading
**Answer: a) Decision-making**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the name-call game, when do you enter the circle?
a) When your name is called  b) Whenever you like  c) Never
**Answer: a) When your name is called**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Thief and Police, what must each player do?
a) Watch the other player closely  b) Sleep  c) Leave the circle
**Answer: a) Watch the other player closely**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What aim does the book state for this family?
a) A fast response to movements and stimuli  b) A long rest  c) Quiet reading
**Answer: a) A fast response to movements and stimuli**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the command game, what happens if you hear the called words wrong?
a) You move the wrong way  b) You win at once  c) Nothing happens
**Answer: a) You move the wrong way**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** In the dribble-hold-throw game, what makes it an attention game?
a) The teacher's calls change, and you must do exactly the called action  b) Nothing changes  c) You choose any action
**Answer: a) The teacher's calls change, and you must do exactly the called action**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) ** What happens in the number game when a mistake is made?
a) The count restarts  b) The game ends forever  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) The count restarts**
