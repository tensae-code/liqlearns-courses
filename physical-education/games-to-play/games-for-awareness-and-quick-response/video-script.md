# Video script — Games for Awareness and Quick Response

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your name! GO! Into the circle — catch!
These games wake up your whole body. Awareness — ንቃተ ህሊና.
Eyes covered. The ball is near. Listen. Feel. FOUND it.
Thief and Police! Watch the other player. Watch everything.
"Outside — inside!" Hear it exactly. Move instantly.
Dribble — HOLD — throw! The call changed! Do the CALLED action, not the last one.
2, 4, 8, 12… a mistake restarts the count. Attention you can hear.
Five Rounds! The ball flies — the round must finish FIRST!
Your number! GO! Circle the ball, back to the centre!
Red — STOP. Green — RUN. Stations: only the ones CALLED!
Focus. The present moment. Nothing else. That is attention.
