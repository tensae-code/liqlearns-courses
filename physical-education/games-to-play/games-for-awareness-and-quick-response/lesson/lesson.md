# Games for Awareness and Quick Response

## Do it first

Stand in the circle. Balls are moving, names are flying. Suddenly — YOUR name! In you dash, and catch. Too slow, and the moment is gone. These games train awareness: your whole body awake, listening, ready.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Grade 3 games — covered eyes, the name call, Thief and Police

A new family arrives in Grade 3: games that build **awareness — ንቃተ ህሊና**. (The chapter body states this family also builds decision-making.)

**Finding a ball with the eyes covered — ዓይንን ተሸፍኖ ኳስ ፈልጎ ማግኘት.** Your eyes are covered. The ball is somewhere near. You feel for it, listen for it, find it. Safety note, always: a marked space, watchers around, slow steps.

**The name call.** When a name is called, that player enters the circle and catches the ball. Your name can come at any moment — awareness is being ready before it comes.

**Thief and Police — ሌባና ፖሊስ.** The chase of this family: thief and police, each watching the other.

**Dribbling inside a circle.** A player dribbles a ball inside the circle, aiming to touch a partner with it — eyes everywhere at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Grade 4 games — the inside/outside command game

The Grade 4 book states this family's aim in words: **a fast response to movements and stimuli**.

**The command game.** A word is called — "outside–inside" or "inside–outside" — and you do the movement of the first and second called word, instantly. Hear wrong, and you move wrong. Awareness is hearing exactly.

**Dribbling in the circle.** A player dribbles a ball **by foot** inside a circle, to touch the children in it — played by **two teams of equal number, skill and size**, as the book sets it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Watch and listen — attention games on changing calls (teacher guide — evidence)

Attention — ትኩረት — is trained by games that punish a lapse.

**Dribble, hold, throw — ኳስ ማንጠር፣ መያዝና መወርወር.** In a row, you dribble, catch and throw — but the teacher's calls **change**. You must watch and listen, doing exactly the called action, not the last one.

**The number game — ይለፈኝ.** The teacher gives a number pattern — the guide's worked example is the called number 4, and the printed counting series **2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24…**. Learners respond with the matching movement or count **without error**. A mistake restarts the count. Attention you can hear.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four Grade 3 games of this family, and the safety rules of the covered-eyes game. Then say what attention means in the changing-calls game.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name-call catching in a circle: one caller, one ball. When your name comes, dash in and catch. Everyone is called twice.
2. Inside/outside commands: a caller calls the pairs of words; players jump the right way instantly. Five clean answers in a row wins the round.
3. Covered-eyes finding: a partner guides, a marked space, slow steps. Find the ball by sound and touch. Swap roles.
