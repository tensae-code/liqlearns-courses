# Video script — Games for Self-Leadership

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Face your partner. They move. You move — the same, exactly. That is leading yourself.
Hold your place. Copy truly. Your body obeys YOU.
Angry? Wait. Your turn comes. The result stands. That is managing your feelings.
Akukulu! The seeker calls — answer, move, do not be caught!
The ball is out! Two minutes! Bring it back — and the rest of us WAIT, in order.
A rule nobody watches is still a rule. Keep it. That is self-discipline.
One minute. Rope turning. Count honestly. Beat the clock — fairly.
