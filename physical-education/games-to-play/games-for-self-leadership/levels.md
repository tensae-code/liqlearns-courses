# Games for Self-Leadership — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Holding a place and copying a partner
Pass mark: copy a partner's movement exactly, twice in a row, and hold your place through a whole game.

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing my feelings — Akukulu and the ball-return game (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: play one of the two games and show the three self-management signs: no leaving in anger, waiting your turn, accepting the result.

## Sub lesson 3 — Self-discipline and Pipali Kol (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: keep a rule in a game with nobody watching, and play Pipali Kol without breaking the turn order for a full round.

## Sub lesson 4 — Beating the clock fairly — timed rope jumps and bicycle legs (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: jump rope inside a set time limit and count your jumps honestly, and pedal the cycling movement for the set count without stopping.

## Sub lesson 5 — The cloth-snatch game — leading yourself at speed
Pass mark: play a full round inside the printed rules: snatch fairly, guard your own cloth, and accept losing it without protest.

## Sub lesson 6 — Self-knowledge games — hoop race, handkerchief-hand, rooster fight
Pass mark: play all three games and name, for one of them, a strength and a limit of your own that the game showed you.

## Sub lesson 7 — Managing myself and thinking positively (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name the conduct list's three honesty items and the four steps of building positive thinking.

## Sub lesson 8 — Confidence, control and calm breathing (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: do the neck and shoulder movements as printed and use active breathing, word and all, to calm down.

## Sub lesson 9 — Rescue from Danger and Change of Place — the Grade 8 self-knowledge games
Pass mark: set out the 20–30 m parallel-line field for Rescue from Danger, and give the reflection answer the book asks: one strength and one thing to correct.

## Sub lesson 10 — Rolling in Admiration and mindful minutes (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: run Rolling in Admiration correctly (dice number = number of compliments) and lead the five mindful-meditation steps in order.

## Sub lesson 11 — Managing myself on purpose — Capture the Flag, Three Legs, Trust (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner does and explains — the marks of a person fit for a purpose, the Capture the Flag markers raid (20 m × 20 m, tagged raiders out), the two-legs-tied race stepped together, and one trust game played safely.

## Sub lesson 12 — Knowing yourself through games — Grade 7
Pass mark: the learner explains — play reveals ability, character and the unknown self — and names one good thing in their own play to strengthen and one weak thing to improve.

## Sub lesson 13 — Growing inside through games — the Grade 8 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner explains — social and emotional learning in the guide's words, why a dropped catch is one of its moments, and the three marks of a well-balanced personality.

## Sub lesson 14 — Managing myself and getting along — what the Grade 3 chapter builds (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner names the chapter's six themes and matches each one to a part of this lesson they have done.
