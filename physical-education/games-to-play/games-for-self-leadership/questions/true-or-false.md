# Games for Self-Leadership — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Holding your place in the game is part of self-leadership.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The guide says the teacher's own behaviour is the model for self-management.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Helping, sharing, greeting and resolving quarrels are social experiences learned in play.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** In the cycling movement, you lie on your back and pedal your legs in the air.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Guarding your own cloth is part of the cloth-snatch game.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Dropping the ball before the "enough" command in እጅ ከፍንጅ saves you.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Accepting the result and trying again is part of building positive thinking.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Self-control means never having feelings.
**Answer: False**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Change of Place builds self-knowledge and self-direction.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Resilience means recovering from setbacks and keeping going.
**Answer: True**
