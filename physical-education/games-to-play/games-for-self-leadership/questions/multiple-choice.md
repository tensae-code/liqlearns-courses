# Games for Self-Leadership — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the copying game, what must you do?
a) Follow your partner's movement and do it the same way  b) Do any movement you like  c) Close your eyes
**Answer: a) Follow your partner's movement and do it the same way**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does "holding a place" (ቦታ መያዝ) mean in these games?
a) Taking your place in the game and keeping it  b) Leaving the game  c) Taking another player's place
**Answer: a) Taking your place in the game and keeping it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What are the three signs of self-management in games?
a) Not leaving in anger, waiting your turn, accepting the result  b) Shouting, leaving, refusing  c) Winning, winning, winning
**Answer: a) Not leaving in anger, waiting your turn, accepting the result**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the ball-return game, how long does the named learner have?
a) 2–3 minutes  b) All day  c) No limit
**Answer: a) 2–3 minutes**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does self-discipline show as, in games?
a) Keeping rules without being watched  b) Playing only when watched  c) Breaking turns
**Answer: a) Keeping rules without being watched**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** In Pipali Kol, what loses the round?
a) Breaking the turn order  b) Answering a call  c) Throwing the ball
**Answer: a) Breaking the turn order**

**7. (Sub lesson 4) ** A time limit is a boundary of what?
a) Time, space and turns  b) Only the score  c) Nothing — limits are not real
**Answer: a) Time, space and turns**

**8. (Sub lesson 4) ** In the cycling movement, what do your hands do?
a) They support you while your legs pedal in the air  b) They hold a ball  c) They cover your eyes
**Answer: a) They support you while your legs pedal in the air**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** In the cloth-snatch game, the cloth is tucked…
a) At the back of your belt  b) In your pocket  c) Under your hat
**Answer: a) At the back of your belt**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** The cloth game begins on which command?
a) "Start"  b) "Stop"  c) "Sit"
**Answer: a) "Start"**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** In the hoop race, the hoop travels…
a) Over the head, down the body, off at the feet  b) Around one arm only  c) Under one foot
**Answer: a) Over the head, down the body, off at the feet**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** In the rooster fight, you lose by…
a) Losing your balance  b) Scoring a point  c) Folding your arms
**Answer: a) Losing your balance**

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** Self-management, in the guide, is carrying out daily tasks…
a) With courage, without being pushed by others  b) Only when watched  c) Only for prizes
**Answer: a) With courage, without being pushed by others**

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** Positive thinking means…
a) Seeing the good path in a situation  b) Ignoring every problem  c) Always winning
**Answer: a) Seeing the good path in a situation**

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** In active breathing, you softly say your relaxing word…
a) As the air leaves, on the exhale  b) Before breathing in  c) While holding your breath
**Answer: a) As the air leaves, on the exhale**

**16. (Sub lesson 8)** The neck movement is done…
a) Slowly, without jerk, five times each way  b) As fast as possible  c) Standing on one leg
**Answer: a) Slowly, without jerk, five times each way**

**17. (Sub lesson 9)** Rescue from Danger is played between two parallel lines…
a) 20–30 m apart  b) 2 m apart  c) In a circle
**Answer: a) 20–30 m apart**

**18. (Sub lesson 9)** After the Chapter 2 games, the book asks you to name…
a) One strength and one thing to correct  b) Three winners  c) The fastest runner
**Answer: a) One strength and one thing to correct**

**19. (Sub lesson 10)** In Rolling in Admiration, the dice number tells you…
a) How many compliments to say about the next person  b) How far to run  c) Who leaves the game
**Answer: a) How many compliments to say about the next person**

**20. (Sub lesson 10)** The first step of mindful meditation in the guide is…
a) Get comfortable in a quiet place  b) Run a lap  c) Shout your name
**Answer: a) Get comfortable in a quiet place**
