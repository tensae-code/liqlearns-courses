# Hands-on — Games for Self-Leadership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Waiting in order

Play the ball-return game with a real counted clock. The runners run; the waiters practise the waiting — in order, without complaint. Both jobs are the lesson.

## Rules unwatched

Set a small game with one clear rule. The teacher steps back, out of sight of the players. Afterwards, the players report honestly: was the rule kept?

## The trust of the belt

Play the cloth-snatch game in a marked space with soft cloths. Afterwards each player names one moment they controlled themselves — a snatch not taken unfairly, a loss accepted.

## Calm-breathing practice

After a hard game, the class sits, breathes in, and breathes out on the word "peace", five breaths together. Notice the heart slow down.
