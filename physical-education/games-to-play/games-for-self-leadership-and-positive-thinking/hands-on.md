# Hands-on — Games for Self-Leadership and Positive Thinking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Word, patience, rest

Three stations: (1) a promise kept — set a partner a small task you promised and do it; (2) a patient task — thread, stack or balance slowly without abandoning it; (3) a rest mat — two minutes of true rest. Move through all three.

## Include everyone

Play a copying game where every player, of every ability, both leads and copies. The group adapts the movements so all can lead.
