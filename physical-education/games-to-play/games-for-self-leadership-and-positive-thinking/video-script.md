# Video script — Games for Self-Leadership and Positive Thinking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your partner shows TWO movements. Hold them both. Now — do them. In order. No miss!
Leading yourself is more than your body. It is your word.
You promised? DO it. That is keeping your word.
The task is hard? Stay. Patience is not abandoning it.
You worked? Now rest. Body and mind recover. Rest is part of training.
You lost? 'Well played.' That is a good sportsperson.
Your friend's body is different. Their ability is different. Respect it. Include them. Play.
