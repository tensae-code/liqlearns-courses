# Games for Self-Leadership and Positive Thinking — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Copying two movements in order
Pass mark: repeat a partner's two movements in order, three times without a miss.

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing myself — my word, my patience, my rest (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name the three parts of self-management and give one play example of each.

## Sub lesson 3 — Good attitudes and respecting difference (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: finish a game you lost with a good sportsperson's acceptance, and include a different-ability player fully in the next game.
