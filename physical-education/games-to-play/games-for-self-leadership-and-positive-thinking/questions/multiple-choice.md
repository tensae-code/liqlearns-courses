# Games for Self-Leadership and Positive Thinking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** How many movements must you copy in the Grade 4 game?
a) Two, in order  b) One only  c) Ten
**Answer: a) Two, in order**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Which is one of the three parts of self-management?
a) Keeping your word  b) Breaking promises  c) Never resting
**Answer: a) Keeping your word**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does taking rest do?
a) It lets the body and mind recover after work  b) It wastes training time forever  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) It lets the body and mind recover after work**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The guide says the person is made of what, together?
a) Body, mind and psychological state  b) Only bones  c) Only muscles
**Answer: a) Body, mind and psychological state**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** What are the two fruits of positive attitudes the guide names?
a) Being a good sportsperson; accepting and respecting diversity  b) Winning; boasting  c) Speed; strength
**Answer: a) Being a good sportsperson; accepting and respecting diversity**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does a good sportsperson accept?
a) Results — and other people  b) Only their own wins  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Results — and other people**
