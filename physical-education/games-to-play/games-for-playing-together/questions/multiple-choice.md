# Games for Playing Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the handkerchief-snatch game, what does the outside player drop?
a) A handkerchief behind one seated player  b) A ball in the middle  c) Nothing — they only walk
**Answer: a) A handkerchief behind one seated player**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What do these pair and group games build?
a) Helping each other  b) Playing alone  c) Winning without rules
**Answer: a) Helping each other**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What do the Grade 2 group games build?
a) Social interaction — playing and helping together  b) Sitting alone quietly  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Social interaction — playing and helping together**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does the book say relay hand-over games build?
a) Team spirit, response to sound, speed and accuracy  b) Only speed  c) Only quiet sitting
**Answer: a) Team spirit, response to sound, speed and accuracy**

**5. (Sub lesson 4) ** What does the book say the five-rounds circle game builds?
a) Working together and hand–eye coordination  b) Jumping height  c) Reading speed
**Answer: a) Working together and hand–eye coordination**

**6. (Sub lesson 4) ** In the collecting game, what is gathered?
a) Small balls, gathered in together by the group  b) Stones thrown away  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Small balls, gathered in together by the group**

**7. (Sub lesson 5) ** In the three-legged game, how do pairs move?
a) With joined middle legs, holding hands, in step  b) Each child runs alone  c) Sitting down
**Answer: a) With joined middle legs, holding hands, in step**

**8. (Sub lesson 5) ** In Abro Mechewet, what happens when the ball is dropped?
a) It is retrieved and play continues  b) The player leaves the game  c) The game ends
**Answer: a) It is retrieved and play continues**

**9. (Sub lesson 6) ** In the Fisherman game, what do caught fish do?
a) They help catch the rest  b) They leave the game forever  c) They become the teacher
**Answer: a) They help catch the rest**

**10. (Sub lesson 6) ** In Form a Family, what must happen at the number call?
a) Groups of exactly that size form, with nobody left out  b) Everyone runs away  c) Only the fastest children group
**Answer: a) Groups of exactly that size form, with nobody left out**

**11. (Sub lesson 7) ** What does the team that wins tug-of-war do?
a) Pulls the other team's marker across the line  b) Lets go of the rope  c) Pulls alone
**Answer: a) Pulls the other team's marker across the line**

**12. (Sub lesson 5) ** In your spare time, the Grade 1 guide's Chapter 3 summary says, you can protect your health by…
a) doing physical activity  b) never moving  c) skipping every game
**Answer: a) doing physical activity**

**13. (Sub lesson 6) ** Social and psychological education in Grade 2, in the guide's summary, is…
a) knowing yourself and others through games played by rules  b) sitting alone all day  c) never deciding anything
**Answer: a) knowing yourself and others through games played by rules**

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** Which of these is done in "Myself and others — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)"?
a) sitting still and watching only  b) a movement from another lesson entirely  c) knowing yourself, controlling your feelings, respecting others' work and building social relationships, learned through games
**Answer: c) knowing yourself, controlling your feelings, respecting others' work and building social relationships, learned through games**

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** A learner doing "Myself and others — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence)" correctly is —
a) knowing yourself, controlling your feelings, respecting others' work and building social relationships, learned through games  b) sitting still and resting  c) watching only, never moving
**Answer: a) knowing yourself, controlling your feelings, respecting others' work and building social relationships, learned through games**
