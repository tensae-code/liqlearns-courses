# Hands-on — Games for Playing Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The full circle

Play handkerchief-snatch and Abro Mechewet back to back. Notice the difference: in one, the circle waits and watches; in the other, the circle passes and turns at the whistle.

## Fisherman spread

Play the Fisherman game in a marked area. Count out loud how the catchers grow: one, two, three… The last free fish wins — then swap and play again.
