# Games for Playing Together — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Grade 1 games — Monday–Tuesday, Akukulu Alnegam, and the handkerchief games
Pass mark: play the handkerchief-snatch game in a full circle — drop, snatch, chase — and name the other four games of this sub lesson.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Grade 2 games — playing together grows
Pass mark: play one Grade 2 group game to its end with the group, and explain the benefit of playing it together.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Grade 3 games — the relay hand-over
Pass mark: hand an object over in a relay at full speed without dropping it, three changes in a row.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Grade 4 games — five rounds around the circle
Pass mark: pass a ball five rounds around a circle without a drop, and say the two things the book says the game builds.

## Sub lesson 5 — Understanding others, the three-legged game, and respecting others (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: walk in step with a partner in the three-legged game, and play Abro Mechewet, changing direction at the whistle and retrieving a dropped ball without blame.

## Sub lesson 6 — The Fisherman, Form a Family, Abo Yaeqob, and Pass It Back (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: play the Fisherman game until one catcher has become many, and form a family of the called size with nobody left out.

## Sub lesson 7 — Follow the leader and tug-of-war (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: follow a leader's movement exactly in a row, and name the three parts of self-knowledge the guide ties to tug-of-war.
