# Video script — Games for Playing Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stand in a circle. The game is coming around — on the outside!
A handkerchief. It drops — behind YOU! Snatch it! Run!
These are the games where the **group** is the player.
Monday–Tuesday! Akukulu Alnegam! Hands on the cup — quick, together!
Three legs, two children, one step. Your partner's step is your step.
The fisherman casts. One fish is caught — now there are two fishermen! The net grows!
Form a family! Three! Quick! Is anyone left out? No one is left out. Ever.
Five rounds around the circle. No drop. That is playing together.

Myself and others — the Grade 2 chapter opener (teacher guide — evidence). Social and psychological education — ማህበራዊና ስነ-ልቦናዊ ትምህርት — means knowing yourself, controlling your feelings, respecting other people's work, and building social relationships. In this society everyone has a share. The chapter's learning is carried by games in three ways: looking after yourself through games, building social relationships through games, and making responsible decisions through games. Its promises: show positive personal attitudes, respect others' feelings and views, value social relationships in cultural sport and games, and take responsibility for your decisions.
