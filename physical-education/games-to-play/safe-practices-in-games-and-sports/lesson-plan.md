# Lesson plan — Safe Practices in Games and Sports

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Safe Practices in Games and Sports
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Safe practices in games and sports: spotting unsafe practices and following the safety rules at home and school.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Unsafe practices in village games and sports** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Guided lesson 1: Unsafe practices in village games and sports — spotting and avoiding unsafe practices at home and school.
2. **Safe practices at home and school** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Guided lesson 2: Safe practices at home and school — basic safety rules to avoid risks in physical activities.
3. **Safety strategies, first aid and emergencies — the Grade 4 Safety unit** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Lesson-title tables, Safety unit: safety strategies; first aid procedures for injury prevention in games, field sports and water sports; emergency situations in goal/field, ball/bat and water sports and procedures for obtaining emergency assistance.
4. **Getting emergency assistance and information** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 1: Procedures of obtaining emergency assistance and information.
5. **Basic first aid procedures — Grade 4** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 2: Basic first aid procedures.
6. **Safety rules and conduct in school sports — Grade 5** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Lesson-title tables, Safety units: safety rules and conduct in school sports; safety procedures in defensive play in ball/bat, net/racket and water sports; offensive player behaviour in field, goal/net and water sports.
7. **Safety rules — emergency situations in games** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 1: Safety rules — emergency situations in games.
8. **Safety practices in water and ball games** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 2: Safety practices in water and ball games.
9. **Safety procedures in defensive play** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 3: Safety procedures in defensive play.
10. **Safety in Sports and First Aid — Grade 6** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 Unit 1 Safety in Sports and First Aid, lessons 1–7: Safety in Sports (sample lesson — equipment checks, right clothing, ground checks); causes of unsafe play and behaviour; safety rules for personnel and equipment use; injury and prevention; player and spectator rules; safe and unsafe play; care, safety and fair play code of conduct.
11. **Safety in Sport and First Aid — Grade 7** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 Unit 1 Safety in Sport and First Aid: injuries from motor and games skills; first aid procedures; causes and conditions of injuries; performing CPR; sample guided lesson: Safety in sports and first aid.
12. **Emergency and risky behaviour — Grade 7** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 Unit 2 Emergency and Risky Behavior: risk environment; risk behaviours — what are they; learning to avoid risks; physical activities and levels of risks; influences of risk behaviours; risky and harmful physical activities; sports and risk behaviour.
13. **Safety, first aid and risky behaviour — Grade 8** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 Unit 1 Safety in Sports and First Aid and Unit 2 Emergency Situation and Risky Behaviour: importance of safety and injury prevention, safe/unsafe play behaviour, first-aid treatment, equipment and facility safety checks, hazardous situations, emergency procedural skills; sample guided lesson "Risky behaviours and Life threatening injuries" (risky behaviour after a landslide).
14. **First aid and safe practices — Grade 9** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Unit 1, Topics 3–4: basic first aid procedures; types of injuries and first aid applications to injuries; safe practices as an integral part of all PE and sport.
15. **Sports injury management — Grade 10** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 (Movement) Unit 1 Safety and Fitness, Topics 1–2: sports injury management; best health practices in sports and risky behaviour.
16. **Minimising injuries and DRSABCD first aid — Grade 11** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 1 (Movement) Unit 1 Safety and Fitness, Topics 1–6: strategies to minimise injuries in sport; facility, equipment and environment checks; first aid in sports — soft tissue injuries and applying the DRSABCD procedure; risk management in water sports.
17. **Keeping Safe and the STOP strategy — Lower Primary** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand: Safety, substrand Keeping Safe (outcomes 3.1.1 / 4.1.1 / 5.1.1): behaviour that promotes safety and avoids risks, assessing risks, the STOP strategy, simple action plans and basic first aid for emergency situations in games and sports.
