# Games for Quick Decisions — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Grade 1 games — Cat and Mouse, Homeless, Yaekob
Pass mark: play Cat and Mouse with a real chase around the circle, and name the other games of this family.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Grade 2 games — Nekahuny and the handkerchief race
Pass mark: race from an equal distance, snatch the handkerchief, and run — and say why the distances must be equal.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Grade 3 game — throw before the runner leaves
Pass mark: aim and throw below the waist at the target in the moment before the runner leaves — three tries, deciding faster each time.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Grade 4 games — follow the change, and find the ball blindfolded
Pass mark: change your movement the instant the leader changes theirs, and find the placed ball blindfolded inside the circle with your partner guiding.

## Sub lesson 5 — Home-less, chasing games, Who Do You Want?, and Let Me In Today (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: play Home-less and one chasing game by their printed rules — marked area, catch by touch, no pushing — and freeze without colliding at the signal in the danger game.

## Sub lesson 6 — Games for responsible decisions
Pass mark: play a decision game carrying your given responsibility to the end, and name one decision you owned.

## Sub lesson 7 — Deciding in the moment — and solving problems in competition (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: make the pass-or-shoot decision before a closing defender three times, and walk the five solver steps on a real game problem.

## Sub lesson 8 — Decision games — below-the-knee dodgeball and the waist chain
Pass mark: survive three dodgeball rounds by jumping the low ball, and keep a waist chain unbroken through two turns.

## Sub lesson 9 — Ilama — and choosing your own challenge
Pass mark: choose a shooting distance, shoot, and evaluate your decision honestly: too close, too far, or well judged?
