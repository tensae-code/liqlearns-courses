# Hands-on — Games for Quick Decisions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The heartbeat games

Play Cat and Mouse, then Home-less. After each, every player says the decision they made and the moment they made it.

## Equal distances

Measure the handkerchief race distances with your own feet and check both sides match. Race. Then measure again — fairness you can check.

## The closing-defender drill

In threes: one attacker, one closing defender, one receiver each side. The attacker must decide and act before the defender arrives; ten decisions each, counted.

## Distance ladder

Mark three shooting distances. Each learner picks one, shoots five, then moves the mark by their own judgement and shoots five more — and writes why they moved it.
