# Video script — Games for Quick Decisions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A mouse runs. A cat runs after it. A gap in the circle — through, or around? DECIDE!
These are the games of the fast decision.
Nekahuny — "you touched me!" The touch decides.
Two runners. Equal distances. One handkerchief. GO!
Aim — below the waist — before the runner leaves the line. Now! Throw!
No home! Find a place! No pushing — decide with your feet, not your hands.
Who do you want? Break the line — by the arm, no tripping. Fair and fast.
Now you. See it. Decide it. Do it.
