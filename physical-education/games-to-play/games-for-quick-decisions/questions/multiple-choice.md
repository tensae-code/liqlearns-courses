# Games for Quick Decisions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Cat and Mouse, who chases whom?
a) The cat chases the mouse around the circle  b) The mouse chases the cat  c) Nobody chases
**Answer: a) The cat chases the mouse around the circle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What do all the games in this family force?
a) A fast decision  b) A long sleep  c) Reading a book
**Answer: a) A fast decision**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Why do both racers start from equal distances in the handkerchief race?
a) So the race is fair — speed and decision decide it  b) So one child always wins  c) It does not matter
**Answer: a) So the race is fair — speed and decision decide it**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) ** When must the throw be made?
a) Before the runner leaves the line  b) After the runner has finished  c) Any time tomorrow
**Answer: a) Before the runner leaves the line**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** Where must the throw aim?
a) Below the waist  b) At the head  c) At the sky
**Answer: a) Below the waist**

**6. (Sub lesson 4) ** In the blindfolded game, who guides the searcher?
a) Their partner  b) Nobody — they run alone  c) The other team
**Answer: a) Their partner**

**7. (Sub lesson 4) ** In the Grade 4 copying-decision game, when do you change your movement?
a) The instant the leader changes theirs  b) A minute later  c) Never
**Answer: a) The instant the leader changes theirs**

**8. (Sub lesson 5) ** In run-and-escape, how is a fleeing learner caught?
a) By a touch  b) By pushing them over  c) By shouting
**Answer: a) By a touch**

**9. (Sub lesson 5) ** In Who Do You Want?, what safety rules does the guide print?
a) Holding by the arm, no tripping  b) No rules at all  c) Tripping is allowed
**Answer: a) Holding by the arm, no tripping**

**10. (Sub lesson 5) ** In the Home-less game, what is forbidden?
a) Pushing  b) Moving  c) Looking for a home
**Answer: a) Pushing**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** A responsible decision in the Grade 5 games includes…
a) Carrying out your given responsibility  b) Letting others decide for you  c) Leaving your post
**Answer: a) Carrying out your given responsibility**

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** In the ball-to-goal game, the decision must be made…
a) Before the defenders close  b) After the game  c) By the referee
**Answer: a) Before the defenders close**

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** The first solver step for a problem in competition is…
a) Know the problem  b) Blame a player  c) Stop playing forever
**Answer: a) Know the problem**

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** In below-the-knee dodgeball, the middle players dodge by…
a) Jumping  b) Lying down  c) Sitting
**Answer: a) Jumping**

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** A waist chain scores by touching…
a) The rival chain's last player  b) The referee  c) The ball
**Answer: a) The rival chain's last player**

**16. (Sub lesson 9)** In "What a decision!", the shooting distance is chosen by…
a) The learner themselves  b) The teacher always  c) The tallest player
**Answer: a) The learner themselves**

**17. (Sub lesson 9)** If a pupil is hurt in Ilama, the first thing is…
a) Help them — first aid before the game  b) Finish the round  c) Move the game elsewhere
**Answer: a) Help them — first aid before the game**

**18. (Sub lesson 10)** Each end of the Two-Houses line has…
a) A 1 m circle  b) A goal post  c) A net
**Answer: a) A 1 m circle**

**19. (Sub lesson 10)** In the Kangaroo Race you run…
a) Hopping, with your legs tied together  b) On one hand  c) Backwards only
**Answer: a) Hopping, with your legs tied together**

**20. (Sub lesson 8)** Responsible decision-making means deciding by…
a) speed alone  b) moral and social standards  c) whatever wins the point
**Answer: b) moral and social standards**

**21. (Sub lesson 8)** A thrower can win a point with a throw above the knee, which the rules forbid. A responsible decision is to…
a) throw it anyway  b) hold the throw and play by the standard  c) hide it from the referee
**Answer: b) hold the throw and play by the standard** — the decision is made by what is right and fair, not by the point.

**22. (Sub lesson 11)** Which of these is done in "Squat and stand; the Green-Yellow-Red game (teacher guide — evidence)"?
a) sitting still and watching only  b) a movement from another lesson entirely  c) squatting and standing to a count, and moving or freezing on the Green-Yellow-Red colour calls
**Answer: c) squatting and standing to a count, and moving or freezing on the Green-Yellow-Red colour calls**

**23. (Sub lesson 11)** A learner doing "Squat and stand; the Green-Yellow-Red game (teacher guide — evidence)" correctly is —
a) squatting and standing to a count, and moving or freezing on the Green-Yellow-Red colour calls  b) sitting still and resting  c) watching only, never moving
**Answer: a) squatting and standing to a count, and moving or freezing on the Green-Yellow-Red colour calls**

**24. (Sub lesson 9) ** Which activity does the Grade 7 guide's review name for responsible decision-making?
a) “What a decision!” — choosing your own shooting distance  b) sleeping through the lesson  c) copying without thinking
**Answer: a) “What a decision!” — choosing your own shooting distance**
