# Games for Quick Decisions — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Homeless, one player has no home place and must find one.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Nekahuny means "you touched me."
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** In Nekahuny, a touch decides the play.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) ** Aiming too long loses the moment in this game.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 4) ** Nobody runs blindfolded — the searching is done with slow steps inside a marked circle.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 5) ** In Gesh-Gedo, at the end of the 10-metre path the roles swap.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 5) ** In the relay, the team that finishes accurately wins.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** Cooperating and helping each other runs through the Grade 5 decision games' rules.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** Putting brotherhood first is one of the guide's problem-solving points.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** In the waist chain, each chain has five players holding waists.
**Answer: True**

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** In "What a decision!", a well-judged distance is both challenging and productive.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 10)** The Two-Houses line is 20–30 metres long.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** A decision can be fast and still be responsible, if it is made by moral and social standards.
**Answer: True**

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** Responsible decision-making means deciding only by what helps your own team.
**Answer: False** — it means deciding by moral and social standards, which include the people around you.

**15. (Sub lesson 11)** In this lesson, "Squat and stand; the Green-Yellow-Red game (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches: squatting and standing to a count, and moving or freezing on the Green-Yellow-Red colour calls.
**Answer: True**

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** In "Squat and stand; the Green-Yellow-Red game (teacher guide — evidence)", the learner sits still and never moves.
**Answer: False** — the learner does the movement.
