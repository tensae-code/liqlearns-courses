# Chess — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nicaragua's Grade 3 PE, chess is learned with a cut-out board and pieces the class makes itself.

**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**

**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**

a) True as the books teach it  b) A swimming stroke  c) A type of ball
**Answer: a) True as the books teach it**
