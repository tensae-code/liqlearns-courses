# Lesson plan — Chess

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Chess
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chess: the board and pieces, how each piece moves, check and checkmate, and playing a game. — taught here through Nicaragua's way: its MINED manuals and books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Learning chess — the board, the pieces and a board you make yourself** — Unidad 4, Aprendiendo a jugar ajedrez — learning chess in PE: board design, starting set-up, how the pieces move, special moves, rules, and a cut-out board and pieces to make
2. **Chess — piece values, check, checkmate and a class tournament** — Unidad 4, Ajedrez — full chess unit: board and building your own board/pieces, piece placement and movement, piece values, check, checkmate, quick games, mate-in-one exercises and a student chess tournament
3. **Chess — its history, living chess and quick games** — Unidad Ajedrez — chess: review, benefits, history, living chess (tablero viviente), check, double check, checkmate, quick games and application games, cut-out board and pieces
4. **Chess — the board's parts and the road to checkmate** — Unidad IV, A jugar ajedrez — chess: board parts (columns, diagonals, centre, ranks), piece names and placement, moves, check/checkmate sequence carried from Grade 5

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
