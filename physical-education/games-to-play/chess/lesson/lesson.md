# Chess

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Learning chess — the board, the pieces and a board you make yourself

Unidad 4, Aprendiendo a jugar ajedrez — learning chess in PE: board design, starting set-up, how the pieces move, special moves, rules, and a cut-out board and pieces to make

## Sub lesson 2 — Chess — piece values, check, checkmate and a class tournament

Unidad 4, Ajedrez — full chess unit: board and building your own board/pieces, piece placement and movement, piece values, check, checkmate, quick games, mate-in-one exercises and a student chess tournament

## Sub lesson 3 — Chess — its history, living chess and quick games

Unidad Ajedrez — chess: review, benefits, history, living chess (tablero viviente), check, double check, checkmate, quick games and application games, cut-out board and pieces

## Sub lesson 4 — Chess — the board's parts and the road to checkmate

Unidad IV, A jugar ajedrez — chess: board parts (columns, diagonals, centre, ranks), piece names and placement, moves, check/checkmate sequence carried from Grade 5
