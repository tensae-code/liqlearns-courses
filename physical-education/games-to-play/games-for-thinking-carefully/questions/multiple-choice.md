# Games for Thinking Carefully — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Yaekob game, when must you do the movement?
a) Only when "Yaekob" is said first  b) On every command  c) Whenever you feel ready
**Answer: a) Only when "Yaekob" is said first**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the Yaekob game's printed rules forbid?
a) Deceiving others in the game  b) Standing in a circle  c) Sitting when told
**Answer: a) Deceiving others in the game**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How wide is the narrow goal?
a) 1 metre  b) 5 metres  c) As wide as you like
**Answer: a) 1 metre**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In group-by-number, what happens to a player left without a group?
a) They are out  b) They win  c) They choose the next number
**Answer: a) They are out**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Special football is won by what?
a) Talking, respect, communication and tactics  b) Emotion and shouting  c) Playing alone
**Answer: a) Talking, respect, communication and tactics**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In special football, who gets special care and priority?
a) The girls  b) The tallest players  c) The goalkeeper only
**Answer: a) The girls**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Deep thinking in activity means…
a) Weighing your decisions and responsibilities before acting  b) Moving as fast as possible  c) Copying the strongest player
**Answer: a) Weighing your decisions and responsibilities before acting**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The deep-thinking circle in practice is…
a) Plan, notice, correct  b) Run, shout, stop  c) Guess, hope, forget
**Answer: a) Plan, notice, correct**
