# Games for Thinking Carefully — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A command without "Yaekob" in front of it must be obeyed at once.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The narrow-goal game is played with the inside of the foot.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In special football, the book asks afterwards whether the winners were really the better thinkers.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Deep thinking means acting first and thinking later.
**Answer: False**
