# Lesson plan — Games for Thinking Carefully

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Games for Thinking Carefully
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** games that make you think carefully before you move — listen for the true command, aim through a narrow goal, group by the number called, and change tactics like a player.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Games for Awareness and Quick Response and Games for Quick Decisions, its neighbours in this title.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Yaekob game — move only when Yaekob is said** — the Grade 5 book's new deep-thinking family: a circle command game where a command counts only when "Yaekob" is said before it, and deceiving others is forbidden.
2. **Thinking games — the narrow goal and group-by-number** — the Grade 6 book's thinking games: an inside-foot pass through a 1-metre goal, and grouping in exactly the number called; critical thinking is named and defined.
3. **Special football — winning by thinking, talking and respect** — the Grade 7 book's special football: mixed girls-and-boys football with modified rules, won by reading the opponents and changing tactics.
4. **Deep thinking in games — plan, notice, correct (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 and Grade 6 teacher guides on deep thinking: the higher thought process by which you weigh your decisions and responsibilities inside the activity.

5. **Circle Target — the Grade 8 deep-thinking game** — the Grade 8 student book's §2.4.1: Circle Target — flat stones thrown at a circle, a burnt stone scoring nothing, played for a set time or rounds.
6. **Deciding well — SWOT and the six problem-solving steps (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 8 teacher guide's §2.3: responsible decision making, SWOT analysis, the six problem-solving steps and the Scavenger Hunt.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Games for Awareness and Quick Response — careful thinking grows out of sharp awareness. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Play the Yaekob command game: move only when "Yaekob" is said, and freeze on any other command.
2. Pass or shoot with the inside of the foot through a 1-metre goal from 10–15 metres.
3. Group in exactly the number called, fast, without leaving anyone out on purpose.
4. Say what deep thinking is in a game: read the other side, plan, and change tactics.
5. Play mixed special football by talking, respect and tactics — not by emotion.

## What you need
A ball, a 1-metre goal made of two markers, and open ground. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Moves on every command → slow the leader down; one command at a time, with a pause to judge it.
- Cannot score through the narrow goal → move closer first; distance grows only after accuracy.
- Plays special football by emotion → stop, name the opponents' strength aloud, and agree one tactic before restarting.
