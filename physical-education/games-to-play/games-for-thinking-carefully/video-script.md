# Video script — Games for Thinking Carefully

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A circle. A voice. "Yaekob, raise your hand!" Hands rise.
"Fold your leg!" — no name! FREEZE. Nobody moves. That is thinking first.
A goal, one metre wide. Aim… inside of the foot… THROUGH!
A number is called — THREE! Groups of three, fast!
Football, girls and boys together. Talk. Respect. Change the plan. Win by thinking.
