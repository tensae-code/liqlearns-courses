# Hands-on — Games for Thinking Carefully

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The narrow-goal ladder

Start at 10 metres from the 1-metre goal. Every two goals scored, step one metre back, up to 15 metres. Accuracy first, distance second.

## Were the winners the better thinkers?

After a special-football or any team game, both teams name one tactic that worked and one that failed. The losing team's best idea is praised by name.
