# Games for Thinking Carefully — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Yaekob game — move only when Yaekob is said
Pass mark: answer three "Yaekob" commands correctly and freeze on two commands given without Yaekob.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thinking games — the narrow goal and group-by-number
Pass mark: score through the 1-metre goal from 10 metres, and group correctly in three called numbers in a row.

## Sub lesson 3 — Special football — winning by thinking, talking and respect
Pass mark: play a special-football game, name one opponent strength you read, and say one tactic your team changed because of it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Deep thinking in games — plan, notice, correct (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: after a game, say what you planned, what you noticed, and what you corrected — all three.
