# Making and Changing Games

## Do it first

Say what you already think this lesson is about, before any explaining — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modifying activities and games

Indonesia's Grade 10 book teaches modifying activities and games to keep learning.

## Sub lesson 2 — Investigating and modifying equipment

Indonesia's Grade 8 book teaches investigating and modifying equipment so everyone can play.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modifying rules and scoring systems

The Grade 8 book teaches modifying rules and scoring systems, and its teacher guide teaches how to teach modification of equipment, rules and scoring.

## Say it to your companion
