# Lesson plan — Leisure and Recreation Activities

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Leisure and Recreation Activities
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Leisure and recreation activities: the choices for free time, in town and country, and why they matter.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Individual leisure and recreational activities** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Guided lesson 9: Individual leisure and recreational activities — the variety of leisure and recreation choices.
2. **Leisure and recreation in town and country — the Grade 4 unit** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Lesson-title tables, Leisure and Recreation unit: leisure activities, recreation activities, rural and urban leisure and recreation, action plans for recreational games, benefits of leisure and recreation.
3. **Indoor and outdoor leisure activities** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 7: Leisure activities — indoor and outdoor recreational activities.
4. **Planning your own weekly leisure and recreation** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Sample lesson 9: Personal weekly plans — planning your own weekly leisure and recreation.
5. **How recreation promotes health — Grade 10** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 2 Unit 4 Leisure and Recreation, Topics 23–24: how recreational activity promotes health; influences of recreation and leisure.
6. **Outdoor pursuits and lifelong recreation — Grade 11** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand 2 Unit 4 Leisure and Recreation, Topics 43–46: goal setting for outdoor opportunities; planning outdoor pursuits; age-specific recreational activities for lifelong health fitness.
7. **Local leisure and recreation — Lower Primary** — Papua New Guinea's teaching: Strand: Physical Activity, substrand Leisure and Recreation (3.3.3 / 4.3.3 / 5.3.3): local leisure and recreation — gardening, fishing, hunting, sailing, weaving, carving, dancing, mountain climbing, morning and evening walks, watching soccer and rugby league.
