# Lesson plan — Relay Games

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Relay Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Relay games: team relays — walking, hopping and running relays that hand over to the next player. — taught here through Jordan's way: its NCCD student books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Relay Walking** — Unit 5 Relay Games, Lesson 1: Relay Walking — walking a set path in a straight line heel-to-toe, returning to tag the next teammate — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 18.
2. **Relay Hopping** — Unit 5, Lesson 3: Relay Hopping — hopping with a balloon held, around the flag and back, combining more than one movement in one game — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 22.
3. **Relay Jumping** — Unit 5, Lesson 4: Relay Jumping — jumping with both feet along a marked path and back in a team relay — Jordan Grade 1 PE Term 2, printed p. 24.
