# Video script — Games for Self-Control

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

These games train one thing: **control of yourself**.
The ball goes down the row. Hand to hand. Nobody runs. Nobody shouts. You wait. You are ready. You pass clean.
Over — and under. The ball travels. You watch. You send it on.
Run the zigzag! In and out. In and out. Fast — but never out of control.
Now find your strength. Throw the ball into the basket. Did it go in? Check yourself. That is knowing what you can do.
Your friend can do something different. That is good. Every child's ability counts.
Hold your partner. Crouch. Jump — together! One rushes, both fall. Together, you fly.
Now you. Play. Wait your turn. Keep the rule. That is self-control.
The impulse comes. You govern it. Wait your turn. Accept the ruling. Play on.
