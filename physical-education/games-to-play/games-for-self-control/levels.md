# Games for Self-Control — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first games — rows, over and under, lines and zigzags
Pass mark: play each of the four games in turn and in place, and say what holding yourself steady means in each one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Knowing my strengths and valuing my own ability (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name one thing you can do well, show it in the basket aiming game, and name one different ability of a friend and value it out loud.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Grade 2 games — holding on, Dirchi Dirchi, passing backwards
Pass mark: jump forward together with a partner without letting go or falling, and pass a ball backwards while kneeling.
