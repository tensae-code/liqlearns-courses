# Hands-on — Games for Self-Control

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rows and baskets

Set up the two-rows passing game and the ball-into-basket aiming game with a real basket or box. Play both. After each game, every player says one thing they did well — honestly.

## Hold and jump

In pairs: crouch, hold each other, jump forward together. Start with one jump. Grow to five in a row. If you fall, laugh, reset, and say what rushed.

## The governed game

Play any class game with one added rule: any protest stops the protester's team for one turn. Afterwards, players name the hardest moment to govern — and how they did it.
