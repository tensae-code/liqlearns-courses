# Games for Self-Control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the two-rows game, what does the ball do?
a) It travels from hand to hand down the row  b) It is kicked as far as it can go  c) It stays with the first child
**Answer: a) It travels from hand to hand down the row** — the game is passing, in turn, down the row.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does the zigzag game train?
a) Running in and out while keeping control of your speed  b) Jumping as high as you can  c) Sitting still
**Answer: a) Running in and out while keeping control of your speed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does the ball-into-basket game train?
a) Setting a target and checking yourself  b) Hiding from the teacher  c) Running away from the game
**Answer: a) Setting a target and checking yourself**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does building self-respect in play teach?
a) Abilities differ, and every child's ability is to be valued  b) Only the best player's ability matters  c) Never try a hard game
**Answer: a) Abilities differ, and every child's ability is to be valued**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** Why must partners in the hold-and-jump game move together?
a) If one child rushes, both fall  b) The game has no rules  c) Only one child plays
**Answer: a) If one child rushes, both fall**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is the benefit of self-control the book gives?
a) You can tell your own task apart from others'  b) You never have to play  c) You always win
**Answer: a) You can tell your own task apart from others'**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Self-control in games means…
a) Controlling impulses, waiting for turns, accepting the judge's and the game's rules  b) Never wanting to win  c) Playing alone
**Answer: a) Controlling impulses, waiting for turns, accepting the judge's and the game's rules**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Which set names the chapter's three built skills?
a) Togetherness, communication, standing for self and others  b) Speed, strength, height  c) Reading, writing, counting
**Answer: a) Togetherness, communication, standing for self and others**
