# Games for Self-Control — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In these games, you cross the line whenever you feel like it.
**Answer: False** — you cross only the way the game says — that is the self-control.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** In the over-and-under game, the ball travels over heads and under arms.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Knowing your strength means showing honestly what you can do well.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** In games, only one child's ability should be valued.
**Answer: False** — every child's ability is to be valued.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) ** Self-control includes working while respecting the rules.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) ** In the kneeling game, the ball is passed forwards.
**Answer: False** — it is passed backwards.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Responsible decision-making plays a key role alongside self-control in the guide's chapter.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Self-control means feeling no impulse at all.
**Answer: False**
