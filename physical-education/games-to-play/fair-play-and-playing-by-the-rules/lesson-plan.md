# Lesson plan — Fair Play and Playing by the Rules

**Course:** Physical Education · **Title:** Games to Play · **Lesson:** Fair Play and Playing by the Rules
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Games to Play (CURRICULUM.md path: Moving Basics → Fitness and Stretching → Gymnastics → Athletics → Ball Games → Games to Play → Traditional Games and Dances → Sport and Its Story).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fair play and playing by the rules: making, accepting and practising a game's rules, and behaving well in play.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of this title.

## Sub lessons
1. **Playing legal — we make the rules** — the El Salvador guide: creating, accepting and practising the rules of a game.
2. **Playing clean** — the El Salvador guide: rules in the game and their regulating role; playing clean (fair play).
3. **Practising the rules of the game** — the El Salvador guide: practising the rules, norms and instructions of games.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Games for Playing Together; Games for Self-Control. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what fair play is: making, accepting and practising a game's rules, and behaving well in play.
2. Name the Grade 1 steps: creating, accepting and practising the rules.
3. Say what rules do in a game, as Grade 2 teaches: they regulate the play.
4. Say what Grade 3 practises: the rules, norms and instructions of games.

## What you need
Nothing — a game the group knows, and the rules the group makes and accepts, as the guide teaches it.

## If the learner is stuck
- A rule feels unfair → the Grade 1 answer: the players create and accept the rules together — say it before the game, not during it.
- Rules blur with instructions → rules say what may and may not be done; norms say how we behave; instructions say how this game is set up — Grade 3 practises all three.
