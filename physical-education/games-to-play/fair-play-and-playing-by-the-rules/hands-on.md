# Hands-on — Fair Play and Playing by the Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the rules

Before a known game, the group creates its rules together, says them aloud, and accepts them. Then play — by exactly those rules.

## Play clean

Play the game again. This time watch for the regulating role: every time a rule settles a disagreement, name it. That is rules regulating play.

## Practise the rules

Run the Grade 3 pass: read the game's rules, norms and instructions aloud, practise the start exactly as instructed, then play.
