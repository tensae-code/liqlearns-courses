# Fair Play and Playing by the Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fair play means…
a) Making, accepting and practising a game's rules, and behaving well in play  b) Winning at any cost  c) Playing with no rules
**Answer: a) Making, accepting and practising a game's rules, and behaving well in play**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's Grade 1 lesson ¡A jugar legal! teaches…
a) Creating, accepting and practising the rules of a game  b) Only running  c) Only throwing
**Answer: a) Creating, accepting and practising the rules of a game**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rules in a game have a…
a) Regulating role  b) Scoring role only  c) No role
**Answer: a) Regulating role**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Grade 3 practises…
a) The rules, norms and instructions of games  b) Only the score  c) Only the teams
**Answer: a) The rules, norms and instructions of games**
