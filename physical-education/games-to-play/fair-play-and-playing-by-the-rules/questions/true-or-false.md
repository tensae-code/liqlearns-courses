# Fair Play and Playing by the Rules — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A rule the players created and accepted is easier to keep.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Playing clean means the rules do not matter.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Practising the rules of games is Grade 3 content in El Salvador's guide.
**Answer: True**
