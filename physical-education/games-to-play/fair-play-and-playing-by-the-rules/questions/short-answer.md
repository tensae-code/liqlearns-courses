# Fair Play and Playing by the Rules — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the Spanish name of the Grade 2 lesson about playing clean?
**Answer: Juguemos limpio.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things do the Grade 1 children do with the rules?
**Answer: Create them, accept them and practise them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do rules regulate, in the Grade 2 lesson?
**Answer: The game — the play itself.**
