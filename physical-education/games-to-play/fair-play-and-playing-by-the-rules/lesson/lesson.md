# Fair Play and Playing by the Rules

## Do it first

Play any game for one minute with no rules at all. It falls apart. Rules are what make the game a game — and playing by them fairly is the thing this lesson teaches. Read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Playing legal — we make the rules

El Salvador's Grade 1 guide (Unit 3 Lesson 2, *¡A jugar legal!* — play legal!) starts with the children themselves: **creating, accepting and practising the rules of a game**. A rule the players made and accepted is a rule they can keep.

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing clean

In Grade 2 (Unit 3 Lesson 2, *Juguemos limpio* — let's play clean) the guide explains what rules are for: **rules in the game and their regulating role** — they regulate the play so it stays fair — and names the way of playing that follows: **playing clean, fair play**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Practising the rules of the game

In Grade 3 (Unit 3 Lesson 2, *Practiquemos las reglas del juego*) the line completes: **practising the rules, norms and instructions of games** — not only knowing them, but practising them until playing by them is the habit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches — fair play and playing by the rules: making, accepting and practising a game's rules, and behaving well in play. — and name each sub lesson's part of it in order.
