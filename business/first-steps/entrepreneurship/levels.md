# Entrepreneurship — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action — organising land, labour and capital to make goods or services for sale at a profit — and the entrepreneur does it with creativity, innovation, willingness to take risks, and the ability to plan and manage projects to their goals — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
