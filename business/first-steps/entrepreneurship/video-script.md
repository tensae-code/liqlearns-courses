# Video script — Entrepreneurship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action — organising land, labour and capital to make goods or services for sale at a profit — and the entrepreneur does it with creativity, innovation, willingness to take risks, and the ability to plan and manage projects to their goals.

Defines entrepreneurship in the book's own way: a person's ability to turn ideas into action, bringing together creativity, innovation and willingness to take risks, together with the ability to plan …. The entrepreneur's personality traits, self-strengths and the advantages of being one. The advantages of doing business as a career opportunity.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
