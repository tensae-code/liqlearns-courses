# Lesson plan — Entrepreneurship

**Course:** Business · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Entrepreneurship
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action — organising land, labour and capital to make goods or services for sale at a profit — and the entrepreneur does it with creativity, innovation, willingness to take risks, and the ability to plan and manage projects to their goals.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 1 — First Steps, lesson 3 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Ideas and Opportunities, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action — organising land, labour and capital to make goods or services for sale at a profit — and the entrepreneur does it with creativity, innovation, willingness to take risks, and the ability to plan and manage projects to their goals.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or market.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.
