# Entrepreneurship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the what-entrepreneurship-is / definition?
**Answer:** Defines entrepreneurship in the book's own way: a person's ability to turn ideas into action, bringing together creativity, innovation and willingness to take risks, together with the ability to plan and manage projects so as to reach set goals (info box inside the natural-woods lesson).

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about traits?
**Answer:** The entrepreneur's personality traits, self-strengths and the advantages of being one.

**3.** Explain the the entrepreneur part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** The entrepreneur: functions performed (conceptualising, researching, planning, accessing finance, organising, operating, evaluating performance, risk bearing, making profit/managing losses), the characteristics of successful entrepreneurs (creative, innovative, flexible, goal-oriented, persistent, calculated risk-takers, motivated, organised)

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the entrepreneur?
**Answer:** The stages of a venture and the entrepreneur's profile: gestation (motivation, building personal capacities, researching and selecting the idea), start-up (writing the project and launching), and strengthening (consolidating by trial and error so the venture survives its first years); leadership as the capacity to influence others toward a goal

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the entrepreneur?
**Answer:** What entrepreneurship is: a way of working that uses opportunities and ideas to create a profitable, value-creating business; the driving engine of a country's economy and a lifestyle of growth, creativity, innovation and moving forward; its types — independent entrepreneurship (small businesses), organisational entrepreneurship, social entrepreneurship and cultural entrepreneurship
