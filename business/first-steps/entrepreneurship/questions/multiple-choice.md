Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about the what-entrepreneurship-is / definition?
a) The aspects of managing people that support business development.
b) Communication inside the firm: written, oral, electronic and gesture media, and downward, upward and lateral flows between and within …
c) A bought business that lasted six months: the lessons are to examine all the business records and recent accounts before buying, check for …
d) Defines entrepreneurship in the book's own way: a person's ability to turn ideas into action, bringing together creativity, innovation and …
**Answer: d) Defines entrepreneurship in the book's own way: a person's ability to turn ideas into action, bringing together creativity, innovation and …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its the what-entrepreneurship-is / definition part.
