# Hands-on — Entrepreneurship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture one person in your area who started something of their own — a stall, a workshop, a service. Name what they organised: what land or place, whose work, what money or tools. Then name one risk they took and one plan they had to make. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: entrepreneurship is turning ideas into action — organising land, labour and capital to make goods or services for sale at a profit — and the entrepreneur does it with creativity, innovation, willingness to take risks, and the ability to plan and manage projects to their goals. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
