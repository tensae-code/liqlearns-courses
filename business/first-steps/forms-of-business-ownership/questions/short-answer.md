# Forms of Business Ownership — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about registration? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The procedure for registering a business entity with the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM).

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about opening? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Introduction to the types of business ownership found in Malaysia.

**3.** Explain the sole trader part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The sole trader: one person owns, finances and controls the business; characteristics (small scale, easy to set up, unlimited liability, personalised service) with advantages and disadvantages (all profits and quick decisions vs unlimited liability, limited finance, lack of continuity)
