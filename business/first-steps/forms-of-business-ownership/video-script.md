# Video script — Forms of Business Ownership

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Every business has an ownership form — sole trader, partnership, company, or a government or non-profit form — and the form fixes who owns the business, who carries its risks and debts, how it is formed and registered, and how large it can grow.

The procedure for registering a business entity with the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM). Introduction to the types of business ownership found in Malaysia. Sole proprietorship and partnership in detail: features, advantages and disadvantages of each.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
