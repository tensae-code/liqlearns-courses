# Lesson plan — Creativity and Innovation

**Course:** Business · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Creativity and Innovation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** creativity makes something new through imagination, and innovation does a thing in a different or better way — together they upgrade products and services, solve everyday problems and grow the market.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 1 — First Steps, lesson 4 of 26 in the course ladder; it stands on Entrepreneurship, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two up → creativity is producing the new thing in the mind (a new solution, method, device or artistic object); innovation is doing the thing in a different or better way in real life — a new product, service, business model or way of marketing.
- Go back to the one thing: creativity makes something new through imagination, and innovation does a thing in a different or better way — together they upgrade products and services, solve everyday problems and grow the market. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
