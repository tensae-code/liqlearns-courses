# Creativity and Innovation

## Do it first

Take one ordinary thing at home — a cup, a bag, a stool. First be creative on paper: sketch one new thing about it that nobody around you sells. Then name the innovation: how you would actually make it and offer it differently or better. That pair — the new idea, then the better doing — is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Creativity

**Creativity.** Creativity is the ability to produce something new through imaginative skills — a new solution to a problem, a new method or device, or a new artistic object; the work must be unique and appropriate. In business it upgrades products and services, develops new and original ideas, makes proper use of limited resources and solves everyday problems. A person becomes creative by noting down thoughts before they are forgotten, thinking beyond the invisible framework around a problem, transferring technology from one field to another, and using the unconscious mind — sleeping on a problem (Uganda's book.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Innovation

**Innovation.** Innovation is doing something in a different or better way. Its types are business model innovation, product innovation, service innovation and marketing innovation. It produces new products that widen customer choice, increases output through new and more effective production methods, improves the quality of products and services, and expands the market by attracting new customers while keeping old ones. The innovator is opportunity-oriented, associates with positive people, is extraordinarily persistent, and holds a compelling vision (Uganda's book.)

**Creativity and innovation.** Invention and innovation distinguished: invention is establishing something entirely new (the aeroplane, the car, Bell's telephone, penicillin); innovation is making changes in something that already exists; inventions are protected by patents for 20 years, earn royalties and fees when licensed, and the number of patent applications is an indicator of inventive growth (Tanzania's recorded… (Tanzania's book.)

**Creativity and innovation.** Innovation in Tanzania and where opportunities come from: the innovation ecosystem and Tanzania's Global Innovation Index trend, then the sources of entrepreneurial opportunities — people's needs, wants and problems, customer complaints (unmet needs make customers complain; businesses create value by serving the right products in the right manner to the right customers) (Tanzania's book.)

**Creativity part.** Creativity defined as the ability to create, produce and develop a new and original idea, and the creative entrepreneur's product as something not yet on the market (Malaysia's book.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: creativity makes something new through imagination, and innovation does a thing in a different or better way — together they upgrade products and services, solve everyday problems and grow the market. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: creativity, innovation, creativity and innovation, creativity and innovation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
