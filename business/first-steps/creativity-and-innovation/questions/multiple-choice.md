# Creativity and Innovation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is creativity? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Copying the best-selling product exactly  b) The ability to produce something new through imaginative skills  c) Lowering every price
**Answer: b) The ability to produce something new through imaginative skills** — A new solution to a problem, a new method or device, or a new artistic object (Uganda's book).

**2.** Which of these is a type of innovation? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Product innovation  b) Sleeping late  c) Keeping every old method unchanged
**Answer: a) Product innovation** — The types are business model, product, service and marketing innovation (Uganda's book).
