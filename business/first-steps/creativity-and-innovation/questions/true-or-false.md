# Creativity and Innovation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Creative work in business must be both unique and appropriate. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — A new thing that does not fit the problem is not the creativity this lesson teaches.

**2.** Innovation only means inventing a brand-new product. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Innovation is doing something in a different or better way; it also covers services, business models and marketing.
