# Creativity and Innovation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two ways a person can become more creative. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: note down thoughts before they are forgotten; think beyond the invisible framework around a problem; transfer technology from one field to another; use the unconscious mind — sleep on a problem.

**2.** Name two things innovation does for a business. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any two of: it produces new products that widen customer choice; it increases output through new and more effective production methods; it improves the quality of products and services; it expands the market by attracting new customers while keeping old ones.
