# Hands-on — Creativity and Innovation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one ordinary thing at home — a cup, a bag, a stool. First be creative on paper: sketch one new thing about it that nobody around you sells. Then name the innovation: how you would actually make it and offer it differently or better. That pair — the new idea, then the better doing — is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: creativity makes something new through imagination, and innovation does a thing in a different or better way — together they upgrade products and services, solve everyday problems and grow the market. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
