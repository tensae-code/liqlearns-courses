# Video script — Creativity and Innovation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
creativity makes something new through imagination, and innovation does a thing in a different or better way — together they upgrade products and services, solve everyday problems and grow the market.

Creativity: a new solution, a new method, a new device — first made in the mind. Innovation: the same thing done differently or better, out in the real market, where customers can choose it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
