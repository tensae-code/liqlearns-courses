# Clubs, Societies and Co-operatives — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Non-profit organisations — clubs, churches, societies and welfare groups — formed voluntarily around a shared interest to meet needs private enterprise and government do not provide, yet handling large sums of money ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that non-profit organisations as producers has nothing to do with clubs, societies and co-operatives.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: non-profit organisations as producers is one of its own parts.
