# Clubs, Societies and Co-operatives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about non-profit organisations as producers?
**Answer:** Non-profit organisations — clubs, churches, societies and welfare groups — formed voluntarily around a shared interest to meet needs private enterprise and government do not provide, yet handling large sums of money (Samoan examples from sports clubs to the Red Cross and PTAs).
