# Hands-on — Clubs, Societies and Co-operatives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one club, church group or society you know. Say what shared interest brings its people together, what need it meets that no shop and no government office meets, and what money passes through its hands in a year. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: clubs, churches, societies and welfare groups are producers too — non-profit organisations formed voluntarily around a shared interest, meeting needs that private enterprise and government do not provide, yet handling real money and real work. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
