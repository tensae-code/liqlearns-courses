# Video script — What Is a Business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary.

A firm as one unit of business organisation under one management bringing factors of production together (it may run several plants/branches), set against an industry as all the firms making the same …. What "business" means, the business as an open system turning inputs into outputs, and business resources: people, money, materials and opportunities. Production is organised through business organisations that combine the four factors of production; organisations are sorted by goal and ownership into private enterprise (profit), government ….

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
