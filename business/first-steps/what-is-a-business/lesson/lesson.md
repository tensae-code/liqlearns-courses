# What Is a Business

## Do it first

Look along your street or around your village and pick one business. Name what it makes or does, who runs it, and what it uses — its people, its money, its materials. Then name a second business that makes the same kind of thing: the two together stand inside one industry. That is this lesson.

## A firm, and the industry it stands in

**What a business is.** A firm as one unit of business organisation under one management bringing factors of production together (it may run several plants/branches), set against an industry as all the firms making the same product for one market, with Kenyan examples (Bata, Kenya Commercial Bank, the petroleum and motor industries).

## Business as a system — inputs in, outputs out

**What a business is.** What "business" means, the business as an open system turning inputs into outputs, and business resources: people, money, materials and opportunities.

## Three kinds of organisation — private, government, non-profit

**What a business is / kinds of organisation.** Production is organised through business organisations that combine the four factors of production; organisations are sorted by goal and ownership into private enterprise (profit), government (citizens' welfare) and non-profit (members' needs).

## The three sectors in Samoa's economy

**Business activities and sectors.** The three sectors of the economy as business activity: primary (extracting/harvesting natural resources), secondary (manufacturing/processing raw materials into finished or intermediate goods) and tertiary (services to consumers plus service industries supporting production — transport, finance, communication, marketing, accounting), set in Samoa's economy of agriculture, copra/coconut exports, remittances and tourism.

## Producers in three sectors

**The sectors of business activity.** Producers sorted into primary (obtaining and collecting raw materials — farming, fishing, forestry), secondary (using raw materials to produce other things, in factories) and tertiary (providing services), with an industry defined as a group of producers who produce a similar product.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: what a business is, what a business is, what a business is / kinds of organisation, business activities and sectors. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
