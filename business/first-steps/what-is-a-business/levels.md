# What Is a Business — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
