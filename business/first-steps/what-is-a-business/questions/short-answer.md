# What Is a Business — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about what a business is?
**Answer:** A firm as one unit of business organisation under one management bringing factors of production together (it may run several plants/branches), set against an industry as all the firms making the same product for one market, with Kenyan examples (Bata, Kenya Commercial Bank, the petroleum and motor industries).

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about what a business is?
**Answer:** What "business" means, the business as an open system turning inputs into outputs, and business resources: people, money, materials and opportunities.

**3.** Explain the basic concepts part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** The basics the book builds on: needs vs wants, goods vs services, tangible vs intangible products, and subsistence production before specialisation created surpluses to exchange
