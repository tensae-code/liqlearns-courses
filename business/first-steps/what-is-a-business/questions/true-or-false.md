# What Is a Business — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Production is organised through business organisations that combine the four factors of production; organisations are sorted by goal and ownership into private enterprise (profit), government (citizens' welfare) and ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that the sectors of business activity has nothing to do with what is a business.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the sectors of business activity is one of its own parts.
