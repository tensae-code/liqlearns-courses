# What Is a Business — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A firm as one unit of business ____ under one management bringing factors of production together (it may run several plants/branches), set against an industry as all the firms making the same product for one market, with Kenyan examples (Bata, Kenya Commercial Bank, the petroleum and motor industries).
**Answer:** organisation — the lesson's own word in that sentence.

**2.** ____ sorted into primary (obtaining and collecting raw materials — farming, fishing, forestry), secondary (using raw materials to produce other things, in factories) and tertiary (providing services), with an industry defined as a group of producers who produce a similar product.
**Answer:** Producers — the lesson's own word in that sentence.
