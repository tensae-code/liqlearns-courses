# Lesson plan — What Is a Business

**Course:** Business · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What Is a Business
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 1 — First Steps, lesson 1 of 26 in the course ladder — the first lesson of the ladder; it stands on nothing before it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up a firm and an industry → a firm is ONE business under one management; an industry is ALL the firms making the same product for one market.
- Go back to the one thing: a business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary.
