# Hands-on — What Is a Business

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look along your street or around your village and pick one business. Name what it makes or does, who runs it, and what it uses — its people, its money, its materials. Then name a second business that makes the same kind of thing: the two together stand inside one industry. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a business is an organisation that brings people, money, materials and opportunities together to make goods or services — one firm under one management, standing inside an industry of firms making the same thing, in one of the economy's three sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
