# Business Ideas and Opportunities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the spotting-and-using-opportunities / resources? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Frames the whole subject's entrepreneurship fields as ideas and opportunities plus resources: the entrepreneurial skill is turning ideas and opportunities into work of value by managing the resources at hand, and the resources are sorted into personal (self-awareness), material (means of production, money) and non-material (knowledge, skills, values).

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the finding-an-opportunity-in-available-resources? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Teaches that materials lying around in the local environment (scrap and recycled wood) are a low-cost production resource and an opportunity: they can be turned by creative design into useful goods and decorations instead of being thrown away (info box inside the wood-joinery practical activities).

**3.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about testing an idea against the market? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Ecuador's own way: for each need, tabulating how it is satisfied today — completely, partially, not at all in the locality, or by no existing good or service — and naming the potential venture each gap suggests

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about generating ideas? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Developing individual creativity with the SCAMPER method: working a problem through substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to other uses, eliminate/shrink, and reverse, each with its key questions
