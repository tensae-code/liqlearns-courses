# Lesson plan — Business Ideas and Opportunities

**Course:** Business · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Business Ideas and Opportunities
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business begins with an idea and an opportunity — and the entrepreneurial skill is turning ideas and opportunities into work of value by managing the resources at hand: personal, material and non-material — then choosing what to produce and testing the idea before trusting it.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 1 — First Steps, lesson 2 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on What Is a Business, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ideas, opportunities and the resources at hand** — the spotting-and-using-opportunities / resources; the finding-an-opportunity-in-available-resources.
2. **Choosing what to produce** — choosing the product.
3. **Routes to an idea — and testing it** — idea-routes; linking; evaluation; where business ideas come from; testing an idea against the market; judging opportunities; generating ideas; where business ideas come from / first steps; Ideas-and-creativity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
