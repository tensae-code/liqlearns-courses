# Hands-on — Business Ideas and Opportunities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find one thing near you that is lying unused — scrap wood, an empty corner, an hour of free time. Name one useful product or service it could become. Then list what you would need: what you yourself bring (personal), what things and money (material), and what knowledge and skills (non-material). That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a business begins with an idea and an opportunity — and the entrepreneurial skill is turning ideas and opportunities into work of value by managing the resources at hand: personal, material and non-material — then choosing what to produce and testing the idea before trusting it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
