# Video script — Business Ideas and Opportunities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business begins with an idea and an opportunity — and the entrepreneurial skill is turning ideas and opportunities into work of value by managing the resources at hand: personal, material and non-material — then choosing what to produce and testing the idea before trusting it.

Frames the whole subject's entrepreneurship fields as ideas and opportunities plus resources: the entrepreneurial skill is turning ideas and opportunities into work of value by managing the resources …. Teaches that materials lying around in the local environment (scrap and recycled wood) are a low-cost production resource and an opportunity: they can be turned by creative design into useful goods …. Deciding what goods or services to produce: profitability, the level of competition (offering something new makes a firm "product oriented", improving what exists makes it "market oriented") ….

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
