# Business — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course — Business — organised by **units and levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not
when a school calendar says so. The school grade a book comes
from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never
as the course's organisation. Business is one of the three
courses of the Business / Commerce / Accounting split: the
running of a business itself lives here; trade lives in
Commerce; the books of account live in Accounting.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the Business and
Business Studies books of Dominica, Ecuador, Egypt, Iran, Kenya, Malaysia,
Nigeria and Samoa — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the contents maps,
and the acceptance reckoning.
