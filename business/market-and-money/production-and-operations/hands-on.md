# Hands-on — Production and Operations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Watch one thing being made near you — food, furniture, a mat, a repair. Name the land, labour, capital and enterprise that combined to make it, and one point where the maker divided the work or used a tool to work faster. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: production combines land, labour, capital and enterprise to make goods — and running operations well means managing the firm's scale, its physical resources and its technology, so output is efficient, of good quality, and no more than can be sold. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
