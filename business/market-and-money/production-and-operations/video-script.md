# Video script — Production and Operations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Production combines land, labour, capital and enterprise to make goods — and running operations well means managing the firm's scale, its physical resources and its technology, so output is efficient, of good quality, and no more than can be sold.

Telling the size of a firm: by number of employees, volume of output, floor area of premises, capital invested in assets, production methods/technology, size of market served and sales volume — and …. Economies of scale as a firm grows: internal economies (marketing, financial, risk-bearing, managerial, technical, research and welfare) and external economies from the growth of the whole industry. Diseconomies of scale: internal (managerial diseconomies as control and coordination weaken, rising overhead costs) and external (scramble for raw materials, land, labour and markets).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
