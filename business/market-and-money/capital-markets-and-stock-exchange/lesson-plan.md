# Lesson plan — Capital Markets and the Stock Exchange

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Capital Markets and the Stock Exchange
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** on the capital market a company raises long-term money in the primary market — by offer for subscription, offer for sale, rights issue, private placement or introduction — and investors then trade those securities in the secondary market, through brokers, under the exchange's discipline.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Market and Money, lesson 18 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Finance, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two markets → primary = new money into the company; secondary = existing securities changing hands between investors.
- Go back to the one thing: on the capital market a company raises long-term money in the primary market — by offer for subscription, offer for sale, rights issue, private placement or introduction — and investors then trade those securities in the secondary market, through brokers, under the exchange's discipline. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
