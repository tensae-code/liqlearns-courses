# Video script — Capital Markets and the Stock Exchange

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
On the capital market a company raises long-term money in the primary market — by offer for subscription, offer for sale, rights issue, private placement or introduction — and investors then trade those securities in the secondary market, through brokers, under the exchange's discipline.

Raising funds in the primary market: offer for subscription (and the IPO), offer for sale, rights issue, private placement and introduction, and the prospectus that invites the public. Trading in the secondary market: approaching a broker, limit/market/stop-loss orders, margin credit, and the trading mechanisms (open outcry, call-over, electronic and online trading) and the OTC …. The newer instruments: mutual funds/unit trusts (including REITs and hedge funds), asset-backed securities and exchange-traded funds, and derivatives (forwards, futures, options, swaps).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
