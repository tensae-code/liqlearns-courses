# Capital Markets and the Stock Exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about proposed new lesson on capital markets and the stock exchange? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Raising funds in the primary market: offer for subscription (and the IPO), offer for sale, rights issue, private placement and introduction, and the prospectus that invites the public.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about proposed capital-markets lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trading in the secondary market: approaching a broker, limit/market/stop-loss orders, margin credit, and the trading mechanisms (open outcry, call-over, electronic and online trading) and the OTC market.

**3.** Explain the investments part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Saving vs investing, and the main investments: shares (owning part of a company; buying low and selling high; high-risk growth shares vs low-risk utility shares; capital can be lost); bonds and debentures (IOUs from governments and companies, secured vs unsecured, tradable on stock exchanges); mutual funds (pooled money spread across many shares and bonds to diversify risk); and taking advice from a financial adviser

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about raising funds on the market? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Mutual funds (सामूहिक कोष) treated among the ways business capital is raised, alongside shares and debentures
