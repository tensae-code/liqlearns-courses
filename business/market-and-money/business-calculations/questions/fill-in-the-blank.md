# Business Calculations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ a small job: a plumbing practical activity is built around working out the cost of changing the candles (cartridges) of a home water filter, and its assessment extends the same costing to fitting basins, taps and mixers.
**Answer:** Costing — the lesson's own word in that sentence.

**2.** What a shop price is made of: the price paid for bread ____ of the cost of producing the bread, the cost of marketing the bread and the profit taken by the shopkeeper.
**Answer:** consists — the lesson's own word in that sentence.
