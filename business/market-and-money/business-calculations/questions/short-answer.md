# Business Calculations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the working-out-what-a-job-costs?
**Answer:** Costing a small job: a plumbing practical activity is built around working out the cost of changing the candles (cartridges) of a home water filter, and its assessment extends the same costing to fitting basins, taps and mixers.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the pricing-and-profit-margin?
**Answer:** A worked priced estimate (مقايسة) for supplying and fitting a 3-stage water filter: every item is listed with its unit, quantity and unit price, the line prices are totalled, and a 20% profit is then added to reach the final amount — the book's own way of teaching how a tradesperson prices a job.

**3.** Explain the price, demand and revenue calculations part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** How a price change affects total revenue, worked through with numbers: a 10% price cut on bananas raises weekly revenue ($400 → $432) because demand rises 20%, while the same cut on sea salt lowers revenue ($300 → $283.50) because demand rises only 5%
