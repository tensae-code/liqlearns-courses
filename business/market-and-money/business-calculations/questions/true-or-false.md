# Business Calculations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A full landed-cost build-up for an imported television: production cost + delivery = landed cost, + tariffs/taxes = importer's cost, + importer mark-up, + retailer mark-up, + VAGST = the customer's price — Samoa's ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that the build-up of a selling price has nothing to do with business calculations.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the build-up of a selling price is one of its own parts.
