# Business Calculations

## Do it first

Price one loaf of bread in your head, in steps: what the bread cost to produce, what it cost to bring it to the shop and show it, and the shopkeeper's profit. Add the three steps: that total is the shop price. That is this lesson.

## Costing a small job

**The working-out-what-a-job-costs.** Costing a small job: a plumbing practical activity is built around working out the cost of changing the candles (cartridges) of a home water filter, and its assessment extends the same costing to fitting basins, taps and mixers.

## The priced estimate — costings, then the profit added

**The pricing-and-profit-margin.** A worked priced estimate (مقايسة) for supplying and fitting a 3-stage water filter: every item is listed with its unit, quantity and unit price, the line prices are totalled, and a 20% profit is then added to reach the final amount — the book's own way of teaching how a tradesperson prices a job.

## The landed cost of an imported television

**Project/import costing at business-studies level.** A full landed-cost build-up for an imported television: production cost + delivery = landed cost, + tariffs/taxes = importer's cost, + importer mark-up, + retailer mark-up, + VAGST = the customer's price — Samoa's worked example of how each stage's margin builds the final price.

## What a shop price is made of

**The build-up of a selling price.** What a shop price is made of: the price paid for bread consists of the cost of producing the bread, the cost of marketing the bread and the profit taken by the shopkeeper.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a price is built up step by step — the cost of the job or the product first, then each stage's charges and mark-ups and the tax, with the profit added to reach the final amount the customer pays. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: the working-out-what-a-job-costs, the pricing-and-profit-margin, project/import costing at business-studies level, the build-up of a selling price. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
