# Hands-on — Business Calculations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Price one loaf of bread in your head, in steps: what the bread cost to produce, what it cost to bring it to the shop and show it, and the shopkeeper's profit. Add the three steps: that total is the shop price. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a price is built up step by step — the cost of the job or the product first, then each stage's charges and mark-ups and the tax, with the profit added to reach the final amount the customer pays. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
