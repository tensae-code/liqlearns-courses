# Video script — Business Calculations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A price is built up step by step — the cost of the job or the product first, then each stage's charges and mark-ups and the tax, with the profit added to reach the final amount the customer pays.

Costing a small job: a plumbing practical activity is built around working out the cost of changing the candles (cartridges) of a home water filter, and its assessment extends the same costing to …. A worked priced estimate (مقايسة) for supplying and fitting a 3-stage water filter: every item is listed with its unit, quantity and unit price, the line prices are totalled, and a 20% profit is then …. A full landed-cost build-up for an imported television: production cost + delivery = landed cost, + tariffs/taxes = importer's cost, + importer mark-up, + retailer mark-up, + VAGST = the customer's ….

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
