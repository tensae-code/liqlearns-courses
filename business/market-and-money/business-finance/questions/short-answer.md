# Business Finance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about opening? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The sources of finance available to a business, in overview.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about types? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Internal versus external finance: the types in each family (own capital and retained profit against loans, hire purchase, shares and the rest).

**3.** Explain the financial regulation part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Why financial institutions are regulated: to protect individuals (their savings and fair interest), to protect businesses (qualified providers and honest, impartial advice) and to protect the economy (competition, choice and confidence in the system); consumer protection rules, supervision and inspection by the regulatory authorities

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about sources of income? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Revenue flows beyond the straight sale (TIC page): a venture can also earn through service fees, subscriptions, lending and rentals, licences, commissions and advertising, and must record income by its origin

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about raising and sizing finance? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Writing the plan, part 4 — the financial programme and schedule: a short-term programme for correctly controlling the business's financial resources, stating the amount of costs, the amount of capital needed and how it will be supplied to launch the business

**6.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about sources of finance? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Working capital and venture capital: working capital keeps daily operations continuous — regular production, paying suppliers, workers and staff on time, keeping customers satisfied — and venture capital is the start-up capital for new ventures

**7.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about sources of finance / borrowing dangers? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The guide's financing warning inside step 7: draw up the business plan so the venture can reach formal programmes and lenders with backing, and avoid falling into the hands of moneylenders (usureros)

**8.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about judging an investment? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Net Present Value: the difference between the present value of the cash inflows and the initial investment, discounting at the required rate of return (the discount rate, which rises with the alternatives available and the project's risk); the decision rule — accept if NPV is zero or positive, reject if negative, and the higher the NPV the better the project when ranking;

**9.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about sources of finance? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Sources of business finance: personal savings, donations, inheritance, borrowing, shares, profits, sale of personal property, prizes and rewards, and bonds — a bond being a promise to systematically repay a borrowed amount by the end of a clearly stated period, its investors receiving regular interest payments, fixed or floating
