# Video script — Business Finance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every business needs finance at each of its stages — from its own capital and retained profit, from bank credit, suppliers and customers, and for the long term from the capital market — and the finance skill is knowing the sources and choosing the one that fits the need.

The sources of finance available to a business, in overview. Internal versus external finance: the types in each family (own capital and retained profit against loans, hire purchase, shares and the rest). Why a business needs financing at each stage.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
