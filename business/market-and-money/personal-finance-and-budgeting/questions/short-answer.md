# Personal Finance and Budgeting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does budget analysis do with a budget?
**Answer:** It finds the peaks and troughs, works out the yearly surplus, deficit or balance, prepares a budget forecast, cuts spending to match income, and sets savings targets.

**2.** Name three ways of saving the book teaches, and one fact about each.
**Answer:** Interest-bearing deposit accounts (withdrawal limits and minimum balances); short-term fixed deposits (higher interest, money locked to the term's end); the sou-sou (members contribute equally and take the pool in turn; unregulated, built on trust).

**3.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about a person's own budgeting system?
**Answer:** Improving the family budget (Educación financiera page): the budget's parts — income against basic monthly spending, secondary spending, non-monthly outgoings, unforeseen payments, debt payments, investments and savings — with surplus as the goal and debt as the sign of deficit

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about a person's own money?
**Answer:** What determines saving and investment, and why people do them: savings are determined by availability of financial institutions, knowledge of the saving culture, clear goals for saving and high motivation to save; investment by availability of investment opportunities, favourable government policies and determination to work hard;
