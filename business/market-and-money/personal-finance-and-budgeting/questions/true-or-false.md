# Personal Finance and Budgeting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A fixed deposit usually pays higher interest, but the money is locked until the term ends.
**Answer: True** — That is the short-term fixed deposit as the book teaches it.

**2.** A budget only needs making when income is the same every month.
**Answer: False** — Income that varies, like seasonal work, is exactly why budget analysis looks for peaks and troughs.
