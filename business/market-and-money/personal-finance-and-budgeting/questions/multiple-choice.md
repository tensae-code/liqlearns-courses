# Personal Finance and Budgeting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a budget?
a) A list of things you wish you could buy  b) A spending plan that balances income against expenditure  c) A bank account
**Answer: b) A spending plan that balances income against expenditure** — That is the book's definition, and budget analysis then works the plan.

**2.** What is a sou-sou?
a) A fixed deposit at a bank  b) An informal rotating savings club where members contribute equally and take the pool in turn  c) A type of loan shark
**Answer: b) An informal rotating savings club where members contribute equally and take the pool in turn** — It is unregulated and built on trust.

**3.** What does this lesson teach about a person's own budgeting system?
a) Improving the family budget (Educación financiera page): the budget's parts — income against basic monthly…
b) The family budget (Educación financiera page): a monthly model budget listing income (wages, overtime,…
c) Optimising family debts (Educación financiera page): good or convenient debts — those that buy something that…
**Answer: b) The family budget (Educación financiera page): a monthly model budget listing income (wages, overtime,…** — That is what this lesson teaches in its a person's own budgeting system part (Ecuador's book).

**4.** What does this lesson teach about a person's own money?
