# Personal Finance and Budgeting — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a person controls their own money with a budget — a spending plan that balances income against expenditure, fixed outgoings first and flexible spending after — watched for its peaks and troughs, and with saving done on purpose toward set goals, in ways that run from a bank deposit account to the sou-sou. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
