# Lesson plan — Personal Finance and Budgeting

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Personal Finance and Budgeting
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a person controls their own money with a budget — a spending plan that balances income against expenditure, fixed outgoings first and flexible spending after — watched for its peaks and troughs, and with saving done on purpose toward set goals, in ways that run from a bank deposit account to the sou-sou.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Market and Money, lesson 20 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Calculations, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a budget is only for people with plenty → a budget matters most when money is tight or uneven: it shows the peaks and troughs before they arrive, so spending can be cut to match income in time, not in crisis.
- Go back to the one thing: a person controls their own money with a budget — a spending plan that balances income against expenditure, fixed outgoings first and flexible spending after — watched for its peaks and troughs, and with saving done on purpose toward set goals, in ways that run from a bank deposit account to the sou-sou. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
