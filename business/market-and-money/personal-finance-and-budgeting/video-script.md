# Video script — Personal Finance and Budgeting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
a person controls their own money with a budget — a spending plan that balances income against expenditure, fixed outgoings first and flexible spending after — watched for its peaks and troughs, and with saving done on purpose toward set goals, in ways that run from a bank deposit account to the sou-sou.

Budgeting and financial planning: A budget is a spending plan: it balances income against expenditure before the money is spent.

Ways of saving: Saving is putting money aside — for a rainy day, and for set goals.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
