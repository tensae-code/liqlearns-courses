# Lesson plan — Marketing

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Marketing
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** marketing is directing the flow of goods from producer to consumer — knowing the market through research first, then mixing product, price, place and promotion so purchasing power becomes effective demand.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Market and Money, lesson 15 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on Selling and Customer Care, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What marketing is** — what marketing is; marketing concept; business-functions.
2. **Knowing the market — research first** — market research; market research; branding.
3. **The marketing mix** — marketing mix; the marketing mix in the book's four-part form; consumer behaviour; packaging.
4. **Product and price** — product; price; product/price; the marketing mix and product life cycle; pricing strategies.
5. **Place, selling and new ways of marketing** — place (distribution); the selling-and-marketing-the-product-made; the knowing-what-customers-want / where-the-product-is-sold; opening; preparation; comparison; the role of marketing; knowing the customer; the marketing mix; preparing to sell; the marketing mix / offering; place in the marketing mix; knowing the market; knowing the competition; market research / selling into the market; How-the-product-will-be-marketed; Marketing-the-product-you-made; Marketing-the-product-you-made; Pricing-and-selling; Selling-the-project's-product; What-a-market-is; Marketing-mix; Market-niche; Niche-and-competition-analysis; Market-research; Market-scale part; Marketing-mix part; Market-share part; Marketing-department part; Market research part; Marketing part.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: marketing is directing the flow of goods from producer to consumer — knowing the market through research first, then mixing product, price, place and promotion so purchasing power becomes effective demand.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or market.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.
