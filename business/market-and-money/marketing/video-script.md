The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Marketing is directing the flow of goods from producer to consumer — knowing the market through research first, then mixing product, price, place and promotion so purchasing power becomes effective demand.

States plainly that the useful products and decorations made from recycled wood can be sold and marketed, making the making activity a direct source of money for the maker and a benefit to family …. The same project brief ties the feasibility study to the market: the fretwork models must be useful items in present-day demand (المرغوبة والمطلوبة), and the finished products can be marketed inside …. Marketing and sales as part of starting a business.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
