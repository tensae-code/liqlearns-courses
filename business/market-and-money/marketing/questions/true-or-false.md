# Marketing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The marketing mix as a combination of product, price, place and promotion offered at a point in time, and market segmentation: dividing the market and matching a mix to each segment, with its criteria. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that the marketing mix in the book's four-part form has nothing to do with marketing. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the marketing mix in the book's four-part form is one of its own parts.
