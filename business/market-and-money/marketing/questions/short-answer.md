# Marketing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the selling-and-marketing-the-product-made? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** States plainly that the useful products and decorations made from recycled wood can be sold and marketed, making the making activity a direct source of money for the maker and a benefit to family, school and community.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the knowing-what-customers-want / where-the-product-is-sold? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The same project brief ties the feasibility study to the market: the fretwork models must be useful items in present-day demand (المرغوبة والمطلوبة), and the finished products can be marketed inside the school or outside it.

**3.** Explain the market research part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Market research as the first marketing activity: systematically collecting and analysing information on consumer preferences and market segmentation (dividing the market into groups by age, gender, income etc. to find the target market), consumer behaviour and spending patterns, and competition

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the marketing mix? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The market variables (marketing mix): product, price, place (plaza) and promotion — the 4 Ps — plus a fifth variable for new ventures, personalisation of the sale

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the marketing mix / offering? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Designing the value proposition (part 2): the value proposition is the reason customers prefer one business over others — a chosen bundle of products or services meeting a particular segment's needs — and the ways of developing one, such as novelty and brand/status
