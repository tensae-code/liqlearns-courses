# Hands-on — Business Communication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one message through a business you know: a manager's instruction going down to the workers, a worker's report going up, and two departments talking across to each other. Name the medium of each — spoken, written, electronic, gesture. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: inside a firm, people communicate in writing, in speech, electronically and by gesture — and the messages flow downward from managers, upward from workers, and laterally between departments and people at the same level. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
