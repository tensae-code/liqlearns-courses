# Video script — Business Communication

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Inside a firm, people communicate in writing, in speech, electronically and by gesture — and the messages flow downward from managers, upward from workers, and laterally between departments and people at the same level.

Communication inside the firm: written, oral, electronic and gesture media, and downward, upward and lateral flows between and within departments.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
