# Business Communication — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Communication inside the firm: written, oral, electronic and gesture media, and downward, upward and lateral flows between and within departments.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that communication has nothing to do with business communication.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: communication is one of its own parts.
