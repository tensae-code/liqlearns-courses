# Business Communication

## Do it first

Follow one message through a business you know: a manager's instruction going down to the workers, a worker's report going up, and two departments talking across to each other. Name the medium of each — spoken, written, electronic, gesture. That is this lesson.

## Communication inside the firm

**Communication.** Communication inside the firm: written, oral, electronic and gesture media, and downward, upward and lateral flows between and within departments.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: inside a firm, people communicate in writing, in speech, electronically and by gesture — and the messages flow downward from managers, upward from workers, and laterally between departments and people at the same level. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: communication. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: inside a firm, people communicate in writing, in speech, electronically and by gesture — and the messages flow downward from managers, upward from workers, and laterally between departments and people at the same level.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
