# Lesson plan — Small Business Management

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Starting and Running · **Lesson:** Small Business Management
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Starting and Running (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** small firms live beside large ones for real reasons — small markets, personal services, flexibility and the owner's wish to keep control — and they fail most often through mismanagement, so records, groundwork and enough capital decide whether a small business lasts.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 2 — Starting and Running, lesson 14 of 26 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Communication, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
