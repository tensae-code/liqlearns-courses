# Hands-on — Small Business Management

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find the smallest business near you. Ask (or work out): why is it small — is its market small, its service personal, or does its owner simply want to stay in control? Then name one mistake that could kill it within a year. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: small firms live beside large ones for real reasons — small markets, personal services, flexibility and the owner's wish to keep control — and they fail most often through mismanagement, so records, groundwork and enough capital decide whether a small business lasts. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
