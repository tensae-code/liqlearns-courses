# Small Business Management — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Why small firms ____ to exist beside large ones: small size of the market, nature of the product (personal services), simplicity of organisation, flexibility, quick decision-making, the owner's belief a small firm is more manageable, rising production costs, the need to retain control, and legal constraints.
