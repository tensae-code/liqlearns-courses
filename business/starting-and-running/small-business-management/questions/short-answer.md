# Small Business Management — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about small firms?
**Answer:** Why small firms continue to exist beside large ones: small size of the market, nature of the product (personal services), simplicity of organisation, flexibility, quick decision-making, the owner's belief a small firm is more manageable, rising production costs, the need to retain control, and legal constraints.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about why small businesses succeed or fail?
**Answer:** Why businesses fail and succeed: fraud, neglect, disasters and — the most common cause, about 80% in NZ — mismanagement; good businesses learn from mistakes instead of repeating them.

**3.** Explain the small businesses part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Small businesses and MSMEs in the Caribbean (CARICOM size definitions), cottage industries (home-based, manual, local materials — characteristics, advantages, disadvantages) vs small businesses with premises and paid employees; the functions small businesses perform (jobs, niche and personalised products, competition, choice) and their advantages and disadvantages (easy set-up and flexibility vs skills gaps, difficulty raising finance, inability to match large firms' costs)

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about running a small business?
**Answer:** What makes a project "small": ownership is not separated from management (the owner is the manager), capital is provided by one person or a small group of owners, and the project is small relative to its industry or service sector;

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about running a small business well?
**Answer:** Factors leading to success in business: the personal and entrepreneurial qualities of the owner; clear and definite objectives, with the operating and management guidelines set to achieve them closely followed; proper location, securing inputs (materials, labour, power) and market access at low cost;
