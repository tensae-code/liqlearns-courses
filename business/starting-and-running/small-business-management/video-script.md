# Video script — Small Business Management

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Small firms live beside large ones for real reasons — small markets, personal services, flexibility and the owner's wish to keep control — and they fail most often through mismanagement, so records, groundwork and enough capital decide whether a small business lasts.

Why small firms continue to exist beside large ones: small size of the market, nature of the product (personal services), simplicity of organisation, flexibility, quick decision-making, the owner's …. Why businesses fail and succeed: fraud, neglect, disasters and — the most common cause, about 80% in NZ — mismanagement; good businesses learn from mistakes instead of repeating them. A bought business that lasted six months: the lessons are to examine all the business records and recent accounts before buying, check for new competitors, allow for the owner's hours and family ….

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
