# Video script — The Business Plan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business plan is the written plan of the whole business — its purpose, market, organisation, production, marketing and financial plans — prepared before starting so the entrepreneur, and the financiers, suppliers and customers who read it, can judge whether the business can work; a feasibility study tests the idea first.

Sets a group task to prepare an economic feasibility study (دراسة جدوى اقتصادية) for a small production project: pupils design and produce useful fretwork models for it — the book's only …. What a business plan (Rancangan Perniagaan) is. Why preparing a business plan matters.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
