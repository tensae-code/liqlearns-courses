# The Business Plan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What a business plan is. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: writing a business plan is one of its own parts.
