# The Business Plan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the feasibility-study? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sets a group task to prepare an economic feasibility study (دراسة جدوى اقتصادية) for a small production project: pupils design and produce useful fretwork models for it — the book's only feasibility-study teaching, carried as project work at the end of the fretwork lesson.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about opening? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** What a business plan (Rancangan Perniagaan) is.

**3.** Explain the feasibility study and market research part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The feasibility study before starting: market research from primary and secondary sources, qualitative vs quantitative research, and how the study's findings (viability, costs, sources of finance) feed into the business plan; planning over short, medium and long-term goals

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about structure of the plan? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** The contents of an investment project: executive summary, description of the venture, operational plan (organisation structure, staff functions, production/service processes, technology, investment, costs), communication plan, financial plan (projected income and outgoings, contribution margin, break-even point) and project evaluation

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about what a business plan is? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** The business plan: everyone entering business must have one — a written, organised document stating how a business's activities will be carried out over a set period; a map that answers three questions: where are you, where do you want to go, and how will you reach the destination
