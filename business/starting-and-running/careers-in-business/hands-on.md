# Hands-on — Careers in Business

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person you know and list the different work they have done over their life. One item on the list was a job; the whole list together is their career. Then ask: what did they get better at by staying with one kind of work — and what did staying cost them? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work you build over a life — and business study opens many career paths, where specialising in one occupation brings expertise, security and earnings, but also monotony, and makes change hard when the specialism fades. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
