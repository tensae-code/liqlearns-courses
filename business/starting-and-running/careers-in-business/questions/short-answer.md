# Careers in Business — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two benefits and two drawbacks of occupational specialisation.
**Answer:** Benefits (any two): expertise, security, earnings. Drawbacks: monotony, and change being hard when the specialism becomes obsolete.

**2.** Name three career options that business study opens.
**Answer:** Any three of: production, marketing, finance and accounts, human resources, banking, insurance, selling and customer care, management, entrepreneurship.
