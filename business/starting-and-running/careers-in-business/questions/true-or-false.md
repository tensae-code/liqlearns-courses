# Careers in Business — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A specialist is usually harder to replace than a generalist, which brings security.
**Answer: True** — Security is one of specialisation's named benefits.

**2.** Business study opens only one career path: founding your own business.
**Answer: False** — It opens many: the functional areas of firms, banking, insurance, selling, managing — and founding a business.
