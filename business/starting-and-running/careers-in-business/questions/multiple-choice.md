# Careers in Business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the difference between a job and a career?
a) There is none  b) A job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work built over a life  c) A career is one post; a job is the whole path
**Answer: b) A job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work built over a life** — A person can change jobs several times inside one career.

**2.** Which of these is a drawback of specialising?
a) Expertise  b) Higher earnings  c) Monotony, and change being hard when the specialism becomes obsolete
**Answer: c) Monotony, and change being hard when the specialism becomes obsolete** — Expertise, security and earnings are specialisation's benefits; monotony and hard change are its costs.
