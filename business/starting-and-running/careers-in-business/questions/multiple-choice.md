**1.** What is the difference between a job and a career?
a) There is none  b) A job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work built over a life  c) A career is one post; a job is the whole path
**Answer: b) A job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work built over a life** — A person can change jobs several times inside one career.
