# Lesson plan — Careers in Business

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Starting and Running · **Lesson:** Careers in Business
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Starting and Running (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work you build over a life — and business study opens many career paths, where specialising in one occupation brings expertise, security and earnings, but also monotony, and makes change hard when the specialism fades.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 2 — Starting and Running, lesson 12 of 26 in the course ladder; it stands on Human Resources in Business, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
