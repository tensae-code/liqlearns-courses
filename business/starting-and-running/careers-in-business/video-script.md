# Video script — Careers in Business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
a job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work you build over a life — and business study opens many career paths, where specialising in one occupation brings expertise, security and earnings, but also monotony, and makes change hard when the specialism fades.

Job and career: A job is a single post — the work you hold at one place and time.

Specialising in an occupation: Most careers are built by specialising: settling into one occupation and going deep in it.

Career options in business: Business study opens a wide range of career options: the book surveys the paths business opens — working in the functional areas of firms (production, marketing, finance and accounts, human resources), in banking and insurance, in selling and customer care, in managing and running businesses, and in founding a business of your own as an entrepreneur.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
