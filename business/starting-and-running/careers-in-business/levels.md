# Careers in Business — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a job is one post you hold; a career is the whole path of work you build over a life — and business study opens many career paths, where specialising in one occupation brings expertise, security and earnings, but also monotony, and makes change hard when the specialism fades. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
