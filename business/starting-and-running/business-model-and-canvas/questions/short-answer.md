# Business Model and Canvas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the opening part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The concept of a business model: how a business creates and delivers value, as the foundation the canvas builds on (Bhutan's book.)
