# Business Model and Canvas — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a business model is how a business creates and delivers value, and the Business Model Canvas lays that model out in nine building blocks — the value proposition at the centre — so a model can be built block by block, and a weak model can be diagnosed from its failure signs — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
