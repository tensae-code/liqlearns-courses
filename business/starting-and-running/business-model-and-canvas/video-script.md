# Video script — Business Model and Canvas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business model is how a business creates and delivers value, and the Business Model Canvas lays that model out in nine building blocks — the value proposition at the centre — so a model can be built block by block, and a weak model can be diagnosed from its failure signs.

The concept of a business model: how a business creates and delivers value, as the foundation the canvas builds on. The Business Model Canvas (BMC): its nine building blocks worked through, including the value proposition (the products and services that fulfil customer needs) and the cost structure (the costs of implementing the model, used to decide whether to proceed). Developing the business model block by block in depth (key activities — the activities without which the business cannot operate — and the other blocks), as the Class XII working-out of the canvas. Diagnosing a weak business model and a worked Bhutanese model: the failure signs (weak brand image, high churn, misaligned value proposition and customer needs, inefficient or poorly integrated channels, low switching costs) and a wastepaper-recycling enterprise's model that preserves Bhutan's environment while employing youth.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
