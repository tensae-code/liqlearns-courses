# Business Model and Canvas

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — The business model and its canvas

**Opening part of the proposed lesson.** The concept of a business model: how a business creates and delivers value, as the foundation the canvas builds on (Bhutan's book.)

**Canvas part of the proposed lesson.** The Business Model Canvas (BMC): its nine building blocks worked through, including the value proposition (the products and services that fulfil customer needs) and the cost structure (the costs of implementing the model, used to decide whether to proceed) (Bhutan's book.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Developing and diagnosing the model

**Development part of the proposed lesson.** Developing the business model block by block in depth (key activities — the activities without which the business cannot operate — and the other blocks), as the Class XII working-out of the canvas (Bhutan's book.)

**Diagnosis part of the proposed lesson.** Diagnosing a weak business model and a worked Bhutanese model: the failure signs (weak brand image, high churn, misaligned value proposition and customer needs, inefficient or poorly integrated channels, low switching costs) and a wastepaper-recycling enterprise's model that preserves Bhutan's environment while employing youth (Bhutan's book.)
