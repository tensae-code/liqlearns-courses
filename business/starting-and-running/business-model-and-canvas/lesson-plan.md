# Lesson plan — Business Model and Canvas

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Starting and Running · **Lesson:** Business Model and Canvas
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Starting and Running (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business model is how a business creates and delivers value, and the Business Model Canvas lays that model out in nine building blocks — the value proposition at the centre — so a model can be built block by block, and a weak model can be diagnosed from its failure signs.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Starting and Running, lesson 9 of 26 in the course ladder; it stands on The Business Plan, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
