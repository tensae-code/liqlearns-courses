# Hands-on — Selling and Customer Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the last thing you bought from a person, not a machine. Walk through what the seller did: how they greeted you, showed you the goods, answered your doubts, agreed the price and the terms, and how they treated you after you paid. That whole path is selling and customer care. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: selling is a process with steps — prepare, find the buyer, present the goods, handle objections, close the sale — done on terms both sides understand, and it is finished only by customer care: looking after the buyer before, during and after the purchase so that they come back. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
