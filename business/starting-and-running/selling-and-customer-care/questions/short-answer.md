# Selling and Customer Care — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the five steps of the personal selling process. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Preparation, prospecting, presenting, handling rejections, and closing the sale (with follow-up).

**2.** Give three forms customer service can take. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any three of: warranties, after-sales service, feedback, online chat, toll-free numbers/call centres, suggestion boxes, surveys.
