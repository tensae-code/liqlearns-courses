# Selling and Customer Care — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the right order of the personal selling process? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Preparation, prospecting, presenting, handling rejections, closing the sale  b) Closing, paying, delivering, advertising  c) Prospecting, closing, preparing, presenting
**Answer: a) Preparation, prospecting, presenting, handling rejections, closing the sale** — Those are the book's five steps, in order.

**2.** Under which term of sale does the seller hold the goods while the buyer pays for them? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Consignment  b) Layaway  c) Cash
**Answer: b) Layaway** — In layaway the seller keeps the item until it is paid for; no interest is charged.
