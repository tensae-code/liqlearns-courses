# Selling and Customer Care — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Customer service ends when the customer pays. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — Customer service cares for the customer's needs before, during and after the purchase.

**2.** Empathy, persistence and communication are qualities of successful salespeople. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — The book names empathy, focus, responsibility, persistence, enthusiasm and communication.
