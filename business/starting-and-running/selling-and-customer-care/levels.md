# Selling and Customer Care — depth

This lesson is divided into sub lessons, each proved before the next opens. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — selling is a process with steps — prepare, find the buyer, present the goods, handle objections, close the sale — done on terms both sides understand, and it is finished only by customer care: looking after the buyer before, during and after the purchase so that they come back. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
