# Video script — Selling and Customer Care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
selling is a process with steps — prepare, find the buyer, present the goods, handle objections, close the sale — done on terms both sides understand, and it is finished only by customer care: looking after the buyer before, during and after the purchase so that they come back.

Selling: Personal (direct) selling is done by a salesperson, face to face with the buyer.

Terms of sale: Every sale is made on terms, and each term favours buyer or seller differently: cash (paid at once), credit (goods now, payment later), hire purchase (instalments, ownership at the last payment), layaway (the seller holds the goods while the buyer pays), consignment (goods left with a seller who pays only for what is sold), and cash and trade discounts between businesses.

Customer service: Customer service is caring for the customer's needs before, during and after the purchase.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
