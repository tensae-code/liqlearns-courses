# Human Resources in Business — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about opening? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** What human resource management means in a business.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about roles? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The roles HRM plays in a business (planning manpower, recruiting, developing and keeping people).

**3.** Explain the dispute resolution and trade unions part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Resolving disputes: grievance procedures, and the third-party routes — mediation (facilitates, does not decide), arbitration (both sides accept the arbiter's binding ruling in advance) and conciliation (peacemaker who drafts a non-binding settlement); trade unions, collective bargaining and what makes a union's bargaining position strong
