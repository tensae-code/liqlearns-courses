# Video script — Human Resources in Business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human resource management is the business's work with its people — planning how many are needed, recruiting them, developing and keeping them, and paying them — because a business grows and lasts through the skills and employability of its workers.

What human resource management means in a business. The roles HRM plays in a business (planning manpower, recruiting, developing and keeping people). Applying HRM so the business develops sustainably.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
