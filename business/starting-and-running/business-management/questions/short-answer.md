# Business Management — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about organisation-structure? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Creating an organisation structure for a business.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about opening? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** What management is.

**3.** Explain the stakeholders part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Stakeholders: internal (owners/shareholders, board of directors, managers, employees) and external (government, customers, suppliers, lending institutions, competitors, the community), with the role each group plays and why their interests can conflict

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about leading a team? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The team guide (leader): the qualities a team's guide needs — foresight about the team's results, high scientific and skill competence, strong communication and interaction, high moral standing that builds trust, and the authority to stop the team falling apart; the synergy idea that a team achieves more than the sum of its members (2 + 2 > 4)

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about running an organisation? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Company meetings: the shareholders' annual general meeting, the special/extraordinary general meeting (called for urgent matters such as mergers, bonus shares, buy-back of shares, or converting private to public), and meetings of the board of directors (approving financial statements, recommending dividends, plans and delegation of authority, with dissent recorded in the minutes)
