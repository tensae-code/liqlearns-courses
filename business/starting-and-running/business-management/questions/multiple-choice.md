# Business Management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about organisation-structure? (Sub lesson 4)
a) Creating an organisation structure for a business.
b) Privatisation: transfer of ownership to the private sector by share issue, asset sale or vouchers; why governments do it, its gains and its …
c) Non-profit organisations — clubs, churches, societies and welfare groups — formed voluntarily around a shared interest to meet needs …
d) The pre-production investigation checklist: the market (want and price), supplies/suppliers (reliability, backup suppliers, terms …
**Answer: a) Creating an organisation structure for a business.** — That is what this lesson teaches in its organisation-structure part.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about opening? (Sub lesson 1)
