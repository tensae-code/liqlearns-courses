# Hands-on — Business Management

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one group you belong to — a team, a class, a club. Name who plans what it does, who organises the work, who leads on the day, and who checks it was done well. Those four jobs are the functions of management. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: management is the work of planning, organising, leading and controlling a business's people and work so its objectives are met — done by managers at three levels, through organisation structures, and by joining all the business's functions together. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
