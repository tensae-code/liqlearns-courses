# Video script — Business Management

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Management is the work of planning, organising, leading and controlling a business's people and work so its objectives are met — done by managers at three levels, through organisation structures, and by joining all the business's functions together.

Creating an organisation structure for a business. What management is. Why management is important to a business.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
