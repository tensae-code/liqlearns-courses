# Video script — Starting a Business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Starting well means choosing where to locate, weighing the reasons and the factors before starting, and investigating the market, supplies, finances and process before producing anything — groundwork first, because businesses fail quickly without it.

Choosing where to locate a firm: availability of raw materials (heavy, bulky or perishable inputs pull the firm to their source), nearness to the market (bulky, perishable or fragile products) …. Localisation (similar firms concentrated in one area: its causes, advantages such as a labour pool, support services and shared infrastructure, and disadvantages such as pollution, regional imbalance …. The factors that push a person to start a business.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
