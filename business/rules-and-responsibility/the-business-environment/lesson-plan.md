# Lesson plan — The Business Environment

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Rules and Responsibility · **Lesson:** The Business Environment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business works inside an environment of four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — whose elements press on every decision; and keeping its own surroundings healthy, secure and clean is part of running the business well.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility, lesson 25 of 26 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Ethics and Responsibility, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
