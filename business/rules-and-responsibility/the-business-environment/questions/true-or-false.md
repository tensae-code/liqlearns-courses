# The Business Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The business environment in four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — and the elements of each that press on business decisions.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches that environment has nothing to do with the business environment.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: environment is one of its own parts.
