# The Business Environment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ a healthy business environment: avoiding or reducing each kind of pollution (clean energy, treating/recycling effluent, proper waste disposal and recycling, silencers and ear defenders, protective goggles), ensuring security inside and outside the business, keeping required resources available, and maintaining good interpersonal relations with employees, government, customers, suppliers and the public.
**Answer:** Keeping — the lesson's own word in that sentence.

**2.** The business ____ in four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — and the elements of each that press on business decisions.
**Answer:** environment — the lesson's own word in that sentence.
