# The Business Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environment?
**Answer:** Keeping a healthy business environment: avoiding or reducing each kind of pollution (clean energy, treating/recycling effluent, proper waste disposal and recycling, silencers and ear defenders, protective goggles), ensuring security inside and outside the business, keeping required resources available, and maintaining good interpersonal relations with employees, government, customers, suppliers and the public.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environment?
**Answer:** The business environment in four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — and the elements of each that press on business decisions.

**3.** Explain the government and investors part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** How government creates a safe environment for investors: the central bank can lower interest rates to stimulate lending and expansion; tax incentives boost fledgling markets and encourage start-ups (e.g. incentives for solar equipment); trade policies — lower tariffs and quotas and free-trade zones — cut business costs; government contracting puts public work out to private companies; grants, subsidies, loans and disbursements steer activity; and political stability, transparency and tackling corruption make a country trusted by investors

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the world the business sits in?
**Answer:** The venture and care of the environment: Ecuador's great biodiversity must be protected from profit-only interests, and the Constitution's articles on the rights of nature frame what a venture may do

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the world the business sits in?
**Answer:** The business environment and its components: the internal environment (organisational objectives and policies, structure, culture, resources) and the external environment, split into the task environment (customers, suppliers, competitors, government, financial institutions, pressure groups) and the general / macro environment (political and legal, economic, social-cultural, technological, natural and global environments)
