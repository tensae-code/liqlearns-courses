# The Business Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environment?
**Answer:** Keeping a healthy business environment: avoiding or reducing each kind of pollution (clean energy, treating/recycling effluent, proper waste disposal and recycling, silencers and ear defenders, protective goggles), ensuring security inside and outside the business, keeping required resources available, and maintaining good interpersonal relations with employees, government, customers, suppliers and the public.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environment?
**Answer:** The business environment in four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — and the elements of each that press on business decisions.
