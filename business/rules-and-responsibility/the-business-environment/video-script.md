# Video script — The Business Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business works inside an environment of four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — whose elements press on every decision; and keeping its own surroundings healthy, secure and clean is part of running the business well.

Keeping a healthy business environment: avoiding or reducing each kind of pollution (clean energy, treating/recycling effluent, proper waste disposal and recycling, silencers and ear defenders …. The business environment in four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — and the elements of each that press on business decisions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
