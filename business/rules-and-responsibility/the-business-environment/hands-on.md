# Hands-on — The Business Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand outside one business and look at its surroundings. Name one economic pressure on it (prices, customers' money), one technological, one social, and one political or legal. Then look at the ground and air around it: is its own environment clean and safe? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a business works inside an environment of four kinds — economic, technological, social and political/legal — whose elements press on every decision; and keeping its own surroundings healthy, secure and clean is part of running the business well. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
