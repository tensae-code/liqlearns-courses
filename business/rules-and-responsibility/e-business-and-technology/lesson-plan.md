# Lesson plan — E-Business and Technology

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Rules and Responsibility · **Lesson:** E-Business and Technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility, lesson 20 of 20 in the course ladder; it stands on The Business Environment, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or market.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names platforms but no business use → a platform earns its place by a job done: a document prepared, a product marketed, a buyer reached.
- Go back to the one thing: digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
