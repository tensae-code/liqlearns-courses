# E-Business and Technology

## Do it first

Take one product you know. First, name one document about it that software could prepare — an invoice, a price list, a report. Then name one digital platform where that product could be marketed, and say who would see it there. That is this lesson.

## Software for business work

**Software.** Application software used in business, and using it to prepare business documents and reports.

## What digital technology is

**Opening.** What digital technology is.

## The digital entrepreneur

**Digital-entrepreneurship.** The concept of digital entrepreneurship.

## Platforms that market a product

**Platforms.** The digital technology platforms used to market a product or service.

## Putting a real product on a digital platform

**Application.** Applying digital platforms to market a real product or service.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: software, opening, digital-entrepreneurship, platforms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
