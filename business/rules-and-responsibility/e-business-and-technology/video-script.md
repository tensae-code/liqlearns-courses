# Video script — E-Business and Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms.

Application software used in business, and using it to prepare business documents and reports. What digital technology is. The concept of digital entrepreneurship.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
