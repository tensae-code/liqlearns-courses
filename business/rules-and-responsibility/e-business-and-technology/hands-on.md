# Hands-on — E-Business and Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one product you know. First, name one document about it that software could prepare — an invoice, a price list, a report. Then name one digital platform where that product could be marketed, and say who would see it there. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
