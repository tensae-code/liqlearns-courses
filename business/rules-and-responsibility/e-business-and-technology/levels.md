# E-Business and Technology — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — digital technology lets a business do its work and its marketing in a new way — application software prepares its documents and reports, digital platforms carry its products to buyers, and the digital entrepreneur builds the business itself on those platforms — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
