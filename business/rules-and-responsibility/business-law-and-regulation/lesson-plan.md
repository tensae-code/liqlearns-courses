# Lesson plan — Business Law and Regulation

**Course:** Business · **Title:** Rules and Responsibility · **Lesson:** Business Law and Regulation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** business runs inside the law — contracts and how they are made, ended and enforced; agency; employment; sale of goods and hire purchase; forming and registering a business and keeping its obligations; and the law's protection of ideas, personal data and digital trade.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 4 — Rules and Responsibility, lesson 22 of 25 in the course ladder; it stands on Business Risk and Insurance, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What business law is** — opening.
2. **Contracts — made, kept valid, and ended** — contract; validity; contract; contracts in business; elements of a contract; intention and capacity; types of contract; discharge and remedies.
3. **Agency, employment, sale of goods and hire purchase** — agency; employment; sale of goods; hire purchase; employment law.
4. **Registering, complying — and deregulation** — formation-law; compliance; registration; regulation; regulation; government regulation of business; regulation and deregulation.
5. **Ideas, data and digital business in law** — intellectual-property; intellectual property; digital-law; personal-data; protection; the legal rules; the legal rules / formalising; the legal rules / taxes; the legal rules / where to register; the legal rules / registering step by step; the legal rules / tax registration; the legal rules / local licence; the legal rules / health licence; the legal rules / registering a product; the legal rules / sector licences; the legal rules / environmental permits; the legal rules / protecting the brand.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a contract must always be on paper → the lesson separates deeds, contracts that must be in writing, and contracts needing only written evidence; most everyday contracts form by offer and acceptance.
- Go back to the one thing: business runs inside the law — contracts and how they are made, ended and enforced; agency; employment; sale of goods and hire purchase; forming and registering a business and keeping its obligations; and the law's protection of ideas, personal data and digital trade. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
