# Hands-on — Business Law and Regulation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Watch one sale today — or make a small one. Ask: what was offered, what was accepted, what was paid (the consideration)? You have just seen a contract form. Then name the law standing behind that sale if the goods turned out faulty. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: business runs inside the law — contracts and how they are made, ended and enforced; agency; employment; sale of goods and hire purchase; forming and registering a business and keeping its obligations; and the law's protection of ideas, personal data and digital trade. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
