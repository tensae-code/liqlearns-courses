# Video script — Business Law and Regulation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Business runs inside the law — contracts and how they are made, ended and enforced; agency; employment; sale of goods and hire purchase; forming and registering a business and keeping its obligations; and the law's protection of ideas, personal data and digital trade.

Procedures for managing technology resources, taught the Malaysian way through protecting patents and copyright as business assets. The Acts under which a business entity is formed in Malaysia (business registration, partnership and companies legislation). The responsibilities an entrepreneur must keep meeting after registering (licences, renewals and legal obligations).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
