# Business Law and Regulation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about intellectual-property? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Procedures for managing technology resources, taught the Malaysian way through protecting patents and copyright as business assets.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about formation-law? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** The Acts under which a business entity is formed in Malaysia (business registration, partnership and companies legislation).

**3.** Explain the contracts in business part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Contracts: a voluntary, legally binding agreement (written, verbal or implied) between parties — suppliers, employees, customers, government; the book works through the contracts a business makes daily and what supplier/employment/customer contracts must set out

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the legal rules? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Pre-operational expenses: the set-up costs of complying with Ecuadorian law to create a company — notary payments, the municipal patent (operating permit), and fees to the lawyers who constitute the venture

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the legal rules? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The partnership deed (कबुलियतनामा) and its contents: name and address of the firm, names and addresses of the partners, capital contributions, sharing of profit and loss (equally, by capital ratio, or as agreed), remuneration of working partners, accounts and audit, and other provisions

**6.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about the legal rules / taxes? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The fiscal benefits of being formal: tax benefits from the official list; exports of nationally produced goods and services rendered abroad carry a 0% VAT (IVA) rate; a tax credit of 1.5% of the FOB value of exports can be applied against advance or annual income tax with the tax administration's approval;
