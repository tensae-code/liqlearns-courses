# Business Risk and Insurance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under indemnity, a person can profit from a loss by insuring goods for more than they are worth. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Indemnity puts you back where you were, never better off; over-insurance does not pay more than the loss.

**2.** Utmost good faith means both sides must tell the whole truth when the policy is taken out. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the first principle of insurance.
