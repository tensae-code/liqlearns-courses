# Business Risk and Insurance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How does insurance pay for one member's big loss? (Sub lesson 1)
a) From that member's own premiums alone  b) From the pool that all the insured pay premiums into  c) From a government grant
**Answer: b) From the pool that all the insured pay premiums into** — Insurance is the pooling of risk: the pool compensates whoever suffers the loss.

**2.** Which policy covers a business whose injured customer sues it? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Public/general liability insurance  b) Endowment life insurance  c) Event insurance only
**Answer: a) Public/general liability insurance** — Liability policies cover claims from the public and customers.
