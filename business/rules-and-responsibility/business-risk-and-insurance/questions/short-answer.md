# Business Risk and Insurance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four principles of insurance and say what each one guards. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any four of: utmost good faith (honesty), insurable interest (a real stake), indemnity (no profit from loss), contribution (insurers share), subrogation (insurer recovers from the causer), loss minimisation (act as if uninsured), proximate cause (the insured cause must be the nearest cause).

**2.** Why does insurance make business and trade possible at all? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** It transfers risk to the pool, so a firm will operate even where one uninsured loss — a fire, a lawsuit, an accident — would end it.

**3.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about risk in one's own business? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Risk analysis: listing the venture's potential risks in detail (worked example: community opposition to cutting trees for sports courts), then prioritising them by probability of occurrence and impact, and designing mitigation strategies so the entrepreneur is prepared

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about risk in one's own business? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The role of insurance in business: risk management (theft, fire, natural disaster, legal cases), business continuity, compliance with insurance law, financial stability (the insurer shares the burden of unexpected losses), protection of employees, building reputation and trust, and help with investment and expansion

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about business risk? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The risks of entrepreneurial work and small projects: income is not guaranteed — the owner may not earn large revenues at first and may even lose; the entrepreneur may lose all or part of his investment; high stress from the fear of failure, lost deals and fierce competition; long, exhausting working hours that follow him home;
