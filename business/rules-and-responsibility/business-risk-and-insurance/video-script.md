This lesson is about one thing.
insurance is the pooling of risk: many people pay a premium into one pool, and the pool pays the one who suffers the loss — it holds together only under its principles (utmost good faith, insurable interest, indemnity and the rest), and a business chooses the policy that matches each risk it faces.

Principles of insurance: Insurance works by pooling risk: the insured pays a premium into a pool, and the pool compensates whoever suffers the loss.

Types of business insurance: Insurance (against events that may happen) is distinguished from assurance (against events that must happen, like death); life insurance comes as whole life and endowment policies.
