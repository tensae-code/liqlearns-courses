# Business Ethics and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environmental responsibility?
**Answer:** How production harms the environment and community health: air pollution (acid rain, CFCs and the ozone layer, greenhouse gases, breathing harm), water pollution, noise pollution, destruction of the environment (deforestation, quarries) and solid waste pollution.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about ethics?
**Answer:** Entrepreneurial ethics connected to digital-business law.

**3.** Explain the ethics and the law in business part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Legal vs ethical issues in setting up and running a business (registration, licences, taxes, waste disposal, truthful advertising, money laundering, product safety), the principles to adopt (legitimate firm and capital, code of ethics, environmental and data-handling policies) and the consequences table for unethical/illegal practice (fines, closure, lawsuits, imprisonment, lost reputation)

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about responsibility?
**Answer:** Ventures with social interest: community and associative ventures that use nature's gifts to cover a community's needs with a social and environmental component, guided by three innovation questions (why is this done, what if it were not done, what if it were done differently)

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about responsibility?
**Answer:** Ethical principles for business: integrity (the foundation stone), fairness, transparency (clear, true, understandable information to employees, customers and stakeholders; never deceiving customers), responsibility, respect towards stakeholders, environmental responsibility, and compliance with local, national and international laws and regulations

**6.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about responsibility / the venture's principles?
**Answer:** The venture's values: the ethical principles its work rests on, drawn from the questions what do we want to achieve, how are we, what do we believe in and how do we want to be remembered; the book lists the entrepreneur's own traits as the seed of them — creative, honest, transparent, prudent, bold, risk-taking

**7.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about responsibility / the environment?
**Answer:** The environmental feasibility study: production activity can harm the environment people live in (factory smoke, waste, polluted water, noise, dust), so the project needs a study of the mutual effect between project and environment;
