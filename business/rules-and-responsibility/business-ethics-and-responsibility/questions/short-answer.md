# Business Ethics and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about environmental responsibility?
**Answer:** How production harms the environment and community health: air pollution (acid rain, CFCs and the ozone layer, greenhouse gases, breathing harm), water pollution, noise pollution, destruction of the environment (deforestation, quarries) and solid waste pollution.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about ethics?
**Answer:** Entrepreneurial ethics connected to digital-business law.

**3.** Explain the ethics and the law in business part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Legal vs ethical issues in setting up and running a business (registration, licences, taxes, waste disposal, truthful advertising, money laundering, product safety), the principles to adopt (legitimate firm and capital, code of ethics, environmental and data-handling policies) and the consequences table for unethical/illegal practice (fines, closure, lawsuits, imprisonment, lost reputation)
