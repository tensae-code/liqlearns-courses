# Hands-on — Business Ethics and Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one business near you. List the people and groups it touches: its workers, its customers, its suppliers, the government, and the neighbours who breathe its air and drink its water. Beside each, write one thing the business owes them. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a business owes responsibility beyond profit — to society at large, its suppliers, lenders, shareholders, employees, customers and government — and its production must not poison the air, water and land its neighbours live on; the entrepreneur's ethics govern how it treats people, including their personal data in digital business. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
