# Business Ethics and Responsibility

## Do it first

Name one business near you. List the people and groups it touches: its workers, its customers, its suppliers, the government, and the neighbours who breathe its air and drink its water. Beside each, write one thing the business owes them. That is this lesson.

## Production and the harm it can do

**Environmental responsibility.** How production harms the environment and community health: air pollution (acid rain, CFCs and the ozone layer, greenhouse gases, breathing harm), water pollution, noise pollution, destruction of the environment (deforestation, quarries) and solid waste pollution.

## The entrepreneur's ethics in digital business

**Ethics.** Entrepreneurial ethics connected to digital-business law.

## Responsibility to everyone the business touches

**Responsibility.** Corporate social responsibility owed in turn to society at large, suppliers, lenders, shareholders, employees, customers and government, with Nigerian examples.

**Responsibility.** Areas of social responsibility: responsibility towards employees (fair remuneration, safe working environment, career development), towards investors, towards the environment (pollution control, wise use of raw materials, sustainable production), towards consumers, towards the community and towards the government, together with ethical and legal responsibility (Nepal's book.)

**Responsibility.** Ethical principles for business: integrity (the foundation stone), fairness, transparency (clear, true, understandable information to employees, customers and stakeholders; never deceiving customers), responsibility, respect towards stakeholders, environmental responsibility, and compliance with local, national and international laws and regulations (Nepal's book.)

**Responsibility.** Business ethics and corporate social responsibility introduced together: ethics is directly joined to CSR — the ethical business reduces environmental harm through sustainable practice, joins community development and keeps ethics through its supply chain — and ethics is treated as an indispensable element of long-term success in the modern business environment (Nepal's book.)

**Responsibility.** The benefit to society: the qualitative evaluation of the project weighs its social and community benefits — increased community production, employment generated and needs covered — alongside the financial numbers (Ecuador's book.)

**Responsibility.** Ventures with social interest: community and associative ventures that use nature's gifts to cover a community's needs with a social and environmental component, guided by three innovation questions (why is this done, what if it were not done, what if it were done differently) (Ecuador's book.)

## Corporate social responsibility

A firm's corporate social responsibility in action: the book's supermarket-chain case shows scholarships and community support presented as part of the business's CSR agenda — "good community relations means good business"

## Ethics and the law in business

Legal vs ethical issues in setting up and running a business (registration, licences, taxes, waste disposal, truthful advertising, money laundering, product safety), the principles to adopt (legitimate firm and capital, code of ethics, environmental and data-handling policies) and the consequences table for unethical/illegal practice (fines, closure, lawsuits, imprisonment, lost reputation)

## Environmental protection

Business and the environment: laws exist to stop businesses polluting water and air, contaminating land with hazardous chemicals and dumping waste to cut costs; Environment Agencies implement the policy (Jamaica's NEPA — managing air pollution, monitoring endangered species, environmental impact assessments, permits and licences); international agreements and the UN Sustainable Development Goals; and why complying benefits a business — lower energy, materials and water costs, recycling income, avoided clean-up costs, a stronger brand, easier investment and access to government contracts

## Green technology and waste

Green technology and sustainable business practice: green technology and sustainability defined; how businesses protect the environment — alternatives to fossil fuels, energy efficiency, recyclable products, responsible waste management; government tools — environmentally preferred purchasing, green building policy, tax incentives for green business (Barbados), reforestation; waste management in the Caribbean (Site Waste Management Plans and their cost savings, Barbados's sanitary landfill and recycling, medical waste treatment, re-refining used oil in Antigua); and zoning laws that separate commercial, residential, industrial and agricultural land use

**Responsibility / judging a venture's social worth.** Social evaluation of a venture: a venture is judged not only on profit and payback but on social goals and impact indicators (education level, employment, unemployment in the community), with key questions the entrepreneur must ask about the problems it eases (Ecuador's book.)

**Responsibility / the venture's principles.** The venture's values: the ethical principles its work rests on, drawn from the questions what do we want to achieve, how are we, what do we believe in and how do we want to be remembered; the book lists the entrepreneur's own traits as the seed of them — creative, honest, transparent, prudent, bold, risk-taking (Nicaragua's book.)

**Responsibility / the environment.** The environmental feasibility study: production activity can harm the environment people live in (factory smoke, waste, polluted water, noise, dust), so the project needs a study of the mutual effect between project and environment; (Palestine's book.)
**Business ethics.** Professional ethics for accountants: what professional ethics and a professional code of ethics are, and the Indonesian Institute of Accountants (IAI) code's basic principles — integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality and professional behaviour — taught through the case of an accountant refusing a client's bribe (Indonesia's book.)
