# Video script — Business Ethics and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A business owes responsibility beyond profit — to society at large, its suppliers, lenders, shareholders, employees, customers and government — and its production must not poison the air, water and land its neighbours live on; the entrepreneur's ethics govern how it treats people, including their personal data in digital business.

How production harms the environment and community health: air pollution (acid rain, CFCs and the ozone layer, greenhouse gases, breathing harm), water pollution, noise pollution, destruction of the …. Entrepreneurial ethics connected to digital-business law. Corporate social responsibility owed in turn to society at large, suppliers, lenders, shareholders, employees, customers and government, with Nigerian examples.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
