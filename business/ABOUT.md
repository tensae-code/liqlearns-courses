# About the Business course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the Business and
Business Studies books of Dominica, Ecuador, Egypt, Indonesia, Iran, Kenya, Malaysia,
Nepal, Nicaragua, Nigeria, Palestine, Samoa, Sudan and Uganda. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is
from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons
are the books'.

## How this course is organised

Business is ONE top-level course, one of the three courses of
the Business / Commerce / Accounting split (the split the books
themselves forced: the same school subject carries business
management, trade and bookkeeping side by side, and each part is
stocked in the course it belongs to). Inside the course the
organisation is by **units and levels, not grades**: a learner's
place is found by what they prove, and each lesson stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grade a book comes from appears only in each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **four units**: First Steps (what a
business is, ideas, entrepreneurship, creativity and
innovation, ownership forms, non-profit organisations), Starting and Running (starting, the
business plan, management, people, careers, communication,
small businesses, selling and customer care), Market and Money
(marketing, production, finance, the capital market, business
calculations, personal finance) and Rules and
Responsibility (risk and insurance, law, ethics, the business
environment, e-business). **25 lessons, 564 placements stocked.**
