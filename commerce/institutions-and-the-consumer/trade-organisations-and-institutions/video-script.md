# Video script — Trade Organisations and Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Traders and countries organise: world institutions steady money and lend for development, regions integrate their markets step by step, and west africa's groupings join trade, rivers and money across borders.

Behind world trade stand its financial institutions.

Economic integration is countries of a region freeing trade among themselves while keeping barriers against outsiders — in deepening forms.

The gains: a wider market and economies of scale; specialisation and the trade-creation effect; better and cheaper products through competition.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
