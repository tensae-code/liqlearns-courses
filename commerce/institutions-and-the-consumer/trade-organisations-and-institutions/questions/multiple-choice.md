# Trade Organisations and Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which stage of integration adds free movement of labour, capital and enterprise? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The common market  b) The free trade area  c) The customs union
**Answer: a) The common market** — The common market adds movement of labour, capital and enterprise to the customs union.

**2.** WAMA works towards… (Sub lesson 2)
a) A single army  b) One national language  c) A single monetary zone for West Africa
**Answer: c) A single monetary zone for West Africa** — WAMA, successor to the West African Clearing House, works towards a single monetary zone.

**3.** What does this lesson teach about trade institutions / selling to the state? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Something this lesson does not teach.
b) Registering as a state supplier (proveedor del estado): complete the online form with an email account,…
c) Something this lesson does not teach.
**Answer: b) Registering as a state supplier (proveedor del estado): complete the online form with an email account,…** — That is what this lesson teaches in its trade institutions / selling to the state part (Nicaragua's book).
