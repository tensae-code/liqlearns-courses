# Trade Organisations and Institutions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The IMF was created at Bretton Woods in 1944. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — Kenya's book: created at Bretton Woods 1944, a central bank of central banks.

**2.** Integration has no costs for consumers. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Consumers may be shut off from cheaper or better goods from outside the bloc.
