# Lesson plan — Trade Organisations and Institutions

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Institutions and the Consumer · **Lesson:** Trade Organisations and Institutions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Institutions and the Consumer (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** traders and countries organise: world institutions steady money and lend for development, regions integrate their markets step by step, and West Africa's groupings join trade, rivers and money across borders.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a free trade area and an economic union are the same step → integration deepens in stages: free trade area, customs union, common market, economic union — each stage adds a freedom the one before lacks.
- Go back to the one thing: traders and countries organise: world institutions steady money and lend for development, regions integrate their markets step by step, and West Africa's groupings join trade, rivers and money across borders. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
