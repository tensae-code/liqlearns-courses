# Trade Organisations and Institutions — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons, visible from the start and locked ahead: each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (prove-it check of at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson's whole question set — the gate pool). Pass mark for the whole lesson: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — traders and countries organise: world institutions steady money and lend for development, regions integrate their markets step by step, and West Africa's groupings join trade, rivers and money across borders — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
