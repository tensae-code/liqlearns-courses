# Hands-on — Consumer Protection in Trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember a time a bought thing was not what it promised — too light, fake, or unsafe. Ask: whose job was it to stop that, and where could the buyer complain? This lesson names the rights, the laws and the doors to knock on.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the consumer at the end of the chain needs protecting — against deceptive advertising, fake brands, health hazards and short weight — and the protection stands in three layers: the consumer's own rights, the laws, and the bodies that enforce them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
