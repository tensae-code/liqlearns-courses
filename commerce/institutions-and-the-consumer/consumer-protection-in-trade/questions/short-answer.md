# Consumer Protection in Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four harms that make consumers need protection. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any four of: essential goods and price control abuses, deceptive advertising, health hazards, fake brands, short weight and measure.

**2.** Name the four government protection bodies and one job of each, as far as the lesson gives it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** SON — standards; NAFDAC — food and drugs; CPC — consumer protection; FEPA — environmental protection.
