# Consumer Protection in Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Consumer rights stand alone, with no matching responsibilities. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The book pairs the eight rights with matching consumer responsibilities.

**2.** Consumer associations are a form of protection without government. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — Consumer associations, producer and professional associations and crafts unions police their own side.
