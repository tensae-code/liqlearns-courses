# Consumer Protection in Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which body watches food and drugs in Nigeria, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 3)
a) NAFDAC  b) SON  c) WAMA
**Answer: a) NAFDAC** — NAFDAC is the food and drugs body among the four government bodies named.

**2.** Which harm is answered by the Weights and Measures Act? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Deceptive advertising only  b) Late delivery  c) Short weight and measure
**Answer: c) Short weight and measure** — The Act stands against short weight and measure, one of the opening harms.

**3.** What does this lesson teach about the consumer protection? (Sub lesson 1)

**Answer: a) Why consumers need protection (copycat products, false advertising, collusion, unfair treatment, uninformed …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its consumer protection part (Dominica's book).
