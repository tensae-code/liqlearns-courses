# Video script — Consumer Protection in Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The consumer at the end of the chain needs protecting — against deceptive advertising, fake brands, health hazards and short weight — and the protection stands in three layers: the consumer's own rights, the laws, and the bodies that enforce them.

Nigeria's book opens with the harms: essential goods and price control; deceptive advertising; health hazards; fake brands; and short weight and measure.

The answer begins with consumerism: the eight internationally accepted consumer rights, and the matching consumer responsibilities that stand beside them — rights are held by people who also carry duties.

The laws in detail: the Hire Purchase Act 1965; the Sale of Goods Act's implied terms; the Trade Descriptions Acts; the Standards Organisation Act; the Weights and Measures Act; and the food and drugs legislation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
