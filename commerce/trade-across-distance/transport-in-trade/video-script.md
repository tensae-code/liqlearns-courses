This lesson is about one thing.
Distribution is an aid to trade that lives or dies on transport: goods move direct, or through retailers and wholesalers — and where shipping is slow or missed, a shortage can force a trader onto expensive air-freight.

Distribution is an aid to trade: goods are sold direct, or through retailers and wholesalers as middle people.
