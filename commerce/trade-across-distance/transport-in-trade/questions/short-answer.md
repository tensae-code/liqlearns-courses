# Transport in Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two routes can distribution take?
**Answer:** Selling direct, or selling through retailers and wholesalers as middle people.

**2.** Explain the transport risk an island importer carries.
**Answer:** If shipping schedules are not managed carefully, stock runs out — a shortage — and the trader must pay for expensive air-freight to refill the shelves.

**3.** Explain the multimodal and intermodal transport part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Multimodal vs intermodal transport: multimodal — one specialist carrier contracts for and is legally responsible for the whole journey across several modes; intermodal — each leg contracted separately, each carrier responsible only for its leg, giving the producer more flexibility
