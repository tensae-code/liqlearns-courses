# Transport in Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two routes can distribution take?
**Answer:** Selling direct, or selling through retailers and wholesalers as middle people.

**2.** Explain the transport risk an island importer carries.
**Answer:** If shipping schedules are not managed carefully, stock runs out — a shortage — and the trader must pay for expensive air-freight to refill the shelves.
