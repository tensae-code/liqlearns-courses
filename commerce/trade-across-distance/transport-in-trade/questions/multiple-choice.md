# Transport in Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why must Samoan importing retailers manage shipping schedules carefully?
a) A shortage can force expensive air-freight  b) Ships refuse small loads  c) Retailers own the ships
**Answer: a) A shortage can force expensive air-freight** — That is the book's own reason.

**2.** Through whom may goods be distributed, besides selling direct?
a) Only government offices  b) Only co-operatives  c) Retailers and wholesalers as middle people
**Answer: c) Retailers and wholesalers as middle people** — Distribution sells direct, or through retailers and wholesalers.
