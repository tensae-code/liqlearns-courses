# Transport in Trade

## Do it first

Follow one imported good into Samoa: a carton of tinned fish on a ship. Ask: what happens to the shop's shelves if the ship is late? Write the answer down. That answer is this lesson.

## Distribution and the shipping schedule

Distribution is an aid to trade: goods are sold direct, or through retailers and wholesalers as middle people. Samoa's lesson is about distance: Samoan retailers who import goods must manage shipping schedules carefully, because a shortage can force expensive air-freight. Transport in trade is therefore not only moving goods — it is timing their movement so the shelf never empties and the costly rescue is never needed.

## Modes of transport

Transport in trade: its roles (physical supply, specialisation, mobility of labour and capital, sales, sourcing, security of supply) and the modes compared with advantages/disadvantages — air, road, rail, marine (with containerisation) and pipelines, plus digital delivery as a mode for downloadable products

## Multimodal and intermodal transport

Multimodal vs intermodal transport: multimodal — one specialist carrier contracts for and is legally responsible for the whole journey across several modes; intermodal — each leg contracted separately, each carrier responsible only for its leg, giving the producer more flexibility
