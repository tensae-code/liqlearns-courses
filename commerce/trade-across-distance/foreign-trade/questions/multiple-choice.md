# Foreign Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does C.I.F. stand for? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Cost, insurance and freight  b) Cash in full  c) Credit in francs
**Answer: a) Cost, insurance and freight** — C.I.F. carries cost, insurance and freight in the quoted price.

**2.** A zero quota is called… (Sub lesson 2)
a) A tariff  b) Franco  c) An embargo
**Answer: c) An embargo** — Kenya's book: a quota set at zero is an embargo.
