# Foreign Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three methods governments use to restrict trade. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Any three of: tariffs, quotas, administrative bottlenecks, foreign exchange control, moral persuasion.

**2.** Give two disadvantages of restricting trade. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Any two of: retaliation, low-quality local production, high prices, less consumer choice, perpetual protection, monopolies.

**3.** Explain the pros and cons of trade part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Advantages and disadvantages of international trade: advantages — local businesses expand into new markets and sell at higher volumes, competition forces up quality and down prices, selling in several countries spreads risk, unavailable products can be imported, and countries access resources for a wider range of industries; disadvantages — dumping of very cheap foreign goods, faster depletion of natural resources, large import bills causing foreign currency shortages, unbalanced development, and dependence on other countries for essentials such as food and raw materials

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about trade across borders? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Foreign (external/international) trade between two or more countries, in three kinds: import trade (buying from another country to sell at home), export trade, and re-export / entrepôt trade

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about foreign trade / food exports? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Exporting processed food of agricultural, aquaculture or fishing origin: register as an exporter with IPSA, register the establishment with the Food Safety Directorate, present the importing country's phytosanitary and sanitary requirements and request the inspection, and IPSA issues the inspection certificate;
