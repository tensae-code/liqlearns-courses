# Foreign Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bilateral trade is trade among many countries. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Bilateral is between two countries; multilateral is among many.

**2.** A letter of credit lets the importer's bank guarantee payment to the exporter once documents are presented. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the mechanism the lesson teaches.
