# Video script — Foreign Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Foreign trade is trade between countries, so each can obtain what it does not produce and sell its surplus — run on quoted-price terms, secured by documents such as the letter of credit, and shaped by the restrictions governments impose and the trends now loosening them.

International — foreign — trade is trade between countries, so that each can obtain what it does not produce and dispose of its surplus.

In international trade the quoted price must state which movement costs the exporter bears.

Nigeria's book gives the world system: Incoterms 2010.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
