# What Is Commerce — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Distribution must get goods to consumers in the right quantities, at the right price and time. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is exactly the job the lesson gives distribution.

**2.** Warehousing is not one of the aids to trade. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Samoa's list of the working parts of commerce includes warehousing.
