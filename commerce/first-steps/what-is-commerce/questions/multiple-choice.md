# What Is Commerce — multiple choice

**1.** What is a channel of distribution? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The path goods follow from producer to final consumer  b) The price tag on a good  c) A shop's advertising poster
**Answer: a) The path goods follow from producer to final consumer** — Distribution is the bridge, and the channel is the path across it.

**2.** Which goods need a SHORT channel of distribution? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Goods that keep for years  b) Goods sold only abroad  c) Perishable and bulky goods
**Answer: c) Perishable and bulky goods** — Perishables spoil and bulky goods are costly to handle, so they travel short paths.
