# What Is Commerce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four aids to trade from Samoa's list. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any four of: transportation, communication, trade, accounting, banking, insurance, warehousing, advertising.

**2.** Give three things a business weighs when choosing a channel of distribution. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any three of: nature of the product, nature of the market, services intermediaries offer, the firm's resources and size, competitors' channels, government policy, marketing risks.

**3.** Explain the money in trade part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Money: what it is, its four functions (medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value, means of deferred payment) and the characteristics good money needs (portable, divisible, acceptable, durable, scarce, homogeneous); token money and how money encourages specialisation
