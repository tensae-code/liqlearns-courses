# Video script — What Is Commerce

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Commerce is the work of getting goods from the people who make them to the people who use them — distribution across the gap between producer and consumer, helped by the aids to trade: transport, communication, banking, insurance, warehousing and advertising.

A producer makes goods in one place; the people who need them live somewhere else, and they need them in the right quantities, at the right price and at the right time.

A business does not pick its channel by habit; it weighs real things.

Commerce does not work alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
