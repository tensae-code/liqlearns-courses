# Lesson plan — Retail Trade

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Retail Trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the retailer is the last seller in the chain — the one who sells to the final consumer — and retail trade works through short channels and on credit given and received.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — First Steps, lesson 2 of 15 in the course ladder; it stands on What Is Commerce, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the retailer is the last seller in the chain — the one who sells to the final consumer — and retail trade works through short channels and on credit given and received.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the retailer makes the goods → no: the retailer buys finished goods and sells them on, in small quantities, to the people who will use them.
- Go back to the one thing: the retailer is the last seller in the chain — the one who sells to the final consumer — and retail trade works through short channels and on credit given and received. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
