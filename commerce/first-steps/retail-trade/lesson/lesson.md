# Retail Trade

## Do it first

Visit a small shop in your mind. Write down: where the shopkeeper got the goods, who they sell to, and one thing the shopkeeper lets a trusted customer take now and pay for later. That last seller — and that trust — is this lesson.

## Why retailers need credit

A retailer needs credit for the same family of reasons a wholesaler does. Credit pays for the shop's facilities, lets the retailer finance wholesalers and consumers in turn, makes bulk buying possible, and lets the shop hold stock in hope of a price rise. Credit is not a favour at the edge of retail trade — it is one of the tools the trade runs on.

## The short channels

Not every good travels the long chain. A producer may sell directly to local retailers, who run the shops where consumers buy. Or a producer may sell to a co-operative and take a share of its profits. These short channels keep the path simple: producer, retailer, consumer — with the co-operative channel giving the producer a share in the selling side as well.

## Methods of retailing

Methods of retailing compared: shops (convenience shops, supermarkets), department stores, mail order, e-commerce, tele-marketing and vending machines — with the retailer's place as final link between manufacturer and consumer

**Home trade.** Retail trade: the retailer has a direct relation with the final consumer and satisfies them by selling the wanted goods at the wanted time and place at a fair price (Nepal's book.)
