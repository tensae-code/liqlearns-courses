# Retail Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Retailers may need credit to buy in bulk and to hold stock.
**Answer: True** — Nigeria's book gives bulk buying and speculative stock holding among the retailer's reasons for credit.

**2.** A producer can never sell directly to a retailer.
**Answer: False** — The short channels do exactly that: producer to local retailer.
