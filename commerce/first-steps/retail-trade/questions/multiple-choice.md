# Retail Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who does the retailer sell to?
a) The final consumer  b) Only other retailers  c) Only the producer
**Answer: a) The final consumer** — The retailer is the last seller before the person who uses the good.

**2.** In the co-operative channel, what does the producer get besides the sale?
a) A government licence  b) A free shop  c) A share of the co-operative's profits
**Answer: c) A share of the co-operative's profits** — Samoa's book: the producer sells to a co-operative and gets a share of its profits.
