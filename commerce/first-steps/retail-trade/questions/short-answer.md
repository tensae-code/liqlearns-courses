# Retail Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two reasons a retailer needs credit.
**Answer:** Any two of: facilities, financing wholesalers and consumers, bulk buying, holding stock for an expected price rise.

**2.** Describe the two short channels in this lesson.
**Answer:** Producer sells directly to local retailers who run shops; or producer sells to a co-operative and shares in its profits.

**3.** Explain the methods of retailing part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Methods of retailing compared: shops (convenience shops, supermarkets), department stores, mail order, e-commerce, tele-marketing and vending machines — with the retailer's place as final link between manufacturer and consumer

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about home trade?
**Answer:** Retail trade: the retailer has a direct relation with the final consumer and satisfies them by selling the wanted goods at the wanted time and place at a fair price

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about retail trade?
**Answer:** Retail trade inside the book's production chain: the retail business buys large-quantity goods from wholesalers and packages them into smaller amounts, selling to final consumers, who buy what they can afford in the smaller quantities — the chain runs wholesale (bulk) → retail (small quantities) → final consumer
