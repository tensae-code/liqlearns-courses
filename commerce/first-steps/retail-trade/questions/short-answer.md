# Retail Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two reasons a retailer needs credit.
**Answer:** Any two of: facilities, financing wholesalers and consumers, bulk buying, holding stock for an expected price rise.

**2.** Describe the two short channels in this lesson.
**Answer:** Producer sells directly to local retailers who run shops; or producer sells to a co-operative and shares in its profits.
