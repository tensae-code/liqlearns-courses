# Video script — Buying and Selling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Buying and selling run on agreed terms — the discounts of trade, the quoted price and what it covers, and the moment payment is due — and the trader who knows the terms knows the real price.

Trade runs on three discounts.

Nigeria's book sets the two side by side: know what each is deducted from, and know your trade discounts — a cumulative trade discount builds on what you have already bought, a non-cumulative one does not.

A quoted price must say what it covers.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
