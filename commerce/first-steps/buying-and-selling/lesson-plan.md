# Lesson plan — Buying and Selling

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Buying and Selling
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** buying and selling run on agreed terms — the discounts of trade, the quoted price and what it covers, and the moment payment is due — and the trader who knows the terms knows the real price.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — First Steps, lesson 4 of 15 in the course ladder; it stands on Wholesale Trade, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up trade discount and cash discount → trade discount rewards reselling and is struck off the list price; cash discount rewards paying promptly, inside the stated terms.
- Go back to the one thing: buying and selling run on agreed terms — the discounts of trade, the quoted price and what it covers, and the moment payment is due — and the trader who knows the terms knows the real price. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
