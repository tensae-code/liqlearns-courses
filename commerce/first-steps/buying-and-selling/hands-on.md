# Hands-on — Buying and Selling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a pretend purchase: 100 exercise books for a shop. Write a price, then ask three questions: Is there a discount? What does the price include? When must I pay? Those three questions are this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: buying and selling run on agreed terms — the discounts of trade, the quoted price and what it covers, and the moment payment is due — and the trader who knows the terms knows the real price. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
