# Buying and Selling — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Trade discount and quantity discount are recorded in the books of account. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Kenya's book: neither is recorded in the books of account.

**2.** 'Cash on delivery' and 'payment in advance' name the same moment. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — They are different payment terms — one pays when goods arrive, the other before.
