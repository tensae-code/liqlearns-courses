# Buying and Selling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Distinguish cash discount from trade discount. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cash discount is an allowance for paying promptly within stated terms; trade discount is given to buyers who resell, deducted from the list price (with cumulative and non-cumulative forms).

**2.** List four terms that name when cash is paid. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any four of: payment in advance, cash with order, cash on delivery, spot cash, prompt cash, net cash, monthly account, deferred payment / open account.

**3.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about buying for the business? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Choosing suppliers: suppliers supply the goods and services the venture runs on; the best supplier is not always the cheapest — also judge who gives the best solutions and weigh their location
