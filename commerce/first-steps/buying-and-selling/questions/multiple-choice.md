# Buying and Selling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which discount is an allowance for paying a debt promptly within stated terms? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Cash discount  b) Trade discount  c) Quantity discount
**Answer: a) Cash discount** — Cash discount rewards prompt payment; the other two are not recorded in the books of account.

**2.** What does 'carriage paid' tell the buyer? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The buyer collects at the warehouse  b) Payment is deferred a month  c) The seller carries the delivery cost
**Answer: c) The seller carries the delivery cost** — The quoted-price terms state which movement costs each side bears.
