**1.** In a credit sale, who is the creditor?
a) The seller, who is owed the money  b) The buyer  c) The bank only
**Answer: a) The seller, who is owed the money** — The seller is also the creditor and lender; the buyer is the debtor.

**2.** What does credit change about a purchase?

**Answer: c) When the money is paid — over time, into the future** — Payment is made over a period of time extending into the future.
