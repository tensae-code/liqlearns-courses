# Credit and Hire Purchase — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Explain creditor and debtor in a credit purchase.
**Answer:** The creditor is the seller-lender who hands over goods now and is owed; the debtor is the buyer-borrower who promises to pay later.

**2.** Why does the promise in a credit sale need to be measured exactly in money?
**Answer:** So both sides know exactly what is owed and over what time it will be paid — the book states the promise is measured exactly in money.
