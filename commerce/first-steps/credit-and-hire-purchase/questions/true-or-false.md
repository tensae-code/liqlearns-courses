# Credit and Hire Purchase — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A buyer on credit is a debtor who promises to pay at a later date.
**Answer: True** — That is the lesson's own definition.

**2.** Credit can be used to buy a car, a house and furniture.
**Answer: True** — Samoa's book names exactly these.
