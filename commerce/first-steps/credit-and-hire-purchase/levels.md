# Credit and Hire Purchase — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — buying on credit means taking the goods now and paying over time: the seller becomes the creditor who lends, and the buyer becomes the debtor who owes — and the promise to pay is measured exactly in money — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
