# Video script — Wholesale Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The wholesaler stands in the middle of the chain of distribution — buying in bulk from producers and selling on to retailers — and every middleman in the chain does real work and adds a real charge.

Channels run from the full chain — producer, agent, wholesaler, retailer, consumer — down to the producer selling direct to the consumer.

Kenya's book follows real goods.

Samoa's younger book draws the whole chain in one line: the producer sells to the wholesaler; the wholesaler sells on to the food market, to shops or individual retailers, or to an agent or industry; and they sell to the consumer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
