# Wholesale Trade — multiple choice

**1.** What happens to the final price as the chain of distribution gets longer? (Sub lesson 1)
a) It rises, because each middleman adds costs and profit  b) It always falls  c) It never changes
**Answer: a) It rises, because each middleman adds costs and profit** — Kenya's rule: each middleman adds his costs and profit.

**2.** Which job is breaking bulk? (Sub lesson 1)

**Answer: c) Dividing a large delivery into smaller lots for buyers** — Breaking bulk is one of the intermediaries' listed functions.
