# Wholesale Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four functions of intermediaries. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any four of: reducing transactions, breaking bulk, accumulating bulk, risk taking, providing finance, passing information, product promotion, transport and storage, providing variety, availing goods conveniently.

**2.** Give two reasons a wholesaler needs credit. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any two of: buying facilities and branches, financing producers, selling to retailers on credit, bulk purchasing, holding stock for an expected price rise.
