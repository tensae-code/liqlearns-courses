# Hands-on — Wholesale Trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one good — a sack of rice or a box of soap. Draw every hand it passes through from the farm or factory to the person who eats or uses it. Count the hands. This lesson is about the biggest hand in the middle: the wholesaler.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the wholesaler stands in the middle of the chain of distribution — buying in bulk from producers and selling on to retailers — and every middleman in the chain does real work and adds a real charge. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
