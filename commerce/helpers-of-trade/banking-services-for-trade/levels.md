# Banking Services for Trade — depth

This lesson has 5 sub lessons, visible from the start and locked ahead: each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (prove-it check of at most 5 questions, drawn from the sub lesson's whole question set — the gate pool). Pass mark for the whole lesson: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — banks are trade's money-servants: they take deposits, lend, keep valuables, move money at home and across the world, and stand behind trade with instruments — cheques, bills of exchange, drafts and cards — that let strangers trust each other's promises — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
