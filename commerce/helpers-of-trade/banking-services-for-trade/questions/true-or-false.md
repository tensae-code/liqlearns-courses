# Banking Services for Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Central Bank acts as lender of last resort. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — One of its listed functions in Kenya's book.

**2.** A bill of lading is fully negotiable like a bill of exchange. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer: False** — Bills of lading are only quasi-negotiable.
