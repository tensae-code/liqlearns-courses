# Banking Services for Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four functions of the Central Bank. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any four of: issuing currency, banker to commercial banks, government's bank and adviser, controlling commercial banks, link to the IMF/World Bank, stabilising exchange rates, lender of last resort, clearing cheques, administering the public debt.

**2.** How does a promissory note differ from a bill of exchange? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** A note is a promise to pay made by its maker; a bill is an order directing another party to pay.

**3.** Explain the payment instruments part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** The other common instruments of exchange compared in one table: credit cards, debit (Linx) cards, bills of exchange, electronic transfers, internet banking, tele-banking, money orders, bank drafts, telegraphic/wire transfers (SWIFT) and mobile money/wallets

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about aids to trade? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Auxiliaries to trade, 3 — banks and finance: banks and financial institutions supply the fixed and working capital and loans a business needs to set up and run, and organise and secure trade with services such as bank overdraft, letters of credit and remittance

**5.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about banking services? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Short-term bank credit: the overdraft (الجاري المدين) — facilities up to a set ceiling from which the client draws, interest charged only on the balance actually drawn day by day and stopping on any amount once deposited, cheap when used correctly for short periods but very costly if used heavily and long because of its high annual rate;
