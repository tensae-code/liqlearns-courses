# Banking Services for Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which account is a demand deposit withdrawn by cheque, with an overdraft facility and no interest? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The current account  b) The fixed deposit  c) The SACCO pool
**Answer: a) The current account** — Kenya's current account, in depth: demand deposit, cheque, overdraft, ledger fees, monthly statements.

**2.** What makes an instrument negotiable? (Sub lesson 5)
a) It is printed on special paper  b) A bank stamps it twice  c) A holder takes a good title even from a thief
**Answer: c) A holder takes a good title even from a thief** — Nigeria's definition: the paper carries the right with it.
