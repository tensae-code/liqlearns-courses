# Lesson plan — Communication in Trade

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Helpers of Trade · **Lesson:** Communication in Trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** trade runs on information — communication carries news, orders, prices and feedback between producer, trader and consumer, quickly and cheaply, so each side knows what the other needs and has done.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade, lesson 10 of 17 in the course ladder; it stands on Warehousing, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks communication is only talking → in trade it is the flow of information the whole chain works by: without it the producer makes what nobody ordered, the trader stocks what nobody wants, and the consumer's complaint never travels back.
- Go back to the one thing: trade runs on information — communication carries news, orders, prices and feedback between producer, trader and consumer, quickly and cheaply, so each side knows what the other needs and has done. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
