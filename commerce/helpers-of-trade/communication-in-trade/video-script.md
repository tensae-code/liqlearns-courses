# Video script — Communication in Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
trade runs on information — communication carries news, orders, prices and feedback between producer, trader and consumer, quickly and cheaply, so each side knows what the other needs and has done.

Communication in trade: A trader who hears nothing sells blind; a producer who hears nothing makes blind. The messages are the trade's eyes and ears.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
