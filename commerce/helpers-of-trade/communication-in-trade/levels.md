# Communication in Trade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — trade runs on information — communication carries news, orders, prices and feedback between producer, trader and consumer, quickly and cheaply, so each side knows what the other needs and has done — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
