# Hands-on — Communication in Trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A shopkeeper's shelves are empty of the soap everyone asks for, and full of a soap nobody wants — because no message ever travelled between the buyers, the shop and the maker. That missing flow of messages is what communication in trade fixes. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: trade runs on information — communication carries news, orders, prices and feedback between producer, trader and consumer, quickly and cheaply, so each side knows what the other needs and has done. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
