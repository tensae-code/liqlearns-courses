# Communication in Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A producer who receives no feedback can still know exactly what consumers want.
**Answer: False** — Without feedback travelling back, the producer makes blind and the trader stocks what nobody wants.

**2.** Communication in trade carries only orders, never complaints.
**Answer: False** — Feedback — including complaints — is part of the flow; it tells the producer and trader what to fix.
