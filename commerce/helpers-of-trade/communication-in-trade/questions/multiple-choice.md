# Communication in Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Between whom must information flow for trade to work?
a) Only between two shops  b) Between producer, trader and consumer  c) Only between the government and traders
**Answer: b) Between producer, trader and consumer** — The chain holds together only if information and feedback flow between all three.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about aids to trade?
a) Something this lesson does not teach.
b) Auxiliaries to trade, 2 — communication: trade needs information and feedback to flow between producer,…
c) Something this lesson does not teach.
**Answer: b) Auxiliaries to trade, 2 — communication: trade needs information and feedback to flow between producer,…** — That is what this lesson teaches in its aids to trade part (Nepal's book).
