# Communication in Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two kinds of message that travel from a trader back to a producer.
**Answer:** Any two of: what sold and what did not; buyers' complaints or praise (feedback); new orders; the prices buyers will pay.

**2.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about aids to trade?
**Answer:** Auxiliaries to trade, 2 — communication: trade needs information and feedback to flow between producer, trader and consumer; communication supplies important information in less time and at less cost
