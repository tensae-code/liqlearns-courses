# Warehousing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A warehouse can hold seasonal products until their season comes.
**Answer: True** — Holding large quantities and seasonal products is one of warehousing's jobs.

**2.** Goods in a warehouse are only stored; nothing is done to them.
**Answer: False** — Goods are also graded and conditioned — kept in the right state, for example in cold storage.
