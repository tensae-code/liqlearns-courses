# Warehousing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two directions goods flow through a warehouse.
**Answer:** Inbound — goods flowing in from producers; and outbound — goods flowing out to buyers.

**2.** Give two ways warehousing minimises risk for a business.
**Answer:** Any two of: cold storage for goods that would spoil; insurance on what is stored; safe keeping against theft and damage while goods wait.
