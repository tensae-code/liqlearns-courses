# Warehousing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does just-in-time stock management mean in a warehouse?
a) Storing as much as the building can hold  b) Storing only what is needed  c) Storing goods only after they are sold
**Answer: b) Storing only what is needed** — The book teaches storing only what is needed, so money is not tied up in unneeded stock.

**2.** How can stored goods help a business raise money?
a) They can be given away  b) They can stand as collateral for a loan  c) They cannot
**Answer: b) They can stand as collateral for a loan** — Goods held in the warehouse can secure a loan.
