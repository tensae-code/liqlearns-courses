# Warehousing

## Do it first

A farmer harvests more maize than the town can eat this month. Left in the open it rots, and it can be stolen; in a good store it waits safely until buyers come, month by month. That store — and the work of keeping goods in it — is warehousing. That is this lesson.

## Warehousing

A warehouse is a building — used by producers, wholesalers and retailers — where goods are kept between production and sale. Goods flow in (inbound) and flow out again (outbound) to buyers. Good warehousing manages stock just-in-time: storing only what is needed, so money is not sleeping in unneeded stock. What warehousing does for a business: it holds large quantities and seasonal products until their season comes; goods are graded and conditioned (kept in the right state — cold storage for what would spoil); stored goods can stand as collateral for a loan; and risk is minimised, with cold storage and insurance guarding what waits.

**Aids to trade.** Auxiliaries to trade, 5 — warehousing (गोदाम): storing goods until they are needed, named among the helpers of trade (Nepal's book.)
**Warehousing.** The service functions of exchange: alongside buying and selling, exchange now includes transport, storage, insurance, credit and advertising, and each adds a utility to the goods — transport adds place utility, storage and keeping goods until their season of consumption adds time utility (Sudan's book.)
