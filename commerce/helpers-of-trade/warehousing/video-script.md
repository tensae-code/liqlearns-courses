# Video script — Warehousing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
a warehouse keeps goods safe and in condition between making and selling — taking goods in, holding what is needed and no more, grading and conditioning them, and sending them out — so trade never has to stop for want of stock, and stored goods can even stand as collateral for a loan.

Warehousing: A warehouse is a building — used by producers, wholesalers and retailers — where goods are kept between production and sale.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
