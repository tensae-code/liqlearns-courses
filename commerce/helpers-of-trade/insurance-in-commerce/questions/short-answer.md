# Insurance in Commerce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three principles of insurance and say what each means in a few words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Insurable interest — insure only what you stand to lose; indemnity — no profit from a loss; utmost good faith — the whole truth, or the claim is void.

**2.** Name three services insurance companies give trade, beyond paying claims. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any three of: savings through endowment/education policies, low-interest loans to policy holders, guarantees for borrowing elsewhere, advice on safeguarding property, underwriting new share issues.
