# Insurance in Commerce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three principles of insurance and say what each means in a few words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Insurable interest — insure only what you stand to lose; indemnity — no profit from a loss; utmost good faith — the whole truth, or the claim is void.

**2.** Name three services insurance companies give trade, beyond paying claims. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any three of: savings through endowment/education policies, low-interest loans to policy holders, guarantees for borrowing elsewhere, advice on safeguarding property, underwriting new share issues.

**3.** Explain the insurance of goods and trade debts part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The trade-facing covers inside the book's insurance table: trade credit insurance (against debtors not paying), goods in transit insurance, and marine insurance in its parts (hull, cargo, freight, shipowners' liability)

**4.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about insurance as a trade service? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The concept of insurance and the vocabulary of a life policy: the insured, the nominee (who receives the sum on the insured's death), the policy itself as the legal contract, the premium paid regularly for cover, the sum assured, policy term, death benefit, maturity benefit, savings plans, renewal premium, waiting period and health riders
