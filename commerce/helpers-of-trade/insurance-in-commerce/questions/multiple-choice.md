# Insurance in Commerce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which principle says a loss is made good but never profited from? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Indemnity  b) Insurable interest  c) Utmost good faith
**Answer: a) Indemnity** — Indemnity: insurance puts you back where you were.

**2.** In motor insurance, the 'excess' is… (Sub lesson 2)
a) A bonus for safe driving  b) The broker's commission  c) The first part of a loss the customer funds himself
**Answer: c) The first part of a loss the customer funds himself** — Samoa's book: motor cover carries an excess the customer funds first.

**3.** What does this lesson teach about the insurance of goods and trade debts? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The trade-facing covers inside the book's insurance table: trade credit insurance (against debtors not …  b) The purposes of taxation: taxes are compulsory payments that mainly finance public spending, but are also …  c) Business technology and ICT: technology as methods, systems and devices developed by applying knowledge and …
**Answer: a) The trade-facing covers inside the book's insurance table: trade credit insurance (against debtors not …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its insurance of goods and trade debts part (Dominica's book).
