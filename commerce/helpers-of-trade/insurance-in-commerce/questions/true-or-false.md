# Insurance in Commerce — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** False details given to an insurer can void the claim. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — Utmost good faith: false details void the claim.

**2.** Premiums sit idle until claims are paid. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Premiums are invested to earn the income that pays claims and administration.
