# Lesson plan — Insurance in Commerce

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Helpers of Trade · **Lesson:** Insurance in Commerce
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Helpers of Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** insurance is pooled protection: many pay small premiums into one fund, and the fund pays the few who suffer the loss — governed by insurable interest, indemnity and utmost good faith.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Helpers of Trade, lesson 10 of 13 in the course ladder; it stands on Advertising and Sales Promotion, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks insurance lets you profit from a loss → indemnity forbids it: insurance puts you back where you were, never ahead.
- Go back to the one thing: insurance is pooled protection: many pay small premiums into one fund, and the fund pays the few who suffer the loss — governed by insurable interest, indemnity and utmost good faith. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
