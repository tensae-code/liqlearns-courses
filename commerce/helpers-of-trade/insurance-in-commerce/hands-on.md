# Hands-on — Insurance in Commerce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a fire takes a trader's whole stock in one night. Alone, he is ruined. Now imagine a hundred traders each paid a small sum every year into one fund, and the fund rebuilds his shop. That pool — and its rules — is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: insurance is pooled protection: many pay small premiums into one fund, and the fund pays the few who suffer the loss — governed by insurable interest, indemnity and utmost good faith. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
