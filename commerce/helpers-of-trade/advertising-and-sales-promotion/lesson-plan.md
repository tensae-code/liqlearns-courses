# Lesson plan — Advertising and Sales Promotion

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Helpers of Trade · **Lesson:** Advertising and Sales Promotion
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Helpers of Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business wins customers by promotion in four forms — personal selling, public relations, sales promotion and advertising — each with its own moment, its own tools and its own cost.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Helpers of Trade, lesson 11 of 17 in the course ladder; it stands on Communication in Trade, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up advertising and sales promotion → advertising is paid messages through media, repeated; sales promotion is the short push — samples, coupons, premiums — that moves the customer to buy now.
- Go back to the one thing: a business wins customers by promotion in four forms — personal selling, public relations, sales promotion and advertising — each with its own moment, its own tools and its own cost. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
