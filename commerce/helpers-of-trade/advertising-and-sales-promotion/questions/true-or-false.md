# Advertising and Sales Promotion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Effective advertising targets the likely buyers and is eye-catching, informative and memorable. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — Samoa's test of effective advertising.

**2.** Trade sales promotion includes training and cooperative advertising. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — Nigeria's trade forms: bonuses, free goods, cooperative advertising, training.
