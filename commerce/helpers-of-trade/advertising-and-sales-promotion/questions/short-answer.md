# Advertising and Sales Promotion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four forms of promotion. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Personal selling, public relations, sales promotion and advertising.

**2.** Give three media or methods of advertising used in Samoa. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any three of: word of mouth, radio, newspaper, television, sponsorship, roadside signs.
