# Hands-on — Advertising and Sales Promotion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the last thing you bought because something persuaded you. Was it a person talking, a free sample, a poster, or the shop's good name? Name which one it was. Sorting those four persuaders is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a business wins customers by promotion in four forms — personal selling, public relations, sales promotion and advertising — each with its own moment, its own tools and its own cost. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
