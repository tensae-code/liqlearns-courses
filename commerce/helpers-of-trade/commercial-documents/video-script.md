# Video script — Commercial Documents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every step of a trade leaves a document — enquiry, quotation, order, invoice, delivery note, statement, receipt — and the document is the proof, the instruction and the record of the deal.

Domestic buying and selling leaves a sequence of documents: trade journals and letters of enquiry first, then the quotation, the catalogue and price list, the order, the reference letter, and the pro forma invoice.

Then the goods move, and the papers move with them: the invoice — its contents, its five copies and who gets each; the advice note; the delivery note; and the consignment note / waybill used when an independent carrier carries the goods.

Deals go wrong and then close, on paper: credit notes and debit notes, from seller and from buyer, correct the account; the three-column statement of account sums the month; and the receipt proves the payment that ends the story.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
