# Commercial Documents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The same invoice is the seller's sales invoice and the buyer's purchases invoice. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — One paper, two books — Kenya's teaching.

**2.** A receipt is the document that demands payment for goods delivered. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The invoice demands payment; the receipt proves payment was made.
