# Commercial Documents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does E&OE on an invoice mean? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Errors and omissions excepted — only the seller may correct errors  b) Every order is equal  c) Payment ends on entry
**Answer: a) Errors and omissions excepted — only the seller may correct errors** — Kenya's book works the invoice item by item, E&OE included.

**2.** Which foreign-trade document is a document of title? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The airway bill  b) The freight note  c) The bill of lading
**Answer: c) The bill of lading** — The bill of lading is receipt, contract evidence and document of title; the airway bill is not a document of title.
