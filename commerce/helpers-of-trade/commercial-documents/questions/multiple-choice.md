# Commercial Documents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does E&OE on an invoice mean? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Errors and omissions excepted — only the seller may correct errors  b) Every order is equal  c) Payment ends on entry
**Answer: a) Errors and omissions excepted — only the seller may correct errors** — Kenya's book works the invoice item by item, E&OE included.

**2.** Which foreign-trade document is a document of title? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The airway bill  b) The freight note  c) The bill of lading
**Answer: c) The bill of lading** — The bill of lading is receipt, contract evidence and document of title; the airway bill is not a document of title.

**3.** What does this lesson teach about the business documents? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The document chain of a business transaction in order: purchase requisition → pro forma invoice → purchase …  b) Ledger accounts: larger businesses keep separate ledger accounts for the bank, for each type of equipment and …  c) Business and the environment: laws exist to stop businesses polluting water and air, contaminating land with …
**Answer: a) The document chain of a business transaction in order: purchase requisition → pro forma invoice → purchase …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its business documents part (Dominica's book).
