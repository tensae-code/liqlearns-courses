# Commercial Documents — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List, in order, four documents in the home-trade sequence before the goods move. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any four in sequence from: trade journals / letters of enquiry, quotation, catalogue and price list, order, reference letter, pro forma invoice.

**2.** Name three documents a business must keep, from Malaysia's book. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Invoices, receipts, payment vouchers (and the other records of trade).

**3.** Explain the transport documents part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Transport documents: why they matter (contract of carriage, proof of delivery and condition — 'clean' vs 'dirty', evidence of ownership), the consignment note for inland movement, and the international documents — import licence, bill of lading (document of title, receipt, and terms of carriage) and airway bill (evidence of the air contract, but not a document of title)
