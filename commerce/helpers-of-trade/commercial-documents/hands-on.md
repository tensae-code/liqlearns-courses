# Hands-on — Commercial Documents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the last thing bought on credit for a home or a shop. Ask: what paper proved what was ordered, what was delivered, and what was paid? If any paper is missing, the argument has no answer. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every step of a trade leaves a document — enquiry, quotation, order, invoice, delivery note, statement, receipt — and the document is the proof, the instruction and the record of the deal. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
