# Lesson plan — Arbitration and Trade Disputes

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Helpers of Trade · **Lesson:** Arbitration and Trade Disputes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when traders fall out over a bargain, the dispute can be settled by arbitration — a trusted decider chosen by the trade itself — and a commodity exchange enforces its contracts through the margin traders deposit, a guarantee fund standing behind every trade, and its own arbitration, so bargains hold and quarrels are decided quickly by people who know the trade.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade, lesson 14 of 17 in the course ladder; it stands on Banking Services for Trade, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
