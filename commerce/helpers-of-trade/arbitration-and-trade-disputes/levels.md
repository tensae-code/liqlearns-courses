# Arbitration and Trade Disputes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — when traders fall out over a bargain, the dispute can be settled by arbitration — a trusted decider chosen by the trade itself — and a commodity exchange enforces its contracts through the margin traders deposit, a guarantee fund standing behind every trade, and its own arbitration, so bargains hold and quarrels are decided quickly by people who know the trade. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
