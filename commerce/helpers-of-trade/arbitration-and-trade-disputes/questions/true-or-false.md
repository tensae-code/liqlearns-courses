# Arbitration and Trade Disputes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A trader who has deposited margin can walk away from a bargain at no cost.
**Answer: False** — The margin is deposited exactly so a bargain cannot simply be walked away from.

**2.** The guarantee fund and the arbitration mechanism are two of the ways a commodity exchange enforces its contracts.
**Answer: True** — Together with the margin, they are the exchange's contract-enforcement machinery.
