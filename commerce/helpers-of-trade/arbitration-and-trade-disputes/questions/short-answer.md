# Arbitration and Trade Disputes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three things through which a commodity exchange enforces its contracts, as this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The margin traders deposit, the guarantee fund, and the exchange's arbitration mechanism.

**2.** In your own words, what is arbitration?
**Answer:** Settling a dispute by a trusted decider chosen within the trade itself, instead of fighting the quarrel out in court.
