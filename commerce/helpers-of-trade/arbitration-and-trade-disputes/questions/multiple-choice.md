# Arbitration and Trade Disputes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In a commodity exchange, what is the margin a trader deposits for?
a) A tax on every trade  b) A deposit that makes the bargain binding, so a trader cannot simply walk away from it  c) The exchange's membership badge
**Answer: b) A deposit that makes the bargain binding, so a trader cannot simply walk away from it** — The margin stands behind the trader's promise to complete the trade.

**2.** What does the guarantee fund of a commodity exchange do?
a) It stands behind the exchange's trades, so a bargain holds even if one side fails  b) It sets the price of every commodity  c) It stores the commodities
**Answer: a) It stands behind the exchange's trades, so a bargain holds even if one side fails** — That is the fund's job in enforcing contracts.

**3.** When two traders in the exchange dispute a bargain, where is the dispute settled?
a) It is always left unsettled  b) In the exchange's own arbitration  c) Only in a foreign court
**Answer: b) In the exchange's own arbitration** — The exchange's arbitration mechanism decides disputes between its traders.

**4.** Why does an exchange settle disputes by arbitration instead of leaving every quarrel to the courts?
a) So disputes are decided quickly, by people who know the trade  b) So no one ever has to keep a bargain  c) So prices can be hidden
**Answer: a) So disputes are decided quickly, by people who know the trade** — Arbitration keeps trade moving instead of clogging the courts.
