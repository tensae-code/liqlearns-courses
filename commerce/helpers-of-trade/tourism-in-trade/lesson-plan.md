# Lesson plan — Tourism in Trade

**Course:** Commerce · **Title:** Helpers of Trade · **Lesson:** Tourism in Trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** tourism is a trade service: visitors pay for transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights, so a place earns from tourism as it earns from any other service in trade — and like other trade it can be developed and promoted on purpose, with real gains and real costs to weigh.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit 3 — Helpers of Trade, lesson 15 of 17 in the course ladder; it stands on Arbitration and Trade Disputes, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks tourism is only holidays and sights → it is trade: the visitor buys services all along the way, and a place can organise, develop and promote that trade like any other.
- Go back to the one thing: tourism is a trade service: visitors pay for transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights, so a place earns from tourism as it earns from any other service in trade — and like other trade it can be developed and promoted on purpose, with real gains and real costs to weigh.
