# Video script — Tourism in Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
tourism is a trade service: visitors pay for transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights, so a place earns from tourism as it earns from any other service in trade — and like other trade it can be developed and promoted on purpose, with real gains and real costs to weigh.

**Tourism as a trade service.** Tourism as a trade service: its meaning and types — travel for rest, for sights, for business and more — counted inside commerce because the visitor buys services all along the way, as with transport or banking (Nigeria's book.)

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
