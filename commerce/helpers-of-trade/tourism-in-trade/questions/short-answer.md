# Tourism in Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is tourism as a trade service?
**Answer:** Visitors travelling and paying for the services that receive them — transport, beds, food, guiding and sights — so a place earns from tourism as it earns from other services in trade.

**2.** Name the body the book names for developing tourism in Nigeria.
**Answer:** The Nigerian Tourism Development Corporation.
