# Tourism in Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is tourism counted inside commerce?
a) Because visitors buy services — transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights — all along their way  b) Because tourists grow crops  c) Because tourism needs no services
**Answer: a) Because visitors buy services — transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights — all along their way** — The visitor's spending is trade in services.

**2.** What is the Nigerian Tourism Development Corporation?
a) A private hotel chain  b) The body charged with developing tourism in Nigeria  c) An airline
**Answer: b) The body charged with developing tourism in Nigeria** — The book names it as the corporation behind tourism development in Nigeria.

**3.** Which of these is a type of tourism the book teaches?
a) Travel for rest and sights  b) Travel that buys nothing  c) Travel with no services used
**Answer: a) Travel for rest and sights** — Tourism has types — travel for rest, for sights, for business and more.

**4.** What must a place weigh when it develops tourism?
a) Only the money visitors bring  b) Nothing at all  c) The advantages of tourism against its disadvantages
**Answer: c) The advantages of tourism against its disadvantages** — The book teaches both sides, so development is a weighed decision.
