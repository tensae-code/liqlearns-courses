# Tourism in Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A visitor's spending earns only for the hotel that lodges him.
**Answer: False** — Transport, lodging, food, guiding and gifts all earn from one visit — many local people share the earning.

**2.** Tourism is a service trade, like transport or banking.
**Answer: True** — The visitor buys services, and services are part of trade.
