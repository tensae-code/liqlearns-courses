# Tourism in Trade

## Do it first

A family from far away spends a week in your town. They pay for the bus, a room, meals, a guide and gifts to take home. Count how many local people earned from that one visit. That earning — visitors buying services — is this lesson.

## Tourism in Trade

**Tourism as a trade service.** Tourism as a trade service: its meaning and types — travel for rest, for sights, for business and more — counted inside commerce because the visitor buys services all along the way, as with transport or banking (Nigeria's book.)

**Tourism in Nigeria.** Tourism development in Nigeria and the Nigerian Tourism Development Corporation, the body charged with developing it; selected tourist centres; and the advantages and disadvantages of tourism — what visitors bring to a place, and what a place must watch and weigh (Nigeria's book.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: tourism is a trade service: visitors pay for transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights, so a place earns from tourism as it earns from any other service in trade — and like other trade it can be developed and promoted on purpose, with real gains and real costs to weigh. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: tourism in trade. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
