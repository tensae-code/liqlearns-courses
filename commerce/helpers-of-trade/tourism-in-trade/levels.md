# Tourism in Trade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — tourism is a trade service: visitors pay for transport, lodging, food, guiding and sights, so a place earns from tourism as it earns from any other service in trade — and like other trade it can be developed and promoted on purpose, with real gains and real costs to weigh. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
