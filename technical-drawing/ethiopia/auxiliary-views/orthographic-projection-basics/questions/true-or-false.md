# Orthographic Projection — Points, Lines, Planes and Surfaces — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Orthographic projection shows an object in principal views, but when an object has inclined or oblique planes or features, no principal view shows their true shape. The auxiliary view is the special orthographic view that answers this, built with folding lines and reference lines from the projection of points, lines and planes in space.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
