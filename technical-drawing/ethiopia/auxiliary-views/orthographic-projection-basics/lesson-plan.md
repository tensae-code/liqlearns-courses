# Lesson plan — Orthographic Projection — Points, Lines, Planes and Surfaces

**Course:** Technical Drawing — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 2 — Auxiliary Views · **Lesson:** Orthographic Projection — Points, Lines, Planes and Surfaces
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Auxiliary Views (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Orthographic projection shows an object in principal views, but when an object has inclined or oblique planes or features, no principal view shows their true shape. The auxiliary view is the special orthographic view that answers this, built with folding lines and reference lines from the projection of points, lines and planes in space.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Auxiliary Views (recommended, never forced).
**Guide time for this lesson:** 6 periods (2 theory, 4 practice) (teacher guide, printed pp. 19-24) — in the guide, practice takes the larger share wherever the lesson is split.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.
