# Hands-on — Developing Solids — Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Develop three solids, one by each method: a prism by parallel lines, a cone by radial lines, and one oblique solid by triangulation. Label the method on each pattern.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: Three construction methods develop any solid: parallel-line development for prisms and cylinders, radial-line development for pyramids and cones, and the rule of true length by triangulation for solids whose edges no view shows truly.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
