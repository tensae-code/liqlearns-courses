## ℹ Info

Where two solids meet, their surfaces cross in a line of intersection that must be found before either can be made: find the piercing points and the visible and hidden lines of intersection, determine the line of intersection of two solids such as prisms and cylinders, and carry the intersection curve into the development of each solid.

## Piercing points and the line of intersection

Identify the piercing points — where one solid's edges or surface pierce the other's — and join them in order to determine the line of intersection of the two solids. Use the two methods of finding points of intersection alternately, and distinguish the visible lines of intersection from the hidden ones.

## Carrying the curve into the development

Construct the development of two intersected regular solids — prisms and cylinders — with the intersection curve carried into the flat pattern. A development that ignores the intersection would cut the sheet wrong: the curve belongs in the pattern.
