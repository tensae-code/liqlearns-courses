# Hands-on — Worked Developments — The Answer Plates

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one development you drew earlier in this level and lay it beside the matching solution plate's method. List every difference you find — stations, fold lines, true lengths — and redraw yours to the plate's standard.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: The teacher guide prints full-page worked solution drawings, lettered A to J, as the answers to the development exercises. They are the only place the course's expected drawing standard is fully visible, and they set the accuracy bar for all the development work in this level.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
