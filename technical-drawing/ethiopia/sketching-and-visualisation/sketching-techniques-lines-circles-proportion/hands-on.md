# Hands-on — Sketching Techniques — Lines, Circles and Proportion

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sketch a straight line and mark its midpoint by eye, then check it. Draw one circle by each of the four methods. Then sketch one solid object in two views, keeping the proportions the same in both.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: The hand skills of sketching are demonstrable procedures: sketch straight lines and long lines free-hand, find the midpoint of a line, draw a circle by the guide's four methods, sketch angles and arcs, lay out a sketch using proportion, and sketch multi-view drawings of three-dimensional objects.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
