# Free-hand Sketching — Principles and Place in Design — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Free-hand sketching is a fast form of graphical communication: it organises thoughts, generates ideas, records dimensions and transfers information, and it is the beginning phase of design, where a design's final geometric parameters and constraints are first fixed. It needs no artistic ability — once the basic techniques are known, the limit on a sketch is not the ability to draw but the ability to think through the details of a design. — shown in the learner's own words and in a finished drawing task done to the lesson's standard.
