# Lesson plan — Types of Section — Choosing and Drawing the Right Cut

**Course:** Technical Drawing — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 3 — Sectional Views · **Lesson:** Types of Section — Choosing and Drawing the Right Cut
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Sectional Views (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** There are several types of sectional view, each with particular advantages in describing interior features. The skill is to select the appropriate type for a given object and to perform the sectional view in that preferable type, including constructing the true shapes of cut surfaces.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Sectional Views (recommended, never forced).
**Guide time for this lesson:** 8 periods (2 theory, 6 practice) (teacher guide, printed pp. 42-44) — in the guide, practice takes the larger share wherever the lesson is split.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.
