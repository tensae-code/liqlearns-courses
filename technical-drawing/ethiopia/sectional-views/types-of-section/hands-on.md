# Hands-on — Types of Section — Choosing and Drawing the Right Cut

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one object with interior features. Draw it sectioned in two different types, then write one sentence on which type describes its interior better, and why.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: There are several types of sectional view, each with particular advantages in describing interior features. The skill is to select the appropriate type for a given object and to perform the sectional view in that preferable type, including constructing the true shapes of cut surfaces.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
