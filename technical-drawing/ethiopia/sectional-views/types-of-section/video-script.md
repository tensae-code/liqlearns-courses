# Video script — Types of Section — Choosing and Drawing the Right Cut

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
There are several types of sectional view, each with particular advantages in describing interior features. The skill is to select the appropriate type for a given object and to perform the sectional view in that preferable type, including constructing the true shapes of cut surfaces.

Watch the drawing being made, step by step, in the order the lesson gives.

Identify the types of sectional view and compare and contrast them by their particular advantages in describing interior features. No type is best for everything: each cut reveals some interiors well and others badly.

For a given object, select the type of section that describes its interior best, and perform the sectional view in that type. Where the cut passes obliquely, construct the true shape of the cut surface as part of the drawing. This is the unit's core lesson and most of it is drawing practice.

Remember it this way: There are several types of sectional view, each with particular advantages in describing interior features. The skill is to select the appropriate type for a given object and to perform the sectional view in that preferable type, including constructing the true shapes of cut surfaces.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
