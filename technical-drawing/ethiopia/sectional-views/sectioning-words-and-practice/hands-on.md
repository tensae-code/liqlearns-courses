# Hands-on — Sectioning — Words and Common Practice

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a simple hollow object, mark a cutting-plane line across one view, and draw the section it produces with the cut faces hatched. Label both marks.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: A section view shows interior details clearly: the object is imagined cut, a cutting-plane line shows where the object was cut to obtain the section view, and section lining (hatching) marks the cut faces so the section can be read.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
