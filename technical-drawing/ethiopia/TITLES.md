# Technical Drawing — Ethiopia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Technical Drawing belongs to the country: this course is
Ethiopia's own — built from the Ministry of Education's
Grade 12 Technical Drawing **teacher guide** alone. No
student textbook for this subject exists in any record we
hold, and no title below pretends otherwise: every lesson
stands on teacher-guide evidence, marked as such in its
sources. Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in
path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grade the book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

Titles are the common-sense name of the thing taught, never
the book's unit name: the guide's five units (Sketching and
Visualization; Auxiliary Views; Sectional View;
Dimensioning; Development and Intersection) survive as the
five level names, not as lesson titles.

## The titles

**Level 1 — Sketching and Visualisation**:

- `sketching-and-visualisation/` — Free-hand Sketching —
  Principles and Place in Design; Sketching Techniques —
  Lines, Circles and Proportion; Pictorial Sketching —
  Isometric Sketching Someone Else Could Build From.

**Level 2 — Auxiliary Views**:

- `auxiliary-views/` — Orthographic Projection — Points,
  Lines, Planes and Surfaces; Drawing an Auxiliary View —
  The True Shape of an Inclined Surface; Secondary
  Auxiliary Views — Projecting a Projection.

**Level 3 — Sectional Views**:

- `sectional-views/` — Sectioning — Words and Common
  Practice; Types of Section — Choosing and Drawing the
  Right Cut; Conventional Practice in Sectioning — The
  Agreed Exceptions.

**Level 4 — Dimensioning**:

- `dimensioning/` — Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and
  Reading Direction; Size and Location Dimensions — The
  Theory; Arranging Dimensions — The Two Methods; Placing
  Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes.

**Level 5 — Development and Intersection**:

- `development-and-intersection/` — Surface Development —
  Flat Sheets That Become Things; Developing Solids —
  Parallel Line, Radial Line, Triangulation; Intersections
  — Where Two Solids Cross; Worked Developments — The
  Answer Plates.
