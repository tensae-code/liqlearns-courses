# Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This statement is the lesson's one thing: Placement follows rules: a diameter is dimensioned with a numerical value preceded by the diameter symbol, a radius likewise with the radius symbol; cylinders are dimensioned in the rectangular view where possible; a dimension to the centre of an arc or radius shows a small cross at the centre; and hole depths and the diameter and depth of counter-bored, spot-faced or countersunk holes are specified in notes.
**Answer: True** — it states the lesson's teaching in full.

**2.** A learner who lands on this lesson directly must finish every earlier level before starting it.
**Answer: False** — earlier levels are recommended first, never a forced gate; every lesson stands alone.
