# Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's task once with what you already know: Dimension a block that has a through hole, a blind hole and one rounded corner. Use the diameter and radius symbols correctly, mark the arc centre with a small cross, and write the hole notes. Keep your first attempt — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

Placement follows rules: a diameter is dimensioned with a numerical value preceded by the diameter symbol, a radius likewise with the radius symbol; cylinders are dimensioned in the rectangular view where possible; a dimension to the centre of an arc or radius shows a small cross at the centre; and hole depths and the diameter and depth of counter-bored, spot-faced or countersunk holes are specified in notes.

## Diameters, radii and cylinders

Dimension a diameter with its numerical value preceded by the diameter symbol, and a radius likewise with the radius symbol. Dimension cylinders in the rectangular view where possible — the view where the cylinder shows as a rectangle carries its diameter and length most clearly.

## Centres, holes and notes

Where a dimension is given to the centre of an arc or radius, show a small cross at the centre. Specify the diameter and depth of counter-bored, spot-faced or countersunk holes in a note; the depth of a blind hole may also be specified in a note, being the depth of the full diameter from the surface of the object. The guide's placement rules run as a numbered set past 18 — work through them as a set, not as isolated tips.
