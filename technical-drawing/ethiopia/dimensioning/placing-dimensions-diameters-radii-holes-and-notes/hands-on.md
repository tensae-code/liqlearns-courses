# Hands-on — Placing Dimensions — Diameters, Radii, Holes and Notes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Dimension a block that has a through hole, a blind hole and one rounded corner. Use the diameter and radius symbols correctly, mark the arc centre with a small cross, and write the hole notes.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: Placement follows rules: a diameter is dimensioned with a numerical value preceded by the diameter symbol, a radius likewise with the radius symbol; cylinders are dimensioned in the rectangular view where possible; a dimension to the centre of an arc or radius shows a small cross at the centre; and hole depths and the diameter and depth of counter-bored, spot-faced or countersunk holes are specified in notes.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
