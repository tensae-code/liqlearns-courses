# Video script — Arranging Dimensions — The Two Methods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The lesson is text-first: this script says nothing the lesson text does not already teach.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
There are two methods of arranging dimensions on a drawing. Use them alternately, select dimensions so as to reduce the number of dimension lines, and prepare dimensions across different views for any shape of object.

Watch the drawing being made, step by step, in the order the lesson gives.

Identify the two methods of arranging dimensions and use them alternately on drawings, so you can feel what each does to the drawing's clarity. The arrangement is a choice the drafter makes, not a habit to fall into.

Select dimensions so as to reduce the number of dimension lines: every line removed without losing information makes the drawing easier to read and harder to misread. Perform the different dimensioning techniques for any shapes of object, preparing dimensions across different views rather than crowding them into one.

Remember it this way: There are two methods of arranging dimensions on a drawing. Use them alternately, select dimensions so as to reduce the number of dimension lines, and prepare dimensions across different views for any shape of object.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
