# Hands-on — Arranging Dimensions — The Two Methods

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Dimension the same object twice, once in each arrangement method. Count the dimension lines in each version and say which drawing is clearer, and why.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: There are two methods of arranging dimensions on a drawing. Use them alternately, select dimensions so as to reduce the number of dimension lines, and prepare dimensions across different views for any shape of object.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
