# Lesson plan — Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction

**Course:** Technical Drawing — Ethiopia · **Level folder:** Level 4 — Dimensioning · **Lesson:** Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Dimensioning (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Dimensioning has an alphabet: dimension lines, arrows, figures and extension lines, with basic symbols and forms; and dimension figures are read in one of two systems — the aligned system or the unidirectional system.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Dimensioning (recommended, never forced).
**Guide time for this lesson:** 1 period (teacher guide, printed pp. 54-59) — in the guide, practice takes the larger share wherever the lesson is split.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The lesson teaches through its text first: every picture's and GIF's teaching is also in the text, so the lesson is fully learnable by listening to its text read aloud.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Dimensioning has an alphabet: dimension lines, arrows, figures and extension lines, with basic symbols and forms; and dimension figures are read in one of two systems — the aligned system or the unidirectional system.
2. Do the lesson's drawing task (Do It Yourself, below) to the standard the lesson sets.

Paper (grid paper where the lesson says so), a pencil, and a ruler for checking — the drawing itself is free-hand or construction as the lesson directs. Nothing else.
