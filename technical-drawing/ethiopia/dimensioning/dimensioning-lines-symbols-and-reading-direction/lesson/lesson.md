# Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction

## Do it first

Before you read, try the lesson's task once with what you already know: Dimension one simple part twice: once with figures aligned, once unidirectional. Label every element — dimension line, arrows, figure, extension lines. Keep your first attempt — you will compare it with your work at the end.

## ℹ Info

Dimensioning has an alphabet: dimension lines, arrows, figures and extension lines, with basic symbols and forms; and dimension figures are read in one of two systems — the aligned system or the unidirectional system.

## What dimensioning is and where it applies

Dimensioning states the sizes and positions that words alone cannot fix. This lesson opens with the use of dimensioning and where to apply it on a drawing.

## The elements

Learn the basic symbols, forms and elements of dimensioning: the dimension line that carries the size, the arrows that mark its ends, the figures that state the value, and the extension lines that tie the dimension to the feature it measures. Practise producing dimensioning arrows, lines and letters to standard.

## Two reading directions

Dimension figures are oriented in two systems: in the aligned system the figure sits with its dimension line and is read along it; in the unidirectional system figures are placed to be read one way, from the bottom of the sheet. Know both, and read either.
