# Hands-on — Dimensioning — Lines, Symbols and Reading Direction

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Dimension one simple part twice: once with figures aligned, once unidirectional. Label every element — dimension line, arrows, figure, extension lines.

## Show it with real things
Work the lesson's drawing task on real paper, naming each step aloud as you do it: what you are drawing, why this step comes here, and what the finished drawing must show. Compare the result against the lesson's one thing: Dimensioning has an alphabet: dimension lines, arrows, figures and extension lines, with basic symbols and forms; and dimension figures are read in one of two systems — the aligned system or the unidirectional system.

## Check it for one day
For one day, look for this lesson's teaching in real made things around you — drawings, packages, sheet-metal objects, buildings. Each time you see it, note what you saw and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
