# Health Education course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Health Education is one world course, organised by **levels, not
grades**. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path (see TITLES.md): **Staying Safe → Fair
Play and the Body**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part from the
health sections of Ethiopia's Health and Physical Education
books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the honest account of where the health
sections begin (Grade 5) and the acceptance reckoning.
