# Health Education course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Health Education is one world course, organised by **levels, not
grades**. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path (see TITLES.md): **Staying Safe → Fair
Play and the Body**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part from the
health sections of Ethiopia's Health and Physical Education
books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the honest account of where the health
sections begin (Grade 5) and the acceptance reckoning.

## Unit 1 — Staying Safe

1. **Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise** (`staying-safe/hygiene-for-sport`) — personal hygiene around sport: sweat and dirt left unwashed become a breeding place for bacteria — so wash the body after PE and sport, keep sports…
2. **Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely** (`staying-safe/paying-attention-and-crossing-the-road-safely`) — attention — the mind's focus held on one thing — taught through the books' first traffic-safety teaching: red means stop, green means go, keep left…
3. **Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid** (`staying-safe/preventing-sports-injuries-and-first-aid`)
3a. **Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling** (`staying-safe/self-defence-techniques`) — the Grade 9 technique family taught as described movement steps — basic stance, escaping grabs, blocks and strikes, grappling — with the Grade 9 guide's printed practice safety rules taught in full.
3b. **Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques** (`staying-safe/self-defence-advanced-techniques`) — the Grade 10 pass — advanced escapes, the roundhouse kick and grappling throws; its advanced-strikes part is knowledge-only, last-resort awareness, exactly as the book directs. — how sports injuries happen and how to prevent them — no warm-up, tiredness, wrong kit and disobeyed instructions are the books' named causes — and …
4. **Staying Safe on the Street — Self-Defence Principles and Resolving Conflict** (`staying-safe/staying-safe-on-the-street`) — staying safe before any fight: what self-defence is for, its principles (alertness, decisiveness, speed, surprise), the street-safety rules, the et…
5. **Staying Safe While Exercising** (`staying-safe/staying-safe-while-exercising`) — the books' precautions for fitness exercise: warm up properly, wear correct sport kit, go from easy to hard, rest in the middle, stop on tiredness …
6. **Non-communicable Diseases** (`staying-safe/non-communicable-diseases`) — non-communicable diseases: diabetes, hypertension, asthma, heart disease, cancer and obesity — their causes, risks and prevention — taught in Cuba's Grade 2 teacher guide as what obesity is (a chronic disease of excess body fat, spotted with Cuban BMI percentile tables) and the teacher's prevention work (Cuba's teacher guide, evidence only).
7. **The Body Systems** (`staying-safe/the-body-systems`) — the body systems: skeleton, muscles, digestion, breathing, circulation, nerves, glands, urinary and reproductive systems — how each works — taught in Croatia's vocational Čovjek i zdravlje (student book, publisher SAMPLE) as how the body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems, and blood and its cells as the body's transport and defence system.
8. **What Health Is** (`staying-safe/what-health-is`) — what health is: complete physical, mental and social well-being — and the dimensions and factors that make it up — taught in the United Kingdom's Edexcel GCSE PE Student Book as physical, emotional and social health, fitness and well-being, and the benefits of physical activity for each kind of health.
9. **Diseases of Inactivity** (`staying-safe/diseases-of-inactivity`) — diseases of inactivity: the hypokinetic diseases of sitting still — heart disease, hypertension, obesity and diabetes — and how activity prevents them — taught in the United Kingdom's Edexcel GCSE PE Student Book as the consequences of a sedentary lifestyle, and obesity and body types (somatotypes).
10. **Hardening the Body** (`staying-safe/hardening-the-body`) — hardening the body: using air, sun, water and natural terrain to harden the body and build fitness, safely — taught in Tajikistan's Grades 1–4 Physical Culture book as sun, air and water — our best friends (including walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer), and water and the drinking regime — how much water to drink and when.
11. **Eating a Balanced Diet** (`staying-safe/eating-a-balanced-diet`) — eating a balanced diet: the nutrients and food groups, what each does, and building balanced meals and menus — taught in Turkmenistan's Bedenterbiýe as the foundations of proper nutrition: the nutrition regime, the main composition of food, and the hygiene characterisation of the main food products.
12. **Saying No to Drugs, Alcohol and Tobacco** (`staying-safe/saying-no-to-drugs-alcohol-and-tobacco`) — saying no to drugs, alcohol and tobacco: what these substances do, addiction, secondhand smoke, and refusing and quitting — taught in Turkmenistan's Bedenterbiýe as the harmful habits that damage health (Saglyga zeper ýetirýän zyýanly endikler) — the habits that injure health and why to stay away from them — and in Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life Guide as substance abuse: its types, consequences and risks, with the legal alcohol age in Namibia named as 18 (Healthy Lifestyle Session 4) (Namibia teacher guide, evidence only).
13. **Healthy Habits Every Day** (`staying-safe/healthy-habits-every-day`) — healthy habits every day: the daily lifestyle — food, activity, hygiene, rest and check-ups — that keeps you healthy for life — taught in Jordan's Grade 1 PE Term 2 book as following daily healthy habits, telling healthy from unhealthy pictures and aiming a ball at the picture of a daily healthy habit.
14. **Staying Safe Online** (`staying-safe/staying-safe-online`) — staying safe online: safe use of phones, the internet and social media, and cyberbullying — taught in Jordan's Grade 7 PE Term 2 book as the healthy and best use of technology: useful educational content instead of wasting time, avoiding long sitting in front of screens, regular breaks and exercise, and what technology has done for sport (e.g. the video assistant referee).
15. **Your Body in Exercise** (`staying-safe/your-body-in-exercise`) — your body in exercise: how the heart, lungs, blood and muscles respond to exercise, and monitoring your own state — taught in Jordan's Grade 7 PE Term 2 book as sport and the respiratory system: the respiratory system's organs and gas exchange, and how sport improves it (better gas exchange in the alveoli, more oxygen to the blood, lower asthma risk) with breathing exercises.
16. **Puberty and Growing Up** (`staying-safe/puberty-and-growing-up`) — puberty and growing up: the changes of adolescence in body, feelings and life, and the stages of growth and life — taught in Jordan's Grade 9 PE Term 2 book as adolescence and sport: adolescence as the stage between childhood and maturity, sport as a lifestyle (choosing a suitable sport, a timetable, variety and balance, self-reward), and its benefits for adolescents (physical growth, stronger muscles and bones, mental health, less stress, self-confidence) — and in Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH MHM Guide as what puberty is: the body changes of puberty, hormones, when puberty starts for girls and boys, and the new emotions that come with it (Kiribati teacher guide, evidence only) — and in Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life Guide as puberty: what changes and how to handle it (Healthy Lifestyle Session 7) (Namibia teacher guide, evidence only).
17. **Keeping My Body Clean** (`staying-safe/keeping-my-body-clean`) — keeping my body clean: washing the body, hands, hair and nails, grooming, and clean clothes — taught in Kiribati's Year 3 *Stay Clean and Healthy* big book as a morning washing routine and washing hands again and again through the day, one child's whole day told as a chain of washing moments, and in Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH MHM Guide as hygiene in puberty (teacher guide, evidence).
