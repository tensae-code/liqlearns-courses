# About the Health Education course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the first part from the
health-knowledge sections of Ethiopia's Health and Physical
Education books, read in full, page by page. The one exception
is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## Where the first part comes from — stated honestly

Ethiopia's HPE student books Grades 1–4 carry **no health chapter
at all**; the first true health sections are in Grade 5
(exercise precautions, §1.2; doping, §3.6). There is no hygiene,
nutrition or disease chapter in the Grade 5 book, and none has
been invented for this course. The cleanliness and body-care
teaching of the younger years stands in the Science course,
where Ethiopia's Environmental Science books carry it. Book
entries are named in full in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`;
teacher guides are evidence only, never counted as pupil-taught
(the G1–G8 guide scans held are truncated; G9 is complete, G10
substantively complete).

**Acceptance reckoning:** this course's first part is taught in
1 country (Ethiopia), except any lesson whose sources state it
stands on teacher-guide evidence only — counted nowhere, and
says so.
