# Keeping Teeth Healthy

## Do it first

What do you think this lesson teaches? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — My Mouth, Gums and Teeth

Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 *Health Teacher Guide* teaches, in its Strand 2 teeth lessons: **my mouth, gums and teeth; the uses of the different teeth; and how to look after my teeth, mouth and gums**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for My Teeth

Papua New Guinea's Grade 4 *Health Teacher Guide* teaches, in Strand 2 Unit 2: **care of teeth, and dental products and caring for teeth**.
