# Keeping Teeth Healthy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — My Mouth, Gums and Teeth
Pass mark: Say what Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide teaches about the mouth, gums and teeth: the uses of the different teeth, and how to look after teeth, mouth and gums.

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for My Teeth
Pass mark: Say what Papua New Guinea's Grade 4 guide teaches about care of teeth and dental products for caring for teeth.
