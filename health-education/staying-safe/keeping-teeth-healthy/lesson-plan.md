# Lesson plan — Keeping Teeth Healthy

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Keeping Teeth Healthy
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Keeping teeth healthy: the teeth and mouth, brushing, and the foods that help or harm teeth — taught here through Papua New Guinea's way: its Standards Based Health teacher guides for Grades 3 and 4.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Keeping My Body Clean and Eating a Balanced Diet.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **My Mouth, Gums and Teeth** — Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 Health teacher guide teaches, in its Strand 2 teeth lessons: my mouth, gums and teeth; the uses of the different teeth; and how to look after my teeth, mouth and gums (printed p. 79).
2. **Caring for My Teeth** — Papua New Guinea's Grade 4 Health teacher guide teaches, in Strand 2 Unit 2: care of teeth, and dental products and caring for teeth (printed p. 10, as filed).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Keeping My Body Clean. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide teaches about the mouth, gums and teeth: the uses of the different teeth and how to look after teeth, mouth and gums.
2. Say what Papua New Guinea's Grade 4 guide teaches about care of teeth and dental products.
