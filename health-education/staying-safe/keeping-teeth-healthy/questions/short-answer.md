# Keeping Teeth Healthy — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 guide teach about the teeth?
**Answer: My mouth, gums and teeth; the uses of the different teeth; and how to look after my teeth, mouth and gums.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Papua New Guinea's Grade 4 guide teach about teeth?
**Answer: Care of teeth, and dental products and caring for teeth.**
