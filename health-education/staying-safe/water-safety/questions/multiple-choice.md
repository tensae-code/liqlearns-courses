# Water Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoa's curriculum teaches simple water safety rules for the river, the sea and…
a) Swimming pools  b) The classroom  c) The road
**Answer: a) Swimming pools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How often are Samoa's water safety rules practised?
a) Every year  b) Once only  c) Never
**Answer: a) Every year**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Samoa's curriculum, swimming classes are…
a) Optional  b) Forbidden  c) Only for teachers
**Answer: a) Optional**
