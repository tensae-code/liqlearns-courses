# Hands-on — Your Body in Exercise

The lesson with your hands and your body, not only your reading.

## Feel your breathing change

Sit still for one minute and count your breaths. Then move briskly for two minutes — walk fast, jog on the spot — and count again. Write both numbers: this is your breathing system answering exercise, the book's gas exchange at work.

## A breathing exercise

Do the book's kind of practice: breathe in slowly, hold briefly, breathe out slowly, five times. Write how your breathing feels before and after.
