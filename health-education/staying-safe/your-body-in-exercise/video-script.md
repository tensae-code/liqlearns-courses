# Video script — Your Body in Exercise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You breathe in. You breathe out.
Inside, your breathing system swaps gases — air in, waste gas out.
Sport makes that swapping better, and brings more oxygen to your blood.
That is **your body in exercise**, as the book teaches it.
