# Your Body in Exercise

## Do it first

What happens inside you when you run and your breathing gets fast and deep? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The respiratory system

Jordan's Grade 7 book teaches, in its **Health Concepts and Habits** unit, **Sport and the Respiratory System**. First it teaches the **respiratory system's organs** and **gas exchange** — how the breathing system takes in and gives out gases.

## Sub lesson 2 — How sport improves breathing

Then it teaches **how sport improves it**: **better gas exchange in the alveoli**, **more oxygen to the blood**, and a **lower asthma risk** — and it teaches **breathing exercises** beside the knowledge.

In Jordan's own way, Term 2 closes with "Health Concepts and Habits" — healthy technology use (including technology in sport, e.g. VAR) and the body in exercise.
