# Your Body in Exercise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's lesson teaches the respiratory system's…
a) Organs and gas exchange  b) Shoes and socks  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Organs and gas exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does sport improve breathing, in the book's teaching?
a) Better gas exchange in the alveoli  b) Holding the breath forever  c) Breathing less air
**Answer: a) Better gas exchange in the alveoli**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book says sport brings…
a) More oxygen to the blood  b) Less oxygen to the blood  c) No change to the blood
**Answer: a) More oxygen to the blood**
