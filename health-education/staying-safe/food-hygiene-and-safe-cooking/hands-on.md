# Hands-on — Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cover the food

Find the food in your home or classroom that is uncovered. Cover it, the way El Salvador's book teaches, and say why: flies carry disease on their legs.

## Wash before preparing

Before your next meal, wash your hands and — with an adult — wash and disinfect the vegetables, the way the book's food-preparation lesson teaches.
