# Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flies make food dangerous because they carry, on their legs…
a) Dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites  b) Only water  c) Seeds
**Answer: a) Dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's book teaches keeping food…
a) Covered  b) On the floor  c) Open to flies
**Answer: a) Covered**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When preparing food, the book teaches washing and disinfecting…
a) Vegetables — and washing hands  b) Only the table  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Vegetables — and washing hands**
