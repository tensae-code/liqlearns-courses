# Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two illnesses flies carry onto food, as the book teaches.
**Answer: Any two of: dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites (carried on their legs).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two washing teachings does the food-preparation lesson give?
**Answer: Washing and disinfecting vegetables, and handwashing.**
