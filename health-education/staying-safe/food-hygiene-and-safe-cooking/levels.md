# Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Flies carry disease onto food
Pass mark: Say how flies make food dangerous, as El Salvador's book teaches: they carry dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites on their legs onto food — so keep food covered.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing food safely
Pass mark: Say how to prepare food safely, as the book teaches: wash and disinfect vegetables, and wash hands.
