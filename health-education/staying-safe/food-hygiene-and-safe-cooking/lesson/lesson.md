# Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking

## Do it first

Why must food be covered? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Flies carry disease onto food

El Salvador's Grade 1 *Ciencia, Salud y Medio Ambiente* book teaches, in Unit 4 Lesson 4 ¡Fuera moscas!, that **flies carry dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites on their legs onto food** — it names the symptoms of gastrointestinal illness and teaches **keeping food covered**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing food safely

Unit 4 Lesson 5 La preparación de los alimentos teaches **hygiene when preparing food: washing and disinfecting vegetables, handwashing**, and why street-vended food is often prepared unhygienically.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two food-hygiene teachings — flies carry disease onto food so keep it covered, and wash and disinfect vegetables and wash hands when preparing food — in your own words, the way El Salvador's book teaches them.
