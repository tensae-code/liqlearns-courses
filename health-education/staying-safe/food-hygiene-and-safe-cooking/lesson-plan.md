# Lesson plan — Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Food Hygiene and Safe Cooking
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** food hygiene and safe cooking — taught here through El Salvador's Grade 1 pass: flies carry disease onto food, and hygiene when preparing food.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Eating a Balanced Diet.

## Sub lessons
1. **Flies carry disease onto food** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: flies carry dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites on their legs onto food; symptoms of gastrointestinal illness and keeping food covered.
2. **Preparing food safely** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: hygiene when preparing food — washing and disinfecting vegetables, handwashing, and why street-vended food is often prepared unhygienically.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Eating a Balanced Diet. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how flies make food dangerous, as El Salvador's book teaches: they carry dysentery, typhoid fever and parasites on their legs onto food — so keep food covered.
2. Say how to prepare food safely, as the book teaches: wash and disinfect vegetables, and wash hands.
