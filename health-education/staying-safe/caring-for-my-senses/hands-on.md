# Hands-on — Caring for My Senses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My two eye promises

China's book teaches preventing serious eye disease and preventing eye injuries. Write two promises — one for each — naming one thing you will do and one thing you will avoid, for each kind of prevention.

## Spot the eye danger

Look around the room or yard you are in. List everything you can see that could injure an eye, and beside each one write how you would keep eyes safe from it.
