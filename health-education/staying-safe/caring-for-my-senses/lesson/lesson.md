# Caring for My Senses

## Do it first

What do you do to look after your eyes? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Preventing serious eye disease

China's Senior High *PEP PE & Health* book teaches, inside the lifestyle-and-disease-prevention section of its behaviour-shaping chapter, **preventing serious eye disease**.

Eye health is taught there as disease prevention — part of building healthy behaviour, beside preventing infectious diseases.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preventing eye injuries

The same section teaches **preventing eye injuries** — protecting the eyes from harm, as well as from disease.

In China's own way, this eye teaching sits inside the disease-prevention section: the senses are looked after as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a separate subject.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two eye teachings — preventing serious eye disease and preventing eye injuries — in your own words, the way China's book teaches them.
