# Caring for My Senses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** China's book teaches preventing…
a) Serious eye disease  b) Broken bones  c) Colds only
**Answer: a) Serious eye disease**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The same section also teaches preventing…
a) Eye injuries  b) Sunburn  c) Hunger
**Answer: a) Eye injuries**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This eye teaching sits inside the book's…
a) Lifestyle-and-disease-prevention section  b) Swimming chapter  c) Wushu chapter
**Answer: a) Lifestyle-and-disease-prevention section**
