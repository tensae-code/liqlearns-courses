# Lesson plan — Checking Health Information

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Checking Health Information
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Checking health information: telling trustworthy health information and advertising from harmful claims, and how media shapes health choices — taught here through Thailand's way: its Grade 5 and Grade 10 health books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Eating a Balanced Diet and Using Medicines Safely.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Knowing the Media in Time** — Thailand's Grade 5 SSR Health & PE teaches, in Unit 5, Ch. 2: รู้ทันข้อมูลข่าวสาร — reading health news and advertising critically to choose food and health products wisely (printed pp. 125–136).
2. **Advertising and Health Choices** — Thailand's Grade 10 พว. Health teaches, in Unit 4: how health advertising media influence consumption choices, and what to observe when buying products or services (printed pp. 54–67).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
