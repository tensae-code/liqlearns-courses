## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing the Media in Time

Thailand's Grade 5 *Health & PE* (SSR) teaches, in Unit 5, Ch. 2: **รู้ทันข้อมูลข่าวสาร — reading health news and advertising critically to choose food and health products wisely**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advertising and Health Choices

Thailand's Grade 10 *Health Education* (พว.) teaches, in Unit 4: **how health advertising media influence consumption choices, and what to observe when buying products or services**.
