# Staying Safe in Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulgaria's book teaches that mushroom-poisoning symptoms appear…
a) 8–24 hours after eating  b) Before eating  c) Only after a week
**Answer: a) 8–24 hours after eating**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bulgaria's book teaches that the two snakes dangerous to humans are…
a) The horned viper and the adder  b) Any two garden snakes  c) No snake in Bulgaria
**Answer: a) The horned viper and the adder**
