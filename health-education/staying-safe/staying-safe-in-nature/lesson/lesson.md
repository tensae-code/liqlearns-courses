## Sub lesson 1 — Poisonous mushrooms of Bulgaria

Bulgaria's *Биология и здравно образование за 7. клас* student book teaches **recognising Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms** — the red/white fly agaric, panther cap, death cap and others, shown in photos (За да знам повече: Отровните гъби в България). Poisoning symptoms appear **8–24 hours after eating**, and **a few grams of the green death cap can kill**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Poisonous snakes of Bulgaria

Bulgaria's *Биология и здравно образование за 7. клас* student book teaches **Bulgaria's 6 venomous snakes** (За да знам повече: Отровните змии в България). The two dangerous to humans are the **horned viper (пепелянка)** and the **adder (усойница)**, identified by photo and description. The book teaches what a bite does, and that **medical help must be sought immediately**.
