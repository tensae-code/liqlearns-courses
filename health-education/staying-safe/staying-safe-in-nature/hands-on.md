# Hands-on — Staying Safe in Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My poisonous mushrooms note

Bulgaria's book teaches how to recognise Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms, shown in photos. Write down the mushrooms this lesson names, when poisoning symptoms appear, and what this lesson teaches about the green death cap.

## My poisonous snakes note

Bulgaria's book teaches Bulgaria's venomous snakes and the two dangerous to humans. Write down the two this lesson names, and what this lesson teaches must be done immediately after a bite.
