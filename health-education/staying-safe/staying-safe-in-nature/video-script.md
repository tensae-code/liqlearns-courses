# Video script — Staying Safe in Nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bulgaria's book teaches you to recognise Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms — and that poisoning symptoms can appear 8 to 24 hours after eating.
It also teaches Bulgaria's poisonous snakes — and that after a bite, medical help must be sought immediately.
That is **staying safe in nature** — as Bulgaria's book teaches it.
