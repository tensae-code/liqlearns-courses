# Lesson plan — Staying Safe in Nature

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Staying Safe in Nature
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** staying safe in nature — Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms and poisonous snakes, how to recognise them, the symptoms, and what to do — as Bulgaria's Grade 7 Biology and Health Education student book teaches them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Poisonous mushrooms of Bulgaria** — Bulgaria's teaching: recognising Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms (red/white fly agaric, panther cap, death cap and others, shown in photos), poisoning symptoms appearing 8–24 hours after eating, and that a few grams of the green death cap can kill (printed p. 66).
2. **Poisonous snakes of Bulgaria** — Bulgaria's teaching: Bulgaria's 6 venomous snakes, the two dangerous to humans (horned viper пепелянка and adder усойница) identified by photo and description, what a bite does, and that medical help must be sought immediately (printed p. 114).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Bulgaria's book teaches about recognising Bulgaria's poisonous mushrooms, their poisoning symptoms, and the green death cap.
2. Say what Bulgaria's book teaches about Bulgaria's venomous snakes, the two dangerous to humans, and seeking medical help immediately after a bite.
