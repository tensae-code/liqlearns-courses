# Why Exercise Keeps You Healthy

## Do it first

Does moving and playing sport make a person healthier? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Practising physical activities improves health

Haiti's Grades 10–13 framework programme gives EPS a health theme — **Theme 1: physical activity improves health**. The theme is not a single lesson: it is carried **across the four years** as **conference-debates** — learners meet, present and argue the theme — on **practising physical activities** for the improvement of health. (This lesson comes from a teacher framework programme — it is the teachers' book for the EPS course.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Cooperation and communication for health

The same Theme 1 conference-debates carry a second strand: **cooperation and communication for the improvement of health** — working together and talking together are named, beside practising the activities themselves, as part of how health improves.
