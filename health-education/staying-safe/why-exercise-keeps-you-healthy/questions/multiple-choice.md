# Why Exercise Keeps You Healthy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Theme 1 of Haiti's framework says…
a) Physical activity improves health  b) Activity harms health  c) Health has nothing to do with activity
**Answer: a) Physical activity improves health**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The theme is carried across the four years as…
a) Conference-debates  b) A single test  c) Silence
**Answer: a) Conference-debates**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beside practising physical activities, the debates carry…
a) Cooperation and communication for the improvement of health  b) Only competition  c) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Cooperation and communication for the improvement of health**
