# Hands-on — Why Exercise Keeps You Healthy

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## My conference-debate card

The framework carries its health theme as conference-debates. Make your own debate card: on one side write the claim — "physical activity improves health" — and on the other side write the two strands the debates argue: practising physical activities, and cooperation and communication for the improvement of health.

## Four years of the theme

Draw four boxes, one for each secondary year. In each box, write one physical activity you could practise that year for the improvement of health, the way Theme 1 runs across the four years.
