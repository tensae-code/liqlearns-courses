# Lesson plan — Why Exercise Keeps You Healthy

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Why Exercise Keeps You Healthy
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** why exercise keeps you healthy — what fitness is, its components (health-related and skill-related), and how moving, rest and activity build health across life; taught here through Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre pass: Theme 1 — physical activity improves health, carried as conference-debates across the four years on practising physical activities, cooperation and communication for the improvement of health.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Your Body in Exercise.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Practising physical activities improves health** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): Theme 1 — conference-debates across the four years on practising physical activities for the improvement of health.
2. **Cooperation and communication for health** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): cooperation and communication for the improvement of health, inside the same Theme 1 conference-debates.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
