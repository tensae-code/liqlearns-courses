# Video script — Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order. The strikes part is safety narration only — nothing in it is demonstrated.

---

Narrator: Advanced does not mean careless. The class is on mats. Partners are matched by weight. Protection is on. The teacher watches all the time.
Narrator: Attackers look for someone who seems weak. Stand aware. Walk confidently. Avoid first — that is still the first defence.
Narrator: Some strikes in this book are named only so you know them. They are last-resort knowledge. They are never practised.
Narrator: The roundhouse kick: stance, swing the leg with the knee bent, roll the hip, snap the foot — to a pad your partner holds.
Narrator: A throw is learned live, on mats, with a teacher. Control the arm. Square your hip under theirs. Technique, practised slowly and safely.
