# Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Advanced self-defence classes require the teacher's keen attention and continuous observation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The advanced strikes are practised in pairs like the other techniques.
**Answer: False** — they are knowledge and last-resort awareness only; there is no practice and no drill for them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In class practice, the roundhouse kick is practised to a pad held by a partner.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grappling in hand-to-hand combat means seizing the attacker at close range to gain a physical advantage.
**Answer: True**
