# Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Most attackers look for someone who appears…
a) Weak and easily intimidated  b) Confident and aware  c) Surrounded by people
**Answer: a) Weak and easily intimidated**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In advanced practice, partners are paired…
a) With similar weights — safer and advisable  b) By height only  c) At random
**Answer: a) With similar weights — safer and advisable**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The advanced strikes in this lesson are taught as…
a) Knowledge and last-resort awareness only — never practised  b) A daily drill  c) A game
**Answer: a) Knowledge and last-resort awareness only — never practised**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's caution about kicking is: do not kick above the groin shot, because…
a) The attacker can catch and control your leg  b) It looks untidy  c) It is against sporting rules only
**Answer: a) The attacker can catch and control your leg**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The roundhouse kick starts from…
a) A fighting stance with the left leg forward  b) Sitting down  c) A running start
**Answer: a) A fighting stance with the left leg forward**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** After swinging the right leg forward with the knee bent, you…
a) Roll the hip over and snap the foot out  b) Jump with both feet  c) Turn away
**Answer: a) Roll the hip over and snap the foot out**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In the throw, the attacker's right arm is controlled with…
a) Your left hand, his hand trapped at your armpit  b) Both knees  c) A rope
**Answer: a) Your left hand, his hand trapped at your armpit**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** For the proper throw, your hip is…
a) Squared and under the attacker's hip  b) Turned away  c) Raised on one leg
**Answer: a) Squared and under the attacker's hip**
