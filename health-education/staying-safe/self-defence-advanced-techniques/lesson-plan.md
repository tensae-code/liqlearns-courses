# Lesson plan — Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 10 advanced self-defence techniques — advanced escapes, the roundhouse kick and grappling throws, under the printed mat-class safety regime — with the advanced strikes carried as knowledge-only, last-resort awareness, exactly as their placement directs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Self-Defence Techniques (the Grade 9 stance, escapes, blocks, strikes and grappling) and on Staying Safe on the Street (principles and ethics first).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves — for these techniques, the safe practice form only: a kick to a pad, on mats, with a cooperating partner.

## Sub lessons
1. **Advanced escapes — and the safety regime for advanced practice** — the Grade 10 student book's §8.3.1 with its teacher guide's printed safety regime: attackers look for someone who appears weak; advanced escapes build on the Grade 9 escapes inside a mat class with matched partners and protection.
2. **Advanced strikes — last-resort awareness only** — §8.3: the body's weapons named in the book's printed sequence, as knowledge only — no GIF, no drill, per the placement's own direction.
3. **The roundhouse kick** — printed p. 143: from a left-leg-forward fighting stance, swing the right leg forward with the knee bent, roll the hip over, snap the foot out — practised to a pad.
4. **Grappling throws** — §8.3: seizing at close range for a physical advantage; the throw's control sequence — the attacker's right arm controlled, his hand trapped at your armpit, your hip squared and under his hip.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Self-Defence Techniques (Grade 9 techniques) and Staying Safe on the Street — recommended, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the advanced-practice safety regime and say why attackers look for someone who appears weak.
2. Name the body's weapons in the book's sequence, with the book's caution about kicking high — as awareness only.
3. Describe and practise the roundhouse kick to a pad, step by step.
4. Describe the throw's control sequence and say why a throw is learned live on mats, not copied from a screen.

## What you need
A teacher, a safe well-arranged mat class, protection, pads, partners of similar weight. Picture 35314 and GIF 35303 carry their parts. Sub lesson 2 needs nothing — it is knowledge only.
