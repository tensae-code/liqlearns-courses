# Hands-on — Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques

One line of framing: every practical activity here runs inside the printed safety regime — a safe, well-arranged mat class; respect your partner and classmate; warm up with selected relevant stretching; pairs of similar weight; protection where possible; the teacher's keen attention and continuous observation. There is NO hands-on activity for sub lesson 2 (advanced strikes): that part is knowledge and last-resort awareness only, and it is never drilled.

## Set the mat class
Lay the mats, pair up with someone of similar weight, put protection on, and warm up with the stretching the teacher selects. Recite the regime together before anyone practises.

## Escape review, advanced pace
In your matched pairs, run the Grade 9 escapes slowly — loose hold, bend, turn, break away — then swap roles. Speed comes only when the slow form is clean, and the hold is never around the neck.

## Roundhouse kick to a pad
One partner holds the pad; the other, from a left-leg-forward fighting stance, swings the right leg forward with the knee bent, rolls the hip over and snaps the foot out to the pad — then returns to the stance. Swap roles after each short set.

## The throw, teacher-led only
With the teacher leading and the whole class watching first: control the partner's right arm with your left hand, his hand trapped at your armpit, your hip squared and under his hip — walked through at walking pace on the mats, with a cooperating partner. No one practises the throw without the teacher leading it.
