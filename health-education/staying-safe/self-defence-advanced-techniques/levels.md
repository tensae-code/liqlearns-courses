# Self-Defence — Advanced Techniques — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Advanced escapes — and the safety regime for advanced practice
Pass mark: give the safety regime whole (mat class; respect your partner; stretching warm-up; pairs of similar weights; protection; the teacher's keen attention) and the attacker's-eye fact that opens the part.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advanced strikes — last-resort awareness only
Pass mark: name the body's weapons in the book's sequence, with the caution about kicking above the groin shot — and state that this part is knowledge only, never practised.

## Sub lesson 3 — The roundhouse kick
Pass mark: give the kick's steps in order from the left-leg-forward stance — swing with the knee bent, roll the hip over, snap the foot out — to a pad.

## Sub lesson 4 — Grappling throws
Pass mark: give the throw's control sequence (right arm controlled with your left hand, his hand trapped at your armpit, your hip squared and under his hip).
