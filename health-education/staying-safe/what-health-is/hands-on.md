# Hands-on — What Health Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three kinds of health

Write the book's three kinds of health as three headings — physical, emotional, social. Under each one, write one way physical activity benefits that kind of health, and one thing in your own week that serves it.

## Check your own week

Look at one ordinary week. Mark one activity for each kind of health. If one kind has nothing, plan one small activity for it this week.
