# Lesson plan — What Health Is

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** What Health Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what health is — complete physical, mental and social well-being — and the dimensions and factors that make it up; taught here through the United Kingdom's GCSE pass: physical, emotional and social health, fitness and well-being, and the benefits of physical activity for each kind of health.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Staying Safe While Exercising.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Physical, emotional and social health, fitness and well-being** — the United Kingdom GCSE book's Component 2 Topic 1.1: the three kinds of health — physical, emotional and social — and the benefits of physical activity for each.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Staying Safe While Exercising. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three kinds of health the book teaches: physical, emotional and social health.
2. Say that health, fitness and well-being belong together, as the book's topic joins them.
3. Name a benefit of physical activity for each kind of health, in the book's framing.
