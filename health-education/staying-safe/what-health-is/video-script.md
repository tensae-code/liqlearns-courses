# Video script — What Health Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Health is not just a body that is not ill.
Health is physical — the body well.
Health is emotional — feelings well.
Health is social — life with other people well.
And moving your body helps all three.
That is **what health is** — well-being in body, feelings and life with others.
