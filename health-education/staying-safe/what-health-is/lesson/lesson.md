# What Health Is

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Physical, emotional and social health, fitness and well-being

The United Kingdom's GCSE Physical Education student book opens its health topic with **physical, emotional and social health, fitness and well-being** — health taught in **three kinds**:

1. **Physical health** — the body and how it works.
2. **Emotional health** — feelings and well-being of mind.
3. **Social health** — life with other people.

The topic joins **health, fitness and well-being** together, and teaches **the benefits of physical activity for each** kind of health — moving the body is not only for the body: it serves emotional and social health too.

In the United Kingdom's own way, this sits inside PE at GCSE as an examined academic subject — health, psychology and the sociology of sport taught as knowledge beside anatomy, physiology and training theory.

## Say it to your companion
