# What Health Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the United Kingdom book, the three kinds of health are…
a) Physical, emotional and social  b) Only physical  c) Bones, muscles and skin
**Answer: a) Physical, emotional and social**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's health topic joins together…
a) Health, fitness and well-being  b) Health and illness only  c) Fitness and examinations
**Answer: a) Health, fitness and well-being**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical activity, in the book's topic, brings benefits…
a) For each kind of health  b) For physical health only  c) For no kind of health
**Answer: a) For each kind of health**
