# Therapeutic Diets

## Do it first

What do you already know about this lesson's thing — Therapeutic diets: eating plans for illness — overweight, diabetes (with blood-glucose monitoring), kidney, bone and heart disease. Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What therapeutic diets are

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **what therapeutic diets are, who benefits, their types and texture modification of food**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Diet for overweight

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Diet for diabetes and blood glucose monitoring

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **types and causes of diabetes are taught in Non-communicable Diseases; here the diet side: finger-prick and other blood-glucose monitoring methods and how to read the results, the plate method, and the link between type 2 diabetes and overweight**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Diet for renal disease

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **low-protein, low-phosphorus and related dietary guidance for kidney disease**.

## Sub lesson 5 — Diet for bone disease

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease**.

## Sub lesson 6 — Diet for heart disease

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **healthier fats and the DASH diet plan for heart patients**.
