What therapeutic diets are: what therapeutic diets are, who benefits, their types and texture modification of food.
Diet for overweight: energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight.
Diet for diabetes and blood glucose monitoring: types and causes of diabetes are taught in Non-communicable Diseases; here the diet side: finger-prick and other blood-glucose monitoring methods and how to read the results, the plate method, and the link between type 2 diabetes and overweight.
Diet for renal disease: low-protein, low-phosphorus and related dietary guidance for kidney disease.
Diet for bone disease: dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease.
Diet for heart disease: healthier fats and the DASH diet plan for heart patients.
That is **therapeutic diets** — as the United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches it.
