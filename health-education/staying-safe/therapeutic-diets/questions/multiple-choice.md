# Therapeutic Diets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a therapeutic diet for?
a) An eating plan for illness — a diet matched to a person's condition, as the UAE book teaches it  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) An eating plan for illness — a diet matched to a person's condition, as the UAE book teaches it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What guides a diet for overweight?
a) Energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is blood glucose monitored, as the book teaches it?
a) By finger-prick and other monitoring methods, reading the results  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) By finger-prick and other monitoring methods, reading the results**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What dietary guidance does the book give for renal disease?
a) Low-protein, low-phosphorus and related guidance for kidney disease  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Low-protein, low-phosphorus and related guidance for kidney disease**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the book teach for bone disease?
a) Dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What diet plan does the book name for heart disease?
a) Healthier fats and the DASH diet plan  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Healthier fats and the DASH diet plan**
