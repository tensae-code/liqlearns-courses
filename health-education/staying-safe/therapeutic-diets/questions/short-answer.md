# Therapeutic Diets — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a therapeutic diet for?
**Answer: An eating plan for illness — a diet matched to a person's condition, as the UAE book teaches it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What guides a diet for overweight?
**Answer: Energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is blood glucose monitored, as the book teaches it?
**Answer: By finger-prick and other monitoring methods, reading the results.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What dietary guidance does the book give for renal disease?
**Answer: Low-protein, low-phosphorus and related guidance for kidney disease.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the book teach for bone disease?
**Answer: Dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What diet plan does the book name for heart disease?
**Answer: Healthier fats and the DASH diet plan.**
