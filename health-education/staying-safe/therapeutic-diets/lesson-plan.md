# Lesson plan — Therapeutic Diets

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Therapeutic Diets
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Therapeutic diets: eating plans for illness — overweight, diabetes (with blood-glucose monitoring), kidney, bone and heart disease.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside First Aid for Emergencies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What therapeutic diets are** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what therapeutic diets are, who benefits, their types and texture modification of food.
2. **Diet for overweight** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: energy balance and dietary guidance for losing weight.
3. **Diet for diabetes and blood glucose monitoring** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: types and causes of diabetes are taught in Non-communicable Diseases; here the diet side: finger-prick and other blood-glucose monitoring methods and how to read the results, the plate method, and the link between type 2 diabetes and overweight.
4. **Diet for renal disease** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: low-protein, low-phosphorus and related dietary guidance for kidney disease.
5. **Diet for bone disease** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: dietary recommendations and foods to limit for bone disease.
6. **Diet for heart disease** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: healthier fats and the DASH diet plan for heart patients.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
