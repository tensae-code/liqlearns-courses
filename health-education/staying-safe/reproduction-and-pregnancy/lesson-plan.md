# Lesson plan — Reproduction and Pregnancy

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Reproduction and Pregnancy
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sexual and reproductive health — puberty, reproduction and keeping safe — as Zambia's Health Teacher Training Manual teaches them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Knowing My Body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
