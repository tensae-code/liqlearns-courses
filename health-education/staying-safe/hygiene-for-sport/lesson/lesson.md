# Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise

## Do it first

After a hard game, your shirt smells. Your skin is salty with sweat. What is growing in that sweat and dirt while you sit in it? The books answer plainly — and the answer is why this lesson exists.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wash after exercise — the pupil's lesson

During exercise, the **sweat glands release sweat and waste through the skin**. Left unwashed, that sweat-dirt becomes a **breeding place for bacteria** — and with bacteria come **bad smell and disease exposure**.

So the book's rule is simple and firm: **washing the body after PE and sport is essential**. And kit is part of the body-rule: **dirty sports kit harms yourself and others** — the person beside you breathes it too.

The book's tasks work the rule through hygiene **before and after** activity: clean to start, washed to finish.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three things, three benefits (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide defines **personal hygiene** as **the care one takes for one's body in terms of cleanliness** — in activity it keeps everyone fit and well enough to keep enjoying what they love, and it matters equally for those who maintain sport facilities.

**The three basic things to stay healthy in activity:**

1. **Always clean and cover wounds**, to prevent the entry of germs and dirt.
2. **Wash your hands, or have a shower.**
3. **Clean your sport clothes and equipment.**

And its benefits come in three kinds: **health** benefits (maintain health and ability through cleanliness and a healthy diet), **social** benefits (washing regularly makes you good company), and **psychological** benefits (looking healthy and clean improves self-image and self-satisfaction).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the sweat–bacteria chain, the three basic things, and why dirty kit is not only your own business.

## Do It Yourself

1. After your next exercise: wash, and put the kit to wash the same day. Note how long it took — the rule is cheap.
2. Inspect your kit bag by the book: anything dirty staying in the bag? Clean it or air it today.
3. Teach the three basic things to someone at home, in order, without the book.
