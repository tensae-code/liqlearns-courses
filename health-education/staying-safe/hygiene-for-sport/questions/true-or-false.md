# Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sweat glands release sweat and waste through the skin during exercise.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Personal hygiene is the care one takes for one's body in terms of cleanliness.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning sport clothes and equipment is one of the three basic things.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Kit can stay dirty in the bag all week without harming anyone.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Bacteria breed in sweaty clothes if they are not washed.
**Answer: True**
