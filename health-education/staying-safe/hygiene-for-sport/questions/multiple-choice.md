# Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unwashed sweat-dirt on the skin becomes…
a) A breeding place for bacteria  b) A protection  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) A breeding place for bacteria**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Washing the body after PE and sport is…
a) Essential  b) Forbidden  c) Only for champions
**Answer: a) Essential**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dirty sports kit harms…
a) Yourself and others  b) Nobody  c) Only the kit
**Answer: a) Yourself and others**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three basic things begin with…
a) Clean and cover wounds  b) Buy new kit  c) Rest all day
**Answer: a) Clean and cover wounds**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Looking healthy and clean improving self-image is which benefit kind?
a) Psychological  b) Health  c) Social
**Answer: a) Psychological**
