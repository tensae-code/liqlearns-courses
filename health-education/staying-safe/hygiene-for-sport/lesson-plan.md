# Lesson plan — Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** personal hygiene around sport: sweat and dirt left unwashed become a breeding place for bacteria — so wash the body after PE and sport, keep sports kit clean, cover cuts, and clean hands, clothes and equipment, for your own health and everyone else's.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Staying Safe While Exercising.

## Sub lessons
1. **Wash after exercise — the pupil's lesson** — the Grade 7 student book's hygiene section: sweat glands, bacteria breeding in unwashed sweat-dirt, washing the body after PE/sport as essential, and dirty kit harming self and others.
2. **Three things, three benefits (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 7 teacher guide's hygiene lesson: personal hygiene defined; the three basic things to stay healthy in activity; and the health, social and psychological benefits.

3. **Clean after exercise — shower, towel, feet (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 9 teacher guide's hygiene insert: the definition, the five hygiene groups, showering soon after exercise, and foot care — drying between the toes; Picture 14013 shows the foot rule.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Staying Safe While Exercising. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why washing after exercise is essential, in the book's chain: sweat glands release sweat and waste through the skin; unwashed sweat-dirt breeds bacteria; bad smell and disease exposure follow.
2. Name the guide's three basic things: clean and cover wounds, wash hands or shower, clean sport clothes and equipment.
3. Name the three benefit kinds hygiene brings in activity: health, social and psychological.
4. State the kit rule: dirty sports kit harms yourself and others.

## What you need
Water and soap where available, a clean cloth, and the class's own kit to inspect.

## If the learner is stuck
- No shower is available → the guide's own alternative stands: wash your hands and wash with the water there is; the rule is cleanliness, not plumbing.
- "My kit smells fine" → the book's test is time: sweat-dirt breeds bacteria whether it smells yet or not; wash by routine, not by smell.
