# Hands-on — Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cover, wash, clean

The three steps as one drill after a real class game: wounds cleaned and covered, hands washed, kit gathered for washing. The class ends clean, by routine, not by reminder.

## Kit inspection

Pairs inspect each other's kit against the rule — clean or dirty, aired or shut away — and each learner fixes one finding on the spot.
