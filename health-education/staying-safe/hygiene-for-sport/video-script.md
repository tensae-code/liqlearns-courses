# Video script — Hygiene for Sport — Wash After Exercise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You played. You sweated. The sweat carries waste through your skin.
Leave it — and bacteria move in. Smell. Sickness. No thank you.
Cover the cut. Wash the body. Clean the kit.
Clean player, good company — in body, in mind, in the team.
