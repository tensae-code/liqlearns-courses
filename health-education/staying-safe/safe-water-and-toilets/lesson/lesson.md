## Sub lesson 1 — WASH in Schools (KWinS)

Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide opens with **WASH in Schools (KWinS)**: what **WASH** stands for — water, sanitation and hygiene — taught as a school programme. The guide teaches **children's rights to safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene**.

WASH is not one person's job. The guide names the **roles of students, parents, community, schools and teachers** — each one has a part in keeping the water safe, the toilets basic and clean, and hygiene improving. (This lesson comes from a teacher guide — it is the teachers' book for the school programme.)
