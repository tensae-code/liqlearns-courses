# Hands-on — Safe Water and Toilets

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Walk your school's WASH

Walk through your school (or home) and list: where the drinking water comes from, where the toilets are, and where hands can be washed with soap. Mark each one safe or not safe, the way the guide teaches children's rights to safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene.

## Who does what?

Write the five roles the guide names — students, parents, community, schools and teachers — and one thing each one does for WASH in schools.
