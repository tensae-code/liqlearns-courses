# Safe Water and Toilets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Kiribati's guide, the school programme is called…
a) KWinS — WASH in Schools  b) A sports day  c) A reading club
**Answer: a) KWinS — WASH in Schools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide teaches that children have a right to…
a) Safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene  b) Miss school  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide, who has a role in WASH in schools?
a) Only the teacher  b) Students, parents, community, schools and teachers  c) Only visitors
**Answer: b) Students, parents, community, schools and teachers**
