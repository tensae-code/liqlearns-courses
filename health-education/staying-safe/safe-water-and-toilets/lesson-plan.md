# Lesson plan — Safe Water and Toilets

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Safe Water and Toilets
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** safe water and toilets — clean drinking water and how to make it safe, toilets and latrines, sanitation and WASH; taught here through Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide pass: WASH in Schools (KWinS), children's rights to safe water, sanitation and hygiene, and who does what.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Keeping My Body Clean.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **WASH in Schools (KWinS)** — Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): what WASH stands for, children's rights to safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene, and the roles of students, parents, community, schools and teachers.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Keeping My Body Clean. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what WASH stands for, as the guide teaches it.
2. Say what children have a right to — safe drinking water, basic sanitation and improved hygiene.
3. Name who has a role in WASH in schools: students, parents, community, schools and teachers.

- WASH feels like letters only → anchor it in the guide's own way: WASH in Schools (KWinS) is a school programme — walk through your own school and name the water, the toilets and the washing places.
