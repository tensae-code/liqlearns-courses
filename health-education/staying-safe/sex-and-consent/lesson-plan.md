# Lesson plan — Sex and Consent

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Sex and Consent
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sex and sexuality, sexual identities, sexual behaviour and response, privacy, consent, values and sexuality, and pornography — as Sierra Leone's AHLS Teacher's Manual teaches them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Reproduction and Pregnancy.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

2. **Types of sexual identities** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS2 Lesson 1.2: Types of sexual identities — different sexual identities (printed p. 50).
3. **Sexual behaviour and response** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS2 Lesson 1.3: Sexual behaviour and response — sexual behaviour and the body's responses (printed p. 52).
4. **Protection of privacy** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS2 Lesson 1.4: Protection of privacy — privacy for one's body and information (printed p. 55).
5. **The importance of consent** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS2 Lesson 1.5: Importance of consent — consent in relationships and sexual activity (printed p. 57).
6. **Values and sexuality** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS2 Lesson 1.6: Values and sexuality — personal and community values around sexuality (printed p. 60).
7. **Pornography and adolescents** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS3 Lesson 1.5: Pornography — pornography and its effects on adolescents (printed p. 95).
