# Lesson plan — Common Illnesses and Caring for the Sick

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Common Illnesses and Caring for the Sick
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the named common illnesses — malaria, worm infestations, diarrhoeal diseases, acute respiratory tract infections, tuberculosis and other common infections — and caring for the sick, as Zambia's Health Teacher Training Manual teaches them to school health teachers.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Knowing My Body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Malaria** — Zambia's teaching: cause, signs, prevention (nets, spraying) and care/referral (Chapter 6: Malaria, printed p. 62).
2. **Worm infestations** — Zambia's teaching: bilharzia (schistosomiasis) and intestinal worms, how they spread and how deworming and hygiene stop them (Chapter 7: Worm Infestations, printed p. 76).
3. **Diarrhoeal diseases** — Zambia's teaching: causes, dehydration danger, oral rehydration and prevention (Chapter 8: Diarrhoeal Diseases, printed p. 82).
4. **Acute respiratory tract infections** — Zambia's teaching: acute respiratory tract infections (ARTI) — signs, care and when to refer (Chapter 10: Acute Respiratory Tract Infections (ARTI), printed p. 101).
5. **Tuberculosis** — Zambia's teaching: signs, spread through coughing, treatment support and stigma (Chapter 11: Tuberculosis, printed p. 108).
6. **Other common infections** — Zambia's teaching: eye, ear and skin infections, urinary tract infection, meningitis and mumps: recognising and responding (Chapter 12: Other Common Infections, printed p. 122).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
