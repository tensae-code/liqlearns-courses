# Hands-on — Common Illnesses and Caring for the Sick

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My common-illnesses card

Zambia's manual teaches malaria, worm infestations, diarrhoeal diseases, acute respiratory tract infections, tuberculosis and other common infections. For each illness in this lesson, write its name and, beside it, the one thing this lesson teaches about it — its cause or signs, its prevention, or its care — using only what the lesson itself teaches.

## Prevention at my school

Write the prevention steps this lesson names — nets and spraying against malaria, deworming and hygiene against worms, oral rehydration and prevention for diarrhoeal diseases — as a checklist for your own home or school, and tick each one your home or school already does.
