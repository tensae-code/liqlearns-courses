# Video script — Common Illnesses and Caring for the Sick

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zambia's manual for school health teachers teaches the common illnesses one by one.
Malaria — its cause, signs, prevention with nets and spraying, and care and referral. Worms — bilharzia and intestinal worms, stopped by deworming and hygiene. Diarrhoeal diseases — the dehydration danger, oral rehydration and prevention. Chest infections, tuberculosis, and the eye, ear and skin infections.
That is **common illnesses and caring for the sick** — as Zambia's book teaches them.
