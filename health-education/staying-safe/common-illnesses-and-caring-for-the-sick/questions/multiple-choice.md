# Common Illnesses and Caring for the Sick — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zambia's manual teaches that malaria is prevented with…
a) Nets and spraying  b) Eating sweets  c) Sleeping late
**Answer: a) Nets and spraying**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bilharzia is also called…
a) Schistosomiasis  b) Tetanus  c) Mumps
**Answer: a) Schistosomiasis**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For diarrhoeal diseases, the manual teaches the danger of…
a) Dehydration  b) Sunburn  c) Tooth decay
**Answer: a) Dehydration**
