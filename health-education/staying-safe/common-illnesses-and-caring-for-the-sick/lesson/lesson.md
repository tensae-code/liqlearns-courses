## Sub lesson 1 — Malaria

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 6: Malaria, **cause, signs, prevention (nets, spraying) and care/referral**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Worm infestations

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 7: Worm Infestations, **bilharzia (schistosomiasis) and intestinal worms, how they spread and how deworming and hygiene stop them**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Diarrhoeal diseases

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 8: Diarrhoeal Diseases, **causes, dehydration danger, oral rehydration and prevention**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Acute respiratory tract infections

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 10: Acute Respiratory Tract Infections (ARTI), **acute respiratory tract infections (ARTI) — signs, care and when to refer**.

## Sub lesson 5 — Tuberculosis

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 11: Tuberculosis, **signs, spread through coughing, treatment support and stigma**.

## Sub lesson 6 — Other common infections

Zambia's *Health Teacher Training — Participants Manual* teaches, in Chapter 12: Other Common Infections, **eye, ear and skin infections, urinary tract infection, meningitis and mumps: recognising and responding**.
