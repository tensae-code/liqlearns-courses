# Staying Safe Online

## Do it first

How do you use technology well — and how can it be used badly? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The healthy and best use of technology

Jordan's Grade 7 book closes Term 2 with **Health Concepts and Habits**. Its first lesson is **The Healthy and Best Use of Technology**: use technology for **useful educational content instead of wasting time**, **avoid long sitting in front of screens**, and **take regular breaks and exercise**.

## Sub lesson 2 — What technology has done for sport

The same lesson looks at **what technology has done for sport and sports equipment** — the book's example is the **video assistant referee** (VAR), technology helping the referee in the game itself.

In Jordan's own way, Term 2 closes with "Health Concepts and Habits" — healthy technology use (including technology in sport, e.g. VAR) and the body in exercise.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what the book teaches — the healthy and best use of technology, and one thing technology has done for sport — in your own words, the way the Jordan book teaches it.
