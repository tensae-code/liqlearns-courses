# Hands-on — Staying Safe Online

The lesson with your hands and your head, not only your eyes.

## My screen day

Write down yesterday's screen time as honestly as you can: what you used, and for how long. Beside each use, mark it the book's way — useful educational content, or time wasted.

## Breaks and exercise

Set one regular break in your next screen hour: stand up, move, exercise a little — the book teaches regular breaks and exercising beside screen time. Note when you took it.
