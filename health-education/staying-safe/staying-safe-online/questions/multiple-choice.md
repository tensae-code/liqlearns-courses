# Staying Safe Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The healthy use of technology, in the book's teaching, means…
a) Using useful educational content instead of wasting time  b) Watching screens all day  c) Never learning anything
**Answer: a) Using useful educational content instead of wasting time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches avoiding…
a) Long sitting in front of screens  b) Regular breaks  c) Exercise
**Answer: a) Long sitting in front of screens**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's example of technology in sport is…
a) The video assistant referee  b) A bigger ball  c) Longer matches
**Answer: a) The video assistant referee**
