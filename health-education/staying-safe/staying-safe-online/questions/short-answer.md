# Staying Safe Online — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the healthy and best use of technology?
**Answer: Using useful educational content instead of wasting time, avoiding long sitting in front of screens, and taking regular breaks and exercising.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one thing technology has done for sport, as the book teaches it.
**Answer: The video assistant referee (VAR) — technology helping in sport and sports equipment.**
