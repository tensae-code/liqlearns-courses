# Puberty and Growing Up — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is adolescence?
**Answer: The stage between childhood and maturity.**

**2. (Sub lessons 2–3)** How is sport a lifestyle for adolescents, and what does it give them?
**Answer: By choosing a suitable sport, keeping a timetable, variety and balance, and self-reward — and it gives physical growth, stronger muscles and bones, mental health, less stress and self-confidence.**
