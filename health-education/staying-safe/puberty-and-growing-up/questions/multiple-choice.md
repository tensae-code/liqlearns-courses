# Puberty and Growing Up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adolescence, in the book's teaching, is…
a) The stage between childhood and maturity  b) Old age  c) The first year of school
**Answer: a) The stage between childhood and maturity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sport as a lifestyle, in the book's teaching, includes…
a) Choosing a suitable sport and keeping a timetable  b) Playing once and stopping  c) Only watching sport
**Answer: a) Choosing a suitable sport and keeping a timetable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One benefit of sport for adolescents the book names is…
a) Stronger muscles and bones  b) Weaker bones  c) More stress
**Answer: a) Stronger muscles and bones**
