# Hands-on — Puberty and Growing Up

The lesson with your hands and your head, not only your eyes.

## My sport timetable

Make the book's lifestyle plan on paper: choose a sport suitable for you, and write a weekly timetable for it — with variety and balance, the way the book teaches. Add one self-reward you will give yourself when you keep the timetable for a month.

## What sport gives me

From the book's list — physical growth, stronger muscles and bones, mental health, less stress, self-confidence — circle the two benefits you feel most in your own life, and write one line on each.
