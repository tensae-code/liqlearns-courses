# Lesson plan — Puberty and Growing Up

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Puberty and Growing Up
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** puberty and growing up — the changes of adolescence in body, feelings and life, and the stages of growth and life; taught here through Jordan's Grade 9 pass: adolescence and sport — adolescence as the stage between childhood and maturity, sport as a lifestyle, and what sport does for adolescents.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Staying Safe While Exercising.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Adolescence** — Jordan's Grade 9 teaching: adolescence as the stage between childhood and maturity.
2. **Sport as a lifestyle** — Jordan's Grade 9 teaching: choosing a suitable sport, a timetable, variety and balance, self-reward.
3. **What sport does for adolescents** — Jordan's Grade 9 teaching: physical growth, stronger muscles and bones, mental health, less stress, self-confidence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Health Is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what adolescence is, in the book's teaching: the stage between childhood and maturity.
2. Say how sport becomes a lifestyle, in the book's teaching: a suitable sport, a timetable, variety and balance, self-reward.
3. Name the book's benefits of sport for adolescents: physical growth, stronger muscles and bones, mental health, less stress, self-confidence.
