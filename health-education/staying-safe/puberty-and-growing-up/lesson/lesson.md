# Puberty and Growing Up

## Do it first

What changes between childhood and being grown up — and what place does sport have in those years? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adolescence

Jordan's Grade 9 book teaches, in its **Health Concepts and Habits** unit, **Adolescence and Sport**. It teaches **adolescence as the stage between childhood and maturity** — the years of growing up itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sport as a lifestyle

Inside that stage, the book teaches **sport as a lifestyle**: **choosing a suitable sport**, keeping **a timetable**, and keeping **variety and balance** — with **self-reward** when you keep to it.

## Sub lesson 3 — What sport does for adolescents

And it teaches the **benefits for adolescents**: **physical growth**, **stronger muscles and bones**, **mental health**, **less stress**, and **self-confidence**.

In Jordan's own way, the final health unit speaks to adolescents directly — sport as a lifestyle through the teenage years.

## Say it to your companion
