# Hands-on — Vaccines and Immunisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My tetanus safety list

El Salvador's book teaches that tetanus bacteria live in soil, dust and rusty metal, and that vaccination prevents tetanus. Write a list of the rusty-metal and dirty places in your home or school where you must take care, and beside the list write the one sentence the book teaches: vaccination prevents tetanus.

## Check the wound rule

Write what you would do first with a wound or scrape, using the book's wound-cleaning teaching from the First Aid lesson: clean it with water and soap and cover it with clean gauze, never touching wounds with dirty hands.
