# Lesson plan — Vaccines and Immunisation

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Vaccines and Immunisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** vaccines and immunisation — taught here through El Salvador's Grade 1 pass: what tetanus is and that vaccination prevents it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Knowing My Body.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. ## Sub lessons
1. **What tetanus is** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: what tetanus is, where its bacteria live (soil, dust, rusty metal), and its symptoms.
2. **Vaccination prevents tetanus** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: vaccination prevents tetanus.
