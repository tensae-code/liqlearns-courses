# Vaccines and Immunisation

## Do it first

What do you think vaccination does? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What tetanus is

El Salvador's Grade 1 *Ciencia, Salud y Medio Ambiente* book teaches, in Unit 3 Lesson 1 ¡Cuidado con el tétano!, **what tetanus is** — where its bacteria live (soil, dust, rusty metal) and its symptoms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vaccination prevents tetanus

The same lesson teaches that **vaccination prevents tetanus**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two tetanus teachings — what tetanus is and where its bacteria live, and that vaccination prevents it — in your own words, the way El Salvador's book teaches them.
