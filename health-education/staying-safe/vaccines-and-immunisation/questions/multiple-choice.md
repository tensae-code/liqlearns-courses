# Vaccines and Immunisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's book teaches that tetanus bacteria live in…
a) Soil, dust and rusty metal  b) Clean water only  c) Cooked food
**Answer: a) Soil, dust and rusty metal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches that tetanus is prevented by…
a) Vaccination  b) Eating sweets  c) Sleeping late
**Answer: a) Vaccination**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tetanus is taught in El Salvador's book in the lesson called…
a) ¡Cuidado con el tétano!  b) ¡Qué Delicia!  c) Una amiga para conservar
**Answer: a) ¡Cuidado con el tétano!**
