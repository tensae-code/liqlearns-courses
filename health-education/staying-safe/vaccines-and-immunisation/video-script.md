# Video script — Vaccines and Immunisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A vaccine protects you before you get ill.
El Salvador's Grade 1 book teaches tetanus this way: its bacteria live in soil, dust and rusty metal — and vaccination prevents it.
That is **vaccines and immunisation** — starting with tetanus, as El Salvador's book teaches it.
