**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

The practice safety rules taught in sub lesson 3 are the Grade 9 teacher guide's own printed rules for this practice (printed pp. 113–116): no one creates a problem on the partner's body; correct each other on the spot; swap roles so everyone defends and attacks. The moving pictures show the books' safe practice form — slow work with a cooperating partner on mats, a loose hold never around the neck, strikes to a pad and never to a head or neck. The front kick and the strikes are taught as the student book's described steps; in class practice they are done to pads under a teacher, as the guide directs. Grappling at this grade is principles and the safe hold only — the student placement itself names no grappling moving picture (principles only at this grade). This lesson stocks the Grade 9 technique placements in the Staying Safe title of the Health Education course, where the companion safety-knowledge lesson (Staying Safe on the Street) already stands.
