# Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The basic stance
Pass mark: step into the stance and give its four fundamentals (feet positioned properly, torso correctly, arms for defence, head defensively) and its two effects (less target exposed; hands ready for a faster block).

## Sub lesson 2 — Escaping grabs
Pass mark: give the steps of all three escapes in order — bear hug, hands trapped, side headlock — and state that every escape ends in getting away.

## Sub lesson 3 — Blocks and strikes — and the practice safety rules
Pass mark: describe both blocks and the wiper succession, the front kick's steps, the heel palm and the elbow strike — and recite the practice safety rules in full.

## Sub lesson 4 — Grappling
Pass mark: define grappling, name what it includes (throws, takedowns, ground fighting toward submission), and state why technique, not strength, decides it.
