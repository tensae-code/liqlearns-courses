# Lesson plan — Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 9 physical techniques of self-defence, in the book's order — the basic stance, escaping grabs, blocks and strikes, grappling — taught as described movement steps with the books' own practice safety rules carried in full.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Staying Safe on the Street — the principles, street-safety rules and ethics come first; the techniques are the last resort that lesson orders last.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves — for these techniques, the safe practice form: slow, paired, on mats, no contact to a head or neck.

## Sub lessons
1. **The basic stance** — the Grade 9 student book's §8.3.1 with its teacher guide: a sideways base that reduces target exposure, hands ready for a faster block, looking deceptively passive; the four fundamentals — feet, torso, arms, head.
2. **Escaping grabs** — §8.3.2 with its guide: the bear-hug escape, the hands-trapped escape and the side-headlock escape, each ending in getting away; pair practice, swapping roles.
3. **Blocks and strikes — and the practice safety rules** — §8.3.3 with its guide: inward and outward blocks like a windshield wiper, the front kick, the heel palm strike and the elbow strike — and the guide's printed practice safety rules, taught as part of the practice teaching, in full.
4. **Grappling** — §8.3.4 with its guide: holding, seizing and fastening at close range; throws, takedowns and ground fighting toward submission; technique, not strength, wins.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Staying Safe on the Street (the principles and ethics) — recommended, never a gate.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Step into the basic stance and name its four fundamentals.
2. Describe the three escapes, step by step, and say what every escape ends with.
3. Show the inward and outward blocks in succession and describe the front kick, heel palm strike and elbow strike as the book's steps.
4. Recite the practice safety rules in full and explain what grappling is and why technique beats strength.

## What you need
A teacher, mats, a striking pad, proper sportswear. Pictures 35310–35313 and GIFs 35300–35302 carry the parts. No practice without the safety rules of sub lesson 3.

## If the learner is stuck
- The stance feels like a fighting pose → return to the book's point: it looks deceptively passive; readiness, not challenge, is the aim.
- Practice turns rough → stop, recite the safety rules, restart slowly. The rules are part of the technique.
