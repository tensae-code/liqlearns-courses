# Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling — ordering

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stepping into the basic stance: (a) bring the arms up for defence (b) set one foot in its proper place (c) hold the head defensively (d) turn the torso a little sideways
**Answer: b) set one foot in its proper place — d) turn the torso a little sideways — a) bring the arms up for defence — c) hold the head defensively**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The bear-hug escape: (a) strike and run (b) bend forward from the waist (c) turn in with an elbow (d) throw the elbows side to side
**Answer: b) bend forward from the waist — d) throw the elbows side to side — c) turn in with an elbow — a) strike and run**
