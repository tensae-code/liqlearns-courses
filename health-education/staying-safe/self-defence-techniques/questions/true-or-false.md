# Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The basic stance looks deceptively passive while keeping you ready.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An escape is successful when you get free and get away from the attacker.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In practice, strikes are aimed at a partner's head so the practice feels real.
**Answer: False** — practice strikes go to a pad, slowly; no one creates a problem on the partner's body.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Grappling includes throws, takedowns and ground fighting leading to submission.
**Answer: True**
