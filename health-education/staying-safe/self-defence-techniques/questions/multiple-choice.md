# Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The basic stance turns the body slightly sideways to…
a) Reduce target exposure and put the hands ready for a faster block  b) Look taller  c) Start a fight
**Answer: a) Reduce target exposure and put the hands ready for a faster block**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The four fundamentals of the stance are…
a) Feet, torso, arms, head  b) Speed, surprise, strength, height  c) Eyes, ears, voice, hands
**Answer: a) Feet, torso, arms, head**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the bear-hug escape from behind, the first move is to…
a) Bend forward from the waist  b) Fall down  c) Shout and wait
**Answer: a) Bend forward from the waist**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the side-headlock escape, you turn into the attacker's side to…
a) Avoid being choked  b) Get closer to fight  c) See better
**Answer: a) Avoid being choked**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The inward and outward blocks in succession move like…
a) A windshield wiper  b) A door closing  c) A flag waving
**Answer: a) A windshield wiper**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The elbow strike is used at close range because…
a) A punch or kick cannot gather momentum there  b) It is the strongest strike of all  c) It needs no stance
**Answer: a) A punch or kick cannot gather momentum there**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Grappling means…
a) Taking hold at close range to lock the opponent in a difficult position  b) Running away  c) Blocking from far away
**Answer: a) Taking hold at close range to lock the opponent in a difficult position**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In grappling, the winner is the one with…
a) The better technique  b) The bigger body  c) The louder voice
**Answer: a) The better technique**
