# Video script — Self-Defence Techniques — Stance, Escapes, Blocks and Strikes, Grappling

A short read-aloud coaching script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order, with the books' safety notes on screen throughout.

---

Narrator: These skills are the last resort. Avoid first. Words next. Escape after.
Narrator: Step into the stance. Feet set. Body turned a little. Hands up, ready to block. Head held safe. You look calm — and you are ready.
Narrator: If someone holds you: bend, turn, break away — and run to a busy place. In practice the hold is loose, the mats are down, and partners swap roles.
Narrator: Blocks sweep in and out, like a wiper in front of your head. Strikes are practised to a pad — never to a partner's head. No one hurts a partner. Correct each other kindly. Swap roles.
Narrator: Grappling is a close hold. The better technique wins — not the bigger strength.
