# Preventing Common Diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Session 6 of Namibia's guide teaches…
a) The common diseases in Namibia and staying well  b) Only sports rules  c) Diseases of another country only
**Answer: a) The common diseases in Namibia and staying well**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's common-diseases teaching is about…
a) Diseases nobody has  b) The diseases Namibians commonly face  c) Only rare diseases
**Answer: b) The diseases Namibians commonly face**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Alongside naming the common diseases, the session teaches…
a) Staying well  b) Ignoring illness  c) Nothing else
**Answer: a) Staying well**
