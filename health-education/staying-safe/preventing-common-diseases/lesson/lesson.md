## Sub lesson 1 — The common diseases in Namibia and staying well

Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaches **the common diseases in Namibia and staying well** in its **Healthy Lifestyle Session 6**. The session is about the diseases Namibians themselves commonly face, and about **staying well** against them — taught, like every Healthy Lifestyle session in this guide, through key messages and discussion in the team circle, riding on a PE activity. (This lesson comes from a teacher guide — it is the teachers' book for the PE sessions.)
