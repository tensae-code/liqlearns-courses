# Hands-on — Preventing Common Diseases

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The diseases of my place

Write the diseases that are common where you live — the way Namibia's session names the common diseases in Namibia. Beside each one, write one thing people do to stay well.

## Staying well list

From your list, pick the staying-well actions and write them as your own staying-well list for this week.
