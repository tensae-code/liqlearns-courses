# Feeding Mothers and Young Children — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers?
a) The first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) The first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about feeding babies and young children?
a) First foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) First foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about feeding older people and the sick?
a) Ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes**
