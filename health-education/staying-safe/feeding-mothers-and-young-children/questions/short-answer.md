# Feeding Mothers and Young Children — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers?
**Answer: The first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about feeding babies and young children?
**Answer: First foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about feeding older people and the sick?
**Answer: Ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes.**
