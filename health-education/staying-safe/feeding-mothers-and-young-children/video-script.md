# Video script — Feeding Mothers and Young Children

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers: the first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding.
Feeding babies and young children: first foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food.
Feeding older people and the sick: ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes.
That is **feeding mothers and young children** — as the DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales Food Hygiene book teaches it.
