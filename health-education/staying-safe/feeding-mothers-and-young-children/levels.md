# Feeding Mothers and Young Children — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers
Pass mark: The first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feeding babies and young children
Pass mark: First foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food.

## Sub lesson 3 — Feeding older people and the sick
Pass mark: Ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes.
