# Feeding Mothers and Young Children

## Do it first

What do you already know about this lesson's thing — Feeding mothers and young children: feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers and young children in the first 1,000 days. Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers

The DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales Food Hygiene book teaches: **the first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feeding babies and young children

The DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales Food Hygiene book teaches: **first foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Feeding older people and the sick

The DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales Food Hygiene book teaches: **ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes**.
