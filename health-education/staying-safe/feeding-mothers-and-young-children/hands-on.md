# Hands-on — Feeding Mothers and Young Children

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers

Write this part from the book as a short list — the first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding — and beside each item write what it means for a real person, as the book places it.

## Feeding babies and young children

Write this part from the book as a short list — first foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food — and beside each item write what it means for a real person, as the book places it.

## Feeding older people and the sick

Write this part from the book as a short list — ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes — and beside each item write what it means for a real person, as the book places it.
