# Lesson plan — Feeding Mothers and Young Children

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Feeding Mothers and Young Children
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Feeding mothers and young children: feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers and young children in the first 1,000 days.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Eating a Balanced Diet.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Feeding pregnant and breastfeeding mothers** — DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales, Food Hygiene: the first 1,000 days, extra needs (iron, folate), foods to favour/avoid, exclusive breastfeeding.
2. **Feeding babies and young children** — DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales, Food Hygiene: first foods from 6 months, enriched porridges (oil, groundnut/fretin powder, egg), preschool meals, strict hygiene for children's food.
3. **Feeding older people and the sick** — DR Congo Humanities 1 Techniques Sociales, Food Hygiene: ageing needs and textures, feeding in diarrhoea (oral rehydration), simple diets for hypertension and diabetes.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
