# Video script — Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No warm-up. Tired body. Wrong kit. Ignored instructions. THAT is how injuries happen.
Build fitness. Grow speed slowly. Match the work to the player. Hard moves — with the teacher.
Someone is hurt! First aid — quick, careful, HERE, until real care arrives.
R — Rest. I — Ice. C — Compression. E — Elevation. And R — Referral.
And never touch blood. Gloves on. Help safely.
