# Hands-on — Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bandage wrap

Practise a firm, careful compression wrap on a partner's ankle over a sock: firm, elastic, not cutting circulation — fingers and toes stay warm and pink.

## Injury-cause hunt

Watch a class game for five minutes with the causes list: no warm-up? tired players? wrong kit? instructions disobeyed? Report each cause you spot and its prevention.
