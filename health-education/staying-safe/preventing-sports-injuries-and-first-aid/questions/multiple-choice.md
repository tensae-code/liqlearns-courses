# Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the book's diagrammed injury causes?
a) Reading a book  b) No proper warm-up  c) Exercising while tired
**Answer: a) Reading a book**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hard and complex moves should be done…
a) Only with teacher support  b) Alone at home  c) As fast as possible
**Answer: a) Only with teacher support**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the guide's named injury kinds?
a) Sprains  b) Hunger  c) Sunburn
**Answer: a) Sprains**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** First aid is given…
a) Where the accident happened, until health-facility care arrives  b) Only at a hospital  c) The next day
**Answer: a) Where the accident happened, until health-facility care arrives**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In RICE, the C stands for…
a) Compression (ማሰር)  b) Carrying  c) Calling out
**Answer: a) Compression (ማሰር)**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** RICER's fifth step, Referral, means…
a) See a doctor or physiotherapist  b) Return to the game  c) Remove the bandage
**Answer: a) See a doctor or physiotherapist**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Ice in RICER is applied for…
a) 15–20 minutes  b) Two hours  c) Ten seconds
**Answer: a) 15–20 minutes**
