# Video script — Technology in Healthcare

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Technology in healthcare: what medical technology is, healthcare in the past, and a timeline of major advances 1922–today.
Advances in medical technology: the advances that changed history — vaccines, imaging, penicillin — and future technology: 3D bioprinting, medical drones, nanotechnology, implants.
Healthcare innovations in the UAE: electronic health records, DHA augmented-reality platforms, smart ambulances, and a timeline of medicine in the UAE.
Artificial intelligence in healthcare: what AI is, its benefits and uses — data, robotic surgery, virtual nurses, image analysis — and the UAE Strategy for AI 2031.
Digital applications and devices for health: personal medical devices — heart rate, blood pressure and glucose monitors — and health-improvement apps used in the UAE.
Jobs in medical technology: the new jobs created by medical technology — AI specialists, 3D bioprinting, precision-medicine pharmacy — and UAE university courses for them.
That is **technology in healthcare** — as the United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches it.
