# Technology in Healthcare — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technology in healthcare
Pass mark: Major advances from 1922 to today, and healthcare in the past.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advances in medical technology
Pass mark: Vaccines, imaging and penicillin — with 3D bioprinting, drones, nanotechnology and implants as future technology.

## Sub lesson 3 — Healthcare innovations in the UAE
Pass mark: Electronic health records, DHA augmented-reality platforms and smart ambulances.

## Sub lesson 4 — Artificial intelligence in healthcare
Pass mark: Data, robotic surgery, virtual nurses and image analysis — under the UAE Strategy for AI 2031.

## Sub lesson 5 — Digital applications and devices for health
Pass mark: Heart rate, blood pressure and glucose monitors, and health-improvement apps.

## Sub lesson 6 — Jobs in medical technology
Pass mark: AI specialists, 3D bioprinting and precision-medicine pharmacy jobs.
