# Lesson plan — Technology in Healthcare

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Technology in Healthcare
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Technology in healthcare: medical technology past and future — imaging, records, AI, devices and the new health jobs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside First Aid for Emergencies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Technology in healthcare** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what medical technology is, healthcare in the past, and a timeline of major advances 1922–today.
2. **Advances in medical technology** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the advances that changed history — vaccines, imaging, penicillin — and future technology: 3D bioprinting, medical drones, nanotechnology, implants.
3. **Healthcare innovations in the UAE** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: electronic health records, DHA augmented-reality platforms, smart ambulances, and a timeline of medicine in the UAE.
4. **Artificial intelligence in healthcare** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what AI is, its benefits and uses — data, robotic surgery, virtual nurses, image analysis — and the UAE Strategy for AI 2031.
5. **Digital applications and devices for health** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: personal medical devices — heart rate, blood pressure and glucose monitors — and health-improvement apps used in the UAE.
6. **Jobs in medical technology** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the new jobs created by medical technology — AI specialists, 3D bioprinting, precision-medicine pharmacy — and UAE university courses for them.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
