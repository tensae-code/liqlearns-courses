# Technology in Healthcare

## Do it first

What do you already know about this lesson's thing — Technology in healthcare: medical technology past and future — imaging, records, AI, devices and the new health jobs. Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Technology in healthcare

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **what medical technology is, healthcare in the past, and a timeline of major advances 1922–today**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Advances in medical technology

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **the advances that changed history — vaccines, imaging, penicillin — and future technology: 3D bioprinting, medical drones, nanotechnology, implants**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Healthcare innovations in the UAE

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **electronic health records, DHA augmented-reality platforms, smart ambulances, and a timeline of medicine in the UAE**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Artificial intelligence in healthcare

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **what AI is, its benefits and uses — data, robotic surgery, virtual nurses, image analysis — and the UAE Strategy for AI 2031**.

## Sub lesson 5 — Digital applications and devices for health

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **personal medical devices — heart rate, blood pressure and glucose monitors — and health-improvement apps used in the UAE**.

## Sub lesson 6 — Jobs in medical technology

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **the new jobs created by medical technology — AI specialists, 3D bioprinting, precision-medicine pharmacy — and UAE university courses for them**.
