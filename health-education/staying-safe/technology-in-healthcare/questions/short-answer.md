# Technology in Healthcare — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book's timeline of medical technology cover?
**Answer: Major advances from 1922 to today, and healthcare in the past.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which advances does the book say changed history?
**Answer: Vaccines, imaging and penicillin — with 3D bioprinting, drones, nanotechnology and implants as future technology.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which UAE innovations does the book name?
**Answer: Electronic health records, DHA augmented-reality platforms and smart ambulances.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What uses of AI in healthcare does the book name?
**Answer: Data, robotic surgery, virtual nurses and image analysis — under the UAE Strategy for AI 2031.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which personal devices does the book name?
**Answer: Heart rate, blood pressure and glucose monitors, and health-improvement apps.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which new jobs does medical technology create, as the book names them?
**Answer: AI specialists, 3D bioprinting and precision-medicine pharmacy jobs.**
