# Knowing My Body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide's teaching, body water is…
a) 40–60% of body weight  b) 5% of body weight  c) All of body weight
**Answer: a) 40–60% of body weight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The body's water sits…
a) Only in the stomach  b) Inside and outside the cells  c) Only in the blood
**Answer: b) Inside and outside the cells**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The body's water comes from…
a) Drinks, food and metabolic water  b) Drinks only  c) Air only
**Answer: a) Drinks, food and metabolic water**
