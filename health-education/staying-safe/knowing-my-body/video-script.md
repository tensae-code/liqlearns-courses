# Video script — Knowing My Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your body is full of water.
Water is 40 to 60 percent of your body weight.
It sits inside your cells, and outside your cells.
It comes from drinks, from food, and from metabolic water — water the body itself makes.
That is **knowing my body** — starting with the water inside it.
