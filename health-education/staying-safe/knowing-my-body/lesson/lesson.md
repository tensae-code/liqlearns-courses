# Knowing My Body

## Do it first

How much of your body do you think is water? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The water inside the body — how much, and where it sits

Afghanistan's Grade 7 Physical Education Teacher's Guide teaches, in its water-and-sport theory chapter, **the water inside the body** (teacher guide — evidence): **body water is 40–60% of body weight**.

The water sits in two places: **inside the cells** and **outside the cells**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where the body's water comes from — and the water in foods

The same teaching says where the body's water comes from — three places: **drinks**, **food**, and **metabolic water** — water the body itself makes.

The guide also gives **a table of the water content of common foods** — food is a real source of the body's water, and the table shows how much each food carries.

In Afghanistan's own way, this body knowledge is taught inside the PE guide as theory — in the water-and-sport chapter, beside the teaching on water and the exercising body — written for the teacher to pass on orally.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how much of body weight is water, where the water sits, and the three places it comes from — in your own words, the way the Afghanistan guide teaches them.
