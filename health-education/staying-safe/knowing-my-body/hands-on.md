# Hands-on — Knowing My Body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## How much of me is water?

The guide teaches that body water is 40–60% of body weight. Write your weight, then work out 40% and 60% of it — the guide's range for the water inside a body of your weight.

## Inside and outside the cells

Draw one cell as a circle. Write "inside the cells" inside the circle and "outside the cells" outside it — the guide's two places where the body's water sits. Under the drawing, write the three places the water comes from: drinks, food and metabolic water.

## The water in my foods

The guide gives a table of the water content of common foods. List five foods you ate today and sort them into two groups — foods you think hold a lot of water, and foods you think hold little — ready to check against a table like the guide's.
