# Knowing My Body — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The water inside the body — how much, and where it sits
Pass mark: say that body water is 40–60% of body weight in the guide's teaching, and that the water sits inside and outside the cells.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where the body's water comes from — and the water in foods
Pass mark: name the three places the body's water comes from — drinks, food and metabolic water — and say that the guide gives a table of the water content of common foods.
