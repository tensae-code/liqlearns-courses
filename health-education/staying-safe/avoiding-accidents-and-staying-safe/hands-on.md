# Hands-on — Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the dangers

Walk your classroom or room with an adult. List every dangerous place or object you can spot — glass, lamps, bookcases, cables, sockets — the way El Salvador's book teaches spotting them.

## Make a No tocar sign

Make your own "No tocar" danger sign, as the book teaches, and put it (with an adult's help) beside one real danger you spotted.
