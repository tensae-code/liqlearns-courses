# Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe

## Do it first

Look around your classroom. What could hurt someone? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Spotting dangerous places and objects in the classroom

El Salvador's Grade 1 *Ciencia, Salud y Medio Ambiente* book teaches, in Unit 3 Lesson 3 Un aula segura, **spotting dangerous places and objects in the classroom** — glass windows, ceiling lamps and bookcases are its named examples.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electric dangers — and "No tocar" signs

The same lesson teaches **never touching electric cables and sockets**, and **making "No tocar" (Do not touch) danger signs** to warn others.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the safe-classroom teachings — the dangerous places and objects to spot, never touching cables and sockets, and the "No tocar" signs — in your own words, the way El Salvador's book teaches them.
