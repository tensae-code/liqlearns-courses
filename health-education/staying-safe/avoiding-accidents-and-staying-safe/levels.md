# Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Spotting dangerous places and objects in the classroom
Pass mark: Name dangerous places and objects in a classroom, as El Salvador's book teaches: glass windows, ceiling lamps and bookcases.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electric dangers — and "No tocar" signs
Pass mark: Say that you never touch electric cables and sockets, and that "No tocar" signs warn of danger.
