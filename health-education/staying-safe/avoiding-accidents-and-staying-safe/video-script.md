# Video script — Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A safe classroom is one where you spot the dangers first.
El Salvador's Grade 1 book teaches: watch glass windows, ceiling lamps and bookcases — never touch electric cables and sockets — and make a No tocar sign.
That is **avoiding accidents and staying safe** — starting with the classroom, as El Salvador's book teaches it.
