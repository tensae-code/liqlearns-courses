# Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's book teaches spotting dangers in the classroom, including…
a) Glass windows, ceiling lamps and bookcases  b) Only the blackboard  c) Only pencils
**Answer: a) Glass windows, ceiling lamps and bookcases**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches you must never touch…
a) Electric cables and sockets  b) Your books  c) Your chair
**Answer: a) Electric cables and sockets**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A "No tocar" sign means…
a) Do not touch — danger  b) Please touch  c) Sit here
**Answer: a) Do not touch — danger**
