# Medical Words

## Do it first

What do you already know about this lesson's thing — Medical words: medical terminology, medical records, and understanding and following medical instructions. Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What medical terminology is

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **what medical terminology is and why it matters for professionals and patients**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building medical words

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **how medical words are built and read — root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels (e.g. hyperglycaemia)**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Medical record reports

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **the six parts of a medical record — personal information, history, complaint, examination, test results and diagnosis, treatment plan — and the UAE unified medical records system**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Responding to medical instructions

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **common medical terms, preparing for and using a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency**.

## Sub lesson 5 — Spelling and pronouncing medical terminology

The United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches: **why exact spelling and pronunciation matter, look-alike words, syllables and word stress**.
