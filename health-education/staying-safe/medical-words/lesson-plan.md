# Lesson plan — Medical Words

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Medical Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Medical words: medical terminology, medical records, and understanding and following medical instructions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside First Aid for Emergencies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What medical terminology is** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what medical terminology is and why it matters for professionals and patients.
2. **Building medical words** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: how medical words are built and read — root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels (e.g. hyperglycaemia).
3. **Medical record reports** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the six parts of a medical record — personal information, history, complaint, examination, test results and diagnosis, treatment plan — and the UAE unified medical records system.
4. **Responding to medical instructions** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: common medical terms, preparing for and using a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency.
5. **Spelling and pronouncing medical terminology** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: why exact spelling and pronunciation matter, look-alike words, syllables and word stress.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: First Aid for Emergencies. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. It is the shared language professionals and patients need, as the book teaches it.

4. Using common medical terms, preparing for a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency.
