# Medical Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does medical terminology matter?
a) It is the shared language professionals and patients need, as the book teaches it  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) It is the shared language professionals and patients need, as the book teaches it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are medical words built?
a) From root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) From root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many parts does a medical record have, as the book teaches them?
a) Six parts, from personal information to the treatment plan  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Six parts, from personal information to the treatment plan**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does responding to medical instructions include?
a) Using common medical terms, preparing for a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Using common medical terms, preparing for a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Why do exact spelling and pronunciation matter?
a) Because look-alike words can change the meaning — syllables and word stress help say them right  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) Because look-alike words can change the meaning — syllables and word stress help say them right**
