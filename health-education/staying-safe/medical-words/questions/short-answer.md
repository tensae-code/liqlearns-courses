# Medical Words — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does medical terminology matter?
**Answer: It is the shared language professionals and patients need, as the book teaches it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are medical words built?
**Answer: From root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many parts does a medical record have, as the book teaches them?
**Answer: Six parts, from personal information to the treatment plan.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does responding to medical instructions include?
**Answer: Using common medical terms, preparing for a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Why do exact spelling and pronunciation matter?
**Answer: Because look-alike words can change the meaning — syllables and word stress help say them right.**
