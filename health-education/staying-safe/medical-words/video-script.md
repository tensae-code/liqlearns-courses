# Video script — Medical Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What medical terminology is: what medical terminology is and why it matters for professionals and patients.
Building medical words: how medical words are built and read — root words, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels (e.g. hyperglycaemia).
Medical record reports: the six parts of a medical record — personal information, history, complaint, examination, test results and diagnosis, treatment plan — and the UAE unified medical records system.
Responding to medical instructions: common medical terms, preparing for and using a doctor's visit, and communicating in a medical emergency.
Spelling and pronouncing medical terminology: why exact spelling and pronunciation matter, look-alike words, syllables and word stress.
That is **medical words** — as the United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches it.
