# Hands-on — Keeping My Body Clean

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## My day of washing moments

Draw your own day as a chain of washing moments, the way the Year 3 book tells one child's day: waking, washing, eating, playing, eating again, sleeping. Mark every place where hands are washed.

## Check the routine

For one day, tick each part of the morning routine as you do it: face washed, teeth brushed, bathed, clean clothes on, hair combed.
