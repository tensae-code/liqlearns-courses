# Keeping My Body Clean — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the morning washing routine in the Year 3 book?
**Answer: Washing the face on waking, brushing teeth and bathing, putting on a clean dress and combing the hair.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Kiribati's Year 3 book teach hygiene?
**Answer: As a daily routine — one child's whole day told as a chain of washing moments, not as rules.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the WASH guide teach about hygiene in puberty?
**Answer: Bathe and change clothes more often, and wash face, hands, feet, underarms, groin and under the fingernails with soap, because the glands are more active.**
