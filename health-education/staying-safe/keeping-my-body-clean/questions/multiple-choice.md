# Keeping My Body Clean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Year 3 book's story, the child's day begins with…
a) Washing the face on waking, brushing teeth and bathing  b) Playing at school  c) Sleeping
**Answer: a) Washing the face on waking, brushing teeth and bathing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's story, hands are washed…
a) Only once, in the morning  b) Before breakfast, after playing at school and before lunch  c) Never
**Answer: b) Before breakfast, after playing at school and before lunch**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The WASH guide teaches that in puberty you wash more often because…
a) The glands are more active, so there is more oil and stronger sweat  b) The water is warmer  c) School starts later
**Answer: a) The glands are more active, so there is more oil and stronger sweat**
