# Keeping My Body Clean — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Morning washing routine
Pass mark: tell the morning routine in order — washing the face on waking, brushing teeth and bathing, putting on a clean dress and combing the hair.

## Sub lesson 2 — Washing hands again and again through the day
Pass mark: say when hands are washed through the day in the book's story — before breakfast, after playing at school and before lunch — and that the day ends eating healthy food and sleeping on a clean bed and pillow.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hygiene in puberty
Pass mark: say that in puberty more active glands mean more oil and stronger sweat, so the guide teaches bathing and changing clothes more often, washing face, hands, feet, underarms, groin and under the fingernails with soap.
