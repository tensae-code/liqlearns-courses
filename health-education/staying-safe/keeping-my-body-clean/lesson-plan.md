# Lesson plan — Keeping My Body Clean

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Keeping My Body Clean
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** keeping my body clean — washing the body, hands, hair and nails, grooming, and clean clothes; taught here through Kiribati's Year 3 big-book pass: a child's whole day told as a chain of washing moments, and Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide pass: hygiene in puberty.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Hygiene for Sport.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Morning washing routine** — Kiribati's Year 3 teaching: washing the face on waking, brushing teeth and bathing, putting on a clean dress and combing the hair.
2. **Washing hands again and again through the day** — Kiribati's Year 3 teaching: hands washed before breakfast, after playing at school and before lunch; eating healthy food and sleeping on a clean bed and pillow.
3. **Hygiene in puberty** — Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): more active glands mean more oil and stronger sweat, so bathing and changing clothes more often, washing face, hands, feet, underarms, groin and under the fingernails with soap.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Healthy Habits Every Day. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the morning washing routine in order, the way the Year 3 book's story tells it.
2. Say when hands are washed again through the day in the book's story.
3. Say why puberty means bathing and changing clothes more often, as the WASH guide teaches it.
