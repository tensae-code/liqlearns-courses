# Video script — Keeping My Body Clean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wake up. Wash your face.
Brush your teeth. Bathe.
Put on clean clothes. Comb your hair.
Wash your hands before you eat. Wash them after you play. Wash them again.
That is **keeping my body clean**, as the book teaches it.
