# How Medicines Work — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two big ideas explain how drugs act, as the book names them?
**Answer: Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics, working through receptors.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which drug class does the book name as an example?
**Answer: NSAIDs, among the main drug classes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do routes of administration decide?
**Answer: How a drug gets into the body and how fast each route works.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the book teach about antibiotics?
**Answer: What antibiotics do and their side effects.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What calculations does pharmacy use, as the book teaches them?
**Answer: Pharmacy abbreviations, drug formulas and calculating IV rates.**
