# Lesson plan — How Medicines Work

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** How Medicines Work
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How medicines work: pharmacy — how drugs act on the body, drug classes, antibiotics, routes into the body, and pharmacy measurements.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside First Aid for Emergencies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **How drugs act on the body** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics and receptors: how drugs act on the body.
2. **Classes of drugs** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the main drug classes, including NSAIDs.
3. **Routes of administration** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: oral and other routes into the body and how fast each works.
4. **Antibiotics** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what antibiotics do and their side effects.
5. **Measurements and formulas** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: pharmacy abbreviations, drug formulas and calculating IV rates.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: First Aid for Emergencies. Recommended, never forced.
