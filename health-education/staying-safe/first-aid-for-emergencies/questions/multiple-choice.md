# First Aid for Emergencies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zambia's manual teaches examining a sick learner using the…
a) ThinkMD guide  b) Food pyramid  c) BMI table
**Answer: a) ThinkMD guide**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The child health referral system teaches referring a sick child fast to the…
a) Health facility  b) Market  c) Sports field
**Answer: a) Health facility**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Front-line care is the first-response care given…
a) Before referral  b) After the school year ends  c) Only at home
**Answer: a) Before referral**
