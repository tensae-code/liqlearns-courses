# Video script — First Aid for Emergencies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When a learner is sick, Zambia's manual teaches the health teacher what to do first.
Examine the sick learner with the ThinkMD guide and decide the next step. Give the first-response care. And when the child must go, refer fast to the health facility.
That is **first aid for emergencies** — the front-line care in Zambia's book.
