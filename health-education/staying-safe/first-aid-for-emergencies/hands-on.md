# Hands-on — First Aid for Emergencies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My sick-learner steps

Zambia's manual teaches the health teacher to examine a sick learner with the ThinkMD guide, decide the next step, give first-response care, and refer the child fast to the health facility when referral is needed. Write those steps in order — examine, decide, first-response care, refer — as a card you could keep at school.

## My nearest health facility

Write the name of the health facility nearest your school or home — the place a sick child would be referred to, as Zambia's referral chapter teaches.
