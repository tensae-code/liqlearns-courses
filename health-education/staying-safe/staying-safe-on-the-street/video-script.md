# Video script — Staying Safe on the Street — Self-Defence Principles and Resolving Conflict

A short read-aloud script in a super-simple voice, made later when videos are made. The narrator teaches the lesson's own content, in its own order.

---

Narrator: Self-defence is not a fight looking for a place to happen. It is awareness first, words next, escape after — skills last.
Narrator: Know your path. Be expected. Walk confidently. Trust your instincts.
Narrator: In a conflict, listen with your eyes, forgive, and stick with the facts.
