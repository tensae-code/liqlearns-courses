# Staying Safe on the Street — Self-Defence Principles and Resolving Conflict — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-defence begins, as the first act of defence, with…
a) Violence avoidance and de-escalation  b) Striking first  c) Showing off
**Answer: a) Violence avoidance and de-escalation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The four ethics virtues are…
a) Fairness, integrity, responsibility, respect  b) Speed, strength, height, luck  c) Silence, secrecy, force, fear
**Answer: a) Fairness, integrity, responsibility, respect**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Principle Five is…
a) Speed — 'speed is your salvation'  b) Silence  c) Strength
**Answer: a) Speed — 'speed is your salvation'**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A closed-fist blow to the head will nearly always…
a) Wreck your own hand  b) Be safe  c) Win a medal
**Answer: a) Wreck your own hand**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The 'bash and dash' means…
a) Distract or harm the assailant, then run to an active area and call someone trusted  b) Run first, think later  c) Hide and wait
**Answer: a) Distract or harm the assailant, then run to an active area and call someone trusted**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The third strategy is to use the skills…
a) Only when necessary  b) Every day  c) To show off
**Answer: a) Only when necessary**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In a conflict, the most important information is often communicated…
a) Nonverbally  b) In writing  c) By officials
**Answer: a) Nonverbally**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Training widens your options to…
a) Avoid, slow down, de-escalate or interrupt an attack  b) Only fight  c) Only run
**Answer: a) Avoid, slow down, de-escalate or interrupt an attack**
