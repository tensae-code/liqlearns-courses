# Hands-on — Staying Safe on the Street — Self-Defence Principles and Resolving Conflict

One line of framing: these activities use real things — the field, the street, the classroom and home.

## The safe-route map
Draw your route home or to school. Mark the lit, busy, known parts green and the shortcuts the book warns against in red. Walk the green route in your mind, naming the rules you use.

## Words first
In threes — two in disagreement, one mediator — resolve a borrowed-item dispute using only the lesson's techniques: nonverbal listening, facts and honest feelings, willingness to forgive.
