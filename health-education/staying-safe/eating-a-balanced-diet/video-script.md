# Video script — Eating a Balanced Diet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Proper nutrition has foundations.
First, the nutrition regime — eating ordered as a regime.
Second, the main composition of food — what food is mainly made of.
Third, the hygiene characterisation of the main food products.
That is **eating a balanced diet**, as the book's foundations teach it.
