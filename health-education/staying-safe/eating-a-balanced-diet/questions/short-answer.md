# Eating a Balanced Diet — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the nutrition regime?
**Answer: Eating ordered as a regime, as the book's foundations of proper nutrition teach it.**

**2. (Sub lessons 1–3)** Name the book's three foundations of proper nutrition.
**Answer: The nutrition regime, the main composition of food, and the hygiene characterisation of the main food products.**
