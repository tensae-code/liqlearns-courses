# Eating a Balanced Diet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first foundation of proper nutrition in the Turkmenistan book is…
a) The nutrition regime  b) Only buying food  c) Skipping meals
**Answer: a) The nutrition regime**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's second foundation teaches…
a) The main composition of food  b) Only how to cook rice  c) Nothing about food
**Answer: a) The main composition of food**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's third foundation is…
a) The hygiene characterisation of the main food products  b) Ignoring hygiene  c) Only the price of food
**Answer: a) The hygiene characterisation of the main food products**
