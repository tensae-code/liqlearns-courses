# Lesson plan — Eating a Balanced Diet

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Eating a Balanced Diet
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** eating a balanced diet — the nutrients and food groups, what each does, and building balanced meals and menus; taught here through Turkmenistan's university pass: the foundations of proper nutrition — the nutrition regime, the main composition of food, and the hygiene characterisation of the main food products.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Hygiene for Sport.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The nutrition regime** — Turkmenistan's Part XI teaching: the nutrition regime — eating ordered as a regime, as the book's foundations of proper nutrition set it out.
2. **The main composition of food** — Turkmenistan's Part XI teaching: the main composition of food — what food is mainly made of.
3. **The hygiene characterisation of the main food products** — Turkmenistan's Part XI teaching: the hygiene characterisation of the main food products — how the main food products are characterised for hygiene.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
