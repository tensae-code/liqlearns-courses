# Eating a Balanced Diet

## Do it first

What does it mean to eat properly? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The nutrition regime

Turkmenistan's Bedenterbiýe book teaches, in Part XI, the **Foundations of Proper Nutrition**. Its first foundation is the **nutrition regime** — eating is not left to chance: it is ordered as a regime.

## Sub lesson 2 — The main composition of food

The same Part XI teaching carries the **main composition of food** — what food is mainly made of, so that eating properly rests on knowing what is in the food itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — The hygiene characterisation of the main food products

Part XI closes with the **hygiene characterisation of the main food products** — the main food products are characterised for hygiene, so that proper nutrition also means food handled and judged hygienically.

In Turkmenistan's own way, this teaching sits inside the PE theory course as a closing block: health is taught there as what health is, how to eat properly, the habits that damage health, and the medical control that watches over mass sport.
