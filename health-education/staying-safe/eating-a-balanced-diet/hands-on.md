# Hands-on — Eating a Balanced Diet

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## My nutrition regime

Write down, for one day, when you eat and what you eat — the book teaches a nutrition regime: eating ordered as a regime. Look at your day and note where your eating follows a regime and where it does not.

## What my food is made of

Take one meal you ate today. Under the book's second foundation — the main composition of food — write what that meal is mainly made of, as far as you know it.

## Food products and hygiene

List the main food products in your home. Beside each one, write one hygiene point you already keep with it — the book's third foundation is the hygiene characterisation of the main food products.
