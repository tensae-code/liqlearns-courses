# Lesson plan — Keeping Our Environment Healthy

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Keeping Our Environment Healthy
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** keeping our environment healthy — clean home, school and surroundings, waste, pollution, natural resources and a healthy community environment; taught here through Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide pass: care for the environment — recycling groups, the effects of litter, and reusing waste material.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Safe Water and Toilets.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Care for the environment** — Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Healthy Lifestyle Session 2 — recycling groups, the effects of litter, and reusing waste material.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Safe Water and Toilets. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the guide's three parts of caring for the environment: recycling groups, the effects of litter, and reusing waste material.
2. Say what a recycling group does with waste, in the guide's teaching.
3. Say why reusing waste material is part of caring for the environment, as the session teaches it.

## What you need
Nothing is bought for this lesson — paper and a pencil for the hands-on tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Caring for the environment feels too big → anchor it in the guide's own three parts: a recycling group, the litter you can see and its effects, and one piece of waste reused instead of thrown away.
