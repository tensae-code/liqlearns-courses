# Hands-on — Keeping Our Environment Healthy

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Sort the waste

List the waste you can find at home or school today. Mark each item: recycle, reuse, or litter risk — the three things Namibia's session works through.

## Reuse one thing

Take one piece of waste material and reuse it for something real. Write what it was and what it is now.
