# Keeping Our Environment Healthy

## Do it first

Look around where you are. What waste can you see, and what happens to it? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Care for the environment

Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaches **care for the environment** in its **Healthy Lifestyle Session 2**. The session works through three things: **recycling groups**, **the effects of litter**, and **reusing waste material**. Caring for the environment is practical in this guide: waste is sorted and recycled in groups, litter is faced for what it does, and waste material is reused instead of thrown away. (This lesson comes from a teacher guide — it is the teachers' book for the PE sessions.)
