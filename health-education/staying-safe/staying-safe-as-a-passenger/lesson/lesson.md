## Sub lesson 1 — I am a passenger

Ukraine's *«Здоров'я, безпека та добробут»* Grade 5 student book teaches **safe behaviour as a passenger in transport** — its section «Я — пасажир / пасажирка» (I am a passenger). The Grade 5 course teaches safety as a habit of independence: the child as pedestrian, passenger and trip-goer, at home and online.
