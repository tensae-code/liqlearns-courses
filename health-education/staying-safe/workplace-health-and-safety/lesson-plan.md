# Lesson plan — Workplace Health and Safety

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Workplace Health and Safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** workplace health and safety — ergonomics and correct working posture, preventing back pain, and safe, healthy childcare environments — as Iran's books teach them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Correct posture, ergonomics and preventing back pain** — Iran's teaching: correct posture and lifting in study and work, preventing back and neck pain (Grade 12 Health and Hygiene, Ch.6 Lesson 13, printed p. 157), and body posture, workstation fit and avoiding strain in practical sports work (Grades 10–12 Student Companion, PE, Ch.4 ergonomics part, printed p. 83).
2. **Safety of the childcare environment — open spaces** — Iran's teaching: hazards, safe play equipment and accident prevention outdoors in a pre-school centre (Grade 10 Pre-school Environment Health and Safety, Module 2, printed p. 53).
3. **Safety of the childcare environment — closed spaces** — Iran's teaching: fire, electricity, furniture and emergency safety indoors in a pre-school centre (Grade 10 Pre-school Environment Health and Safety, Module 4, printed p. 173).
4. **Children's sleep, rest and play safety** — Iran's teaching: safe sleep arrangements, rest and supervised free time for children (Grade 10 Pre-school Environment Health and Safety, Module 5, printed p. 235).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Iran's books teach about correct posture and ergonomics: correct posture and lifting in study and work, workstation fit, and avoiding strain and back pain.
2. Say what Iran's book teaches about safety in a childcare centre's open spaces: hazards, safe play equipment and accident prevention outdoors.
3. Say what Iran's book teaches about safety in a childcare centre's closed spaces: fire, electricity, furniture and emergency safety indoors.
4. Say what Iran's book teaches about children's sleep, rest and play safety: safe sleep arrangements, rest and supervised free time.
