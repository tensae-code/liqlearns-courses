# Workplace Health and Safety

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Correct posture, ergonomics and preventing back pain

Iran's *Health and Hygiene, Grade 12* teaches **preventing musculoskeletal disorders (back pain): correct posture and lifting in study and work, preventing back and neck pain**. Iran's *Student Companion Book, PE field, Grades 10–12* teaches, in its ergonomics part, **body posture, workstation fit and avoiding strain in practical sports work**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safety of the childcare environment — open spaces

Iran's *Controlling Health and Safety of the Environment of Pre-school Centres, Grade 10* teaches **safety control of the open environment: hazards, safe play equipment and accident prevention outdoors**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Safety of the childcare environment — closed spaces

Iran's *Controlling Health and Safety of the Environment of Pre-school Centres, Grade 10* teaches **safety control of the closed environment: fire, electricity, furniture and emergency safety indoors**.

## Sub lesson 4 — Children's sleep, rest and play safety

Iran's *Controlling Health and Safety of the Environment of Pre-school Centres, Grade 10* teaches **health and safety of the child's sleep, rest and leisure: safe sleep arrangements, rest and supervised free time**.
