# Workplace Health and Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iran's Grade 12 book teaches correct posture and lifting to prevent…
a) Back and neck pain  b) Colds  c) Toothache
**Answer: a) Back and neck pain**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Iran's childcare book, safety control of the open environment covers hazards, safe play equipment and accident prevention…
a) Outdoors  b) Indoors  c) At sea
**Answer: a) Outdoors**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Iran's childcare book teaches fire, electricity, furniture and emergency safety as part of the safety of…
a) Closed environments  b) Open environments  c) Swimming pools
**Answer: a) Closed environments**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Iran's childcare book teaches safe sleep arrangements, rest and…
a) Supervised free time  b) Homework  c) Cooking
**Answer: a) Supervised free time**
