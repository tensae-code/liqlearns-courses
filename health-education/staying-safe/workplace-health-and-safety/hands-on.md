# Hands-on — Workplace Health and Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Check your posture

Iran's books teach correct posture and lifting in study and work, and workstation fit. Sit at your usual study or work place, check your posture and how the place fits your body, and write down one thing you would correct.

## Walk a safety check

Iran's childcare book teaches safety control as inspection — open spaces first, then closed spaces. Walk one outdoor place and one indoor place where children play, list the hazards you find in each, and write how each hazard could be prevented.
