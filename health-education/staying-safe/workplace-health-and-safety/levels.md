# Workplace Health and Safety — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Correct posture, ergonomics and preventing back pain
Pass mark: Say what Iran's books teach about correct posture and ergonomics: correct posture and lifting in study and work, workstation fit, and avoiding strain and back pain.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safety of the childcare environment — open spaces
Pass mark: Say what Iran's book teaches about safety in a childcare centre's open spaces: hazards, safe play equipment and accident prevention outdoors.

## Sub lesson 3 — Safety of the childcare environment — closed spaces
Pass mark: Say what Iran's book teaches about safety in a childcare centre's closed spaces: fire, electricity, furniture and emergency safety indoors.

## Sub lesson 4 — Children's sleep, rest and play safety
Pass mark: Say what Iran's book teaches about children's sleep, rest and play safety: safe sleep arrangements, rest and supervised free time.
