# Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Attention, and what the traffic lights say (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: define attention, say what red and green mean, and tell where you walk and where you cross.

## Sub lesson 2 — The road-traffic movement game (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: walk the traffic game road in both directions, stopping at red, going at green, and crossing only at the marked crossing.
