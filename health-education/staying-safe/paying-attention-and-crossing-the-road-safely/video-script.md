# Video script — Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

RED card! STOP! GREEN card — go!
Your mind on ONE thing. That is **attention** — ትኩረት.
Red means stop. Green means go. The road has a language — learn it.
Walk on the LEFT. መንገድ ስንሄድ ሁሌም ግራችንን ይዘን.
Cross ONLY at the crossing — የእግረኛ መሻገሪያ.
Attention brings you home. Walk awake.
