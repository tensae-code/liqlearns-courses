# Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is attention (ትኩረት)?
a) The mind's focus held on one thing or task  b) Looking everywhere at once  c) Daydreaming
**Answer: a) The mind's focus held on one thing or task**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does a red light mean, and a green light?
a) Red — stop; green — go  b) Red — go; green — stop  c) Both mean run
**Answer: a) Red — stop; green — go**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the traffic game, where may you cross?
a) Only at the marked crossing  b) Anywhere on the road  c) In the middle, running
**Answer: a) Only at the marked crossing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** In which directions is the traffic-game road walked?
a) Both directions — the rules do not change  b) Only one direction  c) Backwards only
**Answer: a) Both directions — the rules do not change**
