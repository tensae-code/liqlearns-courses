# Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The book teaches: keep left when walking on the road.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** You may cross the road anywhere you like.
**Answer: False** — cross at the pedestrian crossing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The traffic game road is 10–15 metres long, with beanbags placed 5–6 in a row.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** In the traffic game you stop at the red card and go at the green.
**Answer: True**
