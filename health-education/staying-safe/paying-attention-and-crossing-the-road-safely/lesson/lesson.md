# Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely

## Do it first

You are walking the 'road'. A red card rises — STOP. A green card — go. But where do you cross? Only at the crossing — and only with your whole mind awake. This lesson is about that waking: **attention**, and the road rules it keeps you alive by.

## Sub lesson 1 — Attention, and what the traffic lights say (teacher guide — evidence)

**Attention — ትኩረት** — is defined in the guide in one line: **the mind's focus held on one thing or task**.

The same pages teach the first traffic safety in this whole series — **የመንገድ ትራፊክ ደህንነት**, road traffic safety:

- A **red light means stop**; **green means go**.
- **Keep left when walking on the road** — መንገድ ስንሄድ ሁሌም ግራችንን ይዘን.
- Cross at the **pedestrian crossing** — የእግረኛ መሻገሪያ.

Attention is not a school-only skill. On the road, it is the skill that brings you home.

## Sub lesson 2 — The road-traffic movement game (teacher guide — evidence)

The teaching becomes a game — **የመንገድ ትራፊክ እንቅስቃሴ ጨዋታ**, the road-traffic movement game:

- **Beanbags are placed 5–6 in a row** to make the 'road'.
- Learners move along the road, **10–15 metres**.
- At the **red card: stop**. At **green: go**.
- Crossing happens **only at the marked crossing**.
- The road is walked **in both directions** — the rules do not change with the direction.

Play it until the rules live in your feet: stop, go, keep left, cross at the crossing — with your attention on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what attention is, what red and green mean, and the two road rules: keep left; cross at the crossing.

## Do It Yourself

1. Play the traffic game with cards and a chalk crossing. Walk both directions. A player who crosses elsewhere restarts.
2. On your real walk to school, count the crossings you use. Did you keep left? Did your attention stay on?
3. Teach a younger child the two cards: red — stop; green — go. Let them teach you back.
