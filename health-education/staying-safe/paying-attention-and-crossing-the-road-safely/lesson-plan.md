# Lesson plan — Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — Staying Safe (CURRICULUM.md path: Staying Safe → Fair Play and the Body).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** attention — the mind's focus held on one thing — taught through the books' first traffic-safety teaching: red means stop, green means go, keep left when walking, and cross only at the pedestrian crossing, practised as a movement game.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own walk to school. It is the first true safety-knowledge lesson the HPE books in hand carry.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Attention, and what the traffic lights say (teacher guide — evidence)** — attention defined — the mind's focus held on one thing or task; the traffic-light teaching text: red means stop, green means go; keep left when walking on the road; the pedestrian crossing.
2. **The road-traffic movement game (teacher guide — evidence)** — beanbags placed 5–6 in a row; learners move along the 'road' 10–15 metres, stopping at the red card, going at green, crossing only at the marked crossing, in both directions — attention and road safety practised as a game.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: none — this is an opening lesson of Staying Safe.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what attention (ትኩረት) is: the mind's focus held on one thing or task.
2. Say what the traffic cards mean: a red light means stop, green means go.
3. Cross the way the book teaches: keep left when walking on the road, and cross only at the pedestrian crossing (የእግረኛ መሻገሪያ).
4. Play the road-traffic movement game by its rules: stop at the red card, go at green, cross only at the marked crossing.

## What you need
Red and green cards, beanbags or markers for the 'road', and a marked crossing on the ground. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Crosses on green without looking → the book's order holds: attention first, then the card. Add the look — left, right, left — at the marked crossing before stepping.
- Treats the game as racing → the game is won by correct stops and crossings, not by speed.
