# Hands-on — Paying Attention and Crossing the Road Safely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the road

Lay beanbags 5–6 in a row, mark a crossing with chalk, make red and green cards. Play the full game both directions.

## Attention walk

Walk a real short road together: keep left, stop at the crossing, attention on, cross together. Name every safe thing you did.
