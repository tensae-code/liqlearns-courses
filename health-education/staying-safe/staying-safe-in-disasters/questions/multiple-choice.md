# Staying Safe in Disasters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the Earth shakes, El Salvador's book teaches first to…
a) Keep calm  b) Run outside shouting  c) Hide under glass
**Answer: a) Keep calm**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In an earthquake you shelter…
a) Beside a strong desk, hands over your head  b) Beside a glass window  c) On top of a bookcase
**Answer: a) Beside a strong desk, hands over your head**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A simulacro is…
a) A drill — practising what to do  b) A kind of food  c) A song
**Answer: a) A drill — practising what to do**
