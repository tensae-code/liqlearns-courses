# Lesson plan — Staying Safe in Disasters

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Staying Safe in Disasters
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** staying safe in disasters — taught here through El Salvador's Grade 1 pass: what tremors and earthquakes are, keeping calm, sheltering beside a strong desk, and taking part in a drill.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Avoiding Accidents and Staying Safe.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **What tremors and earthquakes are — and keeping calm** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: what tremors and earthquakes are, and keeping calm.
2. **Sheltering in an earthquake — and the drill** — El Salvador's Grade 1 teaching: sheltering beside a strong desk with hands over the head, and taking part in a drill (simulacro).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
