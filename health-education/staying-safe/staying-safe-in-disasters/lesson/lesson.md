# Staying Safe in Disasters

## Do it first

What would you do if the ground started to shake? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What tremors and earthquakes are — and keeping calm

El Salvador's Grade 1 *Ciencia, Salud y Medio Ambiente* book teaches, in Unit 3 Lesson 2 Cuando la Tierra se mece, **what tremors and earthquakes are** — and **keeping calm** when the Earth shakes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sheltering in an earthquake — and the drill

The same lesson teaches **sheltering beside a strong desk with hands over the head**, and **taking part in a drill (simulacro)** so you know what to do.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the earthquake teachings — keep calm, shelter beside a strong desk with hands over your head, and take part in the drill — in your own words, the way El Salvador's book teaches them.
