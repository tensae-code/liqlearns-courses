# Hands-on — Staying Safe in Disasters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the shelter

With a teacher or adult, practise the book's shelter: go beside a strong desk and put your hands over your head. Say, while you do it, the two words the book teaches first: keep calm.

## My drill plan

Draw your classroom or room. Mark the strong desk or table you would shelter beside in an earthquake, and write where your class drill (simulacro) gathers.
