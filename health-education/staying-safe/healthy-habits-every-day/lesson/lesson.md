# Healthy Habits Every Day

## Do it first

What do you do every day that keeps you healthy? And what do you do that does not? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Healthy habits

Jordan's Grade 1 book teaches health inside its **Movement and Health** unit. Its healthy-habits lesson teaches **following daily healthy habits** — the good things done every day, as a habit, not once.

The book teaches it with pictures: the child **tells healthy from unhealthy** in the pictures — this habit is healthy, that one is not — and then plays the unit's game, **aiming a ball at the picture of a daily healthy habit**.

In Jordan's own way, health enters at Grade 1 inside a "Movement and Health" unit — carrying the school bag correctly and daily healthy habits are taught as movement lessons with picture activities.
