# Healthy Habits Every Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Jordan Grade 1 book's healthy-habits lesson teaches…
a) Following daily healthy habits  b) Skipping every habit  c) Nothing about habits
**Answer: a) Following daily healthy habits**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's picture activity, the child…
a) Tells healthy from unhealthy pictures  b) Throws the pictures away  c) Only colours the pictures
**Answer: a) Tells healthy from unhealthy pictures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's game, the ball is aimed at…
a) The picture of a daily healthy habit  b) The picture of an unhealthy habit  c) The wall
**Answer: a) The picture of a daily healthy habit**
