# Healthy Habits Every Day — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book's healthy-habits lesson teach?
**Answer: Following daily healthy habits — and telling healthy habits from unhealthy ones.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book teach telling healthy from unhealthy?
**Answer: With pictures — the child tells healthy from unhealthy pictures and aims a ball at the picture of a daily healthy habit.**
