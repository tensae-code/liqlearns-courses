# Hands-on — Healthy Habits Every Day

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Healthy or unhealthy?

Draw two columns — healthy, unhealthy. Write or draw the habits of your own day in the column each one belongs in, the way the book's pictures teach you to tell them apart.

## Aim at the healthy habit

Play the book's game with a soft ball and picture cards you draw yourself: lay the cards out, and aim the ball at the picture of a daily healthy habit.
