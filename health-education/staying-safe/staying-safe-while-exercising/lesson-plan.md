# Lesson plan — Staying Safe While Exercising

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Staying Safe While Exercising
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the books' precautions for fitness exercise: warm up properly, wear correct sport kit, go from easy to hard, rest in the middle, stop on tiredness or pain, and cool down properly — most exercise injuries are preventable beforehand.
**This lesson's own level:** it is the opening lesson of the Staying Safe title; the road-safety lesson of this title is its neighbour.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The six precautions — the pupil's list** — the Grade 5 student book's §1.2: warm up properly, wear correct kit, easy to hard, rest in the middle, stop on tiredness or pain, cool down properly.
2. **Clothes, place, warm-up — the safety check (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 teacher guide's §1.2: fitness activity must stop for a safety check first — clothing, jewellery, dangerous objects and electric wires, mats and safe places, measured fitness, a full warm-up and a cool-down with rest.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — this is an opening lesson of its title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the Grade 5 book's six precautions in order.
2. Say why the book teaches them: without proper care, injury can occur and block your fitness goal — and most injuries are preventable beforehand.
3. Add the teacher guide's safety checks: proper (not loose) clothing and no jewellery, a checked place away from dangerous objects and electric wires, mats and safe places, and knowing your own fitness level.
4. Do a full safe session yourself: check, warm up, work easy-to-hard, rest, cool down.

## What you need
Proper sport kit, a checked open place, and a mat where the movement needs one.

## If the learner is stuck
- Learners skip the warm-up to save time → the warm-up is part of the exercise in the book's count, not an extra before it.
- "Stop on pain" feels like quitting → name it as the book does: a precaution that protects the fitness goal itself.
