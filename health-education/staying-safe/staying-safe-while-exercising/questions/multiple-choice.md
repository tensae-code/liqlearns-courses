# Staying Safe While Exercising — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Most exercise injuries, says the book, are…
a) Preventable beforehand  b) Impossible to prevent  c) Always serious
**Answer: a) Preventable beforehand**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What must you do if you feel tiredness or pain in a movement?
a) Stop the movement  b) Push harder  c) Hide it
**Answer: a) Stop the movement**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Movements in a session go…
a) From easy to hard  b) From hard to easy  c) Hardest first
**Answer: a) From easy to hard**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before fitness activity starts, the guide says to stop for…
a) A safety check  b) A snack  c) A song
**Answer: a) A safety check**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these must be taken off or left aside for exercise?
a) Jewellery and hard/sharp items  b) Sport shoes  c) A water bottle nearby
**Answer: a) Jewellery and hard/sharp items**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Accidents during activity come from, in the guide's answer…
a) Carelessness and wrong equipment use  b) Bad luck only  c) Exercising at all
**Answer: a) Carelessness and wrong equipment use**

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set lists the precautions the book's summary exercises ask for — before, during and after exercise?
a) warm up properly, wear proper and correct sport kit, do movements from easy to hard, take enough rest in the middle, stop if you feel tiredness or pain, and cool down properly after training  b) start with the hardest movement, wear loose kit, and never rest  c) skip the warm-up and keep hard, sharp items on the body
**Answer: a) warm up properly, wear proper and correct sport kit, do movements from easy to hard, take enough rest in the middle, stop if you feel tiredness or pain, and cool down properly after training**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's summary answer key lists this safety precaution, in its own words…
a) use a level field with nothing sharp in it, and work away from dangerous things like electric wires and flammable things  b) work beside electric wires  c) exercise in loose clothing with jewellery on
**Answer: a) use a level field with nothing sharp in it, and work away from dangerous things like electric wires and flammable things**
