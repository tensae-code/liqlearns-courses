# Staying Safe While Exercising

## Do it first

Think of a PE session. What could hurt someone in it? A cold body pulled hard. Loose clothes catching. A sharp stone in the jumping place. This lesson is the books' answer: the care you take *before* and *during* exercise, so the injury never happens.

## Sub lesson 1 — The six precautions — the pupil's list

Fitness is **the ability to do daily tasks efficiently without tiring**, and physical activity matters greatly in a healthy lifestyle. But **without proper care during exercise, injury can occur and block your fitness goal — and most injuries are preventable beforehand**.

The book's precautions, in its order:

1. **Warm the body up properly** before starting.
2. Wear **proper, correct sport kit**.
3. Do movements **from easy to hard**.
4. Take **enough rest in the middle** of a session.
5. **Stop the movement if you feel tiredness or pain**.
6. After training, do **proper cool-down movements**.

The book's own task sends you out: in groups, ask a professional about exercise injuries in your area caused by lack of care — their causes, and the care that should have been taken — and present it to the class.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clothes, place, warm-up — the safety check (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide puts it first: **fitness activity must stop for a safety check** before it starts. Its rules:

- Wear **proper (not loose) exercise clothing**; nothing else on the body.
- **No jewellery or hard/sharp items**; work **away from dangerous objects** — **electric wires** are named.
- Use **mats and safe places** for the movements.
- **Know your fitness**: measure your health level and your fitness level.
- **Warm up fully** before activity; at the end, do **five-to-ten minutes of warm-up-style easing movements** (the summary key's measure) and **cool down and rest**.

The guide's assessment answer names the causes plainly: accidents during activity come from **carelessness and wrong equipment use** — the warm-up and checking the place prevent them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the six precautions in order, then add the guide's first rule: stop for the safety check before anything starts.

## Do It Yourself

1. Run a home session by the book: check the place, warm up, easy-to-hard, rest in the middle, stop if anything hurts, cool down. Tick each precaution as you do it.
2. Do the book's task in miniature: ask one adult about an exercise injury they have seen and what care was missing. Write the cause and the care.
3. Check your own kit against the guide: loose clothing? jewellery? Wrong shoes? Fix one thing.
