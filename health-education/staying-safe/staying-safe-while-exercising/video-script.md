# Video script — Staying Safe While Exercising

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stop! Before the exercise — the safety check!
Proper kit. No jewellery. A place with nothing sharp, no wires.
Warm up! Easy… then hard. Rest in the middle.
Tired? Pain? STOP. That is a rule, not a weakness.
Cool down. Rest. Most injuries never happen — because YOU prevented them.
