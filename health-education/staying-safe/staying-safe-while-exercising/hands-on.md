# Hands-on — Staying Safe While Exercising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The safety-check walk

Before any class activity, two learners walk the exercise place with the checklist: sharp objects, wires, surface, space. Nothing starts until they report it safe.

## Warm-up, then cool-down

Lead the class through a proper warm-up, one easy-to-hard activity, a rest, and a proper cool-down — the full arc of the six precautions in one session.
