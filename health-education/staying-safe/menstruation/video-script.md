# Video script — Menstruation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every month, the body has a cycle. That is menstruation.
It is not an illness. It is not shameful. It is no reason to miss school.
In Kiribati, a girl's first period is celebrated — Te Katekateka — and an elder teaches her how to care for herself.
That is **menstruation**, as the guide teaches it.
