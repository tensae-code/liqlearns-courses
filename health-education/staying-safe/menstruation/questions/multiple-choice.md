# Menstruation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide teaches that menstruation is…
a) The monthly cycle  b) An illness  c) A reason to miss school
**Answer: a) The monthly cycle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Te Katekateka is…
a) Kiribati's celebration of a girl's first menstruation, three days learning with a female elder  b) A school exam  c) A sports festival
**Answer: a) Kiribati's celebration of a girl's first menstruation, three days learning with a female elder**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Most girls in Kiribati use…
a) Washable cloth pads (pasador)  b) No pads at all  c) Paper from exercise books
**Answer: a) Washable cloth pads (pasador)**
