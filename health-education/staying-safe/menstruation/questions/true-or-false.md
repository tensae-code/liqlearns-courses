# Menstruation — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide teaches that menstruation is not an illness and not shameful.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Te Katekateka, a girl learns hygiene practices from a female elder.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide teaches that cloth pads are washed with soap and sun-dried.
**Answer: True**
