# Menstruation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the guide teach menstruation is NOT?
**Answer: Not an illness, not shameful, and no reason to miss school.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two hygiene practices a girl learns in Te Katekateka.
**Answer: Any two of: warm-water bathing for stomach pain, making a sanitary pad from white soft cloth, washing cloths with soap and sun-drying them, Taberanikai herbs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does Menstrual Hygiene Management in school matter?
**Answer: Without it girls face social exclusion, missed schooling and health risks; schools need girl-friendly facilities with water and soap and safe disposal.**
