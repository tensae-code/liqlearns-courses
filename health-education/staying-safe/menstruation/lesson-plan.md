# Lesson plan — Menstruation

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Menstruation
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** menstruation — the monthly cycle, menstrual hygiene, and cultural celebrations of first menstruation; taught here through Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide pass: what menstruation is, Te Katekateka — Kiribati's own celebration of a girl's first menstruation — and menstrual hygiene management in school.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Puberty and Growing Up.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What menstruation is** — Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): the monthly cycle, how long periods last, the facts of menstruation, and what menstruation is NOT (not an illness, not shameful, no reason to miss school).
2. **Menstruation and culture: Te Katekateka** — Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Kiribati's celebration of a girl's first menstruation, three days with a female elder learning hygiene practices and the closing family celebration.
3. **Menstrual Hygiene Management in school** — Kiribati's Years 5–6 WASH guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): why MHM matters, girl-friendly facilities with water and soap, safe disposal and washing of cloth pads (pasador), and the class activity of making sanitary pads.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Puberty and Growing Up. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what menstruation is and what it is not, as the guide teaches it.
2. Tell what Te Katekateka is and what a girl learns in it.
3. Say how cloth pads (pasador) are washed and dried, and why girl-friendly facilities with water and soap matter.
