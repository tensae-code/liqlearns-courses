# Menstruation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — What menstruation is
Pass mark: say what menstruation is — the monthly cycle and how long periods last — and what it is NOT: not an illness, not shameful, no reason to miss school.

## Sub lesson 2 — Menstruation and culture: Te Katekateka
Pass mark: tell what Te Katekateka is — Kiribati's celebration of a girl's first menstruation, three days with a female elder learning hygiene practices (warm-water bathing for stomach pain, making a sanitary pad from white soft cloth, washing cloths with soap and sun-drying, Taberanikai herbs) and the closing family celebration.

## Sub lesson 3 — Menstrual Hygiene Management in school
Pass mark: say why MHM matters (social exclusion, missed schooling, health), what girl-friendly facilities provide (water and soap), and how cloth pads (pasador) are safely washed, dried and disposed of.
