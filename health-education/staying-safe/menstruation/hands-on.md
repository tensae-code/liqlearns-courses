# Hands-on — Menstruation

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The monthly cycle on a calendar

Draw a calendar month and mark a monthly cycle on it, the way the guide teaches the cycle and how long periods last.

## Wash and dry, step by step

Write the steps for washing a cloth pad (pasador) the way the guide teaches them: wash with soap, sun-dry, and dispose of pads safely. Compare your steps with the guide's teaching in the lesson.
