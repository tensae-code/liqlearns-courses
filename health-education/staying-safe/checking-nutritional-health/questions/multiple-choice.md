# Checking Nutritional Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is nutritional assessment?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is anthropometry?

**Answer: a) Measuring weight, height and body build accurately**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are biochemical methods in the ABCDE assessment?

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What are dietary methods in the ABCDE assessment?
a) The D: measuring what a person eats  b) The opposite of what the book teaches  c) Something the book does not teach
**Answer: a) The D: measuring what a person eats**
