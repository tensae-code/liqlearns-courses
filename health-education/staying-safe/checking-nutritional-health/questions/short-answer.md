# Checking Nutritional Health — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is nutritional assessment?
**Answer: Checking a person's nutritional status — and what malnutrition means, as the book teaches it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is anthropometry?
**Answer: Measuring weight, height and body build accurately.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are biochemical methods in the ABCDE assessment?
**Answer: The B: laboratory measures.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What are clinical methods in the ABCDE assessment?
**Answer: The C: clinical signs of nutritional status.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What are dietary methods in the ABCDE assessment?
**Answer: The D: measuring what a person eats.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What do environmental factors cover?
**Answer: How environment affects a person's nutritional status.**
