# Video script — Checking Nutritional Health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What nutritional assessment is: what nutritional assessment is, why it matters, and what malnutrition means.
Anthropometry: measuring weight, height and body build accurately.
Biochemical methods: the B of the ABCDE assessment: laboratory measures.
Clinical methods: the C of the ABCDE assessment: clinical signs of nutritional status.
Dietary methods: the D of the ABCDE assessment: measuring what a person eats.
Environmental factors: how environment affects a person's nutritional status.
That is **checking nutritional health** — as the United Arab Emirates Health Sciences book teaches it.
