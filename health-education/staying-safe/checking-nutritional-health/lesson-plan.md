# Lesson plan — Checking Nutritional Health

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Checking Nutritional Health
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Checking nutritional health: nutritional assessment — measuring weight, height and BMI, and the clinical, dietary and laboratory checks.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside First Aid for Emergencies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What nutritional assessment is** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: what nutritional assessment is, why it matters, and what malnutrition means.
2. **Anthropometry** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: measuring weight, height and body build accurately.
3. **Biochemical methods** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the B of the ABCDE assessment: laboratory measures.
4. **Clinical methods** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the C of the ABCDE assessment: clinical signs of nutritional status.
5. **Dietary methods** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: the D of the ABCDE assessment: measuring what a person eats.
6. **Environmental factors** — United Arab Emirates Health Sciences: how environment affects a person's nutritional status.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
