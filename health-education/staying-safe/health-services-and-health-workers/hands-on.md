# Hands-on — Health Services and Health Workers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the agencies

Samoa's guide teaches health agencies and where to find them — AIDS awareness and family planning among them. Write the name of one health agency or service near you and what it helps with.

## My village co-operative

Samoa's guide names village co-operatives such as Asiasiga Maumaga (taro growers), whose labour-intensive work keeps them fit. Write about a group in your community whose shared work keeps people active and healthy.
