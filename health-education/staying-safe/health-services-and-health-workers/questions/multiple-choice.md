# Health Services and Health Workers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's guide, health promotion involves…
a) The family  b) Only clinics  c) Nobody

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Samoa's guide teaches learners to find health agencies for AIDS awareness and…

**Answer: a) Family planning**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Asiasiga Maumaga in Samoa's guide is a village co-operative of…
a) Taro growers  b) Drivers  c) Builders
