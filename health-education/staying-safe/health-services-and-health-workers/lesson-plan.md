# Lesson plan — Health Services and Health Workers

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Health Services and Health Workers
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** health promotion through the family and the village — family health issues, family involvement in health promotion, health agencies and where to find them, effective use of community resources, the rights of all community members to good health, and village co-operatives — as Samoa's Years 11–12 Teacher Guide teaches them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Preventing Common Diseases.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Family health and family involvement** — Samoa's teaching: family health issues, and family involvement in health promotion (Units 3–4, printed pp. 37–46, 65–83).
2. **Health agencies and community resources** — Samoa's teaching: health agencies and where to find them (AIDS awareness, family planning and others), and effective use of community resources (Units 3–4, printed pp. 37–46, 65–83).
3. **Rights to good health and village co-operatives** — Samoa's teaching: the rights of all community members to good health, and village co-operatives such as Asiasiga Maumaga (taro growers) whose labour-intensive work keeps them fit (Units 3–4, printed pp. 37–46, 65–83).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
