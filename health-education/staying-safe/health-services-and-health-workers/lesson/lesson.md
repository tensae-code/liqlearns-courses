Samoa's *Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide, Years 11 and 12* teaches **family health issues, and family involvement in health promotion**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Health agencies and community resources

Samoa's *Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide, Years 11 and 12* teaches **health agencies and where to find them, and effective use of community resources**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rights to good health and village co-operatives

Samoa's *Health and Physical Education Teacher Guide, Years 11 and 12* teaches **the rights of all community members to good health, and village co-operatives such as Asiasiga Maumaga (taro growers)**.
