# Protecting Myself from Abuse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Instead of corporal punishment, Zambia's manual teaches…
a) Positive discipline  b) More punishment  c) Silence
**Answer: a) Positive discipline**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When abuse is recognised, the manual teaches the teacher's duty to…
a) Protect and report  b) Hide it  c) Ignore it
**Answer: a) Protect and report**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ending child marriage is about the harms of early marriage and keeping girls…
a) In school  b) At home only  c) At work
**Answer: a) In school**
