# Hands-on — Protecting Myself from Abuse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Positive discipline instead

Zambia's manual teaches the harms of corporal punishment and positive discipline instead. Write two lists: what corporal punishment does, as this lesson teaches it, and what positive discipline does instead.

## Protect and report

Zambia's manual teaches the teacher's duty to protect and report abuse. Write the name of the person or place at your school a learner could tell if they were being hurt — the getting-help step this lesson teaches.
