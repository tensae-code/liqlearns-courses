## Sub lesson 1 — Harmful habits that damage health

Turkmenistan's Bedenterbiýe book gives a whole part of its course to this teaching — Part XII, **Harmful Habits That Damage Health** (Saglyga zeper ýetirýän zyýanly endikler): the **habits that injure health**, taught plainly as habits to **stay away from**.

The teaching stands inside the book's closing health block, between the foundations of proper nutrition and the medical control that watches over mass sport: health is kept by eating properly — and by keeping away from the habits that damage it.

In Turkmenistan's own way, this is health taught inside the PE theory course: what health is, how to eat properly, the habits that damage health, and the medical control that watches over mass sport.
