# Hands-on — Saying No to Drugs, Alcohol and Tobacco

The lesson with your hands and your head, not only your eyes.

## Habits that help, habits that harm

Write two headings — habits that help health, habits that harm health. Under each one, write the habits you know from your own day. The book's teaching is the second list: the harmful habits that damage health.

## My staying-away plan

Pick one harmful habit from your second list — your own, or one around you. Write one sentence on how you will stay away from it, the way the book teaches: these habits injure health, so stay away from them.
