# Lesson plan — Saying No to Drugs, Alcohol and Tobacco

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Saying No to Drugs, Alcohol and Tobacco
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** saying no to drugs, alcohol and tobacco — what these substances do, addiction, secondhand smoke, and refusing and quitting; taught here through Turkmenistan's university pass: the harmful habits that damage health (Saglyga zeper ýetirýän zyýanly endikler) — the habits that injure health and why to stay away from them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Hygiene for Sport.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that the book names a part of its course for the harmful habits that damage health.
2. Say, in the book's framing, that these habits injure health and are to be stayed away from.
