# The Body Systems — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each cell's shape fits its job.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Blood is the body's transport and defence system.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Platelets carry oxygen.
**Answer: False**
