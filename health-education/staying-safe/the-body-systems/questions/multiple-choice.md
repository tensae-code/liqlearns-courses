# The Body Systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Croatia book, the body is built in this order…
a) Cells through tissues and organs to organ systems  b) Organs first, then cells  c) Only organ systems, with no cells
**Answer: a) Cells through tissues and organs to organ systems**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a cell's shape relate to its work, in the book?
a) Each cell's shape fits its job  b) Shape has nothing to do with the job  c) All cells have the same shape
**Answer: a) Each cell's shape fits its job**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Red blood cells…
a) Carry oxygen  b) Defend against microbes  c) Make the blood clot
**Answer: a) Carry oxygen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** White blood cells…
a) Defend against microbes  b) Carry oxygen  c) Make the blood clot
**Answer: a) Defend against microbes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Platelets…
a) Help the blood clot  b) Carry oxygen  c) Defend against microbes
**Answer: a) Help the blood clot**
