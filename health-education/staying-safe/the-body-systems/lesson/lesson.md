# The Body Systems

## Do it first

How is your body built — what is the smallest living building block, and how do the blocks become a whole body? And what does your blood actually do all day? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The human body — harmony of structure and function

Croatia's vocational biology book — *Čovjek i zdravlje* (Man and Health) — opens with the human body as a harmony of structure and function: **the body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems**.

Each part's shape fits its job: **how each cell's shape fits its job** is part of how the whole body works. The section also places **metabolism** and **responding to stimuli** inside that same picture of a body built to work together.

Croatia's own way frames all of this around homeostasis: first how each body system keeps the body in balance "in the service of health", then the same systems out of balance, then the human life cycle and sexual health.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transport of substances through the body in the service of health

The book's next section — transport of substances through the body in the service of health — teaches **blood and its cells** as the body's transport and defence system. Only the opening pages of this section are in the publisher sample read for this course.

Blood's cells and their jobs, as the opening teaches them:

1. **Red cells carrying oxygen.**
2. **White cells defending against microbes.**
3. **Platelets clotting.**
