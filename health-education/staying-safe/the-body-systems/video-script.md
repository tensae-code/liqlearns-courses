# Video script — The Body Systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your body is built in layers.
Cells first. Then tissues. Then organs. Then organ systems working together.
Each cell's shape fits its job.
And blood carries what the body needs — red cells carry oxygen,
white cells defend against microbes, platelets help the blood clot.
That is **the body systems** — built together, and served by transport.
