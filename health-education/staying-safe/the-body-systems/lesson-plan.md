# Lesson plan — The Body Systems

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** The Body Systems
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the body systems — skeleton, muscles, digestion, breathing, circulation, nerves, glands, urinary and reproductive systems — how each works; taught here through Croatia's vocational pass: how the body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems, and how blood transports substances through the body in the service of health.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Staying Safe While Exercising.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The human body — harmony of structure and function** — Croatia vocational Čovjek i zdravlje §1.1: how the body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems, how each cell's shape fits its job, metabolism, and responding to stimuli.
2. **Transport of substances through the body in the service of health** — Croatia vocational Čovjek i zdravlje §1.2 (opening only, in the sample): blood and its cells — red cells carrying oxygen, white cells defending against microbes, platelets clotting — as the body's transport and defence system.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Staying Safe While Exercising. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how the body is built, in the book's order: cells through tissues and organs to organ systems.
2. Say how a cell's shape fits its job, as the book teaches it.
3. Name metabolism and responding to stimuli as part of how the body works, as the book places them.
4. Name blood's cells and their jobs: red cells carrying oxygen, white cells defending against microbes, platelets clotting.
5. Say that blood is the body's transport and defence system, in the book's framing — transport of substances through the body in the service of health.
