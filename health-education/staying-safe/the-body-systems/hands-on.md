# Hands-on — The Body Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the layers

Draw the book's building order as a ladder — cells, tissues, organs, organ systems — and write beside one example at each level what it does. Say, for each one, how its shape fits its job, as the book teaches it.

## Blood's three jobs

Write blood's three cell jobs from the book as a list — red cells carrying oxygen, white cells defending against microbes, platelets clotting — and beside each one write what would go wrong without it.
