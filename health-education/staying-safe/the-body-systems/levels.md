# The Body Systems — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The human body — harmony of structure and function
Pass mark: say how the body is built from cells through tissues and organs to organ systems, say how a cell's shape fits its job, and name metabolism and responding to stimuli.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transport of substances through the body in the service of health
Pass mark: name blood's cells and their jobs — red cells carrying oxygen, white cells defending against microbes, platelets clotting — and say that blood is the body's transport and defence system.
