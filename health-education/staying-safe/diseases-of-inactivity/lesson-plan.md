# Lesson plan — Diseases of Inactivity

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Diseases of Inactivity
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** diseases of inactivity — the hypokinetic diseases of sitting still — heart disease, hypertension, obesity and diabetes — and how activity prevents them; taught here through the United Kingdom's GCSE pass: the consequences of a sedentary lifestyle, and obesity and body types (somatotypes).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Staying Safe While Exercising.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The consequences of a sedentary lifestyle** — the United Kingdom GCSE book's Component 2 Topic 1.2, first part: what inactivity does to health.
2. **Obesity and body types (somatotypes)** — the United Kingdom GCSE book's Component 2 Topic 1.2, second part: obesity and body types (somatotypes).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Staying Safe While Exercising. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a sedentary lifestyle is, in the book's framing: a life of sitting still and inactivity.
2. Name consequences of a sedentary lifestyle for health, as the book teaches them.
3. Say what obesity is, as the book places it inside this topic.
4. Name body types (somatotypes) as the book's way of describing body build inside this topic.
