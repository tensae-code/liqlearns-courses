# Diseases of Inactivity

## Do it first

What happens to a body that sits still day after day — is sitting still harmless, or does it do harm? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The consequences of a sedentary lifestyle

The United Kingdom's GCSE Physical Education student book teaches **the consequences of a sedentary lifestyle**: a **sedentary** life is a life of **sitting still and inactivity**, and the book teaches **what inactivity does to health** — inactivity itself is a health risk, not a neutral rest.

This lesson's wider framing names the diseases of sitting still — the hypokinetic diseases: **heart disease, hypertension, obesity and diabetes** — and teaches that **activity prevents them**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Obesity and body types (somatotypes)

Inside the same topic, the book teaches **obesity** — and **body types (somatotypes)**, its way of describing how bodies are built differently.

Obesity is placed here, inside the inactivity topic: it belongs with the consequences of a sedentary lifestyle, as the book orders it.

## Say it to your companion
