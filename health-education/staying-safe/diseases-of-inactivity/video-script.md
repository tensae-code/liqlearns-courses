# Video script — Diseases of Inactivity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A body that sits still all day pays for it.
A sedentary lifestyle — sitting, not moving — has consequences for health.
Obesity is taught in the same place: too much stored fat harms the body.
And bodies come in types — somatotypes — each one still needs to move.
That is **diseases of inactivity** — the harm of sitting still, and why moving matters.
