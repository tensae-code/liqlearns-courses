# Hands-on — Diseases of Inactivity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the sitting

For one ordinary day, list the long periods of sitting still — lessons, screens, travel, homework. Add up the total. Then mark two places where a short walk or active break could break the sitting up, and do one of them today.

## Body types, honestly

Write the word the book uses for body types — somatotypes — and one sentence on what it means: bodies are built differently. Beside it, write why every body type still needs activity, in the book's framing of this topic.
