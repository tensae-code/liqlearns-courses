# Diseases of Inactivity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sedentary lifestyle is…
a) A life of sitting still and inactivity  b) A life of daily training  c) A kind of sport
**Answer: a) A life of sitting still and inactivity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's topic, inactivity…
a) Does harm to health  b) Has no effect on health  c) Is the same as rest after exercise
**Answer: a) Does harm to health**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Obesity is taught in the book…
a) Inside the sedentary-lifestyle topic  b) In a chapter on examinations  c) Nowhere in the book
**Answer: a) Inside the sedentary-lifestyle topic**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Somatotypes are…
a) Body types — the book's description of body build  b) Kinds of exercise  c) Kinds of food
**Answer: a) Body types — the book's description of body build**
