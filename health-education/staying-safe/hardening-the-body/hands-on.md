# Hands-on — Hardening the Body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meet sun, air and water

Write the book's three friends of the body as three headings — sun, air, water. Under each one, write one way the book's teaching uses it to harden the body.

## Barefoot in summer

The book names walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer. Where it is safe and clean, try a short barefoot walk on grass, as the book teaches — and write how your feet felt before and after.

## My drinking regime

Write down, for one day, when you drink water and how much — the book teaches a drinking regime: how much water to drink and when. Compare your day with that idea and note one change you would make.
