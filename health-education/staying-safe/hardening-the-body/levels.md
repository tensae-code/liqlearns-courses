# Hardening the Body — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sun, air and water — our best friends
Pass mark: say that the body is hardened with sun, air and water, as the book teaches, and name its barefoot example — walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water and the drinking regime
Pass mark: say that the book teaches a drinking regime — how much water to drink and when.
