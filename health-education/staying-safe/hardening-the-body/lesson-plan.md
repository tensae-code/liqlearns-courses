# Lesson plan — Hardening the Body

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** Hardening the Body
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hardening the body — using air, sun, water and natural terrain to harden the body and build fitness, safely; taught here through Tajikistan's Grades 1–4 pass: sun, air and water as our best friends (including walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer), and water and the drinking regime — how much water to drink and when.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Hygiene for Sport.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sun, air and water — our best friends** — Tajikistan's Part 1 teaching: hardening the body with sun, air and water, including walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer.
2. **Water and the drinking regime** — Tajikistan's Part 1 teaching: how much water to drink and when.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Hygiene for Sport. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what hardening the body means in the book's teaching: using sun, air and water to harden the body.
2. Name the book's own barefoot example: walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer.
3. Say that the book also teaches a drinking regime — how much water to drink and when.
