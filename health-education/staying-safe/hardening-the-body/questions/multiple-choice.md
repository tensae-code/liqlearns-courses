# Hardening the Body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Tajikistan book, the body is hardened with…
a) Sun, air and water  b) Only medicines  c) Staying indoors
**Answer: a) Sun, air and water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's own barefoot example is walking barefoot…
a) On grass and sand in summer  b) On ice in winter  c) It names no example
**Answer: a) On grass and sand in summer**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's drinking regime teaches…
a) How much water to drink and when  b) Never to drink water  c) Only what to eat
**Answer: a) How much water to drink and when**
