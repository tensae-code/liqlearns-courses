# Video script — Hardening the Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sun, air and water are our best friends.
Meeting them hardens the body.
In summer, walk barefoot on grass and sand.
And drink water the way the book teaches — how much, and when.
That is **hardening the body**.
