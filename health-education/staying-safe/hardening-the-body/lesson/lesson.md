# Hardening the Body

## Do it first

What could sun, air and water do for your body? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sun, air and water — our best friends

Tajikistan's Grades 1–4 Physical Culture book teaches, in Part 1, **Sun, Air and Water — Our Best Friends**: the body is **hardened** — made stronger and more used to the world — with **sun, air and water**.

The book's own example is **walking barefoot on grass and sand in summer** — the feet meet the ground itself as part of hardening.

In Tajikistan's own way, this health knowledge opens the book before any sport: hardening by sun, air and water is Part 1 theory every child reads first, before the doing of Part 2.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water and the drinking regime

The same Part 1 teaching carries **Water and the Drinking Regime**: water is not only for hardening from outside — the book also teaches **how much water to drink and when**.
