# Video script — Non-communicable Diseases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some diseases do not spread from person to person.
Obesity is one of them — a chronic disease of excess body fat.
Too much high-calorie processed food. Too much sitting at screens.
Cuba's teachers spot it early with percentile tables,
guide parents on healthy eating, and get children playing more.
That is **non-communicable diseases** — and how to prevent them.
