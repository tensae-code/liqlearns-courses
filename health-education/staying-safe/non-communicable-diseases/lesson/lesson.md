## Sub lesson 1 — What obesity is — and how Cuba spots it (teacher guide — evidence)

Cuba's Grade 2 PE teacher book carries a full public-health chapter, and its obesity section — *Sugerencias para el trabajo con la obesidad: ¿qué es la obesidad?* — teaches that **obesity is a chronic disease of excess body fat**.

Its **conditioning factors** are named plainly: **hypercaloric processed food** and **sedentary screen habits**.

And Cuba does not leave spotting it to guesswork: overweight and obesity are identified with **Cuban BMI percentile tables** — **overweight is the 90–97th percentile, and obesity is above the 97th**. In Cuba's way, the PE teacher is made responsible for spotting, monitoring and preventing childhood overweight using those national tables.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preventing overweight and obesity — the teacher's work (teacher guide — evidence)

The book's next section — *Elementos básicos para el manejo del sobrepeso y la obesidad* — teaches **why obesity must be avoided**: it carries **cardiovascular risk, type 2 diabetes, and orthopaedic and psychosocial harm**.

Then it gives the **teacher's prevention work**:

1. **Early identification and weight monitoring.**
2. **Healthy-eating guidance to parents.**
3. **More active play and less sedentary screen time.**
