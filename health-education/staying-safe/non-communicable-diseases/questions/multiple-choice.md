# Non-communicable Diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Cuba book, obesity is…
a) A chronic disease of excess body fat  b) A disease that spreads from person to person  c) Only a matter of looks
**Answer: a) A chronic disease of excess body fat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cuba's BMI percentile tables call a child overweight at…
a) The 90–97th percentile  b) The 50th percentile  c) Any weight at all
**Answer: a) The 90–97th percentile**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Obesity in the tables is…
a) Above the 97th percentile  b) Below the 90th percentile  c) Exactly the 50th percentile
**Answer: a) Above the 97th percentile**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The teacher's prevention work includes…
a) Early identification and weight monitoring  b) Waiting until adulthood  c) More screen time
**Answer: a) Early identification and weight monitoring**
