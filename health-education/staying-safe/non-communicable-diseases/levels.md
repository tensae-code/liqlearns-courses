# Non-communicable Diseases — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — What obesity is — and how Cuba spots it (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: say what obesity is in the book's terms, name its conditioning factors, and state Cuba's percentile rule (overweight 90–97th percentile, obesity above 97th).

## Sub lesson 2 — Preventing overweight and obesity — the teacher's work (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name why obesity must be avoided and the teacher's prevention work — early identification and weight monitoring, healthy-eating guidance to parents, more active play and less sedentary screen time.
