# Hands-on — Non-communicable Diseases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the factors

Look at one ordinary day — yours or a child's you know. List where hypercaloric processed food appears and where sedentary screen time appears, as the Cuba book names those conditioning factors. Change one of each: swap one food, and turn one screen period into active play.

## The prevention list

Write the teacher's prevention work from the book as a checklist — early identification and weight monitoring, healthy-eating guidance to parents, more active play, less sedentary screen time — and tick which parts your own school or family already does.
