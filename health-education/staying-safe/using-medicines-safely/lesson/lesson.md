## Sub lesson 1 — Medicines and using them rationally

Turkey's *Sağlık Bilgisi ve Trafik Kültürü* (Health Knowledge & Traffic Culture) secondary elective student book teaches **medicines and using them rationally** — its section İlaçlar: Akılcı İlaç Kullanımı (Medicines: Rational Use of Medicines), covering painkillers and antibiotics. Turkey's one full health textbook joins health knowledge to traffic culture in a single elective.
