**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Namibia's guide, sport and education are used together against…
a) HIV  b) Rain  c) Nothing

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide names Namibia as…
a) A country with no HIV  b) One of the world's highest-prevalence countries for HIV  c) A country that does not teach about HIV
**Answer: b) One of the world's highest-prevalence countries for HIV**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Healthy Lifestyle Session 3, the HIV and AIDS key messages are taught through…
