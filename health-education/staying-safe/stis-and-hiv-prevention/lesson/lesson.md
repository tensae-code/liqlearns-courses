## Sub lesson 1 — Sport and education against HIV

Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide has a chapter called **Sport 4 Development in Namibia**. It teaches **using sport and education against HIV** — in a country the guide names as **one of the world's highest-prevalence countries** for HIV. Sport is not separate from the health teaching here: the games and the education work together against HIV. (This lesson comes from a teacher guide — it is the teachers' book for the PE sessions.)

## Sub lesson 2 — HIV and AIDS: key messages through games

The guide's **Healthy Lifestyle Session 3** teaches **HIV and AIDS through games**: the **key messages** about HIV and AIDS are carried by the games themselves, and worked through in the team circle, the way every Healthy Lifestyle session in this guide rides on a PE activity.
