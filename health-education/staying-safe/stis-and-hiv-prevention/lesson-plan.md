# Lesson plan — STIs and HIV Prevention

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Staying Safe · **Lesson:** STIs and HIV Prevention
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Staying Safe (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** STIs and HIV prevention — how sexually transmitted infections and HIV spread and do not spread, preventing them, and caring without stigma; taught here through Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide pass: sport and education used against HIV, and HIV and AIDS key messages taught through games.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Staying Safe title beside Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Sport and education against HIV** — Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Chapter 2, Sport 4 Development in Namibia — using sport and education against HIV in one of the world's highest-prevalence countries.
2. **HIV and AIDS: key messages through games** — Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Healthy Lifestyle Session 3 — HIV and AIDS key messages taught through games.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Preventing Sports Injuries and First Aid. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends

2. Say that Namibia is named in the guide as one of the world's highest-prevalence countries for HIV.
3. Say how the guide's Healthy Lifestyle session teaches HIV and AIDS: through games, with key messages.
