# Hands-on — STIs and HIV Prevention

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## A game that carries a message

Pick a simple game you know. Write down one HIV and AIDS key message — in your own words, from this lesson — that the game could carry, the way Namibia's guide teaches its key messages through games.

## Sport against HIV

Write two lines: one thing sport does in the guide's Sport 4 Development teaching, and one thing education does, both aimed against HIV.
