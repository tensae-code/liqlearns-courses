# Health Education course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Health Education is one world course. The first stocked part is
small and honest about why: Ethiopia teaches health inside its
Health and Physical Education books, and in the books in hand the
first true health-knowledge sections arrive at Grade 5 — staying
safe while exercising, and clean sport. The younger books'
cleanliness and body-care teaching stands in the Science course,
where Ethiopia's Environmental Science books carry it. This
course opens with what the HPE books themselves teach as health
knowledge, and grows as more countries' books are stocked.

**Staying Safe → Fair Play and the Body**

1. **Staying Safe** — exercising without harm: warm-up,
   progression, proper kit, rest, stopping on pain, cool-down —
   the precautions in the books' own order.
2. **Fair Play and the Body** — doping: what it is, what it does
   to the body and to sport, and why it is banned.
