# Fair Play in Sport — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The values carried by games and physical activities
Pass mark: say that the framework teaches identifying the values carried by games and physical activities, inside a theme of democratic culture, peace and non-violence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing conflict in and out of play
Pass mark: say that the framework teaches managing conflict in and out of play.

## Sub lesson 3 — Refereeing, the rules, and respect
Pass mark: say that the framework teaches learning refereeing and applying the rules, respecting authority and the adversary.

## Sub lesson 4 — Tournaments for peace and camaraderie
Pass mark: say that the framework teaches organising recreational tournaments for peace and camaraderie at school.
