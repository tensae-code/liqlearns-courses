# Hands-on — Fair Play in Sport

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The values in a game

Pick a game you know. Write down the values it carries — the good things playing it teaches — the way the framework's first step teaches: identifying the values carried by games and physical activities.

## A tournament for peace

Plan a small recreational tournament at school on paper: the games, who plays, who referees, and the rules everyone agrees to apply. At the top, write its purpose the way the framework names it: peace and camaraderie.
