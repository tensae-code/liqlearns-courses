# Lesson plan — Fair Play in Sport

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Fair Play and the Body · **Lesson:** Fair Play in Sport
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Fair Play and the Body (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fair play in sport — sportsmanship, sporting values and ethics, responsibility in sport, and answering aggression and violence in sport without violence; taught here through Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre pass: fair play as democratic culture — the values carried by games, managing conflict, refereeing and respect, and tournaments for peace.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Fair Play and the Body title beside Family and Friends.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **The values carried by games and physical activities** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): identifying the values carried by games and physical activities.
2. **Managing conflict in and out of play** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): managing conflict in and out of play.
3. **Refereeing, the rules, and respect** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): learning refereeing and applying the rules, respecting authority and the adversary.
4. **Tournaments for peace and camaraderie** — Haiti's Grades 10–13 Programme-Cadre teaching (teacher programme — evidence): organising recreational tournaments for peace and camaraderie at school.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Family and Friends. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name values that games and physical activities carry.
2. Say how conflict in and out of play is managed in the framework's teaching.
3. Say what refereeing asks of a player: applying the rules, and respecting authority and the adversary.
4. Say what the school tournaments are for: peace and camaraderie.
