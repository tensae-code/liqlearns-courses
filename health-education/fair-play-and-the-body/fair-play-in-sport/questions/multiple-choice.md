# Fair Play in Sport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Haiti's framework, fair play is taught as…
a) Civic education — democratic culture, peace and non-violence  b) Only winning  c) A punishment
**Answer: a) Civic education — democratic culture, peace and non-violence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The framework teaches managing conflict…
a) Only inside the game  b) In and out of play  c) Never
**Answer: b) In and out of play**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Learning refereeing includes…
a) Applying the rules and respecting authority and the adversary  b) Changing the rules to win  c) Ignoring the referee
**Answer: a) Applying the rules and respecting authority and the adversary**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The school recreational tournaments are organised for…
a) Peace and camaraderie  b) Money prizes  c) Leaving people out
**Answer: a) Peace and camaraderie**
