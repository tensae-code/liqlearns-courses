# Lesson plan — Getting Along and Solving Conflicts

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Fair Play and the Body · **Lesson:** Getting Along and Solving Conflicts
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Fair Play and the Body (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Getting along and solving conflicts: communication, solving conflicts peacefully, standing up to bullying, and answering anger without violence — taught here through Pakistan's way: its Urdu-medium Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Health and Physical Education books, Grades 6–10.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Fair Play and the Body title beside Family and Friends and Making Good Decisions.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Conflicts** — Pakistan's Grade 6 book teaches Chapter 6: Conflicts (تنازعات) — what conflicts are and settling them peacefully (printed p. 39).
2. **Conflict Resolution Management** — Pakistan's Grade 7 book teaches Chapter 8: Conflict Resolution Management (تنازعات کے حل کا انتظام) (printed p. 62).
3. **Precautions for Dealing with Disputes** — Pakistan's Grade 8 book teaches Chapter 6: Precautions for Dealing with Disputes — handling quarrels safely (printed p. 55).
4. **Bullying** — Pakistan's Grade 9 book teaches Chapter 6: Bullying (غنڈہ گردی) (printed p. 30).
5. **Prevention of Bullying in School** — Pakistan's Grade 10 book teaches Chapter 4: Prevention of Bullying in School (سکول میں غنڈہ گردی کی روک تھام) (printed p. 21).
