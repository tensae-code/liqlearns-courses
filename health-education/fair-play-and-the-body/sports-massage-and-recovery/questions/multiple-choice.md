# Sports Massage and Recovery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide's teaching, massage acts on…
a) The nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton and skin  b) Only the skin  c) Only the hair
**Answer: a) The nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton and skin**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Massage's three sporting uses are…
a) Before, between and after competition  b) Only after competition  c) Only in training
**Answer: a) Before, between and after competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before a game, massage is used to…
a) Reduce the athlete's pre-game anxiety  b) Replace warming up  c) Choose the team
**Answer: a) Reduce the athlete's pre-game anxiety**
