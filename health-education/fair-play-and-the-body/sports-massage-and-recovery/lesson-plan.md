# Lesson plan — Sports Massage and Recovery

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Fair Play and the Body · **Lesson:** Sports Massage and Recovery
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Fair Play and the Body (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sports massage and recovery — what massage does for muscles, circulation and the mind, and its uses before, between and after competition; taught here through Afghanistan's Grade 10 pass: what massage does for the nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton and skin, its three sporting uses (before, between and after competition), and massage to reduce an athlete's pre-game anxiety (teacher guide — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Fair Play and the Body title beside The Athlete's Mind.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What massage does for the body** — Afghanistan's Grade 10 teaching (teacher guide — evidence): what massage does for the nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton and skin.
2. **Massage before, between and after competition — and calming pre-game anxiety** — Afghanistan's Grade 10 teaching (teacher guide — evidence): massage's three sporting uses — before, between and after competition — and massage to reduce an athlete's pre-game anxiety.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Athlete's Mind. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five parts of the body the guide says massage acts on: the nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton and skin.
2. Name massage's three sporting uses in the guide: before, between and after competition.
3. Say what massage can do for an athlete before a game, in the guide's teaching: reduce pre-game anxiety.
