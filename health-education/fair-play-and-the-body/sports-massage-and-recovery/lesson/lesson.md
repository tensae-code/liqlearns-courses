# Sports Massage and Recovery

## Do it first

An athlete's muscles are worked hard in a game. What could massage — skilled rubbing and pressing of the body — do for that athlete? Say what you think first — then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What massage does for the body

Afghanistan's Grade 10 Teaching Guide for Physical Education teaches **sports massage** (teacher guide — evidence): massage does something for **five parts of the body** — the **nervous system**, the **muscles**, **blood circulation**, the **skeleton** and the **skin**.

Massage is body care and recovery in the guide: it works on the body as a whole, not on one sore spot alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Massage before, between and after competition — and calming pre-game anxiety

The guide gives massage **three sporting uses**: **before** competition, **between** competitions, and **after** competition.

And massage has a job for the mind as well as the body: it is used **to reduce an athlete's pre-game anxiety** — the worry before the game.

In Afghanistan's own way, this sits inside the Grade 10 PE guide's theory course — the course that also makes the PE teacher the school's first-aider — where sports massage is taught as part of the teacher's body-care knowledge.
