# Hands-on — Sports Massage and Recovery

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five parts, one massage

Write the guide's five parts of the body as five headings — nervous system, muscles, blood circulation, skeleton, skin. The guide teaches that massage does something for each one; under each heading, write the heading's name again in your own words as a label for what a massage giver is working on.

## Before, between, after

Draw a competition day as a line with three marks — before, between, after. At each mark, write the guide's use of massage at that time, and at the "before" mark add the guide's second job: reducing the athlete's pre-game anxiety.

## Calm before the game

Before your next hard task — a game, a test, a performance — sit quietly for two minutes and breathe slowly, the calm the guide seeks from pre-game massage. Write one line on how your body and mind felt before and after.
