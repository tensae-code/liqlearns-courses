## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing yourself

China's Senior High *PEP PE & Health* book teaches **growing mental health** in its section on **mental health and social adaptation** （心理健康与社会适应）. Its first named part is **knowing yourself**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Adapting to society

The same section's second named part is **adapting to society** — fitting into the life around you as you grow.

In China's own way, mental health is taught inside the behaviour-shaping chapter, beside lifestyle, nutrition and environment: the mind and social adaptation are part of health itself.

## Say it to your companion
