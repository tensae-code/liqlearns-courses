# Hands-on — Mental Health and Wellbeing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Knowing myself

China's book names knowing yourself as part of growing mental health. Write three short lines about yourself — one thing you are good at, one thing that is hard for you, and one thing you want to get better at.

## Adapting to society

Write about one place where you have to fit in with other people — home, school, a team, or another group. Name one thing that helps you fit in there, and one thing that makes it hard.
