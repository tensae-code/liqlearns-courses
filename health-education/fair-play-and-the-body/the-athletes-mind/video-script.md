# Video script — The Athlete's Mind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the body performs, the mind gets ready.
Arousal — awake and ready, not flat, not frantic.
Stress and anxiety — managed, not left to run the show.
Motivation — the reason that keeps you going.
That is **the athlete's mind** — prepared, like the body is trained.
