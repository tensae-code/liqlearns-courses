## Sub lesson 1 — Mental preparation for performance

The United Kingdom's GCSE Physical Education student book teaches **mental preparation for performance** in three named parts:

1. **Arousal** — the mind and body's state of readiness for performing.
2. **Stress and anxiety management** — stress and anxiety are part of performance, and the book teaches managing them.
3. **Motivation** — the drive that keeps a performer working toward performance.

Mental preparation stands beside physical training in the book: the mind is prepared the way the body is trained. This lesson's wider framing calls this mental hygiene in sport — pressure, concentration, imagery, goal-setting, motivation and confidence.

In the United Kingdom's own way, this sits inside PE at GCSE as an examined academic subject — the psychology of sport taught as knowledge beside anatomy, physiology and training theory.

## Say it to your companion
