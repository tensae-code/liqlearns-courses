# Hands-on — The Athlete's Mind

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Before a hard task

Pick one hard task this week — a test, a game, a performance. Before it, work through the book's three parts in writing: note your arousal (flat, ready, or frantic), name one way you will manage stress and anxiety, and write your motivation — the reason you want to do it well. After the task, mark what helped.

## Watch the readiness line

Across one day of practice or study, note three moments — before, during, after — and rate your readiness at each: too flat, ready, too frantic. The book's lesson is that performance needs arousal managed, not ignored.
