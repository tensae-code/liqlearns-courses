# The Athlete's Mind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's mental preparation has three named parts. They are…
a) Arousal, stress and anxiety management, and motivation  b) Sleep, food and kit  c) Speed, strength and stamina
**Answer: a) Arousal, stress and anxiety management, and motivation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Arousal is…
a) The mind and body's state of readiness for performing  b) A kind of injury  c) A training diet
**Answer: a) The mind and body's state of readiness for performing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's teaching, stress and anxiety in performance are…
a) Managed  b) Impossible to feel  c) A reason to stop all sport
**Answer: a) Managed**
