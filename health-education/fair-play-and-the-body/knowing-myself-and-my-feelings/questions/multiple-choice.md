# Knowing Myself and My Feelings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoa's curriculum teaches personal identity together with…
a) Self-esteem  b) Swimming  c) First aid
**Answer: a) Self-esteem**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these does Samoa's identity teaching name?
a) Stereotyping and media  b) Road rules  c) Food groups
**Answer: a) Stereotyping and media**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Samoa's teaching names the challenges of…
a) Teenage life  b) Old age only  c) Toddler life
**Answer: a) Teenage life**
