# Hands-on — Knowing Myself and My Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Who I am

Samoa's curriculum teaches personal identity — who you are and where you are from. Write three sentences: who you are, where you are from, and one personal strength.

## Messages around me

Samoa's curriculum names social messages, stereotyping and media. Write one social message you have heard and say whether it helps or harms self-esteem, as this lesson teaches it.
