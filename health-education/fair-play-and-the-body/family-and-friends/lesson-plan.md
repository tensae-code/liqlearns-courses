# Lesson plan — Family and Friends

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Fair Play and the Body · **Lesson:** Family and Friends
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Fair Play and the Body (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** family and friends — family roles and love, choosing and keeping friends, manners and respect, diversity and including everyone; taught here through Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide pass: healthy relationships, and gender and sex — sex is not gender.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Fair Play and the Body title beside The Athlete's Mind.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Healthy relationships** — Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Healthy Lifestyle Session 5 — healthy relationships.
2. **Gender and sex: sex is not gender** — Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaching (teacher guide — evidence): Healthy Lifestyle Session 8 — gender and sex, teaching that sex is not gender.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
