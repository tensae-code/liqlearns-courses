## Sub lesson 1 — Healthy relationships

Namibia's Grades 10–11 PE4Life guide teaches **healthy relationships** in its **Healthy Lifestyle Session 5**. Like every Healthy Lifestyle session in this guide, it rides on a PE activity and works through its key messages and discussion points in the **team circle** — the team itself is where relationships are practised. (This lesson comes from a teacher guide — it is the teachers' book for the PE sessions.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Gender and sex: sex is not gender

The guide's **Healthy Lifestyle Session 8** teaches **gender and sex**, and it teaches the difference plainly: **sex is not gender**. The two words do not mean the same thing, and the session works through that difference in the team circle.

## Say it to your companion
