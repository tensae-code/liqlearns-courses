# Hands-on — Family and Friends

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## My team circle

Write the names of your own team — family, friends, classmates. Beside each name, write one thing that keeps that relationship healthy, the way Session 5 teaches healthy relationships.

## Two words, two meanings

Write two short lines: one headed "sex", one headed "gender". Under each, write in your own words what the guide's Session 8 teaching — sex is not gender — means for that word.
