# Video script — Doping — What It Is and What It Costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A tablet is offered. "Faster — no training needed."
That is doping: strength NOT earned by nature or training.
Cheating — AND harm. That is why the world bans it.
1968: the first tests. 1999: WADA. Ethiopia has its own anti-doping office.
The cost? Health. Mind. Name. Money. Career. Win honestly — or do not call it winning.
