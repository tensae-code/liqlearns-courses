# Doping — What It Is and What It Costs — true or false

Say if it is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Doping closes the Grade 5 book's health-and-fitness chapter.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Doping gives strength earned by nature and training.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dependence and craving are among doping's harms to the mind.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A ban can cover competing, training and coaching.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The IAAF banned stimulants from 1928.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** A doping ban is only the beginning — health, mental-health, social and financial results follow.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Doping is fundamentally contrary to the spirit of sport.
**Answer: True**
