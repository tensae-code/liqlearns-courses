# Doping — What It Is and What It Costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Doping means using…
a) Performance-enhancing substances  b) Proper sport kit  c) A warm-up
**Answer: a) Performance-enhancing substances**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Doping is forbidden because it is…
a) Both cheating and harmful  b) Too expensive  c) A secret
**Answer: a) Both cheating and harmful**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first doping tests in sport were held at…
a) Mexico 1968  b) Athens 1896  c) Sydney 2000
**Answer: a) Mexico 1968**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** WADA, the world anti-doping agency, was founded in…
a) 1999  b) 1967  c) 2010
**Answer: a) 1999**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is NOT one of the areas in the Grade 6 harm map?
a) Cooking  b) Health  c) The economy
**Answer: a) Cooking**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A caught elite athlete's income…
a) Stops — fines and lost earnings  b) Doubles  c) Is protected
**Answer: a) Stops — fines and lost earnings**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The longest ban the Grade 7 guide gives is…
a) Life, in repeat or most serious cases  b) One week  c) One race
**Answer: a) Life, in repeat or most serious cases**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Under Sanction, a caught athlete's results are…
a) Disqualified, medals forfeited  b) Kept quietly  c) Improved
**Answer: a) Disqualified, medals forfeited**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Doping's recorded use goes back to…
a) 668 BCE  b) 1896  c) 1999
**Answer: a) 668 BCE**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** The third offence's sanction in the Grade 8 book is…
a) A lifetime ban  b) A warning  c) One month
**Answer: a) A lifetime ban**

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** Sport, says the book, is a contest of…
a) Athletes — not doctors and drug factories  b) Factories  c) Medicines
**Answer: a) Athletes — not doctors and drug factories**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Non-sterile or shared needles in doping risk…
a) Hepatitis and HIV/AIDS  b) Better vision  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Hepatitis and HIV/AIDS**

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** Under anti-doping rules, 'not knowing'…
a) Is not an excuse  b) Cancels the ban  c) Wins the medal back
**Answer: a) Is not an excuse**

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** The 2021 WADA Code has…
a) 27 articles  b) 3 articles  c) 100 articles
**Answer: a) 27 articles**

**15. (Sub lesson 7)** National anti-doping programmes work through…
a) Education, deterrence and detection  b) Silence  c) Advertising
**Answer: a) Education, deterrence and detection**

**16. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Grade 7 guide's review answers, doping affects…
a) mental and physical health and social interaction, and it brings disqualification  b) only the athlete's shoes  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) mental and physical health and social interaction, and it brings disqualification**
