# Doping — What It Is and What It Costs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The parts run from the youngest book's teaching to the oldest, as the placements stack them.

## Sub lesson 1 — What doping is — honest sport
Pass mark: define doping and say which chapter of the Grade 5 book it closes, and why that place fits.

## Sub lesson 2 — Banned — and the fight against it (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: give the guide's definition and the four dates of the fight in order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Doping's harms — the Grade 6 map
Pass mark: name the four areas of the student book's harm map and one harm in each of the guide's three families.

## Sub lesson 4 — What doping costs — the Grade 7 reckoning
Pass mark: name the four consequence families and the longest ban the guide gives.

## Sub lesson 5 — Stopping doping — history, rules and bans (Grade 8)
Pass mark: order the fight's dates (668 BCE use; 1928 IAAF ban; 1966 federation testing; 1967 IOC ban; 1999 WADA) and give the three offences' sanctions as printed.

## Sub lesson 6 — Consequences — health, sanctions and a ruined name (Grade 9)
Pass mark: name one health consequence (needle infection risk: hepatitis/HIV) and two sport-life consequences (disqualification/forfeiture; reputation damage or isolation), and state the 'not knowing' rule.

## Sub lesson 7 — The Anti-Doping Rules — the WADA Code (Grade 10)
Pass mark: say what the 2021 Code's 27 articles govern, who applies the Code, and the guide's spirit-of-sport line.
