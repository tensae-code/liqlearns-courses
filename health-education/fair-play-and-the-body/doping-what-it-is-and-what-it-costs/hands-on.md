# Hands-on — Doping — What It Is and What It Costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The clean-sport pledge wall

Each learner writes one line — what they will win honestly and how — and the lines are posted together as the class's clean-sport wall.

## Cost the tablet

In groups, take the Do It First offer and price it by the guide's four families: what could that one tablet cost the runner in health, name, money and career? Present the reckoning.
