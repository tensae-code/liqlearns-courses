# Lesson plan — Making Good Decisions

**Course:** Health Education · **Title:** Fair Play and the Body · **Lesson:** Making Good Decisions
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Fair Play and the Body (Health Education course).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** life skills, interpersonal communication, decision-making and peer pressure — as Sierra Leone's AHLS Teacher's Manual teaches them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in the Fair Play and the Body title beside Knowing Myself and My Feelings.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs here are 2 seconds, drawn, and show the movement the way it really moves.

## Sub lessons
1. **What life skills are** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS1 Lesson 3.1: Concept of life skills — what life skills are and why adolescents need them (printed p. 33).
2. **Interpersonal communication skills** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS1 Lesson 3.3: Interpersonal communication skills — listening, speaking and relating to others (printed p. 38).
3. **Decision-making skills** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS1 Lesson 3.4: Decision-making skills — how to make considered decisions (printed p. 41).
4. **Good and bad peer pressure** — Sierra Leone's teaching: SSS1 Lesson 3.5: Good and bad peer pressure — recognising peer pressure and responding to it (printed p. 43).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lessons — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lessons' whole question set is the gate pool.
